# wicklow_educate_together_secondary_school_2019_20 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users affiliated with Wicklow Educate Together, a secondary school, for the period 2019-20. It is intended to gather details such as form name, role, school category, and submission date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form name.
2. Select the form type (Student, Teacher, Parent, or Staff).
3. Choose the relevant category (Education or Administration).
4. Enter the date and time of submission.
5. Enter the name of the school.
6. Type a description of the form.
7. Add any comments or remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief name for the form, such as "Form 1" or "Secondary School Survey".
* **Form Type** (`form_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of form you are submitting, which could be "Student", "Teacher", "Parent", or "Staff".
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the category that best describes the form, which might be "Education" or "Administration".
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting the form in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting the form in the correct 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Type in the name of the school you are affiliated with.
* **Description** (`form_description`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of the form, explaining its purpose or relevance.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about the form.

## Tips
* Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
* If you need help with form submissions, contact form@gic.mx.
