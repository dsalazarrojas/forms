# Educational Outreach Impact Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Outreach Impact Award Nomination Form is designed to collect information for the nomination or recommendation of outstanding educational institutions or individuals for the Educational Outreach Impact Award.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing the required information for the nomination or recommendation.
- Ensure that you are submitting a new nomination or recommendation, not duplicating an existing one.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false):
  First name of the person being nominated or the person making the nomination.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false):
  Last name of the person being nominated or the person making the nomination.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false):
  Email address of the person being nominated or the person making the nomination. 
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false):
  A field for email information, not further specified.
* **Award Category** (`award_category`, select_multiple, required: false):
  Select one or more from Award for Educational Leadership, Award for Community Service, Award for Innovative Practices.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false):
  Name of the school or institution being nominated or the nominee's school.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required: false):
  Address of the school or institution being nominated or the nominee's school.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required: false):
  Email address of the school or institution being nominated or the nominee's school.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required: false):
  Phone number of the school or institution being nominated or the nominee's school.
* **Committee Name** (`committee_name`, text, required: false):
  Name of the committee or group associated with the school or institution being nominated or the nominee's school.
* **Committee Email** (`committee_email`, email, required: false):
  Email address of the committee or group associated with the school or institution being nominated or the nominee's school.
* **Committee Phone** (`committee_phone`, text, required: false):
  Phone number of the committee or group associated with the school or institution being nominated or the nominee's school.
* **Award Year** (`award_year`, select_one, required: false):
  Select the year of the award for which you are nominating or recommending.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required: false):
  Brief description of the project or work being nominated or recommended.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required: false):
  Name of the person making the nomination or recommendation.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`, text, required: false):
  Title of the person making the nomination or recommendation.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required: false):
  Email address of the person making the nomination or recommendation.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, required: false):
  Phone number of the person making the nomination or recommendation.
* **Nominator Organization** (`nominator_organization`, text, required: false):
  Name of the organization associated with the person making the nomination or recommendation.
* **Nominator Position** (`nominator_position`, text, required: false):
  Position of the person making the nomination or recommendation within their organization.
* **Nomination Type** (`nomination_type`, select_multiple, required: false):
  Select one or more from Nomination or Recommendation.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required: false):
  Date of the nomination or recommendation.
* **Nominator Note** (`nominator_note`, note, required: false):
  Additional comments or information from the person making the nomination or recommendation.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, text, required: false):
  Website of the school or institution being nominated or the nominee's school.
* **School Facebook** (`school_facebook`, text, required: false):
  Facebook page of the school or institution being nominated or the nominee's school.
