# Medical Academy Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help potential students of the Medical Academy to express their interest in pursuing education and training in various medical specialties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your preferred medical specialty.
3. Provide any additional information about yourself, if needed.
4. Check the box indicating whether you consent to the academy's terms and conditions.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, required: false): This field is a placeholder for the form header and is not a required field.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the academy's records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted by the academy's admissions team.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_one, required: false): Select the medical specialty that interests you the most.
	+ Cardiology: The study of the heart and blood vessels.
	+ Dermatology: The study of skin and its disorders.
	+ Endocrinology: The study of hormones and glands.
	+ Gastroenterology: The study of the digestive system.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please describe them in this field.
* **Preferred Specialty** (`specialty_preference`, select_one, required: false): Select the medical specialty that you are most interested in pursuing.
	+ Cardiology: The study of the heart and blood vessels.
	+ Dermatology: The study of skin and its disorders.
	+ Endocrinology: The study of hormones and glands.
	+ Gastroenterology: The study of the digestive system.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): If you have any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to your application, please provide it in this field.
* **Consent** (`consent`, checkbox, required: false): Check the box to indicate that you have read and agree to the academy's terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to submit your application.
