# School Counselor Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for school counselors to document and track student interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Student Name** field with the student's full name.
2. Enter the **Counselor Name** field with your own name and title (e.g., "John Doe, School Counselor").
3. Select the **School Year** field to indicate the student's current school year (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
4. For the **Student Grade** field, select one or multiple relevant grade levels (e.g., 9, 10, 11, or 12) to indicate the student's grade level.
5. In the **Reason for Counseling** field, provide a brief explanation for the student's presence in counseling.
6. Describe the **Counseling Outcomes** field to detail the results of the session.
7. In the **Additional Notes** field, enter any additional information or comments relevant to the student's session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Counselor Name** (`counselor_name`, text, required): Enter your own name and title.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, select_one, optional): Select the student's current school year (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple grade levels relevant to the student (e.g., 9, 10, 11, or 12).
* **Reason for Counseling** (`reason_for_counseling`, text, optional): Briefly explain why the student is in counseling.
* **Counseling Outcomes** (`counseling_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the results of the counseling session.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or information about the student's session.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool or resource assigned to the student (e.g., "chatjimmy" or "Other").
