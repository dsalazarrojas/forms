# Educational Technology Prototype Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Technology Prototype Application Form is a tool to gather information from users who want to propose a new educational technology idea. This form will help us understand their idea, its potential, and feasibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for your educational technology proposal.
2. Enter your name and email address so we can contact you.
3. Describe your proposal in the description field.
4. Choose the correct options for "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.
5. If you need to provide more information, use the "Note" field.
6. Finally, submit your proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`edtech_title`, text, optional): Enter a descriptive title for your proposal.
* **Name** (`edtech_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Description** (`edtech_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your proposal.
* **Email** (`edtech_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`edtech_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Select One** (`edtech_select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the options below.
	+ Option A
	+ Option B
	+ Option C
* **Select Multiple** (`edtech_select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the options below.
	+ Option A
	+ Option B
	+ Option C
* **Date** (`edtech_date`, date, optional): Enter a date.
* **Time** (`edtech_time`, time, optional): Enter a time.
* **Note** (`edtech_note`, note, optional): If you need to provide more information, use this field.
* **Submit** (`edtech_submit`, text, optional): Click the submit button to send your proposal.
