# Proportional Relationships Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proportional Relationships Assessment form is designed to evaluate the understanding of proportional relationships between variables in an educational context.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name and email address as the teacher.
2. Enter the student's name and age.
3. Choose the math problem you would like to assess.
4. Provide the student's answer to the math problem.
5. Enter your answer to the math problem as the teacher.
6. If applicable, select the correct answer from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the teacher.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Age** (`student_age`, number, optional): Enter the student's age.
* **Math Problem** (`math_problem`, text, optional): Select the math problem you would like to assess.
* **Student Answer** (`student_answer`, text, optional): Provide the student's answer to the math problem.
* **Teacher Answer** (`teacher_answer`, text, optional): Provide your answer as the teacher.
* **Correct Answer** (`correct_answer`, select_one, optional): Select the correct answer from the provided options.
