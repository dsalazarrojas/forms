# Judaism Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Judaism Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge on the topic of Judaism, specifically in the areas of education and community. The quiz is meant to be a comprehensive test of your understanding of various aspects of Judaism, and the results are used to gauge your proficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, follow these steps:

1.  Review the introduction page and ensure you understand the context of the quiz.
2.  Select your category of interest: Jewish Education, Jewish Community, or Other.
3.  Enter your name, if you'd like to be identified.
4.  Provide your email address, if you'd like to receive feedback on your results.
5.  Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
6.  Select your result from the provided options.
7.  Add any additional notes or comments, if necessary.
8.  Select the date of the quiz.
9.  Submit the form to receive your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`id: 1`): This is the initial page of the quiz, providing background information and context.
*   **Category** (`id: 2`): Choose the category that best describes your interest in Judaism: Jewish Education, Jewish Community, or Other.
*   **Name** (`id: 3`): Enter your name, if you'd like to be identified. This field is optional.
*   **Email** (`id: 4`): Enter your email address, if you'd like to receive feedback on your results. This field is optional.
*   **Result** (`id: 5`): Select your score from the provided options: 100%, 75-89%, or 90-100%. This field is optional.
*   **Notes** (`id: 6`): Add any additional comments or notes, if necessary. This field is optional.
*   **Quiz Date** (`id: 7`): Enter the date you took the quiz. This field is optional.
*   **Submit** (`id: 8`): Click the "Submit" button to receive your results.

## Tips

*   Answer the questions carefully and honestly to ensure accurate results.
*   Make sure to select the correct category that best describes your interest in Judaism.
*   Enter your email address, if you'd like to receive feedback on your results.
*   Use the "Notes" field to provide any additional context or explanations.
*   Select the correct date you took the quiz.
