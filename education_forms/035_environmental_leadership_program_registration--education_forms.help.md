# Environmental Leadership Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Environmental Leadership Program Registration form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to register for the program. This form helps to gather essential details about the participants, their availability, training commitments, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please follow these steps:
  1. Fill in your participant details.
  2. Enter your name and email address.
  3. Choose your availability and training commitment.
  4. Select your participant school and nonprofit organization (if applicable).
  5. Provide your community group (if applicable).
  6. Describe your participant commitment.
  7. Choose a training program that suits your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to capture any additional information about the participant that is not specified elsewhere in the form.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant who is registering for the program.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the participant who is registering for the program.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the available dates and times for the participant.
* **Training Commitment** (`training_commitment`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the level of commitment the participant is willing to make for the training program.
* **Participant School** (`participant_school`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant's school (if applicable).
* **Nonprofit Organization** (`nonprofit_organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant's nonprofit organization (if applicable).
* **Community Group** (`community_group`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant's community group (if applicable).
* **Participant Commitment** (`participant_commitment`, text, required/optional): Describe the participant's commitment to the program.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the training program that suits the participant's needs.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any issues or concerns while completing the form, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
