# Nursery Year End Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for providing feedback about our nursery program. We value your input and would like to hear your thoughts on your experience with our nursery. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey and help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by providing a brief title for the feedback you'd like to give us.
2. Enter your name and email address so we can get in touch with you.
3. Share your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
4. Describe your overall experience at our nursery.
5. Tell us about the teaching quality you received.
6. Share your thoughts on the care your child received.
7. Select any areas where you think we could improve.
8. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback about Our Nursery** (`nursery_year_end_feedback_survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of your feedback.
- **Name** (`nursery_year_end_feedback_survey_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
- **Email** (`nursery_year_end_feedback_survey_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
- **Phone** (`nursery_year_end_feedback_survey_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like us to contact you, please enter your phone number.
- **Overall Experience** (`nursery_year_end_feedback_survey_experience`, text, optional): Describe your overall experience at our nursery.
- **Teaching** (`nursery_year_end_feedback_survey_teaching`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the teaching quality you received.
- **Care** (`nursery_year_end_feedback_survey_care`, text, optional): Tell us about the care your child received.
- **Area of Improvement** (`nursery_year_end_feedback_survey_improvements`, select_multiple, optional): Select any areas where you think we could improve.
- **Additional Comments** (`nursery_year_end_feedback_survey_additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
- Please keep your answers brief and to the point.
- Be honest and specific about your experience.
- If you have any concerns or complaints, please let us know so we can address them.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, we're all ears and appreciate your input.
