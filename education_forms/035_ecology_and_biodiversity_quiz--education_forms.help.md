# Ecology And Biodiversity Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their experience with ecology and biodiversity. It's essential to provide honest and accurate responses to help us better understand this crucial subject.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers to the quiz questions on **page 1**.
2. Enter your name in the **Name** field on **page 2**.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field on **page 2**.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field on **page 2**.
5. Provide the date of your most recent experience related to ecology and biodiversity in the **Date** field on **page 3**.
6. Choose a time of day for your most recent experience in the **Time** field on **page 3**.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Note** field on **page 3**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz**: Please select all the options that apply to your experience with ecology and biodiversity. You can choose one or multiple options.
  * 'Yes' - You have experience with ecology and biodiversity.
  * 'No' - You do not have experience with ecology and biodiversity.
* **Name**: Enter your full name, as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your most recent experience related to ecology and biodiversity.
* **Time**: Choose a time of day for your most recent experience.
* **Note**: Add any additional information or comments about your experience.
