# music_lesson_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_lesson_intake_form is a questionnaire designed to collect information from students who want to schedule music lessons with an instructor. The form is meant to be a helpful way for students to communicate their needs and interests to the instructor before booking a lesson.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply fill out the form fields as prompted. You can skip any fields you are not comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to be used for communication purposes.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to be used for communication purposes.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for lesson scheduling and communication.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for lesson scheduling and communication.
* **Birthdate** (Birthdate, date, optional): Enter your date of birth, if you wish to be grouped in a specific age category for lesson planning.
* **Instrument** (Instrument, select_one, optional): Select one or more instruments you would like to learn or are currently studying. This will help the instructor tailor the lessons to your needs.
* **Music Lesson Goals** (Music Lesson Goals, text, optional): Describe your goals or expectations for the music lessons. For example, "I want to learn to play a new song" or "I want to improve my technique."
* **Available Time** (Available Time, time, optional): Enter the time slots you are available for lessons. This will help the instructor schedule your lessons at a convenient time.
