# Student Leadership Project Funding Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for student leadership project funding requests. It helps the funding committee review and evaluate the projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form carefully** before starting to fill it out.
2. **Fill out all required fields**, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. **Provide accurate and detailed information** for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name**: Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description**: Describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Funding Amount**: Enter the amount of funding you are requesting (e.g., $1,000).
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as the student lead or contact person.
* **Contact Person**: Enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **School Year**: Select the year in which you are currently studying (e.g., 2024-2025).
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level (e.g., High School, College/University).
* **Project Type**: Select the type of project you are proposing (e.g., Student-led Project, Faculty-led Project, Other).
* **Project Type Description**: Describe the type of project you are proposing (e.g., if you selected "Other", provide a brief description).
* **Project Budget**: Enter the budget for your project.
* **Budget Justification**: Explain how you plan to use the funds (e.g., equipment, travel).
* **Student Contact Information**: Enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **School/University**: Enter your school or university name.
* **Student Project Category**: Enter your project category.
* **Faculty Support**: List the faculty members who support your project.
* **Project Timeline**: Enter the dates for your project (e.g., start and end dates).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time**: Enter the time zone you are in.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Upload File**: Upload any relevant files or documents supporting your project.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any other relevant information about your project.

Note: The form is designed to be filled out by the student lead or contact person. If you have any questions or need help, please reach out to your institution's administration or funding committee.
