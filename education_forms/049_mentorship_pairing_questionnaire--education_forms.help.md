# Mentorship Pairing Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help the system find suitable mentors or mentees based on your preferences and needs. It's essential to answer the questions honestly to ensure accurate pairing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, thinking about what would be the best fit for you in a mentorship.
2. Be honest in your responses, as they will help the system create the most suitable pairing.
3. If you're unsure about a question, try to answer based on your best understanding.
4. Review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1** (`page_1`, not applicable, not required): This page serves as an introduction to the mentorship pairing process. It does not require input.
* **Page 2** (`page_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to your needs:
	+ `Yes`
	+ `No`
* **Page 3** (`page_3`, text, required): Describe your goals and objectives for the mentorship. Be specific about what you aim to achieve or learn.
* **Page 4** (`page_4`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you can use for communication with your mentor or mentee. This will be used to contact you about the pairing.

## Tips
* Be open-minded and flexible when considering potential pairings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the system administrators for assistance.
* Remember that accuracy and honesty in your responses will lead to the best possible pairing.
