# philippine-history-quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The philippine-history-quiz form is designed to test users' knowledge of historical events and figures from the Philippines. It is meant to be a fun and engaging way to learn about the country's rich history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "start" field, which is the beginning of the quiz.
2. Answer the questions posed to you, selecting the correct options from the drop-down menus or entering your answers in the text fields.
3. For each question, choose the correct answer from the options provided.
4. Take note of any additional comments or questions you have in the "note" field.
5. If you'd like, enter your name in the "submitted-by" field and a timestamp in the "submitted-on" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field, if you'd like to be contacted about your submission.
7. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
8. Finally, click the "end" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start** (text, required: no): Enter the starting point of the quiz.
* **question1** (date, required: no): Enter the date of a historical event.
* **answer1** (select one, required: no): Choose the correct answer to question 1.
* **question2** (time, required: no): Enter the time of a historical event.
* **answer2** (select multiple, required: no): Choose multiple correct answers to question 2.
* **question3** (text, required: no): Enter any additional information about question 3.
* **note** (note, required: no): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **submitted-on** (time, required: no): Enter the timestamp of submission.
* **submitted-by** (text, required: no): Enter your name.
* **email** (email, required: no): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required: no): Enter your phone number.
* **end** (text, required: no): Enter the end of the quiz.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate dates and times for the historical events.
* Choose the correct answers for each question based on your knowledge of Philippine history.
* Enter any additional comments or questions in the "note" field.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, don't hesitate to ask your teacher or instructor.
