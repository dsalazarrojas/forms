# Future of Learning Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from individuals interested in pursuing a career in education, professional development, and innovation. The information gathered will be reviewed by the selection committee to assess potential eligibility for the Future of Learning Initiative.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and honest information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is for providing any relevant personal details that you believe would be helpful for the selection committee to know. Please keep this brief and concise.
* **Background Information** (`Background Information`, `text`, optional)
	+ In this field, please provide a brief overview of your background, including any relevant work experience, education, or other relevant information.
* **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you agree to share your contact information and other personal details with the selection committee.
* **Background Interests** (`Background Interests`, `text`, optional)
	+ In this field, please list any background interests that you think would be relevant for the selection committee to consider.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, optional)
	+ Please provide your email address or other contact information for future correspondence.
* **Goals** (`Goals`, `number`, optional)
	+ If applicable, please specify the number of years you would like to commit to the Future of Learning Initiative.
* **Age** (`Age`, `number`, optional)
	+ Please enter your age.
* **Education** (`Education`, `text`, optional)
	+ If applicable, please specify your highest level of education.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional)
	+ Please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional)
	+ Please provide your phone number.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, optional)
	+ Please provide your address.
* **Education Level** (`Education Level`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Select your highest level of education completed (Bachelor, Master, Phd).
* **Experience Level** (`Experience Level`, `select_one`, optional)
	+ Select the number of years of experience you possess.
* **Certification** (`Certification`, `select_multiple`, optional)
	+ Please select any relevant certifications you possess.
* **Experience Prophets** (`Experience Prophets`, `number`, optional)
	+ If applicable, please specify the number of years you have spent on a project similar to the Future of Learning Initiative.
* **Prophets** (`Prophets`, `text`, optional)
	+ In this field, please provide any information that you think is relevant to your experience and qualifications.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and honest information in all fields.
* Be sure to check your email address for any spelling mistakes before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the selection committee.
* Submission of multiple applications will delay the review process.
