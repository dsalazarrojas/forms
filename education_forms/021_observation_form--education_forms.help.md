# Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Observation Form is designed for team leaders to observe and evaluate employee performance, providing feedback and input on specific tasks and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Team Leader Observation Form" field, and enter the relevant details about the employee you're observing.
2.  In the "Observation Date" field, select the date you observed the employee's performance.
3.  In the "Observation Time" field, choose the time of the observation.
4.  Provide the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
5.  Enter a brief description of the employee's performance in the "Employee Performance" field.
6.  Assess the employee's performance level in the "Performance Level" field.
7.  Add any additional comments on performance in the "Performance Comment" field.
8.  Enter any comments on the observation in the "Observation Comments" field.
9.  Finally, include any comments from the team leader in the "Team Leaders Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Team Leader Observation Form (1)
*   **Team Leader Observation Form** (text, required): Enter the relevant details about the employee you're observing.
### Team Leader Observation Form Employee (2)
*   **Team Leader Observation Form Employee** (text, required): This field is intended for team leaders to provide feedback on the employee's performance.
### Observation Date (3)
*   **Observation Date** (date, not required): Enter the date of the observation for record-keeping purposes.
### Observation Time (4)
*   **Observation Time** (time, not required): Choose the time of the observation to provide context.
### Employee Name (5)
*   **Employee Name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the employee being observed.
### Employee Performance (6)
*   **Employee Performance** (text, not required): Briefly describe the employee's performance during the observation.
### Performance Level (7)
*   **Performance Level** (text, not required): Assess the employee's performance level, providing feedback on strengths and areas for improvement.
### Performance Comment (8)
*   **Performance Comment** (text, not required): Add any additional comments on the employee's performance.
### Observation Comments (9)
*   **Observation Comments** (text, not required): Enter any comments on the observation process or the employee's performance.
### Team Leaders Comments (10)
*   **Team Leaders Comments** (text, not required): Include any comments from the team leader, providing a more personal and detailed assessment of the employee's performance.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible while observing and reporting on employee performance.
- Keep the assessment fair and unbiased, considering individual strengths and areas for improvement.
- Use the form as a tool to support employee growth and development within the team.
- Review and update the form regularly to ensure it meets the team's evolving needs.
