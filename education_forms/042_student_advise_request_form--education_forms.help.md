# Student Advise Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Advise Request Form is designed to help students who are looking for academic advise on specific topics. This form is intended for internal use by the academic advisors at our institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Student Information section with your details.
2.  Specify the topics you are interested in by providing a brief description in the "What topics are you interested in?" field.
3.  Describe your interests in the "Please describe your interests" field.
4.  Provide your email address and phone number in the "What is your email address?" and "What is your phone number?" fields, respectively.
5.  Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Information
*   **Student Information** (`student_advise_request_form_fields_1`, `text`, **optional**): Please fill out this field with your student ID number or any other relevant student information.
*   **What topics are you interested in?** (`advise_request_topic`, `text`, **optional**): Specify the topics you are interested in. Be as specific as possible so that our advisors can better understand your needs.
*   **Please describe your interests** (`advise_request_description`, `text`, **optional**): Describe your interests in detail so that our advisors can provide you with the best possible guidance.
*   **What is your email address?** (`contact_email`, `email`, **optional**): Provide your email address so that our advisors can get in touch with you.
*   **What is your phone number?** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **optional**): Provide your phone number so that our advisors can contact you for further questions or discussions.
