# Signal Words Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Signal Words Assessment form is designed to evaluate your reading skills, specifically your ability to identify and interpret signals from text.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form by providing your responses to each question.
2. Answer each question carefully, as your responses will be used to calculate your assessment results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **signal_words_assessment** (1):
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Enter a brief description of the assessment purpose.
* **student_name** (2):
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **signal_words_assessed** (3):
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Choose the signal words that you can identify and interpret from the text.
	+ acknowledge
	+ recognise
	+ recall
	+ infer
	+ interpret
	+ generalise
	+ predict
	+ reorganise
	+ speculate
* **reading_transitions** (4):
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Choose the types of reading materials that you are most comfortable with.
	+ read aloud
	+ text only
	+ text and image
	+ text and diagram
	+ text and chart/graph
	+ text and table
	+ text and formula
* **reading_speed** (5):
  * Type: Number
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Enter your reading speed in words per minute (wpm) to help us understand your reading pace.
* **reading_level** (6):
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Choose the level of reading that you are most familiar with.
	+ A2
	+ B1
	+ B2
	+ C1
	+ C2
	+ C1+
* **reading_accuracy** (7):
  * Type: Number
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Enter your reading accuracy as a percentage to help us understand how well you can identify and interpret signals.
* **reading_comprehension** (8):
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Describe how well you understand the text.
* **reading_focus** (9):
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Choose the areas of reading that you focus on most often.
	+ main idea
	+ supporting idea
	+ other
* **reading_difficulty** (10):
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: Optional
  * Explanation: Choose the difficulty level of the texts you most often read.
	+ easy
	+ moderately difficult
	+ very difficult

## Tips

- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- The assessment results will provide you with insights into your reading skills.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for support.
