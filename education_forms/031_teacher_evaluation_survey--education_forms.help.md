# Teacher Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The teacher evaluation survey is designed to gather feedback from students, parents, and other stakeholders on a teacher's performance. It helps to identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into sections. Please fill out each section in order from top to bottom, making sure to answer the questions thoroughly. Do not skip any sections or questions as they are all relevant to the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, `not required`): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully and then fill out the rest of the form.
### Teacher Rating (Page 2)
* **Teacher Rating** (`Teacher Rating`, `select_one`, `not required`): Rate the teacher's overall performance using one of the following options: Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement.
### Feedback Comments (Page 2)
* **Feedback Comments** (`Feedback Comments`, `text`, `not required`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have for the teacher's performance.
### Suggestions (Page 3)
* **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, `text`, `not required`): Suggest any areas where the teacher can improve their performance.
### Rating Why (Page 3)
* **Rating Why** (`Rating Why`, `text`, `not required`): Explain the reasons for your rating of the teacher's performance.
### Teacher Rating Score (Page 4)
* **Teacher Rating Score** (`Teacher Rating Score`, `number`, `not required`): Provide a numerical value between 1-5 to rate the teacher's performance.
### Teacher Rating Likert (Page 4)
* **Teacher Rating Likert** (`Teacher Rating Likert`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Choose one or more of the following options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
### Teacher Rating Comments (Page 4)
* **Teacher Rating Comments** (`Teacher Rating Comments`, `text`, `not required`): Provide any comments on the teacher's rating.
### Teacher Supportive (Page 5)
* **Teacher Supportive** (`Teacher Supportive`, `select_one`, `not required`): Answer how often the teacher is supportive: Always, Most of the time, Some of the time, or Never.
### Teacher Helpful (Page 5)
* **Teacher Helpful** (`Teacher Helpful`, `select_one`, `not required`): Answer how helpful the teacher is: Extremely Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Not Very Helpful, or Not Helpful At All.
### Teacher Fair (Page 6)

### Student Feedback (Page 7)

### Teacher Helpful Comments (Page 8)

### Teacher Rating Scale (Page 8)

### Student Satisfaction (Page 9)
