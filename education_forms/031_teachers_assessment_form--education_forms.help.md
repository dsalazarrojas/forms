# Teachers Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form was created to assess the performance of teachers. It consists of 8 pages, each with a text field. The teacher will be assessed on their ability to complete each page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
*   Answer the questions on each page as accurately as possible.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as "required".
*   You can use the space provided for each question to write your answers.
*   Once you have completed all the pages, click on the "Submit" button to finish the form.
