# Child Behavior Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Child Behavior Questionnaire is designed to help professionals, such as educators and caregivers, assess a child's behavior and development. This form gathers information on various aspects of a child's behavior, including cooperation, attention span, peer interaction, emotional regulation, and more. The collected data will aid in identifying potential areas of concern and provide recommendations for support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's name.
2. Provide the date of birth.
3. Enter the child's age.
4. Select the grade level (if applicable).
5. Enter the name and relationship of the person completing the form (e.g., parent, teacher, etc.).
6. Select the relationship between the person completing the form and the child.
7. Choose the duration of observation.
8. Rate the child's cooperation with adults.
9. Rate the child's ability to follow instructions.
10. Rate the child's attention span and focus.
11. Rate the child's interaction with other children.
12. Rate the child's ability to regulate emotions.
13. Rate the frequency of tantrums or outbursts.
14. Describe how the child handles anger.
15. Rate signs of anxiety or worry.
16. Rate the child's activity level.
17. Rate the child's social skills development.
18. Describe any concerning behaviors observed.
19. List the child's behavioral strengths.
20. Provide any recommendations or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child's name**: Enter the name of the child being observed.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the date of the child's birth.
* **Age**: Enter the child's current age.
* **Grade level**: If applicable, select the child's current grade level.
* **Name of person completing form**: Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **Observer relationship**: Select the relationship between the person completing the form and the child (e.g., parent, teacher, etc.).
* **Duration of observation**: Select the duration of time the person has known the child.
* **Cooperation**: Rate the child's level of cooperation with adults.
* **Follows instructions**: Rate the child's ability to follow instructions.
* **Attention span**: Rate the child's attention span and focus.
* **Peer interaction**: Rate the child's interaction with other children.
* **Emotional regulation**: Rate the child's ability to regulate emotions.
* **Tantrums frequency**: Rate the frequency of the child's tantrums or outbursts.
* **Anger management**: Describe how the child handles anger.
* **Anxiety signs**: Rate signs of anxiety or worry.
* **Activity level**: Rate the child's activity level.
* **Social skills development**: Rate the child's social skills development.
* **Concerning behaviors**: Describe any concerning behaviors observed.
* **Behavioral strengths**: List the child's behavioral strengths.
* **Recommendations**: Provide any recommendations or concerns.
