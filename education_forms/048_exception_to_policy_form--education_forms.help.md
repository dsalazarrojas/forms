# Exception To Policy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Exception To Policy Form" is a document used to request an exception to a school policy. This form is designed for students who require accommodations or adjustments to the normal school policies due to exceptional circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "student_id" field.
3. Choose the date of the exception in the "request_date" field. Select the date when the exception should be applied.
4. Enter a brief reason for the exception in the "reason" field.
5. Enter the student's email address in the "student_email" field.
6. Enter the student's phone number in the "student_phone" field.
7. Select the student's grade level from the dropdown list in the "student_grade" field.
8. Enter your name and contact information in the "submitter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name** (`student_name`, text): Enter student's name.
* **student_id** (`student_id`, text): Enter student's ID number.
* **request_date** (`request_date`, date): Choose the date of the exception.
* **reason** (`reason`, text): Enter reason for the exception.
* **student_email** (`student_email`, email): Enter student's email address.
* **student_phone** (`student_phone`, text): Enter student's phone number.
* **student_grade** (`student_grade`, select one): Select student's grade level.
* **submitter** (`submitter`, text): Enter your name and contact information.
* **submit** (`submit`, text): This field is reserved for internal use.
*   **Note**: Only the first six fields are required to complete the form.
*   **Important**: Ensure that the "student_grade" field is selected from one of the available options.
