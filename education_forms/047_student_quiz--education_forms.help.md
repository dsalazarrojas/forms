# Student Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Quiz form is designed to collect data about students' performance on a quiz. This form helps teachers and educators identify areas where students need extra support or review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the field labeled "Student Name".
2. Select the grade level of the student from the options provided for "Grade Level".
3. Choose the subject areas where the student excelled or struggled from the "Subject Area" options.
4. Enter any relevant information about the student's practice or experience in the "Practice Area" field.
5. Indicate whether the student has knowledge or understanding of the "Knowledge Topic" by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. Enter the date of the quiz in the "Quiz Date" field.
7. Select the time the student took the quiz in the "Time" field.
8. Add any additional notes about the student's performance in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the student's email address for any future communication.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select the grade level of the student.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more subject areas where the student excelled or struggled.
* **Practice Area** (`practice_area`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the student's practice or experience.
* **Knowledge Topic** (`knowledge_topic`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the student has knowledge or understanding of the "Knowledge Topic" by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the quiz.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time the student took the quiz.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the student's performance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address for any future communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form only once for each student.
* Use the form for its intended purpose, collecting data about students' performance on a quiz.
* Double-check the student's grade level and subject areas to ensure accuracy.
* Keep the "Notes" field concise and focused on key observations.
* Enter the student's email address correctly to ensure future communication can be sent to them.
