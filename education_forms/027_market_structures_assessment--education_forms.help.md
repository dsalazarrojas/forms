# Market Structures Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Structures Assessment form is designed to gather information from schools about their market structures. This form is a critical component of the assessment process, and it is essential to complete it accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
3.  Select your school year from the "School Year" field.
4.  Choose the market structures that apply to your school from the "Market Structures" field.
5.  Select additional market structures that apply to your school from the "Market Structures 2" field.
6.  Choose the most accurate description of your school's market structures from the "Market Structures 3" field.
7.  Enter your school location in the "School Location" field.
8.  Enter your school district in the "School District" field.
9.  Select additional market structures that apply to your school from the "Market Structures 4" field.
10.  Enter your total score in the "Total Score" field.
11.  Choose the most accurate description of your school's market structures from the "Market Structures Prophets" field.
12.  Enter your school address in the "School Address" field.
13.  Enter your school phone number in the "School Phone" field.
14.  Enter your school email in the "School Email" field.
15.  Enter your school website in the "School Website" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a student.
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school name.
*   **School Year** (`school_year`, number, optional): Select the year of your school.
*   **Market Structures** (`market_structures`, select_one, optional): Choose the market structures that apply to your school.
	+   Goals
	+   Price equilibrium
	+   Market failures
	+   Market structures
*   **Market Structures 2** (`market_structures_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional market structures that apply to your school.
	+   Option 1
	+   Price equilibrium
	+   Market failures
	+   Market structures
*   **Market Structures 3** (`market_structures_3`, select_one, optional): Choose the most accurate description of your school's market structures.
	+   Price equilibrium
	+   Market failures
	+   Market structures
*   **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter your school location.
*   **School District** (`school_district`, text, optional): Enter your school district.
*   **Market Structures 4** (`market_structures_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional market structures that apply to your school.
	+   Goals
	+   Price equilibrium
	+   Market failures
	+   Market structures
*   **Total Score** (`total_score`, number, optional): Enter your total score.
*   **Market Structures Prophets** (`market_structures_prophets`, select_one, optional): Choose the most accurate description of your school's market structures.
	+   Price equilibrium
	+   Market failures
	+   Market structures
*   **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter your school address.
*   **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter your school phone number.
*   **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter your school email.
*   **School Website** (`school_website`, text, optional): Enter your school website.
