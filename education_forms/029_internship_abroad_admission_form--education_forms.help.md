# Internship Abroad Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students applying for an internship program abroad.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Student Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** if applicable.
2. Select your **Nationality** and **Date of Birth** accurately.
3. Choose your **Language Proficiency** from the list.
4. Enter the name of the **University or Institution** you are applying to.
5. Provide the name of the **Program or Course** you are applying for.
6. Select your current **Level of Education** (High School, Undergraduate, or Graduate).
7. Explain your **Reason for Participating** in the internship program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (student_name)**: A text field for entering the student's name.
* **Email (email)**: A field for entering the student's email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: A field for entering the student's phone number.
* **Nationality (nationality)**: A field for selecting the student's nationality.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: A date field for entering the student's date of birth.
* **Language Proficiency (language_proficiency)**: A dropdown menu for selecting the student's language proficiency.
* **University or Institution (university_applying_to)**: A field for entering the name of the university or institution the student is applying to.
* **Program or Course (program_applying_for)**: A field for entering the name of the program or course the student is applying for.
* **Student's Current Level of Education (education_level)**: A dropdown menu for selecting the student's current level of education.
* **Reason for Participating (reason_for_participating)**: A text field for explaining the student's reason for participating in the internship program.
