# Course Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about course materials ordered by customers for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required information in the User Input field.
2. Select your preferred Payment Gateway from the options provided.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number.
5. Enter the Title of the course you are ordering.
6. Enter a brief Description of the course.
7. Select the type of Course Materials you require (Textbook, Manual, Online Resource).
8. Enter the Quantity of the course materials you need.
9. Enter the Price of the course materials.
10. Enter the Order Total (automatically calculated).
11. Provide any additional Order Details.
12. Enter your address in the User Address field.
13. Enter your City.
14. Enter your State.
15. Enter your Zip code.
16. Enter the address for shipping in the Shipping Address field.
17. Enter the City for shipping.
18. Enter the State for shipping.
19. Enter the Zip code for shipping.
20. Select the Date and Time of the order.
21. Enter any additional Order Comments.
22. Enter your Name.
23. Enter your Title.
24. Enter your Email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required): This is the main field where you can enter any additional information about your order. Please use this field to provide any relevant details that may be useful for us to process your order.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, Authorize, Square) that you would like to use for your payment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your Email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your Phone Number.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required): Enter the Title of the course you are ordering.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, text, required): Enter a brief Description of the course.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, select_one, required): Select the type of Course Materials you require (Textbook, Manual, Online Resource).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the Quantity of the course materials you need.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the Price of the course materials.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): This field will be automatically calculated based on the previous inputs.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your order if necessary.
* **User Address** (`user_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **User City** (`user_city`, text, required): Enter your City.
* **User State** (`user_state`, text, required): Enter your State.
* **User Zip** (`user_zip`, text, required): Enter your Zip code.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the address for shipping.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter the City for shipping.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, optional): Enter the State for shipping.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, optional): Enter the Zip code for shipping.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the Date for the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Select the Time for the order.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional Comments about your order.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your Name.
* **User Title** (`user_title`, text, optional): Enter your Title.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your Email address.

Note: Fields with `required` set to `false` are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
