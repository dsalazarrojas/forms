# Educational Course Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a certificate for students who have completed an educational course. It's used to standardize the process of creating a certificate, ensuring consistency in its content and appearance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

1. Provide your **Student Name** in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the **Course Name** you have completed.
3. Choose the date you **Course Date**.
4. Enter your **Instructor Name**.
5. Enter your **Institution Name**.
6. Provide the **Institution Address**.
7. Enter the number of **Course Hours** completed.
8. Choose the date for your **Certificate Date**.
9. Enter your **Instructor Title**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on the certificate. This field is required to be filled out.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you have completed. This field is optional.
* **Course Date**: Enter the date you completed the course. This field is optional.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of your instructor. This field is optional.
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution where you attended the course. This field is optional.
* **Institution Address**: Enter the address of the institution where you attended the course. This field is optional.
* **Course Hours**: Enter the number of hours you spent on the course. This field is optional.
* **Certificate Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your certificate. This field is optional.
* **Instructor Title**: Enter your instructor's title (e.g., Professor, Teacher, etc.). This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields correctly.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your instructor or the institution for clarification.
* Ensure that you have completed all the course requirements before submitting this form.
