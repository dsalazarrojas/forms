# University Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for university admission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to provide valid email addresses.
3. If applicable, select 'Yes' for any extracurricular activities you've participated in.
4. Ensure you've checked the university's eligibility criteria before filling out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school you attended.
* **Degree** (`degree`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you are currently pursuing or have pursued higher education ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed your degree.
* **GPA** (`gpa`, number, required/optional): Enter your cumulative GPA on a 4.0 scale.
* **SAT Score** (`sat_score`, number, required/optional): Enter your total SAT score.
* **SAT Section 1** (`sat_section1`, number, required/optional): Enter your score for the first SAT section.
* **SAT Section 2** (`sat_section2`, number, required/optional): Enter your score for the second SAT section.
* **Sat Section3** (`sat_section3`, number, required/optional): Enter your score for the third SAT section.
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of any extracurricular activities you've participated in.
* **Activity Duration** (`activity_duration`, text, required/optional): Enter the duration of the activity (e.g., '1 year', '2 years', etc.).
* **Activity Frequency** (`activity_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' for any extracurricular activities you're still participating in, or 'No' for activities you've completed.

## Tips
- Double-check the university's eligibility criteria before filling out this form.
- Ensure you've provided accurate and complete information.
- If you have any questions, feel free to contact the university's admissions office.
