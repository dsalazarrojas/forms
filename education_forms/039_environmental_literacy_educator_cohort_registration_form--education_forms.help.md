# Environmental Literacy Educator Cohort Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Literacy Educator Cohort Registration Form is designed to help us better understand your needs as an educator interested in promoting environmental literacy. Please answer the questions below to help us tailor our support for you and your peers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Select the "Educator Information" fields to provide us with your basic details.
2. In the "Teaching Details" sections, describe your teaching methods, location, and grade level.
3. Choose your preferred program type and program length.
4. Select the areas of environmental education you are interested in teaching.
5. If you have any certifications or credentials, let us know.
6. Finally, submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educator Information 1** (`educator_info`, `text`, required): Provide your name and contact information to help us reach out to you.
* **Educator Information 2** (`educator_info`, `text`, required): Please provide additional details about yourself as an educator.
* **Educator Information 3** (`educator_info_3`, `text`, required): This field is meant to collect further information about your teaching background and qualifications.
* **Teaching Details** (`teaching_details`, `text`, required): Describe your teaching methods and approaches.
* **Teaching Details 2** (`teaching_details_2`, `text`, required): Continue describing your teaching methods and approaches.
* **Teaching Details 3** (`teaching_details_3`, `text`, required): Further describe your teaching methods and approaches.
* **Teaching Details 4** (`teaching_details_4`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about your teaching methods and approaches.
* **Teaching Methods** (`teaching_methods`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you typically teach: In-person, Virtual, or Hybrid.
* **Teaching Location** (`location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select where you usually teach: School, University, or Other.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the grade level you typically teach: K-8, 9-12, or College.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide the subject you usually teach.
* **Teaching Areas** (`teaching_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the areas of environmental education you are interested in teaching: Environmental Education, Sustainability, or Community Engagement.
* **Program** (`program`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional program information.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the program you are interested in.
* **Program Level** (`program_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of the program: Elementary, High School, or College.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number.
* **Teaching Hours** (`teaching_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours you typically teach.
* **Program Length** (`program_length`, `select_one`, optional): Select the length of the program: 6-12 months, 1-2 years, or 3-5 years.
* **Certification/Credential** (`certification_credential`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if you have any certifications or credentials.
* **Certification/Credential 2** (`certification_credential2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select if you have any further certifications or credentials.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of program: Full-time or Part-time.
* **Program Type 2** (`program_type2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of program again.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about the program.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out all required fields (marked as `required` in the form) accurately and thoroughly.
