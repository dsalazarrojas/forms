# Country Profile Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Country Profile Submission Form is designed to collect information about countries, focusing on their educational systems. This form helps individuals or organizations submit data for a country's profile, ensuring accuracy and consistency in the submitted information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required information in each field. You can save your progress and come back later if needed.
2. Ensure that the submitted data is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Information**: This field is for you to provide a brief description of your submission, which will help us understand the context of your data.
* **Country Name**: Please enter the name of the country you are submitting information for. This is a critical field, as it helps us identify the country's profile.
* **Country Description**: Provide a brief description of the country's profile, which will give us a better understanding of the submitted data.
* **Key Facts**: Enter key facts about the country, such as its educational system, policies, or notable features.
* **Country Info URL**: If you have a reliable source of information about the country, please provide the URL for further reference.
* **Submission Form Information Note**: This field is for you to add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Education Facts**: Select the categories of educational facts you are submitting for the country (Basic, Tertiary, Adult, or Vocational). This will help us understand the scope of the educational system in the country.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your submissions.
* Proofread your work carefully before submitting.
* Ensure that your submission is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us at <form@gic.mx>
