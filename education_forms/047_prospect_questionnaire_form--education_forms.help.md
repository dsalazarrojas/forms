# Prospect Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about prospects, which will be used to create a comprehensive profile for future reference. It's crucial that you accurately fill out the form to ensure the best possible service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have all the required information before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date details.
3. Review each field carefully and make sure you understand what is being requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address in this field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number in this field. If you have no phone number, please leave this field blank.
* **Address 1 (Address 1)** (`address_1`, text, Optional): Enter your address for the first line in this field.
* **Address 2 (Address 2)** (`address_2`, text, Optional): Enter your address for the second line in this field.
* **Address 3 (Address 3)** (`address_3`, text, Optional): Enter any additional address information in this field.
* **Date (Date)** (`date`, date, Optional): Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD. If this field is not filled, no specific date will be saved.
* **Time (Time)** (`time`, time, Optional): Enter the time in the format HH:MM. If this field is not filled, no specific time will be saved.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided guidelines or consult with your contact person.
* Fill out the form with the best effort, and do not leave any field blank unnecessarily.
