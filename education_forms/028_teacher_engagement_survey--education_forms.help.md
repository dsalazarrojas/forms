# Teacher Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a teacher's engagement and satisfaction with their school, colleagues, and students. It is intended to be a tool for teachers to provide feedback and insights that will help inform decisions and policies affecting their work environment and the education provided to students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and school name in the respective fields.
2. Select your department from the list of options.
3. Choose your engagement level with your school, colleagues, and students.
4. Rate your communication skills, classroom management, and teaching methodologies.
5. Indicate your level of technology integration, parent-teacher collaboration, and school administration support.
6. Rate your satisfaction with various aspects of your work, including teacher support, school administration, and parent-teacher collaboration.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Other Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Teacher Name** (text)
*   This is the field where you enter your name as a teacher.

### 2. **School Name** (number)
*   This field is where you enter your school's name.

### 3. **Department** (select multiple)
*   Select all the departments that you are affiliated with.

### 4. **Teacher ID** (number)
*   This field is not currently used.

### 5. **Engagement Level** (select one)
*   Choose how engaged you feel with your school, colleagues, and students.

### 6. **Communication Skills** (number)
*   Rate your communication skills with your colleagues and students.

### 7. **Classroom Management** (select multiple)
*   Select how you manage your classroom.

### 8. **Teaching Methodologies** (select multiple)
*   Choose the teaching methodologies you use in your classes.

### 9. **Technology Integration** (select multiple)
*   Indicate how much technology you integrate into your teaching.

### 10. **Parent-Teacher Collaboration** (select multiple)
*   Rate your level of collaboration with parents.

### 11. **Teacher Support** (number)
*   This field is not currently used.

### 12. **School Administration** (select multiple)
*   Rate the level of support you receive from school administration.

### 13. **Teacher Satisfaction** (number)
*   Rate your satisfaction with your job as a teacher.

### 14. **Teacher Loyalty** (number)
*   This field is not currently used.

### 15. **School Satisfaction** (number)
*   Rate your satisfaction with your school.

### 16. **Teacher Satisfaction** (number)
*   This field is intended to rate your satisfaction with your job as a teacher.

### 17. **Student Attendance** (number)
*   This field is not currently used.

### 18. **Student Engagement** (select multiple)
*   Rate the level of student engagement in your classes.

### 19. **Teacher Turnover** (number)
*   This field is not currently used.

### 20. **Other Comments** (text)
*   Provide any additional feedback or comments you have.
