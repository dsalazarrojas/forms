# Continuing Education Program Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer a candidate to our continuing education programs. A referral can be submitted by anyone, including colleagues, managers, and instructors, who believe that a candidate would benefit from our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the necessary information in the following steps:
- Click on the form link to access the submission page.
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about the candidate and your referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Referrer Information
* **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as per your company or institution's records.
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address associated with your work or institution.
* **Relationship to Candidate**: Describe your relationship to the candidate (e.g., colleague, manager, instructor).

### Candidate Information
* **Candidate Full Name**: Enter the full name of the candidate.
* **Candidate Email Address**: Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Candidate Phone Number**: Enter the candidate's phone number (optional).
* **Candidate Current Career Level**: Choose the candidate's current career level:
	+ Entry Level (0-2 Years)
	+ Mid-Career (3-7 Years)
	+ Senior Professional (8-15 Years)
	+ Executive/Director (15+ Years)
* **Recommended Educational Program**: Choose a program that you think would benefit the candidate the most:
	+ Advanced Strategic Leadership
	+ Technical Project Management
	+ Specialized Medical Coding
	+ Digital Content Strategy
	+ Conflict Resolution and Mediation
* **Why are you referring this candidate**: Briefly explain why you think this candidate would be a good fit for the program, mentioning specific strengths or career goals (up to 150 words).
* **Date of Referral**: Enter the date you are submitting this referral.

### Referral Details
* **May we mention your name**: Choose if you want your name to be mentioned when we contact the candidate:
	+ Yes - Feel free to use my name
	+ No - I prefer this referral to be anonymous
* **Notify me if the candidate is admitted**: Choose if you want to be notified when the candidate is admitted:
	+ True
	+ False

Tips:
- Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the candidate.
- Choose a program that you genuinely believe the candidate would benefit from.
- Be specific about why you are referring the candidate.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
