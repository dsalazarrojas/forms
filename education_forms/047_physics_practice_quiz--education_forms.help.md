# Physics Practice Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physics Practice Quiz form is designed to collect user preferences and settings for a physics practice quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user input in the text field provided.
2. Select the physics topic you are interested in from the drop-down menu.
3. If you are part of a study group, select the group(s) you belong to.
4. If you want to schedule a quiz, enter the start and submission times and dates in the respective fields.
5. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide about yourself.
* **Physics Topic** (`physics_topic`, select_one, optional): Choose the area of physics you would like to practice, e.g. Classical Mechanics, Quantum Mechanics, or Electromagnetism.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a student.
* **Study Group** (`study_group`, select_multiple, optional): If you are part of a study group, select the group(s) you are part of.
* **Quiz Start Time** (`quiz_start_time`, date, optional): Enter the start time of your scheduled quiz.
* **Quiz Start Date** (`quiz_start_date`, time, optional): Enter the start date of your scheduled quiz.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you will submit your quiz.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you will submit your quiz.
