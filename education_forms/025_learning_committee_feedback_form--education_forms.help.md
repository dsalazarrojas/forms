# Learning Committee Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from participants on the learning program's effectiveness, support, and overall satisfaction. This form is used to help the program team identify areas of improvement and provide better support to future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions based on your honest opinion.
2. Provide detailed and specific feedback on each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback on the program** (Page 1, required):
	+ Please provide your honest feedback on the learning program, including what you liked and disliked about it.
* **Feedback on the program support** (Page 2, optional):
	+ If you have any feedback on the program's support services, please provide it here.
* **How would you rate the program?** (Page 3, required):
	+ Choose one of the following ratings to express your opinion:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **Date and Time of Completion** (Page 4, optional):
	+ Please enter the date and time you completed the program.
* **Contact Information** (Page 5, optional):
	+ If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include your contact information so we can follow up with you.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
- Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve the program.

Note: This form is designed to be completed in a way that is easy to understand and submit. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
