# Radioactivity Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a knowledge-assessment tool designed to evaluate your understanding of radioactivity, a process where unstable atomic nuclei lose energy by emitting radiation. The form is meant to be a quiz that tests your knowledge on various aspects of radioactivity, including its definition, uses, effects on humans, and related concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and provide a concise answer in the space provided.
2. Type your answers directly into the corresponding text fields.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form, as changes are allowed.
