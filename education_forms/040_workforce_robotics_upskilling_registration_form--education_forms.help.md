# Workforce Robotics Upskilling Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register employees interested in participating in upskilling programs in robotics. It will collect information about the employee's upskilling status, contact details, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one or more training programs you are interested in from the "Select training programs" dropdown menu.
2. Choose your upskilling start and end dates from the calendar.
3. Select your upskilling status from the "Registration status" dropdown menu.
4. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
5. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional comments" field.
