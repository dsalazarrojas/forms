# high_school_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and process student enrollment data for high school students, including personal details, guardian contact information, and other required documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Student Information** page with the student's details:
	* Ensure all required fields are filled out as accurately as possible.
2. On the **Guardians Contact** page, fill out the contact information for both Guardians 1 and 2 (if applicable):
	* If there is only one guardian, fill out only the **Guardian 1 Name** and **Guardian 1 Contact** fields.
3. On the **Student Address** page, enter the student's home address:
	* Be as specific as possible (house number, street name, city, state/province, zip/postal code).
4. On the **Student DOB** page, enter the student's date of birth:
	* Use the MM/DD/YYYY format (month/day/year).
5. On the **Student Documents** page, you can add any relevant documents (e.g., immunization records, medical certificates, etc.):
	* Use a plain text note to describe the document(s) you're uploading and the reason(s) for uploading them.
6. On the **Student Documents Upload** page, upload any supporting documents:
	* Please ensure that only supported file formats (e.g., PDF, JPEG, DOCX) are uploaded.
7. If applicable, fill out the **Student Transcript** page with the student's academic records:
	* Attach relevant documents like diplomas, report cards, or other academic achievements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (Page 1): Enter the student's name, date of birth, and other required details.
* **Guardians Contact** (Page 2): Enter the names and contact information of both Guardians 1 and 2 (if applicable).
* **Student Address** (Page 3): Enter the student's home address, including house number, street name, city, state/province, and zip/postal code.
* **Student DOB** (Page 4): Enter the student's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Guardian 1 Name** and **Guardian 1 Contact** (Page 5): Enter the contact information for Guardian 1.
* **Guardian 2 Name** and **Guardian 2 Contact** (Page 6): Enter the contact information for Guardian 2 (if applicable).
* **Student Documents** (Page 7): Enter a brief note describing any relevant documents uploaded (e.g., immunization records, medical certificates).
* **Student Documents Upload** (Page 8): Upload supporting documents as plain text notes or supported file formats (e.g., PDF, JPEG, DOCX).
* **Student Transcript** (Page 9): Attach relevant academic records like diplomas, report cards, or other achievements.

## Tips
* Please ensure the student's details are accurately and thoroughly filled out as required.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section above.
* Upload only supported file formats to avoid errors.
* Be as specific as possible when filling out fields.
