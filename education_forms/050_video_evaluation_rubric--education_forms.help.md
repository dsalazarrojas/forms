# Video Evaluation Rubric - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Evaluation Rubric form is designed to provide a structured and fair review process for video submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the video carefully and ensure you have a clear understanding of its content and submission details.
2.  For each section of the form, answer the questions based on your assessment of the video.
3.  Be sure to provide clear and concise comments in the "Reviewer Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text, Optional): This section is for you to comment on the video's introduction. Please provide a brief description of the video's introduction and any initial thoughts you may have.
* **Video Content** (Select One, Required): Please select 'Yes' if the video content is relevant and engaging, or 'No' if it is not.
* **Production Value** (Number, Required): Please rate the video's production value on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Creativity** (Select Multiple, Optional): Please select 'Yes' if the video is creative and innovative, or 'No' if it is not.
* **Storytelling** (Select Multiple, Optional): Please select 'Yes' if the video effectively tells a story, or 'No' if it does not.
* **Engagement** (Select One, Required): Please select 'Yes' if the video is engaging, or 'No' if it is not.
* **Overall Quality** (Number, Required): Please rate the video's overall quality on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Audio Quality** (Select Multiple, Optional): Please select 'Good' if the audio quality is satisfactory, or 'Poor' if it is not.
* **Video Length** (Number, Optional): Please provide the video's length in minutes.
* **Submission** (Select Multiple, Optional): Please select 'Yes' if the video was submitted on time, or 'No' if it was not.
* **Submission Date** (Date, Required): Please enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (Time, Required): Please enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Status** (Select One, Required): Please select 'Active' if the video is active, or 'Inactive' if it is not.
* **Evaluation Date** (Date, Required): Please enter the date of evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (Time, Required): Please enter the time of evaluation.
* **Reviewer Comments** (Note, Optional): Please provide any additional comments you may have about the video.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by a reviewer, who has reviewed the video and is providing feedback on its quality and criteria.
