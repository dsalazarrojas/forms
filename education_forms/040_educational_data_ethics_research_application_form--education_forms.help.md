# Educational Data Ethics Research Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information for approval and ethical review of educational research projects. Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and that the information provided is true.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **General Information**: Enter the project's general details.
2. **Institution ID**: Provide your institution's ID (if applicable).
3. **Contact Person**: Enter the person in charge of the project.
4. **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email.
5. **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
6. **Research Information**: Enter a description of the research project.
7. **Research Type**: Select "Yes" if the research involves human subjects, "No" otherwise.
8. **Research Purpose**: Describe the purpose of the research project.
9. **Research Description**: Enter a detailed description of the research project.
10. **Research Duration**: Specify the project duration in months.
11. **Number of Researchers**: Enter the number of researchers involved in the project.
12. **Research Start Date**: Enter the project start date.
13. **Institutional Review**: Enter the institution's review status (if applicable).
14. **Ethics Committee Review**: Enter the ethics committee's review status (if applicable).
15. **Review Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the review process.
16. **Approval Status**: Select "Active" if approved, "Inactive" otherwise.
17. **Approval Date**: Enter the date of approval (if applicable).
18. **Institutional Approval**: Select "Yes" if approved by the institution, "No" otherwise.
19. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
20. **Research Acknowledgement**: Select "Yes" if the project acknowledges the institutional review, "No" otherwise.
