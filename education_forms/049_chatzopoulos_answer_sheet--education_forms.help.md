## Purpose
The Chatzopoulos Answer Sheet is designed to collect student answers for a test or exam.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID Number" field.
3. Select your class section from the provided options.
4. Enter the date of the test or exam.
5. For each question, select your answer from the provided options.
6. Enter your total number of questions on the test.
7. Optionally, enter the time limit for the test.
8. Confirm that this work is your own, without unauthorized assistance.
9. Submit the form with your answers and the time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
  - **Student ID Number** (Student ID, text, required): Enter your student ID number provided by your institution.
  - **Class Section** (Class Section, text, required): Select the class or section you are enrolled in.
  - **Test Date** (Test Date, date, required): Enter the date of the test or exam.
  - **Question 1 Answer** (Question 1, select_one, required): Select your answer for the first question.
  - **Question 2 Answer** (Question 2, select_one, required): Select your answer for the second question.
  - ...
  - **Question 10 Answer** (Question 10, select_one, required): Select your answer for the tenth question.
  - **I Confirm This Work is My Own** (I Confirm This Work is My Own, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Confirm" to indicate that this work is your own.
  - **Time of Submission** (Time of Submission, time, required): Enter the time at which you are submitting this form.
