# Community Learning Partnership Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for organizations that want to partner with our organization for community learning initiatives. It helps us understand your partnership goals, expertise, and interests to create mutually beneficial relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To register your organization for our community learning partnership program, please follow these steps:

1. **Complete the form**: Fill out all the required fields marked as "required" by typing in the corresponding text fields or selecting from the drop-down menus.
2. Check your email for an activation link sent to the email you provided to activate and submit your form.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Organization Full Name (Page 1)**: Enter your full name as recognized by your organization.
* **Organization Classification (Page 2)**: Choose the best description of your organization from the provided options.
	+ Non-Profit or Charity (NGO): You operate as a non-profit organization or charity.
	+ Public School or Educational Institution: You're an educational institution or school.
	+ Community Volunteer Group: You're a group of volunteers focused on community service.
	+ Government Agency or Local Authority: You're a government agency or local authority.
	+ Private Sector Business: You're a for-profit organization with a community focus.
* **Primary Point of Contact (Page 2)**: Provide your full name as the primary contact person for this partnership.
* **Primary Contact Email (Page 2)**: Enter your email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number (Page 2)**: Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Core Areas of Educational Expertise (Page 3)**: Select all the areas where your organization has expertise in education.
	+ Literacy and Early Education: Literacy, numeracy, and early education programs.
	+ STEM (Science, Tech, Engineering, Math): Science, technology, engineering, and math education and training.
	+ Arts, Culture, and Design: Arts, cultural, and design education and training.
	+ Vocational and Job Training: Vocational training and job skills development.
	+ Soft Skills and Leadership: Soft skills, leadership, and personal development training.
	+ Public Health and Safety Awareness: Public health and safety awareness and education.
* **Proposed Primary Partnership Role (Page 3)**: Choose the main role your organization will play in the partnership.
	+ Learning Resource Provider: Provide educational resources such as materials, tools, or equipment.
	+ Workshop or Course Instructor: Teach workshops or courses as part of the partnership.
	+ Venue Host and Facility Provider: Provide a physical location for events or activities.
	+ Marketing and Outreach Partner: Assist in marketing and outreach efforts for the partnership.
	+ Financial and Scholarship Sponsor: Offer financial support or scholarships.
* **Intended Target Demographic for Partnership (Page 4)**: Describe the group you primarily serve with your programs and services.
* **Summary of Previous Collaborative Experience (Page 4)**: List any similar partnerships or collaborations you've had.
* **Interest in Joint Grant Applications or Funding (Page 4)**: Indicate your interest level in applying for joint grants or funding.
	+ Yes, very interested: You're very interested in applying for grants and funding.
	+ Potentially interested: You might be interested in applying for grants and funding.
	+ No, not interested: You're not interested in applying for grants and funding.
* **Date of Partnership Registration (Page 4)**: Enter the date you're submitting this partnership registration.
* **Electronic Signature (Page 5)**: Type your full name for verification purposes.
* **I confirm the information is correct and truthful (Page 5)**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and truthful.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your email or contact info].
