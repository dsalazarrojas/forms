# Learning Management System Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from learners on their experience with the Learning Management System (LMS). The collected data will be used to improve the system and make it more user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. You can save your progress and come back to complete the form at a later time.
3. You can skip questions that are not applicable to you.
4. Make sure to review each question carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User info** (`User info`, `text`, required: false): This field collects your basic information such as your name and email address. Please enter your name and email address to help us contact you in case we need to follow up with you.
* **Rate your experience** (`Rate your experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks you to rate your experience with the LMS. Please select one or more of the options that best describe your experience:
	+ Very poor
	+ Poor
	+ Neutral
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, required: false): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the LMS.
* **Contact info** (`Contact info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to enter any contact information you would like to have us contact you back. Please enter your name and email address or any other contact information you would like to have us reach out to you.
* **Courses enrolled** (`Courses enrolled`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks you to select the courses you have enrolled in through the LMS. Please select the courses that apply to you:
	+ Math
	+ Science
	+ English
