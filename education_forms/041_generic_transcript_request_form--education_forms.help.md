# Generic Transcript Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generic Transcript Request Form is used to request a transcript of a user's academic or professional records. This form is intended for individuals who need to obtain official verification of their academic or professional credentials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Choose a date for the Request Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your full first name as it appears on your records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name as it appears on your records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive the transcript.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for further information.
* **Request Date**: Select a date for the transcript request. If you're unsure of the exact date, you can choose a date closest to the date you think you requested the transcript.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorization to request a transcript before submitting this form.
* If you have submitted this form previously, your request will likely be flagged for review to prevent duplicates.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
