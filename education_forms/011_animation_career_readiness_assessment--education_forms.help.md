# Animation Career Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animation Career Readiness Assessment is designed to evaluate your suitability for an animation career. The form will guide you through a series of questions and prompts that will help you assess your educational background, experience, and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
- For select-one and select_multiple questions, choose the option that best describes your situation.
- For text questions, provide a brief but detailed description of your answer.
- Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask for help if you need it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Your complete name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, which will be used to send you assessment results.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, optional): Your contact phone number, which will help us reach out to you.
* **Current School or Program** (`5`, `text`, optional): The name of the school or program you are currently attending or have attended.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest educational level achieved, such as High School Diploma, Associate Degree, Bachelor's Degree, or Diploma from an Animation School.
* **Years of Animation Experience** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long you have been studying animation, with options from Less than 1 year to 5+ years.
* **Animation Specializations** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the animation specializations that you are familiar with or have experience in, such as 2D Traditional Animation, 3D CGI Animation, Motion Graphics, and more.
* **Relevant Coursework Completed** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the relevant courses you have taken, such as Principles of Animation, Character Design, Digital Illustration, and more.
* **Number of Portfolio Pieces** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Choose how many finished animation works you have completed.
* **Rate the Quality of Your Portfolio** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Self-assess the quality of your portfolio, with options from Excellent - Professional level to Beginner - Early stages.
* **Portfolio Website or Link** (`14`, `text`, optional): Provide the URL of your online portfolio.
* **Internship Experience** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you have animation internship experience, with options from Yes, multiple internships to No experience.
* **Industry Knowledge** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of understanding of the animation industry, with options from Excellent - Very informed to Minimal - Just starting.
* **Software Proficiency** (`19`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the software programs you are proficient in, such as Maya, Blender, After Effects, and more.
* **Overall Technical Proficiency** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Rate your technical skills, with options from Expert - Advanced technical skills to Novice - Very new to software.
* **Collaboration and Teamwork** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well you collaborate and work with others, with options from Excellent team player to Prefer working alone.
* **Meeting Deadlines and Time Management** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Select how well you manage your time and meet deadlines, with options from Excellent - Always on time to Very challenging - Frequently late.
* **Career Goals** (`24`, `text`, optional): Describe your career aspirations and goals.
