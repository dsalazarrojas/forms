# Educational Program Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Program Volunteer Application Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in becoming volunteers for educational programs. This form helps us understand your availability and commitment to volunteer with our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information.
2. Enter your available date and time.
3. Specify the number of hours you can volunteer per week.
4. Choose your available days of the week to volunteer.
5. Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer (morning, afternoon, or evening).
6. Commit to the number of weeks you are willing to volunteer per month.
7. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **program_details** (`program_details`,  text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about the educational program you're interested in or any specific details about yourself that might be helpful for us to know.
* **name** (`name`,  text, required): This is your name.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`,  email, required): This is your email address where we can contact you.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`,  text, required): This is your phone number where we can contact you.
* **address** (`address`,  text, optional): This is your address.
* **date_available** (`date_available`,  date, required): This is the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **time_available** (`time_available`,  time, required): This is the time you are available to volunteer.
* **volunteer_hours** (`volunteer_hours`,  number, required): This is the number of hours you can volunteer per week.
* **volunteer_frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`,  select_multiple, required): This is the frequency of days you are available to volunteer (Monday to Sunday).
* **volunteer_days_available** (`volunteer_days_available`,  select_multiple, required): This is the time of day you are available to volunteer (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **volunteer_time_commitment** (`volunteer_time_commitment`,  number, required): This is the number of weeks you are committed to volunteer per month.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by *) to ensure your application is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
