# primary_school_parent_payments_online_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for primary school parents to input payment information for their child. The form will be used to record and store each payment transaction, ensuring accurate and transparent payment tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the payment method and amount of the payment you wish to record.
2. Choose the payment date for the transaction.
3. Enter the payment amount and reference number.
4. Provide student and school IDs for the child and school associated with the payment.
5. Confirm the payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the payment method and amount?** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the payment method and amount, e.g., "Online payment of $100 for lunch fees".
* **Select payment method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more methods from the available options (Online, Offline, Bank Transfer).
* **Enter payment date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Select the date the payment was made.
* **Enter payment amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Enter payment reference** (`payment_reference`, text, required): Enter a reference number for the payment.
* **Student School ID** (`school_id`, number, required): Enter the school ID associated with the student.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student ID number.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the payment (Active or Inactive).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date_2`, date, required): Confirm the payment date for the transaction.
* **Confirm payment method** (`payment_method_confirmation`, select_multiple, required): Confirm the payment method (Yes or No).

## Tips

* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submission.
* Ensure you have entered the correct student and school IDs.
* Confirm the payment method to avoid any discrepancies.
* Keep track of payment references for future reference.
