**Form Purpose**
The Educational Policy Forum Registration Form is designed to gather information from individuals attending a particular educational event, such as workshops, conferences, or seminars. The form is meant to collect contact information for event organizers and registrants, and to record details about the event. This helps ensure accurate attendance records and enables effective communication and coordination.

**How To Complete This Form**
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1.  Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**.
2.  Enter your institution's name in the **Institution** field.
3.  Select your **Role** (Yes or No) to indicate whether you are an attendee or an organizer.
4.  If you chose "Yes" in the previous step, enter any additional details about your role in the **Other Role** field.
5.  Fill in the event's **Name**.
6.  Enter the date of the event in the **Event Date** field.
7.  Enter the time of the event in the **Event Time** field.
8.  Provide the event's **Venue**.
9.  Indicate if you will **Register** (Yes or No) for the event.
10.  Choose the methods you will use for **Registration** (Yes or No).
11.  Select the relevant department from the list or enter any additional department information if it's not listed (e.g., "Other").
