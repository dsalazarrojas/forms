# Continuing Education Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Continuing Education Admission Form is a tool used to collect personal and academic information from applicants for continuing education programs. This information is necessary for admission and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the personal identification section.
2. Enter your legal full name, date of birth, and the last four digits of your Social Security Number/ID.
3. Move on to the academic background section and select your highest degree earned.
4. Enter the name of your most recent educational institution.
5. Choose your current employment status.
6. In the admission interests section, select the program that interests you the most.
7. Provide a statement of intent explaining why you are applying to this program.
8. Choose how you will deliver your previous transcripts.
9. Finally, sign the application date and provide your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Full Name** (Full Name): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Last 4 digits of SSN/ID**: Enter the last four digits of your Social Security Number or ID for student record matching purposes.
* **Highest Degree Earned**: Select your highest degree earned from the options provided.
* **Name of Most Recent Educational Institution**: Enter the name of your most recent educational institution.
* **Current Employment Status**: Choose your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Continuing Education Program**: Select the program that interests you the most from the options provided.
* **Statement of Intent**: Provide a statement of intent explaining why you are applying to this program (max 200 words).
* **How will you provide previous transcripts**: Choose how you will deliver your previous transcripts from the options provided.
* **Date of Application**: Enter the date you submitted this application.
* **Applicant Digital Signature**: Type your full name for digital signature purposes.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, and do not hesitate to contact us if you need further assistance.
