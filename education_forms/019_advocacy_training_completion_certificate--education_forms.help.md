# Advocacy Training Completion Certificate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides instructions on how to complete the Advocacy Training Completion Certificate Request form, which is designed to generate a personalized certificate of completion for participants who have attended Advocacy Training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please provide participant details**: Click on the "Participant Details" section and enter your first name, last name, and email address. This information will appear on your certificate.
2. **Select your training module**: Click on the "Training Module Completed" dropdown menu and select the specific course you completed (e.g., "Introduction to Advocacy" or "Digital Campaigning").
3. **Provide completion date**: Enter the date you finished the course in the "Date of Completion" field.
4. **Optional: Provide instructor name**: If an instructor was assigned to your session, enter their name in the "Instructor Name" field.
5. **Optional: Provide feedback**: If you have any comments or feedback about the training, enter it in the "Overall Satisfaction" and "Key Takeaway" fields.
6. **Enter verification code**: Enter the verification code provided at the end of the session in the "Course Verification Code" field.
7. **Review and submit**: Review all the information you provided and click the "Submit" button to generate your personalized certificate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it should appear on your certificate.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it should appear on your certificate.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you will receive the digital certificate.
* **Organization**: If you are representing an organization, enter it here (optional).
* **Training Module Completed**: Select the specific course you completed from the dropdown menu.
* **Date of Completion**: Enter the date you finished the course.
* **Instructor Name**: If an instructor was assigned to your session, enter their name (optional).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the training (optional).
* **Key Takeaway**: Enter the most valuable thing you learned from the training (optional).
* **Course Verification Code**: Enter the verification code provided at the end of the session.
* **Verification Code**: This is a critical field required to verify your completion of the training. If you are unsure, contact the course organizers.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information, as it will appear on your digital certificate.
* Review all the fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the course organizers for assistance.
