# Before And After School Care Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Before And After School Care Registration Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians for enrolling their children in a before and after school care program. This form is a crucial document that requires accurate and complete information to ensure successful registration and care arrangements for the child.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the academic year for which you are enrolling your child.
2.  Enter your child's full name and student ID (if known).
3.  Choose the program type and schedule that suits your needs.
4.  Select the attendance plan for your child, considering their availability and your needs.
5.  If you are enrolling your child on a part-time plan, specify the requested attendance days.
6.  Provide the billing information, including the email address for payment and the responsible party for payment.
7.  Confirm your eligibility for childcare subsidy (if applicable).
8.  Choose whether you would like to enroll in automatic monthly billing.
9.  Read and agree to the tuition and late pick-up fee policy.
10.  Acknowledge the non-refundable registration fee.
11.  Finally, provide your printed signature as the guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Academic Year** (Field 2): Choose the academic year for which you are enrolling your child.
* **Student Name** (Field 3): Enter your child's full name.
* **School Student ID** (Field 4): Enter your child's student ID if you know it.
* **Program Selection** (Field 6): Choose the program type and schedule that suits your needs.
* **Attendance Plan** (Field 7): Select the attendance plan for your child, considering their availability and your needs.
* **Requested Attendance Days** (Field 8): If you are enrolling your child on a part-time plan, specify the requested attendance days.
* **Responsible Party for Payment** (Field 10): Provide the contact information for the person responsible for payment.
* **Billing Email Address** (Field 11): Enter the email address for billing and payment information.
* **Subsidy Eligibility** (Field 12): Confirm your eligibility for childcare subsidy (if applicable).
* **Automatic Monthly Billing** (Field 13): Choose whether you would like to enroll in automatic monthly billing.
* **Tuition Policy Agreement** (Field 15): Read and agree to the tuition and late pick-up fee policy.
* **Registration Fee Acknowledgment** (Field 16): Acknowledge the non-refundable registration fee.
* **Guardian Signature** (Field 17): Provide your printed signature as the guardian.
* **Date** (Field 18): Enter the current date for the registration.

Please review each field carefully and complete the form accurately to ensure successful registration of your child in the before and after school care program.
