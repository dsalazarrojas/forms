# Student Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experiences in a course. It helps instructors and course administrators understand how well students perceive the course content, instruction, and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
2. Provide the name of your teacher or instructor.
3. Select your grade level in the course (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, etc.).
4. Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the course.
5. Rate the quality of instruction provided by the teacher.
6. Evaluate how clear and effective the course materials were.
7. Rate how well the teacher explained the material.
8. Rate how well the teacher communicated the material.
9. Indicate your level of satisfaction with the course.
10. Assess the relevance of the course to your major or minor.
11. Rate how well the course was organized.
12. Provide any additional comments about the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of your teacher or instructor.
* **What grade are you in?** (`course_grade`, select_one, optional): Select your current grade level in the course. (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, etc.)
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course. (Choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)
* **How would you rate the quality of instruction?** (`teacher_quality`, select_one, optional): Evaluate how well you think the teacher performed in explaining the course material. (Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **How clear and effective were the course materials?** (`course_materials`, select_one, optional): Rate how clear and effective you found the course materials to be. (Choose from Very Clear, Somewhat Clear, Neutral, Somewhat Confusing, Very Confusing)
* **How well did the instructor explain the material?** (`teacher_help`, select_one, optional): Rate how well the teacher explained the material. (Choose from Always, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, Never)
* **How well did the instructor communicate the material?** (`communication_skills`, select_one, optional): Evaluate how well the teacher communicated the material. (Choose from Very Well, Somewhat Well, Neutral, Somewhat Poorly, Very Poorly)
* **How satisfied are you with this course?** (`student_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the course. (Choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)
* **How relevant is this course to your major/minor?** (`course_relevance`, select_one, optional): Rate the relevance of the course to your major or minor. (Choose from Very Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Neutral, Somewhat Irrelevant, Very Irrelevant)
* **How well was the course organized?** (`course_organization`, select_one, optional): Rate how well the course was organized. (Choose from Very Well, Somewhat Well, Neutral, Somewhat Poorly, Very Poorly)
* **Additional Comments** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the course.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* Select only one option for each rating question.
* Take your time to complete the form thoughtfully, as it will help instructors and course administrators make informed decisions about the course.
