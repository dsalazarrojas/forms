# Student Organization College Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Organization College Membership Form is used to collect and manage information related to student college membership. This form is typically used by college administrators or club leaders to track membership status, student details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student information section with accurate details about the student, such as their name and email.
2. Enter the student's contact information, including phone number and student ID.
3. Select the membership level and status as required.
4. Choose the date and time the student joined the college or organization.
5. Confirm whether the student is a committee member or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Club Name**: Enter the name of the college or organization the student is a part of.
* **Student Information**: Enter the student's full name and other details as required.
* **Contact Information**: Fill in the student's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Membership Details**: Select the membership level and status as required. This may include options such as "Active", "Pending", and "Inactive".
* **Date Joined**: Enter the date the student joined the college or organization in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time Joined**: Enter the time the student joined in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID as per the college or organization's requirements.
* **Club Leader**: Enter the name of the club leader if applicable.
* **Club Email**: Enter the club email if applicable.
* **Club Phone**: Enter the club phone number if applicable.
* **Committee**: Choose whether the student is a committee member or not.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the student's membership.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Last Updated**: Enter the last updated date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Version**: Enter the version of the form as per the college or organization's requirements.
* **Status**: Select the status of the form as approved, pending, or denied.
