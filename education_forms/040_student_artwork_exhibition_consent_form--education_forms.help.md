# Student Artwork Exhibition Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Artwork Exhibition Consent Form is used to gather information from students and their parents for the exhibition of student artworks. This form is a prerequisite for students to participate in the exhibition and ensures that parents have given their consent for the artwork to be displayed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the parent's preferred option for participation in the exhibition.
2. Enter the artwork's details, such as title, year, media, and description.
3. Enter the student's contact information, including name, grade level, and age.
4. Enter the artwork's dimensions and weight.
5. Sign the form with the student's and parent's signature.
