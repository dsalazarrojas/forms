# Academic Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Data Sharing Consent Form is a document that allows participants to grant or deny consent for the sharing of their data for various purposes, such as research collaboration, publication, and teaching. The form ensures that participants' data is used ethically and in accordance with their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction section, which explains the purpose and scope of the form.
2. Provide your full legal name in the "Participant name" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Participant email" field.
4. Describe the dataset and its contents in the "Description of data to be shared" field.
5. Choose the purpose of data sharing from the "Purpose of data sharing" dropdown menu.
6. Select the consent duration from the "Consent duration" dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, specify any restrictions on data use in the "Restrictions on data use" field.
8. Describe the measures taken to protect personal or sensitive data in the "Data protection measures" field.
9. Sign the form by typing your full name in the "Electronic signature name" field and the date of consent in the "Signature date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`Participant name`, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Participant email** (`Participant email`, required): Enter your contact email address for consent follow-up.
* **Institution** (`Institution or department`, optional): Identify the institution or department you are affiliated with.
* **Description of data to be shared** (`Description of data to be shared`, required): Provide a brief summary of the dataset and its contents.
* **Purpose of data sharing** (`Purpose of data sharing`, required): Choose how the shared data will be used (e.g., research collaboration, publication, teaching, public access).
* **Consent duration** (`Consent duration`, required): Select the period for which consent is granted (indefinite, 1 year, 3 years, until project end, or other).
* **Restrictions on data use** (`Restrictions on data use`, optional): Specify any limitations or required approvals for data use.
* **Data protection measures** (`Data protection measures`, optional): Describe the measures taken to protect personal or sensitive data.
* **Electronic signature name** (`Electronic signature name`, required): Type your full name to sign the consent.
* **Signature date** (`Signature date`, required): Enter the date of consent.

## Tips
Make sure to read the form carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any field. Provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your consent is valid and enforceable.
