# Special Education Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Admission Form is a crucial document that helps us understand a student's educational background, needs, and goals. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best possible support for the student.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Student Information section with the student's name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Choose the student's education level from the options provided.
3. Provide additional information about the student's programs, if applicable.
4. Fill in the Parent Information section with the parent's name, contact phone number, email, and relationship with the student.
5. Confirm the student's program information and any additional details.
6. Sign and date the form, ensuring both the student and parent have signed and dated it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (Student Name, <id: 4>): Enter the student's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, <id: 5>): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, <id: 7>): Provide the student's contact information, including address and phone number.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, <id: 8>): Enter the student's contact name.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, <id: 9>): Enter the student's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, <id: 10>): Enter the student's contact phone number.
* **Guardian Information** (Guardian Information, <id: 11>): Provide the guardian's contact information and relationship with the student.
* **Guardian Name** (Guardian Name, <id: 12>): Enter the guardian's name.
* **Relationship** (Relationship, <id: 13>): Select the guardian's relationship with the student (Parent, Guardian, or Relative).
* **Guardian Phone** (Guardian Phone, <id: 14>): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Student Education Level** (Student Education Level, <id: 15>): Select the student's education level from the options provided.
* **Student Education Level Other** (Student Education Level Other, <id: 16>): If the student's education level is not listed, provide the specific education level in this field.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, <id: 17>): Provide any additional information about the student.
* **Student Additional Info Other** (Student Additional Info Other, <id: 18>): If you have any additional information about the student, provide it here.
* **Student Signature** (Student Signature, <id: 19>): Sign the student's section of the form.
* **Date Signed** (Date Signed, <id: 20>): Date the student signed the form.
* **Parent Signature** (Parent Signature, <id: 21>): Sign the parent's section of the form.
* **Parent Signature Date** (Parent Signature Date, <id: 22>): Date the parent signed the form.
* **Parent Info Additional** (Parent Info Additional, <id: 23>): Provide any additional information from the parent.
* **Student Program Information** (Student Program Information, <id: 24>): Describe the student's program.
* **Program Name** (Program Name, <id: 25>): Enter the program's name.
* **Program Start Date** (Program Start Date, <id: 26>): Enter the program's start date.
* **Program End Date** (Program End Date, <id: 27>): Enter the program's end date.
* **Student Program Info Other** (Student Program Info Other, <id: 28>): If you have any additional information about the program.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (Parent/Guardian Name, <id: 29>): Enter the parent's or guardian's name.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (Parent/Guardian Phone, <id: 30>): Enter the parent's or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (Parent/Guardian Email, <id: 31>): Enter the parent's or guardian's email address.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and complete as possible when filling in the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the special education department for guidance.
* Both the student and parent/guardian must sign and date their respective sections of the form.
