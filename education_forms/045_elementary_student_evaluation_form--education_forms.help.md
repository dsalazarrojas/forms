# Elementary Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Elementary Student Evaluation Form is used to assess a student's academic performance, strengths, and areas of development. It helps teachers and educators identify areas where a student excels and areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the student's basic information, including their name, grade level, and date of birth.
2. Describe the student's strengths, weaknesses, and areas of development in the respective fields.
3. Add any additional notes or comments about the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, required): Enter the student's current grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Strengths** (`student_strengths`, note, required): Write a list of the student's strengths, such as excellent communication skills or exceptional math abilities.
* **Student Weaknesses** (`student_weaknesses`, note, required): Write a list of the student's weaknesses, such as difficulty with reading comprehension or trouble with social interactions.
* **Student Development Areas** (`student_development_areas`, note, required): Write areas of development, such as improving writing skills or enhancing critical thinking abilities.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or observations about the student.

## Tips
* Be specific and concrete when describing the student's strengths, weaknesses, and areas of development.
* Use the notes field to provide any additional context or information not covered in the other fields.
* This form should be completed in a confidential and professional manner.
