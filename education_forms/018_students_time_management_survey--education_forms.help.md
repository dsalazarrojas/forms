# Students Time Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Students Time Management Survey is designed to gather information about your study habits, time use, and daily and weekly activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions and select all that apply.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Survey Questions

* **Survey Questions** (`select_multiple`, not required):
    Select all that apply to indicate your study habits, time use, and daily and weekly activities.

* **Study Habits** (`text`, not required):
    Describe your current study habits, including your schedule, routine, and any notable practices.

* **Time Use** (`date`, not required):
    Enter the date and time you typically wake up and go to bed on a usual weekday.
    
* **Daily Activities** (`select_one`, not required):
    Select 'Yes' if you have any of the following daily activities:
    * Do you have a morning routine?
        - Yes
        - No
*   **Weekly Activities** (`select_multiple`, not required):
    Select all that apply to indicate your weekly activities:
    * Do you have any fixed appointments or classes?
        - Yes
        - No
    * Do you participate in any sports or physical activities?
        - Yes
        - No
    * Do you work or volunteer outside of class?
        - Yes
        - No
