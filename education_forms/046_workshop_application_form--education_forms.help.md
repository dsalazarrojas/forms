## Purpose
The workshop_application_form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in a workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

### Step 1: Fill out the form thoroughly
Each field is crucial in ensuring that you are a good fit for the workshop.

### Step 2: Be honest
Please provide truthful information about your employment status, job title, department, and any other fields that require specific details.

### Step 3: Use the correct format for email and phone fields
When filling out the email field, use the standard email format (e.g., `example@domain.com`). For the phone field, use the international format (e.g., `+1-123-456-7890`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information**: Enter your general information about yourself.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, so workshop organizers can get in touch with you.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Expectations**: Describe your expectations from participating in the workshop.
* **Current Employer**: If you are employed, enter your current employer's name.
* **Job Title**: If you are employed, enter your job title.
* **Department**: If you are employed, enter your department or team name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., `example@domain.com`).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., `+1-123-456-7890`).
* **Notes**: Use this field for any additional information you think is relevant.
