# Pedagogical Innovation Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from the educational community on pedagogical innovation, allowing us to better understand their needs, concerns, and ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Information section with your name, and any other relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide your contact information, such as contact phone number and contact email address.
3. Clearly state your consultation request in the Consultation Request section.
4. Select the area of innovation you're interested in (Artificial Intelligence, Augmented Reality, Internet of Things, or Virtual Reality).
5. If applicable, select your role within the educational institution (Teacher, Administrator, Parent, or Student).
6. Enter your time zone and date.
7. Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.
8. Finally, sign and submit the form on the specified submit date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number and email address to enable us to get in touch with you.
* **Consultation Request** (`consultation_request`, `text`, required: false): Describe your consultation request in as much detail as possible.
* **Innovation Area of Interest** (`innovation_area_of_interest`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the area of innovation you're interested in.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **School or University Name** (`school_or_university_name`, `text`, required: false): If you're affiliated with a school or university, enter the name here.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your role within the educational institution (Teacher, Administrator, Parent, or Student).
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `time`, required: false): Enter your time zone.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of submission.
* **User Signature** (`user_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign your form to indicate your consent.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Once you're done, click this button to submit your form.
