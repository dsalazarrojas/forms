# Degree Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Degree Verification Form is used to collect information for degree verification purposes. This form is used by educational institutions to verify the authenticity of degrees and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the relevant options for the "Education Forms" field, which will determine the type of degree verification process.
2. Enter the name of the educational institution where you earned your degree.
3. Provide the email address of the institution.
4. Enter your institution's phone number.
5. Enter the institution's address.
6. Enter the degree verification number (if applicable).
7. Select the date of the degree verification.
8. Choose the background check company (if applicable).
9. Enter the background check ID (if applicable).
10. Select the date of the background check (if applicable).
11. Choose the degree verification type.
12. Enter any additional degree verification notes.
13. Finally, select the verification status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Forms**: This field determines the type of degree verification process.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have completed a degree verification in the past 2 years.
	+ Select 'No' if you have never completed a degree verification in the past.
* **Educational Institution**: This field is the name of the institution where you earned your degree.
* **Email**: This field is the email address of the institution.
* **Phone**: This field is the phone number of the institution.
* **Institution Address**: This field is the physical address of the institution.
* **Degree Verification Number**: This field is the unique number assigned to the degree verification process.
* **Degree Verification Date**: This field is the date of the degree verification process.
* **Background Check Company**: This field is the company that performed the background check (if applicable).
* **Background Check ID**: This field is the ID number assigned by the background check company (if applicable).
* **Background Check Date**: This field is the date of the background check (if applicable).
* **Degree Verification Type**: This field determines the type of degree verification process.
	+ Select Type 1 for a basic degree verification.
	+ Select Type 2 for a comprehensive degree verification.
	+ Select Type 3 for an advanced degree verification.
* **Degree Verification Notes**: This field is for any additional comments or notes about the degree verification process.
* **Verification Status**: This field determines the status of the degree verification process.
	+ Select Status 1 for pending degree verification.
	+ Select Status 2 for degree verification completed.
	+ Select Status 3 for degree verification declined.

## Tips
* Ensure that you answer all required fields to ensure accurate degree verification.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the relevant institution or background check company.
