# Curriculum Modernization Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Modernization Support Request Form is designed to help teachers and educators request support for curriculum modernization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Select the subject area that best describes your request.
3. Provide a detailed description of your request.
4. Select the support format that works best for you (Email, Phone, In-person).
5. Schedule a meeting to discuss your request.
6. Select the course level and grade level that applies to your request.
7. Confirm the submission date and time.
8. Provide any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required):
	+ This field is for your first name, please enter it here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required):
	+ This field is for your last name, please enter it here.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of your school here.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required):
	+ This is your school email address, please enter it here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required):
	+ This is your school phone number, please enter it here.
* **Select a subject** (`subject`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose one or more of the following subjects:
		- Curriculum Development
		- Curriculum Review
		- Curriculum Implementation
* **Description** (`description`, text, required):
	+ Provide a detailed description of your curriculum modernization request.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have a specific department related to your request, you can enter it here.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, required):
	+ Enter the course code related to your request here.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of the course related to your request here.
* **Course Level** (`course_level`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the level of the course:
		- High School
		- Middle School
		- Elementary School
* **Course Grade Level** (`course_grade_level`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the grade level of the course:
		- 9th-12th Grade
		- 6th-8th Grade
		- K-5th Grade
* **Select a support format** (`support_format`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how you would like to receive support:
		- Email
		- Phone
		- In-person
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date for the meeting to discuss your request.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required):
	+ Enter the time for the meeting to discuss your request.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have a specific location for the meeting, you can enter it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them here.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have a specific submitter, you can enter it here.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have a specific submitter, you can enter it here.
* **Submitted to** (`submitted_to`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have a specific submitter, you can enter it here.
* **Updated by** (`updated_by`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have a specific updater, you can enter it here.
* **Updated** (`updated`, date, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have an updated date, you can enter it here.
* **Updated to** (`updated_to`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have an updated date, you can enter it here.
* **Status** (`status`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not required, but if you have a specific status, you can enter it here.
