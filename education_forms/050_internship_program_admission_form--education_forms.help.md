# Internship Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for the internship program. It is used to gather relevant details about the applicant's application, contact information, and education history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the "Application Details" page.
2. Move on to the "Contact Information" page, where you will provide your contact details.
3. Finally, submit your "Education History" page with your relevant educational background information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Details** (Text, Optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your application and why you are interested in the internship program.
* **Contact Information** (Text, Optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Education History** (Text, Optional): Share your educational background, including relevant courses, institutions, and any notable achievements.
