# University Lecturer Employment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Lecturer Employment Application Form is used by potential candidates to apply for lecturer positions at the university. This form is intended for applicants who are interested in joining the university's academic staff. The form should only be submitted if the applicant is serious about pursuing a lecturer role at the university.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used as the primary contact point for communication.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4.  Choose the specific "Position Applied For" from the drop-down menu. This will help the hiring team understand which role you are interested in.
5.  Select all relevant "Job Type" options that apply to you. This will help the hiring team understand your qualifications and experience.
6.  Enter your preferred start date for employment in the "Employment Start Date" field.
7.  Enter your expected end date of employment in the "Employment End Date" field. Please note that this is not a fixed commitment, but rather a preferred end date for the role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number that can be reached for further communication.
* **Position Applied For** (select_one, required): Select the position you are applying for (e.g. Lecturer A, B, or C).
* **Job Type** (select_multiple, required): Select all relevant job types that apply to you (e.g. teaching, research, or administration).
* **Employment Start Date** (date, required): Enter your preferred start date for the employment.
* **Employment End Date** (date, required): Enter your preferred end date of employment.
