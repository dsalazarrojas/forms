# Tuition And Refund Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help educational institutions acknowledge and document their tuition and refund policies. It is meant for internal use to track and communicate changes to these policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
2. Select the date of your tuition and refund policy in the "Tuition and Refund Policy Date" field.
3. Enter the version number of your tuition and refund policy in the "Tuition and Refund Policy Version" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's name.
*   **Tuition and Refund Policy Date** (`tuition_and_refund_policy_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your tuition and refund policy.
*   **Tuition and Refund Policy Version** (`tuition_and_refund_policy_version`, text, optional): Enter the version number of your tuition and refund policy.
