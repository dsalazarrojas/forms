# Faculty Work Load - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Faculty Work Load form is used to collect information about a faculty member's availability, including their work schedule and any additional details they may need to share. This data will likely be used to support administrative tasks or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the faculty member's workload in the required numerical field.
2. Provide the date of your availability or workload in the date field.
3. Share your workload time in the time field.
4. Add any additional notes about your workload in the note field.
5. Enter your email address in the email field.
6. Provide your phone number in the phone field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are available to take on additional workload in the single-select field.
8. Select multiple options from the list to indicate your workload status in the multiple-select field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Faculty Work Load** (`faculty_work_load`, number, required): Enter the faculty member's workload in this field.
- **Date** (`faculty_work_load_date`, date, required): Provide the date of your availability or workload.
- **Time** (`faculty_work_load_time`, time, required): Share your workload time in this field.
- **Note** (`faculty_work_load_note`, note, required): Add any additional notes about your workload.
- **Email** (`faculty_work_load_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`faculty_work_load_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number.
- **Select One** (`faculty_work_load_select_one`, single-select, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are available to take on additional workload.
- **Select Multiple** (`faculty_work_load_select_multiple`, multiple-select, required): Select multiple options from the list to indicate your workload status.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information in the numerical fields to ensure correct representation of your workload.
- Use the date field to indicate a specific date of availability or workload.
- In the select fields, choose the options that apply to your situation.
- Ensure your email and phone number are up-to-date and accurate.
