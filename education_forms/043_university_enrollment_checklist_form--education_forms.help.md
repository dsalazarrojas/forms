# university_enrollment_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The university enrollment checklist form is used to collect information from students and parents/guardians about the student's contact information, identification, and course selection.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, fill out the contact information section, providing the student's and parent/guardian's contact details.
- Next, provide the student's identification details in the student_details section.
- Then, fill out the guardian_details section with the parent/guardian's information.
- After that, choose the student's course selection from the options provided.
- If required, upload any relevant documents in the document_upload section.
- Provide the date and time of the enrollment in the date and time sections.
- Leave any additional notes in the note section.
- Finally, provide your email and phone numbers in the email and phone sections.
- Sign the form with the student's signature in the student_signature section and the parent/guardian's signature in the parent_signature section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_information** (text, required/optional): Provide the student's and parent/guardian's name, address, and contact number(s).
* **student_details** (text, required/optional): Enter the student's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **student_identification** (text, required/optional): Provide the student's identification number, passport number, or any other government-issued ID number.
* **guardian_details** (text, required/optional): Enter the parent/guardian's name, contact information, and relationship to the student.
* **course_selection** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the student's course selection from the options provided.
* **document_upload** (text, required/optional): Upload any relevant documents such as identification, proof of address, or medical certificates.
* **date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the enrollment.
* **time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of the enrollment.
* **note** (note, required/optional): Leave any additional notes about the enrollment process.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter the student's and parent/guardian's email address(es).
* **phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the student's and parent/guardian's phone number(s).
* **student_signature** (text, required/optional): Sign the form with the student's signature.
* **parent_signature** (text, required/optional): Sign the form with the parent/guardian's signature.
