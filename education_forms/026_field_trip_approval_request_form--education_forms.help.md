# Field Trip Approval Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for a field trip, which is an educational trip that may take place outside of school premises.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the School Principal Approver from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide detailed information about the student trip, including dates, location, duration, and budget.
3. List the student participants and their supervisors.
4. Provide emergency contacts and student medical information (if applicable).
5. Explain the field trip justification.
6. Indicate whether the school admin and sponsor company have approved the trip.
7. Select the trip approval status and provide comments (if any).
8. Sign with your student and school principal signature (if applicable).
9. Add any other notes or comments.
10. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Principal Approver**: Select the school principal who will be responsible for approving this field trip.
* **Student Trip Details**: Enter a detailed description of the trip, including the location, dates, and duration.
* **Trip Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of the field trip.
* **Trip Location**: Enter the location of the field trip.
* **Trip Duration**: Enter the number of days the trip will take place.
* **Trip Budget**: Enter the estimated cost of the trip.
* **Student Participants**: List the students participating in the field trip.
* **Student Supervisors**: List the student supervisors who will be accompanying the students on the trip.
* **Emergency Contacts**: Provide emergency contact information for the students and the student supervisors.
* **Student Medical Info**: If any student has medical conditions that may impact their participation in the trip, enter this information here.
* **Field Trip Justification**: Briefly explain the purpose and educational value of the field trip.
* **School Admin Approval**: Indicate whether the school administration has approved the field trip.
* **Sponsor Company Approval**: Indicate whether the sponsor company has approved the field trip.
* **Trip Approval Status**: Select the current status of the trip, which can be Approved, Pending Approval, or Denied.
* **Trip Approval Comments**: If there are any comments or concerns from the approvers, enter them here.
* **Student Signature**: Sign below to confirm that you are the student requesting the field trip.
* **School Principal Signature**: Sign below if you are the school principal approving or denying the trip.
* **Sponsor Company Signature**: Sign below if you are the sponsor company representative approving or denying the trip.
* **Other Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the field trip.
* **Form Submitter**: Enter your name and title.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Form Status**: Select the current status of the form, which can be Active, Archived, or Completed.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form you are using.
* **Last Updated**: Enter the last date the form was updated.
* **Last Updated By**: Enter the name of the last person who updated the form.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required" in the form definition.
