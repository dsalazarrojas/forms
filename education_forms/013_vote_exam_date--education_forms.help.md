# Vote Exam Date - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect exam dates from users. Please fill out this form to provide the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Exam Date" field and select or input your exam date.
2. Select the exam date from the "Select Exam Date" dropdown menu.
3. If you have any additional comments or notes, type them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exam Date** (`date`, `optional`):
	+ This field allows you to input your exam date.
* **Select Exam Date** (`select_one`, `optional`):
	+ Choose the exam date from the dropdown menu.
* **Note** (`note`, `optional`):
	+ Type any additional comments or notes you have regarding your exam date.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
