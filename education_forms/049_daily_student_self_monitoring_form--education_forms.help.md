# Daily Student Self Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Student Self Monitoring Form is a tool for students to track their daily progress and attendance in school. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student ID number in the Student ID field.
2. Select your Class from the Class dropdown menu.
3. Select your Subject from the Subject dropdown menu.
4. Enter the Date of the day you are submitting the form.
5. Enter any additional Notes about your day.
6. Enter your Teacher ID number.
7. Enter your Teacher Name.
8. Select your Assigned Teacher from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Id** (`student_id`, Number, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter your unique Student ID number.
* **Class** (`class_id`, Select One, Required/Optional: Optional): Select your current class.
* **Subject** (`subject`, Select One, Required/Optional: Optional): Choose the subject you are currently taking.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter the date you are submitting the form for.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter any additional notes about your day.
* **Teacher Id** (`teacher_id`, Number, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter your teacher's ID number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Assigned Teacher** (`assigned_teacher`, Select One, Required/Optional: Optional): Select your assigned teacher.

## Tips
* Please only submit one form per day.
* Make sure to select the correct Class and Subject for your current schedule.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your teacher or school administration for clarification.
