# Semiotics Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Semiotics Knowledge Quiz form is designed to assess users' knowledge and understanding of semiotic concepts. This form is used to collect data from users about their experiences, preferences, and opinions related to semiotics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), which are:
	* form_12 (Select One)
	* form_13 (Select One)
	* form_20 (Select One)
	* form_23 (Select One)
2. You can leave the optional fields blank if you are not sure or do not have the information.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
