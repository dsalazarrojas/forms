# Teacher Timetable Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Timetable Form is a tool used by teachers to provide information about their teaching schedule, classroom details, and other relevant data. This form is not mandatory but helps the institution's administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your name, surname, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Describe your classroom details, such as the class name and any other relevant information.
4. Specify your class start and end times.
5. Indicate whether you have a timetable (Yes/No) and choose the subjects you're teaching.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Teacher Information
* **teacher_name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **teacher_surname** (text, required): Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **teacher_email** (email, required): Enter your official email address for communication.
* **teacher_phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Classroom Details
* **class_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the class you're teaching.
* **class_start_time** (time, required): Enter the start time of your class.
* **class_end_time** (time, required): Enter the end time of your class.

### Timetable
* **timetable** (select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have a timetable by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. If you have a timetable, you can choose the subjects you're teaching.

### Other Information
* **subject** (text, required): Enter the subject(s) you're teaching.
