# Learning Outcomes Benchmarking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the learning outcomes of students in the education system. This form helps to track student performance and identify areas where students may need additional support or resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select only one answer from the provided options.
4. For open-ended questions, provide a clear and concise answer.
5. Make sure to complete all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
