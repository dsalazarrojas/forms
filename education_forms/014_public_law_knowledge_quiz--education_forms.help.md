# public_law_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a public law knowledge quiz. It collects information about the quiz, such as correct answers, points assigned to each question, and total points.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the correct answers to the quiz questions.
2. Select the type of quiz (Multiple Choice, Short Answer, or Essay).
3. Enter the question number and points assigned to each question.
4. Enter the points assigned to the correct answer.
5. Enter the total points of the quiz.
6. (Optional) Provide additional text about the quiz questions.
7. (Optional) Enter any other details about the quiz type.
8. Click the "Submit Quiz" button to finalize the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Type** (`quiz_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of quiz you are creating (Multiple Choice, Short Answer, or Essay).
* **Question Number** (`question_number`, number, required): Enter the number of questions in the quiz.
* **Question Points** (`question_points`, number, required): Enter the points assigned to each question.
* **Correct Answer Points** (`correct_answer_points`, number, required): Enter the points assigned to the correct answer.
* **Total Points** (`total_points`, number, required): Enter the total points of the quiz.
* **Quiz Questions Text** (`quiz_questions_text`, text, optional): Provide any additional text about the quiz questions.
* **Other** (`quiz_type_other`, text, optional): Enter any other details about the quiz type.
* **Correct Answer** (`correct_answer`, text, required): Enter the correct answer to the question.
* **Submit Quiz** (`submit_quiz`, text, optional): Click the "Submit Quiz" button to finalize the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the correct answers to ensure they are accurate.
* Use the correct quiz type to ensure the quiz is formatted correctly.
* Enter the correct question number and points to ensure the quiz is scored accurately.
