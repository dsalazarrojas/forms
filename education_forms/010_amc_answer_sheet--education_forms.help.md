# AMC Answer Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AMC Answer Sheet form is designed to collect student information and their answers to a series of questions. This form is used to track student progress and performance on assessments, likely in an educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your complete legal name in the "Student Full Name" field.
2. Enter your unique student ID number in the "Student ID Number" field.
3. Enter the date when the exam was taken in the "Exam Date" field.
4. Select the type of exam you were taking from the options provided in the "Exam Type" field.
5. Enter your current grade level in school in the "Grade Level" field.
6. Enter the name of your school in the "School Name" field.
7. Enter the name of your teacher or proctor in the "Teacher or Proctor Name" field.
8. For questions 1-14, select the correct answer from the options provided for each question.
9. For questions 15-25, only enter information if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (Required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Student ID Number** (Required): Enter your unique student ID number.
* **Exam Date** (Required): Enter the date when the exam was taken.
* **Exam Type** (Required): Select the type of exam you were taking from the options provided.
* **Grade Level** (Required): Enter your current grade in school.
* **School Name** (Required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher or Proctor Name** (Required): Enter the name of your teacher or proctor.
* **Question 1 Answer** (Optional): Select the correct answer from the options provided.
*   ...
* **Question 25 Answer** (Optional): Select the correct answer from the options provided.

## Tips

* Please review the questions carefully to ensure you are answering the correct question.
* If a question is not relevant to your exam, please do not answer it.
* If you are unsure about a question, please ask your teacher or proctor for clarification.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* This form is designed to collect student information and performance data, so please answer honestly and accurately.
