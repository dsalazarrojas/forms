# Curriculum Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess and evaluate the progress of students in a given curriculum. It is intended to be used by both students and instructors to provide a clear understanding of a student's strengths, weaknesses, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the relevant student and course information**: Choose the correct student and course from the drop-down menu or enter the information manually.
2. **Complete each section**: Answer every question in each section to provide a comprehensive evaluation.
3. **Enter accurate and honest feedback**: Provide constructive feedback that is accurate and honest about the student's performance.
4. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed all sections, click the submit button to finalize your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Student Info)**: Enter the student's name, student ID, or other relevant student information.
* **Course Information (Course Info)**: Enter the course name, course number, or other relevant course information.
* **Student Progress (Student Progress)**: Describe the student's current progress in the course, including any notable achievements or areas of improvement.
* **Assessment (Assessment)**: Enter a numerical value to evaluate the student's current assessment level (e.g., 1-100).
* **Strengths (Strengths)**: List the student's strengths in the course, such as their exceptional knowledge or skills.
* **Weaknesses (Weaknesses)**: List the student's weaknesses or areas that need improvement in the course.
* **Comments (Comments)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the student's performance.
* **Course Rating (Course Rating)**: Enter a numerical value to evaluate the course overall (e.g., 1-100).
* **Overall Rating (Overall Rating)**: Enter a numerical value to evaluate the student's overall performance (e.g., 1-100).
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the student's email address so that they can be contacted with any further evaluation updates.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter the student's phone number so that they can be easily contacted with any further evaluation updates.
