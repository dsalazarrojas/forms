# BCC Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BCC Student Registration Form is designed to collect information from students for educational registration purposes. This form helps gather essential details that are crucial for student enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
3. Review and confirm information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal first name as it appears on your birth certificate.
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal last name as it appears on your birth certificate.
* **Student Middle Name** (`student_middle_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your middle name if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the given options.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full home address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, `text`, required): Describe your relation to your emergency contact.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current or entering grade level.
* **Previous School Attended** (`previous_school`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your previous school.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies that the school should be aware of.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you will get to school.
* **Lunch Program Participation** (`lunch_program`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your participation in the lunch program.
* **Interest in After-School Activities** (`after_school_activities`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your areas of interest in after-school activities.
* **Enrollment Term** (`enrollment_term`, `select_one`, required): Select the academic term for your enrollment.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your registration.
* **Parent or Guardian Consent** (`parent_signature_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you consent to your child's enrollment.

Note: The guide will be updated as new fields and features are added to the form.
