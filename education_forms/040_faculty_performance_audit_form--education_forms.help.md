# Faculty Performance Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Faculty Performance Audit Form is a tool used to collect information about a faculty member's performance and evaluation. It is used for assessment and evaluation purposes, such as for promotions, tenure, or other personnel decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Audit Form** (`audit_form`, text, required: false): This is the main section of the form where you can add any comments or observations about the faculty member's performance.
2. **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the faculty member's department.
3. **Faculty Member** (`faculty_member`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the faculty member being evaluated.
4. **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Select the role of the faculty member from the options provided, such as Faculty Member, Department Chair, or Director.
5. **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the evaluation.
6. **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or observations about the faculty member's performance.
7. **Department** (`department_id`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the department(s) that the faculty member is associated with from the options provided.
8. **Submit** (`submit`, date, required: true): Click the submit button to complete the form and submit the data.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant personnel or the form's administrator.
* If you are filling out the form for multiple faculty members, please create a new instance of the form for each individual.
