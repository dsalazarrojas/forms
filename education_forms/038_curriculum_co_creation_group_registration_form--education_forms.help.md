# Curriculum Co Creation Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Co Creation Group Registration Form is used for registering groups or projects for curriculum co-creation. This form captures relevant information about the group, its purpose, and its details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your education background in the "Education Background" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This field is required to ensure that we can contact you regarding your registration.
3. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
4. Select your role in the "Role" field.
5. If you have a school, please enter it in the "School" field.
6. If your group has a department, please enter it in the "Department" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Enter the name of your group in the "Group Name" field.
9. Select whether your group is for "Yes" or "No" in the "Group Type" field.
10. Choose one or more purposes for your group in the "Purpose" field.
11. If your group has a location, please enter it in the "Location" field.
12. If your group has a start and end date, please enter them in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
13. If applicable, enter the frequency of your group's meetings in the "Frequency" field.
14. If you have specific time requirements for your group, please enter them in the "Time Required" field.
15. If you wish, you may provide a description of your group in the "Description" field.
16. Enter your name and email address in the "Submitter Email" and "Submitter Name" fields, respectively.
17. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone Number" field.
18. If your submitter has a department, please enter it in the "Submitter Department" field.
19. Select your submitter's role in the "Submitter Role" field.
20. If your submitter has a location, please enter it in the "Submitter Location" field.
21. If your submitter has specific time requirements, please enter them in the "Submitter Time Required" field.
22. If applicable, enter the frequency of your submitter's availability in the "Submitter Frequency" field.
23. If you wish, you may provide comments about your submission in the "Submitter Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Background**: Enter your relevant education background information (e.g., degree, institution).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Role**: Select your role in the group or project.
* **School**: If your group is affiliated with a school, enter the name of the school.
* **Department**: If your group is affiliated with a department, enter the department name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Group Name**: Enter the name of your group or project.
* **Group Type**: Select whether your group is "Yes" or "No".
* **Purpose**: Choose one or more purposes for your group.
* **Location**: If your group has a location, enter it.
* **Start Date**: If your group has a start date, enter it.
* **End Date**: If your group has an end date, enter it.
* **Frequency**: If your group meets at a specific frequency, enter it.
* **Time Required**: If your group has specific time requirements, enter them.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your group or project.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your name and email address.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name.
* **Submitter Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Department**: If your submitter has a department, enter it.
* **Submitter Role**: Select your role as the submitter.
* **Submitter Location**: If your submitter has a location, enter it.
* **Submitter Time Required**: If your submitter has specific time requirements, enter them.
* **Submitter Frequency**: If your submitter has a frequency of availability, enter it.
* **Submitter Comments**: Provide any comments about your submission.
