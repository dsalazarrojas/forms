# Student Declaration Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Declaration Template is a form designed to collect information and agreements from students. It can be used for various purposes, such as academic or administrative activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing the requested information in the corresponding fields.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Declaration Template** (`student_declaration_template`, text, optional): This is a free-text field where you can enter any relevant information about your declaration.
* **Student Assessment Declaration** (`student_assessment_declaration`, text, optional): This field is similar to the first field, where you can enter additional information about your assessment.
* **Assessment Agreement** (`assessment_agreement`, select one, optional):
	+ Select "Yes" if you agree with the terms and conditions of your assessment.
	+ Select "No" if you don't agree or have further questions.
* **Declaration Agreement** (`declaration_agreement`, select one, optional):
	+ Select "Yes" if you agree with the terms and conditions of your declaration.
	+ Select "No" if you don't agree or have further questions.
* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, optional): This field is for additional comments or notes about your student status.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, optional): This field is for your digital or physical signature, indicating your agreement.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your administrator or teacher.
