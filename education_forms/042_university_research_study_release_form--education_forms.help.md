# University Research Study Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Research Study Release Form is used to gather information about participants in research studies, including their demographics, study details, and study progress. This form is a critical document that ensures transparency, consent, and accurate tracking of study progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide participant information: Enter the participant's name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Enter study details: Provide the study's start and end dates, location, and duration.
3. Update participant status: Select the participant's current status (Current, Former, or Never).
4. Track study completion: Enter the study's completion date and status.
5. Record participant progress: Enter notes about the participant's progress and signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the participant's name, ensuring accurate and consistent spelling.
* **Study Details** (`study_details`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of the study, including its objectives and goals.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the participant's contact information, such as their email and phone number.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, optional):
	+ Ensure that the participant has provided consent to participate in the study.
* **Contact Consent** (`contact_consent`, text, optional):
	+ Confirm that the participant has given consent for contact.
* **Research Informed Consent Form** (`research_informed`, text, optional):
	+ Attach or paste a copy of the informed consent form used in the study.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the participant's current status (Current, Former, or Never).
* **Study Completion** (`study_completion`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the study's completion date and status.
* **Participant Status Update** (`participant_status_update`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the participant's current status (Active, Completed, Inactive, or Withdrawn).
* **Participant Completion** (`participant_completion`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the participant's completion date and status.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the participant's phone number type (Work, Cell, or Other).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Study Start Date** (`study_start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the study's start date.
* **Study End Date** (`study_end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the study's end date.
* **Research Start Date** (`research_start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the research's start date.
* **Research End Date** (`research_end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the research's end date.
* **Research Completion Date** (`research_completion_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the research's completion date.
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the location where the study will take place.
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the study's duration.
* **Research Completion Status** (`research_completion_status`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the research completion status.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any notes about the participant.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Attach or paste the participant's signature.
* **Research Signature** (`research_signature`, text, optional):
	+ Attach or paste the research's signature.
* **Research Notes** (`research_notes`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any notes about the research.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure data integrity.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and consistently.
- Double-check the participant's status and study progress regularly.
