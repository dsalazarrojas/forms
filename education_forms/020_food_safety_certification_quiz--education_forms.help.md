# Food Safety Certification Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Safety Certification Quiz is designed to assess the user's understanding of food safety practices. This quiz will help to identify areas of improvement and provide a certification to users who pass the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your personal details such as date of birth, email, phone number, and date.
2. Select the options that best answer the food safety questions.
3. Select the category and subcategory that best fit your information.
4. Choose the location where you are working.
5. Fill out the assigned to and submitted by fields with the corresponding information.
