# Admissions Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Admissions Application form is a comprehensive platform for students to submit their applications for various degree programs at our institution. The form is designed to collect information about the applicant, including demographic details, educational background, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form carefully**: Before starting the application, take a few minutes to review the form's contents to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. **Fill in required fields**: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled in accurately.
3. **Be accurate and honest**: Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and honest, as it will be used to assess your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (id: 2): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email Address** (id: 3): Enter a valid email address that is used for communication purposes only.
- **Phone Number** (id: 4): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Address** (id: 5): Enter your current residential address.
- **City** (id: 6): Enter the city of your current residence.
- **State/Province** (id: 7): Enter the state or province of your current residence.
- **ZIP/Postal Code** (id: 8): Enter the postal code of your current residence.
- **Country** (id: 9): Enter the country of your current residence.
- **Date of Birth** (id: 10): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Program Applying For** (id: 11): Select the program you are applying for.
- **Degree Level** (id: 12): Select the level of degree you are pursuing (e.g., Certificate, Diploma, Associate, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate, Other).
- **Start Term** (id: 13): Select the term you wish to start (e.g., Fall, Spring, Summer, Winter).
- **Start Year** (id: 14): Enter the year you wish to start.
- **Education Background** (id: 15): Describe your educational background, including previous institutions attended and degrees earned.
- **Previous Institution** (id: 16): If applicable, enter the name of your previous educational institution.
- **GPA** (id: 17): Enter your cumulative GPA (if applicable).
- **Standardized Test Scores** (id: 18): Enter any standardized test scores you have achieved (e.g., SAT, ACT, GRE, GMAT).
- **Work Experience** (id: 19): Describe any relevant work experience.
- **Extracurricular Activities** (id: 20): Describe any extracurricular activities you have been involved in.
- **Personal Statement** (id: 21): Write a personal statement about your goals and motivations for applying.
- **References** (id: 22): Provide contact information for your references.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 23): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 24): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (id: 25): Describe your relationship to your emergency contact.
- **Housing Request** (id: 26): Select whether you require on-campus housing or not.
- **Financial Aid Status** (id: 27): Select your financial aid status (e.g., Applied, Awarded, Declined, Not Applying, Pending).
- **Special Accommodations** (id: 28): If applicable, describe any special accommodations you require.
- **Application Date** (id: 29): Enter the date of submitting this application.
- **Student Signature** (id: 30): Provide an electronic signature to confirm your application.
