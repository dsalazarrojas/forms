# Academic Peer Review Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Review Publication Consent Form is a document that allows reviewers to express their consent for the publication of their peer review, including how they want to be credited and if any embargo requests are needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as the reviewer in the **Reviewer name** field.
2.  Provide your email address in the **Reviewer email** field for publication correspondence.
3.  Enter the title of the manuscript being reviewed in the **Manuscript title** field.
4.  Select your preference for publication in the **Consent to publish the review** field:
    -  Yes, publish the review
    -  Yes, publish anonymized review
    -  No, do not publish
5.  If you choose to be cited, specify your preferred citation method in the **Citation preference** field.
6.  If you have any embargo requests, enter them in the **Embargo requests** field.
7.  Describe the conditions under which the review may be republished in the **Terms for republication** field.
8.  Sign your full name to confirm your consent in the **Signature name** field.
9.  Enter the date you're giving your consent in the **Signature date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reviewer name** (Reviewer name, text, required): 
  This is where you enter your name as the reviewer granting consent.
- **Reviewer email** (Reviewer email, email, required): 
  This is where you provide your contact email for publication correspondence.
- **Manuscript title** (Manuscript title, text, required): 
  This field is for the title of the manuscript being reviewed.
- **Consent to publish the review** (Consent to publish the review, select_one, required): 
  This is where you decide if the review should be published, whether fully or anonymized.
- **Citation preference** (Citation preference, text, required): 
  If you wish to be credited, specify your preferred citation method here.
- **Embargo requests** (Embargo requests, text, required): 
  Enter any requested embargo period here if you need one.
- **Terms for republication** (Terms for republication, text, required): 
  Describe the conditions under which your review may be republished.
- **Signature name** (Signature name, text, required): 
  Sign your full name to confirm your consent.
- **Signature date** (Signature date, date, required): 
  Enter the date on which you give your consent.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately to avoid delays in the publishing process.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your agreement or contact the relevant parties.
- Always use clear and complete contact information for correspondence.
