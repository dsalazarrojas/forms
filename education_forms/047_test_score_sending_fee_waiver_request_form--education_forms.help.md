# Test Score Sending Fee Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request a fee waiver for sending test scores. It is intended to be completed by students who are unable to afford the fees associated with sending their test scores and need a fee waiver to be considered for test score sending.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the form in the "Form Title" field.
2. Select the category for which the form is being submitted (e.g., "Education Forms", "General", etc.).
3. Provide a brief description of your request in the "Description" field.
4. Enter the school name and student name in their respective fields.
5. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
6. Confirm the student's email address in the "Confirm Student Email" field.
7. Select whether or not you would like to upload any supporting documents.
8. Attach the required documentation (if selected).
9. Provide your E-Signature in the "E-Signature" field.
10. Enter the school name and E-Signature of the school representative (if required).
11. Select the student's grade level.
12. If this is not the first student, enter the school name and student name of the second student.
13. Enter the student's email address of the second student (if applicable).
14. Confirm the student's email address of the second student (if applicable).
15. Select whether or not to upload any supporting documents for the second student.
16. Attach the required documentation for the second student (if selected).
17. Provide your E-Signature for the second student.
18. Enter the school name and E-Signature of the school representative for the second student (if required).
19. Select the student's grade level for the second student.
20. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title**: Enter a brief title for the form, e.g., "Request for Test Score Fee Waiver".
- **Category**: Select the category for which the form is being submitted (e.g., "Education Forms", "General", etc.).
- **Description**: Briefly describe the reason for requesting a fee waiver.
- **School Name**: Enter the name of the school the student is attending.
- **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student requesting the fee waiver.
- **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
- **Confirm Student Email**: Confirm the student's email address.
- **Supporting Document Upload**: Select whether or not to upload any supporting documents.
- **Documentation**: Attach any required documentation (if selected).
- **E-Signature**: Provide your E-Signature.
- **School Signature**: Enter the school name and E-Signature of the school representative (if required).
- **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level.
- **School Name 2**: Enter the name of the school the second student is attending (if applicable).
- **Student Name 2**: Enter the name of the second student (if applicable).
- **Student Email 2**: Enter the email address of the second student (if applicable).
- **Confirm Student Email 2**: Confirm the email address of the second student (if applicable).
- **Supporting Document Upload 2**: Select whether or not to upload any supporting documents for the second student.
- **Documentation 2**: Attach any required documentation for the second student (if selected).
- **E-Signature 2**: Provide your E-Signature for the second student.
- **School Signature 2**: Enter the school name and E-Signature of the school representative for the second student (if required).
- **Student Grade Level 2**: Select the student's grade level for the second student.
- **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.
