# Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Request Form is designed to collect information from users about their interest in a product or service. This form is meant to be a starting point for further contact and discussion. Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure that we can respond to your request effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Company, Email, and Description).
2. If applicable, include your phone number for further communication.
3. Choose the correct product or service that you are interested in.
4. Type a brief description of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your given first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your given last name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address. This will be used for further contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you wish to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Product or Service** (`product_service`, text, required): Choose the product or service that you are interested in.
* **Description** (`description`, note, required): Type a brief description of your request. This will help us understand your needs better.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate while filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* We do not guarantee any responses or actions taken from the form submissions.
