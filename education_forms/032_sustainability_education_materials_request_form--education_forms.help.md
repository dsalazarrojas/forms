# sustainability_education_materials_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sustainability education materials request form is designed to collect information from schools and organizations to support their education and training needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Request Type" option that best suits your needs: "Request for Resources", "Request for Training", or "Request for Consultation".
2. Choose the type of resources or training you are interested in, selecting all that apply from the list.
3. Indicate the audience that will benefit from the requested resources or training.
4. Select your preferred delivery method: "In-person", "Virtual", or "Email".
5. Choose the output format that you would like to receive: "PDF", "Video", or "Document".
6. Enter any additional comments or information about your request.
7. Provide your contact information: email, phone number, name, and address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one, required): This field helps us understand the type of request you are making (Resources, Training, or Consultation).
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the types of resources or training that interest you.
* **Resource Requests** (`resource_requests`, text, not required): Use this field to provide any additional information about the resources you are requesting.
* **Training Requests** (`training_requests`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about the training you are requesting.
* **Audience** (`audience`, select_multiple, required): Select all the audiences that will benefit from the requested resources or training.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred delivery method: "In-person", "Virtual", or "Email".
* **Output Format** (`output_format`, select_multiple, not required): Select the output format that suits your needs: "PDF", "Video", "Document".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Use this field to add any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of your school or institution.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of your school or institution.
* **School Fax** (`school_fax`, text, not required): Enter the fax number of your school or institution.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of your school or institution.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, not required): Enter the city of your school or institution.
* **School State** (`school_state`, text, not required): Enter the state of your school or institution.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, text, not required): Enter the zip code of your school or institution.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Email** (`organization_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of your organization.
* **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of your organization.
* **Organization Fax** (`organization_fax`, text, not required): Enter the fax number of your organization.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of your organization.
* **Organization City** (`organization_city`, text, not required): Enter the city of your organization.
* **Organization State** (`organization_state`, text, not required): Enter the state of your organization.
* **Organization Zip** (`organization_zip`, text, not required): Enter the zip code of your organization.
