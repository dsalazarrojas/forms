# Student Offense Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report incidents involving students, ensuring consistency and accuracy in the reporting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

*   Gather the necessary information about the student involved.
*   Fill in the student's information (Section 1: Student Information) with the student's ID and name.
*   Describe the details of the offense (Section 4: Offense Details) in a clear and concise manner.
*   Select the grade of the offense using the grade scale (Section 5: Offense Grade).
*   Document the actions taken in response to the incident (Section 6: Actions Taken).
*   Summarize the conversation with the guardian (Section 7: Conversation Summary).
*   Obtain a signature from both the student (Section 8: Student Signature) and the guardian (Section 9: Guardian Signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information (1)**: This section collects the student's identification number and name.
  *   Enter the student's ID number.
  *   Enter the student's name.
*   **Student ID (2)**: Enter the student's ID number.
*   **Student Name (3)**: Enter the student's name.
*   **Offense Details (4)**: Provide a clear and concise description of the incident.
*   **Offense Grade (5)**: Select a grade for the offense using the scale below:
  *   1: Minor offense
  *   2: Moderate offense
  *   3: Major offense
*   **Actions Taken (6)**: Describe the actions taken in response to the incident.
*   **Conversation Summary (7)**: Summarize the conversation with the guardian.
*   **Student Signature (8)**: Obtain a signature from the student to confirm they have seen the report.
*   **Guardian Signature (9)**: Obtain a signature from the guardian to confirm they have been informed.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you complete the form accurately and truthfully.
*   Keep in mind that this form is a record of incidents and should be used for administrative purposes only.
*   If you are unsure about any information, seek guidance from your supervisor or HR department.
