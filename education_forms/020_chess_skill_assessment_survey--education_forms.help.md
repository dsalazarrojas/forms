# Chess Skill Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your chess skills, preferences, and goals. It will help us understand your level of expertise, experience, and interests related to chess.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully and honestly.** Answer each question based on your actual experience and skills.
2. **Read each question carefully.** Make sure you understand what is being asked before answering.
3. **Select the correct answer options.** Choose the option that best describes your situation.
4. **Submit the form.** Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send it in.
