# Distance Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their distance learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the course materials using the feedback_rating field. 
3. Provide a brief comment about your experience in the rating_comment field.
4. Choose how you felt about a specific option (select_one) and multiple options (select_multiple).
5. Provide the date and time of your last online session.
6. Enter your phone number (optional, but if you do have one, please share).
7. Add any additional notes you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you wish to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your institutional or personal email address.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the course materials on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, optional): Provide a brief comment about your experience.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose how you felt about a specific option.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options that apply to you.
* **Session Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of your last online session.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your feedback, as it will help us improve the course.
* Try to keep your comments and ratings brief and to the point.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, feel free to share them with us in the notes field.
