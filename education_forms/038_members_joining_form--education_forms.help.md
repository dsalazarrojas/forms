# Members Joining Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Members Joining Form is a crucial tool for the organization to keep track of its members accurately. It collects essential information such as name, email, phone number, and date of joining.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information page with the required details about the new member.
2. Proceed to the Name field and enter the member's full name as it appears on their identification document or as they like to be called.
3. In the Email field, enter the member's valid email address.
4. In the Phone field, enter the member's primary phone number.
5. On the Join Date field, select the date the member joined the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (text, required):** Enter a brief description of the member's status or any additional notes about them.
* **Name (text, optional):** Enter the member's full name as it appears on their identification document or as they like to be called.
* **Email (email, optional):** Enter the member's valid email address.
* **Phone (text, optional):** Enter the member's primary phone number.
* **Join Date (date, optional):** Select the date the member joined the organization.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information about the new member to ensure correct registration.
* You can only fill out this form once for each member joining the organization.
* If you need to make any changes to the submitted information, please submit a new entry.
