# Symbolism And Theme Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Symbolism And Theme Assessment is a comprehensive form designed to evaluate a student's understanding of symbolism and theme in literature. This form is intended to provide an overview of the student's ability to analyze and interpret symbolic elements and themes in literary works.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the passage provided at the beginning of the form.
2. Review the questions carefully and select the options that best answer each question.
3. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the options that best match your analysis.
4. For text questions, provide a clear and concise answer in the provided text box.
5. Make sure to answer each question carefully, as the answers will be used to evaluate your understanding of symbolism and theme.
