# Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This student survey is designed to gather feedback from students on their experience and needs in the educational environment. The information collected will help us understand areas of improvement and provide better support to students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and select all that apply to you.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Ensure you answer all required questions (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Curriculum (1)
* **Curriculum**: 
  This question is to understand your experience with the curriculum offered.
  Please select all that apply to you:
  - Yes, I have had a positive experience with the curriculum.
  - No, I have not had a positive experience with the curriculum.

### Facilities (2)
* **Facilities**: 
  Please rate your satisfaction with the facilities available to you:
  You can enter a numerical value between 1 and 10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.

### Materials (3)
* **Materials**: 
  Please describe any materials or resources you feel are necessary or missing from the curriculum:
  Enter your thoughts and suggestions in the text box below.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to add them in the "Materials" field.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
