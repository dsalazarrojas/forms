# Educational App Testing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential testers who are interested in participating in educational app testing. It's a simple and easy-to-use form that helps us understand your testing requirements and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the right details.
3. Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tester Application (1)**: Provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, contact information, and any relevant experience or qualifications.
- **Testing Goals (2)**: Describe the testing goals you're interested in, such as testing educational apps, games, or other related projects.
- **Testing Pilot Details (3)**: Share any specific testing pilot requirements or conditions you'd like to be included in the testing process.
- **Contact Information (4)**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to get in touch with you about the testing process.
- **Tester Details (5)**: Share any relevant details about yourself as a tester, such as your experience with testing, technical skills, or availability for testing.
- **App Pilot Details (6)**: If you're interested in testing a specific app, describe the app's features, goals, and any relevant information about the testing process.
- **Testing Schedule (7)**: Provide a rough idea of when you'd like to start and end the testing process.
- **Tester Qualification (8)**: If you have any relevant qualifications or certifications, please share them with us to help us better understand your background and experience.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any delays in the testing process.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, don't hesitate to ask.
* Once submitted, the form will be reviewed, and we'll get in touch with you to discuss further details.
