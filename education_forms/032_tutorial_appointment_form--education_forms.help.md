# Tutorial Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutorial Appointment Form is designed to help students and tutors schedule appointments for tutorial sessions. This form is used to collect information from both students and tutors to set up a session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of form you are filling out (Student or Tutor).
2. Fill out the required fields for your selected form type.
3. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): This field is for you, the student, to provide some basic information about yourself.
* **Tutor Info** (`tutor_info`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): This field is for you, the tutor, to provide some basic information about yourself.
* **Session Info** (`session_info`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Describe the type of session you are looking for (e.g., "Math homework help").
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Describe the details of the session (e.g., "Wednesday at 2 PM, in the library").

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information in each field.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, include them in the "Session Info" field.
* If you have any scheduling conflicts, contact the form administrators for assistance.
