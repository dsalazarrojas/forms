# Student Weekly Music Practice Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Weekly Music Practice Record Form is designed to help students track their music practice habits, setting goals and achieving progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select your instrument from the "Instrument" field.
3. Enter the duration of your practice session in the "Practice Duration" field.
4. (Optional) Enter your practice frequency in the "Practice Frequency" field.
5. Write any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used for communication with your instructor.
7. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Select the dates of your practice sessions in the "Practice Session Dates" field.
9. Enter the times of your practice sessions in the "Practice Session Times" field.
10. In the "Practice Session Goals" field, write down your goals and objectives for each practice session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or identification documents.
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, `text`, required): Select the instrument you are practicing, e.g., piano, guitar, violin, etc.
* **Practice Duration** (`practice_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of time you spent practicing, e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.
* **Practice Frequency** (`practice_frequency`, `text`, optional): Enter how often you practice, e.g., daily, weekly, etc.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Write any additional comments or notes about your practice sessions.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication with your instructor.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication with your instructor.
* **Practice Session Dates** (`practice_session_dates`, `date`, required): Select the dates of your practice sessions.
* **Practice Session Times** (`practice_session_times`, `time`, required): Enter the times of your practice sessions.
* **Practice Session Goals** (`practice_session_goals`, `note`, required): Write down your goals and objectives for each practice session.
