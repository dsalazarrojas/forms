# High School Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess a high school student's progress and skills in various areas. It helps educators, teachers, or school administrators evaluate a student's strengths and weaknesses, providing valuable insights for improvement and development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more options from the "Student Progress" dropdown menu that best describe the student's current progress.
2. Choose one option from the "Subject Areas" dropdown menu that represents the area(s) where you are evaluating the student.
3. Provide any additional comments or hints about the student's progress in the "Subject Areas Hints" text field.
4. Answer the question about the student's self-improvement skills.
5. Answer the question about the student's problem-solving skills.
6. Answer the question about the student's time management skills.
7. Answer the question about the student's communication skills.
8. Answer the question about the student's collaboration skills.
9. Answer the question about the student's presentation skills.
10. Answer the question about the student's leadership skills.
11. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the student's progress.
12. Add any other comments or feedback in the "Comments" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more options that best describe the student's current progress, such as Self-Improvement, Problem-Solving, or Time-Management.
* **Subject Areas** (`subject_areas`, select_one, optional): Select one option that represents the area where you are evaluating the student, such as English, Math, Science, or History.
* **Subject Areas Hints** (`subject_areas_hints`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or hints about the student's progress in the selected subject area.
* **Self-Improvement** (`self_improvement`, text, optional): Describe the student's self-improvement skills, such as their ability to set goals, develop self-awareness, or improve time management.
* **Problem-Solving** (`problem_solving`, text, optional): Describe the student's problem-solving skills, such as their ability to analyze problems, find solutions, or work with others.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, text, optional): Describe the student's time management skills, such as their ability to prioritize tasks, use calendars, or set reminders.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional): Describe the student's communication skills, such as their ability to express themselves clearly, listen actively, or work with others.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, text, optional): Describe the student's collaboration skills, such as their ability to work with others, share ideas, or compromise.
* **Presentation** (`presentation`, text, optional): Describe the student's presentation skills, such as their ability to present ideas clearly, use visual aids, or engage the audience.
* **Leadership** (`leadership`, text, optional): Describe the student's leadership skills, such as their ability to motivate others, make decisions, or take charge.
* **Student Progress Hints** (`student_progress_hints`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or hints about the student's progress.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any other comments or feedback about the student's progress.
