# Flight School Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flight School Student Registration Form is a form used to collect information from students who are enrolling in flight school. This form is used to capture essential details that will help us get to know you better and provide a tailored experience for you throughout your flight training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* Select your preferred course from the list provided in the "Course" field.
* Enter your address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: no)
	+ This field is used to capture your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: no)
	+ This field is used to capture your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: no)
	+ This field is used to capture a valid email address that will be used for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: no)
	+ This field is used to capture your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: no)
	+ This field is used to capture your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Course** (`course`, select_one, required: no)
	+ Select your preferred course from the options provided.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: no)
	+ This field is used to capture your address.
* **Submit** (submit, text, required: no)
	+ Click this button to submit your registration form.
