# Academic Advising Caseload Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the department determine the optimal advisor-to-student ratio for each advisor in the academic department. The goal is to ensure that each advisor has a manageable caseload that allows for effective advising and student support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Select your current advising role (Full-time academic advisor, Part-time academic advisor, Faculty advisor, Peer advisor, or Other).
3. Choose your years of advising experience.
4. Enter your current caseload size.
5. Select the types of students you primarily advise (Freshmen, Sophomores, Juniors and seniors, Transfer students, Graduate students, or At-risk or probationary students).
6. Enter your average number of advising meetings per week.
7. Rate your current workload (Manageable, Somewhat heavy, Heavy, or Overwhelming).
8. Provide a recommended ideal caseload size and explain your rationale.
9. If necessary, select additional support you need to manage your caseload (Additional advising staff, Better technology and tools, More training or professional development, Reduced administrative duties, Peer advisor support, or Other).
10. Finally, enter how caseload size impacts the quality of advising you provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advisor full name** (`advisor_name`, **text**, **required**): Enter your full name in the format First name Last name.
* **Email address** (`advisor_email`, **email**, **required**): Enter your university email address.
* **Department or college** (`department_or_college`, **text**, **required**): Enter the name of the department or college you advise for.
* **Advising role** (`advisor_role`, **select_one**, **required**): Select your advising role (Full-time academic advisor, Part-time academic advisor, Faculty advisor, Peer advisor, or Other).
* **Years of advising experience** (`years_advising`, **select_one**, **required**): Select the number of years you have been advising (Less than 1 year, 1 to 3 years, 3 to 5 years, 5 to 10 years, or More than 10 years).
* **Current number of students in your caseload** (`current_caseload_size`, **number**, **required**): Enter the number of students currently in your caseload.
* **Types of students you primarily advise** (`student_population_types`, **select_multiple**, **required**): Select all the types of students you primarily advise.
* **Average number of advising meetings per week** (`average_meetings_per_week`, **number**, **required**): Enter the average number of advising meetings you have per week.
* **How would you rate your current workload** (`current_workload_rating`, **select_one**, **required**): Rate your current workload (Manageable, Somewhat heavy, Heavy, or Overwhelming).
* **Recommended ideal caseload size** (`recommended_caseload`, **number**, **required**): Enter the recommended number of students you believe would allow for effective advising.
* **Rationale for your recommended caseload** (`recommendation_rationale`, **text**, **required**): Enter a brief explanation of why you recommend this caseload size.
* **Additional support needed** (`additional_support_needed`, **select_multiple**, **optional**): Select additional support you need to manage your caseload.
* **Impact on students** (`impact_on_students`, **text**, **required**): Enter how caseload size impacts the quality of advising you provide.
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`additional_comments`, **text**, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have.
