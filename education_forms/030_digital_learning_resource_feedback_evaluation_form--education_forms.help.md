# digital_learning_resource_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital_learning_resource_feedback_evaluation_form is designed to gather feedback from users about digital learning resources. It is used to collect information about the resource's quality, relevance, and usefulness. This feedback will help us improve and refine our digital learning resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the questions carefully.
2. Answer each question based on your actual experience with the digital learning resource.
3. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*)).
4. Use the comment section to provide any additional information that may be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **resource title (Text, Optional)**: This is the title of the digital learning resource you are evaluating. Please enter the actual title of the resource.
* **resource rating (Number, Optional)**: Rate the resource's quality on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **comments (Text, Optional)**: Use this section to add any additional comments or feedback about the resource. This can include suggestions for improvement or areas where the resource excels.
* **suggest improvements (Text, Optional)**: If you have ideas for how the resource could be improved, enter them here.
