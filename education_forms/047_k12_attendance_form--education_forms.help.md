# K12 Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The K12 Attendance Form is a tool used to record attendance information of K12 students. It is used to collect data on student attendance, including the date and time of attendance, reasons for absence, tardiness, and early leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the student from the dropdown menu in the first field.
* Enter the attendance date in the "Attendance Date" field.
* Select the attendance time in the "Attendance Time" field.
* Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the teacher was absent.
* Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student was absent.
* Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student was late.
* Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student left early.
* For fields marked "Yes" or "No", choose the correct option that matches the situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student (student)**: Select the student whose attendance is being recorded.
* **Attendance Date (attendance_date)**: Enter the date of the student's attendance in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/01/2022).
* **Attendance Time (attendance_time)**: Select the time of the student's attendance in the format HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **Reason For Absence (reason_for_absence)**: Choose "Yes" if the student was absent for a reason (e.g., illness, family emergency).
* **Teacher Absence (teacher_absence)**: Choose "Yes" if the teacher was absent.
* **Student Absence (student_absence)**: Choose "Yes" if the student was absent.
* **Late Tardiness (late_tardiness)**: Choose "Yes" if the student arrived late.
* **Early Leave (early_leave)**: Choose "Yes" if the student left early.
* **Reason For Tardiness (reason_for_tardiness)**: Choose "Yes" if the student's late arrival was due to a reason (e.g., traffic, personal issue).

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct student for each attendance record.
* Be accurate when entering the attendance date and time.
* Choose "Yes" or "No" only if the situation applies.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to your school's attendance policy for guidance.
