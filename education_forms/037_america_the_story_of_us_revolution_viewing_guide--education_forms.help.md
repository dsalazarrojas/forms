# America The Story Of Us Revolution Viewing Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to be used in conjunction with a video or documentary about the American Revolution. The form will guide users through a comprehensive viewing guide, asking questions and prompting reflection before, during, and after watching the content.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions before, during, and after watching the video.
* Fill in the answers in the corresponding fields.
* Read through the sections carefully to make sure you haven't missed any important questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. -- Student Information --
* **student_name** (Student Name): Please enter your full name.
* **Class Period**: Please enter your class period or group ID.
* **Date**: Enter the date you watched the video.
