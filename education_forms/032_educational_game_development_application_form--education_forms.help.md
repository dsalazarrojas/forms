# Educational Game Development Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Game Development Application Form is a comprehensive application form designed for educational game developers to submit their project proposals. This form helps us evaluate the project's feasibility, assess the applicant's expertise, and ensure that the project aligns with our goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name in the field provided.
2. Provide a brief description of your project concept in the Concept Description field.
3. Choose your preferred Tech Stack from the dropdown menu.
4. Define your project Goal in a short paragraph.
5. If you have a working prototype, provide the Prototype URL.
6. List the members of your team, including their names and email addresses.
7. Enter your contact Email and Phone number.
8. Select the Category that best describes your project.
9. Write a detailed Description of your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and surname as you would like to be addressed.
* **Concept Description** (`concept_description`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your project idea, focusing on its unique selling points and how it fits with our goals.
* **Tech Stack** (`tech_stack`, `text`, required): Select the relevant technologies you plan to use for the project (e.g., Unity, Unreal Engine, C#).
* **Goal** (`goal`, `text`, required): Clearly state the project's objective, including any specific outcomes, metrics, or targets.
* **Prototype** (`prototype`, `text`, optional): If you have a working prototype, provide the URL or a brief description of it.
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, required): List the names and email addresses of your team members, including yourself.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant categories that best describe your project (Education Forms, Gamer Forms).
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Write a detailed description of your project, including its goals, objectives, and how it aligns with our goals.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
* Use clear and concise language in your answers.
* Proofread your application before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We recommend having a working prototype ready before submitting your application.
