# parent_acknowledgement_form_for_covid_19_school_procedures - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain acknowledgement from parents or guardians about COVID-19 school procedures, ensuring that they understand and agree to follow the school's protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and make sure you understand the terms and conditions.
2. Select "Yes" if you acknowledge that you have read and will comply with all COVID-19 school procedures.
3. If you are not able to e-sign, please ask for assistance.
4. Fill in your name and contact information if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (page 1):
	+ A brief welcome note explaining the purpose of the form.
* **Terms and conditions** (page 2):
	+ A section where you will read and agree to the school's COVID-19 procedures.
	+ Please take note of any specific requirements or regulations that apply to your child.
* **Acknowledgement** (page 3):
	+ Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and will comply with the school's COVID-19 procedures.
* **e-Signatures** (page 4):
	+ Select "Yes" to e-sign and confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
	+ This is essential to complete the form.
* **Parent Name** (page 5):
	+ Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (page 6):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (page 7):
	+ Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Parent Contact Information** (page 8):
	+ Fill in any additional contact information, if necessary.
