# Sustainable Teaching Toolkit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Teaching Toolkit Request Form is designed to collect information from educators about their needs and preferences for access to the Sustainable Teaching Toolkit. This form helps the toolkit's administrators to understand the users' requirements and provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by the required label).
3. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
4. Submit the form to receive access to the Sustainable Teaching Toolkit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form (Page 1)** (`request_form`, text, required): This is the first page of the form where you can enter your name and contact information. Please fill out your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Request Form (Page 2)** (`request_form_page1`, text, required): This page is for you to describe why you need access to the Sustainable Teaching Toolkit. Be as detailed as possible and provide specific examples of how you plan to use the toolkit.
* **Form Page 2 (Page 3)** (`form_page_2`, text, required): This page is for you to specify which sections of the toolkit you are interested in (e.g., lesson planning, assessment, curriculum development).
* **Form Page 3 (Page 4)** (`form_page_3`, text, required): This page is for you to indicate your preferred communication method (e.g., email, phone, in-person meeting).
* **Form Page 4 (Page 5)** (`form_page_4`, text, required): This page is for you to provide any additional information about your request.
* **Form Page 5 (Page 6)** (`form_page_5`, text, required): This page is for you to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of using the toolkit.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
* The Sustainable Teaching Toolkit is a free resource, and you are free to use, modify, and distribute it as you see fit.
