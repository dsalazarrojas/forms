# Animal Welfare Education Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to register for animal welfare education workshops. It helps us understand your personal details, interests, and preferences to tailor the workshop experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, please follow these steps:
2. Select the relevant fields and provide the required information for each section.
3. Ensure that you have read and understood the instructions for each field.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Please provide your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Please provide your family surname.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): We will use this email address to send workshop details and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Please provide a contact number for enrollment confirmation.
* **Organization or Affiliation** (`organization_affiliation`, optional): If you are representing an organization, please provide its name.

### Areas of Interest
* **Areas of Interest in Animal Welfare** (`interest_areas`, required): Select all areas that apply to your interests.
	+ Animal Care and Nutrition
	+ Wildlife Protection
	+ Animal Behavior
	+ Shelter Operations
	+ Veterinary Health
	+ Animal Rights and Advocacy

### Experience and Preferences
* **Your Experience with Animals** (`experience_level`, required): Choose the option that best describes your level of experience.
	+ Beginner - No formal training
	+ Intermediate - Some experience or coursework
	+ Advanced - Professional experience
	+ Expert - Specialized knowledge
* **Preferred Workshop Format** (`preferred_workshop_format`, required): Select how you would like to attend the workshop.
	+ In-Person
	+ Online Live
	+ Self-Paced Online
	+ Hybrid

### Availability and Contact
* **Preferred Workshop Dates** (`availability_dates`, required): Select dates when you are available.
	+ Weekday Mornings
	+ Weekday Afternoons
	+ Weekday Evenings
	+ Weekend Mornings
	+ Weekend Afternoons
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, required): What best describes your role.
	+ Student
	+ Educator
	+ Shelter Staff
	+ Volunteer
	+ Community Member
	+ Professional
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, optional): Name of person to contact in case of emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, optional): Phone number of your emergency contact.

### Additional Information
* **Any Dietary or Accessibility Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, optional): Please describe any special needs.
* **What are your learning goals for this program** (`specific_goals`, optional): What would you like to achieve.
* **How did you hear about this program** (`referral_source`, optional): Where did you learn about this workshop.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, optional): Please share any other information you think we should know.

### Agreements and Consent
* **I agree to the Code of Conduct for this workshop** (`code_of_conduct`, required): Read our workshop guidelines and policies.
* **I consent to be photographed during the workshop** (`photography_consent`, optional): Images may be used for promotional purposes.
* **I consent to the collection and use of my data** (`data_privacy_consent`, required): Your information will be kept confidential.
* **I consent to the collection and use of my data** (`data_privacy_consent`, optional): Your information will be kept confidential.

Please note that all fields marked as `required` must be completed to ensure accurate and effective registration. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
