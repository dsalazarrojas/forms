# Teacher Observation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on teachers' performance from observers. It is intended to provide a structured way to evaluate and document your observations of a teacher's behavior. Please use this form to provide constructive feedback that is fair and specific.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Overall Impression** (`What is your overall impression of this teacher?`): Answer this question with a brief description of your overall impression of the teacher's performance.
2.  **Observation Date** (`Observation Date`): Enter the date on which you observed the teacher.
3.  **Teaching Practices** (`Teaching`): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu to describe the teacher's teaching practices.
4.  **Who Rated This Teacher?** (`Who rated this teacher?`): Select all applicable options from the dropdown menu to indicate who rated this teacher.
5.  **Rater Email** (`Rater Email`): Enter the email address of the person who rated this teacher.
6.  **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`): Provide any additional comments you have about the teacher's performance.
7.  **Rating 1-5** (`1-5`): Rate the teacher's performance on a scale of 1 to 5.
8.  **Rating 2-5** (`Teacher Observation Form Second Rating`): Rate the teacher's performance on a scale of 2 to 5.
9.  **Rating 3-5** (`Teacher Observation Form Third Rating`): Rate the teacher's performance on a scale of 3 to 5.
10. **Second Page**
    *   **Impact** (`What was the impact of the teacher?`): Answer this question with a brief description of the teacher's impact on you or others.
    *   **Additional Comments** (`Teacher Observation Form Additional Comments Section 2`): Provide any additional comments you have about the teacher's performance.
11. **Second Section Title**
    *   **Section Title** (`Select one option`): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu to describe the teacher's performance in the second section.
12. **Third Page**
    *   **Note**: This section is intended to allow for additional comments or notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Overall Impression** (`What is your overall impression of this teacher?`): A brief description of your overall impression of the teacher's performance.
*  **Observation Date** (`Observation Date`): The date on which you observed the teacher.
*  **Teaching Practices** (`Teaching`): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu to describe the teacher's teaching practices.
    +  **Title**: This option describes the teacher's approach to the subject matter and ability to communicate it to students.
    +  **Teaching**: This option describes the teacher's ability to teach the subject matter effectively.
    +  **Communication**: This option describes the teacher's communication skills and ability to engage students.
    +  **Organization**: This option describes the teacher's ability to organize the lesson or class effectively.
    +  **Patience**: This option describes the teacher's ability to be patient with students.
    +  **Use of Materials**: This option describes the teacher's use of teaching materials.
*  **Who Rated This Teacher?** (`Who rated this teacher?`): Select all applicable options from the dropdown menu to indicate who rated this teacher.
    +  **Administrator**: This option indicates that the administrator rated this teacher.
    +  **Teacher**: This option indicates that the teacher rated this teacher.
    +  **Student**: This option indicates that the student rated this teacher.
*  **Rater Email** (`Rater Email`): Enter the email address of the person who rated this teacher.
*  **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`): Provide any additional comments you have about the teacher's performance.
*  **Rating 1-5** (`1-5`): Rate the teacher's performance on a scale of 1 to 5.
*  **Rating 2-5** (`Teacher Observation Form Second Rating`): Rate the teacher's performance on a scale of 2 to 5.
*  **Rating 3-5** (`Teacher Observation Form Third Rating`): Rate the teacher's performance on a scale of 3 to 5.
*  **Second Page**
    +  **Impact** (`What was the impact of the teacher?`): Answer this question with a brief description of the teacher's impact on you or others.
*  **Second Section Title**
    +  **Section Title** (`Select one option`): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu to describe the teacher's performance in the second section.
        -  **Title**: This option describes the teacher's approach to the subject matter and ability to communicate it to students.
        -  **Teaching**: This option describes the teacher's ability to teach the subject matter effectively.
        -  **Communication**: This option describes the teacher's communication skills and ability to engage students.
        -  **Organization**: This option describes the teacher's ability to organize the lesson or class effectively.
        -  **Patience**: This option describes the teacher's ability to be patient with students.
        -  **Use of Materials**: This option describes the teacher's use of teaching materials.
*  **Third Page**
    +  **Note**: This section is intended to allow for additional comments or notes if needed.

## Tips
*   When filling out this form, please be honest and fair in your evaluation.
*   Use specific examples from your observations to support your answers.
*   Please keep your comments concise and focused on the teacher's performance.
*   If you have any additional questions or need clarification, please refer to the "Purpose" section of this guide or contact your supervisor.
