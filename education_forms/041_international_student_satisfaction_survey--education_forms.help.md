# international_student_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and sentiment from international students on their experience with the institution or program they are part of.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Choose the most accurate option that best describes your sentiment for each question.
3. Make sure to review each page carefully before proceeding to the next.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. international_student_satisfaction_survey_page_1_label (Text)**: This is the introduction page, which provides context and instructions for the survey.
* **2. how_did_you_feel_before_arriving_in_the_country (Select One)**: On a scale of 0-10, how did you feel about your experience before arriving in the country? 
    • 'Yes': I felt positive about my experience before arriving in the country.
    • 'No': I felt negative about my experience before arriving in the country.
* **3. how_did_you_feel_about_the_academic_program (Select Multiple)**: What aspects of the academic program did you feel positive about?
    • 'Yes': Choose all that apply.
* **4. satisfaction_with_academic_services (Select Multiple)**: How satisfied are you with the academic services provided by the institution or program?
    • 'Satisfied'
    • 'Dissatisfied'
* **5. satisfaction_with_social_services (Select Multiple)**: How satisfied are you with the social services provided by the institution or program?
    • 'Satisfied'
    • 'Dissatisfied'
* **6. overall_satisfaction (Text)**: What is your overall sentiment about your experience with the institution or program?
* **7. international_student_satisfaction_survey_page_7_label (Select One)**: On a scale of 0-10, how satisfied are you with the institution or program? 
    • 'Satisfied'
    • 'Dissatisfied'
* **8. international_student_satisfaction_survey_page_8_label (Select Multiple)**: What aspects of the institution or program did you feel positive about or negative about?
    • 'Satisfied'
    • 'Dissatisfied'
* **8. international_student_satisfaction_survey_page_8_label (Select Multiple)**: This question is a duplicate of 7. Please answer it accordingly.
