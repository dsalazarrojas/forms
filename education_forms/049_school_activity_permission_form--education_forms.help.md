# School Activity Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by schools to collect permission from parents or guardians for a specific school activity or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Ensure you have all necessary details about the activity/event.
* Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Activity Permission Form 1** (`school_activity_permission_form_1`, text, optional): Please enter the title or name of the school activity or event you are seeking permission for.
* **School Activity Permission Form 2** (`school_activity_permission_form_2`, text, optional): Enter the date and time of the school activity or event.
* **School Activity Permission Form 3** (`school_activity_permission_form_3`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the school activity or event.
* **School Activity Permission Form 4** (`school_activity_permission_form_4`, text, optional): If applicable, list any specific permissions or approvals required, such as permission to travel.
* **School Activity Permission Form 5** (`school_activity_permission_form_5`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any necessary contact information for the activity or event, such as a phone number or email address.

## Tips
* Be specific and detailed in your answers to ensure accurate communication with parents/guardians.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* This form is designed for internal use within the school; please do not distribute or share it with external parties.
