# Career Path Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your career path and provide insights into your strengths, interests, and preferences. The assessment is intended to be a starting point for career exploration and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question honestly and thoroughly. Select the most accurate answer from the available options, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields. Make sure to select up to 5 career field interests and 3 career values that best describe you. If you need help with any of the questions, feel free to ask!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in your assessment.
- **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with your results.
- **Age Group** (`participant_age`, `select_one`, required): Choose the age group that best describes you.
- **Current Education Level** (`current_education`, `select_one`, required): Select the highest level of education you have completed or are currently pursuing.
- **Current Career Status** (`career_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the description of your current career status.
- **Years of Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have worked professionally.
- **Rate Your Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of communication skills.
- **Rate Your Analytical and Problem-Solving Skills** (`analytical_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of analytical and problem-solving skills.
- **Rate Your Leadership and Team Skills** (`leadership_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of leadership and team skills.
- **Rate Your Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of technical skills.
- **Rate Your Creativity and Innovation** (`creativity_innovation`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of creativity and innovation.
- **Rate Your Interpersonal and People Skills** (`interpersonal_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of interpersonal and people skills.
- **Your Strongest Abilities** (`strongest_abilities`, `text`, required): Describe your strongest abilities or skills.
- **Skills You Need to Develop** (`skills_to_develop`, `text`, optional): Describe the skills or areas you would like to develop.
- **How Would You Describe Your Personality?** (`personality_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the personality characteristics that apply to you.
- **Ideal Work Environment** (`work_environment_preference`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the work environments that best suit you.
- **Career Field Interests** (`career_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose up to 5 career field interests.
- **Career Values** (`career_values`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to 3 career values that matter most to you.
- **Your Preferred Work Style** (`work_style`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred work style.
- **Risk Tolerance Level** (`risk_tolerance`, `select_one`, optional): Select your level of risk tolerance.
- **Desired Salary Range** (`salary_expectations`, `select_one`, optional): Select your desired salary range.
- **Purpose of This Assessment** (`assessment_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select the purpose of this assessment.
- **Additional Information About Your Career Goals** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any other relevant details about your career goals.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your answers.
- Select the options that best fit your situation.
- Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
- Don't worry if you're not sure about some of the questions; the assessment is a starting point for guidance and exploration.
