# Educational Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Grant Application Form is designed to collect relevant information for educational grant applications. This form is intended for educational institutions to submit their grant requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your applicant information (Page 1)
    *   Enter your name, and any other relevant details in the "Applicant Info" field.
2.  Provide your institution's information (Page 1)
    *   Enter your institution's name in the "Institution Info" field.
3.  Describe the grant details (Page 1)
    *   Type in the details of the grant you are applying for in the "Grant Details" field.
4.  Specify the grant amount (Page 1)
    *   Enter the grant amount you are applying for, if applicable.
5.  Select your preferred currency for the grant (Page 1)
    *   Choose your preferred currency from the provided options (USD, EUR, JPY).
6.  Choose your start date for the grant (Page 1)
    *   Select the start date of the grant period, if applicable.
7.  Choose your end date for the grant (Page 1)
    *   Select the end date of the grant period, if applicable.
8.  Add any additional grant amount details (Page 1)
    *   Provide any additional details about the grant amount, if necessary.
9.  Select your application status (Page 1)
    *   Choose the status of your application (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
10.  Choose your application type (Page 1)
    *   Select the type of grant you are applying for (Educational Grants, Scholarship Grants, Other).
11.  Enter your institution's name (Page 1)
    *   Type in your institution's name.
12.  Enter contact person's information (Page 1)
    *   Provide the name of the contact person for your institution.
13.  Enter contact person's email (Page 1)
    *   Type in the email of the contact person.
14.  Enter contact person's phone number (Page 1)
    *   Type in the phone number of the contact person.
15.  Select grant category (Page 1)
    *   Choose the category of the grant (Academic, Research, Community Service).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info (Applicant Info, text, required)**: This field is intended for you to enter your name.
* **Institution Info (Institution Info, text, required)**: In this field, you should enter your institution's name.
* **Grant Details (Grant Details, text, required)**: Here, you can provide a description of the grant you are applying for.
* **Grant Amount (Grant Amount, number, optional)**: Enter the grant amount you are applying for, if applicable.
* **Grant Currency (Grant Currency, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose your preferred currency for the grant from the options (USD, EUR, JPY).
* **Grant Start Date (Grant Start Date, date, optional)**: Select the start date of the grant period, if applicable.
* **Grant End Date (Grant End Date, date, optional)**: Select the end date of the grant period, if applicable.
* **Grant Amount Details (Grant Amount Details, text, optional)**: Provide any additional details about the grant amount, if necessary.
* **Application Status (Application Status, select_one, optional)**: Choose the status of your application (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Application Type (Application Type, select_one, optional)**: Select the type of grant you are applying for (Educational Grants, Scholarship Grants, Other).
* **Institution Name (Institution Name, text, optional)**: Type in your institution's name.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person, text, optional)**: Provide the name of the contact person for your institution.
* **Contact E-mail (Contact E-mail, email, optional)**: Type in the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, optional)**: Type in the phone number of the contact person.
* **Grant Category (Grant Category, select_one, optional)**: Choose the category of the grant (Academic, Research, Community Service).

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Be as detailed as possible with the grant amount and details.
*   Check your application status carefully before submitting.
