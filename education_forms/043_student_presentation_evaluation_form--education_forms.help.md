# Student Presentation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate student presentations, capturing their overall performance, communication skills, and other essential aspects of their delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the required information: First Name, Presentation Title, and Presentation Topic.
- Provide feedback and ratings on the students' performance, using the scale provided for each section.
- Fill in any overall comments or remarks about the student's presentation.
- Ensure to fill in the submission information (Submitted By, Submitted On, and Submitted At).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the first name of the student presenting.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the last name of the student presenting.
- **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email of the student or the contact person.
- **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the student or the contact person.
- **Presentation Title** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the presentation's title.
- **Presentation Topic** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the presentation's topic.
- **Overall Rating** (Number, Optional): Rate the student's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
- **Communication Skills** (Number, Optional): Rate the student's communication skills on a scale of 1-5.
- **Creativity** (Number, Optional): Rate the student's creativity on a scale of 1-5.
- **Confidence** (Number, Optional): Rate the student's confidence on a scale of 1-5.
- **Organization** (Number, Optional): Rate the student's organization on a scale of 1-5.
- **Time Management** (Number, Optional): Rate the student's time management on a scale of 1-5.
- **Body Language** (Number, Optional): Rate the student's body language on a scale of 1-5.
- **Overall Comments** (Text, Optional): Fill in any general remarks about the student's presentation.
- **Submitted By** (Text, Required): Enter your name as the person submitting the evaluation.
- **Submitted On** (Date, Required): Enter the date the presentation was evaluated.
- **Submitted At** (Time, Required): Enter the time the presentation was evaluated.

## Tips
* Ensure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the provided scales for rating sections to provide accurate and fair feedback.
* Fill in any additional comments to provide constructive feedback.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate.
