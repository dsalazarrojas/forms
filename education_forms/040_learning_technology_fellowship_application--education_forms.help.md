# Learning Technology Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for candidates to apply for the Learning Technology Fellowship, a program aimed at supporting individuals in developing innovative learning solutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the following fields:
    * Candidate Details
    * Project Proposals
    * CV Upload (if applicable)
2.  For university information:
    * University Name
    * University Type
    * Program Name
    * Department
3.  Enter project details:
    * Project Start Date
    * Project Duration
    * Project End Date
4.  Add contact information:
    * Contact Title
    * Contact Email
    * Contact Phone
5.  Fill out supervisor information:
    * Supervisor Name
    * Supervisor Email
    * Supervisor Position

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, required: false): This is the first step in the application process. Please provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, background, and relevant experience.
*   **Project Proposals** (`project_proposals`, text, required: false): In this section, describe your proposed project, including its goals, objectives, and any relevant details that will help us understand your project proposal.
*   **CV Upload** (`cv_upload`, text, required: false): If you have a CV or resume, you can upload it here to provide more information about your background and experience.
*   **University Name** (`university_name`, text, required: false): Please provide the name of the university you are currently affiliated with or the one you graduated from.
*   **University Type** (`university_type`, select_one, required: false): Please select 'Yes' if you are currently a student, 'No' otherwise.
*   **University Name** (`university_name_2`, text, required: false): This is an additional field for university name. Please provide the name of your current or past university.
*   **University Type** (`university_type`, select_one, required: false): This is another field to confirm or select your university type.
*   **Program Name** (`university_program`, text, required: false): Please provide the name of the program you are currently enrolled or have completed.
*   **Department** (`university_department`, text, required: false): Provide the name of the department you are or have been affiliated with.
*   **University Address** (`university_address`, text, required: false) and (`university_address_2`, text, required: false): Please provide the address of your current or past university.
*   **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when your project will start.
*   **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of your project.
*   **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when your project will end.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required: false): Please provide the name of your supervisor or mentor.
*   **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required: false): Enter your supervisor's email address.
*   **Supervisor Position** (`supervisor_position`, text, required: false): Provide the title or position of your supervisor.
*   **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, select_one, required: true): Select your title (e.g., Dr, Mr, Ms, Prof, Dr).
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number.
