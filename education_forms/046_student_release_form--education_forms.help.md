# Student Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Release Form is a document used to facilitate the transfer of educational records from one school to another or for students to obtain records from a previous school. This form helps to gather essential information from the student and the relevant school administrator or witness, ensuring the process is efficient and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's information in the "Student Info" section, including their name, release reason, and date of release.
2. Select the school administrator's title from the drop-down list in the "School Admin" section.
3. The student must provide their signature in the "Student Signature" field.
4. If a witness is present, they must provide their contact information in the "Witness Info" field and sign in the "Witness Signature" field.
5. The school administrator must sign in the "School Signature" field, acknowledging the release of the student's records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Info
* **Student Name** (`student_info`, text, required): The student's full name.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, text, required): A brief explanation for the release of the student's records.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): The date the student's records are to be released.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, note, required): A note for the contact information of the student.
* **School Admin** (`school_admin`, select_one, required): Select the school administrator's title from the list.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): The student must sign their name.
* **Witness Info** (`witness_info`, text, required): The contact information of the witness.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, required): The witness must sign their name.
* **Witness Title** (`witness_title`, text, required): The title of the witness.
* **School Signature** (`school_signature`, text, required): The school administrator must sign their name, acknowledging the release of the student's records.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary authority to release the student's records before filling out this form.
* If a witness is present, ensure they have a clear understanding of their role in the process.
* Verify that the school administrator's signature is accurate and authentic.
* Keep the witness's contact information up-to-date to ensure they can be reached if needed.
