# study_group_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The study_group_communication_form is used to share meeting information and member details within a group setting, such as in educational institutions or group projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the relevant information:

1. **Meeting Info**: Enter a brief description of the meeting or event.
2. **Meeting Name**: Enter the name of the meeting or event.
3. **Meeting Date**: Select the date of the meeting or event using the date picker.
4. **Meeting Time**: Select the time of the meeting or event using the time picker.
5. **Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the meeting or event.
6. **Resource Sharing**: Enter any resources that will be shared during the meeting or event.
7. **Member List**: Select which group members will be attending the meeting or event. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each member.
8. **Member Details**: Enter any additional details about each member attending the meeting or event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Info** (`meeting_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the meeting or event.
- **Meeting Name** (`meeting_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the meeting or event.
- **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the meeting or event using the date picker.
- **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the meeting or event using the time picker.
- **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the meeting or event.
- **Resource Sharing** (`resource_sharing`, text, optional): Enter any resources that will be shared during the meeting or event.
- **Member List** (`member_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select which group members will be attending the meeting or event. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each member.
  - **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about each member attending the meeting or event.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all group members have the necessary information.
- If a member is not attending, select 'No' for the Member List field.
- Use the date and time pickers to select the correct date and time of the meeting or event.
- Enter any additional details about each member in the Member Details field.
