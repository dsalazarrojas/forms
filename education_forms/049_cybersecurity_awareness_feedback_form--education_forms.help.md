# Cybersecurity Awareness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Awareness Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with the cybersecurity awareness training program. This form will help us understand your perceptions of the training's effectiveness, suggest ways to improve the program, and gather information about your preferences and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and answer them truthfully.
2.  For select_one fields, choose the option that best reflects your response.
3.  For select_multiple fields, choose all that apply.
4.  For note fields, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. How effective do you feel the training was?**: This question asks for your assessment of the training's effectiveness in increasing your knowledge and skills.
  * Please choose one of the options: Very effective, Somewhat effective, Not effective, or Unsure.
* **2. What suggestions do you have for our cybersecurity awareness training program?**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide any suggestions or ideas you have to improve the program.
  * Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **3. Do you have any comments or suggestions about the training?**: Similar to question 2, this is an open-ended question where you can provide any comments or suggestions about the training.
  * Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **4. Email address**: This is where you can provide your email address if you want to be contacted for further feedback or questions.
  * Please enter a valid email address.
* **5. Phone number**: This is where you can provide your phone number if you want to be contacted for further feedback or questions.
  * Please enter a valid phone number.
* **6. What level of training were you attending?**: This question asks for your level of experience with cybersecurity training.
  * Please choose one of the options: Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **7. Have you attended any other cybersecurity training?**: This question asks if you have attended any other cybersecurity training programs.
  * Please choose one of the options: True or False.
* **8. If yes, how many other trainings have you attended?**: This question asks for the number of other trainings you have attended.
  * Please choose one of the options: Less than 5 times or 5 or more times.
* **9. If yes, what type of training were you attending?**: This question asks for the type of training you attended.
  * Please choose all that apply.
* **10. If yes, who provided the training?**: This question asks for the organization that provided the training.
  * Please choose all that apply.
* **11. How many times have you attended this type of training?**: This question asks for the frequency of training attendance.
  * Please choose one of the options: Less than 5 times or 5 or more times.
* **12. Where was the training held?**: This question asks for the location of the training.
  * Please choose one of the options: Yes or No.
* **13. How many hours did the training last?**: This question asks for the duration of the training.
  * Please choose one of the options: Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, or More than 6 hours.
* **14. When was the training held?**: This question asks for the date of the training.
  * Please enter a valid date.
* **15. What time of day was the training held?**: This question asks for the time of day the training was held.
  * Please choose one of the options.
* **16. How likely are you to participate in future cybersecurity training?**: This question asks for your likelihood of participation in future training programs.
  * Please choose one of the options: Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not very likely, or Not likely at all.
* **17. How satisfied are you with the training?**: This question asks for your satisfaction with the training program.
  * Please choose one of the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Not satisfied at all.
* **18. How interested are you in participating in future cybersecurity training?**: This question asks for your interest in participating in future training programs.
  * Please choose one of the options: Very interested, Somewhat interested, Not very interested, or Not interested at all.
* **19. Do you have any concerns about cybersecurity?**: This question asks for any concerns you have about cybersecurity.
  * Please choose one of the options: True or False.
* **20. Do you have any suggestions for improving our cybersecurity awareness training?**: This question asks for any suggestions you have to improve the program.
  * Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **21. Do you have any comments about the training?**: This question asks for any comments or feedback about the training.
  * Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **22. Is this the end of the feedback form?**: This question asks if you have completed the form.
  * Please choose one of the options: True or False.
* **23. Email address**: This is where you can provide your email address if you want to be contacted for further feedback or questions.
  * Please enter a valid email address.
* **24. Phone number**: This is where you can provide your phone number if you want to be contacted for further feedback or questions.
  * Please enter a valid phone number.
* **25. Participant ID**: This is a unique identifier for you as a participant.
  * Please enter a valid participant ID.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and honestly.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please contact us for clarification.
* We appreciate your feedback and suggestions to improve our cybersecurity awareness training program.
