# Cognitive Learning Science Research Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your educational background, research experience, research interests, skills, and availability for a research position or project. Please fill out this form to help us understand your qualifications and fit for the opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the personal information section, which includes your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Next, provide information about your current institution and academic background, including your degree level, field of study, and year in program.
3. If applicable, provide your GPA or academic standing.
4. Describe your prior research experience and any published work or presentations.
5. Indicate your primary research interest and provide a detailed description of your research interests.
6. List any relevant research skills you possess, such as research design, data analysis, or lab techniques.
7. Specify your technical skills, such as software or programming languages.
8. Provide information about your availability, including your preferred start date and weekly time commitment.
9. Finally, describe why you are interested in this research opportunity and what your career goals are.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Personal Information --
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### -- Educational Background --
* **Current Degree Level** (`degree_level`, select_one, required): Select your current degree level (e.g., High School, Bachelor, Master, PhD, Post-doctoral, Other).
* **Field of Study or Major** (`field_of_study`, text, required): Enter the name of your field of study or major.
* **Year in Program** (`year_in_program`, number, required): Enter the year you are currently in the program.
* **GPA or Academic Standing** (`gpa`, number, optional): If available, enter your GPA or academic standing.

### -- Research Experience --
* **Prior Research Experience** (`research_experience`, text, required): Describe your research background, including any research projects or studies you have been involved in.
* **Publications or Presentations** (`publications`, text, optional): List any published work or presentations related to your research experience.

### -- Research Interests --
* **Primary Research Interest** (`primary_interest`, select_one, required): Select your primary research interest (e.g., Cognitive Development, Learning Theories, Memory Research, Educational Psychology, Neuroscience of Learning, Assessment Methods, Other).
* **Detailed Interest Description** (`interest_details`, text, required): Elaborate on your research interests.

### -- Skills and Qualifications --
* **Key Research Skills** (`research_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant research skills you possess (e.g., Research Design, Data Analysis, Literature Review, Lab Techniques, Statistical Methods, Qualitative Methods, Coding, Other).
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, text, optional): List any technical skills you have, such as software or programming languages.

### -- Availability --
* **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are available to begin.
* **Weekly Time Commitment** (`commitment_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week.

### -- Motivation and Goals --
* **Why are you interested in this research?** (`motivation`, text, required): Describe why you are interested in this research opportunity.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, optional): Describe how this research opportunity relates to your career goals.

### -- Additional Information --
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your application.
