# Remote Work Best Practices Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees understand and evaluate the effectiveness of remote work best practices in their work environment. It assesses the employees' knowledge and attitude towards remote work policies and assesses the impact of these policies on their work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category under which you want to evaluate the remote work best practices.
2. Provide a brief description of the remote work policy you are training employees on.
3. Choose the training objectives for the remote work policy.
4. Assess the effectiveness of the remote work policy using the provided assessments.
5. Provide feedback on the policy, including positive, negative, and neutral feedback.
6. Add comments on any additional feedback or suggestions.
7. Enter your email address to receive a copy of the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Choose the category under which you want to evaluate the remote work best practices. Select "Education Forms" if the policy is related to education.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the remote work policy you are training employees on. This will help the trainer to understand the context of the policy.
* **Training Objectives**: Specify the objectives of the training on the remote work policy. This will help the trainer to understand what the employees should learn from the training.
* **Training Outcome**: Choose how effective you think the remote work policy will be after the training. Select "Effective", "Ineffective", or "Somewhat Effective" depending on your assessment.
* **Policy**: Choose the remote work policies you want to train employees on. Select one or more of the following options:
	+ **Work from Home**: Train employees on working from home policies.
	+ **Flexible Hours**: Train employees on flexible hour policies.
	+ **Telecommuting**: Train employees on telecommuting policies.
* **Feedback**: Provide positive, negative, and neutral feedback on the remote work policy. Choose one or more of the following:
	+ **Positive**: Provide feedback that is positive about the policy.
	+ **Negative**: Provide feedback that is negative about the policy.
	+ **Neutral**: Provide feedback that is neutral about the policy.
* **Assessment 1-10**: Answer the following assessments on the effectiveness of the remote work policy:
	+ **Assessment 1-10**: Assess the effectiveness of the remote work policy using the options:
		- **Strongly Disagree**: Strongly disagree with the policy.
		- **Somewhat Disagree**: Somewhat disagree with the policy.
		- **Neutral**: Neither agree nor disagree with the policy.
		- **Somewhat Agree**: Somewhat agree with the policy.
		- **Strongly Agree**: Strongly agree with the policy.
* **Review**: Add any additional feedback or suggestions on the remote work policy.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments on the remote work policy.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive a copy of the completed form.
