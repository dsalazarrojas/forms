# Childcare Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childcare Program Registration Form is designed to gather information about your child's registration in our program. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a successful and safe experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the correct program you are enrolling your child into from the "Program Selection" field.
2. Fill out the necessary fields with accurate and complete information.
3. For fields with multiple options, select the most accurate answer.
4. Use the available space for additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date**: The date on which you are registering your child in our program.
* **Child First Name**: The legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name**: The legal last name of the child.
* **Child Nickname**: A preferred name or nickname for the child (optional).
* **Child Date of Birth**: The child's birth date.
* **Child Age**: The child's current age in years.
* **Child Gender**: The child's gender (select from options).
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: The legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: The legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: The primary email address for contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: The primary phone number for contact.
* **Home Address**: The full street address.
* **City**: The city of residence.
* **State**: The state or province.
* **Zip Code**: The postal or zip code.
* **Program Selection**: Select the program you are enrolling your child into.
* **Start Date**: The desired start date for the program.
* **Schedule Days**: Select the days your child will attend the program (select multiple days).
* **Drop-Off Time**: The expected drop-off time.
* **Pick-Up Time**: The expected pick-up time.
* **Siblings Enrolled**: Indicate if you have other children enrolled in the program.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: How you learned about our program (select from options).
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies your child has.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: List any dietary restrictions your child has.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: The full name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: The relationship of the emergency contact to the child (select from options).
* **Authorized Pickup Persons**: List all people authorized to pick up your child.
* **Photo and Video Consent**: Indicate if you consent to photos and videos of your child (select from options).
* **Transportation Needed**: Indicate if you need transportation services.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you will use.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or questions.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check the "Program Selection" field to ensure you are enrolling the correct program.
- Select all authorized pickup persons and emergency contacts accurately.
- Review and confirm all information before submitting the form.
