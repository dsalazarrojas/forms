# Admission Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a mandatory registration form for students applying to the institution. It collects essential information required for the admission process. Please ensure you complete it accurately and honestly to facilitate a smooth and efficient admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read each section and ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Fill out the form with your correct personal and academic details.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`student_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City** (`student_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province** (`student_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`student_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Country** (`student_country`, text, required): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Program Applying For** (`program_applying_for`, text, required): Select the program you are applying for.
* **Degree Level** (`degree_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of degree you are pursuing (e.g., Certificate, Diploma, Associate, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate, Other).
* **Start Term** (`start_term`, select_one, required): Select the term you wish to start (e.g., Fall, Spring, Summer, Winter, Other).
* **Start Year** (`start_year`, number, required): Enter the year you wish to start.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required): Describe your educational background.
* **Previous Institution** (`previous_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of your previous educational institution (if applicable).
* **GPA** (`gpa`, number, optional): Enter your cumulative GPA (if applicable).
* **Standardized Test Scores** (`standardized_test_scores`, text, optional): Enter your scores from standardized tests (e.g., SAT, ACT, GRE, GMAT, Other).
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe any relevant work experience.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (`extracurricular_activities`, text, optional): Describe any extracurricular activities.
* **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, text, optional): Write a personal statement about your goals.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide contact information for your references.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter your relationship to your emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Friend).
* **Housing Request** (`housing_request`, select_one, optional): Select whether you require on-campus housing.
* **Financial Aid Status** (`financial_aid_status`, select_one, optional): Select your financial aid status (e.g., Applied, Awarded, Declined, Not Applying, Pending).
* **Special Accommodations** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): Enter any special accommodations you require.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this registration form.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Enter your electronic signature to confirm registration.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be completed for a successful submission.
