# Talent Show Auditions Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a talent show. This includes providing details such as email address, phone number, and a note about themselves and their desired performance time slot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "submitter email" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "submitter phone" field.
3. Provide a note about yourself and your performance in the "submitter note" fields.
4. Select your preferred category from the "submitter category" field.
5. Choose your desired time slot for your performance from the "submitter date" and "submitter time" fields.
6. Select any additional categories you would like to be considered for from the "submitter category multiple" field.
7. Fill out the "submitter note" fields for any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting.
* Ensure that your email address, phone number, and note fields are filled out correctly.
* Select only the categories that are relevant to your performance.
* Use the correct date and time format.
* Review the fields carefully before submitting your application.
