# Intent To Withdraw From Academy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to formally declare a student's intent to withdraw from the academy. This process helps maintain accurate records and ensures a smooth transition for the student.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (1, `student_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your academy records.
* **Contact Details** (2, `contact_details`, optional): Provide your contact information (phone number and email) so we can reach out to you for follow-up questions.
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (20, `reason_for_withdrawal_1`, required): Select one of the following reasons for withdrawing from the academy:
	+ Illness
	+ Financial
	+ Academic
	+ Personal
	+ Other (please specify in the next field)
* **Reason for Withdrawal Other** (19, `reason_for_withdrawal_other`, optional): If you selected "Other" as your reason for withdrawal, provide a brief explanation.
* **Date of Withdrawal** (13, `date_of_withdrawal`, required): Enter the date you plan to withdraw from the academy.
* **Time of Withdrawal** (14, `time_of_withdrawal`, required): Enter the time you plan to withdraw from the academy.
* **Follow Up** (5, `follow_up`, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted after submitting this form:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Other (please specify in the next field)
* **Contact Method Other** (7, `contact_method_other`, optional): If you selected "Other" as your contact method, provide a brief explanation.
* **Contact Number** (8, `contact_number`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (9, `contact_email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Next Steps** (15, `next_steps_1`, optional): Provide any additional information about your next steps.
* **Next Steps 2** (16, `next_steps_2`, optional): Provide any further information about your next steps.
* **Next Steps 3** (17, `next_steps_3`, optional): Provide any additional information about your next steps.
* **Next Steps 4** (18, `next_steps_4`, optional): Provide any further information about your next steps.
* **Next Steps Follow Up** (11, `next_steps_follow_up`, optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up questions:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Other (please specify in the next field)
* **Next Steps Follow Up Other** (12, `next_steps_follow_up_other`, optional): If you selected "Other" as your contact method for follow-up, provide a brief explanation.
