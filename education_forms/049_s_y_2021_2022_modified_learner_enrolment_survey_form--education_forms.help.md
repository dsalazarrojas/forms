# Modified Learner Enrolment Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and background information from learners. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed responses to help us better understand your learning needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the basic personal details, including your first name and last name.
2. Enter your date of birth and preferred learning modality (select all that apply).
3. If you have an email address, enter it for our communication purposes.
4. Provide your telephone number if you have one.
5. Describe your learning capacity and any relevant details.
6. Select your preferred language.
7. If you have had any previous education, please enter the details in the provided text field.
8. Lastly, if you hold any previous degree(s), please enter the details in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Preferred Learning Modality** (`preferred_learning_modality`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ways you prefer to learn (e.g., online, in-person, self-paced).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for our communication purposes.
* **Telephone** (`telephone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your telephone number if you have one.
* **Learning Capacity** (`learning_capacity`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current learning capacity and any relevant details.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred language.
* **Previous Education** (`previous_education`, `text`, required: false): If you have had any previous education, please enter the details here.
* **Previous Degree** (`previous_degree`, `text`, required: false): If you hold any previous degree(s), please enter the details here.
