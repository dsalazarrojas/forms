# Migrant Student Academic And Career Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Migrant Student Academic and Career Plan Form is designed to help migrant students create a plan that aligns with their goals, skills, and interests. This form will help students identify and achieve their academic and career objectives by providing a framework to think about their strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the Personal Details page (page 1).
2. Provide information about your education history, career goals, and career opportunities in the relevant pages (pages 2, 3, 4, and 6).
3. If necessary, add any additional comments or information about your personal references, education certificate, personal counseling, parent consent, teacher consent, student consent, school administration, school district administration, or other school administration (pages 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15).
4. Make sure to sign the parent, teacher, and student consent pages (pages 10, 11, and 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (page 1)** (`personal_details`, text, required): Please fill in your name, date of birth, and student ID number (if applicable).
* **Education History (page 2)** (`education_history`, text, required): Describe your current education level, school name, grade level, and relevant courses.
* **Career Goals (page 3)** (`career_goals`, text, required): Specify your short-term and long-term career goals, including your desired profession, job title, and salary range.
* **Career Opportunities (page 4)** (`career_opportunities`, text, required): Describe any relevant skills, courses, or certifications you have that align with your career goals.
* **Personal References (page 5)** (`personal_references`, text, optional): Provide information about 2-3 people who can vouch for your character and work ethic.
* **Education Programs (page 6)** (`education_programs`, text, required): List any programs you're interested in or have already completed that can help you achieve your career goals.
* **Additional Comments (page 7)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any extra information you feel is relevant to your plan.
* **Education Certificate (page 8)** (`education_certificate`, text, optional): Provide any relevant education or professional certificates you have.
* **Personal Counseling (page 9)** (`personal_counseling`, text, optional): Share any challenges or concerns you have about your academic or career progress.
* **Parent Consent (page 10)** (`parent_consent`, text, required): Sign and date this page to confirm that you have your parent's permission to create this plan.
* **Teacher Consent (page 11)** (`teacher_consent`, text, required): Sign and date this page to confirm that you have your teacher's permission to create this plan.
* **Student Consent (page 12)** (`student_consent`, text, required): Sign and date this page to confirm that you have your own consent to create this plan.
* **School Administration (page 13)** (`school_administration`, text, optional): Provide information about your school's administration that is relevant to your plan.
* **School District Administration (page 14)** (`school_district_administration`, text, optional): Share any information about your school district that is relevant to your plan.
* **Other School Administration (page 15)** (`other_school_administration`, text, optional): List any other school administration or resource that is relevant to your plan.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
