# Academic Faculty Research Contribution Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Faculty Research Contribution Assessment Form is a critical tool for documenting and assessing the research contributions of academic faculty members. The form helps to ensure that all relevant information about faculty research is collected, evaluated, and used to make informed decisions about faculty careers and promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and effectively, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required information, including your faculty name, email, and department.
2. List your research projects and grants during the reporting period in the "Research projects in reporting period" field.
3. Enter the number of publications you have achieved during the reporting period in the "Number of publications" field.
4. Describe any grants you have been awarded and their funding levels in the "Grants awarded" field.
5. Provide a description of the significant outcomes and impacts of your research in the "Research impact and outcomes" field.
6. Document any departmental and community service contributions you have made in the "Collaboration and service contributions" field.
7. Finally, provide a summary evaluation of your contributions and recommendations in the "Overall assessment and recommendations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Faculty Name (Faculty name)
* This field is required and should be filled out with your full name as it appears on your university records.

### Faculty Email (Faculty email)
* This field is also required and should be entered in the format of your university email address (e.g., @university.edu).

### Department (Department)
* This field is required and should be filled out with your academic department or unit.

### Research projects in reporting period (Research projects in reporting period)
* This field is optional but recommended for listing your major research projects and grants during the reporting period.

### Number of publications (Number of publications)
* This field is optional but recommended for entering the whole number of publications you have achieved during the reporting period.

### Grants awarded (Grants awarded)
* This field is optional but recommended for describing any grants you have been awarded and their funding levels.

### Research impact and outcomes (Research impact and outcomes)
* This field is optional but recommended for describing the significant outcomes and impacts of your research.

### Collaboration and service contributions (Collaboration and service contributions)
* This field is optional but recommended for documenting any departmental and community service contributions you have made.

### Overall assessment and recommendations (Overall assessment and recommendations)
* This field is required and should be a summary evaluation of your contributions and recommendations for future growth and development.
