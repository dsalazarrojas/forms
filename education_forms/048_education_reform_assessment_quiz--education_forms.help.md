# Education Reform Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Reform Assessment Quiz is designed to help you assess the level of education reform in a school or institution. This form will guide you through the process of evaluating the effectiveness of education reform efforts and provide a general assessment tool for administrators and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field carefully, as they are all required fields.
2. For fields with options, select the relevant options that apply to your school or institution.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your responses, as they will help us better understand the state of education reform in your school or institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you wish to receive any feedback or results from this assessment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to receive any feedback or results from this assessment.
* **Reform Type** (`select_one_reform_type`, select one, optional): Select the type of reform your school or institution is undergoing. Options: Yes, No.
* **Educational Areas** (`select_multiple_educational_areas`, select multiple, optional): Select the areas of education being reformed. Options: Yes, No.
* **Reform Score** (`score_reform`, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high, to indicate the level of reform in your school or institution.
* **Implementation Score** (`score_implementation`, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high, to indicate the level of implementation of reform in your school or institution.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the state of education reform in your school or institution.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date the reform was implemented or is planned to be implemented.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time the reform was implemented or is planned to be implemented.
* **Reform Areas** (`select_multiple_reform_areas`, select multiple, optional): Select the areas of reform being implemented. Options: Yes, No.
* **Reform Score** (`reform_score`, number, optional): Enter a score from 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high, to indicate the level of reform in your school or institution.
* **Comments Reform** (`comments_reform`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the state of reform in your school or institution.
* **Submit Reform** (`submit_reform`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the form.
* **Date Reform** (`date_reform`, date, optional): Select the date the reform was implemented or is planned to be implemented.
* **Time Reform** (`time_reform`, time, optional): Select the time the reform was implemented or is planned to be implemented.
