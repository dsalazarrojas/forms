# field_trip_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "field_trip_incident_form" is a form used to report incidents that occur during school field trips. It helps school administrators and staff collect essential information to ensure student safety and respond promptly to incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the ID of the school where the field trip took place.
2. Select the type of transportation used for the trip (bus, car, or other).
3. Enter the date of the trip in the specified format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4. Enter the start and end times of the trip.
5. Provide the location where the incident occurred.
6. Describe the incident in detail.
7. Enter the number of students and staff members involved.
8. Choose the type of incident that occurred (medical, behavioral, or property).
9. Provide any additional details about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school_id**: Enter the ID of the school where the field trip took place.
* **trip_type**: Select the type of transportation used for the trip (bus, car, or other).
* **trip_date**: Enter the date of the trip in the specified format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **trip_start_time**: Enter the start time of the trip.
* **trip_end_time**: Enter the end time of the trip.
* **location**: Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* **incident**: Describe the incident in detail.
* **students**: Enter the number of students involved in the incident.
* **staff**: Enter the number of staff members involved in the incident.
* **incident_type**: Choose the type of incident that occurred (medical, behavioral, or property).
* **incident_details**: Provide any additional details about the incident that may be relevant to the report.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If additional information is available, provide it in the "incident_details" field.
* If unsure about any field, contact your school administrator for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all information is correct.
