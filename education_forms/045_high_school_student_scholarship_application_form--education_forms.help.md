# High School Student Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Student Scholarship Application Form is designed to help high school students apply for a scholarship to support their further education. The form gathers essential information about the student and their guardian or parent to ensure a fair evaluation of the scholarship application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Provide accurate and complete information about yourself and your guardian or parent.
3. Choose the correct options for each field.
4. Select "Submit" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: This field is for the student's name.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Student Email**: This field is for the student's email address.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: False
* **Student Phone**: This field is for the student's phone number.
	+ Type: Text

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
