# Keep School Excellence Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Keep School Excellence Application Form" is a form designed to gather information about a school's excellence application period, status, and other relevant details. This form is typically filled out by school administrators or staff members to update or manage school applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus, if any.
2.  Enter or select relevant information in the text fields.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
4.  Review your submissions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Excellence Form** (`school_excellence_form`, text, optional): This field is the main section of the form where you can enter or update information about the school excellence application.
* **School Id** (`school_id`, number, optional): This field is used to store the unique identifier of the school.
* **Application Period** (`application_period`, date, optional): Choose the period for which you are applying to the School Excellence program.
* **Start Date** (`application_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the application process began.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the application process ended.
* **Status** (`application_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of the school application (Active or Inactive).
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the school.
* **Contact** (`school_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the school.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Review your form submissions for any mistakes or inconsistencies before finalizing them.
