# Diploma Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Diploma Verification Form" is a document intended for verifying the authenticity of diplomas or certificates. It collects personal details and document information to ensure accurate identification and authentication.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name (First and Last Name) in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (DD/MM/YY format is recommended).
3. If required, provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if you have one).
5. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Verification Type" field to indicate the status of your verification.
6. Upload the scanned copy of your diploma or certificate in the "Document Upload" field.
7. If needed, select multiple files to be verified in the "Select File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YY format (e.g., 01/01/2000).
* **Address**: Provide your current address (optional).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if required).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Verification Type**: Select "Yes" if the document has been verified, otherwise choose "No".
* **Document Upload**: Upload the scanned copy of your diploma or certificate.
* **Select File**: Select multiple files to be verified (if needed).

## Tips
- Ensure the uploaded document is clear and legible.
- If you have multiple files to upload, please select them all.
- Please verify that your email address is accurate before submitting the form.
- This form is intended for a single user or individual. If you are representing an organization, please fill this form for each person separately.
