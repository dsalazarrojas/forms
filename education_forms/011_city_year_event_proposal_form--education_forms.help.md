# City Year Event Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit proposals for City Year events that aim to create positive social impact in local communities. It gathers essential information about the proposer, event, and proposed project to help the review committee make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a proposal, please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information. Ensure that you provide all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the City Year team for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposer Name** (`Proposer Name`, text, *): This is the name of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Organization or School** (`Organization or School`, text, *): The name of the organization or school associated with the proposal.
* **Proposer Email** (`Proposer Email`, email, *): A valid email address for the proposer.
* **Proposer Phone Number** (`Proposer Phone Number`, text, *): The contact number for the proposer.
* **Event Name** (`Event Name`, text, *): The name of the proposed event.
* **Event Description** (`Event Description`, text, *): A description of the event, including its purpose and expected outcomes.
* **Proposed Event Date** (`Proposed Event Date`, date, *): The date the event is proposed to take place.
* **Event Duration** (`Event Duration`, text, *): The length of time the event is expected to run.
* **Proposed Location** (`Proposed Location`, text, *): The location where the event will be held.
* **Expected Number of Participants** (`Expected Number of Participants`, number, *): The estimated number of people expected to attend the event.
* **Target Audience** (`Target Audience`, select_one, *): The age group the event is intended for (e.g., Youth - K-12, Young adults - 18-25, etc.).
* **Learning or Development Outcomes** (`Learning or Development Outcomes`, text, *): A description of what participants will learn or gain from the event.
* **Expected Community Impact** (`Expected Community Impact`, text, *): A description of how the event is expected to benefit the community.
* **How does this align with City Year mission** (`How does this align with City Year mission`, text, *): An explanation of how the proposed event aligns with City Year's mission.
* **Estimated Budget** (`Estimated Budget`, number, *): The approximate total cost of the event.
* **Proposed Funding Sources** (`Proposed Funding Sources`, text, *): A description of where funding for the event will come from.
* **Required Resources and Materials** (`Required Resources and Materials`, text, *): A list of necessary resources or materials for the event.
* **Ready for Approval** (`Ready for Approval`, select_one, *): A confirmation that the proposal is ready for approval or requires further work.

Please note that all fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the City Year team for guidance.
