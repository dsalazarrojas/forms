# After School Pick Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The After School Pick Up Form is used to collect information from parents or authorized individuals picking up students after school hours. This form helps schools ensure that students are released to the right person and that their safety is assured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary authorization to pick up the student. If you are picking up someone else's child, please have their parent or authorized adult sign the form.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review and verify the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's complete legal name.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter the student's identification number.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's current grade level.
* **Classroom Teacher** (`student_classroom_teacher`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student's classroom teacher.
* **School Name** (`student_school`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **Pick-Up Person Full Name** (`pickup_person_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the person picking up the student.
* **Relationship to Student** (`pickup_person_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select the relationship of the pick-up person to the student (e.g., parent, guardian, grandparent, etc.).
* **Pick-Up Person Phone Number** (`pickup_person_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person picking up the student.
* **Pick-Up Person ID** (`pickup_person_id`, `text`, required): Enter the driver's license or other ID number of the pick-up person.
* **Pick-Up Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the student will be picked up.
* **Pick-Up Time** (`pickup_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time when the student will be picked up.
* **Pick-Up Location** (`pickup_location`, `select_one`, required): Select where the student will be picked up (e.g., school main entrance, school side entrance, etc.).
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how the student will be transported home (e.g., personal vehicle, school bus, walking, etc.).
* **Authorized on File** (`authorized_on_file`, `select_one`, required): If the pick-up person is already authorized on file, select 'True'. If not, select 'False'.
* **Special Pick-Up Authorization** (`special_pickup_authorization`, `select_one`, required): If this is a one-time special authorization, select 'True'. If not, select 'False'.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the student's emergency contact.
* **Parent/Guardian Notification** (`parent_guardian_notification`, `select_one`, required): If the parent or guardian has been notified of this pick-up arrangement, select 'True'. If not, select 'False'. If not required, select 'Not required'.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Enter any special instructions regarding the pick-up.
* **Medical Alerts** (`medical_alerts`, `select_one`, required): If the student has any medical alerts, select 'True'. If not, select 'False'.
* **Medical Alert Details** (`medical_alert_details`, `text`, optional): If there are medical alerts, provide details about the alert.
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the staff member verifying the pick-up.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the staff member releasing the student.
* **Pick-Up Confirmed** (`pickup_confirmed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the student has been safely released to the authorized person.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the school administration for assistance.
