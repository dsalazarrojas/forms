# Teacher Aide Conduct Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Aide Conduct Assessment is a form used to evaluate the performance, strengths, and areas for improvement of a teacher aide. This assessment will help identify where a teacher aide excels and where they need additional support or training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the assessment questions and ensure you have the necessary information and context to provide honest and accurate feedback.
2. Answer each question based on your experiences and observations of the teacher aide's work.
3. Select the most appropriate answers from the available options.
4. Add any additional comments or suggestions you believe are relevant to the teacher aide's performance.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Aide Conduct Assessment Form** (Note): This form is the main section where you will answer the questions and provide feedback.
* **Rating 1** (Select One: Good, Fair): Evaluate the teacher aide's performance in one specific area, e.g., lesson planning. Select "Good" if they excel in this area or "Fair" if they need improvement.
* **Rating 2** (Select One: Good, Fair): Evaluate the teacher aide's performance in another specific area, e.g., classroom management. Select "Good" if they excel in this area or "Fair" if they need improvement.
* **Rating 3** (Select One: Good, Fair): Evaluate the teacher aide's performance in another specific area, e.g., communication. Select "Good" if they excel in this area or "Fair" if they need improvement.
* **Rating 4** (Select One: Good, Fair): Evaluate the teacher aide's performance in another specific area, e.g., time management. Select "Good" if they excel in this area or "Fair" if they need improvement.
* **Rating 5** (Select One: Good, Fair): Evaluate the teacher aide's performance in another specific area, e.g., problem-solving. Select "Good" if they excel in this area or "Fair" if they need improvement.
* **Teacher Aide Strengthening** (Text): Provide suggestions or areas where the teacher aide can improve or grow.
* **Teacher Aide Areas for Improvement** (Text): Identify specific areas where the teacher aide needs additional support or training.
* **Overall Performance** (Select One: Yes, No): Indicate whether the teacher aide's overall performance is satisfactory or not.
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or suggestions you believe are relevant to the teacher aide's performance.
