# 1st PUC Application Form 2021 2022 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for the 1st PUC application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, ensuring that all fields are completed before submission.
2. Enter your complete legal name in the "Full name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address that can be used for communication.
4. Enter your phone number (optional) for urgent updates.
5. Enter your date of birth for age verification.
6. Select your gender.
7. Your age will be calculated automatically based on your date of birth.
8. Enter your home address, including city, state, and postal code.
9. Date of submission is the date you are submitting this form.
10. Choose the type of application (new, renewal, transfer, or amendment).
11. Briefly describe your motivation for applying.
12. Select the course of interest.
13. Choose one integrated exam preference (if applicable).
14. Enter your previous school's name and board (e.g., CBSE, ICSE, State).
15. Enter the year of completion of your previous PU.
16. Enter the percentage of marks in your previous PU.
17. Select all subjects taken in your previous PU.
18. List any supporting documents you will submit (e.g., marksheet, ID proof).
19. Add any additional comments or notes that may help process your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (text, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email address** (email, required): We will use this to contact you.
* **Phone number** (phone, optional): Optional, but useful for urgent updates.
* **Date of birth** (date, required): For age verification.
* **Gender** (select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes you.
* **Age** (integer, required): Calculated from date of birth.
* **Home address** (text, required): Include city, state, and postal code.
* **Application date** (date, required): Date you are submitting this form.
* **Type of application** (select_one, required): Select the reason for your submission.
* **Purpose or reason for applying** (text, required): Briefly describe your motivation.
* **Course of interest** (select_one, required): Select the course you wish to pursue.
* **Integrated exam preference** (select_one, required): Choose one if applicable.
* **Name of your previous school** (text, required): Include full official name.
* **Board of your previous school** (text, required): e.g., CBSE, ICSE, State.
* **Year of completion of previous PU** (integer, required): 4-digit year (e.g., 2020).
* **Percentage of marks in previous PU** (decimal, required): Enter a number between 0 and 100.
* **Subjects taken in previous PU** (select_multiple, required): Select all that apply.
* **Supporting documents (list)** (text, optional): List any documents you will submit (e.g., marksheet, ID proof).
* **Additional comments or notes** (text, optional): Share any other information that may help us process your application.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address for communication.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your motivation and course of interest.
* Attach supporting documents as a list, if required.
* Keep your additional comments or notes brief, but helpful.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or seek assistance.
