# Environmental Literacy Research Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and project details from individuals seeking funding for environmental literacy research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to ensure accuracy and avoid duplication.
2. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
3. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
4. Enter the name of your university in the **University Name** field.
5. Enter the name of the program you are applying for in the **Program Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the university you are affiliated with.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the program you are applying for.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you have permission to access and represent your university and program.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the necessary information for your application.
