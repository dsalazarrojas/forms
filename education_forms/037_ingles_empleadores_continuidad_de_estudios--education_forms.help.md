# ingles_empleadores_continuidad_de_estudios - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document information about employees' educational background and continuity of studies. This includes personal details such as first name, last name, email, phone number, and a brief description. You will also be asked to provide category information, title, form ID, and details about the assigned tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Write a brief Description of your educational background and continuity of studies.
6. Select the Category relevant to your educational background.
7. Enter your Title.
8. Enter the Form ID.
9. Specify the Output File.
10. If applicable, provide details about the Assigned tool.
