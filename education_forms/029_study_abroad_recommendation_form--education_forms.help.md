# Study Abroad Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who are planning to study abroad and need to provide information about themselves and their recommenders. It is used to gather necessary documentation for a study abroad program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the Student Details section with your name and any other requested information.
* Select your program interests from the provided options.
* Provide your educational background and employment status.
* Answer the recommendation questions to provide context for your recommender's response.
* Fill out the Recommender Details section with your recommender's contact information.
* Sign the form with your recommender's signature and your own signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Details** (Student Details): This section is for you to provide your name and any other requested information.
* **Student Program Interests** (Student Program Interests): Select the program interests that you are interested in.
* **Student Educational Background** (Student Educational Background): This is where you can provide information about your educational background.
* **Student Employment Status** (Student Employment Status): Select your current employment status.
* **Recommendation Details** (Recommendation Details): This section is for you to provide context for your recommender's response.
* **Recommender Details** (Recommender Details): This is where your recommender can provide their contact information.
* **Recommender-Student Relationship** (Recommender-Student Relationship): Indicate the nature of your relationship with your recommender.
* **Recommender Position** (Recommender Position): This is where your recommender can state their position or title.
* **Recommenders Recommendations** (Recommenders Recommendations): This section is for your recommender to provide their recommendations.
* **Recommenders Contact Information** (Recommenders Contact Information): This is where your recommender can provide their contact information.
* **Recommenders Signature** (Recommenders Signature): Your recommender must sign this section to confirm their recommendations.
* **Students Signature** (Students Signature): You must sign this section to confirm that you have submitted accurate information.
* **Student Name** (Student Name): This is where you can provide your name. 

## Tips
* Make sure to have your recommender fill out the Recommender Details section with their contact information and signature.
* Ensure that you and your recommender sign the form to confirm that you have submitted accurate information.
* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
