# School Attendance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Attendance Survey is designed to collect information about student attendance from classes. This form will help educators track attendance records and identify areas for improvement in education institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Select the corresponding option for each field.
3. Submit the completed form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as a student.
* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your teacher or instructor.
* **Class** (`class_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the class you were absent from.
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the attendance record.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the class.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the class.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, text, optional): Describe the reason for your attendance or absence.
* **Student Absence** (`student_absence`, text, optional): Describe the reason for your absence from class.
