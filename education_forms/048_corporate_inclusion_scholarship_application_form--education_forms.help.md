# corporate_inclusion_scholarship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "corporate_inclusion_scholarship_application_form" is designed to collect information from students about their contact details, educational background, motivation statement, employment status, and other relevant information to assess their eligibility for a foundation inclusion scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Describe your educational background, including your current or expected education level.
3. Write a clear and concise motivation statement explaining why you are eligible for the scholarship.
4. Select your current employment status.
5. Choose your expected education level.
6. Enter your expected graduation date and year.
7. Confirm your foundation inclusion scholarship status.
8. Select your HR Team review status.
9. Select your DEI Leader review status.
10. Select your Foundation review status.
11. Add any additional notes about your application.
12. Enter the scholarship amount you are requesting.
13. Choose the scholarship frequency (one-time or recurring).
14. Select the scholarship status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Please provide your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Educational Background** (`educational_background`, `text`, required): Describe your current or expected education level, including your degree and institution.
* **Motivation Statement** (`motivation_statement`, `text`, required): Write a clear and concise statement explaining why you are eligible for the scholarship.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your current or expected education level from the options provided.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`expected_graduation_date`, `date`, required): Enter your expected graduation date.
* **Expected Graduation Month** (`expected_graduation_month`, `number`, required): Enter the month of your expected graduation date.
* **Expected Graduation Year** (`expected_graduation_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of your expected graduation date.
* **Foundation Inclusion Scholarship** (`foundation_inclusion_scholarship`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your status as a foundation inclusion scholarship recipient.
* **HR Team Review** (`hr_team_review`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your application with the HR team.
* **DEI Leader Review** (`dei_leader_review`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your application with the DEI leader.
* **Foundation Review** (`foundation_review`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your application with the foundation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your application.
* **Scholarship Amount** (`scholarship_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of scholarship you are requesting.
* **Scholarship Frequency** (`scholarship_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the frequency of the scholarship (one-time or recurring).
* **Scholarship Status** (`scholarship_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your scholarship.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have provided accurate and truthful information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the scholarship committee.
