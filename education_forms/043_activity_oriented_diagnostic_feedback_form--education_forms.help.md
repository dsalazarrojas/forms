# Activity Oriented Diagnostic Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from educators and trainers on the effectiveness of specific activities they have conducted with their students or trainees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the educator or trainer who is providing feedback.
2. Enter the name of the course where the activity took place.
3. Enter the title of the specific activity being evaluated.
4. Enter the date the activity was conducted.
5. Describe the group that participated in the activity.
6. Evaluate how well the activity aligned with the stated learning objectives.
7. Evaluate the participant engagement level during the activity.
8. Evaluate the appropriateness of the activity's difficulty level.
9. Evaluate the time allocated to the activity.
10. Evaluate the resources used in the activity.
11. Describe the insights gained about participant learning from the activity.
12. Provide recommendations for revising the activity (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Evaluator Name
• **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the educator or trainer providing feedback.

### Course Name
• **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course where the activity took place.

### Activity Name
• **Name of Activity Evaluated** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the title of the specific activity being evaluated.

### Date of Activity
• **Date of Activity** (`activity_date`, date, required): Enter the date the activity was conducted.

### Participant Group
• **Participant Group** (`participant_group`, text, required): Describe the group that participated in the activity.

### Alignment with Learning Objectives
• **Alignment with Learning Objectives** (`learning_objective_alignment`, select_one, required): Evaluate how well the activity aligned with stated learning objectives.
	+ Excellent alignment
	+ Good alignment
	+ Moderate alignment
	+ Poor alignment
	+ No alignment

### Participant Engagement Level
• **Participant Engagement Level** (`participant_engagement_level`, select_one, required): Evaluate the participant engagement level during the activity.
	+ Very high
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
	+ Very low

### Appropriateness of Activity Difficulty
• **Appropriateness of Activity Difficulty** (`activity_difficulty`, select_one, required): Evaluate the appropriateness of the activity's difficulty level.
	+ Too easy
	+ Slightly easy
	+ Just right
	+ Slightly difficult
	+ Too difficult

### Time Utilization
• **Time Utilization** (`time_utilization`, select_one, required): Evaluate the time allocated to the activity.
	+ Too much time
	+ Slightly too much
	+ Just right
	+ Slightly too little
	+ Too little time

### Resource Utilization
• **Resource Utilization** (`resource_utilization`, select_one, required): Evaluate the resources used in the activity.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Adequate
	+ Inadequate
	+ Poor

### Diagnostic Insights Gained
• **Diagnostic Insights Gained** (`diagnostic_insights_gained`, text, required): Describe the insights gained about participant learning from the activity.

### Recommendations for Revision
• **Recommendations for Revision** (`recommendations_for_revision`, text, optional): Provide recommendations for revising the activity (if applicable).

### Additional Observations
• **Additional Observations** (`additional_observations`, text, optional): Enter any other observations about the activity.
