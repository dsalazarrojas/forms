# Basketball Camp Detailed Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information from athletes and their parents/guardians for the basketball camp registration process. The form includes fields for athlete and parent/guardian contact information, medical history, and liability release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the athlete's information, including their first name, last name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the athlete's grade level and basketball experience level.
3. Provide the parent/guardian's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
4. Select the emergency contact's name, relationship, and phone number.
5. Choose the camp session and dates.
6. Check if there are any medical conditions or allergies and list any medications.
7. Select the insurance provider (if applicable).
8. Read and agree to the liability waiver.
9. Confirm that all provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Athlete Information
* **First name**: Enter the athlete's first name.
* **Last name**: Enter the athlete's last name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the athlete's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email address**: Enter the athlete's email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter the athlete's phone number.
* **Grade level**: Select the athlete's current grade level from the provided options.
* **Phone number**: Enter the athlete's phone number.

### Parent/Guardian Information
* **Parent/Guardian name**: Enter the parent/guardian's name.
* **Phone number**: Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Email address**: Enter the parent/guardian's email address.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship between the athlete and the emergency contact.
* **Phone number**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.

### Basketball Experience
* **Basketball experience level**: Select the athlete's level of basketball experience from the provided options.
* **Years playing**: Enter the number of years the athlete has played basketball.
* **Primary position**: Select the athlete's primary position on the court.

### Camp Details
* **Camp session**: Select the camp session the athlete will attend.
* **Camp dates**: Enter the dates of the camp session.

### Health Information
* **Medical conditions or allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies the athlete has.
* **Medications**: List any medications the athlete is currently taking.
* **Insurance provider**: Enter the insurance provider (if applicable).

### Liability Release
* **Liability waiver**: Select "I Agree" to confirm the liability waiver.
* **Parent/Guardian consent**: Select "I Consent" to confirm the parent/guardian's consent.
* **Registration confirmation**: Select "I Confirm" to confirm that all provided information is accurate.

Note: If you're unsure about any field, please review the provided explanations above or reach out to the camp staff for assistance.
