# Active Learning Methodologies Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information from educators on their current practices and perceptions regarding active learning methodologies. It will help identify areas where educators may need support or resources to effectively implement active learning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your primary role in the educational environment from the dropdown menu.  
2. Choose the level of education you primarily teach.  
3. Indicate your years of teaching experience.  
4. Choose your level of familiarity with active learning methodologies.  
5. Select whether you currently use active learning strategies in your teaching.  
6. Select the active learning strategies you currently use in your teaching.  
7. Choose the challenges you face when implementing active learning.  
8. Indicate whether you have observed improvement in student engagement with active learning.  
9. Indicate whether you have observed improvement in learning outcomes with active learning.  
10. Select the types of professional development you feel you need to better implement active learning.  
11. Evaluate your institution's support for active learning.  
12. If you have any, share your success stories or examples of active learning implementation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Role in Education (1)**: Choose the role that best describes your primary position in the educational environment.
*   **Education Level Taught (2)**: Select the level of education you primarily teach.
*   **Years of Teaching Experience (3)**: Indicate the number of years you have spent teaching.
*   **Familiarity with Active Learning Methodologies (4)**: Choose how familiar you are with active learning methodologies.
*   **Do You Implement Active Learning Strategies (5)**: Select whether you currently use active learning strategies in your teaching.
*   **Commonly Used Active Learning Strategies (6)**: Select the active learning strategies you currently use in your teaching.
*   **Challenges in Implementing Active Learning (7)**: Select all the challenges you face when implementing active learning.
*   **Improvement in Student Engagement (8)**: Indicate whether you have observed improvement in student engagement with active learning.
*   **Improvement in Learning Outcomes (9)**: Indicate whether you have observed improvement in learning outcomes with active learning.
*   **Professional Development Needs (10)**: Select the types of professional development you feel you need to better implement active learning.
*   **Institutional Support Level (11)**: Evaluate your institution's support for active learning.
*   **Additional Resources Desired (12)**: If you have any, share what additional resources would help you implement active learning.
*   **Success Stories or Examples (13)**: Share any success stories or examples of active learning implementation.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question honestly, and to the best of your ability.  
*   If you are unsure about any question, please choose the best answer you can.
*   If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them in the "Additional Resources Desired" field.
*   Your responses will be kept confidential and used to inform and support educators in implementing active learning.
