# School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Admission Form is used to collect necessary information about a student's admission to the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the **Enter Student Name** field.
2. Enter a valid email address for the **Email** field.
3. Provide the student's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
4. Enter the name of the school the student is applying to in the **School Name** field.
5. Enter the guardian's name in the **Guardian Name** field.
6. Enter the guardian's address in the **Guardian Address** field (if applicable).
7. Enter the guardian's phone number in the **Guardian Phone** field (if applicable).
8. Select the student's school level (High or Low) in the **Student School Level** field (if applicable).
9. Select the guardian's relationship with the student (Yes or No) in the **Guardian Relationship** field (if applicable).
10. Provide a valid email address for the student in the **Student Email** field.
11. Sign the **Parent Signature** field with the parent's signature.
12. Sign the **Student Signature** field with the student's signature.
13. Sign the **School Signature** field with the school's signature.
14. Enter the current date in the **Date** field.
15. Enter the current time in the **Time** field.
16. Enter the name of the school the student is applying to in the **School** field.
17. (Optional) Enter additional comments in the **Additional Comments** field (if applicable).
18. (Optional) Provide the guardian's document in the **Guardian Document** field (if applicable).
19. (Optional) Provide the student's document in the **Student Document** field (if applicable).
20. Sign the **Teacher Signature** field with the teacher's signature (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for the student.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school the student is applying to.
* **Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the guardian.
* **Guardian Address**: Enter the guardian's address (if applicable).
* **Guardian Phone**: Enter the guardian's phone number (if applicable).
* **Student School Level**: Select the student's school level (High or Low) (if applicable).
* **Guardian Relationship**: Select the guardian's relationship with the student (Yes or No) (if applicable).
* **Student Document**: Provide the student's document (if applicable).
* **Guardian Document**: Provide the guardian's document (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments (if applicable).
* **Parent Signature**: Sign with the parent's signature.
* **Student Signature**: Sign with the student's signature.
* **School Signature**: Sign with the school's signature.
* **Date**: Enter the current date.
* **Time**: Enter the current time.
* **School**: Enter the name of the school the student is applying to.
* **Teacher**: Enter the name of the teacher (if applicable).
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Teacher Signature**: Sign with the teacher's signature (if applicable).
* **Student Email**: Enter a valid email address for the student.
* **Guardian Phone**: Enter the guardian's phone number (if applicable).
