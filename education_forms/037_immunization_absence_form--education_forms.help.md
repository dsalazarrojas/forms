# Immunization Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Immunization Absence Form is a tool designed to collect information from students who need to report their absence due to immunization. This form helps students, parents, and healthcare professionals communicate and document the necessary details for the student's absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, making sure to provide accurate and complete answers.
2. Select multiple vaccines from the list if applicable.
3. Enter the correct dates and times for the absence.
4. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of absence.
5. Sign the form with a student signature and a healthcare provider signature (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, ID number, or other identifying information.
* **Vaccines** (`vaccine_info`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the vaccines you are currently undergoing or have recently received. Select all that apply.
* **Date of Absence** (`date_of_absence`, date, optional): Enter the start and end dates of your absence.
* **Time of Absence** (`time_of_absence`, time, optional): Enter the start and end times of your absence.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, optional): Briefly explain the reason for your absence due to immunization.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, note, optional): Sign your name to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, note, optional): If applicable, sign with your healthcare provider to confirm their involvement.
* **School District** (`school_district`, text, optional): Enter your school district's name or code.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely, as this information will be used for official purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
* This form is for internal use only and is not intended for external sharing or distribution.
