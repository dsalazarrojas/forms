# Student Technology Use Rubric - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Technology Use Rubric is designed to assess and document a student's technology use, including the type of technology used, its usage, and the student's ratings of their own and teacher's performance. This form provides a standardized and comprehensive evaluation tool for students and educators to track and improve technology use in the classroom.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field (page 1).
* Select the type of technology used by the student in the "Technology Type" field (page 2).
* Describe the technology used by the student in the "Technology Used" field (page 3).
* Rate the student's own performance with the teacher in the "Teacher Rating" field (page 4).
* Rate the teacher's performance with the student in the "Student Rating" field (page 5).
* Provide comments and suggestions in the "Comments" and "Suggestions" fields (pages 6-7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Page 1) (Student Name, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Technology Type** (Page 2) (Technology Type, select multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose all technology types that the student used, including Computer, Tablet, Smartphone, or Other.
* **Technology Used** (Page 3) (Technology Used, text, required: false)
	+ Describe the specific technology used by the student, such as a laptop or a mobile app.
* **Technology Usage** (Page 4) (Technology Usage, note, required: false)
	+ Enter a brief description of how the student used the technology, such as for research or entertainment.
* **Teacher Rating** (Page 5) (Teacher Rating, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the student's performance with the teacher on a scale of 1-5.
* **Student Rating** (Page 6) (Student Rating, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the teacher's performance with the student on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (Page 7) (Comments, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any comments or feedback from the teacher or student.
* **Suggestions** (Page 8) (Suggestions, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any suggestions or feedback from the teacher or student.
* **Teacher Comments** (Page 9) (Teacher Comments, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any comments or feedback from the teacher.
* **Student Comments** (Page 10) (Student Comments, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any comments or feedback from the student.
* **Teacher Suggestions** (Page 11) (Teacher Suggestions, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any suggestions or feedback from the teacher.
* **Student Suggestions** (Page 12) (Student Suggestions, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any suggestions or feedback from the student.
* **Teacher Observations** (Page 13) (Teacher Observations, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any observations or comments from the teacher.
* **Student Observations** (Page 14) (Student Observations, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any observations or comments from the student.
* **Parent Permission** (Page 15) (Parent Permission, text, required: false)
	+ Obtain written permission from the parent or guardian to use the technology.
* **Student Signature** (Page 16) (Student Signature, date, required: false)
	+ Date and sign the form when complete.
* **Teacher Signature** (Page 17) (Teacher Signature, date, required: false)
	+ Date and sign the form when complete.
* **School Name** (Page 18) (School Name, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the school where the student is enrolled.
* **Classroom Name** (Page 19) (Classroom Name, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the classroom where the student is enrolled.
* **Teacher Name** (Page 20) (Teacher Name, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the teacher who completed this form.
* **Student Grade** (Page 21) (Student Grade, number, required: false)
	+ Enter the student's grade level.
* **Teacher Grade** (Page 22) (Teacher Grade, number, required: false)
	+ Enter the teacher's grade level.
* **Student Attendance** (Page 23) (Student Attendance, number, required: false)
	+ Enter the student's attendance rate.
* **Teacher Attendance** (Page 24) (Teacher Attendance, number, required: false)
	+ Enter the teacher's attendance rate.
* **Technology Use Rationale** (Page 25) (Technology Use Rationale, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the rationale or justification for the student's technology use.
