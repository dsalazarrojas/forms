# Student Peer Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Peer Evaluation Form is a tool used to facilitate peer feedback and self-assessment among students. It is designed to help students evaluate their peers' performance, provide feedback for improvement, and reflect on their own strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Peer Comments**: Please provide specific, constructive comments about your peer's work or behavior. Focus on providing feedback that is helpful, clear, and respectful.
2.  **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall impression of your peer's work or behavior on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 represents '1 - Below Average' and 5 '5 - Excellent'.
3.  **Peer Name**: Provide the name of your peer.
4.  **Peer Student Identifier**: Enter your peer's Student ID.
5.  **Self Rating**: Select one or more ratings that reflect your self-assessment of your own work or behavior, using the same rating scale as above.
6.  **Student ID**: Enter your Student ID.
7.  **Feedback for Students Below**: Provide feedback or suggestions for students who are performing below your expectations.
8.  **Feedback for Students Above**: Provide feedback or suggestions for students who are performing above your expectations.
9.  **Suggestions for Improvement**: Share any suggestions that you think could help improve students' work or behavior.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Peer Comments**: Please use this field to provide specific comments about your peer's work or behavior, keeping it constructive and respectful.
* **Overall Rating**: This is a numeric rating system that helps you evaluate your peer's overall performance or behavior, ranging from '1 - Below Average' to '5 - Excellent'.
* **Peer Name**: This is your peer's name, which is used to identify the student being evaluated.
* **Peer Student Identifier**: This is your peer's Student ID, which helps identify them uniquely within the system.
* **Self Rating**: This field allows you to rate your own work or behavior using the same scale as the overall rating, reflecting your self-assessment.
* **Student ID**: This is your Student ID, which is used to identify you uniquely within the system.
* **Feedback for Students Below**: If you have a peer who is performing below your expectations, use this field to provide feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **Feedback for Students Above**: If you have a peer who is performing above your expectations, use this field to provide feedback or suggestions.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: This field is for sharing any suggestions you believe could help improve students' work or behavior.
