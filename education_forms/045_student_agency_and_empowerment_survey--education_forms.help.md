# Student Agency And Empowerment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your experiences and perceptions as a student in your educational environment. Your feedback will help us better understand what works well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
Please take a few minutes to complete this form, answering all questions honestly. For each question, select the option that best represents your answer. If a question does not apply to your situation, please select the option that says "No" or "Not Applicable".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **classroom_ambiance**: How would you rate the overall atmosphere in your classroom?
	+ Select one of the following options: excellent, average, fair, very poor
* **teacher_support**: How much support do you receive from your teachers?
	+ Select all that apply (Yes, No)
* **student_confidence**: How confident do you feel about your academic abilities?
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is very low and 10 is very high
* **teacher_involvement**: How much involvement do you have in class discussions?
	+ Select all that apply (Yes, No)
* **student_voice**: How comfortable do you feel about expressing your opinions in class?
	+ Enter a brief description of your feelings
* **choice_and_responsibility**: Do you feel you have the freedom to make choices and decisions?
	+ Select one of the following options: Yes, No
* **school_climate**: How do you feel about the overall climate in your school?
	+ Select one of the following options: Yes, No
* **teacher_support_for_differnce**: Do you feel supported by your teachers when it comes to differences between people?
	+ Select one of the following options: Yes, No
* **student_engagement**: How engaged do you feel in class?
	+ Select one of the following options: Yes, No
* **teacher_support_for_difference**: Do you feel supported by your teachers when it comes to differences between people?
	+ Select one of the following options: Yes, No
* **student_responsibility**: Do you feel you have the freedom to make choices and take responsibility for your actions?
	+ Select one of the following options: Yes, No
* **student_confidence_in_teachers**: How confident do you feel about your teachers' abilities?
	+ Select one of the following options: Yes, No
* **teacher_climate**: How would you rate the overall support from your teachers?
	+ Select one of the following options: Yes, No

## Tips
Please be as honest as possible when answering these questions. Your feedback is crucial for us to understand and improve the educational environment. If you have any concerns or questions about this form, please don't hesitate to reach out.
