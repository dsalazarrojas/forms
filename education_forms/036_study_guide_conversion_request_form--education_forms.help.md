# Study Guide Conversion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Guide Conversion Request Form is designed to collect information from users for conversion requests related to study guides. This form is intended for users who need to request the conversion of a specific study guide into a different format.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the study guide you would like to convert, including its title and ID if known.
2. Select the desired format for the conversion (e.g., PDF, EPUB, EPUB 3).
3. Provide any additional notes regarding the format conversion (e.g., specific settings or options).
4. Confirm your request by selecting "Submit" and entering any additional information as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Guide Conversion Request** (`study_guide_conversion_request`): A brief description of the study guide you would like to convert, including its title and ID if known.
* **Requested Format** (`format_request`): Select the desired format for the conversion (e.g., PDF, EPUB, EPUB 3).
* **Format Notes** (`format_notes`): Provide any additional notes regarding the format conversion (e.g., specific settings or options).
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`): Confirm your request by selecting "Submit" and enter any additional information as prompted.
* **Study Guide ID** (`study_guide_id`): Enter the ID of the study guide you would like to convert.
* **Study Guide Name** (`study_guide_name`): Enter the name of the study guide you would like to convert.
* **Author** (`author`): Enter the author of the study guide (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the status of your request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding the status of your request.
* **Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date you would like to request the conversion (optional).
* **Time** (`request_time`): Enter the time you would like to request the conversion (optional).
* **Note** (`request_note`): Enter any additional notes regarding your request (optional).
* **Additional Notes** (`study_guide_conversion_request_note`): Enter any additional notes regarding the conversion request (optional).
* **File** (`file`): This field is currently not used in this form, please do not enter any information here.
