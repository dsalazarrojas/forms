# MultiPage Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The MultiPage Course Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from learners on a multi-page course, providing an opportunity for instructors to understand their students' thoughts and opinions on the course content, materials, and teaching methods.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each representing a different aspect of the course. Please complete each section carefully and honestly.
2. Enter your response in the text fields as prompted.
3. If a field is marked as required, please ensure that you provide a response for that field.
4. If you need help or clarification on any question, please contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Course Title
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the course you are evaluating. This should be the official title of the course as it appears on the course materials or website.

### Page 2: Subject Matter
* **Subject Matter** (`course_title`, text, required/optional): Describe the subject matter or topic covered in the course. Please provide a brief summary of what the course is about.

### Page 3: Course Materials
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, text, required/optional): Describe the materials used during the course, including textbooks, handouts, software, or other resources. Please provide a list or brief description of each material used.

### Page 4: Instructing Techniques
* **Instructing Techniques** (`instructing_techniques`, text, required/optional): Describe the teaching methods and techniques used by the instructor during the course. This could include lecturing, discussions, group activities, or other methods. Please provide examples of specific techniques used.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide detailed feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, feel free to suggest them.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
