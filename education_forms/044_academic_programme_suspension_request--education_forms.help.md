# Academic Programme Suspension Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by students to request suspension of their academic programme. It helps the university gather necessary information for processing the request.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill out your information in the required fields.
2.  Ensure that you have attached any necessary supporting documents, if applicable.
3.  Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name (<name>, <text>, required>):** Full legal name of the student.
    Please fill out your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Student Identification Number (<id>, <text>, required>):** University or registration ID number.
    Enter your university or registration ID number for identification purposes.
*   **Programme Name (<name>, <text>, required>):** Programme to be suspended.
    Select the name of the academic programme you wish to suspend.
*   **Suspension Start Date (<date>, <date>, required>):** Requested start date.
    Enter the date you would like the suspension to begin.
*   **Expected End Date (<date>, <date>, optional>):** Expected date to resume studies.
    Enter the date you expect to resume your studies, if known.
*   **Reason for Suspension (<name>, <text>, required>):** Describe the circumstances requiring suspension.
    Provide a clear explanation of why you need to suspend your academic programme.
*   **Supporting Documents (<name>, <text>, optional>):** Medical or other supporting evidence.
    Attach any relevant documents that support your suspension request.
*   **Contact Method During Suspension (<name>, <select_one>, optional>):** How the university can reach you.
    Select the method you prefer for the university to contact you during suspension (Email, Phone, or No contact).
*   **Student Signature Name (<name>, <text>, required>):** Type full name to sign request.
    Type your full name to confirm your request.
*   **Submission Date (<name>, <date>, required>):** Date of request submission.
    Enter the date you are submitting this request.

## Tips

*   Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Attach any supporting documents relevant to your request.
*   Review your submission for errors before submitting.
