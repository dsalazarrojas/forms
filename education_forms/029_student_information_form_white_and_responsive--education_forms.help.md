# Student Information Form White And Responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and demographic information about students to be used for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Start by entering your personal identity information.
2.  Then, provide details about your family members.
3.  Answer questions about your participation in school sports.
4.  Answer questions about your home equipment and internet access.
5.  Choose your plans after graduating from high school.
6.  Optionally, you can upload a photo and answer any additional questions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Identity
* **Personal Identity** (`personal_identity`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name, nickname, or other identifying information about yourself.
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, `text`, optional): List any family members, such as parents, siblings, or guardians.
* **Upload a photo** (`photo_upload`, `text`, optional): Upload a photo of yourself for identification purposes.

### Sports and Interests
* **Do you play on a school sports team?** (`sports`, `select_one`, optional): Answer with True or False.
* **Does he/she have a computer at home?** (`home_equipment`, `select_one`, optional): Answer with True or False.
* **Does he/she have internet access at home?** (`internet_access`, `select_one`, optional): Answer with True or False.

### Future Plans
* **What is he/she planning after graduating from high school?** (`graduation_planning`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more of the options: Attend College, Attend University, Other.
* **Additional questions** (`additional_questions_1` to `additional_questions_10`, `text`, optional): Answer any additional questions provided in the form.

### Note
Please answer all fields that are marked as required unless specified otherwise. Make sure to be honest and accurate when providing your information.
