# School Meal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their school meal experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
2. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) as they are necessary for processing.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that best fit your response.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name* (8)**: Enter your school's name.
* **Goals** (1): Think about your goals and what you ate. This field is optional.
* **Food Prophets** (2): Think about the food you ate. This field is optional.
* **Quality** (3): Think about the quality of the food. This field is optional.
* **Time Spent Eating* (4)**: Enter the amount of time you spent eating. 
* **Rating** (5): Choose how good you think the food was. Options: very good, okay, not good, not bad. This field is optional.
* **Food Preferences* (6)**: Choose all the foods you would prefer. Options: pizza, burger, fries, salad, chicken. This field is optional.
* **Food** (7): Think about the food. This field is optional.
* **Staff** (9): Choose how many staff members were involved in your meal experience. Options: 1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-10. This field is required.
* **Staff Rating** (10): Choose how good you think the staff was. Options: very good, okay, not good, not bad. This field is required.
* **Comments** (11): Add any additional comments you'd like to share. This field is optional.
* **Food Prophets Scale** (12), **Food Scale** (13), **Time Spent Eating Scale** (15), **School Meal Scale** (16), **School Meal Rating** (17), **School Meal Scale 2** (18), **School Meal Scale 3** (19), **School Meal Scale 4** (20), **School Meal Scale 5** (21), **School Meal Scale 6** (22), **School Meal Scale 7** (23), **School Meal Scale 8** (24), **School Meal Scale 9** (25): These fields are all part of a series of scales used to gauge your experience. Choose the scale that best fits your response.
