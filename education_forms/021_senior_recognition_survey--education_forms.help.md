# Senior Recognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about senior achievements, recognition, and ceremony details from school staff or administrators. It will help administrators to create a comprehensive recognition program and yearbook. The form is intended to be completed by school staff or administrators to gather all the necessary information for planning and executing a senior recognition ceremony.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly to the best of your abilities.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the "Senior Achievements" and "Recognition Ideas" questions.
3. Enter the senior's contact information, including their email address.
4. Provide a few photos of the senior as requested.
5. Fill out the school's information, including name, address, phone number, and email address.
6. Enter the ceremony details, including date, time, venue, and ideas for the ceremony.
7. Enter any additional recognition or yearbook ideas.
8. Fill out the author and title of any story about the senior.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Senior Acheivements**: Select 'Yes' if the senior has achieved anything notable, such as academic awards, sports achievements, or community service. 
* **Senior Stories**: Enter a brief story about the senior, including their accomplishments and achievements.
* **Senior Contact**: Enter the senior's email address so that they can be notified about the recognition and ceremony.
* **Senior Photos**: Upload a few photos of the senior.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where the senior attended.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school where the senior attended.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the school.
* **Ceremony Ideas**: Enter any ideas you have for the ceremony, such as songs, dances, or speeches.
* **Ceremony Date**: Select a date for the ceremony, if available.
* **Ceremony Time**: Select a time for the ceremony, if available.
* **Ceremony Venue**: Enter the venue for the ceremony.
* **Ceremony Photos**: Upload photos related to the ceremony.
* **Recognition Ideas**: Select 'Yes' if you have any ideas for recognition, such as awards or certificates.
* **Recognition Program**: Enter any ideas for the recognition program.
* **Yearbook Ideas**: Enter any additional ideas for the yearbook.
* **Yearbook Photos**: Upload photos for the yearbook.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization or company that is recognizing the senior.
* **Organization Email**: Enter the email address of the organization or company.
* **Organization Phone**: Enter the phone number of the organization or company.
* **Organization Address**: Enter the address of the organization or company.
* **Story Title**: Enter a title for the story about the senior.
* **Story Body**: Enter the body of the story about the senior.
* **Story Author**: Enter the author of the story about the senior.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form and complete the survey.
