# MBA Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MBA Form is designed for individuals applying to an MBA program. This form requires information about the applicant's personal details, educational background, and achievements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in a sequential manner, starting from the first page.
2.  Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the first page.
3.  Provide your Birth Date and Graduation Date in the second page.
4.  Upload your Resume, choose your MBA Program, and fill out the rest of the fields as required.
5.  Finally, enter your personal details such as Address and Phone Number in the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as per the official records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as per the official records.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email ID for communication purposes.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your graduation from your previous degree.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required): Upload your resume in PDF format.
* **MBA Program** (`mba_program`, `text`, optional): Mention the specific MBA program you are applying to.
* **GPA** (`gpa`, `number`, optional): Enter your GPA from your previous degree.
* **GRE** (`gre`, `number`, optional): Enter your GRE score.
* **GMAT** (`gmat`, `number`, optional): Enter your GMAT score.
* **TOEFL** (`toefl`, `number`, optional): Enter your TOEFL score.
* **SAT** (`sat`, `number`, optional): Enter your SAT score.
* **Quantitative** (`gre_quantitative`, `number`, optional): Enter your GRE Quantitative score.
* **Verbal** (`gre_verbals`, `number`, optional): Enter your GRE Verbal score.
* **Writing** (`gre_writing`, `number`, optional): Enter your GRE Writing score.
* **Total** (`gre_total`, `number`, optional): Enter your total GRE score.
* **Percentile** (`gre_percentile`, `number`, optional): Enter your percentile from GRE.
* **Score** (`gre_score`, `number`, optional): Enter your GRE score.
* **MBA University** (`mba_university`, `text`, optional): Mention the MBA university you graduated from.
* **MBA Duration** (`mba_duration`, `text`, optional): Mention the duration of your MBA program.
* **MBA Field of Study** (`mba_field_of_study`, `text`, optional): Mention the field of study for your MBA degree.
* **MBA Start Date** (`mba_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your MBA program.
* **MBA End Date** (`mba_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of your MBA program.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
