# Economic Education Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about economic education courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including "course evaluations 1", "course evaluations 2", "course evaluations 3", "submission date", "submission time", "course evaluations 4", and "course evaluations 5".
2. Choose the correct options for "course evaluations 4" and "course evaluations 5" from the dropdown menus.
3. Select all relevant options for "course evaluations 5" from the multi-select dropdown menu.
4. Provide any additional notes about your submission in the "submission notes" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "submission phone" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "submission email" field.

* **Instructor info** (`instructor_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter the name of your instructor.
* **Course evaluations** (`course_evaluations`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your evaluation of the course.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.

* **Course name** (`course_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter the name of the course you took or are currently taking.
* **Course id** (`course_id`, number, optional): This field is for you to enter the ID of the course you took or are currently taking.
* **Student major** (`student_major`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your major.
* **Course rating 1** (`course_rating_1`, number, optional): This field is for you to enter your rating of the course.
* **Course rating 2** (`course_rating_2`, number, optional): This field is for you to enter your rating of the course.
* **Course rating 3** (`course_rating_3`, number, optional): This field is for you to enter your rating of the course.
* **Instructor name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter the name of your instructor.
