# Educational Kit Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about delivering educational kits to schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the delivery information, including the kit number.
2. Select the delivery method (e.g., School, Email, Phone, Pickup).
3. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, phone number, and any additional comments.
4. Provide the school name and address if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Kit Number**: Enter the unique number assigned to the kit.
* **Delivery Method**: Choose how you want to deliver the kit (e.g., School, Email, Phone, Pickup).
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you want to deliver the kit.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person for the delivery.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about the delivery.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school receiving the kit.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school receiving the kit.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the delivery information and contact details for accuracy.
* If you are unable to deliver the kit in person, please select a delivery method that is suitable for your situation.
* If you have any additional comments or information about the delivery, please add them in the comments field.
