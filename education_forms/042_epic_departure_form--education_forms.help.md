# epic_departure_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The epic_departure_form is a form used to document the departure details of a student from a flight academy. It includes fields for start and end times, flight academy details, student information, and contact details. This form is typically used by flight academy staff to record the departure times and dates of students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start and end times for the student's departure.
2. Enter the flight academy details, including the flight academy name, phone number, and address.
3. Provide student information, such as the student's name, student ID, and phone number.
4. Enter contact details for the student's arrival date and time.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about the student's departure.
