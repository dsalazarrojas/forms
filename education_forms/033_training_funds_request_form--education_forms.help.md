# Training Funds Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Training Funds Request Form" is used by employees to request training funds for approved courses or workshops that can benefit their professional development. To request training funds, you will need to fill out the form with your information and course details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select "Employee Details" and fill out your personal information, including your name and employee ID.
2.  In the "Course Details" section, provide the title of the course you are interested in and a short description of why you need it.
3.  In the "Additional Information" section, include any relevant details about your training requirements or needs.
4.  In the "Requested Funds" field, enter the amount of money you need for the course.
5.  In the "Submitted By" field, enter your name as the person submitting the request.
6.  In the "Submitted Date" field, select the date the form was submitted.
7.  In the "Status" field, choose the current status of your request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
8.  In the "Comments" field, enter any comments or feedback from your supervisor or the training department.
9.  In the "Form Submitted" field, enter your name as the person who submitted the form.
10. In the "Assigned Tool" field, enter any relevant information about the tool or equipment used for the training course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, Text, optional): Please enter your name and employee ID in this field. This information will help identify you as the requesting party.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, Text, optional): Enter the title of the course you are interested in, including any relevant course numbers or codes. This information will help the training department identify the course you are requesting.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, Text, optional): Provide any relevant details about your training requirements or needs. This information will help the training department understand your reasons for requesting the course.
* **Requested Funds** (`requested_funds`, Number, optional): Enter the amount of money you need for the course. This amount should include any registration fees, textbooks, or other costs associated with the course.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, Text, optional): Enter your name as the person submitting the request. This information will help track who is requesting the training.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, Date, optional): Select the date the form was submitted. This will help the training department keep track of the form submission dates.
* **Status** (`status`, Select, optional): Choose the current status of your request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending). This will help the training department keep track of your request status.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, required): Enter any comments or feedback from your supervisor or the training department. This is a required field to help track communication and feedback.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, Text, optional): Enter your name as the person who submitted the form. This information will help track who submitted the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the tool or equipment used for the training course. This information will help the training department keep track of the tools used for the course.
