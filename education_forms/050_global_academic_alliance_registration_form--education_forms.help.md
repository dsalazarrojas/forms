# Global Academic Alliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your institution or organization from the Global Academic Alliance. By filling out this form, you will be able to showcase your institution's details and connect with other educational partners worldwide.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out each field carefully**, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. **Save your work** as you go, in case you need to refer back to this guide or if you need to make changes.
3. **Submit the form** once you have completed all required fields (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Partner Name** (partner_name, text, optional)
Enter your institution or organization's name as it appears on official documents and websites.

### **Email** (partner_email, email, optional)
Enter your contact email address for the Global Academic Alliance. This will be the primary contact method for future communications.

### **Phone** (partner_phone, text, optional)
Enter your institution or organization's phone number. This will help us reach you for any questions or follow-up.

### **Terms and Conditions** (partner_terms_and_conditions, note, optional)
Here, you can agree to the Global Academic Alliance terms and conditions. Please read and understand the terms carefully before accepting.

### **Institution** (partner_institution, text, optional)
Enter your institution or organization's name as it appears on official documents and websites.

### **Type** (partner_type, select_one, optional)
Select 'Yes' if your institution or organization is a partner of the Global Academic Alliance, and 'No' otherwise.

### **Description** (partner_description, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of your institution or organization, including its mission, vision, or unique features.

### **Tags** (partner_tags, select_multiple, optional)
Select all relevant categories that describe your institution or organization. This will help us and other partners find you more easily.

### **Address** (partner_address, text, optional)
Enter your institution or organization's physical address. This is only needed if you want to receive physical mail or be contacted in person.

### **Website** (partner_website, text, optional)
Enter your institution or organization's website URL.

### **Social Media** (partner_social_media, text, optional)
Enter your institution or organization's social media handles (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn).

### **Image** (partner_image, text, optional)
Enter a URL to a high-quality image representing your institution or organization.

## Tips
* Ensure the information you provide is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for help.
* Double-check your submission before submitting the form.
