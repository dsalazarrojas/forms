# Learning Progress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Progress Assessment form is used to track and record the progress of students in a learning program. This form captures relevant information such as student ID, date, assessment type, score, comments, and contact details. It helps administrators and instructors to monitor and assess student performance over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Student ID of the student you are assessing.
2. Select the Date of the assessment.
3. Choose the type of assessment performed (Yes or No).
4. Enter the score of the assessment.
5. Provide any comments about the student's progress or performance.
6. Enter the student's email address (optional).
7. Enter the student's phone number (optional).
8. Enter the date of the assessment (this will automatically be populated from the previous field if left blank).
9. Provide any comments from the teacher about the student's performance (optional).
10. If desired, include comments from the student about their own performance (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID**: A unique identifier for the student being assessed.
* **Date**: The date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Type**: Choose 'Yes' if the student has been assessed, 'No' if not.
* **Score**: Enter the student's score or grade for the assessment.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback about the student's progress or performance.
* **Email**: The student's email address (optional).
* **Phone**: The student's phone number (optional).
* **Assessment Date**: The date of the assessment (this field will be auto-populated if left blank).
* **Teacher Comments**: Provide any comments from the teacher about the student's performance (optional).
* **Student Comments**: Provide any comments from the student about their own performance (optional).
