# Reopening School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians about their child's experience and concerns related to school reopening. It is intended for parents who have previously indicated that they do not intend to enroll their child in the current school year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your response to the question "Parent Response" by choosing one of the options provided.
2. Enter the name of your child's school in the "School Name" field.
3. Choose the grade level of your child's school year from the "Grade Level" dropdown list.
4. In the "Teacher Concerns", "Student Concerns", and "Parent Concerns" fields, type any concerns or issues you or your child's teacher or school staff have encountered related to school reopening.
5. In the "Other Comments" field, provide any additional comments or information you would like to share about your child's experience.
6. In the "Survey Completion" field, provide any additional information or comments about completing this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Response** (`parent_response`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you are a parent who has previously indicated that you do not intend to enroll your child in the current school year.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose the grade level of your child's school year.
* **Teacher Concerns** (`teacher_concerns`, text, required: false): In this field, type any concerns or issues your child's teacher or school staff have encountered related to school reopening.
* **Student Concerns** (`student_concerns`, text, required: false): In this field, type any concerns or issues your child has experienced related to school reopening.
* **Parent Concerns** (`parent_concerns`, text, required: false): In this field, type any concerns or issues you have experienced related to school reopening.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, required: false): In this field, provide any additional comments or information you would like to share about your child's experience.
* **Survey Completion** (`survey_completion`, text, required: false): In this field, provide any additional information or comments about completing this survey.
