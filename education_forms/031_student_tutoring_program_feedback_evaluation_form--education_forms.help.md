# student_tutoring_program_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The student tutoring program feedback evaluation form is designed to gather insights and suggestions from students who have participated in the tutoring program. Your feedback will help us improve the program to better support students like you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the sections as described below. If you need assistance or clarification on any of the questions, please don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/true): Please fill in your name for reference.
- **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required/true): Choose a rating from 1-5 to help us understand how well the program met your expectations. A higher rating indicates a better experience.
- **Helpful** (`helpful`, `select_one`, required/true): Check "Helpful" if the program was beneficial to you. If you didn't find it helpful, choose "Not helpful" or "Neither helpful nor not helpful".
- **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select all options that apply to how the program could be improved, even if it's not significantly improved.
- **Frequency** (`frequency`, `number`, required/true): Choose how often you would like to receive program services, from 1-5.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/true): Share your thoughts and experiences with the program. Be specific about what worked and what didn't.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/false): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use this field.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/true): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and improvements.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/false): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your number.
- **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required/true): Click "True" to submit your form and "False" to cancel.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when providing comments and suggestions.
- Choose the most accurate rating and helpful options that apply to your experience.
- Select all options that apply for suggestions, even if they're not significantly improved.
- Remember to enter a valid email address so we can contact you with updates and improvements.
