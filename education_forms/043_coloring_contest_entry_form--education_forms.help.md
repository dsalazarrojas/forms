# Coloring Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coloring Contest Entry Form is designed to collect participant information and entry details for the coloring contest. This form is a single-entry form for both adults and children, ensuring that each entry submitted is accurate and meets the required criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Entrant name, Age, and Category.
2. If applicable, provide your School or Group name.
3. If applicable, provide the Guardian contact information.
4. Upload your artwork by providing the Upload link or reference.
5. Select the Consent to Publish option to agree on public display.
6. Add any Additional Notes if necessary.
7. Finally, click Submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entrant name** (Entrant name, text, required): Enter your full name as the participant.
* **Age** (Age, number, required): Enter your age as the participant.
* **School or group** (School or group, text, optional): If you're entering as part of a school or group, provide the name here.
* **Guardian contact** (Guardian contact, text, optional): If you're under 18, provide your guardian's contact information.
* **Entry title** (Entry title, text, optional): Provide an optional title for your artwork.
* **Upload link or reference** (Upload link or reference, text, required): Provide the link or reference to your uploaded artwork.
* **Consent to publish** (Consent to publish, select_one, required): Select 'True' to agree on public display of your artwork.
* **Category** (Category, select_one, required): Choose the age category for your artwork.
* **Additional notes** (Additional notes, text, optional): Add any additional information about your entry if necessary.
