# Safety Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Training Registration Form is used to collect information from participants about their safety training experiences. It allows us to track and record participant data for training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name to identify yourself as a participant.
2. Choose the training program you are participating in.
3. Select the date you attended the training.
4. Enter the time you attended the training.
5. Specify the location where the training took place.
6. Enter the name of the instructor who conducted the training.
7. Enter your contact phone number.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Provide any additional notes about your training experience.
10. Optionally, include any notes that you have about yourself as a participant.
