# Erasmus Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information for the Erasmus program application. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as required.
2. Enter your personal details, such as name, email, and date of birth.
3. Enter your nationality, phone number, and address.
4. If you are a current student or have previously studied in a certain area, select it from the "Study Area" field.
5. Provide your motivation for applying to the Erasmus program.
6. If you have previously studied in any language other than your native language, select the language and enter your level of proficiency.
7. Enter your academic background, if applicable.
8. If you have a motivation letter or language certificate, attach them or enter a description.
9. Upload your CV and transcript.
10. If you have any references, provide them.
11. If you are interested in specific study programs, select them from the "Study Programs" field.
12. If you are applying to a specific host institution, select it from the "Host Institution" field, and enter the corresponding details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Nationality** (4, text, required): Enter your nationality as it appears on your official documents.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Enter your phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Address** (6, text, required): Enter your address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Study Area** (7, text, required): If you have previously studied in any area, select it from the options.
* **Motivation** (8, text, required): Enter a brief explanation of why you wish to apply for the Erasmus program.
* **Language** (9, text, required): Enter the language you are proficient in, aside from your native language.
* **Language Level** (10, select_one, required): Select your level of proficiency in the language you have entered.
* **Academic Background** (11, text, optional): If you have any academic background that is relevant to your application, enter it here.
* **Academic Level** (12, text, optional): If you have any previous academic level, enter it here.
* **Motivation Letter** (13, note, optional): If you have a motivation letter, attach it or describe it here.
* **Language Certificate** (14, text, optional): If you have a language certificate, attach it or describe it here.
* **CV** (15, text, required): Upload your CV.
* **Transcript** (16, text, required): Upload your transcript.
* **References** (17, text, optional): If you have any references, provide them here.
* **Study Programs** (18, text, optional): If you are interested in specific study programs, select them from the options.
* **Host Institution** (19, select_multiple, optional): If you are applying to a specific host institution, select it from the options.
* **Study Area Host** (20, select_multiple, optional): If you are applying to a specific host institution, select the study area you are interested in.
* **Academic Level Host** (21, select_multiple, optional): If you are applying to a specific host institution, select the academic level you are interested in.
* **Host Department** (22, text, optional): If you are applying to a specific host department, enter it here.
* **Host Institution Address** (23, text, optional): If you are applying to a specific host institution, enter the address here.
* **Host Institution Email** (24, email, optional): If you are applying to a specific host institution, enter their email address.
* **Host Institution Phone** (25, text, optional): If you are applying to a specific host institution, enter their phone number.
