# Geology Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geology Course Registration form is used to collect personal details, academic status, session preferences, and workshop information from students to help course administrators manage course registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Select your academic status in the "Academic Status" field.
* Choose your session preferences in the "Session Preferences" field.
* Select the workshop you want to attend in the "Workshop" field.
* Choose your preferred session dates and time in the "Session Dates" and "Session Time" fields, respectively.
* Add any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.
* Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
* Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field.
* Confirm your status in the "Confirm Status" field.
* Confirm the workshop you want to attend in the "Confirm Workshop" field.
* Confirm your session dates in the "Confirm Dates" field.
* Confirm your session time in the "Confirm Time" field.
* Confirm any additional information in the "Confirm Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Academic Status (Select One)**: Select your current academic status from the available options.
* **Session Preferences (Select Multiple)**: Choose your preferred session options.
* **Workshop (Select One)**: Select the workshop you want to attend.
* **Session Dates (Date)**: Choose your preferred session dates.
* **Session Time (Time)**: Choose your preferred session time.
* **Additional Info (Note)**: Add any additional information about your registration.
* **Confirm Email (Email)**: Re-enter your email address to confirm.
* **Confirm Phone (Text)**: Re-enter your phone number to confirm.
* **Confirm Status (Select One)**: Confirm your current academic status.
* **Confirm Workshop (Select One)**: Confirm the workshop you want to attend.
* **Confirm Dates (Date)**: Confirm your session dates.
* **Confirm Time (Time)**: Confirm your session time.
* **Confirm Info (Note)**: Confirm any additional information about your registration.
