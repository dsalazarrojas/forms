# Educational Scholarship Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educational Scholarship Claim Form is designed to collect information from students who wish to claim an educational scholarship. This form helps to gather details about the student's application type, academic level, program of study, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Select the type of application you are submitting (e.g., Application, Addendum, etc.).
3. Enter the date you submitted your application.
4. Provide your student number.
5. Enter the name of the educational institution you are affiliated with.
6. Select your academic level (e.g., High School, College/University, etc.).
7. Enter the name of the academic program you are enrolled in.
8. Enter the course of study you are undertaking.
9. Enter your status as a student.
10. Enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).
11. If required, upload any relevant attachments.
12. Finally, select the appropriate submission option (e.g., Accept, Reject, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or academic records. This is a required field.
* **What type of application you are submitting**: Select the type of application you are submitting (e.g., Application, Addendum, etc.). This field is not required.
* **Date Application Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted your application. This field is not required.
* **Student number**: Enter your student ID or number assigned to you by your educational institution. This field is not required.
* **Educational institution name**: Enter the name of the institution where you are studying. This field is not required.
* **Academic level**: Select your current academic level (e.g., High School, College/University, etc.). This field is not required.
* **Academic program name**: Enter the name of the academic program you are enrolled in. This field is not required.
* **Course of study**: Enter the name of the course you are currently studying. This field is not required.
* **Student status**: Enter your current student status (e.g., full-time, part-time, etc.). This field is not required.
* **Contact information**: Enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, etc.). This field is not required.
* **Upload attachments**: If required, upload any relevant attachments to support your application. This field is not required.
* **Submit application**: Select the submission option (e.g., Accept, Reject, etc.). This field is not required.

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your entries for any mistakes or inconsistencies.
* Attach any required documents or materials to support your application.
* Submit your application with care, as it will be reviewed thoroughly.
