# Student Group Funding Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Group Funding Request Form is designed for students to request funding for a specific group or project. This form helps the student to provide necessary information to process their funding request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your group name, description, and request date.
3. Provide a clear justification for your request.
4. Specify the amount of funding you are requesting.
5. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
6. If necessary, provide additional information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Name (Group Name)**: Enter the name of the group or project that you are requesting funding for.
* **Group Description (Group Description)**: Enter a short description of the group or project.
* **Request Date (Request Date)**: Enter the date you are requesting funding for.
* **Request Justification (Request Justification)**: Provide a clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting funding.
* **Request Amount (Request Amount)**: Enter the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Contact Name (Contact Name)**: Enter your name as the contact person for the group or project.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: Enter your email address as the contact person for the group or project.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter your phone number as the contact person for the group or project (optional).
* **Additional Info (Additional Info)**: Provide any additional information about your request.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you are not requesting funding for the same project or group multiple times.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Provide all necessary information to process your funding request.
