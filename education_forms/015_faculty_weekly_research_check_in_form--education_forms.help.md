# Faculty Weekly Research Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Weekly Research Check In Form is designed to help faculty members keep track of their weekly research progress. It collects information about the research they are conducting, any obstacles they may face, and their next steps to stay on track.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your **Project** from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the **Research Progress** you have made since your last check in.
3. If you have faced any **Obstacles**, type them in the text field.
4. Indicate if you **Need Support** and how much time you will dedicate to your project this week.
5. Enter the **Date Last Check In** and **Time Last Check In** as accurately as possible.
6. Choose the method you used for your last check in (e.g., **In-person**, **Phone**, **Video Conference**).
7. If you plan to **Next Check In** at a specific date and time, enter it in the format MM/DD/YY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Faculty Member**: This is the name of the person completing the form.
* **Project**: Select the research project you are working on from the dropdown menu. This helps the form creator understand which project your work is related to.
* **Research Progress**: Choose all the options that apply to the progress you made on your project this week. This could include things like:
	+ Made significant discoveries or breakthroughs
	+ Conducted experiments or interviews
	+ Collected data
	+ Made progress on writing a paper or report
* **Obstacles Encountered**: If you have faced any challenges or problems while working on your project, type them in here. This helps the form creator understand where you may need additional support.
* **Support Needed**: If you need help or support from the form creator or others, indicate how much time you will dedicate to your project this week. A number is not a requirement if you do not need support.
* **Date Last Check In**: Enter the date you last checked in.
* **Time Last Check In**: Enter the time you last checked in.
* **Last Check In Method**: Choose the method you used for your last check in (e.g., In-person, Phone, Video Conference).
* **Next Check In Date**: If you plan to check in at a specific date and time in the future, enter it here. This helps the form creator stay on schedule with your progress.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or notes about your progress, type them here.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your **Research Progress** and **Obstacles Encountered**.
- Use the correct format for entering **Date Last Check In** and **Time Last Check In** (e.g., MM/DD/YY and HH:MM AM/PM).
- Make sure to select all relevant options for **Research Progress**.
- If you are not sure what to enter for **Last Check In Method**, select all that apply.
- If you need to **Next Check In Date**, make sure it is in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
