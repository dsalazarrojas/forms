# Site Layout Knowledge Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Layout Knowledge Check form is designed to collect information about your knowledge and experiences with the site layout. This data will help us better understand your current role in the organization and your interaction with the site's layout. Please fill in the form accurately to provide us with valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your current role in the organization from the options provided.
2. If prompted, enter the date when you last checked the site's layout.
3. If necessary, provide your thoughts on common site layout mistakes in the text area.
4. Enter your preferred email address (if you want to be contacted).
5. Enter your preferred phone number (if you want to be contacted).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current role in the organization?** (`Administrator`, `Designer`, `Manager`, optional): This question helps us understand your position within the organization and how you interact with the site's layout. Select the option that best describes your role.
* **How often do you usually check the site's layout?** (`date`, optional): If you regularly inspect the site's layout, please enter the date of your last check. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **What are some common site layout mistakes?** (`note`, optional): If you have encountered common mistakes while working with the site's layout, provide a brief description in this text area.
* **What is your preferred email address?** (`email`, optional): If you would like to be contacted regarding this form, please enter your preferred email address.
* **What is your preferred phone number?** (`text`, optional): If you would like to be contacted regarding this form, please enter your preferred phone number.
