# Pronouns Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pronouns Quiz form is designed to help users identify and explore their personal pronouns and gender identity. It's a tool for users to discover and learn about their own pronouns in a safe and interactive way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Pronouns Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Enter your answer to each question on the form, using the numbers provided.
2. Type your answer to each question in the corresponding text field.
3. Review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your personal pronoun preferences.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **question1** (`question 1`, number, required/optional): Please type a number that corresponds to the first question asked. This field is currently not required.
* **answer1** (`answer1`, text, required/optional): Type your response to the first question, and we'll use it to help you understand your pronouns.
* **question2** (`question 2`, number, required/optional): This is the second question asked, please enter the number that corresponds to it.
* **answer2** (`answer2`, text, required/optional): Answer this question in the text field.
* **question3** (`question3`, number, required/optional): Enter the number for this question.
* **answer3** (`answer3`, text, required/optional): Provide your response to this question.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit your answers and explore your personal pronouns.
