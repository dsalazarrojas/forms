# Enrollment Charge Clearance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrollment Charge Clearance Request form is used to submit a request for clearance of an enrollment charge for a student. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the clearance process is initiated correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, paying attention to the required fields.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions from the student before submitting the form.
3. Double-check that you are not duplicating a previous submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Academic Year** (`academic_year`, Select One, Required): Select the academic year for which the enrollment charge needs to be cleared.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief explanation for the request.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, Optional): Enter the contact number of the department or submitter.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the student.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Enter the department name or code.
* **Department Contact** (`department_contact`, Text, Optional): Enter the department contact person or number.
* **Department Phone** (`department_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the department phone number.
* **Department Email** (`department_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the department email address.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the submitter phone number.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, Text, Optional): Enter the department or submitter's department name or code.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, Text, Optional): Enter the submitter's position or title.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any comments or notes for the submitter.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, Text, Optional): Enter the submitter's agreement or confirmation.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, Text, Optional): Enter the submitter's signature or name.
* **Submitter Date** (`submitter_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter Time** (`submitter_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitter Department 2** (`submitter_department_2`, Text, Optional): Enter the department or submitter's department name or code 2.
* **Submitter Department 3** (`submitter_department_3`, Text, Optional): Enter the department or submitter's department name or code 3.
