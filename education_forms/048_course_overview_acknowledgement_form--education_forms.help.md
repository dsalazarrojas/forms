# Course Overview Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students and parents about a course, ensuring that all necessary details are captured accurately. It is used in educational settings to collect data about courses, students, and instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Provide accurate information for each field, taking into account any hints or guidance provided.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure no duplicates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Title** (`Course Title`, text, required): Enter the title of the course you are acknowledging.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter your student name as it appears on your records.
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Guardian Name** (`Guardian Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your guardian, if applicable.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Select the date you are acknowledging the course.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): If applicable, select the time you are acknowledging the course.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you are taking the course, if applicable.
* **Instructor** (`Instructor`, text, required): Enter the name of your instructor for the course.
* **Acknowledgement** (`Acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Select 'True' to acknowledge the course details, indicating you have reviewed and understood the course overview.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your instructor or course administrator for clarification.
- This form is for educational purposes only, and the information collected will be used to improve the course and educational experience.
