# Vocational School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to enroll in a vocational school program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the student's personal information section.
2. Select the program that interests you the most.
3. Provide emergency contact information.
4. Confirm parent consent and student consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (id: 1, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Student Email** (id: 2, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone** (id: 3, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Program Interest** (id: 4, select_one, required): Select the program that interests you the most.
* **Student Date of Birth** (id: 5, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Student Gender** (id: 6, select_multiple, required): Select your gender.
* **Student Address** (id: 7, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Student Program Start Date** (id: 8, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start the program.
* **Student Program End Date** (id: 9, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to end the program.
* **Student School Attendance** (id: 10, time, optional): Enter your school attendance.
* **Student School Attendance Comment** (id: 11, note, optional): Add any relevant comments about your school attendance.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 12, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (id: 13, select_one, required): Select your relation to the contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 14, text, required): Enter your contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (id: 15, text, optional): Enter the address of your contact.
* **Parent Consent** (id: 16, select_one, required): Confirm if a parent or guardian consented to your enrollment.
* **Student Consent** (id: 17, select_one, required): Confirm if you consent to the terms of your enrollment.
* **Student Signature** (id: 18, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm your information.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (id: 19, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm your emergency contact's information.
* **Parent Signature** (id: 20, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm your parent or guardian's information.
* **Student Signature Date** (id: 21, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Emergency Contact Signature Date** (id: 22, date, optional): Enter the date your emergency contact signed the form.
* **Parent Signature Date** (id: 23, date, optional): Enter the date your parent or guardian signed the form.
* **School Administered** (id: 24, select_one, required): Confirm if the school has administered the program.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
