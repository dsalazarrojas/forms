# COVID 19 Relief Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for COVID 19 relief scholarships, which are designed to support students affected by the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form accurately and completely.
2. Answer each question truthfully.
3. Make sure to attach any required supporting documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name_full`, text, required): This is your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level_val`, text, required): Please select your current grade level.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): If applicable, enter the name of your parent or guardian who is providing financial support.
* **Household Income Change** (`income_change_cat`, select_one, required): Select the statement that best describes how your household income has changed due to the pandemic:
	+ Significant decrease (50%+)
	+ Moderate decrease (25-50%)
	+ Slight decrease (<25%)
	+ No change
* **Reason for Application** (`application_reason_text`, text, required): Describe how the pandemic has impacted your ability to pay school fees.
* **Type of Supporting Documentation Available** (`documentation_type_cat`, select_one, required): Select the type of supporting documentation you have available to demonstrate your financial situation:
	+ Unemployment letter
	+ Payroll stubs
	+ Tax return
	+ Employer letter
* **Current School Fees Balance** (`current_fees_balance`, number, required): Enter the current balance of your school fees.
* **Scholarship Amount Requested** (`scholarship_amount_requested`, number, required): Enter the amount of scholarship you are requesting.
* **Detailed Statement of Financial Need** (`financial_need_statement`, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation of your financial situation and how you are unable to pay school fees.
* **Student Academic Progress Summary** (`academic_progress_summary`, text, optional): Briefly describe your academic performance this year. (This field is optional and does not affect your application.)
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_no`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **I Authorize the School to Verify the Information Provided** (`application_auth_ack`, select_one, required): Select "True" to authorize the school to verify the information provided.
