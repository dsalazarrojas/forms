# thomasville_city_schools_chromebook_checkout_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the checkout process of a Chromebook for the students of Thomasville City Schools. It captures essential information needed to track ownership and usage history of the device.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Make sure you have a valid reason for checking out the Chromebook. If you're not sure, check with your school staff for guidance.
2. Enter your **User Name**, which is the name of the staff member or student who is checking out the Chromebook.
3. Select the **Date Issued**, which is the date the Chromebook is being checked out.
4. Enter the **Student Name** of the student borrowing the Chromebook.
5. Enter the **Student ID** number assigned to the student.
6. Choose the **Grade Level** of the student from the dropdown options.
7. If applicable, enter your **Parent Signature**, which confirms the parent/guardian's approval.
8. Enter your **School Staff Name**, who is responsible for issuing the Chromebook.
9. If applicable, enter the **Student Signature**, indicating the student's agreement to the checkout terms.
10. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Enter the name of the staff member or student who is checking out the Chromebook.
* **Date Issued**: Select the date the Chromebook is being checked out. This helps track the start date of the checkout period.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student borrowing the Chromebook.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student ID number assigned to the student. This is used to track student ownership and usage history.
* **Grade Level**: Choose the grade level of the student from the dropdown options. This helps the school track student's usage in different grade levels.
* **Parent Signature**: If the student is under 18, enter the parent/guardian's signature to confirm approval. This is optional and only required for minor students.
* **School Staff Name**: Enter the name of the school staff member responsible for issuing the Chromebook.
* **Student Signature**: If applicable, enter the student's signature indicating their agreement to the checkout terms. This is optional and only required if the student is 18 or older.
* **Student Signature**: This field is intended for students above 18 years old or for students who are responsible for their own devices. If the student is above 18, they must sign the contract to take ownership of the device.
*   **Parent/Guardian Signature**: If the student is under 18 years old, this field should be filled out by a parent or legal guardian.
*   **School Staff Name**: This field should be filled out by a school staff member who issues the device.
*   **Student Name**: This is the name of the student borrowing the Chromebook.
*   **Student ID**: This is the student ID number assigned to the student.
*   **Grade Level**: Choose the grade level of the student from the dropdown options.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is accurate.
*   If you need to make any changes to the Chromebook checkout process, please use the "Update Chromebook Checkout" form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact your school staff for guidance.
