# student_led_education_reform_project_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student-led education reform project application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information. Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your project.
* **School District** (`school_district`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether your project is affiliated with a school district.
* **School Type** (`school_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of school that is associated with your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Assigned Organizations** (`assigned_organizations`, `select_multiple`, optional): If your project is affiliated with any organizations, select them here.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school associated with your project.
* **District Address** (`district_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the school district.
* **School City** (`school_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city of the school.
* **School State** (`school_state`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the state of the school.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code of the school.
* **Assigned Users** (`assigned_users`, `select_multiple`, optional): If there are users assigned to your project, select them here.
* **School Country** (`school_country`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the country of the school.
* **School Employees** (`school_employees`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the number of school employees.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, `number`, optional): Enter the budget for your project if applicable.
* **Project Funding** (`project_funding`, `select_one`, optional): Check if your project has funding.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current status of your project.
* **Assigned Organizations 2** (`assigned_organizations_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): If your project is affiliated with any additional organizations, select them here.
* **Project Start Time** (`project_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of your project if applicable.
* **Project End Time** (`project_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of your project if applicable.
* **School Board Status** (`school_board_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the school board.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the assigned user if applicable.

Note: The optional fields can be skipped if not applicable to your project.
