# Foundation Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Foundation Skills Assessment form is designed to collect essential contact information from individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and truthfully information.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that you have not already submitted the form to avoid duplication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Fill out your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Fill out your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Fill out your age as you perceive it or check the "Age" field is not required.
* **Contact EMail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address used for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Fill out your contact phone number without any special characters.
* **Contact Phone Type** (`contact_phone_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of your contact phone number (Mobile, Landline, Other).
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more phone number(s) you would like to contact you.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, note, optional): Describe your contact address in detail.
* **Contact Phone Extension** (`contact_phone_extension`, time, optional): Fill out the time of your contact phone number, if applicable.
 
## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
