# Dorm Room Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are requesting to change their dorm room. The form asks for various details such as the student's personal information, reasons for the request, and other relevant details to ensure the room change process is smooth and efficient. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the "New Room Request" option that best suits your needs from the dropdown menu.
2. Briefly explain your reason for requesting the room change.
3. Choose your preferred room type and location.
4. Select your current and new residence hall from the dropdown menus.
5. Enter your current and new room numbers (if applicable).
6. If you have any comments or additional information, you can add them in the "Comments" field.
7. Finally, submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Please fill in your name and other personal details as required.
* **New Room Request**: Select the option that best describes your reason for requesting a room change.
* **Reason for Request**: Provide a brief explanation for your room change request.
* **Room Type**: Choose the type of room you are requesting.
* **Room Location**: Select the location of the room you are requesting.
* **Current Residence Hall**: Select your current residence hall from the dropdown menu.
* **Current Room Number**: Enter your current room number (if applicable).
* **New Residence Hall**: Select your new residence hall from the dropdown menu.
* **New Room Number**: Enter your new room number (if applicable).
* **Reason for Leaving**: If you are moving out of your current room, provide an explanation.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you for the room change process.
* **Form Version**: This field is automatically populated and should not be filled in.
* **Submission Date**: This field is automatically populated and should not be filled in.
* **Submitted By**: This field is automatically populated and should not be filled in.
* **Review Date**: This field is automatically populated and should not be filled in.
* **Reviewed By**: This field is automatically populated and should not be filled in.
* **Status**: Select the status of your room change request.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or information about your request.
* **Review Comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Decision**: Select the outcome of your room change request.
* **Action Taken**: Enter any actions taken as a result of your room change request.

Please note that some fields are automatically populated and should not be filled in, and some fields are required for the form to be submitted.
