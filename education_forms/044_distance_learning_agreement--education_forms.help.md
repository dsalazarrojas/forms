# Distance Learning Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Distance Learning Agreement form, which is a document designed to outline the terms and conditions for a remote or online learning environment. This form is often used in educational settings to ensure that both students and instructors are on the same page and understand their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and provide your information accurately.
2. Fill in the required fields with your details:
	* Instructor Name
	* Instructor Title
	* Instructor Institution
3. If you are a student, fill in:
	* Student Name
	* Student Email (Note: You can use either student_email or student_email_2 field, both seem to be optional, but please be careful not to enter duplicate information.)
4. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Instructor Name** (Instructor Name, text, required/optional): This field is for the instructor's name who will be teaching the course.
* **Instructor Title** (Instructor Title, text, required/optional): This field is for the instructor's title or position.
* **Instructor Institution** (Instructor Institution, text, required/optional): This field is for the name of the institution where the instructor works.
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required/optional): This field is for the student's name who will be taking the course.
* **Student Email** (Student Email, email, required/optional): This field is for the student's email address, which will be used for communication. Please note that you can use either student_email or student_email_2 field for this purpose.
* **Student Email (second instance)** (Student Email, email, required/optional): This field is a duplicate of the previous one, it seems to be a mistake in the form design. Please ignore this field and use the first one instead.

## Tips
* Make sure to use the correct email address for the student and instructor to ensure smooth communication.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact form@gic.mx for further assistance.
