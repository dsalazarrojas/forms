# School Virtual Engagement Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Virtual Engagement Registration Form is designed to collect information from students participating in virtual events or programs. This form helps us understand who is attending, their interests, and preferences for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section of the form thoroughly and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you're unsure or do not have the necessary information.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Information
* **Student Name** (1. Student Information): Your name as a student.
* **Student ID (if applicable)** (1. Student Information): Your student ID number, if applicable.
* **Email (if applicable)** (1. Student Information): Your email address, if applicable.
* **Phone Number (if applicable)** (1. Student Information): Your phone number, if applicable.

### Contact Information
* **Address** (2. Contact Information): Your home address or mailing address.
* **Phone Number** (2. Contact Information): Your phone number.
* **Email** (2. Contact Information): Your email address.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (2. Contact Information): Name of a parent or guardian, if applicable.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone Number** (2. Contact Information): Phone number of a parent or guardian, if applicable.

### Event Information
* **Event Name** (3. Event Information): Name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (3. Event Information): Date of the event.
* **Event Time** (3. Event Information): Time of the event.

### Registration Information
* **Registration Date** (4. Registration Information): Date of your registration.
* **How did you hear about this event? (4. Registration Information): Source of how you learned about the event (e.g., social media, school website, flyer).
* **Do you require accommodations? (4. Registration Information): Yes or No, if you need any accommodations due to a disability.
* **Special Instructions for Facilitators** (4. Registration Information): Any special instructions or requests you have for facilitators or event staff.
