# internship_acceptance_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to declare a student's acceptance or declination of an internship opportunity. It ensures that all parties involved are on the same page and understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title and your name.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the internship.
3. Specify the number of hours you will work per week.
4. Provide details about your work schedule, phone number, and email.
5. Select your internship agreement (Accept or Decline).
6. Sign and date your agreement (or leave a note if you prefer not to sign).
7. Have your supervisor and co-signer sign and date their agreement (or leave a note if they prefer not to sign).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`Form Title`, text, required: false): Enter a title for the internship, which will help identify it for future reference.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the student participating in the internship.
* **University** (`University`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the university you are affiliated with.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to start the internship.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to end the internship.
* **Hours Per Week** (`Hours Per Week`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours you will work per week during the internship.
* **Work Hours** (`Work Hours`, number, required: false): Enter the total number of hours you will work during the internship.
* **Work Hours Hint** (`Work Hours Hint`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about your work schedule or hours.
* **Work Location** (`Work Location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you will be working.
* **Work Location Address** (`Work Location Address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your work location.
* **Work Phone** (`Work Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Email** (`Work Email`, email, required: false): Enter your work email.
* **Internship Agreement** (`Internship Agreement`, select_one, required: false): Select either Accept or Decline to indicate your agreement to the internship.
* **Student Signature** (`Student Signature`, note, required: false): Sign your agreement, or leave a note explaining why you are not signing.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`Supervisor Signature`, note, required: false): Sign your agreement, or leave a note explaining why you are not signing.
* **Co-Signer Signature** (`Co-Signer Signature`, note, required: false): Sign your agreement, or leave a note explaining why you are not signing.
* **Student Agreement** (`Student Agreement`, note, required: false): Sign your agreement, or leave a note explaining why you are not signing.
* **Supervisor Agreement** (`Supervisor Agreement`, note, required: false): Sign your agreement, or leave a note explaining why you are not signing.
* **Co-Signer Agreement** (`Co-Signer Agreement`, note, required: false): Sign your agreement, or leave a note explaining why you are not signing.
* **Internship Agreement Date** (`Internship Agreement Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your agreement.
* **Internship** (`Internship`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information about the internship.
* **Supervisor Approval Date** (`Supervisor Approval Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date your supervisor approved the internship agreement.
* **Co-Signer Approval Date** (`Co-Signer Approval Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date your co-signer approved the internship agreement.
