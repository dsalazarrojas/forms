# Green Building Standards Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering participants to the Green Building Standards Seminar. Please fill it out accurately to help us manage your attendance and provide you with the necessary information for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** accurately.
2. Enter your **Email** address for communication purposes.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for emergency contact or follow-up.
4. Select the **Seminar Date** you plan to attend.
5. Choose a suitable **Seminar Time** for your schedule.
6. Indicate your **Building Standard** preference (Yes or No).
7. If applicable, add any **Additional Comments** about your participation or preferences.
8. If you're representing a company, please enter your **Company** name.
9. Provide your **Job Title** and **Department** (if applicable).
10. Indicate whether you will **Attend** the seminar (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your official email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact or follow-up.
* **Seminar Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date you plan to attend the seminar from the available options.
* **Seminar Time** (Time, Required): Choose a seminar time that suits your schedule from the available options.
* **Building Standard** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you have or do not have a building standard preference.
* **Additional Comments** (Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here.
* **Company** (Text, Optional): If you're representing a company, enter the name here.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Department** (Text, Optional): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Seminar Attendance** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose whether you will attend the seminar (Yes or No).

## Tips
* Ensure you complete all required fields to ensure accurate registration and communication.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
