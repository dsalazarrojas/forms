# Potty Training Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registration purposes only. Please fill it out accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can provide the best possible experience for you and your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form sections in the order they appear.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Details
* **Parent Details**: This section is for providing information about the parent or guardian of the child. Please fill out your name and contact information for our records.

### Child Details
* **Child Details**: This section is for providing information about the child registering for the course. Please fill out their name, age, and any relevant medical information.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details**: This section is for providing information about how you would like to be contacted regarding the course. Please fill out your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, etc.).

### Course Details
* **Course Details**: This section is for providing information about the course you are registering for. Please fill out the course name and any additional information about the course.

### Payment Details
* **Payment Details**: This section is for providing payment information for the course. Please fill out your payment method and any relevant payment details.

### Notes
* **Notes**: This section is for any additional information you would like to provide about your registration. Please use this space to share any important details about your child or your registration.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We appreciate your participation in our potty training course and look forward to providing you with a positive experience.
