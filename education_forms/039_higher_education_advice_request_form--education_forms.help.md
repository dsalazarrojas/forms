# higher_education_advice_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students seeking advice on higher education options.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply answer the following questions as thoroughly as possible:

1. **Student Info**:
	* This field is required and should include your name, email address, and other relevant information about yourself.
2. **Academic Achievements**:
	* This field is optional and should include any academic achievements or accomplishments you would like to highlight.
3. **College Application Info**:
	* This field is optional and should include any information about your college application process.
4. **Special Circumstances**:
	* This field is optional and should include any special circumstances that may affect your college search.
5. **College Search**:
	* Choose whether you are searching for a college or university.
6. **Academic Year**:
	* Choose the academic year you are currently in or plan to graduate in.
7. **Graduation Month**:
	* Choose the month you plan to graduate in.
8. **Scholarship Info**:
	* Choose whether you have received or are seeking a scholarship.
9. **College Type**:
	* Choose the type of college or university you are interested in (Private, Public, or Community College).
10. **Graduation Date**:
	* Enter your expected graduation date.
11. **Graduation Time**:
	* Enter your expected graduation time.
12. **Graduation Status**:
	* Choose whether you have graduated, are in progress, or not applying to college.
13. **Graduation Year Start**:
	* Enter the year you started your studies.
14. **Graduation Year End**:
	* Enter the year you plan to graduate.
15. **Academic Program**:
	* Choose the academic program you are interested in (Bachelor, Master, or Doctorate).
16. **Academic Program Year**:
	* Choose the year of the academic program you are interested in.
17. **College Search Location**:
	* Choose where you would like to search for colleges (On-campus or Off-campus).
18. **Search Location Other**:
	* If you would like to search for colleges in a location other than on or off-campus, enter that location here.
19. **Special Needs**:
	* Enter any special needs or accommodations you require.
20. **Other Needs Info**:
	* Enter any other needs or requirements you have.
21. **College Search Preferences**:
	* Enter any specific preferences for your college search.
22. **Search Preferences Info**:
	* Enter any additional information about your search preferences.
23. **College Search Location Other**:
	* If you would like to search for colleges in a location other than on or off-campus, enter that location here.
24. **Other College Search Location**:
	* If you would like to search for colleges in a location other than on or off-campus, enter that location here.
25. **Additional Comments**:
	* Enter any additional comments or information about your college search.
