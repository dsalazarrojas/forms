# Language Proficiency Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess an individual's language proficiency level, specifically to gauge their confidence in using a particular language. It's intended for language learners, teachers, or students to evaluate their progress and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please select the options that best reflect your confidence level for each question.
2. For time-related questions, estimate the time spent in hours or days.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select "Yes" or "No" according to your situation.
4. Provide any additional comments about your language proficiency level or areas of improvement in the "Comments" field.

* **Time Spent Learning Language**: Estimate the time spent learning the language, in hours or days.
  * This question helps to understand your learning process and time commitment.
