# generic_scholarship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This generic scholarship application form is used to collect information from students and their parents/guardians to determine their eligibility and requirements for scholarships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step guide below:

*   Fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
*   Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student applying for the scholarship.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the student's contact person.
*   **Student Address**: Enter the student's home address.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
*   **Student Gender**: Choose the student's gender (Male or Female).
*   **Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the student's guardian or parent.
*   **Guardian Phone**: Enter the guardian's phone number.
*   **Scholarship Type**: Choose the type of scholarship the student is applying for (Merit or Need).
*   **School Attended**: Enter the name of the school the student is attending.
*   **Grade Level**: Choose the student's grade level (9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
*   **Student Status**: Choose all applicable student status (Full-time, Part-time, or International).
*   **Scholarship Amount**: Enter the amount of scholarship the student is applying for.
*   **Student GPA**: Enter the student's GPA (if applicable).
*   **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
*   **Parent Phone**: Enter the parent's phone number.
*   **Parent Email**: Enter the parent's email address.
*   **Parent Relation**: Enter the relation between the parent and the student (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
*   **Student Address 2**: Enter the student's secondary address (if applicable).

## Tips
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to increase the chances of being awarded a scholarship.
*   Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the scholarship committee for assistance.
