# AI Grading Pilot Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The AI Grading Pilot Participation Consent Form is designed to collect information from participants to evaluate the accuracy, consistency, and fairness of AI-assisted grading compared to traditional human-only grading. This form is intended for undergraduate and graduate students, faculty members, and teaching assistants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your name, email, and department or school affiliation.
3. Indicate your role within the institution.
4. Consent to participate in the pilot and data sharing.
5. Choose your preference for data anonymization.
6. Confirm your understanding of the right to withdraw and academic integrity.
7. Sign with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Please enter your institutional email address where you can be contacted.
* **Your Role in the Institution** (`participant_role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role in the institution from the list.
	+ Undergraduate Student
	+ Graduate Student
	+ Faculty / Instructor
	+ Teaching Assistant (TA)
* **Department / School** (`institution_department`, `text`, required): Please enter your department or school affiliation.
* **About the Pilot Study** (`study_details_note`, `note`, required): This section provides more information about the pilot study.
* **Purpose of the Pilot** (`pilot_purpose_description`, `note`, optional): This section describes the purpose and goals of the pilot study.
* **Have you read the Pilot Participation Information Sheet?** (`information_sheet_read`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate if you have read the information sheet provided.
	+ Yes, I have read it.
	+ No, I need a copy.
* **Participation Consent** (`participation_consent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your consent to participate in the pilot.
	+ Yes, I consent to participate.
	+ No, I do not wish to participate.
* **Data Sharing for Research** (`data_sharing_consent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your consent to share your anonymized data with the research team.
	+ Yes, I consent.
	+ No, I do not consent.
* **Data Anonymization Preference** (`anonymization_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you prefer your data to be anonymized.
	+ Complete Anonymization (No identifying links)
	+ Linked Pseudonymization (Allows withdrawal of data later)
* **Right to Withdrawal Acknowledgment** (`right_to_withdraw_ack`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of the right to withdraw from the pilot at any time.
	+ I understand I can withdraw from the pilot at any time without academic penalty.
	+ Other
* **Would you like to receive the final summary report of this pilot?** (`research_feedback_optin`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you want to receive a summary report.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Academic Integrity Acknowledgment** (`academic_integrity_promise`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of the academic integrity policies.
	+ I understand that this pilot does not change the existing academic integrity policies of the institution.
	+ Other
* **Confirmation Signature** (`digital_signature_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Sign with your digital signature to confirm your participation and agreement to the terms.
	+ I confirm my participation and agreement to the terms above.
	+ Other
* **Date** (`date_of_consent`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your consent.
* **Contact Information** (`pilot_coordinator_contact`, `note`, optional): The contact information for the pilot coordinator, which is `ai-pilot@university.edu`.
