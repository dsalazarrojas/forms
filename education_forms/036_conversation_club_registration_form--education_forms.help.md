# Conversation Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants who want to join the Conversation Club. It will help the club organizers understand your language skills, preferences, and availability to match you with suitable conversation partners.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your legal name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2.  If you have a date of birth, please provide it to help with age grouping.
3.  Select your nationality and native language from the options.
4.  Choose the language you want to practice and select your current proficiency level.
5.  Answer whether you have any language certifications and provide details if applicable.
6.  Select your preferred session time (morning, afternoon, evening, weekend, or flexible).
7.  Choose your preferred meeting format (in person, online, or hybrid).
8.  Select the group size you prefer (small, medium, or large).
9.  Choose the topics you are interested in (select all that apply).
10.  Provide details about your language learning goals.
11.  If you have previous language exchange experience, select the relevant option.
12.  If you have been studying the language for a certain period, select the corresponding option.
13.  Select all the days you are available to meet.
14.  Choose how often you want to meet (once a week, twice a week, three times a week, once a month, or flexible).
15.  Select when you would like to start the conversation club (immediately, within 1 week, within 2 weeks, or next month).
16.  If you can teach your native language, select the corresponding option.
17.  Provide any special interests or hobbies that might help with matching with conversation partners.
18.  If you have any accessibility needs, mention them in the text field.
19.  Answer how you heard about the conversation club (website, social media, friend, school or university, advertisement, or other).
20.  If applicable, provide additional comments or information about yourself.
21.  Finally, confirm that you agree to follow club guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your legal name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth for age grouping.
* **Nationality**: Select your country of origin.
* **Native Language**: Choose the language you speak as a native speaker.
* **Target Language**: Select the language you want to practice.
* **Current Proficiency Level**: Select your current level in the target language (Beginner, Elementary, Intermediate, Upper Intermediate, Advanced, or Native).
* **Language Certification**: Answer whether you have any language certifications.
* **Certification Details**: Provide details about any language certifications.
* **Preferred Session Time**: Select when you prefer to meet (morning, afternoon, evening, weekend, or flexible).
* **Preferred Meeting Format**: Choose how you prefer to meet (in person, online, or hybrid).
* **Group Size Preference**: Select the size of group you prefer (small, medium, or large).
* **Topics You Are Interested In**: Select all the topics you are interested in (Daily Life, Travel, Culture, Business, Technology, Arts and Literature, Current Events, Hobbies, Food and Cooking, Sports).
* **Your Language Learning Goals**: Describe what you want to achieve in the language you are learning.
* **Previous Language Exchange Experience**: Select your previous experience with language exchange.
* **How Long Have You Been Studying This Language**: Select how long you have been studying the language (Less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, over 5 years).
* **Available Days**: Select all the days you are available to meet.
* **How Often Do You Want to Meet**: Select how often you want to meet (once a week, twice a week, three times a week, once a month, flexible).
* **When Would You Like to Start**: Select when you want to start (immediately, within 1 week, within 2 weeks, next month).
* **Can You Teach Your Native Language**: Select whether you can teach your native language.
* **Special Interests or Hobbies**: Provide any special interests or hobbies that might help with matching with conversation partners.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Mention any accessibility needs you have.
* **How Did You Hear About the Conversation Club**: Select how you heard about the conversation club (website, social media, friend, school or university, advertisement, other).
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about yourself.
* **I Agree to Follow Club Guidelines**: Confirm that you agree to follow club guidelines.
* **Registration Date**: This is automatically filled in by the system and should not be filled by the user.
