**Student Leadership Application Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Student Leadership Application Form is a tool used to assess a student's leadership skills, experience, and goals. It is designed to help students who are interested in taking on a leadership role within their school or community. The form asks for personal and contact information, leadership skills, experience, and goals, as well as an agreement to be a leader. The goal of this form is to help students showcase their leadership qualities and to identify potential leaders within the school or community.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your personal details such as your name, school name, and contact information.
2.  In the Expectation and Agreement Section, indicate your understanding of your role as a leader and your commitment to being a responsible leader.
3.  Describe your leadership skills, previous leadership experiences, and leadership goals.
4.  Answer questions about your education, previous leadership roles, and leadership training.
5.  In the Leadership Quote and Leadership Photo sections, share a quote that inspires you about leadership and upload a photo that represents your leadership qualities.
6.  Fill in your education and leadership position (if applicable) and leadership position (if applicable).
7.  Finally, submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Student School** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your school or college.
*   **Contact Information** (Text, Optional): Enter your email address or phone number so that we can contact you.
*   **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Expectation and Agreement Section** (Text, Optional): In a few sentences, indicate your understanding of your role as a leader and your commitment to being a responsible leader.
*   **Student Background** (Text, Optional): Describe a little about yourself and what makes you a good fit for this leadership position.
*   **Expectation Section** (Text, Optional): In a few sentences, explain what you can expect from the leadership position and what you are willing to do.
*   **Agree** (Select One, Optional): Choose "I agree" if you understand and accept your role as a leader and the responsibilities that come with it.
*   **What kind of leadership skills do you have?** (Text, Optional): List your leadership skills, such as communication, decision-making, and problem-solving.
*   **List your previous leadership experiences** (Text, Optional): Describe your previous leadership experiences, including roles and accomplishments.
*   **What kind of leadership goals do you have?** (Text, Optional): Share your leadership goals and objectives.
*   **How committed are you to being a student leader?** (Text, Optional): Rate your commitment level, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
*   **Why do you want to be a student leader?** (Text, Optional): Explain why you want to be a student leader and what motivates you.
*   **What is your level of education?** (Select One, Optional): Choose from High School, College, or University.
*   **Have you ever been in any student leadership role?** (Select One, Optional): Choose "True" if you have been in any student leadership role, "False" otherwise.
*   **Have you received any leadership training?** (Select One, Optional): Choose "True" if you have received leadership training, "False" otherwise.
*   **Previous Leadership Experience** (Text, Optional): Describe your previous leadership experiences.
*   **Leadership Skills** (Text, Optional): List your leadership skills, such as communication, decision-making, and problem-solving.
*   **Why Leadership?** (Text, Optional): Explain why leadership is important to you and what motivates you.
*   **Leadership Quote** (Text, Optional): Share a quote that inspires you about leadership.
*   **Leadership Photo** (Text, Optional): Upload a photo that represents your leadership qualities.
*   **Education** (Text, Optional): Share your level of education.
*   **Leadership Position** (Text, Optional): Share your leadership position, if applicable.
*   **Leadership Education** (Text, Optional): Share your level of education.
*   **Submit** (Text, Optional): Click on "Submit" to submit your application.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
*   Be honest and clear in your responses.
*   Proofread your application before submitting it.
