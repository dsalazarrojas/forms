# student_of_the_year_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for recognizing students who have achieved outstanding academic performance or have made significant contributions to their field. It is used to nominate students for awards or recognition programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's personal details, including their first name, last name, email, and student ID.
2. Select the student's academic program.
3. Enter the student's GPA.
4. If applicable, select the award recipients (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd) or provide additional information in the "Other Award Recipients" field.
5. Select the year of the award.
6. Enter the details of the nomination form submitter.
7. Enter the name of the nominator.
8. Enter the department and university information (if applicable).
9. Select the year of graduation (if applicable).
10. Enter the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's academic program (e.g., Arts, Engineering, Science, or Other).
* **GPA** (`gpa`, `number`, required): Enter the student's GPA.
* **Award Recipients** (`award_recipients`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the award recipients (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd) or provide additional information in the "Other Award Recipients" field.
* **Other Award Recipients** (`award_recipients_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any other award recipients not listed in the "Award Recipients" field.
* **Year Of Award** (`year_of_award`, `select_one`, optional): Select the year of the award.
* **Nomination Form Submitter** (`nomination_form_submitter`, `text`, required): Enter the details of the nomination form submitter.
* **Nominator** (`nominator`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the nominator.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department information (if applicable).
* **University** (`university`, `text`, required): Enter the university name.
* **Year Of Graduation** (`year_of_graduation`, `select_one`, optional): Select the year of graduation.
* **Date Of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of submission.

Note: The fields marked as "required" cannot be left blank.
