# Library Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Feedback Form is designed to allow patrons to provide feedback, suggestions, or complaints about your library's service. This form helps us gather information on areas of improvement and areas of success in our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To get started, simply select the "Library Feedback Form" from the relevant library page.
2.  Review the form carefully and fill out each field as instructed.
3.  Provide clear and concise feedback, suggestions, or complaints in the designated fields.
4.  Once you have completed the form, submit it for our review.

*   **Name** (`2`, `name`, required: false): Please fill in your name as you would like it to appear in our records.
*   **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted for further information or clarification, please provide your email address.

## Tips

*   Please be specific and clear in your feedback, suggestions, or complaints.
*   Provide your contact information so we can follow up with you if needed.
*   If you'd like to remain anonymous, please respect your wishes and do not fill in any contact information.
*   If you're unsure about any field or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to help.
