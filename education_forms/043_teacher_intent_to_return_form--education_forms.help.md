# Teacher Intent To Return Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from teachers regarding their intent to return to work after a leave of absence. This data is used for planning and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all the required fields.
2.  Fill out the optional fields as needed.
3.  Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Teacher Name** (text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **School Year** (number, optional): Enter the year you will be returning to school or the year you are planning to return to school. If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Grade Level** (select one, optional): Select the grade level you will be teaching when you return to school.
*   **Subject Area** (text, optional): Enter the subject area you will be teaching when you return to school.
*   **Return Plans** (text, optional): Describe your plans for returning to school, including any specific dates or details about your return.
*   **Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your return plans.
*   **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for phone contact.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Be as specific as possible when answering the optional fields.
*   Review and submit the form once complete.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to your school administration for support.
