# Academic Reflection Podcast Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the academic reflection podcast, a platform where students can express their thoughts and opinions on various academic topics. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your consent to participate in the podcast, allowing us to record and distribute your voice and thoughts on the chosen topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Choose your consent options for the podcast participation, recording, distribution, publication, and other related aspects.
3. Provide your electronic signature at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Required): Please fill out your full name as you want it to be displayed on the podcast.
* **Email Address** (Required): Provide your contact email for any future communication regarding the podcast.
* **Institution** (Required): Please specify your university or organization affiliation.
* **Podcast Title** (Required): Fill out the title of the podcast episode you are participating in.
* **Podcast Topic** (Required): Describe the main topic or theme of the podcast episode.
* **Recording Date** (Required): Choose the date when the podcast recording will take place.
* **Consent to Participate** (Required): Choose one of the options to indicate your consent to participate in the podcast.
	+ "Yes, I consent": You agree to participate in the podcast.
	+ "Yes, with conditions": You agree to participate but have conditions.
	+ "No, I do not consent": You do not wish to participate.
* **Consent to Recording** (Required): Choose one of the options for recording your participation in the podcast.
	+ "Yes, I consent": You agree to be recorded.
	+ "Yes, audio only": You agree to be recorded but with only audio content.
	+ "Yes, with restrictions": You agree to be recorded but with specific restrictions.
	+ "No, I do not consent": You do not wish to be recorded.
* **Consent to Distribution** (Required): Choose one of the options for distributing your podcast participation.
	+ "Yes, for educational purposes": You agree to the podcast being distributed for educational purposes.
	+ "Yes, with restrictions": You agree to the podcast being distributed but with specific restrictions.
	+ "No, only for course participants": You only agree to the podcast being distributed to course participants.
	+ "No, I do not consent": You do not wish for the podcast to be distributed.
* **Consent to Publication** (Required): Choose one of the options for publishing your podcast participation.
	+ "Yes, I consent": You agree to the podcast being published online.
	+ "Yes, with editorial review": You agree to the podcast being published but with an editorial review.
	+ "No, not for publication": You do not wish for the podcast to be published.
	+ "Conditional on anonymity": You agree to publication but on the condition of anonymity.
* **Purpose of Podcast** (Required): Describe the intended purpose of the podcast episode.
* **Access Restriction Preferences** (Required): Choose one of the options for accessing the podcast.
	+ "Open to public": The podcast will be open to the public.
	+ "Restricted to institution": The podcast will be restricted to your institution.
	+ "Limited to course participants": The podcast will be limited to course participants.
	+ "Private to session attendees": The podcast will be private to session attendees.
	+ "Other restrictions": You have other preferences for accessing the podcast.
* **Recording Retention Period** (Required): Choose one of the options for retaining the podcast recording.
	+ "One semester": The recording will be retained for one semester.
	+ "One academic year": The recording will be retained for one academic year.
	+ "Three years": The recording will be retained for three years.
	+ "Permanent": The recording will be retained permanently.
	+ "Until I request removal": The recording will be retained until you request its removal.
* **Special Requests** (Optional): If you have any specific requests regarding the podcast recording, please describe them here.
* **Withdrawal Rights Acknowledged** (Required): Choose one of the options to acknowledge your understanding of withdrawal rights.
	+ "Yes, I understand": You understand that you can withdraw your consent at any time.
	+ "No, need clarification": You do not understand or need clarification on withdrawal rights.
* **Participant Signature** (Required): Type your electronic signature as a confirmation of your consent.

## Tips
* Make sure to review all the fields carefully before submitting your form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure we can create a valuable and respectful experience for everyone.
