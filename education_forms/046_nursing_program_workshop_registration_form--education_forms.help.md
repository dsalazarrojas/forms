# nursing_program_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a nursing program workshop. The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants who wish to attend the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and double-check your information before submitting.
2. Enter the number of applicants in the `Applicant Count` field.
3. Enter your name in the `Applicant Name` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
6. Select a workshop day from the options provided in the `Workshop Day` field.
7. If you have any additional information or comments, type them in the `Additional Information` field.
8. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the `Submit` button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Count** (`<number>`, required/optional): The number of applicants.
* **Applicant Name** (`<text>`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`<email>`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`<text>`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Day** (`<select_one>`, required/optional): Select a workshop day from the options provided: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Sunday.
* **Workshop Time** (`<time>`, required/optional): Please note that this field is not currently used in the form, but if you select a workshop time, it would typically be in the format 08:00 AM to 03:00 PM.
* **Additional Information** (`<note>`, required/optional): If you have any additional information or comments, type them here.
* **Submit** (`<text>`, required/optional): Once you have filled out all the required fields, click this button to complete the form.
