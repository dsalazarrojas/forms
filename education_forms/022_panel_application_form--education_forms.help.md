# Panel Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Panel Application Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in joining a panel. The form gathers general user details, contact information, and specific membership preferences. Please ensure you have reviewed the form carefully and are eligible to apply before submitting your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Panel Application Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields have been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your full name, username, or any other identifying information that you'd like to associate with your panel application.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your contact details, such as your email address and phone number, to help us get in touch with you about your application.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, Select, Required/Optional): Choose the type of membership you are interested in applying for (Free, Premium, or Business).
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, Date, Required/Optional): The date you are submitting your application. Please use a format that is easy to read (e.g., month/day/year).
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, Time, Required/Optional): The time you are submitting your application.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Required/Optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to get in touch with you via phone.
 

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields have been completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide.
* We will contact you via email or phone with any queries about your application.
