# Sea Level Changes Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sea Level Changes Quiz is a form designed to gather information about sea level changes and their impacts on communities. By filling out this form, you will contribute to a larger research project aimed at understanding people's perceptions and experiences regarding sea level changes.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill in the provided answers based on your understanding and experiences with sea level changes.
*   You can fill out the form in any order, and you don't need to complete it in one session.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Sea Level Info** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide any relevant information you have about sea level changes in your community. Keep it as concise as possible.
*   **What is sea level rise?** (`sea_level_rise`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the provided options that best describes what you understand sea level rise to be.
    + Option 1: Sea level rise is a gradual increase in the sea level due to global warming.
    + Option 2: Sea level rise is a sudden increase in the sea level due to a natural disaster.
    + Option 3: Sea level rise is not something I've thought about.
    + Option 4: Sea level rise is not a concern in my area.
    + Option 5: Other (please specify in the note field)
*   **How do you typically measure sea level?** (`measurement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the methods you use to measure sea level changes in your community.
    + Option 1: Tide stick
    + Option 2: Satellite imagery
    + Option 3: Sea level gauge
    + Option 4: Other (please specify in the note field)
*   **What are the potential impacts of sea level rise on your community?** (`impacts`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the potential impacts you think sea level rise may have on your community.
    + Option 1: Erosion
    + Option 2: Flooding
    + Option 3: Saltwater infiltration
    + Option 4: Increased salinity
    + Option 5: Increased sedimentation
    + Option 6: Loss of biodiversity
    + Option 7: Loss of habitat
    + Option 8: Displacement
    + Option 9: Other (please specify in the note field)
*   **What do you think can be done to mitigate sea level rise?** (`sea_level_mitigation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the methods you think can be used to mitigate sea level rise in your community.
    + Option 1: Educate
    + Option 2: Build flood defenses
    + Option 3: Adapt to new conditions
    + Option 4: Move to higher ground
    + Option 5: Sea level gates
    + Option 6: Restore wetlands
    + Option 7: Other (please specify in the note field)
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, you can write them here.

## Tips

*   Be as thorough and honest as possible when filling out the form.
*   If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for help.
*   Remember, this form is for research purposes only, and your responses will contribute to a better understanding of sea level changes.
