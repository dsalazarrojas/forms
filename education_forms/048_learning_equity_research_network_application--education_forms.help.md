# Learning Equitry Research Network Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from new applicants to the Learning Equitry Research Network. This information will be used to facilitate the application process and ensure that you are correctly registered as a new member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Read each section carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
2. For fields with multiple options, select only one option for that field.
3. For fields that ask for your contact email, please ensure that it is up-to-date and accurate.
4. For fields that ask for your contact phone number, please enter your valid phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member application** (`member_application`, note, required/optional): This section is where you provide a brief introduction to your application.
* **Background information** (`review_background`, text, required/optional): Please provide details about your background, this will help us to better understand your qualifications.
* **Research interests** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the research interests that apply to you from the options provided.
* **Network participation** (`network_participation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to participate in the network from the options provided.
* **University participation** (`university_participation`, number, required/optional): This field is currently not used, please do not fill this out.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your up-to-date contact email address.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your valid contact phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields accurately.
* Review your application carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact the support team.
