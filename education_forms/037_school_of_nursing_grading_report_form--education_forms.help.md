# School of Nursing Grading Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School of Nursing Grading Report Form is used to record the grades and comments for student assignments and evaluations in the School of Nursing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the course you are grading for.
2. Enter the student's ID number.
3. Select the grading period (e.g., Quarter 1, Quarter 2, etc.).
4. Enter the name of the assignment you are grading.
5. Choose the grade for the assignment from the provided options (e.g., 0-49, 50-59, etc.).
6. Provide any additional comments about the student's performance.
7. Enter your name and title for signature purposes.
8. Enter any additional comments or feedback for the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are grading for.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Grading Period** (`grading_period`, select_one, optional): Select the grading period for the assignment (e.g., Quarter 1, Quarter 2, etc.).
* **Assignment Name** (`assignment_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assignment being graded.
* **Grade** (`grade`, select_one, optional): Choose the grade for the assignment from the provided options (e.g., 0-49, 50-59, etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the student's performance.
* **Signature** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Enter your name and title for signature purposes.
* **Grading Comments** (`grading_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback for the student.
