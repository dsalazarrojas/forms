# Honor Society Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Honor Society Recommendation Form is designed to collect information from recommenders about students who have excelled academically and demonstrated excellence in their studies. This form helps to evaluate a student's eligibility for an honor society membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name and email address in the "student_name" and "student_email" fields respectively.
2. Enter the name of the recommender in the "recommender_name" field.
3. Provide the name of the institution where the student is studying in the "institution_name" field.
4. Select the student's current grade level from the "student_grade_level" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the type of institution where the student is studying from the "institution_type" dropdown menu.
6. If necessary, add any additional comments about the student in the "comments" field.
7. Indicate whether you have submitted previous forms for this student in the "submitted_by" field.
8. Finally, submit the form when you have completed the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student you are recommending.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Recommender Name** (`recommender_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the recommender.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the institution where the student is studying.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required: false): Select the student's current grade level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, Master's, Doctoral).
* **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of institution where the student is studying (University, High School, Mentor, Other).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments about the student (optional).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you have submitted previous forms for this student (Yes, No).
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required: false): Date when the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all the necessary fields.
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and honest.
* Do not submit duplicate forms for the same student.
* Submit the form when you have completed all the required fields.
