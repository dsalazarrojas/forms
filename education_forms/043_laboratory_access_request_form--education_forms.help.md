# Laboratory Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Access Request Form is used to request access to a laboratory facility. This form is used to gather information about your access request, including the date and time you want to access the laboratory, the type of access you need, the staff assigned to you, and your confirmation of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your information as a requestor in the "Requestor Information" field.
2.  Provide the laboratory facility information in the "Laboratory Facility Information" field.
3.  Select the date you want to access the laboratory in the "Lab Access Date" field.
4.  Choose the time you want to access the laboratory in the "Lab Access Time" field.
5.  Specify the duration of your access in the "Lab Access Duration" field.
6.  Select the type of access you need in the "Lab Access Type" field.
7.  Provide any comments about your access request in the "Lab Access Comment" field (optional).
8.  Choose the staff assigned to you in the "Assigned Staff" field.
9.  Confirm your request in the "Request Confirmation" field.
10.  Provide any comments about your request in the "Request Comments" field (optional).
11.  Enter your name as the person submitting the request in the "Submitted By" field.
12.  Date and time of submission in the "Submitted At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Information** (`requestor_info`, text, required): Enter your name, title or position, and contact information.
* **Laboratory Facility Information** (`lab_facility_info`, text, required): Enter the name of the laboratory facility you are requesting access to.
* **Lab Access Date** (`lab_access_date`, date, required): Select the date you want to access the laboratory.
* **Lab Access Time** (`lab_access_time`, time, required): Choose the time you want to access the laboratory.
* **Lab Access Duration** (`lab_access_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your access in hours or minutes.
* **Lab Access Type** (`lab_access_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of access you need (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Lab Access Comment** (`lab_access_comment`, note, optional): Provide any comments about your access request.
* **Assigned Staff** (`assigned_staff`, select_multiple, required): Choose the staff members who will be assigned to you for your access request.
* **Request Confirmation** (`request_confirmation`, select_multiple, required): Confirm your request by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Request Comments** (`request_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments about your request.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, required): Date and time of submission.
