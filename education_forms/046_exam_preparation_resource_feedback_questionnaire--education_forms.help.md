# exam_preparation_resource_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from students on the resources and materials provided for an exam preparation course. Your input will help improve the course resources and provide a more effective learning experience for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you are a student of the institution before submitting feedback.
- Select the correct role for your position at the educational institution in the "What is your role at the educational institution?" field.
- Choose all relevant study materials from the list in the "What study materials have you used for this course?" field.
- For the "How useful were the resources provided?" field, enter a number between 0 and 5, where 0 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
- For the "On a scale of 0-5, how satisfied are you with the resources provided?" field, enter a number between 0 and 5.
- Enter any additional feedback you may have for the educators in the "Do you have any additional feedback for the educators?" field.
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you would like to be contacted for further questions or discussions.
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you would like to be contacted via phone.
- Enter the date you submitted the form in the "Date" field.
- Enter the time you submitted the form in the "Time" field.
- Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional comments or suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your role at the educational institution?** (`role 1`, `role 2`, `role 3`, required: false): Select the correct role that you hold at the educational institution from the list.
  - This field helps us understand your perspective and position within the institution.
- **What study materials have you used for this course?** (`study material 1`, `study material 2`, `study material 3`, required: false): Select all relevant study materials from the list.
  - This field helps us understand what resources you used for the course.
- **How useful were the resources provided?** (`number`, required: false): On a scale of 0-5, rate how useful you found the resources provided.
  - This field helps us understand your satisfaction with the resources.
- **On a scale of 0-5, how satisfied are you with the resources provided?** (`number`, required: false): On a scale of 0-5, rate your satisfaction with the resources provided.
  - This field helps us understand your satisfaction with the resources.
- **Do you have any additional feedback for the educators?** (`note`, required: false): Enter any additional feedback or suggestions you may have for the educators.
  - This field is open for you to provide any comments or suggestions.
- **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further questions or discussions.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us reach out to you if needed.
- **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us reach out to you if needed.
- **Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted the form.
  - This field helps us track when you submitted the form.
- **Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time you submitted the form.
  - This field helps us track when you submitted the form.
- **Additional comments or suggestions** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
  - This field is open for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
