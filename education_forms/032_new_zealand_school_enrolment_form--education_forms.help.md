# New Zealand School Enrolment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Zealand School Enrolment Form is a document used for enrolling students in a school in New Zealand. It requires information about the student and their guardian/parent to complete the student's record and facilitate their enrolment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Start by entering the student's name in the 'Student Name' field.
2. Enter the school's name in the 'School Name' field.
3. Enter the student's date of birth in the 'Date of Birth' field.
4. Enter the student's address in the 'Student Address' field.
5. Select the student's gender from the options provided in the 'Gender' field.
6. Enter the name of the student's parent/guardian in the 'Parent/Guardian Name' field.
7. Enter the parent/guardian's phone number in the 'Parent/Guardian Phone' field.
8. Enter the student's email address in the 'Email' field.
9. Select the student's year level from the options provided in the 'Year Level' field.
10. Enter the school's address in the 'School Address' field.
11. Enter the school's phone number in the 'School Phone' field.
12. Select the student's nationality from the options provided in the 'Nationality' field.
13. Select the student's native language from the options provided in the 'Native Language' field.
14. Enter the name of the student's guardian/parent in the 'Guardian/Parent Name' field.
15. Enter the guardian/parent's phone number in the 'Guardian/Parent Phone' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the student's name.
* **School Name (school_name)**: Enter the school's name.
* **Date of Birth (student_date_of_birth)**: Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Address (student_address)**: Enter the student's home address.
* **Gender (student_gender)**: Select the student's gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Parent/Guardian Name (parent_name)**: Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone (parent_phone)**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number. (Required)
* **Email (student_email)**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Year Level (school_year_level)**: Select the student's year level (Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary).
* **School Address (school_address)**: Enter the school's address.
* **School Phone (school_phone)**: Enter the school's phone number. (Required)
* **Nationality (student_nationality)**: Select the student's nationality (Australian, New Zealand, British, or Other).
* **Native Language (student_language)**: Select the student's native language (English, Maori, or Other languages).
* **Guardian/Parent Name (guardian_name)**: Enter the name of the student's guardian or parent.
* **Guardian/Parent Phone (guardian_phone)**: Enter the guardian/parent's phone number. (Optional)

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form) are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* If unsure about any field, consult with the school staff or administration.
