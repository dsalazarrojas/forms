# School Employee Attendance - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Employee Attendance form is a tool used to capture attendance information for school employees, including their work shifts, positions, and locations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee information, including your name, school ID, and employee ID.
2. Select your work shift (morning, afternoon, or evening).
3. Enter your position and department.
4. Specify your location.
5. Enter your employee ID number and email.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your supervisor's ID, name, email, and phone number.
8. Fill out any additional notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Employee Information** (`school_employee_attendance_form_1`, text, required/optional): This is the main section for your employee information. It includes fields for your first name, last name, school ID, employee ID, and other relevant details.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **School ID** (`school_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your school ID number.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
- **Time In** (`time_in`, time, required/optional): Enter your time of arrival at work.
- **Time Out** (`time_out`, time, required/optional): Enter your time of departure from work.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.
- **Position** (`position`, text, required/optional): Enter your position within the school.
- **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter your department.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter your location within the school.
- **Shift** (`shift`, select_one, required/optional): Select your work shift (morning, afternoon, or evening).
- **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
- **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your work email address.
- **Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.
- **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's ID number.
- **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
- **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
- **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
- **QR Code** (`qr_code`, note, required/optional): This is a special note field for any QR code-related information.
- **QR Code URL** (`qr_code_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL associated with the QR code.
- **Notes** (`notes`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional notes relevant to your attendance.
- **QR Code Notes** (`qr_code_notes`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional QR code-related notes.
- **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional notes relevant to your attendance.
- **Notes 3** (`notes_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional notes relevant to your attendance.
