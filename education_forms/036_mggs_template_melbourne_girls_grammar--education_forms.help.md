# MGGS Template Melbourne Girls Grammar - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a template designed to collect contact information and other details for students attending Melbourne Girls' Grammar (MGGS) school.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*), and the optional fields as needed.
2. For the "Year Level" field, select the correct year level of the student from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the student's "Student Name" and "School Name" in the respective text fields.
4. Enter the student's "Email" address.
5. Enter the student's "Contact Number", if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name (text, required):** Enter the name of the school where the student attends.
* **Year Level (select_one, optional):** Select the year level of the student.
* **Student Name (text, optional):** Enter the name of the student.
* **Email (email, optional):** Enter the student's email address.
* **Contact Number (text, optional):** Enter the student's contact number, if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the school's administration or contact person for clarification.
* This form is a template, so you can adapt it for different purposes or events within the school.
