# critical_thinking_curriculum_project_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your school's or institution's project application for the Critical Thinking Curriculum Project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
* Be accurate and clear in your responses to ensure the form is properly submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and title.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program you are applying for.
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the program.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the program will be conducted.
* **Dates Start End** (`dates_start_end`, date, optional): Enter the start and end dates of the program.
* **Time Of Year** (`time_of_year`, time, optional): Enter the time of year when the program will take place.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, number, optional): Enter the budget range for the program.
* **Curriculum Area** (`curriculum_area`, select_one, optional):
	+ Critical Thinking
	+ Other: Select one of the two options or type in your own.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Other: Select one of the three options or type in your own.
* **Program Status** (`program_status`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Active
	+ On Hold
	+ Inactive
	+ Other: Select one or more of the four options or type in your own.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the project.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If a field is optional, you can leave it blank if it does not apply to your project.
