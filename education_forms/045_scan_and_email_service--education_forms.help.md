# Scan And Email Service - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Scan And Email Service" form is used to submit requests for scanning specific books, allowing users to receive scanned copies by email.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Please fill in your name as the student requesting the scan.
*   Enter your email address for the scanned book to be sent to.
*   Type or select the title of the book you would like to be scanned.
*   Enter the start and end page numbers of the scan.
*   Specify the number of chapters you would like to be scanned.
*   Choose the format you prefer for the scanned book (e.g., PDF, JPEG, PNG, DOCX, or TXT).
*   Indicate whether you would like to receive email notifications for each scan.
*   Provide any additional comments or requests about the scan.
*   Finally, confirm your scan request by selecting 'Yes' and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the student making the request.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where the scanned book will be sent.
*   **Book Title** (`book_title`, `text`, optional): Type or select the title of the book you would like to be scanned.
*   **Start Page** (`start_page`, `number`, optional): Enter the starting page number of the scan.
*   **End Page** (`end_page`, `number`, optional): Enter the ending page number of the scan.
*   **Chapters** (`chapters`, `number`, optional): Specify the number of chapters you would like to be scanned.
*   **Pages Requested** (`pages_requested`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of pages you would like to be scanned.
*   **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred format for the scanned book (e.g., PDF, JPEG, PNG, DOCX, or TXT).
*   **Email Notification** (`email_notification`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you would like to receive email notifications for each scan.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests regarding the scan.
*   **Confirm** (`confirm`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your scan request by selecting 'Yes'.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, optional): Submit your completed scan request.
