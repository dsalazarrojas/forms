# Preceptor Commitment Statement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Preceptor Commitment Statement" is a document that captures the commitment of a preceptor to mentor a student in a clinical rotation. It ensures that the preceptor understands their role, responsibilities, and expectations in guiding the student throughout the rotation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the preceptor_name field.
2. Enter the program name where you will be mentoring the student.
3. Select your start date for the rotation.
4. Select your end date for the rotation.
5. Choose how you plan to acknowledge your commitment.
6. Enter your name as the preceptor.
7. Choose how you plan to sign your acknowledgment.
8. Enter your title as the reviewer.
9. Choose how you plan to sign your acknowledgment.
10. Enter your name as the reviewer.
11. Enter the date you sign your acknowledgment.
12. Sign your acknowledgment as the preceptor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This field is a header section of the form where you can enter any relevant information.
* **Preceptor Name**: This field is for you, the preceptor, to enter your name.
* **Program Name**: This field is for you to enter the name of the program where you will be mentoring the student.
* **Start Date**: This field is for you to enter the start date of the rotation.
* **End Date**: This field is for you to enter the end date of the rotation.
* **Signature**: This field is for you to choose how you plan to acknowledge your commitment.
* **Preceptor Signature**: This field is for you to enter your signature as the preceptor.
* **Reviewer Name**: This field is for you, the reviewer, to enter your name.
* **Reviewer Title**: This field is for you to enter your title as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Signature**: This field is for you to choose how you plan to sign your acknowledgment.
* **Reviewer Signature Date**: This field is for you to enter the date you sign your acknowledgment.
* **Reviewer Signature**: This field is for you to enter your signature as the reviewer.
* **Preceptor Acknowledgment**: This field is for you to enter a statement acknowledging your commitment as the preceptor.
