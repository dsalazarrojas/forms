# research_skills_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess research skills in various fields such as data collection, analysis, and interpretation. It is designed to evaluate a student's or researcher's competence in these areas in an educational or academic setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. For each section, provide a clear and concise answer to the questions.
3. Make sure to answer all the questions in each section before proceeding to the next.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of your research project or topic. This should include the main objectives, goals, and scope of your research.
* **Data Collection**: Describe your methods for collecting data, including sources and techniques used.
* **Data Analysis**: Explain how you analyzed the data collected, including any statistical methods or software used.
* **Data Interpretation**: Interpret your findings and explain how they relate to the research questions or objectives.
* **Knowledge Application**: Explain how you applied your knowledge and skills to the research project.
* **Feedback and Evaluation**: Provide feedback on what you learned from the project, including any challenges and successes.
* **Final Comments**: Add any final thoughts or comments about the project.
