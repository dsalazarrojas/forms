# Campus Living Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campus Living Application Form is designed to collect information about your preferences for campus housing, roommates, and emergency contacts. Completing this form helps housing staff match you with suitable roommates and accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information.
2.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  For housing preferences, choose the options that best suit your needs.
4.  If you have special needs or requests, provide detailed information in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
*   **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your university email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Major or Field of Study**: Enter your major or field of study.
*   **Academic Year**: Select your academic year (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, or Other).
*   **Housing Type Preference**: Select the type of housing you prefer (Dormitory, Residence Hall, Apartment, Shared Housing, or Other).
*   **Room Type Preference**: Select the type of room you prefer (Single Room, Double Room, Triple Room, Suite, or No Preference).
*   **Floor Preference**: Select the floor you prefer (Ground Floor, Middle Floor, Top Floor, or No Preference).
*   **Special Housing Accommodations Needed**: If you have special needs or requests, provide detailed information in this field.
*   **Do you want a specific roommate?**: Select whether you want a specific roommate (True, False, or Open to assignment).
*   **Requested Roommate Name**: If you selected "True" above, enter the name of your requested roommate.
*   **Requested Roommate Student ID**: If you selected "True" above, enter the student ID of your requested roommate.
*   **Sleep Schedule**: Select your sleep schedule (Early Bird, Night Owl, Variable, or No Preference).
*   **Smoking Preference**: Select your smoking preference (Non-smoker, Smoker, or No Preference).
*   **Cleanliness Standards**: Select your cleanliness standards (Very Clean, Clean, Moderate, Relaxed, or No Preference).
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
