# english_medium_student_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The english_medium_student_registration_form is used to collect information about English medium students for registration and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Student Details** page.
	* Provide your name, student ID, and any other relevant details.
2. Move on to the **Contact Information** page.
	* Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
3. Go to the **English Medium Details** page.
	* Provide details about your English medium, such as your language proficiency level.
4. Move on to the **Student Programme** page.
	* Select the programme you are registered for from the available options.
5. Go to the **Student Status** page.
	* Select your current student status from the available options.
6. Move on to the **Date of Birth** page.
	* Enter your date of birth.
7. Go to the **Medium of Instruction** page.
	* Select the languages you are proficient in from the available options.
8. Move on to the **Student E-mail** page.
	* Enter your student email address.
9. Finally, go to the **Student Phone** page.
	* Enter your student phone number.
