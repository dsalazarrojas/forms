# Online MCQ Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your online behavior, learning habits, and technology usage. The questions will help us understand your demographics, education level, and interaction preferences to provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Select an option for each question by clicking on the corresponding option.
* Make sure to answer all questions, unless indicated as optional.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
