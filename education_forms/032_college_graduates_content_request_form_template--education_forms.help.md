# College Graduates Content Request Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The College Graduates Content Request Form Template is used to collect information from graduates for inclusion in the yearbook and other college publications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consent to use your information in college publications.
3. Submit the form by the specified deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`student_full_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter your full name as it should appear in the yearbook.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, text, required): This field is used to enter your student ID number.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, required): This field is used to enter the year you graduated.
* **Graduation Semester** (`graduation_semester`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the semester you graduated.
* **Degree Conferred** (`degree_conferred`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the degree you earned.
* **Major or Field of Study** (`major_field`, text, required): This field is used to enter your major or field of study.
* **Honors or Cum Laude Status** (`cum_laude_status`, select_one, required): This field is used to select your honors or Cum Laude status.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, email, required): This field is used to enter your email address for contact and correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, text, required): This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your home address.
* **City, State and Zip Code** (`city_state_zip`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your city, state, and zip code.
* **Hometown** (`hometown`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the city and state where you are from.
* **High Quality Headshot Photo Submitted** (`photo_submission`, select_one, required): This field is used to select whether you have submitted a high-quality headshot photo.
* **Permission to Use Photo in Yearbook and Publications** (`photo_permission`, select_one, required): This field is used to grant permission for your photo to be used in the yearbook and other college publications.
* **Graduation Quote or Message** (`quote_or_message`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a meaningful or funny quote or message.
* **Key Accomplishments During College** (`accomplishments_list`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your key accomplishments during college.
* **Clubs and Organizations Involved In** (`clubs_organizations`, text, optional): This field is used to list all clubs and organizations you were involved in during college.
* **Post-Graduation Plans** (`post_graduation_plans`, text, required): This field is used to enter your post-graduation plans.
* **Career Field or Industry** (`career_field`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the career field or industry you are entering.
* **Special Interests or Hobbies** (`special_interests`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any special interests or hobbies.
* **Special Recognition or Shout-Out** (`special_recognition`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any special recognition or shout-outs.
* **Consent to Use Information in College Publications** (`media_release_consent`, select_one, required): This field is used to grant permission for your information to be used in college publications.
* **Permission to Contact for Alumni Network** (`alumni_contact_permission`, select_one, required): This field is used to grant permission for others to contact you through the alumni network.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_deadline`, date, required): This field is used to enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Quote or Message** (`quote_or_message`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a meaningful or funny quote or message. 

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Check the options for select_one fields carefully before submitting.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the yearbook staff.
- Please submit your form by the specified deadline to be featured in the yearbook and other college publications.
