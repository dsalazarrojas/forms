# University Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Enrollment Form is designed to collect contact information and demographic details from prospective university students. This data will help us understand who's interested in our institution and what they're looking for in an educational program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

- Fill in your First Name on the "First Name" field.
- Enter your Last Name on the "Last Name" field.
- Provide your Email address on the "Email" field. Please ensure it's a valid Email format (username@domain.com).
- Fill in your Phone number on the "Phone" field.
- Enter your Date of Birth on the "Date of Birth" field in a format that includes the day, month, and year (MM/DD/YYYY).
- If applicable, enter your High School Name on the "High School Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name. This will be used as a reference to identify you in our system.
  - Please ensure that you enter your actual first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
  - Please ensure that you enter your actual last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your valid email address. This will be used to contact you with updates and information about our programs.
  - Please use the standard email format (username@domain.com).
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This will be used to contact you for any updates or follow-ups.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - Please enter your actual date of birth.
- **High School Name** (`high_school_name`, `text`, optional): If you've attended high school, please enter the name of the school.
  - This is for your reference and our records.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or issues, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Our team reviews all submitted forms and responds within a certain time frame.
- Please be patient and we will get back to you as soon as possible. 

Note: This guide assumes that the form fields' labels and types will be self-explanatory or easily understandable for most users. However, if additional clarification is needed or if you have any further assistance, please let us know.
