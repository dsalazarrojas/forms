# degmnhs_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps users complete the degmnhs_enrollment_form for student enrollment into degree programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the "date_of_birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
4. Enter your **Contact Number** in the "contact_number" field.
5. Provide your **Email** in the "email" field in the format your_email@example.com.
6. Enter your **Address** in the "address" field.
7. Select the **School Name** of your institution in the "school_name" field.
8. Choose the **Program** you are applying for from the options provided.
9. Select your **Level** of education (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Ph.D., etc.) from the options provided.
10. Click the **Submitted** note to indicate that you have completed the form.
11. If applicable, select your **Student Type** from the options provided.
12. Enter the contact information of your **Parent/Guardian** in the "parent_contact" field.
13. Enter your **Parent's Name** in the "parent_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (first_name)**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: A date field to enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Email (email)**: An email field to enter your email address in the format your_email@example.com.
* **Address (address)**: A text field to enter your physical address.
* **School Name (school_name)**: A text field to enter your school name.
* **Program (program)**: A select field to choose the program you are applying for.
* **Level (level)**: A select multiple field to select your level of education (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Ph.D., etc.).
* **Submitted (submitted)**: A note field to indicate that you have completed the form.
* **Student Type (student_type)**: A select field to select your student type.
* **Parent Contact (parent_contact)**: A text field to enter your parent/guardian's contact information.
* **Parent Name (parent_name)**: A text field to enter your parent/guardian's name.
