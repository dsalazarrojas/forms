# TELPAS Writing - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on writing-related activities. It may be used by educators or researchers to collect data on writing habits and practices of students or teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each field carefully before providing input.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields and provide any relevant information for optional fields.
3. Double-check your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. writing_form_label (writing_form_label, text, required)**: Enter a brief description of your writing activity or experience.
* **2. writing_label_1 (writing_label_1, text, optional)**: Provide any additional information related to your writing activity or experience.
* **3. writing_label_2 (writing_label_2, select_multiple, optional)**: Select all options that apply to your writing activity or experience.
* **4. writing_date (writing_date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of your writing activity or experience.
* **5. writing_time (writing_time, time, optional)**: Enter the time of your writing activity or experience.
* **6. writing_email (writing_email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **7. writing_phone (writing_phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **8. writing_note (writing_note, note, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your writing activity or experience.
* **9. writing_select_1 (writing_select_1, select_one, optional)**: Select the most suitable option for your writing activity or experience.
* **10. writing_select_2 (writing_select_2, select_one, optional)**: Select the most suitable option for your writing activity or experience.
* **11. writing_integer (writing_integer, number, optional)**: Enter a number related to your writing activity or experience.
* **12. writing_decimal (writing_decimal, number, optional)**: Enter a decimal number related to your writing activity or experience.
* **13. writing_text (writing_text, text, optional)**: Enter any additional text related to your writing activity or experience.
* **14. writing_time_2 (writing_time_2, time, optional)**: Enter the time of your writing activity or experience.
* **15. writing_email_2 (writing_email_2, email, optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **16. writing_phone_2 (writing_phone_2, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **17. writing_note_2 (writing_note_2, note, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your writing activity or experience.
* **18. writing_select_3 (writing_select_3, select_multiple, optional)**: Select all options that apply to your writing activity or experience.
* **19. writing_select_4 (writing_select_4, select_one, optional)**: Select the most suitable option for your writing activity or experience.
* **20. writing_integer_2 (writing_integer_2, number, optional)**: Enter a number related to your writing activity or experience.
* **21. writing_decimal_2 (writing_decimal_2, number, optional)**: Enter a decimal number related to your writing activity or experience.
* **22. writing_text_2 (writing_text_2, text, optional)**: Enter any additional text related to your writing activity or experience.
* **23. writing_time_3 (writing_time_3, time, optional)**: Enter the time of your writing activity or experience.
* **24. writing_email_3 (writing_email_3, email, optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **25. writing_phone_3 (writing_phone_3, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please double-check your input before submitting the form.
