# Academic Peer Coaching Circle Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Peer Coaching Circle Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants interested in joining the peer coaching circle. The form is used to gather registration details, preferences, and expectations from participants to ensure a successful and effective peer coaching circle experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field, which will be used for scheduling and communication purposes.
3. If applicable, provide your affiliation (department or organization) in the "Affiliation" field.
4. Select your preferred coaching focus areas from the options provided in the "Coaching focus areas" field.
5. Indicate your availability for coaching sessions in the "Availability for sessions" field.
6. Share your prior experience with peer coaching, if any, in the "Prior peer coaching experience" field.
7. Specify your expectations from the peer coaching circle in the "Expectations" field.
8. Choose your preferred method of communication (email, phone, or messaging app) in the "Communication preference" field.
9. Add any additional notes or special requirements in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: Please provide your full name for registration purposes.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address to ensure efficient communication and scheduling.
* **Affiliation**: If you're affiliated with a department or organization, please provide this information.
* **Coaching focus areas**: Select all the areas you want to develop or improve, such as teaching practice, research mentoring, leadership skills, or career development.
* **Availability for sessions**: Indicate your preferred days or times for coaching sessions.
* **Prior peer coaching experience**: Share any relevant experience you have with peer coaching.
* **Expectations**: Specify what you hope to achieve or gain from the peer coaching circle.
* **Communication preference**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted (email, phone, or messaging app).
* **Additional notes**: Add any special requirements or comments you'd like to share with the peer coaching circle organizers.
* **Tips**: Please be specific and clear with your expectations and preferences to ensure a successful peer coaching circle experience.
