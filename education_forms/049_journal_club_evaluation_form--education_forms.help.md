# Journal Club Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Journal Club Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback on journal articles presented in a journal club setting. This feedback is used to help journal club organizers and participants evaluate the quality and impact of each paper.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field as requested.
2. Fill in the text fields (Paper Title, Paper Authors, Presentation Quality, and Recommendations) with your honest and concise feedback.
3. Choose the overall impact of the paper (Low, Medium, or High) from the dropdown options.
4. Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Journal Club Evaluation** (`journal_club_evaluation_form`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your evaluation of the journal club presentation. 
* **Paper Title** (`paper_title`, text): Enter the title of the paper presented. 
* **Paper Authors** (`paper_authors`, text): Enter the authors of the paper presented. 
* **Presentation Quality** (`presentation_quality`, text): Enter your feedback on the presentation quality of the paper. 
* **Paper Quality** (`paper_quality`, number): Enter a numerical value to rate the quality of the paper (e.g., 1-10). 
* **Overall Impact** (`overall_impact`, select_one): Choose the overall impact of the paper (Low, Medium, or High). 
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text): Enter any recommendations for future presentations or improvements. 

## Tips
* Be honest and concise in your feedback.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (none in this form).
* Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness. 

Note: This help guide is a general explanation of the form and its fields. The specific requirements and expectations may vary depending on the context and purpose of the form in your organization.
