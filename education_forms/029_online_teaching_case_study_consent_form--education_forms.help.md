# Online Teaching Case Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect consent information from participants involved in online teaching activities. It gathers contact and demographic details to help the institution or organization understand who is participating and what their roles are.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your correct information.
2. If you have multiple roles, select all that apply.
3. Make sure to provide your actual date of birth (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
4. Read the agreement carefully and check the box to indicate your acceptance of the terms.
5. Double-check your entered details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (Participant Name, Participant Email, required)**: Please fill in your name and email address accurately.
* **Roles (Teacher, Tutor, Mentor, Coach, optional)**: Select all roles that apply to your involvement in the online teaching activity.
* **Participant Name (Participant Name 2, required)**: Fill in your name again, this time for the second participant (if applicable).
* **Participant Email (Participant Email 2, required)**: Enter your email address accurately.
* **Participant Role (Participant Role 2, optional)**: Select your role in the online teaching activity (if different from the first participant).
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth, optional)**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).
* **Agreement (Agreement, required)**: Carefully read the terms and check the box to indicate your acceptance.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the form creator or the organization running the online teaching activity.
* Double-check your entered details for accuracy before submitting the form.
