# science_project_report_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The science project report upload form is designed for students, teachers, and schools to provide information about science projects. This form helps collect data for research, educational, or administrative purposes within the educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information. The fields marked as "required" are necessary for a complete submission.
2. Ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your science project or report.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Project Category** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category of your science project or report from the options: Physics, Biology, Chemistry, or Mathematics.
* **Project Description** (`4`, `note`, required): Briefly describe your science project or report, including its purpose, methodology, and results.
* **Files** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether you have files associated with your science project or report. Please note that this field is required.
* **Date Submitted** (`6`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you submitted your science project or report.
* **Time Submitted** (`7`, `time`, required): Enter the time when you submitted your science project or report.
* **School Name** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school where you completed your science project or report.
* **Teacher Name** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your teacher or supervisor.
* **Project Type** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of your science project or report from the options: Group, Individual, Team, or School-Wide.
* **Student Name** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the student who completed the project.
* **Student Email** (`12`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Student Phone** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Teacher Email** (`14`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your teacher or supervisor.
* **Teacher Phone** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your teacher or supervisor.
* **Teacher Address** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of your teacher or supervisor's school.
* **Student Address** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter your home address, if different from your school address.
* **School Address** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the address of your school.
* **School Phone** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your school.
* **School Email** (`20`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your school.
* **School Website** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter the website of your school.
* **Teacher School** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your teacher's school.
* **Notes** (`23`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your science project or report.
* **File 1** (`24`, `text`, optional): Upload any files associated with your science project or report. Please note that this field is optional.
* **File 2** (`25`, `text`, optional): Upload another file associated with your science project or report. Please note that this field is optional.
* **File 3** (`26`, `text`, optional): Upload another file associated with your science project or report. Please note that this field is optional.
* **File 5** (`27`, `text`, optional): Upload another file associated with your science project or report. Please note that this field is optional.
