# Intonation Patterns Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intonation Patterns Assessment form is used to gather information about intonation patterns in music or speech samples. Please complete this form to provide details about the observed intonation patterns in each section. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section and provide your observations about the intonation patterns in the corresponding section.
2. Use the text fields to describe your observations in detail.
3. Select the correct option for submission: `Submit` to complete the form or `Cancel` to discard your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section 01** (`section_01`, text, required: false): 
    Please describe the intonation pattern of the sample in this section. What is your observation about the rise and fall of pitch?
* **Section 02** (`section_02`, text, required: false): 
    Describe the intonation pattern of the sample in this section. Are there any notable variations or deviations from the overall pattern?
* **Section 03** (`section_03`, text, required: false): 
    Describe any other interesting intonation patterns or observations you made about the sample.
* **Section 04** (`section_04`, text, required: false): 
    Describe any additional intonation patterns or observations.
* **Section 05** (`section_05`, text, required: false): 
    Describe any intonation patterns or observations related to the rhythm or meter of the sample.
* **Section 06** (`section_06`, text, required: false): 
    Describe any other intonation patterns or observations.
* **Section 07** (`section_07`, text, required: false): 
    Summarize your overall observations about the intonation patterns in the sample.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): 
    Select `Submit` to complete the form and send your observations.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, select_one, required: false): 
    Select `Cancel` to discard your work and start again.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible in your descriptions to help us better understand the intonation patterns.
* If you're unsure about what to describe, try reading through the sample again and making more observations.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us.
