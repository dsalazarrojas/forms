# elementary_school_pta_membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students and parents to apply for membership in the Elementary School PTA. It collects essential information for the association's records and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the First Name, Last Name, and Date Reported fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter a valid Email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide a valid Phone Number for emergency contact.
4. Select the correct Student Grade level (1-5) from the provided options.
5. Enter the name of the Teacher who recommended you.
6. Get your Parent Signature and Student Signature on file.
7. Choose the type of membership you wish to apply for (Full, Single, or Family).
8. If applicable, provide Additional Comments for the membership committee's consideration.
9. Fill out the Parent Name, Student Name, School Address, Parent Address, Student Phone, Parent Phone, Emergency Contact Name, and Emergency Contact Phone fields.
10. Choose the relationship of the Emergency Contact from the provided options (Parent, Guardian, or Other, specify the relationship in the "Other Relationship" field if not listed).
11. If applicable, provide Medical Information, Allergy, Emergency Procedures, and Medical Condition details for emergency purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
  - **Date Reported**: Enter the current date (MM/DD/YYYY) for reporting this application.
  - **Email**: Enter a valid Email address that can be used for contact purposes.
  - **Phone Number**: Enter a valid Phone number for emergency contact.
  - **Student Grade**: Select your current grade level (1-5) to indicate your eligibility for membership.
  - **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the Teacher who recommended you for membership.
  - **Parent Signature**: Get your Parent's signature on file, acknowledging your application.
  - **Student Signature**: Get your Student's signature on file, acknowledging your application.
  - **Membership Type**: Choose the type of membership you wish to apply for (Full, Single, or Family).
  - **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the membership committee's consideration.
  - **Parent Name**: Enter your Parent's name as it is written on your identification.
  - **Student Name**: Enter your Student's name as it is written on their identification.
  - **School Address**: Enter the address of the school where your child is enrolled.
  - **Parent Address**: Enter your home address.
  - **Student Phone**: Enter your Student's Phone number for emergency contact.
  - **Parent Phone**: Enter your Parent's Phone number for emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
  - **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
  - **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship of the emergency contact from the provided options (Parent, Guardian, or Other, specify the relationship in the "Other Relationship" field if not listed).
  - **Other Relationship**: Enter the relationship of the emergency contact if not listed (e.g., Grandparent, Aunt/Uncle, etc.).
  - **Medical Information**: If applicable, provide any medical information that may be necessary for emergency purposes.
  - **Allergy**: If applicable, list any allergies or medical conditions that may be necessary for emergency purposes.
  - **Emergency Procedures**: If applicable, list any emergency procedures that may be necessary for emergency purposes.
  - **Medical Condition**: If applicable, list any medical conditions that may be necessary for emergency purposes.
