# Education For Sustainability Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Education For Sustainability Attitudes Survey" is a tool designed to understand the attitudes and perspectives of educators on sustainability in education. This survey is intended to collect feedback and insights that can help inform future educational initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the educator roles that apply to you.
2. Choose your attitude towards sustainability in education.
3. Identify the challenges you face in promoting sustainability in your work.
4. If you have any comments, please provide them in the text box below.
5. Review and confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educator Roles** (`educator_roles`): Select all the roles that apply to you as an educator. This will help us understand your professional context and perspective.
	+ Educator: Teachers, professors, or instructors.
	+ Administrator: School administrators or district administrators.
	+ Community of practice: Those who facilitate collaboration and knowledge-sharing within a community or organization.
	+ None: Not applicable.
* **Attitudes** (`attitudes`): Choose the attitude that best describes your stance on sustainability in education. This will help us gauge your support and enthusiasm for sustainability initiatives.
	+ Positive: You strongly support and believe in the importance of sustainability in education.
	+ Negative: You have concerns or reservations about sustainability in education.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral or unsure about sustainability in education.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`): Identify one or more challenges you face in promoting sustainability in your work. This will help us understand the obstacles you encounter.
	+ Sustainability literacy: Lack of knowledge or skills in understanding and addressing sustainability.
	+ Resource efficiency: Limited resources, such as time, money, or materials.
	+ Environmentalism: Concerns about the impact of sustainability on the environment.
	+ None: Not applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Use this text box to provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about sustainability in education.
* **Submission** (`submission`): Confirm or cancel your submission.
	+ Submit: Confirm your answers and comments.
	+ Cancel: Cancel your submission and start over.
* **Role Comments** (`role_comments`): Add any comments related to your educator role.
* **Attitude Comments** (`attitude_comments`): Add any comments related to your attitude towards sustainability in education.
* **Challenge Comments** (`challenge_comments`): Add any comments related to the challenges you face in promoting sustainability in education.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`): Add any comments related to your submission.
* **Comments 1** (`comments_1`): This text box is available for any additional comments you may have.

Note: The fields with a note type are text boxes where you can add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the topic.
