# Learner Conduct Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Learner Conduct Agreement Form is designed to capture essential information and agreements from learners before they begin a training program or course. This form helps to establish clear expectations and guidelines for learner conduct, ensuring a smooth and successful learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant training program from the provided options.
3. Ensure you have provided all necessary details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Learner Name** (`learner_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or government-issued documentation.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Provide your email address for communication and contact purposes.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are agreeing to the terms and conditions.
* **Learner Organisation** (`learner_organisation`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your learner organisation or institution.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign here to indicate you have read, understand, and will abide by the learner conduct agreement.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional): Enter your trainer's name or the name of the person you are dealing with.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, optional): Select the relevant training program you are participating in.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Enter the duration of your training session.

Note: Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any issues or misunderstandings. If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your trainer or supervisor.
