# Student Pre Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for students to pre-register with the school before the start of the new semester. It gathers basic information from students and their parents to ensure that the school has up-to-date and accurate information for the new semester.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student information on the first page.
2. Provide your parent's information on the second page.
3. Enter your contact information on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Info (Student Info)
* **Student Info**: This section is for you to enter your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a student.
*   Type: Text input field
*   Required: No

### Parent Info (Parent Info)
*   **Parent Info**: This section is for you to enter your parent's personal details. This could include their name, contact information, etc.
*   Type: Text input field
*   Required: No

### Contact Info (Contact Info)
*   **Contact Info**: This is for you to enter your contact details, such as your address, phone number, or email.
*   Type: Text input field
*   Required: No

## Tips
*   Please fill in accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues during the registration process.
*   If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to reach out to the school administration for clarification.
*   You can contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for any form-related inquiries.
