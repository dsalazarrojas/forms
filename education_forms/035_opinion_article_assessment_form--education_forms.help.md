# Opinion Article Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opinion Article Assessment Form is designed to collect assessments of opinion articles. It aims to gather ratings, comments, and other feedback to help users evaluate and improve articles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the article title in the `Article Title` field.
2. Enter the article URL in the `Article URL` field.
3. Provide the author's name in the `Author` field.
4. Select the category of the article in the `Category` field.
5. Rate the article in the `Rating` field.
6. Type your comments in the `Comments` field.
7. Indicate if you recommend the article in the `Recommended By` field.
8. Choose the rating scale in the `Rating Scale` field.
9. Type any feedback in the `Feedback` field.
10. Select if you recommend the article for others in the `Recommendation` field.
11. Enter the review date in the `Review Date` field.
12. Enter the submission date of the article in the `Submission Date` field.
13. Select the type of article in the `Article Type` field.
14. Type the justification for your rating in the `Rating Justification` field.
15. Indicate if you recommend the article for others in the `Recommended For` field.
16. Enter your rating score in the `Rating Score` field.
17. Provide the name of the reviewer in the `Reviewer` field.
18. Select the type of review in the `Review Type` field.
19. Enter the name of the person who submitted the article in the `Submitted By` field.
20. Enter the unique article ID in the `Article ID` field.
21. Enter the date and time for the second review in the `Review Date 2` and `Review Time` fields.
22. Enter the submission date and time for the second review in the `Submission Date 2` field.
23. Enter the date range for the review in the `Review Date Range` field.
24. Enter the title of the article (if it has multiple titles) in the `Article Title 2` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Article Title** (`article_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the article being assessed.
* **Article URL** (`article_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the article.
* **Author** (`author`, text, optional): Enter the name of the article's author.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select the category of the article.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the article on a scale.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Type any comments about the article.
* **Recommended By** (`recommended_by`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you recommend the article.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Choose the rating scale for the article.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Type any feedback about the article.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_multiple, optional): Select if you recommend the article for others.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Article Type** (`article_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of article.
* **Rating Justification** (`rating_justification`, text, optional): Type the justification for your rating.
* **Recommended For** (`recommended_for`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you recommend the article for others.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, number, optional): Enter your rating score.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, text, optional): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Review Type** (`review_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of review.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the article.
* **Article ID** (`article_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique article ID.
* **Review Date 2** (`review_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the second review.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, optional): Enter the time for the second review.
* **Submission Date 2** (`submission_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the submission date of the second review.
* **Review Date Range** (`review_date_range`, date, optional): Enter the date range for the review.
* **Article Title 2** (`article_title_2`, text, optional): Enter the title of the article (if it has multiple titles).
