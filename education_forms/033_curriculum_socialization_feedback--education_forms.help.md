# Curriculum Socialization Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Socialization Feedback form is designed to gather information from participants on their experience with the curriculum socialization process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the form within 24 hours after the session you participated in.
2. The form is divided into several sections, each with a specific field that collects different types of information.
3. For each section, follow the instructions below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title** (Program Title, text, optional): This field collects the title of the program you participated in.
* **Session ID** (Session ID, number, optional): This field is used to identify the specific session you attended.
* **Participant Rating** (Participant Rating, select_one, optional): This field is used to rate your overall satisfaction with the program, with options 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Rating Comment** (Rating Comment, text, optional): This field is used to elaborate on your participant rating. Please provide specific comments on your experience with the program.
* **Overall Rating** (Overall Rating, select_one, optional): This field is used to rate your overall satisfaction with the program, with options 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): This field is used to provide additional comments on your experience with the program.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): This field is used to collect your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): This field is used to collect your phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Comments 2** (Comments 2, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional comments on your experience with the program.
* **Overall Comments** (Overall Comments, note, optional): This field is used to provide additional comments on your overall experience with the program.
