# Math And AI Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form aims to gather information on your math and AI experience. It is designed to help us improve our services by understanding individual perspectives on math and AI topics. Your input will help us better understand how AI is integrated into math tasks and how it affects your daily work or studies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. For select fields, choose the options that best fit your experience.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to provide additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Math Proficiency** (Text): This section is for you to describe your current math proficiency. Please provide a brief description of your math skills or knowledge.
* **2. AI Tool Usage** (Select multiple): Indicate which AI tools you use for math-related tasks.
* **3. Integration of AI into math tasks** (Select one): Choose the tool you most commonly use for integrating AI into math tasks.
* **4. Math Subjects** (Text): Describe the math subjects you are familiar with or have experience in.
* **5. AI Usage in Education** (Select multiple): Indicate whether you use AI in educational settings, such as teaching or research.
* **6. AI Proficiency** (Select one): Choose your level of proficiency with AI tools.
* **7. Collaboration** (Text): Describe your experience with collaborative work involving math and AI.
* **8. Training Providers** (Text): If you've had training on AI, please describe your experience.
* **9. Math Proficiency Scale** (Number): Rate your math proficiency on a scale of 1-10.
* **10. AI Integration** (Select multiple): Indicate which AI systems you've integrated into math tasks.
* **11. Math Proficiency Level** (Select one): Rate your math proficiency level as High, Low, or Medium.
* **12. AI Use Cases** (Text): Describe the specific AI use cases you've encountered or implemented.
* **13. Collaboration Frequency** (Text): Indicate how often you collaborate with others on math and AI tasks.
* **14. Math Subjects Taught** (Text): If you teach math, list the subjects you've taught.
* **15. AI Integration Level** (Select one): Rate your level of AI integration in math tasks as High, Low, or Medium.

Note: This form is optional, and your responses will help us improve our services. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us for clarification.
