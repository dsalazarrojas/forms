# Data Science In Education Project Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Science In Education Project Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from individuals or organizations participating in a research project related to data science in education. This form helps ensure that all parties involved are aware of the project's purpose, data collection, and usage, and that they provide their consent to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the project in the `Project Name` field.
2. Enter the name of the lead researcher or project lead in the `Lead Researcher` field.
3. List the institutions involved in the project in the `Research Institutions` field.
4. Fill in your name in the `Name` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `Email` field, ensuring it is in the correct format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
6. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
7. Review the options in the `Consent` field and check the boxes for each statement you are willing to accept. This indicates your informed consent to participate in the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the project, which helps identify the research project and its objectives.
  \* This field is required to ensure the project can be properly documented and communicated.
- **Lead Researcher** (`project_lead`, text, optional): Enter the name of the researcher or project lead in charge of the project.
  \* If applicable, this field helps identify the person in charge and facilitates communication.
- **Research Institutions** (`research_institutions`, text, optional): List the institutions involved in the project, such as universities or organizations.
  \* This field helps track collaboration and potential affiliations.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name, which identifies you as a participant.
  \* This field is required for record-keeping and accountability.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
  \* Ensure this field is accurate for communication and verification purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number.
  \* This field can be used for communication and follow-up.
- **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes for each of the three statements regarding your informed consent.
  \* Ensure you have read and understood the project description and data collection and usage before checking these boxes.

## Tips
- Please take a few minutes to carefully read and review the project description and data collection and usage information provided before checking the consent boxes.
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the project lead or research institution for clarification.

Note: This guide is for the user to follow in completing the form. Ensure you have read and understood the project description and data collection and usage before providing your consent.
