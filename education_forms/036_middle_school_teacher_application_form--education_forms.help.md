# middle_school_teacher_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The middle_school_teacher_application_form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in teaching positions at a middle school. This form will help us evaluate your qualifications, experience, and availability to determine the best fit for our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your basic information as an applicant in the "Applicant Information" section.
2. Add any contact details in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Describe your teaching experience in the "Teaching Experience" section.
4. Select your job opening preferences from the "Job Opening" section.
5. Provide more details about your school experience in the "School Information" section.
6. Select the days you are available to work in the "Availability" section.
7. Select your education level in the "Education Background" section.
8. Choose any teaching certifications you hold in the "Teaching Certifications" section.
9. Write a brief personal statement about yourself in the "Personal Statement" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the basic information about the applicant.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for additional contact details.

### Teaching Experience
* **Teaching Experience** (`teaching_experience`, `text`, required: false): This section asks about the applicant's teaching experience.

### Job Opening
* **Job Opening** (`job_opening`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ English Teacher - 1st-5th grade
	+ Math Teacher - 1st-5th grade
	+ Science Teacher - 1st-5th grade

### School Information
* **School Information** (`school_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for additional school information.

### Availability
* **Availability** (`available_days`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday

### Education Background
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ High School Diploma or Equivalent
	+ Bachelor's Degree or Higher
	+ Master's Degree or Higher
	+ Doctoral Degree

### Teaching Certifications
* **Teaching Certifications** (`teaching_certifications`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Special Education
	+ Reading Endorsement
	+ English as Second Language
	+ Bilingual Endorsement
	+ Gifted and Talented
	+ Teaching English as a Second Language (TEASL)

### Personal Statement
* **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, `text`, required: false): This section allows the applicant to provide additional details about themselves.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Provide as much detail as possible, especially in the "Teaching Experience" and "Personal Statement" sections.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
