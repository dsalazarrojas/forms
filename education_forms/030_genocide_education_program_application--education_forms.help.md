# Genocide Education Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals or students applying to a genocide education program. It includes fields to provide a brief overview of the applicant's background, their motivations for applying, and any relevant personal experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief overview of your background as an applicant.
2. Describe your motivations for applying to the genocide education program.
3. Share any relevant personal experience you have that may be helpful for the review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details**: This field allows you to enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself. This information will help the reviewers understand your background and context.
* **Motivations**: Here, explain why you are interested in the genocide education program. What drives your desire to learn, and how do you see yourself benefiting from this experience?
* **Experience**: Share any relevant experience you have had that may be useful for the review process. This could include work, volunteer, or educational experience related to genocide or related fields.
* **Education Background**: Provide a brief overview of your educational background, including any relevant degrees, certifications, or courses you have completed. This will help the reviewers understand your foundation and potential for success in the program.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as concise and clear as possible when filling out the form.
* Proofread your answers to ensure they are accurate and free of errors.
* If you have any relevant supporting documents or materials, consider attaching them to your application.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you have provided all required and relevant information.
