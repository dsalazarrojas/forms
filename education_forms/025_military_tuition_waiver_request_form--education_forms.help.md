# Military Tuition Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Military Tuition Waiver Request Form is a document intended to gather information from active or former military personnel requesting tuition waivers for educational institutions. This form is a vital part of the evaluation process, ensuring that requests are processed efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Verify that all required fields are filled in before submitting.
3. Attach any supporting documents as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Military Tuition Waiver Request** (`military_tuition_waiver_request_form`): A brief description of the reason for the tuition waiver request.
* **Name** (`name`): The full name of the individual requesting the tuition waiver.
* **Military ID** (`military_id`): The military identification number or other relevant ID number.
* **Branch of Service** (`branch_of_service`): Indicate if the individual is currently serving or has served in the military.
	+ **Yes**: If the individual is currently or has been serving in the military.
	+ **No**: Otherwise.
* **Unit** (`unit`): The specific unit or department the individual was or is serving in (optional).
* **Date of Entry** (`date_of_entry`): The date the individual entered military service.
* **Date of Departure** (`date_of_departure`): The date the individual departed military service.
* **Education Institution** (`education_institution`): The name of the educational institution the individual is or will be attending.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`): The level of education the individual is seeking (e.g., undergraduate, graduate).
* **Degree or Certificate** (`degree_or_certificate`): Indicate if the individual is seeking a degree or certificate.
	+ **Yes**: If the individual is seeking a degree or certificate.
	+ **No**: Otherwise.
* **Education Start Date** (`education_start_date`): The date the individual started their education.
* **Education End Date** (`education_end_date`): The date the individual expects to complete their education.
* **Supporting Documents** (`military_tuition_waiver_request_form_uploads`): Upload any relevant military identification documents, such as a DD-214 form.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Any additional information the individual wants to provide about their request.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
- Attach all necessary supporting documents.
- If you have any doubts or need clarification, contact the institution's administration for assistance.
