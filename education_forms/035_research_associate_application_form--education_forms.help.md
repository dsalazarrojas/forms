# Research Associate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Associate Application Form is used to collect information from applicants for research associate positions. This form is required for all applicants to provide their personal details, research experience, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
5. Upload your resume in the **Resume** field.
6. Describe your research experience in the **Research Experience** field (optional).
7. List any relevant courses you have taken in the **Relevant Courses** field (optional).
8. Select your availability date in the **Availability** field.
9. Enter your expected graduation date in the **Expected Graduation Date** field.
10. Select your primary research topic(s) in the **Research Topics** field.
11. Select any additional research areas you are interested in (if applicable) in the **Research Areas** field.
12. List your professional references in the **References** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Associate Application** (`research_associate_application_form`, text, required): The research associate application form is the title of this form.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address where we can send any necessary documents.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Attach your resume in PDF or docx format.
* **Research Experience** (`research_experience`, text, optional): Describe any relevant research experience you have.
* **Relevant Courses** (`relevant_courses`, text, optional): List any relevant courses you have taken.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required): Enter the date you are available to start.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`expected_graduation_date`, date, required): Enter your expected graduation date.
* **Research Topics** (`research_topics`, select_one, required): Select your primary research topic.
* **Research Areas** (`research_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional research areas you are interested in.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): List any professional references you have.
