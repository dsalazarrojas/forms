# Online Meeting Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Meeting Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback and evaluation from attendees of online meetings. It helps to assess the effectiveness of the meeting, the quality of the content, and the overall experience of the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant feedback.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the required fields.
4. If a field is optional, you can choose to fill it out or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Topic**: A brief description of the meeting topic, if applicable.
* **Presenter Name**: The name of the presenter or the person who conducted the meeting.
* **Meeting Outcome**: A brief description of the meeting's outcome or result.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
* **Content Value**: Rate the content value of the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
* **Organization**: Select one or more options that best describe the organization hosting the meeting.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the meeting.
* **Email Response**: If you would like to respond to any questions or concerns via email, provide your email address.
* **Phone Response**: If you would like to respond to any questions or concerns via phone, provide your phone number.
* **Date Response**: If you would like to respond to any questions or concerns via date, provide the date.
* **Time Response**: If you would like to respond to any questions or concerns via time, provide the time.
* **Select One**: Select one option that best describes your choice.
* **Select Multiple**: Select one or more options that best describe your choice.
* **Time Zone**: Select one option that best describes your time zone.
* **Time Zone**: Select one or more options that best describe your time zone.
* **Date Range**: This field is currently not used in the form, please ignore.
* **Time Range**: This field is currently not used in the form, please ignore.
* **Meeting Date**: The date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time**: The time of the meeting.
* **Duration**: The length of the meeting.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Use the required fields to provide accurate and relevant feedback.
* If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or contact the meeting organizer for clarification.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
