# Course Registration Form In Spanish - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Course Registration Form In Spanish" is a form used to collect detailed information about courses, including their name, start date, instructors, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter the course name in the "Course Name" field.
2.  Select the start date of the course from the calendar.
3.  Choose the instructor of the course from the dropdown menu.
4.  Enter the instructor's email address.
5.  Select the course location (if applicable).
6.  Choose the type of course (if applicable).
7.  Enter a brief description of the course.
8.  Enter the duration of the course in hours.
9.  Choose the level of the course.
10.  Select the status of the course.
11.  Enter the start time of the course.
12.  Enter the end time of the course.
13.  Select the date of the course.
14.  Enter the course address (if applicable).
15.  Enter any additional notes about the course.
16.  Choose any tags associated with the course (if applicable).
17.  Select the files associated with the course (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the course you are registering.
*   **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, `date`, required): Select the date when the course will start from the calendar.
*   **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `select_one`, required): Choose the instructor of the course from the dropdown menu.
*   **Instructor Email** (`instructor_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the instructor.
*   **Course Location** (`course_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the course (if applicable).
*   **Course Type** (`course_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of course from the dropdown menu (if applicable).
*   **Course Description** (`course_description`, `note`, optional): Enter a brief description of the course.
*   **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the course in hours.
*   **Course Level** (`course_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of the course from the dropdown menu.
*   **Course Status** (`course_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the course from the dropdown menu.
*   **Course Start Time** (`course_start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the course.
*   **Course End Time** (`course_end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of the course.
*   **Course Date** (`course_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the course from the calendar.
*   **Course Address** (`course_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the course location (if applicable).
*   **Course Notes** (`course_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the course.
*   **Course Tags** (`course_tags`, `text`, optional): Enter any tags associated with the course (if applicable).
*   **Course Files** (`course_files`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any files associated with the course (if applicable).
