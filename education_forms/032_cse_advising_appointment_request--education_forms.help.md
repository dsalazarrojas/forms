# cse_advising_appointment_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "cse_advising_appointment_request" form is designed to gather information from users who require advising services. This data is used to process and schedule meetings between users and advising staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill in your User Name, which is required for tracking and referencing.
2.  Enter your Email address, which will be used for contacting you about your appointment.
3.  Select your Course ID, which is necessary to ensure you are registered in the correct course.
4.  Specify the Start Date of the advising meeting.
5.  Select the End Date of the advising meeting.
6.  Choose the Duration of your desired meeting.
7.  Select the Meeting Time for your advising session.
8.  Optionally, select a Preferred Date for your meeting if you have a specific date in mind.
9.  Choose the Meeting Duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your University records.
* **Email**: Enter your University email address to ensure we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Course ID**: Enter the ID number of the course you are currently enrolled in.
* **Start Date**: Select the date you would like your advising meeting to take place.
* **End Date**: Select the date you would like your advising meeting to end.
* **Duration**: Enter the length of time you would like your meeting to last.
* **Meeting Time**: Choose a time slot that suits you best.
* **Preferred Date**: If you have a specific date in mind for your meeting, select it here.
* **Meeting Duration**: Enter the length of time you would like your meeting to last.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields carefully.
- Ensure you provide accurate and correct information to avoid delays in processing your request.
- If you have any issues or queries, reach out to our support team for assistance.
- We will do our best to accommodate your preferred date and time, however, availability may be subject to change.
