# School Trip Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Trip Attendance Form is a tool used to record and track student attendance during school trips. It helps teachers and school administrators to ensure that students are accounted for and that parents are notified of their child's participation in the trip. By completing this form, you can provide essential information about the trip, including student details, trip schedules, and parent permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the student's name
*   Select the student's grade level
*   Enter the date of the trip
*   Choose whether the teacher or parent will be attending
*   Enter your name, school address, school phone number, and school website (if applicable)
*   Record the student's attendance during the trip
*   Add any comments or notes related to the student
*   Include any trip details, such as start and end times and location

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's full name as they are registered in the school.
*   **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, optional): Select the student's grade level as registered in the school.
*   **Date of Trip** (`student_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the school trip.
*   **Teacher Attending** (`teacher_attendance`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether the teacher will be attending the trip.
*   **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you are giving or not giving permission for the student to participate in the trip.
*   **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the student.
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the school.
*   **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the school.
*   **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, `number`, optional): Record the student's attendance during the trip.
*   **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `note`, optional): Include your signature as the parent or guardian.
*   **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, `text`, optional): Add any additional trip details, such as start and end times and location.
*   **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the trip.
*   **Trip Start Time** (`trip_start`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the trip.
*   **Trip End Time** (`trip_end`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the trip.
*   **Trip Location** (`trip_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the trip.
*   **Student Absent** (`student_absent`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether the student is absent from the trip.
*   **Trip Duration** (`trip_duration`, `text`, optional): Enter the duration of the trip.
*   **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the school's phone number.
*   **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the parent's email address.
*   **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
*   **School Website** (`school_website`, `text`, optional): Enter the school's website.
*   **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to the student.
*   **School Notes** (`school_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to the school.
*   **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `note`, optional): Include your signature as the parent or guardian.
