# Stay On Campus Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stay On Campus Request Form is a form used to request to stay on campus beyond the regular student accommodation period. This form is typically used by students who need to stay on campus for various reasons such as academic purposes, family emergencies, or personal reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student ID number in the Student ID field.
2. Enter your full name in the Student Name field.
3. Select a date for your stay in the Date field.
4. Choose your intended departure time in the Departure Time field.
5. Provide a clear and concise reason for your request in the Reason for Request field.
6. Select your preferred contact method (Phone or Email) and enter your contact number or email address in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter your valid student ID number as a 9-digit number. This is used to identify your student status and authenticate your identity.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID card.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to stay on campus. This date should be a date in the future.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time, optional): Choose your intended departure time from the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 12:00, 14:00, etc.).
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Provide a clear explanation of why you need to stay on campus beyond the regular accommodation period. This should be a brief and concise statement.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted for any updates or questions about your stay on campus. You can choose between Phone and Email.
* **Contact Number/Email** (`contact_number`, text/email, optional): Enter your contact number or email address depending on the contact method you selected. This is used by the relevant authorities to contact you about your stay on campus.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting to stay on campus.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant department or authority for clarification.
