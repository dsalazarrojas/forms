# elementary_student_reading_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The elementary_student_reading_log form is used to record and track a student's reading habits. By providing information on the start and end dates and times, as well as the title of the book, author, reading location, reading level, and reading time, educators and administrators can monitor students' reading progress and identify any areas for improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start date and start time of the reading session.
2. Enter the end date and end time of the reading session.
3. Enter the title of the book.
4. Enter the author of the book.
5. Select the location where you read the book.
6. Choose the reading level of the book (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
7. Enter the duration of the reading session (in minutes).
8. Provide any additional reading notes.
9. Enter your email address (for form submission).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, required: false): Enter the date when you started reading the book.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, required: false): Enter the time when you started reading the book.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, required: false): Enter the date when you finished reading the book.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, required: false): Enter the time when you finished reading the book.
* **Title of Book** (`title_of_book`, Text, required: false): Enter the title of the book you read.
* **Book Author** (`book_author`, Text, required: false): Enter the author of the book.
* **Reading Location** (`reading_location`, Text, required: false): Enter where you read the book (e.g. classroom, home, library).
* **Reading Level** (`reading_level`, Select One, required: false): Choose the reading level of the book (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Reading Time** (`reading_time`, Number, required: false): Enter the duration of the reading session (in minutes).
* **Reading Notes** (`reading_notes`, Note, required: false): Provide any notes or comments about your reading experience.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, Email, required: false): Enter your email address for form submission purposes.
