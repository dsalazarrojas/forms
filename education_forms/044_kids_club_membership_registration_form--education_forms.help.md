# Kids Club Membership Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Kids Club Membership Registration Form collects information from parents or guardians about their child's membership in the Kids Club. This information is used to create a profile for the child and to ensure their safety and well-being while attending the club.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely, paying close attention to each field as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter your child's first name here. Please make sure to enter the name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter your child's last name here. Please make sure to enter the name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required)
	+ Enter your child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy. This will help us confirm their age and identity.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter your name and address as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required)
	+ Enter your email address that we will use to contact you with updates and news about the club.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter your phone number that we will use to contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required)
	+ Enter your home address where you can be reached during emergencies.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required)
	+ Enter your zip code to help us determine your location.
* **Club Type** (`club_type`, select_one, required)
	+ Select the type of club your child is interested in joining (Kids Club, Art Club, or Sports Club).
* **Special Skills** (`special_skills`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select the special skills your child has, such as dancing, singing, or acting.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required)
	+ Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required)
	+ If your child has any medical conditions that we should be aware of, enter them here.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ If your child has any allergies, select them from the list. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
	+ If you have any additional information or special requests, enter them here.

Note: Please fill out all required fields accurately to ensure that we can process your child's membership request smoothly. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
