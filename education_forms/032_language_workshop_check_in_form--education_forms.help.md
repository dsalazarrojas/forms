# Language Workshop Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Language Workshop Check In Form is used to collect check-in information from participants for language workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **Participant First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Participant Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the language of the workshop you are attending from the **Language** field.
6. Choose your level of proficiency in the selected language from the **Level** field.
7. If applicable, enter any additional notes about yourself or your participation in the **Participant Notes** field.
8. Enter the date of the workshop in the **Date** field.
9. Enter the time you plan to arrive at the workshop in the **Time** field.
10. Select your check-in status from the **Check In Status** field.
11. Select the status of the workshop from the **Workshop Status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Details**: This field is a text input field where you can enter any relevant details about the workshop.
* **Participant First Name**: Enter your first name to identify yourself as a participant.
*   *Optional*
* **Participant Last Name**: Enter your last name to identify yourself as a participant.
*   *Optional*
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the workshop.
*   *Optional*
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the workshop.
*   *Optional*
* **Language**: Choose the language of the workshop you are attending.
*   *Optional*
*   English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese
* **Level**: Choose your level of proficiency in the selected language.
*   *Optional*
*   Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Proficient
*   Please note: If you select a level that is not available or does not match your actual level, please choose a level that is closest to your level.
* **Participant Language**: Choose your native or most spoken language.
*   *Optional*
*   English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese
*   Please note: This field is not necessarily related to your level of proficiency but rather your native or most spoken language.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about yourself or your participation in the workshop.
*   *Optional*
*   Please use this field to provide any relevant information to the workshop facilitators or organizers.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the workshop.
*   *Optional*
*   Format: mm/dd/yy
* **Time**: Enter the time you plan to arrive at the workshop.
*   *Optional*
*   Format: 12:00 AM - 11:59 PM
* **Check In Status**: Select your current check-in status.
*   *Optional*
*   Checked In, Not Checked In
* **Workshop Status**: Select the status of the workshop.
*   *Optional*
*   Started, Finished, Cancelled
* **Participant ID**: Enter your participant ID.
*   *Optional*
*   A unique number assigned to you as a participant.

**Tips**

* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a workshop facilitator or organizer for assistance.
* Please fill out this form honestly and accurately.
* If you need to change any information, please contact the workshop facilitators or organizers.
* Please note that some fields may be marked as optional, but it's still essential to provide accurate information to ensure smooth workshop management.
