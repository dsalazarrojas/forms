# english_language_proficiency_exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
The english_language_proficiency_exam form is designed to assess the English language proficiency of students. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. **English Language Proficiency Exam** (`english_language_proficiency_exam`, text): This is the main title of the form.
2. **Student Information** (`student_info`, number): Enter the student's ID number for identification purposes.
3. **School Information** (`school_info`, text): Enter the name or identifier of the school the student is associated with.
4. **Training Program** (`training_program`, text): Enter the name or identifier of the training program the student is enrolled in.
5. **Training Program Type** (`training_program_type`, select_one): Choose between "in house" or "online" to indicate the type of training program the student is part of.
6. **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date): Enter the date the language proficiency exam was conducted.
7. **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time): Enter the time the language proficiency exam was conducted.
8. **Assessment Note** (`assessment_note`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the assessment.
9. **Student Assessment** (`student_assessment`, select_multiple): Choose all the language skills the student was assessed on, such as reading, writing, listening, and speaking.
10. **School Assessment** (`school_assessment`, select_one): Choose whether the student passed or failed the language proficiency exam.
11. **Student Language Level** (`student_language_level`, select_one): Choose the student's language level, such as intermediate, advanced, or novice.
12. **Student Language Background** (`student_language_background`, select_one): Choose whether the student's language background is native or non-native.
13. **Student Language Assessment Score** (`student_language_assessment_score`, number): Enter the student's language assessment score.
14. **School Language Assessment Score** (`school_language_assessment_score`, number): Enter the school's language assessment score.
15. **Student Language Level 2** (`student_language_level_2`, select_one): Choose the student's language level, which may be different from level 1.
16. **Student Language Background 2** (`student_language_background_2`, select_one): Choose whether the student's language background is native or non-native.
17. **Student Language Assessment Score 2** (`student_language_assessment_score_2`, number): Enter the student's language assessment score.
18. **School Language Assessment Score 2** (`school_language_assessment_score_2`, number): Enter the school's language assessment score.
19. **Email** (`email`, email): Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
20. **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter the student's phone number for communication purposes.
21. **Student Language Proficiency Exam Note** (`student_language_proficiency_exam_note`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the student's language proficiency exam.
22. **School Language Proficiency Exam Note** (`school_language_proficiency_exam_note`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the school's language proficiency exam.
23. **Student Language Proficiency Exam Note 2** (`student_language_proficiency_exam_note_2`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the student's language proficiency exam.
24. **School Language Proficiency Exam Note 2** (`school_language_proficiency_exam_note_2`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the school's language proficiency exam.
25. **Student Language Proficiency Exam Note 3** (`student_language_proficiency_exam_note_3`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the student's language proficiency exam.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the form's required fields.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact your administrator or support team.
