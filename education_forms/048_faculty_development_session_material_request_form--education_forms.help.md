# Faculty Development Session Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Development Session Material Request Form is used to request faculty development sessions, which are designed to support the growth and development of faculty members. This form helps us to understand your needs and preferences for these sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your faculty name and title accurately.
2.  Specify the name of the course you are interested in.
3.  Provide the date and time for the session you are requesting.
4.  Indicate the length of the session you need.
5.  Answer whether materials are requested and ordered for the session.
6.  Confirm your contact information (email and phone number).
7.  Indicate whether you are assigned a tool for the session and whether you have submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty name** (`faculty name`, **Required**): Enter your name as a faculty member.
* **Faculty title** (`faculty title`, **Optional**): Enter your title or position as a faculty member.
* **Course name** (`course name`, **Optional**): Enter the name of the course you are interested in.
* **Session date** (`session date`, **Required**): Select the date for the session you are requesting.
* **Start time** (`start time`, **Required**): Specify the start time for the session you need.
* **End time** (`end time`, **Required**): Specify the end time for the session you need.
* **Session length** (`session length`, **Required**): Indicate the duration of the session you need.
* **Materials requested** (`materials requested`, **Required**): Check if materials are required for the session.
* **Materials ordered** (`materials ordered`, **Required**): Check if materials have been ordered for the session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, **Optional**): Add any additional comments or details about your session needs.
* **Email** (`email`, **Required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted).
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned tool`, **Required**): Indicate whether you have been assigned a tool for the session.
* **Form submit** (`form submit`, **Required**): Confirm that you have submitted this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need any additional support or guidance, contact your administrator.
