# Dance Academy Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential students and their parents/guardians for enrollment in the Dance Academy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information.
2. Select the dance class type you are interested in.
3. Choose your preferred class schedule.
4. Confirm your payment method and frequency.
5. Select your preferred language.
6. Provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: The name of the student who is enrolling. (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **Last Name**: The last name of the student who is enrolling. (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **Email**: The email of the contact person. (<name>, <type> email, required: false)
- **Goals**: Select the type of dance class the student is interested in (Ballet, Contemporary, Tap, Jazz, Ballroom). (<name>, <type> select_one, required: false)
- **Class Date**: The date you are enrolling the student for a class. (<name>, <type> date, required: false)
- **Class Time**: The time you are enrolling the student for a class. (<name>, <type> time, required: false)
- **Contact Number**: The phone number of the contact person. (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **Parent Name**: The name of the parent or guardian. (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **Parent Email**: The email of the parent or guardian. (<name>, <type> email, required: false)
- **Additional Comments**: Any further comments or questions you want to share. (<name>, <type> note, required: false)
- **Payment Method**: How you would like to pay for the class (Cash, Credit Card, Online Banking). (<name>, <type> select_one, required: false)
- **Payment Frequency**: How frequently you want to pay for the classes (Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Quarterly). (<name>, <type> select_multiple, required: false)
- **Payment Terms**: Date of payment (e.g., due date). (<name>, <type> date, required: false)
- **Instructor Name**: The name of the instructor you are interested in. (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **Class Preferences**: Select the types of classes you are interested in (Jazz, Ballet, Tap, Contemporary, Ballroom). (<name>, <type> select_multiple, required: false)
- **Student Level**: Select the student level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced). (<name>, <type> select_one, required: false)
- **Additional Classes**: Are you enrolling for any additional classes? (<name>, <type> select_multiple, required: false)
- **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of the emergency contact person. (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **Emergency Contact Number**: The phone number of the emergency contact person. (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **Medical Conditions**: Any medical conditions you or your child may have. (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **Medication**: Are you or your child on any medication? (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **Allergy**: Do you or your child have any allergies? (<name>, <type> text, required: false)
- **First Language**: The first language you or your child speaks. (<name>, <type> select_one, required: false)
- **Parent Language**: The language of the parent or guardian. (<name>, <type> select_one, required: false)
- **Consent**: Are you giving consent to provide the medical information. (<name>, <type> select_one, required: false)

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and carefully. 
- Please do not hesitate to contact the Dance Academy if you have any questions or doubts about any field. 
- All information will be kept confidential and used only for the student's best possible experience and outcome.
