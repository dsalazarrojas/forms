# Digital Inclusion Barriers In Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the digital inclusion barriers faced by learners, which include device access, internet access, skills, financial barriers, support systems, online equipment, and internet speed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question honestly to the best of your abilities.
2. Select all relevant options that apply to you.
3. Be as detailed as possible when answering text fields.
4. If you have no experience or knowledge about a particular question, please let us know by selecting the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are some device access barriers to learning?** (`device_access`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any difficulties you face in accessing devices, such as computers, laptops, or smartphones, that hinder your learning experience.
* **What are some internet access barriers to learning?** (`internet_access`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any limitations you face in accessing the internet, such as speed, cost, or availability.
* **What are some skills barriers to learning?** (`skills`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any skills or abilities that you lack or need to improve in order to learn online.
* **What are some skills related to digital inclusion barriers to learning?** (`digital_skills`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any skills you have acquired or need to improve in order to overcome digital barriers to learning.
* **What are some digital equipment barriers to learning?** (`digital_equipment`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any digital equipment, such as software or tools, that hinder your learning experience.
* **What are some internet speed barriers to learning?** (`internet_speed`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any issues you face with internet speed that hinder your learning experience.
* **What are some device age barriers to learning?** (`device_age`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any limitations you face due to the age of your device hindering your learning experience.
* **What are some barriers to device internet access?** (`device_internet_access`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any limitations you face due to device internet access hindering your learning experience.
* **What are some other device access barriers to learning?** (`other_device_access`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any other device-related barriers to learning you face.
* **What are some other internet access barriers to learning?** (`other_internet_access`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any other internet-related barriers to learning you face.
* **What are some other skills barriers to learning?** (`other_skills`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any other skills-related barriers to learning you face.
* **What are some other equipment barriers to learning?** (`other_equipment`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any other equipment-related barriers to learning you face.
* **What are some financial barriers to learning?** (`financial_barriers`, text, required): This question is asking you to list any financial difficulties you face that hinder your learning experience.
* **How do you manage your time while learning online?** (`time_management`, text, required): This question is asking you to describe how you balance your time while learning online.
* **Do you participate in online communities related to your area of interest?** (`online_community`, select_one, required): This question is asking you to indicate how often you participate in online communities related to your area of interest.
* **How often do you feel self-aware about your digital skills and abilities?** (`self_awareness`, select_one, required): This question is asking you to indicate how frequently you evaluate your digital skills and abilities.
* **Do you use a support system to help you in times of need?** (`support_system`, select_one, required): This question is asking you to indicate if you use a support system to help you in times of need.
* **What kind of financial support do you have?** (`financial_support`, select_multiple, required): This question is asking you to choose any financial support you receive.
* **What other kind of support do you have for learning?** (`other_support`, select_multiple, required): This question is asking you to choose any other type of support you receive.
* **Do you use any online equipment?** (`online_equipment`, select_one, required): This question is asking you to indicate if you use any online equipment.
* **What type of online equipment do you use?** (`online_equipment_type`, select_multiple, required): This question is asking you to choose any type of online equipment you use.
* **How often do you access online equipment?** (`online_equipment_access`, select_multiple, required): This question is asking you to indicate how frequently you access online equipment.
* **What type of devices do you use for online learning?** (`device_type`, select_multiple, required): This question is asking you to choose any type of devices you use for online learning.
* **What kind of internet speed do you use?** (`internet_speed_type`, select_multiple, required): This question is asking you to choose any type of internet speed you use.
* **What kind of internet access do you use?** (`internet_access_type`, select_multiple, required): This question is asking you to choose any kind of internet access you use.
