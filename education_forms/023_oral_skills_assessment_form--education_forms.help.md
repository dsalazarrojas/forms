# Prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and self-assessment from language learners on various aspects of their language skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your language learning experiences.
2. Provide additional comments if you feel there is more to add to your assessment.
3. Review and submit the form to save your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pronunciation** (`Pronunciation`, Select One, Required): This field asks you to assess your pronunciation skills, choosing from Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, or Very Poor.
* **Fluency** (`Fluency`, Select One, Required): This field asks you to evaluate your fluency skills, selecting from Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, or Very Poor.
* **Vocabulary** (`Vocabulary`, Select One, Required): This field asks you to rate your vocabulary skills, choosing from Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, or Very Poor.
* **Comprehension** (`Comprehension`, Select One, Required): This field asks you to assess your comprehension skills, selecting from Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, or Very Poor.
* **Interaction** (`Interaction`, Select One, Required): This field asks you to evaluate your interaction skills, choosing from Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, or Very Poor.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, Text, Required): This field is for you to provide any additional information or comments about your language skills.
* **Pronunciation Skills** (`Pronunciation Skills`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select the areas you need help with regarding pronunciation, such as Accent, Articulation, Inflection, Intonation, Vowel sounds, and Consonant sounds.
* **Comprehension Areas** (`Comprehension Areas`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select the areas you need help with regarding comprehension, such as Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking.
* **Additional Skills** (`Additional Skills`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select any additional skills you would like to improve, such as Grammar, Pronunciation, Vocabulary, Fluency, and Interaction.
* **Pronunciation Challenges** (`Pronunciation Challenges`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select the challenges you face with pronunciation, such as Word order, Syntactic errors, Articulation difficulties, Vowel sounds, and Consonant sounds.
* **Fluency Challenges** (`Fluency Challenges`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select the challenges you face with fluency, such as Speed, Confidence, Speaking in front of an audience, Using idiomatic expressions, and Understanding nuances.
* **Comprehension Challenges** (`Comprehension Challenges`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select the challenges you face with comprehension, such as Reading speed, Vocabulary building, Contextual understanding, Active listening, and Grammar rules.
* **Interaction Challenges** (`Interaction Challenges`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select the challenges you face with interaction, such as Engaging in conversations, Understanding idiomatic expressions, Asking questions, Giving feedback, and Maintaining eye contact.
* **Additional Resources** (`Additional Resources`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select any additional resources you use to learn a language, such as Language learning apps, YouTube videos, Podcasts, Language exchange websites, and Language learning podcasts.
* **Language Exchange** (`Language Exchange`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select any language exchange opportunities you engage in, such as Language exchange websites, Language exchange apps, Language exchange events, Language conversation practice with a tutor, and Language conversation groups.
* **Self-Assessment** (`Self-Assessment`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select your self-assessment levels, such as Self-assessment 1, Self-assessment 2, Self-assessment 3, Self-assessment 4, and Self-Assessment 5.
* **Feedback from Others** (`Feedback from Others`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks you to select the sources of feedback you receive, such as Feedback from language teacher, Feedback from language partner, Feedback from family member, Feedback from friend, and Feedback from self-reflection.
* **Recommendations** (`Recommendations`, Text, Required): This field is for you to provide any recommendations or suggestions for language learning.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each field honestly, based on your actual language learning experiences.
* Take your time to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need help or clarification, contact your language teacher or language partner.
