# Flight School Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to register individuals for flight school training programs. It requires information about the applicant's details and training preferences for the admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Follow the steps below to complete this form:
  1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
  2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
  3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
  4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
  5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
  6. Choose your preferred course from the "Course" options.
  7. Enter the date you wish to enroll in the "Enrollment Date" field.
  8. Select the type of training you are interested in from the "Training Type" options.
  9. Choose your preferred type of flight certification from the "Flight Certification" options.
  10. Add any additional information relevant to your application in the "Additional Info" text area.
  11. Click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your full first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your full last name.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Course** (`course`, select_one, optional): Choose the course you are interested in (Pilot, Instructor, or Other).
- **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to enroll.
- **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of training you are interested in (Private, Commercial, or Flight).
- **Flight Certification** (`flight_certification`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred type of flight certification (Private Pilot License, Commercial Pilot License, or Flight Instructor License).
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Add any additional information relevant to your application.
- **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Click to send your completed application.

## Tips
- Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information for a successful application.
- Make sure to review your application before submitting for any mistakes or missing fields.
