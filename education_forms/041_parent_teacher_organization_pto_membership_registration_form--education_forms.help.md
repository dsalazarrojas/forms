# Parent Teacher Organization PTO Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new member to the Parent Teacher Organization (PTO) membership. Please use this form only if you have not already registered with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Member Name** in the "Member Name" field. This is the name of the person registering to be a member of the PTO.
2. Enter your **Member Email** in the "Member Email" field. This will be your email address where you can receive important updates and communications from the PTO.
3. Enter your **Member Phone** in the "Member Phone" field. This is your contact phone number.
4. Provide your **Member Address** in the "Member Address" field. This is your address where you can receive mail and updates from the PTO.
5. Enter the **Member School Name** in the "Member School Name" field. This is the school your child attends.
6. Select your **PTO Position** from the dropdown list. This is the role you are interested in taking within the PTO.
7. Choose the **Member Since** date you became a member or when you started attending the school. This is an optional field.
8. Select the **PTO Year** if you are registering for a specific year. This is optional.
9. Add any additional **Member Notes** if you would like to explain your interest in the PTO. This is optional.
10. Review your information carefully and submit the form to register your membership.
