# Graduation Thesis Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graduation Thesis Upload Form is a platform for students to upload their graduation thesis documents. This form enables students to submit their thesis in a structured and controlled manner, allowing for easy tracking and review by administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Upload File**: Select and upload the graduation thesis document from your device.
2. **Enter Student Information**: Enter your name and email address to associate with the thesis document.
3. **Provide File Description**: Enter a brief description of the file to help reviewers understand its contents.
4. **Review and Confirm**: Select the review option to indicate if you would like the document to be reviewed or not.
5. **Submit**: Click the submit button to finalize the upload process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **File Upload**: Upload your graduation thesis document from your device.
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address associated with your university or institution.
* **File Name**: Enter a brief title or name for your thesis document.
* **File Description**: Provide a short description of your thesis document to help reviewers understand its contents.
* **Upload File**: Confirm that you have successfully uploaded your file.
* **Review**: Select one of the options to indicate if you would like the document to be reviewed or not.
	+ Option 1: Request review and approval.
	+ Option 2: Do not review or approve.
	+ Option 3: Request minor revisions.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to finalize the upload process and complete your submission.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the correct version of your thesis document before uploading it.
* Use a clear and concise file name and description to facilitate easy identification.
* Select the review option thoughtfully, as it may affect the approval and progression of your graduation process.
