# Open Education Case Study Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in an education case study. The form is designed to gather data on the participant's name, email, project description, project location, phone number, any additional notes, and category. The information collected will be used to analyze and understand various aspects of education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "participant_name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "participant_email" field. Please note that this field requires a valid email address.
3.  Describe your project in the "participant_project_description" field. This field is a text area where you can provide details about your project.
4.  Enter your project location in the "participant_project_location" field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "participant_phone" field.
6.  If you have any additional notes, you can enter them in the "participant_note" field.
7.  Select your category from the "participant_category" dropdown menu. Please choose one of the available categories (Category A, Category B, or Category C).
8.  Enter your participant ID in the "participant_form_id" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **participant_email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address that you check regularly.
* **participant_project_description** (note, optional): Describe your project in a few sentences or bullet points.
* **participant_project_location** (text, optional): Enter your project location (e.g., city, state, country).
* **participant_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **participant_note** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your project.
* **participant_category** (select_one, optional): Select one of the available categories (Category A, Category B, or Category C).
* **participant_form_id** (number, optional): Enter your participant ID if you have one.
