# Environmental Literacy Among Students Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students about their awareness and attitudes towards environmental issues and their participation in environmental activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the form completely, but it is not required.
* Answer each question honestly.
* You can select multiple options from the "awareness" and "activities" sections.
* For the "email" field, please enter your actual email address.
* For the "phone" and "date" fields, please enter a valid email format and date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* For the "time" field, please enter a valid 24-hour time in the format of HH:MM.
* You can enter any additional comments or information in the "text" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, note, required: false): This is an introductory text that provides context for the survey.
* **Awareness** (`2`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your level of awareness regarding environmental issues. Please select all that apply.
	+ Yes: I am aware of environmental issues.
	+ No: I am not aware of environmental issues.
* **Attitude** (`3`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your attitude towards environmental issues. Please select one of the options.
	+ Yes: I support environmental issues.
	+ No: I do not support environmental issues.
* **Activities** (`4`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your participation in environmental activities. Please select all that apply.
	+ Yes: I participate in environmental activities.
	+ No: I do not participate in environmental activities.
* **Email** (`5`, email, required: false): This is a field for your email address.
* **Phone** (`6`, text, required: false): This is a field for your phone number.
* **Date** (`7`, date, required: false): This is a field for your date of birth or a valid date.
* **Time** (`8`, time, required: false): This is a field for your available time for environmental activities.
* **Text** (`9`, text, required: false): This is a field for any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all the required fields. This form will be submitted once you click the submit button. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
