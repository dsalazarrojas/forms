## Purpose
The dec_jan_online_learning_chromebook_loan_agreement form is used to collect information for the loan of a Chromebook or other electronic device for online learning purposes. This form is used to track and manage student, school, and loan details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Loan Agreement**:
	+ This field allows you to enter any additional notes or terms related to the loan.
* **Loan Terms**:
	+ Select one or more options to apply any additional terms or conditions to the loan.
* **Loan Duration**:
	+ Enter the duration of the loan in months.
* **Student Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the student receiving the loan.
* **School Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the school where the student is enrolled.
* **Student Email**:
	+ Enter the email address of the student receiving the loan.
* **Parent Email**:
	+ Enter the email address of the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Date of Loan**:
	+ Enter the date when the loan was issued.
* **Loan Amount**:
	+ Enter the amount of the loan.
* **Loan Start Date**:
	+ Enter the start date and time of the loan.
* **Loan End Date**:
	+ Enter the end date and time of the loan.
* **Loan Type**:
	+ Select the type of loan being made (e.g. Chromebook Loan, iPad Loan, Laptop Loan).
* **Loan Status**:
	+ Select the current status of the loan (e.g. Active, Inactive, Cancelled).

Note: The fields marked as true have been marked as required fields in the original YAML, while the fields marked as optional have been marked as false.
