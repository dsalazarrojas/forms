# Registration For Designing Playgrounds - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users for designing playgrounds, a program to support children's education and development through play-based learning. The form captures essential information to facilitate smooth communication and planning between parents, educators, and the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a computer or mobile device with a stable internet connection.
2. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
3. Fill out the form with your relevant information.
4. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the playground's materials and communication.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your surname, if applicable.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact and communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide a phone number for additional contact or support.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for relevant age-specific programming or services.
- **Time of Enrollment** (`time_of_enrollment`, time, optional): Select the time you enrolled in the program for record-keeping purposes.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your child's needs or interests.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the child's parent or guardian.
- **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the parent or guardian's contact information.
- **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, optional): Identify the class or group your child belongs to.
- **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, number, optional): Indicate how many children are registered with you.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any specialized equipment or resources assigned to your child for their play-based learning experience.
