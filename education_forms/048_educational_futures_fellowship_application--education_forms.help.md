# Educational Futures Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Futures Fellowship Application is designed to be a comprehensive guide for individuals interested in applying for educational fellowships. It helps users provide a clear and concise application that meets the specific requirements of the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Application Form section, where you can provide your basic contact information.
2. In the Motivation Statement section, explain your interest in the Educational Futures Fellowship and how it aligns with your goals and aspirations.
3. Upload your Curriculum Vitae (CV) in the CV section, which should be a brief document that summarizes your educational background and relevant experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, not required): This is the initial contact information section, where you can provide your name, email, and phone number.
* **Motivation Statement** (`motivation_statement`, text, not required): This section is where you can express your interest in the Educational Futures Fellowship, describing how your goals and aspirations align with the fellowship's objectives. Please keep your statement concise and to the point.
* **CV** (`cv`, text, not required): This is where you can upload your Curriculum Vitae, which should be a brief document that highlights your educational background and relevant experience. Make sure to tailor your CV to the specific requirements of the fellowship.

## Tips
* Please keep all sections concise and clear in your answers.
* Proofread your application carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure your CV is up-to-date and relevant to the fellowship.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
