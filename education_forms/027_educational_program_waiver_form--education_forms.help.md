# Educational Program Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Program Waiver Form is a document that requires participants to acknowledge and accept the risks associated with their participation in the program. It is used to collect information about the participant, their parent (if applicable), and emergency contact details. The form also includes a section for the participant to sign and date their acknowledgement, as well as a section for program staff to sign and date their acknowledgment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Information**
	* Fill in the participant's name, age, and contact information.
	* Check the "Parent Information" field to see if the participant has a parent or guardian listed.
2. **Emergency Contact Information**
	* If you have an emergency contact listed, fill in their name and contact information.
	* Indicate the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact.
3. **Waiver and Acknowledgement**
	* Read through each section carefully and acknowledge that you understand the associated risks.
	* Select "Yes" or "No" for each waiver.
4. **Medical Clearance and Details**
	* If required, fill out the medical clearance section.
	* Provide any necessary medical clearance details.
5. **Signature and Date**
	* Sign and date your acknowledgement as the participant.
	* Sign and date your acknowledgement as staff.
	* Ensure that your signatures are authentic and date/time stamped accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Page 1)
	* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's full name as it appears on their official document.
	* **Age**: Enter the participant's age in years.
	* **Contact Information**: Enter the participant's contact information, including address and phone number.
* **Parent Information** (Page 1)
	* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter the participant's parent or guardian's name if applicable.
	* **Relationship**: Indicate the relationship between the participant and the parent/guardian.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 2)
	* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
	* **Relationship**: Indicate the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact.
	* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the emergency contact.
* **Waiver** (Page 3)
	* **Waiver 1**: Acknowledge that you understand and accept the risks associated with participation.
	* **Waiver 2**: Acknowledge that you understand and accept the medical clearance requirements.
	* **Waiver 3**: Acknowledge that you understand and accept the rules and expectations of the program.
* **Medical Clearance** (Page 4)
	* **Medical Clearance Details**: Provide any necessary medical clearance details.
* **Participant Acknowledgement** (Page 5)
	* **Acknowledge**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understood the risks associated with participation.
* **Participant Signature** (Page 6)
	* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understood the risks associated with participation.
	* **Signature Date**: Date your signature.
* **Staff Acknowledgement** (Page 7)
	* **Acknowledge**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understood the risks associated with participation.
* **Staff Signature** (Page 8)
	* **Staff Signature**: Sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understood the risks associated with participation.
	* **Signature Date**: Date your signature.
