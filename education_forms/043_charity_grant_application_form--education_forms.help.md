# Charity Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Charity Grant Application Form is designed to help individuals apply for financial assistance for educational purposes. This form collects relevant information about the applicant, their educational goals, and their financial situation to determine eligibility and suitability for the grant.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
	* Email Address: Enter your active email address where you can be contacted.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
2. Provide your date of birth for eligibility purposes.
3. Enter your mailing address for contact and communication purposes.
4. Select the type of grant you are applying for:
	* Educational scholarship
	* Vocational training
	* Professional development
	* Research grant
	* Community project
	* Emergency assistance
	* Other
5. Specify the dollar amount you are requesting:
	* Enter the exact amount you are seeking for the grant.
6. Provide details about the program or course you are applying for:
	* Enter the name of the program or course you are enrolled in or plan to enroll in.
7. Enter the name of the institution or organization providing the program or course:
	* Enter the name of the school or organization offering the program or course.
8. Specify the start and end dates of the program or course:
	* Enter the start and end dates of the program or course.
9. (Optional) Enter your current or last GPA:
	* Enter your current or last GPA if applicable.
10. Select your employment status:
	* Employed full-time
	* Employed part-time
	* Unemployed
	* Student
	* Retired
	* Other
11. Select your annual household income:
	* Under $20,000
	* $20,000 to $40,000
	* $40,001 to $60,000
	* $60,001 to $80,000
	* Over $80,000
12. Describe your financial need:
	* Enter a brief statement explaining why you need the grant.
13. Describe your career goals:
	* Enter a brief statement explaining your future plans.
14. Explain how this grant will help you:
	* Enter a statement explaining the impact of the grant on your life.
15. (Optional) Enter any other funding sources:
	* List any other support or funding sources you have or plan to have.
16. (Optional) Provide professional references:
	* List the names and contacts of your professional references.
17. (Optional) Upload or indicate you can mail supporting documents:
	* Check if you have any supporting documents and if you can upload or mail them.
18. Confirm you have read and agree to the grant conditions:
	* Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm your understanding of the grant conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your active email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for eligibility purposes.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your full address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Grant Type** (`grant_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of grant you are applying for:
	+ Educational scholarship
	+ Vocational training
	+ Professional development
	+ Research grant
	+ Community project
	+ Emergency assistance
	+ Other
* **Amount Requested** (`amount_requested`, number, required): Enter the exact amount you are seeking for the grant.
* **Program or Course Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program or course you are enrolled in or plan to enroll in.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or organization offering the program course.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the program or course.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the program or course.
* **Current or Last GPA** (`gpa`, number, optional): Enter your current or last GPA if applicable.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your employment status:
	+ Employed full-time
	+ Employed part-time
	+ Unemployed
	+ Student
	+ Retired
	+ Other
* **Annual Household Income** (`household_income`, select_one, required): Select your annual household income:
	+ Under $20,000
	+ $20,000 to $40,000
	+ $40,001 to $60,000
	+ $60,001 to $80,000
	+ Over $80,000
* **Describe Your Financial Need** (`financial_need`, text, required): Enter a brief statement explaining why you need the grant.
* **Describe Your Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, required): Enter a brief statement explaining your future plans.
* **How Will This Grant Help You** (`how_will_help`, text, required): Enter a statement explaining the impact of the grant on your life.
* **Other Funding Sources** (`other_funding`, text, optional): List any other support or funding sources you have or plan to have.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, optional): List the names and contacts of your professional references.
* **Can You Provide Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, required): Check if you have any supporting documents and if you can upload or mail them.
* **Do You Agree to Terms** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm your understanding of the grant conditions.

## Tips

* Please ensure you answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any questions, contact the administrator for clarification.
* The information provided will be reviewed and considered for grant approval.
