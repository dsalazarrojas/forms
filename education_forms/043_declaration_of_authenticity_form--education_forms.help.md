# Declaration Of Authenticity Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Declaration Of Authenticity Form is used to collect information and declarations from students and their supervisors in an educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID Number" field.
3. Select the date of the declaration in the "Date" field.
4. Select the time of the declaration in the "Time" field.
5. In the "Declaration" field, write a brief statement explaining the reason for the declaration.
6. Sign the "Declaration of Authenticity Form Signature" field with your name.
7. Fill in the signature fields (Signature Field 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6) as required by your supervisor or school authorities.
8. In the "Student Signature" field, sign with your student signature.
9. In the "Teacher Signature" field, sign with your teacher's signature.
10. In the "Student Supervisor Signature" field, sign with your student supervisor's signature.
11. Add any comments in the "Declaration of Authenticity Form Comments" field.
12. Fill in the form number and version in the "Form Number" and "Form Version" fields.
13. Fill in the "Student Supervisor" field with your student supervisor's name.
14. Add any additional comments in the "Form Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the declaration.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of the declaration.
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, text, required): Write a brief statement explaining the reason for the declaration.
* **Declaration of Authenticity Form Signature** (`declaration_of_authenticity_form_signature`, text, required): Sign with your name.
* **Signature Field 1** (`signature_field_1`, date, optional): Sign with your student signature.
* **Signature Field 2** (`signature_field_2`, time, optional): Sign with your student signature.
* **Signature Field 3** (`signature_field_3`, text, optional): Sign with your student signature.
* **Signature Field 4** (`signature_field_4`, number, optional): Sign with your student signature.
* **Signature Field 5** (`signature_field_5`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" as required by your supervisor or school authorities.
* **Signature Field 6** (`signature_field_6`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" as required by your supervisor or school authorities.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Sign with your student signature.
* **Student Declaration** (`student_declaration`, text, required): Write a brief statement explaining your reason for the declaration.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, required): Sign with your teacher's signature.
* **Teacher Declaration** (`teacher_declaration`, text, required): Write a brief statement explaining your reason for the declaration.
* **Student Supervisor Signature** (`student_supervisor_signature`, text, required): Sign with your student supervisor's signature.
* **Student Supervisor Declaration** (`student_supervisor_declaration`, text, required): Write a brief statement explaining your reason for the declaration.
* **Declaration of Authenticity Form Comments** (`declaration_of_authenticity_form_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments.
* **Form Issued By** (`form_issued_by`, text, required): Fill in the form issuer's name.
* **Form Number** (`form_number`, number, required): Fill in the form number.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, number, required): Fill in the form version.
* **Student Supervisor** (`student_supervisor`, text, required): Fill in the student supervisor's name.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments.
