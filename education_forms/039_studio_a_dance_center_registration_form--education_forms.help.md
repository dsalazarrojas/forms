# studio_a_dance_center_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about students at the dance studio, including their name, date of birth, email, and phone number. It is intended for students who are new to the studio or who need to update their information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the dance studio administration for assistance.
* If you need to update any of your information, please fill out this form again with the updated information.
