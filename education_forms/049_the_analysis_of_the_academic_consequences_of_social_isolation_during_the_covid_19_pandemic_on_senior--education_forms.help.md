# The Analysis Of The Academic Consequences Of Social Isolation During The COVID 19 Pandemic On Senior - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about senior citizens who experienced social isolation during the COVID-19 pandemic. The goal is to gather data on how the pandemic affected their academic and mental well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
- If you need help or clarification on any question, do not hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Senior Information (1)** (`demographic_info`, text, optional)
	+ This section is meant to collect some basic demographic information about you.
* **Age (2)** (`age`, number, optional)
	+ Please enter your age.
* **Gender (3)** (`gender`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select your gender: male or female.
* **Education Level (4)** (`education_level`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Please select your education level: High or Low.
* **Previous Experience (5)** (`previous_experience`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Have you had any previous experience with social isolation? Yes or No.
* **Research Areas (6)** (`research_areas`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Are you interested in research related to the COVID-19 pandemic? Yes or No.
* **How many hours do you spend studying in a day? (7)** (`question1`, number, optional)
	+ Please enter the number of hours you spend studying in a day.
* **How many hours do you spend watching TV? (8)** (`question2`, number, optional)
	+ Please enter the number of hours you spend watching TV.
* **What time of day do you usually study? (9)** (`question3`, time, optional)
	+ Please enter the time of day you usually study.
* **Do you have a job or attend school? (10)** (`question4`, select_one, required)
	+ Please select if you have a job or attend school: Yes, No, or Other.
* **How much time do you usually spend on social media? (11)** (`question5`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Please select the time you usually spend on social media: Yes or No.
* **How many hours do you usually sleep in a day? (12)** (`question6`, number, required)
	+ Please enter the number of hours you usually sleep in a day.
* **Have you been to see a mental health professional? (13)** (`question7`, select_one, optional)
	+ Please select if you have been to see a mental health professional: Yes or No.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required questions.
- If a question does not apply to you, select the most suitable option.
- If you need help or clarification, do not hesitate to ask.
