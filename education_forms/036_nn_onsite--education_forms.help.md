# nn_onsite - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to on-site educational programs or events, including student details and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and understand the information being requested.
2. Answer each question or select the relevant options according to your needs.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_list** (`student_list`, select multiple, optional): Select all students who are present at the on-site educational program or event.
* **student_name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student.
* **student_name_2** (`student_name_2`, text, optional): Enter an additional name of a student (if applicable).
* **student_list_2** (`student_list_2`, select multiple, optional): Select all students who are present at the on-site educational program or event (if different from student_list).
* **parent_list** (`parent_list`, select multiple, optional): Select all parents or guardians of the students.
* **date_1** (`date_1`, date, optional): Enter a date related to the on-site educational program or event (if applicable).
* **time_1** (`time_1`, time, optional): Enter a time related to the on-site educational program or event (if applicable).
* **date_2** (`date_2`, date, optional): Enter an additional date related to the on-site educational program or event (if applicable).
* **time_2** (`time_2`, time, optional): Enter an additional time related to the on-site educational program or event (if applicable).
* **note_1** (`note_1`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the on-site educational program or event.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number related to the on-site educational program or event.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter an email address related to the on-site educational program or event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all necessary information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check your selections in the multiple-choice fields to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the relevant instructions or seek clarification from the form administrator.
