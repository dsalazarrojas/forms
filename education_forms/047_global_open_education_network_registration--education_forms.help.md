# Global Open Education Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Open Education Network Registration form is designed to gather information from users who are interested in becoming a member of the Global Open Education Network. The purpose of this form is to capture the essential details about the user and ensure that they have a clear understanding of their role and responsibilities as a member of the Global Open Education Network.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the "Member Details" page.
2. Fill out the required fields, including any additional information as requested.
3. Proceed to the "Interests" page and select your areas of interest.
4. Review and confirm your consent to participate.
5. Enter your email address and phone number on the respective fields.
6. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details (member_details)**:
	+ This field captures your basic member information.
	+ Please enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Interests (interests)**:
	+ Select all the areas where you have an interest in.
	+ You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **Consent (consent)**:
	+ Confirm that you are willing to participate in the Global Open Education Network.
* **Email (email)**:
	+ Enter your valid email address that you use to communicate.
* **Phone (phone)**:
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Phone Type (phone_type)**:
	+ (This field is not explicitly defined in the YAML, so I assumed it's a field that could potentially be included in the future. If this is incorrect, please disregard this explanation.)

## Tips
1. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding to the next page.
2. Double-check the selected fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
