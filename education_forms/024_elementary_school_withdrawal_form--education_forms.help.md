# Elementary School Withdrawal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students and their parents when a student wants to withdraw from the institution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the student's information in the `student_name` field.
2. Enter the student's address in the `student_address` field.
3. Provide the parent's name and contact information in the `parent_name` and `contact_information` fields.
4. Explain the reason for the student's withdrawal in the `reason_for_withdrawal` field.
5. Date and sign the form on the `signature_date` field.
6. Sign the form as a school administrative staff member in the `school_administrative_staff_signature` field.
7. If someone else signs on behalf of the school, sign the form as such in the `other_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their student ID or records.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's home address or current address.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the parent's name, phone number, and email address for easy contact.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Reason for Withdrawal**: Provide a clear and concise explanation of why the student is withdrawing from the institution.
* **Signature Date**: Date and sign the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **School Administrative Staff Signature**: Sign the form as a school administrative staff member to confirm that the withdrawal process has started.
* **Other Signature**: If someone else signs on behalf of the school, enter their name and title below.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide a clear explanation for the reason of withdrawal.
* Ensure that all fields are accurately filled out.
* Sign and date the form before submitting it.
* Submit this form to the relevant school personnel for processing.
