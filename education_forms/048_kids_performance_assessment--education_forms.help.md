# Kids Performance Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kids Performance Assessment form is used to track and document the progress and performance of children in an educational setting. The form provides a structured format for teachers, parents, and administrators to record observations, comments, and ratings on a child's academic, social, and behavioral development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant sections to complete for each child, as indicated by the checkboxes at the top of each page.
2. Enter any teacher notes in the **Teacher Notes** field, providing a brief summary of the child's performance and any relevant comments.
3. Select the level of **Academic Performance** the child has demonstrated.
4. For **Behavior**, select all applicable options that describe the child's behavior in the classroom or educational setting.
5. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Social Skills**, indicating whether the child has demonstrated social skills such as sharing, cooperation, or empathy.
6. Enter any comments from parents in the **Comments From Parents** field, providing feedback on the child's performance and any specific areas of concern or praise.
7. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Comments From Teacher**, **Comments From Tutor**, **Comments From Admin**, **Comments From Child**, and **Comments From Parent**, indicating whether the relevant parties have provided comments.
8. Enter any additional comments from the teacher in the **Teacher Comments** field.
9. Rate the child's **Academic Performance Score**, on a scale of 1-10.
10. Select the level of **Behavior Level** the child has demonstrated, choosing between High or Low.
11. Select the level of **Social Skills Level** the child has demonstrated, choosing between High or Low.
12. Enter any additional comments from the admin in the **Comments From Admin** field.
13. Enter any comments from the child in the **Comments From Child** field.
14. Enter any comments from the parent in the **Comments From Parent** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Notes**: A field for the teacher to record any notes or comments about the child's performance.
* **Academic Performance**: Select the level of academic performance demonstrated by the child (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Behavior**: Select all applicable options that describe the child's behavior in the classroom or educational setting (e.g., Cooperative, Uncooperative, Distracted).
* **Social Skills**: Select whether the child has demonstrated social skills such as sharing, cooperation, or empathy (Yes/No).
* **Comments From Parents**: A field for parents to provide feedback on the child's performance and any specific areas of concern or praise.
* **Comments From Teacher**: A field for the teacher to provide additional comments on the child's performance (Yes/No).
* **Comments From Tutor**: A field for the tutor to provide comments on the child's performance (Yes/No).
* **Comments From Admin**: A field for the admin to provide comments on the child's performance (Yes/No).
* **Comments From Child**: A field for the child to provide comments on their own performance (Yes/No).
* **Comments From Parent**: A field for the parent to provide comments on their child's performance (Yes/No).
* **Academic Performance Score**: Rate the child's academic performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Behavior Level**: Select the level of behavior demonstrated by the child (High/Low).
* **Social Skills Level**: Select the level of social skills demonstrated by the child (High/Low).
* **Comments From Admin**: A field for the admin to provide comments on the child's performance (Yes/No).
* **Comments From Child**: A field for the child to provide comments on their own performance (Yes/No).
* **Comments From Parent**: A field for the parent to provide comments on their child's performance (Yes/No).
