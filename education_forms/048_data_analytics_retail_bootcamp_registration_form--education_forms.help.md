# Data Analytics Retail Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in the Data Analytics Retail Bootcamp. Please complete the form to provide your general information, address, contact information, course information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select the submit option at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant general information you think is necessary for our team to have a successful and comfortable experience at the bootcamp.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Please enter your home or office address so we can reach you for any important announcements or last-minute updates.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Provide your email address or phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Course Information** (`course_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific interests or goals related to data analytics, let us know so we can tailor the course to meet your needs.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any financial concerns or questions about the bootcamp, please enter any relevant payment information here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Submit" to finalize your registration. If you need to cancel or have any issues, please select "Other" and our team will be in touch with you.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use a valid email address and phone number for contact purposes.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our team.
