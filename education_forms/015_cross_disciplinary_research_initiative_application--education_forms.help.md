# Cross Disciplinary Research Initiative Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "Cross Disciplinary Research Initiative Application" form, helping you provide the necessary information for your project review.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the university where the research project will take place in the "University Name" field.
2. Select relevant research areas of interest from the available options in the "Research Areas of Interest" field. If none of the listed options apply, choose "Other" and provide a brief description.
3. Enter the name and institution of the research team lead in the "Research Team Lead" and "Collaborators Institution" fields, respectively.
4. List the team members in the "Research Team Members" field, providing a name and institution for each member.
5. In the "Project Description" field, provide a clear and concise description of the project, including its goals and objectives.
6. Specify the source of funding for the project in the "Project Funding Source" field. If other than the listed options, select "Other" and provide a description.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the project in the "Project Start Date" and "Project End Date" fields, respectively.
8. Select the location(s) where the project will take place in the "Project Location" field. If none of the listed options apply, choose "Other" and provide a description.
9. Choose relevant research areas of expertise from the available options in the "Research Areas of Expertise" field. If none of the listed options apply, choose "Other" and provide a description.
10. Enter the names of collaborators in the "Collaborators Names" field, providing each name separately.
11. Enter the institution(s) of the collaborators in the "Collaborators Institution" field, providing each institution separately.
12. Select the amount of research funds requested in the "Research Funds Requested" field. If other than the listed options, choose "Other" and provide a justification.
13. In the "Research Funds Justification" field, provide a clear and concise explanation for the requested funds.
14. Finally, write a statement of collaboration with other researchers or institutions in the "Research Collaboration Statement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the university where the research project will take place.
* **Research Areas of Interest** (`research_areas_of_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select relevant research areas of interest for your project.
* **Research Team Lead** (`research_team_lead`, text, optional): Enter the name of the research team lead.
* **Research Team Members** (`research_team_members`, text, optional): List the names and institutions of team members.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of the project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Project Funding Source** (`project_funding_source`, select_one, optional): Specify the source of funding for the project. If other, choose "Other" and provide a description.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select the location(s) where the project will take place. If none of the listed options apply, choose "Other" and provide a description.
* **Research Areas of Expertise** (`research_areas_expertise`, select_multiple, optional): Choose relevant research areas of expertise for your project.
* **Collaborators Names** (`collaborators_names`, text, optional): Enter the names of collaborators, providing each name separately.
* **Collaborators Institution** (`collaborators_institution`, text, optional): Enter the institution(s) of the collaborators, providing each institution separately.
* **Research Funds Requested** (`research_funds_requested`, select_multiple, optional): Select the amount of research funds requested. If other than the listed options, choose "Other" and provide a justification.
* **Research Funds Justification** (`research_funds_justification`, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the requested funds.
* **Research Collaboration Statement** (`research_collaboration_statement`, text, optional): Write a statement of collaboration with other researchers or institutions.

Note: All fields marked as "optional" can be omitted if not applicable to your project.
