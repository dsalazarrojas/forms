# University Campus Safety Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Campus Safety Audit Form is designed to identify and track potential hazards and risks on university campuses. It provides a systematic way to report and address hazards, ensuring the safety and well-being of students, staff, and faculty.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the building ID from the options provided.
2. Enter the building name.
3. Choose the zone where the hazard was found.
4. Describe the hazard in the hazard field.
5. Enter the location where the hazard was found.
6. Select the risk level of the hazard (High, Medium, or Low).
7. List any risk factors present.
8. Provide recommended actions for mitigation.
9. Select the follow-up status (Completed, In Progress, or Not Completed).
10. Enter the follow-up date (if applicable).
11. Select the follow-up frequency (if applicable).
12. Enter the name and details of the assigned user and tool.
13. Enter the date and time the assigned user was notified.
14. Enter the date and time the hazard was completed.
15. Enter any comments or notes about the hazard.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Building ID** (`1`): Enter the unique identifier for the building where the hazard was found.
* **Building Name** (`2`): Enter the name of the building where the hazard was found.
* **Zone** (`3`): Select the zone where the hazard was found. This could be a specific area or section of the building.
* **Hazard** (`4`): Describe the hazard found, including any details or circumstances surrounding it.
* **Location** (`5`): Enter the exact location where the hazard was found.
* **Risk Level** (`6`): Select the risk level of the hazard (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Risk Factors** (`7`): List any risk factors present, such as hazardous materials or poor ventilation.
* **Recommended Actions** (`8`): Provide any recommended actions for mitigation or mitigation of the hazard.
* **Follow-up Status** (`9`): Select the current status of the follow-up action (Completed, In Progress, or Not Completed).
* **Follow-up Date** (`10`): Enter the date the follow-up was completed (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`11`): Enter the frequency of follow-up actions (if applicable).
* **Assigned User** (`14`): Enter the name and details of the user assigned to address the hazard.
* **Assigned Tool** (`15`): Enter the tool or equipment used to address the hazard.
* **Assigned Date** (`16`): Enter the date the assigned user was notified.
* **Completed Date** (`17`): Enter the date the hazard was completed.
* **Completed Time** (`18`): Enter the time the hazard was completed.
* **Comments** (`19`): Enter any comments or notes about the hazard.
* **Follow-up Status Notes** (`20`): Enter any additional notes about the follow-up status.
* **Zone ID** (`21`): Enter the unique identifier for the zone where the hazard was found.
* **Building Zone Name** (`22`): Enter the name of the zone where the hazard was found.
* **Notes** (`23`): Enter any additional notes about the hazard.
* **Submitted By** (`24`): Enter the name of the person submitting the hazard report.
* **Submitted Date** (`25`): Enter the date the hazard report was submitted.
