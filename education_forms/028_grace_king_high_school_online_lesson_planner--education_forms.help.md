## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Week (Select One)** (`week`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of week to plan for (Weekly or Multi-Weekly).
* **Lesson Plan (Text Field)** (`lesson_plan`, `text`, required/optional): This is the main content field where you can write a detailed description of the lesson plan.
* **Lesson Topic (Text Field)** (`lesson_topic`, `text`, required/optional): This is the topic of the lesson.
* **Lesson Objectives (Text Field)** (`lesson_objectives`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you can specify the learning objectives of the lesson.
