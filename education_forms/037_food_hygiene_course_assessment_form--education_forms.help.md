# Food Hygiene Course Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and assessment of the food hygiene course content, effectiveness, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Use the options provided for each question, and select only one option for "select_one" type fields.
3. For "select_multiple" type fields, select all applicable options.
4. Provide as much detail as possible in the "text" type fields.
5. Sign and date the "Signature" page to confirm that you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Welcome** (First Page)
* This is an introductory page to the form. Please review it carefully before proceeding.

### **Questions** (Second Page)
* This page lists questions related to the course content. For each question, select all that apply:
	+ **Yes**: The course addressed this topic effectively.
	+ **No**: The course did not address this topic or did not address it effectively.

### **Assessment** (Third Page)
* This page asks about your assessment of the course overall:
	+ **Yes**: The course was effective and met your expectations.
	+ **No**: The course was not effective and did not meet your expectations.

### **Evaluation** (Fourth Page)
* Please provide a written evaluation of the course, describing its strengths and weaknesses.

### **Certification** (Fifth Page)
* Please note if you have any certification or qualifications related to food hygiene.

### **Comments** (Sixth Page)
* This is an optional page for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the course.

### **Signature** (Seventh Page)
* This is the final page for you to sign and date, confirming that you have completed the form.
