## Educational Records Release Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students and educational institutions to facilitate the sharing of educational records between the two parties.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student's personal details, including their name, address, and student ID number.
2. Enter the institution's details, including their name, address, phone number, and fax number.
3. Specify the educational level you are enrolled in (e.g., High School, College, University, etc.).
4. State the reason for your request for records transfer.
5. Confirm your agreement to the sharing of your records with the institution.
6. Sign and date the form as required.
7. Have the institution's representative sign and date the form as well.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Your full name as recorded in the institution's records.
* **Address**: Your current address as recorded in the institution's records.
* **Student ID Number**: Your student ID number as provided by the institution.
* **Institution Name**: The name of the institution you are affiliated with.
* **Institution Address**: The address of the institution.
* **Institution Phone**: The phone number of the institution.
* **Institution Fax**: The fax number of the institution.
* **Educational Level**: The level of education you are currently pursuing (e.g., High School, College, University, etc.).
* **Reason for Request**: The reason why you are requesting the transfer of your educational records.
* **Institution Contact**: The contact person's name and phone number at the institution.
* **Contact Method**: The preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, Fax).
* **Student Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to share your educational records with the institution.
* **Institution Agreement**: Confirm that the institution agrees to protect your privacy according to their guidelines.
* **Student Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Institution Signature**: Sign your name as a representative of the institution to confirm their agreement.
* **Signature Date**: Date of your signature.
* **Signature Time**: Time of your signature.
* **Institution Signature Date**: Date of the institution's signature.
* **Educational Institution Name**: The name of the institution.
* **Institution Address**: The address of the institution.
* **Institution City**: The city where the institution is located.
* **Institution State**: The state where the institution is located.
* **Institution ZIP**: The postal code of the institution.
* **Institution Phone**: The phone number of the institution.
* **Institution Fax**: The fax number of the institution.

### Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Review each field carefully before signing the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the institution's contact person listed above.
