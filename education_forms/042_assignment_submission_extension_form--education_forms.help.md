# Assignment Submission Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for students to request an extension on their assignment submission. It helps instructors evaluate and manage student extension requests effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **Student Name** (full legal name) in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your **Student ID** number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Provide your **Email Address** (school email) in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** (contact number) in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
5. Select the **Course Name** from the given options in the "Course Name" field.
6. Enter the **Course Code** for the selected course in the "Course Code" field.
7. Enter the **Instructor Name** (teacher or professor) in the "Instructor Name" field.
8. Enter the **Assignment Title** (name of assignment) in the "Assignment Title" field.
9. Provide a brief **Assignment Description** in the "Assignment Description" field.
10. Enter the **Original Due Date** (when originally due) in the "Original Due Date" field.
11. Enter the **Original Due Time** (when originally due) in the "Original Due Time" field (optional).
12. Enter the **Requested Extension Date** (new due date) in the "Requested Extension Date" field.
13. Enter the **Requested Extension Time** (new due time) in the "Requested Extension Time" field (optional).
14. Specify the **Number of Days Requested** for the extension in the "Number of Days Requested" field.
15. Select the reason for extension request from the given options in the "Reason for Extension Request" field.
16. Provide a **Detailed Explanation** of the situation in the "Detailed Explanation" field.
17. Indicate if you have **Supporting Documentation** (yes or no) in the "Supporting Documentation" field and select the type of documentation if yes.
18. Specify the **Type of Documentation** if you selected "Supporting Documentation" as "Yes - Will Attach".
19. Choose how much **Progress** you have made on the assignment from the given options in the "How Much Progress Have You Made" field.
20. Provide a description of the **Work Completed** on the assignment in the "Describe Work Completed" field.
21. Describe the **Remaining Work** to be done on the assignment in the "Describe Remaining Work" field.
22. Indicate if you have previously requested extensions for this **Course** or other courses in the "Have You Requested Extensions Before" field.
23. Enter the **Number of Previous Extensions** if applicable.
24. Confirm your **Commitment to Submitting by New Date** in the "I Commit to Submitting by New Date" field.
25. Confirm that you **Understand Late Penalties May Still Apply** in the "I Understand Late Penalties May Still Apply" field.
26. Indicate if you need **Alternative Arrangements** for submitting the assignment in the "Do You Need Alternative Arrangements" field.
27. Describe the **Alternative Arrangement Details** if needed.
28. Select the **Instructor Approval** status (approved, denied, or pending review) from the given options in the "Instructor Approval" field.
29. Provide any **Instructor Comments** in the "Instructor Comments" field (optional).
30. Enter the **Request Date** (today's date) in the "Request Date" field.
31. Type your **Student Signature** in the "Student Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Student ID** (Student ID, text, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Provide your school email.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Course Name** (Course Name, select, required): Select the course name from the given options.
* **Course Code** (Course Code, text, required): Enter the course code for the selected course.
* **Instructor Name** (Instructor Name, text, required): Enter the name of your teacher or professor.
* **Assignment Title** (Assignment Title, text, required): Enter the name of the assignment.
* **Assignment Description** (Assignment Description, text, required): Provide a brief description of the assignment.
* **Original Due Date** (Original Due Date, date, required): Enter the original due date when the assignment was due.
* **Original Due Time** (Original Due Time, time, optional): Enter the original due time when the assignment was due.
* **Requested Extension Date** (Requested Extension Date, date, required): Enter the new due date you are requesting.
* **Requested Extension Time** (Requested Extension Time, time, optional): Enter the new due time you are requesting.
* **Number of Days Requested** (Number of Days Requested, number, required): Specify the number of days you are requesting for the extension.
* **Reason for Extension Request** (Reason for Extension Request, select_one, required): Select the reason for your extension request from the given options.
* **Detailed Explanation** (Detailed Explanation, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation of the situation.
* **Supporting Documentation** (Supporting Documentation, select_one, required): Indicate if you have supporting documentation (yes or no) and select the type of documentation if yes.
* **Type of Documentation** (Type of Documentation, text, optional): Specify the type of documentation if you selected "Supporting Documentation" as "Yes - Will Attach".
* **How Much Progress Have You Made** (How Much Progress Have You Made, select_one, required): Choose how much progress you have made on the assignment from the given options.
* **Describe Work Completed** (Describe Work Completed, text, required): Describe the work you have completed on the assignment.
* **Describe Remaining Work** (Describe Remaining Work, text, required): Describe the remaining work to be done on the assignment.
* **Have You Requested Extensions Before** (Have You Requested Extensions Before, select_one, required): Indicate if you have previously requested extensions for this course or other courses.
* **Number of Previous Extensions** (Number of Previous Extensions, number, optional): Enter the number of previous extensions if applicable.
* **I Commit to Submitting by New Date** (I Commit to Submitting by New Date, select_one, required): Confirm your commitment to submitting by the new date.
* **I Understand Late Penalties May Still Apply** (I Understand Late Penalties May Still Apply, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand late penalties may still apply.
* **Do You Need Alternative Arrangements** (Do You Need Alternative Arrangements, select_one, required): Indicate if you need alternative arrangements for submitting the assignment.
* **Alternative Arrangement Details** (Alternative Arrangement Details, text, optional): Describe the alternative arrangement details if needed.
* **Instructor Approval** (Instructor Approval, select_one, optional): Select the instructor approval status from the given options.
* **Instructor Comments** (Instructor Comments, text, optional): Provide any instructor comments.
* **Request Date** (Request Date, date, required): Enter the request date (today's date).
* **Student Signature** (Student Signature, text, required): Type your student signature in the field.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Provide all necessary supporting documentation and detailed explanations.
* Confirm your commitment to submitting by the new date.
* Understand that late penalties may still apply.
* Indicate if you need alternative arrangements.
* Describe the alternative arrangement details if needed.
* Select the instructor approval status and provide any comments.
* Enter the request date accurately.
* Type your student signature correctly.
