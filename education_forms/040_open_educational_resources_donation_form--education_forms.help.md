# Open Educational Resources Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who wish to donate educational resources to institutions or organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed:

1.  Fill out your personal details: 
    * Enter your First Name.
    * Enter your Last Name.
2.  Provide your contact information: 
    * Enter your Email address.
3.  Enter the details of your donation: 
    * Enter a valid Donation Amount (if donating).
    * Provide a Description of the resource you are willing to donate.
    * Enter your Institution(s) you wish to donate to.
    * Select an institution from the list or add your own institution (if not listed).
4.  Set a date and time for the donation: 
    * Enter a valid Date for the donation.
    * Enter a valid Time for the donation.
5.  Add any Note(s) (optional): 
    * Enter a note or comment regarding the donation.
6.  Submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to share, enter it here.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are willing to donate.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the resource you are willing to donate.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required): Enter the institution(s) you would like to donate to.
* **Select an institution** (`select_institution`, select_one, required): Select an institution from the list to donate to.
* **Select an other institution** (`select_other_institution`, select_multiple, optional): Add any other institution you would like to donate to, if not listed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter a valid date for the donation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter a valid time for the donation.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any note or comment regarding the donation.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Finalize your submission.
