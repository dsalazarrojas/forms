# University Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students applying for admission to the university. It is essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure that your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each section carefully and fill out the fields accordingly. If you are unsure about any field, please ask our admission officer for clarification. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, Email, Required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format of dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Address** (`student_address`, Text, Required): Enter your current address.
* **Country of Citizenship** (`country_of_citizenship`, Select Multiple, Required): Select your country of origin.
* **Country of Residence** (`country_of_residence`, Text, Required): Enter the country where you currently reside.
* **Student Program** (`student_program`, Select One, Required): Choose the program you are applying for (Undergraduate, Postgraduate, or Diploma).
* **Degree** (`student_degree`, Text, Required): Enter your degree type (e.g., Bachelor, Master, etc.).
* **Admission Type** (`admission_type`, Text, Required): Explain the reason for your admission type.
* **Admission Officer** (`admission_officer`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your admission officer.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Application Fee** (`application_fee`, Number, Required): Enter the fee associated with your application.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, Select One, Required): Choose the status of your application (Pending, Rejected, or Approved).
* **Notes** (`application_notes`, Note, Required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, Number, Required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student ID Type** (`student_id_type`, Text, Required): Explain the type of your student ID.

Note: This help guide assumes that all fields are required. However, in actuality, some fields might be optional. Please refer to the form's fields for the correct information.
