# Diffraction Order Measurement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect measurements and data related to diffraction experiments or studies. It is intended to record participant information and diffraction-specific measurements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully to understand the fields and their purpose.
2. Enter your participant's name in the "participant-name" field.
3. Enter your school affiliation in the "school" field.
4. Enter the location of the experiment or study in the "location" field.
5. Measure and enter the diffraction angle of incidence (angle-incident).
6. Measure and enter the diffraction angle of scattering (angle-scattered).
7. Measure and enter the diffraction index (diffraction-index), if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a note field for any additional information or context related to the experiment or study.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as the participant or person conducting the diffraction experiment or study.
* **School**: Enter your school affiliation or institution where the experiment or study was conducted.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the diffraction experiment or study was conducted.
* **Diffraction Angle**: This field is used to record the angle of incidence and scattering in your diffraction measurement.
* **Angle of Incidence**: Measure and enter the angle of incidence in your diffraction measurement.
* **Angle of Scattering**: Measure and enter the angle of scattering in your diffraction measurement.
* **Diffraction Index**: Enter any relevant diffraction index measurement or result from your experiment or study.
