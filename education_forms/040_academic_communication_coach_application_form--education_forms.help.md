# Academic Communication Coach Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants for the Academic Communication Coach position. It will be used to assess your qualifications, experience, and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your personal information, including your full name, email address, phone number, and current university or institutional affiliation.
2. Select your highest degree earned and field of study.
3. Choose your years of teaching or coaching experience and the areas you are qualified to coach in.
4. Describe your coaching philosophy and approach, including how you help students improve their academic communication skills.
5. Indicate your preferred coaching format (in-person, online, or hybrid) and availability for coaching sessions.
6. Select your preferred populations to work with (undergraduate students, graduate students, international students, faculty and staff, or all populations).
7. Provide references (name and title of the reference, email address, and any additional information about your reference).
8. Finally, provide any additional information you would like to share about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
- **Full name**: This is the name you will be known by within the institution. Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear on any university materials, such as business cards or website listings.
- **Email address**: This is the email address that will be used for communication with you. Please ensure it is a valid and active email address.
- **Phone number**: This is your contact number for scheduling and other purposes.
- **Current university or institutional affiliation**: This is the name of your current or previous institution where you were affiliated with.
- **Department**: This is the department or program you are currently or have been affiliated with.

### Qualifications and Experience
- **Highest degree earned**: This refers to the highest academic degree you have achieved.
- **Field of study**: This is the field or discipline of your academic study or research.
- **Teaching or coaching experience**: Select the number of years you have experience in teaching or coaching.
- **Coaching areas**: This is where you indicate the areas where you are qualified to provide coaching services in. You can select all that apply.
- **Relevant certifications or training**: If you have any relevant certifications or training, please list them here.
- **Relevant certifications or training**: You can list any additional certifications or training you have.

### Coaching Approach
- **Describe your coaching philosophy and approach**: Please provide a brief description of how you help students improve their academic communication skills.

### Availability
- **Availability for coaching sessions**: Select your availability for coaching sessions (full-time, part-time, limited).

### References
- **Reference 1 - Name and title**: This is the name and title of your reference, ideally an academic or professional mentor.
- **Reference 1 - Email**: This is the email address of your reference.

### Additional Information
- **Any additional information you would like to share**: This is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application.
