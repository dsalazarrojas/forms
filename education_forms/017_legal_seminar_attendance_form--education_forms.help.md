# legal_seminar_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The legal seminar attendance form is used to capture information from attendees of a legal seminar. This form is necessary for registration and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal and professional information.
2. Enter the seminar-specific details such as title, date, time, and address.
3. Verify your registration date and the name of the person who registered you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written in your identification documents.
### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written in your identification documents.
### Email
* **Email**: Enter your official email address.
### Company Name
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
### Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
### Seminar Title
* **Seminar Title**: Enter the title of the seminar you are attending.
### Date
* **Date**: Enter the date of the seminar you are attending.
### Time
* **Time**: Enter the time of the seminar you are attending.
### Address
* **Address**: Enter the address of the seminar venue.
### Registration Date
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you registered for the seminar.
### Registered By
* **Registered By**: Enter the name of the person who registered you for the seminar.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the seminar organizers for assistance.
* Ensure that you are not re-registering if you have already completed this form for the same seminar.
