# Ethics Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your understanding of ethics, a crucial aspect of many fields, including academia and professional settings. The assessment will help gauge your knowledge on various ethical concepts and principles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. For questions with select options, choose the most suitable answer from the provided choices.
3. For questions that require a numerical or date/time response, enter the correct information.
4. If you're unsure about any question, try to provide the best answer you can.
5. Once you've completed all questions, submit the form to proceed.
