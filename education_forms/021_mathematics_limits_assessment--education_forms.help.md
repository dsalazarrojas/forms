# Mathematics Limits Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a student's understanding of various mathematical concepts. The assessment is used to gauge their confidence level in different areas of mathematics, such as calculus, geometry, algebra, and trigonometry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name.
2. Enter your email address (optional).
3. Select the date and time of the assessment.
4. Choose the mathematical concept being assessed (Calculus, Geometry, Algebra, or Trigonometry).
5. Rate your confidence level in the selected mathematical concept (High, Medium, Low).
6. Provide any additional comments you may have.
7. (Optional) Fill in the name and email of your teacher.
8. Leave any comments for the teacher (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **student_name** (Text)
* Enter your full name.

### **student_email** (Email, Optional)
* If you have an email address, enter it. This will help us contact you with any further instructions or results.

### **assessment_date** (Date)
* Enter the date of the assessment.

### **assessment_time** (Time)
* Enter the time of the assessment.

### **mathematics_concept** (Select One)
* Choose the mathematical concept being assessed (Calculus, Geometry, Algebra, or Trigonometry).

### **confidence_level** (Select Multiple)
* Rate your confidence level in the selected mathematical concept (High, Medium, Low).

### **additional_comments** (Text)
* Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the assessment.

### **teacher_name** (Text, Optional)
* If you have a teacher for this assessment, enter their name.

### **teacher_email** (Email, Optional)
* If you have a teacher for this assessment, enter their email address.

### **teacher_comments** (Text, Optional)
* If you have comments for your teacher, enter them here.

### **Tips**

* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask your teacher.
