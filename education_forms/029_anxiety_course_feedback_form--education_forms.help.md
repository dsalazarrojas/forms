# Anxiety Course Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anxiety Course Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience with the course. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved, enabling us to refine and enhance future courses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer it based on your actual experience with the course.
2. Be honest in your responses, as your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve.
3. Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask for clarification if needed.
4. Submit your feedback when complete.
