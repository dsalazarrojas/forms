# school_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from various stakeholders to assess the performance and progress of a school. It is used to identify areas of improvement and track changes over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and sections.
2. Answer each question honestly and objectively, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For select_multiple and select_one fields, choose one or more relevant options from the list.
4. For text fields, provide a clear and concise answer.
5. Submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - School Information
* **School Information (form_section_1)**: This field is for general information about the school.
### Page 2 - Staff Feedback
* **Staff Feedback (section_title_2)**: Choose one or more options to rate the school's staff performance (Positive, Negative, Neutral).
### Page 3 - Student Feedback
* **Student Feedback (section_title_3)**: Choose one option to rate the school's performance from the student's perspective (Positive, Negative, Neutral).
### Page 4 - Parent Feedback
* **Parent Feedback (parent_feedback)**: Choose one or more options to rate the school's performance from a parent's perspective (Positive, Negative, Neutral).
### Page 5 - School Information Section 5
* **School Information Section 5 (school_info_section_5)**: This field is for additional information about the school.
### Page 6 - Parent Institutional Values
* **Parent Institutional Values (parent_institutional_values)**: Choose one option to rate the school's institutional values from a parent's perspective (Positive, Negative, Neutral).
### Page 7 - Student Institutional Values
* **Student Institutional Values (student_institutional_values)**: Choose one option to rate the school's institutional values from a student's perspective (Positive, Negative, Neutral).
### Page 8 - Staff Feedback
* **Staff Feedback (staff_feedback)**: Choose one or more options to rate the school's staff performance (Positive, Negative, Neutral).

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when providing feedback.
* Provide as much detail as possible in text fields.
* If you are unsure about a question, please contact the form administrator for clarification.
