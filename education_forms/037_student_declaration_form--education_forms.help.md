# Student Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Declaration Form is a comprehensive form used to collect information about a student's personal details, contact information, attendance, and declaration. This form is used for administrative or bureaucratic purposes, such as student registration, attendance tracking, or declaration of personal information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter the student's grade in the "grade" field.
3. Enter the student's email address in the "student_email" field.
4. Enter the student's phone number in the "student_phone" field.
5. Enter the student's address in the "student_address" field.
6. Enter the parent's/guardian's name in the "parent_name" field.
7. Enter the parent's/guardian's contact information in the "parental_contact" field.
8. Enter the parent's/guardian's phone number in the "parental_phone" field.
9. Select the student's attendance status in the "student_attendance" field. You can choose from "Regular attendance", "Irregular attendance", or "Leave of Absence (LOA)".
10. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of the student's attendance in the "attendance_start_date" and "attendance_end_date" fields.
11. Indicate whether the student resides on campus or off campus in the "student_residence" field. You can choose from "Reside on Campus" or "Reside off Campus".
12. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of the student's residence in the "student_residence_start_date" and "student_residence_end_date" fields.
13. Select the student's dormitory in the "student_dormitory" field. You can choose from "Dormitory A", "Dormitory B", or "Dormitory C".
14. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of the student's dormitory stay in the "student_dormitory_start_date" and "student_dormitory_end_date" fields.
15. Enter the student's occupation in the "student_occupation" field.
16. Enter the student's declaration in the "student_declaration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (student_name, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
* **grade** (grade, number, required/optional): Enter the student's grade.
* **student_email** (student_email, email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **student_phone** (student_phone, text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **student_address** (student_address, text, required/optional): Enter the student's address.
* **parent_name** (parent_name, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's/guardian's name.
* **parental_contact** (parental_contact, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's/guardian's contact information.
* **parental_phone** (parental_phone, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's/guardian's phone number.
* **guardian_name** (guardian_name, text, required/optional): Enter the guardian's name.
* **guardian_phone** (guardian_phone, text, required/optional): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **student_signature** (student_signature, note, required/optional): Sign with your name, if you're the student.
* **parent_signature** (parent_signature, note, required/optional): Sign with your name, if you're the parent/guardian.
* **declaration_statement** (declaration_statement, text, required/optional): Enter your declaration statement.
* **student_attendance** (student_attendance, select_one, required/optional): Select your student attendance status.
* **attendance_start_date** (attendance_start_date, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your attendance, if applicable.
* **attendance_end_date** (attendance_end_date, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your attendance, if applicable.
* **student_residence** (student_residence, select_one, required/optional): Select your student residence status.
* **student_residence_start_date** (student_residence_start_date, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your residence, if applicable.
* **student_residence_end_date** (student_residence_end_date, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your residence, if applicable.
* **student_dormitory** (student_dormitory, select_one, required/optional): Select your student dormitory.
* **student_dormitory_start_date** (student_dormitory_start_date, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your dormitory stay, if applicable.
* **student_dormitory_end_date** (student_dormitory_end_date, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your dormitory stay, if applicable.
* **student_occupation** (student_occupation, text, required/optional): Enter your student occupation.
* **student_declaration** (student_declaration, note, required/optional): Enter your declaration statement.

## Tips

* Please fill in all the required fields.
* Make sure to enter accurate information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your school administration or contact person.
* Please sign the "student_signature" and "parent_signature" fields with your actual signature.
