# education-training-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information, training details, and participant answers for educational training programs. This form helps in organizing and registering participants for such programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the participant information section (Page 1).
2.  Provide details about the training session (Page 2).
3.  Enter course information (Page 3).
4.  Enter participant answers (Page 4).
5.  Click the "Submit" button (Page 5) to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info (Page 1)** (optional): This section is for entering basic participant information, such as name and contact details.
* **Training Info (Page 2)** (optional): Enter details about the training session, including the session name, date, and time.
* **Course Info (Page 3)** (optional): Enter course information, such as course title, instructor name, and description.
* **Participant Answers (Page 4)** (optional): Enter participant answers or feedback, if any.
* **Submit (Page 5)** (required): Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.
