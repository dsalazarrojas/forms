# Civil War Map Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Civil War Map Worksheet" form is an educational tool designed to help students analyze the geographical and historical context of the Civil War. It is intended to be completed by students and teachers to gather information about student and class details, and then guide users through a series of questions related to the map analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select your class period from the dropdown menu in the "Class Period" field.
3. Answer the map analysis questions in the subsequent sections, which include:
	* Listing three major Union states (North) in the "List three major Union states (North)" field.
	* Listing three major Confederate states (South) in the "List three major Confederate states (South)" field.
	* Defining a "Border State" in the "What was a "Border State" during the Civil War?" field.
	* Explaining why control of the Mississippi River was strategically important in the "Why was control of the Mississippi River strategically important?" field.
	* Identifying the state where the Battle of Gettysburg took place in the "In which state did the Battle of Gettysburg take place?" field.
	* Identifying one geographic advantage for the North in the "Identify one geographic advantage for the North" field.
	* Identifying one geographic advantage for the South in the "Identify one geographic advantage for the South" field.
	* Explaining the impact of the naval blockade (Anaconda Plan) on Southern ports in the "How did the naval blockade (Anaconda Plan) affect Southern ports?" field.
	* Rating the ease of reading the provided historical map in the "How easy was it to read the provided historical map?" field.
	* Adding any additional comments or observations in the "Any other observations from the map analysis?" field.
4. Enter the date of completion in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as a student.
* **Class Period**: Select your class period from the dropdown menu.
* **List three major Union states (North)**: List three major Union states that played a significant role in the Civil War.
* **List three major Confederate states (South)**: List three major Confederate states that played a significant role in the Civil War.
* **What was a "Border State" during the Civil War?**: Define what a "Border State" is, based on the historical context of the Civil War.
* **Why was control of the Mississippi River strategically important?**: Explain why control of the Mississippi River was important during the Civil War.
* **In which state did the Battle of Gettysburg take place?**: Identify the state where the Battle of Gettysburg took place.
* **Identify one geographic advantage for the North**: Identify one geographic advantage that the North had during the Civil War.
* **Identify one geographic advantage for the South**: Identify one geographic advantage that the South had during the Civil War.
* **How did the naval blockade (Anaconda Plan) affect Southern ports?**: Explain how the naval blockade impacted Southern ports.
* **How easy was it to read the provided historical map?**: Rate the ease of reading the map.
* **Any other observations from the map analysis?**: Add any additional comments or observations about the map analysis.
* **Date**: Enter the date of completion.
