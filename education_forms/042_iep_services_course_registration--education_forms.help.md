# iep_services_course_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The iep_services_course_registration form is designed to collect essential information from participants for course registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Information page (Page 1).
2. Enter your contact information in the Participant Details page (Page 2).
3. Describe your course possibilities and interests in the Course Possibilities page (Page 3).
4. Specify any special needs or requirements in the Special Needs page (Page 4).
5. Click on the "Submit Request" button to submit your registration request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information (Page 1)**: Enter your registration details, including your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Participant Details (Page 2)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Course Possibilities (Page 3)**: Describe the course or service you are interested in, including your level of interest and any specific goals or objectives.
* **Special Needs (Page 4)**: If you have any special needs or requirements, such as accommodations or equipment needs, please specify them here.
* **Submit Request (Page 5)**: Click on this button to submit your registration request.
