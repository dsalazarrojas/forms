# Medical Teaching Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to provide feedback on the medical teaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "First Name", "Last Name", and "Email" fields.
2. Answer the "Feedback" questions in the "Feedback", "Medical Teaching Feedback", and "Feedback on Teaching" fields.
3. Rate the effectiveness of the medical teaching in the "Rating" and "Medical Teaching Rating" fields.
4. Provide additional feedback in the "Additional Feedback" field.
5. Enter the tool used in the "Assigned Tool" field.
6. Optionally, enter any feedback on materials in the "Feedback on Materials" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Feedback** (text, required): Describe your experience with the medical teaching.
* **Medical Teaching Feedback** (text, required): Provide feedback on the effectiveness of the medical teaching.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required): Choose the tool used in the medical teaching session.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the medical teaching session (optional).
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the medical teaching session (optional).
* **Rating** (number, required): Rate the effectiveness of the medical teaching on a scale of 1-5.
* **Medical Teaching Rating** (number, required): Rate the effectiveness of the medical teaching on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback on Teaching** (text, required): Provide feedback on the teaching methods.
* **Feedback on Materials** (text, optional): Provide feedback on the teaching materials.
* **Additional Feedback** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **First Name Hint** (text, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Last Name Hint** (text, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Email Hint** (text, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Feedback Hint** (text, optional): This field is not currently used.
* **Medical Teaching Feedback Hint** (text, optional): This field is not currently used.
