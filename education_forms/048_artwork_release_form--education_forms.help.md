# Artwork Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artwork Release Form is a document that grants permission for an artwork to be used, published, or displayed. It ensures that the artist's rights are respected and that the artwork is used correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the artist's details:
	* Artist First Name: Enter the artist's first name.
	* Artist Last Name: Enter the artist's last name.
	* Artist Email Address: Enter the artist's email address.
	* Artist Phone Number: Enter the artist's phone number (if required).
2. Provide artwork details:
	* Artwork Title: Enter a brief title of the artwork.
	* Artwork Description: Provide a brief description of the artwork, including the medium and dimensions.
	* Date Artwork Was Created: Enter the date the artwork was created (if required).
	* Image or Photograph Attached: Select 'Yes' if an image or photograph of the artwork is attached.
3. Specify usage rights:
	* Rights Granted for Usage of Artwork: Select all applicable usage rights (e.g., Educational Use, Commercial Use, Print Publications).
	* Geographic Territory for Usage: Enter the location where the artwork will be used (e.g., local, national, international).
	* Duration of Usage Rights: Enter the duration of the usage rights (e.g., perpetual, limited term).
4. Confirm terms:
	* Attribution or Credit Required: Select 'Yes' if attribution or credit is required for the artist.
	* Preferred Attribution Format: Enter the preferred format for attribution (if required).
	* Modifications Allowed: Select 'Yes' if modifications to the artwork are allowed.
	* Compensation or Fees: Enter any compensation or fees agreed upon (if required).
	* Date of Release: Enter the date of release.
	* Organization or Individual Receiving Rights: Enter the organization or individual receiving the rights.
5. Sign and agree:
	* Artist Agrees to Release Terms: Select 'Yes, I Agree' to confirm that you have read and understand the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist First Name** (`artist_first_name`, text, required): Enter the artist's first name.
* **Artist Last Name** (`artist_last_name`, text, required): Enter the artist's last name.
* **Artist Email Address** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter the artist's email address.
* **Artist Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, text, optional): Enter the artist's phone number (if required).
* **Artwork Title** (`artwork_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title of the artwork.
* **Artwork Description** (`artwork_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the artwork, including the medium and dimensions.
* **Date Artwork Was Created** (`artwork_creation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the artwork was created (if required).
* **Image or Photograph Attached** (`image_or_photograph_of_work`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if an image or photograph of the artwork is attached.
* **Rights Granted for Usage of Artwork** (`usage_rights_granted`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable usage rights (e.g., Educational Use, Commercial Use, Print Publications).
* **Geographic Territory for Usage** (`geographic_territory`, text, optional): Enter the location where the artwork will be used (e.g., local, national, international).
* **Duration of Usage Rights** (`duration_of_rights`, text, required): Enter the duration of the usage rights (e.g., perpetual, limited term).
* **Attribution or Credit Required** (`attribution_required`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if attribution or credit is required for the artist.
* **Preferred Attribution Format** (`attribution_format`, text, optional): Enter the preferred format for attribution (if required).
* **Modifications Allowed** (`modifications_allowed`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if modifications to the artwork are allowed.
* **Compensation or Fees** (`compensation_or_fees`, number, optional): Enter any compensation or fees agreed upon (if required).
* **Date of Release** (`release_date`, date, required): Enter the date of release.
* **Organization or Individual Receiving Rights** (`organization_receiving_rights`, text, required): Enter the organization or individual receiving the rights.
* **Additional Terms or Conditions** (`additional_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions.
* **Artist Agrees to Release Terms** (`artist_signature`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes, I Agree' to confirm that you have read and understand the terms.

## Tips

* Be sure to review and understand the terms before signing the form.
* Fill in the artist's details accurately and completely.
* Be clear and specific when describing the artwork and its usage.
* Check the 'Image or Photograph Attached' field carefully, as it affects the usage of the artwork.
* Review the 'Rights Granted for Usage of Artwork' and 'Duration of Usage Rights' fields carefully, as they affect the usage and duration of the artwork's rights.
* Check the 'Attribution or Credit Required' field carefully, as it affects the artist's credit.
* Review the 'Modifications Allowed' field carefully, as it affects the modifications to the artwork.
* Enter accurate and complete information for all fields, as it affects the artist's rights and the artwork's usage.
