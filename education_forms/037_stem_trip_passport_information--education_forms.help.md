# stem_trip_passport_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a passport for participants on a STEM trip, collecting their personal and school contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's details as required.
2. Double-check the information before submitting the form.
3. Review the participant's details carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's full name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Participant Date of Birth (DOB)**: Enter the participant's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's school email address or a valid email address associated with them.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's school phone number or a valid phone number associated with them.
* **Participant Address**: Enter the participant's full address, including street, city, state, and country.
* **Participant School Name**: Enter the participant's school name as it appears on their school ID or records.
* **Participant School Email**: Enter the participant's school email address.
* **Participant School Phone**: Enter the participant's school phone number.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the participant's details, please contact them directly for clarification.
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions from the participant to share their information.
* Review the participant's details carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
