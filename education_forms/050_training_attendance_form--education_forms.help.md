# Training Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's attendance at a training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the attendee (Student or Employee).
2. Enter the date of the training session.
3. Enter the start and end times of the training session.
4. Enter the name of the instructor who conducted the training.
5. Enter the name of the course being attended.
6. Enter the ID of the session.
7. Add any additional notes about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee/Student** (`employee_or_student`, `select_one`, required/optional): 
	+ Choose whether the form is for an employee or a student attending the training session.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): 
	+ Enter the date of the training session in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required/optional): 
	+ Enter the start time of the training session in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required/optional): 
	+ Enter the end time of the training session in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, required/optional): 
	+ Enter the name of the instructor who conducted the training session.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, required/optional): 
	+ Enter the name of the course being attended.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, `text`, required/optional): 
	+ Enter the ID of the training session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): 
	+ Add any additional notes about the training session.
