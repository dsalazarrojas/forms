# High School Class Of 2014 Clothing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for a high school class of 2014 clothing order. It helps the class administrators to get details about the order, including the type of clothing and the date and time of collection. This form is a simple and straightforward way to gather the necessary information for the clothing order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by selecting your choice of Hoodie from the options provided.
2. Next, select your choice of T-Shirt(s) from the available options.
3. Please input your expected Date of collection.
4. Input your Time of collection.
5. If you have any additional note or information, you can type it in the Note field.
