# College Move In Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "College Move In Checklist" is a form designed for students to provide information on what they have packed and what they still need to bring during the move-in process. This helps college administrations gather accurate and complete information to facilitate a smooth transition for students.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by filling out the student and parent information sections: Student Name, Parent or Guardian Name, and Move-In Date.
*   Proceed to the Bedding and Bath section, where you will mark what items you have packed and what you still need to bring.
*   Next, move on to the Clothing section, where you will select what items you have packed and what you still need to bring for each category (underwear, shirts, etc.).
*   After that, in the Electronics and Tech section, select what electronics and items you have packed and what you still need to bring.
*   Finally, review your answers carefully and submit the form.
