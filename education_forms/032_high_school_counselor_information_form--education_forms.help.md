# High School Counselor Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and other relevant details from high school students or their representatives. It's used to keep track of student interactions and ensure that the counselor can contact them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your contact method by choosing from the options provided.
2. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Provide your full name in the "User Name" field.
5. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
6. Select your student's grade from the dropdown options.
7. Choose your user type from the options provided (Student, Parent, or Teacher).
8. You can add any additional notes in the "Notes" field if needed.
9. Select the assigned teacher from the options provided.
10. If applicable, choose additional assigned teachers from the multiple selection field.
11. Enter the date and time you submitted this form in the "Submitted On" and "Submitted By" fields.
12. Finally, review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name in this field.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one method of contact (Email, Phone, or Text).
* **Contact Method 2** (`Contact Method 2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple methods of contact (Email, Phone, or Text).
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **User Name** (`User Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's name.
* **Student Grade** (`Student Grade`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your student's grade from the dropdown options.
* **User Type** (`User Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your user type (Student, Parent, or Teacher).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes if needed.
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the assigned teacher.
* **Assigned To 2** (`Assigned To 2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple assigned teachers.
* **Submitted On** (`Submitted On`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of who submitted this form.
* **Submitted By 2** (`Submitted By 2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the name of who submitted this form (John Smith or Jane Doe).
* **Submitted On 2** (`Submitted On 2`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Submitted On 3** (`Submitted On 3`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Double-check your submission before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
* This form is for your convenience and is not required, but it helps us keep track of student interactions.
