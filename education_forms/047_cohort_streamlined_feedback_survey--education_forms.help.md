# Cohort Streamlined Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants in a cohort program, aiming to understand their satisfaction, experiences, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Participant Information**:
	* Please provide your name and email address. 
	* This section is for identification and contact purposes. If you don't feel comfortable sharing your email address, you can leave it blank.
2. **Cohort Name**:
	* Type the name of the cohort you attended.
3. **Program or Course Name**:
	* Type the name of the program or course you participated in.
4. **Overall Satisfaction**:
	* Rate your overall satisfaction with the cohort from 1 (very unsatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
5. **Program Met Expectations**:
	* Rate whether the program met your expectations from 1 (far below expectations) to 5 (far exceeded expectations).
6. **Recommend Program**:
	* Indicate whether you would recommend this program to others.
7. **Content Relevance**:
	* Rate the relevance of the material to your needs.
8. **Content Difficulty Level**:
	* Rate the difficulty level of the material.
9. **Quality of Learning Materials**:
	* Rate the quality of the learning materials.
10. **Instructor Knowledge and Expertise**:
	* Rate the instructor's knowledge and expertise.
11. **Instructor Engagement and Support**:
	* Rate the instructor's engagement and support.
12. **Course Pace**:
	* Rate whether the course pace was appropriate.
13. **Course Length**:
	* Rate whether the course length was appropriate.
14. **Learning Objectives Achievement**:
	* Rate whether you achieved the learning objectives.
15. **New Skill Development**:
	* Rate whether you developed new skills.
16. **Program Strengths**:
	* List the program's strengths.
17. **Areas for Improvement**:
	* List the areas where the program could be improved.
18. **Additional Comments**:
	* Provide any other feedback or suggestions.

Note: You can skip any section that does not apply to your experience.
