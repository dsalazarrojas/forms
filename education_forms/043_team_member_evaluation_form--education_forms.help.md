# team_member_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "team_member_evaluation_form" is a tool for assessing team member performance and skills in a project management context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. For each field, answer based on your actual experiences and observations.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. Complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **project_lead** (`project_lead`, text, required): Enter the name of the project lead.
* **project_lead_rating** (`project_lead_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the project lead's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **team_member** (`team_member`, text, required): Enter the name of the team member being evaluated.
* **team_member_rating** (`team_member_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the team member's performance (Highly, Somewhat, Not at all).

### Page 2
* **communication** (`communication`, text, required): Provide feedback on the team member's communication skills.
* **time_management** (`time_management`, text, required): Rate the team member's time management skills.
* **problem_solving** (`problem_solving`, text, required): Evaluate the team member's problem-solving skills.
* **collaboration** (`collaboration`, text, required): Assess the team member's collaboration skills.
* **initiative** (`initiative`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the team member's initiative.
* **team_player** (`team_player`, text, optional): Enter any additional feedback on the team member's performance.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or suggestions for the team member.

### Page 3
* **team_player_rating** (`team_player_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the team member's ability to work with others (Highly, Somewhat, Not at all).
* **initiative_rating** (`initiative_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the team member's initiative (Highly, Somewhat, Not at all).
* **communication_rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the team member's communication skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **time_management_rating** (`time_management_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the team member's time management skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **problem_solving_rating** (`problem_solving_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the team member's problem-solving skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **collaboration_rating** (`collaboration_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the team member's collaboration skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **feedback_rating** (`feedback_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the team member's willingness to receive feedback (Highly, Somewhat, Not at all).
* **project_lead_rating** (`project_lead_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the project lead's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **team_member_rating** (`team_member_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the team member's overall performance (Highly, Somewhat, Not at all).

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
