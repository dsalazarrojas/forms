# Responsive Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Workshop Registration Form is a tool used to collect information from workshop participants. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth and successful workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have provided all required information before submitting.
2. Enter your name in the field marked "Name".
3. Enter your email address in the field marked "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field marked "Phone".
5. If you have any further comments or suggestions, type them in the field marked "Further Comments".
6. If you have a specific date and time for the workshop, enter it in the fields marked "Date" and "Time".
7. Select one of the options for the field marked "Select One".
8. Select multiple options for the field marked "Select Multiple".
9. Enter a brief description of the workshop in the field marked "Workshop".
9. Finally, select "Send" to submit your registration or "Cancel" to exit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Header** (`Header`, , not required): This is the header of the form that contains general information about the workshop.
* **Workshop Description** (`workshop_description`, , not required): Enter a brief description of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Name** (`name`, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, , not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, not required): If you have any further comments or suggestions, type them here.
* **Further Comments** (`further_comments`, not required): If you have any additional comments, type them here.
* **Date** (`date`, , not required): Select the date for the workshop, if you have a specific date in mind.
* **Time** (`time`, not required): Select the time for the workshop, if you have a specific time in mind.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, not required): Select one option from the list of available options.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select multiple options from the list of available options.
* **Workshop** (`workshop`, not required): Enter a brief description of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Submit** (`submit`, not required): Select "Send" to submit your registration or "Cancel" to exit the form.
