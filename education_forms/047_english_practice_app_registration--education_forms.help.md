# english_practice_app_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a user for the English practice application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Details section with your name, email, and other relevant information.
2. Provide your organization details, including their name, address, and phone number.
3. Enter your teacher's details, including their name, email, phone number, subject, education level, teaching experience, and language proficiency.
4. Accept the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" in the "User Accept" field.
5. Review your answers carefully and submit the form by selecting "Yes" in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**
	+ **User Email**: Please enter your email address.
	+ **User Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Organization Details**
	+ **Name**: Please enter your organization's name.
	+ **Address**: Please enter your organization's address.
	+ **Phone**: Please enter your organization's phone number.
* **Teacher Details**
	+ **Name**: Please enter your teacher's name.
	+ **Email**: Please enter your teacher's email address.
	+ **Phone**: Please enter your teacher's phone number.
	+ **Subject**: Please enter the subject your teacher teaches.
	+ **Education Level**: Please select your teacher's education level.
	+ **Teaching Experience**: Please select your teacher's teaching experience.
	+ **Language**: Please select whether your teacher is proficient in English.
* **User Accept**
	+ **User Accept**: Please select "Yes" if you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Submit**
	+ **Submit**: Please review your answers carefully and select "Yes" to submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team.
