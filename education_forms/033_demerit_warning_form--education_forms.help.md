# Demerit Warning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and record warnings given to students for misbehavior or misconduct.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the warning incident, including the circumstances and actions taken.
2. Enter the student's name and ID number.
3. Select the type of warning that was given (e.g. Misconduct, Rule Violation, Other).
4. Enter the student's date of birth.
5. Provide the student's contact details.
6. Enter the date and time the warning was given.
7. Identify who gave the warning.
8. Indicate the current status of the warning (e.g. Open, Closed, Resolved).
9. Enter the reason for resolving the warning.
10. Enter the date the warning was resolved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warning Details** (id: 1): Enter a brief description of the incident that warranted the warning.
* **Student Name** (id: 2): Enter the student's name to identify them in the system.
* **Student ID** (id: 3): Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
* **Warning Type** (id: 4): Select one of the following options:
	+ Misconduct: for serious misbehavior that requires disciplinary action.
	+ Rule Violation: for minor infractions of school rules.
	+ Other: for any other type of warning not listed above.
* **Description** (id: 5): Enter a detailed description of the incident and actions taken.
* **Student Date of Birth** (id: 6): Enter the student's date of birth to ensure accurate identification.
* **Contact Details** (id: 7): Enter the student's contact details, such as phone number or email.
* **Date Warning Given** (id: 8): Enter the date the warning was given.
* **Time Warning Given** (id: 9): Enter the time the warning was given.
* **Warning By** (id: 10): Identify the person who gave the warning.
* **Warning Status** (id: 11): Select one of the following options:
	+ Open: the warning has been issued but not yet resolved.
	+ Closed: the warning has been resolved.
	+ Resolved: the warning has been resolved, but may still be pending further action.
* **Reason for Resolution** (id: 12): Enter a detailed description of why the warning was resolved.
* **Resolution Date** (id: 13): Enter the date the warning was resolved.
* **Resolution By** (id: 14): Identify the person who resolved the warning.
