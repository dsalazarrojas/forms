# Language Course Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience in a language course. Your input will help us improve the course content, structure, and overall learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions in the corresponding sections, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as *required*), even if you need to provide a brief response.
3. For optional fields, you can choose not to answer if you don't feel it's relevant to your experience.
4. For text fields (e.g., "course_content" and "course_improvement"), feel free to provide as much detail as you like.
5. For select_one and select_multiple fields (e.g., "instructor_performance" and "instructor_support"), choose one or multiple options that best match your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the enrollment of the course?** (`course_enrollment`, number, required/optional): Please indicate how many students were enrolled in the course. This will help us understand the course's demand and popularity.
* **What was your understanding of the course content?** (`course_content`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on what you expected from the course and what you actually learned. Be as specific as possible.
* **How would you rate the instructor's performance?** (`instructor_performance`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the instructor's teaching style, effectiveness, and engagement. Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How well did the instructor support you during the course?** (`instructor_support`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how much support you received from the instructor, such as providing clear instructions, answering questions, or offering guidance. Choose one or multiple options:
	+ Very well
	+ Somewhat well
	+ Not very well
* **Do you have any additional comments on the course?** (`feedback_comments`, note, optional): Share any thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you have about the course. This is your chance to provide more in-depth feedback.
* **What could be improved on the course?** (`course_improvement`, text, optional): Offer specific suggestions on how the course could be improved, such as content, structure, or resources. Be as concrete as possible.
* **Would you take the course again?** (`would_take_again`, select_one, optional): Let us know if you would enroll in the course again, and why or why not. Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
