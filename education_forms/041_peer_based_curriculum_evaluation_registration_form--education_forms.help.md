# Peer Based Curriculum Evaluation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from peers who have reviewed a curriculum. The information collected will help improve the quality and effectiveness of the curriculum.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) with your information.
2. Enter your peer-reviewer name, institution name, and other contact information in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter the title of the curriculum being evaluated.
4. Select the date and time of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional): A field for any additional comments or notes about the curriculum.
* **Peer-Reviewer Name** (`peer_reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the peer reviewer.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution where you reviewed the curriculum.
* **Curriculum Title** (`curriculum_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the curriculum being evaluated.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you evaluated the curriculum.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time on which you evaluated the curriculum.

## Tips
* Please be specific and honest in your feedback to help improve the curriculum.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the registration form field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
