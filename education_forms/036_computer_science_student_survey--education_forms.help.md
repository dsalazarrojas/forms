# Computer Science Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Computer Science Student Survey is designed to gather feedback from students about their experiences with courses and resources in the computer science program. This feedback will help instructors understand student satisfaction, resource availability, and workload balance, as well as identify areas where students need additional support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the link to access the form.
2. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
3. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): This field is optional and is used to collect information about the student's name. If you wish to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.
* **Year of study** (`year_of_study`, `select_one`, required): Choose your year of study from the dropdown menu to indicate your level of study (e.g., Undergraduate 1, Undergraduate 2, etc.).
* **Course enrolled** (`course_enrolled`, `text`, required): Enter the code or title of the course you are currently enrolled in or have completed recently.
* **Satisfaction with instruction** (`satisfaction_with_instruction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the instruction received in your course by selecting one of the following options:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Resource availability** (`resource_availability`, `select_one`, required): Choose an option that best reflects your access to resources and equipment for your course, such as:
	+ Always
	+ Mostly
	+ Sometimes
	+ Never
* **Workload balance** (`workload_balance`, `select_one`, required): Choose an option that best describes your perception of your workload, such as:
	+ Manageable
	+ Heavy
	+ Overwhelming
* **Areas for support** (`areas_for_support`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information or topics where you think you need extra support or guidance.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses to provide accurate and helpful feedback.
* Please answer all required fields to ensure that your feedback is collected accurately.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the computer science program, please include them in the "Areas for support" field.
