# Student Technology Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students about their technology needs. This will help teachers and school administrators understand what technologies students require to succeed in their academic journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. Enter your teacher's name in the "What is the name of your teacher" field.
3. List the technologies you need in the "What technology do you need" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "What is your email" field.
5. Enter your teacher's email address in the "What is your teacher's email" field.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "What is your phone number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your name.
* **What is the name of your teacher**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your teacher's name.
* **What technology do you need**:
	+ A text field where you can list the technologies you require.
* **What is your email**:
	+ An email field where you can enter your email address.
* **What is your teacher's email**:
	+ An email field where you can enter your teacher's email address.
* **What is your phone number**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate information.
- If you need any technology, list all technologies you need.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your teacher or school administrator.
