# Postgraduate Presentation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of postgraduate students or speakers in a presentation setting. It assesses their presentation skills, organization, communication skills, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the presentation and fill out each field accordingly.
2. Rate each field using the options provided (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3. Provide written comments for each field to support your rating.
4. Ensure that you are evaluating a different presenter each time you submit this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a brief summary of the introduction made by the presenter. This should include any notable comments or observations you have about their opening statement.
* **Overall** (`2`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate the overall performance of the presenter on a scale of 1-10. This should include your overall impression of their presentation.
* **Presentation Content** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the content of the presentation. This should include your observations about the clarity, coherence, and effectiveness of the content.
* **Communication Skills** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Rate the communication skills of the presenter using the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor). This should include their ability to convey their message clearly and effectively.
* **Audience Engagement** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the engagement of the audience with the presenter. This should include any notable comments or observations you have about their ability to connect with the audience.
* **Organization** (`6`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate the organization of the presentation on a scale of 1-10. This should include their ability to structure and sequence their ideas.
* **Confidence** (`7`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate the confidence of the presenter on a scale of 1-10. This should include their ability to convey self-assurance and conviction.
* **Creativity** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Rate the creativity of the presentation using the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor). This should include their ability to think outside the box and come up with innovative ideas.
* **Knowledge Transfer** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the presenter's ability to transfer their knowledge to the audience.
* **Body Language** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the body language of the presenter. This should include any notable comments or observations you have about their nonverbal cues.
* **Verbal Communication** (`11`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the verbal communication skills of the presenter.
* **Nonverbal Communication** (`12`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the nonverbal communication skills of the presenter.
* **Organization (Presenter)** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Rate the self-organization of the presenter using the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor). This should include their ability to structure and sequence their ideas.
* **Time Management** (`14`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate the time management of the presenter on a scale of 1-10. This should include their ability to stick to the allotted time.
* **Self-Confidence** (`15`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate the self-confidence of the presenter on a scale of 1-10. This should include their ability to convey self-assurance and conviction.
* **Self-Presentation** (`16`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the self-presentation of the presenter.
* **Self-Organization** (`17`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the self-organization of the presenter.
* **Self-Time Management** (`18`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the self-time management of the presenter.
* **Self-Knowledge Transfer** (`19`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the self-knowledge transfer of the presenter.
* **Self-Body Language** (`20`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the self-body language of the presenter.
* **Self-Verbal Communication** (`21`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the self-verbal communication of the presenter.
* **Self-Nonverbal Communication** (`22`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the self-nonverbal communication of the presenter.
* **Teamwork** (`23`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the presenter's ability to work with a team.
* **Adaptability** (`24`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the presenter's ability to adapt to new situations.
* **Leadership** (`25`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a written comment about the presenter's leadership skills.

## Tips
* Please be honest and objective in your ratings and comments.
* Please provide specific examples and supporting evidence for each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or mentor.
* Remember to submit only one form per presentation, and make sure to evaluate a different presenter each time.
