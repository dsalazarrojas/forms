# Educational Outreach Impact Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Outreach Impact Report Form is used to track the progress and outcomes of educational outreach programs. It helps in evaluating the effectiveness of these programs and identifying areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that you do not submit duplicate reports for the same school.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the form's administrator for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name (school_name)**: Enter the name of the school where the outreach program was conducted.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date on which the program was conducted.
* **School Type (school_type)**: Select the type of school (Public or Private).
* **Number of Students (number_of_students)**: Enter the number of students who participated in the program.
* **School Address (school_address)**: Enter the address of the school where the program was conducted.
* **Program Name (program_name)**: Enter the name of the program being reported.
* **Outcome Measures (outcome_measures)**: Indicate whether the program had any outcome measures (Yes or No).
* **Outcomes (outcomes)**: Enter any additional outcomes or comments about the program.
* **Follow-up Plan (follow_up)**: Enter the date for the follow-up plan.
* **Follow-up Status (follow_up_status)**: Indicate the status of the follow-up plan (True, False, or Not Done Yet).
* **Next Steps (next_steps)**: Select the next steps for the program (Continue, Discontinue, or Not Done Yet).
* **Notes (notes)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the program.
* **Assigned To (assigned_to)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the program.
* **Created By (created_by)**: This field is automatically populated by the system and is not editable.
* **Created At (created_at)**: This field is automatically populated by the system and is not editable.
* **Updated At (updated_at)**: This field is automatically populated by the system and is not editable.
* **Updated By (updated_by)**: This field is automatically populated by the system and is not editable.
* **Updated At 2 (updated_at_2)**: This field is automatically populated by the system and is not editable.
* **School Type 2 (school_type_2)**: Select the type of school (School type, Public, or Private).
* **Number Of Students 2 (number_of_students_2)**: Enter the number of students who participated in the program.
* **School Address 2 (school_address_2)**: Enter the address of the school where the program was conducted.
* **Program Name 2 (program_name_2)**: Enter the name of the program being reported.
* **Outcome Measures 2 (outcome_measures_2)**: Indicate whether the program had any outcome measures (Yes or No).
* **Outcomes 2 (outcomes_2)**: Enter any additional outcomes or comments about the program.
* **Follow Up 2 (follow_up_2)**: Enter the date for the follow-up plan.
* **School Type 2 (school_type_2)**: Select the type of school (School type, Public, or Private).
