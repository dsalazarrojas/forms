# Campus Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help campus administrators schedule events such as lectures, meetings, sports events, and other campus activities in a efficient and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit Event" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Select the category of your event from the "Type of Event" dropdown.
3.  Enter the name of your event in the "Event Name" field.
4.  Choose your preferred date from the calendar view.
5.  Select the start and end times for your event.
6.  Choose the location of your event from the available options.
7.  If necessary, specify the room or area for your event.
8.  Estimate the number of attendees for your event.
9.  Enter the name, email, and phone number of the primary contact person.
10. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Name** (`Event Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your event or activity.
*   **Type of Event** (`Type of Event`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best fits your event, such as Academic Lecture, Student Club Meeting, Sports Event, Social Gathering, Workshop or Training, or Other.
*   **Organization/Department Name** (`Organization or Department Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the campus organization or department hosting the event.
*   **Requested Date** (`Requested Date`, `date`, required): Select your preferred date for the event.
*   **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, required): Select the time when you would like the event to begin.
*   **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, required): Select the time when you would like the event to conclude.
*   **Preferred Location** (`Preferred Location`, `select_one`, required): Choose the location on campus where you would like to hold the event, such as Student Center, Gymnasium, Auditorium, Classroom Building, Outdoor Lawn, Virtual/Online, or Other (please specify).
*   **Room Number or Specific Area** (`Room Number or Specific Area`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify the room or area for your event.
*   **Estimated Number of Attendees** (`Estimated Number of Attendees`, `number`, required): Enter an approximate number of people who will attend the event.
*   **Organizer Name** (`Organizer Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
*   **Organizer Email** (`Organizer Email`, `email`, required): Enter your campus email address.
*   **Organizer Phone Number** (`Organizer Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter a contact phone number.
*   **Do you need special equipment or setup?** (`Do you need special equipment or setup?`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from Projector and Screen, Sound System, Microphone, Tables and Chairs, Catering Service, Parking Accommodations, Accessibility Accommodations, or None.
*   **Additional Notes or Special Requests** (`Additional Notes or Special Requests`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that might be helpful for the event.
*   **I acknowledge that all information provided is accurate** (`I acknowledge that all information provided is accurate`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have provided truthful information.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Be honest about your needs and requirements for the event.
- If you're unsure about any field or have further questions, please reach out to campus administration for assistance.
