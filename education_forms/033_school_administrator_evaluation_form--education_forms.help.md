## Purpose
The school_administrator_evaluation_form is a tool for school administrators to evaluate and provide feedback on various aspects of the school's programs, tools, and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and select the relevant options for each section.
2. Fill out the required fields and provide comments and suggestions where necessary.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required sections.
