# Child Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Safety Checklist Form is a tool to evaluate the safety of a child's environment, helping to identify potential hazards and ensure a safe space for children.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the location or room name where the child safety assessment is being conducted (e.g., Nursery, Playground, Living Room).
2. Choose the date of the assessment.
3. Evaluate the child's environment according to the questions and answers provided. Select "True" if the safety measure is in place, "False" if it's not, or "Not Applicable" if the safety feature is not relevant to the specific environment.
4. Provide any additional safety concerns observed during the assessment in the "Additional safety concerns observed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location or Room Name (Location Name)** (`location_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the room or area being assessed (e.g., Nursery, Playground, Living Room).
* **Date of Evaluation (evaluation_date)** (`date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of the assessment.
* **Outlet Covers in Place? (outlet_covers)** (`select_one`, `True/False/Not Applicable`, required): Check if child-proof outlet covers are installed and functional.
* **Furniture Anchored to Walls? (furniture_anchored)** (`select_one`, `True/False/Not Applicable`, required): Verify that furniture is secured to walls to prevent tip-overs.
* **Sharp Corners Protected? (sharp_corners)** (`select_one`, `True/False/Not Applicable`, required): Check if corners are protected with edge guards or padding.
* **Cleaning Supplies Locked Away? (cleaning_supplies_locked)** (`select_one`, `True/False/Not Applicable`, required): Ensure cleaning supplies are locked away for safe access.
* **Medications Out of Reach? (medications_out_reach)** (`select_one`, `True/False/Not Applicable`, required): Confirm medications are stored in high or locked cabinets.
* **Choking Hazards Removed? (choking_hazards_removed)** (`select_one`, `True/False/Not Applicable`, required): Inspect for the removal of choking hazards such as small items on the floor.
* **Working Smoke Detector Present? (smoke_detector_working)** (`select_one`, `True/False/Not Applicable`, required): Verify the presence and functioning of a smoke detector.
* **First Aid Kit Available? (first_aid_kit)** (`select_one`, `True/False/Not Applicable`, required): Confirm a fully stocked first aid kit is accessible.
* **Gates Secured? (gates_secured)** (`select_one`, `True/False/Not Applicable`, required): Check that gates are secure and functioning properly.
* **Additional Safety Concerns (additional_concerns)** (`text`, `optional`): List any other safety concerns observed during the assessment.
* **Overall Safety Rating (overall_safety_rating)** (`select_one`, `Safe Environment/Minor changes needed/Urgent action required`, required): Rate the overall safety of the child's environment based on the assessment.
