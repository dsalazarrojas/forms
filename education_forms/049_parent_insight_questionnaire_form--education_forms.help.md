**Parent Insight Questionnaire Form - Help Guide**
=====================================================

## Purpose
The Parent Insight Questionnaire Form is designed to gather information from parents and guardians about their child's educational experience. It aims to help teachers, schools, and educational institutions understand the parents' perspective on their child's learning journey.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **School ID** (ID of your child's school)
2.  Enter your **Parent Name** (your name as a parent or guardian)
3.  Enter your **Parent Email** (your email address)
4.  Enter your **Child Name** (your child's name)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **School ID** (School ID): Enter the identification number or code of your child's school.
*   **Parent Name** (Parent Name): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
*   **Parent Email** (Parent Email): Enter your email address. This field is required to ensure that we can contact you for any follow-up questions or to provide updates about your child's progress.
*   **Child Name** (Child Name): Enter your child's name.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your responses are accurately recorded.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
