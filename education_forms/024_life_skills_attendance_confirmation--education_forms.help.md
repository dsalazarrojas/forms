# life_skills_attendance_confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect attendance information for participants in a workshop or training session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Session Details** section with the relevant session dates and time.
2. Enter the **Participant Details** section with the participant's name, email, phone number, address, and any other relevant information.
3. Provide your **Email** address if you want to receive any updates or confirmations from the organizers.
4. Select the **Session Dates** you attended from the dropdown list.
5. If applicable, add your **Session Time** for each selected session date.
6. Sign and agree to the **Participant Agreement** and **Attendance Confirmation Agreement**.
7. Date and sign the **Session Agreement Date** and **Participant Agreement Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details**: This section captures details about the session you attended, including the dates and time.
* **Participant Details**: Enter your name, email, phone number, and address to confirm your attendance.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates or confirmations from the organizers.
* **Session Dates**: Select the dates you attended the session from the dropdown list.
* **Session Time**: Enter the session time if it's different from the default time.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name to confirm your attendance.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number to confirm your contact information.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates or confirmations from the organizers.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address to confirm your location.
* **Session Notes**: Add any notes about the session.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your attendance.
* **Participant Agreement**: Read and sign this agreement to confirm your understanding of the session's terms and conditions.
* **Attendance Confirmation Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your attendance.
* **Attendance Confirmation Agreement**: Read and sign this agreement to confirm your understanding of the attendance confirmation process.
* **Workshop Agreement**: Read and sign this agreement to confirm your understanding of the workshop's terms and conditions.
* **Workshop Details**: Add any additional information about the workshop.
* **Session Agreement**: Read and sign this agreement to confirm your understanding of the session's terms and conditions.
* **Session Agree**: Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement with the session.
* **Agree**: Sign this section to confirm your agreement with the form and all its terms and conditions.
* **Session Agreement Date**: Enter the date of this agreement.
* **Participant Agreement Date**: Enter the date of this agreement.
* **Workshop Agreement Date**: Enter the date of this agreement.
* **Attendance Confirmation Agreement Date**: Enter the date of this agreement.
