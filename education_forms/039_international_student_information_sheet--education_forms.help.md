# International Student Information Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Student Information Sheet is designed to collect details about international students, including their personal and travel-related information. This form is essential for administrative and organizational purposes within educational institutions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's nationality.
3. Provide the student's date of birth (DOB) in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter the contact email and phone number for the student in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
5. Provide the student's address in the "Student Address" field.
6. Enter the student's passport number.
7. Select the type of student ID (if applicable).
8. Mention the student's nationality of origin.
9. Provide the date of arrival and return for international travel.
10. Specify the reason for travel and mode of transport used.
11. Mention the travel agency used.
12. Identify the contact person responsible for the student.
13. Specify the relationship between the contact person and the student.
14. Indicate the student's status.
15. Choose the type of student program the student is enrolled in.
16. Select the student's language (if applicable).
17. If the student's language is not listed, specify the language used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional)
Enter the student's full name.

### 2. **Nationality** (`nationality`, text, optional)
Enter the student's nationality.

### 3. **Date of Birth** (`dob`, date, optional)
Provide the student's date of birth.

### 4. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional)
Enter the student's contact email.

### 5. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional)
Enter the student's contact phone number.

### 6. **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional)
Provide the student's address.

### 7. **Passport Number** (`passport_number`, text, optional)
Enter the student's passport number.

### 8. **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional)
Select the type of student ID (if applicable).

### 9. **Nationality of Origin** (`nationality_of_origin`, text, optional)
Mention the student's nationality of origin.

### 10. **Date of Arrival** (`date_of_arrival`, date, optional)
Provide the date of arrival for international travel.

### 11. **Date of Return** (`date_of_return`, date, optional)
Provide the date of return for international travel.

### 12. **Reason of Travel** (`reason_of_travel`, text, optional)
Specify the reason for travel.

### 13. **Mode of Transport** (`mode_of_transport`, text, optional)
Choose the mode of transport used.

### 14. **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, optional)
Mention the travel agency used.

### 15. **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional)
Identify the contact person responsible for the student.

### 16. **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, text, optional)
Specify the relationship between the contact person and the student.

### 17. **Student Status** (`student_status`, text, optional)
Indicate the student's status.

### 18. **Student Type** (`student_type`, text, optional)
Choose the type of student program the student is enrolled in.

### 19. **Student Category** (`student_category`, text, optional)
Select the category of student.

### 20. **Passport Number Type** (`passport_number_type`, text, optional)
Specify the type of passport number.

### 21. **Visa Status** (`visa_status`, text, optional)
Indicate the student's visa status.

### 22. **Student Level** (`student_level`, text, optional)
Choose the level of the student program.

### 23. **Student Program** (`student_program`, text, optional)
Select the student program.

### 24. **Student Language** (`student_language`, text, optional)
Select the language of the student.

### 25. **Student Language Other** (`student_language_other`, text, optional)
Specify the language used by the student (if not listed).

## Tips
- Please double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- Use proper formatting for dates and phone numbers.
- If any field is not applicable, leave it blank.
- If unsure about any field, consult a designated authority or contact person.
