# Counseling Theories Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counseling Theories Assessment form is designed to gather information about your education level, preferred counseling theories, and rating scales in a counseling context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your education level from the options provided.
2.  Choose the counseling theories you have a preference for.
3.  If applicable, rate the scale provided. This is usually used to gauge your agreement or disagreement with a particular statement.
4.  Describe your experience with a specific case, if you have any.
5.  Select the field of education you are most familiar with.
6.  If you have experience with a case, describe your response to it.
7.  Repeat the rating process for any other applicable cases.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Education Level** (`education_level`, select multiple, **not required**): Choose your level of education that aligns with your experience in the counseling field, such as High School, Some College, Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctorate.
*   **Theories Preference** (`theory_preferences`, select multiple, **not required**): Select the counseling theories you prefer, such as Cognitive Behavioral, Humanistic, Existential, or Solution Focused.
*   **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, number, **not required**): This field is usually used to rate a scale from 0-10, where you rate your agreement or disagreement with a particular statement or theory.
*   **Rating 1** (`theory_rating_1`, number, **not required**): Similar to the previous rating scale, rate another scale from 0-10.
*   **Case Response** (`case_responses`, select multiple, **not required**): If you have experience with a case, select the case as Positive, Negative, or No Case.
*   **Case Description** (`case_description`, text, **not required**): Describe your experience with the case you selected.
*   **Case Response** (`case_description_response`, text, **not required**): Provide your response to the case you described.
*   **Education Field** (`education_field`, select one, **not required**): Choose the field of education you are most familiar with, such as Psychology, Counseling, Social Work, or Other.
*   **Theory Rating 1** (`theory_rating_1`, number, **not required**): Another rating scale from 0-10 for a specific theory.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question truthfully and accurately based on your experience and knowledge.
- If you have no experience with any of the cases, select No Case.
- For the rating scales, rate based on your agreement or disagreement with the statements or theories.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
