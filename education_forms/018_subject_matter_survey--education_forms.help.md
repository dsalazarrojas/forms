# Subject Matter Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the topic of a project or task that you are working on.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you are answering the questions relevant to your project.
2. For each question, choose the best answer that applies to your situation.
3. Fill in any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Topic** (`question_1`, select_one, required/optional): Select the topic that best describes your project or task.
	+ This field is used to help us understand the nature of your project or task.
* **What is your favorite topic?** (`question_2`, text, optional): Please share any information you feel is relevant to your project or task.
	+ This field is used to gather more detailed information about your project or task.
* **How relevant is this topic to your work?** (`question_3`, number, optional): Rate how relevant this topic is to your work.
	+ This field helps us understand how closely related this topic is to your work.
* **Any additional comments?** (`question_4`, note, optional): Share any additional information that may be helpful.
	+ This field is used to gather more information about your project or task.
* **Select Multiple Topics** (`question_5`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple topics that apply to your project or task.
	+ This field helps us understand the breadth of your project or task.
* **How long have you been working on this topic?** (`question_6`, select_one, optional): Rate how long you have been working on this topic.
	+ This field helps us understand the duration of your project or task.
* **How long will you continue to work on this topic?** (`question_7`, select_one, optional): Rate how long you will continue to work on this topic.
	+ This field helps us understand the future plans for your project or task.
* **How long will you need to complete this project?** (`question_8`, date, optional): Enter the date by which you expect to complete this project.
	+ This field helps us understand the timeline for your project or task.
* **What is your availability?** (`question_9`, time, optional): Share your availability for this project or task.
	+ This field helps us understand when you are available to work on this project or task.
* **Email Address** (`question_10`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
	+ This field is used to contact you if we need further information.
* **Phone Number** (`question_11`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is used to contact you if we need further information.
* **What is your name?** (`question_12`, text, optional): Enter your name.
	+ This field is used to identify you.
* **What is your department?** (`question_13`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
	+ This field is used to identify your department.
* **How many people are working on this project?** (`question_14`, number, optional): Enter the number of people working on this project.
	+ This field helps us understand the team size for your project or task.
* **How many projects are you working on right now?** (`question_15`, number, optional): Enter the number of projects you are working on currently.
	+ This field helps us understand your project load.
* **Is this project urgent?** (`question_16`, select_one, optional): Rate whether this project is urgent.
	+ This field helps us understand the priority level of your project or task.
* **Is this project confidential?** (`question_17`, select_one, optional): Rate whether this project is confidential.
	+ This field helps us understand the sensitivity of your project or task.
* **Any questions or concerns** (`question_18`, note, optional): Share any questions or concerns you may have.
	+ This field is used to gather feedback on our process.
* **How can we improve this form?** (`question_19`, note, optional): Share any suggestions on how we can improve this form.
	+ This field is used to gather feedback on our process.
* **Is this form useful?** (`question_20`, select_one, optional): Rate whether this form was useful.
	+ This field helps us understand the effectiveness of this form.
* **Additional Comments** (`question_21`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback.
	+ This field is used to gather more information about your experience with this form.
* **Rate this form** (`question_22`, select_one, optional): Rate how you found this form.
	+ This field helps us understand your experience with this form.
* **Question 23** (`question_23`, number, optional): Rate your rating for question 23.
	+ This field helps us understand your rating for question 23.
* **Question 24** (`question_24`, number, optional): Rate your rating for question 24.
	+ This field helps us understand your rating for question 24.
* **Question 25** (`question_25`, number, optional): Rate your rating for question 25.
	+ This field helps us understand your rating for question 25.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please use the "Any questions or concerns" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form! Your feedback is valuable to us.
