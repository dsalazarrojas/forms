# Make Up Assignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Make Up Assignment Form" is a tool used to collect information from students about their created assignments. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the student who completed the assignment in the **Student Name** field.
2. Select the date the student completed the assignment in the **Assignment Date** field.
3. If the student was absent for any reason, please provide a brief explanation in the **Reason for Absence** field.
4. Enter the date the assignment was submitted by the student in the **Submitted On** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Make Up Assignment** (`make_up_assignment_form`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the assignment.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the student who completed the assignment.
* **Assignment Date** (`assignment_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date the student completed the assignment.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, `text`, required: false): If the student was absent for any reason, provide a brief explanation.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the assignment was submitted by the student.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Double-check the dates to ensure they are accurate.
- If you need assistance or have questions, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
