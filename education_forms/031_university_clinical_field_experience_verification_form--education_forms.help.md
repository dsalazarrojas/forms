# University Clinical Field Experience Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify a student's clinical field experience. It is a necessary step in the evaluation process to confirm the student's involvement in clinical field work, which is an essential part of their academic and professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your student's details in the "Student Details" section.
2.  Provide your clinical details in the "Clinical Details" section.
3.  Confirm your verification status in the "Verification" section.
4.  Enter the number of hours completed for the clinical field experience in the "Hours Completed" section.
5.  Evaluate your clinical field experience in the "Evaluation" section.
6.  Select your role in the "Role" section.
7.  Enter the start and end dates of the clinical field experience in the "Start Date" and "End Date" sections, respectively.
8.  Enter the number of hours worked in the "Hours Worked" section.
9.  Evaluate your clinical field experience on a scale in the "Evaluation Scale" section.
10. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" section (optional).
11. Enter the name of the university where the clinical field experience was completed in the "University Name" section.
12. Sign the form as the supervisor in the "Supervisor Signature" section.
13. Enter your name as the supervisor in the "Supervisor Name" section.
14. (Optional) Enter additional information about the role in the "Role 2" section.
15. Finally, sign the form as the university representative in the "University Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details** (Student Details, required, Student's personal details):
	+ Enter the student's name, student number, or other identifying information.
* **Clinical Details** (Clinical Details, required, Clinical details):
	+ Enter a brief description of the clinical field experience, including location, duration, and any relevant details.
* **Verification** (Verification, required, Verification status):
	+ Confirm whether you have verified the student's clinical field experience.
* **Hours Completed** (Hours Completed, required, Number of hours completed):
	+ Enter the total number of hours completed for the clinical field experience.
* **Evaluation** (Evaluation, required, Evaluation status):
	+ Evaluate whether you agree that the student's clinical field experience meets the expected standards.
* **Role** (Role, required, Role in the clinical field experience):
	+ Select the role the student held during the clinical field experience (e.g., Internship, Field Experience, Research Project).
* **Start Date** (Start Date, required, Date of start):
	+ Enter the start date of the clinical field experience.
* **End Date** (End Date, required, Date of end):
	+ Enter the end date of the clinical field experience.
* **Hours Worked** (Hours Worked, required, Number of hours worked):
	+ Enter the total number of hours worked for the clinical field experience.
* **Evaluation Scale** (Evaluation Scale, required, Evaluation scale):
	+ Evaluate whether you agree that the student's clinical field experience meets the expected standards on a scale.
* **Comments** (Comments, optional, Additional comments):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about the clinical field experience.
* **University Name** (University Name, required, University name):
	+ Enter the name of the university where the clinical field experience was completed.
* **Supervisor Signature** (Supervisor Signature, required, Supervisor's signature):
	+ Sign the form as the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Name** (Supervisor Name, required, Supervisor's name):
	+ Enter your name as the supervisor.
* **University Signature** (University Signature, optional, University signature):
	+ (Optional) Sign the form as the university representative.
* **Role 2** (Role 2, required, Additional role information):
	+ Enter additional information about the role in the clinical field experience (e.g., department, location).
