# Trade School Apprentice Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade School Apprentice Onboarding Form is designed to collect information from new apprentices who have been accepted into the trade school program. This form is used to gather details about the apprentice's program details, student information, contact information, education history, personal details, employment status, training details, emergency contact, and other relevant information. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form by filling in the fields as prompted.
2. Be sure to review all fields for accuracy before submitting.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your trade school administrator for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Details**: Please enter your program details, including your program name, start date, and end date.
* **Student Info**: Please enter your student information, including your name, student ID, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info**: Please enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
* **Education History**: Please enter your education history, including your previous educational institutions and degrees.
* **Personal Details**: Please enter your personal details, including your date of birth.
* **Employment Status**: Please select all employment statuses that apply to you.
* **Training Details**: Please enter your training details, including your training goals and objectives.
* **Emergency Contact**: Please enter your emergency contact information, including their name, phone number, and contact type.
* **Student ID**: Please enter your student ID number.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Phone**: Please enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Type**: Please select your emergency contact type, which can be Home, Work, or School.
* **Address 1, 2, 3**: Please enter your address details.
* **City**: Please enter your city.
* **State**: Please enter your state.
* **Zip**: Please enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Please enter your country.
* **Employment Status 1, 2**: Please select all employment statuses that apply to you.
* **Education Level**: Please select all education levels that apply to you.
* **Education Institution**: Please enter your education institution.
* **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth.
 
**Tips**

* Be sure to review all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your trade school administrator for guidance.
