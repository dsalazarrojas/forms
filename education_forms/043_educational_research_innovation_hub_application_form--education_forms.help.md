# Educational Research Innovation Hub Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather application information for potential research innovation hub resources. Please review the guide below to ensure you provide the required information accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill in your contact information on "User Information" page.
2. Provide your contact details on the "Contact Details" page.
3. Enter your institution's information on the "Institution Information" page.
4. Describe your research project on the "Research Project" page.
5. Outline your budget planning on the "Budget Planning" page.
6. Submit your application on the "Application" page.
7. Add any additional information on the "Additional Information" page.
8. Review and confirm the information on "Another Page" (page 7) and "More Page" (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Page 1)** (`user_information`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Contact Details (Page 2)** (`contact_details`, text, required): Provide your contact information, including phone numbers, email addresses, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Institution Information (Page 3)** (`institution`, text, required): Enter your institution's name and any other relevant information.
* **Research Project (Page 4)** (`research_project`, text, required): Describe your research project, including the topic, objective, and expected outcomes.
* **Budget Planning (Page 5)** (`budget_planning`, text, required): Outline your budget planning, including any financial resources you plan to use or request.
* **Application (Page 6)** (`application`, text, required): Submit your application, including any supporting documents or materials.
* **Additional Information (Page 7)** (`additional_information`, text, required): Add any additional information relevant to your application.
* **Another Page (Page 8)** (`another_page`, text, required): Review and confirm the information provided in the previous pages.
* **More Page (Page 9)** (`more_page`, text, required): Review and confirm the information provided in the previous pages.

Note: Please ensure that you complete all required fields (marked as "required") accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or need clarification, please reach out to form@gic.mx.
