# School Nurse Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Nurse Performance Evaluation Form is designed to help evaluate the performance of school nurses, identify areas of strength and weakness, and provide actionable feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reviewing the form and understanding the criteria being evaluated. 
- Select the relevant options for each field that best describe the performance of the school nurse in each area.
- Provide detailed comments and suggestions for improvement in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
- Ensure that the "Evaluation Date" field is filled in with the date of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief note about the evaluation.
* **School Evaluation**: Select one or multiple options to describe the school's overall performance, such as:
	+ School Performance: How well is the school nurse performing their duties?
* **Staff Evaluation**: Select one option to describe how you feel about the school nurse's performance:
	+ Strong: The school nurse is performing exceptionally well.
	+ Weak: The school nurse is underperforming in some areas.
	+ Indifferent: The school nurse is meeting expectations.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Provide specific comments and suggestions for improvement in the school nurse's performance.
* **Recommendations**: Share specific suggestions or recommendations for the school nurse's future growth and development.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date when the evaluation took place.
