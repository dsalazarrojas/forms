# personal_study_planning_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "personal_study_planning_form" is used to help students plan and organize their studies more efficiently. This form captures essential information about their study schedule, materials, groups, and resources, ensuring they can effectively manage their time and stay on track with their studies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your **Study Schedule** (Page 1): Choose a number representing your available study hours per day/week.
2. Identify the materials you need for your studies, such as **Materials Needed** (Page 2): Select all the materials that apply, like textbooks, online resources, or other.
3. Choose whether you will be studying as an **Individual** or as part of a **Study Group** (Page 3): Select one that best fits your study style.
4. Specify where you will be studying, such as at **Home**, in the **Classroom**, or in the **Library** (Page 4): Choose one or more locations where you will be studying.
5. Indicate the **Study Time** (Page 5): Enter the number of hours you plan to study per day/week.
6. List any **Other Resources Needed** (Page 6): Provide a brief description of any additional resources you require for your studies.
7. Enter your **Email Notification** address (Page 7): Provide your email address where you want to receive notifications.
8. Enter your **Phone Number** (Page 8): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
9. Set a specific **Time** for your studies (Page 9): Select a time of day when you plan to study.
10. Choose a specific **Date** for your studies (Page 10): Select a date when you plan to start your studies.
