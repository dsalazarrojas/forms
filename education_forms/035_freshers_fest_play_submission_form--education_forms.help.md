# Freshers’ Fest Play Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Freshers' Fest Play Submission Form" is designed to collect performance information from first-year students (freshers) at the University of [University Name]. This form allows students to submit their performance details to be showcased during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Select your role (Student, Actor, Musician).
5. Enter your performance experience.
6. Select your performance type (e.g., singing, dancing, acting).
7. Enter your performance length.
8. Choose a submission date for your performance.
9. Choose a performance date.
10. Enter the venue where you will perform.
11. Select a performance time.
12. Enter the length of your performance.
13. Enter your submitter's name.
14. Enter your submitter's email address.
15. Enter your submitter's phone number.
16. Select your submitter's role (Student, Actor, Musician).
17. Enter your submitter's performance experience.
18. Enter your submitter's performance type.
19. Choose a submission date for your submitter.
20. Choose a performance date for your submitter.
21. Enter the venue where your submitter will perform.
22. Select a performance time for your submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant in the Freshers' Fest event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Role**: Select one of the following options: Student, Actor, Musician.
* **Performance Experience**: Describe your performance experience, e.g., years of singing, dancing, or acting experience.
* **Performance Type**: Select the type of performance you will be submitting, e.g., singing, dancing, acting.
* **Performance Length**: Enter the duration of your performance.
* **Submission Date**: Choose a date to submit your performance.
* **Performance Date**: Choose a date for your performance.
* **Venue**: Enter the location where you will perform.
* **Performance Time**: Select a time slot for your performance.
* **Submitter Performance Length**: Enter the duration of your submitter's performance.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the submitter of the performance.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Role**: Select one of the following options: Student, Actor, Musician.
* **Submitter Performance Experience**: Describe your submitter's performance experience.
* **Submitter Performance Type**: Select the type of performance your submitter will be submitting.
* **Submitter Submission Date**: Choose a date to submit the performance.
* **Submitter Performance Date**: Choose a date for the submitter's performance.
* **Submitter Venue**: Enter the location where the submitter will perform.
* **Submitter Performance Time**: Select a time slot for the submitter's performance.
* **Submitter Performance Length 2**: Enter the duration of the submitter's performance.
