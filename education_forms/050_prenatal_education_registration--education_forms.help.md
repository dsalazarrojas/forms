# Prenatal Education Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Prenatal Education Registration" form is a tool used by pregnant women to register for a prenatal class and share their information with the organization. The form gathers details about the individual, such as their name, contact information, class preferences, and medical clearance, to provide relevant education and support during their pregnancy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
2. Select the date and time you prefer for the class.
3. Choose the location for the class.
4. If you have an expected delivery date, enter it accurately.
5. Indicate if you have attended a previous prenatal class, and if so, which type of class you took.
6. If you were referred to the class, select the option that best describes your referral source.
7. If required, provide a medical clearance code or note.
8. Optionally, add any comments or remarks you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mothers Name** (`mother_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name, including any middle or last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Class Date** (`class_date`, date, required): Select the date you prefer for the class.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, time, required): Choose the time of day you would like to attend the class.
* **Class Location** (`class_location`, text, required): Select the location where you would like to attend the class.
* **Expected Delivery Date** (`expected_delivery_date`, date, required): Enter your expected delivery date if known.
* **Have you attended a previous prenatal class?** (`attended_previous_prenatal_class`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have previously attended a prenatal class, with options being 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Referred by** (`referred_by`, select_multiple, not required): If you were referred to the class by someone, select the option that best describes your source, such as 'Doctor' or 'Internet Search'.
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, text, not required): If required, enter a medical clearance code or provide any necessary medical notes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Optionally, add any additional comments or remarks you would like to share about your situation or preferences.
