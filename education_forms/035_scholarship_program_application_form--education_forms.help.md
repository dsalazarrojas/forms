# scholarship_program_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scholarship program application form is designed to collect personal and contact information from applicants, as well as details about their education, program, and scholarship. This form is meant to gather relevant information to support the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the personal information section, which includes details about the applicant's name, address, phone number, and email address.
2. Move on to the contact information section, where you can provide your preferred method of contact.
3. In the education details section, please provide information about your educational background and qualifications.
4. The program information section is where you can submit details about the program you are applying for.
5. In the scholarship information section, provide details about the scholarship you are applying for, including any relevant information about the award or criteria.
6. You can upload documents relevant to your application in the document upload section.
7. Finally, review and submit your application by providing any additional comments or information in the review and submission section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, optional): This is the first section of the form, where you can provide your personal details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This section is for your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Education Details** (`education_details`, text, optional): This is where you provide information about your educational background and qualifications.
* **Program Info** (`program_info`, text, optional): This section is for details about the program you are applying for.
* **Scholarship Info** (`scholarship_info`, text, optional): This is where you provide details about the scholarship you are applying for, including any relevant information about the award or criteria.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, optional): This is where you can upload documents relevant to your application.
* **Upload Additional Docs** (`upload_additional_docs`, text, optional): This is an additional section for uploading any further documents.
* **Review and Submission** (`review_and_submission`, text, optional): This section is for any final comments or information you would like to provide with your submission.
