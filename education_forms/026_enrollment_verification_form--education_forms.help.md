# Enrollment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrollment Verification Form is a document used to verify a student's enrollment status and details. It is used to collect information about a student's enrollment, including their name, contact information, enrollment status, and school-related details. The form is designed to help administrators and staff accurately track and manage student enrollment data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Enrollment Date, and Student ID Number) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Enter your school name, address, and other relevant details.
3.  Choose the correct enrollment status (Active, Inactive, etc.) and category (Undergraduate, Graduate, etc.).
4.  Add any additional notes or comments as necessary.
5.  Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (text, required): Enter the student's first name.
*   **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the student's last name.
*   **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the student's date of birth.
*   **Enrollment Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the student's enrollment.
*   **School Name** (text): Enter the name of the school or institution the student is enrolled in.
*   **Student ID Number** (text): Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
*   **Address** (text): Enter the student's home address.
*   **City** (text, required): Enter the city of the student's home address.
*   **State** (text, required): Enter the state of the student's home address.
*   **Zip** (text, required): Enter the zip code of the student's home address.
*   **Country** (text, required): Enter the country of the student's home address.
*   **School Address** (text): Enter the address of the school or institution.
*   **Phone** (text): Enter the student's contact phone number.
*   **Email** (email, required): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Enrollment Status** (select_one, required): Choose the current enrollment status of the student (Active, Inactive, etc.).
*   **Notes** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about the student's enrollment.
*   **School Name2** (text): Enter the name of the school or institution (if different from the first school name).
*   **Student Type** (text): Enter the type of student (e.g., Undergraduate, Graduate, etc.).
*   **Graduation Status** (select_one): Choose the graduation status of the student (Active, Graduated, etc.).
*   **Enrollment Type** (select_one): Choose the enrollment type (Undergraduate, Graduate, etc.).
*   **Student Category** (select_multiple): Choose the category of student (Active, Inactive, etc.).
*   **Enrollment Category** (select_one): Choose the category of enrollment (Active, Inactive, etc.).
*   **Enrollment Subcategory** (text): Enter the subcategory of the student's enrollment.
*   **School Category** (select_one): Choose the category of school (Public, Private, etc.).
*   **School Subcategory** (select_one): Choose the subcategory of school (Public, Private, etc.).
*   **Student Subcategory** (select_multiple): Choose the subcategory of student (Active, Inactive, etc.).
*   **School Enrollment ID** (text): Enter the school enrollment ID, if applicable.
*   **Student Enrollment ID** (text): Enter the student enrollment ID, if applicable.
*   **School Address2** (text): Enter the address of the school or institution (if different from the first school address).
*   **Student Name2** (text): Enter the name of the student (if different from the first student name).
