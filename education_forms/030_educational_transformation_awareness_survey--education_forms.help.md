# Educational Transformation Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Transformation Awareness Survey is a form designed to collect data on users' awareness of educational transformation concepts. This form is meant to be filled out by users who have been assigned to educational transformation projects. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each field and provide as much detail as possible. If a field is marked as optional, you can leave it blank if you do not have the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Choose the most accurate option that describes your category of work. (required: false)
* **Description 1**: Please provide a brief description of your project or area of focus. (required: false)
* **More Information**: If you have additional information about your project, please provide it here. (required: false)
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that is currently assigned to your project. (required: false)
* **Output File**: If you have an output file related to your project, please provide the file name and location. (required: false)
* **Form ID 1**: Please enter the ID of your first form. (required: false)
* **Form ID 2**: Please enter the ID of your second form. (required: false)

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the project lead for clarification.
* If you have completed this form previously, you might not need to fill it out again. However, if you have new information, please update your answers accordingly.
