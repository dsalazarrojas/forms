# Scholarship Fund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request funding from the Scholarship Fund for a specific purpose. This form is intended for students who are affiliated with a specific organization. Before submitting a request, please review the fields below to ensure you have provided all necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in the following order:
	* Provide your student name and email address
	* Provide your student phone number
	* Select whether you are affiliated with an organization (select "Yes" or "No")
	* Indicate the purpose of your scholarship request
	* Specify the amount of funding you are requesting
	* Choose a deadline for when you need the funding by
	* Select the date you are submitting your request

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your email address associated with your student account.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization Affiliation** (`scholarship_type`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you are affiliated with an organization. Check "No" if you are not affiliated with an organization.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, note, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your scholarship request.
* **Amount Requested** (`amount_requested`, number, optional): Enter the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, optional): Choose a date by which you need the funding.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are submitting your request.

## Tips
- Make sure to submit your request well in advance of your deadline to allow sufficient time for review and processing.
- If you are affiliated with an organization, please ensure that you have the necessary approval from your organization before submitting the request.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the request.
