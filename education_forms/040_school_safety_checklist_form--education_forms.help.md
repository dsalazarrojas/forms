# School Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Safety Checklist Form is used by school administrators and faculty to ensure that all necessary safety measures and protocols are in place at the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the type of form being filled out. Options are Education Forms and Other.
2. Provide a brief description of the form and its purpose.
3. Indicate which safety protocols are in place at the school, such as Fire safety, Emergency evacuation plan, and Bullying prevention.
4. Provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
5. Select the school administrator responsible for the form (Principal, Teacher, or Other).
6. Enter the number of staff and students present on campus.
7. Specify the location of the form (Campus, Classroom, or Other).
8. Indicate which emergency equipment are available on campus, such as CCTV cameras, Metal detectors, and Other.
9. Select which safety trainings have been completed, such as Fire safety training, First aid training, and Other.
10. Specify which types of incidents are reported and how reports are filed.
11. Indicate which safety measures are in place, such as Fire doors, Safety signs, and Other.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the school's safety policies and procedures.
* This form is intended for use in a school setting, and the answers should reflect the specific safety measures and protocols implemented by the school.
