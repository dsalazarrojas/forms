# 2021 NOLA PS OneApp Match - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2021 NOLA PS OneApp Match form is designed to gather feedback from students and families about their experience with the OneApp platform and the matching process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill in all the required fields (indicated by a asterisk *).
2.  Select all that apply for multiple-choice fields.
3.  Provide specific suggestions for improvement in the "What would you improve or change about 2021 NOLA PS OneApp Match?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student full name**: *Enter the student's legal full name.*
* **Parent or guardian name**: *Enter the parent or guardian's legal full name.*
* **Contact email address**: *Enter a valid email address where you would like to receive match notifications.*
* **Contact phone number**: *Leave this field blank if you do not have a preferred phone number.*
* **Student date of birth**: *Enter the student's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.*
* **Current grade level**: *Enter the student's current grade level (e.g., 8 for 8th grade).*
* **Current school name**: *Leave this field blank if the student is not currently enrolled.*
* **Schools applied to**: *Select all schools that the student has applied to through OneApp.*
* **How were schools ranked**: *Select how the student ranked schools through OneApp.*
* **Application submission date**: *Enter the date the OneApp application was submitted.*
* **Match outcome**: *Select the current match outcome, if known (e.g., Matched to top choice, Matched to another ranked choice, etc.).*
* **Overall satisfaction**: *Select how satisfied you are with the 2021 NOLA PS OneApp Match.*
* **Overall rating**: *Rate your experience from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).*
* **Easy to use**: *Select if you found the OneApp platform easy to use.*
* **Fast**: *Select if you found the OneApp platform fast.*
* **Reliable**: *Select if you found the OneApp platform reliable.*
* **Missing feature**: *Select if you missed a feature during the OneApp process.*
* **Confusing**: *Select if you found the OneApp platform confusing.*
* **Well designed**: *Select if you found the OneApp platform well-designed.*
* **Reported GPA**: *Enter the student's GPA, if applicable (0.00 - 4.00 scale).*
* **Special needs or accommodations**: *Select if the student has special needs or accommodations.*
* **District contact**: *Select if the district can contact you for follow-up about this submission.*
* **Improvement suggestions**: *Provide specific suggestions for improving the OneApp platform.*
* **Additional comments or notes**: *Optional comments or notes.*

Note: Some fields are marked as required or optional, please ensure to complete the required fields.
