# Elementary School Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elementary School Records Request Form is a tool used by parents or guardians to request various educational records for their child attending an elementary school. This form is used to gather necessary information for processing requests such as obtaining a student's report card, progress report, or transcript.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct record request type from the "Record Request Type" dropdown menu.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out and the correct information is entered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your child.
* **Student Grade Level** (`Student Grade Level`, number, required: false): Enter your child's grade level at the current time.
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Student Address** (`Student Address`, text, required: false): Enter your child's current address.
* **School Name** (`School Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school your child attends.
* **Record Request Type** (`Record Request Type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of record you are requesting.
	+ **Elementary School Report Card**: Obtain a report card for the current or previous school year.
	+ **Progress Report**: Obtain a progress report for the current or previous school year.
	+ **Transcript**: Obtain a transcript for the current or previous school year.
	+ **Other**: Select this option if you are requesting a different type of record not listed above.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required: false): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Student ID Number** (`Student ID Number`, text, required: false): Enter your child's student ID number.
* **Guardian Phone** (`Guardian Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Guardian Email** (`Guardian Email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays or mistakes in processing your request.
* If you are unsure of any information, please contact the school or district office for assistance.
* If you need to request multiple records, please submit separate forms for each record type.
