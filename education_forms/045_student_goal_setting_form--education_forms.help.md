# Student Goal Setting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Goal Setting Form is a tool to help students set and track their goals in a structured and organized manner. It's a critical part of the student development process, allowing them to identify areas of improvement, set realistic objectives, and establish a plan to achieve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Student Goal" field with a clear and concise statement of what you want to achieve.
2. Select your current "Education Level" from the provided options.
3. In the "Student Objectives" field, describe the specific objectives you want to accomplish.
4. If applicable, fill in the "Behavioral Goal" field to describe any behavioral changes you want to make.
5. In the "Academic Goal" field, describe any academic objectives you want to achieve.
6. In the "Student Goal Setting Process" field, describe the process you'll follow to reach your goals.
7. For "Goal Setting Frequency", select the frequency at which you want to review and set your goals.
8. If necessary, specify any other goals you want to set under "Other (please specify)" fields.
9. Finally, select the "Target Date" and "Target Time" for your goals, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Goal** (`student_goal`, text, required): This field is for you to enter a clear and concise statement of what you want to achieve as a student. Be specific and make sure it's something you can realistically accomplish.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of education you're currently pursuing. This will help us understand your context when reviewing your goals.
* **Student Objectives** (`student_objectives`, text, required): In this field, describe the specific objectives you want to accomplish. For example, if you're aiming to improve your math skills, you might write "Improve math skills to achieve higher grades."
* **Behavioral Goal** (`behavioral_goal`, text, optional): If you want to make any behavioral changes, like developing better time management skills, describe them here.
* **Academic Goal** (`academic_goal`, text, optional): This field is for describing any academic objectives you want to achieve. For example, "Increase reading comprehension" or "Improve study habits."
* **Student Goal Setting Process** (`student_goal_setting_process`, text, optional): Describe the process you'll follow to reach your goals. This could be attending classes regularly, joining a study group, or seeking extra help from tutors.
* **Goal Setting Frequency** (`goal_setting_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you want to review and set your goals. This could be daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Other (please specify)** (`goal_setting_frequency_other`, text, required): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, use this space to specify what that frequency is.
* **Other (please specify)** (`education_level_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the "Education Level" field, use this space to specify your education level.
* **Target Date** (`target_date`, date, required): If applicable, select the date by which you want to achieve your goals. This could be a specific date or a range of dates.
* **Target Time** (`target_time`, time, required): If applicable, select the time by which you want to achieve your goals. This could be a specific time or a range of times.
* **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you want to have follow-up discussions to review your progress.

## Tips
* Be specific and realistic when setting your goals.
* Make sure to review and update your goals regularly to reflect your progress and changes.
* Use this form as a tool to track your progress and stay motivated.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your support team.
