# Campus Vehicle Policy Petition - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Campus Vehicle Policy Petition form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from students and employees on the current campus vehicle policy. This form helps the administration understand the concerns and needs of the campus community, allowing them to make informed decisions about policy updates and changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Petitioner Full Name**, which is your official university ID or your name as it appears on your university records.
2. Enter your **University Email**, which will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions or updates.
3. If you have a **Phone Number**, you can enter it to receive phone notifications about the petition progress.
4. Select your **Current Vehicle Type**, which is your primary mode of campus transportation (e.g., Car, Motorcycle, Bicycle, E-Scooter, Public Transit, or None).
5. In the **Petition Details Section**, briefly explain the main issue or concern you have with the current vehicle policy.
6. Choose the specific **Policy to Address**, which is the main area of concern you want to bring to the administration's attention.
7. Provide a **Description of Concern**, explaining your position or the issue in more detail.
8. Suggest a **Proposed Solution**, which is your suggested change to the policy.
9. Explain the **Impact on Campus Community**, how your suggested change would benefit others.
10. In the **Supporting Signatures** section, you can add supporting statements from other students or employees.
11. Choose your stance on the **Agreement to Petition**, whether you support or oppose the petition.
12. Select whether you **Consent to Share Your Name** with the administration.
13. Enter the **Date of Signature**, which is the date you are signing the petition.
14. Finally, choose whether you want to receive follow-up **Interest** on the petition progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petitioner Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your university records.
	+ Please enter your official university ID or your name as it appears on your university records.
* **Student or Employee ID**: Your official university ID number.
	+ Please enter your university ID number as it is assigned to you.
* **University Email**: Your university email address.
	+ This will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions or updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your mobile phone number.
	+ You can enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Current Vehicle Type**: Your primary mode of campus transportation.
	+ Choose from: Car, Motorcycle, Bicycle, E-Scooter, Public Transit, or None.
* **Petition Details Section**: Briefly explain the main issue or concern you have with the current vehicle policy.
	+ Please be as concise as possible while still conveying your concern.
* **Specific Policy to Address**: Choose the main area of concern you want to bring to the administration's attention.
	+ Choose from: Parking Fees, Overnight Parking, Electric Charging, Bicycle Storage, Transit Subsidy, or Other.
* **Description of Concern**: Explain your position or the issue in more detail.
	+ Please be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Proposed Solution**: Suggest a change to the policy.
	+ This is your proposed solution to the issue.
* **Impact on Campus Community**: Explain how your suggested change would benefit others.
	+ Please think about the broader implications of your proposed solution.
* **Agreement to Petition**: Choose whether you support or oppose the petition.
	+ Please select one of the two options.
* **Consent to Share Name**: Choose whether you consent to share your name with the administration.
	+ Please select one of the two options.
* **Date of Signature**: The date you are signing the petition.
	+ This is the date and time you are signing the petition.
* **Follow-up Interest**: Choose whether you want to receive updates on the petition progress.
	+ Please select one of the two options.
