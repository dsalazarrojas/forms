# Reading Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to gather information on students' reading habits, behaviors, and attitudes. The data collected will help in understanding students' reading experiences, identify areas of improvement, and develop targeted support for students' reading needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, follow these steps:

*   Review the questions carefully and answer them honestly.
*   Answer each question to the best of your ability, and do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly allowed.
*   If you're unsure about any question, ask your instructor or supervisor for guidance.
*   Submit the survey once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **reading_habits_of_students** (`reading_habits_of_students`, text, required: false): This question is designed to gather information about your typical reading habits.
*   **reading_speed_and_comprehension** (`reading_speed_and_comprehension`, text, required: false): This question is about your reading speed and comprehension level.
*   **reading_frequency** (`reading_frequency`, text, required: false): Please answer how often you read in a typical week.
*   **reading_purpose** (`reading_purpose`, text, required: false): Describe the purpose for which you typically read.
*   **reading_interest** (`reading_interest`, text, required: false): What subjects interest you the most when reading?
*   **reading_preference** (`reading_preference`, text, required: false): Please specify your preferred format for reading (e.g., fiction, non-fiction, comics, or audiobooks).
*   **reading_difficulty** (`reading_difficulty`, text, required: false): How would you rate the difficulty level of reading materials you typically consume?
*   **reading_time** (`reading_time`, text, required: false): Please estimate the average time you spend reading daily.
*   **reading_focus** (`reading_focus`, text, required: false): Where do you usually read (e.g., in a classroom, library, or at home)?
*   **reading_satisfaction** (`reading_satisfaction`, text, required: false): How do you rate your reading satisfaction level?

## Tips

*   Be honest and thorough in your answers.
*   Take your time to answer each question carefully.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on any question, don't hesitate to ask your instructor or supervisor.
*   Your participation is greatly appreciated, and your feedback will help inform education programs and policies.
