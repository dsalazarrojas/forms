# Creative Writing Prompt Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Writing Prompt Generator form is designed to help writers find inspiration and get started on their creative writing projects. By filling out the form with your information and preferences, you will be provided with a tailored creative writing prompt.

## How To Complete This Form

To use the form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
*   Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
*   If you are a part of a writing group, please enter your group name in the "Writing Group" field.
*   Select your preferred "Prompt Type" from the dropdown menu.
*   Choose your preferred genres from the "Genre" dropdown menu.
*   Select the desired "Story Length" for your prompt.
*   If you have a specific "Word Count" in mind, please enter it in the corresponding field.
*   Choose your desired "Frequency" of prompts from the dropdown menu.
*   If you have a specific date and time in mind for the prompt, please enter it in the corresponding fields.
*   If you'd like to include any additional information or tags for your prompt, please enter them in the "Description" field.
*   Finally, review your selections to ensure you've provided all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if desired).
*   **Writing Group** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the writing group you belong to (if desired).
*   **Prompt Type** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
    *   Writing Workshop
    *   Group Story
    *   Writing Prompt
    *   Short Story
    *   Flash Fiction
*   **Word Count** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the number of words you'd like to aim for in your writing prompt.
*   **Genre** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the following options:
    *   Fantasy
    *   Science Fiction
    *   Romance
    *   Horror
    *   Mystery
    *   Historical Fiction
*   **Story Length** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
    *   Short Story
    *   Long Story
    *   Novel
    *   Series
*   **Other** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional story length not listed in the above options.
*   **Frequency** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
    *   Once
    *   Regularly
    *   As Often As Possible
*   **Date** (`12`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you'd like to have the prompt generated.
*   **Time** (`13`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you'd like to have the prompt generated.
*   **Location** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific location for the prompt.
*   **Description** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or tags for the prompt.
*   **Tags** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional tags or keywords for the prompt.

## Tips

*   Make sure to select at least one "Prompt Type" and "Genre" option.
*   Ensure you've selected a "Story Length" option.
*   If you've selected "As Often As Possible" for "Frequency", please provide the date and time for the prompt.
