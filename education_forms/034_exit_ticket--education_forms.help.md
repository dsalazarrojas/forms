# Exit Ticket - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exit Ticket form is used to document student exit or dismissal from a class or school. This form is intended for teachers or educators to capture necessary details for administrative or reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the student's name, ID, class name, and exit date, if applicable.
3. Provide any comments or reasons for the student's exit.
4. Ensure you are submitting the form as the correct person, as indicated by the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name in this field.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): Enter the student's ID number in this field.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the teacher who is documenting the student's exit.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the class or school that the student is exiting.
* **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the student's exit, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any comments or reasons for the student's exit.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for assistance or clarification before proceeding.
* This form is intended for administrative or reporting purposes, so please ensure you are submitting it correctly.
