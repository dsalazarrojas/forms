# Fashion Academy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in enrolling in the Fashion Academy. It gathers data from three main categories: applicant information, contact information, and other relevant details such as education history, work experience, references, portfolio, skills, fashion school, certifications, and special talents.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read the form carefully and answer each question honestly and accurately.
2.  Make sure to fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) asterisk.
3.  For multiple-choice fields (select_one and select_multiple), select the most suitable option from the list.
4.  For text fields, provide detailed and clear answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (applicant_info): *Required* This is the first section of the form where you need to provide your basic information, such as name, address, and other contact details.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info): *Required* This section is used to provide your contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
* **Education History** (education_history): *Optional* This section is used to provide information about your educational background, including degrees, institutions, and dates of graduation.
* **Work Experience** (experience): *Optional* This section is used to provide information about your work experience, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **References** (references): *Optional* This section is used to provide information about your professional references, including their names, titles, and contact details.
* **Portfolio** (portfolio): *Optional* This section is used to provide information about your portfolio, including links to your work or samples.
* **Skills** (skills): *Optional* This section is used to provide information about your relevant skills, including programming languages, software proficiency, etc.
* **Fashion School** (fashion_school): *Required* This is a multiple-choice question where you need to select the fashion school you attended or are currently attending.
* **Certifications/Awards** (certifications): *Optional* This section is used to provide information about any relevant certifications or awards you have received.
* **Special Talents/Abilities** (special_talents): *Optional* This section is used to provide information about any special talents or abilities you possess.
* **Communication Preferences** (communication_preferences): *Optional* This section is used to provide information about your preferred communication methods, including language and email frequency.
* **Submit Application** (submit_application): *Required* This is a multiple-choice question where you need to select the most suitable option to submit your application.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues, contact our support team.
- We appreciate your honesty and attention to detail in filling out this form.
