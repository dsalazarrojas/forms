# Course Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of the Course Approval Form is to gather information about a course, its details, and its related approvals from administrators and external sources. This form helps ensure that courses meet the necessary requirements and are properly approved for implementation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the course details in the `Course Details` page.
2. Select the approval type from the `Approvals` page.
3. Enter any additional comments in the `Additional Comments` field.
4. Select the course status (Active, Inactive, or Pending) in the `Status` page.
5. Enter the course location, start and end dates, and start and end times.
6. Provide any additional information about the course in the `Description` field.
7. Upload any relevant documents or files in the `Attachments` field.
8. Specify the external URL label and enter the external URL.
9. Enter any additional comments about the course.
10. Finally, review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Course Details** (`course_approval_form_course_details`): Enter the course title and any other details about the course.
- **Approvals** (`course_approval_form_approvals`): Select whether the course requires Admin or External Approvals.
- **Additional Comments** (`course_approval_form_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the course.
- **Course Name** (`course_approval_form_course_name`): Enter the name of the course.
- **Start Date** (`course_approval_form_start_date`): Enter the start date of the course.
- **End Date** (`course_approval_form_end_date`): Enter the end date of the course.
- **Status** (`course_approval_form_status`): Select the status of the course (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- **Location** (`course_approval_form_course_location`): Enter the location where the course will be held.
- **Additional Comments** (`course_approval_form_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments about the course.
- **External URL** (`course_approval_form_external_url`): Enter the external URL related to the course.
- **Uploads** (`course_approval_form_uploads`): Upload any files or documents related to the course.
- **Attachments** (`course_approval_form_attachments`): Upload any attachments related to the course.
- **Approver** (`course_approval_form_approver`): Enter the name of the approver for the course.
- **Department** (`course_approval_form_department`): Enter the department related to the course.
- **Section** (`course_approval_form_section`): Enter the section related to the course.
- **Room** (`course_approval_form_room`): Enter the room where the course will be held.
- **Start Time** (`course_approval_form_start_time`): Enter the start time of the course.
- **End Time** (`course_approval_form_end_time`): Enter the end time of the course.
- **Total Hours** (`course_approval_form_total_hours`): Enter the total hours of the course.
- **Employees** (`course_approval_form_employees`): Enter the number of employees involved in the course.
- **Students** (`course_approval_form_students`): Enter the number of students involved in the course.
- **Faculty** (`course_approval_form_faculty`): Enter the faculty involved in the course.
- **External URL Label** (`course_approval_form_external_url_label`): Enter a label for the external URL.
- **Description** (`course_approval_form_course_description`): Enter a brief description of the course.
- **Comments** (`course_approval_form_comments`): Enter any comments about the course.

Note: The required fields are marked as `true` in the form schema and are indicated in the explanations above.
