# Transcript Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for requesting transcripts from schools. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the relevant purpose for requesting the transcript from the drop-down list.
2.  Fill out the required fields, including student school email, student school name, student school address, and transcript request reason.
3.  If necessary, provide additional information such as transcript request date and time.
4.  Review your submission carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out.
