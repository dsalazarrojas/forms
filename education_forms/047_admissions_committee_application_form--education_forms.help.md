# Admissions Committee Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Admissions Committee Application Form is a comprehensive document designed to collect essential information about the candidate's background, qualifications, and experience. This information will be used by the Admissions Committee to assess the candidate's suitability for a position on the committee.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. **Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
3. **Phone Number**: Provide your contact phone number for easy communication.
4. **Address**: Enter your current residential address.
5. **City**: Specify the city of your residence.
6. **State/Province**: Indicate the state or province of your residence.
7. **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
8. **Country**: Specify the country of your residence.
9. **Date of Birth**: Enter your birth date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
10. **Current Position**: Describe your current job title or position.
11. **Employer/Organization**: Enter the name of your current employer or organization.
12. **Education Background**: Briefly describe your educational background.
13. **Relevant Experience**: Describe any experience relevant to the admissions committee.
14. **Previous Committee Experience**: Indicate whether you have served on a similar committee before and describe your experience.
15. **Motivation for Joining**: Explain why you are interested in joining the admissions committee.
16. **Availability Commitment**: Specify the number of hours you can commit to the committee per month.
17. **Meeting Availability**: List the times/days you are generally available for committee meetings.
18. **Special Skills/Expertise**: Mention any special skills or expertise you bring to the committee.
19. **Diversity Perspective**: Describe how you would contribute to diverse perspectives on the committee.
20. **Conflict of Interest**: Indicate if you have any potential conflicts of interest and explain them.
21. **Conflict of Interest Details**: If you have a conflict of interest, provide more details.
22. **Reference 1 Name**: Enter the name of your first reference.
23. **Reference 1 Position**: Enter the position/title of your first reference.
24. **Reference 1 Email**: Enter the email address of your first reference.
25. **Reference 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of your first reference.
26. **Reference 2 Name**: Enter the name of your second reference (optional).
27. **Reference 2 Position**: Enter the position/title of your second reference (optional).
28. **Reference 2 Email**: Enter the email address of your second reference (optional).
29. **Reference 2 Phone**: Enter the phone number of your second reference (optional).
30. **Application Date**: Enter the date you submit this application.
31. **Applicant Signature**: E-Sign or provide a handwritten signature to confirm application submission.
