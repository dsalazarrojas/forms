# Writer Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Writer Consultation Form is used to collect information from writers who wish to collaborate with the organization on writing projects. The form gathers personal details, project requirements, and other relevant information to match writers with suitable projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Provide a brief description of the project you are interested in, including its title.
4. Describe the project in more detail in the Project Description field.
5. Choose your project status (Active or Inactive).
6. Select your writer support preferences (Yes or No).
7. If applicable, provide any additional comments about your project.
8. Confirm your agreement to collaborate on the project (Yes or No).
9. If applicable, provide any additional comments about your project.
10. Enter the project deadline and start date (if applicable).
11. Provide your phone number (if applicable).
12. Select your language proficiency (Yes or No).
13. Select your employment status (Active or Inactive).
14. Confirm your education background (Yes or No).
15. Provide a brief description of your experience in the field.
16. List your availability for the project.
17. If applicable, provide any special requests for the project.
18. Enter the name and email address of your project manager.
19. Enter the name of your writer manager.
20. Select whether you have references (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name, as you would like it to appear in correspondence.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, as you would like it to appear in correspondence.
* **Email**: Your email address that we will use to contact you.
* **Project Title**: A brief title of the project you are interested in.
* **Project Description**: A brief description of the project, including the scope and expectations.
* **Project Status**: If the project is currently Active or Inactive.
* **Writer Support Preferences**: Whether you prefer to work on projects that require support or not.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments about your project.
* **Writer Agreement**: Confirm that you are willing to collaborate on the project.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments about your project.
* **Project Deadline**: If you have a specific deadline for the project.
* **Project Start Date**: If you have a specific start date for the project.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Language**: Whether you are proficient in a specific language.
* **Employment Status**: If you are currently employed or not.
* **Education**: Whether you have a background in education.
* **Experience**: A brief description of your experience in the field.
* **Availability**: Your availability for the project.
* **Special Request**: Any special requests you have for the project.
* **Project Manager**: The name of your project manager.
* **Project Manager Email**: The email address of your project manager.
* **Writer Manager**: The name of your writer manager.
* **References**: Whether you have references that we can contact.
* **References**: Whether you have references that we can contact.
