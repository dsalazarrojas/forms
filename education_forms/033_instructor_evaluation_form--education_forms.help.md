# instructor_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and evaluations about an instructor's performance, teaching effectiveness, and overall experience. This information will help to assess the instructor's strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Answer each question according to your honest opinion and experience with the instructor.
3. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.
4. If a field is optional, you can choose to leave it blank or provide additional information if you feel it's relevant.
5. Save your answers when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction** (`id: 1`, `type: note`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or background information about the instructor or the class.

### 2. Instructor Name
* **instructor_name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Please enter the instructor's name.

### 3. Instructor Performance
* **instructor_performance** (`id: 3`, `type: number`, required: false): This field is used to evaluate the instructor's overall performance. Please select a number to indicate your opinion:

  * High (5)
  * Medium (3)
  * Low (1)

### 4. Class Name
* **class_name** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the class.

### 5. Teaching Effectiveness
* **Teaching Effectiveness** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the instructor's teaching methods. Please select all that apply:

  * High (5)
  * Medium (3)
  * Low (1)

### 6. Communication Skills
* **Communication Skills** (`id: 6`, `type: number`, required: false): This field is used to evaluate the instructor's communication skills. Please select a number to indicate your opinion:

  * High (5)
  * Medium (3)
  * Low (1)

### 7. Organization
* **Organization** (`id: 7`, `type: number`, required: false): This field is used to evaluate the instructor's ability to organize the class and materials. Please select a number to indicate your opinion:

  * High (5)
  * Medium (3)
  * Low (1)

### 8. Evaluation Period
* **Evaluation Period** (`id: 8`, `type: date`, required: false): This field is for recording the period of time during which you were evaluating the instructor. Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 9. Comments
* **Comments** (`id: 9`, `type: note`, required: false): Please add any additional comments you have about the instructor.

### 10. Recommended for Future Opportunity
* **Recommended for Future Opportunity** (`id: 10`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Do you think the instructor is suitable for future opportunities?

  * Yes
  * No

### 11. Evaluation
* **Evaluation** (`id: 11`, `type: select_one`, required: false): This field is used to evaluate the overall experience with the instructor. Please select a number to indicate your opinion:

  * High
  * Medium
  * Low

Remember, answers marked as `required: false` are optional and you can choose to leave them blank if not applicable.
