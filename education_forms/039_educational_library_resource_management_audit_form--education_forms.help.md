# educational_library_resource_management_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to conduct a management audit on educational resources within your library or institution. It helps librarians and staff evaluate the condition and availability of resources, and identify potential areas for improvement in resource management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you need to fill out the provided fields with the required information. Please follow these steps:

*   Enter the name of the school or institution.
*   Enter the name of the librarian or staff member performing the audit.
*   Check if the resource is available or not.
*   If the resource is available, check if it is in good condition.
*   Enter the date and time you last accessed the resource.
*   Enter the duration of the last access.
*   Enter the frequency of the last access.
*   Check if the resource is available or not.
*   Enter any findings or observations related to the resource.
*   Enter any follow-up actions needed.
*   Enter next steps for the resource.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **School Name**: (`1`, `text`, optional) Enter the name of the school or institution.
*   **Librarian Name**: (`2`, `text`, optional) Enter the name of the librarian or staff member performing the audit.
*   **Resource Type**: (`3`, `select_one`, optional) Check if the resource is available or not.
*   **Resource Condition**: (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional) If the resource is available, check if it is in good condition.
*   **Date Last Access**: (`5`, `date`, optional) Enter the date you last accessed the resource.
*   **Time Last Access**: (`6`, `time`, optional) Enter the time you last accessed the resource.
*   **Last Access Duration**: (`7`, `number`, optional) Enter the duration of the last access.
*   **Last Access Frequency**: (`8`, `number`, optional) Enter the frequency of the last access.
*   **Resource Availability**: (`9`, `select_one`, optional) Check if the resource is available or not.
*   **Findings**: (`10`, `text`, optional) Enter any findings or observations related to the resource.
*   **Follow-up Actions**: (`11`, `text`, optional) Enter any follow-up actions needed for the resource.
*   **Next Steps**: (`12`, `text`, optional) Enter next steps for the resource.
