# Campus Safety Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your safety experiences and perceptions on campus. Your input will help the university improve its safety measures and create a safer environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the response that best fits your experience or opinion.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you've finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Respondent Information
* **Your Role on Campus (1)**: Select the category that best describes your role on campus, such as Student, Faculty, Staff, etc.
* **Response should be anonymous (3)**: Decide whether you'd like to respond anonymously or include your name with your response.
* **How long have you been on this campus (4)**: Choose the time frame that best represents how long you've been on campus.

### General Campus Safety
* **Overall, how safe do you feel on campus (6)**: Choose how safe or unsafe you feel on campus overall.
* **Safety during daytime hours (7)**: Choose how safe or unsafe you feel on campus during the day.
* **Safety during nighttime hours (8)**: Choose how safe or unsafe you feel on campus at night.
* **Campus is well-lit in the evening (9)**: Decide whether you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **Have you experienced or witnessed safety incidents (11)**: Choose whether you've had any experience with incidents or seen others have them.
* **Type of incident (12)**: If you've experienced incidents, select all that apply.
* **Did you report the incident (13)**: Choose whether you reported the incident or not.

### Safety Infrastructure
* **Campus security presence is adequate (15)**: Decide whether you think the security presence on campus is sufficient.
* **Emergency procedures are clear and accessible (16)**: Choose whether you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **Emergency equipment and phones available (17)**: Decide whether you think emergency equipment and phones are readily available.

### Improvements
* **What are your main safety concerns on campus (19)**: Write down your primary concerns.
* **Suggestions for improving campus safety (20)**: Share any ideas you have to improve campus safety.

### Safety Resources
* **Are you aware of campus safety resources (22)**: Choose whether you're aware of resources or not.
* **Safety resources are easily accessible (23)**: Decide whether you think resources are easy to access.

### Overall Assessment
* **Overall Campus Safety Rating (25)**: Choose the rating that best fits your overall impression of campus safety.
