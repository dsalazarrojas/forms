# School Fee Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Fee Payment Form is designed to collect information related to school fee payments. It is intended for students, parents, or schools to provide details about school fee payments, including school details, payment information, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **School Details** field with relevant information about the school.
2. Provide details about the **Payment Details** in the corresponding field.
3. If you are ordering school supplies, fill in the **Order School Supplies** field.
4. If you are ordering school books, fill in the **Order School Books** field.
5. If you are ordering other items, fill in the **Other Items** field.
6. Enter the **Total Payment** amount in the corresponding field.
7. Confirm the **Confirm Payment** details.
8. If necessary, add your **Student Signature**, **Parent Signature**, and **School Signature**.
9. Select the **School Approval** status as Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
10. Select the **Order Status** status as Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
11. Select the **Payment Status** status as Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
12. Enter the **Payment Method ID**, **Payment Date ID**, and **Payment Amount ID** as required.
13. Enter the **School Details ID**, **Student Details**, and **Payment Total** as required.
14. Enter the **School Email ID**, **School Phone ID**, and **School Address ID** as required.
15. Enter the **Order Supplies ID**, **Order Books ID**, and **Other Items ID** as required.
