# Student Nature Journal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Nature Journal Form is a document used to record and observe student behavior, interests, or skills in an educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the user's name.
2. Enter the date of the observation.
3. Choose the weather condition observed.
4. Describe the location visited.
5. Provide any observations made during the observation.
6. Write any notes or comments about the student.
7. Enter the student's name (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_name** (text, required): This field is to be filled with the name of the person completing this form.
* **date_observed** (date, required): Enter the date on which the observation was made.
* **weather** (select one, required): Choose the current weather condition observed during the observation. Options include:
	+ Clear
	+ Overcast
	+ Light Rain
	+ Light Cloud
	+ Strong Winds
	+ Light Winds
	+ Snow
	+ Rain
	+ Fog
	+ Thunder
	+ Sunny
	+ Cloudy
* **observations** (text, optional): Describe the observations made during the observation. Be as detailed as possible.
* **location_visited** (text, optional): Enter the location visited during the observation.
* **teacher_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher who conducted the observation.
* **notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the observation.
* **student_name** (text, optional): If this form is being filled out by someone other than the student, enter the student's name.
