# Personal Narrative Writing Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Narrative Writing Guide is a form designed to collect information about your background, writing goals, and current position. This information will help you to reflect on your own experiences and goals, and can be used as a reference point for future writing and professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review each section carefully and answer each question thoroughly.
2. Fill in your answers to the best of your ability, without worrying about grammar or spelling errors.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your answer.
4. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)**: This section is for general information about yourself. Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Background Story (2)**: Describe your background, including your education, work experience, and any relevant achievements or accomplishments.
* **Writing Goals (3)**: Select the writing goals that apply to you from the list. This will help us understand what you aim to achieve with your writing.
	+ "Yes" - Indicate that you have these specific writing goals.
* **Current Position (4)**: Select "Yes" if you are currently a student, researcher, or professional in the field of education. This will help us understand your context and role in the field.
* **Narrative Plan (5)**: Select the narrative plans that apply to you from the list. This will help us understand how you approach your writing.
	+ "Yes" - Indicate that you use these specific narrative plans.
* **Next Steps (6)**: Select the next steps you plan to take in your writing career or project. This will help us understand your goals and intentions.
	+ "Yes" - Indicate that you plan to take these specific next steps.
* **Final Checklist (7)**: Review the final checklist to ensure you have covered all necessary points in your narrative writing. This will help you to reflect on your own writing and identify areas for improvement.
*   Select "Yes" for each point if you have achieved or plan to achieve them.
