# Makeup School Instructor Renewal Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup School Instructor Renewal Application form is used for makeup school instructors to renew their teaching certification. This form collects necessary information to process your renewal request.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Review all fields carefully to ensure you have all required information before starting.
*   Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
*   Provide your valid Email address.
*   Enter your Instructor ID.
*   If you have a Teaching Certification Number, provide it.
*   If you have a Teaching Certification Date, provide it.
*   Enter your School Name, which is the name of the school where you work.
*   Enter the date you are requesting renewal.
*   Decide whether you are requesting renewal or not.
*   If you are requesting renewal, provide a brief reason why.
*   Select the documents you are providing to support your renewal request.
*   Sign your name to confirm your identity.
*   Provide your Phone number.
*   Enter your Email address.
*   Enter your Phone number for work or personal use.
*   Enter your School Address.
*   Enter your School Name.
*   Enter the city where your school is located.
*   Enter the state where your school is located.
*   Enter your school's ZIP code.
*   Enter your school's Phone number.
*   Enter your school's Email address.
*   If you are requesting renewal again, enter a new date for the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your last name.
*   **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Instructor ID** (Text, Optional): Provide your instructor ID number.
*   **Teaching Certification Number** (Text, Optional): If you have a teaching certification number, enter it here.
*   **Teaching Certification Date** (Date, Optional): If you have a teaching certification date, enter it here.
*   **School Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the school where you work.
*   **Renewal Request Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you are requesting renewal.
*   **Confirm Renewal** (Select One, Optional): Decide whether you are renewing or not.
*   **Why are you renewing?** (Text, Optional): If you are renewing, provide a brief reason why.
*   **Documents** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the documents you are providing to support your renewal request.
*   **Signature** (Note, Optional): Sign your name to confirm your identity.
*   **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Phone 2** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's phone number.
*   **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your school address.
*   **School Name 2** (Text, Optional): Enter your school name.
*   **School Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your school address.
*   **School City** (Text, Optional): Enter the city where your school is located.
*   **School State** (Text, Optional): Enter the state where your school is located.
*   **School ZIP** (Number, Optional): Enter your school's ZIP code.
*   **School Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your school phone number.
*   **School Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your school email address.
*   **Renewal Request Date 2** (Date, Optional): If you are requesting renewal again, enter a new date.
*   **Confirm Renewal 2** (Select One, Optional): Decide whether you are renewing or not again.

## Tips

*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help.
*   Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in processing your renewal request.
*   Please attach all required documents to support your renewal request.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
