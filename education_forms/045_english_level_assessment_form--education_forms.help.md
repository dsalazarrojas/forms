# English Level Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The English Level Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate a student's English language proficiency level. It gathers information about the student's name, date of birth, native language, English language proficiency level, and other relevant details to assess their language skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student name" field.
2. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
3. Select the student's native language from the dropdown options.
4. Choose the student's English language proficiency level from the dropdown options.
5. If applicable, specify the language school attended.
6. Describe the English language skills being assessed.
7. Provide your assessment of the student's current language level.
8. Add any additional comments from the teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name**: Enter the student's full name as it is written on their official identification documents.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 12/09/1999).
* **Native language**: Select the student's native language from the dropdown options provided.
* **Level of English language proficiency**: Choose the student's English language proficiency level from the dropdown options:
	+ Beginner: For students who have little to no English language proficiency.
	+ Intermediate: For students who have some English language proficiency, but may still struggle with complex tasks.
	+ Advanced: For students who have a good command of English language, but may still make some mistakes.
	+ Native: For students who have a near-native level of English language proficiency.
* **Language school attended**: If the student has attended a language school or received English language instruction, specify the name of the school.
* **English language skills assessed**: Describe the specific English language skills being assessed, such as listening, speaking, reading, or writing.
* **Student language level**: Provide an overall assessment of the student's current English language level, being as specific as possible.
* **Teacher comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback from the teacher about the student's language level.
* **Student language level other (specify)**: If the student's language level does not fit into the provided options, describe their level in this field.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the student's language level and skills.
* Choose the most accurate option for the student's English language proficiency level.
* Use the "Teacher comments" field to provide feedback that will help the student improve their language skills.
* If the student's language level does not fit into the provided options, use the "Student language level other (specify)" field to provide a clear description.
