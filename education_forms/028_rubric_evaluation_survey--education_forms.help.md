# rubric_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a student's performance in various aspects such as performance, communication, and collaboration. It provides an opportunity to assess their strengths and weaknesses and provide constructive feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the student's performance and consider their strengths and weaknesses in each aspect (performance, communication, collaboration, and rubric evaluation).
2. Use the numerical rating scale to rate their performance in each aspect. A higher rating indicates better performance.
3. Provide a brief summary of their performance in the evaluation comments field.
4. If required, provide a score and weightage for their performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **rubric_performance** (`performance`, number, required: false): Rate the student's performance in this aspect. A higher rating indicates better performance.
* **rubric_communication** (`communication`, number, required: false): Rate the student's communication skills in this aspect. A higher rating indicates better communication.
* **rubric_creativity** (`collaboration`, number, required: false): Rate the student's collaboration skills in this aspect. A higher rating indicates better collaboration.
* **rubric_evaluation** (`rubric_evaluation`, text, required: false): Provide a brief summary of the student's professional performance.
* **evaluation_comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about the student's performance.
* **rubric_score** (`rubric_score`, number, required: false): If required, provide a final score for the student's performance.
* **rubric_score_range** (`rubric_score_range`, number, required: false): If required, provide the range for the score calculation.
* **rubric_weightage** (`rubric_weightage`, number, required: false): If required, provide the weightage for the score calculation.
* **rubric_weightage_range** (`rubric_weightage_range`, number, required: false): If required, provide the range for the weightage calculation.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when rating the student's performance.
* Provide constructive feedback in the evaluation comments field to help the student improve.
* If required, ensure that the score and weightage are calculated correctly to avoid any discrepancies.
