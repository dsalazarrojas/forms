# Academic Text Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Academic Text Evaluation Survey" is designed for reviewers to provide an evaluation and feedback on scholarly texts assigned for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name as the reviewer.
2. Enter your email address, which will not be used for any purposes other than communication with us.
3. Select your institutional affiliation.
4. Provide the title of the text being evaluated.
5. Identify the author(s) of the text.
6. Choose the type of text (e.g., textbook, research article, monograph, etc.).
7. Rate the text's clarity and readability.
8. Rate the text's accuracy and up-to-date-ness.
9. Rate the text's relevance to its intended academic audience.
10. Rate the text's organization.
11. Rate the text's effectiveness as a learning resource.
12. Provide a brief description of the text's main strengths.
13. Provide a description of the text's main weaknesses or areas for improvement.
14. Select your overall recommendation for the text's adoption.
15. Add any additional comments for the author or editorial team, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reviewer Full Name
* **Reviewer full name**: Enter your full name as the reviewer. 
### Reviewer Email Address
* **Reviewer email address**: Enter your email address, which will not be used for any purposes other than communication with us.
### Institutional Affiliation
* **Institutional affiliation**: Select your institutional affiliation.
### Text Title
* **Title of the text**: Enter the title of the text being evaluated.
### Author(s)
* **Author(s) of the text**: Enter the author(s) of the text.
### Text Type
* **Type of text**: Choose the type of text (e.g., textbook, research article, monograph, etc.).
### Clarity and Readability
* **How would you rate the clarity and readability of the text**: Rate the text's clarity and readability on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 represents "Poor" and 5 represents "Excellent".
### Accuracy and Up-to-Date-ness
* **How accurate and up-to-date is the content**: Rate the text's accuracy and up-to-date-ness on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 represents "Poor" and 5 represents "Excellent".
### Relevance
* **How relevant is the text to its intended academic audience**: Rate the text's relevance on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 represents "Not relevant" and 5 represents "Extremely relevant".
### Organization
* **How well organized is the text**: Rate the text's organization on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 represents "Poor" and 5 represents "Excellent".
### Effectiveness as a Learning Resource
* **How effective is the text as a learning resource**: Rate the text's effectiveness on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 represents "Not effective" and 5 represents "Extremely effective".
### Main Strengths
* **What are the main strengths of this text**: Provide a brief description of the text's main strengths.
### Main Weaknesses
* **What are the main weaknesses or areas for improvement**: Provide a description of the text's main weaknesses or areas for improvement.
### Overall Recommendation
* **Overall recommendation**: Select your overall recommendation for the text's adoption.
### Additional Comments
* **Any additional comments for the author or editorial team**: Add any additional comments or feedback for the author or editorial team.
