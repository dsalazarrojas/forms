# Accounting Course Waitlist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Course Waitlist form is a survey designed to gather information from potential students about their background, goals, and preferences for an accounting course. This information will be used by instructors and administrators to provide personalized course recommendations and tailor the course to meet the needs of students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select your preferred course format, schedule, and start date.
4. Choose your preferred course topic.
5. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Name
* **Full Name** (\*): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (\*): Enter your email address where you can be reached for course updates and information.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (\*): Provide your phone number where you can be reached for course-related matters.

### Mailing Address
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your city and state (or province) where you reside.

### Current Status
* **Current Status**: Select one of the following options to describe your current status:
	+ Working professional
	+ Full-time student
	+ Part-time student
	+ Career changer
	+ Retired
	+ Other

### Accounting Experience
* **Accounting Experience**: Select one of the following options to describe your previous accounting experience:
	+ None
	+ Basic knowledge (self-taught)
	+ Some coursework completed
	+ Currently working in accounting
	+ Certified professional seeking refresher
	+ Other

### Highest Level of Education Completed
* **Highest Level of Education Completed**: Select one of the following options to describe your highest level of education completed:
	+ High school diploma
	+ Associate degree
	+ Bachelor degree
	+ Master degree
	+ Doctorate
	+ Other

### Primary Goal for Taking This Course
* **Primary Goal for Taking This Course**: Select one of the following options to describe your primary goal for taking this course:
	+ Career advancement
	+ Career change into accounting
	+ Prepare for CPA or certification exam
	+ Personal knowledge
	+ Manage my own business finances
	+ Other

### Course of Interest
* **Course of Interest**: Select one of the following options to describe your preferred course topic:
	+ Introduction to Accounting
	+ Intermediate Accounting
	+ Advanced Accounting
	+ Tax Accounting
	+ Managerial Accounting
	+ Bookkeeping Fundamentals
	+ Other

### Preferred Course Format
* **Preferred Course Format**: Select one of the following options to describe your preferred course format:
	+ In-person
	+ Online (live)
	+ Online (self-paced)
	+ Hybrid

### Preferred Schedule
* **Preferred Schedule**: Select one of the following options to describe your preferred schedule:
	+ Weekday mornings
	+ Weekday evenings
	+ Weekends
	+ Flexible or self-paced

### When Would You Like to Start
* **When Would You Like to Start**: Select one of the following options to describe when you would like to start the course:
	+ As soon as possible
	+ Next month
	+ Next quarter
	+ Next semester
	+ Flexible

### How Did You Hear About This Course
* **How Did You Hear About This Course**: Select one of the following options to describe how you heard about this course:
	+ Website
	+ Social media
	+ Email newsletter
	+ Referral from friend or colleague
	+ Search engine
	+ Other

### Additional Comments or Questions
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Provide any additional comments or questions you may have about the course.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your information and get in touch with you to discuss your course options.
