# Student Test Result Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Test Result Submission Form is a data collection form used to record and track student performance. This form captures essential details related to a student's test results, including scores, attendance, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's unique **student number** (`student_number`) to identify the student.
2. Enter the name of the **teacher** (`teacher_name`) who administered the test.
3. Enter the **test date** (`student_test_date`) when the test was taken.
4. Record the **exam score** (`exam_score`) achieved by the student.
5. Indicate the **test result** (`test_result`) by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
6. Provide **remarks** (`remarks`) about the student's performance if needed.
7. Select the **test grade** (`student_test_grade`) by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
8. Select the **grade distribution** (`student_grade_distribution`) by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
9. Enter the **teacher comments** (`teacher_comments`) regarding the student's performance.
10. Record the **additional comments** (`additional_comments`) about the student.
11. Select **reference** (`reference`) by choosing a specific date.
12. Choose the **attendance status** (`attendance_status`) by selecting 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
13. Select the **conduct status** (`conduct_status`) by choosing 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
14. Select the **health status** (`health_status`) by picking 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
15. Select the **absence status** (`absence_status`) by choosing 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
16. Select the **carry over status** (`carry_over_status`) by choosing 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
17. Record the **attendance percentage** (`attendance_percentage`) as a numerical value.
18. Enter the **average attendance** (`average_attendance`) as a numerical value.
19. Enter the **total attendance** (`total_attendance`) as a numerical value.
20. Select **classroom attendance** (`classroom_attendance`) by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
21. Record the **classroom attendance percentage** (`classroom_attendance_percentage`) as a numerical value.
22. Enter the **classroom average attendance** (`classroom_average_attendance`) as a numerical value.
23. Enter the **classroom total attendance** (`classroom_total_attendance`) as a numerical value.
24. Record the **classroom attendance days** (`classroom_attendance_days`) as a numerical value.
25. Select the **classroom attendance frequency** (`classroom_attendance_frequency`) by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Number** (`student_number`, Number, Required): Enter the unique student identification number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the teacher who administered the test.
* **Student Test Date** (`student_test_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date when the test was taken.
* **Exam Score** (`exam_score`, Number, Required): Record the score achieved by the student in the exam.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, Select One, Optional): Indicate the result of the test by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments about the student's performance.
* **Test Grade** (`student_test_grade`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the test grade.
* **Grade Distribution** (`student_grade_distribution`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the grade distribution.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any comments from the teacher about the student.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the student.
* **Reference** (`reference`, Date, Required): Enter the reference date.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, Select One, Optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the attendance status.
* **Conduct Status** (`conduct_status`, Select One, Optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the conduct status.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, Select One, Optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the health status.
* **Absence Status** (`absence_status`, Select One, Optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the absence status.
* **Carry Over Status** (`carry_over_status`, Select One, Optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the carry over status.
* **Attendance Percentage** (`attendance_percentage`, Number, Required): Record the attendance percentage as a numerical value.
* **Average Attendance** (`average_attendance`, Number, Required): Enter the average attendance as a numerical value.
* **Total Attendance** (`total_attendance`, Number, Required): Enter the total attendance as a numerical value.
* **Classroom Attendance** (`classroom_attendance`, Select One, Optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate classroom attendance.
* **Classroom Attendance Percentage** (`classroom_attendance_percentage`, Number, Required): Record the classroom attendance percentage as a numerical value.
* **Classroom Average Attendance** (`classroom_average_attendance`, Number, Required): Enter the classroom average attendance as a numerical value.
* **Classroom Total Attendance** (`classroom_total_attendance`, Number, Required): Enter the classroom total attendance as a numerical value.
* **Classroom Attendance Days** (`classroom_attendance_days`, Number, Required): Record the classroom attendance days as a numerical value.
* **Classroom Attendance Frequency** (`classroom_attendance_frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate classroom attendance frequency.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Verify the accuracy of the data entered.
* Review the form thoroughly before submitting to ensure all information is correct and complete.
