# Department Of Health Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Department of Health Data Collection Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals regarding their education history, health status, and contact details. This form helps the Department of Health to gather accurate and up-to-date information about individuals for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required to fill out the form accurately.
2. Review each section carefully and fill in the corresponding information.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education History** (education_form_1, note, required: false): This section asks for a brief description of your educational background. Please provide a short paragraph explaining your education history.
* **Health Status** (health_status_1, select_one, required: false): Choose your current health status from the available options:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Education History** (education_form_2, time, required: false): Enter the date and time of your highest level of education (e.g., high school diploma or degree completion).
* **Contact Number** (contact_number_1, text, required: false): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Education History** (education_form_3, select_multiple, required: false): Answer the following question:
	+ Do you have a history of higher education?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Health History** (health_history_1, date, required: false): Enter the date of your last check-up or visit to a healthcare provider.
* **Contact Number 2** (contact_number_2, text, required: false): Enter your secondary contact number.
* **Email** (email_address_1, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Education History** (education_form_4, select_one, required: false): Answer the following question:
	+ Do you have any other relevant education history?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Contact Number 3** (contact_number_3, text, required: false): Enter your tertiary contact number.
* **Department** (department_form_1, select_one, required: false): Answer the following question:
	+ Are you affiliated with any government department?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Contact Number 4** (contact_number_4, text, required: false): Enter your quaternary contact number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the Department of Health for assistance.
* This form is for official purposes only, and data collected will be used to improve health services.
