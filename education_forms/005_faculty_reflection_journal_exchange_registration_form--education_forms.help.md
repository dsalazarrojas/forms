# faculty_reflection_journal_exchange_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information about faculty members participating in a workshop or exchange, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of their reflection journals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your Name, Email, Phone, Institution, Role, Workshop Code, Start Date, and End Date accurately and completely.
3. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for Frequency, depending on your regular participation in workshops or exchanges.
4. If required, provide Additional Information to give context to your participation.
5. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Your full name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address that can be used for communication regarding your participation.
* **Phone**: A working phone number, preferably with an international format (+123 456 7890).
* **Institution**: The name of the institution you are affiliated with.
* **Role**: Your role within the institution (e.g., Professor, Researcher, etc.).
* **Workshop Code**: A unique code for the specific workshop or exchange you are participating in.
* **Start Date**: The date you started participating in the workshop or exchange.
* **End Date**: The expected or actual end date of your participation.
* **Frequency**: Choose 'Yes' if you participate regularly in workshops or exchanges, 'No' otherwise.
* **Additional Information**: If needed, provide any extra details about your participation or the context surrounding it.
