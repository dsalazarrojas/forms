# open_science_education_community_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about individuals interested in joining the open science education community. This form will help us understand who you are, how you can contribute, and what kind of support you might need. By providing your details, you'll be helping us build a stronger community that fosters collaboration and learning among science enthusiasts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Fill out the form completely, but you can skip fields that are marked as optional.
3. Make sure to submit your form when you've finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Registration Form**
 * **Registration Form**: This is the main form section where you can start by providing your registration details.
 
### 2. **Contact Details**
 * **Contact Details**: This field allows you to provide your contact information, such as your email address or phone number. This will help us get in touch with you regarding your community involvement.
 
### 3. **Member Info**
 * **Member Info**: This field is for providing general information about yourself and your interests in the open science education community. Think about your background, skills, or areas of expertise you'd like to contribute.
