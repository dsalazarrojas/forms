# Instrument Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a user's knowledge and experience with musical instruments. It collects information about their familiarity with various aspects of music, including the instrument they play, their experience with it, and their ability in specific skills such as theory and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the musical instrument you are most familiar with from the "Music Instrument" field.
2. If you have played the instrument for a certain number of years, select the correct range in the "Years of Instrument Experience" field.
3. Choose the specific areas where you feel you have a good knowledge of music theory, such as scales, chord progressions, reading notes, or sight reading.
4. If you have a specific goal in mind for your musical development, type it in the "Goals" field.
5. If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Finally, provide your contact email and phone number so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Instrument** (`instrument`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks you to select the musical instrument you have the most experience with. Choose the one that is most relevant to your experience.
* **Years of Instrument Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to measure your level of experience with the instrument. Select the correct range that best describes your experience.
* **Theory Knowledge** (`theory_knowledge`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to assess your knowledge of music theory. Choose the skills you feel you have a good understanding of, such as scales, chord progressions, reading notes, or sight reading.
* **Performance Skills** (`performance_skills`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to record any notable performance skills you possess, such as your ability to play complex pieces or improvisate freely.
* **Daily Practice Time** (`practice_time`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to measure the amount of time you spend practicing your instrument daily. Select the time range that best describes your practice schedule.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to record any specific goals you have for your musical development, such as improving your technique, mastering a new skill, or performing in front of an audience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): This field is used to gather your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to gather your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, required/optional): This field is automatically populated with the date and time you submitted the form.
