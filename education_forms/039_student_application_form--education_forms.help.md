# Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for student registration/enrollment purposes, gathering demographic and personal information from students, their parents, and school administrators.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each section carefully, providing accurate and complete information.
2. Use the options provided for select fields, such as "Student Documentation" and "School Administration".
3. If unsure about any field, leave it blank or contact the relevant authority for assistance.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information**: Enter your personal details, including any relevant student ID or identification numbers.
* **Parent Information**: Provide accurate contact information for your parents or guardians.
* **Student Documentation**: Select the required documentation (ID Card, Birth Certificate, Report Card, or Transcript).
* **Education Background**: Share your relevant education history, including any previous schools attended or academic achievements.
* **Student Address**: Enter your current address, including street address, city, state, or province, and postal code.
* **School History**: Briefly describe your previous school(s) attended, including name, location, and date of attendance.
* **Contact Information**: Enter accurate contact details, including phone number, email, and any additional contact preferences.
* **Student Interests**: Select all relevant interests, such as Art, Sports, Dance, Music, or Theater.
* **Student Education Plan**: Outline your future educational plans, including academic goals and desired programs.
* **School Administration**: Select 'Yes' if the school has an administrative contact person, 'No' otherwise.
* **School Administrator Name**: Enter the name of the school administrator who completed this form.
* **School Administrator Title**: Enter the title of the school administrator who completed this form.
* **School Administrator Comments**: Any additional comments or feedback from the school administrator.

Note: Some fields may require more explanation or clarification. Please contact the relevant authority if you have questions or concerns about any field.
