# Students And Faculty Attendance Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record the attendance of students and faculty members. It provides a clear and accurate overview of their attendance status, which can be used for monitoring and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant student or faculty member from the database or input their unique ID.
2. Fill out the relevant fields with the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Id** (Student Id, number, required): Enter the student's unique ID number.
* **Faculty Id** (Faculty Id, number, required): Enter the faculty member's unique ID number.
* **Date** (Date, date, not required): Enter the date of the attendance record. This is an optional field.
* **Attendance Status** (Attendance Status, select_one, not required): Select the attendance status from the options provided.
	+ Present: Student or faculty member was present on the specified date.
	+ Absent: Student or faculty member was absent from the specified date.
	+ Late: Student or faculty member was late on the specified date.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, not required): Add any relevant comments or additional information about the attendance record.
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, not required): Enter the full name of the student.
* **Faculty Name** (Faculty Name, text, not required): Enter the full name of the faculty member.
* **Attendance Type** (Attendance Type, select_one, not required): Select the type of attendance from the options provided.
	+ Regular: Regular attendance.
	+ Holiday: Attendance on a holiday.
	+ Other: Other type of attendance.
* **Attendance Time** (Attendance Time, time, not required): Enter the time of the attendance record.
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, text, not required): Add any additional information about the attendance record.
