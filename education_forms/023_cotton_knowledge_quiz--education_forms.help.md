# Cotton Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and preferences regarding their engagement with the cotton industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form questions carefully and choose the most accurate response to each question.
2. Answer each question to the best of your knowledge.
3. Select the appropriate options for each question, taking into account the provided options.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, submit the form to proceed.

* **Cotton Knowledge Quiz (1)** (`cotton_knowledge_quiz`, text, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can briefly describe your experience or engagement with the cotton industry.
* **Cotton Types (2)** (`cotton_types`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following types of cotton:
	+ Cotton
	+ Linen
	+ Polyester
	+ Rayon
	+ Acrylic
	+ Nylon
* **Cotton Fibre (3)** (`cotton_fibre`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Cotton
	+ Linen
	+ Tencel
* **Quiz Duration (4)** (`quiz_duration`, number, optional): Please enter the duration of your quiz participation in minutes.
* **Quiz Score (5)** (`quiz_score`, number, optional): Enter your quiz score out of 100.
* **Quiz Score Out of (6)** (`quiz_score_out_of`, number, optional): This field is for the maximum possible score.
* **Quiz Submission (7)** (`quiz_submission`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your submission method:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ maybe
* **Quiz Submission Method (8)** (`quiz_submission_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you submit your quiz:
	+ online
	+ offline
* **Quiz Completion Time (9)** (`quiz_completion_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you take to complete the quiz.
* **Quiz Completion Date (10)** (`quiz_completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your quiz completion.
* **Quiz Completion Time (11)** (`quiz_completion_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your quiz completion.
* **Quiz Completion Notes (12)** (`quiz_completion_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your quiz completion.
* **Quiz Completion Notes (13)** (`quiz_completion_notes`, note, optional): Another field for additional comments or notes.
* **Email Notification (14)** (`email_notification`, select_one, optional): Choose if you want to receive email notifications:
	+ True
