## Artworks Studio Day Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artworks Studio Day application form is a document designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in Artworks Studio Day, a hands-on art workshop for students. This form helps us understand your background, art interests, and preferences to provide the best experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in your details accurately and thoroughly. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): If you are under 18, please enter your date of birth to help us assess your eligibility for certain programs.
* **Current School or Institution** (Current School or Institution, text, required): Please enter the name of your current school or educational institution.
* **Grade or Education Level** (Grade or Education Level, text, required): Please enter your current grade level or education level, e.g., Elementary, High School, College/University.
* **Studio Day Date Applying For** (Studio Day Date Applying For, date, required): Select the date you are applying for Artworks Studio Day.
* **Primary Art Interest or Medium** (Primary Art Interest or Medium, select_one, required): Choose your primary art interest or medium, e.g., Painting, Drawing, Sculpture, etc.
* **Experience Level in Art** (Experience Level in Art, select_one, required): Choose your level of experience in art, e.g., Beginner, Some Experience, Intermediate, etc.
* **Prior Studio Day Participation** (Prior Studio Day Participation, select_one, optional): If you have participated in Artworks Studio Day before, select the number of times you've attended.
* **Art Goals or Interests** (Art Goals or Interests, text, required): Describe what you want to explore or learn in the workshop.
* **Favorite Artists or Inspirations** (Favorite Artists or Inspirations, text, optional): List your favorite artists or inspirations.
* **Workshops or Classes Interested In** (Workshops or Classes Interested In, select_multiple, required): Choose the workshops or classes you are interested in, e.g., Painting Workshop, Drawing Workshop, etc.
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (Accessibility or Accommodation Needs, text, optional): If you have any accessibility or accommodation needs, please let us know so we can provide necessary arrangements.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Parent or Guardian Name, text, optional): If you are under 18, please enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone Number** (Parent or Guardian Phone Number, text, optional): If you are under 18, please enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (Emergency Contact Information, text, optional): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (Dietary Restrictions or Allergies, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know so we can provide suitable accommodations.
* **Permission for Photos or Media Use** (Permission for Photos or Media Use, select_one, required): Choose whether you allow us to take photos or use your media during the workshop.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (Additional Information or Comments, text, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please include them here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Be honest and accurate when filling in your details.
- If you need assistance or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
