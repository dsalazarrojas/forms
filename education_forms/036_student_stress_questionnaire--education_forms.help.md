# Student Stress Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Stress Questionnaire is designed to gather information from students to understand their stress levels, sources of stress, and support systems in place.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the form on one page at a time.
3. Be mindful of the options provided for each question, as they are intended to reflect your current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ 0-12
	+ 13-15
	+ 16-18
	+ 19
* **School Type** (`school_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Public School
	+ Private School
	+ Home Schooled
* **Student Stress Level** (`student_stress_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Common Stressors** (`common_stressors`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Schoolwork overload
	+ Bullying
	+ Financial struggles
	+ Social relationships
	+ Family issues
	+ Health problems
	+ Academic expectations
	+ Personal issues
* **Stress Causes** (`stress_causes`, text, optional): Type in any other common stressors you experience.
* **School Support** (`school_support`, text, optional): Describe the support you receive from your school.
* **Mental Health Support** (`mental_health_support`, text, optional): Describe the mental health support you receive.
* **Academic Support** (`academic_support`, text, optional): Describe the academic support you receive.
* **Family Support** (`family_support`, text, optional): Describe the support you receive from your family.
* **Student English** (`student_english`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Fluent in English
	+ Partially fluent in English
	+ None fluent in English
* **Student English Reading** (`student_english_reading`, text, optional): Describe your English reading level.
* **Student English Writing** (`student_english_writing`, text, optional): Describe your English writing level.
* **Student Math** (`student_math`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Below average
	+ Average
	+ Above average
* **Student Math Support** (`student_math_support`, text, optional): Describe the math support you receive.
* **Student Other Math Support** (`student_other_math_support`, text, optional): Describe other math support you receive.
* **Student English Support** (`student_english_support`, text, optional): Describe English support you receive.
* **Student Math Support Level** (`student_math_support_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ None
	+ One-on-one support
	+ Group support
* **Student Math Tutor** (`student_math_tutor`, text, optional): Describe your math tutor.
* **Student Math Tutor Frequency** (`student_math_tutor_frequency`, text, optional): Describe the frequency of your math tutor.
* **Student Other Support** (`student_other_support`, text, optional): Describe other support you receive.
* **Student Other Tutor** (`student_other_tutor`, text, optional): Describe other tutor you receive.
* **Student Other Tutor Frequency** (`student_other_tutor_frequency`, text, optional): Describe the frequency of your other tutor.
* **Student Tutor Support** (`student_tutor_support`, text, optional): Describe the support from your tutor.
* **Student Other Tutor Type** (`student_other_tutor_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of other tutor you receive.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about any question, describe your situation to the best of your ability.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they reflect your true situation.
