# Library Services Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from library patrons regarding their experience with library services. The information collected will help us understand the effectiveness and quality of our library services, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the library services evaluation form.
2. Select the category related to your evaluation (e.g., book, program, etc.).
3. Provide a detailed description of your experience with the library services.
4. Indicate whether you have used the library services (yes/no).
5. Answer whether you are satisfied with the services (yes/no).
6. Answer whether you feel the library services align with your values and interests (yes/no).
7. Answer whether you would likely visit the library again (yes/no).
8. Indicate whether you would recommend the library services to others (yes/no).
9. Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
10. Enter your name and email address so we can follow up with you.
11. Enter the name and email address of the instructor or librarian providing the service.
12. Enter any additional comments or feedback about the instructor or librarian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Library Services Evaluation Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): A brief title for the evaluation form.
* **Category** (`form_category`, text, optional): Select the category related to your evaluation (e.g., book, program, etc.).
* **Description** (`form_description`, note, required): Provide a detailed description of your experience with the library services.
* **Used Library Services** (`form_questions`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have used the library services (yes/no).
* **Answer** (`form_answer`, select_one, required): Answer whether you are satisfied with the services (yes/no).
* **Level of Education** (`form_education_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of education you have (high/low).
* **Frequency Used** (`form_frequency_used`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have used the services frequently (yes/no).
* **Satisfaction** (`form_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Answer whether you are satisfied with the services (yes/no).
* **Like Mindedness** (`form_likemindedness`, select_one, required): Answer whether you feel the library services align with your values and interests (yes/no).
* **Will Likely Visit** (`form_likely_visit`, select_one, required): Answer whether you would likely visit the library again (yes/no).
* **Would Recommend** (`form_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the library services to others (yes/no).
* **Additional Comments** (`form_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Name** (`form_name`, text, required): Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (`form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Instructor Name** (`form_instructor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the instructor or librarian providing the service.
* **Instructor Website** (`form_instructor_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the instructor or librarian.
* **Instructor Social Media** (`form_instructor_social`, text, optional): Enter the social media handle of the instructor or librarian.
* **Attendee Name** (`form_attended`, text, required): Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`form_attended_name`, email, required): Enter the email address of the attendee.
