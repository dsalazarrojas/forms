# Elementary School Message Recap - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elementary School Message Recap form is designed to help students reflect on their experiences and gather specific information from elementary school students. This form is intended for personal use, and the information collected will not be used for any commercial or marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form by entering your name, the date of the event or period of time being recapped, your teacher's name (if applicable), your group or class name (if applicable), and the subject or activity being discussed.
* Provide a brief message about your experience or thoughts about your elementary school time.
* Enter your school name (if applicable).
* Review and submit your form for personal use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your report card or identification card.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event or period being recapped.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your teacher or instructor for the class or activity being discussed.
* **Group** (`group`, text, optional): Enter the name of your group or class name if applicable.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, optional): Enter the subject or activity being discussed.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Provide a brief message about your experience or thoughts about your elementary school time.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school, if applicable.
