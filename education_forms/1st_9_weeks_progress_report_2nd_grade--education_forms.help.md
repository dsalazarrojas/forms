# 1St 9 Weeks Progress Report   2Nd Grade - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 1St 9 Weeks Progress Report for 2Nd Grade is a tool used by teachers to report on student performance, attendance, and progress over the first 9 weeks of the term. The form helps teachers track student progress and provide feedback to parents or guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's **Full name** in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter the student's **Student ID** (if applicable) in the "Student ID" field.
3. Select the **Date of report** when the progress report was completed.
4. Choose the **Grade/Year level** (2nd Grade) from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the **School or institution name** (if applicable) in the "School or institution name" field.
6. Enter the **Teacher's name** in the "Teacher name" field (including homeroom or subject teacher).
7. Enter the number of **Days present** this term in the "Days present this term" field.
8. Enter the **Average weekly absences** (typical absences per week, if any) in the "Average weekly absences" field.
9. Select the **Subject area** where this report focuses on (e.g., Mathematics, Science, etc.).
10. Enter the student's **Overall average (%)** as a numeric value between 0 and 100.
11. Choose the **Progress trend over the term** from the dropdown menu (e.g., Improving, Stable, Declining, Inconsistent).
12. Evaluate the student's **Behavior and classroom conduct** (e.g., Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory).
13. Select all applicable **Key strengths observed** from the dropdown menu (e.g., Reading fluency, Math problem-solving, etc.).
14. Identify all applicable **Areas for improvement** from the dropdown menu (e.g., Time management, Attention and focus, etc.).
15. Describe any **Interventions or supports provided this term** in the "Interventions or supports provided this term" field.
16. Select how the family was **contacted about progress** (e.g., Parent meeting, Phone call, Email, Not contacted).
17. Summarize any **Parent/guardian response** or concerns in the "Parent/guardian response" field.
18. Provide a concise **Teacher comments and summary** in the "Teacher comments and summary" field.
19. List **Recommended actions or supports** in the "Recommended actions or supports" field.
20. Enter the **Suggested follow-up date** for progress review (if applicable).
21. Enter your **Teacher signature (typed name)**.
22. Enter any **Additional comments or notes** in the "Additional comments or notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter the student's first and last name.
* **Student ID (if applicable)** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter the student's ID, if applicable.
* **Date of report** (`date_of_report`, date, required): Select the date this progress report was completed.
* **Grade/Year level** (`grade_level`, text, required): Select the grade level (2nd Grade).
* **School or institution name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the school or institution name, if applicable.
* **Teacher name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the teacher, including homeroom or subject teacher.
* **Days present this term** (`attendance_days_present`, integer, required): Enter the total number of days the student has attended.
* **Average weekly absences** (`average_daily_absences`, integer, optional): Enter the typical absences per week, if any.
* **Subject area** (`subject_area`, select_one, required): Select the primary subject this report focuses on (e.g., Mathematics, Science, etc.).
* **Overall average (%)** (`overall_average`, decimal, required): Enter the student's overall average as a numeric value between 0 and 100.
* **Progress trend over the term** (`progress_trend`, select_one, required): Choose the description of progress (e.g., Improving, Stable, Declining, Inconsistent).
* **Behavior and classroom conduct** (`behavior_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the student's behavior and classroom conduct (e.g., Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory).
* **Key strengths observed** (`strengths`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **Areas for improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): Identify all that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **Interventions or supports provided this term** (`interventions_provided`, text, optional): Describe any interventions or supports provided this term.
* **Parent/guardian contact method** (`parent_contacted`, select_one, required): Select how the family was contacted about progress (e.g., Parent meeting, Phone call, Email, Not contacted).
* **Parent/guardian response** (`parent_response`, text, optional): Summarize any feedback or concerns from the family.
* **Teacher comments and summary** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Provide a concise narrative of performance and classroom behavior.
* **Recommended actions or supports** (`recommended_actions`, text, optional): List targeted strategies, resources, or referrals.
* **Suggested follow-up date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the optional date to review progress after interventions.
* **Teacher signature (typed name)** (`teacher_signature`, text, required): Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any extra observations.
