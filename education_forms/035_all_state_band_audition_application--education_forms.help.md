# All State Band Audition Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The All-State Band Audition Application is a form used to evaluate and select students for the All-State Band event. This form requires detailed and accurate information about the student's musical background, experience, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Personal Information
Please start by filling out the personal details such as first name, last name, email, date of birth, and school name.

* **First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, required): Your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, required): Your last name as per your identification documents.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Your contact email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Your school name.

### Step 2: Choose Your Musical Background
Please provide details about your musical background, such as grade level, years playing your instrument, primary and secondary instruments, and music theory background.

* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your current grade level.
* **Years Playing Instrument** (`years_playing`, number, required): How many years you have been playing your instrument.
* **Primary Instrument** (`primary_instrument`, select_one, required): Choose your main instrument.
* **Secondary Instrument** (`secondary_instrument`, text, optional): If you have a secondary instrument, please list it here.
* **Music Theory Background** (`music_theory_background`, text, optional): If you have any music theory background, please describe here.

### Step 3: Provide Audition Details
Please provide details about your audition, such as your preferred audition date and time, location, and any additional requirements.

* **Audition Category** (`audition_category`, select_one, required): Select the ensemble you wish to audition for.
* **Prepared Pieces** (`prepared_pieces`, text, required): List the pieces you will perform during the audition.
* **Scales Prepared** (`scales_prepared`, select_multiple, required): Choose the scales you can play.
* **Audition Date Preference** (`audition_date_preference`, date, required): Enter your preferred audition date.
* **Audition Time Preference** (`audition_time_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred audition time.
* **Audition Location Preference** (`audition_location_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred audition location.

### Step 4: Provide Additional Information
Please provide any additional information about your background and qualifications.

* **Previous All-State Experience** (`previous_all_state_experience`, select_one, required): If you have auditioned before, describe your experience.
* **Honor Bands Participated** (`honor_bands_participated`, text, optional): If you have participated in any honor bands, list them here.
* **Music Goals** (`music_goals`, text, optional): Describe your music goals.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other comments or information you wish to share.

### Step 5: Confirm Your Application
Please confirm that you have completed the form accurately and truthfully.

* **Teacher Recommendation Attached** (`teacher_recommendation`, select_one, required): Confirm if your teacher has submitted a recommendation.
* **Application Fee Paid** (`application_fee_paid`, select_one, required): Confirm your application fee status.
* **Recording Submitted** (`recording_submitted`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have submitted a recording.
* **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, optional): If you need a ride to the audition, select this option.
* **Accommodation Needed** (`accommodation_needed`, text, optional): If you have any special needs, please describe here.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select your T-shirt size for the event.
* **Additional Musical Honors** (`additional_honors`, text, optional): If you have any other musical honors, list them here.
* **Why Do You Want to Join All-State Band?** (`why_all_state`, text, required): Describe why you wish to join the All-State Band.

## Tips
- Ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- Please provide detailed and specific information about your musical background and qualifications.
- If you have any special needs or requirements, please describe them in the "Accommodation Needed" field.
- Please submit your application fee and confirm that you have done so.
- If you have a teacher recommendation, please ensure that it is attached to your application.
