# Air Currents Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Currents Knowledge Quiz is designed to test your knowledge of basic meteorological phenomena related to air currents and winds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name in the field labeled "Student Name".
2. Enter the name of your class, for example, "Earth Science 101".
3. Select the correct answer for each of the 8 questions.
4. For each question, choose the best answer from the available options.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (student_name, text, required): Please enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Class Name** (class_name, text, required): Enter the name of your class, for example, "Earth Science 101".
* **1. Convection is:** (q1_convection, select_one, required): Choose the correct definition of convection. You can select from "Transfer of heat by touching", "Transfer of heat by movement of fluid/air", "Transfer of heat by light waves", or "None of the above".
* **2. Where are Jet Streams found?** (q2_jet_stream, select_one, required): Choose the correct location of jet streams. You can select from "In the ocean", "In the upper troposphere", "Underground", or "Near the equator surface".
* **3. The Coriolis Effect causes winds to:** (q3_coriolis, select_one, required): Choose the correct effect of the Coriolis effect. You can select from "Stop completely", "Curve due to Earth's rotation", "Move in straight lines", or "Heat up rapidly".
* **4. A Sea Breeze occurs because:** (q4_sea_breeze, select_one, required): Choose the correct cause of a sea breeze. You can select from "Land heats up faster than water", "Water heats up faster than land", "The moon pulls the air", or "It is raining".
* **5. Trade Winds blow from:** (q5_trade_winds, select_one, required): Choose the correct direction of trade winds. You can select from "West to East", "East to West", "North to South directly", or "South to North directly".
* **6. Air moves from:** (q6_pressure, select_one, required): Choose the correct direction of air movement. You can select from "Low pressure to High pressure", "High pressure to Low pressure", "It does not move based on pressure", or "High pressure to High pressure".
* **7. Wind speed is measured by an:** (q7_speed, select_one, required): Choose the correct instrument for measuring wind speed. You can select from "Thermometer", "Barometer", "Anemometer", or "Hygrometer".
* **8. The Global Circulation Model includes:** (q8_circulation, select_one, required): Choose the correct components of the global circulation model. You can select from "Hadley, Ferrel, and Polar Cells", "Only Hadley Cells", "Only Polar Cells", or "Random Cells".
