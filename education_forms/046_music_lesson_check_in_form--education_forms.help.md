# Music Lesson Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record student attendance and gather information about music lessons for administrative and educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's information (Name, Email, Phone).
2. Select the date and time of the lesson.
3. Indicate the student's attendance status (Yes/No).
4. Add any additional comments or notes for the lesson.
5. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`music_lesson_check_in_form_page_1`): Enter the student's name.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`): Enter the name of the music lesson instructor.
* **Lesson Information** (`music_lesson_check_in_form_page_2`): Enter the date and time of the music lesson.
* **Lesson Date** (`lesson_date`): Enter the date of the music lesson in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Lesson Time** (`lesson_time`): Enter the time of the music lesson in HH:MM format.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the name of the student who attended the lesson.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Lesson Notes** (`lesson_notes`): Enter any notes or comments about the lesson.
* **Attendance** (`attendance`): Select 'Yes' if the student attended the lesson, 'No' otherwise.
* **Music Lesson** (`music_lesson_check_in_form_page_3`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the music lesson.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the lesson.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Use the correct format for dates and times (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM, respectively).
* Enter accurate information for each student's data entry.
