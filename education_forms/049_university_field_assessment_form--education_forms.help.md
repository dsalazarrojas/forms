# university_field_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback and data from university field visits to assess the quality and effectiveness of the field visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate to the first page, "Field Visit."
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as per your experience and observations.
3. Move to the second page, "Student and Faculty Feedback."
4. Complete the feedback fields, providing honest and detailed comments.
5. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Visit** (1, text, required): Enter your observations and notes from the field visit.
* **University Name** (2, text, optional): If applicable, enter the university name for reference and context.
* **Visit Date** (3, date, optional): Enter the date of the field visit, if not already recorded elsewhere.
* **Observation** (4, text, optional): Record any significant observations or findings from the visit.
* **Challenge** (5, text, optional): Mention any challenges or difficulties encountered during the visit.
* **Recommendation** (6, text, optional): Suggest any improvements or recommendations for future visits.
* **Student Comments** (7, text, optional): Gather comments from students about their experience.
* **Faculty Comments** (8, text, optional): Gather comments from faculty regarding their experience.
* **Staff Comments** (9, text, optional): Gather comments from staff about their experience.
* **Student Attendance** (10, select_one, optional): Estimate the student attendance during the visit.
	+ Select from: 5 to 10 students, 11 to 20 students, 21 to 50 students, 51 to 100 students, More than 100 students.
* **Faculty and Staff** (11, select_multiple, optional): Identify the types of personnel who participated in or witnessed the field visit.
	+ Select from: Students, Faculty, Staff.
* **Student Participation** (12, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the level of student participation during the visit.
	+ Select from: High, Medium, Low.
* **Faculty Participation** (13, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the level of faculty participation during the visit.
	+ Select from: High, Medium, Low.
* **Challenges** (14, text, optional): Record any challenges or difficulties encountered during the visit.
* **Recommendations** (15, text, optional): Suggest any improvements or suggestions for future visits.
* **Follow-up** (16, select_one, optional): Determine the need for follow-up visits or actions.
	+ Select from: True, False.
* **Follow-up Date** (17, date, optional): Enter the date for any scheduled follow-up visit.
* **Follow-up By** (18, text, optional): Record who will conduct the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-up Note** (19, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the follow-up visit.
* **Student Satisfaction** (20, select_one, optional): Evaluate the level of student satisfaction with the visit.
	+ Select from: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Faculty and Staff Satisfaction** (21, select_one, optional): Evaluate the level of faculty and staff satisfaction with the visit.
	+ Select from: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your answers, providing context and explanations when necessary.
* Ensure consistency and accuracy in your feedback and data entry.
* Review and edit your answers before submitting the form to avoid errors.
