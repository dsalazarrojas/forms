# Facial Vocabulary Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facial Vocabulary Assessment form is designed to evaluate a student's response to a series of questions related to facial feature recognition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Click on the "Start" button to begin the assessment.
2.  Answer each question based on the student's or patient's observations and descriptions of facial features.
3.  For each question, select the most appropriate answer from the provided options.
4.  Make sure to answer each question thoroughly, as it will help in providing an accurate assessment.
5.  If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the administrator for assistance.

**Facial Feature Recognition** (Page 1):
*   **Facial Feature Recognition** (`first_page`, `text`, optional): This is the first question, where you are asked to describe or identify a specific facial feature. Please answer this question based on your observations and descriptions of facial features.

## Tips

*   Make sure to answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the administrator for assistance.
*   Please note that this form is designed to be completed by the student or patient only, and not by the administrator or anyone else.
