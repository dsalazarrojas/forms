# guests_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from guests, likely for quality control or customer service purposes. This form is designed to be simple and easy to fill out, making it convenient for guests to provide their input.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as completely and accurately as possible.
2. Enter your name and email address (if required or requested).
3. Enter your phone number (if required or requested).
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the introduction field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your feedback or comment. This field is optional and can be left blank if not necessary.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name, as you would like it to be displayed in any future correspondence.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if necessary.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, which will be used for any additional contact or follow-up.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields that are marked as required.
- If you have any specific feedback or comments, please use the introduction field.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the form administrators.
