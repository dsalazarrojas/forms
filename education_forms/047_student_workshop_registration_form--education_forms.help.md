# Student Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Workshop Registration Form is a crucial step in the workshop registration process. By filling out this form, you will be able to register for the workshop and participate in the activities and sessions planned for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form completely, including all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (Page 1)** (`Personal Details`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, student ID, and any other relevant information.

* **Contact Details (Page 1)** (`Contact Details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as your email address, phone number, and address.

* **Professional Background (Page 1)** (`Professional Background`, text, required: false): Share your background and experience in the relevant field of study or work.

* **Level of Involvement (Page 1)** (`Level of Involvement`, number, required: false): Select a level of involvement from 1 to 5 to indicate how actively you plan to participate in the workshop.

* **Responses to Topic-Related Questions (Page 1)** (`Responses to Topic-Related Questions`, text, required: false): Answer any relevant questions related to the workshop topic.

* **Terms and Conditions (Page 1)** (`Terms and Conditions`, text, required: false): Review and accept the terms and conditions of the workshop.

* **Workshop Terms (Page 2)** (`Workshop Terms`, text, required: false): Review and agree to the workshop terms and conditions.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact the workshop organizers for clarification.
*   Review the terms and conditions carefully before accepting them.
