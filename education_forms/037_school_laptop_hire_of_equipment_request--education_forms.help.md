# school_laptop_hire_of_equipment_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a school laptop or other equipment for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your request.
2. Provide a brief description of the equipment you are requesting.
3. Enter the name of the person who assessed the request.
4. Enter the email address of the person submitting the request.
5. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request.
6. If applicable, enter the date and time the equipment was hired.
7. Specify the details of the equipment being requested.
8. Indicate the quantity of equipment being hired.
9. If the equipment is not returned, specify the reason.
10. Enter any additional notes about the request.
11. Finally, enter your name and the date you submitted the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (Education Forms/Other Forms/Forms): Select the category for your equipment request. This will help us understand what type of equipment you are requesting.
- **Description** (Text): Describe the equipment you are requesting in a few sentences. This will help us understand what you need.
- **Assessor Name** (Text): Enter the name of the person who assessed your request.
- **Email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
- **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your request.
- **Date Hired** (Date): If applicable, enter the date and time the equipment was hired.
- **Time Hired** (Time): If applicable, enter the time the equipment was hired.
- **Equipment Details** (Text): Describe the equipment being requested in detail.
- **Equipment Type** (Text): Specify the type of equipment being requested.
- **Quantity Hired** (Number): Enter the number of equipment being hired.
- **Quantity Returned** (Number): If the equipment is being returned, enter the quantity being returned.
- **Reason Not Returned** (Text): If equipment is not being returned, enter a brief explanation.
- **Notes** (Text): Add any additional comments or information about your request.
- **Submitter** (Text): Enter your name so we can identify you as the submitter.
- **Submitted On** (Date): Enter the date you submitted this form.
