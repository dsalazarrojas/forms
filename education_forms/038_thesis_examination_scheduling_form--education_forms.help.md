# thesis_examination_scheduling_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Thesis Examination Scheduling Form" is used to schedule a thesis examination in an academic setting. This form helps to collect all necessary information for the examination process, including defense date, title, and details about the examination process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the defense date in the "Defense Date" field. This is the date on which the thesis examination will take place.
2. Enter the title of the thesis in the "Thesis Title" field.
3. Select the supervisor of the thesis in the "Supervisor" field. This is usually the professor who will be leading the examination.
4. Enter the name of the student submitting the thesis in the "Student Name" field.
5. Enter the names of committee members 1, 2, and 3 in the "Committee Member 1," "Committee Member 2," and "Committee Member 3" fields, respectively.
6. Select the department involved in the examination in the "Department" field. This helps in scheduling and planning the examination process.
7. Enter the name of the coordinator responsible for the examination in the "Coordinator" field.
8. Add any additional notes about the examination in the "Notes" field.
9. Set the defense time in the "Defense Time" field.
10. Enter the location of the examination in the "Defense Location" field.
11. Enter the room number where the examination will take place in the "Defense Room" field.
12. Set the duration of the examination in the "Defense Duration" field.
13. Select the type of examination in the "Defense Type" field.
14. Enter the capacity of the defense room in the "Defense Room Capacity" field.
15. Enter the number of attendees expected for the examination in the "Defense Attendees" field.
16. Set the schedule of the examination in the "Defense Schedule" field.
17. Set the start time of the examination in the "Defense Start Time" field.
18. Set the end time of the examination in the "Defense End Time" fields.
19. Enter the capacity of the defense room 1, 2, 3, and 4 in the "Defense Room Capacity 1," "Defense Room Capacity 2," "Defense Room Capacity 3," and "Defense Room Capacity 4" fields, respectively.

**Please note:** All fields are optional unless otherwise specified as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Defense Date** (date): Enter the date of the thesis examination.
* **Thesis Title** (text): Enter the title of the thesis.
* **Supervisor** (select_one): Select the professor who will be leading the examination.
* **Student Name** (text): Enter the name of the student submitting the thesis.
* **Committee Member 1** (text): Enter the name of the first committee member.
* **Committee Member 2** (text): Enter the name of the second committee member.
* **Committee Member 3** (text): Enter the name of the third committee member.
* **Department** (select_multiple): Select the department involved in the examination.
* **Coordinator** (text): Enter the name of the coordinator responsible for the examination.
* **Notes** (text): Add any additional notes about the examination.
* **Defense Time** (time): Set the time of the examination.
* **Defense Location** (text): Enter the location of the examination.
* **Defense Room** (text): Enter the room number where the examination will take place.
* **Defense Duration** (number): Set the duration of the examination.
* **Defense Type** (select_one): Select the type of examination.
* **Defense Room Capacity** (number): Enter the capacity of the defense room.
* **Defense Attendees** (text): Enter the number of attendees expected.
* **Defense Schedule** (date): Set the schedule of the examination.
* **Defense Start Time** (time): Set the start time of the examination.
* **Defense End Time** (time): Set the end time of the examination.
* **Defense Room Capacity 1** (number): Enter the capacity of the defense room 1.
* **Defense Room Capacity 2** (number): Enter the capacity of the defense room 2.
* **Defense Room Capacity 3** (number): Enter the capacity of the defense room 3.
* **Defense Room Capacity 4** (number): Enter the capacity of the defense room 4.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to enter all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* If you need help with any field, refer to your supervisor or coordinator.
