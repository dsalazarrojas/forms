# Archiving Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help you complete the "Archiving Knowledge Quiz" form, which assesses your understanding and experience in the field of digital archiving.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in your participant information: 
	* Full Name: Enter your full name as it should be recorded in the system.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further questions or updates.
2. Select your experience level in archiving: 
	* Digital Archiving Experience Level: Choose your level of experience in digital archiving. Options are: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Expert.
3. Complete the knowledge assessment questions: 
	* What is the primary goal of digital archiving: Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
	* Which file format is best for long-term preservation: Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
	* What does metadata provide in archives: Select all the correct options from the list.
	* Which is a key consideration for archive storage: Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
	* What is digital obsolescence: Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
	* List three archiving standards or practices you know: Enter the names of three archiving standards or practices you are familiar with.
	* What challenges have you faced with archiving: Select all the correct options from the list.
4. If necessary, specify additional training needs: 
	* Additional Training Needs: Enter any topics where you feel you need additional guidance or training.
5. Rate the difficulty of the quiz and provide feedback: 
	* How would you rate the difficulty of this quiz: Choose from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be recorded in the system.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further questions or updates.
* **Organization or Institution** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization or institution, enter its name.
* **Digital Archiving Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience in digital archiving. Options are: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Expert.
* **What is the primary goal of digital archiving** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct answer from the options provided. Options are: To preserve materials for future access, To make files smaller in size, To encrypt sensitive data, To backup server systems.
* **Which file format is best for long-term preservation** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct answer from the options provided. Options are: JPEG, PDF/A, DOCX, MP4.
* **What does metadata provide in archives** (`question_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the correct options from the list. Options are: Description of content, Access restrictions, Creation date information, File size information.
* **Which is a key consideration for archive storage** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct answer from the options provided. Options are: Temperature control, Network speed, User password complexity, Monitor refresh rate.
* **What is digital obsolescence** (`question_5`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct answer from the options provided. Options are: When data becomes corrupted, When technology used to access files becomes unavailable, When files are deleted, When bandwidth is exceeded.
* **Name three archiving standards or practices** (`question_6`, `text`, optional): Enter the names of three archiving standards or practices you are familiar with.
* **What challenges have you faced with archiving** (`question_7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the correct options from the list. Options are: Storage space limitations, File format compatibility, Cost constraints, Lack of technical expertise, Metadata management.
* **How would you rate the difficulty of this quiz** (`quiz_difficulty`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from the options provided.
* **Topics where you need additional training** (`training_needs`, `text`, optional): Enter any topics where you feel you need additional guidance or training.
* **Additional feedback** (`section_feedback`, `note`, optional): You can provide any additional feedback or suggestions for the form or the quiz.
