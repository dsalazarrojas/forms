# Homeschool Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect attendance information for students in homeschooling settings. It helps parents and educators keep track of student attendance, which is vital for monitoring progress, identifying areas of concern, and making informed decisions about the student's education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Student Name field with the student's name.
2. Enter the student's Grade in the Grade field.
3. Enter the Student ID number (if applicable).
4. Enter the Parent Name in the Parent Name field.
5. Enter the Parent Email (if applicable).
6. Select the Student Attendance status (Absent, Present, Late, or Unscheduled).
7. Enter the Date of attendance.
8. Enter the Time of attendance.
9. Select the Attendance Status (On-time, Late, or Absent).
10. (Optional) Enter comments about the student's attendance in the Comments field.
11. Enter the name and contact information of the submitter in the Submitted By field.
12. Enter the date when the form was submitted.
13. (Optional) Enter your school's name.
14. (Optional) Enter your school's email and phone number.
15. (Optional) Enter your teacher's name.
16. (Optional) Enter your teacher's email and phone number.
17. (Optional) Enter your teacher's address.
18. (Optional) Select your teacher's attendance status (Absent, Present, Late, or Unscheduled).
19. (Optional) Enter comments from the teacher about the student's attendance.
20. (Optional) Enter comments from the student about their attendance.
21. (Optional) Enter any school-specific notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Text, Required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Grade** (Text, Required): Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
* **Student ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's ID number (if applicable).
* **Parent Name** (Text, Required): Enter the parent's name as it appears on the student's records.
* **Parent Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the parent's email address (if applicable).
* **Student Attendance** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the attendance status (Absent, Present, Late, or Unscheduled) for the student.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of attendance.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of attendance.
* **Attendance Status** (Select One, Required): Select the attendance status (On-time, Late, or Absent) for the student.
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any comments or notes about the student's attendance.
* **Submitted By** (Text, Required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Submitted On** (Date, Required): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **School Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's name.
* **School Email** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **School Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your school's phone number.
* **Teacher Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email** (Text, Optional): Enter your teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your teacher's address.
* **Teacher Attendance** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your attendance status (Absent, Present, Late, or Unscheduled).
* **Teacher Attendance Status** (Select One, Optional): Select your attendance status (On-time, Late, or Absent).
* **Teacher Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter comments from the teacher about the student's attendance.
* **Student Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter comments from the student about their attendance.
* **School Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any school-specific notes.
