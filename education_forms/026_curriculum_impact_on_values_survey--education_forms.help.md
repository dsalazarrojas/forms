# Curriculum Impact On Values Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Impact On Values Survey is designed to understand how a curriculum affects student values such as honesty, hard work, respect, responsibility, creativity, and empathy. The survey aims to gather information about the school, curriculum area, school subjects, and student values.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of your school in the **School Name** field.
2. Select the grade level of your school from the **School Grade** dropdown menu.
3. If applicable, select the **Curriculum Area** where the values impact assessment is being conducted.
4. Choose up to three **School Subjects** that are being assessed for their impact on student values.
5. Select one **Student Value** that you believe is most important to the curriculum.
6. Choose one **Curriculum Value** that you believe is impacted by the curriculum.
7. (Optional) Rate your experience with the survey.
8. Provide any additional **Survey Comments** you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **School Grade**: Select the grade level of your school: High School, Middle School, or Elementary School.
* **Curriculum Area**: If applicable, select the area where the values impact assessment is being conducted.
* **School Subjects**: Select up to three school subjects from Mathematics, Science, Language Arts, Physical Education, or Foreign Language.
* **Student Value**: Choose one student value that you believe is most important to the curriculum: Honesty, Hard work, Respect, Responsibility, Creativity, or Empathy.
* **Curriculum Value**: Choose one curriculum value that you believe is impacted by the curriculum: Critical Thinking, Analytical Thinking, Problem-Solving, Communication, or Team Work.
* **Survey Rating**: This field is optional and allows you to provide a rating or feedback on your experience with the survey.
* **Survey Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on your experience with the survey.
