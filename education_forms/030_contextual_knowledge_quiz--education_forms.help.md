# Contextual Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contextual Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to assess your ability to analyze information within its proper context. It consists of several sections that cover theoretical foundations, scenario analysis, and self-evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the most accurate description.
3. Provide brief explanations for open-ended questions.
4. Be honest about your confidence in your answers and whether the quiz was helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student/Participant Name** (`student_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on your quiz results. This is a required field.
* **Quiz Reference Code** (`quiz_id_code`, text, required): This code is provided by your instructor. Enter it exactly as given to ensure you receive credit for your quiz.
* **Which best defines 'Historical Context' in literature** (`question_1_definition`, select_one, required): Choose the most accurate description from the given options:
	+ The sequence of events in the story
	+ The social and political environment of the author's time
	+ The personal biography of the main character
	+ The physical location where the book was published
* **Why is context critical when interpreting data** (`question_2_importance`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation on why context is critical in data interpretation.
* **Scenario - A growth rate of 50 percent is reported. When is this MISLEADING** (`scenario_1_analysis`, select_one, required): Identify the missing contextual factor that makes the growth rate misleading.
	+ When the starting value is very small (e.g., from 2 to 3)
	+ When the timeframe is clearly stated
	+ When comparing to previous years
	+ When the currency is specified
* **Describe how cultural context affects a business negotiation** (`scenario_2_open_ended`, text, required): Provide a brief example of how cultural context influences business negotiations (2-3 sentences).
* **Self-Evaluation** (`section_3_evaluation`, note, required): This section is for you to reflect on your understanding and self-assess your confidence in your answers.
* **Confidence Rating** (`confidence_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence in your answers on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 - Not confident
	+ 2 - Low confidence
	+ 3 - Moderate
	+ 4 - High confidence
	+ 5 - Very confident
* **Was this quiz helpful for your learning** (`feedback_on_quiz`, select_one, optional): Provide feedback on how helpful you found the quiz in your learning process.
* **Estimated time taken to complete (Minutes)** (`time_taken_minutes`, number, optional): Estimate the time you took to complete the quiz.
* **Date and Time of Completion** (`completion_timestamp`, date, required): Record the date and time you completed the quiz.
