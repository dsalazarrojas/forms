# Writing Center Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Writing Center Appointment Request form is a tool used by students and instructors to request support from the writing center. This form helps the writing center team to better understand the student's needs and provide tailored assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter the title of the appointment**, a brief summary of your request.
2. **Select the category** to which your request belongs, such as "Academic Support" or "Research Paper".
3. **Describe your request** in detail, providing background information about your writing project.
4. **Enter a unique form ID** (optional) if you already have one assigned to you.
5. **Choose an output file** if you'd like to receive feedback on a specific file (e.g. a Word document).
6. **Select an assigned tool** if a specific tool has been pre-assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: Enter a brief summary of your appointment request.
* **Category**: Select the type of support you need, such as Academic Support or Research Paper.
* **Description**: Please provide a detailed description of your writing project, including any relevant background information.
* **Form ID**: This field is optional, but if you've been assigned a unique ID, enter it here. This helps the writing center team keep track of your requests.
* **Output File**: If you'd like to receive feedback on a specific file (e.g. a Word document), please upload it here.
* **Assigned Tool**: If a specific tool has been pre-assigned to you, select it here. This ensures you receive targeted support for your writing project.
