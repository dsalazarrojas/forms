# Music Therapy Certification Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who want to apply for the Music Therapy Certification Program. It's a formal recognition of expertise in music therapy, and this application form guides you through the necessary steps to submit your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required contact information (Application Information).
2. Provide your basic contact details (Name, Email, Phone).
3. Enter your birth date.
4. Choose your music therapy degree or certification status.
5. Share your years of experience in music therapy.
6. Describe your education background.
7. Write a personal statement about your qualifications and experience.
8. Confirm your music therapy certification and license status.
9. Choose whether you have a valid music therapy license.
10. Enter the date of your music therapy certification.
11. Confirm your music therapy license renewal status.
12. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information** (`application_information`, Text): Enter your application details, including the purpose and scope of your music therapy certification.
* **Name** (`name`, Text): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, Date): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Music Therapy Degree** (`music_therapy_degree`, Select One): Choose the type of music therapy degree you hold (Music Therapy, Music Therapy (Non-Degree), or Other).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, Number): Enter the number of years you have been practicing music therapy.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, Text): Describe your education background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, Note): Write a personal statement about your qualifications and experience in music therapy.
* **Music Therapy Certification** (`music_therapy_certification`, Select One): Confirm whether you have a music therapy certification.
* **Music Therapy License** (`music_therapy_license`, Select One): Confirm whether you have a valid music therapy license.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, Date): Enter the date of your music therapy certification.
* **Music Therapy License Renewal** (`music_therapy_license_renewal`, Select One): Confirm whether your music therapy license is up to date.
* **Signature** (`signature`, Note): Sign and date the form to confirm your application.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, Date): Enter the date you submit this application.

Note: All fields are optional, but please ensure you complete all required fields to avoid any delays in processing your application. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
