# KA2 Adult Education - Help Guide

## Purpose
The KA2 Adult Education form collects demographic information from adults seeking education opportunities, including names, contact details, and education-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your email address to contact you if needed.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select your date of birth using the date picker or manually enter the date.
5. Choose between 'Yes' or 'No' for the select one field.
6. Choose between 'Yes' or 'No' for the select multiple field.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the note field.
8. Select one of the dates to record the start and end of your education program.
9. Enter the number of hours spent on education in the adult education hours field.
10. Select the type of education hours from the options in the adult education hours type field.
11. Choose one of the options for the adult education status field.
12. Enter the details of your education programs, institution, and field of study in the adult education programs, adult education institution, and adult education field of study fields.
13. Record the start and end dates of your education program in the adult education start date and adult education end date fields.
14. Enter the number of hours spent on education in the adult education hours field.
15. Select the type of education hours from the options in the adult education hours type field.
16. Enter the details of your education programs 2, institution 2, and field of study 2 in the adult education programs 2, adult education institution 2, and adult education field of study 2 fields.
17. Record the start and end dates of your education program 2 in the adult education start date 2 and adult education end date 2 fields.
18. Enter the number of hours spent on education 2 in the adult education hours 2 field.
19. Select the type of education hours 2 from the options in the adult education hours type 2 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (5): Select your date of birth using the date picker or manually enter the date.
* **Select One** (6): Choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Select Multiple** (7): Choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Note** (8): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Date** (9): Record the date.
* **Adult Education Level** (10): Enter the adult education level.
* **Adult Education Programs** (11): Enter the details of your education programs.
* **Adult Education Institution** (12): Enter the details of your adult education institution.
* **Adult Education Field of Study** (13): Enter the details of your adult education field of study.
* **Adult Education Start Date** (14): Record the start date of your adult education program.
* **Adult Education End Date** (15): Record the end date of your adult education program.
* **Adult Education Hours** (16): Enter the number of hours spent on education.
* **Adult Education Hours Type** (17): Select the type of education hours.
* **Adult Education Status** (18): Choose one of the options for the adult education status field.
* **Adult Education Programs 2** (19): Enter the details of your education programs 2.
* **Adult Education Institution 2** (20): Enter the details of your adult education institution 2.
* **Adult Education Field of Study 2** (21): Enter the details of your adult education field of study 2.
* **Adult Education Start Date 2** (22): Record the start date of your education program 2.
* **Adult Education End Date 2** (23): Record the end date of your education program 2.
* **Adult Education Hours 2** (24): Enter the number of hours spent on education 2.
* **Adult Education Hours Type 2** (25): Select the type of education hours 2.
