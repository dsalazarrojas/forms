# Student Healthcare Leaders Attendance Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from student healthcare leaders about their attendance and presence at events. It gathers general information, event-specific details, and contact information to ensure a smooth experience for all attendees. This form helps event organizers understand who is participating and how they can best support their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as needed. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Go through each page of the form, and select the corresponding page name from the drop-down menu at the top right corner.
2. Read each field description carefully before filling in the corresponding field.
3. Fill in the fields according to the descriptions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): Fill in some general information about yourself, such as your name and contact details.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, optional): Describe the event you are attending, including the dates and times you expect to be present.
* **Attendance Info** (`attendance_info`, text, optional): Specify how you plan to attend the event, e.g., in-person or remotely.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign your name and date to confirm your attendance.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information so that organizers can reach out to you if needed.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your participation.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Name and contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies that may cause an allergic reaction in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions that may affect your participation or require special attention.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers directly.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
