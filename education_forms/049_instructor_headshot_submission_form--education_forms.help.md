# instructor_headshot_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `instructor_headshot_submission_form` is a form designed to collect information from instructors regarding their headshots and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your `name` in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is where we'll be able to contact you.
3. Provide a brief description of yourself in the "instructor_details" field. This can include your teaching philosophy, areas of expertise, and any other relevant information you'd like to share.
4. Select your department from the list of options in the "department" field. This will help us understand your area of work.
5. Upload or enter your headshot in the "photo" field. This can be a URL link or a file upload.
6. Finally, click "Submit" to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Instructor Headshot**: Enter a clear and recent headshot of yourself. This will be used for official purposes.
* **name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email**: Enter your email address. This is the address we'll use to contact you.
* **Instructor Details**: Briefly describe your background, interests, and expertise. This can be a few sentences or a short paragraph.
* **Department**: Select your department from the options. This helps us understand your area of work.
* **Photo**: Upload or enter a URL link to your headshot image.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your information to us. Please review your submission before clicking submit to ensure all required fields are filled.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting.
* Use a recent and clear headshot of yourself.
* If you're having trouble uploading or entering a URL link, try using a different web browser or clearing your browser cache.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us at <contact information>.
