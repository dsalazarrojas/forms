> **Study Room Reservation Form - Help Guide**
>
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to reserve a study room in a library or academic institution. It collects information for scheduling purposes to ensure that the desired study space is available during the requested time slot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Select the **Study Spaces** field to choose the specific study room(s) you wish to reserve.
2. If desired, enter a **date** and a specific **start time** and **end time** for your reservation. These fields are not required, but they can be useful for scheduling purposes.
3. Choose the desired **room capacity** to accommodate your group size.
4. Indicate the **room availability** status for the chosen time slot.
5. Finally, submit your reservation request by clicking the **Submit** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This is the heading of the form, providing context and introduction to the reservation process.
* **date** (`id: 2`): Enter the date you wish to reserve the study room. This is an optional field.
* **Study Spaces** (`id: 3`): Select the specific study room(s) you wish to reserve from the provided options. This field is required.
* **start time** (`id: 4`): If desired, enter the start time of your reservation. This field is optional.
* **end time** (`id: 5`): If desired, enter the end time of your reservation. This field is optional.
* **room capacity** (`id: 6`): Choose the room capacity to accommodate your group size. Select from the provided options: 1-20, 21-40, 41-60, or 61+.
* **room availability** (`id: 7`): Indicate whether the room is available or not. Choose from the options "Available" or "Not Available".
* **reservation date** (`id: 8`): Enter the date you wish to reserve the study room. This field is optional.
* **reservation time** (`id: 9`): If desired, enter the time you wish to reserve the study room. This field is optional.
* **Submit** (`id: 10`): Click this button to submit your reservation request.
* **Cancel** (`id: 11`): Click this button to cancel your reservation request.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct study room(s) that fit your group size.
* If you're unsure about the room availability, check with the library staff before submitting your request.
* Be accurate when entering the start and end times to avoid conflicts with other reservations.
