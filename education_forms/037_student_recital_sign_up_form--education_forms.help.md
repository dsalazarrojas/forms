# Student Recital Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to sign up for a recital performance event. It captures student, teacher, and event details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name.
2. Choose the recital date.
3. Select the time for your recital.
4. Choose the grade level of the student.
5. Choose the recital type (e.g. Performance or Audition).
6. Select the instrument you will be playing.
7. Select the recital room where you will perform.
8. Enter the teacher's contact information.
9. Enter your student's contact information.
10. Add any comments or special requests.
11. Review all fields for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their school records.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the recital in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD' (e.g. 2023-02-15).
* **Time**: Enter the time of the recital in the format 'HH:MM' (e.g. 14:30).
* **Grade**: Choose the grade level of the student (e.g. 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Recital Type**: Choose the type of recital (e.g. Performance or Audition).
* **Instrument**: Choose the instrument you will be playing (e.g. Piano, Guitar, etc.).
* **Recital Room**: Select the room where you will be performing.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address (if required).
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number (if required).
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's address (if required).
* **Recital Location**: Enter the location of the recital (if required).
* **Recital Duration**: Enter the expected duration of the recital (if required).
* **School**: Enter the student's school (if required).
* **Teacher Name 2**, **Teacher Name 3**, **Teacher Name 4**: Enter additional teacher contact information (if required).
* **Student Name 2**: Enter an alternate student name (if required).
* **Recital Room 2, Recital Room 3**: Select an alternate recital room (if required).
* **Comments**: Add any comments or special requests.

Note: Some fields are not required, but they can be filled out if the student has additional information to provide or if they are required for specific recital details.
