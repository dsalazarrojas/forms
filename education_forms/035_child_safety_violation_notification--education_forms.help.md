# Child Safety Violation Notification - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to report incidents of child safety concerns that have occurred within your school or organization. This form should only be used to report serious incidents that may have caused or potentially caused harm to a child. If you are unsure whether an incident should be reported, please contact your supervisor or child safety officer.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out each field as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2.  The form is divided into several sections. Each section has its own set of fields that you need to complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Name of Person Reporting**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is to capture the full name of the person reporting the incident.
*  **Your Role**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is to capture the role of the person reporting the incident, which can be one of the following options:
		- Teacher
		- Parent
		- Staff Member
		- Visitor
*  **Date of Incident**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is for the date when the incident occurred.
*  **Time of Incident**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is for the time when the incident occurred.
*  **Location of Incident**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is to capture the specific room or area where the incident occurred.
*  **Type of Violation**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is a select multiple option field where you can choose all that apply from the options:
		- Lack of Supervision
		- Physical Hazard
		- Protocol Breach
		- Unsafe Equipment
*  **Detailed Description of what occurred**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is for providing a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
*  **Was anyone injured?**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is a select one option field to determine if anyone was injured during the incident.
*  **Describe injuries if any**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is for describing any injuries that occurred during the incident and any first aid provided.
*  **Steps taken immediately following the incident**: <label> (<name> , <type> : <plain explanation>)
	+ This field is for describing the steps you took immediately following the incident.

## Tips

Please fill out the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible. If you are unsure of any information or need help, contact your supervisor or child safety officer for guidance.
