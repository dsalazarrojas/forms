# Music Education Scholarship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students applying for music education scholarships. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details about the applicant, their music background, and any supporting documents or information that can strengthen their application. This form should only be filled out by students seeking music education scholarships.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic information in the **Application Info** section.
2. Provide details about yourself in the **Student Info** section.
3. Indicate whether you have any **Supporting Documents** by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Share your **Music Background**, including any relevant experience or achievements in music.
5. Write a brief **Motivation Statement** explaining why you deserve a music education scholarship.
6. Enter any additional **Supporting Information** that you think would be helpful for the scholarship committee.
7. Provide your **Contact Info** for follow-up communication.
8. Review and submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Info**: This section is where you enter your name and any other basic information about yourself.
  * **Student Info**: This section is where you provide more details about yourself, including your interests, goals, and any relevant experience or achievements.
  * **Supporting Documents**: This is where you indicate whether you have any supporting documents, such as recommendations or transcripts, that you would like to include with your application.
  * **Music Background**: This section is where you share your music background, including any relevant experience or achievements in music.
  * **Motivation Statement**: This section is where you write a brief statement explaining why you deserve a music education scholarship.
  * **Supporting Information**: This section is where you enter any additional information that you think would be helpful for the scholarship committee to consider.
  * **Contact Info**: This section is where you provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, so that the scholarship committee can contact you for follow-up communication.
  * **Review Info**: This section is where you review and review the form for completeness and accuracy.
  * **Review Info**: This section is intended for internal use by the scholarship committee and is not meant for the applicant to fill out.
