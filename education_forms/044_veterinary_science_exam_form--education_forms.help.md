# Veterinary Science Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Science Exam Form is designed to collect information from individuals taking a veterinary science exam. This form is used to ensure that the exam process is efficient and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If applicable, enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the date of the exam from the calendar.
6. Choose the time of the exam from the time picker.
7. Select the option corresponding to the exam you are taking (cat, dog, or fish).
8. If you need to select multiple options, enter them in the "Select Multiple" field.
9. If necessary, enter any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
10. Enter the date for your exam in the "Date" field.
11. Choose the time for your exam in the "Time" field.
12. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
13. Answer the veterinary science question in the "Veterinary Science Question" field.
14. Click the "Submit" button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address to contact you about exam details or further information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number, if applicable, for any follow-up communications.
* **Date of Exam**: Select the date of your exam from the calendar.
* **Time of Exam**: Choose the time of your exam from the time picker.
* **Select One**: Select the type of exam you are taking (cat, dog, or fish).
* **Select Multiple**: Select all relevant options (cat, dog, fish, bird, horses) if applicable.
* **Note**: If you need to provide additional comments or information, enter it in this field.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your exam.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your exam.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for any further communications.
* **Veterinary Science Question**: Answer the veterinary science question in this field.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your information.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you have any special requests or needs, enter them in the "Note" field.
- Review your information carefully before submitting.
