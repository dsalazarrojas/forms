# AI And Prosthetics Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The AI And Prosthetics Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge and experience in the field of artificial intelligence (AI) and prosthetics. This quiz aims to gauge your understanding of AI techniques and its applications in prosthetics, as well as your confidence in using AI-powered prosthetics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the first section, "Participant Information", which includes filling out your name, email address, institution or organization, and role in the field of AI and Prosthetics.
2. Move to the "AI & Prosthetics Knowledge Quiz" section, which includes 5 questions about AI and prosthetics:
   - Choose the most commonly used AI technique for prosthetic limb control
   - Identify the primary benefit of AI in prosthetic design
   - Select all AI-driven features you've encountered in prosthetic devices
   - Rate your confidence in using AI-powered prosthetics
   - Estimate the percentage of patients who would benefit from AI integration in prosthetics
3. Fill out the final questions in the "Feedback and Additional Comments" section:
   - How satisfied are you with the quiz
   - What improvements would you suggest for future quizzes
   - Provide any additional comments or feedback

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Use a valid email.
* **Institution or organization** (`institution`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your institution or organization, e.g., university, clinic, research lab.
* **Your role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your role in the field of AI and Prosthetics.
* **Date of completion** (`completion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of completion of the quiz.
* **Which AI technique is most commonly used for prosthetic limb control?** (`q1_ai_technique`, `select_one`, required): Choose the AI technique that is most commonly used for prosthetic limb control.
* **What is the primary benefit of AI in prosthetic design?** (`q2_primary_benefit`, `select_one`, required): Identify the primary benefit of AI in prosthetic design.
* **Which of the following AI-driven features have you encountered in prosthetic devices?** (`q3_features_encountered`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the features you've encountered in prosthetic devices.
	+ Adaptive gait control
	+ Predictive maintenance
	+ Real-time sensory feedback
	+ Automated fitting
	+ Energy harvesting
* **On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate your confidence in using AI-powered prosthetics?** (`q4_confidence_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate your confidence in using AI-powered prosthetics, with 1 being very low and 5 being very high.
* **Estimated percentage of patients who would benefit from AI integration in prosthetics (0-100%)** (`q5_patient_benefit_estimate`, `decimal`, required): Enter a value between 0 and 100.
* **How satisfied are you with the AI And Prosthetics Knowledge Quiz?** (`q6_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select how satisfied you are with the quiz.
* **Which aspects of the quiz were most helpful?** (`q7_helpful_aspects`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the aspects of the quiz that were most helpful.
	+ Clear questions
	+ Relevant content
	+ Engaging format
	+ Time-efficient
	+ Challenging
* **What improvements would you suggest for future quizzes?** (`open_feedback`, `text`, optional): Suggest any improvements you think would be useful for future quizzes.
* **Any additional comments or feedback?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the quiz.

**Tips**

* Please be thorough and honest in your answers.
* If you need help or clarification, feel free to ask.
* The form requires answers to all fields marked as "required" for the form to be valid.
