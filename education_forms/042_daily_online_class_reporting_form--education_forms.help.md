# Daily Online Class Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Daily Online Class Reporting Form" is a self-assessment tool designed to gather feedback from students about their daily online classes. This form aims to provide valuable insights to educators on how to improve the learning experience, teacher effectiveness, and overall class environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields. While most fields are optional, answering them will provide more detailed and accurate feedback.
2. For fields with options, select the option that best describes your experience or opinion.
3. Enter your answers clearly and concisely, providing as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report On Student**: Describe the student's performance and behavior during the class, e.g., "They were on time and participated actively."
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the class or teacher.
* **Report On Subject**: Describe the subject matter of the class and how it was presented.
* **Subject Interest**: Select the level of interest you had for the subject matter. Choose from: 
    * Math
    * English
    * Science
    * History
* **Teacher Support**: Rate how much support you received from the teacher during the class. This field is currently not applicable as it's marked as optional.
* **Student Participation**: Rate your level of participation during the class. Choose from: 
    * High
    * Medium
    * Low
* **Classroom Environment**: Describe the classroom environment and any notable features. 
* **Student Interest**: Rate your level of interest in the class. Choose from: 
    * High
    * Medium
    * Low
* **Classroom Management**: Rate the management of the class, including the organization and time-keeping. Choose from: 
    * Good
    * Fair
    * Poor
* **Teacher Effectiveness**: Provide any comments or feedback about the teacher's teaching method.
* **Student Engagement**: Rate your level of engagement during the class. Choose from: 
    * High
    * Medium
    * Low
* **Classroom Equipment**: Rate the availability and condition of classroom equipment. Choose from: 
    * Good
    * Fair
    * Poor
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall rating for the class, on a numerical scale.
* **Teacher Response**: Confirm if the teacher responded to your questions or concerns. Choose from: 
    * Yes
    * No
