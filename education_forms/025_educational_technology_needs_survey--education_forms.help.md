# educational_technology_needs_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the educational technology needs of students, faculty, and other stakeholders in educational institutions. This will help shape the development and implementation of educational technology solutions that cater to these needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the relevant options for each field. For fields with multiple options, choose the one that best describes your response.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the required label.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to all fields, taking care to provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course goals** (1, number, required: false): This field allows you to describe the primary goals of the course or program. Please provide a brief statement of what the course aims to achieve.
* **Student background** (2, text, required: true): In this field, describe the student's background, including their previous educational experiences, relevant work experience, or any other relevant factors that might impact their educational needs.
* **Student interests** (3, text, required: false): Here, list the student's interests, hobbies, or any relevant factors that might influence their educational needs.
* **Faculty expectations** (4, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the options that best describe the faculty's expectations from the educational technology solutions.
* **Educational background** (5, select_one, required: true): Choose the option that best describes the student's educational background.
* **School name** (6, text, required: false): Provide the name of the school or institution where you are affiliated with.
* **School size** (7, text, required: false): Describe the size of the school or institution you are representing.
* **School type** (8, text, required: false): Indicate the type of school or institution you are affiliated with.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in to ensure the form can be submitted accurately.
* If a field has options, choose the one that best describes your response.
* Provide clear and concise answers to all fields, taking care to provide as much detail as possible.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or guidance.
