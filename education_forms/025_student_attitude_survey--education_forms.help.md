# Student Attitude Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students about their preferences and demographics in order for the educator/instructor to better understand their needs and provide tailored support.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal information, including your name, date of birth, contact details (email, phone), and preferred class (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
2. Indicate what motivates you by selecting one or more options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
3. Answer the question about which class you like by selecting one option.
4. Provide any additional comments or thoughts you might have.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you prefer it to be spelled.
* **Student Class** (`student_class`, select_one, required): Select the class you belong to from the available options.
* **What Motivates You?** (`question_1`, select_multiple, required): Indicate what motivates you to choose from the provided options.
* **Which Class Do You Like?** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Select the class you prefer from the available options.
* **Student Attitude** (`student_attitude`, number, required): This field will likely be used to assess your attitude, but please answer this question as honestly as you can.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time of day that you prefer to study, please enter it in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any thoughts or comments that you would like to share, please write them down here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
 
Note: This form does not include any field to assess "student attitude" as the field named "Student Attitude" is a number field, not a text or note field.
