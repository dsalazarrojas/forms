# Children's Center Mission Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your organization's mission, values, and vision statements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about your organization's basic details, such as its name and type.
2. Write a brief mission statement that summarizes the organization's purpose.
3. Choose up to three primary goals or objectives for your organization.
4. Select the core values that your organization adheres to.
5. Explain how you implement these values.
6. Write a vision statement that describes where you see your organization in the future.
7. Identify your target population and community involvement.
8. List your key stakeholders and how you engage with them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Type of Organization** (`Type of Organization`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of organization from the given options.
* **Year Founded** (`Year Founded`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the year your organization was founded.
* **Contact Person Name** (`Contact Person Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person you want to contact.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Write your mission statement** (`Write your mission statement`, `text`, required): Write a concise statement that summarizes the purpose and goals of your organization.
* **Primary goals or objectives** (`Primary goals or objectives`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose up to three primary goals or objectives for your organization.
* **Core values of your organization** (`Core values of your organization`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the core values that your organization adheres to.
* **Explain how you implement these values** (`Explain how you implement these values`, `text`, optional): Provide an explanation on how you implement these core values.
* **Where do you see your organization in the future** (`Where do you see your organization in the future`, `text`, required): Write a concise statement that describes where you see your organization in the future.
* **Target population served** (`Target population served`, `text`, required): Describe the age groups and demographics of the children your organization serves.
* **How do you impact your local community** (`How do you impact your local community`, `text`, optional): Provide information on how your organization positively impacts the local community.
* **Community partnerships or collaborations** (`Community partnerships or collaborations`, `text`, optional): List any community partnerships or collaborations your organization is involved in.
* **Key stakeholders** (`Key stakeholders`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the key stakeholders that your organization engages with.
* **How do you engage stakeholders** (`How do you engage stakeholders`, `text`, optional): Provide information on how your organization engages its key stakeholders.
* **Current Challenges** (`Current Challenges`, `text`, optional): List any current challenges your organization is facing.
* **What opportunities do you see for growth** (`What opportunities do you see for growth`, `text`, optional): Describe future opportunities for growth for your organization.
