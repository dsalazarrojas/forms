# Training Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to apply for a training program. It will help us gather necessary details about you and your training requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and clear answers to each question.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required".
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Application Form** (`training_application_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main section of the form where you can provide a brief description of your training needs or requirements.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required): Please provide your name and any relevant contact information to help us process your application.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, text, required): Describe the training program you are interested in, including the course name, duration, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address, so we can reach out to you about your application.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you would like to provide an emergency contact person's information, please do so. This is not a required field.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, note, required): Please submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate and clear.
- If you have any supporting documents or additional information, please attach them to your application.
- We appreciate your patience and understanding, and we'll do our best to process your application as soon as possible.
