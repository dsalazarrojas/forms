# Teachers Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from teachers about their qualifications and experience. This form is used to assess the suitability of a teacher for a specific role or position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Provide your email address**: Enter your official email address used for communication.
3. **Enter your contact number**: Leave this field blank if you do not have a phone number.
4. **Select your teacher type**: Choose "Yes" if you are an experienced teacher, or "No" if you do not have teaching experience.
5. **Provide date of experience**: If you selected "Yes" above, enter the date you started teaching.
6. **Provide date of last teaching**: If you selected "Yes" above, enter the date you last taught.
7. **Add any additional notes**: If you have any other relevant information about your experience, use this field to add notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address used for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Leave this field blank if you do not have a phone number.
* **Teacher Type** (`teacher_type`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have teaching experience, or "No" if you do not have teaching experience.
* **Experience** (`experience`, date, optional): If you selected "Yes" above, enter the date you started teaching.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, time, optional): If you selected "Yes" above, enter the date you last taught. 
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): If you selected "Yes" above, enter the date you last taught. 
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any other relevant information about your experience, use this field to add notes.
