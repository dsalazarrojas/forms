# Letter Writing Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Letter Writing Guide form is designed to assist users in writing a formal letter, providing a structured approach to ensure that the letter's purpose, recipient, date, and other key elements are properly addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the purpose of the letter.
2. Select the recipient of the letter (To whom or From whom).
3. Enter the date of the letter.
4. Choose a salutation (Mr., Ms., Dr., Prof., or Other).
5. Enter the greeting (a friendly greeting to welcome the recipient).
6. Write the body of the letter (main content).
7. Enter the closing (a professional closing to end the letter).
8. Enter your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the purpose of this letter?** (`purpose`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the letter's intention or reason for writing.
* **Who is the recipient of this letter?** (`recipient`, `select_one`, required): Select the recipient of the letter from the options To whom or From whom.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to write the letter.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you plan to write the letter (not required but recommended for scheduling purposes).
* **Salutation** (`salutation`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose a salutation (Mr., Ms., Dr., Prof., or Other) to address the recipient.
* **Greeting** (`greeting`, `text`, required): Enter a friendly greeting to welcome the recipient.
* **Body** (`body`, `text`, required): Write the main content of the letter.
* **Closing** (`closing`, `text`, required): Enter a professional closing to end the letter.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Enter your signature.
