# Digital Inclusion Project Video Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather participant information and obtain consent for video recording during project activities. It is a crucial step in ensuring that participants are aware of and agree to being recorded, and that their contact information is accurately captured for follow-up and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read the form carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Choose the correct options for each field, and make sure to select "I consent to being recorded" if you agree to being recorded.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, Text, Optional): This field is used to capture any additional participant information that you want to provide. You can use this field to add any relevant details about your participation in the project.
* **Video Recording Information** (`video_recording`, Select One, Required): Here, you have two options: "Record Video" or "Do Not Record Video". Please select "Record Video" if you agree to being recorded, and "Do Not Record Video" if you do not.
* **Consent** (`consent`, Select Multiple, Required): This field requires you to select both options to confirm that you have read and understood the project's consent guidelines. Please select "I consent to being recorded" and "I do not consent to being recorded" accordingly.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): This is your contact email address where we can reach out to you for follow-up and communication purposes.
* **Name** (`name`, Text, Optional): Please provide your name as it appears on official documents or your preferred name to be used in project-related communications.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Text, Optional): If you are representing an organization, please provide the name of your organization for accurate record-keeping.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): If you have a phone number where you can be reached, please provide it for additional communication purposes.
* **Notes** (`note`, Note, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes that you would like to share with the project team.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have carefully read and understood the project's consent guidelines before selecting your options.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the project team for assistance.
