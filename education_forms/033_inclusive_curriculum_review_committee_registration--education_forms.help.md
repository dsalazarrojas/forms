# Inclusive Curriculum Review Committee Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inclusive Curriculum Review Committee Registration form is designed to collect information about committee members, their areas of expertise, and submission details. This form helps committee members register themselves and submit information that will aid in the review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by providing your member details, including your name and position.
2.  Select your areas of expertise, if applicable.
3.  Provide your consent to participate in the review process.
4.  Enter your submission details, including date, time, location, and comments.
5.  Select reviewers for your submission.
6.  Specify the status of your submission.
7.  Enter the date and time your submission was approved or reviewed.
8.  Enter comments for approval or review.
9.  Specify the status of your submission after review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Member Details
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official university documentation or ID card.
* **Member Position** (`member_position`, `text`, required): Enter your position within the university or institution.
* **Member Email** (`member_email`, `email`, required): Enter your official university email address.
* **Member Phone** (`member_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your university phone number.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted your curriculum.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you submitted your curriculum.
* **Submission Location** (`submission_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where you submitted your curriculum.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about your submission.
* **Reviewers** (`submission_reviewers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the reviewers for your submission.
*   *   Reviewer 1
*   *   Reviewer 2
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your submission.
* **Date Approved** (`submission_date_approved`, `date`, optional): Enter the date your submission was approved.
* **Time Approved** (`submission_time_approved`, `time`, optional): Enter the time your submission was approved.
* **Comments Approved** (`submission_comments_approved`, `text`, optional): Enter comments about your submission.
* **Date Reviewed** (`submission_date_reviewed`, `date`, required): Enter the date your submission was reviewed.
* **Time Reviewed** (`submission_time_reviewed`, `time`, required): Enter the time your submission was reviewed.
* **Comments Reviewed** (`submission_comments_reviewed`, `text`, required): Enter comments about your submission after review.
* **Submission Status Reviewed** (`submission_status_reviewed`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your submission after review.
* **Date Submitted** (`submission_date_submitted`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted your curriculum.
* **Time Submitted** (`submission_time_submitted`, `time`, required): Enter the time you submitted your curriculum.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments_submitted`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about your submission.

*   *   Reviewer 2
*   *   Reviewer 3
*   *   Reviewer 4
*   *   Reviewer 5

Note: The reviewers field allows you to select up to 5 reviewers for your submission.
