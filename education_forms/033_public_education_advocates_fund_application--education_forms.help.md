# public_education_advocates_fund_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_education_advocates_fund_application form is used for applicants to apply for funding for public education initiatives. It gathers information about a project that requires funding, including project details, funding amount, budget, funding start date, supporting documents, education area, and a description of the supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Details
• **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can provide a brief description of your project.
### Funding Amount
• **Funding Amount** (`funding_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of funding you are requesting.
### Budget
• **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter the detailed breakdown of your budget.
### Funding Start Date
• **Funding Start Date** (`funding_start`, date, optional): Enter the date when you plan to start using the funding.
### Supporting Documents
• **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): This field is for attaching any relevant documents that support your application. You can upload files here.
### Education Area
• **Education Area** (`education_area`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided to specify the area of education your project falls under.
### Supporting Documents Description
• **Supporting Documents Description** (`supporting_documents_description`, text, optional): This field is a text area where you can describe the supporting documents you attached.
### Submit
• **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is the final step to submit your application. Please review your answers carefully before clicking submit.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate information.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the support team for assistance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
