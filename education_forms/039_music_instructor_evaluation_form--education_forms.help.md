# Music Instructor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a music instructor's performance, teaching style, and overall quality of instruction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* **Music Instrument**: Enter the instrument being taught (e.g., piano, guitar, violin).
	* **Music**: Rate the quality of the music instruction on a scale of 1-10.
	* **Music Instructor**: Check if the instructor is "Yes" or "No".
	* **Lesson**: Enter the duration of the lesson (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour).
	* **Frequency**: Check the frequency of lessons (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
	* **Music Type**: Check if the music type is "Yes" or "No" (e.g., classical, contemporary).
	* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
	* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
	* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
	* **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name.
	* **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number.
	* **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
	* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the instructor.
	* **Submit**: Click "Submit" to complete the form.
	* **Cancel**: Click "Cancel" to close the form without submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Music Instrument**: Please enter the instrument being taught (e.g., piano, guitar, violin).
* **Music**: This is a numerical rating from 1-10 indicating the quality of music instruction.
* **Music Instructor**: Check "Yes" if the instructor is satisfactory, or "No" otherwise.
* **Lesson**: Enter the duration of the lesson (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour).
* **Frequency**: Check the frequency of lessons (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Music Type**: Check "Yes" or "No" for the type of music being taught (e.g., classical, contemporary).
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the instructor.
* **Submit**: Click to submit the form.
* **Cancel**: Click to close the form without submitting it.
