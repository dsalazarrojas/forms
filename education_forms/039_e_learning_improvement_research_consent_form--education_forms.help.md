# E Learning Improvement Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E-Learning Improvement Research Consent Form is a crucial document that gathers participant information, their consent to participate in a research study, and other relevant details. This form ensures that participants understand the research goals and objectives and agree to participate in the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Please fill in the participant information section with your details. This section is required and should be filled in accurately.
2. Enter your participant ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to you.
3. If you have an email address, please provide it. This is an optional field.
4. If you have a phone number, please provide it. This is an optional field.
5. Please provide a brief description of the research you will be participating in.
6. In the participant agreement section, please indicate your consent to participate in the research.
7. Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Finally, please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent to participate in the e-learning improvement research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: This section captures your personal details, including your name and other identifying information. Please fill this in accurately.
* **Participant ID**: This is a unique identifier assigned to you for the research study. It's essential to fill this in correctly.
* **Email Address**: If you have an email address, please provide it so that we can communicate with you effectively.
* **Phone Number**: This field is optional and only required if you want to be contacted for any updates or further information.
* **Research Description**: Please provide a brief description of the research you will be participating in. This helps us understand your role and expectations.
* **Participant Agreement**: This section captures your consent to participate in the research. Please indicate 'Yes' if you agree to participate.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **E-Learning Improvement Research Consent**: Please select 'Yes' to indicate your consent to participate in the e-learning improvement research.
* **Date**: Finally, please enter the date of submission.
* **Date**: Please select a date for the submission of this form.
