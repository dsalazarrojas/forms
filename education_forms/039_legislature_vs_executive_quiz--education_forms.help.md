# Legislature Vs Executive Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legislature Vs Executive Quiz is a 3-question quiz designed to test your understanding of the US government system, specifically the legislature, executive, and judicial branches.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and choose the most accurate answer from the options provided.
2. Ensure you answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Submit your answers to receive your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **The Government System** (`government_system`, text, required: false): This field is an optional text input where you can describe the government system in your own words.
* **Branches of Government** (`branches_of_government`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the three branches of government:
	+ Yes: You think the US government has one branch.
	+ No: You think the US government has multiple branches.
* **Legislative Branch** (`legislative_branch`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all that apply:
	+ Legislative: The branch responsible for making laws.
	+ Executive: The branch responsible for enforcing laws.
	+ Judicial: The branch responsible for interpreting laws.
* **Executive Branch** (`executive_branch`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ Legislative: The branch responsible for making laws.
	+ Executive: The branch responsible for enforcing laws.
	+ Judicial: The branch responsible for interpreting laws.
* **Judicial Branch** (`judicial_branch`, text, required: false): This field is an optional text input where you can describe the judicial branch in your own words.
* **Powers of Government** (`powers_of_government`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all that apply:
	+ Legislative: The power to make laws.
	+ Executive: The power to enforce laws.
	+ Judicial: The power to interpret laws.
* **What is the highest legislative branch in the US Government?** (`quiz_question_1`, select_one, required: true): Select one of the three options:
	+ Senate
	+ House of Representatives
	+ Supreme Court
* **Who is the highest authority in the US Government?** (`quiz_question_2`, select_one, required: true): Select one of the three options:
	+ The President of the US
	+ The Supreme Court
	+ Congress
* **What type of government is the US?** (`quiz_question_3`, select_one, required: true): Select one of the three options:
	+ Democracy
	+ Monarchy
	+ Oligarchy

Note: Remember to answer honestly and to the best of your abilities. Good luck, and have fun! 

# Tips

* If you're unsure of an answer, try to think of the government system in its simplest form.
* If you need to research, feel free to do so. This is not a test meant to be failed.
* Don't worry if you're not familiar with the government system; this quiz is designed to be fun and educational.
* Take your time, and submit your answers when you're ready.
