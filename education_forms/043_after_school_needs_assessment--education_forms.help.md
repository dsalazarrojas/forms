# After School Needs Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the needs of students in after-school programs. It gathers information on student demographics, academic performance, and behavior to inform the development of after-school programs that cater to those needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Enter the student's complete legal name.
3. Provide the student's current grade level.
4. Enter the name of the school the student attends.
5. Select the date when the assessment is being conducted.
6. Rate the student's current academic performance.
7. Select the subjects where the student struggles.
8. Choose how often the student completes homework.
9. Rate the student's study skills.
10. Indicate the student's preferred learning style.
11. Rate the student's classroom behavior and interactions with peers.
12. Indicate if the student has difficulty focusing or paying attention.
13. Rate the student's emotional regulation.
14. Select the types of academic support the student may need.
15. Choose enrichment activities the student may be interested in.
16. Select the frequency of the student's attendance.
17. Choose if transportation is available for after-school programs.
18. Rate the impact of family income on participation.
19. Rate the level of parental involvement.
20. Enter any additional needs or concerns.
21. Set short-term and long-term academic or behavioral goals.
22. Enter the name of the person completing this assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (Required): Enter the student's complete legal name.
* **Student ID Number** (Required): Enter the student's identification number.
* **Current Grade Level** (Required): Choose the student's current grade level.
* **School Attended** (Required): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **Assessment Date** (Required): Select the date when the assessment is being conducted.
* **Current Grade Performance** (Required): Rate the student's current academic performance.
* **Subject Difficulties** (Required): Select the subjects where the student struggles.
* **Homework Completion** (Required): Choose how often the student completes homework.
* **Study Skills Assessment** (Required): Rate the student's study skills.
* **Learning Style Preference** (Required): Indicate the student's preferred learning style.
* **Classroom Behavior** (Required): Rate the student's classroom behavior.
* **Peer Relationships** (Required): Rate the student's interactions with peers.
* **Attention/Focus Issues** (Required): Indicate if the student has difficulty focusing or paying attention.
* **Emotional Regulation** (Required): Rate the student's emotional regulation.
* **Academic Support Needed** (Required): Select the types of academic support the student may need.
* **Enrichment Activities Interest** (Required): Choose enrichment activities the student may be interested in.
* **Schedule Availability** (Required): Select the frequency of the student's attendance.
* **Transportation Availability** (Required): Choose if transportation is available for after-school programs.
* **Family Income Impact** (Required): Rate the impact of family income on participation.
* **Parental Involvement Level** (Required): Rate the level of parental involvement.
* **Additional Needs or Concerns** (Optional): Enter any additional needs or concerns.
* **Short-Term Goals** (Required): Set short-term academic or behavioral goals.
* **Long-Term Goals** (Required): Set long-term academic or behavioral goals.
* **Assessment Completed By** (Required): Enter the name of the person completing this assessment.

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and accurately to ensure that the after-school program can effectively support the student's needs.
* If you are unsure of the student's answer, use the options provided or leave a note to provide further clarification.
* If you need to provide additional information, use the "Additional Needs or Concerns" field.
