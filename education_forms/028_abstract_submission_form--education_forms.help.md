# Abstract Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting abstracts for presentations, workshops, or other academic works. It collects essential information about the author, presentation details, and abstract content. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form and explain each field in detail.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the "Primary author full name" and "Primary author email address" fields, which are both required fields.
2. Enter your institutional affiliation in the "Institutional affiliation" field.
3. If you have co-authors, list them in the "Co-author names and affiliations" field, with each co-author on a separate line.
4. In the "Abstract title" field, provide a concise title for your abstract.
5. Select the preferred presentation type from the "Preferred presentation type" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the topic area or track for your abstract from the "Topic area or track" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your keywords in the "Keywords" field, separated by commas.
8. Write a 300-word abstract in the "Abstract text" field, including background, methods, results, and conclusions.
9. If applicable, provide the funding source in the "Funding source" field.
10. Declare any conflicts of interest in the "Do you have any conflicts of interest to declare" field, and describe them in the "If yes, describe the conflict of interest" field if necessary.
11. Confirm whether ethics or IRB approval was obtained in the "Has ethics or IRB approval been obtained if required" field.
12. Indicate whether the work has been previously presented or published in the "Has this work been previously presented or published" field.
13. If necessary, specify any special equipment or requirements for your presentation in the "Any special equipment or requirements for your presentation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Primary author full name**: Enter your full name as the primary author of the abstract.
- **Primary author email address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Institutional affiliation**: Enter your institution or university name.
- **Co-author names and affiliations**: List each co-author on a separate line, including their full name and institutional affiliation.
- **Abstract title**: Provide a concise title for your abstract.
- **Preferred presentation type**: Select the type of presentation you prefer, such as oral, poster, workshop, panel discussion, or no preference.
- **Topic area or track**: Choose the relevant topic area or track for your abstract.
- **Keywords**: Enter 3-5 keywords separated by commas.
- **Abstract text**: Write a 300-word abstract, including background, methods, results, and conclusions.
- **Funding source**: If applicable, enter the funding source for your work.
- **Do you have any conflicts of interest to declare**: Declare any potential conflicts of interest.
- **If yes, describe the conflict of interest**: Describe the conflict of interest if you have declared it.
- **Has ethics or IRB approval been obtained if required**: Confirm whether ethics or IRB approval was obtained for your work.
- **Has this work been previously presented or published**: Indicate whether the work has been previously presented or published.
- **Any special equipment or requirements for your presentation**: If necessary, specify any special equipment or requirements for your presentation.

Note: Please review the abstract carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
