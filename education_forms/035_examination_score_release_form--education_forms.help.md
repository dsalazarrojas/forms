# Examination Score Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Examination Score Release Form is used to authorize the release of examination scores from the school's database to a designated recipient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields accurately and completely.
2. Review the options carefully to ensure that they are correct for your specific situation.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to release scores.
4. Double-check that all required fields are filled out accurately and that the options selected are correct.
5. Submit the form once you have completed it accurately.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the correct email addresses of the recipient and sender.
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Review the options carefully to ensure that they are correct for your specific situation.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to release scores.
* Please do not use this form for any other purpose than releasing scores.
* Please do not use this form to request scores that have not been released yet.
* Please do not use this form to release scores if you are not authorized to do so.
