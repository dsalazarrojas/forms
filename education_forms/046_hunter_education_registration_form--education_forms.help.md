# hunter-education-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and verify information about a hunter's education and registration status. This includes details such as first name, last name, date of birth, email address, phone number, education state, education county, hunter number, certification number, state of residence, county of residence, date of education, and notes. The form is required for educational purposes and should be completed accurately to ensure the information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the required fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Enter your Email Address in the email format (e.g., example@example.com).
4. Enter your Phone Number (if required).
5. Select your State and County of Education from the provided options.
6. Enter your Hunter Number and Certification Number.
7. Enter your State and County of Residence.
8. Enter the Date of Education in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
9. Provide any additional Notes you would like to include.
10. Select your Registration Certificate State, County, and State from the provided options.
11. Click the "Submit" button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as it is written on your official documents.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Select Education State** (`select_education_state`, `select_one`, required): Choose your state from the provided options (Utah, Arizona, Nevada).
* **Select Education County** (`select_education_county`, `select_one`, required): Choose your county from the provided options (Utah, Arizona, Nevada).
* **Hunter Number** (`hunter_number`, `number`, required): Enter your hunter number as it is issued by the relevant authority.
* **Certification Number** (`certification_number`, `number`, required): Enter your certification number as it is issued by the relevant authority.
* **State of Residence** (`state_of_residence`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you currently reside.
* **County of Residence** (`county_of_residence`, `text`, required): Enter the county where you currently reside.
* **Date of Education** (`date_of_education`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your education in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click to submit your information.
* **Select Reg Cert** (`select_reg_cert`, `select_one`, required): Choose your state from the provided options (Utah, Arizona, Nevada).
* **Select Reg Cert County** (`select_reg_cert_county`, `select_one`, required): Choose your county from the provided options (Utah, Arizona, Nevada).
* **Select Reg Cert State** (`select_reg_cert_state`, `select_one`, required): Choose your state from the provided options (Utah, Arizona, Nevada).
* **Hunter Edu** (`hunter_edu`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information related to your hunter's education.
