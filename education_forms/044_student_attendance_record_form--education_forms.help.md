# Student Attendance Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Attendance Record Form is designed to collect and document student attendance records. This form is crucial for tracking attendance in academic or educational settings, ensuring the accuracy and updness of attendance records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Student ID in the designated field.
2. Provide the Student Name.
3. Select the Attendance Date for the relevant attendance record.
4. Choose the Attendance Status from the provided options.
5. Add any remarks or comments regarding the attendance record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (1)**: A unique identifier for the student, allowing for easy tracking and referencing of attendance records.
* **Student Name (2)**: The name of the student, providing a clear identification of the individual.
* **Attendance Date (3)**: The date when the attendance record was taken or occurred.
* **Attendance Status (4)**: Select one of the provided options to indicate the student's attendance status.
	+ Value 1: Typically indicates a positive attendance status.
	+ Value 2: Generally indicates an absention or absence from the class or session.
* **Remarks (5)**: Add any additional comments or explanations regarding the attendance record.

## Tips
- Be accurate and concise in providing attendance details.
- Ensure the attendance status accurately reflects the student's actual attendance.
- Keep remarks clear and brief for easy reference.
