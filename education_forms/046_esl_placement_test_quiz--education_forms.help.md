# ESL Placement Test Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ESL Placement Test Quiz is a tool designed to assess the English language proficiency of students in a school setting. This quiz helps teachers evaluate a student's language skills in various areas, such as reading, writing, and speaking. The results of this quiz can inform placement decisions for English as a Second Language (ESL) programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information as a user.
2. Select your student's name from the Student Name dropdown menu.
3. Enter your student's email address.
4. Choose your student's level of proficiency from the Student Level dropdown menu.
5. Choose your class level from the Class Level dropdown menu.
6. Select the date of the test.
7. Enter the start and end times of the test.
8. Enter any teacher comments or suggestions for your student.
9. Enter any student comments or suggestions for their teacher.
10. Review and submit the form.
