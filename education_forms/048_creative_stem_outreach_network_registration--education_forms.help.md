# Creative STEM Outreach Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative STEM Outreach Network Registration form is designed to collect information from users who want to join or be part of the network. This form will help the network administrators to understand more about you and tailor their support to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your address in the "Address" field.
4.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  Enter your Email address in the "Email" field, ensuring it's in the correct format.
6.  Enter the name of the Non-profit organization you are associated with in the "Non-profit Name" field.
7.  List your interests in the "Interests" field.
8.  Describe your experience in the "Experience" field.
9.  Check the "Consent" field to acknowledge you understand the form's purpose and usage.
10.  Select your assigned tool from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
11.  If you chose "other" in the "Assigned Tool" dropdown, enter your preferred tool in the "Assigned Tool Other" field.
12.  Select the category you are interested in from the "Category" dropdown menu.
13.  If you chose "Other" in the "Category" dropdown, enter your category in the "Category Other" field.
14.  Enter your Prophet's ID in the "Prophet's ID" field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name (text)** (`first_name`, required/optional): Enter your given name as it appears on your passport or government ID.
*   **Last Name (text)** (`last_name`, required/optional): Enter your surname as it appears on your passport or government ID.
*   **Address (text)** (`address`, required/optional): Enter your mailing address where you can be contacted.
*   **Phone Number (text)** (`phone_number`, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Email (email)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
*   **Non-profit Name (text)** (`nonprofit_name`, required/optional): Enter the name of the Non-profit organization you are associated with.
*   **Interests (text)** (`interests`, required/optional): List your areas of interest related to creativity and STEM fields.
*   **Experience (text)** (`experience`, required/optional): Describe your relevant experience in creativity and STEM fields.
*   **Consent (note)** (`consent`, required/optional): Check this field to acknowledge you understand the form's purpose and usage.
*   **Assigned Tool (select_one)** (`assigned_tool`, required/optional): Choose from the available tools.
*   **Assigned Tool Other (text)** (`assigned_tool_other`, required/optional): Enter your preferred tool, if you chose "other" in the "Assigned Tool" dropdown.
*   **Category (select_multiple)** (`category`, required/optional): Choose from the available categories, e.g., Education, Non-profit, or Other.
*   **Category Other (text)** (`category_other`, required/optional): Enter your preferred category, if you chose "Other" in the "Category" dropdown.
*   **Prophet's ID (number)** (`prophets_id`, required/optional): Enter your Prophet's ID, if required.

Note: This guide assumes that the "nonprofit_name", "prophets_id" and "phone_number" fields are optional. If they are indeed required, they should be marked as such in the "required" field of the input YAML and the corresponding text in the guide updated accordingly.
