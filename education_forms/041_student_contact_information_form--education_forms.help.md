# Student Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect student contact information for administrative and emergency contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. You may leave fields blank if they do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear in correspondence with our program.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear in correspondence with our program.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your email address as you would like to be contacted.
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number as you would like to be contacted.
* **Address Line 1** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your address line 1 as you would like it to appear in correspondence with our program.
* **Address Line 2** (6, text, required/optional): Enter your address line 2 as you would like it to appear in correspondence with our program.
* **City** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (8, select_one, required/optional): Select the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (9, number, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (10, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (11, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (12, text, required/optional): Enter the relationship of your emergency contact (e.g. parent, guardian, spouse).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (13, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Alternative Emergency Contact Phone** (14, text, required/optional): Enter an alternative phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Other Emergency Contact First Name** (16, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of your other emergency contact.
* **Other Emergency Contact Last Name** (17, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of your other emergency contact.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship** (18, text, required/optional): Enter the relationship of your other emergency contact.
* **Alternative 2nd Emergency Contact Phone** (19, text, required/optional): Enter an alternative phone number for your other emergency contact.
* **Other 2nd Emergency Contact Phone** (20, text, required/optional): Enter a phone number for your other emergency contact.
* **Second Address Line 1** (21, text, required/optional): Enter your secondary address line 1.
* **Second Address Line 2** (22, text, required/optional): Enter your secondary address line 2.
* **Second City** (23, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your secondary address.
* **Second State** (24, select_one, required/optional): Select the state of your secondary address.
* **Second Zip** (25, number, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your secondary address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other** (26, text, required/optional): Enter any other emergency contact relationship.
* **Alternative Emergency Contact Other Phone** (27, text, required/optional): Enter an alternative phone number for any other emergency contact.
* **Other** (28, text, required/optional): Enter any other information.
* **Other** (29, text, required/optional): Enter any other information.
* **Other 2** (31, text, required/optional): Enter any other information.
* **Other 3** (32, text, required/optional): Enter any other information.
* **Other 4** (33, text, required/optional): Enter any other information.
* **Other 5** (34, text, required/optional): Enter any other information.
