# Microbiology Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from students who are taking a microbiology exam. It will capture personal details and preferences for exam scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your school name, student name, date of birth (if required), and student ID number.
2. Provide your email address and phone number.
3. Select a specific exam date and time from the provided options, or choose to take the exam at anytime.
4. Select a specific location for the exam from the provided options, or leave it blank to be assigned a default location.
5. Choose the type of exam you will be taking (e.g., Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Other).
6. Select a category for the exam (e.g., Mandatory or Optional).
7. If applicable, select a specific exam duration in hours.
8. Choose a specific course or subject area for the exam (e.g., Biology 101, Biology 202, or Other).
9. Select your current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
10. Select your level of expertise (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
11. Select a specific exam code (if required).
12. Confirm your exam schedule by signing and submitting the form.
