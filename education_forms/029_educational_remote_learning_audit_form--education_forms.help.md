# Educational Remote Learning Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the current state of remote learning in your educational institution. It aims to gather information on the effectiveness and challenges of remote learning platforms and tools used by educators, students, and administrators.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully, as each section is designed to collect specific information.
2. Answer every question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Be as detailed as possible, but keep your responses concise and clear.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote Learning** (`Remote Learning`, `text`): Please provide a brief description of your remote learning initiative, including its goals, objectives, and key features.
* **Questions** (`Questions`, `text`): Describe the types of questions or discussion topics that are being asked during remote learning sessions. Be specific about the types of questions or topics that are being addressed.
* **Technology** (`Technology`, `text`): List the technologies or platforms used for remote learning, such as Learning Management Systems (LMS) or other online tools.
* **Engagement** (`Engagement`, `text`): Assess the level of engagement among students, including participation, motivation, and overall satisfaction with the remote learning experience.
* **Improvement Needs** (`Improvement Needs`, `text`): Identify areas where improvements are needed in the remote learning initiative, such as technology, support, or training.

	+ High

	+ Training

	+ Resources
	+ Other (please specify)
