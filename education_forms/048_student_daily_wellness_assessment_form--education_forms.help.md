# Student Daily Wellness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Daily Wellness Assessment Form is designed to help educators and staff at the school monitor and support students' overall well-being.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
* Fill in your student's Student ID number in the **Student ID** field.
* Enter the date and time of the assessment in the **Date and Time** field.
* Choose your student's **Student Level** (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
* Select your student's **Overall Mood** (Happy, Sad, Angry, Fearful, Anxious, Confused, or Bored).
* Enter your student's **Physical Activity Level** (a number from 1 to 10).
* Rate your student's **Sleep Quality** (e.g., good, fair, poor).
* Choose your student's **Hunger Level** (Hungry, Satisfied, A little hungry, or Not Hungry).
* Select your student's **Hydration Level** (a number from 1 to 10, optional).
* Choose the student's **Overall Wellness Status** (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* Finally, enter any **Comments or Concerns** you may have about the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Enter the student's response to the question asked in this section.
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the assessment.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's unique identification number.
* **Name of Student**: Enter the student's name.
* **Student Level**: Choose the student's grade level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
* **Current Grade Level**: Enter the student's current grade level (if applicable).
* **Overall Mood**: Select the student's current mood (Happy, Sad, Angry, Fearful, Anxious, Confused, or Bored).
* **Physical Activity Level**: Rate the student's physical activity level on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Sleep Quality**: Rate the student's sleep quality (e.g., good, fair, poor).
* **Overall Wellness Status**: Choose the student's overall well-being status (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or concerns you have about the student.
