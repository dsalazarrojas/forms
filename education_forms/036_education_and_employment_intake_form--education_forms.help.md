# Education And Employment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Education And Employment Intake Form" is designed to collect comprehensive information about an individual's educational and employment experiences. This form will help relevant entities to assess the user's qualifications and background.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the "Education And Employment Intake Form" on the relevant platform or system.
2. Fill out the form by answering each question thoroughly and accurately.
3. Ensure you are providing details about your education and employment experiences only.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page (main-page)** (`text`, required):
    This section is where you will provide a brief introduction about yourself and your educational background. Please provide your name, relevant contact information, and a short overview of your education history.
* **Other Education** (`text`, required):
    This section is used to provide details about any additional education or training received after your primary education. Please describe any diplomas, certifications, or courses taken that are not included in your primary education.
* **Other Employment** (`text`, required):
    This section is where you will list any employment history or work experiences beyond your primary education. Please include job titles, work periods, and relevant job descriptions.

## Tips
1. Be as thorough as possible when answering each question.
2. Use clear and concise language in your answers.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance before proceeding.
