# Enrollment Package Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrollment Package Agreement form is a collection of fields that gather student information necessary for enrollment in a program or package. This form helps ensure that all required data is collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Complete each field according to your personal information and circumstances.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enrollment Application** (`enrollment_application`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for your enrollment application. Please enter your application as per your understanding.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your basic information such as name, address, and contact details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide contact details for yourself and emergency contacts.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required: `false`): In case of an emergency, who should be contacted? Please enter their contact information.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for you to describe your current student status.
* **Student Status 2** (`student_status_2`, `text`, required: `false`): If you are unsure about your current status, please check with your program coordinator or academic department for guidance.
* **Student Status Description** (`student_status_description`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a brief description of your current student status.
* **Package Agreement** (`package_agreement`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for you to enter any agreement you have with the package provider.
* **Package Agreement Terms** (`package_agreement_terms`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter any additional terms or conditions related to your package agreement.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date of Birth Day** (`date_of_birth_day`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the day of your birth.
* **Date of Birth Month** (`date_of_birth_month`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the month of your birth.
* **Date of Birth Year** (`date_of_birth_year`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the year of your birth.
* **Time of Admission** (`time_of_admission`, `time`, required: `false`): This field is for the time of your admission.
* **Package Start Date** (`package_start_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the package start date as per your understanding.
* **Package End Date** (`package_end_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the package end date as per your understanding.
* **Total Enrollment Fees** (`total_enrollment_fees`, `number`, required: `false`): If required, please enter the total enrollment fees paid or pending.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Please select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select your preferred payment methods (e.g., credit card, cash, bank transfer).
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Please sign to confirm this information.
* **Student Signature 2** (`student_signature_2`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Please sign to confirm this information.
* **Student Signature Date** (`student_signature_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Student Signature 3** (`student_signature_3`, `time`, required: `false`): This field is for you to confirm your signature.
* **Package Signature** (`package_signature`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for you to enter your package signature.
* **Package Signature Date** (`package_signature_date`, `date`, required: `false`): This field is for you to enter the date of your package signature.

## Tips
1. Be accurate and thorough when completing the form.
2. Double-check your entries before submitting.
3. If unsure about any field, please consult your program coordinator or academic department for guidance.
