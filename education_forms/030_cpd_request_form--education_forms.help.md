# CPD Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The CPD Request Form is a tool used to document and track Continuing Professional Development (CPD) requests from employees. This form helps to ensure that training and development requests are properly submitted, approved, and tracked within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information:
	* Employee Full Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel record.
	* Employee ID Number: Enter your unique employee ID number.
	* Work Email Address: Enter your work email address.
	* Department: Enter your department or team name.
	* Job Title or Position: Enter your current job title or position.
2. Select the type of CPD training you are requesting:
	* Type of CPD Training Requested: Choose the type of CPD training you are seeking (e.g., Course or workshop, Conference attendance, etc.).
3. Provide details about the training:
	* Training Title or Program Name: Enter the title or name of the training program you are requesting.
	* Training Provider or Organization: If applicable, enter the name of the training provider or organization.
	* Training Location: Enter the location of the training (city, venue, or online).
	* Duration of Training: Select the duration of the training (e.g., Single day, 2-3 days, etc.).
4. Specify the request details:
	* Requested Start Date: Enter the date when you would like the training to start.
	* Estimated Cost: Enter the estimated cost of the training.
	* Business Justification: Explain how this training will support your role and organization.
	* Learning Objectives: Specify what you hope to learn or achieve from this training.
5. Set the request priority:
	* Priority Level: Indicate the urgency of the request (High, Medium, or Low).
	* Manager Approval Status: Select the status of manager approval (Approved, Pending approval, or Not yet discussed).
6. Add any additional notes:
	* Additional Notes or Comments: Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel record.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Work Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Job Title or Position** (`position`, text, required): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Direct Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct manager.
* **Date of Request** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are requesting the training.
* **Type of CPD Training Requested** (`training_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of CPD training you are seeking.
* **Training Title or Program Name** (`training_title`, text, required): Enter the title or name of the training program you are requesting.
* **Training Provider or Organization** (`training_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the training provider or organization.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the training (city, venue, or online).
* **Duration of Training** (`training_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the training (e.g., Single day, 2-3 days, etc.).
* **Requested Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like the training to start.
* **Estimated Cost** (`cost`, number, required): Enter the estimated cost of the training.
* **Business Justification** (`business_justification`, text, required): Explain how this training will support your role and organization.
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, text, required): Specify what you hope to learn or achieve from this training.
* **Skills Gap** (`skill_gap`, text, optional): Enter any skills gap this training will address.
* **Priority Level** (`priority`, select_one, required): Indicate the urgency of the request (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Select the status of manager approval (Approved, Pending approval, or Not yet discussed).
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the request.
