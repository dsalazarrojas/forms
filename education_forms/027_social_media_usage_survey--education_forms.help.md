# Social Media Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information on your social media usage patterns, including the platforms you use, how often you use them, and how they affect your life.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the options that best describe your social media usage.
2. Answer the questions honestly, and make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your gender?**: Choose the option that best describes your gender. If you identify as Male, Female, or Other, select the corresponding option.
* **Which social media do you use?**: Choose one or more of the social media platforms you use regularly.
* **How often do you use social media?**: Select the option that best describes how often you use social media.
* **How much time do you spend on social media daily?**: Enter the estimated amount of time you spend on social media platforms.
* **Do you use social media for socializing?**: Select True if you use social media to interact with friends and family.
* **Do you participate in online communities?**: Choose True if you are part of online communities or forums.
* **Do you use social media for online shopping?**: Select True if you have made purchases or browsed products on social media platforms.
* **Do you think social media usage can be dangerous for you?**: Choose True if you believe social media usage has a negative impact on your mental health.
* **Are you concerned about online privacy?**: Select True if you worry about sharing your personal information online.
* **Do you have an email account?**: Choose True if you have a personal email address.
* **Do you have a phone number?**: Select True if you have a personal phone number.
* **What is your location?**: Enter your city, state, or country.
* **How old are you?**: Enter your age.
* **What is your level of education?**: Choose your highest level of education completed.
* **What is your occupation?**: Enter your current job or profession.
* **What is your marital status?**: Choose your current marital status.
* **Do you think social media usage differs by gender?**: Choose True if you believe social media usage varies between men and women.
* **What do you think is the main reason for gender differences in social media usage?**: Enter your thoughts on the subject.

Note: The form has been designed to collect data, and the information provided will be used to analyze and understand social media usage patterns. All responses will be kept confidential.
