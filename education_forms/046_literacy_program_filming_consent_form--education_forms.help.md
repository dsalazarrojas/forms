# Literacy Program Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent information from participants in a Literacy Program filming project. It ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the recording and potential use of their likeness, voice, or other personal data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Fill in the provided information in the corresponding sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the Literacy Program?** (`literacy_program_filming_consent_form_page_1`): Please provide a brief description of the literacy program you are participating in.
- **Participant Information** (`participant_information_page`): Provide your contact information, including name and email address.
- **Filming Details** (`filming_details_page`): Describe the specific filming activity or event you are participating in, including location and date/time.
- **Release of Information** (`release_of_information_page`): Indicate which types of media you are comfortable with being recorded or photographed.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details_page`): Provide additional contact information, such as phone number or social media handle.
- **Submission** (`submission_page`): Confirm that you are submitting your consent to the filming project.
- **Consent** (`consent_page`): Sign to indicate your consent to the filming project.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments_page`): Provide any additional information or comments about your participation.
- **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature_page`): Sign to confirm your identity and consent.
- **Date Signed** (`date_signed_page`): Date and time of signing.
- **Time Signed** (`time_signed_page`): Time of signing.
- **Name Signed** (`name_signed_page`): Your name, as signed.
- **Contact Signed** (`contact_signed_page`): Contact information, as signed.
- **Title Signed** (`title_signed_page`): Title or position, as signed.

## Tips
- Make sure to sign and date the form after completing it.
- Ensure that you understand what you are consenting to before signing.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact the filming project administrators.
