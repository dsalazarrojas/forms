# Non Custodial Parent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Custodial Parent Form is used to collect information about non-custodial parents, allowing you to update or modify existing records in our system. Please ensure that you have all necessary information before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the Custodial Parent 1 section with information about the primary custodial parent.
2. Complete the Custodial Parent 2 section with information about the secondary custodial parent.
3. Select the reason for the non-custodial parent relationship from the drop-down options or provide a custom reason if needed.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custodial Parent 1 Name** (`custodial_parent_1_name`): Enter the name of the primary custodial parent.
* **Custodial Parent 1 Occupation** (`custodial_parent_1_occupation`): Enter the occupation of the primary custodial parent.
* **Custodial Parent 1 Residence** (`custodial_parent_1_residence`): Enter the residence of the primary custodial parent.
* **Custodial Parent 1 Address** (`custodial_parent_1_address`): Enter the address of the primary custodial parent.
* **Custodial Parent 1 Income** (`custodial_parent_1_income`): Enter the annual income of the primary custodial parent.
* **Custodial Parent 2 Name** (`custodial_parent_2`): Enter the name of the secondary custodial parent.
* **Custodial Parent 2 Occupation** (`custodial_parent_2_occupation`): Enter the occupation of the secondary custodial parent.
* **Custodial Parent 2 Residence** (`custodial_parent_2_residence`): Enter the residence of the secondary custodial parent.
* **Custodial Parent 2 Address** (`custodial_parent_2_address`): Enter the address of the secondary custodial parent.
* **Custodial Parent 2 Income** (`custodial_parent_2_income`): Enter the annual income of the secondary custodial parent.
* **Non Custodial Parent Reason** (`non_custodial_parent_reason`): Select the reason for the non-custodial parent relationship from the options or provide a custom reason if needed.
* **Non Custodial Parent Reason Other** (`non_custodial_parent_reason_other`): If you selected the "Other" option, enter a brief description of the reason.
* **Custodial Parent Signature Date** (`custodial_parent_signature_date`): Enter the date the custodial parent(s) signed the form.
* **Custodial Parent Signature** (`custodial_parent_signature`): Enter the time the custodial parent(s) signed the form.
* **Non Custodial Parent Signature Date** (`non_custodial_parent_signature_date`): Enter the date the non-custodial parent signed the form.

This guide is designed to help you complete the "Non Custodial Parent Form" accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please refer to your system administrator or contact our support team.
