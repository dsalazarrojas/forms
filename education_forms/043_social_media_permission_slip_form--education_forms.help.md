# Social Media Permission Slip Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect permission from individuals, especially minors, to share their personal and potentially identifying information on social media platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name, email (if applicable), and other details in the designated fields.
* Select the correct options for "Parent", "Child", "Photo", "Audio", "Video", and "Content" fields according to your situation.
* Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* Provide any additional details that you feel are relevant to your situation in the "Other Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email (if applicable)** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): If you have an email account, provide it to facilitate contact and sharing of your content.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you are providing this permission slip.
* **Parent (Parent/Guardian)** (`Parent`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best represents your relationship status. If you are a minor (less than 18 years old), select "Parent". If you are 18 years old or older, select "Option 2".
* **Child (Minor)** (`Child`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): If you are a parent or guardian, select the options that best represent your minor child's status.
* **Photo** (`Photo`, `text`, required): Provide your consent to share your photo on social media platforms.
* **Audio** (`Audio`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your consent to share your audio recordings on social media platforms.
* **Video** (`Video`, `text`, required): Provide your consent to share your video content on social media platforms.
* **Content** (`Content`, `text`, required): Provide your consent to share your content on social media platforms.
* **Other Details** (`Other Details`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any other relevant information that you feel is important to share, enter it here.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `note`, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of sharing your personal information on social media platforms.
