# wymrt_supervisor_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for employees to provide feedback to their supervisor after completing a training program or session.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the shift during which you completed your training.
2. Enter the date you started and ended your training.
3. Provide any feedback you have about your supervisor's performance during the training.
4. Enter the number of completed training hours, minutes, and seconds.
5. Enter your supervisor's name, title, and department.

- **Shift**: Enter the shift during which you completed your training.
