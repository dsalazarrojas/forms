# Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Withdrawal Form is used to document a student's withdrawal from a program or course. This form is necessary for administrative and statistical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name.
2. Enter the student's ID number.
3. Provide the reason for the student's withdrawal.
4. Enter the date and time of the withdrawal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (student_name, text, required):
	+ Enter the student's full name.
* **Student ID** (student_id, number, required):
	+ Enter the student's ID number.
* **Reason for Withdrawal** (reason_for_withdrawal, text, required):
	+ Explain the reason for the student's withdrawal.
* **Date of Withdrawal** (date_of_withdrawal, date, required):
	+ Enter the date of the withdrawal in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Withdrawal** (time_of_withdrawal, time, required):
	+ Enter the time of the withdrawal in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the necessary authority to complete this form.
- Double-check the student's name and ID number for accuracy.
- If the student's withdrawal is due to a specific event or incident, provide a detailed explanation in the reason for withdrawal field.
- If the student's withdrawal occurs outside of regular working hours, ensure to document the time and date of the withdrawal accordingly.
