# Hybrid Classroom Participation Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Classroom Participation Behavior Survey is designed to gather insights into your typical participation behavior in classes. This information will help us understand your preferences and challenges in learning environments, which is crucial for creating engaging and supportive educational experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, select your preferred method of navigation. You can choose to navigate the form by page or by field.
- To progress, click on the "Next" button to go to the next page or the "Submit" button to submit your completed form.
- If you need help with any field, click on the field label for a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is the first page of the survey, providing a brief overview of the purpose and instructions for the survey.
* **How do you usually participate in classes?**: This question allows you to select how you typically engage with classes, which is essential for understanding your learning style and preferences.
* **How many hours do you usually spend in a class?**: This question helps us understand your time commitment to classes, which is vital for scheduling and resource allocation.
* **When do you usually start attending classes?**: This question allows us to know when you usually begin attending classes, which is crucial for planning and organization.
* **What is your preferred time to attend classes?**: This question gives us insight into your preferred time slot for classes, which can help tailor learning experiences to your needs.
* **How do you usually communicate with instructors or peers?**: This question explores your communication preferences, which is essential for effective collaboration and support.
* **What are some common challenges you face while participating in classes?**: This question helps us understand any obstacles you may encounter, allowing us to develop targeted support and solutions.
* **What is your email address?**: This field collects your contact information for any follow-up communication or support.
* **What is your phone number?**: This field provides an additional contact method for any further assistance or questions.
* **Do you have any comments about the class?**: This open-ended field allows you to share any feedback or suggestions about your learning experience.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions to ensure the best possible insights.
- If you have any difficulties with the form or experience any errors, feel free to contact us.
- Your responses will be used to improve the learning experience for everyone.
