# High School Transition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to collect feedback from high school students and their parents on the transition process to the next stage. Your input will help us understand your experiences and opinions, which will inform our support systems and resources to make the transition smoother for future students.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2.  Please provide specific examples and details when answering the questions.
3.  You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
4.  If you have any additional suggestions or comments, please include them in the text box provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Feedback**: This section is for you to share your thoughts and opinions about your transition experience.
*   **What was the most challenging part of the transition?** Please answer this question in detail, and try to be as specific as possible about the challenges you faced.
*   **Parent Feedback**: This section is for parents to share their thoughts and opinions about your transition experience.
*   **What support systems or resources do you think would be helpful?** Please suggest specific resources or support systems that could help make the transition smoother.
*   **Support Systems**: This question asks you to select one option that best describes the support system you would find most helpful.
*   **Resources**: Please select all the resources that you think would be helpful for future transitions.
*   **How can we improve support systems for you?** This question is for you to suggest ways to improve the support systems and resources.
*   **How can we improve support systems for students?** This question is for parents to suggest ways to improve the support systems and resources for future students.
*   **Overall Satisfaction**: This section is for you to rate your overall satisfaction with the support systems and resources provided.
*   **What would you rate your overall satisfaction with the support systems and resources?** Please rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being extremely satisfied.
*   **Why did you feel this way?** This question is for you to provide more details about your satisfaction and any suggestions you have for improvement.
