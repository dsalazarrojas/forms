# Geography Practice Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geography Practice Exam form is designed to help students practice and assess their knowledge in the geography field. It is meant to be used as a supplement to traditional teaching methods, such as lectures and classroom discussions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Provide your email address in the Student Email field.
3. Answer the question posed by your teacher in the Question field.
4. Enter your answer to the question in the Answer field.
5. If your teacher provided a reference number for the question, enter it in the Question Number field.
6. Enter your teacher's name in the Teacher Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your personal first name.
- **Student Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address used by your institution or education provider.
- **Question** (`question`, text, optional): Answer the question posed by your teacher.
- **Answer** (`answer`, text, optional): Enter your response to the question.
- **Question Number** (`question_number`, number, optional): If your teacher provided a reference number for the question, enter it here.
- **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's name who posed the question.
