# Listening Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your listening habits and preferences. It is intended to gather data about your favorite music genres, how you usually listen to music, and your favorite music streaming platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the correct answer for each question from the provided options.
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If a question is not marked as required, you can skip it.
* Use the options provided to make your selections.
* Click submit to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What is your favorite music genre** (`test_page_1`, text, Not Required): Please select your favorite music genre from the options provided (Rock, Pop, Classical, Jazz). You can choose one genre that you enjoy listening to the most.
- **Select your favorite genre** (`test_page_2`, select_multiple, Not Required): Select all the music genres you enjoy listening to (Rock, Pop, Classical, Jazz).
- **When do you usually listen to music?** (`test_page_3`, time, Not Required): Choose the time of day you usually listen to music.
- **What is your favorite artist?** (`test_page_4`, select_one, Not Required): Select your favorite artist from the options (Beyonce, Adele, Lady Gaga, Taylor Swift).
- **How many hours do you usually listen to music each day?** (`test_page_5`, number, Not Required): Enter the number of hours you usually spend listening to music each day.
- **What type of music do you like to listen to when you work?** (`test_page_6`, select_one, Not Required): Choose the type of music you like to listen to while working (Classical, Jazz, Pop, Instrumental).
- **Do you like to sing along to the song while listening?** (`test_page_7`, select_one, Not Required): Select 'True' if you like to sing along to the song, 'False' otherwise.
- **What time of day do you usually listen to music?** (`test_page_8`, select_multiple, Not Required): Select all the times of day you usually listen to music (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
- **What is your favorite music streaming platform?** (`test_page_9`, select_multiple, Not Required): Choose all the music streaming platforms you use (Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, Google Play).
- **How do you usually discover new music?** (`test_page_10`, select_multiple, Not Required): Select all the ways you discover new music (Radio, Social Media, Online Search, Music Store).
- **How many hours do you usually spend listening to music on your phone?** (`test_page_11`, number, Not Required): Enter the number of hours you usually spend listening to music on your phone.
- **What is your favorite music genre?** (`test_page_12`, select_one, Not Required): Select your favorite music genre from the options (Rock, Pop, Classical, Jazz).
- **Do you usually listen to music when you exercise?** (`test_page_13`, select_one, Not Required): Select 'True' if you usually listen to music while exercising, 'False' otherwise.
- **What time do you usually listen to music?** (`test_page_14`, time, Not Required): Choose the time you usually listen to music.
- **What type of equipment do you use to listen to music?** (`test_page_15`, select_multiple, Not Required): Select all the types of equipment you use to listen to music (Headphones, Earbuds, Speakers, Earplugs).
- **How often do you listen to music?** (`test_page_16`, select_multiple, Not Required): Select all the frequencies of how often you listen to music (Often, Rarely, Sometimes, Never).
- **What is your favorite music style?** (`test_page_17`, select_one, Not Required): Select your favorite music style from the options (Rock, Pop, Classical, Jazz).
- **Where do you usually listen to music?** (`test_page_18`, select_multiple, Not Required): Select all the places where you usually listen to music (Home, Gym, Car, Public Space).
