# Back To School Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students and their families for back-to-school events such as New Student Orientation, School Supply Drop-off, Meet the Teacher Night, Back-to-School Picnic, and Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) Meeting.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your child receives correct event information and that you are included in relevant activities.
2.  Make sure to select all events that you and your family will be attending.
3.  If you are interested in volunteering for the events, select the corresponding option.
4.  Enter any dietary requirements that your child may have, so that we can accommodate their needs.
5.  Provide a valid email address to receive updates and reminders about the events.
6.  If you have any questions or concerns, please include them in the "Any questions about the upcoming school year?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name_event`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child registering.
    *   This field is required to ensure that we have a way to contact you with important event information.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name_event`, text, required): Enter the name of the student registering for the events.
    *   This field is required to ensure that we have correct identification for your child.
*   **Grade Level (Coming Year)** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your child's grade level for the upcoming year.
    *   This field is required to determine which events are relevant to your child.
*   **Which events will you be attending?** (`specific_events_attending`, select_multiple, required): Choose which events you will be attending from the available options.
    *   This field is required so that we can plan accordingly for the events.
*   **Total number of family members attending** (`total_family_attendees`, number, required): Enter the total number of family members that will be attending the events.
    *   This field is required to ensure that we have enough supplies and staff for the events.
*   **Are you interested in volunteering for this event?** (`volunteer_interest_event`, select_one, required): Select whether you are interested in volunteering for the events.
    *   This field is required so that we can plan for volunteers.
*   **Any dietary requirements for the picnic?** (`dietary_requirements_school`, text, optional): Enter any dietary requirements that your child may have.
    *   This field is optional, but it's essential to ensure that we can accommodate their needs.
*   **Email Address for Updates** (`contact_email_event`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can receive updates about the events.
    *   This field is required so that you can stay informed about the events.
*   **Mobile Phone for Text Reminders** (`mobile_for_reminders`, text, optional): Enter your mobile phone number to receive text reminders about the events.
    *   This field is optional, but it's a good way to stay informed about the events.
*   **Any questions about the upcoming school year?** (`general_questions_event`, text, optional): Enter any questions or concerns you may have about the upcoming school year.
    *   This field is optional, but it's an opportunity for us to address any concerns you may have.
