# Teacher Emotional Health Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help support staff quickly understand the severity and frequency of emotional support needed by teachers. It is intended for teachers who are experiencing or have experienced difficulties in their emotional well-being and are seeking support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit. The following fields are required to be filled out:

* Name
* Email
* School

The optional fields are:
* Phone
* School
* Subject Area
* Problem Statement
* Support Needed
* Frequency of Support Needed
* Support Duration

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your work phone number, if available.
* **School** (`school`, `text`, optional): Enter your school name.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, `text`, optional): Enter the subject area you teach.
* **Problem Statement** (`problem_statement`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the difficulties you are experiencing in your emotional well-being.
* **Support Needed** (`support_needed`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of support you need:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Frequency of Support Needed** (`frequency_of_support_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the frequency of support you need:
	+ Every day
	+ Every 2 weeks
	+ Every 3 weeks
	+ Every 4 weeks
	+ Every 6 weeks
	+ Never
* **Support Duration** (`support_duration`, `date`, optional): Select the date when you expect the support to end.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): This is not a field to fill out, but a button to submit your request.
