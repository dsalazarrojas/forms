# physical_education_fitness_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The physical education fitness log form is designed to collect and record details about physical exercises and activities. This form will help users track their progress, monitor their fitness goals, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Exercise Name of the activity you performed.
2.  Select the Exercise Date of the activity.
3.  Enter the Exercise Time spent on the activity.
4.  Describe the Exercise Intensity level of the activity.
5.  Add any Exercise Notes about the activity.
6.  Enter the Set Number for the activity.
7.  Choose the Exercise Type from the list of options (e.g., Squats, Lunges, Pushups, or Other).
8.  Enter the number of Reps for the activity.
9.  Enter the Exercise Weight used during the activity.
10.  Enter the number of Exercise Sets completed for the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Exercise Name**: Enter the name of the exercise or activity you performed.
    Required: No
*   **Exercise Date**: Enter the date you performed the exercise or activity.
    Required: No
*   **Exercise Time**: Enter the amount of time spent on the exercise or activity.
    Required: No
*   **Exercise Intensity**: Describe the level of intensity you experienced during the activity (e.g., light, moderate, strenuous).
    Required: No
*   **Exercise Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the activity.
    Required: No
*   **Set Number**: Enter the number of sets you completed for the exercise or activity.
    Required: No
*   **Exercise Type**: Choose the type of exercise or activity from the list of options.
    Required: No
*   **Reps**: Enter the number of repetitions completed for the exercise or activity.
    Required: No
*   **Exercise Weight**: Enter the weight used during the exercise or activity.
    Required: No
*   **Exercise Sets**: Enter the number of exercises performed in a set.
    Required: No
