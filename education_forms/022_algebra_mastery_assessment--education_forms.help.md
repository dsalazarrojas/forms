This form is used to evaluate students' algebra skills, specifically for assessments and quizzes in the middle school and high school mathematics curriculum. This form is designed to be used to gather data from students on their ability to solve problems involving linear and quadratic equations, exponents, radical expressions, and other algebraic concepts.

The purpose of this form is to:

*   Assess students' understanding of algebraic concepts, such as solving equations, simplifying expressions, and factoring polynomials.
*   Identify areas where students need additional support or review.
*   Monitor students' progress in algebra over time.

This form is meant to be used by students themselves, not by teachers or educators. The form will ask a series of questions, some of which are multiple-choice, others of which require students to provide written explanations or numerical answers.

To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information and answers to questions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Student Name**: Enter your full name.
2.  **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number.
3.  **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
4.  **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level from the provided options.
5.  **Assessment Date**: Enter the date you completed this assessment.
6.  **Teacher Name**: Enter your teacher's name.
7.  **Section 1: Solve for x: 5x - 3 = 27**: Enter the value of x that makes the equation true.
8.  **Section 1b: Solve for y: 2(y + 4) = 20**: Enter the value of y that makes the equation true.
9.  **Section 1c: Solve: 3x + 5 = 2x + 15**: Enter the value of x that makes the equation true.
10.  **Section 2: Simplify: 4(2x - 3) + 5x**: Enter the simplified expression.
11.  **Section 2b: Simplify: 3x^2 + 2x - x^2 + 5x**: Enter the simplified expression.
12.  **Section 3: Factor: x^2 + 7x + 12**: Enter the factored form of the expression.
13.  **Section 3b: Factor: x^2 - 25**: Enter the factored form of the expression.
14.  **Section 4: What is the slope of y = 3x + 5?**: Enter the slope of the linear equation.
15.  **Section 4b: What is the y-intercept of y = -2x + 7?**: Enter the y-intercept of the linear equation.
16.  **Section 4c: Find the slope between points (2, 3) and (6, 11)**: Enter the slope between the two points.
17.  **Section 5: Solve the system: x + y = 8, x - y = 2**: Enter the values of x and y that make the system true.
18.  **Section 6: Solve: x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0**: Enter both solutions to the quadratic equation.
19.  **Section 7: Simplify: (x^3)(x^4)**: Enter the simplified expression.
20.  **Section 7b: Simplify: (x^5)/(x^2)**: Enter the simplified expression.
21.  **Section 8: Simplify: sqrt(144)**: Enter the simplified radical expression.
22.  **Section 8b: Simplify: sqrt(50)**: Enter the simplified radical expression.
23.  **Section 9: If 3 times a number plus 7 equals 31, what is the number?**: Enter the numerical answer to the word problem.
24.  **Section 10: Solve: 2x - 4 > 10**: Enter the inequality solution.
25.  **Self-Assessment: Algebra Skills**: Select how you feel about your algebra skills.
26.  **Areas of Strength**: Identify any topics you feel confident about.
27.  **Areas Needing Help**: Identify any topics you are struggling with.
28.  **Study Time This Week**: Estimate the number of hours you spent studying this week.
29.  **Additional Comments**: Provide any other feedback or comments about this assessment.

## Tips

*   For numerical problems, be sure to enter only the numerical value(s) that solve the problem.
*   For multiple-choice problems, choose the correct answer from the provided options.
*   For written explanations, type your response in the space provided.

By completing this form, you will be providing valuable feedback to yourself and your instructor about your algebra skills.
