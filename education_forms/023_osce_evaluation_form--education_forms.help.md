# osce_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The OSCE evaluation form is designed to gather feedback from users about their overall experience with the OSCE program. It will help to identify areas of improvement and contribute to the betterment of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide a brief introduction to this OSCE evaluation form.
2. Rate your level of satisfaction with the OSCE program.
3. Rate your overall experience with the OSCE program.
4. Specify what you liked most about the OSCE program.
5. Specify what you liked least about the OSCE program.
6. Rate the quality of teaching in the OSCE program.
7. Provide your expectations for the OSCE program future.
8. Suggest improvements for the OSCE program.
9. Rate the value of the OSCE program.
10. Rate the relevance of the OSCE program to your field of practice.
11. Provide suggestions for developing the OSCE program.
12. Provide any final thoughts or comments about the OSCE program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction (page 1, field 1)
*   **Introduction (text, optional)**: Please provide a brief introduction to this OSCE evaluation form. This field is not required, but it is recommended to provide some context about your experience with the OSCE program.

### 2. Satisfaction with OSCE program (page 1, field 2)
*   **Satisfaction with OSCE program (select_one, required)**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the OSCE program. You can choose from the following options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.

### 3. Overall experience with OSCE program (page 1, field 3)
*   **Overall experience with OSCE program (select_one, required)**: Rate your overall experience with the OSCE program. You can choose from the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 4. OSCE likes (page 2, field 4)
*   **OSCE likes (text, optional)**: Please provide your reasons for liking the OSCE program. This field is not required, but it is recommended to provide concrete examples.

### 5. OSCE dislikes (page 2, field 5)
*   **OSCE dislikes (text, optional)**: Please provide your reasons for disliking the OSCE program. This field is not required, but it is recommended to provide concrete examples.

### 6. OSCE teaching quality (page 3, field 6)
*   **OSCE teaching quality (select_one, required)**: Rate the quality of teaching in the OSCE program. You can choose from the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 7. Expectations for OSCE program future (page 3, field 7)
*   **Expectations for OSCE program future (text, optional)**: Please provide your expectations for the OSCE program. This field is not required, but it is recommended to provide concrete suggestions.

### 8. OSCE improvements (page 4, field 8)
*   **OSCE improvements (text, optional)**: Please provide your suggestions for improving the OSCE program. This field is not required, but it is recommended to provide concrete ideas.

### 9. OSCE value (page 4, field 9)
*   **OSCE value (select_one, required)**: Rate the value of the OSCE program. You can choose from the following options: High, Medium, Low.

### 10. OSCE relevance (page 5, field 10)
*   **OSCE relevance (select_one, required)**: Rate the relevance of the OSCE program to your field of practice. You can choose from the following options: High, Medium, Low.

### 11. OSCE suggestions (page 5, field 11)
*   **OSCE suggestions (text, optional)**: Please provide your suggestions for developing the OSCE program. This field is not required, but it is recommended to provide concrete ideas.

### 12. Final thoughts and comments (page 6, field 12)
*   **Final thoughts and comments (text, optional)**: Please provide any final thoughts or comments about the OSCE program. This field is not required, but it is recommended to provide any last-minute reflections.
