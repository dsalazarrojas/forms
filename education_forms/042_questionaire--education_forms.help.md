# Questionaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about students, including their personal details, educational background, and family status. It is meant to be a comprehensive tool for gathering data that will help in understanding the demographics of students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please make sure it is a valid email address.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Select your gender from the options provided in the "gender" field.
6. Choose one or more nationalities from the options provided in the "nationality" field.
7. Enter your address in the "address" field. Please note that this field is not required.
8. Enter the name of your school in the "school name" field.
9. Enter the year you graduated in the "graduation year" field. If you are still studying, you can enter '0' or leave it blank.
10. Enter your age in the "age" field.
11. Select your educational background from the options provided in the "education background" field.
12. Enter any additional student data in the "student data" field.
13. Select your family status from the options provided in the "family status" field.
14. Select your employment status from the options provided in the "employment status" field.
15. Choose one or more income ranges from the options provided in the "income range" field.
16. Select your educational aim from the options provided in the "education aim" field.
17. Enter your family's income in the "family income" field.
18. Enter the number of family members in the "family member count" field.
