## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and study preferences from students or applicants seeking to enroll in Masoretic Studies. Please note that this form is a public adaptation of an AI-generated form and not affiliated with any specific institution or platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number (fields 1-3).
2. Provide your educational background and any relevant information about your qualifications or experiences (fields 4-6).
3. Select your preferred study format and format for each subsequent field (fields 7-16).

6. Finally, review and submit the form.
