# Template For Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Template For Quiz form is designed to generate a customized quiz template for educational purposes. It collects information for a student's quiz results, including their name, date, and quiz questions with their answers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields:
	* Name: Enter the student's name.
	* Date: Enter the date of the quiz.
	* Questions: Select the answers to each question.
2. Answer each question by selecting the correct answer from the options provided.
3. Enter the pass rate, which is the percentage of questions answered correctly.
4. Enter the email address where you want to send the certificate.
5. Choose who to send the certificate to: Student or HR.
6. Decide if you want to send an email with the certificate.
7. If sending an email, enter the subject and body of the email.
