# Arabic Alphabet Coloring Activity - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be filled out by students and teachers/instructors who have participated in the Arabic Alphabet Coloring Activity. It helps the organizers understand how well the activity was received and what improvements can be made for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Full Name**, **Student Age**, and **Teacher or Instructor Name** in the respective fields.
2. Select the **Class Section** you belong to (if applicable).
3. Choose which Arabic letters were covered during the activity.
4. Rate the activity's difficulty level and your own engagement level.
5. Select any **Learning Materials** used during the activity (if any).
6. Share any **Additional Notes or Observations** about the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (Participant Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your school record or ID card.
* **Student Age** (Student Age, number, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Teacher or Instructor Name** (Teacher or Instructor Name, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher or instructor who facilitated the activity.
* **Activity Date** (Activity Date, date, required): Enter the date when you completed the coloring activity.
* **Class Section** (Class Section, text, optional): If you are in a class, select your class section.
* **Which Arabic letters were covered?** (Which Arabic letters were covered?, select_multiple, required): Choose the Arabic letters that were covered during the activity. The options are:
	+ Aleph
	+ Ba
	+ Ta
	+ Tha
	+ Jeem
	+ Ha
	+ Khah
	+ Dal
* **How would you rate the activity difficulty?** (How would you rate the activity difficulty?, select_one, required): Rate the difficulty level of the activity on a scale from Very Easy to Very Challenging.
* **Student engagement level** (Student engagement level, select_one, required): Rate your engagement level during the activity on a scale from Not Engaged to Very Engaged.
* **What learning materials were used?** (What learning materials were used?, select_multiple, optional): Select any learning materials you used during the activity. The options are:
	+ Colored pencils
	+ Markers
	+ Crayons
	+ Paint
	+ Digital tablet
* **Additional notes or observations** (Additional notes or observations, text, optional): Share any feedback or observations about the activity.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified as "optional". Make sure to answer all required fields for the data to be accurately recorded.
