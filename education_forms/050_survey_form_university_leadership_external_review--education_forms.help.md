# Survey Form University Leadership External Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the university leadership from external entities. It helps the university leadership understand their perception in the eyes of external parties, enabling them to make informed decisions to improve their leadership.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete each field as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2.  Please ensure that you are a valid external entity (not a university staff or student).
3.  If you have already completed this form, please do not duplicate your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Survey Form University Leadership External Review** (`survey_form_university_leadership_external_review`, `text`, required: false): This is the heading of the form, which is used to title the feedback section.
*   **University Leadership** (`university_leadership`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience or perception of the university leadership. Please provide specific examples or anecdotes to make your feedback more informative.
*   **External Review** (`external_review`, `text`, required: false): Share any relevant feedback or comments you may have about the university leadership from an external perspective. This could be from your personal experience, industry standards, or public reviews.

## Tips
*   Please answer each question as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
*   If you have any supporting evidence or examples, feel free to include them with your feedback.
*   Your feedback is valuable and will help the university leadership improve their performance.
