# Multicultural Classroom Development Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from teachers and school staff to provide tailored support and training to improve a school's multicultural classroom environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with the requested information for your training needs and preferences.
*   Make sure to provide accurate and complete details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Name** (`user_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Provide your email address so that we can contact you with updates about your training sessions.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number to reach you for any further inquiries.
*   **Training Date** (`training_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date you would like to attend the training session.
*   **Training Time** (`training_time`, Time, Optional): Choose the time slot you prefer for the training session.
*   **Training Location** (`training_location`, Text, Optional): Specify the location where you would like to attend the training session.
*   **Training Type** (`training_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more of the following options:
    *   Diversity
    *   Multicultural Development
    *   Other
*   **Education Level** (`education_level`, Text, Optional): Enter your current education level (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral).
*   **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Enter your department or team name (e.g., Mathematics, English, etc.).
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter your job title or position (e.g., Teacher, Head of Department, etc.).

## Tips

*   Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that we can tailor our training support to your needs.
*   If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Other" option under "Training Type".
*   We will contact you via your provided email address with updates about your training sessions.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
