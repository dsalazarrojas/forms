# Teacher Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about potential teachers who are interested in joining the team. The form helps in understanding the candidate's preferences, qualifications, and availability, making it easier to match them with suitable positions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

### Step 1: Select Your Interest
Select whether you are interested in being a teacher by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the options.

### Step 2: Provide Contact Information
Enter your first name, last name, email, and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.

### Step 3: Share Your Details
Provide a brief description of your education background, mentioning your relevant experience, job description, and available dates and times.

### Step 4: Share Additional Information
Choose your available options for work, such as part-time, full-time, or contract work. Indicate your salary expectations and whether you have teaching specializations, languages, or certifications.

### Step 5: Attach Relevant Links
Attach links to your resume, image, and social media profile (if applicable).

### Step 6: Finalize Your Availability
Specify your location, available days, hours, and seconds.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Recruitment Form (`teacher_recruitment_form`)**: Select whether you are interested in being a teacher.
* **First Name (`first_name`)**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name (`last_name`)**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email (`email`)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (`phone`)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`)**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Experience (`experience`)**: Mention your relevant experience in years.
* **Education Background (`education_background`)**: Provide a brief description of your education background.
* **Job Description (`job_description`)**: Share a brief description of your job description.
* **Available Date (`available_date`)**: Choose the dates you are available to work.
* **Salary (`salary`)**: Indicate your salary expectations.
* **Resume Link (`resume_link`)**: Attach a link to your resume (if applicable).
* **Image Link (`image_link`)**: Attach a link to your image (if applicable).
* **Social Media Link (`social_media_link`)**: Attach a link to your social media profile (if applicable).
* **Location (`location`)**: Specify your location.
* **Gender (`gender`)**: Choose your gender.
* **Available for Job (`available_for_job`)**: Select whether you are available for part-time, full-time, or contract work.
* **Teacher Specializations (`teacher_specializations`)**: Indicate whether you have teaching specializations.
* **Teacher Languages (`teacher_languages`)**: Indicate whether you have teaching languages.
* **Teacher Certifications (`teacher_certifications`)**: Indicate whether you have teaching certifications.
* **Available Days (`available_days`)**: Specify your available days.
* **Available Hours (`available_hours`)**: Specify your available hours.
* **Available Seconds (`available_seconds`)**: Specify your available seconds.

### Tips

* Make sure to complete all the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Attach relevant links to your resume, image, and social media profile.
* Be specific about your availability to work.

This help guide will assist users in understanding the form's purpose, completing it accurately, and providing relevant information for the recruitment process.
