# Geology Course Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about a geology course. The feedback will help the instructor and department understand what they do well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the course on a scale of 1-6 for each of the course ratings fields (Course Rating 1-7).
2. Provide feedback about the instructor in the Instructor Evaluation field.
3. Share any suggestions you have for improving the course in the Student Suggestions and Course Improvements fields.
4. Enter the instructor's name, title, department name, and department email for the instructor and department feedback.
5. Add any comments you have about the course, instructor, and department in the Course Comments, Instructor Comments, Department Comments, and Department Comments 2 fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Rating 1**: Rate the overall course on a scale of 1-6.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2-3: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 4: Neutral
	+ 5: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 6: Very satisfied
* **Instructor Evaluation**: Share your thoughts about the instructor's teaching style, communication, and effectiveness.
* **Student Suggestions**: Share any suggestions you have to improve the course.
* **Course Improvements**: Share any suggestions you have to improve the course.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the instructor's name as it appears on their business card or email signature.
* **Instructor Title**: Enter the instructor's title as it appears on their business card or email signature.
* **Instructor Rating**: Rate the instructor's effectiveness on a scale of 1-6.
* **Department Name**: Enter the department's name as it appears on their website or business card.
* **Department Email**: Enter the department's email address as it appears on their website or business card.
* **Department Phone**: Enter the department's phone number as it appears on their website or business card.
* **Course Title**: Enter the title of the course as it appears on the course schedule.
* **Course Section**: Enter the section number as it appears on the course schedule.
* **Course Number**: Enter the course number as it appears on the course schedule.
* **Course Year**: Enter the year the course is being taught.
* **Course Suggestions**: Share any suggestions you have to improve the course.
* **Department Comments**: Share any comments you have about the department.
* **Course Comments**: Share any comments you have about the course.
* **Course Comments 2**: Share any additional comments you have about the course.
* **Instructor Comments**: Share any comments you have about the instructor.
* **Course Rating 2-7**: Rate the course again on a scale of 1-6.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2-3: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 4: Neutral
	+ 5: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 6: Very satisfied

Note: All fields are optional, but please complete as many as you can to provide thorough feedback.
