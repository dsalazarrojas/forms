### Child Travel Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child to participate in school trips or excursions. It helps the school gather essential information about the trip and the child's needs to ensure their safety and well-being during the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Trip or Excursion**: The name of the trip or excursion as listed on the trip flyer.
* **Date of Trip**: The date of the trip or excursion. Please select the start date of the trip.
* **Student Full Name**: The full name of your child, as registered in school.
* **Teacher and Class Information**: Provide the name of the teacher and the class your child is in (e.g., Mr. Smith - Grade 4).
* **Do you consent to your child participating in this trip?**: Please answer "Yes" if you give your consent for your child to attend the trip. Otherwise, select "No."
* **Transport Consent**: Select all approved transportation methods your child will use during the trip.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Provide your primary mobile number where you can be reached on the day of the trip in case of an emergency.
* **List any medications needed during the trip**: If your child requires any medications during the trip, please list them here.
* **Food allergies or dietary restrictions**: If your child has any food allergies or dietary restrictions, please list them here for meal planning purposes.
* **I authorize emergency medical treatment if required**: Please select "I Authorize" if you consent for your child to receive emergency medical treatment during the trip.
* **Date of Consent**: The date and time you are signing this form to confirm your consent.
