# Student COVID 19 Self Certification And Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help students certify their COVID-19 status, which is crucial for health and safety reasons. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure the well-being of others and to facilitate contact tracing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form to understand the questions and options.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. Select the correct options for each question.
4. Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Symptom Screening** (`student_covid_19_self_certification_and_verification_form_page`): This is the first page of the form, where you will be asked to report your daily symptoms. Please answer each question truthfully, and select all that apply.
* **Has any of the following symptoms?** (`student_covid_19_self_certification_and_verification_form_page_2`): This page will ask you to confirm or deny the presence of various COVID-19 symptoms. Select all that apply.
* **Have you traveled recently?** (`student_covid_19_self_certification_and_verification_form_page_3`): Please select 'True' if you have traveled recently, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you come into close contact with anyone who has COVID-19?** (`student_covid_19_self_certification_and_verification_form_page_4`): Please select 'True' if you have been in close contact with someone who has COVID-19, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you been in close contact with someone with COVID-19?** (`student_covid_19_self_certification_and_verification_form_page_5`): This question is similar to the previous one, please select 'True' if you have been in close contact with someone who has COVID-19, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you taken a COVID-19 test?** (`student_covid_19_self_certification_and_verification_form_page_6`): Please select 'True' if you have taken a COVID-19 test, and 'False' otherwise.
* **How long has it been since you took the test?** (`student_covid_19_self_certification_and_verification_form_page_7`): This question is optional, and you can leave it blank if you haven't taken a test.
* **Have you traveled recently?** (`student_covid_19_self_certification_and_verification_form_page_8`): Please select 'True' if you have traveled recently, and 'False' otherwise.

Note: This form is designed to be completed accurately and truthfully. Failure to do so may affect your safety and the safety of others.
