# End Of Year Teacher Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from teachers on a student's overall performance, academic growth, and behavioral performance at the end of the year. This information will be used to support the student's development and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the student's name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Fill out your name in the `Teacher Name` field.
3. Fill out the name of the school administrator in the `School Administrator` field.
4. Select the overall performance of the student from the `Overall Performance` dropdown.
5. Select the areas of academic growth that the student has shown in the `Academic Growth` field.
6. Select the behavioral performance of the student from the `Behavioral Performance` dropdown.
7. If applicable, fill out any comments or suggestions in the `Comments and Suggestions` field.
8. If applicable, list the student's strengths in the `Student Strengths` field.
9. If applicable, list areas for improvement for the student in the `Areas for Improvement` field.
10. If applicable, list recommendations for the student in the `Recommendations` field.
11. Select the overall evaluation of the student from the `Overall Evaluation` dropdown.
12. Fill out any additional comments in the `Additional Comments` field.
13. If applicable, select the student's progress from the `Student Progress` dropdown.
14. If applicable, list areas for improvement for the student in the `Areas for Improvement` field.
15. Fill out any comments in the `Comments` field.
16. Fill out the date of submission in the `Evaluation Date` field.
17. If applicable, fill out the time of submission in the `Evaluation Time` field.
18. Fill out the name of the person who submitted the form in the `Submitted By` field.
19. Fill out the date of submission in the `Submitted Date` field.
20. If applicable, fill out the time of submission in the `Submitted Time` field.
21. Fill out your signature in the `Teacher Signature` field.
22. Fill out the signature of the school administrator in the `School Administrator Signature` field.
23. Fill out the signature of the parent in the `Parent Signature` field.
24. Fill out the name of the person or department the form is submitted to in the `Submitted To` field.
25. If applicable, fill out the evaluation form ID in the `Evaluation Form ID` field.
