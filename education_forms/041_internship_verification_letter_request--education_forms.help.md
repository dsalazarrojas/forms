# Internship Verification Letter Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request verification of an internship experience from an employer. This can be used for academic, professional, or personal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student section with your name, email, school information, and verification date.
2. Fill out the employer section with their name, email, phone, address, and contact information.
3. Complete the submission details section with the verification status and any additional notes.
4. Fill out the submitter section with your name, email, phone, address, and department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, Email, Required): Enter your university-provided email address.
- **School Information** (`school_info`, Text, Required): Enter your school name and address.
- **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your employer or supervisor.
- **Employer Email** (`employer_email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of your employer or supervisor.
- **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of your employer or supervisor.
- **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, Text, Required): Enter the address of your employer or supervisor.
- **Employer Contact** (`employer_contact`, Text, Required): Enter the contact person's name and title.
- **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your internship verification.
- **Verification Status** (`verification_status`, Select One, Required): Choose the status of your verification request.
- **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief reason for submitting this form.
- **Additional Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
- **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, Note, Optional): Add any additional details about your submission.
- **Submitter Name** (`submitter`, Text, Required): Enter your name.
- **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
- **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
- **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, Text, Required): Enter your address.
- **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, Text, Required): Enter your department or affiliation.
