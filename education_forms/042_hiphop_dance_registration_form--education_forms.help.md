# Hiphop Dance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for crew members and their managers to register for the Hiphop Dance Competition. Please fill out the form below to provide us with your crew's information and to indicate your acceptance of the terms and conditions of the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the crew member fields (names, ages, phones, and emails) for both members of your crew.
2. Enter your manager's information (name, email, phone, and address).
3. Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the competition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Crew members** (`crew_members`, text, optional): Enter the names of your crew members.
* **Manager information** (`manager_information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your crew's manager information.
* **Accept terms and conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, optional): Select "I accept" if you agree to the competition terms and conditions. If you select "I do not accept", your registration will not be processed.
* **Manager name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your crew manager's name.
* **Manager email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager address** (`manager_address`, text, optional): Enter your crew manager's address.
* **Member 1 name** (`member_one_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the first crew member.
* **Member 1 age** (`member_one_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the first crew member.
* **Member 1 phone** (`member_one_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the first crew member.
* **Member 1 email** (`member_one_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the first crew member.
* **Member 2 name** (`member_two_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second crew member.
* **Member 2 age** (`member_two_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the second crew member.
* **Member 2 phone** (`member_two_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the second crew member.
* **Member 2 email** (`member_two_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the second crew member.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields for both crew members and your crew manager.
- If you have multiple crew members beyond two, please contact us to arrange for additional spaces to be added to the form.
- We reserve the right to verify the accuracy of the provided information, and we may contact you if we have concerns.
- Please review your form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
