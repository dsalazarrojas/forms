# Teacher Of The Month Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you nominate a teacher for the "Teacher Of The Month" award.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone).
- Provide the name of the person who is nominating the teacher (Teacher Nominator Name).
- Enter a brief reason why you are nominating this teacher (Reason for Nomination).
- Specify the date of the event or occasion when you witnessed the teacher's exceptional work (Date of Event).
- Provide the time of the event (Time of Event).
- Select the month of the event (Month Year) from the dropdown list.
- Select all the months that this teacher's exceptional work has been demonstrated in the past year (Month).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first_name): Enter the first name of the person to be nominated.
- **Last Name** (last_name): Enter the last name of the person to be nominated.
- **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the person submitting the nomination.
- **Phone** (phone): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the nomination.
- **Teacher Nominator Name** (teacher_nominator_name): Enter the name of the person nominating the teacher.
- **Reason for Nomination** (reason_for_nomination): Provide a brief explanation of why this teacher deserves the award.
- **Date of Event** (date_of_event): Enter the date when you witnessed the teacher's exceptional work.
- **Time of Event** (time_of_event): Provide the time of the event when you witnessed the teacher's exceptional work.
- **Month of Event** (month_year): Select the month of the event or occasion from the dropdown list.
- **Months with Exceptional Work** (month): Select all the months that this teacher has demonstrated exceptional work in the past year.
- **Month Year** (month_year): Select the month of the year when the event or occasion happened.
  Note: This field is used to select the month of the year, and should not be confused with the "Month of Event" field.
- **Month** (month): Select all the months that this teacher has demonstrated exceptional work in the past year.
  Note: This field is a multiple-select field, allowing you to select multiple months.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to provide specific details about the teacher's exceptional work when providing the Reason for Nomination.
- If the event or occasion is on-going, you can leave the Date of Event blank.
- If the event or occasion is not a specific date, you can use the first day of the month.
- Be honest and provide accurate information to help the teacher receive the recognition they deserve.
