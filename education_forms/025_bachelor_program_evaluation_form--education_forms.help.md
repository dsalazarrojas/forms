# Bachelor Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the quality and effectiveness of the bachelor's programs. It aims to collect data from students to help program directors and deans identify areas of improvement and enhance the programs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and answer according to your honest opinion and experience.
2. For each field, choose the corresponding rating or answer based on your feedback.
3. If a field is required, please fill it in accordingly. If it's not required, feel free to provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Bachelor Program Quality Evaluation --** (Note): This is a heading section that introduces the form and its purpose.
* **Bachelor Program Name** (Student Program Name, text, required): Please enter the name of your bachelor's program, e.g., BSc Computer Science, BA English.
* **Current Year of Study** (Current Year of Study, select_one, required): Choose the current year you are in the program from the list:
	+ Year 1
	+ Year 2
	+ Year 3
	+ Year 4
	+ Recent Graduate
* **Relevance of the curriculum to your career goals** (Curriculum Relevance Rating, number, required): Rate how relevant the curriculum is to your career goals on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 = Not relevant
	+ 2 = Somewhat relevant
	+ 3 = Neutral
	+ 4 = Relevant
	+ 5 = Highly relevant
* **Overall quality of instruction and teaching** (Teaching Quality Rating, number, required): Rate the quality of instruction and teaching on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 = Poor
	+ 2 = Average
	+ 3 = Neutral
	+ 4 = Good
	+ 5 = Excellent
* **Quality of academic support and advising** (Academic Support Rating, number, required): Rate the quality of academic support and advising on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 = Poor
	+ 2 = Average
	+ 3 = Neutral
	+ 4 = Good
	+ 5 = Excellent
* **Quality of facilities and learning resources (Library, Labs)** (Facilities Resources Rating, number, required): Rate the quality of facilities and learning resources on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 = Poor
	+ 2 = Average
	+ 3 = Neutral
	+ 4 = Good
	+ 5 = Excellent
* **Satisfaction with opportunities for student engagement** (Student Engagement Opportunities, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with opportunities for student engagement on a scale of 1-5:
	+ 1 = Not satisfied
	+ 2 = Somewhat satisfied
	+ 3 = Neutral
	+ 4 = Satisfied
	+ 5 = Highly satisfied
* **I feel empowered and respected as a student in this program** (Empowerment and Respect, select_one, required): Choose how you feel about being a student in the program:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **How would you describe the overall learning environment?** (Program Environment Description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your overall learning environment experience.
* **What are the greatest strengths of this program?** (Strengths of Program, text, required): List the greatest strengths of the program.
* **What areas need the most improvement?** (Areas for Improvement, text, required): Suggest areas that need the most improvement.
* **Would you recommend this program to others?** (Recommend Program, select_one, required): Choose whether you would recommend the program to others:
	+ Yes, highly recommend
	+ Yes, with reservations
	+ No
* **Any other comments for the Dean or Program Director?** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
