# Physics Formula Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physics Formula Calculator is a tool used to calculate physical properties related to sound, gravity, and motion. This form allows users to input relevant values and obtain the corresponding results.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, simply select the desired property to calculate, and input its corresponding value. You can choose which calculations to perform by filling in the desired fields. If a field is not filled, the corresponding calculation will be skipped.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Formula (1)
* **Formula** (`Formula`, `note`, optional): This field is a note section for you to write down any additional comments or references related to the formula you're using.

### Speed of Sound (2)
* **Speed of Sound** (`speed_of_sound`, `number`, optional): Enter the speed of sound, typically measured in meters per second (m/s). This value is used to calculate the speed of sound in a medium with a specific density and temperature.

### Density of Water (3)
* **Density of Water** (`density_of_water`, `number`, optional): Enter the density of water, typically measured in kilograms per cubic meter (kg/m³). This value is used in calculations involving sound waves in water.

### Gravitational Force (4)
* **Gravitational Force** (`gravitational_force`, `number`, optional): Enter the gravitational force, typically measured in newtons (N). This value is used in calculations involving falling objects or objects with mass.

### Acceleration of Gravity (5)
* **Acceleration of Gravity** (`acceleration_gravity`, `number`, optional): Enter the acceleration of gravity, typically measured in meters per second squared (m/s²). This value is used in calculations involving falling objects or objects with mass.

### Energy of Motion (6)
* **Energy of Motion** (`energy_of_motion`, `number`, optional): Enter the energy of motion, typically measured in joules (J). This value is used in calculations involving kinetic energy or potential energy.

## Tips
- Make sure to input values in the correct units to ensure accurate calculations.
- If you're unsure about a specific calculation, refer to the formula used in the "Formula" field for more information.
- You can calculate multiple properties by filling in multiple fields and reviewing the results.
