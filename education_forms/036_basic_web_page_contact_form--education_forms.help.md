# Basic Web Page Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture contact information and messages from users, allowing the web page owners or administrators to respond or follow up with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, which is a required field.
2. Enter your email address, which is also a required field.
3. If you have a company name, enter it in the Company name (optional) field. If not, leave it blank.
4. Fill in your phone number, which is not required but optional.
5. Fill in the subject of your message, which is a required field.
6. Type in your message, which is also a required field.
7. Choose your preferred contact method: Email, Phone, or Text Message.
8. Decide if you want to be notified when the message is received.
9. Confirm that the provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name (required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email address (required)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone number (optional)**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Company name (optional)**: If you are contacting us on behalf of your company, enter the company name.
* **Subject (required)**: Briefly describe the purpose or topic of your message.
* **Your message (required)**: Type in a detailed description of your message.
* **Preferred contact method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted: Email, Phone, or Text Message.
* **Notify me when message is received**: Decide if you want to be notified when the message is received.
* **I confirm this information is accurate**: Confirm that the provided information is accurate.
