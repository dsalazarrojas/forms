# Electronic Feasibility Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electronic Feasibility Questionnaire Template is a form designed to gather information about the electronic feasibility of a project or idea. It's used to evaluate the potential success of a project or initiative, particularly in the education sector.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following pages:
1. **General Information** (Page 1)
2. **What are your goals** (Page 2)
3. **Leadership** (Page 3)
4. **Quality** (Page 4)
5. **Facilities** (Page 5)
6. **Feedback** (Page 6)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief overview of the project or idea.

### What are your goals (Page 2)
* **What are your goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Describe the main objectives of your project or idea.

### Leadership (Page 3)
* **Leadership** (`leadership`, text, optional): Identify the key leaders involved in the project or idea.

### Quality (Page 4)
* **Quality** (`quality`, text, optional): Describe the expected quality standards for the project or idea.

### Facilities (Page 5)
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, text, optional): Outline the required facilities or resources for the project or idea.

### Feedback (Page 6)
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about the project or idea.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when answering each question.
- Take your time to ensure you provide clear and concise responses.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
- The information you provide will help us better understand your project or idea, so make sure to be as detailed as possible.
