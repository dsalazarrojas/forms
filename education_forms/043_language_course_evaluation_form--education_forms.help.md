# Language Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Language Course Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about users' experiences and opinions about language courses offered by the institution. This form is used to gather feedback on the quality and effectiveness of the courses, allowing the institution to assess and improve its language course offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the **User** field, which is your name or identifier.
2. Select the **Language Course** that you are evaluating from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose your evaluation for the **Course Evaluation** field, either "Yes" or "No", indicating your overall satisfaction with the course.
4. If you have comments or thoughts about the course, type them in the **Evaluation** field.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, provide them in the **Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User** (1, text, required): Your name or identifier.
* **Language Course** (2, select_one, required): The language course you are evaluating.
* **Course Evaluation** (3, select_multiple, required): Your overall satisfaction with the course, indicated by "Yes" or "No".
* **Teacher** (4, text, optional): The name of the teacher for the course (if you know it).
* **Evaluation** (5, text, optional): Your written feedback or evaluation of the course.
* **Comment** (6, note, optional): Any additional comments or feedback you have about the course.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when evaluating the course and providing feedback.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification or assistance.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help the institution improve its language course offerings.
