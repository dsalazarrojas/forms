# Cervical Collar Removal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cervical Collar Removal Form is used to document the removal of a cervical collar, which is a device used to stabilize the neck. This form is essential for ensuring a smooth and safe removal process, and it helps the medical staff to keep track of patient information and treatment progress.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the Cervical Collar Removal Form:

1. Ensure that the patient is present and aware of the information being collected.
2. Fill in the patient's full legal name, date of birth, medical record number, and contact phone number.
3. Provide the admitting diagnosis or reason for the collar application.
4. Indicate the spinal clearance status, which is the result of a physical exam or imaging tests.
5. Describe the imaging results, if any.
6. Rate the patient's current neck pain score from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain).
7. Obtain the patient's consent for collar removal, ensuring they understand the risks and benefits of the procedure.
8. Record the name of the authorizing physician and the staff member performing the removal.
9. Check if the patient has received post-removal instructions.
10. Document the date and time of collar removal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Legal Name**: Enter the patient's full legal name as it appears on their identification.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. This helps ensure correct record matching.
* **Medical Record Number (MRN)**: Enter the patient's unique hospital or clinic identifier.
* **Patient Contact Phone**: Enter the patient's contact phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Admitting Diagnosis or Reason for Collar**: Describe the reason the cervical collar was applied.
* **Spinal Clearance Status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Clinically Cleared (Physical Exam)
	+ Radiographically Cleared (Imaging)
	+ Both Clinically and Radiographically Cleared
	+ Not Yet Cleared
* **Summary of Imaging Results**: Briefly describe the results of X-ray, CT, or MRI findings.
* **Current Neck Pain Score (0-10)**: Rate the patient's current neck pain from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain).
* **Patient Consent for Collar Removal**: Select one of the following options:
	+ I Consent and Agree
	+ I Decline at this Time
* **Risks and Benefits Explained**: Select one of the following options:
	+ I Acknowledge and Understand
	+ Other
* **Authorizing Physician Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who ordered the removal.
* **Staff Member Performing Removal**: Enter the name of the nurse or technician performing the removal.
* **Post-Removal Instructions Provided**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes - Instructions Provided
	+ No - Not Yet Provided
* **Date of Removal**: Enter the current date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time of Removal**: Enter the specific time the collar was removed.
* **Clinical Notes Post-Removal**: Document any changes in neurological status or pain after the collar removal.
* **Patient Witness Signature Date**: Enter the current date as a witness to the patient's consent.
* **Staff Witness Signature Date**: This field is for internal staff use only and should not be filled by the patient.
