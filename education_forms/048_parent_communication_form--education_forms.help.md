# Parent Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Communication Form is designed to facilitate communication between parents and teachers/school administrators on matters related to their child's education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the field-by-field explanations below.
2. Ensure you use the correct email format for the "Email" field.
3. Select the correct category from the list provided in the "Category" field.
4. Fill in the "Subject" field with a clear and concise subject matter.
5. Fill in the date of the communication in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)** (`Student Name`, `required: false`): Enter your child's full name.
* **Teacher Name (teacher_name)** (`Teacher Name`, `required: true`): Enter the name of your child's teacher.
* **Email (email)** (`Email`, `required: true`): Enter your email address. Please ensure it's in the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone (phone)** (`Phone`, `required: false`): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Message (message)** (`Message`, `required: false`): Type in your message to the teacher or school administrator.
* **Category (category)** (`Select One`, `required: true`): Choose the category that best describes the purpose of your communication. Options are:
	+ Parent
	+ Teacher
	+ School Administrator
	+ Caregiver
* **Subject (subject)** (`Subject`, `required: true`): Enter a clear and concise subject matter that describes your message.
* **Date (date)** (`Date`, `required: true`): Enter the date of the communication in a format that can be easily understood (e.g., `2024-03-15`).

## Tips
- Be as clear and concise as possible when filling in the form.
- Ensure that you have spelled your child's name correctly.
- If you need help, don't hesitate to contact the school or your child's teacher.
