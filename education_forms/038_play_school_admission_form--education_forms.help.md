# play_school_admission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians to enroll their child in a school or educational institution. Please complete the form carefully to ensure a smooth and efficient enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form before starting.
2. Click on each field to enter your details, and make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you're unsure about any field, you can ask your child's teacher or school administrator for guidance.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want your child to start at the school.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your child's name as per their official documents, such as passport or birth certificate.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have a valid email address, enter it to stay updated about school events and communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number to reach you in case of emergencies or important updates.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required): Select your child's grade level (First, Second, Third, or Fourth).
- **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required): Please sign your name as a parent or guardian, indicating your agreement to the school's policies.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If there's someone you'd like to contact in case of emergencies, enter their name and phone number.

## Tips

- Please ensure your child's information is accurate and up-to-date.
- Use a valid email address for the **Email** field.
- For **Parent Signature**, use your actual signature.
- You can ask your child's teacher or school administrator for help if you need assistance filling out the form.
