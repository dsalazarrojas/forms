# Veterinary Education Resource Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Veterinary Education Resource Access Request" form is designed to allow you to request access to educational resources that will improve your knowledge and skills as a veterinarian or veterinary student. This form will help us understand your needs and provide you with the necessary resources to enhance your education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information (fields 7-9) so that we can contact you regarding your request.
2. Choose the type of institution you are affiliated with (field 2) to help us understand who is requesting the resource.
3. Identify the type of resource you are seeking (field 4) to ensure we provide you with the correct material.
4. Provide the title of the resource you are requesting (field 1).
5. Specify the purpose of your request (field 6) so that we can understand why you need the resource.
6. Fill out any other relevant fields as required or necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, required: false): This is the title of your request. You can enter a brief description of the resource you are requesting.

### Institution Type
* **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, required: false): Choose the type of institution you are affiliated with from the following options:
	+ University
	+ Clinic
	+ Educator

### Institution Name
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, required: false): If you are affiliated with a specific institution, enter the name of the institution here. If not, you can leave this field blank.

### Resource Type
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, required: false): Choose the type of resource you are seeking from the following options:
	+ Textbook
	+ Video
	+ Document

### Resource Name
* **Resource Name** (`resource_name`, required: false): If you are requesting a specific resource, enter the title of the resource here. If not, you can leave this field blank.

### Purpose
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, required: false): Explain the reason for your request. This will help us understand why you need the resource and ensure we provide you with the correct material.

### Contact Information
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, required: false): Enter your first name so that we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, required: false): Enter your last name so that we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you regarding your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your request correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us and we will be happy to help.
* If you need help with a specific resource, please let us know and we will do our best to assist you.
