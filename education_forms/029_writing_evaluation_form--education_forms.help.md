## Writing Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide constructive feedback on the quality and effectiveness of a piece of writing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the provided text carefully and take notes if needed.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
3. Use the space provided to elaborate on your answers.
4. Make sure to include specific examples and examples to support your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, not required): Please provide a brief summary of the text you are evaluating. Try to capture the main argument, thesis, or point of the text.
* **Thesis** (`Thesis`, `text`, not required): Identify the main argument or central claim of the text. Be specific and try to distill the text's core idea.
* **Writing** (`Writing`, `text`, not required): Describe the writing style, tone, and language used in the text. Consider factors like clarity, coherence, and persuasiveness.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, not required): Share your thoughts, suggestions, and criticisms about the text. Be constructive and specific, and try to provide actionable advice for the writer to improve.
