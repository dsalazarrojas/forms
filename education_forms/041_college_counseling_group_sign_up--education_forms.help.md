# College Counseling Group Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from students who want to sign up for group counseling sessions. The information will be used to determine which group would be the best fit for you and to ensure that you're ready to participate in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Be sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g. ID card, passport).
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g. ID card, passport).
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your university email address. This is the preferred email address we will use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Student ID Number** (5, text, required): Enter your university ID number.
* **Class Year** (6, select_one, required): Select the current year you are in school.
* **Major or Field of Study** (7, text, optional): If you're unsure about your major or field of study, you can leave this field blank.
* **Sign-Up Date** (8, date, required): Enter the date you're planning to sign up for group counseling.
* **Type of Group You Are Interested In** (9, select_multiple, required): Select all the groups you're interested in (e.g. Anxiety Management, Depression Support).
* **Specific Group Preference** (10, text, optional): If you have a specific group in mind, enter it here.
* **Have You Participated in Group Counseling Before** (11, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you've participated in group counseling before.
* **Tell Us About Your Previous Experience** (12, text, optional): If applicable, describe your previous group counseling experience.
* **Availability** (13, select_multiple, required): Select the times of the day you're available to attend group sessions.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (14, select_one, required): Select the time of day you prefer to attend group sessions.
* **Group Size Preference** (15, select_one, optional): Select the size of group you prefer to be in.
* **What Are Your Goals for Joining a Group** (16, text, required): Enter your goals for joining a group (e.g. improve mental health, reduce stress).
* **Any Concerns About Group Counseling** (17, text, optional): If you have any concerns, enter them here.
* **Are You Currently in Individual Counseling** (18, select_one, required): Select 'Yes On Campus' if you're currently in individual counseling on campus.
* **Current Counselor Name** (19, text, optional): If you're currently in individual counseling, enter the name of your counselor.
* **Are You Currently Taking Any Psychiatric Medications** (20, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you're currently taking psychiatric medications.
* **Have You Ever Had Thoughts of Harming Yourself** (21, select_one, required): Select 'Yes Currently' if you're experiencing thoughts of self-harm.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (22, text, required): Enter the name of the person you'd like to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (23, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person you'd like to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (24, text, required): Enter how you're related to the emergency contact.
* **How Did You Hear About Group Counseling** (25, select_one, required): Select how you heard about group counseling.
* **Consent to Participate in Group Counseling** (26, select_one, required): Select 'Yes I Consent' to participate in group counseling.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (27, select_one, required): Select 'Yes I Agree' to maintain confidentiality of group members.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (28, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
