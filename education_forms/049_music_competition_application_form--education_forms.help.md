# Music Competition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from musicians applying for a music competition. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and successful application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form fields as required**: Ensure that all mandatory fields (required: true) are filled out completely and accurately. You can refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for more details on each field.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Double-check the accuracy of your input in each field to avoid any potential issues with your application.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've completed all the required fields, review your submission carefully and click the submit button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Competition Application Form** (`music_competition_application_form`, text, required: false): This field is the title of the form, which you are not required to fill out. You can leave it blank if you wish.
* **Musician Name** (`musician_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Music Group Name** (`music_group_name`, text, required: false): If you're part of a music group, enter their name; otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Music Type** (`music_type`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ Application Fee: The fee associated with applying for the competition.
	+ Entry Fee: The fee for participating in the competition.
	+ Performance Fee: The fee for performing at the competition.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Accepted: Your application has been accepted.
	+ Declined: Your application has been declined.
	+ Pending: Your application is still under review.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to perform on in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, text, required: false): Enter the instrument(s) you play.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Music Genre** (`music_genre`, text, required: false): Enter the genre(s) of music you specialize in.
* **Performance Location** (`performance_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you plan to perform.
* **Performance Duration** (`performance_duration`, text, required: false): Enter the duration of your performance.
* **Performance Start Time** (`performance_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of your performance.
* **Performance End Time** (`performance_end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of your performance.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of your input in each field to avoid any potential issues with your application.
* Make sure to follow the competition rules and regulations carefully.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the competition rules or contact the competition organizers for clarification.
