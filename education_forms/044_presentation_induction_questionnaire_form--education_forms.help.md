# Presentation Induction Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Presentation Induction Questionnaire Form is designed to collect essential details about presentations. This form will help ensure that both organizers and presenters are well-prepared and on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the "background information" field, which is required. This field helps provide context for the presentation.
- Select the type of presentation you are giving in the "presentation-type" field. This field is also required and has three options.
- Fill out your details as a presenter in the "presenter" field, which is required.
- If applicable, select the presentation materials you will be using in the "presentation-materials" field.
- Provide the date and time of your presentation in the "date-time" field, if applicable.
- If relevant, specify the duration of your presentation in the "duration" field.
- In the "comments" field, provide any additional comments or information about your presentation.
- Optionally, provide follow-up information in the "follow-up" field.
- Evaluate your presentation in the "presentation-evaluation" field, which is required and has three options.
- Add any additional comments about your presentation in the "additional-comments" field, which is optional.
- Leave any feedback about your presentation experience in the "feedback" field, which is optional.
- If you have a rating for your presentation, provide it in the "rating" field.
- If you have a contact email, provide it in the "email" field.
- Finally, provide your phone number in the "phone" field, which is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **background-information**: The background information field is a text field where you provide essential context about your presentation.
* **presentation-type**: This field allows you to select the type of presentation you are giving (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **presenter**: This field is for you to fill out your details as a presenter.
* **presentation-materials**: Select all applicable presentation materials you will be using for your presentation.
* **date-time**: Provide the date and time of your presentation, if applicable.
* **duration**: Specify the duration of your presentation, if relevant.
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your presentation.
* **follow-up**: Optionally, provide follow-up information about your presentation.
* **presentation-evaluation**: Evaluate your presentation using one of the three options (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **additional-comments**: Add any additional comments about your presentation.
* **feedback**: Leave feedback about your presentation experience.
* **rating**: Provide a rating for your presentation.
* **email**: Provide your contact email.
* **phone**: Provide your phone number, which is optional.
