# Key Stage Lesson Plan Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Key Stage Lesson Plan Form is designed to assist you in creating a detailed lesson plan that covers all the key elements of a lesson, including the key stage, subject, resources, and evaluation criteria. This form is meant to be a helpful tool to ensure a thorough and structured approach to lesson planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, such as the key stage and subject.
2. Provide a brief description of the topic you will be teaching.
3. Outline your teaching objectives and the resources you will use.
4. Describe your teaching method and how you will evaluate student performance.
5. Outline the outcome criteria and assessment methods.
6. Provide a review of the lesson and next steps.
7. Finally, fill in any additional information, such as references and created by details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lesson Plan** (`lesson_plan`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can enter a brief description of the lesson.
* **Key Stage** (`key_stage`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to select the key stage for your lesson (1, 2, or 3).
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the subject being taught in this lesson.
* **Topic** (`topic`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief description of the topic you will be covering in this lesson.
* **Teaching Objectives** (`teaching_objectives`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Outline the specific learning objectives for this lesson.
* **Resources** (`resources`, `text`, required: false)
	+ List the resources you will use for this lesson.
* **Teaching Method** (`method`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Describe the teaching method you will use for this lesson.
* **Outcome Criteria** (`outcome_criteria`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Define the criteria for evaluating student performance.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Describe how you will evaluate student performance.
* **Review** (`review`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide a review of the lesson and suggest next steps.
* **Assessment** (`assessment`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Outline the methods for assessing student performance.
* **Conclusion** (`conclusion`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Summarize the key points of the lesson.
* **Action Points** (`action_points`, `text`, required: false)
	+ List any action points or next steps for the students.
* **Reference** (`reference`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional references or resources.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Suggest any next steps for the students.
* **Review By** (`review_by`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Specify the person or group responsible for reviewing the lesson.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the person or group who created this lesson plan.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the date this lesson plan was reviewed.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the person or group who approved this lesson plan.
