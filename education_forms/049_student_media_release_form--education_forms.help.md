# Student Media Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Media Release Form is used to obtain permission from students and parents for the use of student images, names, and other identifying information in school publications, such as yearbooks, school newspapers, and websites. This form is typically filled out by a student's parent or guardian, providing consent for the school to use the student's media for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's first name.
2. Fill in the student's last name.
3. Enter a valid email address for the parent or guardian.
4. Enter a valid email address for the school.
5. Choose the desired permissions for photo and name use.
6. Indicate the publication venue and type (e.g. "Print" or "Electonic publication").
7. Fill in the other publication venue (if applicable).
8. Specify the other publication type (if applicable).
9. Sign and date the form with the student's signature.
10. Sign the form with the parent or guardian's signature.
11. Enter the current date and time.
12. Fill in the student's name, school name, grade level, and teacher's name.
13. Fill in the school's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, Required): This field is for the student's first name.
* **Last name** (Text, Required): This field is for the student's last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): This field is for a valid email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Name** (Text, Required): This field is for the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent Email** (Email, Required): This field is for a valid email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Photo Permission** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether to give permission for the school to use the student's photo.
	+ Yes, I give permission
	+ No, not at this time
	+ I don't want to give permission, contact me for an alternative
* **Name Permission** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether to give permission for the school to use the student's name.
	+ Yes, I give permission
	+ No, not at this time
	+ I don't want to give permission, contact me for an alternative
* **Publication Permission** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate the desired permission for publication.
	+ Print
	+ Electronic publication
	+ Website
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Where will you be published?** (Text, Required): Specify where the student's media will be published.
* **Publication type** (Text, Required): Specify the type of publication (e.g. print, digital, video).
* **Student signature** (Note, Required): Sign the form with the student's signature.
* **Parent signature** (Note, Required): Sign the form with the parent or guardian's signature.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Enter the current date.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Enter the current time.
* **Student Name** (Text, Required): Fill in the student's name.
* **School Name** (Text, Required): Fill in the school name.
* **Student Grade** (Text, Required): Fill in the student's grade level.
* **Parent Grade** (Text, Required): Fill in the parent or guardian's grade level.
* **Teacher Name** (Text, Required): Fill in the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Role** (Text, Required): Fill in the teacher's role.
* **School Email** (Email, Required): Fill in the school's email address.
* **School Phone** (Text, Required): Fill in the school's phone number.
* **Student Phone** (Text, Optional): Fill in the student's phone number (if available).
* **Other publication venue** (Text, Optional): Specify an alternative publication venue if desired.
* **Other publication type** (Text, Optional): Specify an alternative publication type if desired.
