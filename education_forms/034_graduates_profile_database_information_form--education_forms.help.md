# graduates_profile_database_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from graduates about their profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Profile Name** in the text field provided. This is a required field but not mandatory.
2. Fill in your **graduation date** if available, in the provided date field.
3. If applicable, enter your **graduation year** in the number field.
4. Select your **major** from the text field.
5. Enter your **university name** in the text field.
6. If available, provide your **contact email** in the provided email field.
7. Enter your **contact phone number** in the text field.
8. If applicable, provide a **profile picture** in the text field.
9. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the **graduation country** field if applicable.
10. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the **graduate degree** field if applicable.
11. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each major field (**major_field**, **major_field_2**).
12. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the **university country** field if applicable.
13. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the **graduation month** field if applicable.
14. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the **graduation year month** field if applicable.
15. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the **graduation day** field if applicable.
16. Enter your **contact number** in the text field, if available.
17. For the **profile description**, enter any additional information you'd like to share.
18. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each **graduation city** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Profile Name**: This is the text field where you can enter your graduate profile name.
* **graduation_date**: If you have a graduation date, you can enter it in this date field.
* **graduation year**: If you know your graduation year, you can enter it in this number field.
* **major**: Select your major from the provided options.
* **university_name**: Enter your university name in this text field.
* **contact_email**: If you have a contact email, you can enter it in this email field.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your contact phone number in this text field.
* **profile_picture**: If you have a profile picture, you can upload it in this text field.
* **graduation_country**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have information about your graduation country.
* **graduate_degree**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have information about your graduate degree.
* **major_field** and **major_field_2**: These are multiple-choice fields where you can select 'Yes' or 'No' for each option.
* **university_country**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have information about your university country.
* **graduation_month**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have information about your graduation month.
* **graduation_year_month**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have information about your graduation year month.
* **graduation_day**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have information about your graduation day.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number in this text field.
* **profile_description**: Enter any additional information about your profile in this text field.
* **graduation_city**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each city field (if applicable).

## Tips
- Ensure that you have all the required fields filled out before submitting the form.
- Use the correct format for the date, number, and other fields as requested.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for help or clarification.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
