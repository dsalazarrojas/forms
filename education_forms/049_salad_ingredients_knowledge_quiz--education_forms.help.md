# Salad Ingredients Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess users' knowledge about salad ingredients, specifically their preferences and experiences related to salads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the multiple-choice questions about your favorite salad ingredients, how you like to have your salads, and your preferred salad dressing.
2. Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about salads.
3. Fill in your name, last name, and email address so we can contact you for further questions or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **What is your favorite salad ingredient?** (select one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Lutet
	+ Tomato
	+ Cucumber
	+ Carrot
	+ Beet
	+ Onion
	+ Bell pepper
	+ Celery
* **How do you like your salad?** (select multiple): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Plain
	+ With dressing
	+ With cheese
	+ With fruits
	+ With nuts
* **What is your favorite salad dressing?** (select one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Mayonnaise
	+ Ranch
	+ Thousand island dressing
	+ Balsamic vinagerre
	+ Blue cheese
	+ Caesar
	+ Homemade
* **What do you learn from this quiz?** (select one): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Additional comments** (text): Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about salads.
* **How long does it take to complete the quiz?** (time): Estimate the time it takes to complete the quiz.
