# Trade School Student Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the student's trade school experience, including their background, program enrollment, and discharge reason.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in the following fields:
	*   Student Name
	*   Student Date of Birth
	*   Program Title
	*   Program Start Date
	*   Program End Date
	*   Discharge Reason
	*   School Name
	*   School City
	*   School State
	*   School Zip

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name**: <label>Student Name</label> (`<name>student_name`, `<type>text`, `required>false`): Enter the student's full name.
*   **Student Date of Birth**: <label>Student Date of Birth</label> (`<name>student_date_of_birth`, `<type>date`, `required>true`): Enter the student's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   **Program Title**: <label>Program Title</label> (`<name>program_title`, `<type>text`, `required>true`): Enter the title of the program the student enrolled in.
*   **Program Start Date**: <label>Program Start Date</label> (`<name>program_start_date`, `<type>date`, `required>false`): Enter the date the student started the program in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   **Program End Date**: <label>Program End Date</label> (`<name>program_end_date`, `<type>date`, `required>true`): Enter the date the student completed the program in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   **Discharge Reason**: <label>Discharge Reason</label> (`<name>discharge_reason`, `<type>text`, `required>true`): Enter the reason for the student's discharge from the program.
*   **Assigned Teacher**: <label>Assigned Teacher</label> (`<name>assigned_teacher`, `<type>select_one`, `required>true`): Select the teacher who was assigned to the student.
*   **Student Email**: <label>Student Email</label> (`<name>student_email`, `<type>email`, `required>false`): Enter the student's email address.
*   **Student Phone**: <label>Student Phone</label> (`<name>student_phone`, `<type>text`, `required>false`): Enter the student's phone number.
*   **School Name**: <label>School Name</label> (`<name>schoool_name`, `<type>text`, `required>true`): Enter the name of the school the student attended.
*   **School Address**: <label>School Address</label> (`<name>schoool_address`, `<type>text`, `required>false`): Enter the address of the school the student attended.
*   **School City**: <label>School City</label> (`<name>schoool_city`, `<type>text`, `required>true`): Enter the city of the school the student attended.
*   **School State**: <label>School State</label> (`<name>schoool_state`, `<type>text`, `required>true`): Enter the state of the school the student attended.
*   **School Zip**: <label>School Zip</label> (`<name>schoool_zip`, `<type>text`, `required>true`): Enter the zip code of the school the student attended.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority or representative for further guidance.
