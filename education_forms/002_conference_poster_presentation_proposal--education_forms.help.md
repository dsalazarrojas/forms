# Conference Poster Presentation Proposal - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a conference poster presentation proposal. Presenters can provide details about their work, presentation preferences, and requirements to ensure a successful presentation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your complete name as it should appear on the poster.
2. Enter your current academic or professional title.
3. Enter the name of your university, company, or research institution.
4. Enter your primary contact email address.
5. Enter your contact phone number (optional).
6. If you have co-presenters, list their names and affiliations.
7. Enter the full title of your poster presentation.
8. Enter a brief summary of your research or project (max 250 words).
9. Enter 3-5 keywords that describe your work.
10. Select the primary category for your poster presentation.
11. Choose your preferred presentation style.
12. Specify if you need any additional audio-visual support.
13. Select the size of your poster if it has special requirements.
14. Indicate if your work has been presented before or not.
15. Check if you are interested in publishing your work.
16. Check your status as a student.
17. Indicate if you would like to be considered for a travel grant.
18. Provide any additional accessibility requirements.
19. List 2-3 key references if applicable.
20. Check the certification box to confirm original authorship and agree to presentation terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Presenter Full Name**:
	+ A field to enter your name as it will be displayed on the poster.
* **Professional Title**:
	+ A field to enter your current title.
* **Institution**:
	+ A field to enter the name of your university, company, or research institution.
* **Email Address**:
	+ A field to enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**:
	+ An optional field for your contact phone number.
* **Co-Presenters**:
	+ A field to list names and affiliations of co-presenters if applicable.
* **Poster Presentation Title**:
	+ A field to enter the full title of your poster presentation.
* **Abstract**:
	+ A field to enter a brief summary of your research or project (max 250 words).
* **Keywords**:
	+ A field to enter 3-5 keywords that describe your work.
* **Research Category**:
	+ A dropdown menu to select the primary category for your poster presentation.
* **Presentation Format Preference**:
	+ A dropdown menu to choose your preferred presentation style.
* **Audio-Visual Requirements**:
	+ A dropdown menu to specify if you need any additional audio-visual support.
* **Poster Dimensions**:
	+ A dropdown menu to select the size of your poster if it has special requirements.
* **Has this work been presented before?**:
	+ A dropdown menu to indicate if your work has been presented before or not.
* **Interest in Publication**:
	+ A dropdown menu to check if you are interested in publishing your work.
* **Are you currently a student?**:
	+ A dropdown menu to check your status as a student.
* **Travel Grant Interest**:
	+ A dropdown menu to indicate if you would like to be considered for a travel grant.
* **Special Accommodations Needed**:
	+ An optional field to provide any additional accessibility requirements.
* **Key References**:
	+ An optional field to list 2-3 key references if applicable.
* **Submission Agreement**:
	+ A dropdown menu to confirm original authorship and agree to presentation terms.
