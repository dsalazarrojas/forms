# Costume History Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's background, preferences, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out this form, please start by selecting the relevant sections and questions that apply to you.
- Fill out the fields with your personal information, such as your first and last name, birth date, and birthplace.
- Choose your favorite food, animal, and historical period from the provided options.
- Select one historical figure that interests you.
- Choose multiple school subjects and hobbies that you enjoy.
- You can add favorite books, historical events, movies, music, games, sports, and artists as text inputs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you prefer it to be known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you prefer it to be known.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Birth Place** (`birth_place`, text, optional): Enter the city or town where you were born.
* **Parents** (`parents`, select multiple, required): List your parents' names (e.g., Alice and Bob).
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select one, required): Choose your favorite type of food (e.g., Pizza, Sushi, Tacos).
* **Favorite Animal** (`favorite_animal`, select one, required): Choose your favorite type of animal (e.g., Dog, Cat, Fish).
* **School Subjects** (`school_subjects`, select multiple, required): Choose multiple school subjects you enjoy (e.g., History, Literature, English, Math, Science, Geography).
* **Favorite Hobbies** (`favorite_hobby`, select multiple, required): Choose multiple hobbies or activities you enjoy (e.g., Playing sports, Drawing, Painting, Reading, Hiking).
* **Historical Period** (`historical_period`, select one, required): Choose a historical period that interests you (e.g., Ancient, Medieval, Renaissance, Modern).
* **Historical Figure** (`historical_figure`, select one, required): Choose a historical figure that interests you (e.g., Leonardo da Vinci, Albert Einstein, William Shakespeare).
* **Favorite Book** (`favorite_book`, text, optional): Enter the title of your favorite book.
* **Historical Event** (`historical_event`, select multiple, optional): Choose historical events that interest you (e.g., Battle of Gettysburg, The Great Fire of London, The French Revolution).
* **Favorite Movie** (`favorite_movie`, text, optional): Enter the title of your favorite movie.
* **Favorite Music** (`favorite_music`, text, optional): Enter your favorite music style or genre.
* **Favorite Game** (`favorite_game`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favorite game.
* **Favorite Sport** (`favorite_sport`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favorite sport.
* **Favorite Song** (`favorite_song`, text, optional): Enter the title of your favorite song.
* **Favorite Band** (`favorite_band`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favorite band.
* **Favorite Artist** (`favorite_artist`, text, optional): Enter the name of your favorite artist.
* **Favorite Band2** (`favorite_band2`, text, optional): Enter the name of your second favorite band.
* **Favorite Sport2** (`favorite_sport2`, text, optional): Enter the name of your second favorite sport.
* **Favorite Band3** (`favorite_band3`, text, optional): Enter the name of your third favorite band.
* **Favorite Hobby 2** (`favorite_hobby_2`, text, optional): Enter another hobby or activity you enjoy. 

**Tips**
- Please be honest about your information and preferences.
- Select all relevant fields that apply to you.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or ask for guidance.
