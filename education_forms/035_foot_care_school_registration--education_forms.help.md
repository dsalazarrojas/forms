# Foot Care School Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students for a foot care school course. It is designed to collect information from students to facilitate course administration, communication, and record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type in your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the date you plan to attend the course in the "Course Date" field.
6. Choose the time you plan to attend the course in the "Course Time" field.
7. Enter the number of hours you plan to attend the course in the "Course Duration" field.
8. Select the topic of the foot care course you are interested in from the "Course Topic" field.
9. Choose the status of your course registration from the "Course Status" field (Active, Cancelled, or Completed).
10. If you have any additional comments or information, please enter it in the "Additional Comments" field.
11. Finally, select your student details from the "Student Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Your phone number.
* **Course Date** (5, date, required/optional): The date you plan to attend the course.
* **Course Time** (6, time, required/optional): The time you plan to attend the course.
* **Course Duration** (7, number, required/optional): The number of hours you plan to attend the course.
* **Course Topic** (8, text, required/optional): The topic of the foot care course you are interested in.
* **Student Details** (9, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Student A
	+ Student B
	+ Student C
* **Course Status** (10, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Active
	+ Cancelled
	+ Completed
* **Additional Comments** (11, text, required/optional): Any additional comments or information you have.
