# Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience in a particular course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the course name to identify the course you are evaluating.
2. Enter your student name, if required.
3. Select the semester you took the course.
4. Provide your rating of the course, if applicable.
5. Add any comments about your experience.
6. Enter your email address and phone number, if applicable.
7. Select any course experiences from the provided options.
8. Identify areas where the course could be improved.
9. Finally, provide an overall rating of the course, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name (1)**: Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
* **Student Name (2)**: Enter your student name, if required.
* **Semester (3)**: Select the semester you took the course from the options: Spring, Fall, or Summer.
* **Rating (4)**: Provide a numerical rating of your experience, if applicable.
* **Comments (5)**: Add any comments or feedback about your experience with the course.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted with the results.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted with the results.
* **Course Experience (8)**: Select how you experienced the course from the provided options: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Course Improvement (9)**: Identify areas where the course could be improved from the provided options: Quality of instruction, Content quality, Organization, or Other.
* **Overall (10)**: Provide an overall rating of your experience, if applicable.
