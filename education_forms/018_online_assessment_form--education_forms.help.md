# Online Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Assessment Form is a tool to gather information from users about their online assessment experience. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter a brief description of your question in the **What is your question?** field. This will help us understand what you are trying to assess or learn.
2. Enter your assessment score out of the given scale or criteria in the **Assessment Score** field. You can enter a numerical value.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the **Comments** field. This will help us understand your thoughts and concerns.
4. Select whether you were **Yes** or **No** for the **Assigned User** field. This field might indicate whether you were assigned to complete this form by someone else or not.
5. Enter the name of the user who assigned you to complete this form in the **User Name** field. This will help us identify who instructed you to complete this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your question?**: Enter a brief description of your question or topic you are trying to assess or learn.
* **Assessment Score**: Enter your assessment score out of the given scale or criteria. You can enter a numerical value.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience. This can include your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date you completed this form. This will help us track completion dates.
* **Assessment Time**: Enter the time you completed this form. This will help us track completion times.
* **Assigned User**: Select **Yes** if you were assigned to complete this form by someone else, or **No** if you initiated this form on your own.
* **User Name**: Enter the name of the user who assigned you to complete this form, if applicable.
