# Public School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for public school enrollment purposes. Please fill out this form to provide your child's details and confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start at the beginning of the form and work your way through each section, filling out all required fields.
2. Provide general information about yourself and your child (page 1).
3. Enter your contact and emergency contact information (page 2).
4. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the school (pages 3-5).
5. Enter student information, including name, date of birth, and any relevant health information (page 6).
6. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions (pages 7-10).
7. Review and confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions again (pages 11-15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email**: Provide your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.

### Parent/Guardian Information (Page 2)
* **Name**: Enter the name of the person filling out this form.
* **Relationship**: Check the box that best describes your relationship to the student (e.g., Parent, Guardian, Grandparent, etc.).
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information (email, phone number, etc.).

### Agreement Details (Pages 3-5)
* **Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the school. You must agree to the terms and conditions to complete the enrollment process.

### Student Information (Page 6)
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Health Information**: Enter any relevant health information (e.g., medical conditions, allergies, etc.).

### Confirmation of Terms and Agreements (Pages 7-10, 11-15)
* **Agree**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the school.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school administration.
