# Student Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request student records for academic or administrative purposes within the educational institution.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permission to request student records.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official documents.
- **Exam Results** (`exam_results`, text, optional): Provide any relevant exam results or scores for the student.
- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): Enter the student's current or previous grade level.
- **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student's teacher or instructor.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student's school or institution.
- **School Year** (`school_year`, text, optional): Enter the academic year the student is enrolled in.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's valid email address.
- **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
- **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary permission to access and share student information.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or support staff.
