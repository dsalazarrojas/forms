# Advanced Course Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from referers to recommend candidates for advanced courses. The completed form will be used to support the candidate's enrollment in the course.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you are a legitimate referrer and that the candidate is a real person.
- Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Name** (`referrer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation or ID.
* **Professional Role and Department** (`professional_role_and_department`, text, required): Enter your professional role and department.
* **Referrer Email** (`referrer_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address as listed on your university or work documentation.
* **Candidate Full Name** (`candidate_full_name`, text, required): Enter the candidate's name as it appears on their ID or official documentation.
* **Student ID or Current Position** (`student_id_or_current_position`, text, required): Enter the candidate's student ID or current position.
* **Recommended Advanced Course Title** (`recommended_advanced_course_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the advanced course you are referring the candidate for.
* **Term for Referral** (`term_for_referral`, select_one, required): Select the term when you are expecting the candidate to start the course (Fall, Spring, or Summer).
* **Candidates Academic Strengths** (`candidates_academic_strengths`, text, required): Explain why this candidate is a good fit for the course.
* **Strength of Recommendation** (`strength_of_recommendation`, select_one, required): Select the level of recommendation (Highly Recommended, Recommended, or Recommended with Reservations).
* **Digital Signature of Referrer** (`digital_signature_of_referrer`, text, required): Type your full name as it appears on your official documentation or ID.
* **Date of Referral** (`date_of_referral`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this referral.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed before submitting the form.
