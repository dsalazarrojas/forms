# Education Equity Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Equity Fellowship Application Form is a collection of questions designed to gather information about your background, education, and interests to help you qualify for the Education Equity Fellowship. This form will help us understand your motivation, skills, and availability for the fellowship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each field carefully and accurately, using the format instructed for each field type (e.g., text, email, date, select multiple, etc.).
2. Make sure to review each field's hint for any additional guidance or examples.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or contact the fellowship administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Equity Fellowship Application Form 1**: A brief description of your motivation and interests for the fellowship. (Text, 1 line)
* **Applicant Name**: Your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, driver's license). (Text, 1 line)
* **Applicant Contact Information**: Your address, phone number, and any other contact information we can use to reach you. (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Applicant Email**: Your email address associated with your academic or professional accounts (Email, 1 line)
* **Applicant Phone**: Your phone number where we can reach you (Text, 1 line)
* **Educational Institution**: Your current or recent institution where you are studying or working. (Text, 1 line)
* **Program of Study**: The name of the program or field you are pursuing or have pursued. (Text, 1 line)
* **Fellowship Program**: Choose the type of fellowship you are interested in (Select Multiple, 1-2 lines)
	+ Equity Fellow
	+ Program Assistant
	+ Other
	+ None of the above
* **Application Date**: The date you are submitting your application (Date, 1 line)
* **Expected Graduation Date**: The date you expect to graduate from your current program (Date, 1 line)
* **Current Institutional Role**: A description of your role in your current or recent institution (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Previous Experience**: A description of your previous experience (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Educational Background**: A summary of your educational background (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Areas of Interest**: A list of topics or areas you are interested in (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Languages Spoken**: The languages you are proficient in (Select Multiple, 1-2 lines)
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ Other
	+ None of the above
* **Work Experience**: A description of your work experience (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Skills**: A list of skills you possess (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Motivation**: A statement of your motivation for applying for the fellowship (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Commitment**: A statement of your commitment to the fellowship's goals and objectives (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Availability**: Your availability for the fellowship (Select One, 1 line)
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Flexible
	+ None of the above
* **Salary Range**: The salary you are expecting for the fellowship (Number, 1 line)
* **Salary Frequency**: A description of your salary expectations (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Salary Frequency Details**: Any further details about your salary expectations (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Other Details**: Any other information you would like to provide (Text, 1-2 lines)
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your completed application form (Text, 1 line)
