## Tips

*   **Teachers Name**: 
    *   This field is for the teacher to enter their name, which will be used to identify them in the feedback.
*   **Teachers Title**: 
    *   This field is for the teacher to enter their title or position in the school.
*   **Teacher Feedback**: 
    *   This is where the teacher can provide feedback about the student's social skills, such as their behavior, communication, and interaction with peers and instructors.
*   **Teachers Suggestions**: 
    *   This field is for the teacher to suggest ways the student can improve their social skills.
*   **Staffs Name**: 
    *   This field is for the staff member to enter their name.
*   **Staff Feedback**: 
    *   This is where the staff member can provide feedback about the student's social skills, similar to the teacher's feedback.
*   **Staffs Suggestions**: 
    *   This field is for the staff member to suggest ways the student can improve their social skills.
*   **Class Name**: 
    *   This field is for the teacher to enter the class name or group the student is in.
*   **Class Level**: 
    *   This field is for the teacher to enter the class level or grade of the student.
