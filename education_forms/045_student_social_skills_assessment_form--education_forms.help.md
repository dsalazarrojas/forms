# Student Social Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from teachers and staff members about students' social skills, including their behavior, communication, and interaction with peers and instructors. It's meant to be a helpful tool for teachers to assess students' progress and provide guidance for development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the teacher's name and title in the respective fields.
2.  Provide feedback about the student's social skills, focusing on their strengths and areas for improvement.
3.  Offer suggestions for the student to work on their social skills.
4.  Enter the staff member's name who is providing feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teachers Name**: This field is for the teacher to enter their name, which will be used to identify them in the feedback.
    *   **Teachers Title**: This field is for the teacher to enter their title or position in the school.
    *   **Teacher Feedback**: This is where the teacher can provide feedback about the student's social skills, such as their behavior, communication, and interaction with peers and instructors.
    *   **Teachers Suggestions**: This field is for the teacher to suggest ways the student can improve their social skills.
    *   **Staffs Name**: This field is for the staff member to enter their name.
    *   **Staff Feedback**: This is where the staff member can provide feedback about the student's social skills, similar to the teacher's feedback.
    *   **Staffs Suggestions**: This field is for the staff member to suggest ways the student can improve their social skills.
    *   **Class Name**: This field is for the teacher to enter the class name or group the student is in.
    *   **Class Level**: This field is for the teacher to enter the class level or grade of the student.
