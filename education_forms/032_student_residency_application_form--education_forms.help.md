# Student Residency Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Residency Application Form is designed to collect information about students applying for residency at an educational institution or facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your student information, including your occupation, education background, contact details, and demographic information.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately, as the information you provide will be used to assess your eligibility for residency.
3. If you are unable to complete a question, do not leave it blank. Instead, provide any relevant information in the "Student Type Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Student Occupation**
* **Student Occupation**: (`student_occupation`, text, optional)
	+ This is a text field where you can enter your current student occupation.
### **2. Student Name**
* **Student Name**: (`student_name`, text, optional)
	+ This is a text field where you can enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
### **3. Education Background**
* **Education Background**: (`education_background`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all the relevant education backgrounds that apply to you.
	+ Options: Yes, No
