# CLE Credit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
Continuing Legal Education (CLE) credit is a process to help lawyers stay updated on their professional development and improve their skills and knowledge. This form is used to submit an application for CLE credit hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, the applicant needs to provide accurate and complete information to the best of their ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Applicant Name** (Applicant name, text, required): The applicant's name should match the name used for the course or event. This will be displayed on the certificate.
• **Bar Number** (Bar number, text, optional): If you have a bar number (a number assigned to you by your law association), enter it here to help verify your identity.
• **Email Address** (Email address, email, required): Enter your email address to receive confirmation of your application and any further communications.
• **Course Title** (Course title, text, required): Enter the title of the CLE course or program you attended.
• **Date of Course** (Course date, date, required): Enter the date you completed the CLE course or program.
• **Credit Hours Requested** (Credit hours requested, number, required): Enter the number of hours you would like to receive for the CLE credit.
• **Supporting Document Upload** (Supporting document upload, text, optional): If you have documentation to support your CLE credit, such as attendance records or certificates, upload it here. This helps the administrator verify your credit.
• **Approval Status** (Approval status, select_one, optional): This field is for internal use by administrators only.
• **Administrator Notes** (Administrator notes, text, optional): This field is for internal notes by the administrator and should not be filled out.
