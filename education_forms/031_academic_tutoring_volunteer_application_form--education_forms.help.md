# Academic Tutoring Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants who want to become volunteer tutors, providing support to their peers in academic areas they are qualified to tutor.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details.
2.  Choose your current status as an undergraduate student, graduate student, faculty member, alumnus, or community member.
3.  Select the subjects you are qualified to tutor, based on your academic background and expertise.
4.  Indicate your level of tutoring experience, if any.
5.  Specify your availability for tutoring sessions, including the times of the day and week.
6.  Choose the number of hours you can commit to tutoring per week.
7.  Select your preferred format for tutoring sessions, whether in-person, online, or both.
8.  Briefly explain why you want to volunteer as a tutor.
9.  Confirm that you are willing to undergo a background check, if required.
10.  Finally, indicate your agreement to follow the tutoring program's policies and maintain professional conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name**: Enter your first name. This field is mandatory to ensure we have accurate identification.
*   **Last name**: Enter your last name. This field is also mandatory for the same reason.
*   **Email address**: Enter your email address. This field is required to allow us to contact you.
*   **Phone number**: Enter your phone number. This field is required for scheduling and other important communications.
*   **Student ID number**: If you are a student, enter your student ID number if you have one.
*   **Current status**: Select your current status as a student, faculty member, or alumnus.
*   **Major or field of expertise**: Enter the subject area where you have expertise to tutor others.
*   **GPA**: If you are a student, report your current GPA, which is optional but helps us assess your qualifications.
*   **Subjects to tutor**: Select all the subjects where you are qualified to provide tutoring support.
*   **Tutoring experience**: Indicate your level of experience, if any, in tutoring or teaching.
*   **Experience details**: Briefly describe your relevant experience, if any.
*   **Availability**: Select all the times of the day and week when you are available for tutoring sessions.
*   **Hours per week**: Specify the number of hours you can commit to tutoring per week.
*   **Preferred format**: Choose the format you prefer for tutoring sessions, whether in-person, online, or both.
*   **Reason for volunteering**: Explain why you want to volunteer as a tutor.
*   **Background check consent**: Confirm that you are willing to undergo a background check, if required.
*   **Agreement**: Confirm that you will follow the tutoring program's policies and conduct yourself professionally.

**Tips**

*   Please be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, ask our team for guidance.
*   We appreciate your time and effort to complete the form thoroughly.
