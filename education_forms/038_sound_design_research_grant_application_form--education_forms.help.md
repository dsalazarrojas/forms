# Sound Design Research Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound Design Research Grant Application Form is a document used to provide essential information for applicants who are applying for a sound design research grant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your grant application details on Page 1 of the form.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name on the "Applicant Name" field.
3. Provide your email address on the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number on the "Phone" field.
5. Describe your project on the "Project Title" field.
6. Estimate the project's budget on the "Project Budget" field (use a numerical value).
7. Specify the duration of your project on the "Project Duration" field (use a time format, e.g., hours, days, months).
8. Upload your first supporting document on the "Supporting Document 1" field.
9. Upload your second supporting document on the "Supporting Document 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Grant Application**: Enter a brief description of your grant application on this page.
  - This field is used to provide the main details of your project.
- **Applicant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - This field is required to identify you as the applicant.
- **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted regarding your application.
  - This field is used to contact you regarding your application.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached regarding your application.
  - This field is used to contact you regarding your application.
- **Project Title**: Enter a descriptive title for your project.
  - This field is used to describe your project in brief.
- **Project Budget**: Enter an estimated budget for your project.
  - This field is used to estimate the project cost.
- **Project Duration**: Enter the duration of your project in time format.
  - This field is used to estimate the project timeline.
- **Supporting Document 1**: Upload a supporting document to provide further details about your project.
  - This field is used to provide additional information about your project.
- **Supporting Document 2**: Upload a second supporting document to provide further details about your project.
  - This field is used to provide additional information about your project.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
- Make sure to attach all required supporting documents.
- Double-check your details before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
