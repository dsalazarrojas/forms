# Educational Aid Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Aid Application Form is designed to collect information from students who are seeking financial assistance to support their educational needs. This form helps the school's aid office assess each student's eligibility for aid and allocate resources accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and email address.
2. Fill in your contact information (Phone, Address, Country, State, City, and Zip).
3. Provide your School Name and School Address.
4. Enter your Student ID, Date of Birth, and Date of Submission.
5. Specify your Grade Level, School Year, and Student Type.
6. Select your School Status (Active or Inactive).
7. Indicate your Student Status (Active or Inactive).
8. Specify your Aid Request and Reason for Aid Request.
9. Enter the Aid Amount you are seeking.
10. Indicate the source of your Aid Funding (e.g. 'Yes' for government assistance, 'No' for other sources).
11. If you are seeking aid from an other source, provide additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or government records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or government records.
* **Email**: Enter your active email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your home or school address.
* **Country**: Select 'Yes' if you are a citizen of your country of residence, 'No' otherwise.
* **State**: Select all the states in which you reside or have lived in.
* **City**: Enter the city in which you currently reside.
* **Zip**: Enter your postal code.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your current or intended school.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of your school.
* **Student ID**: Enter your unique student ID number (if available).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY'.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date when you submitted this form.
* **School Year**: Select 'Yes' if you are currently in school, 'No' otherwise.
* **Student Type**: Select 'Yes' if you are an active student, 'No' otherwise.
* **Student Status**: Select 'Active' if you have been enrolled in the last 6 months, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **School Status**: Select 'Active' if your school is currently operating, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Aid Request**: Briefly describe the aid you are seeking.
* **Reason for Aid Request**: Explain why you are requesting aid.
* **Aid Amount**: Enter the amount of aid you are requesting.
* **Aid Funding Source**: Select 'Yes' if you are seeking aid from a government source, 'No' otherwise.
* **Other**: Select 'Yes' if you are seeking aid from an other source, and provide details in the field below.
* **Other Aid Funding Source Details**: If you selected 'Yes' for 'Aid Funding Source' and 'Other', provide additional information about your aid funding source.
