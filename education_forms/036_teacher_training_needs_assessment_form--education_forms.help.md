# Teacher Training Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Training Needs Assessment Form is designed to help teachers identify their training needs and provide valuable insights to administrators and trainers to create targeted support programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. This will ensure that the training needs are captured correctly.
2. **Provide your teacher name** in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. **Enter your school's name** in the "School Name" field.
4. **Enter your school's email address** in the "School Email" field, if applicable.
5. **Enter your school's address** in the "School Address" field, if applicable.
6. **Select all the training needs that you require** from the list in the "Training Needs" field. You can select multiple options by holding the Ctrl key (or Command key on Mac) and clicking on each option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional)
Your name as a teacher.

### **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional)
Your school's name where you work.

### **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional)
Your school's email address, if applicable.

### **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional)
Your school's address, if applicable.

### **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the training needs that you require:
* **Basic Computer Skills**: Basic computer skills training, such as Microsoft Office or Google Suite.
* **Communication and Interpersonal Skills**: Communication and interpersonal skills, such as conflict resolution or team building.
* **Classroom Management**: Classroom management, such as discipline or classroom organization.
* **Curriculum Design**: Curriculum design, such as developing a new curriculum or instructional materials.
* **Data Analysis**: Data analysis, such as statistical analysis or data visualization.
* **Leadership and Mentoring**: Leadership and mentoring, such as leading a team or mentoring new teachers.
* **Lesson Planning**: Lesson planning, such as creating engaging lesson plans or differentiated instruction.
* **Multicultural and Diversity Skills**: Multicultural and diversity skills, such as cultural awareness or diversity training.
* **Technology Integration**: Technology integration, such as integrating technology into the classroom or learning management systems.
* **Test Development and Evaluation**: Test development and evaluation, such as creating and administering tests or assessments.
* **Time Management**: Time management, such as prioritizing tasks or managing time effectively.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers to ensure the effectiveness of the training program.
* Select all the training needs that you genuinely require, as it will help in creating targeted support programs.
* If you are unsure about any of the training needs, you can always consult with your school administrators or trainers for guidance.
