# Teaching Assistant Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and opinions from users about their experience with the teaching assistant. This information helps us understand how the teaching assistant can be improved to better serve the users in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Fill in the required fields with your first and last name.
* If you have an email address or a contact number where we can reach you, please provide it.
* Answer the questions about your experience with the teaching assistant, including your role in the course, how long you worked with the assistant, and what it improved or did not improve for you.
* Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the teaching assistant.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for the questions about your experience with the teaching assistant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further information.
* **Role In Course**: Check "Yes" if you had a role in the course, and "No" if not.
* **How Many Courses Teaching Assistant Worked with**: If you have worked with the teaching assistant in multiple courses, enter the number.
* **How Long Teaching Assistant Worked**: If you have worked with the teaching assistant for a certain amount of time, enter the time duration (e.g. 1-2 weeks, 1-2 months, etc.).
* **How Satisfying Was Your Experience**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Will Recommend Teaching Assistant**: Check "Yes" if you would recommend the teaching assistant, and "No" if not.
* **How Did Teaching Assistant Help**: Check "Yes" for the areas where the teaching assistant helped you, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Teaching Assistant Improved**: Check "Yes" for the areas where the teaching assistant improved, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Teaching Assistant Did Not Improve**: Check "Yes" for the areas where the teaching assistant did not improve, and "No" otherwise.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the teaching assistant.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number if you would like to be contacted for further information.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further information.
* **Contact Department**: Enter the department that you are affiliated with.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool that was assigned to you.
* **Created By**: Enter the person who created the form (if known).
* **Updated By**: Enter the person who last updated the form (if known).
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
