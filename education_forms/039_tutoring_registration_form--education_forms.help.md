# Tutoring Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutoring Registration Form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in seeking tutoring services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not already submitted this form.
2. Read through each field explanation to understand what information is being requested.
3. Enter your information accurately and completely.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This field is not required, but it is recommended that you enter a unique identifier for your registration. This can be any number, but it will help us to track your form submission more easily.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name, as you would like to be addressed.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Please select how you would like to be contacted. You can choose between:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Learning Needs** (`learning_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_one, optional): Please select your level of expertise:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Subject** (`subject`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
- Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- If you have already registered and need to update your information, please contact us.
