# HBCU Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HBCU Initiative Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants about their roles, interests, and contact details. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your registration is successful and to help us tailor our initiative to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details, including your name and any additional information you'd like to share.
2. Choose your roles related to the HBCU Initiative.
3. List up to three interest statements about your initiative.
4. Enter your participant's interest statements, if applicable.
5. Select the date and time you would like to participate.
6. Enter your email address and phone number.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.
8. Provide any additional participant details you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (1)**: Enter your name and any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Roles**: Choose one or multiple roles related to the HBCU Initiative (e.g., "HBCU Staff", "HBCU Student", "Other" - specify).
* **Interest Statements**: List up to three interest statements about your initiative.
* **Participant Interest Statements**: Enter your participant's interest statements, if applicable.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to participate in the format mm/dd/yy (e.g., 01/15/2024).
* **Time**: Choose the time you would like to participate in the format HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 02:30 PM).
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format XXX-XXXXX@YYYYY (e.g., johndoe@example.com).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **Participant Details 2**: Enter any additional participant details you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your participation in the HBCU Initiative and look forward to connecting with you!
