# Number Identification Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess students' understanding of numbers and their properties in a systematic way.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **User Input** (1): A text field where users can enter any relevant information or comments.
2. Fill in the **Number Range** (2) field with a range of numbers as required or desired.
3. In the **Start Value** (3) field, enter the starting number of the selected range.
4. In the **End Value** (4) field, enter the ending number of the selected range.
5. If necessary, select the **Number ID** (5) field with a corresponding number range from the options provided.
6. For the **Correct Answer** (6) field, select all correct answers from the provided options.
7. Enter a score in the **Score** (7) field, as required.
8. In the **Note** (8) field, add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
9. If you wish to add a contact method, fill in the **Email** (9) field with a valid email address.
10. Optionally, add your **Phone** (10) number for further contact.
11. Provide the date you completed the form in the **Date** (11) field.
12. Enter the time you completed the form in the **Time** (12) field.
13. If you have any further comments or notes, add them in the **Note 2** (13) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (1)** (`User Input`, `text`, required): A field where users can enter any relevant information or comments.
* **Number Range (2)** (`Number Range`, `number`, optional): A field where you can enter a range of numbers as required or desired.
* **Start Value (3)** (`Start Value`, `text`, required): Enter the starting number of the selected range.
* **End Value (4)** (`End Value`, `text`, required): Enter the ending number of the selected range.
* **Number ID (5)** (`Number ID`, `select_one`, optional): Select a number range from the provided options.
* **Correct Answer (6)** (`Correct Answer`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all correct answers from the provided options.
* **Score (7)** (`Score`, `number`, required): Enter a score as required.
* **Note (8)** (`Note`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
* **Email (9)** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for further contact.
* **Phone (10)** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Add your phone number for further contact.
* **Date (11)** (`Date`, `date`, required): Provide the date you completed the form.
* **Time (12)** (`Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Note 2 (13)** (`Note 2`, `note`, optional): Add any further comments or notes.
