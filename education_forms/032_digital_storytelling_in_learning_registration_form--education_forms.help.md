# digital_storytelling_in_learning_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant details and preferences for a learning or digital storytelling experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details on page 1.
2. Answer the background information questions on page 2.
3. Select your preferred learning channels on page 3.
4. Choose your preferred content formats on page 4.
5. Select your preferred topics on page 5.
6. Optionally, add any background details on page 6.
7. Choose your participant preferences on page 7.
8. Finally, select your preferred delivery channels on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (1, Participant Details, Required): Enter your name, email, and other relevant details to help us understand who you are.
- **Participant Background** (2, participant_background, Optional): Share any background information that will help us understand your context and experiences.
- **Preferred Learning Channels** (3, preferred_learning_channels, Optional): Select all the channels through which you would like to learn (e.g., online, offline, social media).
- **Preferred Content Formats** (4, preferred_content_formats, Optional): Choose the content formats you prefer (e.g., video, audio, text).
- **Preferred Topics** (5, preferred_topics, Optional): Select the topics you are interested in learning more about.
- **Background Details** (6, background_details, Optional): Provide any additional background details that may be helpful for our team to know.
- **Participant Preferences** (7, participant_preferences, Optional): Choose how you would like to be involved in the learning process (e.g., actively, passively).
- **Preferred Delivery Channels** (8, preferred_delivery_channels, Optional): Select the delivery channels you prefer to receive the learning content (e.g., email, social media, in-person).
