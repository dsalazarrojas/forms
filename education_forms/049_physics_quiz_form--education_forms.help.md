# physics_quiz_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge of physics-related concepts. It has six fields that cater to different aspects of physics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field as instructed. The form is divided into different sections, each with its own set of questions.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. The form is not timed, so take your time and complete it at your own pace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_form** (1, start_form, optional): This is the introduction to the form. It is an optional section that provides context to the questions that follow.
* **what_is_physics** (2, what_is_physics, required): This question asks for a brief explanation of what physics is. Answer in a few sentences.
* **what_is_a_mole** (3, what_is_a_mole, optional): This question asks you to choose one or more correct answer(s) from the options provided. Choose one or more that apply.
	+ mass
	+ energy
	+ velocity
* **what_is_einstein** (4, what_is_einstein, required): This question also asks you to choose one or more correct answer(s) from the options provided. Choose one or more that apply.
	+ mass
	+ energy
	+ velocity
* **calculate_result** (5, calculate_result, required): This is a numerical field where you will be asked to perform a calculation. Follow the instructions provided.
* **result** (6, result, optional): This is a field where you can enter your final result. Enter the calculated value here.
* **end_form** (7, end_form, optional): This is the final section. It is an optional note where you can add any additional comments or thoughts.

## Tips
* Be honest in your answers. The form is designed to test your knowledge, not to trick you.
* If you are unsure of any question, take a moment to think about it before proceeding.
* Don't worry if you make mistakes. The form is for your learning and improvement.
