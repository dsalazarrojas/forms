# AI Classroom Integration Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in integrating AI technology into their classrooms or educational institutions. The form aims to gather essential details about the user's role, grade level, subject area, and preferred learning style to provide tailored support and consultation on AI integration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Personal Information** section with your full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Select your **Role** in the educational institution (e.g., Teacher, Administrator, IT Specialist, etc.).
3. Enter your **School or Institution Name** and **District Name** (if applicable).
4. Choose the **Grade Level** you are interested in integrating AI technology for.
5. Select the **Subject Area** you would like to focus on.
6. Indicate your **Preferred Learning Style** (e.g., Visual, Auditory, Reading/Writing, Hands-on/Kinesthetic).
7. Provide an **Approximate Number of Study Hours** per week.
8. Select your **Preferred Contact Method** (e.g., Email, Phone, Video call, In-person).
9. Specify a **Preferred Contact Time** window (if you have a specific time in mind).
10. Choose your **Urgency Level** for the integration process (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Critical).
11. Estimate your **Budget** for the integration process (if you have a rough estimate).
12. Select a **Desired Implementation Start Date** for the AI integration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information

* **Full Name**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address**: We will use this to send confirmation and follow-up emails.
* **Phone Number**: Optional, for urgent contact.
* **Role**: Select the position that best describes you in the educational institution.
* **School or Institution Name**: If you are representing an organization, provide its name.
* **District Name**: If applicable, provide your school district name.

### Request Details

* **Grade Level**: Specify the primary grade or year level you are focusing on.
* **Subject Area**: Choose the main subject for AI integration (e.g., Mathematics, Science, Language Arts, etc.).
* **AI Tools of Interest**: Select all that apply (e.g., Adaptive learning, Chatbots, Personalized feedback, etc.).
* **Preferred Learning Style**: Select the style that best suits your teaching or learning preferences (e.g., Visual, Auditory, Reading/Writing, Hands-on/Kinesthetic).
* **Approximate Study Hours**: Enter the approximate number of hours students spend studying per week.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like us to reach you (e.g., Email, Phone, Video call, In-person).
* **Preferred Contact Time**: Specify a time window for contact (e.g., 10:00–12:00).
* **Urgency Level**: Choose your level of urgency for the integration process (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Critical).
* **Budget Estimate**: Enter a rough estimate of available funds for AI integration (in USD).
* **Desired Implementation Start Date**: Specify when you would like to begin the integration process.

### Challenges

* **Main Challenges**: Select all that apply (e.g., Time management, Difficult material, Lack of resources, etc.).

### Additional Comments

* **Additional Comments or Notes**: Provide any other information that will help us tailor our support to your specific needs.

## Tips

* Please fill out all required fields to ensure we provide personalized support.
* If you are unsure about a field or option, feel free to ask us, and we will guide you through the process.
* We will use the information provided to tailor our support and consultation to your specific needs.
