# Electronics Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students for an electronics course. It is used to validate the student's identity, course selection, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the student's details, including name and contact information.
2. Select the courses you would like to register for.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Confirm your student name, email, and phone number.
5. Confirm your payment method and amount.
6. Review and submit the form for final verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details (1)**: Enter your name and contact information in this field.
* **Payment Details (2)**: Enter your payment method and amount.
* **Course Choice (3)**: Select the courses you would like to register for. Choose from the options: Programming, Web Development, or Data Science.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Student Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Student Name Confirmation (6)**: Confirm your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Student Email Confirmation (7)**: Confirm your email address.
* **Student Phone Confirmation (8)**: Confirm your phone number.
* **Course Selection Confirmation (9)**: Confirm your course selection.
* **Payment Amount (10)**: Enter the amount you are paying.
* **Payment Method Confirmation (11)**: Confirm your payment method.
* **Student Email 2 (12)**: Enter your secondary email address (if applicable).
* **Student Name 2 (13)**: Enter your secondary name (if applicable).
* **Student Phone 2 (14)**: Enter your secondary phone number (if applicable).
* **Payment Status (15)**: Select the status of your payment (Active or Inactive).
* **Payment Method 2 (16)**: Confirm your secondary payment method (if applicable).

Note: Fields 12, 13, 14 are optional and only required if you have secondary contact information.
