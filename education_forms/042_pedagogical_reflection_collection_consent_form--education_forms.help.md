Pedagogical Reflection Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pedagogical Reflection Collection Consent Form is used to obtain consent from teachers, students, and parents for the collection of pedagogical reflections. This form helps to ensure that all relevant parties have given their consent before the collection process can begin.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Teacher**: Provide your teacher's name.
	* **Email**: Enter your school or institution's email address (in the format student_email@example.com).
	* **Date of Collection**: Select the date when the pedagogical reflection collection will take place.
	* **Time of Collection**: Select the time when the pedagogical reflection collection will take place.
	* **Collection Frequency 2**: Choose the frequency of collection (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
	* **Collection Duration 2**: Enter the duration of the collection (optional).
	* **Consent**: Select "I agree" to give your consent for the collection.
	* **Consent Date**: Select the date when you gave your consent.
	* **Consent Time**: Select the time when you gave your consent.
	* **Review Date**: Select the date when you reviewed the collected data (optional).
	* **Review Time**: Select the time when you reviewed the collected data (optional).
2. Review and fill in any additional fields as necessary:
	* **Student Reflection**: You can add any comments or reflections about your student's pedagogical process.
	* **Teacher Reflection**: You can add any comments or reflections about your teaching process.
	* **Parent Reflection**: You can add any comments or reflections about your child's learning process.
	* **Comments**: You can add any additional comments about the collection process.
	* **Collection Frequency**: Choose the frequency of collection (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
	* **Collection Duration**: Enter the duration of the collection (optional).
	* **Collection Methods**: Select the methods used for collection (e.g., Interviews, Surveys, Observations).
