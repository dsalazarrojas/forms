# session_submission_form_undergraduate_research_forum_2015 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for undergraduate research project submissions. It gathers information about who submitted the project, where it was submitted from, and any additional notes or contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have all the necessary information required for the submission, including your name, contact information, and any relevant details about your research project.
2.  Fill in the required fields, which include the submitter's name, email address, and notes sections.
3.  Select the correct options for the submitted from field, which might indicate where the submission was made from.
4.  Choose the correct submitter's email address.
5.  Fill in any additional information in the notes section if necessary.
6.  Ensure that you have selected the correct options for the submitter and submitted from fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Submission Page** (`session_submission_page`, text, required/optional): This field is not required but will be used to provide additional context about the submission.
* **Undergraduate Research Forum** (`undergraduate_research_forum`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide context about the research forum.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required/optional): Please fill in the name of the individual or team submitting the project.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, required/optional): This field is used to track the date and time of the submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you are the primary submitter, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Submitted From** (`submitted_from`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if the submission was made from a specific location, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Use this field to provide additional comments or details about the submission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Fill in your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please provide your phone number.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required/optional): Fill in the submitter's email address.
