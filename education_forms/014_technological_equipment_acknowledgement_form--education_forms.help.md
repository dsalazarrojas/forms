# Technological Equipment Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technological Equipment Acknowledgement Form is used to record and document information about technological equipment in a school setting. It helps track the status, delivery date and time, and any remarks or notes about the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the form to identify the equipment being acknowledged.
2. Enter the school ID, school name, and technician ID in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the type of equipment from the options provided.
4. Choose the status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
5. Enter any remarks or notes about the equipment.
6. Provide the delivery date and time in the specified format.
7. Enter any delivery notes, if applicable.
8. Add any additional notes about the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technological Equipment Acknowledgement Form (form_title)** (`text`, not required): This is the title of the form that identifies the equipment being acknowledged.
* **School ID (school_id)** (`text`, not required): Enter the ID of the school where the equipment is located.
* **School Name (school_name)** (`text`, not required): Enter the name of the school where the equipment is located.
* **Technician ID (technician_id)** (`text`, not required): Enter the ID of the technician responsible for the equipment.
* **Equipment Type (equipment_type)** (`text`, not required): Select the type of equipment from the provided options.
* **Equipment Status (equipment_status)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
* **Remarks (remarks)** (`text`, not required): Enter any additional remarks or notes about the equipment.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)** (`date`, not required): Enter the date the equipment was delivered.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)** (`time`, not required): Enter the time the equipment was delivered.
* **Delivery Note (delivery_note)** (`note`, not required): Enter any additional delivery notes.
* **Notes (notes)** (`text`, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the equipment.
