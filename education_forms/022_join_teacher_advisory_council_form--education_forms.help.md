# Join Teacher Advisory Council Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from teachers to evaluate their suitability for the Teacher Advisory Council. We are interested in learning about your qualifications, experience, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
2. Please ensure that you are a qualified teacher and have not previously submitted a similar application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Application** (`teacher_application`, text, optional): A brief introduction about your application and why you want to join the council.
* **Teacher Information** (`teacher_information`, text, optional): Provide your full name, email address, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, optional): List your educational qualifications, including degrees and institutions attended.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your teaching experience, including the name of the school or institution, role, and duration of employment.
* **Taching Certifications** (`teaching_certifications`, text, optional): List any relevant teaching certifications or training you have received.
* **Teacher Specialty** (`teacher_specialty`, text, optional): Specify your area of specialization, e.g., mathematics, science, etc.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your email address, phone number, and any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Any other relevant information about yourself or your qualifications that you feel is important to mention.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
