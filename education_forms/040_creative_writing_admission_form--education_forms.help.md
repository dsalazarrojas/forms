# Creative Writing Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Writing Admission Form is a document used to submit creative writing projects for admission to a particular program or organization. It is intended for individuals who wish to share their original creative writing work with the world.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have a clear understanding of the purpose and requirements of the form.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing your name, email address, and any other required information.
* Be clear and concise in your statement of purpose, explaining why you are submitting your creative writing project for admission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This field is for your personal name, which will be used to identify you as the author of the creative writing project. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This field is for your last name, which will be used to identify you as the author of the creative writing project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for your email address, which will be used to communicate with you about your submission. Please ensure your email is active and accessible.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number, which will be used to contact you about your submission. If you don't provide a phone number, please ensure your email address is active and accessible.
* **Statement of Purpose** (`statement_of_purpose`, text, optional): This field is for you to explain why you are submitting your creative writing project for admission. Be clear and concise in your explanation, and ensure it accurately represents your goals and motivations.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use a valid email address and phone number if applicable.
* Double-check your statement of purpose for clarity and accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department or organization.
