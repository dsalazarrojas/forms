# Online Learning Reflection Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Online Learning Reflection Story Consent Form is designed to collect information from users who are sharing their experiences and reflections on online learning. This form is intended to gather demographic information, details about the reflection, and permission for submission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your demographic information, such as your name and contact details.
2. Describe your reflection story, including the online learning experience you want to share.
3. Choose who has permission to view your submission: Parent/Guardian, Teacher, or Other (e.g., Researcher).
4. Provide any additional contact information if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and other relevant contact details.
* **Reflection Information** (`reflection_info`, text, required/optional): Describe your reflection story, including the online learning experience you want to share.
* **Submission Permission** (`submission_permission`, select_multiple, required): Choose who has permission to view your submission:
	+ Parent/Guardian: A parent or guardian who has authority over you.
	+ Teacher: A teacher or educational institution representative.
	+ Other: Anyone else who you give permission to.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): If required, provide any additional contact information to help us reach you.
