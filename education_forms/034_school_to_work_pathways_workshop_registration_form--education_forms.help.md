# School To Work Pathways Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the School To Work Pathways Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
* Select your school grade level from the "School Grade Level" dropdown menu.
* If you know the date of the workshop, enter it in the "Workshop Date" field. If not, leave it blank.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
* Select your assigned teacher from the "Assigned Teacher" dropdown menu.
* Leave the "Notes" field blank if you have no additional information to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (Student Name, text, required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your school records.
* **School Grade Level (School Grade Level, select_one, required)**: Select your current grade level in school.
* **Workshop Date (Workshop Date, date, optional)**: If you know the date of the workshop, enter it here. If not, leave it blank.
* **Email (Email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Assigned Teacher (Assigned Teacher, select_one, optional)**: Select your assigned teacher for the workshop.
* **Notes (Notes, note, optional)**: Leave this field blank if you have no additional information to add.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that we can contact you and keep you up to date with workshop information.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your assigned teacher or the workshop organizers.
