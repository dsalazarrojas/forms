# Teaching Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teaching Job Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals who are applying for a teaching position. This form will help our institution to assess your qualifications and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your first and last name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your current or previous employment status, including your employment status and the name of the organisation you are employed by.
3. If employed, enter the date you started your current job and the name of your department.
4. If not employed, enter your job position level and department you are interested in.
5. Ensure all required fields are filled in, and click submit to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **First Name** (`contact_information_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`contact_information_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`contact_information_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`contact_information_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.

### Employment Status
* **Employment Status** (`education_employment_status`, text, optional): Select your current employment status.
* **Employed By** (`education_employment_status_employed_organisation`, text, optional): If employed, enter the name of your current or previous employer.
* **Employed Date** (`education_employment_status_employed_date`, date, optional): If employed, enter the date you started your current or previous job.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Job Position Level** (`job_position_level`, text, optional): Enter your job level or rank.
* **Department** (`job_position_department`, text, optional): Enter the department you are working in.
* **Employment Status (2)** (`employment_status`, text, optional): Select your employment status (this field is redundant and will not be used for further processing).
* **Employment Status Employed Organisation** (`employment_status_employed_organisation`, text, optional): If employed, enter the name of your current or previous employer (this field is redundant and will not be used for further processing).
* **Employed Date (2)** (`employment_status_employed_date`, date, optional): If employed, enter the date you started your current or previous job (this field is redundant and will not be used for further processing).

### Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in, and click submit to submit your application.
* If you have any issues while filling out the form, please contact our support team.
