# Building Fund Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Fund Pledge Form is designed to collect pledge information for the "Building Fund" in the "Education" category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Donor Full Name** in the "Donor Full Name" field.
2. If applicable, enter your **Spouse Name** in the "Spouse Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your **Mailing Address** in the "Mailing Address" field.
6. Enter your **City** in the "City" field.
7. Enter your **State** in the "State" field.
8. Enter your **ZIP Code** in the "ZIP Code" field.
9. Select the **Fund Campaign** that this pledge is for.
10. Enter the **Total Pledge Amount** for this pledge.
11. Select the **Payment Frequency** for this pledge.
12. Select the **Payment Duration** for this pledge.
13. Enter the **First Payment Date** for this pledge.
14. Select the **Payment Method** for this pledge.
15. If paying by credit card, select the **Credit Card Type**.
16. If you wish to make an **Anonymous Pledge**, select the corresponding option.
17. If this is an **In Honor or Memory of**, enter the relevant information.
18. Select whether your employer will **Match this Gift**.
19. Indicate your interest in **Volunteering**.
20. If you have any **Additional Skills or Services** to offer, enter them here.
21. Select your **Communication Preference**.
22. Select where to send the **Tax Receipt**.
23. Enter any **Additional Comments**.
24. Finally, confirm your **Pledge Acknowledgment**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as the donor.
* **Spouse Name** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter your spouse's name.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Mailing Address** (Text, Required): Enter your full mailing address.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (Text, Required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (Text, Required): Enter your postal code.
* **Pledge Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your pledge.
* **Fund Campaign** (Select One, Required): Select the fund campaign you are pledging to.
* **Total Pledge Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the total amount of your pledge.
* **Payment Frequency** (Select One, Required): Select how you will make your payments.
* **Payment Duration** (Select One, Required): Select the duration of your payments.
* **First Payment Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date when you will start making payments.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Select how you will make your payments.
* **Credit Card Type** (Select One, Optional): If paying by credit card, select the type of card.
* **Anonymous Pledge** (Select One, Required): Select if you wish to remain anonymous.
* **In Honor or Memory Of** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the relevant information.
* **Employer Matching Gift** (Select One, Required): Select if your employer will match your gift.
* **Volunteer Interest** (Select One, Required): Indicate your interest in volunteering.
* **Skills or Services** (Text, Optional): If you have any skills or services to offer, enter them here.
* **Communication Preference** (Select One, Required): Select how you would like to be updated.
* **Tax Receipt** (Select One, Required): Select where to send your tax receipt.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or requests.
* **Pledge Acknowledgment** (Select One, Required): Confirm your pledge acknowledgment.
* **Spouse Name** (Text, Optional): If you have a spouse, enter their name here.
* **First Payment Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date when you will start making payments.
* **Credit Card Type** (Select One, Optional): If paying by credit card, select the type of card.
* **Other** (Text, Optional): If none of the above apply, select "Other" and enter your own label.
