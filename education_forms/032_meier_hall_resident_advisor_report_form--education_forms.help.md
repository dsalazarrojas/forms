# Meier Hall Resident Advisor Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meier Hall Resident Advisor Report Form is designed to gather information about residents of Meier Hall, enabling staff and administrators to better understand their needs and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by carefully reading through the form instructions and understanding the purpose of each field.
- Start by filling out the `goals` page (Page 1), where you will be asked to provide a brief description of the resident's goals.
- Proceed to the `Personal Info` page (Page 2), where you will enter the resident's personal details, such as their name and contact information.
- On the `Date` page (Page 3), you will enter the date for which you are reporting information about the resident.
- The `Type` page (Page 4) asks you to select one of the provided options to categorize the resident.
- On the `Note` page (Page 5), you will have space to provide any additional notes about the resident.
- The `Phone` and `Email` pages (Pages 6-7) are optional, but if you need to contact the resident, please fill out these fields accordingly.
- The `Text` pages (Pages 8-13) are also optional and can be used to provide any other relevant information about the resident.
- Finally, on the `Date 1` and `Date 2` pages (Pages 14-15), you will enter any additional dates relevant to the report.
- The `Type 1` page (Page 16) is another select-one field for categorizing the resident.
- On the `Select 1` and `Select 2` pages (Pages 17-18), you will select options from the provided lists to indicate the resident's status.
