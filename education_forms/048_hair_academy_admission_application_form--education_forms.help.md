# Hair Academy Admission Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hair Academy Admission Application Form is used to gather information from prospective students about their interests, contact details, and other relevant data. This form helps our admissions team review and evaluate applications for admission to our institution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	* Enter your active email address where you can be contacted.
4. **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required: false)
	* Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. **Program of Interest** (`program_of_interest`, select_one, required: false)
	* Select the program you are interested in pursuing at the Hair Academy:
		+ Hair Design
		+ Hair Coloring
		+ Hair Cutting
		+ Hair Extension
		+ Hair Styling
		+ Hair Treatment
6. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your contact number where we can reach you for further communication.
7. **Admission Date** (`admission_date`, date, required: false)
	* Enter your expected admission date or preferred date of admission.
8. **Address** (`applicant_address`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your current address where you can be contacted.
9. **City** (`city`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your city of residence.
10. **State** (`state`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your state/province of residence.
11. **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your postal code/zip code.
12. **Message** (`applicant_message`, text, required: false)
	* Enter any additional information you would like to share with us.
