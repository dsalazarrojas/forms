# Subclavian Anatomy Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge of subclavian anatomy. It consists of two multiple-choice questions that will assess your understanding of the subclavian vessels, nerves, and muscles. The questions will cover the location and identification of these structures.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the "Get Started" button to start the quiz.
2.  For each question, carefully read the text carefully, and choose your answer from the options provided.
3.  Select "Submit" to submit your answers, and review your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **question_1 (Text)** (`question_1`, text, Optional):
    This field will display the first question of the quiz.
*   **answer_1 (Text)** (`answer_1`, text, Optional):
    Enter your answer to the first question.
* **question_2 (Select One)** (`question_2`, select_one, Optional):
    This field asks you to select the correct option for the second question.
*   **answer_2 (Select Multiple)** (`answer_2`, select_multiple, Optional):
    Select all that apply for the correct answer to the second question.

## Tips

*   Make sure to carefully review each question before selecting your answers.
*   Select the "Submit" button to review your results after completing all questions.
