# symmetry_concepts_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate users' understanding of symmetry concepts, including line and rotational symmetry. It is used in educational settings to assess students' knowledge and provide feedback or assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each required field with the requested information.
2. Use the options provided in the "Line Symmetry" and "Rotational Symmetry" fields to select the correct answer.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field, select your grade level from the available options in the "Grade Level" field, and select your subject of study in the "Subject" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can enter them in the "Additional Comments" field. However, this field is not required.
5. Finally, select a date for your submission in the "Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Grade Level** (2, number, required): Select your current grade level from the available options.
* **Subject** (3, text, required): Select the subject you are currently studying.
* **Line Symmetry** (4, select_one, required): Select the type of line symmetry you are familiar with.
	+ Dihedral angle: A type of line symmetry that involves a rotation around a line.
	+ Reflection line: A line that reflects an object or shape.
* **Rotational Symmetry** (5, select_multiple, required): Select the types of rotational symmetry you are familiar with.
	+ Dihedral angle: A type of rotational symmetry involving a dihedral angle.
	+ Reflection axis: An axis around which a shape or object can be rotated to produce symmetry.
	+ None of the above: If you are not familiar with any of the above options.
* **Additional Comments** (6, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have.
* **Submission** (7, date, required): Select a date for your submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer the required fields honestly and accurately.
* Use the options provided to answer the questions, and do not introduce any additional information not requested by the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, do not hesitate to ask for help.
