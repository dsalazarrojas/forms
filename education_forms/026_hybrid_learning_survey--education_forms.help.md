# Hybrid Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Learning Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback and insights from students, teachers, and administrators on the hybrid learning model. The data collected will help the institution understand the strengths and weaknesses of the current model and make informed decisions for its improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Hybrid Learning Survey, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible in the text box.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields (none are marked as required in this form).
4. Click submit to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, `text`, not required): Please share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about your learning experience in the current hybrid learning model.
* **School Customization** (`school_customization`, `text`, not required): If you have ideas or recommendations for customizing the learning experience, please share them in this field.
* **Student Devices** (`student_devices`, `text`, not required): Describe the devices you use for learning, such as laptops, tablets, or smartphones.
* **Student Learning Goals** (`student_learning_goals`, `text`, not required): Share your learning goals, such as improving grades, developing skills, or exploring interests.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, `text`, not required): Share your thoughts on the level of support provided by teachers, such as guidance, feedback, or resources.
* **Hybrid Learning Model** (`hybrid_learning_model`, `text`, not required): Share your insights on the hybrid learning model, such as its strengths, weaknesses, or suggestions for improvement.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you are unsure about an answer, it's okay to leave it blank or note your question.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
