# sva_late_work_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track late work submitted by students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Enter the name of the assignment that was submitted late in the "Assignment Name" field.
4. Describe the reason for the late submission in the "Late Work Description" field.
5. Enter the name of the teacher who assigned the work in the "Teacher Name" field.
6. Enter the due date of the assignment in the "Due Date" field.
7. Enter the date the student received the assignment in the "Received By" field.
8. Enter the date the student submitted the work in the "Submitted On" field.
9. Select the reason for the late submission from the drop-down menu in the "Late Work Reason" field.
10. Select the status of the late work from the drop-down menu in the "Late Work Status" field.
11. Enter any comments or feedback from the teacher in the "Late Work Comments" field (optional).
12. Enter the tool assigned to the student for the assignment in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Enter the name of the student who submitted the work in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the student who submitted the late work.
* **Student ID** (number, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Assignment Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the assignment that was submitted late.
* **Late Work Description** (text, required): Describe the reason for the late submission.
* **Teacher Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the teacher who assigned the work.
* **Due Date** (date, required): Enter the original due date of the assignment.
* **Received By** (text, required): Enter the date the student received the assignment.
* **Submitted On** (date, required): Enter the date the student submitted the work.
* **Late Work Reason** (select_one, required): Select the reason for the late submission from the drop-down menu.
* **Late Work Status** (select_multiple, required): Select the status of the late work from the drop-down menu.
* **Late Work Comments** (text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the teacher.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, required): Enter the tool assigned to the student for the assignment.
* **Submitted By** (text, required): Enter the name of the student who submitted the work.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
* Use the drop-down menus to select the correct reason and status for the late work.
* Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Late Work Comments" field if needed.
