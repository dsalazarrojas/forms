# Youth Ministry Internship Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Ministry Internship Evaluation Form is designed to help supervisors and evaluators evaluate the performance of youth interns and provide feedback for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, paying attention to each field that requires input.
* Review and fill out the relevant fields for the evaluation period.
* Enter your supervisor's name and title if you are a supervisor or evaluator.
* Rate and recommend the intern's performance using the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Internship Details** (`internship_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief summary of the internship experience.
* **Supervisor name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the intern's supervisor.
* **Evaluation Form Date** (`evaluation_form_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Form Time** (`evaluation_form_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Form Location** (`evaluation_form_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Form Comments** (`evaluation_form_comments`, text, optional): Provide comments about the intern's performance.
* **Intern Name** (`intern_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the intern.
* **Supervisor Name 2** (`supervisor_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second supervisor or evaluator.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the second supervisor or evaluator.
* **Evaluation Form Rating** (`evaluation_form_rating`, number, optional): Rate the intern's performance using the provided rating scale.
* **Evaluation Form Recommendation** (`evaluation_form_recommendation`, select_one, optional): Recommend the intern for future opportunities or not.
* **Program Area** (`program_area`, select_one, optional): Select the area of the youth ministry program the intern was involved in.
* **Skills Used** (`skills_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select the skills used by the intern.
* **Areas of Growth** (`areas_of_growth`, text, optional): Provide areas where the intern can grow and improve.
* **Evaluation Form Recommendation Reason** (`evaluation_form_recommendation_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the evaluation form recommendation.
* **Evaluation Form Rating Reason** (`evaluation_form_rating_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the evaluation form rating.
* **Evaluation Form Comments 2** (`evaluation_form_comments_2`, text, optional): Provide additional comments about the intern's performance.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the evaluation form.
* **Supervisor Title 2** (`supervisor_title_2`, text, optional): Enter the title of the second supervisor or evaluator.
* **Evaluation Form Date 2** (`evaluation_form_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the second evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Form Time 2** (`evaluation_form_time_2`, time, optional): Enter the time of the second evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Form Location 2** (`evaluation_form_location_2`, text, optional): Enter the location of the second evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Form Comments 3** (`evaluation_form_comments_3`, text, optional): Provide additional comments about the intern's performance.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, optional): Provide comments from the supervisor or evaluator.
* **Form Complete** (`form_complete`, note, optional): Sign and date the evaluation form completion.
