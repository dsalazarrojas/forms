# Arbor Day Poster Contest School Winner Regional Judge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arbor Day Poster Contest School Winner Regional Judge Form is used to collect information from regional judges to evaluate and rate school winners of the Arbor Day Poster Contest. This form helps the judges to assess the artistic quality, tree representation, message clarity, creativity and originality, and overall impression of each winning entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your judge information: Please provide your full name, email address, and phone number in the fields marked as required.
2. Choose the grade level category for each winning entry.
3. Rate each entry based on the specified criteria (artistic quality, tree representation, message clarity, creativity and originality, and overall impression) using the number rating scale of 1-10.
4. Provide any constructive comments or feedback for each entry.
5. Enter the date of the judgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Judge Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official record.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your email address as it is registered with our organization.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number as it is registered with our organization.
* **Professional Affiliation**: This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **Contest Year**: Select the year of the contest for which you are judging.
* **Regional Division**: Select the geographic region that this entry represents.
* **Grade Level Category**: Please select the grade level category of the entry (K-2, 3-5, 6-8, 9-12).
* **Artistic Quality Rating (1-10)**: Rate the artistic quality of the entry on a scale of 1-10.
* **Tree Representation Rating (1-10)**: Rate how well the entry represents trees on a scale of 1-10.
* **Arbor Day Message Clarity (1-10)**: Rate the clarity of the Arbor Day message on a scale of 1-10.
* **Creativity and Originality (1-10)**: Rate the creativity and originality of the entry on a scale of 1-10.
* **Overall Impression (1-10)**: Rate your overall impression of the entry on a scale of 1-10.
* **Judge Comments and Feedback**: Please provide any constructive comments or feedback for the student.
* **Date of Judgment**: Please enter the date of the judgment.

## Tips
* Please rate each entry based on your honest and unbiased judgment.
* Use the number rating scale for each criteria to provide a clear and consistent evaluation.
* Do not leave any required fields blank.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact the contest organizers.
