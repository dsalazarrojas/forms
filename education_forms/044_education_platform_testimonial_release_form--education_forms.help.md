# Education Platform Testimonial Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who wish to share their experiences with the education platform. It captures essential information about the student, their school, and their testimonial. This form helps us understand your opinions and feedback to improve the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name.
2. Enter the name of your school.
3. Provide a testimonial about your experience with the education platform.
4. If you've uploaded media (e.g., photos, videos), specify the title of the media.
5. If you've uploaded media, specify the date you released it for use by the platform.
6. Confirm that you've released the media for use by the platform (Consent).
7. Specify the type of media you've uploaded (e.g., photo, video).
8. Optionally, provide a reason for releasing the media.
9. Confirm the date you signed the release statement (Signature Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification card or as you prefer to be referred to.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school as it is officially recognized.
* **Testimonial**: Share your honest and constructive feedback on your experience with the education platform. Describe how the platform has helped or hindered your learning.
* **Release Statement**: Please review this statement carefully before signing:
    "I, [Student Name], hereby grant [Education Platform Name] permission to use my testimonial, media, and signature for promotional and educational purposes."
* **Media Upload**: Upload media related to your testimonial, such as photos or videos.
* **Signature**: This is a digital signature, and you'll be required to sign this statement to release the media.
* **Media Title**: If you've uploaded media, provide a title for it.
* **Release Date**: If you've released media, specify the date you released it for use by the platform.
* **Consent**: Confirm that you've released the media for use by the platform. A "Yes" response indicates you've granted permission.
* **Media Type**: Specify the type of media you've uploaded (e.g., photo, video).
* **Release Reason**: Provide an optional reason for releasing the media, such as "I'm proud to share my experience."
* **Signature Date**: Confirm the date you signed the release statement.
* **Media URL**: This field is not applicable as it's not currently used. You can disregard it.
* **Consent**: Confirm that you've released the media for use by the platform.
* **Media Type**: This field is not applicable as it's a redundant field with the "Media Type" field above. You can disregard it.
* **Release Reason**: Provide an optional reason for releasing the media.
* **Signature Date**: Confirm the date you signed the release statement.
