# Version 2 Of Course Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Version 2 Of Course Registration Form" is designed to collect information from students registering for a course. This form captures the student's personal details, course specifics, and relevant dates. The form aims to provide a clear and concise way to register students for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the course.
2. Provide your student name.
3. Enter your student email address.
4. Enter your student phone number.
5. Enter your student address.
6. Select the start date of the course.
7. Select the end date of the course.
8. Enter the duration of the course.
9. Provide a brief description of the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the course you are registering for.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter your student email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your student phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required: false): Enter your student address.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, required: false): Select the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`, date, required: false): Select the end date of the course.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the course in days, weeks, months, or years.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the course you are registering for.

## Tips
Please ensure you complete all the required fields accurately to ensure successful registration.
