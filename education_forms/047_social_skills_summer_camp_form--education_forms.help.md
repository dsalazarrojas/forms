# Social Skills Summer Camp Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about participants and staff members attending a social skills summer camp. It gathers personal details, skills, and any relevant medical or special needs information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant's details, including name, email, phone number, address, and diet requirements.
2. Provide any medical or special needs information, such as allergies or disabilities.
3. Indicate your participant's relationship to the summer camp (e.g., camper, staff member).
4. If you are a staff member, fill in your own details, including name, email, phone number, address, and diet requirements.
5. Provide any medical or special needs information, such as allergies or disabilities.
6. Indicate your relationship to the summer camp (e.g., staff member, director).
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to each skill question to indicate whether your participant has the corresponding skill.
