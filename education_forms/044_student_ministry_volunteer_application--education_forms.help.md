# student_ministry_volunteer_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to apply for volunteer roles within the ministry. It is designed to gather information about the student's qualifications, availability, and preferences for various volunteer roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your preferred volunteer role from the "volunteer_role" dropdown menu.
6. Provide a brief description of your qualifications in the "qualifications" field.
7. Enter the date of your availability in the "availability" field.
8. Select the current status of your application from the "application_status" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the date of submission in the "date_of_application" field.
10. If assigned, enter your assigned tool in the "assigned_tool" field.
11. Enter your assigned user ID in the "assigned_user_id" field.
12. Enter your assigned tool user ID in the "assigned_tool_user_id" field.
13. Add any additional comments in the "comments" field.
14. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, `select_one`, optional): Select the volunteer role you are interested in:
	+ Ministry of Outreach
	+ Ministry of Worship
	+ Ministry of Education
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your qualifications or experience relevant to the volunteer role.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of your application:
	+ Pending
	+ Approved
	+ Denied
* **Date of Application** (`date_of_application`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): If assigned, enter the tool you have been assigned.
* **Assigned User ID** (`assigned_user_id`, `number`, optional): If assigned, enter your assigned user ID.
* **Assigned Tool User ID** (`assigned_tool_user_id`, `number`, optional): If assigned, enter your assigned tool user ID.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `time`, required): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `time`, required): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Application Form URL** (`application_form_url`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the application form.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review the form for errors before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities.
* This guide is intended to be a general help guide and might not cover every possible scenario. If you have specific questions or need further clarification, please contact the form's administrators.
