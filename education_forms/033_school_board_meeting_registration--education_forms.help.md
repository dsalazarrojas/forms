# School Board Meeting Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for a school board meeting. It captures necessary information for planning and coordination. Ensure that you are registering only for meetings you are attending or planning to attend. Duplicates should not be submitted. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
*   Fill out the form for each attendee.
*   Fill in the required fields, marked as required.
*   Choose the correct options for participation and accessibility requests.
*   Confirm your meeting attendance.
*   Optionally, add comments and questions for the meeting.
*   Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee's Name** (`Attendee Details`, text, `required`): Enter the name of the person or group registering for the meeting.
*   **Title** (`Attendee Details`, text, `required`): Enter the title or position of the attendee.
*   **Meeting Date** (`Meeting Details`, text, `optional`): Enter the date of the meeting, if known.
*   **Meeting Time** (`Meeting Details`, text, `optional`): Enter the time of the meeting, if known.
*   **Location** (`Meeting Details`, text, `optional`): Enter the location of the meeting, if known.
*   **Participation Type** (`Participation Details`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose how you plan to participate in the meeting:
    *   Member of the School Committee
    *   Staff Member
    *   Guest
*   **Comments and Questions** (`Comments and Questions`, text, `optional`): Add any comments or questions for the meeting.
*   **Accessibility Requests** (`Accessibility Requests`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Choose any accessibility requests:
    *   Accessible meeting materials
    *   Sign language interpreter
    *   Other (please specify)
*   **Meeting Attendance** (`Meeting Attendance`, checkbox, `required`): Confirm that you will be attending the meeting:
    *   Option A
    *   Option B
*   **Meeting Agenda Items** (`Meeting Agenda Items`, text, `optional`): Add any specific agenda items to discuss during the meeting.
*   **Accessibility Requests (continued)** (`Accessibility Requests`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Repeat the accessibility requests if needed. 

## Tips

*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure you have filled out all required fields.
*   Use the provided options for participation and accessibility requests to avoid confusion. 
*   If you have any questions or issues, contact the meeting organizer.
