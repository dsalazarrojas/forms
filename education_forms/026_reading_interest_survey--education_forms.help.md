# reading_interest_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The reading interest survey is a tool used to collect data on students' reading habits and interests. It is designed to help teachers and school staff understand students' reading preferences and identify areas for improvement in promoting reading among students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your title and school name in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the types of materials you typically read (fiction, non-fiction, etc.).
3. Specify how often you read (e.g., daily, several times a week, rarely, etc.).
4. Indicate where you usually read (e.g., home, school, library, etc.).
5. Provide any additional information about your reading habits.
6. Submit the form to complete your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title**: Enter a brief title that describes your survey.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name as a teacher or staff member.
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of your class or grade level.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number (if applicable).
* **Student Grade**: Enter your grade level (if applicable).
* **How Often Do Your Students Read**: Select the frequency with which your students read.
* **Book Type**: Select the types of materials you typically read (e.g., book, magazine, comic, etc.).
* **How Often Do Your Students Read What**: Select the specific types of materials you read (e.g., fiction, non-fiction, etc.).
* **How Often Do Your Students Read Where**: Select the location where you usually read (e.g., home, school, library, etc.).
* **Why Do Your Students Read**: Provide a brief explanation of why you read (e.g., for pleasure, for school, etc.).
* **What Do Your Students Read For**: Provide a brief explanation of what you read for (e.g., for entertainment, for education, etc.).
* **Reading Frequency At Home**: Indicate if you read at home.
* **Reading Frequency At School**: Indicate if you read at school.
* **Reading Frequency At Library**: Indicate if you read at the library.
* **Reading Frequency At Park**: Indicate if you read at a park.
* **Reading Frequency At Other**: Provide a brief explanation of where you usually read (if other).
* **Reading Frequency Other**: Provide any additional information about your reading habits.
* **Reading Interest Scales**: Select the types of materials you are interested in reading (e.g., fiction, non-fiction, etc.).
* **Reading Interest Scales What**: Provide any additional information about your reading interests.
* **Reading Interest Scales Other**: Provide any additional information about your reading interests.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete your survey.
