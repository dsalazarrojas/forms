# design_course_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "design_course_enrollment_form" is a tool for course design and enrollment information collection. This form helps administrators gather essential details about courses, students, and their enrollment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "design_course_enrollment_form," follow these steps:
1. Fill out the user information section with your name and any relevant details that identify you as a student or instructor.
2. Provide course information in the designated field, including the course name or description.
3. If applicable, enter your contact information so that the course administrators can reach out to you.
4. Add any additional comments or details you'd like to share about the course or your experience.
5. Select your enrollment status from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information** (Text): This section is for entering your name and any relevant details that identify you as a student or instructor.

### Course Information
* **Course Information** (Text): Enter the course name or description you are interested in or already enrolled in. This helps the administrators to understand the context of your form submission.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (Text): If you'd like to be contacted about the course or need assistance, enter your contact details here. This could be your email, phone number, or any other relevant contact method.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (Text): This field is for providing any extra information you'd like to share about the course or your experience. Use this to ask questions or provide suggestions.

### Enrollment Status
* **Enrollment Status** (Select Multiple): Choose from the options:
	+ Active: You are currently enrolled in the course.
	+ Inactive: You are not enrolled in the course but are interested.
	+ Pending: Your enrollment status is pending, awaiting confirmation.

### Student Application ID
* **Student Application ID** (Number): Enter your student application ID if required. This helps the administrators to identify you in the system.
