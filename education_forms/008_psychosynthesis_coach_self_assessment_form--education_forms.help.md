# Psychosynthesis Coach Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Psychosynthesis Coach Self Assessment Form is designed to collect relevant information from clients about their growth goals, mental health, and other relevant factors to better understand their needs and create a tailored coaching plan. This form will help coaches create a more effective and personalized coaching experience for their clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct options for each section, following the instructions provided below.
2. Provide accurate and honest information to the best of your ability.
3. Use clear and descriptive answers for text fields.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: This is where you can enter your name, email address, and any other identifying information. Please ensure that this is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Coaching Level**: This section will ask you to select your level of coaching experience. Please choose between "New" and "Seasoned".
* **Growth Goals**: This is an open-ended text field for you to describe your goals and what you want to achieve.
* **Personal Growth**: This field is for you to provide any additional information about your personal growth.
* **Mental Health**: Please select all the mental health issues that apply to you from the dropdown list.
* **Emotional Intelligence**: This field is for you to describe your level of emotional awareness and self-regulation.
* **Self-Awareness**: This field is for you to describe your self-awareness and introspection skills.
* **Relationship Skills**: This field is for you to describe your relationship skills and abilities.
* **Communication Skills**: This field is for you to describe your communication skills and abilities.
* **Problem Solving**: This field is for you to describe your problem-solving skills and abilities.
* **Decision Making**: This field is for you to describe your decision-making skills and abilities.
* **Time Management**: This field is for you to describe your time management skills and abilities.

## Tips

* Please answer truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
* Use clear and descriptive answers for text fields.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any question, please contact the coach for guidance.
* If you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it to the coach.
