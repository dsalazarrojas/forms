# Exam Grade Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exam Grade Entry Form is used to record and track student exam grades in a specific course. This form is essential for maintaining accurate student records and supporting academic progress monitoring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field (field 1).
2. If applicable, enter the grade received by the student in the **Grade Received** field (field 2).
3. Select the grade scale achieved by the student using the **Grade Scale** dropdown options (field 3).
4. Enter the student's ID number in the **Student ID** field (field 4).
5. Enter the date of the exam in the **Exam Date** field (field 5).
6. Enter the name of the teacher who administered the exam in the **Teacher Name** field (field 6).
7. Enter the name of the course the exam was taken in the **Course Name** field (field 7).
8. Add any comments or remarks about the exam in the **Comments** field (field 8).
9. Select the score range achieved by the student from the **Score Range** dropdown options (field 9).
10. Choose the grading scale used to determine the student's grade from the **Grading Scale** dropdown options (field 10).
11. Indicate whether the final exam grade is applicable or not using the **Final Exam Grade** dropdown options (field 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name (field 1)
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name in this field.

### Grade Received (field 2)
* **Grade Received**: If the student received a grade for the exam, enter it here.

### Grade Scale (field 3)
* **Grade Scale**: Select the grade scale achieved by the student using the available options (A, B, C, D, F).

### Student ID (field 4)
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number to identify them.

### Exam Date (field 5)
* **Exam Date**: Enter the date of the exam.

### Teacher Name (field 6)
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher who administered the exam.

### Course Name (field 7)
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course the exam was taken in.

### Comments (field 8)
* **Comments**: Add any comments or remarks about the exam.

### Score Range (field 9)
* **Score Range**: Select the score range achieved by the student (Low, High, Medium).

### Grading Scale (field 10)
* **Grading Scale**: Choose the grading scale used to determine the student's grade (0-50, 51-70, 71-85, 86-100).

### Final Exam Grade (field 11)
* **Final Exam Grade**: Indicate whether the final exam grade is applicable or not (Not Applicable, Applicable).
