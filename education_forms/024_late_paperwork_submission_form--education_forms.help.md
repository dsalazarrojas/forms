# Late Paperwork Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit late paperwork for various reasons such as leave requests or attendance. It is essential to provide accurate information to ensure that the submitted paperwork is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Select the Document Type from the list (e.g., Attendance, Leave Request).
3. Enter the Document Date when your paperwork was due.
4. Enter the Submission Date when you are submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Document Type (Attendance, Leave Request)**: Select from the list the type of paperwork you are submitting for. This field is required.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number as it appears on your paycheck stub or other official documents.
* **Document Date**: Enter the date when your paperwork was originally due. This field is optional, but it helps us process your submission correctly.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date when you are submitting this form. This field is optional.
