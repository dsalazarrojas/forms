# Parent Contact Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record contact information for parents and guardians. It captures essential details about the interactions between school staff and parents, including the date of contact, method of contact, and any notes from the conversation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Contact Log" page.
2. Fill in the Date Visited field with the date of the contact.
3. Choose the relevant contact method from the list.
4. Select the contact status from the available options.
5. Enter the parent's name (or names) if applicable.
6. Add any contact notes from the conversation.
7. If assigned to a specific staff member, select the relevant option(s).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Log** (`parent_contact_log_form`, text, not required): This is the main section where you can record the contact information. Enter any relevant details about the contact.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, not required): Enter the date of the contact with the parent or guardian. Format should be in YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY/MM/DD.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, not required): Choose the method of contact that best describes the interaction (e.g., phone call, email, in-person).
* **Contact Status** (`contact_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the contact (e.g., scheduled, completed, no-show).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, not required): Enter the name(s) of the parent or guardian who was contacted.
* **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, text, not required): Add any additional notes from the conversation.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, not required): If the contact was assigned to a specific staff member, select the relevant option(s).
