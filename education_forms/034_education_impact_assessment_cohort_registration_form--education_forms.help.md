# Education Impact Assessment Cohort Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Impact Assessment Cohort Registration Form is designed to gather information about a participant's education background and their registration details for a specific course or program. This form is essential for tracking the progress and outcomes of participants in the cohort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including any relevant details about your education background and experience.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Specify your course information, including the name of the course or program you are registered for.
4. Select whether you have any education information or participant information that may be relevant to your registration.
5. Enter the date you would like to register for the course or program.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments about your registration.
7. Finally, enter a unique identifier for your form submission, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Please enter your name and any relevant details about your education background and experience.
  - *Type:* This field is a text input field, allowing you to enter any relevant information about yourself.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Contact Information (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter your email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your registration.
  - *Type:* This field is a text input field, allowing you to enter your email address and phone number.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Course Information (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter the name of the course or program you are registering for.
  - *Type:* This field is a text input field, allowing you to enter the name of the course or program.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Education Info (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Select whether you have any education information that may be relevant to your registration.
  - *Type:* This field is a multiple choice select field, allowing you to select one or more options from the list.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Participant Information (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Select whether you have any participant information that may be relevant to your registration.
  - *Type:* This field is a single select field, allowing you to select one option from the list.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Registration Date (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter the date you would like to register for the course or program.
  - *Type:* This field is a date input field, allowing you to select a date.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Email (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your registration.
  - *Type:* This field is an email input field, allowing you to enter your email address.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Phone (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you about your registration.
  - *Type:* This field is a text input field, allowing you to enter your phone number.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Notes (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
  - *Type:* This field is a note input field, allowing you to enter any additional information.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Assigned Tool (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter the name of the tool you are using for the course or program.
  - *Type:* This field is a text input field, allowing you to enter the name of the tool.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.
- **Form ID (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter a unique identifier for your form submission, if required.
  - *Type:* This field is a text input field, allowing you to enter a unique identifier.
  - *Required/Optional:* This field is optional.

## Tips
