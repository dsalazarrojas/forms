# School Nurse Visit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Nurse Visit Form is a crucial tool for gathering essential information about student visits to the nurse's office. It helps school staff to:

* Record and track student visits in a standardized manner
* Identify patterns and trends in student health issues
* Provide accurate and timely data for school administrators and teachers

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the School Nurse Visit Form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the student's visit, including their student identifier, date of visit, reason for visit, symptoms, and student name.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Identifier** (`student_identifier`, number, required/optional: false):
	+ This field is where you enter the student's unique identifier, such as their student ID number or student number.
	+ Please enter the correct student identifier to ensure accurate tracking and record-keeping.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required/optional: false):
	+ This field is where you enter the date the student visited the nurse's office.
	+ Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY to ensure accurate sorting and analysis.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ This field is where you describe the reason for the student's visit to the nurse's office.
	+ Be as specific as possible when providing the reason for the visit.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ This field is where you list any symptoms the student is experiencing.
	+ Be thorough and accurate when describing the symptoms.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ This field is where you enter the student's name.
	+ Ensure you spell their name correctly to avoid errors in record-keeping.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the school nurse or administration before proceeding.
* Keep the form in a clean and organized state to ensure easy reading and reference.
