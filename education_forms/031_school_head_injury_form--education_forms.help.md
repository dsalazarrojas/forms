# School Head Injury Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Head Injury Form is a tool for school authorities to document and track incidents involving students. This form helps to gather essential information about the incident, student, and school, ensuring accurate and consistent reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name, age, and gender in the relevant fields.
2. Provide detailed information about the incident, including the date and time it occurred.
3. If necessary, enter the parent's name and contact information.
4. Enter the school's name and address, as well as the incident location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident details** (`incident_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student age** (`student_age`, number, optional): Enter the student's age.
* **Student gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, required): Select the student's gender from the available options.
* **Date of incident** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident description** (`incident_description`, note, optional): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **Parent name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Student phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **School name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the school's name.
* **School address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the school's address.
* **Incident location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and consistent reporting.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to the school's incident reporting guidelines for guidance.
* If you need to add any additional information, use the incident description field.
