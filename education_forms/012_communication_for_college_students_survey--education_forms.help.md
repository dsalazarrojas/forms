# Communication For College Students Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather insights from students on their experiences and expectations regarding faculty communication in college settings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read each question carefully, and select the response that best reflects your opinion.
*   Be honest in your answers, as they will help faculty improve communication.
*   If you have any suggestions, feel free to provide them in the "Suggestions to Improve Communication" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student name** (Student name, text, Optional): This field is for your name, which is optional. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can leave it blank.
*   **Student email** (Student email, email, Optional): This field is for your email address, which is optional. This will allow us to follow up with you if needed.
*   **Program or major** (Program or major, text, Optional): This field is for your program or major of study. If you are unsure, please select the closest match.
*   **Satisfaction with faculty communication** (Satisfaction with faculty communication, select_one, True): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with faculty communication. Please select one of the five options.
*   **Clarity of course expectations** (Clarity of course expectations, select_one, True): This question inquires about the clarity of course expectations. Please select one of the four options.
*   **Preferred communication channel** (Preferred communication channel, select_one, True): This question asks about your preferred means of communication. Please select one of the four options.
*   **Preferred frequency of updates** (Preferred frequency of updates, select_one, True): This question inquires about how often you would like updates. Please select one of the four options.
*   **Suggestions to improve communication** (Suggestions to improve communication, text, False): This field is for any suggestions you may have on how faculty can improve communication. If you have any ideas, please feel free to share them.

## Tips

*   Please answer honestly, as your feedback is valuable to us.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please select the option that best reflects your opinion.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
