# Literature Comprehension Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess students' understanding of literature texts by evaluating their ability to comprehend and analyze passages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the provided literature text carefully.
2. Answer the questions based on your understanding of the text.
3. Fill out your submission time and date.
4. Enter your Student ID (if required by your instructor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reading Check** (`reading_check`, text, optional): This field is a text input where you can briefly describe your understanding of the passage.
* **Question 1** (`question1`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best represents your understanding of the passage.
* **Question 2** (`question2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that represent your understanding of the passage.
* **Question 3** (`question3`, number, optional): Enter a numerical answer that reflects your understanding of the passage.
* **Question 4** (`question4`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that represent your understanding of the passage.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time at which you submitted this form.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date at which you submitted this form.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your Student ID (if required by your instructor).

## Tips
- Make sure to read the provided literature text carefully before answering the questions.
- If you are unsure about any question, choose the option that best represents your understanding.
- If you need to submit your form multiple times, please ensure that each submission is completed at a different time and date.
