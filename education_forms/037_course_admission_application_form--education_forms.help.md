# Course Admission Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from students who are interested in being admitted to a course. It is intended to be filled out by students to provide the necessary details required for the admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your details as requested.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (although some fields are optional, it's still a good idea to fill them out if possible).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name
**Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Required/Optional: Required)
The student's name is the name of the student who is applying for the course. Please fill out your name in the format "First Name Last Name".

### Student Email
**Student Email** (`student_email`, Email, Required/Optional: Optional)
Your email address is used to communicate with you about your admission application. If you have a preferred email address for us to contact you, please fill it out. If not, you can leave this field blank.

### Student Phone
**Student Phone** (`student_phone`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
Your phone number is an optional field, but please consider filling it out so we can contact you if needed.

### Date of Birth
**Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, Date, Required/Optional: Required)
Please fill out your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format. This helps us to verify your age and identity.

### Address
**Address** (`address`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
Your current address is an optional field, but if you would like us to send you any additional information or materials, please fill out your address so we can ship them to you.

### Course
**Course** (`student_course`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
The course you are applying for is the name of the course you are interested in. Please fill out the name of the course as it appears on our website or in our course catalog.
