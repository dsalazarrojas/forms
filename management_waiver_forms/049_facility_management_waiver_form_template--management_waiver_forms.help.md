# Facility Management Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form template is used to collect necessary details for facility management purposes. Please fill it out accurately to ensure that your facility's operational and management needs are met efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the provided fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Select the correct Facility Type from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Management Waiver Form** (`facility_management_waiver_form`): This is the main form title. Please ensure it is filled in as provided.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter the name of the company associated with this facility.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`): Select one of the provided options to categorize the type of facility being managed.
	+ Option 1: Commercial
	+ Option 2: Residential
	+ Option 3: Industrial
	+ Option 4: Other
* **Contact** (`contact`): Enter the contact person's name and details for future reference.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number for future reference.

## Tips

* Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your facility management team or refer to the facility management guidelines.
* Review this form carefully before submission to prevent any errors or duplication of efforts.
