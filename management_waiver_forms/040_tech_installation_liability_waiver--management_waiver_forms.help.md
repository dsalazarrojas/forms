# Tech Installation Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to serve as a liability waiver for tech installation, collecting key details about the customer and the installation process, including customer consent, installation details, signature and dates from both customers and technicians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2.  Fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly. Required fields are denoted by a asterisk (\*) symbol.
3.  For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank or use them as needed for additional information.
4.  Double-check your entries for accuracy before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Customer Consent
*   Customer Consent: A field to obtain customer agreement and understanding of the installation process and terms. Please enter your consent in this field.

### 2. Installation Details
*   Installation Details: A field to provide details about the installation, such as equipment, date, and address. Please enter the relevant details accurately.

### 3. Signature
*   Signature: A field for customer signature, confirming their understanding and agreement with the terms outlined in the form. Please sign your name in this field.

### 4. Technician Details
*   Technician Details: A field to provide details about the technician who will perform the installation. Please enter the technician's name and contact information.

### 5. Customer Contact
*   Customer Contact: A field to provide contact information for the customer. Please enter the customer's name, email, and phone number.

### 6. Installation Date
*   Installation Date: A field to provide the date of the installation. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 7. Tech Equipment
*   Tech Equipment: A field to provide details about the equipment being installed. This field is not required but can be used for additional information.

### 8. On-Site Warranty
