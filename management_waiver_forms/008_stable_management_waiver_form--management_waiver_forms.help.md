# Stable Management Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for waiver and release purposes from riders or their representatives. It should only be filled out by individuals who have the authority to do so.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* City
	* State
	* ZIP
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Rider Experience
	* Rider Age
	* Emergency Contact Name
	* Emergency Contact Phone
	* Parent Signature
	* Rider Signature

2. Leave the optional field (Emergency Contact Relationship) blank unless you need to provide additional context for the emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it is typically spelled.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it is typically spelled.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): This is your current address. Please enter your full address if you have one.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Please enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Please enter your state of residence.
* **ZIP** (`zip`, text, required): Please enter your postal code.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Rider Experience** (`rider_experience_years`, number, required): Please enter the number of years you have experience as a rider.
* **Rider Age** (`rider_age`, number, required): Please enter your age.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): If applicable, please describe the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Please sign below to indicate approval.
* **Rider Signature** (`rider_signature`, text, required): Please sign below to indicate agreement and understanding of the waiver form.
