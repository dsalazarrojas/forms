# Safety Management Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Management Waiver Form Template is used to document employee compliance with safety regulations and protocols when working with safety-sensitive areas or equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the safety manager in the "Safety Manager" field.
3. Enter the name of the employee in the "Employee Name" field.
4. Enter the date of birth of the employee in the "Employee Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter the address of the employee in the "Employee Address" field.
6. Enter the phone number of the employee in the "Employee Phone" field.
7. Enter the email address of the employee in the "Employee Email" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Employee Type" field to indicate whether the employee has been cleared to work with safety-sensitive areas or equipment.
9. Enter the date the employee has completed the safety waiver in the "Waiver Date" field.
10. Enter the time the employee has completed the safety waiver in the "Waiver Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (safety_management_waiver_form_template_2)**: Enter the name of the company that the employee is working for.
* **Safety Manager (safety_management_waiver_form_template_3)**: The name of the safety manager who has cleared the employee to work with safety-sensitive areas or equipment.
* **Employee Name (safety_management_waiver_form_template_4)**: The name of the employee who is completing the waiver form.
* **Employee Date of Birth (safety_management_waiver_form_template_5)**: The date of birth of the employee.
* **Employee Address (safety_management_waiver_form_template_6)**: The address of the employee.
* **Employee Phone (safety_management_waiver_form_template_7)**: The phone number of the employee.
* **Employee Email (safety_management_waiver_form_template_8)**: The email address of the employee.
* **Employee Type (safety_management_waiver_form_template_9)**: Select "Yes" to indicate that the employee has been cleared to work with safety-sensitive areas or equipment, or "No" otherwise.
* **Waiver Date (safety_management_waiver_form_template_10)**: The date the employee has completed the safety waiver.
* **Waiver Time (safety_management_waiver_form_template_11)**: The time the employee has completed the safety waiver.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* Ensure that you have obtained clearance from the safety manager before completing this form.
* Keep a record of all completed waivers for future reference.
