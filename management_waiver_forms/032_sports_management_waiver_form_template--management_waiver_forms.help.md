# sports management waiver form template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports management waiver form template is used to collect information from athletes and their families for emergency contact and medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Date of Birth
	* Emergency Contact First Name
	* Emergency Contact Last Name
	* Emergency Contact Phone
	* Emergency Contact Email
	* Guardian First Name
	* Guardian Last Name
	* Guardian Phone
	* Guardian Email
	* Parent First Name
	* Parent Last Name
	* Parent Phone
	* Parent Email
	* Guardian Relationship
	* Parent Relationship
	* Emergency Contact Designation
2. If you're an athlete, fill out the medical condition field if applicable.
3. For emergency contact purposes, select one of the options for the "Emergency Contact Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your or your athlete's first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your or your athlete's last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter a phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the athlete.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (6, text, required): Enter the first name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (7, text, required): Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (8, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (9, email, required): Enter a valid email address for the emergency contact.
* **Guardian First Name** (10, text, optional): Enter the first name of the guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian Last Name** (11, text, optional): Enter the last name of the guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian Phone** (12, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian Email** (13, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian Relationship** (14, text, required): Describe the relationship between the athlete and the guardian.
* **Parent First Name** (15, text, optional): Enter the first name of the parent (if applicable).
* **Parent Last Name** (16, text, optional): Enter the last name of the parent (if applicable).
* **Parent Phone** (17, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the parent (if applicable).
* **Parent Email** (18, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the parent (if applicable).
* **Parent Relationship** (19, text, required): Describe the relationship between the athlete and the parent.
* **Medical Condition** (20, text, optional): If the athlete has a medical condition, enter a brief description.
* **Emergency Contact Designation** (21, text, required): Describe the emergency contact designation (e.g., parent, guardian, emergency contact person).
* **Medical Procedures** (22, text, optional): If applicable, enter any medical procedures the athlete has undergone.
* **Allergies** (23, text, optional): If applicable, enter any allergies the athlete has.
* **Medical Notes** (24, text, optional): If applicable, enter any medical notes or comments.
* **Emergency Contact Consent** (25, select_one, required): Select whether you grant permission for the emergency contact to contact you or not.

## Tips

* Double-check the required fields to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to its label and hint for guidance.
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
