# DJ Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by event organizers to book a DJ for their events. It gathers essential information such as event details and DJ name to ensure a smooth booking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your event in the **Event Name** field.
2. Enter the name of the DJ you would like to book in the **DJ Name** field.
3. Select the date of your event from the calendar in the **Event Date** field.
4. Choose the time of your event in the **Event Time** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your event.
- **DJ Name** (`dj_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the DJ you would like to book.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your event from the calendar.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of your event.
