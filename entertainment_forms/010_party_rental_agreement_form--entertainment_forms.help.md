# Party Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Rental Agreement Form is a document designed to collect information about a party event, ensuring all necessary details and agreements are in place before the party takes place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the necessary information about your party event, including party space, date, time, and guests.
* Review the party space carefully and enter any details that may be relevant to the event.
* Select a party agreement that applies to your event (Party Equipment Rental Agreement, Party Space Rental Agreement, or Party Catering Contract).
* Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
* Enter your party email address in case we need to contact you with any further questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Space**: Enter the space where your party will take place, including address or location if applicable.
* **Party Date**: Select the date of your party event.
* **Party Time**: Select the time of your party event.
* **Party Guests**: Enter the estimated number of guests attending your party.
* **Party Food**: Enter any specific food requirements or dietary restrictions for your guests.
* **Party Drinks**: Enter any specific drink requirements or preferences for your guests.
* **Party Equipment**: Enter any specific equipment or services you would like to rent for your party.
* **Party Catering**: Enter any specific catering preferences or requirements for your event.
* **Party Notes**: Enter any additional notes or instructions for your party.
* **Party Agreement**: Select the agreement that best describes your party event (Party Equipment Rental Agreement, Party Space Rental Agreement, or Party Catering Contract).
* **Party Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm your agreement.
* **Party Email**: Enter your email address in case we need to contact you with any further questions or updates.
