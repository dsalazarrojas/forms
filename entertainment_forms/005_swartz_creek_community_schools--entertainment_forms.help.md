# Swartz Creek Community Schools - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for scheduling and booking purposes within Swartz Creek Community Schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the booking date for the event in the "Booking Date" field.
2. Specify the event time in the "Event Time" field.
3. Enter the name of the performer or event in the "Performer Name" field.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, in the corresponding fields.
5. Provide any additional event details in the "Event Details" field.
6. Enter any relevant information about the assigned tool in the "Assigned Tool" field (if applicable).
7. Optionally, enter a unique identifier for the form in the "Form ID" field (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Date (<type> date):** Enter the date for which you are booking the event.
* **Event Time (<type> time):** Enter the time of the event.
* **Performer Name (<type> text):** Enter the name of the performer or event.
* **Contact Name (<type> text):** Enter your name.
* **Contact Email (<type> email):** Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone (<type> text):** Enter your phone number.
* **Event Details (<type> text):** Enter any additional details about the event.
* **Assigned Tool (<type> text):** Enter any information about the tool assigned for the event (if applicable).
* **Form ID (<type> text):** Enter a unique identifier for the form (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
