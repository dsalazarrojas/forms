# thanksgiving_potluck_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "thanksgiving_potluck_survey" form is designed to collect information for a Thanksgiving potluck event. It helps organizers gather essential details about the event, including the host's contact information, event details, and potluck preferences. This form is meant to facilitate communication and ensure that guests with dietary restrictions are accommodated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Event Information** section, providing a brief description of the event and any relevant details.
2. In the **Host Information** section, enter your name and contact information, so we can get back to you with updates.
3. In the **Potluck Details** section, describe the potluck dish you plan to bring, including its name and ingredients.
4. In the **Dishes** section, enter the name of your dish and any relevant information about its ingredients and preparation methods.
5. In the **Guest Information** section, provide information about the number of guests you are bringing and their ages if necessary.
6. For **Food Preferences**, choose the types of dishes you are willing to eat from the provided options.
7. In the **Dietary Restrictions** section, select any dietary restrictions you have, such as gluten-free, vegan, or dairy-free.
8. Finally, in the **Contact Information** section, enter your contact information so we can get back to you with updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Information** (`event_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the event and any relevant details to help us understand the context.
* **Host Information** (`host_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name and contact information so we can get back to you with updates.
* **Potluck Details** (`potluck_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the potluck dish you plan to bring, including its name and ingredients.
* **Dishes** (`dish_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your dish and any relevant information about its ingredients and preparation methods.
* **Guest Information** (`guest_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about the number of guests you are bringing and their ages if necessary.
* **Food Preferences** (`food_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the types of dishes you are willing to eat from the provided options, such as Turkey, Ham, Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any dietary restrictions you have, such as Gluten-free, Vegan, Dairy-free, etc.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information so we can get back to you with updates.
