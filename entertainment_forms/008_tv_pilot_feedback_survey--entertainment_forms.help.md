# tv_pilot_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about a TV pilot. It helps us understand their thoughts on the content, production, and overall quality of the pilot. The feedback gathered will be used to improve future episodes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the pilot that you watched.
2. Enter the title of the pilot in the "Pilot Title" field.
3. Enter the season and episode numbers in the corresponding fields.
4. Rate the pilot in the "Rating" field.
5. Choose whether you recommend this pilot or not in the "Do you recommend this pilot?" field.
6. If you want to provide additional comments, you can do so in the "Pilot Comments" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. If you want to provide a contact number, you can do so in the "Contact Number" field.
9. In the "What we missed" field, describe what you think was missing from the pilot.
10. In the "What we got right" field, describe what you think was well-executed in the pilot.
11. In the "What we would change" field, suggest changes you would make to improve the pilot.
12. If you have additional comments, you can enter them in the "Additional comments" field.
13. Finally, choose whether you recommend this pilot to producers or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pilot Feedback Survey**:
	* A short description of the pilot that you watched.
- **What category does this pilot belong to?** (`pilot_category`):
	* Select the correct category from the options provided (Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, Reality, Romance, Sci-Fi, Thriller, Western, Other).
	* This field helps us understand the type of content of the pilot.
- **Pilot Title** (`pilot_title`):
	* Enter the title of the pilot as it was presented.
- **Pilot Season** (`pilot_season`):
	* Enter the season number of the pilot episode.
* **Pilot Episode** (`pilot_episode`):
	* Enter the episode number of the pilot episode.
* **Rating** (`rating`):
	* Rate the pilot on a scale (likely a 5-point or 1-10 point scale).
* **Do you recommend this pilot?** (`pilot_recommendation`):
	* Choose whether you recommend this pilot or not.
* **Pilot Comments** (`pilot_comments`):
	* Provide any additional comments about the pilot.
* **Email** (`email`):
	* Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`):
	* Enter your contact number so we can contact you if needed.
* **What we missed** (`pilot_what_we_missed`):
	* Describe what you think was missing from the pilot.
* **What we got right** (`pilot_what_we_got_right`):
	* Describe what you think was well-executed in the pilot.
* **What we would change** (`pilot_what_we_would_change`):
	* Suggest changes you would make to improve the pilot.
* **Additional comments** (`pilot_additional_comments`):
	* Provide any additional feedback about the pilot.
* **Do you recommend to producers?** (`pilot_recommendation_to_producers`):
	* Choose whether you recommend this pilot to the producers or not.
