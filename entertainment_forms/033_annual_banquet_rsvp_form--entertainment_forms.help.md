# Annual Banquet RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Banquet RSVP Form is designed to collect information from guests to prepare for the annual banquet event. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Guest Information**: Fill in your first name, last name, and email address to ensure the banquet organizers can contact you for updates and reminders.
2. **Attendance Confirmation**: Confirm whether you will attend or not, and if so, provide the number of guests in your party. 
3. **Meal Preferences**: Choose your main course and additional entrée for your party (if any).
4. **Special Accommodations**: If needed, specify any special requests or requirements (seating, accessibility, etc.).
5. **Transportation**: Confirm if you need parking assistance or other transportation arrangements.
6. **Event Details**: Fill in the banquet date and location.
7. **Updates and Reminders**: Choose whether you want to receive updates and reminders about the banquet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your given name (John).
* **Last Name**: Your surname (Doe).
* **Email Address**: Your email address (e.g., [johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com)).
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Organization or Department**: Your workplace or department (if applicable).
* **Will you attend the annual banquet**: Confirm if you will attend or not.
* **Number of Guests**: Total people in your party (including yourself).
* **Guest Names**: List names of people in your party (if any).
* **Entrée Selection**: Choose your main course (Prime Rib, Grilled Salmon, etc.).
* **Additional Entrée for Guest**: Choose the entrée for any additional guests.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Specify any food allergies or preferences.
* **Seating or Table Preference**: Specify any special seating or table requests.
* **Accessibility or Mobility Needs**: Specify any accessibility or mobility needs.
* **Do you need parking assistance**: Confirm if you need parking assistance.
* **Any Transportation Needs**: Specify any transportation needs (e.g., airport pickup, etc.).
* **Banquet Date**: The date of the event (if known).
* **Event Location**: The location of the event (if known).
* **Keep me updated about the banquet**: Confirm if you want to receive updates and reminders.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you provide to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or questions, fill in the "Additional Comments or Questions" field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary fields are filled out.
