# Song Queue Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Song Queue Submission Form is designed to collect information about songs to be played on a scheduled playlist. This form helps us process and confirm your song requests, ensuring that they are accurately added to the playtime schedule and that we can efficiently communicate with you about your submissions.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete all required fields.
2. Select your preferred song genre.
3. Enter a valid email address to receive communication and updates.
4. Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
5. Optionally, enter a message to provide additional information about your submission.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Song Request** (`Song Request`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the song you wish to submit, including the artist name and title.
* **Song Genre** (`Song Genre`, `select_one`, required): Select the genre of music you are submitting to help us better understand the song's style.
* **Song Title** (`Song Title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the song you are submitting.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached to confirm your song submission.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, `email`, required): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Play Time** (`Play Time`, `time`, optional): If you have a preferred play time for your song, enter it here.
* **Preferred Play Time** (`Preferred Play Time`, `time`, optional): If you prefer a specific play time for your song, enter it here.
* **Artist** (`Artist`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide more information about the artist, you can enter it here.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted directly.
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your submission, such as special requests or comments.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Enter a valid email address that you regularly check to receive updates and confirmations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* We will review and process your song submission within a timely manner.
