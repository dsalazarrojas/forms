# Discord Role Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Discord Role Application Form with ease. The form is used to gather information for role assignments on our Discord server, ensuring that you get assigned the correct role based on your provided details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Discord Tag, and Email (if applicable)
2. Describe why you want our role and what you'll contribute to the community
3. Provide your goals and expectations for your role
4. Select your role(s) of interest (if applicable)
5. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Discord Tag** (Text, Required): Enter your Discord username or tag that you use on the server.
* **Email** (Text, Optional): If you'd like to provide an email address for communication purposes, please do so.
* **Role** (Text, Optional): Describe the specific role you're interested in or are already assigned.
* **Server** (Text, Optional): If you're applying for a specific server, please provide the name of the server.
* **Role Duration** (Text, Optional): If you have a preferred duration for your role, please specify it.
* **Why do you want our role?** (Text, Required): Briefly describe why you're interested in our role. Be clear and specific about your motivations and goals.
* **What will you contribute?** (Text, Optional): Describe your goals and expectations for your role, including any skills or experience you bring to the position.
* **Goals** (Text, Optional): Outline your short-term and long-term goals for the community and your role.
* **Other Discord Tag** (Text, Optional): If you have an alternate Discord tag, please provide it.
