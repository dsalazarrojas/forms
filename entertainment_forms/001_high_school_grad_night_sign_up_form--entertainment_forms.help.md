# High School Grad Night Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Grad Night Sign Up Form is used to collect information from parents, teachers, and students for the high school graduation event. This form will help us track student attendance, teacher attendance, and other important details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your parent/teacher permission status and student grade level (if applicable).
3. Choose your preferred date and time for the event (if applicable).
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the Note field (if applicable).
5. Select the student's graduation year and attendance status (if applicable).
6. Enter the student's School ID and School Name (if applicable).
7. Enter the teacher's name and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the first name of the person signing up.
- **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the last name of the person signing up.
- **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the person signing up (if applicable).
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the person signing up (if applicable).
- **Grade** (Number, Optional): Select the student's grade level (if applicable).
- **Parent Permission** (Select One, Optional): Choose "Student," "Parent," or "Teacher" as the permission status.
- **Student Information** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more of the following options for the student:
  • Student ID
  • Student Name
  • Student Grade
  • Student Email
  • Student Phone
- **Date** (Date, Optional): Choose the preferred date for the event (if applicable).
- **Time** (Time, Optional): Choose the preferred time for the event (if applicable).
- **Note** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the event (if applicable).
- **Grad Year** (Select One, Optional): Select the student's graduation year.
- **Student Attendance** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the student's attendance status:
  • Present
  • Absent
  • Late
- **Teacher Attendance** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the teacher's attendance status:
  • Present
  • Absent
  • Late
- **School ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the school ID (if applicable).
- **School Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the school name (if applicable).
- **Teacher Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's name (if applicable).
- **Teacher Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's phone number (if applicable).
- **Student Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's name (if applicable).
- **Student ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the student ID (if applicable).
- **Student Grade** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's grade (if applicable).
- **Teacher Email** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's email (if applicable).
- **Teacher School** (Text, Optional): Enter the teacher's school (if applicable).
- **Student School** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's school (if applicable).
- **Graduation Year** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's graduation year (if applicable).
- **Graduation Date** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's graduation date (if applicable).
