# trip_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trip_survey form is used to gather information about users' travel plans. It can be used by both personal and professional users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the purpose of your trip from the options provided.
2. Choose the dates of your travel.
3. Select the location(s) you will be visiting.
4. Choose the type of trip you are taking.
5. Enter the duration of your trip.
6. Add any additional trip details.
7. Provide the name and contact information of your contact person (if applicable).
8. Indicate if this trip is a group trip (yes/no).
9. Select the status of your trip (active/inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the purpose of your trip? (`trip_purpose`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the reason for your trip from the provided options.
* **When are you traveling? (`travel_dates`, date, optional)**: Enter the start and end dates of your trip.
* **Where are you traveling to? (`travel_locations`, select_one, optional)**: Choose the location(s) you will be visiting.
* **What type of trip is this? (`travel_type`, select_one, optional)**: Select the type of trip you are taking.
* **How long is your trip? (`travel_duration`, number, optional)**: Enter the length of your trip.
* **Can you please enter any additional trip details? (`trip_details`, text, optional)**: Add any extra information about your trip.
* **Who is your contact person? (`contact_person`, text, optional)**: Provide the name and contact information of your contact person (if applicable).
* **Is this a group trip? (`travel_group`, select_one, optional)**: Select yes or no if this is a group trip.
* **What is your trip status? (`trip_status`, select_one, optional)**: Choose active or inactive to indicate the status of your trip.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure proper submission.
* If you have trouble selecting an option from the provided list, try clearing your browser cache.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the form administrator.
