# Streaming Moderator Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Streaming Moderator Application Form is for streaming moderators to apply for available shifts at a streaming platform. Please fill out this form to express your interest and availability to work as a moderator.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
2. Review the form to ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Once you've completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 1"> First Name
* **First Name** (text, optional): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.

### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 2"> Last Name
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.

### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 3"> Availability
* **Availability** (time, optional): Please select the time of day you are available to work as a moderator. This can be Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.

### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 4"> Preferred Shift
* **Preferred Shift** (select_one, optional): Please select your preferred shift. You can choose from Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.

### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 5"> Available From
* **Available From** (date, optional): Please select the date you are available to start working as a moderator. This should be in the format dd/mm/yyyy.

### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 6"> Available Until
* **Available Until** (date, optional): Please select the date you are available to stop working as a moderator. This should be in the format dd/mm/yyyy.

### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 7"> Scheduling Availability
* **Scheduling Availability** (select_multiple, optional): Please select the days of the week you are available to work as a moderator. You can choose multiple days.

### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 8"> Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Please enter your contact email address so we can reach out to you about your application and scheduling.

### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 9"> Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of emergency.

### <i class="fa fa-circle" title="Field 10"> Additional Contact Info
* **Additional Contact Info** (note, optional): Please provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to increase your chances of being selected for a shift.
* If you have any scheduling conflicts, please let us know in the "Scheduling Availability" field.
* We will review all applications carefully and contact you if we need more information or if you are selected for a shift.
