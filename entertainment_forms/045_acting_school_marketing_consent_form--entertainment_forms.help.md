# Acting School Marketing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain marketing consent from students or their guardians for the use of photos, videos, and other marketing materials by the Acting School.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, paying attention to the required fields.
2. Enter your name as the student or guardian providing consent.
3. Enter your contact email and phone number (if applicable).
4. Choose the correct options for consent for photos and video recordings.
5. Specify the duration of consent and the usage scope.
6. If you need to revoke consent, specify the process for revocation.
7. Finally, provide a signature as the consenting adult or student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Marketing consent --** (`consent_header`, `note`, required: false): This is an introductory note explaining the purpose of the form.
* **Student name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your full name as the student or guardian providing consent.
* **Guardian name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, required: false): If you are a guardian providing consent for a minor student, please enter your full name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: true): Enter the email address where you can be contacted for marketing consent confirmation.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Consent for photos** (`consent_for_photos`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose whether to allow the use of photos or not.
	+ Select 'Yes allow use' to consent to the use of photos.
	+ Select 'Do not allow' to decline the use of photos.
* **Consent for video** (`consent_for_video`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose whether to allow the use of video recordings or not.
	+ Select 'Yes allow use' to consent to the use of video recordings.
	+ Select 'Do not allow' to decline the use of video recordings.
* **Duration of consent** (`duration_of_consent`, `text`, required: false): If you want to limit the duration of consent, please specify the time period.
* **Usage scope** (`usage_scope`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate where the marketing materials may be used.
	+ Select 'Website' for website usage.
	+ Select 'Social media' for social media usage.
	+ Select 'Print materials' for print materials usage.
	+ Select 'Advertising' for advertising usage.
* **Revocation process** (`revocation_process`, `text`, required: false): If you need to revoke consent in the future, please describe the process.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: true): Type your full name as the consenting adult or student.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required: true): Date when consent was provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are aware of the purpose and usage scope of this form before filling it out.
- Fill out the form carefully and attentively.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the Acting School's administration.
