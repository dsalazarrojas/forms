# Script Pitch Meeting Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by users to request a meeting with the entertainment team to pitch their script.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form**: Enter the required and optional information in the corresponding fields.
2. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to send the form to the entertainment team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Script Details**: A brief description of your script, ideally one sentence that captures the core idea and hook.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Logline**: A brief summary of your script, ideally one sentence that captures the core idea and hook.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Meeting Preferred Time**: The time you prefer for the meeting, formatted according to the 12-hour clock (e.g., 12:00 PM).
	+ **Type:** time
	+ **Required:** false
* **Meeting Preferred Date**: The date you prefer for the meeting, formatted according to the calendar (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
	+ **Type:** date
	+ **Required:** false
* **Meeting Preferred Time AM/PM**: Whether the meeting time should be in the morning or afternoon.
	+ **Type:** select_one
	+ **Required:** false
* **Meeting Location**: The physical location where you'd like to have the meeting.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Contact Name**: Your name and contact details for follow-up communication.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Contact Email**: Your email address for follow-up communication.
	+ **Type:** email
	+ **Required:** false
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number for follow-up communication.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Contact Agent**: If you have a specific agent assigned to your project, enter their name.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Script File**: Upload a file containing your script for review.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false
* **Logline File**: Upload a file containing your logline for review.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** false

## Tips

* Make sure to provide all required information to increase the likelihood of getting your script reviewed.
* Upload files in a format that is easily readable and downloadable.
* Be clear and concise in your logline and script description.
* Be respectful and professional in your communication.
* Be prepared to discuss your script and its development during the meeting.
