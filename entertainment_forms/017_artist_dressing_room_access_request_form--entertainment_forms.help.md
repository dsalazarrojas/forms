# Artist Dressing Room Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Artist Dressing Room Access Request Form is a necessary step for artists, crew members, and other individuals to gain access to the artist dressing room during performances or events. This form helps the artist and their team review and approve or deny access to ensure a secure and respectful environment.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Information
Before starting, please make sure you have all the necessary information ready, including your name, role, email, phone number, and any special requirements or restrictions.

### Step 2: Fill Out the Form
Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be reviewed and used for approval or denial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Please provide your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Role or Title** (`requester_role`, `text`, required): Describe your role or title related to the performance or event.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your contact phone number for any additional information or questions.
* **Artist or Performer Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required): Identify the artist or performer's name for whom you are requesting access.
* **Date of Access Needed** (`access_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you need access to the dressing room (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time of Access** (`access_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time you plan to access the dressing room (HH:MM AM/PM format).
* **Duration of Access - Hours** (`duration_hours`, `number`, optional): Indicate how many hours you will need access to the dressing room (e.g., 1-8 hours).
* **Type of Access** (`access_type`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Before Performance
	+ During Performance
	+ After Performance
	+ All Day
* **Number of People Requiring Access** (`number_of_people`, `number`, required): Specify the total number of people requesting access.
* **Purpose of Access** (`access_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Preparation
	+ Guest Visit
	+ Equipment Setup
	+ Media Interview
	+ Other
* **Will Artist Accompany Group** (`accompany_artist`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the artist will be accompanying your group.
* **Security Clearance Status** (`security_clearance`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Pre-Approved
	+ Pending Approval
	+ No Clearance
* **Special Requirements or Restrictions** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): List any special needs or concerns that require consideration.
* **Valid Identification Provided** (`identification_provided`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm if valid identification was provided.
* **Artist Dressing Room Policy Acknowledged** (`policy_acknowledged`, `select_one`, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm you have acknowledged the artist's policy.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Share any additional information or requests.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully and accurately before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or doubts, contact the artist or their management team for guidance.
* Be prepared to provide valid identification and confirm the accuracy of your information.
* This form is designed to be completed by the person requesting access, not by the artist or their team.
