# halloween_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about users' preferences during the Halloween season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best fit your preferences for each section. You can choose to answer any or all of the sections, and you can always skip any section if you don't have a preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your favorite Halloween movie** (`halloween_1`, text, required: false): This section is where you can select your favorite Halloween movie. If you have multiple favorite movies, you can select each one separately.
* **What is your favorite Halloween character** (`halloween_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose from the list of popular Halloween characters. Select only one that you have a strong preference for.
* **Which type of candy do you prefer** (`halloween_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the types of candy that you prefer. You can choose one, two, or multiple options.
* **What is your age** (`halloween_4`, number, required: false): Enter your age. Please note that this section is not required, so you can skip it if you don't want to share your age.
* **How much would you like to spend** (`halloween_5`, number, required: false): Select the amount you would like to spend. Please enter a whole number, as we can't process partial amounts.
