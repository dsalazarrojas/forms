# dj_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect complaints or issues from clients regarding DJs who have performed at events organized by the client. The form is intended to help event organizers identify areas of improvement and address the concerns of their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title that describes the nature of your complaint.
2. Enter your name and contact information so that we can follow up with you directly.
3. Select the date of the event where you experienced the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: Enter a short description of your complaint or issue (max 100 characters)
  This field is where you can briefly describe your complaint or issue with the DJ's performance.
  Please be as specific as possible to help us identify the problem.
* **Client Name**: Enter your name (e.g., John Doe)
  This is where you can enter your name so we can follow up with you directly.
  Please make sure to provide your correct name to ensure we can contact you.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event where you experienced the issue
  This field is where you can select the date of the event where you had a problem with the DJ's performance.
  Please choose the correct date to help us associate the complaint with the right event.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your issue to help us understand the problem.
* Do not submit multiple forms for the same issue.
* If you are unsure about what to enter in any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* Once submitted, you will receive a confirmation email to ensure your form has been received.
