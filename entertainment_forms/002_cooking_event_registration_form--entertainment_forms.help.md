# Cooking Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cooking Event Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees for event organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Enter your name in the "Attendee Name" field (field 2).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (field 3).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (field 4).
5. Select the date of the event you plan to attend in the "Event Date" field (field 5).
6. Select the time of the event you plan to attend in the "Event Time" field (field 6).
7. Choose any enrolled events you have participated in (field 7).
8. Enter the date and time of the enrolled events you have participated in (fields 8 and 9).
9. Select the location of the enrolled events you have participated in (field 10).
10. Choose your payment method for the event (field 11).
11. Enter the payment amount for the event (field 12).
12. Add any additional notes or comments about your registration (field 13).
13. Enter the title of this form (field 14).
14. Enter a description of the form (field 15).
15. Select the category of the form (field 16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (field 1): Enter the title of the event you are registering for.
* **Attendee Name** (field 2): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (field 3): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (field 4): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (field 5): Select the date of the event you plan to attend.
* **Event Time** (field 6): Select the time of the event you plan to attend.
* **Enrolled Events** (field 7): Choose any events you have previously attended.
* **Enrolled Event Date** (field 8): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Enrolled Event Time** (field 9): Enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Enrolled Event Location** (field 10): Enter the location of the event you attended.
* **Payment Method** (field 11): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (field 12): Enter the amount you paid for the event.
* **Notes** (field 13): Add any comments or questions you have.
* **Form Title** (field 14): Enter a title for this form.
* **Form Description** (field 15): Enter a description of this form.
* **Form Category** (field 16): Select the category of this form.
