# Talent Registration Form For Casting Agency - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration platform for talent to share their profiles with the casting agency. It's used to submit your acting, modeling, or other performance-based experiences, skills, and credentials. By filling out this form, you'll increase your visibility to casting agents who may be interested in considering you for potential projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all required documents and media ready for upload.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
3. For fields marked as `required`, please fill them out.
4. If a field is not required, feel free to leave it out.
5. For fields with upload limitations, be mindful of file size and format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address you have access to.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Acting Experience** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your acting experience, including any notable projects or roles.
* **Headshots** (`6`, `note`, required/optional): Upload up to 5 images in formats jpg, png, gif, or pdf.
* **Reels** (`7`, `note`, required/optional): Upload up to 5 videos in formats mp4, mov, or mpg.
* **Resume** (`8`, `note`, required/optional): Upload up to 5 files in formats pdf, doc, docx, or txt.
* **Submit** (`9`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether to submit your application or cancel the process.
* **More Headshots** (`10`, `note`, required/optional): Upload more images in addition to the headshots field. Note that you are limited to uploading 10 images.
