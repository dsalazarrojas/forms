# Christmas Wish List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a child's preferences, interests, and contact information from their parent or guardian. The collected data will be used to create a personalized Christmas wish list that aligns with the child's tastes and hobbies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the child's information section, which includes their name, age, and grade level.
2. Next, provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Select your child's main hobbies or interests from the list provided.
4. If applicable, provide your child's favorite book series or genre, favorite TV shows or movies, and any special messages or thank you notes you'd like to add.
5. Finally, provide up to three specific gift items your child would like, including their approximate prices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child's Full Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Please provide your child's full name.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your child's age.
* **Grade in School** (`grade`, text, optional): If your child is in school, please enter their grade level.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Main Hobbies or Interests** (`hobbies`, select_multiple, required): Select all the main hobbies or interests your child enjoys.
* **Favorite Book Series or Genre** (`favorite_books`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your child's favorite book series or genre.
* **Favorite TV Shows or Movies** (`favorite_shows`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your child's favorite TV shows or movies.
* **Wish Item 1** (`wish_one`, text, required): Enter a specific gift item your child would like, with an approximate price.
* **Approximate Price** (`wish_one_price`, number, optional): Enter the approximate price of Wish Item 1.
* **Wish Item 2** (`wish_two`, text, required): Enter a specific gift item your child would like, with an approximate price.
* **Approximate Price** (`wish_two_price`, number, optional): Enter the approximate price of Wish Item 2.
* **Wish Item 3** (`wish_three`, text, required): Enter a specific gift item your child would like, with an approximate price.
* **Approximate Price** (`wish_three_price`, number, optional): Enter the approximate price of Wish Item 3.
* **Additional Wish Items (Optional)** (`additional_wishes`, text, optional): If you have additional wish items, enter them as a comma-separated list.
* **Special Message or Thank You** (`special_message`, text, optional): If you'd like to add a special message or thank you note, enter it here.
