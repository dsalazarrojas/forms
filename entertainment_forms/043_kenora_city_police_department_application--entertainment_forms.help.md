# kenora_city_police_department_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows citizens of Kenora City to report incidents or submit general inquiries to the police department. It is an important tool for the department to stay informed and responsive to the needs of its community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the Address and Phone fields.
2. Enter your name in the Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. If you have any additional comments or details to share, provide them in the Notes field.
5. If this incident or inquiry is related to a specific date, enter it in the Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your full address, including street number, apartment number (if applicable), and postal code.
* **Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that the police department can contact you for further clarification or action.
* **Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so that the police department can contact you via email if necessary.
* **Notes** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or details about your incident or inquiry.
* **Date** (`date`, required/optional): If this incident or inquiry is related to a specific date, enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing information in the Notes field.
* If you are reporting an incident, please provide as much information as possible about what happened.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the police department for assistance.
