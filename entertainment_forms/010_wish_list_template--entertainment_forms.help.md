# wish_list_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wish_list_template is a form designed to collect personal wishes or preferences for a specific category. This form is intended to be used in an entertainment setting, such as birthday parties or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on "Add Item" to add a new item to your wish list.
2. Enter your wish or preference in the text field provided.
3. Choose a category for your wish from the "category" field. You can select one category only.
4. Select any applicable sub-categories from the "sub_category" field. You can select multiple sub-categories.
5. Review your completed form to ensure everything is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **wish_list** (`wish_list`, text, required): Enter your personal wish or preference in this field.
* **category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose a category for your wish from the list provided.
* **sub_category** (`sub_category`, select_multiple, required): Select any applicable sub-categories for your wish from the list provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to be specific and concise in your wishes to help us understand your preferences.
* If you have any further details or comments, you can add them in the wish list field.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
