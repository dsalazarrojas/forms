# Music DVDs Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for music DVD requests. It includes fields for music format, favorite music genres, favorite music artists, music DVD count, price per music DVD, shipping details, and order date. The form is used to process music DVD orders and ensure accurate shipping and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct music format (e.g., VHS, DVD, CD, or MP3).
3. Indicate if you have a music store (yes/no).
4. Choose your favorite music genres and artists (separate with commas).
5. Enter the number of music DVDs you wish to purchase.
6. Choose the price per music DVD.
7. Select the price currency (e.g., USD, EUR, or JPY).
8. Enter special requests, if any.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Indicate if you have a shipping address (yes/no).
12. Choose the shipping method (e.g., USPS, UPS, or FedEx).
13. Choose the shipping address (yes/no).
14. Enter shipping notes, if any.
15. Enter the music DVD shipping cost.
16. Enter the music DVD tax.
17. Enter the music DVD total cost.
18. Enter the music DVD total amount.
19. Select the music DVD status (e.g., pending, shipped, or received).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Has Music Store**: Indicates if you have a music store or not. (required: false)
* **Music Format**: Selects the music format you're interested in. (required: false)
*   - VHS (Video Home System)
*   - DVD (Digital Versatile Disc)
*   - CD (Compact Disc)
*   - MP3 (MPEG Audio Layer 3)
* **Favorite Music Genres**: Selects your favorite music genres, separating with commas. (required: true)
* **Favorite Music Artists**: Selects your favorite music artists, separating with commas. (required: true)
* **Number of Music DVDs**: Enters the number of music DVDs you wish to purchase. (required: false)
* **Price Per Music DVD**: Enters the price per music DVD. (required: false)
* **Price Currency**: Selects the price currency. (required: true)
* **Special Requests**: Enters any special requests, if applicable. (required: false)
* **Email**: Enters your email address. (required: true)
* **Phone**: Enters your phone number. (required: true)
* **Music DVD Shipping Method**: Selects the shipping method you prefer. (required: true)
*   - USPS (United States Postal Service)
*   - UPS (United Parcel Service)
*   - FedEx (Federal Express)
* **Music DVD Shipping Notes**: Enters any shipping notes, if applicable. (required: false)
* **Order Date**: Enters the order date. (required: true)
* **Music DVD Shipping Cost**: Enters the music DVD shipping cost. (required: true)
* **Music DVD Tax**: Enters the music DVD tax. (required: true)
* **Music DVD Total Cost**: Calculates the music DVD total cost. (required: true)
* **Music DVD Total Amount**: Calculates the music DVD total amount. (required: true)
* **Music DVD Status**: Selects the music DVD status. (required: true)
*   - pending
*   - shipped
*   - received
