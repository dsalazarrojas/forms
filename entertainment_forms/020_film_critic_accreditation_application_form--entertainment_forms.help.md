# Film Critic Accreditation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Critic Accreditation Application Form is designed to collect information about your experience, roles, and media credentials in the film industry. This information will be reviewed to determine your eligibility for accreditation as a film critic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your media details, if applicable.
2. Select your roles in the film industry (e.g. film critic, journalist, or reviewer).
3. Provide a brief description of your experience in the film industry.
4. Share a link to your review portfolio, if you have one.
5. Describe your experience with film festivals, if applicable.
6. Select your primary identity as a member of the press (e.g. film critic, journalist, or reviewer).
7. Enter your press card information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Details**: If you are a member of the press, please provide your media outlet(s) and your role within that outlet.
* **Roles**: Select all the roles that apply to you in the film industry (e.g. film critic, journalist, or reviewer). This will help us understand your areas of expertise.
* **Experience**: Please provide a brief description of your experience in the film industry. This can include the length of time you have been working in the industry, notable reviews or articles you have written, etc.
* **Review Portfolio**: If you have a public online presence, such as a blog or website, where your reviews are published, please share a link to your portfolio.
* **Film Festival Experience**: If you have experience attending film festivals, please describe your experience in a few sentences.
* **Press Identity**: Please select your primary identity as a member of the press (e.g. film critic, journalist, or reviewer).
* **Press Card**: If you have a press card from a reputable industry organization, please enter the information here.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to us for guidance.
* Duplicate submissions will be automatically rejected.
* We will review your application carefully and respond as soon as possible.
