# Jazz Festival Performance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for jazz musicians who want to apply for a spot at the jazz festival. The festival organizers will use the information provided to review and select performers for the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Performer Information page with your name, contact information, and any other details you'd like to share about yourself.
2. On the Performance Details page, provide information about your music style, experience, and any special requirements you may have (e.g., instruments, equipment needs).
3. In the Availability page, tell us about your availability for the festival, including specific dates and times you are available to perform.
4. In the Confirmation page, please confirm that you have read and understand the festival rules and guidelines.
5. Finally, fill out the Submission Info page with any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performer Information** (`performer_info`, text, required/optional): This is the page where you can introduce yourself and share any additional information about your background and experience as a performer.
* **Performance Details** (`performance_details`, text, required/optional): Here, you can describe your music style, including the type of jazz music you perform (e.g., swing, blues, jazz standards), and provide any other relevant details about your performance.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required/optional): Please list the specific dates and times you are available to perform during the festival. This will help us schedule your performance.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, text, required/optional): Check this box to confirm that you have read and understood the festival rules and guidelines.
* **Submission Info** (`submission_info`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to add them here.
