# Movie Night Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Movie Night Scheduling Form is designed to provide essential details for movie night events. This form serves as a central location for collecting information about the selected movie, its schedule, location, and host details. By submitting this form, you'll be helping the organizers in creating an enjoyable and well-organized movie night for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by providing the following information:
    *   Fill out the "Movie Night Details" field with a short description of the movie night event.
    *   In the "Movie Location" field, specify where the movie night will take place.
    *   Choose one or more "Movie Schedule" options that fit your event. This could be 7:00 PM, 8:00 PM, or 9:00 PM.
    *   In the "Host Details" field, provide the host's name and contact information so that attendees can reach out with questions or concerns.
    *   If desired, describe the movie in the "Movie Description" field to give attendees a better understanding of the film.
    *   Choose the "Movie Genre" that best fits the selected movie.
    *   You may also enter the movie's ratings and number of votes in the "Movie Ratings" and "Movie Votes" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Movie Night Details** (movie_night_details, text, required): Enter a brief description of the movie night event.
*   **Movie Location** (movie_location, text, required: false): Enter the location where the movie night will take place.
*   **Movie Schedule** (movie_schedule, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more schedules for the movie night.
*   **Host Details** (host_details, text, required): Enter the host's name and contact information so attendees can get in touch with them.
*   **Movie Description** (movie_description, text, required: false): Describe the movie in a short paragraph to give attendees a better understanding.
*   **Movie Genre** (movie_genre, select_one, required): Choose the movie genre that best fits the selected movie.
*   **Movie Ratings** (movie_ratings, number, required: false): Enter the movie ratings if desired.
*   **Movie Votes** (movie_votes, number, required: false): Enter the number of movie votes.
