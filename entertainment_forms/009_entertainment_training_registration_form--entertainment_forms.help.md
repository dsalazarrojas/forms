# Entertainment Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Training Registration Form is used to collect information from participants about the workshop they plan to attend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter a workshop name.
3. Enter a valid workshop date.
4. Enter your name and email address (if provided).
5. Select a valid workshop location.
6. Choose a workshop duration.
7. Select a valid workshop price.
8. Select a tool assigned to you (if applicable).
9. Confirm that you have been assigned a tool (if applicable).
10. Select your participant status.
11. Provide any additional comments or notes.
