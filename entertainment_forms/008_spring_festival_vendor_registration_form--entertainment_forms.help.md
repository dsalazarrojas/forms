# spring-festival-vendor-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for vendors to register for the Spring Festival, a popular entertainment event. It collects general information, vendor application details, and electronic signatures to confirm vendor participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information available.
2. Fill in your general information in page 1, including your name and any relevant details.
3. In page 2, provide your vendor application, including a brief description of your product or service.
4. On page 3, select your electronic signature option to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
5. On page 4, select your contact information preferences, including whether you want to be contacted by email, phone, or both.
6. If you select "Other" as your contact method, please provide additional details in the "Contact-Info-Other" field.
7. Finally, confirm your electronic signature by selecting the date you signed this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)**: This page collects general information about you as a vendor. Please fill in your name and any relevant details you feel are necessary for us to know.
* **Vendor Application (Page 2)**: Here, you can provide a brief description of your product or service. This will help us understand what you are offering at the festival.
* **E-Signature (Page 3)**: Please select one of the two options to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions. This is a requirement for vendor participation.
* **Contact-Info (Page 4)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted by selecting one or more of the options: Email, Phone, or Both. If you select "Other", please provide further details in the "Contact-Info-Other" field.
* **Contact-Info-Other (Page 4)**: This field is only required if you selected "Other" as your contact method. Here, you can provide any additional information about your preferred contact method.
* **E-Signature-Confirm (Page 4)**: Date of when you electronically signed this form.

### Tips
* Please fill in all required fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
* Ensure that your electronic signature is accurate and reflects your actual signature.
