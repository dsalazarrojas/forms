# Bucket List Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a person's bucket list, which is a list of experiences and goals they want to achieve in their lifetime.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Select your age range in the "Age Range" field.
4. Enter the top place you want to visit on your bucket list in the "Top Place You Want to Visit" field.
5. Describe why you want to visit this place in the "Why Do You Want to Visit This Place" field.
6. Choose a dream food experience in the "Dream Food Experience" field.
7. Select an adventure activity you want to try in the "Adventure Activity You Want to Try" field.
8. List a new skill or hobby you want to learn in the "New Skill or Hobby to Learn" field.
9. Choose a book you want to read in the "Book to Read" field.
10. Select a movie or show to watch in the "Movie or Show to Watch" field.
11. Enter a personal goal you want to achieve in the "Personal Goal to Achieve" field.
12. Select your experience priorities in the "Experience Priorities" field.
13. Choose when you want to accomplish these goals in the "Bucket List Timeline" field.
14. Estimate your budget for major goals in the "Estimated Budget" field.
15. List any support or resources you need to achieve your goals in the "Support or Resources Needed" field.
16. Describe what inspires your bucket list in the "What Inspires Your Bucket List" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`person_name`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email Address** (`person_email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted about your bucket list.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, optional): Select your age range to help us better understand your priorities.
* **Top Place You Want to Visit** (`place_visit`, required): Choose a place you've always wanted to visit.
* **Why Do You Want to Visit This Place** (`reason_visit`, optional): Describe why this place is important to you.
* **Dream Food Experience** (`food_experience`, optional): Choose a food experience you've always wanted to try.
* **Adventure Activity You Want to Try** (`adventure_activity`, optional): Select an adventure activity you've always wanted to try.
* **New Skill or Hobby to Learn** (`skill_to_learn`, optional): List a new skill or hobby you want to learn.
* **Book to Read** (`book_to_read`, optional): Choose a book you've always wanted to read.
* **Movie or Show to Watch** (`movie_to_watch`, optional): Select a movie or show you've always wanted to watch.
* **Personal Goal to Achieve** (`personal_goal`, optional): Enter a personal goal you want to achieve.
* **Experience Priorities** (`experience_priority`, optional): Select what matters most to you.
* **Bucket List Timeline** (`timeline_vision`, optional): Choose when you want to accomplish these goals.
* **Estimated Budget** (`budget_estimate`, optional): Estimate your budget for major goals.
* **Support or Resources Needed** (`support_needed`, optional): List any support or resources you need.
* **What Inspires Your Bucket List** (`inspiration_source`, optional): Describe what inspires your bucket list.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* You can always come back and update your answers later if you change your priorities.
* This form is for your own personal information, so feel free to be as creative and honest as you want.
