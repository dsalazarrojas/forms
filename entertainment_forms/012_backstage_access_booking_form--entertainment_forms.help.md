# Backstage Access Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Backstage Access Booking Form is a tool for event organizers to collect essential information from individuals seeking backstage access to an event. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are gathered for efficient and secure access arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2.  Review each section carefully to ensure you've provided all necessary details.
3.  Submit the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1.  **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required)
    *   Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2.  **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required)
    *   Provide a valid email address for communication and updates.

### 3.  **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, required)
    *   Enter your phone number for efficient communication.

### 4.  **Organization/Employer** (`organization`, `text`, optional)
    *   If applicable, provide your organization or employer's name.

### 5.  **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional)
    *   If applicable, provide your job title.

### 6.  **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required)
    *   Enter the name of the event you're requesting backstage access for.

### 7.  **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required)
    *   Enter the date of the event you're requesting access for.

### 8.  **Venue Name** (`event_venue`, `text`, required)
    *   Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.

### 9.  **Type of Access Requested** (`access_type`, `select_one`, required)
    *   Choose the type of access you're requesting (e.g., Backstage Pass, VIP Pass, etc.).

### 10. **Number of Passes Needed** (`number_of_passes`, `integer`, required)
    *   Specify the number of passes you require.

### 11. **Names of Additional Guests (if applicable)** (`additional_guests`, `text`, optional)
    *   If you're accompanying others, list their names.

### 12. **Purpose of Backstage Access** (`purpose`, `text`, required)
    *   Explain the purpose of your backstage access request.

### 13. **Relevant Credentials or Affiliations** (`credentials`, `text`, optional)
    *   If applicable, provide any relevant credentials or affiliations.

### 14.  **Event Policies and Safety Guidelines** (`waiver_agreement`, `select_one`, required)
    *   Confirm that you agree to all event policies and safety guidelines.

### 15.  **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required)
    *   Confirm that you acknowledge the liability waiver.

### 16.  **Photography and Recording Consent** (`photo_release`, `select_one`, required)
    *   Confirm that you consent to photography and recording.

### 17.  **Accessibility or Special Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional)
    *   If applicable, provide any accessibility or special needs information.

### 18.  **Dietary Restrictions (if applicable)** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional)
    *   If applicable, list any dietary restrictions.

### 19.  **Additional Information or Requests** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional)
    *   Provide any additional information or requests.

### 20.  **Confirm Accuracy and Completeness** (`booking_confirmation`, `select_one`, required)
    *   Confirm that the provided information is accurate and complete.

## Tips
*   Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   Review each section carefully before submission.
*   If you're unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.
*   This form is a critical part of the backstage access request process, so please fill it out thoroughly and accurately.
