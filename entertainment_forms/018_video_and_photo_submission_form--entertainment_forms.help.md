# Video And Photo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users for entertainment-related purposes, such as events, contests, or showcases.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form fields and their corresponding hints to understand the type of information required.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can enter any relevant information about your submission.
* **Video Upload** (`video_upload`, time, required: false)
	+ A field for uploading a video file. Please ensure that the file is in a supported format (e.g., MP4, AVI, etc.).
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, text, required: false)
	+ A field for uploading a photo file. Please ensure that the file is in a supported format (e.g., JPEG, PNG, etc.).
* **Description** (`description_input`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can enter a brief description of your submission.
* **Category Select** (`category_select_one`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select the category that best fits your submission from the options provided (Entertainment, Sports, Music).
* **Tags** (`tags_input`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field where you can enter any relevant tags or keywords related to your submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false)
	+ A field for selecting the submission date. Please use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Video Duration** (`video_duration_input`, number, required: false)
	+ A field for entering the video duration in seconds.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, required: false)
	+ A field for uploading any additional files related to your submission.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, required: false)
	+ A field for entering your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, required: false)
	+ A text field for entering your phone number.
* **Note** (`note_input`, note, required: false)
	+ A field for entering any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select multiple options from the provided list (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Description Select Multiple** (`description_select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select multiple options from the provided list (Yes, No).

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and files to complete the form accurately.
- Use the provided hints as a guide to complete each field.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the form administrator or support team.
