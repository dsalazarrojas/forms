# Virtual Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a virtual workshop. Please fill out the form to provide your personal details and be part of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the registration process.
2. Fill out each field carefully, and make sure to enter the required information.
3. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name to identify yourself. This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide your first name for identification purposes.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications about the virtual workshop. This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide your email address for communication purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to provide an alternative contact method. This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide your phone number for further communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* Review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* If you're having trouble filling out the form, please let us know, and we'll be happy to assist you.
