# Halloween Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals invited to a Halloween potluck event. It helps the event organizers gather the necessary information to plan and host the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** with the correct information.
2. Decide whether you will attend the event by selecting **Will you come?** with the corresponding option (1, 2, or 3).
3. If you will attend, specify how many guests you will bring by selecting options for **RSVP 2**.
4. Enter a brief description of the **Dish to Bring** you plan to contribute.
5. If applicable, specify your **First Dish** and **Second Dish** you will bring.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name, as it should be spelled out in full (e.g., John).
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name, as it should be spelled out in full (e.g., Smith).
- **Email**: Enter your email address that can be used to contact you.
- **Will you come?**: Decide whether you will attend the event by selecting the corresponding option:
  • 1: Yes, I will attend
  • 2: No, but I may be able to contribute a dish
  • 3: No, I will not attend
- **RSVP 2**: If you select option 1 for **Will you come?**, specify how many guests you will bring (1, 2, or 3).
- **Dish to Bring**: Enter a brief description of the dish you will bring, for example, "pumpkin pie" or "vegetable salad".
- **First Dish**: If you bring a dish, specify the first dish name, for example, "pumpkin pie".
- **Second Dish**: If you bring a dish, specify the second dish name, for example, "sugar cookies".

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help the event organizers plan the event correctly.
- If you are unable to bring a dish, please let us know so we can plan accordingly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers at [Contact email].
