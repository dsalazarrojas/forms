# Wedding Dress Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Dress Quiz is a form designed to help you discover your ideal wedding dress style based on your preferences. Answer a series of questions about your style, and we'll guide you through a wedding dress style that suits you best.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply select your preferences for each question and submit your answers. Each question will guide you through your desired wedding dress style.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Wedding Dress Style**
* **A-Line**: A classic and elegant style that falls in line with your body shape.
* **Ball Gown**: A traditional, full, and fairytale-like style with a fitted bodice and a full skirt.
* **Mermaid**: A fitted, flared style that hugs your body from the hips to your ankles.

### 2. **Wedding Dress Color**
* **Beige**: A natural, earthy color that complements most skin tones.
* **Blue**: A cool and calming color that can evoke a sense of calmness.
* **Green**: A vibrant and natural color that brings a sense of life to your wedding dress.
* **Pink**: A sweet and feminine color that adds a touch of romance to your look.
* **White**: A classic and pure color that symbolizes purity and innocence.

### 3. **Wedding Dress Neckline**
* **Sweetheart**: A delicate and romantic neckline that adds a touch of charm to your look.
* **Round**: A soft and rounded neckline that adds a touch of elegance to your style.
* **V-Neck**: A classic and versatile neckline that suits most body types.
* **Square**: A bold and modern neckline that adds a touch of edginess to your look.

### 4. **Wedding Dress Sleeve**
* **Cap**: A short and fitted sleeve that provides coverage and style.
* **No Sleeve**: A sleeveless design that adds a touch of freedom and elegance to your look.
* **(Multiple selection options will be displayed in a dropdown)**

### 5. **Wedding Dress Length**
* **Floor Length**: A long and flowing dress that drapes beautifully around your body.
* **Tea Length**: A mid-length dress that falls just above the knee.
* **Knee Length**: A short and stylish dress that falls just above the knee.

### 6. **Wedding Dress Beads**
* **No Beads**: A simple and understated design with no beading.
* **Beaded**: A dress with beading that adds a touch of elegance and sophistication.
* **Beads with Flowers**: A dress with beading and floral patterns that adds a touch of romance and whimsy.

### 7. **Wedding Dress Sash**
* **No Sash**: A simple and classic design without a sash.
* **With Sash**: A dress with a sash that adds a touch of elegance and sophistication.
* **With Ribbon**: A dress with a ribbon sash that adds a touch of playfulness and charm.

### 8. **Wedding Dress Bodice**
* **No Bodice**: A simple and relaxed design without a bodice.
* **With Delicates**: A dress with a delicate and lacy bodice that adds a touch of elegance and sophistication.
* **With Brocade**: A dress with a brocade bodice that adds a touch of luxury and refinement.

### 9. **Wedding Dress Hem**
* **Hand-Embroidered**: A hem that is delicately embroidered for a touch of elegance.
* **Machine Embroidered**: A hem that is machine-embroidered for a more modern look.
* **No Hem**: A simple and classic design without a hem.

### 10. **Additional Comments**
* Enter any additional comments or details about your desired wedding dress style.
