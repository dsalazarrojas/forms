# Holiday Party Song Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Party Song Request Form is a simple tool for party organizers to collect song requests from attendees. By filling out this form, you can help create a playlist that fits the party vibe and mood.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the **Song Selection** section to choose a song that fits the party vibe.
* Select the **Mood** and **Style** for the party.
* Choose whether or not to submit your song selection.
* Provide your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** if you want to be contacted about the party details.
* Enter the **Event Date** and **Event Time** if you're hosting an event.
* Select the **Event Location** and provide any **Event Details** if you're hosting an event.
* Click the **Submit** button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Song Selection** (`song_selection`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select a song that fits the party vibe.
* **Song Genre** (`song_genre`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a genre of music to set the mood for the party.
* **Mood** (`mood`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the mood you want to set for the party.
* **Song Style** (`song_style`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the style of music you'd like to hear.
* **Submit Your Song** (`submit_song`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Decide whether or not to submit your song selection for the party playlist.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to submit your form and have your song selection added to the party playlist.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about party details.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about party details.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event you're hosting.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event you're hosting.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether or not you're hosting the event.
* **Submit Event** (`submit_event`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Decide whether or not to submit event details for the party playlist.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional details about the event you're hosting.
