# st_patricks_day_parade_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The st_patricks_day_parade_entry_form is a form designed for participants and performers to register for the St. Patrick's Day Parade. This form gathers essential information about the participant/performer, parade details, sponsor information, and other relevant details. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly will ensure a successful entry experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Provide your participant details in the **Participant Details** section.
2.  Enter your parade details in the **Parade Details** section.
3.  Complete the **Performer Details** section with any necessary information about your performance.
4.  If you are a performer, please ensure you fill out this section thoroughly.
5.  Add any sponsor information in the **Sponsor Details** section.
6.  A brief description of your entry in 500 characters or less can be provided in the **Review** section.
7.  If you are a performer, please note that this section is optional.
8.  Enter the contact person's details in the **Contact Person** section.
9.  Select "Yes" or "No" in the **Agreement** section to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms.
10. Finally, provide any additional information in the **Additional Info** section (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter your personal details as a participant.
*  **Parade Details** (`parade_details`, text, required: false): Enter the details about the parade you are participating in.
*  **Performer Details** (`performer_details`, text, required: true): This section is required for performers to include all necessary information about your performance.
*  **Sponsor Details** (`sponsor_details`, text, required: false): If you have a sponsor, enter their details here.
*  **Review** (`review`, text, required: false): A brief description of your entry in 500 characters or less.
*  **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's details.
*  **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required: true): Select "Yes" to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms.
*  **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information that may be helpful.
