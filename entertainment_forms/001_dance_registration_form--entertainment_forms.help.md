# Dance Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather registration information from individuals who wish to participate in a dance-related event. Please note that this form is not intended for general contact or feedback, but rather for specific event registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select a dance category that best represents the type of dance you will be performing.
2. Enter your name in the text field provided.
3. Provide your email address in the email field. This is the address that will be used for communication regarding the event.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field. This will be used for any last-minute event updates or notifications.
5. If you have any additional comments or information you would like to provide, you can enter them in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select a dance category** (Select One): Choose the category that best represents the type of dance you will be performing. This is a selection of three options: Dance Category 1, Dance Category 2, and Dance Category 3.
* **Registrant name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or driver's license. This is a required field and is used to verify your identity.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address. This is the address that will be used for communication regarding the event. This is a required field.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number. This will be used for any last-minute event updates or notifications. This is an optional field.
* **Comments** (Note): If you have any additional comments or information you would like to provide, you can enter them here. This is an optional field.
