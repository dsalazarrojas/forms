# Music Event Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about performers' and attendees' availability for music events. It is intended to be filled out by event organizers, managers, or other stakeholders to get accurate information about who will be available during specific events. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Music Event Availability Survey" link to access the form.
*   Enter the performer's name and select it from the list.
*   Enter the event details such as the start and end dates and times. 
*   Input the performer's email and other required details.
*   Choose the attendee's name and email, then select their arrival status as early, late, or not applicable.
*   If the attendee is not attending, select 'False'.
*   Optionally add any additional notes about the event or attendee's status.
*   Click "Submit" to complete and send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Performer Name**: Enter the name of the musician or performer that will be participating in the event.
*   **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event for which the performer will be participating.
*   **Start Date** (and **End Date**): Select the date and date for which the performer will be available during the event. 
*   **Start Time** (and **End Time**): Choose the start and end times for which the performer will be performing.
*   **Performer Email**: Enter the email of the musician or performer.
*   **Attendee Name**: Enter the name of the person that will be attending the event.
*   **Attendee Email**: Enter the email of the person that will be attending the event.
*   **Is Early Arrival**: Indicate if the attendee will arrive early during the event.
*   **Is Late Arrival**: Indicate if the attendee will arrive late during the event (if not applicable, select 'Not Applicable').
*   **Is Absent**: Indicate if the attendee will not be attending the event (if not applicable, select 'False').
*   **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the event or attendee's status. 

## Tips
*   Be accurate in your responses as they will be used for planning and organization purposes.
*   Double-check your input for correctness before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out before clicking "Submit".
*   If you need to add or change any fields in the future, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
