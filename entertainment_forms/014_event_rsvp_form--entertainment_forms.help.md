# event_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event rsvp form is a tool used to collect RSVP (response to an invitation) information from attendees for upcoming events. It helps event organizers manage their guest lists, ensure accurate headcounts, and communicate with attendees about event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **email** address in the second field.
3. Enter your **contact number** in the third field.
4. Select a date for the event in the **date** field.
5. Choose a time for the event in the **time** field.
6. Select a date for your RSVP response in the **RSVP Date** field.
7. Optionally, add a brief **RSVP Message** if you have any special requests or comments.
8. Choose the **Event Details** for your attendance by selecting one of the provided options (e.g., "Event 1", "Event 2", "Event 3").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the event guest list.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address so that event organizers can contact you with updates and details.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date**: Select the date of the event you will be attending.
* **Time**: Choose the time of the event you will be attending.
* **RSVP Date**: Choose the date you are responding to the event invitation by.
* **RSVP Message**: Add any special requests or comments you have regarding your attendance (optional).
* **Event Details**: Select the event you will be attending from the provided options.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your RSVP is recorded accurately.
* If you have any special requests or comments, include them in the RSVP Message field.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure it is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you are unable to attend, please let event organizers know and they will remove you from the guest list.
* If you have any issues completing the form, contact event organizers for assistance.
