# Outdoor Movie Night Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Movie Night Registration Form is designed to collect information for outdoor movie night events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the event name in the `Event Name` field.
2. Enter your first name and last name in the `First Name` and `Last Name` fields respectively.
3. Provide your email address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Choose a specific time for the event in the `Time` field.
6. Select the date of the event in the `Date` field.
7. Provide the title of the movie being shown in the `Movie Title` field.
8. Choose whether or not the participant has a preference for a specific genre of movies in the `Genre` field.
9. Enter the number of seats you would like to reserve in the `Seats` field.
10. If you have any special instructions or requests, enter them in the `Special Instructions` field.
11. Choose any extra services you would like to have for the event in the `Extra Services` field.
12. Select the type of ticket you would like to purchase in the `Ticket Type` field.
13. Enter the price of the ticket in the `Ticket Price` field.
14. Choose whether your ticket is active or inactive in the `Payment Status` field.
15. Select the payment method used to purchase the ticket in the `Payment Method` field.
16. Enter the date of the payment in the `Payment Date` field.
17. Select whether you are an individual or an organization in the `Participant Type` field.
18. Choose your participant status in the `Participant Status` field.
19. Enter your participant name in the `Participant Name` field.
20. Provide your participant email address in the `Participant Email` field.
21. Enter your participant phone number in the `Participant Phone` field.
22. If you have any notes for your participant information, enter them in the `Participant Notes` field.
23. Enter the date of registration in the `Registration Date` field.
24. If you have any comments, enter them in the `Comments` field.
