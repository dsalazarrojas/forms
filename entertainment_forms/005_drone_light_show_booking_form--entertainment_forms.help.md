# Drone Light Show Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage information about drone light shows, including event details, drone specifications, and user contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Show Details" section with the required information about the event.
2. In the "Drone Details" section, select the drone model and color that will be used for the show.
3. Provide your contact information in the "User Details" section.
4. Fill in the "Event Details" section with the event date, time, duration, location, and capacity.
5. Choose the event type, status, and tags in the corresponding sections.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Show Details**: Enter a brief description of the drone light show event.
* **Drone Details**:
	+ **Drone Model**: Choose the model of the drone that will be used, such as Mavic 2, Mavic 3, Mavic Mini, Mavic Air, or select "Other" to specify a different model.
	+ **Drone Color**: Select the color of the drone that will be used, such as Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
* **User Details**:
	+ **Name**: Enter your name.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Details**:
	+ **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
	+ **Event Time**: Enter the start and end time of the event.
	+ **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event.
	+ **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
	+ **Event Capacity**: Enter the maximum number of people that can attend the event.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event, such as Light Flight, Light Show, or Drone Demo.
* **Event Status**: Choose the status of the event, such as Upcoming, Past, or Cancelled.
* **Event Tags**: Select any tags that apply to the event, such as Tag 1, Tag 2, or Tag 3.
* **Show Tags**: Select any tags that apply to the show, such as Tag 1, Tag 2, or Tag 3.
* **Show Name**: Enter a name for the show.
* **Show Description**: Enter a description of the show.
* **User Tags**: Select any tags that apply to you as a user, such as Tag 1, Tag 2, or Tag 3.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
* **Show Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the show.
* **Show Rating**: Rate the show on a scale of 1-5.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Date Range**: Enter the date range of the event.

Please note that some fields may not be required, depending on the specific use case. Always review the requirements for each field before submitting the form.
