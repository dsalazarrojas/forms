# Virtual Concert Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Concert Registration Form is used to collect information from users for virtual concert events. This includes user details, concert specifics, and payment preferences. The information collected helps us plan and execute the virtual concerts successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Full Name**.
2. Enter your **Email Address**.
3. Input your **Contact Number**.
4. Specify the **Concert Date** you plan to attend.
5. Choose a **Concert Time** that suits you.
6. Enter the **Concert Duration** you expect.
7. Provide **Concert Details** about the event.
8. Enter the **Ticket Price** for the virtual concert.
9. Specify the **Number of Tickets** you require.
10. Confirm that you are **Attending** the virtual concert.
11. Choose your preferred **Payment Method**.
12. Enter any **Payment Details** if applicable.
13. Leave **Additional Comments** if you need to mention anything important.
14. Confirm that you have reviewed the **Concert Details** correctly.
15. Select the correct **Confirmation of Concert Details** that match your understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required/optional): This is the name you want to be associated with your concert registration.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required/optional): Your email address will be used for communication and updates about the virtual concert.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number for further communication and updates.
* **Concert Date** (`concert_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you plan to attend the virtual concert.
* **Concert Time** (`concert_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time you plan to watch the virtual concert.
* **Concert Duration** (`concert_duration`, text, required/optional): Enter the duration of the virtual concert you plan to attend.
* **Concert Details** (`concert_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any specific details about the virtual concert you want to attend.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of the ticket you are paying for.
* **Number of Tickets** (`number_of_tickets`, number, required/optional): Specify how many tickets you require for the virtual concert.
* **Are You Attending** (`is_attending`, select one, required/optional): Confirm if you are attending the virtual concert or not.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, required/optional): Choose your preferred method of payment (Credit Card, PayPal, Cash).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional payment details if applicable.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Leave any additional comments you need to mention.
* **Confirm Concert Details** (`confirm_concert_details`, select multiple, required/optional): Confirm that you have correctly reviewed the concert details.
* **Confirmation of Concert Details** (`confirmation_of_concert_details`, select multiple, required/optional): Select all the details that match your understanding of the virtual concert.
