# Christmas Light Removal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from customers who need their Christmas lights removed. It's intended for customers who have previously installed Christmas lights on their properties and are now seeking assistance with removal. The information collected will help the service provider schedule and prepare for the removal process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your property address, including the city and state.
3. Choose the type of property you have (e.g., single-family home, townhouse, etc.).
4. Select the areas to be covered during the removal process (e.g., roof lights, gutter lights, etc.).
5. Specify the approximate total coverage area of your property.
6. If applicable, provide the number of stories on your property.
7. Choose a preferred removal date and time frame (morning, afternoon, evening, or flexible).
8. Specify how you would like to store the removed lights (e.g., store them, have them stored at our facility, etc.).
9. If applicable, make any special requests or specify any damage to the lights.
10. Finally, provide any special instructions or notes about accessing your property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official identification. 
* **Last Name**: Your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address. This is where we will communicate with you about the removal process.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number. This is an optional field but helps us reach you if we need to discuss the details.
* **Property Address**: Your property's physical address. Please enter this in the correct format (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345).
* **City**: The city where your property is located.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where your property is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: The postal code for your property.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you have (e.g., Single family home, Townhome, etc.).
* **Area to be Covered**: Select all the areas that need to be covered during the removal process.
* **Approximate Total Coverage Area**: Estimate the total coverage area of your property in square feet or acres.
* **Number of Stories**: If your property has multiple stories, please specify the number.
* **Preferred Removal Date**: Choose a date for the removal process. Please be flexible if we cannot accommodate your first choice.
* **Preferred Time Frame**: Choose a time frame for the removal process (morning, afternoon, evening, or flexible).
* **Storage of Lights**: Specify how you would like to store the removed lights.
* **Are any lights damaged or broken?**: If applicable, specify if any of the lights are damaged or broken.
* **Safe Location for Storage**: If you have a specific safe location for storing the removed lights, please specify.
* **Special Instructions**: If there are any special instructions or requests, please specify here.
* **How will our team access the property?**: Specify how our team should access your property during the removal process (e.g., be present, leave a key, etc.).
* **Special Requests**: If you have any other special requests, please specify here.
