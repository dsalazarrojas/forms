# Housewarming RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Housewarming RSVP Form is designed to collect guest information and track attendance for a housewarming party. This form helps hosts gather essential contact details, party preferences, and RSVP status from invitees.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has multiple sections, and you will need to complete each section as follows:

1. **Host Name**: Enter the name of the person hosting the party.
2. **Host Email**: Enter the email address of the person hosting the party.
3. **Date**: Select the date of the party.
4. **Time**: Select the time of the party.
5. **Location**: Enter the location of the party.
6. **RSVP By**: Select the date by which you need to RSVP.
7. **Name**: Enter your name.
8. **Email**: Enter your email address.
9. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
10. **Attending**: Select which guests are attending the party.
11. **Message**: If you'd like to include any additional comments or notes for the host, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Host Name**: Enter the name of the person hosting the party.
* **Host Email**: Enter the email address of the person hosting the party.
* **Date**: Select the date of the party from the calendar.
* **Time**: Select the time of the party from the available options.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the party.
* **RSVP By**: Select the date by which you need to RSVP by.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure the host can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to provide easy communication.
* **Attending**: Select all guests who are attending the party.
* **Message**: Include any additional notes or comments for the host.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Double-check your RSVP status to ensure you are attending the party correctly.
* Use the provided date and time to confirm your attendance.
* If you have any special requests or needs, include them in the **Message** field.
* Don't forget to include your phone number to provide easy communication.
