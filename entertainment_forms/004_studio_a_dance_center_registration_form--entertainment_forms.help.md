# Studio A Dance Center Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Studio A Dance Center Registration Form is used to collect information from clients registering for our dance classes. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences, ensuring that we can provide the best experience for our students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in your **First Name** in the designated field.
2. Next, enter your **Last Name** in the field provided.
3. Please enter your **Email** address for contact and communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone** number to reach you in case of emergencies.
5. If applicable, enter your **Student Name** if you are registering a student.
6. Enter the **Parent Email** of the student, if applicable.
7. Provide the **Emergency Contact** information, including their name and phone number.
8. Select the **Class Days** you wish to attend.
9. Choose the **Class Time** that suits your schedule.
10. Choose a **Payment Type** from the options provided, such as Full, Drop-in, or Session.
11. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, or Other.
12. Add any additional **Notes** about your registration.
13. Select the type of **Student** you are (Full Time, Part Time, Private, or Trial).
14. Choose the level of **Class Level** you wish to enroll in (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
15. Select the **Dance Style** you would like to learn (Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop).
16. If you have an **Emergency Contact Relationship**, please specify it.
17. Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone** number.
18. Enter the **Emergency Contact Email**.
19. Select the **Payment Status** of your registration (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
20. Choose the current **Class Status** of your registration (Active, Pending, Canceled).
21. Enter your **Student ID** if you have one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your name as it should be spelled.
* **Last Name**: Surname or family name.
* **Email**: Used for contact and communication with us.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for us to reach you.
* **Student Name**: If you are enrolling a student, enter their name here.
* **Parent Email**: If you are enrolling a student, enter the parent's email here.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter their name and phone number for emergency purposes.
* **Class Days**: Choose the days of the week you wish to attend classes.
* **Class Time**: Select the time that suits your schedule.
* **Payment Type**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your classes (Full, Drop-in, Session).
* **Payment Method**: Select how you wish to pay (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Other).
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or questions you might have.
* **Student Type**: Select your student type (Full Time, Part Time, Private, Trial).
* **Class Level**: Choose the level of classes you wish to join (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Dance Style**: Select the dance style you prefer (Ballet, Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter their email address.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
* **Class Status**: Select the status of your class registration (Active, Pending, Canceled).
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID if you have one.
