# Music Collaboration Contract Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Collaboration Contract Request Form is a crucial document for music collaborations, outlining the terms and conditions of each project. It helps ensure clarity, fairness, and respect for all parties involved in the collaboration. By filling out this form, you will be able to specify the details of your collaboration, including music license, payment terms, contract length, start and end dates, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk \*.
2. Choose the correct music license and payment method for your project.
3. Select a collaboration duration and contract length that suits your needs.
4. Specify the start and end dates of your collaboration.
5. Fill in your contact and payment information.
6. Confirm your agreement to terms and conditions.
7. Finally, sign and date the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collaboration Terms** (`collaboration_terms`): Describe the terms of your collaboration, including any specific requirements or agreements between you and your collaborator.
* **Collaboration Details** (`collaboration_details`): Add any additional details about your collaboration, such as project goals or objectives.
* **Music License** (`music_license`): Choose the type of music license you want to apply for your project. Options include:
	+ Public Domain: The music will not be copyrighted or owned by anyone.
	+ Royalty-Free: No royalties will be paid for the music.
	+ Mechanical: Royalties will be paid for mechanical reproductions (e.g., prints, records).
	+ Performance Rights: Royalties will be paid for public performances (e.g., live performances, broadcasts).
* **Collaboration Duration** (`collaboration_duration`): Specify the length of your collaboration. This field is not required but can help clarify the project timeline.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`): Describe any special terms or conditions of your contract, such as payment methods or deadlines.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Specify how you want to receive payment for your work, such as per hour or per album.
* **Music License Agreement** (`music_license_agreement`): Confirm that you agree to the terms of the music license you have chosen.
* **Collaboration Fee** (`collaboration_fee`): Choose a collaboration fee that suits your needs. Options are 100, 200, or 500.
* **Music License Fee** (`music_license_fee`): Choose a music license fee that suits your needs. Options are 100, 200, or 500.
* **Contract Length** (`contract_length`): Specify the length of your contract in years or months.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Choose the start date of your collaboration.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Choose the end date of your collaboration.
* **Collaboration Notes** (`collaboration_notes`): Add any additional notes about your collaboration.
* **Artist Info** (`artist_info`): Fill in your artist name and any relevant biographical information.
* **Producer Info** (`producer_info`): Fill in the name and contact information of your producer.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Fill in your contact information, such as name and phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Fill in your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Fill in your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose the payment method for your project. Options include:
	+ Bank Transfer: Transfer money through a bank.
	+ PayPal: Use PayPal for payment.
	+ Cash: Pay in cash.
	+ Other: Specify another payment method.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Confirm that your payment has been made or is in progress.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Specify any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Terms And Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign and date your contract to confirm your agreement.
* **Date** (`date`): Date your contract to confirm the signing date.
* **Time** (`time`): Specify the time of your contract signing. 

By following this guide, you will be able to accurately and efficiently fill out the Music Collaboration Contract Request Form and ensure a smooth and successful music collaboration experience.
