# Tea Day Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from end-users about their experience with the "Tea Day" event. This feedback will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, but do not feel obligated to provide information that you do not want to share.
3. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Tea Day Event Details
**Tea Day Event Details** (`tea_day_event_details`, text, required: false)
Please provide a brief description of what happened at the "Tea Day" event you attended. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.

### 2. How satisfied are you with the Tea Day event?
**How satisfied are you with the Tea Day event?** (`tea_day_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)
Choose one of the following options to describe your level of satisfaction:
* Very Satisfied
* Somewhat Satisfied
* Neutral
* Somewhat Dissatisfied
* Very Dissatisfied

### 3. Satisfaction score
**Satisfaction score** (`tea_day_satisfaction_score`, number, required: false)
Please provide a numerical score from 1 to 5 to describe your level of satisfaction. A higher score indicates a better experience.

### 4. What did you enjoy most about the event?
**What did you enjoy most about the event?** (`what_you_enjoyed`, text, required: false)
Be as specific as possible about what you enjoyed most about the event. This could be a particular activity, food, or interaction that stood out to you.

### 5. What could be improved?
**What could be improved?** (`what_could_improve`, text, required: false)
Think about what we could have done differently to make the event better for you. This will help us identify areas for improvement.

### 6. Comments
**Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false)
If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the event, please share them here.

### 7. Contact Info
**Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required: false)
Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.

### 8. Phone Number
**Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false)
If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.

### 9. Email
**Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Please provide your email address so we can contact you with any questions or follow-ups.

### 10. Name
**Name** (`name`, text, required: false)
Please let us know your name so we can address you by it in our follow-up communications.

Note: All fields are optional, so you can choose to answer only the questions that are relevant to your experience.
