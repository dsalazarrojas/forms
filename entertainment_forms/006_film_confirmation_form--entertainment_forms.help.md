# film_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track film-related project information, including confirmation, details, and production specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have all the required information available.
2. Select the correct options for each field, taking into account the specific needs of your film project.
3. Enter your film's details, such as title, production status, release date, and language.
4. Select the relevant options for your film's production company, production status, production country, and country code.
5. Add any additional information about your film's production, such as poster, image, and trailer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title**: Enter the title of your film.
* **contact_information_label**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have contact information available.
* **contact_information_label**: Enter your contact information if you selected 'Yes'.
* **upload_your_file**: Enter a file name or description to upload your film file.
* **festival_submission_label**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have festival submission information available.
* **screenplay**: Enter your film's screenplay information.
* **genre_label**: Select the genre of your film.
* **film_type_label**: Select the type of your film.
* **production_details_label**: Select the production details of your film, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **director_label**: Enter your film's director information.
* **writer_label**: Enter your film's writer information.
* **cast_label**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have cast information available.
* **crew_label**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have crew information available.
* **release_date_label**: Enter your film's release date.
* **runtime_label**: Enter your film's runtime.
* **language_label**: Select the language of your film.
* **production_company_label**: Enter your production company information.
* **production_status_label**: Select the production status of your film.
* **production_country_label**: Select the country where your film was produced.
* **production_country_code_label**: Select the country code for your film's production location.
* **production_city_label**: Enter the city where your film was produced.
* **production_poster_label**: Enter your film's poster image.
* **production_image_label**: Enter your film's image.
* **production_trailer_label**: Enter your film's trailer.
* **production_video_label**: Enter your film's video.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure you have all the necessary information available for each field.
- If you're unsure about a field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
