# previous_year_party_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users' experiences and preferences related to parties and social events. It helps the event organizers understand user preferences and tailor their events accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the following questions about your party preferences:
	* **Favorite Dessert**: Choose your favorite dessert from the list (Watermelon, Brownies, or Ice Cream).
	* **Favorite Game**: Select your favorite type of game (Video Game, Board Game, or Escape Room).
	* **Attended Events**: Enter the number of events you attended last year.
	* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you attended (Party, Concert, or Festival).
	* **Favorite Food**: Enter your favorite food.
	* **Favorite Drink**: Enter your favorite drink.
	* **Party Theme**: Briefly describe the party theme you enjoyed the most or would like to see at an event.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers to help event organizers understand your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Party Theme
* **Party Theme**: (`party_theme`, `text`, `optional`): Briefly describe a party theme you enjoyed the most or would like to see at an event.

### Favorite Dessert
* **Favorite Dessert**: (`favorite_dessert`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose your favorite dessert from the list (Watermelon, Brownies, or Ice Cream).

### Favorite Game
* **Favorite Game**: (`favorite_game`, `select_one`, `true`): Select your favorite type of game (Video Game, Board Game, or Escape Room).

### Attended Events
* **Attended Events**: (`attended_events`, `number`, `true`): Enter the number of events you attended last year.

### Event Type
* **Event Type**: (`event_type`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose the type of event you attended (Party, Concert, or Festival).

### Favorite Food
* **Favorite Food**: (`favorite_food`, `text`, `true`): Enter your favorite food.

### Favorite Drink
* **Favorite Drink**: (`favorite_drink`, `text`, `true`): Enter your favorite drink.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions to help event organizers understand your preferences.
* If you have trouble choosing a favorite dessert, game, event type, food, or drink, just choose your top choice.
* If you attended multiple events of the same type, you can enter the number of times you attended each type.
