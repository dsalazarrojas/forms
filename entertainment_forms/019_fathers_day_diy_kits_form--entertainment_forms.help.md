# Fathers Day DIY Kits Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for creating customized Fathers Day DIY Kits. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that their kit is created correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field. This is the name that will be displayed on the kit.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field. This is the name that will be displayed on the kit.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third field. This will be used for communication and confirmation of the order.
4. Enter your **Address** in the fourth field. This is the address where the kit will be delivered to.
5. Select a **Date** for the delivery in the fifth field. Please choose a date that works best for you.
6. Choose the **Order Type** you would like to place in the sixth field. Please select from the available options: Kit 1, Kit 2, or Kit 3.
7. Enter the **Quantity** of kits you would like to order in the seventh field. Please enter a whole number.
8. Enter any **Notes** about your order in the eighth field. This is a good place to add any special requests or instructions.
9. Enter your **Phone** number in the ninth field. This will be used for communication and confirmation of the order.
10. Choose the **Assigned Tool** that will be used to create your kit in the tenth field. Please select from the available options: chatjimmy or other.
11. Select whether you would like to receive a **Output File** in the eleventh field. Please choose from the options: Yes or No.
12. Enter the **Form ID** of any other forms that you would like to link to this order in the twelfth field. Please enter a whole number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address where the kit will be delivered to.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for the delivery.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of kit you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of kits you would like to order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any special requests or instructions for your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool that will be used to create your kit.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, select_one, optional): Select whether you would like to receive a file with your order.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, number, optional): Enter the IDs of any other forms that you would like to link to this order.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues with your order, please contact us.
