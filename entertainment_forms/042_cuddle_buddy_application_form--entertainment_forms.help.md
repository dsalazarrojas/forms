# Cuddle Buddy Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cuddle Buddy Application Form is designed for individuals who are interested in being matched with a Cuddle Buddy, a volunteer who will visit and spend time with them, providing companionship and social interaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are eligible to be matched with a Cuddle Buddy. Check with your healthcare provider or social worker to confirm.
2. Fill out the form with your required information, including your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number.
3. Provide your preferred contact method (phone or email) in case the Cuddle Buddy coordinator needs to reach out to you.
4. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This is the primary contact method for the Cuddle Buddy coordinator.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number that you can be reached at, or leave it blank if you do not have a phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any difficulty filling out this form, you can contact our support team for assistance.
* You will be contacted by a Cuddle Buddy Coordinator to discuss your application and match you with a volunteer once your form is submitted.
