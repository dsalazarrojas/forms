# Secret Santa Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Form is designed to collect information from participants in a Secret Santa gift exchange, allowing them to exchange gifts with each other.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email to participate in the Secret Santa exchange.
2. If you have a preference for a specific person to draw as the recipient of your gift, enter their email address in the "recipient email" field.
3. If you'd like to leave a message for the person who draws you as a recipient, enter a message in the "Message" field.
4. Submit the form to participate in the Secret Santa exchange.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name**: Your name, which will be used to identify you as a participant.
* **name_of_person**: The name of the person you'd like to draw as the recipient of your gift, if you have a preference.
* **email**: Your email address, which will be used to contact you about the gift exchange.
* **recipient email**: The email address of the person you'd like to draw as the recipient of your gift, if you have a preference.
* **Message**: A message for the person who draws you as a recipient, if you'd like to leave one.
