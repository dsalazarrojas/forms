# Streaming Trivia Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user data about their streaming habits and preferences. This information will help us better understand the streaming market and its users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading through the form carefully and understanding each question.
2. Begin with the "Introduction" page, where you will be asked to provide a brief overview of your streaming habits.
3. Select your preferred music genre from the list provided on the "Music Preference" page.
4. On the "Stream Platforms" page, select all the platforms you have used to stream content in the past.
5. Enter the average time you spend live streaming on the "Live Stream Frequency" page.
6. On the "Stream Time Spent" page, enter the amount of time you spend streaming per session.
7. If you have a favorite streamer, enter their name on the "Favorite Streamer" page.
8. Answer whether or not you attend live events on the "Live Event Attendance" page.
9. Select the types of content you have watched on the "Content Type Watched" page.
10. On the "Stream Time Spent Weekly" page, enter the average time you spend streaming per week.
11. Choose the personality of your favorite streamer on the "Streamer Personality" page.
12. Select the types of online discussions you participate in on the "Online Discussions" page.
13. Answer whether or not you participate in an online community on the "Online Community" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Page 1)**: Please provide a brief overview of your streaming habits and preferences.
* **Music Preference (Page 2)**: Select one of the following music genres: `pop`, `rock`, `hip-hop`, `classical`, `jazz`, or `country`.
	* This question will help us understand what kind of music you enjoy streaming.
* **Stream Platforms (Page 3)**: Select all the platforms you have used to stream content in the past, such as `Netflix`, `Hulu`, `Disney+`, `Amazon Prime Video`, or `YouTube`.
	* This question will help us understand which platforms you have explored for streaming content.
* **Live Stream Frequency (Page 4)**: Enter the average time you spend live streaming.
	* This question will help us understand how much time you spend engaging in live streaming activities.
* **Stream Time Spent (Page 5)**: Enter the amount of time you spend streaming per session.
	* This question will help us understand how long your streaming sessions typically last.
* **Favorite Streamer (Page 6)**: Enter the name of your favorite streamer.
	* This question will help us understand who you enjoy watching most.
* **Live Event Attendance (Page 7)**: Answer whether or not you attend live events.
	* This question will help us understand if you participate in live events associated with streaming.
* **Content Type Watched (Page 8)**: Select all the types of content you have watched, such as `music`, `video`, or `live-streams`.
	* This question will help us understand what kind of content you enjoy streaming.
* **Stream Time Spent Weekly (Page 9)**: Enter the average time you spend streaming per week.
	* This question will help us understand how much time you dedicate to streaming activities.
* **Streamer Personality (Page 10)**: Choose the personality of your favorite streamer.
	* This question will help us understand the qualities you value in a streamer.
* **Online Discussions (Page 11)**: Select all the types of online discussions you participate in, such as `chat`, `social media`, `forums`, or `discord`.
	* This question will help us understand how you engage with other streamers and the community.
* **Online Community (Page 12)**: Answer whether or not you participate in an online community.
	* This question will help us understand if you are part of a larger streaming community.
