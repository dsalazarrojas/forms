# Contact Form With HeaderFooter Customized - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about visitors' interests and preferences, which will help our company to better understand their needs and provide personalized experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
- First, select the "Contact Form With HeaderFooter Customized" from the available options.
- Fill out the form fields according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Visitor Information --**: This section is used to collect visitor information, which includes your full name and email address. Please enter your full name and primary email address in the corresponding fields.
* **Your Full Name**: This field is used to collect your full name. Enter your name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Your Email Address**: This field is used to collect your primary email address. Please enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Your Phone Number**: This field is used to collect your phone number. Enter your mobile or home phone number.
* **-- Entertainment Preferences --**: This section is used to collect your entertainment preferences.
* **Preferred Entertainment Type**: Select one or more of the following options: Movies/Cinema, Live Concerts, Theater/Broadway, Theme Parks, Gaming Events, or Other.
* **Favorite Genre**: This field is used to collect your favorite genre of entertainment. You can enter a specific genre, e.g., Action, Comedy, Drama.
* **-- Discount Request Details --**: This section is used to request a discount code or provide additional details.
* **Subject**: Select the reason for your inquiry from the dropdown menu: Discount Code Request, Promotional Inquiry, Group Booking Inquiry, Feedback/Love, or Other.
* **Your Detailed Message**: This field is used to provide more information about your message. Please enter a detailed message explaining your request or inquiry.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted: Email or Phone Call.
* **Date of Submission**: This field is used to record the date of your submission. Today's date will be automatically populated.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to send your information to our server.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
- Select all applicable options for "Preferred Entertainment Type" and "Preferred Contact Method".
- Enter a detailed message in the "Your Detailed Message" field to help us understand your request or inquiry better.
