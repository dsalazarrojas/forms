# Theater Cast Media Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture media-related information from performers, including their details, consent, and media usage preferences. This information helps in organizing and planning media activities such as photo shoots, video shoots, and live shows.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your performer information in the "Performer Information" field.
2. Indicate your guardian's consent by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Guardian Consent" field.
3. Choose your preferences for media usage by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Media Usage" field.
4. Specify the number of photo shoots and video shoots you are available for in the "Photo Shoots" and "Video Shoots" fields.
5. If you have any additional comments or requirements for photo shoots, enter them in the "Photo Shoots Additional" field.
6. If you have any additional comments or requirements for video shoots, enter them in the "Video Shoots Additional" field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for each media show you are available for in the "Media Shows" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or requirements for media shows, enter them in the "Media Shows Additional" field.
9. Enter the details of your shows in the "Show Details" field.
10. If necessary, specify the date for each show in the "Show Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Information** (`performer_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, contact information, or any other relevant details.
* **Guardian Consent** (`guardian_consent`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if your guardian gives consent, "No" if they do not.
* **Media Usage** (`media_usage`, select_one, required: false): Choose "True" if you are available for media usage, "False" if you are not.
* **Photo Shoots** (`photo_shoots`, number, required: false): Enter the number of photo shoots you are available for.
* **Video Shoots** (`video_shoots`, number, required: false): Enter the number of video shoots you are available for.
* **Photo Shoots Additional** (`photo_shoots_additional`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or requirements for photo shoots.
* **Video Shoots Additional** (`video_shoots_additional`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or requirements for video shoots.
* **Media Shows** (`media_shows`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" for each media show you are available for.
* **Media Shows Additional** (`media_shows_additional`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or requirements for media shows.
* **Show Details** (`show_details`, note, required: false): Enter the details of your shows.
* **Show Date** (`show_date`, time, required: false): Specify the date for each show, if necessary.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the media usage question.
* Ensure you have your guardian's consent before proceeding.
* Enter the correct number of photo and video shoots you are available for.
* Enter any additional comments or requirements for photo and video shoots.
* Enter any additional comments or requirements for media shows.
* Enter the details and date of your shows accurately.
