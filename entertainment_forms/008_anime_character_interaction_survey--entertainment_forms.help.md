# Anime Character Interaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Anime Character Interaction Survey is a form designed to gather information about your interactions with anime characters. This form is intended to help us understand how often you engage with characters from your favorite anime shows and the types of activities you enjoy doing with them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the age group that best represents your age.
2. Choose the anime genre that you enjoy most.
3. Type in the name of your favorite anime character.
4. Select how often you interact with characters (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, rarely).
5. Choose all the ways you like to interact with characters (e.g., fan art, roleplay, discussion forums, cosplay, fan fiction).
6. Indicate if you are part of any fandom community.
7. Select your favorite medium to engage with characters (e.g., anime, manga, games, novels).
8. Provide any additional comments about interacting with characters.
9. If you're comfortable, select a date for submission. You can also choose to have the date auto-entered for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Group** (`respondent_age_group`, Select your age group): This field asks for your age group to help us understand your demographic.
* **Favorite anime genre** (`favorite_genre`, Choose the genre you enjoy most): Select the anime genre that you enjoy the most, such as Shonen, Shojo, Seinen, Isekai, or Slice of Life.
* **Favorite character** (`favorite_character`, Provide the character name): Type in the name of your favorite anime character.
* **Interaction frequency** (`interaction_frequency`, Select how often you discuss or roleplay characters): Select how often you interact with characters, such as daily, weekly, monthly, or rarely.
* **Preferred interaction type** (`preferred_interaction_type`, Select all that apply): Choose all the ways you like to interact with characters, such as fan art, roleplay, discussion forums, cosplay, or fan fiction.
* **Fandom community** (`fandom_community`, Yes or no): Indicate if you are part of any fandom community.
* **Favorite medium to engage with characters** (`favorite_medium`, Choose one): Select your preferred medium to engage with characters, such as anime, manga, games, or novels.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, Any other thoughts about character interaction): Provide any additional thoughts or comments about interacting with characters.
* **Submission date** (`submit_date`, Auto-entered or select a date): Select a date for submission, or choose to have the date auto-entered for you.
