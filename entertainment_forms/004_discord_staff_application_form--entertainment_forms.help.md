# Discord Staff Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Discord Staff Application Form is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to join our Discord staff team. The form collects general information and specific details that will aid in the evaluation process. Please fill it out accurately and comprehensively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field completely and truthfully.
2. Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Required: No)**:
	+ A section where you can introduce yourself and provide a brief background on why you're interested in joining our staff team.
* **Social Media (Required: No)**:
	+ A place to list any relevant social media profiles or platforms you use for work or personal use.
* **Availability (Required: No)**:
	+ Share your schedule and availability to work on our Discord platform. This includes days and hours you can be online.
* **Staff Position (Required: No)**:
	+ Indicate which staff position you're applying for, such as Moderator, Admin, or any other role within our community.
* **Additional Information (Required: No)**:
	+ Attach any relevant documentation or links that support your application.
* **Contact Information (Required: No)**:
	+ Provide a way for us to contact you, including phone number, email, or other contact methods.

## Tips
- Be honest and genuine in your responses.
- Use clear and concise language.
- Attach any supporting documents or links when needed.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
