# st_patricks_day_pub_crawl_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The St. Patrick's Day pub crawl sign-up form collects information from participants for a fun and festive event. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us get to know you better and plan a great experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Participant Info** field with your name, email, or other contact details.
2. Provide your **Contact Info** to enable us to communicate with you efficiently.
3. In the **Pub Crawl Info** field, let us know if you have any specific preferences or interests for the pub crawl event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required): Please enter your name, email, or other contact details to help us get to know you better.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information to enable efficient communication and follow-up.
* **Pub Crawl Info** (`pub_crawl_info`, text, required): Share any specific preferences or interests you have for the pub crawl event.

## Tips
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- Double-check your input before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us directly.
- Have fun and enjoy the St. Patrick's Day pub crawl event!
