# Stage Performance Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide feedback on a stage performance you have attended. Your input will help improve future shows and enhance the overall audience experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Show Name, Show Date, and Reviewer Name.
2. Provide your honest feedback in the Performance Details field.
3. If you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback, provide your Email and Phone Number.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Show Name** (Show Name, text, required): Enter the name of the show you attended.
* **Show Date** (Show Date, date, required): Enter the date you saw the show.
* **Performance Details** (Stage Performance Feedback Request Form 1, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the performance, including any notable moments or aspects you enjoyed or disliked.
* **Stage Performance Details** (Stage Performance Details, number, optional): This field is reserved for internal purposes and is not applicable for audience feedback.
* **Show Time** (Show Time, time, optional): Enter the time you saw the show (if applicable).
* **Show Location** (Show Location, text, optional): Enter the venue where you saw the show.
* **Reviewer Name** (Reviewer Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your contact information.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your feedback.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the Performance Details field.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any further comments or concerns, feel free to include them in the Performance Details field.
