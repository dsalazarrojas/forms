## mini_golf_hole_design_submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mini_golf_hole_design_submission form is used by designers to submit their mini golf course ideas, designs, and details for review and consideration by a project lead. This form collects essential information about each hole, including creative ideas, sketches, and specifications, as well as contact information and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring you've provided all required information.
2.  For each field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below for more detailed guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creative Course Idea** (`creative_course_idea`, text, optional): Briefly describe your creative idea for the mini golf course.
* **Sketch of the Hole** (`sketch`, note, required): Share a note or brief description of your design concept, this field is used for designers to convey their vision.
* **Additional Hole Details** (`details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about your design, such as materials or features you'd like to include.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your mini golf course.
* **Designer Name** (`designer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the designer of the course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further discussion.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached for further discussion.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting your design.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time you are submitting your design.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you'd like to be assigned to the project or "No" if you're not available or interested.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" for each project lead you'd like to be assigned to.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully and ensure you've completed all required fields.
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your creative course idea and additional hole details.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Submitting your design at the right time can help ensure it's considered promptly.
