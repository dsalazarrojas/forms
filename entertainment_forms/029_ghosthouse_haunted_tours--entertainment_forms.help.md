# GhostHouse Haunted Tours - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GhostHouse Haunted Tours form is designed to help customers book and manage tour details for a spooky experience. This form collects essential information to tailor the tour to the customer's preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Booking Date** for the desired tour.
2. Choose the type of tour you would like to book by selecting one of the options under **Choose A Tour**.
3. Select the time of the tour you would like to book.
4. Choose the location for the tour from the list of options.
5. Enter a name for the tour in **Tour Name**.
6. Enter your contact phone number and email in **Contact Phone** and **Contact Email** fields respectively.
7. Finally, enter the price of the tour for the customer's reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Booking Date**: Enter the date you would like to go on the tour. This is a required field, but you can choose any date that suits you.
- **Choose A Tour**: Select the type of tour you would like to book. This will help us tailor the tour to your preferences.
- **Tour Time**: Select the time of the tour you would like to go on. Be sure to choose a time that fits your schedule.
- **Location**: Choose one or more locations for the tour. Please note that choosing multiple locations may affect the tour price and duration.
- **Tour Name**: Enter a name for the tour so we can identify it easily. This is a required field.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you for tour updates and reminders.
- **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address so we can send you tour updates and confirmations.
- **Tour Price**: Enter the price of the tour, which will be displayed on your booking confirmation.
