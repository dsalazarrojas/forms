# swimming_pool_rental_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The swimming_pool_rental_form is designed to allow customers to book a swimming pool rental service. This form is used to gather necessary information from customers, including their personal details and payment preferences, to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, follow these steps:
  1. Enter your first name in the **customer_first_name** field.
  2. Enter your last name in the **customer_last_name** field.
  3. Provide your email address in the **customer_email** field.
  4. Enter your phone number in the **customer_phone** field.
  5. Choose the start and end dates of your booking in the **start_date** and **end_date** fields respectively.
  6. Select the desired time of your booking in the **time** field.
  7. Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided in the **payment_method** field.
  8. Enter the amount to be paid in the **payment_amount** field.
  9. Add any additional notes about your booking in the **notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_first_name** (Text): Enter your first name here.
* **customer_last_name** (Text): Enter your last name here.
* **customer_email** (Email): Enter your email address here. This will be used for communication about your booking.
* **customer_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number here.
* **start_date** (Date): Select the start date of your booking.
* **end_date** (Date): Select the end date of your booking.
* **time** (Time): Select the time of your booking.
* **payment_method** (Select One):
	+ **Credit Card**: Pay via credit card.
	+ **PayPal**: Pay via PayPal.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: Pay via bank transfer.
* **payment_amount** (Number): Enter the amount to be paid for your booking.
* **notes** (Note): Add any additional notes about your booking here.
