# Halloween Costume Rental Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to rent costumes for Halloween. Please fill it out to submit your requests for a costume rental.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the costumes you'd like to rent by checking the boxes next to each costume on the first page.
2. Fill out your contact information, including your name, email, and address.
3. Provide a description of the costume you'd like to rent.
4. If you'd like to rent multiple costumes, click on "Rent More Costumes" and select the other costume(s).
5. Upload any additional images of the costume to help us process your request.
6. Once you've completed all the necessary fields, click on "Confirm Submission" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Costume**: 
	+ Select the costume(s) you'd like to rent from the list below.
* **Costume Name**: 
	+ Type the name of the costume you've selected.
* **Costume Description**: 
	+ Describe the costume you'd like to rent in a brief paragraph.
* **Cost of the Costume**: 
	+ Enter the price you'd like to pay for the costume.
* **Costume Image URL**: 
	+ Please enter the URL of an image of the costume for our reference.
* **Contact Information**: 
	+ Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number (if you have one).
* **Additional Information**: 
	+ If there's anything else we need to know about you or your rental request, please enter it here.
* **Upload Another Image**: 
	+ Please upload another image of the costume to help us process your request.
* **Rent More Costumes**: 
	+ If you'd like to rent another costume, select "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
* **Confirm Submission**: 
	+ Once you've completed all the fields, click on "Confirm Submission" to send your request.
