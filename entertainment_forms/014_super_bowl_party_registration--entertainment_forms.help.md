# Super Bowl Party Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Super Bowl Party Registration form is a collection of questions to gather information about you and your party plans for the Super Bowl party. By filling out this form, you will be able to:
* Register your presence at the party
* Provide details about your party preferences and needs
* Ensure the party hosts are prepared for your arrival

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.
2. Make sure to save your progress if you need to stop and come back later.
3. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Super Bowl Party Registration**: This field is optional and is used for any additional comments or information about the party.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of each guest attending the party.
* **Guest Count**: Enter the total number of guests attending the party.
* **Food and Beverage Preferences**: Select one or more of the following options: Burgers, Hot Dogs, Veggies, Chicken Wings, or Chili.
* **RSVP**: Select "Attending" if you plan to attend the party, or "Not Attending" if you are unable to attend.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or notes about your party plans.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Food**: Select one of the following options: Main Course, Appetizers, or Snacks.
* **Beverage**: Select one of the following options: Soft Drink, Beer, or Wine.
* **Game Time Request**: Choose a game time for the party.
* **Arrival Time**: Choose the time you plan to arrive at the party.
* **Parking Request**: If you have a vehicle, select the parking arrangement you require.
* **Comment**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your party plans.
* **Party Size**: Enter the number of guests attending your party.
* **Party Host**: Enter the name of the person hosting the party.
* **Game Watching Experience**: Select one of the following options: Traditional, Modern, or Rugby.

* **Game Video Setup**: Select one of the following options: TV or Projector.
* **Party Location**: Select one or more of the following options: Backyard, Living Room, or Front Porch.
* **Parking Arrangement**: Select one of the following options: Street Parking, Home Parking, or Public Parking.
