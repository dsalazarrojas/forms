# video_storyboard_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a visual representation of a video storyboard template for planning and organizing the sequence of events in a video production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Each page of the storyboard will have its own section in the form. 
2. Fill out each page section with the required information, which may include text, dates, times, and other relevant details.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
