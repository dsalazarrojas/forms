# Recipe Sharing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recipe Sharing Form is designed to collect information from users about their favorite recipes. By filling out this form, users can share their recipes with the community and potentially gain access to a collection of shared recipes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Info section with your name, email address, and other relevant details.
2. In the Recipe Info section, provide a detailed description of your recipe, including ingredients, cooking instructions, and any relevant tags or categories.
3. In the Sharing Info section, specify how you would like to share your recipe (e.g., publicly, privately, or with a specific group).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info
* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to fill in your name, email address, and any other relevant details about yourself. Please provide the required information to help us get in touch with you if needed.

### Recipe Info
* **Recipe Info** (`recipe_info`, `text`, required): This section is where you can provide a detailed description of your recipe. Please include ingredients, cooking instructions, and any relevant tags or categories to help others understand and recreate your dish.

### Sharing Info
* **Sharing Info** (`sharing_info`, `text`, optional): In this section, you can specify how you would like to share your recipe. Choose from the options: publicly, privately, or with a specific group. This will determine how your recipe will be made available to the community.

## Tips
* Be creative and descriptive in your recipe description to help others understand and make your dish.
* Use relevant tags and categories to help others find your recipe.
* Choose the correct sharing option to control how your recipe is shared.
