# Stage Video Screen Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Video Screen Update Request Form is a tool used by stakeholders to request updates to existing stage videos in the entertainment industry. It ensures that all necessary information is gathered for efficient processing and updating of stage video screens.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before submitting the form.
3. Check for similar requests already submitted by colleagues or our internal records to avoid duplication of effort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screen Id** (`screen_id`, Number): This is the unique identifier for the stage video screen you want to update.
* **Screen Title** (`screen_title`, Text): Enter the current title of the stage video screen you want to update.
* **Screen Description** (`screen_description`, Text): Provide a brief description of the stage video screen you want to update.
* **Screen Status** (`screen_status`, Select One): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' depending on the current status of the stage video screen.
* **Screen Type** (`screen_type`, Select Multiple): Select all applicable screen types you want to update.
* **Screen Category** (`screen_category`, Select One): Select the category of the screen you want to update.
* **Screen Tags** (`screen_tags`, Text): Enter any relevant tags for the screen you want to update.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, Text): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, Email): Enter your email address or the email address of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, Text): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, Text): Enter any comments or additional information related to the update request.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`, Text): Enter the name of your organization or the organization of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, Text): Enter your position or the position of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, Text): Enter any additional notes or information related to the submission.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, Text): Enter the name of your department or the department of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Date** (`submitter_date`, Date): Enter the date you submitted the request.
* **Submitter Time** (`submitter_time`, Time): Enter the time you submitted the request.
* **Submitter Status** (`submitter_status`, Select One): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' status for your submission.
* **Submitter Message** (`submitter_message`, Note): Enter any additional message or information related to your submission.
* **Submitter Attachments** (`submitter_attachments`, Note): Upload any supporting files or attachments related to your submission.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, Text): Enter any additional comments or information related to your submission.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, Note): Enter any additional notes or information related to your submission.
* **Submitter Organization URL** (`submitter_organization_url`, Text): Enter the URL of your organization or the organization of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Position URL** (`submitter_position_url`, Text): Enter the URL of your position or the position of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter URL** (`submitter_url`, Text): Enter the URL of your or the person submitting the request.
 
Tips: Ensure that all submitted information is accurate and relevant to the request. Double-check for any spelling or grammar errors before submitting. 
Please note that this form is for updating stage videos in the entertainment industry and not for requesting new videos.
