# ประกashion Show Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from vendors who want to participate in the "ประกashion" show. Fill out the form to apply for a spot in the show, and our team will review your application carefully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields (indicated by asterisk \*).
3. If you have any questions or need help, contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (Required): This is the name of your company or entity that will be participating in the show.
* **Contact Name** (Optional): This is the name of the person we should contact for more information or clarification.
* **Contact Email** (Required): This is the email address of the person we should contact for more information or clarification. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Optional): This is the phone number we can use to contact you for more information or clarification.
* **Product Description** (Required): This is where you describe your product or service that you want to showcase in the show.
* **Product Image** (Optional): If you have a relevant image of your product, you can upload it here.
* **Category** (Required): This is the category under which your product or service falls. Choose one of the provided options.
* **Price** (Required): This is the price you will be offering for your product or service at the show.
* **Description** (Optional): This is where you can provide more information about your product or service.
* **Additional Comments or Details** (Optional): This is where you can add any additional comments or details that might be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Use a valid email address for contact email.
- Choose a category that accurately represents your product or service.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
