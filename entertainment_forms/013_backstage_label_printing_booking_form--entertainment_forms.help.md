# Backstage Label Printing Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Backstage Label Printing Booking Form is designed to help event organizers and production teams book label printing services for their events. This form helps us understand your printing requirements and ensure a smooth process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Submit your event details**: Please fill in the following sections:
	* **Event Name**: Enter the name of your event.
	* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event.
	* **Event Location**: Enter the location of your event.
2. **Provide contact information**: Please provide the following contact details:
	* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
	* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
3. **Choose label options**: Please select the following label options:
	* **Label Type**: Choose the type of label you need (e.g., Name Tags, ID Badges, etc.).
	* **Number of Labels to Print**: Enter the number of labels you need.
	* **Label Size**: Select the size of the label you need (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
	* **Material Type**: Choose the material type (e.g., Paper, Plastic, Laminated).
	* **Custom Size**: If you select a custom size, please enter the dimensions.
4. **Define label content**: Please select the label content template and enter any special instructions.
5. **Deliveries and instructions**: Finally, please provide the following:
	* **Rush Delivery**: Indicate if you need rush delivery.
	* **Delivery Location/Address**: Enter the delivery location or address.
	* **Special Instructions or Notes**: Add any special instructions or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of your event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, **date**, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, **text**, required): Enter the location of your event.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, **email**, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, **phone**, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Label Type** (`label_type`, **select_one, required): Choose the type of label you need (e.g., Name Tags, ID Badges, etc.).
* **Number of Labels to Print** (`quantity`, **integer, required): Enter the number of labels you need.
* **Label Size** (`label_size`, **select_one, required): Select the size of the label you need (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, **select_one, required): Choose the material type (e.g., Paper, Plastic, Laminated).
* **Custom Size** (`custom_size`, **text, required): If you select a custom size, please enter the dimensions.
* **Label Content Template** (`label_content`, **text, required): Select the label content template (e.g., Name, title, ID number, instructions, etc.).
* **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, **text, required): Enter your color preferences.
* **Include Branding Elements** (`branding_elements`, **select_one, required): Indicate if you want to include branding elements.
* **Will You Provide Custom Design File?** (`design_provided`, **select_one, required): Indicate if you will provide a custom design file.
* **File Format (if provided)** (`file_format`, **text, required): Enter the file format if you provide a custom design file.
* **Need Rush Delivery?** (`rush_delivery`, **select_one, required): Indicate if you need rush delivery.
* **Delivery Location/Address** (`delivery_location`, **text, required): Enter the delivery location or address.
* **Special Instructions or Notes** (`special_instructions`, **text, required): Enter any special instructions or notes.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all the fields for accurate and complete information.
- If you need any clarifications or have any questions, please feel free to contact us.
- We will respond to your form submission as soon as possible to ensure a smooth experience.
