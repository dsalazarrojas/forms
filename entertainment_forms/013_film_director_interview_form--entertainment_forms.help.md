# Film Director Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Film Director Interview Form is designed to collect information from film directors about their background, experience, and interests. This form is meant to gather data for internal purposes, such as for a film-related project or initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) on each page.
2. Answer each question or field with the information requested.
3. Review your responses before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Page** (`first_page`, *text*, *required: false*): This field is a placeholder page and should be left blank.
* **Director Bio** (`director_bio`, *text*, *required: true*): Describe your background and experience as a film director.
* **Filmography** (`filmography`, *text*, *required: true*): List your notable film credits, including titles, roles, and relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, *text*, *required: true*): Provide your contact information, including phone number, email, and address.
* **First Page 2** (`first_page_2`, *text*, *required: false*): This field is a placeholder page and should be left blank.
* **First Page 3** (`first_page_3`, *text*, *required: false*): This field is a placeholder page and should be left blank.
* **First Page 4** (`first_page_4`, *text*, *required: false*): This field is a placeholder page and should be left blank.

### Additional Pages
* **Director Interest** (`director_interest`, *select_one*, *required: true*): Indicate your interest in the project by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Film Genre** (`film_genre`, *select_multiple*, *required: true*): Select all genres that apply to your film experience.
* **Interview Response** (`interview_response`, *select_multiple*, *required: true*): Indicate your response to the interview questions.
* **Film Language** (`film_language`, *select_one*, *required: true*): Select the language you typically use in your films.
* **Film Director** (`film_director`, *select_one*, *required: true*): Indicate your experience as a film director by selecting "Yes" or "No".

Tips:
* Use proper formatting and grammar when filling in the text fields.
* Select only relevant options for the select fields.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
