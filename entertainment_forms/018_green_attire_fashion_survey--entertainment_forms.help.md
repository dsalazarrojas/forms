# Green Attire Fashion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Green Attire Fashion Survey is designed to understand your attitudes and behaviors related to fashion and sustainability. The survey will ask you a series of questions to gather information on your preferences, habits, and perceptions of sustainable fashion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Take a few minutes to carefully review the form below.
2. Answer each question honestly, and choose the options that best reflect your thoughts and preferences.
3. You can save your progress and complete the form later if needed.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click the submit button to finalize your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sustainable Practices** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your adoption of sustainable practices in fashion, such as using a reusable water bottle, carrying a tote bag, or buying second-hand clothes.
* **Fashion Preferences** (Select one):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your favorite style of fashion, such as classic, modern, or bohemian.
* **Favorite Colors** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your favorite colors, such as red, blue, or green.
* **Clothing Sizes** (Select one):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your preferred clothing size, such as small, medium, or large.
* **Fashion Buys** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your fashion purchasing habits, such as buying new clothes, buying second-hand clothes, or shopping online.
* **Purchase Decisions** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your decision-making process when making a fashion purchase, such as budget, quality, or style.
* **Environmental Impact** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your perception of the environmental impact of your fashion purchases, such as concern for the environment, concern for animal welfare, or concern for human rights.
* **Fashion Brands** (Select one):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your favorite fashion brand, such as Gucci, Nike, or Levi's.
* **Fashion Styles** (Select one):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your favorite fashion style, such as minimalist, maximalist, or eclectic.
* **Purchase Frequency** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about how often you purchase new clothes, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Favorite Clothing** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your favorite types of clothing, such as tops, bottoms, or dresses.
* **Clothing Types** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your preferred types of clothing, such as dresses, tops, or pants.
* **Clothing Quality** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your perception of clothing quality, such as Good, Poor, or Average.
* **Eco-Friendly Clothing** (Select all that apply):
	* A multiple-choice question that asks about your perception of eco-friendly clothing, such as sustainable materials, fair trade, or animal-friendly.
