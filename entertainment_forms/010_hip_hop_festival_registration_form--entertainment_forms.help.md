# hip_hop_festival_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees to the hip hop festival, allowing us to gather contact and interest information to keep you updated about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your attendee information (page 1)
2. Share your personal info (page 2)
3. Choose your area of interest (page 3)
4. Rate your experience (page 4)
5. Provide your contact details (page 5)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, `text`, not required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional personal details you'd like us to know.
- **Area of Interest** (`area_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the areas that interest you the most among: hip hop, music, dance, art, or food.
  * If you're interested in more than one area, please select all that apply.
- **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, not required): Rate your experience with the festival on a scale from 1 to 5.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your valid email address for festival updates and offers.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
- **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name (if different from your attendee info). 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure you receive festival updates and offers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact our festival team.
* Please note that we will not share your contact information with anyone else without your consent.
