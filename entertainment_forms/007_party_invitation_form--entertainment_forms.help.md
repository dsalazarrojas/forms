# Party Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a party, including its name, date, time, location, address, and contact details of the host. It helps in planning and organizing the party effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant details:

1. Enter the name of the party in the `Party Name` field.
2. Select the date of the party in the `Date` field.
3. Choose the time of the party in the `Time` field.
4. Enter the location of the party in the `Location` field.
5. Provide the address of the party in the `Address` field.
6. Enter the name of the host in the `Host Name` field.
7. Enter the email of the host in the `Host Email` field.
8. Enter the phone number of the host in the `Host Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Name** (`party_name`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief name for the party.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the party.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time of the party.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the party will take place.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the party location.
* **Host Name** (`host_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the host.
* **Host Email** (`host_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the host.
* **Host Phone** (`host_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the host.
