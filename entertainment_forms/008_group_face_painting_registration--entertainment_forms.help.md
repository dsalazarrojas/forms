# Group Face Painting Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Group Face Painting Registration" form is intended for entertainment professionals to register their details and event information. This form helps event organizers gather necessary information from face painters to provide a smooth and enjoyable experience for all involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the "Enter details about you" section.
2. Select the type of event you will be attending.
3. Choose a date for your event.
4. Enter the time of your event.
5. Provide the venue of your event.
6. Enter your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Enter details about you
* **Enter details about you (Name)** (`entertainer_details`, text, required): This field is where you can enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a face painter.
* **Event information (Select)** (`event_details`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of event you will be attending, such as "School event", "Corporate event", "Party", or "Festival".
* **Preferred dates (Date)** (`event_dates`, date, optional): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event start time (Time)** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your event.
* **Event venue (Text)** (`event_venue`, text, optional): Enter the venue where your event will take place.
* **Contact information (Text)** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your event information for accuracy to avoid confusion.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
* This form is designed to be simple and straightforward, so take your time and fill it out carefully.
