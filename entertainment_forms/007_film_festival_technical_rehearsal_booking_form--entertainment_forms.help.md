# Film Festival Technical Rehearsal Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you book a technical rehearsal for your film festival. It provides a platform for you to select a time slot for your technical rehearsal, which is essential for ensuring a smooth and stress-free festival experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the form fields as instructed**. The form has four fields where you'll be asked to provide specific information.
2. Make sure to select a valid time slot for your technical rehearsal from the options provided in the "Technical Rehearsal Booking" field.
3. Complete the remaining text fields ("Film Festival Technical Rehearsal Booking Form 2", "Film Festival Technical Rehearsal Booking Form 3", and "Film Festival Technical Rehearsal Booking Form 4") with any additional information you want to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Film Festival Technical Rehearsal Booking Form**: This is the title of the form, which is not required but serves as a label.
* **Technical Rehearsal Booking**: Select a time slot for your technical rehearsal from the options provided:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
* **Film Festival Technical Rehearsal Booking Form 2**: This field is for additional information related to your technical rehearsal.
* **Film Festival Technical Rehearsal Booking Form 3**: This field is for further details about your technical rehearsal.
* **Film Festival Technical Rehearsal Booking Form 4** (Optional): This field is for any additional notes or comments about your technical rehearsal.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
* Double-check your selections and information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
