# Entertainment Business Services Intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Business Services Intake form is a tool used to collect information from clients about their entertainment business needs. This includes details about their events, budgets, contact preferences, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the `Client Inquiry` field with a brief description of your event or project.
2.  Proceed to the `Event Details` field and enter any relevant information about your event, such as dates and times.
3.  In the `Budget` field, enter your budget for the event.
4.  Select your preferred contact method in the `Contact Preferences` field.
5.  Enter the date of your event in the `Event Date` field.
6.  Enter the time of your event in the `Event Time` field.
7.  In the `Venue Preferences` field, select your preferred venue options.
8.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
9.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
10.  Finally, enter your full name in the `Client Name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Inquiry** (text, required/optional): A brief description of your event or project. This could include any information that will help us understand your requirements.
*   **Event Details** (text, required/optional): Enter any relevant details about your event, such as dates and times.
*   **Budget** (number, required/optional): Enter your budget for the event.
*   **Contact Preferences** (select multiple, required/optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., call, email, SMS).
*   **Event Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of your event.
*   **Event Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of your event.
*   **Venue Preferences** (select one, required/optional): Select your preferred venue type (e.g., private, public, outdoor).
*   **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Client Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your full name.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that we can better serve you.
*   If you have any additional requirements or needs, please let us know in the `Client Inquiry` field.
*   We will review and verify the information collected through this form before proceeding.
