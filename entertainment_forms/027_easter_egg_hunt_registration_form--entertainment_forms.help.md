# Easter Egg Hunt Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Egg Hunt Registration Form is meant for participants to register for an Easter Egg Hunt event. It collects the necessary information from participants to facilitate a fun and memorable experience during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with your name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Fill in your Phone Number.
5. (Optional) If you want to participate, please note that the Date of Birth field is not required.
6. Choose the Time of Event for the event.
7. Select the Event Date for the Easter Egg Hunt.
8. Choose the Event Time.
9. Fill in the Event Location.
10. If you're participating with a group, please fill in the Easter Egg Count.
11. Choose your Preferred Language.
12. Select the Preferred Contact Method(s) (e.g., Email, Phone, or Mail).
13. (Optional) If you have an Emergency Contact, fill in their name.
14. (Optional) If your Emergency Contact has a Phone, fill it in.
15. (Optional) If your Emergency Contact has an Email, fill it in.
16. (Optional) If your Emergency Contact has an address, fill it in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX-XXX-XXXXX`.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): This is the date you were born. It's not necessary to fill this out.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, Time, Optional): This is the time of the event, but it's not necessary to fill this out.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date, Required): This is the date of the Easter Egg Hunt event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, Time, Required): This is the time of the Easter Egg Hunt event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, Text, Required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Easter Egg Count** (`easter_egg_count`, Number, Required): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, Select One, Required): Choose the language you prefer to be contacted in.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the contact method you prefer (Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, Text, Optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.

Note: The fields marked as "Optional" do not need to be filled in, but if you have the information, please fill it in for our records.
