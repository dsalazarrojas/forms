# Wedding Reception Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details about a wedding reception, including the date, time, location, and other important information for the wedding guests. The form is divided into several sections to make it easier to fill out.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Wedding Details**: Please provide the date and time of the wedding ceremony and reception. You can use the date fields to select the date and time.
2. **Guest Information**: Enter the name and address of your guest, so we can ensure they receive the invitation.
3. **Special Requests**: If there are any special requests or instructions for the guests, such as dress code or parking information, please enter them here.
4. **Reception Information**: Let us know if the reception will have a reception area for guests, and if so, what the dress code will be.
5. **Parking Information**: If there will be parking available for guests, please let us know.
6. **Contact Information**: If you have any contact information for the wedding party, such as a phone number or email, please enter it here.
7. **Photo Information**: If you have any photo information for the wedding party, such as a photographer's name or contact information, please enter it here.

* **Wedding Details** (page 1):
	+ **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of the wedding ceremony and reception.
	+ **Wedding Time**: Enter the time of the wedding ceremony and reception.
* **Wedding Location**: Enter the location of the wedding ceremony and reception.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of your guest.
