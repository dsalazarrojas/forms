# Stage Performance Transport Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Performance Transport Request Form is a tool to provide necessary information for stage performances, such as theater shows, festivals, or concerts. This form helps to organize and plan for the transport of performers and their crew to ensure timely and efficient execution of events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about the performance.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Double-check that you have not submitted a transport request for the same event previously.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Details** (`performer_details`): Enter a brief description of the performance, including the type of event and any specific details about the performance.
* **Crew Details** (`crew_details`): Provide a list of crew members involved in the performance, including their names and contact information.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`): Specify any equipment or resources required for the performance, such as sound systems or lighting.
* **Transport Details** (`transport_details`): Enter the transport specifics, including the departure and arrival times, and the number of passengers to be transported.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the transport team to know.
* **Performer Name** (`performer_name`): Enter the name of the performer involved in the performance.
* **Performer Type** (`performer_type`): Select the type of performer (e.g., Actor, Dancer, Musician).
* **Crew Size** (`crew_size`): Enter the number of crew members involved in the performance.
* **Transport Method** (`transport_method`): Select the transport method (e.g., Car, Van, Bus).
* **Transport Cost** (`transport_cost`): Enter the estimated transport cost for the selected transport method.
