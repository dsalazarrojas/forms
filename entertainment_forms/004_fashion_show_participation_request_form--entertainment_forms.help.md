# Fashion Show Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Show Participation Request Form is designed to collect information from participants interested in taking part in a fashion show. This form helps us understand your preferences, skills, and availability to better organize the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Make sure to provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
3.  Specify the date and time you are available to participate.
4.  Indicate your role preference during the show (e.g., model, designer, etc.).
5.  Describe your fashion item, including any relevant images or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Please provide your name as it appears on your identification.
    *   **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
    *   **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please provide it for potential contact.
    *   **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): Select the date you are available to participate.
    *   **Event Time** (Event Time, time, required): Choose the time you are available to participate.
    *   **Fashion Item** (Fashion Item, text, optional): Briefly describe your fashion item, including any relevant images or information.
    *   **Role** (Role, select one, required): Select your preferred role during the show (e.g., model, designer, etc.).
    *   **Fashion Item Description** (Fashion Item Description, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your fashion item.
    *   **Fashion Item Image** (Fashion Item Image, text, optional): Upload any relevant images of your fashion item.
    *   **Participation Type** (Participation Type, select multiple, required): Indicate whether you are interested in being a model, designer, or both.
