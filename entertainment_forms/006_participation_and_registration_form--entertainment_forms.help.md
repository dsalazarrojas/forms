# Participation And Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in events, activities, or initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Event Participation** field with a brief description of the event or activity you're interested in participating in.
2. Proceed to the **Participant Details** field and provide your personal information, including your name, email, and other relevant details.
3. If you're planning to participate in fundraising, fill out the **Fundraising** field with your fundraising goals and methods.
4. Share your **Past Experience** if applicable.
5. If you have any medical history or concerns, describe them in the **Medical History** field.
6. Carefully review and select your answer for **Terms and Conditions**.
7. Confirm your acceptance of the terms by selecting **Confirm Terms**.
8. Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the **Participant Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Participation (Required: false)
* This field is for a brief description of the event or activity you're interested in participating in.

### Participant Details (Required: true)
* This field requires your personal information, including your name, email, and any other details that are relevant to your participation.

### Fundraising (Required: false)
* If applicable, fill out this field with your fundraising goals and methods.

### Past Experience (Required: false)
* Share your relevant past experience, if applicable.

### Medical History (Required: false)
* Describe any medical history or concerns you have.

### Terms and Conditions (Required: true)
* Read and select your answer carefully, indicating whether you accept or decline the terms.

### Confirm Terms (Required: true)
* Confirm your acceptance of the terms selected.

### Participant Notes (Required: false)
* Add any additional notes or comments you want to share with the organizers.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure successful registration.
* Double-check your information before submitting.
* Review the terms carefully to understand your responsibilities as a participant.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organizers for assistance.

Please review the form carefully and submit your completed form to register your participation.
