# Theater Audience Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone at the theater.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to include all required fields (denoted by an asterisk *).
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for boolean fields.
4. If you have any special requests or accessibility needs, provide detailed comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (User Name, text, optional): This is your name.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Your contact number.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Accessibility Request** (Accessibility Request, text, optional): Please enter any special requests or accessibility needs.
* **Arrival Time** (Arrival Time, time, required): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the theater.
* **Confirm Arrival Time** (Confirm Arrival Time, time, optional): Confirm your planned arrival time.
* **Entry Confirmed** (Entry Confirmed, select_one, required): Confirm that you have a ticket and that you will be entering the theater.
* **Number of Guests** (Number of Guests, number, optional): How many people are in your group.
* **Ticket Number** (Ticket Number, text, optional): Your ticket number.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Any additional comments or information.
* **Is First Time Visitor** (Is First Time Visitor, select_one, optional): If this is your first time visiting the theater.
* **Is Wheelchair Accessible** (Is Wheelchair Accessible, select_one, required): If you need assistance due to a mobility issue.
* **Is Service Animal Allowed** (Is Service Animal Allowed, select_one, optional): If you are bringing a service animal.
* **Parking Information** (Parking Information, select_one, optional): If you need special parking arrangements.
* **Is First Time Attendee** (Is First Time Attendee, select_one, required): If this is your first time attending a show at this theater.
* **Confirm First Time Attendee** (Confirm First Time Attendee, select_one, optional): Confirm your status as a first-time attendee.
* **Is Wheelchair Accessibility Request** (Is Wheelchair Accessibility Request, select_one, optional): If you need assistance due to a mobility issue.
* **Confirm Wheelchair Accessibility Request** (Confirm Wheelchair Accessibility Request, select_one, optional): Confirm your request for assistance.
* **Is Service Animal** (Is Service Animal, select_one, optional): If you are bringing a service animal.
* **Confirm Service Animal** (Confirm Service Animal, select_one, optional): Confirm that you are bringing a service animal.
* **Is Accessible** (Is Accessible, select_one, optional): If you need accessible seating.
* **Confirm Accessible** (Confirm Accessible, select_one, optional): Confirm your need for accessible seating.
* **Has Ticket** (Has Ticket, select_one, required): Confirm that you have a ticket.
* **Confirm Ticket** (Confirm Ticket, select_one, optional): Confirm your ticket purchase.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, text, optional): Any additional comments or information.
