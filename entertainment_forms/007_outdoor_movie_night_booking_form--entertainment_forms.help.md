# Outdoor Movie Night Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Outdoor Movie Night Booking Form is designed to collect information for an outdoor movie night event. This form will help you gather essential details about the event, including the event name, start and end times, attendees, movie title, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant event information.
2. Choose the correct options for each field based on your event needs.
3. Ensure you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event, such as "Summer Movie Night".
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" if attendees are expected, "No" if not.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 20:00 for 8:00 PM).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 22:00 for 10:00 PM).
* **Movie Title** (`movie_title`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the title of the movie being shown.
* **Seats Preferred** (`seats_preferred`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the preferred seating options, such as "Yes" for preferred, "No" for not preferred.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person or organization.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the contact person or organization.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event in the format "DD/MM/YYYY" (e.g., 01/06/2023).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or information about the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of event, such as "Movie Night".
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 20:00 for 8:00 PM).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is identical to the "Event Date" field above.
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or information about the contact person or organization.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields (DD/MM/YYYY and 24-hour format, respectively).
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to the form's purpose and the explanations above.
