# lip_sync_performance_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lip_sync_performance_feedback form is used to collect feedback on lip sync performances. It's used by instructors or adjudicators to evaluate and improve the skills of performers in a dance or music competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the feedback you want to provide on a lip sync performance.
2. Select the synchronization level you observed during the performance.
3. Rate the performer's creativity, using the rating field.
4. Add any additional comments about the performance in the "comments" field.
5. Choose multiple stage presences from the dropdown list.
6. Optionally, provide a rating for the performance.
7. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Synchronization** (number, required: false): Rate the synchronization of the performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Stage Presence** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple stage presences from the dropdown list, such as 1, 2, 3, or 4. This will help identify areas where the performer excels or needs improvement.
* **Creativity** (text, required: false): Rate the performer's creativity, explaining what specifically stood out to you and why.
* **Comments** (note, required: false): Provide specific comments about the performance, such as what they got right or wrong.
* **Rating** (number, required: false): Optionally, provide a overall rating for the performance.
