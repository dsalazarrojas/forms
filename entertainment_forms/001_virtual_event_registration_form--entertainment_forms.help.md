# Virtual Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a virtual event. It collects your name and contact information for event organizers to contact you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth in the respective fields.
3. Enter your Email and Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Your valid email address. This is where event organizers will contact you for event updates and information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Your phone number. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for event organizers to reach you if needed.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Your date of birth. This is required for event organizers to verify your identity. Please format it as MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD.
