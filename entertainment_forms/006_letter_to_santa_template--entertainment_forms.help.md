# Letter To Santa Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help Santa understand your holiday wishes and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above. Start by answering the questions on each page, working from left to right.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your name?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_1`, text, required): This is the first question on the form. Please provide your name as it is written on your birth certificate or as you like it to be written by Santa.
* **What's your favorite toy?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_2`, text, required): Please tell Santa about your favorite toy or gift that you would like to receive this holiday season.
* **Why do you want this toy?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_3`, text, required): Explain to Santa why you want this toy, so he can understand your wishes better.
* **What's your favorite thing to do?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_4`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the things you enjoy doing in your free time.
* **How old are you?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_5`, number, required): Please provide your age as of the current date.
* **Do you want to share this letter with Santa?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_6`, select_one, required): Please select whether you want to share this letter with Santa or not.
* **What's the best part about being a kid?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_7`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the things you enjoy about being a kid.
* **Do you have any other questions for Santa?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_8`, text, optional): If you have any questions for Santa, please provide them here.
* **Where are you from?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_9`, text, required): Please provide the name of your city, state, or region.
* **Who is helping you write this letter?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_10`, text, required): Please provide the name of the person helping you write this letter.
* **What's your favorite color?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_11`, select_one, required): Please select your favorite color from the options provided.
* **What's your favorite animal?** (`letter_to_santa_template_page_12`, select_one, required): Please select your favorite animal from the options provided.
