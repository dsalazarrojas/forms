# streaming_service_engagement_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their streaming service engagement. It helps us understand how often you consume streaming content, your overall user experience, content quality, and satisfaction with the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability.
2.  Select your answers from the provided options for each question.
3.  You can leave any optional field blank, but keep in mind that providing feedback on all fields will help us better understand your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How often do you consume streaming content?** (`content_consumption`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer this question by selecting all the options that apply to your streaming content consumption. This will help us understand your streaming habits.
- **What was your overall user experience like?** (`user_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the options that best describes your experience with the streaming service. This will give us an idea of how you perceive the service overall.
- **How often do you consume streaming content?** (`content_quality`, `number`, required: false): This question is a bit confusing, as it seems to be a duplicate of the previous one. However, it seems to ask about content consumption frequency. Answer this question by typing a whole number (e.g., 1-10 times per week).
- **How satisfied are you with the streaming service?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the options that best describes your level of satisfaction with the streaming service. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction.
- **Comments** (`comment`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback on your experience with the streaming service. Feel free to share any thoughts or suggestions you have!
