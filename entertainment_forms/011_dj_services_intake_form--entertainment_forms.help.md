# DJ Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect event details and client information for entertainment services. This information will be used to schedule and plan events such as DJ sets, weddings, or other types of events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Enter the required event details in the "Event Details" field on page 1.
3. Enter the required client information in the "Client Info" field on page 1.
4. Ensure that you have reviewed and entered accurate information for both the event details and client info.
5. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): This field is used to describe the event where you will be providing your services. Enter the date, time, location, and any other relevant details about the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide more details about the event. Enter any additional information that may be necessary for the event.

### Client Info
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect information about the client. Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and information to proceed with the event.
* Double-check the information entered in the form before submission.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, do not hesitate to contact the form creator for support.
