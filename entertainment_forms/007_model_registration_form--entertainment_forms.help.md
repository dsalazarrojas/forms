# Model Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Registration Form is used to gather detailed information about a model, such as their name, contact details, and health status. This form is essential for our team to accurately record and keep track of the model's data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with accurate and up-to-date information about the model.
2. For the fields that are not marked as required, you can leave them blank if you do not have the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the model's first name. This field is not required.
* **Last Name**: Enter the model's last name. This field is not required.
* **Phone**: Enter the model's phone number. This field is not required.
* **Email**: Enter the model's email address. This field is not required. Please note that this field is used for contact purposes, so make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Height**: Enter the model's height in centimeters. This field is not required.
* **Weight**: Enter the model's weight in kilograms. This field is not required.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact details about the model, such as their address or other contact methods. This field is not required.
* **Health Info**: Select the model's current health status:
	+ Healthy: The model is healthy and in good condition.
	+ Sick: The model is not healthy and requires medical attention.
	+ Dead: The model is deceased.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Measurements**: Select the model's measurements:
	+ 30-34
	+ 35-39
	+ 40-44
	+ 45-49
	+ 50-54
	+ 55-59
	+ 60-64
	+ 65-69
	+ 70-74
	+ 75-79
	+ 80-84
	+ 85-89
	+ 90-94
	+ 95-99
	+ 100+
	+ This field is not required.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the model's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format. This field is not required.
* **Time of Registration**: Enter the time of registration. This field is not required.
* **Agency Contact**: Enter the contact information for the agency responsible for the model. This field is not required.
