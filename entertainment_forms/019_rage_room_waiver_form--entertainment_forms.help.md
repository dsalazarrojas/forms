# rage_room_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "rage_room_waiver_form" is a form designed to collect information from participants in an activity that may pose some risks or health concerns. It helps the organizers and staff to assess the participant's suitability for the activity and provide necessary guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Be honest and accurate when answering questions about your medical history and current health status.
3. If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for clarification or support.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency contact first name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact last name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Participant** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship to the participant.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Height (inches)** (`participant_height`, `number`, required): Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight (pounds)** (`participant_weight`, `number`, required): Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Medical condition** (`participant_medical_condition`, `text`, required): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in the activity, enter them here.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in this activity?** (`participant_medical_condition_2`, `text`, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to this question.
* **Please describe your medical condition** (`participant_medical_condition_3`, `text`, required): If you answered "yes" to the previous question, describe your medical condition here.
* **Have you ever had any previous injuries or medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in this activity?** (`participant_medical_condition_4`, `text`, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to this question.
* **Please describe your previous injuries or medical conditions** (`participant_medical_condition_5`, `text`, required): If you answered "yes" to the previous question, describe your previous injuries or medical conditions here.
* **Are you taking any medications that may affect your ability to participate in this activity?** (`participant_medical_condition_6`, `text`, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to this question.
* **List any medications** (`participant_medical_condition_7`, `text`, required): If you answered "yes" to the previous question, list your medications here.
* **Do you take any medications for a medical condition or injury that may affect your ability to participate in this activity?** (`participant_medical_condition_8`, `text`, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to this question.
* **Please describe your medications** (`participant_medical_condition_9`, `text`, required): If you answered "yes" to the previous question, describe your medications here.
* **Are you currently pregnant or breastfeeding?** (`participant_medical_condition_10`, `text`, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to this question.
* **Are you taking any blood thinners or anticoagulants?** (`participant_medical_condition_11`, `text`, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to this question.
* **Do you take any blood pressure medications?** (`participant_medical_condition_12`, `text`, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to this question.
* **Do you take any other medications?** (`participant_medical_condition_13`, `text`, required): Answer "yes" or "no" to this question.
