# Solo Performer Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from solo performers about their background, experience, and training habits. The data collected will be used to tailor training services to meet their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields.
2. Enter your phone number (optional).
3. Select your primary area of discipline (Instrumental/Composition, Dance, Theater, Visual Arts, or Other).
4. Indicate your level of experience (years or months).
5. Specify your training habits (Regularly, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
6. Select any support needs you have (Coaching, Financial Support, Performance Opportunities, or Other).
7. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Discipline** (`discipline`, `select_one`, required): Choose one area where you excel in (Instrumental/Composition, Dance, Theater, Visual Arts, or Other).
* **Experience** (`experience`, `number`, required): Indicate your level of experience in years or months.
* **Training Habits** (`training_habits`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all training habits that apply (Regularly, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never).
* **Support Needs** (`support_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all support needs you have (Coaching, Financial Support, Performance Opportunities, or Other).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your background and experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when providing information about your background and experience.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification or assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the training program administrators.
* The data collected will be kept confidential and used solely for the purpose of providing tailored training services.
