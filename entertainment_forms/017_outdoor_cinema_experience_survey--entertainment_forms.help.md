# Outdoor Cinema Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about users' experiences with outdoor cinema screenings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the movie title in the "Movie Title" field.
2. Select the location where you watched the movie from the "Location" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the movie length in minutes in the "Movie Length" field.
4. Enter the movie start time in 24-hour format (e.g., 20:00) in the "Movie Start Time" field.
5. Enter the number of shows (screenings) you attended in the "Showtime" field.
6. Select the ticket price range from the "Ticket Price" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the number of seats you occupied in the "Number of Seats" field.
8. Select the options that apply to the food and drinks you consumed during the movie from the "Food and Drinks" dropdown menu.
9. Enter your subjective assessment of the sound quality (1-10) in the "Sound Quality" field.
10. Select the quality of the movie from the "Movie Quality" dropdown menu.
11. Select your overall experience (e.g., positive, negative, neutral) from the "Overall Experience" dropdown menu.
12. Add any additional comments about your experience in the "Comments" field.
