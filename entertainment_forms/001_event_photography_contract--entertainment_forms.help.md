# Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide information about their event and photography preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
Please read through each section carefully and fill in the required information. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your photographer for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as the client or representative of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event. If the event is recurring, please enter the start date.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Provide the location of the event, including the venue and address if applicable.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of event (Event, Wedding, Birthday, Party, or Ceremony).
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your photographer's name, or leave blank if you don't know.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the contract in days, hours, or minutes.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required: false): Agree to the payment terms and conditions with your photographer.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, required: false): This is a field for any additional notes or agreements between you and your photographer.
* **Photography Services** (`photography_services`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional services you require from your photographer, such as Service, Coverage, Post-Editing, Retouching, or Print Release.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required: false): Enter your address if you're providing it to your photographer.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number if you're providing it to your photographer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email if you're providing it to your photographer.
* **Client Message** (`client_message`, text, required: false): Leave a message for your photographer, if desired.
* **Photographer Message** (`photographer_message`, text, required: false): This is a field for your photographer to leave a message or notes for you.
* **Contract Reviewed** (`contract_reviewed`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you've reviewed the contract terms with your photographer.
