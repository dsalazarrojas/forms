# Band Member Identification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from band members, including their basic details, contact information, and availability. Providing accurate and up-to-date information will help the band management or organizer to communicate effectively, schedule rehearsals and shows, and ensure that the band functions smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your legal name in the "Member full name" field.
3. Add your preferred name or stage name in the "Preferred name or stage name" field, if applicable.
4. Select your primary role in the band from the options provided in the "Role in band" field.
5. List all the instruments you play in the "Instruments played" field.
6. Enter the number of years you have been playing professionally or regularly in the "Years of experience" field.
7. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact email address" field, which will be used for band communications.
8. If you have a mobile or landline phone number, enter it in the "Contact phone number" field.
9. In case of an emergency, provide the name and phone number of the person who should be contacted in the "Emergency contact name" and "Emergency contact phone" fields.
10. Add any availability notes, such as typical rehearsal and show times, in the "Availability notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Preferred name or stage name**: Enter your preferred name or stage name that you are known by in the music industry.
* **Role in band**: Select your primary role in the band, such as lead vocalist, guitarist, or drummer.
* **Instruments played**: List all the instruments you are proficient in playing.
* **Years of experience**: Enter the number of years you have been playing professionally or regularly.
* **Contact email address**: Enter the email address where you can be contacted by the band management or organizer.
* **Contact phone number**: Enter your mobile or landline phone number, if you have one.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Availability notes**: Add any notes about your typical availability for rehearsals and shows.
