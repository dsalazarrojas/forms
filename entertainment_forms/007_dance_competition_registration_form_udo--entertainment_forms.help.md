# Dance Competition Registration Form UDO - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Competition Registration Form is designed for dancers to register for a dance competition. This form collects necessary information from the dancer to help the competition organizers plan and execute the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form information section (page 1).
2. Enter your name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide your school name if you are a student or affiliated with a dance school.
4. Answer questions about your dance experience and age category.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Form Information
* **Form Information** (`form_info`, `note`, not required): This section is used to provide any additional information about the form and its purpose.

### Page 2: Dancer Information
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number.

### Page 3: Dance School
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, not required): If you are a student or affiliated with a dance school, enter your school name.
* **Dance School** (`dance_school`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check if you are affiliated with a dance school.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, not required): Check if you fit into this category.
* **Division** (`division`, `select_one`, not required): Check if you fit into this division.
* **Category Type** (`category_type`, `select_one`, not required): Check if you fit into this category type.
* **Solo** (`solo`, `select_one`, not required): Check if you are a solo dancer.
* **Age Category** (`solo_age`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check your age category.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the competition organizers.
