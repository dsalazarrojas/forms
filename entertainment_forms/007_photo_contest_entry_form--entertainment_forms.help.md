# Photo Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Contest Entry Form is designed to collect contact information and photos from users participating in a photo contest. This form is intended for anyone interested in sharing their photos and participating in the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your contact information in the "Contact Information" page. This section includes fields for your name, email address, and other relevant details.
2. Upload up to 3 photos, each with a file size limit of 5MB, in the "Photos" page.
3. In the "Category" field, select the category that best fits your photo (Animals, Food, or Travel).
4. Optionally, add a tag to help us understand the theme or content of your photo.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, required): Provide your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Photos** (photos, text, required): Upload up to 3 photos that fit the category you've selected. Please ensure each photo is within the 5MB file size limit.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Share any additional comments or stories about your photo.
* **Photo 1** (photo1, text, optional): Upload your first photo.
* **Photo 2** (photo2, text, optional): Upload your second photo.
* **Photo 3** (photo3, text, optional): Upload your third photo.
* **Category** (category, select_one, required): Select the category that best fits your photo (Animals, Food, or Travel).
* **Tag** (tag, text, optional): Add a tag to help us understand the theme or content of your photo.
* **Submit** (submit, text, required): Confirm your entry by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Tips
* Please ensure each photo is within the 5MB file size limit.
* If you have any issues with the form or uploading photos, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We appreciate your participation in the photo contest and look forward to reviewing your entry!
