# Online Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you RSVP (Respond to a Social Event) for an online party. It collects information about the event and your response status. The goal is to inform the party host about your attendance and any special details you'd like to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Birthday Details** and select the relevant options.
2. List the guests attending under **Guests**.
3. Select your **RSVP Status** to let the host know if you can attend or not.
4. Add any additional comments under **Comment**.
5. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** (if applicable).
6. If the event has a specific **Birthday Date**, enter it in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
7. If the event has a specific **Time**, enter it in the format `hh:mm AM/PM`.
8. Select the date you plan to **RSVP** in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
9. Choose the **Party Details** that apply to you (if applicable).
10. Select the **Event Type** that best describes the event.
11. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** (if applicable) in the format `hh:mm AM/PM`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Birthday Details**: Enter your birthday in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Guests**: List the names of the guests attending the party, one per line.
* **RSVP Status**: Select the option that best describes your response to the party invitation.
* **Comment**: Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the host.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the host can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Birthday Date**: If the party has a specific birthday, enter it in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the party in the format `hh:mm AM/PM`.
* **RSVP Date**: Enter the date you plan to RSVP in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Party Details**: Choose the options that apply to you, as they relate to the party.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event that best describes it.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event in the format `hh:mm AM/PM`.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event in the format `hh:mm AM/PM`.
