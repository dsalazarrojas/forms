# Music Distribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the status of music distribution, including whether the music is distributed, the genre and license agreement details, and the agreement date and time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **musician_name** field with the name of the musician who owns the music.
2. Enter the **musician_email** field with the email address of the musician.
3. Enter the **musician_phone** field with the phone number of the musician.
4. Select **music_publisher** to indicate whether the music is distributed by a publisher.
5. Select one or more **music_genre** options that match the genre of the music.
6. Select one or more **music_license** options that match the type of license agreement for the music.
7. If necessary, enter the **music_license_agreement_note** field with any additional notes about the license agreement.
8. If applicable, enter the **music_license_agreement_date** and **music_license_agreement_time** fields with the date and time of the license agreement.
9. Select **music_publisher_agreement** to indicate whether the music publisher agrees with the license agreement.
10. Enter the **music_publisher_address** field with the address of the music publisher.
11. Enter the **music_publisher_phone** field with the phone number of the music publisher.
12. Enter the **music_publisher_email** field with the email address of the music publisher.
13. Enter the **music_publisher_website** field with the website of the music publisher.
14. If applicable, enter the **music_publisher_license_agreement_date** and **music_publisher_license_agreement_time** fields with the date and time of the music publisher's license agreement.
15. Enter any additional notes about the music publisher's license agreement in the **music_publisher_license_agreement_note** field.
16. Finally, select **submit** to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **musician_name**: Enter the name of the musician who owns the music.
* **musician_email**: Enter the email address of the musician.
* **musician_phone**: Enter the phone number of the musician.
* **music_publisher**: Select 'Yes' if the music is distributed by a publisher, 'No' otherwise.
* **music_genre**: Select one or more genres that match the type of music. For example, if the music is a pop song, select 'Pop'.
* **music_license**: Select one or more license options that match the type of license agreement for the music. For example, if the license agreement is for commercial use, select 'Commercial Use'.
* **music_license_agreement_note**: Enter any additional notes about the license agreement.
* **music_license_agreement_date**: Enter the date of the license agreement.
* **music_license_agreement_time**: Enter the time of the license agreement.
* **music_publisher_agreement**: Select 'Yes' if the music publisher agrees with the license agreement, 'No' otherwise.
* **music_publisher_address**: Enter the address of the music publisher.
* **music_publisher_phone**: Enter the phone number of the music publisher.
* **music_publisher_email**: Enter the email address of the music publisher.
* **music_publisher_website**: Enter the website of the music publisher.
* **music_publisher_license_agreement_date**: Enter the date of the music publisher's license agreement.
* **music_publisher_license_agreement_time**: Enter the time of the music publisher's license agreement.
* **music_publisher_license_agreement_note**: Enter any additional notes about the music publisher's license agreement.
* **submit**: Select 'Yes' to complete and submit the form.
