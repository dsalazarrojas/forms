# Concert Goer Future Event Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your preferences for future concerts and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your attendee name and contact email (if you wish to receive occasional event updates).
2. Select up to three preferred music genres from the available options.
3. Choose the type of venue you prefer (e.g., indoor, outdoor, or festival).
4. Indicate your typical ticket price range.
5. Select your preferred day for events (if applicable).
6. If you're willing, indicate your willingness to travel for your favorite artists.
7. Select up to three amenities that are most important to you (e.g., seating comfort, food and beverage).
8. Indicate your interest in subscribing to events or purchasing a season pass.
9. Leave any additional comments or suggestions to help us improve events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ticket or ID.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address to receive occasional event updates.
* **Preferred music genres**: Select up to three music genres that you enjoy listening to, such as Rock, Pop, Electronic, etc.
* **Preferred venue type**: Choose the type of venue you prefer for events, such as Arena, Theater, Outdoor festival, etc.
* **Typical ticket price range**: Select your usual ticket price range when attending events.
* **Preferred show day**: If you have a preference for weekday or weekend events, select the option that suits you best.
* **Willingness to travel**: Indicate how far you are willing to travel for your favorite artists, or select "Local only" if you're not interested in traveling far.
* **Most important amenities**: Select up to three amenities that are most important to you, such as Seating comfort, Food and beverage, Accessibility, etc.
* **Interest in event subscription**: Indicate your interest in subscribing to events or purchasing a season pass.
* **Other comments or suggestions**: Share any additional thoughts or suggestions that can help us improve future events.

## Tips

* Be honest about your preferences, and select all applicable options.
* If you're unsure about a particular preference, choose the option that best represents your feelings.
* Feel free to add any comments or suggestions to help us understand your needs and preferences better.
