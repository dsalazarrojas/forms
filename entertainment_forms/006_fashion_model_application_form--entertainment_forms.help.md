# Fashion Model Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fashion Model Application Form is designed to gather essential information from aspiring fashion models to assess their fit for a modeling career. This form helps us evaluate your qualifications, experience, and personal details to determine your potential as a fashion model.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your contact details, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
2. Describe your experience in the fashion industry, including any relevant skills or training.
3. Select your eye and hair color.
4. Provide your agency information (if applicable).
5. Enter your personal details, including your height and weight.
6. Choose your emergency contact's name, relationship, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form (Page 1)** (`application_form`, text, required: false): Briefly introduce yourself and describe your interest in becoming a fashion model.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: true): Enter your contact email and phone number (if you have one).
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required: true): Include any relevant experience in the fashion industry, such as modeling gigs, fashion shows, or fashion-related education.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all relevant skills, such as language proficiency or modeling skills.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, note, required: false): Upload or describe your portfolio, including any relevant photos or videos.
* **Photo** (`photo`, number, required: false): Upload a photo of yourself.
* **Agency Information** (`agency_info`, select_one, required: true): Select your agency from the options provided.
* **Model Details** (`model_details`, text, required: true): Enter your personal details, including your height and weight.
* **Model Information** (`model_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Model Statistics** (`model_stats`, text, required: false): Enter any additional statistics or metrics about yourself.
* **Height** (`model_stats_height`, number, required: true): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`model_stats_weight`, number, required: true): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Eye Color** (`eye_color`, select_one, required: true): Select your eye color.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, select_one, required: true): Select your hair color.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: true): Enter your email.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Address** (`contact_address`, text, required: true): Enter your address.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required: true): Select your availability for modeling gigs.
* **Language** (`language`, select_multiple, required: false): Select languages you are proficient in.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: true): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: true): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required: true): Select your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: true): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required: true): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, required: false): Enter any additional submission information.
