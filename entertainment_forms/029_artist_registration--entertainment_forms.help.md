# Artist Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Registration form is a tool to collect essential information about artists, bands, or groups to facilitate collaboration, promotion, and discovery in the entertainment industry. This form helps artists get their work noticed and potentially connect with other artists, promoters, or booking agents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Artist Registration form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `Email Address` field. This must be a valid email address where you can be contacted.
4. Enter your phone number in the `Phone Number` field. This must be a valid phone number where you can be reached.
5. If applicable, provide your band or group name in the `Band or Group Name` field.
6. Enter the primary music genre you're associated with in the `Primary Music Genre` field.
7. List any relevant sub-genres or styles in the `Sub-genres or Related Styles` field, separated by commas.
8. Provide the year your band or artist formed in the `Year Band or Artist Formed` field, if applicable.
9. List the names and instruments of your band members in the `Band Member Names and Instruments` field, if applicable.
10. Write a 100-200 word biography about yourself or your band in the `Artist or Band Biography` field, describing your artistry, style, and any relevant experience.
11. List any notable works or albums you've released in the `Notable Works or Albums` field.
12. If you've received awards or recognition, list them in the `Awards or Recognition Received` field.
13. Share your social media links in the `Social Media Links` field, separated by commas.
14. If applicable, list the streaming platforms where your work is available in the `Available on Streaming Platforms` field, separated by commas.
15. Choose your registration category or type from the options in the `Registration Category or Type` field.
16. Select your experience level as an artist in the `Experience Level` field.
17. Indicate whether you are available for booking in the `Available for Booking` field.
18. If you have a typical performance fee range, enter it in the `Typical Performance Fee Range` field.
19. Finally, provide any additional information about yourself or your band in the `Additional Information` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`artist_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`artist_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Band or Group Name** (`band_name`, text, optional): Provide your band or group name, if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Website or Portfolio URL** (`website_url`, text, optional): Provide a URL to your website or portfolio, if applicable.
* **Primary Music Genre** (`music_genre`, text, required): Select the primary music genre you're associated with.
* **Sub-genres or Related Styles** (`subgenres`, text, optional): List any sub-genres or styles relevant to your music, separated by commas.
* **Year Band or Artist Formed** (`formation_year`, number, optional): Enter the year your band or artist formed, if applicable.
* **Band Member Names and Instruments** (`band_members`, text, optional): List the names and instruments of your band members, if applicable.
* **Artist or Band Biography** (`artists_bio`, text, required): Write a 100-200 word biography about yourself or your band, describing your artistry, style, and experience.
* **Notable Works or Albums** (`notable_works`, text, optional): List any notable works or albums you've released.
* **Awards or Recognition Received** (`awards_recognition`, text, optional): List any awards or recognition you've received.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, text, optional): Share your social media links, separated by commas.
* **Available on Streaming Platforms** (`streaming_platforms`, text, optional): List any streaming platforms where your work is available, separated by commas.
* **Registration Category or Type** (`registration_category`, select_one, required): Choose your registration category or type from the options.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, optional): Select your experience level as an artist.
* **Available for Booking** (`booking_status`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are available for booking.
* **Typical Performance Fee Range** (`performance_fee`, text, optional): Enter your typical performance fee range, if applicable.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself or your band.
