# Film Crew Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us find the right talent for our film projects. By providing us with your contact information, experience, skills, and availability, you'll be helping us understand how you can best contribute to our crew.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible. Ensure you provide us with your most up-to-date contact details. If you're unsure about any of the fields or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (1)**:
	* A text field to enter your name (1), email address (8), and phone number (7).
* **Experience (2)**:
	* A text field to briefly describe your experience in the film industry, if applicable.
* **Role (3)**:
	* A text field to specify the role you're interested in applying for, such as Camera, Lighting, or Audio.
* **Skills (4)**:
	* A text field where you can list your relevant skills, such as cinematography, editing, or sound design.
* **Availability (5)**:
	* A number field to indicate how many hours per week you're willing to commit to a project, if applicable.
* **Contact Info 2 (6)**:
	* A repeat of the contact fields from the top section (name, phone number, and email address), to help us reach out to you about potential opportunities.
* **Have you worked with our crew before? (10)**:
	* A select_one field to indicate if you've collaborated with us before.
* **What role would you like to take in the crew? (11)**:
	* A select_multiple field to choose from available roles in our crew (Camera, Lighting, Audio, Grip, Electric).
* **What other skills do you have? (12)**:
	* A text field to provide any additional skills you bring to the table.
* **How many hours are you available per week? (13)**:
	* A number field to indicate your availability for the project, if applicable.
* **Contact Info 2 (14-16)**:
	* A repeat of the contact fields (name, phone number, and email address), to help us reach out to you about potential opportunities.
* **Message (18)**:
	* A text field for any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
- Ensure you're honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Don't hesitate to reach out if you have questions or need clarification about any of the fields.
- We'll review your application and be in touch with you soon after.
