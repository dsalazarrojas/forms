# Match Maker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Match Maker Form is designed to collect information from clients about their personal details, contact information, and other relevant aspects. This form is meant to be completed by clients or users who are interested in getting matched with a partner or service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Match Maker Form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and its purpose to understand what is required.
2. Enter your personal details, including your relationship status and any other relevant information.
3. Provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you.
4. Answer questions about your smoking status and any other relevant aspects.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`Client Information`, `text`, required: `false`): This is the first field where you can enter your name and other relevant details.
* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Please select your personal details, such as Male, Female, or Other.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, or other relevant details.
* **Smoking Status** (`Smoking Status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Please select your smoking status, such as True, False, Rarely, or Never.
* **Relationship Status** (`Relationship Status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select your relationship status, such as Single, In a Relationship, Married, or Other.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We will review your information and get in touch with you shortly.

Note: The above guide is based on the provided YAML schema, where each field is clearly explained and easy to understand. The form is designed to be simple and user-friendly, making it easy for clients to complete and get matched with a partner or service provider.
