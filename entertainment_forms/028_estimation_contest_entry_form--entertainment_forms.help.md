# Estimation Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estimation Contest Entry Form is used to submit estimations for a contest. Please fill out the form with your details to participate in the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Estimate the value of the item in the "estimate value" field.
4. Select the currency of your estimate in the "estimate currency" field.
5. Guess the date of the event in the "guess date" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "contact email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "contact phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (first name)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter your first name.
* **Last name (last name)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter your last name.
* **Estimate value (estimate value)** (`number`, **optional**): Estimate the value of the item.
* **Estimate currency (estimate currency)** (`select_one`, **optional**): Select the currency of your estimate (USD, EUR, CNY, JPY, CHT).
* **Guess date (guess date)** (`date`, **optional**): Guess the date of the event.
* **Contact email (contact email)** (`email`, **optional**): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact phone (contact phone)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the contest organizers for clarification.
