# Trunk Or Treat Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from judges for Trunk or Treat events. Judges will use this form to score events based on categories such as decorations, food, and entertainment. The form will help event organizers and judges to provide valuable feedback to participants and improve future Trunk or Treat events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the event and select the correct category.
2. Describe the event in as much detail as possible.
3. Score the event for decorations, food, and entertainment using numbers.
4. Provide feedback on the event.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Choose whether to submit or save the form for later, or exit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (1): Enter the name of the event you are judging.
* **Category** (2): Select the category that best describes the event, such as Decorations, Food, or Entertainment.
* **Event Description** (3): Describe the event in as much detail as possible. This will help event organizers and judges understand the event better.
* **Decorations** (4): Score the event's decorations, where higher scores indicate better decorations. Use numbers to score.
* **Food** (5): Score the event's food, where higher scores indicate better food. Use numbers to score.
* **Entertainment** (6): Score the event's entertainment, where higher scores indicate better entertainment. Use numbers to score.
* **Overall** (7): Provide an overall score for the event. Use numbers to score.
* **Feedback** (8): Leave any feedback or comments you have about the event.
* **Email** (9): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with questions or follow-up.
* **Phone** (10): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Submit** (11): Once you have completed the form, choose whether to submit it or save it for later, or exit the form completely.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use numbers to score events for decorations, food, and entertainment.
* Provide detailed feedback in the Feedback field to help event organizers and participants improve future events.
* Use your actual email address and phone number to ensure that we can contact you with questions or follow-up.
