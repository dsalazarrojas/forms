# Music Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and manage music records, including artist, album, release date, and purchase details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Music Inventory Information** field with a brief description of the music record.
2. Select the **Artist** field to indicate whether the music record is by a specific artist or not.
3. Choose the **Album** field to indicate whether the music record is part of a specific album or not.
4. Enter the **Record ID** number, if applicable.
5. Select the **Genre** field to choose one or multiple genres for the music record.
6. Enter the **Price** of the music record, if applicable.
7. Select the **Release Date** field to choose the date of release for the music record.
8. Choose the **Record Condition** field to indicate the condition of the music record.
9. Select the **Purchase Date** field to choose the date of purchase for the music record.
10. Enter the **Purchase Value** of the music record, if applicable.
11. Choose the **Purchase Currency** field to select the currency of purchase for the music record.
12. Enter the **Purchase Cost** of the music record, if applicable.
13. Enter the **Record Weight** of the music record, if applicable.
14. Choose the **Record Size** field to select the size of the music record.
15. Choose the **Record Type** field to indicate the type of music record.
16. Add any additional notes about the record's condition in the **Condition Note** field, if applicable.
17. Add any additional notes about the purchase in the **Purchase Note** field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Inventory Information** (`music_inventory_info`): A short description of the music record.
* **Artist** (`artist`): Whether the music record is by a specific artist or not. 
* **Album** (`album`): Whether the music record is part of a specific album or not. 
* **Record ID** (`record_id`): A unique number for the music record, if applicable.
* **Genre** (`genre`): Select one or multiple genres for the music record.
* **Price** (`price`): The price of the music record, if applicable.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`): The date of release for the music record.
* **Record Condition** (`record_condition`): The condition of the music record.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`): The date of purchase for the music record.
* **Purchase Value** (`purchase_value`): The value of purchase for the music record, if applicable.
* **Purchase Currency** (`purchase_currency`): The currency of purchase for the music record.
* **Purchase Cost** (`purchase_cost`): The cost of purchase for the music record, if applicable.
* **Record Weight** (`record_weight`): The weight of the music record, if applicable.
* **Record Size** (`record_size`): The size of the music record.
* **Record Type** (`record_type`): The type of music record.
* **Condition Note** (`condition_note`): Notes about the record's condition, if applicable.
* **Purchase Note** (`purchase_note`): Notes about the purchase, if applicable.
