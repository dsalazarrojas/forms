# stage_play_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather registration information for stage plays.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your team name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter the script details in the "Script" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name (text, required: false)**: Enter the name of your team or production house.
* **Script (text, required: false)**: Provide a detailed description of the script.
* **Original Play Name (text, required: false)**: Note: This field is likely intended for the original play name or title, but its description is unclear. Please use this field to enter the original play's title or name, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you need to add or remove pages from the form, consider reaching out to the form owner for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact <form@gic.mx>.
