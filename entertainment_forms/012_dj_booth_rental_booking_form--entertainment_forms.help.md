# DJ Booth Rental Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to book a DJ booth for an event. It collects information about the event, including event details, dates, times, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with the relevant information.
2. Enter the event details, including event dates and times.
3. Provide contact information, including name and phone number.
4. Choose the payment method and price.
5. Submit the form to send your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Select the start and end times of the event.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time of the event.
* **End Time**: Select the end time of the event.

### Contact Information
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the person booking the event.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person booking the event.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person booking the event.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person booking the event.
* **Message**: Enter any additional message to the booking request.

### Event Details
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event (Music, Dance, Theatre, etc.).
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Venue**: Enter the venue of the event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method (Online, Cash, Bank Transfer).
* **Price**: Enter the price of the DJ booth rental.
* **Submit**: Submit the booking form to send your request.

### Additional Information
* **Event Dates**: Enter the dates of the event.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
