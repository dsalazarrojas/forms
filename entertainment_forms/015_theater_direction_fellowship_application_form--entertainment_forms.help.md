# Theater Direction Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Direction Fellowship Application Form is a formal application process for applicants to apply for a direction position in a theater company or similar organization. The form is designed to collect various details about the applicant, including their contact information, performance experience, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure completeness and correctness.
3. Upload any required documents or media as specified in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Theater Name** (`theater_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the theater company or organization you're applying to.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Describe the role you're applying for in the theater direction position.
* **Performance Experience** (`performance_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options that describe your performance experience.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): Enter your available dates and times for interviews or performances.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you're submitting the application.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time you're submitting the application.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select who submitted the application on your behalf.
* **References** (`references`, select_one, required): Select your relevant references.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your application.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any further comments about your application.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files or documents.
* **Upload Link** (`upload_link`, text, optional): Upload any link to a relevant file or document.
* **Uploaded File** (`uploaded_file`, text, optional): Upload any file that was uploaded.
* **Upload Video** (`upload_video`, text, optional): Upload any video that's relevant to your application.
* **Uploaded Video** (`uploaded_video`, text, optional): Upload any video that was uploaded.
* **Upload Audio** (`upload_audio`, text, optional): Upload any audio that's relevant to your application.
* **Uploaded Audio** (`uploaded_audio`, text, optional): Upload any audio that was uploaded.
* **Upload Image** (`upload_image`, text, optional): Upload any image that's relevant to your application.
* **Uploaded Image** (`uploaded_image`, text, optional): Upload any image that was uploaded.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, optional): Upload any document that's relevant to your application.
* **Uploaded Document** (`uploaded_document`, text, optional): Upload any document that was uploaded.

**Tips**:

* Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure completeness and correctness.
* Use accurate and truthful information in all fields.
* Upload only relevant and required files or media.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
