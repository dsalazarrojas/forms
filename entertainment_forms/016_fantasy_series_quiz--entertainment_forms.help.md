## Fantasy Series Quiz - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including your name and email address.
2. Answer the questions about your favorite fantasy worlds, books, movies, and characters.
3. Select your favorite fantasy worlds, books, and movies, as well as your favorite characters.
4. Provide a trivia score, which is likely a measure of how well you know fantasy series trivia.
5. Optionally, submit your answers and proceed to the next stage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info**: Enter your name and email address in this field. This will help us contact you with any further questions or updates.
* **Name**: Provide your full name in this field. This will help us identify you among other participants.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here. This will be used for communication purposes only.
* **Favorite Fantasy World**: Select "Yes" if you have a favorite fantasy world. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Fantasy Book**: Select "Yes" if you have read a fantasy book. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Fantasy Movie**: Select "Yes" if you have watched a fantasy movie. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Favorite Character**: Select "Yes" if you have a favorite character from a fantasy series. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Trivita Score**: This field is likely a measure of how well you know fantasy series trivia. Enter a number that represents your score.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, click this button to proceed to the next stage.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately.
