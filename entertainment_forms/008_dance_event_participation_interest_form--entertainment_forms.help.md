# Dance Event Participation Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Event Participation Interest Form is a tool used to collect information from potential participants who are interested in attending a dance event. The form is designed to help event organizers understand who is interested in participating and what types of dance styles and levels of experience they have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and understand what information is being collected.
2. Answer each question truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Info (page 1)
* **General Info** (`General Info`, `number`, required: false): This field is not used in this form and can be ignored.

### Availability (page 2)
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ Available: Indicate that you are available to participate in the event.
	+ Unavailable: Indicate that you are not available to participate in the event.

### Dance Preferences (page 3)
* **Dance Preferences** (`Dance Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more dance styles that interest you. Options:
	+ Hip Hop
	+ Contemporary
	+ Ballroom
	+ Other

### Additional Info (page 4)
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your participation in the event.

### Contact Email (page 5)
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address for us to contact you.

### Contact Phone (page 6)
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.

### Additional Contact Info (page 7)
* **Additional Contact Info** (`Additional Contact Info`, `text`, required: true): Provide any additional contact information you feel is necessary.

### Interest (page 8)
* **Interest** (`Interest`, `number`, required: true): This field will ask you to rate your level of interest in the event, with higher numbers indicating stronger interest.

### Dance Experience (page 9)
* **Dance Experience** (`Dance Experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Yes: Indicate that you have experience with dancing.
	+ No: Indicate that you have little to no experience with dancing.

### Dance Level (page 10)
* **Dance Level** (`Dance Level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ High: Indicate that you are an experienced dancer.
	+ Low: Indicate that you are a beginner or have limited experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your information is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* If you are not interested in participating in the event, please do not complete this form.
