# secret_sister_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The secret_sister_form is a tool used to collect information for an event related to an individual's Secret Sister.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your name as it is known to the Secret Sister in the "Secret Sister Name" field.
6. Indicate your relationship with the Secret Sister in the "Relationship" field by selecting one or more of the available options (e.g., "Mom", "Dad", "Grandparent", etc.).
7. If the Secret Sister has a location, enter it in the "Location" field.
8. Enter the date of birth for the Secret Sister in the "Date of Birth" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
9. If applicable, select the type of event related to the Secret Sister in the "Event Type" field.
10. If applicable, select the date of the event related to the Secret Sister in the "Event Date" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Secret Sister Name** (`secret_sister_name`, text, required): Enter the name as it is known to the Secret Sister.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available options to indicate your relationship with the Secret Sister (e.g., "Mom", "Dad", etc.).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): If the Secret Sister has a location, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth for the Secret Sister in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event related to the Secret Sister (e.g., "Music", "Dance", etc.).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): If applicable, select the date of the event related to the Secret Sister in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

**Tips**

* Make sure to double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant stakeholders for clarification.
* This form is for informational purposes only, and the information collected will be used to process and store for future reference.
