# Independent Film Promotion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations that want to promote an independent film through various media channels. It collects necessary information for efficient and effective promotion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details to ensure we can get in touch with you for any follow-up questions.
2. Provide the title of the film you want to promote.
3. If applicable, select the type of talent involved in the film (e.g., Actor, Actress, Dancer, Voice Actor).
4. If applicable, select the types of contributors involved in the film (e.g., Model, Singer, Dancer).
5. Write a brief description of the film to provide a clear understanding of the project.
6. If you have a marketing agreement in place, indicate whether it's 'True' or 'False'.
7. Indicate your consent to promote the film by selecting 'Agree' or 'Disagree'.
8. Enter your contact number for further communication.
9. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
10. If you have any supporting files for the film (e.g., images, videos), you can upload them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required: false): Fill in your contact information, including your name and contact number, so we can get in touch with you for any follow-up questions.
* **Film Title** (film_title, text, required: false): Enter the title of the film you want to promote.
* **Talent Info** (talent_info, select_one, required: false): Select the type of talent involved in the film (e.g., Actor, Actress, Dancer, Voice Actor).
* **Contributor Info** (contributor_info, select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of contributors involved in the film (e.g., Model, Singer, Dancer).
* **Film Description** (film_description, note, required: false): Write a brief description of the film to provide a clear understanding of the project.
* **Marketing Agreement** (marketing_agreement, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you have a marketing agreement in place (e.g., 'True', 'False').
* **Consent** (consent, select_one, required: false): Indicate your consent to promote the film by selecting 'Agree' or 'Disagree'.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required: false): Enter your contact number for further communication.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Upload Film File** (upload_film_file, time, required: false): If you have any supporting files for the film, you can upload them here.
* **Upload Contribution File** (upload_contribution_file, time, required: false): If you have any contribution files, you can upload them here.
