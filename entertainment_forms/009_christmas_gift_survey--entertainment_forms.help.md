# Christmas Gift Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about Christmas gift-giving habits, preferences, and experiences. The data collected will help retailers, marketers, or anyone interested in understanding consumer behavior around Christmas gift-giving.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring you answer all required fields.
2. For questions with options, select the most accurate answer that applies to you.
3. If a question asks for a text response, provide your thoughts or experiences in the provided space.
4. Ensure you are honest and provide accurate information to help us better understand Christmas gift-giving habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name if you'd like to be associated with your responses.
* **Email Address (2)**: Provide your email address for survey updates. This is required for us to send you any follow-up questions or information related to the survey.
* **Age Group (3)**: Select your age group from the provided options.
* **Gender (4)**: Select your gender if you'd like to provide demographic information. This is optional.
* **When Do You Start Shopping (6)**: Choose when you typically start buying Christmas gifts.
* **Total Gift Budget (7)**: Select your total gift budget for the holiday season.
* **Number of Gift Recipients (8)**: Choose how many people you will be buying gifts for.
* **Preferred Shopping Method (10)**: Select how you prefer to shop for Christmas gifts.
* **Favorite Retailers (11)**: Select the places where you shop most for Christmas gifts.
* **Gift Type Preference (12)**: Choose the types of gifts you prefer to give.
* **Most Challenging Aspect (13)**: Select the most difficult part of gift shopping for you.
* **Preferred Gift Categories (15)**: Choose the categories of gifts that interest you most.
* **Eco-Friendly Important (16)**: Select how important it is for gifts to be eco-friendly to you.
* **Personalized Gifts Value (17)**: Choose how much you value personalized gifts.
* **Do You Use Wish Lists (19)**: Select whether you create or share wish lists.
* **Regifting Acceptable (20)**: Choose your view on regifting, or giving someone else's previously used gift.
* **Biggest Pet Peeve (21)**: Describe the type of gift you dislike receiving the most.
* **Best Gift Ever (22)**: Share a memorable gift experience you've had.
* **Suggestions for Retailers (23)**: Provide suggestions on how retailers can improve the gift-giving experience. 

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Thank you for taking the time to participate in this survey!
