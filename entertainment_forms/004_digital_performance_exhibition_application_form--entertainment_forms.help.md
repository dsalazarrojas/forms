# Digital Performance Exhibition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Digital Performance Exhibition Application Form, which is designed for artists to apply to perform at a digital performance exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your artist details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Proposal" field, describe your performance proposal, including the type of performance, duration, and any specific requirements.
3. If you have any media links (e.g., website, social media, or online portfolios), please provide them in the "Media Links" field.
4. Make sure to review and submit the form for review by the exhibition organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Details (artist_details)**:
	+ This field is used to collect your name, contact information, and any other essential details.
	+ Please fill in your full name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Proposal (proposal)**:
	+ This field is used to describe your performance proposal, including the type of performance, duration, and any specific requirements.
	+ Please provide a detailed description of your performance, including any necessary technical specifications or special requests.
* **Media Links (media_links)**:
	+ This field is used to collect links to your online presence, such as your website, social media, or online portfolios.
	+ Please provide links to any relevant media that demonstrate your artistic skills or experience.
* **Tips**

* Make sure to proofread your form before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the exhibition organizers at form@gic.mx.
