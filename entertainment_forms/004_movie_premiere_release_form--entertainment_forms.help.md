# Movie Premiere Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a movie premiere, including contact details of individuals attending, media permission, and screening details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the movie's **Name** field with the title of the movie.
2. Enter your **Email** in the field provided to ensure you can be reached about the movie premiere.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for further contact.
4. Select one of the options in the **Media Permission** field to decide if the media is allowed to cover the event.
5. Choose the **Screening Date** for the movie premiere.
6. Select the **Screening Time** for the event.
7. Specify the **Screening Location** where the movie will be played.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: A text field to fill in the name of the movie.
* **Email**: A text field to enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Media Permission**: A dropdown menu to select whether the media is allowed to cover the event (Yes/No).
* **Screening Date**: A date field to choose the date of the movie premiere.
* **Screening Time**: A time field to select the time of the movie premiere.
* **Screening Location**: A text field to specify the venue or location of the movie premiere.
