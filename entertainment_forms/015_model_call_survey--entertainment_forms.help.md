# Model Call Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in working as models, actors, singers, or dancers. We will use this information to understand your availability, talent type, experience, and other relevant details to better match you with potential opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the "Name" field. Please provide your full name as it appears on your documents.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. This is where we will contact you if we need more information or if we are interested in working with you.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This will help us contact you for further discussion.
4. Select your **Availability** in the format **MM/DD/YYYY** (e.g., 02/01/2024).
5. Select your **Availability Time** (e.g., 08:00) using 24-hour format.
6. Choose all that apply from the **Talent Type** options: Model, Actor, Singer, Dancer, or Other.
7. Select your **Talent Experience** from the options: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced.
8. Please provide a brief description of your **Talent Description** in the "Talent Description" field. This will help us understand your background and qualifications.
9. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the **Submit** button to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This is your full name as it appears on your documents.
* **Email Address**: This is where we will contact you if we need more information or if we are interested in working with you.
* **Phone Number**: This will help us contact you for further discussion.
* **Availability**: Please select your available dates in the format **MM/DD/YYYY** (e.g., 02/01/2024).
* **Availability Time**: Select your available time in the 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Talent Type**: Choose all that apply from the options: Model, Actor, Singer, Dancer, or Other.
* **Talent Experience**: Select your level of experience from the options: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced.
* **Talent Description**: This is a brief description of your background and qualifications.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click this button to send your response.
