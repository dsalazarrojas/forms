# Participant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect participant information for events, conferences, or meetings. It helps to gather details about the participant, such as their name, contact information, and on-site contact details. This information is crucial for event organizers to efficiently manage participant registration, communication, and on-site arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the participant, including their first name, last name, and email address.
2. If an on-site contact is provided, fill in their name, last name, phone number, and company (if applicable).
3. If an on-site address is required, enter the contact's address.
4. Select the on-site check-in time and date for the participant.
5. Add any additional participant notes, if necessary.
6. Add any on-site notes, if required.
7. Finally, review your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`primary_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): The participant's first name.
* **Last Name** (`primary_contact_last_name`, `text`, required/optional): The participant's last name.
* **E-mail** (`participant_email`, `email`, required/optional): The participant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required/optional): The participant's phone number.
* **Company** (`participant_company`, `text`, required/optional): The participant's company name (if applicable).
* **On Site Contact Name** (`on_site_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): The on-site contact's name.
* **On Site Last Name** (`on_site_contact_last_name`, `text`, required/optional): The on-site contact's last name.
* **On Site Phone Number** (`primary_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): The on-site contact's phone number.
* **On Site Address** (`on_site_address`, `text`, required/optional): The on-site address of the contact.
* **On Site Checkin Time** (`on_site_checkin_time`, `time`, required/optional): The time the participant will arrive on site.
* **On Site Checkin Date** (`on_site_checkin_date`, `date`, required/optional): The date the participant will arrive on site.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Any additional information about the participant.
* **On Site Notes** (`on_site_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Any additional information about the on-site contact.
