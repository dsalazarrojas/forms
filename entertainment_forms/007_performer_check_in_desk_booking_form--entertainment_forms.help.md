# Performer Check In Desk Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performer Check In Desk Booking Form is used to collect information from performers who are scheduled to perform at an event. This form helps the event management team to gather essential details and manage the check-in process smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your performer's name and contact details in the "Performer Contact" field.
2. Choose the arrival slot that matches your performance schedule in the "Arrival Slot" field.
3. Provide any special requirements that you need to accommodate in the "Special Requirements" field (if necessary).
4. Add any relevant event information in the "Event Information" field (if necessary).
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Contact** (`Performer Details`, `text`, required/required): Enter your name and contact information as required.
* **Arrival Slot** (`Arrival Slot`, `number`, required/required): Choose the arrival time slot that matches your performance schedule.
* **Special Requirements** (`Performer Special Requirements`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any special requirements that you need to accommodate, such as equipment or space needs.
* **Event Information** (`Event Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any relevant information about the event, such as event name or venue.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your performance or needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form once and accurately to avoid duplication.
* Review and verify your information before submitting the form to ensure that it is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event management team for assistance.
