# Event DJ Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request event DJ services for a specific event, such as a wedding or corporate party. This form is used by event planners to provide the necessary information about their event to receive the best possible service from a DJ.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the event details, including event type, date, time, and venue.
2. Select the number of DJs you require for your event.
3. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
4. Provide any additional information you feel is necessary for the event.
5. Submit the form to receive a response from a representative of the DJ service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Select the type of event you are hosting, such as a wedding or corporate party.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Number of Guests** (`num_guests`): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Venue** (`venue`): Enter the location where the event will be held.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **DJs Wanted** (`djs_wanted`): Select the number of DJs you require for your event.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Enter any additional information you feel is necessary for the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
