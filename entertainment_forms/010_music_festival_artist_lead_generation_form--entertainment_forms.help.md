# Music Festival Artist Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about artists who want to perform at the music festival. It ensures that all necessary details are captured accurately, making it easier for festival organizers to manage artist submissions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Festival Name** where you would like to perform. This is the name of the festival you are interested in performing at.
2. Choose a **Performing Date** for your set. Select a date when you are available to perform.
3. Enter your **Artist Name** as it appears on your promotional materials (e.g., social media, website).
4. Provide your **Social Links** (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter) so that festival organizers can easily find you online.
5. Write a brief **Bio** about yourself, including your music style, experience, and any notable performances.
6. Enter your **Contact Number** so that festival organizers can reach you directly.
7. Provide your **Email** for festival organizers to contact you.
8. Upload any relevant **File** (e.g., demo, bio, press kit) that will help festival organizers evaluate your submission.
9. Choose your **Genre** of music (e.g., rock, pop, jazz) to help festival organizers categorize you correctly.
10. Select one of the options under **Submit** to choose how you would like to proceed with your submission. You can choose from:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Festival Name** (text): Enter the name of the festival where you would like to perform.
- **Performing Date** (date): Choose a date when you are available to perform.
- **Artist Name** (text): Enter your artist name as it appears on your promotional materials.
- **Social Links** (select multiple): Provide your social links so that festival organizers can easily find you online.
  - Select one or more of the options to include your social media links.
- **Bio** (text): Write a brief bio about yourself, including your music style, experience, and notable performances.
- **Contact Number** (text): Enter your contact number so that festival organizers can reach you directly.
- **Email** (email): Provide your email address for festival organizers to contact you.
- **File** (text): Upload any relevant files that will help festival organizers evaluate your submission.
- **Genre** (select one): Choose your genre of music to help festival organizers categorize you correctly.
  - Select one of the options to choose your genre of music.
- **Submit** (select one): Choose how you would like to proceed with your submission.
  - Select one of the options to choose how you would like to proceed.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is complete and easy for festival organizers to review.
- Double-check your social links to ensure they are accurate and up-to-date.
- Attach relevant files to showcase your music and style.
- Choose the correct genre to increase your chances of being considered for the festival.
- Be clear and concise in your bio and file submissions.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact festival organizers directly.
