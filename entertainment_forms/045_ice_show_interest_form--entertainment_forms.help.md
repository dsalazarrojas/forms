# Ice Show Interest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interest and availability to participate in events related to figure skating, ice hockey, or other ice-related sports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field (required).
3. Provide your email address in the `email` field (required).
4. Enter your phone number in the `contact_number` field (required).
5. Select your preferred availability from the dropdown menu in the `availability` field (morning, afternoon, or evening). You can choose one or multiple options.
6. Select your interested activities from the dropdown menu in the `interested_in` field (figure skating, ice hockey, or ice hockey goalie). You can choose one or multiple options.
7. Enter any additional event details in the `event_details` field.
8. Enter the name of the contact person or organization responsible for your interest in the `contact_person` field.
9. Enter the name of the skating club or organization you belong to in the `skating_club` field.
10. Finally, enter your role in the event in the `role_in_event` field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
• **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
• **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name. This field is required.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This field is required.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This field is optional.

### Date of Birth
• **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (optional).

### Birth Place
• **Birth Place** (`birth_place`, text, optional): Enter the place of your birth (optional).

### Skating Experience
• **Skating Experience** (`skating_experience`, text, optional): Describe any prior experience you have in figure skating, ice hockey, or other ice-related sports (optional).

### Previous Experience
• **Previous Experience** (`previous_experience`, text, optional): Enter any relevant experience or background information (optional).

### Interested In
• **Interested In** (`interested_in`, select_multiple, required): Select which activities you are interested in participating in: figure skating, ice hockey, or ice hockey goalie. This field is required.

### Availability
• **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred availability for events: morning, afternoon, or evening. You can select one or multiple options. This field is required.

### Event Details
• **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional event details or requirements (optional).

### Contact Person
• **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person or organization responsible for your interest in the event (required).

### Contact Number
• **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number (required).

### Skating Club
• **Skating Club** (`skating_club`, text, optional): If you belong to a skating club or organization, please enter its name (optional).

### Role In Event
• **Role In Event** (`role_in_event`, text, optional): Describe your role in the event (if applicable, e.g., organizer, participant, or spectator).

### Event Type
• **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of event you are interested in (optional).

Note to the user: Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
