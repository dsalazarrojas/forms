# Music Distribution Discussion Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for collecting information related to music distribution discussions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the music distribution discussion in the "Music Distribution Discussion" field.
2. Select the distribution date in the "Distribution Date" field using the calendar interface.
3. Choose the distribution time in the "Distribution Time" field using the time selector.
4. Enter the name of the artist in the "Artist" field.
5. Select one or more labels that best describe the music distribution in the "Labels" field.
6. Choose the music genre in the "Genre" field from the provided options.
7. Enter any additional comments about the music distribution in the "Comments" field.
8. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Add any additional message or notes about the music distribution in the "Message" and "Notes" fields.

* **Artist** (`music_distribution_artist`, text, optional): Enter the name of the artist related to the music distribution.
* **Labels** (`music_distribution_labels`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more labels that describe the music distribution.
* **Genre** (`music_distribution_genre`, select one, optional): Choose the music genre from the provided options.
* **Booking** (`music_distribution_booking`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the music distribution booking.
* **Comments** (`music_distribution_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about the music distribution.
* **Email** (`music_distribution_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for any further communication.
* **Phone** (`music_distribution_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
* **Message** (`music_distribution_message`, text, optional): Add a personal message or any additional information about the music distribution.
