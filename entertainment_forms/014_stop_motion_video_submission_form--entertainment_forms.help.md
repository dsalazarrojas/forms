# Stop Motion Video Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stop Motion Video Submission Form is a tool for users to submit their stop motion videos for review and potential inclusion in a contest or festival. This form collects information about the user and their video submission, including contact details and video metadata.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form completely, but make sure you have only filled it out once per submission.
- Enter your user information in the fields provided.
- Enter a title for your video submission.
- Enter a brief description of your video submission.
- Upload your stop motion video file.
- Select the type of file you are uploading (video or image).
- Choose the category for your video submission (festival, school, or contest).
- Enter your contact email address.
- Enter your contact phone number.
- Select the date and time of your video submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (User Information, text, required: false):
  This field is for your basic contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
- **Entry Title** (Entry Title, text, required: false):
  Enter a brief title for your video submission.
- **Entry Description** (Entry Description, text, required: false):
  Enter a brief description of your video submission.
- **File Upload** (File Upload, text, required: false):
  Upload your stop motion video file to the specified location.
- **File Type** (File Type, select_one, required: false):
  Select the type of file you are uploading, either video or image.
- **Category** (Category, select_multiple, required: false):
  Choose the category for your video submission, such as festival, school, or contest.
- **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required: false):
  Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
- **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required: false):
  Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
- **Submission Date** (Submission Date, date, required: false):
  Select the date you are submitting your video.
- **Submission Time** (Submission Time, time, required: false):
  Select the time you are submitting your video.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all required fields to ensure a complete submission.
- Ensure that your file upload is correct and matches the type you selected.
