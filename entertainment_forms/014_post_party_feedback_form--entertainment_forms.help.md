# Post Party Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Party Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from attendees and hosts of parties. It helps hosts understand their guests' experiences, gather valuable insights, and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Party Host's Name in the "Party Host Name" field.
2. Select the date you attended the party in the "Party Date" field.
3. Enter the start and end times of the party in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
4. If applicable, choose the party's theme from the "Party Theme" field.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Attended Party" field, indicating whether you attended the party.
6. Choose the type of feedback you have in the "Feedback Type" field, such as "Attended the party", "Did not attend the party", "Only sent gifts and did not attend", etc.
7. Enter your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
8. If you wish to provide your email address, enter it in the "Email" field, which will be used to contact you for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Host Name (1)**: Enter the name of the party host who invited you to the party.
* **Party Date (2)**: Enter the date you attended the party.
* **Start Time (3)**: Enter the start time of the party in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **End Time (4)**: Enter the end time of the party in 24-hour format (e.g., 16:30).
* **Party Theme (5)**: If you wish, select the party's theme, which may help the host tailor future events.
* **Attended Party (6)**: Select "Yes" if you attended the party, or "No" if you did not.
* **Feedback Type (7)**: Choose the type of feedback you have, such as "Attended the party" or "Did not attend the party".
* **Feedback (8)**: Enter any comments, suggestions, or experiences about the party here.
* **Email (9)**: If you wish to provide your email address, do so here, which may be used for further communication.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form to help the host provide the best possible experience in the future.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the "Feedback" field.
* If you did not attend the party but sent gifts, please select "Only sent gifts and did not attend" as your feedback type.
* Remember to enter your email address if you wish to receive any follow-up questions or responses from the host.
