# Film Dialogue Clip Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Dialogue Clip Usage Consent Form is a document that captures a participant's consent for using a clip of their dialogue in a film.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's details in the Participant Details section.
2. Provide a brief description of the clip in the Clip Description section.
3. Add a description of why their dialogue clip is required for the film in the Description section.
4. Indicate whether the participant has signed consent for their dialogue clip in the Signed Consent section.
5. Enter the title of the clip in the Clip Title section (if applicable).
6. Enter the date of the clip in the Clip Date section (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): This section is for capturing the participant's name, contact information, and any other relevant details. You can enter any information you need to contact or identify the participant.
- **Clip Description** (`clip_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the clip that will be used, including the context and any relevant details.
- **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Add a description of why the participant's dialogue clip is required for the film. This can include how the clip will be used and why it's essential for the project.
- **Signed Consent** (`signed_consent`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the participant has signed consent for their dialogue clip by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the drop-down menu.
- **Clip Title** (`clip_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the clip, if applicable.
- **Clip Date** (`clip_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the clip, if applicable.
