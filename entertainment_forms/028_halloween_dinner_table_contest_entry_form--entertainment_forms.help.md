# Halloween Diner Table Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Diner Table Contest Entry Form is a form designed to collect personal and contest-related information from participants. This form will be used to gather essential details from individuals who wish to participate in the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field. This is a required field.
2. Provide your **Last Name** in the second field. This is also a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third field. This is a required field and should be a valid email address.
4. Fill in your **Phone** number in the fourth field. This is a required field.
5. Choose your **Date of Birth** in the fifth field. This is a required field and should be a valid date.
6. Select your **Contest Date** in the sixth field. This is a required field and should be a valid date.
7. Choose your **Category** from the list in the seventh field. This is a required field and will help determine your entry in the contest.
8. Write a brief **Description** of your entry in the eighth field. This is a required field and should be a concise description of your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is the name you are known by. Please fill in your first name in the format as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, or ID card).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is the surname associated with your first name. Please fill in your last name in the format as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, or ID card).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address you use for correspondence and communication. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This is the phone number you can be reached at. Please fill in your phone number in the format of your country's standard phone number format.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, date, required): This is the date of your birth. Please select a valid date that represents when you were born.
* **Contest Date** (`contest_date`, date, required): This is the date of the contest. Please select a valid date that represents the contest date.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): This is the category under which you would like to be listed in the contest. Choose one of the options provided.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): This is a brief description of your entry. Please write a concise and clear explanation of your entry that will make it stand out.
