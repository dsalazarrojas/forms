# Pie Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pie Judging Form is a tool for judges to evaluate the quality of pies at a competitive event. The form is designed to collect scores and feedback from multiple judges to help determine the winner of the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select the appropriate competitor from the dropdown list of options.
- Enter the score for each pie, using whole numbers from 1 to 5.
- Fill in the date the pie was judged.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **competition_pies** (`text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of the pie you are evaluating.
* **judges_score** (`number`, optional): Enter a score for the pie, using whole numbers from 1 to 5.
* **score_1** (`number`, optional): Enter a score for the pie, using whole numbers from 1 to 5.
* **score_2** (`number`, optional): Enter a score for the pie, using whole numbers from 1 to 5.
* **score_3** (`number`, optional): Enter a score for the pie, using whole numbers from 1 to 5.
* **score_4** (`number`, optional): Enter a score for the pie, using whole numbers from 1 to 5.
* **score_5** (`number`, optional): Enter a score for the pie, using whole numbers from 1 to 5.
* **competitor** (`select_one`, required): Select the competitor whose pie is being evaluated. Please choose from the list of available options.
* **date_judged** (`date`, optional): Enter the date the pie was judged.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct competitor and enter scores for each pie.
- Use whole numbers from 1 to 5 for the scores.
- Enter the date the pie was judged to ensure accurate tracking of scores and feedback.
