# Theater Audience Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
Theater Audience Information Collection form is designed to gather information from individuals attending events or performances at the theater. It will help the theater management to create an attendance list, build an email list for future promotions and events, and gather feedback for evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Theater Audience Information Collection form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, which is your given name.
2. Enter your **Last Name**, which is your surname.
3. Enter your **Email**, which should be a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone**, which should be your contact phone number.
5. Select the **Performance Date**, which is the date you will be attending the event.
6. Select the **Performance Time**, which is the time of the performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for future communications.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Performance Date** (5): Select the date of the performance you will be attending.
* **Performance Time** (6): Select the time of the performance you will be attending.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Double-check your selections to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the theater management.
