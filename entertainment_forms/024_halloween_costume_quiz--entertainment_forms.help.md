# Halloween Costume Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a fun and interactive way to collect information about users' Halloween preferences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select your favorite Halloween movies, books, games, music, and costume styles from the given options.
3. Choose the number of costumes you plan to wear on Halloween.
4. Select your age group.
5. Indicate the size of the Halloween party you like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Favorite Halloween Movie** (`Favorite Halloween Movie`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of Halloween movie that you enjoy the most, such as Vampires, Horror, Comedies, or Thrillers.
* **Favorite Halloween Book** (`Favorite Halloween Book`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the authors you like, including H.P. Lovecraft, Stephen King, Anne Rice, or Edgar Allan Poe.
* **Favorite Halloween Game** (`Favorite Halloween Game`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the types of Halloween games you enjoy, such as Candyman, Resident Evil, Halloween, or Silent Hill.
* **Favorite Halloween Music** (`Favorite Halloween Music`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of music that you enjoy during Halloween, such as Electronic, Classical, Rock, or Pop.
* **Halloween Costume Style** (`What's your favorite Halloween costume style?`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of costume style that you enjoy, such as Classic Horror, Superhero, Historical Reenactment, or Comedic.
* **Number of Costumes** (`How many costumes do you plan to wear?`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the number of costumes you plan to wear on Halloween, such as Just 1, 2-3, or 4 or more.
* **Age** (`How old are you?`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your age group, such as 13-17, 18-24, 25-44, 45-64, or 65+.
* **Halloween Party Size** (`What size of Halloween party do you like?`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the size of the Halloween party you like, such as Small (less than 20 people), Medium (20-50 people), or Large (more than 50 people).
