# Virtual Happy Hour Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Happy Hour Registration Form is designed to collect information from users about their upcoming virtual events, such as webinars, meetings, or other online gatherings. This form helps to streamline the registration process and ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Host Name, Event Date, Event Time, and Assigned Tool.
2. Provide the names of up to 5 guests (Guest 1-5 Name) for your virtual event.
3. Specify whether you plan to allow additional guests (Additional Guests).
4. Enter your email address for contact.
5. Optionally, provide any additional notes or details (Note).
6. Select the category (Category) and assigned tool (Assigned Tool) for your event.
7. Confirm that you would like to create an output file (Output File).
8. Verify the form ID for internal tracking purposes (Form ID).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Host Name
*   **Host Name** (`host_name`, `text`, required)
    This field captures the name of the host or organizer of the virtual event.

### Event Date
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required)
    Enter the date of your virtual event.

### Event Time
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required)
    Enter the time of your virtual event.

### Guest List
*   **Guest List** (`guest_list`, `select_multiple`, optional)
    Indicate whether you plan to allow guests for your virtual event. Choose from: Yes, No.

### Host Phone
*   **Host Phone** (`host_phone`, `text`, optional)
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Guest 1 Name
*   **Guest 1 Name** (`guest_name_1`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the name of your first guest.

### Guest 2 Name
*   **Guest 2 Name** (`guest_name_2`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the name of your second guest.

### Guest 3 Name
*   **Guest 3 Name** (`guest_name_3`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the name of your third guest.

### Guest 4 Name
*   **Guest 4 Name** (`guest_name_4`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the name of your fourth guest.

### Guest 5 Name
*   **Guest 5 Name** (`guest_name_5`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the name of your fifth guest.

### Email
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional)
    Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Additional Guests
*   **Additional Guests** (`additional_guests`, `select_multiple`, optional)
    Indicate whether you plan to allow additional guests for your virtual event. Choose from: Yes, No.

### Note
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional)
    Enter any additional notes or details about your virtual event.

### Assigned Tool
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required)
    Select the tool assigned to your virtual event. Choose from: Chat, Meeting, Webinar, Other.

### Category
*   **Category** (`category`, `text`, optional)
    Enter a category or description for your virtual event.

### Form ID
*   **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, optional)
    Verify the form ID for internal tracking purposes.

### Output File
*   **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, optional)
    Confirm that you would like to create an output file for your virtual event.

### Form ID (again)
*   **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, optional)
    This field is redundant and for internal purposes only. Please ignore.
