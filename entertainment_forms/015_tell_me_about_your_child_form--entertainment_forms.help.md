# Tell Me About Your Child Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a child, including their school environment, characteristics, and home environment. It is intended to provide valuable data for educational or assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the text fields with relevant information about your child.
2. Select the options that best describe your child's characteristics.
3. Enter your child's date of birth.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **tell_me_about_your_child_form**: This field is a text box where you can enter a brief description or statement about your child.
* **school_environment**: This field is a text box where you can describe your child's school environment, such as their classroom or school setting.
* **child_characteristics**: This field is a multiple-choice option where you can select the characteristics that best describe your child, such as their personality traits or behaviors.
* **home_environment**: This field is a text box where you can describe your child's home environment, such as their family dynamics or living situation.
* **collaboration**: This field is a number field where you can rate your child's collaboration with others, such as their ability to work with peers or teachers.
* **support**: This field is a text box where you can describe the support systems in place for your child, such as their family or social support networks.
* **notes**: This field is a text box where you can add any additional notes or comments about your child.
* **date_of_birth**: This field is a date field where you can enter your child's date of birth.
