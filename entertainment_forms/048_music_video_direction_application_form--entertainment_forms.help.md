# Music Video Direction Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants to apply for music video director positions. It collects personal contact information, work experience, and creative portfolio to assess the applicant's suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your email address for communication.
4. Attach your resume for us to review.
5. Share a link to your reel, a video showcasing your directing work.
6. Attach or link your portfolio, a collection of your work.
7. Share a link to your portfolio, if you have one.
8. Briefly describe your director experience.
9. Describe your previous work experience.
10. Share your education background.
11. List your previous director experience.
12. Describe your previous director work experience.
13. List your work experience.
14. Share your portfolio link experience.
15. Describe your previous experience.
16. Provide a contact person's name and their contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Resume** (Resume, Text, Optional): Attach or link your resume for us to review.
* **Reel** (Reel, Text, Optional): Share a link to a video showcasing your directing work.
* **Portfolio** (Portfolio, Text, Optional): Attach or link your creative portfolio.
* **Portfolio Link** (Portfolio Link, Text, Optional): Share a link to your portfolio.
* **Director Experience** (Director Experience, Text, Optional): Briefly describe your director experience.
* **Previous Work Experience** (Previous Work Experience, Text, Optional): Describe your previous work experience.
* **Education** (Education, Text, Optional): Share your education background.
* **Contact** (Contact, Text, Optional): A contact person's name and their contact information.
* **Reel Link** (Reel Link, Text, Optional): Share a link to your reel.
* **Resume Link** (Resume Link, Text, Optional): Share a link to your resume.
* **Portfolio Experience** (Portfolio Experience, Text, Optional): A description of your portfolio link experience.
* **Reel Experience** (Reel Experience, Text, Optional): A description of your reel experience.
* **Education Background** (Education Background, Text, Optional): A description of your education background.
* **Previous Director Experience** (Previous Director Experience, Text, Optional): A description of your previous director experience.
* **Previous Director Work Experience** (Previous Director Work Experience, Text, Optional): A description of your previous director work experience.
* **Work Experience** (Work Experience, Text, Optional): A description of your work experience.
* **Portfolio Link Experience** (Portfolio Link Experience, Text, Optional): A description of your portfolio link experience.
* **Previous Experience** (Previous Experience, Text, Optional): A description of your previous experience.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, Text, Optional): A contact person's name and their contact information.
* **Submit** (Submit, Text, Optional): Submit your application.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and relevant information will improve your chances of being considered for the role.
