# music_workshop_participant_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music workshop participant recording consent form is used to obtain consent from workshop participants to record their music workshop sessions. This form is typically used to ensure that participants are aware of and agree to the recording of their workshop sessions, which may be used for educational or promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "participant_name" field.
2. If you are a parent or guardian, enter your name in the "parent_or_guardian_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
5. Select the type of workshop you are participating in from the "workshop_type" field.
6. Choose your consent for recording using the "recording_consent" field.
   - Select "Audio" if you consent to audio recording.
   - Select "Video" if you consent to video recording.
   - Select "Both" if you consent to both audio and video recording.
7. Confirm your consent for audio recording in the "recording_consent_audio" field.
   - Select "Audio" if you confirm your consent for audio recording.
   - Select "Both" if you confirm your consent for both audio and video recording.
8. Confirm your consent for video recording in the "recording_consent_video" field.
   - Select "Video" if you confirm your consent for video recording.
   - Select "Both" if you confirm your consent for both audio and video recording.
9. Enter the date you signed the form in the "signed_date" field.
10. Enter the time you signed the form in the "signed_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name (`participant_name`, text, required):** Enter your name as a participant in the workshop.
- **Parent or Guardian Name (`parent_or_guardian_name`, text, optional):** If you are a parent or guardian, enter your name.
- **Email Address (`email_address`, email, optional):** Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, optional):** Enter your phone number.
- **Workshop Type (`workshop_type`, select_one, optional):** Select the type of workshop you are participating in.
- **Recording Consent (`recording_consent`, select_multiple, optional):** Choose your consent for recording:
  - Select "Audio" if you consent to audio recording.
  - Select "Video" if you consent to video recording.
  - Select "Both" if you consent to both audio and video recording.
- **Recording Consent (Audio) (`recording_consent_audio`, select_multiple, optional):** Confirm your consent for audio recording:
  - Select "Audio" if you confirm your consent for audio recording.
  - Select "Both" if you confirm your consent for both audio and video recording.
- **Recording Consent (Video) (`recording_consent_video`, select_multiple, optional):** Confirm your consent for video recording:
  - Select "Video" if you confirm your consent for video recording.
  - Select "Both" if you confirm your consent for both audio and video recording.
- **Signed Date (`signed_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date you signed the form.
- **Signed Time (`signed_time`, time, optional):** Enter the time you signed the form.
