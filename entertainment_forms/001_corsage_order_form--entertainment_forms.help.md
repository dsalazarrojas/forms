# Corsage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for corsage orders for events and occasions. Fill out this form to place an order with your preferred details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your customer information, including **Customer Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Address**.
- Select the **Corsage Type**, which can be either **Standard** or **Deluxe**.
- Choose the desired **Flower Type** (select all that apply).
- Enter the **Quantity** of corsages you would like to order.
- Select the **Color** you prefer for your corsage (Red, Pink, or Yellow).
- Choose the **Delivery Date** for your order.
- Select the time of **Delivery Time**.
- Add any **Comments**, if you have any specific requests or instructions.
- Fill in your **Card Number**, **Card Type**, and confirm your **CVV** for payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info (Text)**: Enter your name and contact information to contact you for delivery confirmation and any order updates.
*   **Customer Name (Text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or government-issued ID.
*   **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates about your order.
*   **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for any urgent updates or delivery changes.
*   **Address (Text)**: Enter your address for delivery purposes.
  - **City (Text)**: Enter the city where you would like the corsage to be delivered.
  - **State (Text)**: Enter the state where you would like the corsage to be delivered.
  - **Zip (Text)**: Enter the zip code where you would like the corsage to be delivered.
- **Corsage Type (Select One)**: Choose from **Standard** or **Deluxe** to customize your corsage experience.
- **Flower Type (Select Multiple)**: Select all the flower types you would like to include in your corsage.
  - **Roses**: Choose roses for your corsage.
  - **Lilies**: Choose lilies for your corsage.
  - **Carnations**: Choose carnations for your corsage.
- **Quantity (Number)**: Enter the number of corsages you would like to order.
- **Color (Select One)**: Select from **Red**, **Pink**, or **Yellow** for your corsage.
- **Delivery Date (Date)**: Enter the date you would like to receive your corsage.
- **Delivery Time (Time)**: Select the time of day you would like to receive your corsage.
- **Comments (Text)**: Add any special requests or instructions for your order.
- **Payment Method (Select One)**: Choose from **Online** or **In-store** for payment.
- **Special Requests (Text)**: Add any other requests or instructions for your order.
- **Card Number (Text)**: Enter your credit card number for payment.
- **Card Type (Select One)**: Choose from **Visa** or **Mastercard** for payment.
- **CVV (Text)**: Enter your CVV for payment.
- **Expire Month (Text)**: Enter the month your card expires.
- **Expire Year (Text)**: Enter the year your card expires.
- **CVV/CVV Confirmation (Select One)**: Confirm that your CVV is correct.
  - **True**: Confirm your CVV is correct.
  - **False**: Confirm your CVV is incorrect.
