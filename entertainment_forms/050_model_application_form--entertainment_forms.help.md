# Model Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for a model position. The form is divided into several sections, and each section focuses on a specific aspect of the applicant's experience, skills, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your first name and last name in the first two fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the third field, ensuring it is a valid email address.
3. Provide your phone number in the fourth field, following the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
4. Attach your resume file in the fifth field.
5. Enter a link to your portfolio in the sixth field.
6. For the model experiences (fields 7-17), select the relevant options that apply to your experience. You can select multiple options for fields 8, 14, and 15.
7. Enter your goals for the model in the nineteenth field.
8. Select the number of years of experience you have for fields 19 and 20.
9. Fill in the text fields (21-24) with a brief description of your experience and qualifications.
