# team_meeting_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team meeting application form is used to collect information from team members who wish to apply for a team meeting. This form is intended for team members to provide their details and preferences to schedule a meeting that suits their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Meeting Applicant ID (if required by the team lead or manager).
2. Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
3. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
4. Choose the date of the team meeting you wish to attend by selecting a date from the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Applicant ID**: If you have been provided with a unique ID by the team lead or manager, enter it here to help the team lead or manager identify you in their records.
* **Name**: This is your name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so that the team lead or manager can contact you for further instructions or meeting confirmations.
* **Team Meeting Date**: Select the date you would like to attend the team meeting using the calendar interface. Please ensure you select a date that is in the future, not past.

## Tips
* Ensure you have permission to access this form, as it is only available to team members.
* If you are unsure about the form fields or have any questions, contact the team lead or manager for clarification.
* Double-check your form details before submitting to ensure accuracy and prevent delays in meeting scheduling.
