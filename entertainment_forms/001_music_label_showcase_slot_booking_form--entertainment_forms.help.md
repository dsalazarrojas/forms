# Music Label Showcase Slot Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music Label Showcase Slot Booking Form is a tool used to book slots for live music performances at a venue. This form helps artists provide information about themselves and their technical requirements to ensure a successful show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Artist Details** section with your name, email address, and other relevant contact information.
2. Choose your **Time Slots** by selecting one or more time slots from the dropdown menu.
3. Select your **Technical Requirements** such as stage and sound, backline, stage and sound, and stage and soundboard.
4. Choose your **Venue Preferences** by selecting one or more options from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter your **Preferred Booking Day** and **Preferred Start Time** and **Preferred End Time** for the show.
6. Add any **Note** about the booking.
7. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** contact information.
8. Fill in the **Category**, **Artist Name**, and other relevant social media links such as **Artist Facebook**, **Artist Instagram**, **Artist Twitter**, **Artist Youtube**, **Artist Spotify**, **Artist Soundcloud**, and **Artist Bandcamp**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Details**: This section is used to input your name and other relevant contact information.
* **Time Slots**: Choose one or more time slots from the dropdown menu that you are interested in booking.
* **Technical Requirements**: Select one or more technical requirements that you will need for the show, such as stage and sound, backline, or stage and sound and soundboard.
* **Venue Preferences**: Choose one or more options from the dropdown menu that you prefer for the venue, such as acoustic, electric, or none.
* **Preferred Booking Day**: Enter the day you prefer to book the slot.
* **Preferred Start Time**: Enter the start time of the show.
* **Preferred End Time**: Enter the end time of the show.
* **Note**: Add any additional information or notes about the booking.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Category**: Enter the category of the artist.
* **Artist Name**: Enter the name of the artist.
* **Artist URL**: Enter the artist's URL.
* **Artist Facebook**: Enter the artist's Facebook link.
* **Artist Instagram**: Enter the artist's Instagram link.
* **Artist Twitter**: Enter the artist's Twitter link.
* **Artist Youtube**: Enter the artist's Youtube link.
* **Artist Spotify**: Enter the artist's Spotify link.
* **Artist Soundcloud**: Enter the artist's Soundcloud link.
* **Artist Bandcamp**: Enter the artist's Bandcamp link.
* **Artist Twitter 2**: Enter the artist's second Twitter link.
* **Artist Facebook 2**: Enter the artist's second Facebook link.
* **Artist Instagram 2**: Enter the artist's second Instagram link.
* **Artist Youtube 2**: Enter the artist's second Youtube link.
