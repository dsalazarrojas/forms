# TV Series Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in a TV series production. It's essential to ensure that participants understand their rights and release ownership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Choose Your Role**: Select the role you played in the production (e.g., actor, crew member, etc.).
3. **Enter Your Email**: Enter your official email address.
4. **Add Your Contact Information**: Provide any additional contact information you're comfortable sharing.
5. **Select Media Use**: Choose which types of media you're comfortable with being used for the production (e.g., Still Photography, Interview, B-roll, Background, Screen, Voiceover, Audio).
6. **Confirm Release Ownership**: Select whether you own the rights to your release (Yes or No).
7. **Consent to Media Use**: Choose which types of media you consent to being used (e.g., Still Photography, Interview, B-roll, Background, Screen, Voiceover, Audio). You must select all applicable options.
8. **Provide Filming Location**: Choose between On Location or Studio.
9. **Production Information (Optional)**: If applicable, enter the production company and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **TV Series Release Form**: This is the title of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Participant Role**: Select the role you played in the production.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Participant Contact**: Provide any additional contact information you're comfortable sharing.
* **Media Use**: Choose which types of media you're comfortable with being used for the production (e.g., Still Photography, Interview, B-roll, Background, Screen, Voiceover, Audio).
	+ This option allows you to select multiple media types you're comfortable with.
* **Release Ownership**: Select whether you own the rights to your release (Yes or No).
* **Participant Consent**: Choose which types of media you consent to being used. This option is required to ensure you're aware and agree to the media use.
	+ This option also allows you to select multiple media types you consent to.
* **Filming Location**: Choose between On Location or Studio.
* **Production Company**: Enter the production company (if applicable).
* **Production Contact**: Enter the production contact information (if applicable).
