# Creative Residency Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Creative Residency Program Application Form is for artists to apply to a Creative Residency Program, which provides funding and support for artistic projects. This form gathers information about the artist and their project, including art piece name, project dates, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information.
2. Provide a title for your art piece.
3. Enter the start and end dates of your project.
4. Choose the type of art piece you will be creating (e.g. Film, Theater, Dance, Visual Arts).
5. Indicate how you will be applying (e.g. Yes, No).
6. Select the locations where the project will take place (e.g. In-studio, On-site, Both).
7. Enter the duration of your project.
8. Provide the venue where your project will be held.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the artist applying for the residency program.
* **Art Piece Name** (`art_piece_name`, text, optional): Enter a title for your art piece, such as "The Great Expectation" or "The Art of Life".
* **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`, text, optional): This is a brief statement about your art and your artistic goals.
* **Contact Email** (`artist_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your application.
* **Contact Phone** (`artist_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your application.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your project.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, text, optional): Select the type of project you will be creating, such as "Film", "Theater", "Dance", or "Visual Arts".
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, text, optional): Enter the current status of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): Enter your project budget.
* **Art Piece Type** (`art_piece_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of art piece you will be creating.
* **Application Method** (`application_method`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how you will be applying to the program (e.g. "Yes", "No").
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select the location(s) where your project will take place.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of your project.
* **Project Venue** (`project_venue`, text, optional): Enter the venue where your project will be held.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's best to fill in as much information as possible to help us understand your project better.
