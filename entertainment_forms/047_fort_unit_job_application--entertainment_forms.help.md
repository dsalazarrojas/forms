# fort_unit_job_application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather detailed information about individuals applying for jobs within the entertainment industry.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Ensure you have a valid email address, which will be used for verification purposes.
*   Complete all required fields as specified in the form.
*   Fill in your details accurately, as they will be used for reference purposes.

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your contact number.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter your current or desired job title.
*   **Experience** (`experience`, Text, Optional): Describe your work experience.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`, Text, Optional): Write a brief summary of your job responsibilities.
*   **Skills** (`skills`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select skills you possess, such as programming languages or tools used in your work.
*   **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, Time, Optional): Enter the hours you work per week.
*   **Job Type** (`job_type`, Text, Optional): Select the type of job you are applying for.
*   **Job Status** (`job_status`, Select One, Optional): Select the status of your job, such as active or inactive.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Age** (`age`, Number, Optional): Enter your age.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your gender.
*   **Job Type Other** (`job_type_other`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of job you are applying for, if it's not listed in the main job type field.
*   **Job Status Other** (`job_status_other`, Select One, Optional): Select the status of your job, if it's not listed in the main job status field.
*   **Job Title Other** (`job_title_other`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your job title.
*   **Job Type Other Details** (`job_type_other_details`, Text, Optional): Enter details about your job type.

*   **Job Type 2** (`job_type_2`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of job you are applying for, if it has two options.
*   **Job Status 2** (`job_status_2`, Select One, Optional): Select the status of your job, if it has two options.
*   **Job Description 2** (`job_description_2`, Text, Optional): Write a brief summary of your second job.
*   **First Name 2** (`first_name_2`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name, if you are applying for more than one job.
