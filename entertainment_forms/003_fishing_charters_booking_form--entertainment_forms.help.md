# Fishing Charters Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishing Charters Booking Form is a crucial tool for charter operators to gather necessary details for a smooth and enjoyable fishing experience. This guide will walk you through each field to ensure you provide the required information accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the **Booking details** field with the specific date and time for your charter.
2.  Provide the **Charter details** field with the type of fishing charter you are booking (e.g., half-day or full-day).
3.  Select the **Guest details** field to specify the number of guests and their respective ages or preferences.
4.  Fill in the **Contact details** field with your name, email, and phone number for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Booking details**: Enter the date and time of your fishing charter booking.
  *   **Charter details**: Choose the type of fishing charter you're booking (e.g., half-day or full-day).
  *   **Guest details**: Select the number of guests and their respective ages or preferences.
  *   **Contact details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number for communication with the charter operator.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the charter details to ensure you're booking the correct type and duration.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, include them in the contact details field or mention them to the charter operator directly.
- Ensure that the contact details are accurate to receive timely updates and confirmation from the charter operator.
