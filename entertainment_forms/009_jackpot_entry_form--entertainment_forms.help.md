# Jackpot Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jackpot Entry Form is designed to collect information from users interested in participating in a jackpot contest or competition. This form helps to gather the necessary details to process their entry, ensuring a smooth and efficient experience for the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the Rider Information section.
2. Share your guesses (if applicable) in the Guesses section.
3. Fill in your payment details in the Payment Information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rider Information** (`Rider Information`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information as a contestant or participant.
* **Guesses** (`Guesses`, text, optional): If you have any guesses, such as numbers, numbers combinations, or other relevant information, enter it here.
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, text, required): Provide your payment details, including any necessary identification or payment references, to complete your entry.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to avoid any issues with your entry.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is for contest entries only, and any other use is not allowed.
