# Theater Prop Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Theater Prop Maintenance Request Form is used to report issues with theater props that require maintenance or repair. Fill out this form to ensure that the necessary information is collected for efficient maintenance and repair of theater props.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are not duplicating a request.
2. Fill out as much information as possible in each field.
3. Provide details about the prop in question, including its condition.
4. Choose the correct repair type and priority level for the issue.
5. Enter the required date and time for maintenance.
6. Provide additional information, if necessary.
7. Assign the issue to the correct person, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Theater Prop Details**: This field is used to describe the prop in question, including its name, type, and condition.
* **Maintenance Request**: Describe the issue with the prop, including the problem and any specific details about the maintenance needed.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the maintenance request, such as the location where the prop was last used or how it was handled.
* **Prop Name**: Enter the name of the prop that requires maintenance.
* **Prop Type**: Enter the type of prop, such as stage prop, costume, or set dressing.
* **Condition**: Describe the condition of the prop, including any visible signs of damage or wear and tear.
* **Repair Type**: Choose the type of repair needed for the prop, such as "Repair", "Maintenance", or "Adjustment".
* **Priority**: Set the priority level for the maintenance request, such as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Date**: Enter the date when the maintenance is needed, ideally the next available date.
* **Time**: Enter the time when the maintenance is preferred.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person requesting maintenance, if applicable.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person requesting maintenance, if applicable.
* **Assigned To**: Choose the person to be assigned to this maintenance request, such as "chatjimmy", "JohnDoe", or "JaneDoe".
* **Description**: Describe the maintenance request in detail, including the specific issue and the steps needed to resolve it.
* **Status**: Use this field to update the status of the maintenance request, such as "In Progress", "Completed", or "On Hold".
* **Status Note**: Use this field to add any additional notes about the status of the maintenance request.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to aid the maintenance team in addressing the issue efficiently.
* Ensure that the form is filled out completely and accurately to prevent errors and delays.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to reach out to the maintenance team for assistance.
* If you need to make changes to the maintenance request, update the relevant fields and notify the assigned person.
* Keep a record of the maintenance request for future reference.
