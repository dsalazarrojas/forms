# Visit Entertainment Venues Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect visitor information and feedback from entertainment venues. It helps venue administrators understand visitor preferences and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the visiting name, email, and phone number (if applicable) in the first page.
2. Enter the event name and date in the second page.
3. Select the event time in the third page.
4. Provide any feedback and rating you'd like to share in the last two pages.
5. Review and submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visiting Name** (`visitor_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Visitor Email** (`visitor_email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to share your email with the venue, enter it here.
* **Visitor Phone** (`visitor_phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to share your phone number with the venue, enter it here.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the event you attended.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time of the event you attended.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_text`, `note`, optional): Share any additional feedback you'd like to provide to the venue.
* **Rating** (`feedback_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
