# Traveling Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your travel preferences, which will be used to create a personalized travel plan for you. Please answer the questions accurately to ensure you receive tailored recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by selecting the relevant options for each question.
- Answer all fields, as most are not required to be filled in, to get the most out of this form.
- For select fields, choose the relevant option that best matches your preference.
- For numerical fields, enter a valid number.
- For date fields, enter a date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- For email fields, enter a valid email address.
- For text fields, enter a brief description or answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Traveling Preferences Survey**: This is the title of the survey. Please ignore this field.
- **Preferred Destination**: 
  • This is where you want to travel to. Select one of the following options: Europe, Asia, Africa, or Americas.
  • Please choose a location that best matches your travel desire.
- **Travel Length**: 
  • Enter the number of days you want to travel for.
  • A numerical value will be used to provide a more accurate travel plan.
- **Preferred Time Period**: 
  • Enter the date (in YYYY-MM-DD format) when you want to travel.
  • Please be as accurate as possible to ensure the best travel recommendations.
- **Accommodation Preferences**: 
  • This is the description of your accommodation preferences.
  • Enter your preferences as a brief description.
- **Budget**: 
  • Enter the number that represents your budget for the trip.
  • A numerical value will be used to provide more accurate travel recommendations.
- **Preferred Language**: 
  • This is the language you would like to travel in.
  • Select one or more of the following options: English, Spanish, French.
  • Please choose a language that best matches your proficiency.
- **Mode of Transportation**: 
  • This is the mode of transport you prefer to use during travel.
  • Select one of the following options: Plane, Train, Bus.
  • Please choose an option that best suits your travel style.
- **Email**: 
  • Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
  • We will use this address to provide you with travel updates.
- **Phone**: 
  • Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
  • Please ensure it is a valid phone number.
