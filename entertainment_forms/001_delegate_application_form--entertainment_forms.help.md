# Delegate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to become delegates in a specific event. Fill out this form carefully to increase your chances of being selected as a delegate.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Make sure to provide your correct contact information so we can reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event you wish to be a delegate for.
*   **Your Name** (`your_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your phone number and email address so we can contact you.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you wish to be a delegate for.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event you wish to be a delegate for.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event you wish to be a delegate for.
*   **Bio** (`bio`, note, required): Briefly introduce yourself and explain why you are interested in being a delegate for this event.
*   **Delegate Experience** (`delegate_experience`, text, required): Share your previous experience in events or related fields.
*   **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required): Select how available you are for the event:
    *   **Available**
    *   **Available with restrictions**
    *   **Unavailable**
*   **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, required): Enter a short description of the type of event you wish to be a delegate for.
*   **Delegate Interest** (`delegate_interest`, text, required): Tell us about your specific interests and why you want to be a delegate for this event.
*   **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, required): Share your motivation for being a delegate, such as personal interest or professional gain.

## Tips

*   Fill out the form carefully and honestly.
*   Double-check your contact information.
*   Make sure your answers are concise and clear.
*   If any field has a specific format or requirement, follow it exactly as indicated.
