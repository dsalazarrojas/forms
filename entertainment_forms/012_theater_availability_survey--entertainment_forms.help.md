# Theater Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the theater understand your availability and preferences for upcoming events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose the days you are available to attend events on the first page.
2. Select your preferred time of day on the second page.
3. Enter the number of people in your group on the third page.
4. Indicate any seating preferences on the fourth page.
5. If you have any accessibility needs, please list them on the fifth page.
6. Choose how you would like to submit this survey on the sixth page.
7. Enter your contact information on the seventh page.
8. Enter your email address on the eighth page.
9. Enter your phone number on the ninth page.
10. Enter your name on the tenth page.
11. Enter your address on the eleventh page.
12. Enter your city on the twelfth page.
13. Enter your state on the thirteenth page.
14. Enter your zip code on the fourteenth page.
15. Add any additional comments on the fifteenth page (optional).
