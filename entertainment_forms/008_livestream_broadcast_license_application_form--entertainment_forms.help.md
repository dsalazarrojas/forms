# Livestream Broadcast License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a livestream broadcast license application. It gathers details such as broadcast date, location, description, content type, and target audience to process the application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Be clear and concise in your answers to ensure accurate processing.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizer Name**: Enter your name as the organizer of the livestream broadcast.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Broadcast Date**: Enter the date of the livestream broadcast.
* **Broadcast Time**: Enter the time of the livestream broadcast.
* **Broadcast Location**: Enter the location of the livestream broadcast.
* **Broadcast Description**: Enter a brief description of the livestream content.
* **Content Type**: Select the type of content being broadcast (e.g., Streaming, VOD, Live, Podcast).
* **Content Length**: Enter the length of the livestream content.
* **Content Language**: Enter the language of the livestream content.
* **Content Genre**: Enter the genre of the livestream content (e.g., Drama, Comedy, Music, Sports).
* **Content Category**: Select the category of the livestream content (e.g., Drama, Comedy, Music, Sports).
* **Content Target Audience**: Select the target audience for the livestream content (e.g., Public, Private, Paid).
* **Content Target Duration**: Enter the duration of the livestream content.
* **Content Target Resolution**: Enter the resolution of the livestream content.
* **Content Target File Size**: Enter the file size of the livestream content.
* **Upload Link**: Enter the upload link for the livestream content.
* **Target Audience**: Select the target audience for the livestream content (e.g., Public, Private, Paid).
* **Target Platform**: Select the platform where the livestream will be broadcast (e.g., YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).
* **Target Country**: Select the country where the livestream will be targeted.
* **Target Language**: Select the language of the livestream content.
* **Target Region**: Enter the region where the livestream will be targeted.
* **Target City**: Enter the city where the livestream will be targeted.
* **Target Country Region**: Enter the country region where the livestream will be targeted.
* **Target Zip**: Enter the zip code where the livestream will be targeted.
