# Parade Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Parade Entry Form is designed to collect information for parade entries, allowing participants to provide their details in an organized and efficient manner. This form helps the organizers collect necessary information to process the entries and ensure a smooth experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your entry type from the "Entry Type" dropdown menu.
6. Select whether you would like to pay an entry fee from the "Entry Fee" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the theme of your parade entry.
8. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
9. Enter your city in the "City" field.
10. Enter your state in the "State" field.
11. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for updates and information.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Entry Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of entry you would like to submit (e.g., float, performance, or other).
* **Entry Fee** (select_multiple, optional): If you would like to pay an entry fee, select the corresponding option.
* **Theme** (text, optional): Describe the theme of your parade entry.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address, which will be used for communication and logistics.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (text, optional): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip** (text, optional): Enter your zip code for postal delivery.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact us at your email address provided.
* Ensure that the address and zip code you enter are accurate for communication and delivery purposes.
* If you have any issues with submitting the form, try refreshing the page or reaching out to our support team.
