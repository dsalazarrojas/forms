# Halloween Haunt Party Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Haunt Party Form is designed to collect information about partygoers attending a Halloween party. This information helps the party organizers create a better experience for all attendees. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure a safe and enjoyable night.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name and email address.
2. If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
3. You can optionally select your dress code for the party. Choose from our list of spooky options.
4. Select any of the following powers that you possess: 
   - Mind control
   - Telekinesis
   - Shape-shifting
   - Invisibility
   - Flight
   - Super Strength
   - Super Speed
   - Super Stamina
5. Specify if you are attending the party alone or with someone.
6. If you are attending with someone, enter their name and select their relationship to you.
7. You can provide any special requests you have for the party, like specific food or drinks.
8. Tell us a little about who or what you're haunted by. Be honest, but don't reveal too much information.
9. Select your level of experience with our haunted activities. This will help us tailor the event to your abilities.
10. If you're familiar with our terrifying tales, let us know if you'd like to hear more.
11. If you've attended previous haunts, tell us about it.
12. If you've attended previous haunts, specify how many times you've gone.
13. Add any additional notes about your experience or concerns.
14. Finally, please sign your name so we can make sure it's really you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partygoer Name**: Enter your name as it is known to you.
* **Partygoer EMail**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Partygoer Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Partygoer Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of the person you're attending with.
* **Partygoer Attending With Relationship**: (Optional) Select the relationship between you and the person you're attending with.
* **Partygoer Attending With Phone**: (Optional) Enter the phone number of the person you're attending with.
* **Partygoer Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, like food or drink preferences, enter them here.
* **Partygoer Haunted By**: Be honest with us about what haunts you. This will help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **Partygoer Haunt Level**: Select your level of experience with our haunted activities.
* **Partygoer Terrifying Tales**: If you'd like to hear more of our terrifying tales, let us know.
* **Partygoer Would Recommend**: If you enjoy our haunted event, let us know and tell us why.
* **Partygoer Would Recommend Reason**: (Optional) If you'd like to recommend our event, enter the reason why.
* **Partygoer Would Recommend Source**: (Optional) If you'd like to recommend our event, enter who or what recommended it to you.
* **Partygoer Attend Previous Haunt**: If you've attended a previous haunt, let us know.
* **Partygoer Attended Previous Haunt**: If you've attended a previous haunt, specify how many times you've been.
* **Partygoer Notes**: Add any additional notes or concerns you have about your experience.
* **Partygoer Signature**: Finally, please sign your name so we can make sure it's really you.
