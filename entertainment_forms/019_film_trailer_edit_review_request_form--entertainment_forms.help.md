# Film Trailer Edit Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by film editors and stakeholders to review and edit film trailers for submission to film festivals, film markets, and other related events. The form is used to gather necessary information for review and feedback, ensuring that the trailer is edited and released on time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the film trailer in the "Form Control 1" field.
2. Choose the deadline for the review and edit from the options provided in "Deadlines and Dates".
3. Enter any necessary edit notes or comments in the "Edit Notes" field.
4. Provide any relevant links or reference files for the reviewer in the "Links" field.
5. Attach any reference files required for review in the "Reference Files" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Control 1** (`text`, required): Enter the title of the film trailer being reviewed.
* **Deadlines and Dates** (`select_one`, required): Choose the deadline for the review and edit from the options provided.
	+ 10 days: The review and edit should be completed within 10 days.
	+ 14 days: The review and edit should be completed within 14 days.
	+ 21 days: The review and edit should be completed within 21 days.
* **Edit Notes** (`text`, optional): Enter any necessary edit notes or comments for the reviewer.
* **Links** (`text`, optional): Provide any relevant links or resources for the reviewer.
* **Reference Files** (`text`, optional): Attach any reference files required for review.
