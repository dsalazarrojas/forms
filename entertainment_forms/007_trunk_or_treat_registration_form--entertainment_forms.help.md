# Trunk or Treat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trunk or Treat Registration Form is a simple form used to collect information from attendees about themselves and their children. This information will help us better plan and manage the Trunk or Treat event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your **Attendee Name**.
2. Provide your **Email** and **Contact Preferred Phone**, if applicable.
3. Enter your **Address**, if you wish to receive any updates or notifications about the event.
4. If you are attending with your family, fill in the **Number of Children**, **Age of Youngest Child**, and **Age of Oldest Child**.
5. If you are driving, provide the **Trunk or Treat Vehicle**, **Trunk or Treat Vehicle Type**, and **Trunk or Treat Vehicle Year**.
6. Click submit to send your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (Text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Email** (Email): Provide your email address if you want to receive any updates or notifications about the event.
* **Contact Preferred Phone** (Text): Enter your preferred phone number if you want us to contact you.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your address if you want to receive any mail or updates about the event.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): This field is not required but will help us know which children may be attending with you.
* **Start Time** (Time): This field is not required but will help us plan the event schedule.
* **End Time** (Time): This field is not required but will help us plan the event schedule.
* **Trunk or Treat Vehicle** (Text): Enter the name of your vehicle.
* **Trunk or Treat Vehicle Type** (Select One): Choose the type of vehicle you will be driving (Car, Truck, SUV, Van, Tricycle, Other).
* **Trunk or Treat Vehicle Year** (Number): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Number of Children** (Number): Enter the number of children attending with you.
* **Number of Adults** (Number): Enter the number of adults attending with you.
* **Age of Youngest Child** (Number): Enter the age of the youngest child.
* **Age of Oldest Child** (Number): Enter the age of the oldest child.
