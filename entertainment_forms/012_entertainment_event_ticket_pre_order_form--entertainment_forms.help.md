# Entertainment Event Ticket Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in purchasing tickets for an entertainment event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the required questions on each page to ensure that your information is accurately recorded.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Info (Page 1)
* **Event Info** (text, optional): This page is a general information page about the event. You can provide any relevant details about the event, such as the event title, date, time, and location.

### Ticket Details (Page 2)
* **Ticket Details** (text, optional): This page is designed to collect more specific information about the ticket you want to purchase. Please provide any additional details about the ticket, such as the type of ticket, seat number, or any special requirements.

### Contact Info (Page 3)
* **Contact Info** (text, required): Please provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email address. This will help us to get in touch with you to confirm your ticket purchase.

### Additional Info (Page 4)
* **Additional Info** (text, optional): This page is an open-ended field for any additional information you would like to provide about your ticket purchase. You can use it to ask questions, make special requests, or provide any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We will review your form submission and get back to you as soon as possible.
