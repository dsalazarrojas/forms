# Performing Arts Terms Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to acknowledge an individual's understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of performing arts events, workshops, or productions. It ensures that the individual has read, understood, and agrees to the terms and conditions before participating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Provide the name of the production or event you are participating in in the "Production Name" field.
7. Select the type of production or event you are participating in from the "Production Type" dropdown menu.
8. Read and select the relevant terms and conditions from the "I have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions" field.
9. If applicable, enter the date of your participation in the "Date of Participation" field.
