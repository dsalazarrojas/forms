# Paintball Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paintball Waiver Form is a document that participants must complete before participating in a paintball game or event. It's a mandatory requirement to ensure that participants understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the "Event Planner", "Event Details", "Participant Info", and "Parent Consent" sections.
2. Answer the "Participant Signature" section with either "Yes" or "No".
3. Provide the "Event Organizer" and "Emergency Contact" information, including their phone number, email, and address.
4. Fill in the "Date" and "Time" of the event.
5. Enter the "Phone Number" and "Email" of the participant.
6. Provide the "Address" of the participant.
7. Answer the "Emergency Contact" section with either "Yes" or "No".
8. Enter the "Emergency Phone", "Emergency Email", "Emergency Address", and "Relationship" information.
9. Provide the alternate "Emergency Phone", "Emergency Email", and "Alternate Emergency Address" information.
10. Enter the "Event Organizer Phone", "Event Organizer Email", and "Event Organizer Address" information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Planner** (`Event Planner`, text, required): This is the person organizing the paintball event. Please enter their name.
  - **Event Details** (`Event Details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the event, including the type of paintball game or activity.
  - **Participant Info** (`Participant Info`, text, required): Enter your name, age, and any other relevant information about yourself.
  - **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
  - **Waiver** (`Waiver`, text, required): Please write a brief statement agreeing to the terms and conditions of the event.
  - **Parent Consent** (`Parent Consent`, select_one, required): If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian sign this section.
  - **Event Organizer** (`Event Organizer`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the event organizer.
  - **Participant Waiver** (`Participant Waiver`, text, required): Please write a brief statement agreeing to the terms and conditions of the event.
  - **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
  - **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
  - **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
  - **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
  - **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your address.
  - **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, select_one, required): Indicate if you want someone to be your emergency contact by selecting "Yes" or "No".
  - **Emergency Phone** (`Emergency Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Email** (`Emergency Email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Address** (`Emergency Address`, text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
  - **Relationship** (`Relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Phone Alternate** (`Emergency Phone Alternate`, text, required): Enter an alternate phone number of your emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Email Alternate** (`Emergency Email Alternate`, email, required): Enter an alternate email address of your emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Address Alternate** (`Emergency Address Alternate`, text, required): Enter an alternate address of your emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Phone 2** (`Emergency Phone 2`, text, required): Enter a second alternate phone number of your emergency contact.
  - **Event Organizer Phone** (`Event Organizer Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
  - **Event Organizer Email** (`Event Organizer Email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
  - **Event Organizer Address** (`Event Organizer Address`, text, required): Enter the address of the event organizer.
