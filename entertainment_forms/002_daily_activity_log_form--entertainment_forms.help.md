# daily_activity_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily activity log form is used to record and track daily activities. This form should be filled out daily and only once for each day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you are logging the activity for in the "Date Logged" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the activity in the "Activity Name" field.
3. Select the type of activity from the list of options in the "Activity Type" field.
4. Enter the start and end times of the activity in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the location where the activity took place in the "Location" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the activity in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date Logged** (Date): Enter the date you are logging the activity for. This should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Activity Name** (Text): Enter a brief description of the activity, such as "Meeting with John" or "Exercise routine".
* **Activity Type** (Select One): Select the type of activity from the list of options, such as "Work", "Leisure", or "Exercise".
* **Start Time** (Time): Enter the start time of the activity, such as 8:00 AM.
* **End Time** (Time): Enter the end time of the activity, such as 5:00 PM.
* **Location** (Text): Enter the location where the activity took place, such as "Office" or "Home".
* **Notes** (Note): Add any additional comments or notes about the activity, such as "Went for a walk during lunch" or "Had a meeting with a client".
