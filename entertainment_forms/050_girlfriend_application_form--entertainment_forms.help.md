# Girlfriend Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Girlfriend Application Form is a form designed to collect information about a person's preferences, interests, and availability for a potential girlfriend. This form is intended to help the user understand what type of person they might be looking for in a partner, and to provide a basis for further conversation and matching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What about you** (`what about you`, text, required: false): This is an optional field where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: true): This field is for your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone number`, text, required: true): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **What do you like** (`interests`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple options that best describe your interests.
* **What are your work hours** (`availability`, select_one, required: false): Select an option that best describes your work schedule.
* **Where do you live** (`location`, text, required: true): Enter the city, state, or region you reside in.
* **What do you prefer** (`preferences`, text, required: false): If you have any specific preferences, please enter them here.
* **About you** (`about_yourself`, text, required: false): Tell us a little bit about yourself.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, text, required: false): If you have a favorite food, please enter it here.
* **Why Girlfriend** (`why_girlfriend`, text, required: false): Tell us why you're looking for a girlfriend.
* **Favorite Hobby** (`favorite_hobby`, text, required: false): If you have a favorite hobby, please enter it here.
* **How did you hear about us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required: false): Select an option that best describes how you found out about this application.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current relationship status.
* **How long single** (`how_long_as_single`, number, required: true): Enter how long you've been single.
* **Favorite Color** (`favorite_color`, text, required: false): If you have a favorite color, please enter it here.
* **Best Friend Name** (`best_friend_name`, text, required: false): Enter your best friend's name.
* **Best Friend Number** (`best_friend_number`, text, required: false): Enter your best friend's phone number.
* **Best Friend Email** (`best_friend_email`, email, required: false): Enter your best friend's email address.
* **Best Friend Relationship** (`best_friend_relationship`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of relationship you have with your best friend.
* **Family Status** (`family_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current family status.
* **Family Member Names** (`family_member_names`, text, required: false): If you live with or know your family members, please list them here.
* **Family Member Relationships** (`family_member_relationships`, select_multiple, required: false): If you have any family members, select the relationship options that apply to them.
* **How didn't find us** (`how_didnt_find`, text, required: false): If you have any feedback on how you found out about this application, please enter it here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: true): Select the "Submit" option to complete and submit your application.

Note: Please make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
