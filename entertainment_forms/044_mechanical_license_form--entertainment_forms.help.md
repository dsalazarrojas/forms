# Mechanical License Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical License Form is a crucial document used to acquire the necessary rights and permissions for a song to be performed or recorded by others. It ensures that the original creators of the music, the songwriters and publishers, are compensated fairly and correctly for their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Number**: Enter your form number, which can be found on the first page of the form.
2. **Artist Name**: Enter the name of the artist or songwriters whose work you wish to use. This is a crucial field, as it helps ensure that the correct rights holders are notified.
3. **Song Title**: Enter the title of the song you want to use. Make sure to double-check the spelling and title accuracy.
4. **Song Year**: Enter the year the song was released or written.
5. **Song Genre**: Check the box next to 'Yes' if the song matches the genre you're planning to use it in.
6. **Record Label**: Enter the name of the record label that originally released the song.
7. **Record Label Address**: Enter the physical address of the record label where you can send your license request.
8. **Record Label City**: Enter the city where the record label is based.
9. **Record Label State**: Check the box next to 'Yes' if the record label agrees with your license request.
10. **Record Label Zip**: Enter the postal code of the record label.
11. **Record Label Phone**: Enter the phone number of the record label.
12. **Publisher Name**: Enter the name of the publisher of the song.
13. **Publisher Address**: Enter the physical address of the publisher.
14. **Publisher City**: Enter the city where the publisher is based.
15. **Publisher State**: Check the box next to 'Yes' if the publisher agrees with your license request.
16. **Publisher Zip**: Enter the postal code of the publisher.
17. **Publisher Phone**: Enter the phone number of the publisher.
18. **Record Publisher**: Enter the name of the record publisher.
19. **Record Publisher Address**: Enter the physical address of the record publisher.
20. **Record Publisher City**: Enter the city where the record publisher is based.
21. **Record Publisher State**: Check the box next to 'Yes' if the record publisher agrees with your license request.
22. **Record Publisher Zip**: Enter the postal code of the record publisher.
23. **Record Publisher Phone**: Enter the phone number of the record publisher.
24. **Record Publisher Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person at the record publisher.
25. **Record Publisher Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the record publisher's contact person.
