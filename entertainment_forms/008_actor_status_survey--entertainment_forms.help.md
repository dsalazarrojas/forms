# Actor Status Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actor Status Survey is a comprehensive form designed to collect detailed information about your current professional status, allowing casting directors and production teams to make informed decisions about potential casting opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
* Make sure to include your contact email and phone number.
* Review and update your form only when you have new information to report.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Here's a detailed explanation of each field:

* **Actor Full Name** (Required)
 	+ The Actor Full Name field is a required field where you should enter your full name as it appears on your professional documents.
* **Current Agency Representation** (Required)
 	+ In this field, select the name of the agency representing you, or 'Independent' if you're not represented by any agency.
* **Union Status** (Required)
 	+ Choose your union status from the options provided: SAG-AFTRA, AEA (Equity), ACTRA, Non-Union, or Financial Core.
* **Current Work Status** (Required)
 	+ Select your current work status: Actively Seeking Work, Currently Booked (Short-term), On Long-term Production, On Hiatus / Sabbatical.
* **Availability for the Next 3 Months** (Required)
 	+ Select your availability for the next three months: Full Availability, Partial Availability (Some conflicts), No Availability.
* **Primary Geographic Market** (Required)
 	+ Enter the primary market where you are currently working or willing to work, e.g. New York, Los Angeles, London, Atlanta.
* **Willingness to Travel for Work** (Required)
 	+ Choose your willingness to travel for work: Willing to travel internationally, Willing to travel domestically only, Local work only.
* **Valid Passport** (Required)
 	+ Indicate whether you have a valid international passport: True or False.
* **Recent Significant Credits (Last 6 Months)** (Optional)
 	+ If you've had recent significant credits, include project title and role in this field.
* **New Skills or Certifications Acquired** (Optional)
 	+ List any new skills or certifications you've acquired, e.g. stunt training, new language, professional license.
* **Health and Safety Certifications** (Optional)
 	+ If applicable, list any health and safety certifications you've obtained, e.g. COVID Compliance, First Aid.
* **Contact Email** (Required)
 	+ Enter your professional contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Required)
 	+ Provide your professional phone number.
* **Date of Update** (Required)
 	+ Enter the current date for accurate tracking and record-keeping.

## Tips

* Make sure to update your form when you have new information to report.
* If unsure about any field, seek guidance from the casting director or production team.
* Double-check your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
