# Performer and Promoter Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the agreement between a performer and a promoter for an event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll through the form pages and fill in the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (page 1): Provide a brief description of the event, including date, time, and location.
* **Performer Name** (page 2): Enter the name of the performer.
* **Promoter Name** (page 2): Enter the name of the promoter.
* **Event Date** (page 3): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (page 3): Enter the start and end time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (page 4): Enter the length of the event in minutes.
* **Event Location** (page 4): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Type** (page 4): Select the type of event (e.g., concert, show, meet).
* **Event Venue** (page 5): Enter the venue of the event.
* **Performance Fee** (page 5): Enter the fee charged to the performer for their appearance.
* **Promoter Fee** (page 5): Enter the fee charged to the promoter.
* **Payment Method** (page 6): Select the method of payment (e.g., cash, check, wire transfer).
* **Terms and Conditions** (page 7): A space for noting any special terms or conditions.
* **Signed Date** (page 8): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Signed By** (page 8): Enter the name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Event Status** (page 9): Select the status of the event (e.g., upcoming, past, present).
* **Promoter Agreement** (page 9): Enter any additional agreements made with the promoter.
* **Performer Agreement** (page 10): Enter any additional agreements made with the performer.
* **Contract Start Date** (page 11): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (page 11): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Status** (page 12): Select the status of the contract (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Payment Terms** (page 13): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Notes** (page 14): A space for noting any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned Form ID** (page 15): Enter the assigned form ID.
* **Assigned Tool ID** (page 16): Enter the assigned tool ID.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you are not duplicating an existing agreement.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant parties for clarification.
