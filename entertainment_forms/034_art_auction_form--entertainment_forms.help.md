# Art Auction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Auction Form is a tool designed to gather information from potential buyers for an art auction. This form is used to collect personal and contact details of the bidder, artwork specifics, and payment and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your bidder information: 
   - Bidder Name: Your name as the bidder.
   - Bidder ID or Number: Your unique identifier or number as the bidder.
   - Email Address: Your email address where you can be contacted.
   - Phone Number: Your phone number where you can be reached.
2. Provide artwork details:
   - Artwork Lot Number: The number assigned to the artwork you are bidding on.
   - Artwork Title: The title of the artwork you are bidding on.
   - Artist Name: The name of the artist who created the artwork.
   - Medium or Technique: The material or method used to create the artwork.
3. Specify your bid information:
   - Opening Bid Amount: The starting bid amount for the artwork.
   - Current Bid Amount: The current highest bid amount for the artwork.
   - Your Bid Amount: The amount you are willing to bid on the artwork.
   - Reserve Price Met: Whether or not the reserve price has been met.
4. Choose your payment and shipping options:
   - Preferred Payment Method: The payment method you prefer to use.
   - Payment Terms: The payment terms you are comfortable with.
   - Shipping Address: The address where you would like the artwork delivered.
   - Delivery Preference: Your preferred delivery option.
5. Confirm your bid and additional notes:
   - Bid Increment: The minimum bid increment you are willing to accept.
   - Additional Notes or Special Requests: Any additional information you would like to share with the auctioneer.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the auctioneer.
* The information collected will be used solely for the purpose of facilitating the bidding process.
* The auctioneer will contact you with any updates or information related to your bid.
* Please be honest in your bid amount to avoid any issues during the auction process.
