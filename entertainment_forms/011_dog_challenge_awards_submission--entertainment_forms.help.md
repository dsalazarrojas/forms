# Dog Challenge Awards Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dog Challenge Awards Submission form is intended for participants who wish to submit their dog's information and qualifications for the recognition program. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all sections carefully, ensuring you provide accurate and complete information for your dog.
2. Review each field below for further guidance on what to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Details (`entry_details`)**: A brief description of the submission, including a title and any other relevant information about your dog's challenge.
* **Name (`name`)**: Enter the full name of your dog, as it appears on their collar or registration documents.
* **Photo URL (`photo_url`)**: Upload or enter the URL of a photo that showcases your dog's unique features or achievements.
* **Email (`email`)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further communication or updates.
* **Phone (`phone`)**: Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted for additional information or to confirm your submission.
* **Type (`entry_type`)**: Choose 'Yes' if your dog is participating in the challenge, or 'No' if they are not participating.
* **Award Program (`award_program`)**: Select all award programs your dog is participating in (choose 'Yes' for each program your dog is involved in).
* **Contact Details (`contact_details`)**: Provide any additional contact information or details about your dog, such as their age, breed, or training background.
* **Entry Date (`entry_date`)**: Enter the date of the challenge or event your dog participated in.
* **Entry Time (`entry_time`)**: Enter the time of the challenge or event your dog participated in.
* **Photo Title (`photo_title`)**: Enter a title for the photo you submitted, describing your dog's features or achievements.
* **Entry Description (`entry_description`)**: Provide a detailed description of your dog's challenge or achievement, including any notable accomplishments or milestones.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the specific field explanation above.
* Double-check your submission for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.
