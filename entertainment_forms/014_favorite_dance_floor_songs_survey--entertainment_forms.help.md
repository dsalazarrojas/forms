# Favorite Dance Floor Songs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your favorite dance floor songs and provide valuable feedback for future event organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Favorite Dance Floor Songs Survey" title to access the form.
2. Read each field's label carefully and fill in the information accordingly.
3. You will be able to select multiple songs as favorites.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Favorite Dance Floor Songs Survey
* **Favorite Dance Floor Songs Survey** (`favorite_dance_floor_songs_survey`, text, required): This is the title of the form. Please ignore this field, as it is already filled in.
### Music Genre
* **Music Genre** (`music_genre`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if you like the music genre of Electronic/Dance music. 
### Favorite Songs
* **Favorite Songs** (`song_title`, select_multiple, required): Select all the songs that you enjoy the most on the dance floor from the list. You will be able to select multiple songs.
### Event Planner
* **Event Planner** (`event_planner`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if you plan to plan any events or parties. 

## Tips
* Make sure to answer honestly about your preferences.
* Take as much time as you need to select all the songs you like.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, you can ask for help.
