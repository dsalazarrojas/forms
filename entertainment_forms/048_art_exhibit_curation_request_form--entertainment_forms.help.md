# Art Exhibit Curation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way for curators or proposers to submit their art exhibit curation requests to the art organization. This form helps to gather essential information about the exhibit, including the curator's contact details, exhibit concept, title, and logistical requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by filling out your personal information**: This includes your full name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure that your contact details are accurate and up-to-date.
2.  **Provide your title or role**: This is your professional title or role as a curator or proposer.
3.  **Conceptualize your exhibit**: Describe the main idea or concept behind your proposed exhibit.
4.  **Submit your exhibit title and description**: Provide a clear and concise title and description of your exhibit.
5.  **Plan your logistics**: Specify the exhibition duration, estimated number of artworks, types of art forms included, and space requirements.
6.  **Featured artists**: List the names or details of the participating artists.
7.  **Confirm artist commitment**: Indicate whether the artists have confirmed their commitment to participate.
8.  **Plan your special elements**: If you have any special programming, events, or interactive elements, describe them here.
9.  **Marketing and promotion**: Outline your marketing and promotion plan for the exhibit.
10. **Additional information**: If there's any additional information or attachments you'd like to provide, please include them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Title or Role**: Provide your professional title or role as a curator or proposer.
* **Proposed Exhibit Title**: Provide a clear and concise title of your exhibit.
* **Exhibit Theme or Concept**: Describe the main idea or concept behind your proposed exhibit.
* **Detailed Exhibition Description**: Provide a detailed description of your exhibit.
* **Curator Vision and Goals**: Explain what you hope to achieve with your exhibit.
* **Proposed Exhibition Duration**: Specify the start and end dates of your exhibition.
* **Estimated Number of Artworks**: Enter the estimated number of artworks to be included in the exhibit.
*   **Types of Art Forms Included**: Select all applicable art forms included in the exhibit.
*   **Space Requirements**: Specify the space requirements, such as wall space, floor space, climate control, etc.
*   **Featured Artists**: List the names or details of the participating artists.
*   **Do Artists have confirmed Commitment**: Indicate whether the artists have confirmed their commitment to participate.
*   **Will there be an opening reception?**: Select whether there will be an opening reception.
*   **Special Programming or Events**: Describe any special programming, events, or interactive elements.
*   **Interactive or Immersive Elements**: Describe any interactive or immersive elements.
*   **Marketing and Promotion Plan**: Outline your marketing and promotion plan for the exhibit.
*   **Estimated Budget for Exhibition**: Enter your estimated budget for the exhibition.
*   **Additional Information or Attachments**: Provide any additional information or attachments you'd like to include with your submission.
