# Band Performance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Band Performance Request Form is a tool for event planners and organizers to request the services of a band for their events. This form helps to gather essential information about the event, the band's requirements, and the payment terms. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information:
	* Provide your full name in the "Requester Full Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, so we can respond to your request.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, in case we need to contact you directly.
2. Enter event details:
	* In the "Event Name" field, provide the name or title of your event.
	* Choose the type of event you are planning in the "Event Type" field. This will help us match the right band for your event.
	* Select the date of your event in the "Event Date" field.
	* Choose the start time of your event in the "Event Start Time" field.
3. Choose your band preferences:
	* Select the type of music genre you are interested in for your event in the "Genre Preference" field.
	* Choose the duration of the performance in the "Requested Performance Duration" field.
4. Specify your event details:
	* Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place in the "Venue Name" field.
	* Provide the full address of the venue in the "Venue Address" field.
	* Specify the maximum capacity of the venue in the "Venue Capacity" field, if applicable.
5. Set up the payment terms:
	* Choose how you will pay the band in the "Payment Terms" field.
6. Add any additional information:
	* If there are any specific requirements for the band's performance, such as technical riders or hospitality needs, please specify in the "Setup Requirements" and "Hospitality Requirements" fields.
	* If you have any additional notes or information about the event, please provide in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Full Name**: Your full name.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for us to respond to your request.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number, in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Organization Name** (optional): The name of the organization or company hosting the event.
* **Organization Type** (optional): The type of organization hosting the event.
* **Band Requested**: The name of the band you are interested in.
* **Event Name**: The name or title of your event.
* **Event Type**: The category of your event.
* **Event Date**: The date of your event.
* **Event Start Time**: The start time of your event.
* **Venue Name**: The name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Venue Address**: The full address of the venue.
* **Venue Capacity**: The maximum number of attendees you expect.
* **Expected Attendance**: The number of people you expect to attend.
* **Requested Performance Duration**: The length of the performance.
* **Setup Requirements**: Any specific requirements for the band's setup, such as stage and sound system.
* **Budget Offered**: The compensation amount you are willing to offer.
* **Payment Terms**: How you will pay the band.
* **Genre Preference**: The type of music genre you are interested in for your event.
* **Technical Rider Required**: Whether you need a technical rider for the event.
* **Hospitality Requirements**: Any specific needs for food, beverages, or accommodations.
* **Marketing and Promotion**: How you will promote the event.
* **Recording Allowed**: Whether the band is allowed to record the performance.
* **Additional Notes**: Any other information about the event.
* **How Did You Hear About This Band**: The source of your interest in the band.
