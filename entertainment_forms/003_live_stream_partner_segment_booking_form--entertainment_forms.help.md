# Live Stream Partner Segment Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a segment for a live stream. A segment is a specific time slot within a live stream where a partner can showcase their content. This form helps us to gather necessary details from partners to book a suitable segment for them in the live stream.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Partner Details" form with your name and any relevant information about your brand.
2. Select the "Segment Slot" where you would like to book your segment.
3. Provide the "Segment Booking" details, including the type of content you want to showcase.
4. Specify the "Segment Start Time" and "Segment End Time" for the booked segment.
5. Set the "Segment Duration" for the booked segment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Details**: Please enter your name and any relevant information about your brand. This will help us to get in touch with you and ensure that we can accommodate your segment booking requirements.
* **Segment Slot**: Select a time slot for your segment from the available options. This will determine when your segment will be shown during the live stream.
* **Segment Booking**: Please describe the type of content you want to showcase during your segment. This can be a live performance, interview, or any other type of content you would like to feature.
* **Segment Start Time**: Enter the start time for your segment in 24-hour time format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
* **Segment End Time**: Enter the end time for your segment in 24-hour time format (e.g., 15:00 for 3 PM).
* **Segment Duration**: Set the duration of your segment in minutes. This will determine how long your segment will be shown during the live stream.
