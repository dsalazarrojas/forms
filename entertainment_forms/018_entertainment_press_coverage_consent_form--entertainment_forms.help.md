# Entertainment Press Coverage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Press Coverage Consent Form is used to collect information from users who are willing to provide their data for press coverage. This form helps in ensuring that users understand what they are consenting to and their data is collected in a structured manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question "I accept that" with "Yes" or "No" depending on your preference.
2. If you answered "Yes" to the first question, fill out your name, organization, role, and contact details in the second section.
3. You can choose the type of file you want to upload (image or document) and select the file from your device.
4. Fill out the file name and description if you want to.
5. Finally, indicate whether you have completed the form by selecting "True" or "False".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I accept that**: Choose "Yes" if you agree to provide your data for press coverage, and "No" if you do not.
* **Name**: Fill in your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Organization**: Enter your affiliation or institution where you are representing.
* **Role**: Specify your role or position within your organization.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name of the contact person who can be reached for more information.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Consent Given**: Indicate whether you have given consent for press coverage.
* **Consent Date**: Fill out the date when you gave your consent.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your press coverage.
* **File Upload**: Upload a file related to your press coverage.
* **File Type**: Choose the type of file you are uploading (image or document).
* **File Size**: Select the size of your file (small, medium, large).
* **File Name**: Give your file a name for reference.
* **File Description**: Briefly describe the file you are uploading.
* **Form Completed**: Indicate whether you have completed this form.
