# Film Premiere Luxury Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Premiere Luxury Sponsorship Form is designed to collect information from sponsors for film premiere luxury sponsorship opportunities. This form helps event organizers gather relevant details, including contact information and sponsorship specifics, to facilitate effective communication and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your sponsorship details in the Sponsor Details field.
2. Enter your name and company name in the Sponsor Name and Sponsor Company fields.
3. Add your address in the Sponsor Address field.
4. Provide your contact person's name and email in the Contact Person and Contact Email fields.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the Contact Phone field.
6. Choose one or multiple sponsorship packages from the Select Package dropdown menu.
7. Add any additional information about the sponsorship in the Additional Info field.
8. Confirm the number of sponsorships in the Sponsorships Count field.
9. Select the relevant package and confirm the price in the Package Price field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sponsor Details** (`id: 1`): Enter a brief description of your sponsorship.
- **Sponsor Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your name as the representative of the sponsor.
- **Sponsor Company** (`id: 3`): Enter your company name.
- **Sponsor Address** (`id: 4`): Enter your company's address.
- **Contact Person** (`id: 5`): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`id: 6`): Enter the email of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`id: 7`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Select Package** (`id: 8`): Choose one or multiple packages from the dropdown menu. Available options are Gold, Silver, and Bronze.
- **Event Details** (`id: 9`): Provide any relevant event details.
- **Event Date and Time** (`id: 10`): This field is currently not visible in the current form design but is mentioned, so please ignore this field for the current guide.
- **Select Package** (`id: 11`): Confirm the selected package number.
- **Sponsorships** (`id: 12`): Confirm the number of sponsorships.
- **Additional Info** (`id: 13`): Add any additional information about the sponsorship.
- **Sponsorships Count** (`id: 14`): Confirm the number of sponsorships.
- **Package Price** (`id: 15`): Confirm the price of the selected package.
- **Payment Details** (`id: 16`): This field is currently not visible in the current form design but is mentioned, so please ignore this field for the current guide.
