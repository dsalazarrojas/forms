# ticket_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ticket_order_form is used to order tickets for events. You will need to provide event details, select a package, specify the quantity of tickets, and confirm various details to complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide event details in the Event Details field.
2. Select the package type from the Select Package field.
3. Enter the quantity of tickets in the Quantity field.
4. Choose the type of tickets for each ticket in the Ticket Type field.
5. Select the date for the event in the Date field.
6. Enter the time for the event in the Time field.
7. Provide a note for the tickets in the Note field (optional).
8. Enter your email address in the Email field (optional).
9. Enter your phone number in the Phone field (optional).
10. Confirm your email address in the Confirm Email field.
11. Confirm your phone number in the Confirm Phone field.
12. Confirm your selected package type in the Confirm Package field.
13. Confirm the quantity of tickets in the Confirm Quantity field.
14. Confirm the type of tickets in the Confirm Ticket Type field (optional).
15. Confirm the date for the event in the Confirm Date field.
16. Confirm the time for the event in the Time field.
17. Confirm your tickets in the Confirm Tickets field.
18. Confirm additional tickets in the Confirm Tickets 2 and Confirm Tickets 3 fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (Event Details, text, optional): Enter the title of the event you are ordering tickets for.
* **Select Package** (Select Package, select, required): Choose the package type for the event: Standard, Premium, or VIP.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of tickets you want to order.
* **Ticket Type** (Ticket Type, select multiple, optional): Select the type of tickets for each ticket (Standard, Premium, or VIP).
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Add a note or description for the tickets.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (Confirm Email, email, required): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (Confirm Phone, text, required): Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Package** (Confirm Package, select, required): Confirm the package type you selected.
* **Confirm Quantity** (Confirm Quantity, number, required): Confirm the quantity of tickets.
* **Confirm Ticket Type** (Confirm Ticket Type, select multiple, optional): Confirm the type of tickets (if selected).
* **Confirm Date** (Confirm Date, date, required): Confirm the date of the event.
* **Confirm Time** (Confirm Time, time, required): Confirm the time of the event.
* **Confirm Tickets** (Confirm Tickets, number, required): Confirm the number of tickets.
* **Confirm Tickets 2** (Confirm Tickets 2, number, optional): Confirm additional tickets (if applicable).
* **Confirm Tickets 3** (Confirm Tickets 3, number, optional): Confirm additional tickets (if applicable).
* **Confirm Tickets 2** and **Confirm Tickets 3** fields are only required if you are ordering more than one ticket.
