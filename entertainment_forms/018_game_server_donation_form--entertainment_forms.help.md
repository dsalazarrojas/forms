# game_server_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The game server donation form is a way for users to support their favorite game server by making a donation. This form helps us understand who's donating and how to provide updates and rewards to them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Stream ID, which is a unique identifier for your stream account.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
3. Enter your player name, which is the name associated with your stream account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stream ID** (`stream_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your Stream ID, which is a unique identifier for your stream account. This is not required, but it helps us understand your stream identity.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **player_name** (`player_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your player name, which is the name associated with your stream account. This is not required, but it helps us get to know you better.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any) to ensure your donation is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your email address you provided in the form.
