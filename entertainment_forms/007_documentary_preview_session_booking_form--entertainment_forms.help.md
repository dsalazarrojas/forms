# Documentary Preview Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a documentary preview session with a documentary expert. It collects necessary information for scheduling a session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name**, which is required to identify you.
2. Enter your **Contact Email**, which is used to contact you for further information.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**, which is used to reach you for any last-minute updates.
4. Choose a **Date** for the documentary preview session.
5. Select a **Start Time** for the session.
6. Choose an **End Time** for the session.
7. Select the type of session you want to book: **Film**, **Documentary**, or **Lecture**.
8. Select the **Booking Status**: **Pending**, **Confirmed**, or **Cancelled**.
9. Optionally, add a **Note** to provide additional information.
10. You can also leave a **User Message** if you want to communicate something to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Choose a date for the session.
* **Start Time**: Choose the start time for the session.
* **End Time**: Choose the end time for the session.
* **Session Type**: Select the type of session you want to book, either Film, Documentary, or Lecture.
* **Booking Status**: Choose the status of your booking, either Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled.
* **Note**: This is an optional field where you can add any additional information.
* **User Message**: This is an optional field where you can communicate any information you want to share with us.
* **Session ID**: This is a unique identifier for the session, which you will receive after booking.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication.
* **Booking Status**: This field is automatically updated based on your booking status.
