# Art Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Festival Registration Form is a tool used by artists, vendors, volunteers, and speakers to register for a festival. By filling out this form, participants will provide necessary contact and logistical information to the festival organizers, which will be used for communication, booth allocation, and performance scheduling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as the festival participant.
2. Enter your contact email address, which will be used for festival communications.
3. Select your role at the festival from the provided options (Artist, Vendor, Volunteer, or Speaker).
4. If applicable, provide any booth requirements (e.g., size, power, or table needs).
5. If applicable, describe any performance needs (e.g., AV staging or seating requirements).
6. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
7. If you have liability insurance, provide the necessary details.
8. If applicable, share any social media or website links for promotion.
9. Select the days you will attend the festival.
10. Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with the festival organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the festival participant.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Provide your contact email address, which will be used for festival communications.
* **Role at festival** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role at the festival from the options: Artist, Vendor, Volunteer, or Speaker.
* **Booth requirements** (`booth_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you need any special booth requirements (e.g., size, power, or table), provide the details in this field.
* **Performance needs** (`performance_needs`, `text`, optional): If you need any special performance setup or equipment, describe the requirements in this field.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance details** (`insurance_details`, `text`, optional): If you have liability insurance, provide the necessary details (e.g., policy number, insurance provider).
* **Social media or website** (`social_links`, `text`, optional): Share any social media or website links for promotion and festival exposure.
* **Attendance days** (`attendance_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which days you will attend the festival.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any other information you'd like to share with the festival organizers.
