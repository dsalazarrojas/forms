# Theater Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Membership Subscription Form is designed to facilitate the membership sign-up process for users. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide your information and complete the subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the membership sign-up process, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Membership Sign Up page with your membership details.
2. Provide your payment information and choose your preferred payment method on the Payment Information page.
3. Enter your member information on the Member Information page.
4. Select any special benefits you're interested in on the Special Benefits page.
5. Enter your contact information on the Contact Information page.
6. Read and agree to the Payment Terms page.
7. Submit your membership application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Sign Up** (`membership_sign_up`, text, required):
	+ This is the starting point for your membership sign-up process. Please fill out this page with your desired membership details, including any relevant information about your subscription.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your payment information, including your preferred payment method. If you don't have a preferred payment method, you can leave this field blank.
* **Member Information** (`member_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your member information, including any relevant details about yourself.
* **Special Benefits** (`special_benefits`, text, optional):
	+ If you're interested in any special benefits, such as discounts or promotions, select them here.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional):
	+ Read and agree to our payment terms and conditions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided: credit card, bank transfer, or other.
* **Member Discount** (`member_discount`, number, optional):
	+ If you're eligible for a member discount, enter the discount amount here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional):
	+ Once you've completed all the required fields, submit your membership application.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth membership sign-up process.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
- Review all fields carefully before submitting your application to avoid errors.
