# virtual_fan_panel_broadcast_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The virtual_fan_panel_broadcast_consent_form is a form designed to collect information and consent from virtual fans who wish to participate in a broadcast or event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Participant Details section with your relevant information.
2. Read and acknowledge the Consent Acknowledgment section.
3. Sign your E-Signature to confirm your consent.
4. If necessary, fill out the Additional Participant Details section with any further information about yourself.
5. Review and sign your E-Signature 3 to confirm your consent again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, optional): This section is for you to provide your details such as your name, email address, and other relevant information about yourself.
* **Consent Acknowledgment** (Consent Acknowledgment, note, optional): Please read and acknowledge that you have understood the terms and conditions of the broadcast.
* **E-Signature** (E-Signature, text, optional): Sign with your electronic signature to confirm your consent.
* **E-Signature 2** (E-Signature 2, text, optional): This is a duplicate E-Signature to further confirm your consent.
* **Participant Details 2** (Participant Details 2, text, optional): If you are not the primary participant, please provide additional details about yourself in this section.
* **Consent 2** (Consent 2, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the consents that apply to you.
* **Additional Participant Details** (Additional Participant Details, text, optional): If there is any additional information about yourself that you would like to provide, please enter it here.
* **E-Signature 3** (E-Signature 3, text, optional): This is another duplicate E-Signature to confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure, please refer to the event organizer or the broadcast details.
