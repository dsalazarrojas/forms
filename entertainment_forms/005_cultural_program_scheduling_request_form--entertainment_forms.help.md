# Cultural Program Scheduling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule cultural programs, such as concerts, performances, or events. Fill out the form below to request a scheduling for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the "Contact Information" field with your name and contact information.
2. Choose the "Event Name" and "Event Type" fields to accurately describe your event.
3. Select the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields to choose the date and time you would like to schedule the event.
4. Fill out the "Venue" field with the location of your event.
5. In the "Description" field, provide a brief description of your event.
6. If necessary, attach any supporting documents or materials in the "Attachments" field.
7. Select the "Submission" field to submit your request.
8. Choose the "Approval" and "Status" fields to indicate how you would like the approval process to proceed.
9. Fill out the "Requester" field with the name of the person requesting the scheduling.
10. Fill out the "Approver" fields with the contact information of the person approving the scheduling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_2)**: This field is required to contact you for any follow-up questions or concerns. Fill out your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Event Name (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_6)**: This field should accurately describe the name of your event.
* **Event Type (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_7)**: Choose the type of event you are requesting to schedule. This will help us understand the nature of your event.
* **Performance Date (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_8)**: Select the date you would like to schedule the event.
* **Performance Time (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_9)**: Select the time you would like to schedule the event.
* **Venue (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_10)**: This field is used to describe the location of your event.
* **Description (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_11)**: Provide a brief description of your event.
* **Attachments (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_12)**: If necessary, attach any supporting documents or materials to help us better understand your request.
* **Submission (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_13)**: Select "Submit" to send us your scheduling request.
* **Approval (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_14)**: Choose how you would like the approval process to proceed.
* **Status (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_15)**: Select the status of your event, such as "Pending", "Approved", "Rejected", etc.
* **Requester (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_16)**: Fill out the name of the person requesting the scheduling.
* **Approver (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_17)**: Fill out the contact information of the person approving the scheduling.
* **Approver Email (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_18)**: Fill out the email address of the person approving the scheduling.
* **Approver Phone (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_19)**: Fill out the phone number of the person approving the scheduling.
* **Approver Contact (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_20)**: Fill out any additional contact information of the person approving the scheduling.
* **Comments (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_21)**: Leave any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Requested Date (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_22)**: Select the date you would like to schedule the event, but please note that this field is not required.
* **Requested Time (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_23)**: Select the time you would like to schedule the event, but please note that this field is not required.
* **Approved Date (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_24)**: This field will automatically be filled out once your request has been approved.
* **Approved Time (cultural_program_scheduling_request_form_25)**: This field will automatically be filled out once your request has been approved.
