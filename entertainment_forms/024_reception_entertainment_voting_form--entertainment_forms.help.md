> # Reception Entertainment Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reception Entertainment Voting Form is designed to gather user opinions and feedback on reception entertainment types. Your input will help us tailor our entertainment offerings to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name and email address (if you choose to provide it).
2. Choose your favorite entertainment type (Dance, Music, Film, or Game).
3. Rate your satisfaction with a number (1-5).
4. Provide any feedback or comments about our entertainment offerings.
5. If you want to, provide your phone number and select additional entertainment types that interest you.
6. Select your preferred date and time for entertainment events.
7. Add any comments or notes about your entertainment preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Your first name is required to identify you in our database.
* **Email** (email): Your email address is optional and will only be used for internal communication with the reception team.
* **Favorite Entertainment Type** (select_one): Choose one entertainment type that you enjoy the most: Dance, Music, Film, or Game.
* **Rating** (number): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback** (text): Share any thoughts or suggestions about our entertainment offerings.
* **Phone** (text): Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted for further entertainment discussions.
* **Select Entertainment Type** (select_multiple): Choose multiple entertainment types that interest you.
* **Select Entertainment Type 2** (select_multiple): Choose multiple entertainment types that interest you (this is a duplicate of the previous field).
* **Date** (date): Select a date for entertainment events.
* **Time** (time): Select a time for entertainment events.
* **Comments** (text): Add any additional comments or notes about your entertainment preferences.
