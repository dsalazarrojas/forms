# Event Performer Recording Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Performer Recording Release Form is a document used to record the consent of an artist for live events. This form is used by event organizers to ensure that the artist understands the scope of the event and their role in it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the event in the **Event Title** field.
2. Select the date of the event from the calendar.
3. Choose the type of event from the options (Concert, Conference, Live Event).
4. Enter your name as the **Artist Name**.
5. Choose whether you grant recording permission (With Consent, Without Consent).
6. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
7. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
8. If applicable, enter the name of the event organizer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Title** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the event, for example, "Concert for Charity".
- **Event Date** (2, date, required/optional): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
- **Event Type** (3, select_one, required): Choose the type of event from the options: Concert, Conference, or Live Event.
  - If you are a musician, select "Concert" if you will be performing on stage.
  - If you are a speaker, select "Conference" if you will be giving a presentation.
  - If you are an artist or performer who will be participating in a live event, select "Live Event".
- **Artist Name** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the performing artist.
- **Recording Permission** (5, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you grant recording permission for the event. Select "With Consent" if you allow recording, and "Without Consent" if you do not.
- **Contact Info** (6, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, such as your phone number and address.
- **Email** (7, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Event Organizer** (8, text, required/optional): If you are working with an event organizer, enter their name.
