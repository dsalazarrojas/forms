# Bumperz Packages - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bumperz Packages form is designed to gather information about your event and your preferences for bumper car rentals. This form will help us understand your needs and provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.
2. Select the correct options for each field as needed.
3. Provide any additional information or comments in the Additional Information field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Inquiry Date** (1): Enter the date you are inquiring about.
- **Your Name** (2): Enter your full name.
- **Email Address** (3): Enter your contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (4): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Event Type** (5): Select the type of event you are planning (e.g. Birthday Party, Corporate Event, etc.).
- **Event Date** (6): Enter the date of your event.
- **Event Location** (7): Enter the location where the event will be held.
- **Event Duration** (8): Select the length of your event (e.g. Half Day, Full Day, etc.) (Optional).
- **Expected Attendance** (9): Enter the expected number of guests attending.
- **Primary Age Group** (10): Select the age group that will be attending (e.g. Children Only, Teens, etc.).
- **Package Type of Interest** (11): Select the type of bumper car package you are interested in (e.g. Standard, Deluxe, etc.).
- **Number of Bumper Cars Needed** (12): Enter the number of bumper cars you need.
- **Additional Attractions Interested In** (13): Select any additional attractions or entertainment options you are interested in (e.g. Inflatable Rentals, Games and Prizes, etc.).
- **Venue Setup Requirements** (14): Enter any specific venue setup or logistics requirements.
- **Special Requests or Customization** (15): Enter any special requests or custom features you would like.
- **Insurance Coverage Needed** (16): Select if you need insurance coverage for your event.
- **Safety or Accessibility Concerns** (17): Enter any safety or accessibility concerns you have.
- **Budget Range** (18): Select your approximate budget for the event.
- **Staffing or Operator Support** (19): Select if you need operator support or a full staff.
- **Previous Rental Experience** (20): Select if you have rented bumper cars before.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (21): Select your preferred method of contact.
- **Additional Information** (22): Enter any additional information or comments about your event.

Note: The fields with an asterisk (*) after the label are required and must be filled out.
