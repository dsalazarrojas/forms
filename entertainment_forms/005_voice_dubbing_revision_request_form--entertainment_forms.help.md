# Voice Dubbing Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voice Dubbing Revision Request Form is used to request revisions on voice dubbing files. This form helps to gather all necessary information to ensure that the revisions are properly handled and completed in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief title for the revision request.
2. Provide details about the project, including any specific instructions or requirements.
3. Select and upload files that need revision.
4. Choose the priority level of the revision task (Low, Medium, or High).
5. Enter your email address and contact phone number.
6. Set a specific date and time for the completion of the task.
7. Identify who the task will be assigned to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voice Dubbing Revision Request Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required):
	+ Enter a brief and descriptive title for your revision request.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the project, including any specific requirements or instructions.
* **Select a File** (`select_files`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Choose the files that need revision from the options provided. Multiple files can be selected.
* **Revision Priority** (`select_priority`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the priority level for the revision task. Options are: Low, Medium, and High.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the revision status.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Date and Time** (`date` and `time`, date and time, required):
	+ Set a specific date and time for the completion of the task.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required):
	+ Identify who the task will be assigned to for further action.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required):
	+ Add any additional comments or instructions for the assignee.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field to ensure that the revision request is handled correctly.
* If multiple files need revision, select all required files using the "Select a File" option.
* Choose the correct priority level for your revision task to ensure timely completion.
* Enter a correct email address and phone number to receive updates on the revision status.
* Be specific with the date and time for task completion to avoid delays.
