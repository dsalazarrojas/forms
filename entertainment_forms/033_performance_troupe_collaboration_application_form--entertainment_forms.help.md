# Performance Troupe Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for artists and performers who wish to collaborate on a performance project. It aims to gather essential information about the troupe, its contact details, and preferences for collaboration and performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2.  For performance preferences, select all relevant options that apply to your troupe.
3.  Review the form for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Troupe Details**: Enter the name, contact information, and any additional details about your performance troupe.
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: true**
*   **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information to ensure we can reach you for further collaboration.
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: true**
*   **Performance Preferences 1, 2, 3**: Select all relevant performance types that apply to your troupe.
    *   **Type: select_multiple**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Collaboration Preferences**: Enter any special requests or requirements for collaboration.
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Collaboration Details**: Enter any additional details about your collaboration preferences.
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Review Preferences**: Enter any specific review requirements or preferences.
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: false**
*   **Submit**: Review and submit your application. Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
    *   **Type: text**
    *   **Required: true**
