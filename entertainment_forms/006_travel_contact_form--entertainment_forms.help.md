# Travel Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel contact form is designed to gather information from customers about their travel plans and preferences. This information will be used by the travel company to tailor their services to meet the customer's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
2. Indicate if you have travel arrangements or not.
3. Select your travel preferences for hotel and flight.
4. Enter your Home Address, Phone number, and Email address.
5. Indicate if you need to provide travel documents or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`travel_contact_form`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Travel Arrangements** (`travel_arrangements`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the box(es) next to 'Yes' if you have made travel arrangements.
* **Hotel Preference** (`hotel_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a hotel preference.
* **Flight Preference** (`flight_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a flight preference.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required/optional): Please enter your home address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the format xxxxxxxxxx@xxx.xxx.
* **Travel Documents** (`travel_documents`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the box(es) next to 'Yes' if you need to provide travel documents.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter all required fields to ensure that your information is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
