# music_video_location_permit_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a permit for a music video shoot at a specific location.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the necessary options for your shoot, including whether it is a yes or no for each field.
2. Make sure to provide all required information to ensure that your permit request is processed efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Shoot Details** (`shoot_details`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether your shoot is for a commercial, event, or other purpose.
  - Select 'Yes' if your shoot is for a commercial, event, or other purpose that requires a permit.
  - Select 'No' if your shoot is not for any of these reasons.
- **Shoot Location** (`shoot_location`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the location for your shoot.
  - Select the location where you plan to shoot your music video.
  - Please note that location selection is not exhaustive and may not include all locations.
- **Shoot Date** (`shoot_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for your shoot.
  - Enter the date you plan to shoot your music video.
  - Format is MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Shoot Time** (`shoot_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for your shoot.
  - Enter the time you plan to shoot your music video.
  - Format is HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Shoot Duration** (`shoot_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of your shoot.
  - Enter the estimated duration of your shoot.
  - Please note that this field is not used in the permit request processing.
- **Crew Details** (`crew_details`, note, required/optional): Provide details about your crew.
  - Enter any additional information about your crew that is relevant to the permit request.
  - Please keep this field brief and to the point.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you have a contact person for your shoot.
  - Select 'Yes' if you have a contact person for your shoot.
  - Select 'No' if you do not have a contact person for your shoot.
- **Shoot Requirements** (`shoot_requirements`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose any special requirements for your shoot.
  - Select 'Yes' if you have any special requirements (e.g., power, parking, etc.).
  - Select 'No' if you do not have any special requirements.
- **Shoot Equipment** (`shoot_equipment`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose any special equipment you will use during your shoot.
  - Select 'Yes' if you will use any special equipment during your shoot.
  - Select 'No' if you do not use any special equipment.
- **Parking Details** (`parking_details`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the parking arrangements for your shoot.
  - Select 'Yes' if you have arranged parking for your crew and equipment.
  - Select 'No' if you do not have parking arrangements.
- **Location Details** (`location_details`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose any special location details.
  - Select 'Yes' if you have any special location details.
  - Select 'No' if you do not have any special location details.
- **Shoot Notes** (`shoot_notes`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose any additional shoot notes.
  - Select 'Yes' if you have any additional shoot notes.
  - Select 'No' if you do not have any additional shoot notes.
