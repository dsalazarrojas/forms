# DJ Service Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the DJ Service Information Form. Please follow these steps to ensure you provide the necessary information about your event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll through each page of the form until you reach the field that requires your attention.
2. Answer each question to the best of your abilities.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (event type, contact phone, contact email).
4. If a field is optional, use it to provide additional information that might be relevant to your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): This field is a single-select field asking if the event is yes or no.
* **Event Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Music Preferences** (`music_preferences`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select multiple music preferences.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of your event.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the event organizer.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the event organizer.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Enter a brief message about your event.
* **Music Preferences 2** (`music_preferences_2`, text, optional): You can enter any additional music preferences.
* **More Music Preferences** (`music_preferences_3`, text, optional): You can enter any additional music preferences.
* **DJ Equipment** (`music_preferences_4`, text, optional): You can specify the equipment you need for the DJ service.
* **Music Style** (`music_preferences_5`, text, optional): You can specify the music style you're looking for.
* **Sound System** (`music_preferences_6`, text, optional): You can specify the sound system you're looking for.
* **Sound System 2** (`music_preferences_7`, text, optional): You can specify the sound system you're looking for.
* **Equipment Preference** (`music_preferences_8`, text, optional): You can specify any equipment preferences.
* **Music Style 2** (`music_preferences_10`, text, optional): You can specify a music style.
* **Music Style 3** (`music_preferences_11`, text, optional): You can specify another music style.
* **Music Preferences 12** (`music_preferences_12`, text, optional): You can specify more music preferences.
* **Music Preferences 13** (`music_preferences_13`, text, optional): You can specify even more music preferences.
* **More Equipment Preferences** (`music_preferences_14`, text, optional): You can specify more equipment preferences.
* **More Sound Systems** (`music_preferences_15`, text, optional): You can specify more sound systems.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): You can enter any additional information.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): You can specify the contact name.
