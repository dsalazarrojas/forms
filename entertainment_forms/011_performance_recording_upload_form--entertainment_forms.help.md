# Performance Recording Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Recording Upload Form is designed to collect and manage performance recordings of artists. This form helps to upload and store performance details in a centralized system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "video_upload" field to upload a video recording.
2. Enter the "performer_name" field with the name of the artist performing.
3. Enter the "date" field with the date of the performance.
4. Upload the "file_upload" field with the relevant file for the performance (e.g., an image or document).
5. Enter the "audio_upload" field with an audio recording (if available).
6. Enter a valid "email" field for the artist or performer.
7. Enter the "phone" field with the artist or performer's phone number (if available).
8. Provide a brief description of the artist or performer in the "performer_bio" field.
9. Enter the "video_url" field with a URL for the video recording (if available).
10. Enter the "audio_url" field with a URL for the audio recording (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video Upload** (`video_upload`, `text`, required: false): Upload a video recording of the performance by dragging and dropping a file or selecting a file from your local machine. The file must be in a supported format (e.g., MP4, MOV, AVI).
* **Performer Name** (`performer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the artist or performer.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the performance in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, `text`, required: false): Upload a file related to the performance, such as an image or document.
* **Audio Upload** (`audio_upload`, `text`, required: false): Upload an audio recording of the performance.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for the artist or performer.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the artist or performer.
* **Performer Bio** (`performer_bio`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the artist or performer.
* **Video URL** (`video_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter a URL for the video recording (if available).
* **Audio URL** (`audio_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter a URL for the audio recording (if available).

## Tips
* Ensure that the uploaded files are in the correct format.
* If uploading a video, ensure that it is not already in the system to avoid duplication.
* If uploading an audio file, ensure that it is not already in the system to avoid duplication.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the system administrator for guidance.
