# lemonade_artwork_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lemonade_artwork_submission_form is a user-facing form designed for artists to submit their artworks for consideration in the Lemonade Art Program. This form collects information about the artwork, artist, and submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your artwork in the **Artwork Title** field.
2. Provide your name as the **Artist Name**.
3. Select the type of artwork you are submitting (Artwork, Design, or Other).
4. Write a brief description of your artwork in the **Artwork Description** field.
5. Enter the date and time you are submitting your artwork (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
6. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
7. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artwork Title** (`artwork_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of your artwork, a brief and descriptive name that captures the essence of your work.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required: false): Provide your name as the artist behind the artwork.
* **Artwork Type** (`artwork_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of artwork you are submitting (Artwork, Design, or Other).
* **Artwork Description** (`artwork_description`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of your artwork, including any relevant details or inspirations.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you are submitting your artwork (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you are submitting your artwork (format: HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication and feedback purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your artwork, if necessary.
