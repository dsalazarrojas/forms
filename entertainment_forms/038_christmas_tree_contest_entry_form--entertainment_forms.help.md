# Christmas Tree Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Christmas Tree Contest Entry Form is designed to collect information from contestants who wish to participate in a Christmas tree decorating contest. This form helps the contest organizers gather necessary details about the contestant's tree, including its type, size, location, and decoration style. By submitting this form, contestants are eligible to be judged and potentially win a prize. It is essential that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure fairness and consistency in the judging process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** fields with your personal details. These fields are required to identify you as a contestant.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you with contest updates and results.
3. Provide your **Address**, **City**, and **State or Province**, and **ZIP or Postal Code** for your contact information.
4. Select the type of tree you are entering with the **Tree Type** field. Choose from Real tree, Artificial tree, Tabletop tree, or Decorated object.
5. Enter the **Tree Height** of your tree.
6. Specify the **Tree Location** where your tree is situated. You can choose from Residential home, Business location, Community space, or Other.
7. Indicate your preferred **Decoration Style** for the judges to consider during the competition. Select from Traditional, Modern, Rustic, Themed, Artistic, or Other.
8. Briefly describe your **Tree Description**, telling us about the theme, design, and any special features of your tree.
9. Select the contest category you think your tree best fits into with **Which Category Are You Entering?** You can choose from Most Creative, Most Traditional, Most Colorful, Best Use of Lights, Best Theme, People's Choice, or Other.
10. Explain what makes your tree special in the **What Makes Your Tree Special?** field.
11. Give your consent to post photos of your tree and for media and press coverage with the **Permission to Post Photos** and **Media/Press Permission** fields.
12. If applicable, express your interest in prizes and specify the type of prize you prefer with the **Prize Interest** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Provide your house or residence address.
* **City**: Enter the city where your tree is located.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where your tree is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the zip or postal code of your location.
* **Tree Type**: Select the type of tree you are entering (Real tree, Artificial tree, Tabletop tree, or Decorated object).
* **Tree Height**: Enter the height of your tree in feet.
* **Tree Location**: Specify where your tree is situated (Residential home, Business location, Community space, or Other).
* **Decoration Style**: Indicate the style of your tree's decorations (Traditional, Modern, Rustic, Themed, Artistic, or Other).
* **Tree Description**: Briefly describe your tree, including its theme, design, and any special features.
* **Which Category Are You Entering?**: Choose the category that best fits your tree's characteristics.
* **What Makes Your Tree Special?**: Explain what makes your tree unique and worthy of recognition.
* **Permission to Post Photos**: Give your consent to post photos of your tree on the contest's social media channels.
* **Media/Press Permission**: Give your consent for media and press coverage of your tree.
* **Prize Interest**: If you're interested in prizes, specify the type of prize you prefer (Any prize, Specific prize, Donation to charity preferred).
