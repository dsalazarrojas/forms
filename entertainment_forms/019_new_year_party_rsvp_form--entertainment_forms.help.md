# New Year Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Year Party RSVP Form is a simple form designed to help you let us know if you can attend the party. This information will help us make the necessary arrangements for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Guest Name in the first field.
2. Select whether you are Attending Party or not in the second field.
3. Choose the date and time you plan to RSVP by in the third field.
4. If you have selected to attend the party, choose the time you plan to arrive at the party in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): This is where you enter your name as a guest. Please enter your real name as it will be displayed on the guest list.
* **Attending Party** (`attending_party`, select_one, required/optional): Choose "Yes" if you are attending the party and "No" if you are not attending the party. This will help us determine how many guests are coming.
* **RSVP By** (`rsvp_by`, date, required/optional): Please select a date for when you plan to RSVP by. This will ensure we receive your response on time.
* **Time** (`rsvp_at_time`, time, required/optional): If you are attending the party, choose a time when you plan to arrive at the party. This will help us plan for your arrival.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in the form accurately and completely.
* If you're attending the party, please select the date and time for when you plan to arrive.
* If you're not attending the party, please let us know so we can make necessary arrangements.
