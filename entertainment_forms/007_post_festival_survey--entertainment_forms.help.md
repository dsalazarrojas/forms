# post_festival_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The post_festival_survey form is designed to collect feedback and survey information from users after a festival event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5. Select a date for when you attended the festival in the `date` field.
6. Select a time for when you attended the festival in the `time` field.
7. Write a note about your experience in the `notes` field.
8. Choose whether you selected one or more of the provided options in the `select_one` field.
9. Choose multiple options for the questions in the `select_multiple` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **last_name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **date** (Date, Required: False): Select a date when you attended the festival. This will help us track your visit.
* **time** (Time, Required: False): Select a time when you attended the festival. This will help us track your visit.
* **notes** (Note, Required: False): Write a note about your experience at the festival. This will help us understand your thoughts and opinions.
* **select_one** (Select One, Required: False): Choose whether you selected one of the options provided. If you did not select any option, please select "None".
* **select_multiple** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Choose multiple options that best describe your experience at the festival. You can select one or more of the provided options.
