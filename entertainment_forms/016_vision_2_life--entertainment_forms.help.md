# vision_2_life - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vision_2_life" form is designed to collect demographic information from users for entertainment-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form submit field with your submission information.
2. Enter your first name in the first-name field.
3. Enter your last name in the last-name field.
4. Provide your email address in the email field.
5. Enter your contact number in the contact-number field.
6. Enter the name of your agency in the agency field.
7. Enter the name of the contact person in the contact-person field.
8. Enter your current position or job title in the position field.
9. If applicable, enter your experience in years in the experience field.
10. If applicable, upload your resume in the resume field.

* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your submission information. You can type in any relevant details about your submission, such as a project or a portfolio.
* **First Name** (`first-name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last-name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact-number`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your contact number.
* **Agency** (`agency`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the name of your agency.
* **Contact Person** (`contact-person`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the name of the contact person.
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your current position or job title.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, optional): This field is where you can enter your experience in years, if applicable.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): This field is where you can upload your resume, if applicable.
