# international_dance_competition_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for an international dance competition. It is intended for use by event organizers or coordinators to gather essential details about each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure that you have provided all necessary details.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, so that we can reach out to you with updates and information about the competition.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): List the name and phone number of a contact person who can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship to Participant** (`emergency_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship to the participant, e.g., parent, guardian, or teacher.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the participant that is relevant to the competition.
* **Competition Information** (`competition_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant information about the competition, such as the style or category you will be competing in.
* **Studio Information** (`studio_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter details about the studio or school where you are representing, if applicable.
* **Participant Image** (`participant_image`, `number`, required: false): Upload a photo of the participant for identification purposes.
* **Participant Dance** (`participant_dance`, `text`, required: false): Choose the type of dance you will be performing.
* **Participant Style** (`participant_style`, `text`, required: false): Select the style of dance you will be performing.
* **Participant Level** (`participant_level`, `text`, required: false): Choose your level of experience or skill.
* **Competition Category** (`competition_category`, `text`, required: false): Select the category of the competition you will be participating in.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your age group.
* **Competition Level** (`competition_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the level of competition.
* **Competition Style** (`competition_style`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the style of competition.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, required: false): Enter the participant's age.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Participant Height** (`participant_height`, `number`, required: false): Enter the participant's height.
* **Participant Weight** (`participant_weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter the participant's weight.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes or information about the participant.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have permission from the participant to collect and process their data.
* If you are unsure about any field or requirement, contact the event organizers or coordinators for clarification.
