# Shooting Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a shooting event, ensuring their safety and the organizers' responsibility. The form will collect personal details, safety information, and waiver consent to participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in all the required fields.
- The form has two sections: Participant Information and Event Information.
- Participant Information section includes details about the participant's personal info, safety information, and waiver signed.
- Event Information section includes information about the event, shooting date, start and end times, and event supervision.
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields and ensure the participant's waiver is signed.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **shooting_waiver_form** (Text Field): Write a brief statement explaining the purpose of the form, and the participant's understanding of the event and associated risks.
* **participant_name** (Text Field): Enter your name as the participant.
* **participant_age** (Number Field): Provide your age for age verification purposes.
* **participant_gender** (Select Field): Select your gender (Female, Male, Other).
* **participant_contact_number** (Text Field): Enter your contact number in case we need to reach you.
* **participant_email** (Email Field): Enter your email address for event updates.
* **participant_address** (Text Field): Enter your address for contact and medical purposes.
* **shooting_date** (Date Field): Enter the date of the shooting event.
* **shooting_start_time** (Time Field): Enter the start time of the shooting event.
* **shooting_end_time** (Time Field): Enter the end time of the shooting event.
* **participant_dress_code** (Note Field): Note any specific dress code or equipment required for the event.
* **waiver_signed** (Select Field): Sign to confirm you have read and understood the participant waiver (True/False).
* **parent_name** (Text Field): Enter your parent's name if you are under 18 years old (optional).
* **parent_email** (Email Field): Enter your parent's email address for communication purposes (optional).
* **parent_phone** (Text Field): Enter your parent's contact number (optional).
* **insurance_details** (Text Field): If you have any insurance coverage, please specify the details.
* **medical_certification** (Select Field): If you have any medical certification, please select (Yes/No).
* **shooting_location** (Text Field): Enter the shooting location.
* **shooting_type** (Select Field): Select the type of shooting event (Pistol, Rifle, Bow, Other).
* **shooting_range** (Select Field): Select whether you have shooting range experience (Yes/No).
* **participant_authorized** (Select Field): Indicate your authorized participation level (True/False).
* **shooting_equipment** (Select Field): Select any required shooting equipment (Helmet, Safety Glasses, Earplugs, Other).
* **first_aids** (Select Field): Select any first aid kit required or available (First Aid Kit, Fire Extinquisher, Other).
* **safety_inspection** (Select Field): Indicate the safety inspection required (True/False).
* **shooting_supervision** (Select Field): Indicate the shooting supervision level (True/False).

Note: Please review the form carefully and ask for clarification if you are unsure about any field.
