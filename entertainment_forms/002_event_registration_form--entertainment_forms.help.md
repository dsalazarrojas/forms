# Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Registration Form is designed to collect information about an event that a user wants to register. This form helps event organizers gather necessary details about the event before it takes place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address and Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
4. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
5. Specify the venue of the event in the "Event Venue" field.
6. Describe the details of the event in the "Event Details" field.
7. Choose the type of event you are registering for from the "Event Type" options.
8. Select the time zone of the event from the "Event Timezone" options.
9. Finally, select the tool assigned to you from the "Assigned Tool" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Venue** (`event_venue`, text, required: false): Enter the venue of the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, required: false): Describe the details of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of event you are registering for.
* **Event Timezone** (`event_timezone`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the time zone of the event.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the tool assigned to you for the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your selections in "Event Type", "Event Timezone", and "Assigned Tool" fields for accuracy.
* This form is for registration purposes only, and the event organizer will use the provided information to coordinate the event.
