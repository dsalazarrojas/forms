# Waterpark Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general, contact, visit, age verification, emergency contact, medical information, vehicle information, payment, and signature from waterpark visitors. The form is meant to be filled out by visitors to provide required information for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the General Information section on page 1.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. If you have any visit-related information, please provide it in the Visit Information section.
4. Select your correct age group in the Age Verification section.
5. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their information in the Emergency Contact section.
6. If you have any medical information, please provide it in the Medical Information section.
7. If you have vehicle information, please provide it in the Vehicle Information section.
8. Finally, enter your payment information in the Payment Information section.
9. Sign and save your registration by filling out the signature section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **General Information (required)**: This section requires your name, email, and phone number.
  - **Name (required)**: Enter your full name for registration purposes.
  - **Email (required)**: Provide your email address for contact.
  - **Phone Number (required)**: Enter your phone number for contact.

- **Contact Information (required)**:
  - **Contact Name (required)**: Provide the name of the contact person.
  - **Contact Phone Number (required)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
  - **Contact Email (required)**: Provide the email address of the contact person.

- **Visit Information (optional)**:
  - **Visit Date (optional)**: Enter the date of your visit.
  - **Visit Time (optional)**: Enter the time of your visit.
  - **Visit Duration (optional)**: Enter the duration of your visit.
  - **Visit Purpose (optional)**: Provide the purpose of your visit.

- **Age Verification (required)**: Select your correct age group.

- **Emergency Contact (optional)**:
  - **Emergency Contact Name (optional)**: Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
  - **Emergency Contact Phone Number (optional)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
  - **Emergency Contact Email (optional)**: Provide the email address of the emergency contact person.

- **Medical Information (optional)**:
  - **Medical Condition (optional)**: Provide any medical conditions you have.
  - **Medication (optional)**: List any medications you are currently taking.
  - **Allergy (optional)**: List any allergies you have.

- **Vehicle Information (optional)**:
  - **Vehicle Type (optional)**: Choose the type of vehicle you will be using.
  - **Vehicle License (optional)**: Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.

- **Payment Information (required)**:
  - **Payment Method (required)**: Select the payment method you will be using.
  - **Payment Amount (required)**: Enter the total payment amount.

- **Sign and Save (required)**: Sign and save your registration form.
