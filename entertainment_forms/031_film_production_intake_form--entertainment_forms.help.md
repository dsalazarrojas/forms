# Film Production Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential details about film projects, enabling the team to stay on top of project information and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Film Project Info** field with a brief description of the project.
2. In the **Production Company Info** field, provide the relevant contact information of the production company.
3. In the **Crew Info** field, list the crew members involved in the project.
4. Choose either **Yes** or **No** for **Budget Info** to indicate if budgeting is involved.
5. If budgeting is involved, provide more details about the location in the **Location Info** field.
6. Select either **Yes** or **No** for **Production Equipment** to indicate if equipment usage is involved.
7. In the **Crew Schedule** field, list the schedule details of the crew.
8. In the **Production Services** field, list any required production services.
9. Choose the **Project Status** from the options provided to indicate the project's current status.
10. In the **Additional Info** field, provide any additional context or details about the project.
11. Finally, select whether project files are available or not in the **Project Files** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Film Project Info (id 1)**: This field is for describing the film project in a short paragraph. Please include a brief overview of the project, including the title and purpose.
* **Production Company Info (id 2)**: This is where you'll input the contact information of the production company, including the company name, address, and phone number.
* **Crew Info (id 3)**: List all the key crew members involved in the project, including their names and roles.
* **Budget Info (id 4)**: This field is for indicating if budgeting is involved in the project. If **Yes**, you may need to fill in additional details for further clarification.
* **Location Info (id 5)**: If budgeting is involved, please provide details about the location where the project will take place.
* **Production Equipment (id 6)**: This field is used to indicate if and what kind of production equipment is needed for the project. Select **Yes** for equipment usage.
* **Crew Schedule (id 7)**: List the schedule details of the crew, including any important dates and times.
* **Production Services (id 8)**: If any additional production services are required, list them here.
* **Project Status (id 9)**: Choose the correct status of the project from the given options: **Active** or **Inactive**.
* **Additional Info (id 10)**: Provide any extra details that don't fit into other fields.
* **Project Files (id 11)**: Indicate if the project files are available or not.
* **Note**: This form is meant to be used for one-time or occasional updates. For regular project status monitoring, please refer to other established processes.
