# Piano Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information for the Piano Festival. This form is used to gather essential details from individuals who plan to attend the festival, ensuring a smooth and organized experience for all attendees. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure your accurate information is received.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Participant Name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Participant Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Participant Phone** field.
4. Select your date of birth in the **Participant Date of Birth** field.
5. Enter your address in the **Participant Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. Please provide your full name, including your first and last names.
* **Participant Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you regularly use. This will help us communicate with you effectively.
* **Participant Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter a phone number that you can be contacted on. Please use a format like "+123 456 7890".
* **Participant Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Select your date of birth in the correct format, like "MM/DD/YYYY" (month/day/year). This helps us verify your age eligibility for some festival activities.
* **Participant Address** (text, required/optional): Enter your home or residence address. This helps us in case we need to contact you or send festival materials to you.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth experience for all attendees.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, feel free to contact us through your provided email or phone number.
* Review this form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have entered all required fields.
* If you have any issues while filling this form, reach out to us for assistance.
