# Dance Festival Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals and their parents/guardians for a dance festival event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with your personal and relationship information.
2. Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address. Ensure it is a valid email format (e.g., `example@email.com`).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, please enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Relationship** (`parent_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, please describe your relationship with the parent or guardian (e.g., child, son, daughter).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Ensure that each field is filled out by the correct person (you or your parent/guardian).
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
