# Create Your Own Game Show - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Create Your Own Game Show" form is designed to help you design a customized game show concept. Fill out the form by providing details about your game show, and it will be used to create a unique and engaging experience for your audience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in a sequential manner, starting from "Game Show Concepts" and going down to "Game Show Duration".
- Make sure to provide clear and concise answers for each field.
- You can select multiple options for fields that allow multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Show Concepts** (`game_show_prophets`, Text, Optional): This field allows you to brainstorm and list your initial ideas about the game show. You can type down any concept, theme, or idea that comes to your mind.
* **Game Show Concept** (`game_show_concept`, Text, Optional): Here, you can elaborate on your game show concept, describing its core elements and what makes it unique.
* **Audience** (`target_audience`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of audience you want to target with your game show. This will help us understand who your game show is for.
* **Prizes** (`prizes`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether your game show will have prizes or not.
* **Game Show Scores** (`game_show_scores`, Select Multiple, Optional): Determine whether your game show will have scores or not.
* **Game Show Rules** (`game_show_rules`, Text, Optional): Provide any specific rules or regulations that will govern your game show.
* **Game Show Duration** (`game_show_duration`, Text, Optional): Specify the duration of your game show, including the number of episodes and the frequency of the show.

## Tips
- Be creative and have fun while filling out the form!
- If you're stuck or need help, you can always come back and review your answers.
- Remember, the more details you provide, the better we can tailor your game show to your needs.
