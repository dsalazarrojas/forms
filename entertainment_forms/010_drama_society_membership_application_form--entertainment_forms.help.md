# drama_society_membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The drama society membership application form is designed to collect personal and society information from potential members. This form helps the drama society to understand the applicant's interests, skills, and background, ensuring a proper fit for the applicant and the society.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Provide your Email Address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone field.
4. Select your Interest(s) from the options provided (Acting, Singing, or Dancing).
5. Enter your Membership Fees in the text field.
6. Select the type of Drama Society you are interested in (Community Theater, Professional Theater, or School Drama Club).
7. (Optional) Fill in the Message field with any additional information you think would be useful for the drama society.
8. (Optional) Provide any Additional Info about the drama society you're applying to.
9. (Optional) Enter the Drama Society Name, Description, Address, Contact, Website, and other social media links if available.
10. Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (id: 1, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (id: 3, Email, Required): Enter your email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (id: 4, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Interests** (id: 5, Select One, Required): Select up to one interest from the provided options (Acting, Singing, or Dancing).
* **Membership Fees** (id: 6, Number, Required): Enter the membership fees you are willing to pay.
* **Drama Society** (id: 7, Select One, Required): Select the type of drama society you are interested in (Community Theater, Professional Theater, or School Drama Club).
* **Message** (id: 8, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information you think would be useful for the drama society.
* **Drama Society Type** (id: 9, Select One, Required): Select the type of drama society you are interested in (Community Theater, Professional Theater, or School Drama Club).
* **Additional Info** (id: 10, Note, Optional): Provide any extra details about the drama society you're applying to.
* **Drama Society Name** (id: 11, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the drama society you're applying to, if not already provided.
* **Drama Society Description** (id: 12, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the drama society you're applying to.
* **Drama Society Address** (id: 13, Text, Optional): Enter the address of the drama society.
* **Drama Society Contact** (id: 14, Text, Optional): Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
* **Drama Society Website** (id: 15, Text, Optional): Enter the website of the drama society.
* **Drama Society Facebook** (id: 16, Text, Optional): Enter the Facebook link of the drama society.
* **Drama Society Twitter** (id: 17, Text, Optional): Enter the Twitter link of the drama society.
* **Drama Society Instagram** (id: 18, Text, Optional): Enter the Instagram link of the drama society.
* **Drama Society YouTube** (id: 19, Text, Optional): Enter the YouTube link of the drama society.
* **Drama Society LinkedIn** (id: 20, Text, Optional): Enter the LinkedIn link of the drama society.
* **Drama Society Website URL** (id: 21, Text, Optional): Enter the URL of the drama society's website.
* **Drama Society Image** (id: 22, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the drama society's image.
* **Drama Society Image URL** (id: 23, Text, Optional): Enter the URL of the drama society's image.
* **Drama Society Image Alt** (id: 24, Text, Optional): Enter the alt text for the drama society's image.
* **Drama Society Image Title** (id: 25, Text, Optional): Enter a title for the drama society's image.
