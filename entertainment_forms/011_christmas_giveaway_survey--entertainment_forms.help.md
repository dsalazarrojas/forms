# Christmas Giveaway Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information on your preferences and behavior related to giveaways. Your participation will help us understand what types of prizes and entry methods are most appealing to you, as well as how often you'd like to see giveaways run. Your input will be valuable in helping us improve our giveaway process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Full Name**: Enter your full name, if you'd like to share it.
2. **Email Address**: Provide your email address so we can notify you about survey results and future giveaways (if applicable).
3. **Age Group**: Select your age range from the provided options.
4. **Location**: Enter the city or region you're from.
5. **Have You Participated Before**: Choose how many times you've participated in our giveaways.
6. **Prize Interest Level**: Rate your level of interest in prizes.
7. **Preferred Prizes**: Select the types of prizes you're interested in.
8. **Preferred Prize Value**: Choose the prize value range that appeals to you.
9. **Preferred Entry Method**: Select the method you prefer for entering giveaways.
10. **Ideal Giveaway Frequency**: Tell us how often you'd like to see us run giveaways.
11. **What Motivates You to Enter**: Select all that apply from the list of motivations.
12. **Barriers to Entry**: Choose any barriers that stop you from entering giveaways.
13. **What Makes You Trust a Giveaway**: Select all that apply from the list of trust factors.
14. **Winner Notification Preference**: Choose how you'd like winners to be notified.
15. **Public Winner Announcement**: Decide if you'd like winners to be announced publicly.
16. **How Can We Improve**: Share any ideas you have for improving our giveaways.
17. **Favorite Giveaway Experience**: Share a positive experience you've had with our giveaways.
18. **Would You Recommend Us**: Tell us if you'd recommend our giveaways to others.
19. **Would You Recommend Us** 2: Select whether you'd recommend our giveaways to others.
