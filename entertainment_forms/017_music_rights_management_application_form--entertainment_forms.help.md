# Music Rights Management Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information for music rights management. It includes fields for artist and music work details, ownership status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your artist name and any other relevant information about your music work.
2.  Select the ownership status of your music work.
3.  Choose the rights organization that will be handling your music rights.
4.  Enter the application fee for your music work.
5.  Indicate whether you agree to the contract terms.
6.  Select whether you have music rights for your work.
7.  Enter your contact information, including your email address.
8.  Provide any additional information about your artist submission.
9.  Enter any submission notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Artist Info** (`artist_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your artist name and any other relevant information about your music work.
  This field allows you to provide more details about your music work, such as the genre, album, or release date.

- **Music Work** (`music_work`, `text`, required: false): Enter a descriptive title for your music work.
  This field helps to accurately identify your music work for the rights management process.

- **Ownership Status** (`ownership_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the ownership status of your music work.
  Options: Active, Inactive

  *   Active: The music work is actively managed by the rights organization.
  *   Inactive: The music work is not actively managed by the rights organization.

- **Rights Organization** (`rights_organization`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the rights organization that will be handling your music rights.
  Options: Yes, No

  *   Yes: You have chosen to have the rights organization handle your music rights.
  *   No: You will not be using the rights organization for your music rights management.

- **Application Fee** (`application_fee`, `number`, required: false): Enter the application fee for your music work.
  This field is used to calculate the total cost of the rights management process.

- **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you agree to the contract terms.
  Options: Yes, No

  *   Yes: You agree to the contract terms.
  *   No: You do not agree to the contract terms.

- **Music Rights** (`music_rights`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you have music rights for your work.
  Options: Yes, No

  *   Yes: You have music rights for your work.
  *   No: You do not have music rights for your work.

- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email address.
  This field helps to ensure that you can be contacted and updated throughout the rights management process.

- **Artist Submission** (`artist_submission`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about your artist submission.
  This field is for you to provide any additional context or details about your music work.

- **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any submission notes for your music work.
  This field is for you to provide any additional comments or questions you have about the rights management process.
