# Documentary Sound Mixing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to gather information from artists and production teams for sound mixing requests related to documentary projects. It will help us understand the project's requirements, timeline, and budget constraints to provide the best possible sound mixing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your documentary project in the "Sound Mixing Request" field.
2. Provide the name and email of the artist or production team member responsible for the project.
3. Select the sound mixing services you need from the "Sound Mixing Service" dropdown menu.
4. Choose a mix deadline from the calendar.
5. Enter the budget for the mix in the "Mix Budget" field.
6. Select the type of mix you require from the "Mix Type" dropdown menu.
7. Describe your project's mix requirements in the "Mix Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sound Mixing Request**: Enter a brief title for your documentary project.
  This field will be used as a reference for your project.
- **Artist**: Enter the name of the artist or production team member responsible for the project.
  This field is required for communication and project tracking purposes.
- **Artist Email**: Enter the email address of the artist or production team member responsible for the project.
  This field is required for communication and project tracking purposes.
- **Sound Mixing Service**: Select the sound mixing services you need from the dropdown menu.
  This field will help us understand the services required for your project.
- **Mix Deadline**: Choose a deadline for the mix completion.
  This field is used to set expectations and schedule the mix delivery.
- **Mix Budget**: Enter the budget allocated for the mix.
  This field is used to estimate the cost of the mix services.
- **Mix Type**: Select the type of mix you require from the dropdown menu.
  This field will help us understand the mixing style and requirements for your project.
- **Mix Description**: Provide a description of the mix requirements for your project.
  This field is used to gather further details about the project's sound mixing needs. 

## Tips
- Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us provide the best possible sound mixing services.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
