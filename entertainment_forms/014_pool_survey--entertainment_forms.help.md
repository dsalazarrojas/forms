# Pool Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pool Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences and opinions on different aspects of a pool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
3. Complete all required fields.
4. You can skip fields that are not required unless you're answering "Don't know" or "No answer available".
5. Click "Submit" to send the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your purpose here?** (Text field) - This question is optional and asks you to briefly explain the purpose of your visit or why you visited the pool. Please provide a short and clear answer.
* **Pool condition** (Select one: very good, good, average, poor) - This question asks you to rate how you think the pool condition is. Please choose one of the available options that best describes your experience.
* **Pool cleanliness** (Select multiple: very clean, clean, average, poor) - This question asks you to rate how clean you think the pool area is. Please select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **How satisfied are you?** (Number) - This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with your experience at the pool. Please enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Water quality** (Select one: excellent, very good, good, average, poor) - This question is optional and asks you to rate how good you think the water quality is. If you haven't noticed anything unusual, you can answer "Don't know" or "No answer available".
* **Pool atmosphere** (Select multiple: very relaxing, relaxing, average, poor) - This question asks you to rate how relaxing or pleasant you find the pool atmosphere. Please select all the options that apply to your experience.

## Tips
* Be honest and clear in your answers.
* If you're unsure or don't know the answer, please answer "Don't know" or "No answer available".
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them in the optional "What is your purpose here?" field.
