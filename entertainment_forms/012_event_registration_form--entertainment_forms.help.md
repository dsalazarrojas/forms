# Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Registration Form is a tool used to register attendees for events. It captures essential information such as contact details, event preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to allow us to communicate with you regarding the event.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for further contact.
4. Enter your **Address** for event logistics and coordination purposes.
5. Select the correct **Event Date** for the event you're registering for.
6. Choose the correct **Event Time** for the event.
7. Indicate whether you'll be attending from a **Destination Map** (yes/no).
8. Select your **Attending** preference for the event (yes/no).
9. Confirm your **Attendance** preference for the event (yes/no).
10. Add any **Note** or comment about your attendance if necessary.
11. Enter more information about the event in the **Event Details** field.
12. Add any additional **Comments** about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name here.
- **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address here.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
- **Address** (`address`, text, Optional): Enter your address for event logistics.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, Optional): Select the date of the event.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, Optional): Choose the time of the event.
- **Destination Map** (`destination_map`, select_one, Optional): Indicate if you'll be attending from a destination map (yes/no).
- **Attending** (`attending`, select_multiple, Optional): Select your attending preference for the event (yes/no).
- **Confirm Attendance** (`confirm_attendance`, select_multiple, Optional): Confirm your attending preference for the event (yes/no).
- **Note** (`note`, text, Optional): Add any note or comment about your attendance.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, Optional): Enter more information about the event.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): Add any additional comments about your registration.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, Optional): Enter more information about the event.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): Add any additional comments about your registration. 

Note: The fields are labeled as Optional, but they are all required for the form to be successfully submitted. The form will not allow submission without filling out all fields.
