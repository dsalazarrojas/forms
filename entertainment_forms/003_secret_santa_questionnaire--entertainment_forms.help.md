# Secret Santa Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about participants' preferences for a Secret Santa gift exchange.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, enter your name in the "What is your name" field.
2. In the "To whom would you like to give a gift" field, select one or more people you would like to give a gift to.
3. In the "What gift would you like to give" field, indicate whether you would like to give a gift or not.
4. If you have a budget in mind, enter it in the "Budget for the gift" field. Note that this field is optional.
5. Finally, enter a message to include with your gift in the "Message to include with the gift" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name (text)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the Secret Santa directory.
* **To whom would you like to give a gift (select_multiple)**: Choose one or more people you would like to give a gift to. You can select multiple options if you would like to give gifts to multiple people.
* **What gift would you like to give (select_one)**: Indicate whether you would like to give a gift or not. If you choose to give a gift, you will be able to specify the type of gift in the next field.
* **Budget for the gift (number)**: If you have a budget in mind for the gift, enter it in this field. This field is optional.
* **Message to include with the gift (text)**: Enter a message to include with your gift. This can be a personal message or a joke to make the gift more special.
