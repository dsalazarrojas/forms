# Talk Show Guest Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talk Show Guest Questionnaire is a form designed to help producers of a talk show gather information about potential guests. This information will be used to determine suitability and schedule guests accordingly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
2. Select the areas of expertise you are comfortable discussing on the talk show using the "Topics" field.
3. Choose a day of the week that you are available to appear on the show using the "Availability" field.
4. Confirm your willingness to participate in the show by selecting "I consent to participate" in the "Participation Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Guest Details (Page 1)
* **Title (guest_details)**: Please provide a brief title or name that will be used to introduce you on the show.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number so that we can get in touch with you regarding your participation in the show.

### Topics (Page 1)
* **Topics (topics)**: Select one or multiple topics you are comfortable discussing on the show. This will help us determine the relevance of your expertise to our show.

### Availability (Page 2)
* **Availability (availability)**: Choose a day of the week that you are available to appear on the show. Please be specific and select only one day.

### Participation Consent (Page 2)
* **Participation Consent (participation_consent)**: Please confirm your willingness to participate in the show by selecting "I consent to participate". If you do not consent, we will not consider you for the show.

### Contact Details (Page 2)
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number so that we can get in touch with you regarding your participation in the show.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to increase your chances of being considered for the talk show.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We will review your application carefully and will be in touch with you soon if you are selected for the show.
