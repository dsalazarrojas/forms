# Gala Model Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gala Model Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in being part of the Gala Model, a platform that promotes and showcases local talent. This form helps us get to know you better and understand your qualifications, preferences, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Contact Info** (contact_info)
* A space to provide your contact details, such as name, address, and any other relevant information for us to get in touch with you.

### **Model Details** (model_details)
* A brief description of your modeling experience, qualifications, and any notable performances or achievements.

### **Photos** (photos)
* A selection of photos that showcase your modeling personality, skills, or experience.

### **Experience** (experience)
* A list of your modeling experience, including any relevant training, workshops, or performances.

### **Availability** (availability)
* Your availability for modeling gigs, events, or shoots, including dates and times.

### **Contact Preferences** (contact_preferences)
* A space to provide your preferences for contact methods, such as phone or email.

### **Phone** (contact_preferences_phone)
* Your preferred phone number for us to contact you.

### **Email** (contact_preferences_email)
* Your preferred email address for us to contact you.

### **Bio** (bio)
* A brief personal description that helps us understand your personality and style.
