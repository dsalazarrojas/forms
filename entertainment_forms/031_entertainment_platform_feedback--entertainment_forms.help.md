# Entertainment Platform Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on your experience with our entertainment platform. Your input will help us improve our content and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields that apply to your experience. You can skip any fields that don't apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Satisfaction** (`user_satisfaction`, `number`, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our platform, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Videos Watched** (`watched_videos`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose all videos you watched on our platform during your experience.
* **Watched Videos List** (`watched_videos_list`, `text`, not required): List the titles or IDs of the videos you watched, separated by commas or semicolons. For example, "Video 1, Video 2, Video 3".
* **Videos Watched Count** (`watched_videos_count`, `number`, not required): Provide the number of videos you watched on our platform during your experience.
* **Average Video Length** (`watched_videos_average_time`, `number`, not required): Estimate the average length of the videos you watched, in minutes.
* **User Satisfaction Suggestions** (`user_satisfaction_suggestions`, `text`, not required): Provide any suggestions you have for how we can improve user satisfaction on our platform.
* **Satisfaction Score** (`user_satisfaction_score`, `number`, not required): Rate your satisfaction score, on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 10 being very satisfied.
* **User Satisfaction Comment** (`user_satisfaction_comment`, `text`, not required): Provide a brief comment on why you felt satisfied or dissatisfied with our platform.
* **Videos Watched Rating** (`watched_videos_rating`, `select_one`, not required): Choose how satisfied you are with the videos you watched, on a scale of Good to Fair.
* **Average Video Rating** (`watched_videos_average_rating`, `number`, not required): Provide your estimated average rating for the videos you watched, out of 5.
