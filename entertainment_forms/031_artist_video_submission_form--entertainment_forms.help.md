# Artist Video Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Video Submission Form is designed for artists and bands to submit their videos to the platform for review and potential feature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the artist's name, contact information, and music genre.
2. Provide a brief artist biography.
3. Enter the video title, description, and upload a file or provide a URL.
4. Specify the video duration, production date, and type.
5. Choose the type of video, audio quality, and video quality.
6. Select the music rights status and whether you have featured or guest artists.
7. Enter production credits and submission purpose.
8. Agree to usage rights for review and promotion.
9. If applicable, indicate if this is a new submission or a repeat.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Artist or Band Name (<label>Artist or Band Name</label>)
• *Artist or Band Name (required):* Enter the name of the artist or band submitting the video.

### Primary Contact Name (<label>Primary Contact Name</label>)
• *Primary Contact Name (required):* Enter the name of the primary contact person for the artist or band.

### Contact Email Address (<label>Contact Email Address</label>)
• *Contact Email Address (required):* Enter the email address of the primary contact person.

### Contact Phone Number (<label>Contact Phone Number</label>)
• *Contact Phone Number (required):* Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.

### Music Genre (<label>MUSIC Genre</label>)
• *Music Genre (required):* Select the genre of music the artist or band performs.

### Artist Biography (<label>Artist Biography</label>)
• *Artist Biography (50-150 words, required):* Provide a brief biography of the artist or band, including their history, style, and any notable achievements.

### Years Active in Industry (<label>Years Active in Industry</label>)
• *Years Active in Industry (optional):* Enter the number of years the artist or band has been active in the industry.

### Video Title (<label>Video Title</label>)
• *Video Title (required):* Enter a title for the video being submitted.

### Video Description (<label>Video Description</label>)
• *Video Description (required):* Provide a brief description of the video, including what it's about.

### Video File or URL Upload (<label>Video File or URL Upload</label>)
• *Video File or URL Upload (required):* Upload a video file or provide a URL to a publicly accessible video.

### Video Duration - Minutes (<label>Video Duration - Minutes</label>)
• *Video Duration - Minutes (required):* Enter the length of the video in minutes.

### Production or Release Date (<label>Production or Release Date</label>)
• *Production or Release Date (optional):* Enter the date the video was produced or released.

### Type of Video (<label>Type of Video</label>)
• *Type of Video (required):* Select the type of video being submitted, such as Performance, Music Video, etc.

### Audio Quality (<label>AUDIO Quality</label>)
• *AUDIO Quality (optional):* Select the audio quality of the video, such as Professional, High Quality, etc.

### Video Quality (<label>VIDEO Quality</label>)
• *VIDEO Quality (optional):* Select the video quality of the video, such as 4K, 1080p, etc.

### Music Rights Status (<label>MUSIC Rights Status</label>)
• *MUSIC Rights Status (required):* Select the music rights status, such as Original, Licensed, etc.

### Featured or Guest Artists (<label>Featured or Guest Artists</label>)
• *Featured or Guest Artists (optional):* List any featured or guest artists in the video.

### Production Credits - Director, Producer, etc. (<label>Production Credits - Director, Producer, etc.</label>)
• *Production Credits - Director, Producer, etc. (optional):* List the production team and credits.

### Submission Purpose (<label>Submission Purpose</label>)
• *Submission Purpose (required):* Select the purpose of the submission, such as Competition, Festival Consideration, etc.

### Agree to Usage Rights for Review and Promotion (<label>Agree to Usage Rights for Review and Promotion</label>)
• *Agree to Usage Rights for Review and Promotion (required):* Confirm that you agree to the usage rights for review and promotion.

### Previous Submissions to This Platform (<label>Previous Submissions to This Platform</label>)
• *Previous Submissions to This Platform (optional):* Indicate if this is a repeat submission to the platform.
