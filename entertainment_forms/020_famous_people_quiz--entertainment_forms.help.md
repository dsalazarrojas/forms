# Famous People Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge about famous people. It's not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the text field on the first page.
2. If you want to continue, you can skip the form or proceed to the next question on the second page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your name in this text field to identify yourself.
* **Birth Date**: If you want to share your birth date, enter it in the date field. If you don't want to share, you can skip this field.
* **Famous People Quiz Question 1**: Answer this question in the text field. You can type your answer and submit it.

## Tips
* You can skip the form if you don't want to share any information.
