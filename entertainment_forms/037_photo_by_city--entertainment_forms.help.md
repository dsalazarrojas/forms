# photo_by_city - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about photos taken by users in different cities, allowing them to share their experiences and connect with other users who have also visited the same places.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form on one or more pages.
2. Each page has several fields where you can enter information about your photo.
3. Please make sure to complete all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First page (first_page)**: Enter the title of this page to provide a brief description of your photo.
* **City Name (city_name)**: Enter the name of the city where the photo was taken.
* **Location (location)**: Check the box if you want to indicate that the photo was taken at a location that is a tourist attraction or landmark.
* **Photo Taken Date (photo_taken_date)**: Enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Photo Taken Time (photo_taken_time)**: Enter the time the photo was taken.
* **Photo Location (photo_location)**: Enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Tourist Fact (tourist_fact)**: Enter any interesting or notable facts about the location.
* **Friend Name (friend_name)**: If you took the photo with a friend, enter their name.
* **Friend Email (friend_email)**: If you want to share the photo with your friend, enter their email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and honest information about your photo.
* If you have multiple photos from the same city, you can fill out the form multiple times to share all your photos.
* If you are not sure about a field, you can ask for help or clarification from the form administrators.
