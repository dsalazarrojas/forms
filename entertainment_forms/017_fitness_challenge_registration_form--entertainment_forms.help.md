# Fitness Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Challenge Registration Form is designed for individuals to register for a fitness challenge event. This form captures the necessary information for participants to be registered for the challenge. Please ensure you only submit the form once for each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the form title and description in the `Form Title` field.
2. Enter your name and age in the `Name` and `Age` fields, respectively.
3. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Read and agree to the `Challenge Terms` and `Challenge Rules` by typing "I understand and agree" in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your registration.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Challenge Terms** (`challenge_terms`, text, required): Type "I understand and agree" to confirm you have read the challenge terms.
* **Challenge Rules** (`challenge_rules`, text, required): Type "I understand and agree" to confirm you have read the challenge rules.
 
## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all the required fields to avoid form submission issues.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
