**Escape Room And Party Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and liability agreements from individuals participating in escape room and party events. It helps us ensure that all attendees are aware of the rules and risks associated with these activities and can provide a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Information** page with your details.
2. Provide your **Party Details**.
3. Enter your **Contact Details**.
4. Read and agree to the **Waiver Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please fill out this page with your name, email, and phone number to help us get in touch with you if needed.
* **Party Details** (`party_details`, text, optional): Describe the party you are attending, including the number of people and any specific needs or requirements.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, text, optional): This field is not used in this version of the form.
* **Waiver Terms and Conditions** (`waiver_terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Please select 'True' to indicate that you have read and agree to the waiver terms and conditions. If you are not participating in the waiver, please select 'Not Applicable'.
