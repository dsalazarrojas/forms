# Theater Festival Form Braintree - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees of the Theater Festival Braintree.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the required fields.
2. Enter your Email Address to allow us to contact you with festival updates and news.
3. Provide your Phone number, which will be used for festival communication.
4. Select one of the three festival options that you plan to attend.
5. If you wish, select one or multiple options from the Select Multiple field.
6. Choose the Date and Time of your visit to the festival.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for festival updates and news.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for festival communication.
* **Which festival** (`select_one_option`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the three festival options to indicate which one you plan to attend.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or multiple options from the list to indicate your interest in attending multiple festivals.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to visit the festival in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you plan to visit the festival in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about your visit.
