# Anime Character Quiz Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anime Character Quiz Template is a form designed to help you explore the characteristics of anime characters. It asks for basic participant information and three questions to determine the preferred traits of the character being analyzed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name or nickname in the "Participant name or nickname" field. This is optional.
2. Provide an email address if you want to receive results (if provided, results will be sent to this email).
3. Answer the three questions: 
  * Which trait best describes the character: select one from the options (Brave, Cunning, Kind, Sarcastic, Ambitious) 
  * What role the character usually plays in the story: select one from the options (Protagonist, Antagonist, Supporting, Comic relief) 
  * Character alignment: select one from the options (Heroic, Neutral, Villainous) 
4. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Any additional notes" field if you want to.
5. Set a date when you complete the quiz by selecting a date from the calendar.
6. Opt-in to receive your score by selecting "True" in the final option (if not opted in, you can only see your score by accessing the form again after it has been submitted).

  * Required: False
