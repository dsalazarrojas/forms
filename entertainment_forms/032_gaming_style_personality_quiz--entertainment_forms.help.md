# PRAGaming Style Personality Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a short, user-friendly way to discover your gaming personality. Answer the questions, and get a glimpse into your preferences for different types of games, playing habits, and emotional experiences while gaming.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply:
1. Choose the types of games you enjoy playing from the first dropdown list.
2. Select how often you typically play games from the second dropdown list.
3. Pick the genres you like best from the third dropdown list.
4. Choose your preferred playtime (how long you like to play) from the fourth dropdown list.
5. Select the type of player you are (solo, co-op, or multi-player) from the fifth dropdown list.
6. Choose the emotions you typically experience while playing from the sixth dropdown list.
7. Click 'Submit' to view your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of games do you enjoy?** (Select One)
	+ Action: You prefer fast-paced games that require quick reflexes and fast decision-making.
	+ Adventure: You enjoy exploring new worlds, solving puzzles, and completing quests.
	+ Role-playing: You like creating and customizing characters, making choices that impact the story.
	+ Simulation: You enjoy realistic simulations of real-world activities, like running a city or managing a farm.
	+ Strategy: You like planning and executing long-term plans, often with a competitive edge.
* **How often do you play games?** (Select One)
	+ Daily: You play games every day, either for short or long sessions.
	+ A few times a month: You play games regularly, but not daily.
	+ Rarely: You don't play games very often.
* **Which genre do you like best?** (Select Multiple)
	+ Fantasy: You enjoy games set in fictional worlds with magical or magical elements.
	+ Sci-fi: You like exploring futuristic or sci-fi worlds, often with futuristic technologies.
	+ Sports: You enjoy games focused on physical activities, like sports or racing.
	+ Simulation: You enjoy games that simulate real-world activities, like running a city or managing a farm.
* **How long do you prefer to play?** (Select One)
	+ Short: You prefer playing for a few minutes or hours.
	+ Medium: You like playing for a few hours, but not all day.
	+ Long: You enjoy playing for extended periods, sometimes for many hours.
* **What type of player are you?** (Select One)
	+ Solo: You play games alone, enjoying the solo experience.
	+ Multiplayer: You play with others, enjoying the social aspect.
	+ Co-op: You play with others, working together towards a common goal.
* **How do you feel when playing?** (Select Multiple)
	+ Excited: You feel energized and engaged while playing.
	+ Relaxed: You enjoy a calm and peaceful experience while playing.
	+ Frustrated: You feel annoyed or irritated when you make mistakes or lose.
	+ Anxious: You feel apprehensive or worried about making mistakes.
	+ Bored: You feel uninterested or uninspired by the game.
