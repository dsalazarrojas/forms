# video_submit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects metadata about videos, including title, description, categories, tags, video URL, and thumbnail URL, for our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the video.
2. Provide a brief description of the video.
3. Select one or more categories for the video from the list of options (Action, Comedy, Drama).
4. Add any relevant tags for the video (funny, interesting, cool).
5. Enter the URL of the video.
6. (Optional) Enter the URL of the thumbnail image.
