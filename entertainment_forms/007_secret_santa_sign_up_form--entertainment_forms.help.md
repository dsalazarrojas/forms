# Secret Santa Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow participants to sign up for a Secret Santa gift exchange. The purpose of this form is to collect information about each participant, including their name, contact details, and gift preferences, which will be used to match participants with a gift-giver within their desired budget.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Email** field. This will be used to contact you about your gift-giver and other Secret Santa details.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Select your gift preferences from the **Gift Preferences** field. You can choose from "I prefer a book", "I prefer a game", "I prefer a movie night", or "None" if you don't have any preferences.
5. Set a limit for your gift within the **Limit** field. You can choose from "$10", "$20", or "$30" as a budget limit, or select "No limit" if you don't have a budget constraint.
6. In the **Submitter Name** field, enter the name of the person submitting on your behalf.
7. In the **Submitter Phone** field, enter the phone number of the person submitting on your behalf.
8. In the **Submitter Email** field, enter the email address of the person submitting on your behalf.
9. In the **Submitter Note** field, add any additional comments or notes for your gift-giver.
10. If you have any additional details for your submitter, such as date and time, you can provide them in the **Submitter Date** and **Submitter Time** fields.
11. Select the location where your gift-giver will submit the gift in the **Submitter Location** field. You can choose from "Office", "Home", or "Other".
12. If you have a preferred tool or device for the gift-giver to use, you can select it in the **Submitter Tool** field.
13. Finally, add any additional comments or notes for your gift-giver in the **Submitter Comments** field.
