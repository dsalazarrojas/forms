# Children Theater Audition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's basic information, audition preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by providing the child's basic information, including their first name, last name, date of birth, and age.
2. Next, provide your contact details as a parent or guardian, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. If you have any previous theater experience or special skills, list them in the corresponding fields.
4. Select a preferred audition date and time that suits you and your child.
5. Indicate how you heard about this audition and whether you consent to your child participating in the audition process.
