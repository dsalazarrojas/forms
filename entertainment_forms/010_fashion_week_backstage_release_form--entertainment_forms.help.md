# Fashion Week Backstage Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Week Backstage Release Form is a document used to obtain the necessary information from participants to grant permission for them to take part in backstage activities during Fashion Week. This form helps to ensure that all participants are aware of the rules and guidelines for backstage access and have provided their contact information for communication and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and guidelines.
2. Complete the form by providing your details and consent to participate in backstage activities.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Backstage Consent** (`backstage_consent`, note, optional): Check the box to indicate your consent to participate in backstage activities. If you do not wish to participate, please leave this field blank.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your role and involvement in Fashion Week. This can include your job title, company, or any other relevant information.
* **Particpant Name** (`particpant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Particpant Email** (`particpant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Particpant Phone** (`particpant_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for communication and follow-up purposes.
