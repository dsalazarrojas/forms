# Hotel Guest Preference Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from guests about their preferences and requirements for their stay at the hotel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide as much information as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (those marked as "required" in the form).
3. For fields with multiple options, select all applicable options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Booking Details** (`booking_details`): This field is where you can enter any additional information about your booking.
- **Guest Dietary Restrictions** (`guest_dietary_restrictions`): Please select all dietary restrictions that apply to you (e.g. "Yes" for vegetarian, "No" for gluten-free).
- **Room Requirements** (`room_requirements`): Select the room type that best suits your needs (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`): If you have any special requests or requirements, enter them in this field.
- **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`): Select all dietary restrictions that apply to you (e.g. "Yes" for vegetarian, "No" for gluten-free).
- **Guest Preferences** (`guest_preferences`): Enter any additional comments or preferences you have about your stay.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information about your stay.
- **Arrival and Departure Dates** (`arrival_and_departure_dates`): Enter your arrival and departure dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`): Enter your estimated arrival time.
- **Arrival Time Note** (`arrival_time_note`): If you have any notes about your arrival time, enter them in this field.
- **Departure Time** (`departure_time`): Enter your estimated departure time.
- **Guest Type** (`guest_type`): Select your type (e.g. Option 1, Option 2).
- **Special Needs** (`special_needs`): Enter any special needs or requirements you have (e.g. wheelchair accessibility).
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
- **Additional Services** (`additional_services`): Select all additional services you would like to have during your stay (e.g. "Yes" for room service).
- **Special Request Comments** (`special_request_comments`): Enter any additional comments about your special requests.
- **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`): Enter your arrival time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Departure Time** (`departure_time`): Enter your departure time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Guest Type** (`guest_type`): Select your type (e.g. Option 1, Option 2).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you have any special requests or needs, be sure to indicate them in the corresponding fields.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask hotel staff for assistance.
