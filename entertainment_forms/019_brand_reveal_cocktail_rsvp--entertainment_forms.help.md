# Brand Reveal Cocktail RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Reveal Cocktail RSVP form is designed for attendees to provide essential information about themselves for event organization, communication, and networking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Provide your **Email Address** for event confirmation. This is also a required field.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** for day-of contact. This is a required field.
4. If you are bringing a guest, provide their **Name** in the "Plus One Name" field.
5. Confirm your **Attendance** by selecting "Yes, Excited to Attend," "No, Cannot Make It," or "Maybe, Will Confirm Soon."
6. Enter the **Event Date** of the cocktail reveal.
7. Choose your preferred **Signature Cocktail** from the available options.
8. Select your **Dietary Requirements**, such as "None," "Vegetarian," "Vegan," "Gluten-Free," "Dairy-Free," "Nut Allergy," "Shellfish Allergy," "Kosher," or "Halal."
9. Confirm you will adhere to the **Dress Code** by selecting "I Will Dress Accordingly" or "I Have Questions About Dress Code."
10. Choose your **Transportation Plan**, such as "Driving Myself," "Ride Share or Taxi," "Public Transportation," "Walking," or "Carpooling."
11. Indicate if you **Need Parking** information by selecting "True" or "False."
12. Select how you are **Connected to the Brand**, such as "Customer," "Business Partner," "Media," "Industry Professional," "Friend of Founder," or "Other."
13. (Optional) Enter your **Social Media Handle**, such as your Instagram or Twitter handle.
14. (Optional) Provide any **Song Requests** you may have.
15. If this event is part of a **Special Occasion**, such as a birthday, anniversary, or promotion, select the appropriate option.
16. (Optional) Add any **Additional Notes** you'd like to share with the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Full Name** (required, text): Enter your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **2. Email Address** (required, email): Provide your email address for event confirmation and communication.
* **3. Phone Number** (required, text): Enter your phone number for day-of contact.
* **4. Plus One Name** (optional, text): If you're bringing a guest, enter their name here.
* **5. Attendance Confirmation** (required, select_one): Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes, Excited to Attend," "No, Cannot Make It," or "Maybe, Will Confirm Soon."
* **6. Event Date** (required, date): Enter the date of the cocktail reveal event.
* **7. Event Time** (required, time): Enter the start time of the event.
* **8. Signature Cocktail Preference** (required, select_one): Choose your preferred cocktail style.
* **9. Dietary Requirements** (required, select_multiple): Select your dietary restrictions, such as "None," "Vegetarian," "Vegan," etc.
* **10. Dress Code Acknowledgment** (required, select_one): Confirm you'll adhere to the dress code by selecting "I Will Dress Accordingly" or "I Have Questions About Dress Code."
* **11. Transportation Plan** (required, select_one): Choose your transportation plan, such as "Driving Myself," "Ride Share or Taxi," etc.
* **12. Parking Needed** (required, select_one): Indicate if you need parking information by selecting "True" or "False."
* **13. Connection to Brand** (required, select_one): Select how you are connected to the brand, such as "Customer," "Business Partner," etc.
* **14. Social Media Handle** (optional, text): Enter your social media handle, such as your Instagram or Twitter handle.
* **15. Photo Consent** (required, select_one): Confirm if you consent to having your photos posted on social media by selecting "Yes, I Consent" or "No, Please Do Not Post."
* **16. Song Requests** (optional, text): Provide any song requests you may have.
* **17. Special Occasion** (required, select_one): If this event is part of a special occasion, such as a birthday or anniversary, select the appropriate option.
* **18. Additional Notes** (optional, text): Add any additional information you'd like to share with the organizers.
