# Artist Promotion Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Promotion Sponsorship Application Form is a tool used by artists and bands to submit their application for various types of sponsorships. By filling out this form, you can apply for different types of sponsorships, such as tour promotion, album release, festival appearance, equipment sponsorship, or marketing support. The form helps us to understand your project and determine whether it fits the criteria for sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your artist or band name and the primary contact name and email address.
2. Provide your contact phone number, music genre or style, and years active in the industry.
3. Choose the type of sponsorship you are requesting by selecting the corresponding option from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the project or campaign name and project details and scope.
5. Specify the campaign duration in months.
6. Choose a proposed start date for your project or campaign.
7. Enter the budget required for your project or campaign.
8. If applicable, provide a breakdown of your budget by category.
9. If you have a media kit or press kit attached, indicate that it is yes.
10. Finally, fill out any additional information you feel is relevant to your project or campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Band Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your artist or band.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact person's phone number.
* **Music Genre or Style** (`music_genre`, text, required): Enter the type of music you produce.
* **Years Active in Industry** (`years_active`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been active in the industry.
* **Type of Sponsorship Requested** (`sponsorship_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of sponsorship you are requesting. This can be Tour Promotion, Album Release, Festival Appearance, Equipment Sponsorship, Marketing Support, or Other.
* **Project or Campaign Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your project or campaign.
* **Project Details and Scope** (`project_details`, text, required): Describe the details and scope of your project or campaign.
* **Campaign Duration - Months** (`campaign_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your campaign in months.
* **Proposed Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the proposed start date for your project or campaign.
* **Budget Required** (`budget_required`, number, required): Enter the budget required for your project or campaign.
* **Budget Breakdown by Category** (`budget_breakdown`, text, optional): If you have a breakdown of your budget by category, enter it here.
* **Total Social Media Following** (`social_media_following`, number, optional): Enter the approximate combined followers on your social media platforms.
* **Target Audience Demographics** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Describe the demographics of your target audience.
* **Expected Campaign Reach** (`expected_reach`, text, optional): Estimated impressions or attendees for your campaign.
* **Benefits to Sponsor** (`sponsor_benefits`, text, required): Describe the benefits you hope to gain from this sponsorship.
* **Previous Sponsors or Partners** (`previous_sponsors`, text, optional): List any previous sponsors or partners you have had.
* **Media Kit or Press Kit Attached** (`media_kit_attached`, select_one, required): If you have a media kit or press kit attached, select yes.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your project or campaign.

## Tips

* Ensure that your form is complete and accurate before submitting.
* Please attach any required media kits or press kits as attachments.
* If you are unsure about any section, contact us for clarification.
