# Battle Of The Bands Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Battle Of The Bands Application form is designed to collect essential details from bands and artists participating in the "Battle Of The Bands" competition. This form will be used to evaluate and schedule the performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your band.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you have any specific technical requirements for performance, please list them in the "Specific stage or monitor requirements" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Official Band Name** (`band_name`, text, required): Please enter the official name of your band.
* **Primary Musical Genre** (`musical_genre`, text, required): Enter the primary musical genre of your band (e.g. Rock, Indie, Metal, Pop, Jazz).
* **Number of Band Members** (`number_of_members`, number, required): Please enter the number of members in your band.
* **Hometown / Based In** (`band_location`, text, required): Enter the city or town where your band is based.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your band.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Link to Music** (`music_sample_link`, text, required): Please provide a link to a sample of your music on Spotify, SoundCloud, or YouTube.
* **Social Media Handles** (`social_media_links`, text, optional): If applicable, provide links to your band's social media handles (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok).
* **List all equipment you will bring** (`equipment_provided_by_band`, text, required): List all the equipment you will bring for your performance (e.g. Amps, Drum kit (shells only), Pedals).
* **Specific stage or monitor requirements** (`stage_requirements`, text, optional): If you have specific technical requirements for performance, please specify them in this field.
* **We agree to the competition rules and performance schedule** (`participation_agreement`, select_one, required): Please select "I Agree" to confirm that you have read and agree to the competition rules and performance schedule.
* **Application Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you are submitting this application.
