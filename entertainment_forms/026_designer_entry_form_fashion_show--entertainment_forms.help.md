# Designer Entry Form Fashion Show - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Designer Entry Form Fashion Show is a tool for designers to submit their information and details about their work, team, and availability for a fashion show event. This form collects contact information, collection details, and other relevant data for event organizers to consider for the show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Designer Details** section with your name and contact information.
2. In the **Availability** section, provide your work dates, hours of availability, and any other relevant details for the event organizers.
3. The **Collection Details** section is where you can describe your fashion collection in a brief summary and include any additional details about your work.
4. In the **Contact Details** section, provide your address, email, and phone number for event organizers to reach out to you.
5. If you're part of a team, fill in the **Team Details** section with your team members' names and contact information.
6. In the **Organization Details** section, provide information about your work affiliation or the name of your brand.
7. Use the **Notes** and **Additional Notes** sections to add any other relevant information about your work or team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Designer Details**: Enter your name and contact information, such as your email and phone number.
- **Availability**: Enter your work dates and hours of availability, including any specific days or periods when you are unavailable.
- **Collection Details**: Describe your fashion collection in a brief summary, including any notable features or themes.
- **Contact Details**: Enter your address, email, and phone number for event organizers to reach out to you.
- **Team Details**: List each team member's name and contact information, including email and phone number.
- **Organization Details**: Enter information about your work affiliation or the name of your brand.
- **Notes**: Add any additional information about your work or team that is not covered in other sections.
- **Additional Notes**: Add any last-minute thoughts or details that you think event organizers would find helpful.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your submission is complete.
- Double-check your contact information and collection details for accuracy.
- Use clear and concise language in your responses to help event organizers understand your work and availability.
- If you're part of a team, make sure to list each team member's contact information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
