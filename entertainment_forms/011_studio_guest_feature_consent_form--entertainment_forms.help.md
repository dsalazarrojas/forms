# studio_guest_feature_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The studio_guest_feature_consent_form is used to capture consent and contact information from guests who are visiting a studio or facility. This form is likely used by studios or facilities to collect necessary information from guests for communication and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing your contact information in the "Contact Information" field. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menu to indicate your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your signature by signing above the "Signature" field.
3. Enter the date and time you signed the form in the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed" fields, respectively.
4. Enter your email signature in the "Email Signature" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
6. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
7. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
9. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
10. Provide the name of the guest in the "Name of Guest" field.
11. Enter the contact information for the guest, including their address, phone number, and email.
12. Enter the name of the host in the "Name of Host" field.
13. Enter the contact information for the host, including their address, phone number, and email.
14. Provide the signature of the host by signing above the "Signature of Host" field.
15. Enter the date and time the host signed the form in the "Date Signed Host" and "Time Signed Host" fields, respectively.
16. Enter the email signature of the host in the "Email Signature Host" field.
17. Enter the contact number of the host in the "Contact Number Host" field.
18. Enter the contact email of the host in the "Contact Email Host" field.
19. Add any additional notes or comments from the host in the "Note Host" field.
