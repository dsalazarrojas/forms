# Short Movie Contest Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Short Movie Contest Application Form is used to collect information from users for a contest related to short movies. This form is designed to gather data required for submission, and all fields must be filled in correctly to be considered eligible for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Provide a title and description of your short movie.
3. Select the correct category and genres for your movie.
4. If your movie has received any awards, select them from the provided list.
5. Add any relevant tags for your movie.
6. Upload any supporting files related to your movie.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Please enter your name as a string of characters that will be used for communication during the contest.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further information about the contest.
* **Movie Title**: Enter a title for your short movie that accurately represents its content.
* **Movie Description**: Enter a brief description of your short movie that provides context for the contest judges.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date your movie was submitted for the contest.
* **Movie Length**: Enter the length of your short movie in minutes.
* **Category**: Select the primary category for your movie (Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror).
* **Genre**: Select all applicable genres for your movie (Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror).
* **Awards**: If your movie has received an award, select it from the provided list.
* **Tags**: Enter any relevant tags for your movie that describe its content or style.
* **Upload Files**: If required, upload any additional files related to your movie that support your submission.
