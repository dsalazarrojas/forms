# Film Restoration Workroom Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a workroom for film restoration services. It collects information about the customer, the booking dates and time, the desired restoration suite, and any additional services required. The form also includes fields for customer contact information and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the customer's name from the "Customer Info" dropdown list.
2. Enter the desired booking dates in the "Booking Dates" field.
3. Choose the desired booking time from the "Booking Time" dropdown list.
4. Select the restoration suite from the "Restoration Suite" dropdown list.
5. Choose any additional services required from the "Additional Services" dropdown list.
6. Enter any additional comments or instructions in the "Notes" field (optional).
7. Select how the customer would like to be contacted from the "Contact Method" dropdown list.
8. Enter the customer's email address if they prefer to be contacted via email.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm submission.
