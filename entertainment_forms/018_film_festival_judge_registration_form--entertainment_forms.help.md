# Film Festival Judge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register individuals as film festival judges. It's a mandatory requirement for anyone interested in participating in the festival's judging process. Please ensure you complete this form accurately to help us understand your qualifications and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have a valid email address for communication purposes.
- Provide your first and last name as you'd like them to be displayed on any festival-related materials.
- Select your role as a judge, curator, programmer, or producer.
- If you have any experience as a judge, select the relevant years of experience.
- If you have specific dates you're available to judge, please select them.
- If you have a preferred time to judge, please select it.
- Provide any additional notes or comments about your qualifications or experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Festival Name
Festival Name (`festival_name`, text, **required**)
This is the name of the festival you're interested in judging.

### First Name
First Name (`first_name`, text, **required**)
Enter your first name as you'd like it to be displayed on any festival-related materials.

### Last Name
Last Name (`last_name`, text, **required**)
Enter your last name as you'd like it to be displayed on any festival-related materials.

### E-mail
E-mail (`email`, email, **required**)
Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.

### Phone
Phone (`phone`, text, **optional**)
If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.

### Role
Role (`role`, select_one, **required**)
Select your role as a judge, curator, programmer, or producer.

### Judge Experience
Judge Experience (`judge_experience`, select_multiple, **optional**)
Select your level of judging experience: 5 years, 2-10 years, 10+ years, or Less than 5 years.

### Available Dates
Available Dates (`available_dates`, date, **optional**)
Select specific dates you're available to judge.

### Preferred Time
Preferred Time (`preferred_time`, time, **optional**)
Select your preferred time to judge.

### Notes
Notes (`notes`, note, **optional**)
Provide any additional notes or comments about your qualifications or experience.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete the form accurately and truthfully to help us understand your qualifications and experience.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact us at [your email address].
- We'll review your application and be in touch with you soon.
