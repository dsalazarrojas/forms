# Movie Awards Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Movie Awards Nomination Form is a tool for submitting nominations for movies. It helps ensure that all nominations are submitted in a consistent and structured format, making it easier for the organizers to review and process the submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the movie being nominated.
2. Select the genre of the movie (e.g., Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, Romance).
3. Enter the release year of the movie.
4. Provide a reason for nominating the movie.
5. Enter a message explaining why you are nominating the movie (optional).
6. Select the category for the nomination (e.g., Drama, Horror, Movie, Music, Theater).
7. Click the "Submit" button to submit the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Movie Name
* **Movie Name** (`movie_name`, text, required): Enter the title of the movie you are nominating.

### Genre
* **Genre** (`movie_genre`, select_multiple, required): Select the genres that the movie belongs to (e.g., Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, Romance).

### Release Year
* **Release Year** (`release_year`, number, required): Enter the year the movie was released.

### Nomination Reason
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Provide a brief reason for nominating the movie.

### Nomination Message
* **Nomination Message** (`nomination_message`, text, optional): Enter a message explaining why you are nominating the movie (optional).

### Category
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category for the nomination (e.g., Drama, Horror, Movie, Music, Theater).

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit the nomination.
