# safari_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide information about their safari experiences. Your input will help us improve our services and better cater to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your input on the first field, "user_input". Rate or score your experience (1-100).
2. Share any additional comments or descriptions about your safari experience in the "safari_info" field.
3. Select your location from the "user_location" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "user_phone" field.
5. Provide your email address in the "user_email" field.
6. Select the date of your safari experience from the "user_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input** (Number, Optional): Rate or score your experience.
* **safari_info** (Text, Optional): Share any additional comments or descriptions about your safari experience.
* **user_location** (Select One, Optional): Select your location.
* **user_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **user_email** (Email, Optional): Provide your email address.
* **user_date** (Date, Optional): Select the date of your safari experience.
