# Magazine Reader Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect data from magazine readers to gather insights on their reading habits, preferences, and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form fields and ensure that you understand what each field is for.
2.  Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing your responses to the required fields.
3.  Ensure that you have not already submitted your survey response.
4.  Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Magazine Reader Survey 1** (`magazine_reader_survey_1`, text, optional): This is an optional text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the magazine.
*   **Magazine Reader Survey 2** (`magazine_reader_survey_2`, number, required): This is a required field where you need to provide a numeric value between 1 and 5, representing your level of interest in the magazine. You can choose from the following options: 1 = Not interested, 2 = Somewhat interested, 3 = Interested, 4 = Very interested, 5 = Extremely interested.
*   **Magazine Reader Survey 3** (`magazine_reader_survey_3`, email, required): This is a required field where you need to provide your email address. Please ensure that you enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
*   **Magazine Reader Survey 4** (`magazine_reader_survey_4`, select\_multiple, optional): This is an optional field where you can choose multiple options from the list. Please select all the choices that apply to you.
*   **Magazine Reader Survey 5** (`magazine_reader_survey_5`, select\_one, required): This is a required field where you need to select one option from the list. Please choose the option that best represents your preference.
*   **Magazine Reader Survey 6** (`magazine_reader_survey_6`, date, required): This is a required field where you need to enter a date in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/01/2022).
*   **Magazine Reader Survey 7** (`magazine_reader_survey_7`, time, optional): This is an optional field where you can select a time of day that you prefer to read the magazine. Please select from the following options: 8:00 am, 12:00 pm, 3:00 pm, 6:00 pm, 9:00 pm.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you complete all the required fields to submit your survey response.
*   If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
