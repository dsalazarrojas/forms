# Film Pitch Presentation Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book and track film pitch presentations between a production company and a potential client.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the required fields**, including "Film Pitch Session", "Date", and "Presenter". 
2. **Book the presentation** by selecting "Active" under "Booking Status". 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client** (`film_pitch_presentation_booking_form_1`): Enter the name of the client.
* **Production Company** (`production_company`): Enter the name of the production company.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Film Pitch Session** (`film_pitch_session`): Select the time for the film pitch presentation.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the date for the film pitch presentation.
* **Presenter** (`presenter`): Enter the name of the person presenting the film.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`): This field determines the current status of the booking. Select "Active" to mark the presentation as booked.
* **Booking Note** (`booking_note`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the booking. 

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and completely before submitting the form. 
* Double-check that the "Booking Status" is set to "Active" to mark the presentation as booked. 
* If a presentation has already been booked or is still pending, do not create a new entry for it.
