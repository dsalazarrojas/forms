# Christmas Gift List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your gift preferences for the upcoming holiday season. Completing this form will help the company provide you with a personalized gift that suits your tastes and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and details ready before filling out the form.
2. Start by filling in your personal details such as your full name, employee ID, and department.
3. Select the gift category that interests you most.
4. Specify your top three gift item choices.
5. Optionally, provide any additional information or preferences such as your preferred colors, size, or brands.
6. Indicate if you have already received a company gift this year.
7. Choose your delivery option.
8. Provide any special notes or messages if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel records.
* **Employee ID**: Your company-issued employee ID number.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the list.
* **Email Address**: Enter your company email address, preferably the one used for official communications.
* **Gift Category Preference**: Choose the category of gifts that interests you most (e.g., electronics, home, fashion, etc.).
* **First Wish Item**: Enter your top choice gift item.
* **Estimated Price Range**: Choose the approximate price range of your desired gift.
* **Second Wish Item** (optional): Enter your alternative gift choice (if you have one).
* **Third Wish Item** (optional): Enter another gift option (if you have one).
* **Color Preference**: Select your preferred colors for gifts (if applicable).
* **Size Preference**: Choose the size of the gift item (if it's a wearable item).
* **Brand Preferences**: List any specific brands you have a liking for.
* **Already Received Company Gift This Year**: Indicate if you have already received a company gift this year.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: List any dietary restrictions you may have when receiving food gifts.
* **Delivery Option**: Select how you would like to receive your gift (office delivery, home delivery, or pick up).
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address (if you have chosen home delivery).
* **Special Notes**: Provide any additional information or notes if you have any.
* **Message to Company**: Enter a message or feedback if you wish to express it.
