# Reporter And Announcer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from reporters and announcers for media or event coverage purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your details in the provided fields, following the format and instructions for each section.
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field to ensure your data is collected correctly.
- If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below for further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Reporter Details**
	* **Reporter Name**: A text field where you can enter your name as a reporter or announcer.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide your full name for identification purposes.
### 2. **Assignment Details**
	* **Assignment Details**: A text field where you can enter your assignment or coverage details.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide a brief description of your assignment or coverage.
### 3. **Assignment Start Date**
	* **Assignment Start Date**: A date field where you can select the start date of your assignment or coverage.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide the start date for better tracking and reference purposes.
### 4. **Assignment End Date**
	* **Assignment End Date**: A text field where you can enter the end date of your assignment or coverage.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide the end date for better tracking and reference.
### 5. **Event Coverage**
	* **Event Coverage**: A select_multiple field where you can choose the event coverage options provided.
	* Please select all that apply to your coverage.
### 6. **Event Name**
	* **Event Name**: A text field where you can enter the name of the event.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide the event name for better reference.
### 7. **Event Start Date**
	* **Event Start Date**: A date field where you can select the start date of the event.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide the start date for better tracking and reference.
### 8. **Event End Date**
	* **Event End Date**: A text field where you can enter the end date of the event.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide the end date for better tracking and reference.
### 9. **Event Location**
	* **Event Location**: A text field where you can enter the location of the event.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide the event location for better reference.
### 10. **Contact Info**
	* **Contact Info**: A text field where you can enter your contact information.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide your contact information for future reference.
### 11. **Consent**
	* **Consent**: A select_one field where you can choose whether you consent to the terms.
	* Please select 'True' if you agree to the terms, and 'False' if you don't.
### 12. **Assigned By**
	* **Assigned By**: A text field where you can enter the person or organization assigned to you.
	* This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide the assigned person or organization for reference.

Note: I've assumed the event coverage options are not specified in the original YAML structure, so I've included a generic description. Please review and refine the event coverage options if available.
