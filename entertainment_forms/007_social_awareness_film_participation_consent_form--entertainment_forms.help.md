# Social Awareness Film Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Awareness Film Participation Consent Form is designed for participants in a film production, specifically for a social awareness campaign or project. This form gathers information about participants, their preferences for photo and video recordings, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information (page 1: participant_info).
2. Enter your name, which will be used to identify you in the film.
3. Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you.
4. Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.
5. Enter your address, which will be used to determine your location.
6. Choose whether you want to have your photo taken during the shoot (page 6: photo_consent).
7. Choose whether you want to be on camera during the shoot (page 7: video_consent).
8. Briefly describe the purpose of the film or the story it tells (page 8: film_description).
9. Choose how you would like to be contacted (page 9: contact).
10. Enter the emergency contact person's name (page 10: emergency_contact).
11. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (page 11: emergency_relationship).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Page 1)**: This section collects basic information about you, the participant. Please fill in your name and any other required fields as instructed.
* **Participant Name (Page 2)**: Please enter your full name, as you want to be identified in the film.
* **Email (Page 3)**: This is your contact email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (Page 4)**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and emergency purposes.
* **Address (Page 5)**: This is your physical address, which will be used to determine your location.
* **Photo Consent (Page 6)**: Choose whether you want to have your photo taken during the shoot. If you choose "Yes", your photo will be captured for the film. If you choose "No", your photo will not be taken.
* **Video Consent (Page 7)**: Choose whether you want to be on camera during the shoot. If you choose "Yes", you will be recorded on camera. If you choose "No", you will not be recorded.
* **Film Description (Page 8)**: Briefly describe the purpose of the film or the story it tells. This will help us understand the context of the project.
* **Contact (Page 9)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted. You can choose from "On set", "Via phone", "Via email", or "Not at all". Please select all applicable options.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 10)**: Enter the name and relationship of the emergency contact person. This is a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Relationship (Page 11)**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse). This will help us understand their connection to you.
