# thanksgiving_softball_camp_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is used to collect registration information for the Thanksgiving Softball Camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your participant's information:
   - **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on your registration.
   - **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
   - **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
   - **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
   - **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
   - **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
   - **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
   - **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
   - **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
   - **Participant Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
   - **Participant Shoe Size**: Enter your shoe size.
   - **Participant Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have.
   - **Participant Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
   - **Participant Medication**: List any medication you are currently taking.
   - **How Participant's Medication is Administered**: Describe how your medication is administered.
   - **Participant Medication Dosage**: Describe your medication dosage.
   - **Participant Special Instructions**: Provide any special instructions that may be necessary for your care.
   - **Participant Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.
