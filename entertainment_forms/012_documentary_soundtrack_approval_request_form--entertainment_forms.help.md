# Documentary Soundtrack Approval Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request approval for the soundtrack of a documentary. This form is used by content creators to provide information about the project and request approval for its soundtrack.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit. Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional):
  This field is where you can enter a brief description of the project, including its title, director, and a brief overview.
* **Soundtrack Details** (`soundtrack_details`, text, optional):
  This field is where you can provide information about the soundtrack, including composer, performer, and any other relevant details.
* **Submitter Details** (`submitter_details`, text, optional):
  This field is where the submitter can enter their name and job title within the organization.
* **Submitter Contact** (`submitter_contact`, text, optional):
  This field is where the submitter can enter their contact information, including name, phone number, and email address.
* **Approval Request** (`approval_request`, text, optional):
  This field is where the submitter can specify the type of approval they are requesting.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, number, optional):
  This field is a signature field where the submitter can enter their digital signature.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional):
  This field is where the submitter can enter the date they submitted the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional):
  This field is a unique identifier for the form submission.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional):
  This field is where the submitter can enter their email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional):
  This field is where the submitter can enter their phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
* This form is intended for internal use within the organization. If you are an external collaborator or partner, please contact the relevant department for information on how to submit your request.
