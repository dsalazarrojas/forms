# TV Series Catalog Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TV Series Catalog Form is designed to collect information about TV series to create a comprehensive catalog. This form will help users submit or update TV series data in a database or platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the title of the TV series.
2. Choose the genre of the TV series from the provided options.
3. Enter the number of episodes in the TV series.
4. Enter the release year of the TV series.
5. Enter the number of seasons in the TV series.
6. Provide a brief description of the TV series.
7. Select multiple tags that describe the TV series.
8. Enter the creator of the TV series.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **TV Series**: (<code>TV Series</code>, <type>text</type>, required: false)
	+ Enter the title of the TV series, e.g., "Game of Thrones".
* **Genre**: (<code>Genre</code>, <type>select_one</type>, required: false)
	+ Choose one of the available genres:
		- Action
		- Adventure
		- Comedy
		- Drama
		- Horror
		- Romance
* **Episodes**: (<code>Episodes</code>, <type>number</type>, required: false)
	+ Enter the number of episodes in the TV series.
* **Release Year**: (<code>Release Year</code>, <type>number</type>, required: false)
	+ Enter the release year of the TV series.
* **Seasons**: (<code>Seasons</code>, <type>number</type>, required: false)
	+ Enter the number of seasons in the TV series.
* **Description**: (<code>Description</code>, <type>note</type>, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief description of the TV series.
* **Tags**: (<code>Tags</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required: false)
	+ Select multiple tags that describe the TV series, e.g., "Sci-Fi", "Action", "Comedy".
* **Creator**: (<code>Creator</code>, <type>text</type>, required: false)
	+ Enter the creator of the TV series.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field to ensure that the TV series data is up-to-date and correct.
* Use the correct genre and tags to describe the TV series accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the provided explanations above or contact the platform administrators for assistance.
