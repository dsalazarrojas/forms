# Livestream Host Casting Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential livestream hosts who are interested in working with us. Please fill out this form to help us get to know you better and determine if you're a good fit for our livestream hosting needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email address, and other relevant contact information.
2. Attach your demo reel and any other requested materials.
3. Specify your availability and time zone.
4. Share any relevant experience and notes about your work.
5. Provide links to your social media profiles and website.
6. Attach any supporting documents, such as your resume and portfolio.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Demo Reel** (`Demo Reel`, `text`, required): Attach a video file or provide a link to a publicly accessible video that showcases your hosting skills.
* **Experience** (`Experience`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your relevant experience in the livestream hosting field.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Other).
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): Enter your preferred contact information (phone number or email address).
* **Social Media** (`Social Media`, `text`, required): Share any additional social media profiles you'd like to share.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **LinkedIn** (`LinkedIn`, `text`, required): Enter your LinkedIn profile link.
* **Facebook** (`Facebook`, `text`, required): Enter your Facebook profile link.
* **Twitter** (`Twitter`, `text`, required): Enter your Twitter profile link.
* **Instagram** (`Instagram`, `text`, required): Enter your Instagram profile link.
* **YouTube** (`YouTube`, `text`, required): Enter your YouTube profile link.
* **Skype** (`Skype`, `text`, required): Enter your Skype username.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `date`, required): Choose a date and time you're available to host a livestream.
* **Time Zone** (`Time Zone`, `select_one`, required): Choose your time zone.
* **Availability Details** (`Availability Details`, `text`, required): Enter more details about your availability.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about your experience and availability.
* **Image URL** (`Image URL`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of your profile picture.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of your resume.
* **Portfolio** (`Portfolio`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of your portfolio.
* **Video Link** (`Video Link`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of your video.
* **Website URL** (`Website URL`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of your website.
* **Agencies** (`Agencies`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you have any agencies you've worked with in the past.

## Tips
Remember to fill out this form completely and accurately to help us get to know you better. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
