## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, including the country code if you are outside your local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, `date`, required): Select the date that best applies to your situation.
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best applies to your situation.
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, `number`, optional): Enter a whole number that represents a quantity or rating.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional relevant information that may be helpful.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all options that apply to your situation.
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, optional): Enter an estimated value in decimal format if applicable.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any final comments or closing remarks.
