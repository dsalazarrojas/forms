# Screenplay Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Screenplay Upload Form is a tool for uploading and submitting screenplays to festivals, programs, or production companies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title of the screenplay in the **Screenplay Upload Form** field.
2. Enter your name as the author of the screenplay in the **Author Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Author Email** field.
4. Select the **Festival or Program** to which you are submitting your screenplay. You can choose between **Film Festival**, **TV Production Company**, and **Writing Program**.
5. Upload your screenplay file in the **Screenplay File** field.
6. Select the type of production company or festival you are submitting to in the **File Type** field. You can choose between **PDF**, **DOCX**, and **Word**.
7. Select the **Upload Status** of your screenplay. You can choose between **Pending**, **Approved**, and **Rejected**.
8. Add any additional **Notes** you have regarding your screenplay.
9. Select any **Festivals or Programs** you are submitting your screenplay to. You can choose multiple options from the list.
10. Finally, click **Submit** to complete the form and upload your screenplay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Screenplay Upload Form**: Enter the title of your screenplay. This field is not required, but it helps authors to identify their work.
* **Author Name**: Enter your name as the author of the screenplay.
* **Author Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Screenplay File**: Upload your screenplay file in one of the allowed formats.
* **Production Company**: Select the type of production company or festival you are submitting to.
* **Festival or Program**: Select the specific festival or program you are submitting your screenplay to.
* **Upload Date**: This field is automatically filled in when you upload your screenplay file.
* **Submit**: Click to complete the form and upload your screenplay.
* **File Type**: Select the format of your uploaded screenplay file.
* **Upload Status**: Select the current status of your screenplay upload.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your screenplay.
* **Festivals or Programs**: Select any additional festivals or programs you are submitting your screenplay to.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload your screenplay file in one of the allowed formats (PDF, DOCX, or Word).
* Select the correct **Festival or Program** for accurate tracking.
* Use the **Notes** field for any additional comments or questions you have.
* Select the correct **Upload Status** to reflect the current status of your screenplay.
