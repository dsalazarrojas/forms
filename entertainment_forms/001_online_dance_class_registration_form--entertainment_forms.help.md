# online_dance_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "online_dance_class_registration_form" is designed to gather information from users to facilitate the registration process for a dance class. 
It requires essential details such as name, email, contact phone number, class date, class time, participant count, preferred dance style, instructor name, and confirmation of registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field. 
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. 
3. If you wish to be contacted, provide your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field. 
4. Choose the date of the dance class you wish to attend in the "Class Date" field. 
5. Select the time of the dance class you wish to attend in the "Class Time" field. 
6. If you're planning to attend with friends or family, enter the number of participants in the "Participant Count" field. 
7. Choose one or more dance styles you'd like to learn in the "Dance Style" field. 
8. Enter the name of the instructor you'd like to take the class with in the "Instructor Name" field. 
9. You can provide additional comments or instructions in the "Message" field, but it's not required. 
10. Finally, confirm your registration by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required, text): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (required, email): Enter your valid email address for communication with the instructor and other class attendees.
* **Contact Phone** (optional, text): If you'd like to be contacted about the class, provide your contact phone number.
* **Class Date** (required, date): Select the date of the dance class you wish to attend.
* **Class Time** (required, time): Choose the time slot of the dance class you wish to attend.
* **Participant Count** (optional, number): If you're attending with others, enter the number of participants.
* **Dance Style** (required, select multiple): Choose the dance style(s) you'd like to learn.
* **Instructor Name** (required, text): Enter the name of the instructor you'd like to take the class with.
* **Message** (optional, note): Provide any additional comments or instructions for the instructor.
* **Confirmation** (optional, select one): Confirm your registration by selecting "Yes". 

Note: Ensure you fill out all required fields (marked as "required") to ensure successful submission. 
If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out.
