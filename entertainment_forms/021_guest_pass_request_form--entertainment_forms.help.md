# Guest Pass Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Pass Request Form is used for students to request guest passes for events or performances held within the campus or institution. This form is essential for obtaining a guest pass for a specific event or performance where the student's student status needs to be verified.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information for your guest, including their name and email. This ensures that the guest can be easily contacted with details about the event.
2. Enter your student number for verification purposes. This helps the administration to quickly identify you as a valid student.
3. Fill out your student information (name and email), so we can contact you with details about your request and any updates.
4. Submit the form, and our team will process your request as soon as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Students Name**: This is where you enter your name as a student.
* **Students Number**: This is your unique student number for verification purposes.
* **Students Email**: This is your student email address where you can be contacted with details about your request.
* **Guest's Name**: This is where you enter the name of the guest you are requesting a pass for.
* **Guest's Email**: This is the email address of your guest, so we can contact them about the event or performance.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the permission of your guest before requesting a pass for them.
* Double-check that you have entered your student and guest information correctly.
* If you are unable to attend an event or performance you have requested a pass for, please let us know as soon as possible.
