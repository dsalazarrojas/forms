# Music Festival Ticket Holder Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from ticket holders about their experience with the music festival. Your responses will help us improve our event organization and provide better services to our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Provide accurate information for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
4. Use the options provided for select_one fields.
5. Use the correct format for date, time, and email fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ticket Holder Information** (`ticket_holder_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about yourself as a ticket holder. Please feel free to share any relevant details that might help us better understand your needs.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you have a contact number and we can reach you via phone, otherwise choose 'No'.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted via email, please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have a phone number and we can contact you via phone, otherwise choose 'No'.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the music festival. Your feedback is valuable to us.
* **Ticket Purchase Details** (`ticket_purchase_details`, text, optional): Provide any specific details about your ticket purchase, such as the date, time, or method of purchase.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, optional): Share any additional event-related information that might be helpful for us to know.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): If you know the event date, please provide it in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): If you know the event time, please provide it in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): If you know the event location, please provide it.
* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have a ticket of the specific type, otherwise choose 'No'.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, optional): If you know the ticket price, please provide it.
* **Ticket Quantity** (`ticket_quantity`, number, optional): If you know the number of tickets you purchased, please provide it.
* **Attendee Information** (`attendee_info`, text, optional): If you are attending with someone, please provide their information.
* **Ticket Status** (`ticket_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your ticket (Active or Inactive).
* **Ticket Status Hint** (`ticket_status_hint`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your ticket status.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the event (Active or Inactive).
* **Event Status Hint** (`event_status_hint`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the event status.
* **Ticket Holder Information Hint** (`ticket_holder_info_hint`, text, optional): Share any additional information about yourself as a ticket holder.
* **Event Date Hint** (`event_date_hint`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the event date.
* **Ticket Purchase Information** (`ticket_purchase_info`, text, optional): Share any additional information about your ticket purchase.
* **Ticket Holder Contact** (`ticket_holder_contact`, text, optional): Share your contact information.
* **Ticket Holder Email** (`ticket_holder_email`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted via email, please provide your email address.
* **Ticket Purchase Time** (`ticket_purchase_time`, time, optional): If you know the time of purchase, please provide it in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Ticket Holder Comments** (`ticket_holder_comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the music festival or tickets.
