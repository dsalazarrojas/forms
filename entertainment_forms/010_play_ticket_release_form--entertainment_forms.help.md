# play_ticket_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The play_ticket_release_form is a document used to release play tickets for events. It helps to manage ticket distribution and ensures that tickets are allocated fairly among participants. Completing this form will help the event organizers to keep track of ticket allocations and release them to the correct individuals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Performance**: Enter the name of the performance (e.g., "Theater Production 2024").
	* **Quantity**: Enter the number of tickets you are releasing.
	* **Name**: Enter the name of the person receiving the tickets.

You may also be asked to fill in additional information:

	* **Delivery**: Select how you would like the tickets to be delivered (e.g., In Person, Email, Phone, or Print and Send).
	* **Date**: Enter the date you would like the tickets to be released.
	* **Time**: Enter the time you would like the tickets to be released.
	* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person receiving the tickets.
	* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person receiving the tickets.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance**: The name of the performance or event for which you are releasing tickets.
* **Quantity**: The number of tickets you are allocating to the person.
* **Delivery**: Choose how you would like the tickets to be delivered:
	+ In Person: Hand over the tickets in person to the person.
	+ Email: Send the tickets via email to the person.
	+ Phone: Discuss the ticket details over the phone with the person.
	+ Print and Send: Print the tickets and send them via postal mail.
* **Date**: The date when you would like the tickets to be released.
* **Time**: The time when you would like the tickets to be released.
* **Email**: The email address where you can send the tickets to the person.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the person to whom you can call and discuss ticket details.
* **Name**: The name of the person receiving the tickets.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in the correct information to ensure accurate ticket allocation and release.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the event organizers for clarification.
