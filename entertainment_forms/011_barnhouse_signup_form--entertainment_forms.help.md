# Barnhouse Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barnhouse Signup Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in attending or joining the Barnhouse events. This form is used to gather details about you, such as your contact information, event interests, and other relevant details to help us tailor our services and communication to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your **Full Name**, including both your first and last name.
2. Enter your **Email Address**, which will be used for event updates and confirmations.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for us to reach you.
4. If you're interested in attending an event, choose which type of event you're interested in from the list (**Event Interest**).
5. Select the typical size of your **Group Size**.
6. Let us know if you're interested in joining our **Membership Program**.
7. If you're interested in **Volunteering**, choose how frequently you'd like to help.
8. Select your **Preferred Volunteer Roles**, if applicable.
9. If you'd like to **Receive Newsletters**, let us know.
10. Choose which social media platforms you're on, for us to keep in touch (**Social Media Following**).
11. Tell us how you **Heard About Us**.
12. If you have any **Dietary Preferences** for event catering, please let us know.
13. If you have any **Accessibility Needs**, please describe them in this field.
14. Select your age group for demographic purposes (**Age Group**).
15. Enter your **ZIP Code** to help us understand where you're located.
16. If someone referred you to the Barnhouse, please mention their name (**Referral Source**).
17. If you have any **Special Requests** or comments, please share them with us.
18. Finally, hit submit to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (id 1, Required): Enter your first and last name to identify yourself.
* **Email Address** (id 2, Required): Enter your email address for event updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (id 3, Required): Provide your phone number for us to reach you.
* **Event Interest** (id 5, Required): Choose which types of events you're interested in (Live music concerts, Wedding receptions, etc.).
* **Group Size** (id 6, Required): Select the typical number of people you attend with.
* **Membership Interest** (id 7, Required): Let us know if you're interested in joining our membership program.
* **Volunteer Interest** (id 8, Required): Choose how often you'd like to volunteer (Yes, Maybe, No).
* **Volunteer Roles** (id 9, Optional): Select roles you'd enjoy volunteering for (Event setup, Ticket taking, etc.).
* **Newsletter Signup** (id 10, Required): Choose to receive newsletters with upcoming event updates.
* **Social Media Following** (id 11, Optional): Choose which social media platforms you're on (Facebook, Instagram, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (id 12, Required): Tell us how you found out about the Barnhouse.
* **Dietary Preferences** (id 13, Optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please let us know.
* **Accessibility Needs** (id 14, Optional): If you need any accessibility accommodations, please describe them.
* **Age Group** (id 15, Required): Select your age group for demographic purposes.
* **ZIP Code** (id 16, Required): Enter your zip code to help us understand where you're located.
* **Referral Source** (id 17, Optional): If someone referred you, please mention their name.
* **Special Requests or Comments** (id 18, Optional): Share any other comments or requests with us.
