# Gamer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from gamers to help us better understand the gaming community and their preferences. Your input will aid in improving our gaming experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your gamer ID, a unique identifier for your gaming profile, in the "Gamer ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address that you use for gaming communities or services.
* **Gamer ID** (`gamer_id`, number, optional):
	+ Enter a unique identifier for your gaming profile, such as your Steam ID or PlayStation Network ID.
