# DJ Entertainment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a DJ's entertainment services, such as their name, contact information, and event specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your DJ name in the "DJ Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. Please use the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" for the best results.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to contact you about events.
4. Fill in the name of the event you are performing at in the "Event Name" field.
5. Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field. Use the standard "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
6. Choose the start time of the event in the "Event Time" field. Use the "HH:MM AM/PM" format.
7. Enter the duration of the event in the "Event Duration" field. For example, 3 hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **DJ Name (Text)**: Enter your DJ name as you want it to be listed.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Your contact phone number, in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address that event planners or coordinators can use to contact you about events.
* **Event Name (Text)**: The name of the event you are performing at, such as a wedding or birthday party.
* **Event Date (Date)**: The date of the event, using the standard "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
* **Event Time (Time)**: The start time of the event, using the "HH:MM AM/PM" format.
* **Event Duration (Text)**: The length of time you will perform, e.g., 3 hours.
* **Event Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of event (Wedding, Birthday, Corporate) you are performing at.
* **Event Location (Text)**: The location of the event, if you want to include it.
