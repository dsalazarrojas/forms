# film_trailer_distribution_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of film trailer distribution. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that all necessary information is captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read the form carefully and understand its purpose.
- Fill out each field accurately and completely.
- Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Distribution Form** (`film_trailer_distribute_form`, text, optional): This is a free-text field where you can enter any relevant information about the distribution.
- **Trailer Details** (`trailer_details`, text, optional): In this field, enter any details about the trailer, such as its format (e.g., SDH, HD, etc.) or any other specific details about the trailer.
- **Authorization** (`distribution_authorization`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the individuals or organizations authorized to distribute the film trailer. You can choose 'None' if you don't want to select any options.
- **Partner Signature** (`partner_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date of the partner's signature. This field is not used to store the actual signature, but rather the date of when the signature was made.
- **Distribution Date** (`distribution_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the film trailer will be distributed. This field is for recording the scheduled distribution date.
- **Production Company** (`production_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the production company involved in the film trailer distribution.
- **Rights Holder** (`rights_holder`, text, optional): Enter the name of the entity holding the rights to the film.
- **Distributor** (`distributor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the distributor of the film trailer.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields if you need to submit the form for official use.
- Review this guide if you have any doubts or questions about the form.
