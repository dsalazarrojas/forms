# entertainment_pr_campaign_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for entertainment industry campaigns, which may involve publicity, promotions, or other forms of exposure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct campaign type from the "Publicity Type" field.
2. If required, choose the channels for publicity from the "Publicity Channels" field.
3. Enter the campaign details, including the "Campaign Name", "Campaign Start Date", "Campaign End Date", and "Campaign Duration".
4. Provide contact information, including the "Contact Name", "Contact Email", and "Contact Phone".
5. If you are a publicist, enter your name, title, and contact information.
6. Finally, select the "Submit" or "Save and Continue Editing" option to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Publicity Type** (`publicity_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of publicity you are seeking, such as Press Release, Media Interviews, or Social Media.
* **Publicity Channels** (`publicity_channels`, select_multiple, required): Choose the channels you would like to use for publicity, such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, or Email.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your campaign.
* **Campaign Start Date** (`campaign_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your campaign.
* **Campaign End Date** (`campaign_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your campaign.
* **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your campaign.
* **Campaign Budget** (`campaign_budget`, number, optional): Enter the budget for your campaign.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the contact person.
* **Publicist Name** (`publicist_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a publicist.
* **Publicist Title** (`publicist_title`, text, optional): Enter your title as a publicist.
* **Publicist Email** (`publicist_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as a publicist.
* **Publicist Phone** (`publicist_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as a publicist.
* **Publicity Image** (`publicity_image`, text, optional): Enter a description of the image or visual asset you would like to use for publicity.
* **Publicity Video** (`publicity_video`, text, optional): Enter a description of the video asset you would like to use for publicity.
* **Publicity Audio** (`publicity_audio`, text, optional): Enter a description of the audio asset you would like to use for publicity.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select this option to complete and submit the form, or choose "Save and Continue Editing" to save the form for later.
* **Save and Continue Editing** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Choose this option to save the form and return to it later.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Not applicable for this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Not applicable for this form.
* **Press Release** (`press_release`, select_one, optional): Not applicable for this form.
* **Media Interviews** (`media_interviews`, select_one, optional): Not applicable for this form.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, optional): Not applicable for this form.
* **Yes** (`yes`, select_one, optional): Not applicable for this form.
* **No** (`no`, select_one, optional): Not applicable for this form.
