# Baby Names Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their preferences and priorities when it comes to choosing a baby name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the parent or parents for whom the baby name is being chosen.

### 2. **Expected Due Date** (`expected_due_date`, date, optional)
Enter the expected due date of the baby. This information will help us understand the timing of the naming decision.

### 3. **Gender Preference** (`gender_preference`, select_one, optional)
Choose whether you have a preference for the baby's gender.

*   Boy: You prefer a boy as a name choice.
*   Girl: You prefer a girl as a name choice.
*   No preference: You have no preference for the baby's gender.
*   Undisclosed: You do not want to disclose your preference.

### 4. **Top Three Favorite Names** (`favorite_names_top3`, text, optional)
Enter your top three favorite names. These can be comma-separated for easy entry.

### 5. **Preferred Name Theme** (`name_theme`, select_one, optional)
Choose the theme that best describes your naming preference.

*   Family names: Names that have family ties or are traditional to your family.
*   Traditional: Classic, well-known names.
*   Modern: Names that are currently popular or trendy.
*   Biblical: Names from the Bible.
*   Nature inspired: Names inspired by nature or the outdoors.

### 6. **Importance of Name Meaning** (`name_meaning_importance`, select_one, optional)
Rate the importance of the name's meaning to you.

*   Not important: The name's meaning is not a concern.
*   Somewhat important: The name's meaning is somewhat important.
*   Very important: The name's meaning is very important.

### 7. **Initials Preference** (`initials_preference`, text, optional)
Enter any initials or combinations you would prefer to avoid or include in the name.

### 8. **Family Names Considered** (`family_names_considered`, text, optional)
Enter any family names that are currently under consideration for the baby's name.

### 9. **Additional Comments** (`submit_comments`, text, optional)
Enter any additional comments or thoughts about the naming process.

### 10. **Consent to Publish** (`consent_to_publish`, select_one, optional)
Choose whether you consent to have your name suggestions shared in community polls.

*   True: You consent to have your name suggestions shared.
*   False: You do not consent to have your name suggestions shared.
