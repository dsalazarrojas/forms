# schedule a zoom meeting form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The schedule a Zoom meeting form is used to plan and schedule a new meeting with colleagues or clients. It collects essential details such as start and end times, attendees, and location to ensure a smooth and well-organized meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting title in the "Meeting Details" field.
2. Add or remove attendees as needed using the "Attendees" field.
3. Set the start and end times for the meeting using the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
4. Choose the correct timezone for the meeting in the "Timezone" field.
5. Enter the meeting location in the "Location" field.
6. Select the type of meeting from the "Meeting Type" field.
7. Enter the Zoom Meeting ID and Join Password, if applicable.
8. Fill in registrant details for attendees, including name, email, phone number, address, company, and other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Details** (`Meeting Details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the meeting.
  * This field helps identify the meeting's purpose and agenda.
- **Attendees** (`Attendees`, `select_multiple`, required): Select attendees for the meeting.
  * This field allows you to add or remove attendees from the meeting.
- **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, required): Set the start time of the meeting in 12-hour format.
  * This field is essential for scheduling the meeting's timing.
- **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, required): Set the end time of the meeting in 12-hour format.
  * This field is crucial for ensuring the meeting does not run over its scheduled time.
- **Timezone** (`Timezone`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct timezone for the meeting.
  * This field helps ensure that the meeting's timing is accurate across different time zones.
- **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required): Enter the meeting location.
  * This field helps attendees know where to join the meeting.
- **Meeting Type** (`Meeting Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of meeting (Conference, Meeting, or Workshop).
  * This field helps categorize and filter meetings.
- **Zoom Meeting ID** (`Zoom Meeting ID`, `number`, required): Enter the Zoom Meeting ID.
  * This field is used for Zoom meeting setup.
- **Join Password** (`Join Password`, `text`, required): Enter the Join Password for the Zoom meeting.
  * This field ensures secure entry for attendees.
- **Registrant Name** (`Registrant Name`, `text`, required): Enter registrant name.
  * This field captures attendee details.
- **Registrant Email** (`Registrant Email`, `email`, required): Enter registrant email.
  * This field captures attendee contact information.
- **Registrant Phone** (`Registrant Phone`, `text`, required): Enter registrant phone number.
  * This field captures attendee contact information.
- **Registrant Address** (`Registrant Address`, `text`, required): Enter registrant address.
  * This field captures attendee location.
- **Registrant Company** (`Registrant Company`, `text`, required): Enter registrant company.
  * This field captures attendee affiliation.
- **Registrant Country** (`Registrant Country`, `text`, required): Enter registrant country.
  * This field captures attendee country of origin.
- **Registrant City** (`Registrant City`, `text`, required): Enter registrant city.
  * This field captures attendee city of residence.
- **Registrant State** (`Registrant State`, `text`, required): Enter registrant state.
  * This field captures attendee state of residence.
- **Registrant Zip** (`Registrant Zip`, `text`, required): Enter registrant zip code.
  * This field captures attendee postal code.
- **Registrant Notes** (`Registrant Notes`, `text`, required): Enter registrant notes.
  * This field allows for additional attendee information.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the meeting details before submitting the form.
- Review the attendees list to ensure everyone is correctly added or removed.
- Use the correct timezone to avoid scheduling conflicts.
- Enter accurate registrant details for a smooth and successful meeting.
