# Virtual Trick Or Treat Party Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering for a Virtual Trick Or Treat Party. It helps the party organizers plan the party according to your preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "party_details" field, which asks for the name of the party you want to join.
2. Provide your "contact_info" in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Answer the "RSVP" question, choosing whether you want to attend the party or not.
4. Choose your preferences for "trick or treat" in the "Do you want a treat or just attend the party?" field.
5. Select how long you want to stay for the party in the "How long do you want to stay for the party?" field.
6. If you have a favorite Halloween character, choose one from the "What is your favorite Halloween character?" field.
7. Select your favorite Halloween candy in the "What is your favorite Halloween candy?" field.
8. If you have any dietary restrictions, choose from the options in the "Do you have any dietary restrictions?" field.
9. Enter the number of guests you are bringing in the "How many guests are you bringing?" field.
10. Finally, answer the "Do you like treats?" and "Trick or Treat" questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your party name?** (`party_details`, `text`, required): Fill out the name of the party you want to join.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **RSVP** (`RSVP`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether you want to attend the party or not.
* **Do you want a treat or just attend the party?** (`trick_treat_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether you want a treat or just attend the party.
* **How long do you want to stay for the party?** (`stay_time`, `time`, required): Select the length of time you want to stay for the party.
* **What is your favorite Halloween character?** (`favorite_halloween_character`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have a favorite Halloween character, choose one from the options.
* **What is your favorite Halloween candy?** (`favorite_halloween_candy`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your favorite Halloween candy from the options.
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions?** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, choose from the options.
* **How many guests are you bringing?** (`guest_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of guests you are bringing.
* **Do you like treats?** (`trick_treat`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer whether you like treats or not.
* **Trick or Treat** (`trick_treat_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer whether you like trick or treat or not.
* **Trick or Treat preferences** (`trick_treat_preferences_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your preferences for trick or treat. 

Note: This form is for registration purposes only. Please fill it out accurately and completely to help the party organizers plan the party according to your needs and preferences.
