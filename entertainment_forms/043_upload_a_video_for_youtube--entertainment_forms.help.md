# Upload A Video For YouTube - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for uploading a video to YouTube, a popular video-sharing platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Upload Video**: Enter the URL of the video you want to upload.
2. **Video Url**: Enter the URL of the video you want to upload.
3. **Description**: Provide a brief description of the video (optional).
4. **Tags**: Add relevant tags to help others find your video (optional).
5. **Public**: Choose whether to make your video public or private.
6. **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the video (optional).
7. **Comments Status**: Specify the status of the comments (optional).
8. **Comments Text**: Enter any additional text for the comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Upload Video** (`upload_video_for_youtube_form`, text, required): Enter the URL of the video you want to upload.
- **Video Url** (`video_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the video you want to upload. This should be the same as the "Upload Video" field above.
- **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the video to help viewers understand its content.
- **Tags** (`tags`, text, optional): Add relevant tags to help others find your video. Use commas to separate multiple tags.
- **Public** (`public_status`, select_one, required): Choose whether to make your video public or private. Select "Public" to make your video visible to everyone, or "Private" to restrict access to those with a link.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes about the video, such as a transcript or a list of credits.
- **Comments Status** (`comments_status`, text, optional): Specify the status of the comments, such as "open" or "closed".
- **Comments Text** (`comments_text`, text, optional): Enter any additional text for the comments, such as a description or a disclaimer.
