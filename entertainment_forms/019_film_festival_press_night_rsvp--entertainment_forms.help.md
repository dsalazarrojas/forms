# Film Festival Press Night RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from press and media representatives to help the festival team manage attendance and guest expectations for the press night of a film festival.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select **Yes** or **No** from the **Press Night** dropdown menu if you or your team would like to attend the press night. If you're unsure about attending, please select **No**.
- Please confirm if you will be bringing any media confirmation equipment (e.g., camera, microphone, etc.) by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Media Confirmation** field.
- If you will be attending, please provide your **Name** in the text field.
- If you will be providing an email address, please enter it in the **Email** field.
- If you will be providing a phone number, please enter it in the **Phone** field.
- If you will be attending, please select **Photography**, **Video**, or **Audio** from the **Consent** dropdown menu to indicate the type of media you'll be using.
- If you have any additional guest details, please type them in the **Guest Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Press Night (press_night)
- Label: Press Night
- Type: select_multiple
- This field is used to indicate if you or your team will be attending the press night. Please select **Yes** if you plan to attend.

### Media Confirmation (media_confirmation)
- Label: Media Confirmation
- Type: select_one
- This field is used to confirm if you will be bringing any media equipment. Please select **Yes** if you'll be bringing equipment.

### Name (name)
- Label: Name
- Type: text
- This field is used to collect your or your team's name.

### Email (email)
- Label: Email
- Type: email
- This field is used to collect your or your team's email address.

### Phone (phone)
- Label: Phone
- Type: text
- This field is used to collect your or your team's phone number.

### Consent (consent)
- Label: Consent
- Type: select_one
- This field is used to indicate the type of media you'll be using. Please select **Photography**, **Video**, or **Audio** if you'll be using any of these media types.

### Guest Details (guest_details)
- Label: Guest Details
- Type: note
- This field is used to provide any additional guest details.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the festival team.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the **Guest Details** field.
