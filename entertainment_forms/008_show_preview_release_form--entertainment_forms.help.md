# Show Preview Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Show Preview Release Form" is used to gather all necessary information about a show or recording. This information includes participant details, event details, and other required documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Enter the title of the show in the "Show Title" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the show in the "Show Date" and "Show Time" fields.
5. Confirm if there will be any recording in the "Recording Type" field.
6. Enter the date of the recording in the "Recording Date" field.
7. Enter the time of the recording in the "Recording Time" field.
8. Enter the location of the recording in the "Recording Location" field.
9. Confirm participant's consent to record in the "Participant Consent" field.
10. Enter any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
11. Enter the participant's signature in the "Participant Signature" field.
12. Enter your contact information as the form submitter in the "Form Submitter" field.
13. Enter your phone number as the form submitter in the "Form Submitter Phone" field.
14. Enter the participant's contact information in the "Participant Contact Info" field.
15. Enter the production team's name in the "Production Team" field.
16. Enter the event organizer's name in the "Event Organizer" field.
17. Enter the recording equipment used in the "Recording Equipment" field.
18. Enter the sound quality of the recording in the "Sound Quality" field.
19. Confirm participant's consent for the second recording in the "Participant Consent 2" field.
20. Enter the participant's second signature in the "Participant Signature 2" field.
21. Enter the location of the second recording in the "Recording Location 2" field.
22. Enter the participant's contact information for the second recording in the "Participant Contact Info 2" field.
23. Enter your information as the second form submitter in the "Form Submitter 2" field.
24. Enter your phone number as the second form submitter in the "Form Submitter 2 Phone" field.
25. Enter the participant's name for the second recording in the "Participant Name 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant involved in the show or recording.
* **Show Title** (`show_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the show or recording.
* **Show Date** (`show_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the show or recording in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Show Time** (`show_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the show or recording in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Recording Type** (`recording_type`, select_one, required): Confirm if there will be any recording for the show or recording.
* **Recording Date** (`recording_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the recording in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Recording Time** (`recording_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the recording in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the recording took place.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm participant's consent to record.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the show or recording.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required): Enter the participant's signature.
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, text, required): Enter your contact information as the form submitter.
* **Form Submitter Phone** (`form_submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the form submitter.
* **Participant Contact Info** (`participant_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the participant's contact information.
* **Production Team** (`production_team`, text, required): Enter the production team's name.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): Enter the event organizer's name.
* **Recording Equipment** (`recording_equipment`, text, required): Enter the equipment used for the recording.
* **Sound Quality** (`sound_quality`, text, required): Enter the sound quality of the recording.
* **Participant Consent 2** (`participant_consent_2`, select_one, required): Confirm participant's consent for the second recording.
* **Participant Signature 2** (`participant_signature_2`, note, required): Enter the participant's second signature.
* **Recording Location 2** (`recording_location_2`, text, required): Enter the location of the second recording.
* **Participant Contact Info 2** (`participant_contact_info_2`, text, required): Enter the participant's contact information for the second recording.
* **Form Submitter 2** (`form_submitter_2`, text, required): Enter your information as the second form submitter.
* **Form Submitter 2 Phone** (`form_submitter_2_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the second form submitter.
* **Participant Name 2** (`participant_name_2`, text, required): Enter the participant's name for the second recording.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Double-check your contact information as the form submitter.
- Ensure that the participant's contact information is up-to-date.
- Confirm participant's consent before proceeding with the recording.
- Enter the correct recording location and equipment used.
- Enter the correct sound quality of the recording.
- Double-check the participant's name and contact information for the second recording.
