# Black Friday Photo Contest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit their photos for the Black Friday photo contest. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to be eligible for prizes and potential publication on the company's website or social media channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Black Friday Photo Contest Entry" page.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* Full Name
	* Email Address
	* Age
	* Mailing Address (required for prize delivery)
	* Photo Title
	* Photo Description
	* Date Photo Was Taken
	* Photo URL (link to your photo)
	* Consent to publish the photo (Yes, publish with my name or Yes, publish anonymously)
	* Agreement to official contest rules and prize terms (I agree or I do not agree)
3. Optionally, fill out the non-required fields:
	* Phone Number (optional)
	* Location of Photo (optional)
	* Primary Product in Photo (optional)
	* Photo Category (select one from the options: Shopping Scene, Product Close-up, Celebration Moment, Before & After, Family/Group Activity)
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Contestant Name, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, required): Enter your email address for prize delivery and communication.
* **Age** (Age, required): Enter your age to ensure you are eligible for prizes.
* **Mailing Address** (Mailing Address, required): Enter your address for prize delivery, if applicable.
* **Photo Title** (Photo Title, required): Give your photo a creative title that captures the moment.
* **Photo Description** (Photo Description, required): Describe the story behind your Black Friday photo.
* **Date Photo Was Taken** (Date Photo Was Taken, required): Enter the date when the photo was taken.
* **Photo URL** (Photo URL, required): Link to your photo online.
* **Location of Photo** (Location of Photo, optional): If applicable, enter where the photo was taken.
* **Primary Product in Photo** (Primary Product in Photo, optional): If applicable, enter the main item featured in the photo.
* **Photo Category** (Photo Category, required): Select one of the available categories: Shopping Scene, Product Close-up, Celebration Moment, Before & After, Family/Group Activity.
* **Do you allow us to publish this photo?** (Consent to Publish, required): Choose whether you want your photo to be published with your name or anonymously.
* **I agree to official contest rules and prize terms** (Consent to Rules, required): Confirm that you agree to the contest rules and prize terms.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Ensure that your photo is suitable for public viewing and meets the contest rules.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid mistakes.
* By submitting this form, you agree to the contest rules and prize terms.
