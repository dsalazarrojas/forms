# Fortune Teller Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fortune Teller Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your experience with the tarot reading service. This form aims to provide valuable insights to help us improve our service and provide better experiences for you and other customers. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the duration of the tarot reading service you received: 
   - 10 minutes
   - 30 minutes
   - 60 minutes

2. Choose the type of tarot reading service you received:
   - Please select from the options provided

3. Rate your overall experience:
   - 1-2 (Poor)
   - 3 (Average)
   - 4 (Good)
   - 5 (Excellent)

4. Provide any feedback or comments about your experience:
   - Please enter your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions here

5. Add any additional comments:
   - Please enter any other thoughts, concerns, or suggestions here

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tarot Reading Service** (`tarot_reading_service`, note, optional): Please select the duration of the tarot reading service you received.
* **Reader** (`reader`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tarot reader who provided your service.
* **Reading Length** (`reading_length`, number, optional): Choose the length of the tarot reading service you received.
* **Reading Type** (`reading_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of tarot reading service you received.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about your experience.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the service.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the quality of the service.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions.

**Tips**

* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, we are here to listen and learn from your feedback.
