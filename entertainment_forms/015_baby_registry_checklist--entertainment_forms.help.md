# Baby Registry Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help expectant parents create a tailored baby registry, making it easier for friends and family to give meaningful gifts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Mom-to-Be").
2.  Select the expected due date for your baby's arrival.
3.  Choose the essential items you need for your baby, such as a crib, mattress, and changing table.
4.  Choose the feeding items you prefer, like bottles, a breast pump, or a high chair.
5.  Select your preferred clothing sizes for your baby.
6.  Indicate the travel gear you'll need, such as a car seat, stroller, or travel crib.
7.  Leave any notes for gift-givers regarding your gift preferences.
8.  Enter the shipping address where you'd like gifts to be sent.
9.  Provide a link to your online baby registry, if you have one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent name (Parent name)** (`parent_name`, text, **Optional**)
    *   Enter your name and title, if you'd like.
*   **Due date (Expected delivery date)** (`due_date`, date, **Optional**)
    *   Enter the date you expect your baby to arrive.
*   **Nursery items needed (Check items to add to registry)** (`nursery_items_needed`, select_multiple, **Optional**)
    *   Choose the essential items for your baby's nursery, such as a crib, mattress, and changing table.
*   **Feeding items (Bottles breast pump high chair)** (`feeding_items`, select_multiple, **Optional**)
    *   Select the feeding items you prefer, such as bottles, a breast pump, or a high chair.
*   **Preferred clothing sizes (Newborn 0-3 3-6 6-12 months)** (`clothing_sizes`, text, **Optional**)
    *   Choose the sizes you'd like for your baby's clothes.
*   **Travel gear needed (Car seat stroller carrier)** (`travel_gear`, select_multiple, **Optional**)
    *   Indicate the travel gear you'll need, such as a car seat, stroller, or travel crib.
*   **Gift preferences (Any notes for gift givers)** (`gift_preferences`, text, **Optional**)
    *   Leave any notes for gift-givers regarding your gift preferences.
*   **Shipping address (Where gifts should be sent)** (`shipping_address`, text, **Optional**)
    *   Enter the address where you'd like gifts to be shipped.
*   **Registry link (Optional link to online registry)** (`registry_link`, text, **Optional**)
    *   Provide a link to your online registry, if you have one.
*   **Checklist completion status (Update as items are added)** (`completion_status`, note, **Optional**)
    *   Keep track of the checklist's completion status as items are added.
