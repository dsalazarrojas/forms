# Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Competition Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants for competition registration. Please follow the guidelines below to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
2. Ensure that all information is accurate and truthful.
3. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Competition Name** (*): Enter the name of the competition.
* **Competition Date** (*): Enter the date of the competition.
* **Competition Location** (*): Enter the venue or location of the competition.
* **First Name** (*): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (*): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (*): Enter a contact email.
* **Phone Number** (*): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (*): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (*): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (*): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (*): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (*): Select your country of residence.
* **Date of Birth** (*): Enter your birth date.
* **Age Division** (*): Select your competition age division.
* **Gender** (optional): Select your gender.
* **Skill Level** (*): Select your skill level.
* **Competition Category** (*): Select your competition category.
* **Entry Title** (*): Enter the name of your performance or entry.
* **Entry Description** (*): Enter a brief description.
* **Team Members** (optional): List team members if applicable.
* **Coach or Instructor** (optional): Enter name if applicable.
* **Organization or School** (optional): Enter your affiliation.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (optional): Enter any special requirements.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (*): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (*): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (*): Select the relationship to you.
* **Medical Conditions** (optional): Enter any medical conditions.
* **Entry Fee Amount** (*): Enter the registration fee.
* **Payment Method** (*): Select payment method.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** (*): Select agree to terms.
* **Liability Waiver** (*): Select agreement to liability claims.
* **Media Consent** (*): Select consent for photos and videos.
* **Participant Signature** (*): Type your full name.
* **Signature Date** (*): Enter the date of signing.
