# irish_culture_awareness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand your level of awareness and knowledge about Irish culture. Your responses will be used to identify areas of interest and potential barriers to increasing awareness, and will inform future initiatives and programs aimed at promoting cultural understanding and appreciation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best answer each question.
3. Be honest and objective in your responses.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, required): A brief introduction or statement about your background and experience with Irish culture.
* **Irish Culture Awareness Questions** (`irish_culture_awareness_questions`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your level of awareness or understanding of various aspects of Irish culture, such as history, customs, and traditions.
* **Irish Traditions** (`irish_traditions`, select_one, required): Choose one option that best describes your level of familiarity with Irish traditions.
* **Cultural Events** (`cultural_events`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of interest or participation in cultural events related to Irish culture.
* **Irish Language** (`irish_language`, text, required): Describe your experience or interest in the Irish language.
* **Cultural Leadership** (`cultural_leadership`, select_one, required): Choose one option that best describes your level of understanding of cultural leadership in Irish culture.
* **Irish Culture Awareness Goals** (`irish_culture_awareness_goals`, text, required): Describe your goals or objectives related to increasing awareness and appreciation of Irish culture.
* **Irish Culture Awareness Impact** (`irish_culture_awareness_impact`, text, required): Describe how you think awareness and appreciation of Irish culture can positively impact your community or society.
* **Irish Culture Awareness Barriers** (`irish_culture_awareness_barriers`, text, required): Identify potential barriers or challenges to increasing awareness and appreciation of Irish culture.
* **Irish Culture Awareness Recommendations** (`irish_culture_awareness_recommendations`, select_one, required): Choose one option that best describes your level of agreement with recommendations to improve cultural awareness.
