# Improv Character and Costume Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your character and its needs for our improv performances and events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and fill it in according to the instructions provided.
2. Please provide as much detail as possible for each field, especially for fields like character_name, character_age and costume_description.
3. You can choose from multiple options for fields like experience_level, performance_frequency and show_frequency.
4. If you have any additional information or notes, please use the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Character Name** (`character_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your character, e.g. "The Queen of Spades".
* **Character Age** (`character_age`, number, required): Enter the age of your character, if applicable.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select one option from the list:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Character Type** (`character_type`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your character, e.g. "Silly, lovable, and eccentric".
* **Costume Needs** (`costume_needs`, text, required): Enter any specific costume needs or requirements for your character, e.g. "Must wear a wig".
* **Costume Description** (`costume_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your character's costume, e.g. "A lavish, over-the-top gown".
* **Performance Frequency** (`performance_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Show Frequency** (`show_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Performance Type** (`performance_type`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your character's performance, e.g. "Physical comedy, improv, dance".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your character.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted about future performances.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted about future performances.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when filling in the form.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact our improv team.
* This form will help us better understand your character's needs and preferences, so we can tailor our shows and performances to suit you and your character.
