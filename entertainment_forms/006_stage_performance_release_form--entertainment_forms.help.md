# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a performer's performance, including contact details, email address, phone number, and permission date. The form also requires a description of the performance, the type of permission granted, and whether certain actions were taken (broadcast, photographs, recordings). The form aims to gather relevant information for future reference and to ensure that all necessary steps are taken for the performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1.  Fill in the "Title" field with the title of the performance.
2.  Enter the name of the performer in the "Performer" field.
3.  Fill in the "Contact Information" field with any relevant contact details.
4.  Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Email" field.
5.  Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone" field (optional).
6.  Select the "Date of Performance" field and enter the date when the performance took place.
7.  In the "Type of Permission" field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the permission was granted or not.
8.  Enter a description of the performance in the "Description" field (optional).
9.  If applicable, enter the date when the permission was granted in the "Permission Date" field.
10.  Enter the name of the person responsible for the performance in the "Assigned to" field.
11.  Enter any notes about the performance in the "Notes" field (optional).
12.  In the "Broadcast" field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the performance was broadcast or not (optional).
13.  In the "Photographs" field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether photographs were taken or not (optional).
14.  In the "Recordings" field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether recordings were made or not (optional).

*   **Broadcast** (`broadcast`, optional, ): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the performance was broadcast or not.
