# talent_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Registration Form is a tool used to collect essential information about a talent's qualifications, experience, and contact details. This form enables us to create a comprehensive profile for future reference and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Upload your resume (if applicable) in the "Resume" field.
2. Provide your profile photo in the "Profile Photo" field.
3. Fill out your contact information, including your Email, Contact Info, and "Job Title" fields.
4. Describe your profile, skills, and experience in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resume** (`resume`, `note`, required: false): Upload your resume for our reference.
* **Profile Photo** (`profile_photo`, `note`, required: false): A photo that represents you.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication and reference purposes.
* **Profile** (`profile`, `text`, required: false): Describe your professional profile, including your skills, expertise, and relevant experience.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title or current role.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `number`, required: false): Provide an estimate of your relevant experience (e.g., years).
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if you possess specific skills.
	+ Select "No" if you do not possess specific skills.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter an additional job title or current role.
* **Resume 2** (`resume_2`, `note`, required: false): Upload an additional resume (if applicable).
* **Profile Photo 2** (`profile_photo_2`, `note`, required: false): A photo that represents you.
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter additional contact information.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, required: false): Enter an additional email address.
* **Profile 2** (`profile_2`, `text`, required: false): Describe your additional professional profile.
* **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your third job title or current role.
* **Experience 2** (`experience_2`, `number`, required: false): Provide an estimate of your additional experience.
* **Skills 2** (`skills_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if you possess specific skills.
	+ Select "No" if you do not possess specific skills.
* **Job Title 4** (`job_title_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your fourth job title or current role.
* **Resume 3** (`resume_3`, `note`, required: false): Upload a third resume (if applicable).
* **Profile Photo 3** (`profile_photo_3`, `note`, required: false): A photo that represents you.
* **Contact Info 3** (`contact_info_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter additional contact information.
* **Email 3** (`email_3`, `email`, required: false): Enter a third email address.
* **Profile 3** (`profile_3`, `text`, required: false): Describe your additional professional profile.
* **Experience 3** (`experience_3`, `number`, required: false): Provide an estimate of your additional experience.
* **Skills 3** (`skills_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if you possess specific skills.
	+ Select "No" if you do not possess specific skills.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your email and contact information for accuracy.
* Use clear and concise language when describing your profile, skills, and experience.
* If you are uploading multiple resumes or photos, ensure they are relevant and in the correct format.
