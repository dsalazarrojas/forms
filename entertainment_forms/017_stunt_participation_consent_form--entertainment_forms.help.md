# Stunt Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stunt Participation Consent Form is used to gather information from participants about their involvement in stunts, including their experience, frequency, and safety concerns. This form ensures that participants are aware of the risks and are willing to participate in the stunt while also providing the production team with necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **General Info** section with your basic contact information.
2.  In the **Stunt Info** section, provide details about the stunt you are participating in.
3.  In the **Participant Info** section, enter your personal information, including your name, relationship with the emergency contact, and their contact information.
4.  If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and relationship with you in the **Emergency Contact** section.
5.  Enter your email address in the **Email Address** section.
6.  If you have any prior stunt experience, please describe it in the **Stunt Experience** section.
7.  Rate the **Stunt Frequency** of your participation on a scale of 1-10.
8.  Indicate the **Stunt Danger Level** you are comfortable with, which will be used to assess the risk of the stunt.
9.  Check the box in the **Consent to Participate** section to indicate your willingness to participate in the stunt.
10.  If applicable, sign the **Witness Signature** and **Production Team Signature** sections as required.
11.  In the **Stunt Participation** section, indicate whether or not you are participating in the stunt.
12.  If you are participating, provide details in the **Stunt Participation Details** section.
13.  Rate the **Stunt Participation Danger Level** you are comfortable with, which will be used to assess the risk of your participation.
14.  Enter the frequency of your stunt participation in the **Stunt Participation Frequency** section.
15.  Enter the date and time of your stunt participation in the **Stunt Participation Date** and **Stunt Participation Time** sections.
16.  If there's any other information about your stunt participation, please provide it in the **Other Stunt Info** section.
17.  Finally, sign the **Production Team Signature 1-6** sections as required.
