This form is designed to collect information from participants of an online quiz competition. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the participants and their answers to a set of questions. The form will be used to track participant data and provide a fair chance for all participants to compete in the quiz.

This guide is intended to ensure that participants understand what information is being collected and how to complete the form correctly. Please read this guide carefully before proceeding.

## Purpose
The purpose of the online quiz competition form is to collect information about the participants and their answers to a set of questions. This form will help us track participant data and provide a fair chance for all participants to compete in the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Participant Details" page with your information.
2. On the "Question Set" page, select one of the options provided.
3. On the "Submit Answers" page, choose your selected option from the previous page.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Please fill out this page with your details, including any relevant information about yourself.

### Question Set
* **Question Set** (`question_set`, text, optional): This page is where you will select one of the options provided. Please read the options carefully and choose the one that best fits your answer.

### Submit Answers
* **Submit Answers** (`submit_answers`, select_one, optional): On this page, you will submit your selected option from the previous page. Please make sure to choose the correct option to ensure your answers are accurate.
