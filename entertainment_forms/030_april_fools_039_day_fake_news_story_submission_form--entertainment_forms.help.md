# April Fools Day Fake News Story Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for creating and submitting fictional news stories for entertainment purposes. It's not meant to be taken seriously and is intended to be humorous and lighthearted.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with accurate information. Start by providing your contact information, then craft a compelling story with a clear headline, choose the correct category, and indicate your consent to publish. You can also add additional notes if you'd like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Information** (`Submitter Information`, `note`, required: false): This section allows you to provide your name and contact information. It's optional, but we appreciate it when you do.
* **Author Name** (`Author Name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your name as you'd like to be credited for the story.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required: true): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (optional)** (`Phone Number (optional)`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your number here.
* **Story Details** (`Story Details`, `note`, required: false): You can add any additional information about your story here.
* **Story Headline** (`Story Headline`, `text`, required: true): Write a clear and attention-grabbing headline for your story.
* **Story Category** (`Story Category`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the category that best fits your story (Technology, Celebrity, Political, Sports, Business, Science, or Other).
* **Story Content** (`Story Content`, `text`, required: true): Type out your fake news story. Be creative and have fun with it!
* **Humor Style** (`Humor Style`, `select_one`, required: false): If you have a specific humor style in mind (Satirical, Absurd, Clever, Sarcastic, Quirky), choose it here.
* **Target Audience** (`Target Audience`, `text`, required: false): If you're writing for a specific audience, let us know here.
* **Approximate Word Count** (`Approximate Word Count`, `number`, required: false): Estimate how many words your story will have.
* **Consent to Publish** (`Consent to Publish`, `select_one`, required: true): Please indicate whether you give consent to publish your story.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other comments or questions, enter them here.
