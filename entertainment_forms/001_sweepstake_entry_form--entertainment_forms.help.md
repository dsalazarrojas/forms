# Sweepstake Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to participate in a sweepstake. The collected data will be used to contact the user for further instructions or updates.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Scroll down to each section and review the available fields. Each section has a specific set of fields that require attention.
2.  Start with the first section and enter your name in the **name** field.
3.  Enter your valid email address in the **email** field.
4.  If applicable, enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
5.  Choose your preferred option(s) from the **select one** or **select multiple** fields.
6.  Enter the required date in the **date** field if applicable.
7.  Enter the time if necessary in the **time** field.
8.  Leave a note with any additional information in the **note** field, if needed.
9.  Choose additional options if required in the **select multiple 2** field.
10. Enter your name in the **name 2** field, if it's a duplicate field.
11. Finally, choose the required options from the **select one 2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **name** (text, required): Enter your name.
*   **email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address.
*   **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
*   **select one** (select_one, optional): Select the preferred option from the available options.
*   **select multiple** (select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options that apply to you.
*   **date** (date, optional): Enter the required date, if needed.
*   **time** (time, optional): Enter the required time if necessary.
*   **note** (note, optional): Add any additional information in the note field.
*   **select multiple 2** (select_multiple, optional): Choose additional options if required.
*   **name 2** (text, optional): Enter your name again, if required.
*   **select one 2** (select_one, optional): Select the required option from the available options.
*   **phone 2** (text, optional): Enter your phone number again, if required.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to submit the form correctly.
*   Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
*   Choose the correct options for **select one** and **select multiple** fields to ensure accurate data collection.
