# Happiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your overall happiness and well-being. Your responses will help us identify areas of concern and suggest support opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

- Carefully read each question and select the answer that best represents your current state.
- For questions that ask for written responses, provide detailed and honest answers.
- Make sure to complete all required questions (marked as required).
