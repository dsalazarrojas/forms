# Film Academy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect personal and interest information from potential Film Academy applicants. 
It will be used to gather data for future reference and communication with registered users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **first_name** (if you wish to share your name).
2. Provide your **last_name** (if you wish to share your name).
3. Enter your **email** address to be contacted.
4. Enter your **phone** number for future communication.
5. Select your **birth_date** (month and year you were born).
6. If you have a specific **audition_plan**, please share it with us.
7. Describe your **plan_to_become**, if you have one.
8. Choose your **submit** option:
	* **I am interested in the opportunity**: If you're ready to join the Film Academy.
	* **I have a plan in place**: If you're not yet ready to join but plan to do so in the future.
	* **I do not have a plan**: If you're not interested in joining the Film Academy.
9. Finally, choose when you are **submit2** ready:
	* **Yes, today**: If you're ready to proceed with the application process now.
	* **Later, but soon**: If you're not ready to apply immediately but plan to do so in the near future.
	* **No, not interested**: If you've decided not to join the Film Academy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name**: Enter your first name if you wish to share it with us.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name if you wish to share it with us.
* **email**: A valid email address is required to be contacted by us.
* **phone**: A valid phone number is required for future communication.
* **birth_date**: Select your birth date in the format MM/YYYY.
* **audition_plan**: Describe your specific plans for auditioning at the Film Academy.
* **plan_to_become**: Describe your plan to become a part of the Film Academy.
* **submit**: Select one of the three options to indicate your interest in joining the Film Academy.
* **submit2**: Choose your readiness to proceed with the application process.
