# Chocolate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information on your demographics, chocolate preferences, consumption habits, and factors that influence your purchasing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions carefully, as they are intended to gather accurate information about your preferences.
2. For fields with ratings or selections, choose the options that best fit your responses.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please provide your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group from the options provided.
* **How often do you eat chocolate** (`consumption_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how frequently you consume chocolate.
* **Average Monthly Chocolate Spending** (`average_spending`, select_one, required): Rate your average monthly chocolate spending on the provided scale.
* **Your Favorite Chocolate Types** (`favorite_types`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of chocolate that you prefer.
* **Dark Chocolate Preference** (`dark_chocolate_preference`, select_one, optional): Select the percentage of dark chocolate you prefer.
* **Favorite Flavors** (`flavor_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select all flavors you prefer.
* **Favorite Brands** (`favorite_brands`, select_multiple, required): Select all brands you prefer.
* **Loyalty to Brands** (`brand_loyalty_level`, number, required): Rate your loyalty to your favorite brands on the scale of 1 (not loyal) to 5 (very loyal).
* **Importance of Brand Name** (`brand_importance`, number, required): Rate the importance of the brand name in your purchasing decisions on the scale of 1 (not important) to 5 (very important).
* **Purchasing Factors** (`purchase_factors`, select_multiple, required): Select all factors that influence your purchasing decisions.
* **Price Sensitivity** (`price_sensitivity`, number, required): Rate your price sensitivity on the scale of 1 (not sensitive) to 5 (very sensitive).
* **Importance of Quality** (`quality_importance`, number, required): Rate the importance of quality on the scale of 1 (not important) to 5 (very important).
* **Concerns about Health Effects** (`health_concern`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of concern about chocolate's health effects.
* **Interest in Organic Chocolate** (`organic_preference`, select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to pay more for organic chocolate.
* **Interest in New Products** (`new_product_interest`, number, required): Rate your interest in new chocolate products on the scale of 1 (not interested) to 5 (very interested).
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in this field.
