# Community Theater Performance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants and crew members for a Community Theater Performance event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant full name** in the "Participant full name" field. This is the name that will be used to address you for casting notices and schedules.
2. Choose your **Role applied for** from the dropdown list. This indicates whether you are applying for a lead, supporting, ensemble, crew, musician, or other role.
3. Enter your **Contact email** address in the "Contact email" field. This will be used for casting notices and schedules.
4. If you have a **Phone number**, please enter it in the "Phone number" field. This is optional, but it will help us reach you more easily.
5. In the "Availability for rehearsals" field, list the days and times you are available to participate in rehearsals. This will help us create a schedule that fits your needs.
6. If you have any **Prior theater experience**, please briefy list your relevant credits in the "Prior theater experience" field. This will help us understand your qualifications for the role you are applying for.
7. In the "Emergency contact name and phone" field, please list the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency. This is for cast safety.
8. If you have any **Special requirements**, such as accessibility or accommodation needs, please list them in the "Special requirements" field. These will be taken into account when creating the event schedule and arrangements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant full name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it should be spelled and formatted.
* **Role applied for** (select_one, required): Choose the type of role you are applying for (Lead, Supporting, Ensemble, Crew, Musician, Other).
* **Contact email** (email, required): Enter your email address that we can use for casting notices and schedules.
* **Phone number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for easier contact.
* **Availability for rehearsals** (text, required): List the days and times you are available to participate in rehearsals.
* **Prior theater experience** (text, optional): Briefly list your relevant theater experience to help us understand your qualifications.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (text, required): List the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Special requirements** (text, optional): List any special requirements you may have, such as accessibility or accommodation needs.
