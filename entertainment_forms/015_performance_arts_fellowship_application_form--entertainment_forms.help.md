# Performance Arts Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the performance arts fellowship application form, step by step. The form is designed to be completed by artists or organizations that are submitting a proposal for the grant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Start by filling out your artist background information, which includes your resume and a brief artist statement.
2.  Next, describe your project proposal, including your project budget.
3.  Select the type of art form your proposal is related to (Theater, Music, Dance, or Other).
4.  Choose the type of grant you are applying for (Residency Grant, Production Grant, or Other).
5.  Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
6.  Enter your submitter information, including your name, title, organization, and department.
7.  Finally, confirm that you agree to the terms of the grant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Background** (`Artist Background`, `note`, `false`): This section is where you can upload your resume and write a brief statement about your background as an artist.
* **Project Proposal** (`Project Proposal`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can describe your project, including your goals and objectives.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can upload your resume, which should include your experience and qualifications as an artist.
* **Artist Statement** (`Artist Statement`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can write a brief statement about your artistic philosophy and goals.
* **Project Budget** (`Project Budget`, `number`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your project budget, which should include all expenses related to your project.
* **Organization Info** (`Organization Info`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter information about the organization you are applying on behalf of.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `select_one`, `false`): This section is where you can select your type of contact information (Artist or Organization, Organization, or Other).
* **Art Form Type** (`Art Form Type`, `select_multiple`, `false`): This section is where you can select the type of art form your project is related to (Theater, Music, Dance, or Other).
* **Grant Type** (`Grant Type`, `select_one`, `false`): This section is where you can select the type of grant you are applying for (Residency Grant, Production Grant, or Other).
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Name** (`Submitter Name`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (`Submitter Email`, `email`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`Submitter Phone`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Organization** (`Submitter Organization`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter the name of your organization.
* **Submitter Position** (`Submitter Position`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your job title or position.
* **Submitter Title** (`Submitter Title`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your title or honorifics.
* **Submitter Department** (`Submitter Department`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter the department you are applying on behalf of.
* **Submitter Address** (`Submitter Address`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your address.
* **Submitter City** (`Submitter City`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your city.
* **Submitter State** (`Submitter State`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your state.
* **Submitter Zip** (`Submitter Zip`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your zip code.
* **Submitter Country** (`Submitter Country`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter your country.
* **Submitter Additional Info** (`Submitter Additional Info`, `text`, `false`): This section is where you can enter any additional information about yourself or your organization.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`Submitter Agreement`, `select_one`, `false`): This section is where you can select your agreement status (True, False, or Undetermined).

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Proofread your application before submitting it to ensure it is accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [contact email or phone number].
* Please note that this form is intended to be completed by artists or organizations that are submitting a proposal for the grant, not by internal staff members of the organization.
* Ensure that you agree to the terms of the grant before submitting your application.
