# Popstar Singing Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in the Popstar Singing Contest. Please provide the required information to register for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed, which are indicated by an asterisk (*).
3. If a field is optional, you can leave it blank if you don't have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *required*): Enter your valid email address.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, *optional*): If you wish to provide your birthdate, please enter it in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Popstar Name** (`popstar_name`, `text`, *optional*): If you have a nickname or stage name associated with your performance, enter it here.
* **Song Title** (`song`, `text`, *optional*): Enter the title of the song you will be performing.
* **Song Link** (`song_link`, `text`, *optional*): If you have a link to your song, enter it here.
* **Upload Song** (`upload_song`, `text`, *optional*): If you have a file to upload, select the correct file type and upload it.
* **Upload Song Label** (`upload_song_label`, `text`, *optional*): Enter a brief description of the song you uploaded.

## Tips
* Please ensure your email address is valid and able to receive emails from us.
* If you're unable to complete the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We'll be in touch shortly after you submit the form to confirm your registration.
