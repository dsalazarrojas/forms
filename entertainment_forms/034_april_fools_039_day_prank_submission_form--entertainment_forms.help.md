# April Fools Day Prank Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect prank ideas for April Fools' Day. It's meant for sharing creative and humorous ideas with others. Please ensure your prank idea doesn't cause harm or discomfort to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your department or team (optional).
3. Describe your prank idea in the `Detailed Description` field.
4. Identify the person or group your prank is targeted at.
5. Estimate the execution time for your prank.
6. List any materials or resources needed for the prank.
7. Assess the safety of your prank (see safety guidelines below).
8. Choose a humor category for your prank (optional).
9. Confirm if you've obtained approval from the target (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Information**: This is a brief introduction to your submission, including a personal note if you'd like to share additional context.
* **Your Name**: This is the name you'd like to be associated with your prank idea. It's essential to provide your real name as it will help us contact you regarding your submission.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for feedback or questions about your prank idea.
* **Department or Team**: If you're submitting on behalf of a department or team, enter their name here (optional).
* **Prank Title or Name**: Give a title or name to your prank idea. This will help us identify and categorize your submission.
* **Detailed Description**: Describe your prank idea in detail. Explain how you plan to execute it, what's funny about it, and any other relevant information.
* **Who is the Target**: Identify the person or group your prank is targeted at. This is essential for us to assess potential risks and safety concerns.
* **Estimated Execution Time**: Estimate how long it will take to execute your prank. This will help us plan and schedule prank events.
* **Materials or Resources Needed**: List any materials or resources required to execute your prank.
* **Safety Assessment**: Select the level of safety for your prank:
	+ Completely Safe: No risk of physical or emotional harm.
	+ Mostly Safe: Some risk of physical or emotional harm, but it's unlikely.
	+ Some Risks: Moderate risk of physical or emotional harm.
	+ Unsafe: High risk of physical or emotional harm.
* **Humor Category**: Choose a category for your prank (optional):
	+ Funny: A classic prank that's likely to make someone laugh.
	+ Hilarious: An extreme prank that's likely to be very funny, but may not be suitable for all audiences.
	+ Classic: A traditional prank that's been done before.
	+ Quirky: A unique prank that's not like anything else.
	+ Creative: A prank that's original and innovative.
* **Approved by Target**: Confirm if you've obtained approval from the target before submitting (if applicable).

## Tips
* Be creative and original in your prank idea. Avoid copying or imitating existing pranks.
* Ensure your prank doesn't cause harm or discomfort to anyone.
* If you're unsure about any field, review this guide carefully before submitting.
