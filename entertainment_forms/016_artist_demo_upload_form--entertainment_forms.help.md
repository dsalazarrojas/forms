# Artist Demo Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Demo Upload Form is designed to collect information and assets from artists or bands for review and evaluation by industry professionals. It is intended to be used by artists or their representatives to submit demos or projects for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your artist or band name in the "Artist or Band Name" field.
2. Provide the primary contact name and email address of the person submitting the demo.
3. Enter the contact phone number of the primary contact person.
4. Enter a brief artist biography, 50-150 words.
5. Select the music genre(s) that best describe your music.
6. Enter the year your band or artist formed.
7. List the names and instruments/roles of band members or collaborators.
8. Provide social media links (Spotify, Instagram, YouTube, etc.).
9. Attach the demo file or audio upload.
10. Enter a title for the demo or project.
11. Describe the demo or project in a brief paragraph.
12. Enter the number of tracks included in the demo.
13. Provide a listening link or streaming URL (Spotify, SoundCloud, etc.).
14. Enter production credits (producer, engineer, recording studio).
15. List any previous releases or notable work.
16. Identify the target audience or intended label genre.
17. Specify the rights to this demo and permissions.
18. Add any additional notes for reviewers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Band Name** (`artist_name`): Enter the name of the artist or band that is submitting this demo.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact`): Provide the name of the person submitting this demo.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Artist Biography** (`artist_bio`): Enter a brief artist biography, 50-150 words.
* **Music Genre(s)** (`genre`): Select the music genre(s) that best describe your music.
* **Year Band or Artist Formed** (`formation_year`): Enter the year your band or artist formed.
* **Band Members or Collaborators** (`band_members`): List the names and instruments/roles of band members or collaborators.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`): Provide social media links (Spotify, Instagram, YouTube, etc.).
* **Demo File or Audio Upload** (`demo_file`): Attach the demo file or audio upload.
* **Demo Title or Project Name** (`demo_title`): Enter a title for the demo or project.
* **Demo Description** (`demo_description`): Describe the demo or project in a brief paragraph.
* **Number of Tracks Included** (`track_count`): Enter the number of tracks included in the demo.
* **Listening Link or Streaming URL** (`listening_link`): Provide a listening link or streaming URL (Spotify, SoundCloud, etc.).
* **Production Credits** (`production_credits`): Enter production credits (producer, engineer, recording studio).
* **Previous Releases or Notable Work** (`previous_releases`): List any previous releases or notable work.
* **Target Audience or Intended Label Genre** (`target_audience`): Identify the target audience or intended label genre.
* **Rights to This Demo and Permissions** (`submission_rights`): Specify the rights to this demo and permissions.
* **Additional Notes for Reviewers** (`additional_notes`): Add any additional notes for reviewers.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled accurately and completely to avoid any delays or issues with the review process.
