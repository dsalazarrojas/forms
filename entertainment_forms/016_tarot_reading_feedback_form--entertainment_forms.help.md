# Tarot Reading Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients who have received a tarot reading. Please fill out this form to provide your comments, rating, and other relevant details about your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (1, text): Enter your name as you want to be addressed. This will help us to know who you are and address any correspondence with you directly.
* **Reading Type** (2, select_one): Choose the type of reading you received (e.g., "Reading Type 1", "Reading Type 2", "Reading Type 3"). This helps us to understand the type of reading you had.
* **Reading Level** (3, select_multiple): Select the levels of depth in the reading you received. You can choose one or multiple levels. For example, if you received a reading with multiple layers, select all the levels that apply.
* **Reading Date** (4, date): Enter the date you received the tarot reading. This will help us to track the timing of your reading.
* **Comments** (5, note): Provide any comments or suggestions you have about your tarot reading experience. This is an opportunity for you to share your thoughts and help us improve our services.
* **Rating** (6, number): Rate your overall experience with the tarot reading. This will help us to evaluate the quality of our services.
* **Contact Us** (7, email): If you have any further comments, questions, or need to contact us, you can enter your email address here. We'll get back to you as soon as possible.
* **Email** (8, email): Enter your email address if you would like to receive further information from us or updates about our services.
* **Phone** (9, text): If you would like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when answering the fields.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us through the "Contact Us" field.
* If you would like to be contacted back, fill out the "Email" and/or "Phone" fields.
