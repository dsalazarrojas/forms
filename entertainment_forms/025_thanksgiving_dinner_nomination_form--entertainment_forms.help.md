# Thanksgiving_Dinner_Nomination_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Thanksgiving_Dinner_Nomination_Form" is a form used to nominate someone or an organization for a special award or recognition related to Thanksgiving. This form gathers basic contact information and nomination details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (name, phone number, email, and address).
2. Provide your nomination name and details (nomination name, date, time, and message).
3. Select one of the options from the provided list (Select one of the following options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as the person or organization nominating someone or something.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number associated with the name provided.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with the name provided.
* **Address**: Enter the address associated with the name provided.
* **Nomination name**: Enter the name of the person or organization being nominated.
* **Nomination date**: Enter the date of the nomination, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Nomination time**: Enter the time of the nomination, in the format 12:00 AM or 11:59 PM.
* **Message**: Enter a message or description explaining why the person or organization is being nominated.
* **Select one of the following options**: Choose one of the provided options to provide additional context or explanation for the nomination.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information.
* Provide a clear and concise message or description for the nomination.
* Take a moment to review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
