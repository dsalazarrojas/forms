# Musical Audition Self Tape Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Musical Audition Self Tape Submission" form is a tool for performers to submit their own auditions for musical performances. This guide will walk you through the form fields and help you complete it successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **performer details**, including your name, contact information, and any relevant details about your performance experience.
2. **Upload your self-tape**. This is an optional field, but including your self-tape will give the reviewing team a better understanding of your performance.
3. **Provide additional materials** that will help the reviewing team understand your abilities, such as music sheets or other relevant documents.
4. Choose the **video type** of your self-tape (Video, Audio, or Other).
5. Decide whether you have **video files** to submit, and select accordingly.
6. **Add a resume** and a **headshot** if you have them available.
7. **Enter your contact information**, including a valid email address and phone number.
8. Optionally, provide a link to your **resume** and/or **headshot** if you have them in a digital format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Details**: This is the first field where you can enter your name, contact information, and any relevant performance experience. Enter your details accurately and thoroughly.
* **Upload Self Tape**: This field is optional but recommended. If you have a self-tape, you can upload it here to showcase your performance.
* **Upload Additional Materials**: If you have music sheets, scripts, or other relevant documents that will help the reviewing team understand your performance, you can upload them here.
* **Email**: Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Video Description**: Provide a brief description of your self-tape, including what type of performance it showcases.
* **Video Length**: Indicate the length of your self-tape.
* **Video Type**: Choose the type of video that best represents your performance (Video, Audio, or Other).
* **Video Files**: If you have video files to submit, select "Yes" and upload them. If not, select "No".
* **Resume**: If you have a resume, please upload it here.
* **Headshot**: If you have a headshot, please upload it here.
* **Resume Link**: If you have a digital resume, you can provide a link to it here.
* **Headshot Link**: If you have a digital headshot, you can provide a link to it here.
