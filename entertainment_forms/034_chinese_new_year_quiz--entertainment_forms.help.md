# Chinese New Year Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a simple quiz that tests the knowledge of users on the basics of Chinese New Year traditions and symbolism.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the participant's name (optional).
2. Answer the four questions:
	* Which animal represents the current zodiac year (required).
	* What color is often associated with Chinese New Year (required).
	* What is commonly given in red envelopes (required).
	* Traditional new year food often served (required).
3. Optionally, enter a numeric score achieved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (text)**: This field is for entering the participant's name. It is optional and can be left blank if not providing a name is preferred.
* **Which animal represents the current zodiac year (select_one)**:
	+ Choose the correct animal representing the current zodiac year.
	+ Options are: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit.
* **What color is often associated with Chinese New Year (select_one)**:
	+ Choose the correct color associated with Chinese New Year.
	+ Options are: Red, Blue, Green, Black.
* **What is commonly given in red envelopes (select_one)**:
	+ Choose the common item given in red envelopes during Chinese New Year.
	+ Options are: Candy, Money, Fruit, Flowers.
* **Traditional new year food often served (select_one)**:
	+ Choose the traditional food often served during Chinese New Year.
	+ Options are: Dumplings, Pizza, Tacos, Soup.
* **Score (number)**: This field is optional and is for entering a numeric score achieved.
