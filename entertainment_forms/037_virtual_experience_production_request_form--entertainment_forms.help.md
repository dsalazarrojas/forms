# Virtual Experience Production Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request a virtual experience production for an event or project. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to help us produce high-quality virtual experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all required fields**: Please fill out all fields marked as required (*).
2. **Enter accurate information**: Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out all required fields, click the **Submit** button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Requirements** (`production_requirements`, `number`, required): This field is used to specify any specific requirements for the virtual experience production, such as equipment or software needed.
* **Production Schedule** (`production_schedule`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more options to indicate the schedule for the virtual experience production.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `email`, required): Enter the name and email address of the contact person responsible for the virtual experience production.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person responsible for the virtual experience production.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person responsible for the virtual experience production.
* **Production Team** (`production_team`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you require a dedicated production team for your virtual experience.
* **Production Type** (`production_type`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you are producing a virtual experience and "No" otherwise.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or information that may be helpful for the production team.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the virtual experience event.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the virtual experience event.
* **Venue** (`venue`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the virtual experience event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the virtual experience event.
* **Event Link** (`event_link`, `text`, required): Enter a link to the virtual experience event.
* **Event Image URL** (`event_image_url`, `text`, required): Enter a URL to an image of the virtual experience event.
* **Event Video URL** (`event_video_url`, `text`, required): Enter a URL to a video of the virtual experience event.
* **Event Audio URL** (`event_audio_url`, `text`, required): Enter a URL to an audio of the virtual experience event.
* **Event File URL** (`event_file_url`, `text`, required): Enter a URL to a file related to the virtual experience event.
* **Event Additional Notes** (`event_additional_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or information that may be helpful for the production team.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, `text`, required): Enter the terms of service for the virtual experience event.
* **Terms of Service Agreement** (`terms_of_service_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have read and agreed to the terms of service.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click the **Submit** button to send your request.

## Tips
