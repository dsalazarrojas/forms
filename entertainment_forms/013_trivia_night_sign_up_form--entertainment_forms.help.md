# trivia_night_sign_up_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about teams participating in a trivia night event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the team name and team size sections.
2. Provide the phone numbers for each team member, including the team lead.
3. If you have more than five team members, provide the names and phone numbers for each additional member.
4. Add any special instructions or equipment your team may need to bring.
5. Review and submit the form once you've completed all the required fields.
