# Price List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Price List" form is a simple text-based form designed to collect information about different menu options or catering services. It appears to be intended for a restaurant or food-related business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections, which appear to be labeled with different menu options or catering services.
2. For each section, fill in the text fields with the relevant information about the corresponding menu option or service.
3. You can add or remove sections as needed, depending on the number of menu options or services you wish to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **build_your_own_menu** (text, required: false): Enter the name or description of your menu option.
- **fully_mobile_catering** (text, required: false): Enter the name or description of your fully mobile catering service.
- **build_your_own_price_list** (text, required: false): Enter the name or description of your build-your-own price list option.
- **fully_mobile** (text, required: false): Enter the name or description of your fully mobile option.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling in the text fields, as this information will be displayed in the form's output.
- You can add or remove sections as needed, depending on the number of menu options or services you wish to include.
