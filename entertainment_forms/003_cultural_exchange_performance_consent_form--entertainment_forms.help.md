# Cultural Exchange Performance Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants and emergency contacts for a cultural exchange performance. It serves as a record of their understanding and agreement to be part of the performance, as well as to have them acknowledge the roles they will be taking on.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and fill out the required information for both the participant and emergency contact sections.
2. For the participant section:
	* Enter your participant details in the first field.
	* If applicable, select your roles in the second field (e.g., performer, crew, etc.).
3. For the emergency contact section:
	* Enter the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
4. Review and acknowledge the form by signing and dating the signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information as requested.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information (phone number, address, etc.) of the person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Roles** (Roles, select multiple, required/optional): Select all roles you will be taking on during the performance (e.g., performer, crew, etc.).
* **Signature** (Signature, note, required/optional): Sign and date this field to acknowledge that you have read and understand this consent form.
* **Participant Signature** (Participant Signature, note, required/optional): Sign and date this field as the participant to confirm their understanding and agreement.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (Emergency Contact Signature, note, required/optional): Sign and date this field to confirm that the emergency contact has been informed and agrees to be contacted in case of an emergency.
