# Movie Selection Customer Opinion Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Movie Selection Customer Opinion Assessment form is used to collect customer opinions and feedback on their movie selections, helping the entertainment company to better understand customer preferences and create content that caters to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Please answer the questions honestly to provide valuable insights.
- Select your ratings and comments accurately.
- Indicate your favorite genre.
- Enter your email and phone number (optional).
- Choose your selected movies accurately (if applicable).
- For the Rating Frequency question, choose from the options provided or enter a custom answer in the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Movie Selection**: A brief description of the movies you have recently selected or have been interested in.
- **Rating**: On a scale of 1-10, rate how you liked the movie(s) you selected.
- **Comments**: Share any comments or feedback you have about the movie(s).
- **Selected Movies**: Choose the movie(s) you have selected from the list provided.
- **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Favorite Genre**: Choose your favorite genre from the options provided.
- **Rating Frequency**: Choose how often you rate movies on a scale of "Good" to "Fair", or enter a custom answer in the "Other" field.
- **Rating Frequency Other**: If you selected "Other" for the previous question, provide a brief description of your rating frequency.
