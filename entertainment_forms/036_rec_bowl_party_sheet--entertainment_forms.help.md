# RecBowlPartySheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RecBowlPartySheet form is designed to collect party planning information from users. This form helps the company's party planning team to efficiently gather all necessary details for a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the party details on the first page.
2. Select a date and time for the party.
3. Enter the number of guests attending the party.
4. Provide a valid email address for contact purposes.
5. Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
6. Add any relevant notes about the party.
7. Finally, select the "Book" option to submit the party planning form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **party_details** (party_details, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the party to help the party planning team understand the nature of the event.
* **party_time** (party_time, time, not required): Select the time of the party. Please use the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **party_date** (party_date, date, not required): Choose a date for the party.
* **guests** (guests, number, not required): Enter the expected number of guests attending the party.
* **email** (email, email, not required): Enter a valid email address where the party planning team can contact you.
* **phone** (phone, text, not required): Provide your phone number for additional contact information.
* **note** (note, note, not required): Add any relevant notes about the party, such as special requests or requirements.
* **submit** (submit, select_one, not required): Select "Book" to submit the party planning form.
