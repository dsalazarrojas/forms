# Guest Speaker Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Speaker Intake Form is used to collect information about potential guest speakers for events, conferences, or presentations. This form helps to gather relevant details about the speaker, including their name, contact information, and contract specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Use the available options for the "Speaker Type" field to select the appropriate category (Guest, Host, or Performer).
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4. Enter your contract date and time in the "Contract Date" and "Contract Time" fields, respectively.
5. Select a number for the "Contract Duration" field to indicate the length of the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Contract Signed**: Enter a brief description of the contract, if applicable.
* **Speaker Type**: Select the type of speaker you are (Guest, Host, or Performer).
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself that you would like to include.
* **Contract Date**: Enter the date of your contract in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Contract Time**: Enter the time of your contract.
* **Contract Duration**: Select the length of your contract in days.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your contact information and contract specifics for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about a field, you can always ask the event organizer for clarification.
