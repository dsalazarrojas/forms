# Entertainment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather accurate and detailed information for entertainment bookings. Please fill it out carefully and thoroughly to ensure smooth processing of your booking request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Entertainment Act Information" page and fill out the following fields:
	* **Act Name**: Enter the name of the entertainment act you are booking.
	* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the entertainment act or representative.
	* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the entertainment act or representative.
2. Select the "Performance Date" field and choose the date of the performance.
3. Enter the start and end times of the performance in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
4. Choose the act length in the "Act Length" field.
5. Select the act type from the options provided in the "Act Type" field (e.g., solo, duo, group, or other).
6. If applicable, enter the venue address and name in the "Venue Address" and "Venue Name" fields.
7. Add any additional details about the performance in the "Description" field.
8. If applicable, provide contact details of the entertainment agent or representative in the "Contact Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Act Name** (`entertainment_booking_form`, text, required): Enter the name of the entertainment act you are booking.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the entertainment act or representative.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address of the entertainment act or representative.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the performance.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the performance.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the performance.
* **Act Length** (`act_length`, number, required): Choose the length of the performance.
* **Act Type** (`act_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of act (e.g., solo, duo, group, or other).
* **Venue Address** (`venue_address`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the address of the venue.
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the venue.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Add any additional details about the performance.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, note, optional): If applicable, provide contact details of the entertainment agent or representative.
* **Booking Agent** (`booking_agent`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the entertainment agent or representative.

## Tips

* Please double-check the information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the booking team for assistance.
