# Song Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Song Request Form is a tool used to submit requests for specific songs to be played at events. This form collects information from event organizers and requesters, and the team reviews and assigns a suitable song to the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the song you are requesting in the "Song Title" field.
2. Type in the name of the artist who performs the song in the "Artist" field.
3. Select the type of event where the song will be played from the "Event Type" options (Event, Live, Online, or On-demand).
4. Enter the requester's phone number in the "Requester's Phone" field.
5. Enter the requester's email address in the "Requester's Email" field.
6. Type in the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
7. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
8. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
9. Type in your name in the "Requester's Name" field.
10. If applicable, enter your organization's name in the "Requester's Organization" field.
11. Select the tool that will be assigned to this event from the "Assigned Tool" options (Chatjimmy or Chatbot).
12. Type in the name of the event organizer in the "Event Organizer's Name" field.
13. Enter the email address of the event organizer in the "Event Organizer's Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Song Title** (`song_title`, required): Enter the title of the song you are requesting.
* **Artist** (`song_artist`, required): Type in the name of the artist who performs the song.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, required): Select the type of event where the song will be played. Options are Event, Live, Online, or On-demand.
* **Requester's Phone** (`song_requester_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the song.
* **Requester's Email** (`song_requester_email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person requesting the song.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, optional): Type in the name of the event where the song will be played.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Requester's Name** (`song_requester_name`, optional): Type in your name.
* **Requester's Organization** (`song_requester_organization`, optional): If you are representing an organization, type in its name.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, required): Select the tool that will be assigned to this event from the options provided (Chatjimmy or Chatbot).
* **Event Organizer's Name** (`event_organizer_name`, optional): Type in the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer's Email** (`event_organizer_email`, optional): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
