# Band Media Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Band Media Upload Form is designed to collect necessary information from bands and artists seeking to be featured on our music platform. This form will help us understand your band's profile, promotional materials, and requirements for performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the correct genre for your band from the dropdown menu.
3. Ensure that the submission date is accurate and corresponds to the date when you sent in your materials.
4. If you have any additional information or attachments, please provide them in the corresponding fields.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Band name** (*): Enter the official name of your band or act.
* **Primary contact person** (*): Enter the name and email address of your band's manager or representative.
* **Contact email address** (*): Enter the email address we should contact for press communications.
* **Contact phone number**: Enter an optional phone number for urgent contact.
* **Primary genre** (*): Select the genre that best represents your band from the dropdown menu.
* **Band biography**: Enter a short bio for promotional use.
* **Press photos URL**: Enter the link to high-resolution photos or a location where we can find them.
* **Music streaming links**: Enter links to your music streaming platforms (e.g., Spotify, SoundCloud, Bandcamp).
* **Sample tracks description**: Enter the titles and brief descriptions of representative tracks.
* **Rider requirements**: Enter any basic technical and hospitality needs for your performance.
* **Submission date** (*): Enter the date when you sent in your materials.

## Tips
* Please keep your bio and descriptions concise and up-to-date.
* Make sure to check your contact information for accuracy.
* Attach any necessary documents or photos as links or uploads.
* Double-check your submission date to avoid any delays.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us via email.
