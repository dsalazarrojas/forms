# Artist Feature Segment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request features of artists or bands for an entertainment segment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the artist or band name in the "Artist or Band Name" field.
2. Enter the primary contact person's name in the "Primary Contact Name" field.
3. Enter the contact email address in the "Contact Email Address" field.
4. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone Number" field.
5. Select the type of feature segment you would like to create from the options in the "Requested Feature Segment Type" field.
6. Enter the preferred duration of the segment in minutes in the "Preferred Segment Duration - Minutes" field.
7. Describe what you would like the segment to showcase in the "Content Description" field.
8. If applicable, enter your music genre or art category in the "Music Genre or Art Category" field.
9. If desired, provide a brief artist biography in the 50-150 word limit of the "Brief Artist Biography" field.
10. If you have any recent releases or projects, enter them in the "Recent Releases or Projects" field.
11. If you have social media handles, enter them in the "Social Media Handles and Links" field.
12. Enter your preferred filming location in the "Preferred Filming Location" field.
13. Provide a date range for filming in the "Filming Availability Window" field.
14. If you have a specific team size, enter it in the "Expected Team Size for Filming" field.
15. If you have any special equipment or production requirements, enter them in the "Special Equipment or Production Requirements" field.
16. Select the music rights clearance status from the options in the "Music Rights Clearance Status" field.
17. Select whether you would like to archive the segment from the options in the "Rights to Archive Segment" field.
18. Finally, add any additional notes or requests in the "Additional Notes or Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist or Band Name** (artist_name, text, required): Enter the name of the artist or band.
* **Primary Contact Name** (primary_contact, text, required): Enter the primary contact person's name.
* **Contact Email Address** (contact_email, email, required): Enter the contact email address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (contact_phone, text, required): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Requested Feature Segment Type** (segment_type, select_one, required): Select the type of feature segment you would like to create.
* **Preferred Segment Duration - Minutes** (segment_duration, number, required): Enter the preferred duration of the segment in minutes.
* **Content Description** (content_description, text, required): Describe what you would like the segment to showcase.
* **Music Genre or Art Category** (music_genre, text, optional): If applicable, enter your music genre or art category.
* **Brief Artist Biography** (artist_bio, text, required): Enter a brief biography of the artist or band, limited to 50-150 words.
* **Recent Releases or Projects** (recent_releases, text, optional): If applicable, enter recent releases or projects.
* **Social Media Handles and Links** (social_media, text, optional): If applicable, enter social media handles and links.
* **Preferred Filming Location** (filming_location, text, required): Enter your preferred filming location.
* **Filming Availability Window** (filming_availability, text, required): Provide a date range for filming.
* **Expected Team Size for Filming** (team_size, number, optional): If applicable, enter the expected team size for filming.
* **Special Equipment or Production Requirements** (special_requirements, text, optional): If applicable, enter any special equipment or production requirements.
* **Music Rights Clearance Status** (rights_clearance, select_one, required): Select the music rights clearance status from the options.
* **Rights to Archive Segment** (archive_rights, select_one, optional): Select whether you would like to archive the segment from the options.
* **Additional Notes or Requests** (additional_notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or requests.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and clear information in each field to ensure a successful feature segment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
