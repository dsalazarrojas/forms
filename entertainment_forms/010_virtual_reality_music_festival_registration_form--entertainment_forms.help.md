This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in attending the virtual reality music festival. It is used to collect personal data and preferences of the attendees. The form will help us to:

    - Verify the user's identity and check if they have provided correct information before attending the festival.
    - Gather information about the user's musical preferences to better understand the type of music they enjoy.
    - Collect any additional notes or comments from the attendees that they would like to share with us.
    - Track the number of users who attend the festival using a unique ID.

Please note that some fields are optional and can be filled at your discretion. Ensure you fill out all required fields before proceeding with your registration.

# virtual_reality_music_festival_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to register for the virtual reality music festival. It will collect necessary information about you and your preferences to make sure you have a great experience at the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as **required**) with your true information.
2. If you have any additional information or notes, please use the "note" field.
3. If you have a favorite band, please enter it in the "favorite_band" field.
4. If you want to receive any assigned tool for the festival, please select it from the "assigned_tool" field.
5. Finally, click on the "submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **last_name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address to receive updates about the festival.
* **phone_number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number to contact you in case of any updates or changes.
* **music_genre** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of music you enjoy the most.
	+ Pop
	+ Rock
	+ Jazz
	+ Classical
* **favorite_band** (Text, Optional): Enter your favorite band (if applicable).
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **time_of_birth** (Time, Optional): Enter your time of birth (if applicable).
* **note** (Note, Optional): Share any additional notes or comments with us about your experience with the festival.
* **assigned_tool** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose any assigned tool you would like to use at the festival.
	+ Chatjimmy
	+ Other (Please enter your own description)
* **output_file** (Text, Optional): Enter any output file if you have any relevant information to share with us.
* **category** (Text, Optional): Enter your category of interest (if applicable).
* **title** (Text, Optional): Enter any title you would like to use for your profile.
* **form_ids** (Number, Required): Enter a unique number assigned to you by our team.
* **description** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description about yourself to help us better understand who you are.

Note: Please make sure to review your information before clicking "submit" to ensure it is accurate and complete. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
