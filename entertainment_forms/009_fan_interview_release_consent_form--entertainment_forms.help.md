# fan_interview_release_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fan_interview_release_consent_form is used to obtain consent for an interview or recording, ensuring the participant understands and agrees to the terms, including release dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
*   Fill out all required fields. 
*   If the participant is a minor, ensure the parent or guardian signs the form. 
*   Review and agree to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter the participant's name.
*   **Participant Consent (participant_consent)**: Select one or more options regarding consent for the interview or recording.
*   **Parent/Guardian Consent (parent_guardian_consent)**: If the participant is a minor, enter the parent or guardian's name. This field is not required for adults.
*   **Participant Signature (participant_signature)**: The participant's signature, usually a handwritten signature.
*   **Release Date (release_date)**: Enter the date when the participant would like the interview or recording to be released. If left blank, it will be set to the current date.
*   **Recording Date (recording_date)**: Enter the date the interview or recording took place.
*   **Published On (published_on)**: Enter the date the interview or recording will be published.
*   **Participant Age (participant_age)**: Enter the participant's age at the time of the recording.
*   **Parent/Guardian Signature (parent_guardian_signature)**: The parent or guardian's signature, usually a handwritten signature.
*   **Media Contact (media_contact)**: Enter any additional contact information for the media or other relevant parties.
