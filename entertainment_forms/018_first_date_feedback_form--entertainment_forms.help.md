# First Date Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences on a first date. The purpose of this form is to gather information about how the date went, what the user thought of the matchmaker, and their overall impression of the date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields. Each page of the form has one or more fields that you need to fill out. You can navigate between pages using the buttons at the top of the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First date
* **First date** (Required): Enter the date of the first date you had.
*   **Matchmaker** (Optional): Enter a brief description of the matchmaker who set up the date.
*   **First impression** (Optional): Rate your first impression of the date on a scale from Very positive to Very negative.
*   **Communication** (Optional): Rate the communication on the date on a scale from Good to Poor.
*   **Physical attractiveness** (Optional): Rate the physical attractiveness on a scale from 1 to ? (Note: Please rate based on your own subjective experience).
*   **Date location** (Optional): Enter a brief description of the date location.
*   **Overall experience** (Optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to ? (Note: Please rate based on your subjective experience).
*   **Would date again** (Optional): Select yes, no, or undecided if you would consider dating again.

## Tips
*   Please fill out this form as accurately and honestly as possible.
*   You can rate your answers based on your subjective experience.
*   If you have any additional feedback, please feel free to add it in the space provided.
*   Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
