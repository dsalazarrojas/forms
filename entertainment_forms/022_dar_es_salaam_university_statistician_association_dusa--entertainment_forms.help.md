# Dar Es Salaam University Statistician Association (DUSA) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students or staff at Dar Es Salaam University who are involved in statistics-related activities. The collected data will help the association better understand the interests, skills, and statistics gathered by its members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. This form is divided into multiple sections.
2. Please fill out each section with as much detail as possible.
3. You can submit the form when you have finished filling out all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **university_statistician_form**: Please provide a brief description of your involvement or interest in statistics.
* **student_persuading_stats**: Describe your most significant statistics-related activity or achievement that you are proud of.
* **gain_knowledge**: Share any statistics-related knowledge you gained recently or are currently pursuing.
* **statistics_collected**: Summarize any notable statistics you have collected or are currently collecting.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out each section.
* You can use the space provided for each field to elaborate on your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
