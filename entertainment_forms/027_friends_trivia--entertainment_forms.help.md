# Friends Trivia - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Friends Trivia form is a fun and engaging tool designed to test your knowledge about friends. The form asks a series of questions about friends, covering various categories. This form is a great way to assess your friends' knowledge and create friendly competition among your social circle.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Friends Trivia form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question with the best response that comes to mind.
2. Fill in the answer fields provided for each question.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure you've answered all the questions.
4. Submit the form to get your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_name** (`text`, required/optional): This is the name of the form.
* **question_1** (`text`, required/optional): This is the first question about a friend. Try to provide a descriptive answer.
* **answer_1** (`text`, required/optional): This is where you provide your answer to question 1.
* **question_2** (`text`, required/optional): This is the second question about a friend. Think carefully before answering.
* **answer_2** (`text`, required/optional): This is where you provide your answer to question 2.
* **question_3** (`text`, required/optional): This is the third question about a friend. Be specific and honest.
* **answer_3** (`text`, required/optional): This is where you provide your answer to question 3.
* **question_4** (`text`, required/optional): This is the fourth question about a friend. Remember, only one answer is allowed.
* **answer_4** (`text`, required/optional): This is where you provide your answer to question 4.
* **question_5** (`text`, required/optional): This is the final question about a friend. Be accurate.
* **answer_5** (`text`, required/optional): This is where you provide your answer to question 5.
* **category** (`text`, required/optional): This is the category of the form.
* **form_id** (`text`, required/optional): This is the ID of the form.
* **form_name** (`text`, required/optional): This is the name of the form.
* **form_title** (`text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **form_description** (`text`, required/optional): This is the description of the form.
* **form_category** (`text`, required/optional): This is the category of the form.
* **form_assigned** (`text`, required/optional): This is the assigned tool for the form.
* **form_output_file** (`text`, required/optional): This is the output file for the form.
* **form_assigned_tool** (`text`, required/optional): This is the tool assigned to the form.
* **form_id** (`text`, required/optional): This is the unique ID for the form.
* **form_version** (`text`, required/optional): This is the version of the form.
* **form_created_date** (`text`, required/optional): This is the date the form was created.
* **form_modified_date** (`text`, required/optional): This is the date the form was last modified.
* **category_id** (`text`, required/optional): This is the ID of the category.

Note: Each question and answer field is optional, which means you don't have to answer all of them. However, the more questions you answer, the more accurate your results will be.
