# Diver Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diver Registration Form is designed to collect information from divers, including their personal details, certification status, and diving type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address (if you have one).
3. Add your Phone number (if you have one).
4. Provide your Address (if you have one).
5. List your Emergency Contact's Name and Phone number (if applicable).
6. Enter your Date of Birth.
7. Indicate your Medical Certification status (True or False).
8. Provide any relevant notes for your Medical Certification.
9. Select your Diver Type (Recreational, Competition, or Instructor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear on your diver ID and other official records.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on your diver ID and other official records.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your Email address if you have one.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address if you have one.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): List the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): List your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Medical Certification** (select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have a valid medical certification.
* **Medical Certification Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes related to your medical certification.
* **Diver Type** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following:
	+ Recreational
	+ Competition
	+ Instructor
* **Diver Certification** (text, optional): Enter your diver certification (e.g., scuba certification number).

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly, as this information will be used to create your unique diver ID and for other internal purposes.
