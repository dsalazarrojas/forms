# Music Band Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a music band, including their name, genre, and performance details. This information is likely used by music event planners or festival organizers to manage their event schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Band Name** of the music band, which is the name of the band as it appears on their promotional materials.
2. Select the **music_genre** of the band, which is the genre of music they play (e.g. Electronic, Jazz, Rock, Pop).
3. Enter the **Event Name** of the band's performance, which is the name of the specific event they will be performing at.
4. Enter the **contact_phone** number, which is the phone number of the band's manager or representative.
5. Enter the **email** address of the band's manager or representative.
6. Select the **social_media** options that apply to the band, such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.
7. Enter the **Performance Date** of the band's event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Enter the **Performance Time** of the band's event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
9. Enter the **Venue** where the band will be performing.
10. Enter the **Event Description**, which is a brief description of the event.
11. Enter the **Set List** of the band's performance.
12. Enter a brief **Bio** of the band.
13. Enter the **Event URL**, which is a URL to the event's website.
14. Enter the **Event Image URL**, which is a link to an image of the event.
15. Enter the **Band Image URL**, which is a link to an image of the band.
16. Enter the **Facebook URL**, which is a link to the band's Facebook page.
17. Enter the **Instagram URL**, which is a link to the band's Instagram page.
18. Enter the **Twitter URL**, which is a link to the band's Twitter page.
19. Enter the **Vedio URL**, which is a link to a video of the band.
20. Enter the **YouTube URL**, which is a link to the band's YouTube page.
21. Enter the **Sound URL**, which is a link to the band's soundcloud page.
22. Enter the **Website**, which is the band's website.
23. Enter the **Address**, which is the physical address of the event venue.
24. Enter the **Facebook Page**, which is the band's Facebook page.
25. If none of the above options apply, enter your answer in the **Other** field.
