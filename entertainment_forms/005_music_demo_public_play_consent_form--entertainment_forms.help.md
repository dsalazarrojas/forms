# music_demo_public_play_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather artist information for music demos that may be played publicly. It's an internal form designed to collect necessary details from artists about their music, such as consent for public play and terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide artist information in the "Artist Information" field.
2. Enter details about the song in the "Song Information" field.
3. Choose how you want the song to be played (publicly or not) in the "Play Information" field.
4. Enter any additional details in the "Additional Information" field.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Sign the form with your artist's signature and the submitter's (your) signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Information** (`artist_info`, text, required): This is the field where you can provide details about yourself as an artist.
* **Song Information** (`song_info`, text, required): Enter details about the song you're submitting, such as its title, genre, or any other relevant information.
* **Play Information** (`play_info`, select_one, required): Choose how you want the song to be played. Options are:
	+ Public Play: The song will be played publicly on the platform.
	+ Non-Public Play: The song will not be played on the platform.
	+ Other: Choose this option if you have specific preferences for the song's playability.
* **Submit Information** (`submit_info`, text, optional): This field is a general note field for any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Submit Information 2** (`submit_info_2`, note, optional): This is another note field for any additional comments or details.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional details you'd like to provide about the submission.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, text, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of submission.
* **Artist Signature** (`artist_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your artist's signature to confirm consent.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your submitter's (your) signature to confirm submission.
