# kiss_and_tell_valentines_dance_party_rsvp_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The kiss and tell valentines dance party RSVP form is designed to collect information from attendees for a specific event. This form is only for those interested in attending the kiss and tell valentines dance party.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to save your RSVP.
2. Fill in the required fields (Name and RSVP).
3. Fill in the optional fields (Event Name, Number of Guests, Date, Time, Email, Phone, and Message).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name (ID 1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the event you are RSVPing for. This will help the event host know your event name.
* **Name (ID 2)** (`text`, optional): Please enter your name. This is used to address you in any future communications about the party.
* **Number of Guests (ID 3)** (`number`, optional): If you are attending with a guest, enter the number of people in your party.
* **Date (ID 4)** (`date`, optional): If you have a preferred date for the event, enter it here.
* **Time (ID 5)** (`time`, optional): If you have a preferred time for the event, enter it here.
* **RSVP (ID 6)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" to RSVP to the kiss and tell valentines dance party. If you do not select "Yes", your RSVP will not be recorded.
* **Email (ID 7)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address. This is used to contact you about the party.
* **Phone (ID 8)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number. This is used to contact you about the party.
* **Message (ID 9)** (`text`, optional): Leave a message for the event host, such as special requests or questions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event host.
* If you're attending with a guest, make sure to include their name and number of guests in the "Name" and "Number of Guests" fields respectively.
