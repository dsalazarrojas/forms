# Halloween Costume Contest Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect votes for the Halloween costume contest. Users can choose one or more costumes from the provided options on each page and enter their contact information for verification purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Navigate through the multiple pages of the form, selecting your preferred costumes from the list of options on each page.
2. On the final page, enter your contact information: email address, age, and name.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vote for** (`Vote for`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more costumes from the list of options provided.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required): You can provide any additional comments or feedback for your chosen costume. (Note: This field is optional, but we encourage you to use it to let us know why you chose your favorite costume.)
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to help us verify your vote and get in touch with you.
* **Age** (`Age`, `number`, required): Please enter your age for verification purposes.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to help us verify your identity.
## Tips
* Make sure to enter your correct email address, age, and name for verification purposes.
* Take your time to carefully select your favorite costumes, as you can choose one or more options.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, don't hesitate to contact us.
