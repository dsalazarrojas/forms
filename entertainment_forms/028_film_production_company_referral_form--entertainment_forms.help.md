# Film Production Company Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The film production company referral form is used to collect referrals from film production companies. Referrals are used to identify potential clients and partners for the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields. 
2. Make sure to choose the correct film type from the options. 
3. Select team members with whom you would like to collaborate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client**: The client is the film production company that you are referring. Please enter the name of the company.
* **Film Details**: Please provide a brief description of the film. This will help the company understand your referral better.
* **Contact Information**: This includes the contact person, contact number, and email address of the client. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Contact Person**: The contact person is the primary contact for the client. Please enter their name.
* **Contact Number**: The contact number is the phone number of the contact person. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Email**: The email address of the contact person. Please ensure that it is a valid email address.
* **Film Production Company**: Please enter the name of the film production company.
* **Film Type**: Please select the type of film production company from the options provided. This will help the company understand the nature of your referral.
* **Team Member**: Please select all team members with whom you would like to collaborate. This will help the company understand your team's capabilities and experience.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the referral is processed correctly.
* Please do not submit duplicate or incomplete submissions.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the help desk for assistance.
* The form will help the company identify potential clients and partners for future collaboration and business opportunities. 

Note: The form fields may be subject to changes based on company's business requirements. Please refer to the help desk if you encounter any issues or have questions.
