# dance_classes_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students registering for a dance class. It is intended to be completed by students who want to enroll in a dance class and provide necessary details for the instructor and administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student details in the "Student Details" section.
2. Enter the class details you are interested in joining.
3. Provide additional course information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Details** (`Student Details`, `text`, required/optional): This section is where you will enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a student.
* **Class Details** (`Class Details`, `text`, required/optional): Here, you will provide the name or description of the class you are interested in joining.
* **Course Details** (`Course Details`, `text`, required/optional): In this section, you can add any additional information about the course you are registering for, such as the course number or date.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your spelling and grammar in the provided fields.
* If you need to register for multiple classes, you can submit multiple forms.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact us at form@gic.mx.
