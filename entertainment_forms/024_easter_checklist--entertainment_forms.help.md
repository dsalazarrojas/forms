# easter_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The easter_checklist form is designed to help users plan and organize Easter celebrations. It collects information about Easter dates, times, locations, guests, games, activities, and other relevant details. The form is likely used for internal planning and organization purposes within an organization or group.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the Easter date in the **Easter Date** field.
2. Next, select the Easter time in the **Easter Time** field.
3. Enter the location of the Easter celebration in the **Easter Location** field.
4. List the host(s) of the Easter celebration in the **Host** field.
5. Select the guests attending the event in the **Guests** field.
6. Choose the games or activities to be held during the celebration in the **Games** field.
7. Optionally, enter any Easter cooking details in the **Easter Cooking** field.
8. Describe any decorations or other relevant details in the **Decorations** field.
9. Enter any invitation information in the **Invites** field.
10. Mention any favors or gifts given during the celebration in the **Favors** field.
11. Add any notes or additional information in the **Notes** field.
12. Decide if attachments are required or not in the **Attachments** field.
13. Enter any comments or feedback about the celebration in the **Comments** field.
14. Finally, select the status of the celebration in the **Status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Easter Date**: Enter the date of the Easter celebration (e.g., April 12, 2022).
* **Easter Time**: Select the time of the Easter celebration (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Easter Location**: Enter the location of the Easter celebration (e.g., park, home, or restaurant).
* **Host**: List the host(s) of the Easter celebration (e.g., John, Jane, or multiple names).
* **Guests**: Select the guests attending the event (e.g., Guest 1, Guest 2, or multiple selections).
* **Games**: Choose the games or activities to be held during the celebration (e.g., Game 1, Game 2, or multiple selections).
* **Easter Cooking**: Optionally, enter any Easter cooking details (e.g., menu items, recipes, or cooking instructions).
* **Decorations**: Describe any decorations or other relevant details (e.g., balloons, eggs, or flowers).
* **Invites**: Enter any invitation information (e.g., invitation message, RSVP deadline, or invitation format).
* **Favors**: Mention any favors or gifts given during the celebration (e.g., small gifts, treats, or personalized items).
* **Notes**: Add any notes or additional information about the celebration (e.g., special requests, important details, or reminders).
* **Attachments**: Decide if attachments are required or not (e.g., photos, videos, or documents).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the celebration (e.g., positive feedback, suggestions, or concerns).
* **Status**: Select the status of the celebration (e.g., planned, pending, or completed).

This form guides users through planning and organizing an Easter celebration, providing a simple and straightforward way to gather necessary information.
