# Dance Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from dancers who want to participate in a dance competition. The goal is to gather necessary details about the dancer, including their contact information, dance style, and medical status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Dancer Information (fields 1-3).
2. Select your Dance Genre (field 8) and Dance Style (field 9).
3. Choose your Music Style (field 10).
4. Enter your Registration Number (field 11).
5. Select your Category (field 12) and Division (field 13).
6. Enter your Entry Fee (field 14) and any applicable Entry Fee Discount (field 15).
7. Choose your Payment Method (field 16).
8. Provide any Additional Information (field 17).
9. Confirm your agreement with the competition rules (field 18).
10. Enter any Guardian, Parent, or Emergency Contact information (fields 19-21).
11. Provide any Medical Information (field 22).
12. Confirm if you have a Medical Certificate (field 23).
13. Enter any Medical Condition (field 24).
14. Finally, submit your registration (field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dancer Information (1)**: Enter your name, and any other relevant details.
* **Dancer Name (2)**: Enter your name as it will be displayed in the competition.
* **E-mail (3)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Competition Name (5)**: This field is not currently used.
* **Competition Date (6)**: Enter the date of the competition.
* **Time (7)**: Enter the time of the competition.
* **Dance Genre (8)**: Choose one or multiple dance genres that you specialize in.
* **Dance Style (9)**: Choose the style of dance you perform (e.g., Ballet, Contemporary, Hip-Hop, etc.).
* **Music Style (10)**: Choose the type of music you perform to.
* **Registration Number (11)**: Enter your unique registration number.
* **Category (12)**: Choose your category (e.g., Junior, Senior, Adult).
* **Division (13)**: Choose your division (e.g., Junior, Senior, Adult).
* **Entry Fee (14)**: Enter the fee you are willing to pay for entry.
* **Entry Fee Discount (15)**: Enter any applicable discount amount.
* **Payment Method (16)**: Choose how you plan to pay for your entry.
* **Additional Info (17)**: Enter any additional information about your registration.
* **Dancer Agreement (18)**: Confirm that you have read and understood the competition rules.
* **Guardian Info (19)**: Enter any guardian information if applicable.
* **Parent Info (20)**: Enter any parent information if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact (21)**: Enter any emergency contact information.
* **Medical Info (22)**: Enter any medical information.
* **Medical Certificate (23)**: Confirm if you have a medical certificate.
* **Medical Condition (24)**: Enter any medical condition if applicable.
* **Submit (25)**: Complete your registration.

Note: Each field is marked as required or not, but in practice, it is highly recommended to fill all the fields to ensure a smooth competition experience.
