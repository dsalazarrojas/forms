# Music Business Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your music business proposal, including your project type, goals, target market, and contact information. Please fill out the form with as much detail as possible to ensure we can effectively evaluate and discuss your proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Music Business Proposal" field with a brief summary of your project.
2. Select the type of project you're proposing (e.g., Business, Project).
3. Provide a description of your business and its goals.
4. Specify your target market for the project.
5. Outline your marketing strategy for the project.
6. Share your financial plans and projections for the project.
7. Introduce your management team and their roles.
8. Provide the location of your project.
9. Describe your product or service.
10. Outline your product launch plan.
11. Enter your budget for the project.
12. Select the date for your project's agreement.
13. Enter your contact name, email, phone number, and website (if applicable).
14. Upload any additional information or files related to your project.
15. Specify the type and size of your uploaded file (if applicable).
16. Agree to the terms of our partnership (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Music Business Proposal** (`form_name`, text, required/optional): A brief summary of your project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required/optional): The type of project you're proposing (e.g., Business, Project).
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required/optional): A description of your business and its goals.
* **Business Goals** (`business_goal`, text, required/optional): A list of your business goals.
* **Target Market** (`target_market`, text, required/optional): The target audience or market for your project.
* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, text, required/optional): An outline of your marketing plan for the project.
* **Financials** (`financials`, text, required/optional): Your financial plans and projections for the project.
* **Management Team** (`management_team`, text, required/optional): An introduction to your management team and their roles.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): The location of your project.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required/optional): A description of your product or service.
* **Product Launch Plan** (`product_launch_plan`, text, required/optional): An outline of your product launch plan.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required/optional): Your budget for the project.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, required/optional): The timeline for your project.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Your name and contact information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Your email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Your phone number for communication.
* **Contact Website** (`contact_website`, text, required/optional): Your website for communication.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): Any additional information related to your project.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, required/optional): An opportunity to upload files related to your project.
* **File URL** (`file_url`, text, required/optional): A URL to a file related to your project (if you've already uploaded it).
* **File Size** (`file_size`, number, required/optional): The size of your uploaded file (if applicable).
* **File Type** (`file_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): The type of file you've uploaded (e.g., Audio, Video, Image, Document).
* **File Description** (`file_description`, text, required/optional): A description of your uploaded file.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, required): A confirmation that you agree to our terms of partnership.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required/optional): The date you're agreeing to our terms of partnership.

Please note that some fields are optional, but filling them out will help us better understand your proposal.
