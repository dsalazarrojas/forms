# curbside_halloween_event_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed for registration of a Halloween event, allowing participants to provide information about themselves and their plans to attend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your neighborhood name.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Choose whether you will participate in the event.
6. Add any additional notes about your participation.
7. Choose the date of the event.
8. Choose the time of the event.
9. Choose whether you will help with the event.
10. Enter the number of participants you will bring.
11. Enter any additional details about yourself.
12. Enter any comments about participating in the event.
13. Enter any comments from the event organizer.
14. Choose whether you attended the event.
15. Choose whether you dropped out of the event.
16. Enter the address of the event.
17. Enter a description of the event.
18. Enter the name of the event organizer.
19. Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
20. Enter the email address of the event organizer.
21. Enter your age.
22. Enter your gender.
23. Enter your type (e.g. "kid", "adult").
24. Enter the name of your Halloween costume.
25. Enter how spirited you are about the holiday.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **neighborhood_name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your neighborhood.
* **participant_name** (2, text, required): Enter your name.
* **phone** (3, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (4, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **will_participate** (5, select_one, required): Choose whether you will participate in the event.
* **note** (6, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your participation.
* **event_date** (7, date, required): Choose the date of the event.
* **event_time** (8, time, required): Choose the time of the event.
* **will_help** (9, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you will help with the event.
* **participant_number** (10, number, required): Enter the number of participants you will bring.
* **participant_details** (11, text, required): Enter any additional details about yourself.
* **participant_comments** (12, text, required): Enter any comments about participating in the event.
* **organizer_comments** (13, text, optional): Enter any comments from the event organizer.
* **participant_attended** (14, select_one, optional): Choose whether you attended the event.
* **participant_dropped** (15, select_one, optional): Choose whether you dropped out of the event.
* **event_address** (16, text, optional): Enter the address of the event.
* **event_description** (17, text, optional): Enter a description of the event.
* **event_organizer** (18, text, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **event_organizer_phone** (19, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **event_organizer_email** (20, email, optional): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **participant_age** (21, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **participant_gender** (22, text, optional): Enter your gender.
* **participant_type** (23, text, optional): Enter your type (e.g. "kid", "adult").
* **participant_halloween_costume** (24, text, optional): Enter the name of your Halloween costume.
* **participant_holiday_spirit** (25, text, optional): Enter how spirited you are about the holiday.
