### Setlist Submission Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about events and allow users to submit setlists for those events.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event details on page 1.
2. On page 2, enter information about the artist submission.
3. On page 3, enter additional artist submission information.
4. On page 4, enter more event details.
5. On page 5, enter the setlist information.
6. Finally, submit the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Submission** (`artist_submission`, text, required: false): Enter information about the artist or artist submission.
* **Artist Submission 2** (`artist_submission_2`, text, required: false): Enter additional information about the artist or artist submission.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): Enter details about the event.
* **Event Details 2** (`event_details_2`, text, required: false): Enter additional event details.
* **Setlist** (`setlist`, text, required: false): Enter the setlist for the event.
* **Title** (`title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the event or setlist.
* **Artist** (`artist`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the artist is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Submit the form to complete the setlist submission.
