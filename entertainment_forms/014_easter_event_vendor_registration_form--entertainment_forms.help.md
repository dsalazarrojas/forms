# easter_event_vendor_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from vendors participating in Easter events. This information will help event organizers or coordinators to keep track of event details and ensure a smooth execution of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your company name (Vendor Info 1: Company Name)
2. Indicate your business type (Vendor Info 2: Business Type)
3. Enter your business address (Vendor Info 3: Business Address)
4. Enter your phone number (Vendor Info 4: Phone Number)
5. Enter your email (Contact Info 1: Contact Email)
6. Enter your contact name (Contact Info 2: Contact Name)
7. Enter your contact phone number (Contact Info 3: Contact Phone)
8. Enter the event name (Event Info 1: Event Name)
9. Enter the start date of the event (Event Info 2: Start Date)
10. Enter the end date of the event (Event Info 3: End Date)
11. Enter the event time (Event Info 4: Time)
12. Enter the duration of the event (Event Info 5: Duration)
13. Enter the event location (Event Location)
14. Enter a brief description of your business (Vendor Info 5: Business Description)
15. Enter any payment details (Payment Details)
16. List the event participants (Event Participants)
17. Review and agree to the event terms and conditions (Agreement Terms: Terms and Conditions)
18. Review and agree to the vendor agreement (Vendor Agreement: Vendor Agreement)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Info 1: Company Name** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your company name.
* **Vendor Info 2: Business Type** (`text`, required: false): Indicate your business type.
* **Vendor Info 3: Business Address** (`text`, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **Vendor Info 4: Phone Number** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email (Contact Info 1: Contact Email)** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email.
* **Contact Info 1: Contact Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact name.
* **Contact Info 2: Contact Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Info 3: Contact Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Event Info 1: Event Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the event name.
* **Event Info 2: Start Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event Info 3: End Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Event Info 4: Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the event time.
* **Event Info 5: Duration** (`number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the event.
* **Event Location** (`text`, required: false): Enter the event location.
* **Vendor Info 5: Business Description** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Event Participants** (`text`, required: false): List the event participants.
* **Agreement Terms: Terms and Conditions** (`text`, required: false): Review and agree to the event terms and conditions.
* **Vendor Agreement: Vendor Agreement** (`text`, required: false): Review and agree to the vendor agreement.

Note: Fields marked as `required: false` are optional, but it is highly recommended to fill them out for a smoother event execution.
