# Music Promotion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Promotion Form is used to provide information about music artists and their promotional materials. It's a crucial tool for authorized personnel to efficiently collect and manage music promotion data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for "record_label" and "music_style" fields.
3. For "genre" field, select multiple options that best describe the artist's music.
4. If you're unsure about the "music_promotion_form_date" and "music_promotion_form_time" fields, use a standard 24-hour clock (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
5. Enter a brief description of the artist and their music in the "music_promotion_form_description" field.
6. Select the relevant promotional materials for the "music_promotion_form_files" field.
7. Finally, indicate the current status of the artist's music promotion in the "music_promotion_form_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Promotion Form** (`music_promotion_form`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the music promotion.
* **Artist** (`artist`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist.
* **Artist Manager** (`artist_manager`, text, required): Enter the artist's manager's name.
* **Record Label** (`record_label`, select_one, required): Select the type of record label (Major or Independent).
* **Genre** (`genre`, select_multiple, required): Select the genre(s) of music (Rock, Jazz, Classical).
* **Music Style** (`music_style`, select_one, required): Select the music style (Live or Studio).
* **Promotion Date** (`music_promotion_form_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the music promotion (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Promotion Time** (`music_promotion_form_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the music promotion (HH:MM).
* **Nickname** (`music_promotion_form_nickname`, note, required): Enter a nickname for the music promotion.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the artist's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the artist's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the artist's address.
* **Music Promotion Description** (`music_promotion_form_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the artist and their music.
* **Promotional Materials** (`music_promotion_form_files`, select_multiple, required): Select the promotional materials (Resume, Demo Reel, Press Kit).
* **Promotion Status** (`music_promotion_form_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the music promotion (Available or Not Available).
