# Movie Search Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to search for movies based on your personal preferences. Please fill out the form below to provide the necessary information to find movies that suit your taste.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields with the information that best describes your preferences. You can fill out as many or as few fields as you need, depending on your specific search criteria.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Movie Search Preferences** (`movie_search_preferences`, `text`, optional): Describe your favorite type of movie, such as action, comedy, drama, etc.
- **Content Curation** (`content_curation`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific genre or content style in mind (e.g., "family-friendly", "animated", "documentary", etc.), describe it here.
- **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, optional): If you're looking for personalized movie recommendations, mention any specific actors or directors you're interested in (e.g., "Tom Hanks", "The Marvel Cinematic Universe", etc.).
- **Film Clubs** (`film_clubs`, `text`, optional): If you're searching for movies part of a specific film club or group (e.g., "Star Wars", "Marvel", etc.).
- **Classrooms** (`classrooms`, `text`, optional): If you're looking for movies based on a specific classroom or educational setting (e.g., "elementary school", "high school", etc.).

## Tips
- You can fill out as many or as few fields as you need to narrow down your search results.
- Use clear and concise language to help our search engine find the best matches for you.
- If you have any additional preferences or specific requirements, feel free to mention them in the text fields above.
