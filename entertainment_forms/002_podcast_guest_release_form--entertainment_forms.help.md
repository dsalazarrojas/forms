# Podcast Guest Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Podcast Guest Release Form is used to obtain the necessary release from a guest speaker before recording or publishing their podcast episode. The form helps to establish the terms and conditions of their appearance, including their name, contact information, and agreement to be recorded and published.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest's name in the "Guest Name" field. This is a required field and should be filled out with the guest's full name.
2. Provide your guest's contact number in the "Contact Number" field. This field is also required and can be a phone number or email address.
3. Select the date of published on the "Published On" field.
4. Choose your agreement status from the "Agreement" dropdown menu. You can choose from "I agree", "I do not agree", or "I'm not sure".
5. Finally, sign the "Signature" field to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter the guest's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the guest's contact information, such as a phone number or email address.
* **Published On**: Select the date of publication for the podcast episode featuring this guest.
* **Agreement**: Choose whether you agree or do not agree to the terms and conditions of the podcast episode. You can also select "I'm not sure" if you need more information.
* **Signature**: Sign this field to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the podcast episode.
