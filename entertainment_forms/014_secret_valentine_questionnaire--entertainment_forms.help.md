# Secret Valentine Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Valentine Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from the sender to create a personalized and surprise gift for their loved one on Valentine's Day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as follows:

* Fill out the **Valentine Name** field with the name of the person you want to surprise.
* Fill out the **Sender Name** field with your own name, which will remain anonymous.
* In the **Gift Choice** field, select one or more of the options (Gift Card, Chocolate, Flowers, or Candy) that suits your gift preference.
* Write a personal **Message** for the gift recipient in the designated field.
* If you want to add a personal message for yourself as the sender, you can do so in the **Sender Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Valentine Name** (text, required): This is the name of the person you want to surprise with the gift.
* **Sender Name** (text, required): This is your name, which will be kept anonymous.
* **Gift Choice** (select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options for the gift you prefer.
	+ Gift Card: A gift card allows the recipient to choose their own gift.
	+ Chocolate: A classic and romantic choice.
	+ Flowers: A thoughtful and beautiful gift.
	+ Candy: A sweet treat for your loved one.
* **Message** (text, optional): Write a heartfelt message for the gift recipient.
* **Sender Message** (text, optional): This is an optional message for you to add to the gift, which will remain anonymous.

## Tips
* Be creative and personal with your gift choices and message to make it extra special.
* Consider keeping the gift choice and message anonymous to add an extra element of surprise.
* Double-check your information before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
