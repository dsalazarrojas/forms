# Event Menu Preferences Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information and preferences related to an event, ensuring that the event planning process is well-planned and enjoyable for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (if any).
2. Select your preferred options for menu preferences, dietary needs, allergies, and dietary restrictions.
3. Enter any special requests or preferences in the "Special Requests" field.
4. Choose the correct menu items for your event.
5. Select a date and time for the event.
6. Enter the event location.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Planner**: Enter the name or title of the event planner responsible for organizing this event.
* **Menu Preferences**: Specify your dietary preferences, such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.
* **Dietary Needs**: Select "Yes" if you have any dietary needs or restrictions, such as food allergies or intolerances.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or requirements for the event, such as equipment or accommodations.
* **Allergies**: Select "Yes" if you have any allergies or food restrictions.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select "Yes" if you have any dietary restrictions, such as vegetarian or vegan diets.
* **Preferences**: Enter any personal preferences or requests related to the event.
* **Menu Items**: Select the menu items you would like to be included in the event offerings.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the event.
