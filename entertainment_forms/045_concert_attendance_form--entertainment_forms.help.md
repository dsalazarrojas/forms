# Concert Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your concert attendance experience. It helps us understand your satisfaction with the event, venue, and staff, as well as any feedback or comments you may have. Please provide your honest and accurate responses to help us improve future concerts.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections, each with a specific purpose. Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter your attendance ID, which is a unique identifier for your submission.
2. Provide your full name, email address, and phone number as contact details.
3. Enter your date of birth and the concert event name.
4. Choose your ticket type and enter your ticket number.
5. Select the number of people in your party and how you purchased your ticket.
6. Rate your overall experience, sound quality, venue cleanliness, staff friendliness, and security satisfaction.
7. Indicate if you bought merchandise or food and beverages.
8. Select your arrival time and parking method (if applicable).
9. Provide your favorite performance or experience highlight.
10. Indicate if you would attend again, recommend this venue, and provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendance ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your submission, as this will be used for reference purposes.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ticket.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number as a contact method.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birth date to help us understand your age and demographics.
* **Concert Name**: Enter the name of the concert event you attended.
* **Concert Date**: Enter the date of the concert.
* **Concert Venue**: Enter the venue name and location where the concert took place.
* **Ticket Type**: Select the type of ticket you purchased (General Admission, VIP, etc.).
* **Ticket Number**: Enter your ticket number for reference.
* **Number in Party**: Select how many people were in your party attending the concert.
* **How Did You Purchase**: Select how you bought your ticket (Online Website, Box Office, etc.).
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the time you arrived at the venue, if applicable.
* **Parking Method**: Select how you parked (Venue Parking Lot, Nearby Garage, etc.).
* **Merchandise Purchase**: Indicate if you bought merchandise.
* **Food and Beverage Purchase**: Indicate if you bought food or beverages.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Sound Quality**: Rate the sound quality as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Venue Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the venue as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Staff Friendliness**: Rate the staff friendliness as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Security Satisfaction**: Rate the security services as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Favorite Performance**: Provide a brief description of your favorite part of the concert.
* **Would Attend Again**: Select if you would attend another concert at this venue.
* **Would Recommend**: Select if you would recommend this venue to others.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
