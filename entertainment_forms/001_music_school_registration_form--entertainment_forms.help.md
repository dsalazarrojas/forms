# Music School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music School Registration Form is used to collect information from students and parents to register for music classes at the school. This form helps us understand the student's contact details, class preferences, and other relevant information needed to provide the best learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Write your name and contact information in the "Parent Name" field and "Student Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred class type from the "Class Type" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the days you are available to attend class from the "Class Days" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your start time of availability in the "Start Time" field.
7. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
8. If you have a preferred music instrument, enter it in the "Music Instrument" field.
9. Enter your student's address in the "Student Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Student Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you or the student.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your full name and contact information.
* **Parent Name/Student Phone**: This is where you can enter the phone number where you can be reached.
* **Class Type**: Select one of the available class options (Music, Dance, or Theater).
* This field determines the type of class the student will enroll in.
* **Class Days**: Choose which days of the week you are available to attend class.
* Select multiple days if you are available on more than one day.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time of day you are available to attend class.
* Please note that start time can vary depending on class schedules.
* **Age**: Enter the student's age in years.
* **Music Instrument**: If you have a preferred music instrument, enter it here.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's home address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure a successful registration.
* Double-check your contact information and class preferences to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
