# Live Audience Interview Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect consent and necessary information from participants and organizers in live audience interviews. This includes obtaining the participant's details, their consent to be recorded and used in the event, their signature, and the signatures of the organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the participant details, which will help us understand who the participant is and their affiliation with the event.
2. Provide your consent by agreeing to the terms of the event, ensuring you understand the implications of being recorded and used.
3. Sign your name to confirm your consent and participation.
4. Enter the date and time of the event, as this is when the interview took place.
5. Ensure that your signature is verified by an organizer, as this is essential for maintaining the legitimacy of the event.
6. Sign as an organizer to confirm their acceptance of the participant's consent and their responsibility to maintain the legitimacy of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **participant_details** (text, optional): This section helps us understand who you are and your affiliation with this event. Please fill in your name, company or organization, and contact information.

### Consent Details
* **consent_details** (text, required): This section is where you will provide your agreement to be recorded and used in this event. Read the terms and conditions carefully before signing.

### Participant Signature
* **participant_signature** (note, required): A physical signature is essential to confirm your consent. Place your signature here, and make sure it matches your name in the participant details.

### Participant Signature Date
* **participant_signature_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event when the interview took place.

### Organizer Details
* **organizer_details** (text, optional): This section is for the event organizers to fill in their details, including their name, company or organization, and contact information.

### Organizer Signature
* **organizer_signature** (note, required): As an organizer, you are confirming that you have obtained the participant's consent and that you will maintain the legitimacy of the event.

### Organizer Signature Verification
* **organizer_signature_verification** (note, required): This section is for an additional verification of the organizer's signature.

### Event Date
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event when the interview took place.

### Event Time
* **time** (time, required): Enter the time of the event when the interview took place.

### Participant Signature Verification
* **participant_signature_verification** (note, required): This section is for an additional verification of the participant's signature.

### Organizer Signature Verification
* **organizer_signature_verification** (note, required): This is an additional check to ensure the organizer's signature is genuine. Please sign here to confirm.
