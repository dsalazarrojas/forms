# Audio Coordinator Staff Contract - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for creating an Audio Coordinator Staff Contract for a specific staff member. The contract outlines the terms and conditions of the staff member's employment, including their responsibilities, compensation, and payment schedule. Completing this form will provide a comprehensive record of the staff member's agreement to the terms and conditions of their employment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Staff Member Information" section, which includes the staff member's full name, mailing address, email, and phone number.
2. Move on to the "Project Details" section, where you will enter the project name, specific role and duties, and contract start and end dates.
3. In the "Compensation and Payment" section, select the agreed pay rate and payment schedule for the staff member.
4. In the "Legal Agreements" section, indicate whether you accept responsibility for provided equipment and agree to the non-disclosure terms.
5. Finally, sign the contract with your digital signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Member Full Name** (`staff_full_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the staff member.
* **Mailing Address** (`staff_address`, text, required): Enter the mailing address of the staff member.
* **Email Address** (`staff_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the staff member.
* **Phone Number** (`staff_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the staff member.
* **Project or Production Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project or production.
* **Specific Role and Duties** (`role_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the specific role and duties of the staff member.
* **Contract Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Agreed Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, text, required): Enter the agreed pay rate for the staff member (e.g., $50-hr or $400-day).
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, select_one, required): Select the payment schedule for the staff member (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Net 30, 50% Upfront - 50% Completion).
* **I Accept Responsibility for Provided Equipment** (`equipment_responsibility`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you accept responsibility for provided equipment (I Accept, I Decline).
* **I Agree to Non-Disclosure Terms** (`confidentiality_clause`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you agree to the non-disclosure terms (I Agree, I Do Not Agree).
* **Digital Signature (Type Name)** (`digital_signature`, text, required): Enter your digital signature (type name).
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the contract.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review and agree to all terms and conditions before signing the contract.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
