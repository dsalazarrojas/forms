# Superhero Theme Song Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Superhero Theme Song Feedback form is designed to collect data from users about their favorite superhero theme songs. This information will be used to improve the overall experience of users with similar preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details and preferences.
2. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields respectively.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have any theme song suggestions, enter them in the "Theme Song Suggestions" field.
5. Select your favorite artist or band from the list provided in the "Favorite Artist" field.
6. Provide a rating for the superhero theme song in the "Rating" field. You can rate it out of a scale, but no specific scale is provided.
7. Enter any additional comments about the theme song in the "Comments" field.
8. Choose how you would like to submit your feedback in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Theme Song Suggestions** (`theme_songs`, text, optional): Suggest any superhero theme songs you think we should consider.
* **Favorite Artist** (`favorite_artist`, select_one, optional): Choose your favorite artist or band from the list provided. Selecting a favorite artist or band will help us narrow down the options for theme songs that you might enjoy.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Provide a rating for the superhero theme song. You can rate it out of any scale, but the higher the score, the better.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the superhero theme song.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to submit your feedback. Please select only one of the options provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address in the correct format.
* If you have any comments, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any theme song suggestions, enter them in the "Theme Song Suggestions" field.
* You can rate the theme song out of any scale, but the higher the score, the better.
* Please select only one of the options provided in the "Submit" field.
