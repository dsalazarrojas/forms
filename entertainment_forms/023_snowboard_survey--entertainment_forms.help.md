# Snowboard Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users about their experiences with snowboarding events. The survey aims to gather information on the user's satisfaction with snowboarding events, how often they participate in such events, the type of snowboarding they enjoy, and their level of interest in snowboarding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to your snowboarding experience.
2. Choose whether you are satisfied or dissatisfied with the snowboarding event you attended.
3. Enter a number to indicate how often you participate in snowboarding events.
4. Choose the type of snowboarding you enjoy (Yes or No).
5. Select the options that apply to your interest in snowboarding (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Snowboard Survey Form** (`snowboard_survey_form`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your snowboarding experience. This field is not required.
	+ Yes: You are interested in snowboarding.
	+ No: You are not interested in snowboarding.
* **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction`, select_one): Choose whether you are satisfied or dissatisfied with the snowboarding event.
	+ Satisfied: You are satisfied with the event.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are dissatisfied with the event.
* **Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`, number): Enter the number of times you participate in snowboarding events.
* **Snowboard Type** (`snowboard_type`, select_one): Choose the type of snowboarding you enjoy (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: You enjoy this type of snowboarding.
	+ No: You do not enjoy this type of snowboarding.
* **Snowboard Interest** (`snowboard_interest`, select_multiple): Select all the options that apply to your interest in snowboarding.
	+ Yes: You are interested in snowboarding.
	+ No: You are not interested in snowboarding.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Make sure to select all the options that apply to your experience.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, please feel free to provide them in the space provided.
