# fashion show participation form project gain - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants for the "Fashion Show Participation Form Project Gain" event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start from the top and fill out each field one by one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional):
  This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional):
  This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

* **Email** (Email, email, optional):
  This field is for your contact email address. Please enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).

* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional):
  This field is for your contact phone number. Please enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).

* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional):
  This field is for your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).

* **Time of Event** (Time of Event, time, optional):
  This field is for the time you plan to participate in the event. Please enter your time in the correct format (e.g., HH:MM AM/PM).

* **Description** (Description, note, optional):
  This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about your participation. Please enter a brief description of your experience or qualifications.

* **Select One** (Select One, select_one, optional):
  This field allows you to select one option from the list below:
  * One
  * Two
  * Three

* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple, select_multiple, optional):
  This field allows you to select multiple options from the list below:
  * One
  * Two
  * Three

* **Date** (Date, date, optional):
  This field is for the date you plan to participate in the event. Please enter your date in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).

* **Time** (Time, time, optional):
  This field is for the time you plan to participate in the event. Please enter your time in the correct format (e.g., HH:MM AM/PM).
