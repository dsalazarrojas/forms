# Mother's Day Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mother's Day Questionnaire is a form designed to collect opinions and ideas on Mother's Day activities, gifts, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question and its purpose.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Answer the question "What is your favourite Mother's Day gift?" in the text field.
4. Choose one or more options for "What would you like to create for your mother?".
5. Select one option for "Which of the following is your mother?".
6. Choose one or more options for "Who else" is also relevant to your Mother's Day plans?.
7. (Optional) Enter the day and time you would like to celebrate Mother's Day in the "What day" and "What time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Mother's Day Activities
• **mother_s_day_activities** (text, not required): This field is a text field where you can enter any Mother's Day activities you would like to share.

### 2. Name
• **name** (text, not required): Please enter your name for the purpose of this survey.

### 3. Favourite Mother's Day Gift
• **what_is_your_favourite_mother_s_day_gift** (text, not required): Share your favourite Mother's Day gift with us.

### 4. What would you like to create for your mother?
• **what_would_you_like_to_create_for_your_mother** (select multiple, not required): Choose one or more options for what you would like to create for your mother.

### 5. Which of the following is your mother?
• **which_of_the_following_is_your_mother** (select one, not required): Select the option that best describes your mother.

### 6. Who else is also relevant to your Mother's Day plans?
• **who_else** (select multiple, not required): Choose one or more options for who else is relevant to your Mother's Day plans.

### 7. What day and time would you like to celebrate Mother's Day?
• **what_day** (date, not required) and **what_time** (time, not required): (Optional) Enter the day and time you would like to celebrate Mother's Day.
