# Film Restoration Project Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Film Restoration Project Application Form is designed to collect information about your film restoration project, including its title, category, description, budget, location, start and end dates, contact details, and any additional information you'd like to provide. This form is for new projects only, so if you have already submitted a project, please do not resubmit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the project title in the text field provided.
2. Select the most suitable category for your project from the options provided (Conservation, Preservation, or Restoration).
3. Enter a brief description of your project in the text field.
4. Enter your project's budget in numerical format.
5. Choose the location(s) relevant to your project from the options provided (London, Paris, or Berlin).
6. Enter the start and end dates of your project in date format.
7. Provide your contact name and email address, and optional phone number.
8. If you have any additional information to share about your project, provide it in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Film Restoration Project Title** (`film_restoration_project_title`, Text, Optional)
	+ This field is for entering a brief title of your film restoration project.
* **Category** (`category`, Select One, Optional)
	+ Select the category that best describes your project (Conservation, Preservation, or Restoration).
* **Film Description** (`film_description`, Text, Optional)
	+ Enter a brief description of your project in this field.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, Number, Optional)
	+ Enter the budget for your project in numerical format.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Choose the location(s) relevant to your project (London, Paris, or Berlin).
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, Date, Optional)
	+ Enter the start date of your project in date format.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, Date, Optional)
	+ Enter the end date of your project in date format.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Optional)
	+ Provide your name as the contact person for your project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Optional)
	+ Enter your email address as the contact person for your project.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Optional)
	+ Provide your phone number as the contact person for your project.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, Text, Optional)
	+ If you have any additional information about your project, provide it in this field.
* **Submission Terms** (`submission_terms`, Select One, Optional)
	+ Ensure you have read and understood the project submission terms.

## Tips
* Ensure that each project submission is unique and distinct from others.
* Review the submission terms carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
