# Gala Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the attendance and participation of guests at a gala event. It helps in tracking attendance, gathering feedback, and ensuring the overall success of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Registration Information page with your details.
2. Indicate whether you attended the gala event on the Guest Information page.
3. Provide your event and payment information on the Event Details and Payment Info pages.
4. Confirm your attendance and payment status on the Confirmation pages.
5. Add any additional comments or details on the Additional Info page.
