# Christmas Present Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Christmas Present Form is designed to help you gather information and ideas for a thoughtful and personalized gift for a loved one. It guides you in understanding the recipient's preferences, interests, and needs, making the gift-giving process easier and more enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the recipient's full name in the "Recipient Full Name" field.
2. Provide their email address (if applicable) in the "Email Address" field.
3. Choose how you are related to the recipient in the "Your Relationship" field.
4. Select the recipient's age group in the "Recipient Age Group" field.
5. Choose the categories that interest them in the "Gift Category Interest" field (multiple selections allowed).
6. Indicate their favorite colors in the "Favorite Colors" field (multiple selections allowed).
7. For clothing or wearable items, select their size in the "Size Information" field.
8. List their hobby interests in the "Hobby Interests" field (multiple selections allowed).
9. Specify any favorite brands they have in mind in the "Favorite Brands" field.
10. Mention any specific items on their wishlist in the "Wishlist Items" field.
11. Indicate your budget range in the "Gift Budget" field.
12. Choose the type of gift they prefer in the "Gift Type Preference" field.
13. Select what type of gift they prefer, whether practical or fun, in the "Practical vs Fun" field.
14. If giving food gifts, indicate any dietary restrictions they may have in the "Dietary Restrictions" field (multiple selections allowed).
15. List any known allergies they may have in the "Allergies" field.
16. Specify any things you should avoid gifting in the "Things to Avoid" field.
17. Note any past gifts they have received and liked or disliked in the "Past Gifts Received" and "Past Gifts Disliked" fields, respectively.
18. Provide a meaningful gift message idea in the "Gift Message Idea" field.
19. Add any additional notes or information in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient Full Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required): This is the name of the person you are buying the gift for.
* **Email Address** (`recipient_email`, email, optional): If you have the recipient's email address, you can enter it here.
* **Your Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, required): This indicates how you are related to the recipient (e.g., spouse, parent, friend, etc.).
* **Recipient Age Group** (`recipient_age_group`, select_one, required): Choose the age range of the recipient to help with gift suggestions.
* **Gift Category Interest** (`gift_category_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select the categories that interest them, such as electronics, clothing, or sports.
* **Favorite Colors** (`favorite_colors`, select_multiple, optional): List their favorite colors to help with gift ideas.
* **Size Information** (`size_information`, select_one, optional): If you're gifting clothing or a wearable item, select their size range.
* **Hobby Interests** (`hobby_interests`, select_multiple, required): List their hobbies and interests to help with gift ideas.
* **Favorite Brands** (`favorite_brands`, text, optional): Mention any brands they have a liking for.
* **Wishlist Items** (`wishlist_items`, text, optional): List any specific items they have mentioned they'd like to receive.
* **Gift Budget** (`gift_budget`, select_one, required): Indicate your budget range for the gift.
* **Gift Type Preference** (`gift_type_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the type of gift they prefer (e.g., physical product, gift card, experience, etc.).
* **Practical vs Fun** (`practical_vs_fun`, select_one, required): Select whether they prefer practical or fun gifts.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): If giving food gifts, indicate any dietary restrictions they may have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any known allergies they may have.
* **Things to Avoid** (`things_to_avoid`, text, optional): Specify any things you should avoid gifting.
* **Past Gifts Received** (`past_gifts_received`, text, optional): Note any past gifts they have received and liked.
* **Past Gifts Disliked** (`past_gifts_disliked`, text, optional): Note any past gifts they have received and disliked.
* **Gift Message Idea** (`gift_message_idea`, text, optional): Provide a meaningful gift message idea.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or information to help with gift ideas.
