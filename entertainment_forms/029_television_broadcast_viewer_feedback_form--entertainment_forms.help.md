# television_broadcast_viewer_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from viewers of the television broadcast, allowing us to understand their opinions on the quality and content of the broadcast.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as prompted. Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, and be as specific as possible. If you have any comments or additional feedback, please use the "comment" field. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Television Broadcast** (`television_broadcast`, text, required): This field is used to collect the title or description of the television broadcast being viewed.
* **2. What did you watch?** (`what_did_you_watch`, text, required): In this field, please describe the content or program of the broadcast that you watched. Try to be specific about the type of content (e.g. movie, TV show, news report, etc.).
* **3. How Satisfied Are You?** (`how_satisfied`, select_one, required): Select the answer that best describes your level of satisfaction with the broadcast. This will help us understand your overall opinion of the content.
* **4. Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about the broadcast, please use this field to share them with us. This is your chance to provide more details or suggestions.
* **5. Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **6. Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted over the phone for further questions or discussions, please enter your phone number here.
* **7. Other (any additional information)** (`other`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to share with us that wasn't covered in the previous fields, please use this field.
* **8. Submit/Saved** (`submit`, select_one, required): Please select either "Submit" to send your feedback to us immediately or "Save for Later" to collect it for another time.
