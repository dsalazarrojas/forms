# Wedding Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Quote Request Form is a tool designed to help Wedding Vendors collect quote requests from clients. This form is meant to be simple and easy to use, ensuring that you can provide all the necessary information to get a quote that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, contact email, phone number, and any additional comments or notes.
2. Select the type of vendor you are interested in (e.g., Vendor A, Vendor B, Vendor C).
3. Enter your quote request details.
4. Choose a quote validity period.
5. Choose a signature for verification purposes.
6. Submit the form to get your quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Vendors** (`wedding_quote_request_form_1`): This field is optional and allows you to indicate the type of vendor you are interested in. 
* **Vendor Type** (`wedding_quote_request_form_2`): Choose the type of vendor you would like to get a quote from. This is also an optional field.
* **Quote Request** (`wedding_quote_request_form_3`): Enter a detailed description of your quote request. Be as specific as possible to ensure that the vendor understands your requirements.
* **Contact Person** (`wedding_quote_request_form_4`): Provide your name as the contact person for this quote request.
* **Contact Email** (`wedding_quote_request_form_5`): Enter your email address so that the vendor can contact you with any additional questions or updates.
* **Contact Phone** (`wedding_quote_request_form_6`): Provide your phone number so that the vendor can contact you with any urgent matters.
* **Quote Valid Until** (`wedding_quote_request_form_7`): Select a date by which you would like to have the quote valid.
* **Quote Valid Until Time** (`wedding_quote_request_form_8`): Select a time by which you would like to have the quote valid.
* **Note** (`wedding_quote_request_form_9`): This is an optional field where you can add any additional comments or notes.
* **Vendor** (`wedding_quote_request_form_10`): Choose all the vendors you would like to get quotes from. 
* **Quote** (`wedding_quote_request_form_11`): Enter a quote amount (optional).
* **Additional Information** (`wedding_quote_request_form_12`): Add any additional information that might be relevant to your quote request.
* **Signature** (`wedding_quote_request_form_13`): Choose a signature to verify your quote request.
