# public_performance_stage_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_performance_stage_booking_form is used to book the performance stage for events. This form is designed to collect essential information from users about their preferred date, time, and contact details for the performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your event or performance in the "Performance Request" field.
2. Select your preferred date for the event in the "Preferred Date" field.
3. Choose your preferred time for the event in the "Preferred Time" field.
4. If you have any specific technical needs for the performance, enter them in the "Technical Needs" field.
5. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your event in the "Contact Person" field.
6. Enter the contact email of the person responsible for your event in the "Contact E-Mail" field.
7. Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for your event in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Request**: This field is where you enter the title of your event or performance.
* **Preferred Date**: Enter the date you prefer for your event. This date will be considered for your booking.
* **Preferred Time**: Select the time you prefer for your event. This time will be considered for your booking.
* **Technical Needs**: If you have any specific technical requirements for your performance, enter them here.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your event.
* **Contact E-Mail**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for your event.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your event.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate contact information to ensure we can reach you with any further questions or updates.
* If you have any specific requirements for the performance stage, be sure to include them in the "Technical Needs" field.
* For dates, please use the standard date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* If you have any questions about this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Note: This help guide is intended to be read from top to bottom, so make sure to fill out each field before proceeding to the next one.
