# Cotillion Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cotillion Application Form is designed to collect contact and payment information from applicants for the Cotillion event. This form helps the event organization to gather essential details to ensure a smooth and successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are filling out this form to apply for the Cotillion event.
2. Scroll through the form sections and enter your information accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name and other contact details to ensure we can reach you with updates and information about the event.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, text, required: false): If you are an applicant under 18 years old, please provide your parent's or guardian's name and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact details of a person responsible for emergency situations.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred payment option: Full payment upfront, Payment plan, or Other payment method.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable payment methods you prefer (Full payment upfront, Payment plan, Other payment method).

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
* Review and submit the form only after you have completed all the sections.
