# Actor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Actor Application Form is a tool used to collect information from actors applying for acting opportunities. This form helps us to better understand your background, skills, and availability, allowing us to provide the best fit for the casting needs.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, please ensure you have the following information ready:
  - Your full legal name
  - Your contact information (email and phone number)
  - Date of birth for age verification
  - Acting experience and skills
  - Availability for auditions and filming

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (Required): Please enter your full legal name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Stage Name (if applicable)** (Optional): If you have a professional stage name, please enter it here. This is the name you use in performances.
* **Contact Email** (Required): Enter your email address for casting notifications and communication.
* **Contact Phone** (Required): Provide your phone number for casting calls and notifications.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Height** (Required): Enter your height in feet and inches (e.g., 5'9").
* **Weight** (Optional): Enter your weight in pounds (if applicable).
* **Distinguishing Physical Characteristics** (Optional): Describe any notable physical features that may be relevant to your acting roles.
* **Years of Acting Experience** (Required): Enter the number of years you have had professional acting experience.
* **Previous Roles or Credits** (Optional): List any notable acting roles or productions you have been involved in.
* **Special Skills** (Optional): List any special skills you possess that are relevant to acting, such as languages or musical instruments.
* **Union Membership** (Required): Select the union(s) you are a member of, or choose "Non-union" if you are not a member.
* **Availability for Auditions and Filming** (Required): Describe your availability for auditions and filming, including specific dates and times.
