# Documentary Screening Series Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a documentary series for screening at a cinema or other venue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form title.
2. Enter the director of the documentary series.
3. Enter the year of the documentary series.
4. Select the screening type (Film, Video, Television, Radio, Web).
5. Enter the screening length.
6. Select the screening format (3D, 2D, IMAX, IMAX 3D, Digital, 4K, Blu-ray, DVD).
7. Select the language of the documentary series.
8. Select the country where the documentary series is from.
9. Upload any assets for the screening (optional).
10. Enter any additional notes about the documentary series.
11. Select the submit option (Submit, Other).
12. Enter an email address.
13. Enter a phone number (optional).
14. Enter the screening date.
15. Enter the screening time.
16. Select the time zone.
17. Enter any time zone offset (optional).
18. Enter any screening details.
19. Enter the screening location.
20. Select the screening status (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled, Finished).
21. Select the screening type status (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled, Finished).
22. Enter any screening note.
23. Enter the screening date.
24. Enter the screening time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (1, text, required): Enter the title of the documentary series.
* **Director** (2, text, required): Enter the director of the documentary series.
* **Year** (3, number, required): Enter the year of the documentary series.
* **Screening Type** (4, select_multiple, required): Select the type of screening you want (Film, Video, Television, Radio, Web).
* **Screening Length** (5, number, required): Enter the length of the screening.
* **Screening Format** (6, select_one, required): Select the format of the screening (3D, 2D, IMAX, IMAX 3D, Digital, 4K, Blu-ray, DVD).
* **Language** (7, select_one, required): Select the language of the documentary series.
* **Country** (8, select_multiple, required): Select the country where the documentary series is from.
* **Assets** (9, note, optional): Upload any assets related to the screening, such as posters, photos.
* **Notes** (10, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the documentary series.
* **Submit** (11, select_one, required): Select "Submit" to submit the form.
* **Email** (12, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (13, text, optional): Enter a phone number.
* **Date** (14, date, required): Enter the date of the screening.
* **Time** (15, time, required): Enter the time of the screening.
* **Time Zone** (16, select_one, required): Select the time zone of the screening.
* **Time Zone Offset** (17, number, optional): Enter any time zone offset.
* **Screening Details** (18, text, optional): Enter any details about the screening.
* **Screening Location** (19, text, optional): Enter the location of the screening.
* **Screening Status** (20, select_one, required): Select the status of the screening (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled, Finished).
* **Screening Type Status** (21, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the screening type (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled, Finished).
* **Screening Note** (22, text, optional): Enter any notes about the screening.
* **Screening Date** (23, date, required): Enter the date of the screening.
* **Screening Time** (24, time, required): Enter the time of the screening.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Be specific when entering the screening details.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
