# Animation Studio Behind The Scenes Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Animation Studio Behind The Scenes Consent Form is designed to obtain consent from participants visiting the animation studio. This form is used to capture essential information from individuals, including their contact details, role during the studio visit, consent to photos and video, and a date of consent. The form is a required process for ensuring that the studio can use the participants' images and likenesses for promotional materials while maintaining their privacy and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your participant name and contact information (email address) on page 2.
2. Select your role during the studio visit (Visitor, Staff, Contractor, Volunteer) on page 4.
3. Choose whether you consent to photos and video recordings (True or False) on page 5.
4. Type in your signature name on page 7.
5. Select the date of consent on page 8.
6. (Optional) If the participant is under 18, provide the guardian's contact information on page 9.
7. Add any additional notes or restrictions on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant full name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as printed on your ID or passport.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address for consent record and follow-up purposes.
* **Role during studio visit** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role during the studio visit from the options provided (Visitor, Staff, Contractor, Volunteer).
* **Consent to photos and video** (`consent_photos`, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to photos and video recordings by selecting True or False.
* **Consent release summary** (`consent_release`, note, required): Please read the release terms provided by the studio and indicate your understanding.
* **Typed name for signature** (`signature_name`, text, required): Type in your full name to indicate your consent.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Select the date on which you signed this consent form.
* **Guardian contact if under 18** (`guardian_contact`, text, optional): If you are under 18, please provide the contact information of your guardian.
* **Additional notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or special requests here.
