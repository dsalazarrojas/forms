# Friendsgiving Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests about their attendance and preferences for the Friendsgiving potluck event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* Enter your name and email address (fields 1 and 2).
2. Indicate if you will be attending the event (field 3).
3. If you're planning to attend, select your RSVP date (field 4).
4. Provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the host (field 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Name (Field 1)
* **Guest Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as the host would like to address you.

### Email (`<email>`, `email`, required/optional): 
* **Email** (`<email>`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address you'd like the host to contact you with updates or to send you the potluck's details.

### Attending (`<attending>`, `select_one`, required/optional): 
* **Attending** (`<attending>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Let the host know if you'll be attending the Friendsgiving potluck event by selecting either "True" or "False".

### RSVP By (`<rsvp_by>`, `date`, required/optional): 
* **RSVP By** (`<rsvp_by>`, `date`, required/optional): If you're planning to attend, select the date you expect to RSVP to the host. This helps them plan accordingly.

### Additional Info (`<additional_info>`, `note`, required/optional): 
* **Additional Info** (`<additional_info>`, `note`, required/optional): Share any extra details or comments you'd like with the host, such as dietary restrictions or special requests.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help the host plan the event effectively.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to the host.
* Be sure to double-check your RSVP status before the event to confirm your attendance.
