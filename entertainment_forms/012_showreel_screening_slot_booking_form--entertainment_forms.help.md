# Showreel Screening Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users book a screening slot for a showreel. It gathers essential information from users to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Choose a screening date and time that suits you.
3. Add any additional booking notes or messages if necessary.
4. Select a category for the booking (entertainment or other).
5. Review and submit your booking request.
