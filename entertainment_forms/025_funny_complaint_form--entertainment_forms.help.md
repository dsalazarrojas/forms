# Funny Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Funny Complaint Form" is used to collect information about humorous incidents that happened in our organization. This form is designed to be easy to fill out and will help us understand what happened, so we can take action to prevent similar incidents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Funny Complain Form Title" field with a brief description of the incident.
2. In the "Funny Complain Form Description" field, provide a more detailed description of what happened.
3. Select the category of the incident from the "Category" field. This will help us understand the context of the incident.
4. Choose the status of the incident from the "Status" field. This will help us determine the severity of the incident.
5. Add any additional status notes in the "Status Notes" field. This is a field for any additional information you would like to provide about the incident.
6. If the incident involved a customer, please fill out the "Customer" field with their name or a brief description.
7. If the incident involved a complainant, please fill out the "Complainant" field with their name or a brief description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Funny Complain Form Title** (`funny_complaint_form_title`, text, required/optional): A brief description of the incident.
* **Funny Complain Form Description** (`funny_complaint_form_description`, text, required/optional): A more detailed description of what happened.
* **Category** (`funny_complaint_form_category`, select_one, required/true): Select the category of the incident.
* **Status** (`funny_complaint_form_status`, select_multiple, required/true): Choose the status of the incident.
* **Status Notes** (`funny_complaint_form_status_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional status notes about the incident.
* **Customer** (`funny_complaint_form_customer`, text, required/optional): If the incident involved a customer, fill out this field with their name or a brief description.
* **Complainant** (`funny_complaint_form_complainant`, text, required/optional): If the incident involved a complainant, fill out this field with their name or a brief description.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about the category or status of the incident, it's better to err on the side of caution and choose a category/status that you think best fits the incident.
* If you have any additional information about the incident, please add it in the "Status Notes" field.
* If you are reporting a complaint on behalf of someone else, make sure to include their name or a brief description in the "Complainant" field.
* If you are reporting a complaint about a customer, make sure to include their name or a brief description in the "Customer" field.
