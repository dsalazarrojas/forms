# Breaking Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to help individuals break up with someone in a thoughtful and considerate way. It provides a structured format to help navigate the process of ending a relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, taking care to provide as much detail as possible.
2. Review each question to ensure you've considered all relevant factors.
3. Don't worry about grammar or spelling, focus on expressing your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (1): Enter your full name.
- **Their Name** (2): Type the name of the person you're breaking up with.
- **How long have you been together** (3): Choose the duration of your relationship from the given options.
- **Type of Relationship** (4): Select the nature of your relationship from the given options.
- **Primary Reason for Breaking Up** (5): Choose the main reason for the breakup from the given options.
- **Explain the Reason** (6): Provide more details about why you're breaking up.
- **Have you attempted to work things out** (7): Check if you tried counseling or talking about the issues.
- **How emotionally ready are you for this** (8): Rate your emotional preparedness from the given options.
- **When do you plan to have this conversation** (9): Choose the planned timing from the given options.
- **How will you break up** (10): Select the preferred method from the given options.
- **Location for the Breakup Conversation** (11): Type the location where you want to have the conversation.
- **Tone of Your Message** (12): Choose the tone you want to convey in the message.
- **Key Points to Cover** (13): Type the main points you want to communicate.
- **How do you anticipate they will react** (14): Select your expectation of their response from the given options.
- **Do you have emotional support available** (15): Check if you have support from friends, family, or a professional counselor.
- **What is your plan after the breakup** (16): Type your next steps.
- **Do you have shared assets or living situation** (17): Check if you have shared resources or a shared home.
- **Concern about Mutual Friends** (18): Check if you're worried about mutual friends.
- **Write Your Breakup Message** (19): Draft a message expressing your feelings and intentions.

## Tips
- Be kind and compassionate when ending the relationship.
- Anticipate and respect their feelings.
- Plan for a safe and respectful conversation.
- Consider seeking support from friends, family, or a professional counselor.
- Review and revise the form as needed to ensure it accurately reflects your feelings and plans.
