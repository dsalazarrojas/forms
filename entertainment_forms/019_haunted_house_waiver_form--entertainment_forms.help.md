# haunted-house-waiver-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals visiting a haunted house attraction, ensuring they provide their consent and details necessary for medical emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone Number for easy contact in case of an emergency.
3. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their Name and Phone Number.
4. The Waiver Signature is an optional field for individuals to sign and date their waiver.
5. If you have any medical conditions, please provide your Date of Birth, Height, and Weight.
6. If you have any medical devices, please specify them and provide a brief description and condition.
7. Select whether you have any Medical Devices with you, and if so, provide the descriptions and conditions for each.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number for easy contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Text): If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Waiver Signature** (Note): This is an optional field for individuals to sign and date their waiver. If you're not signing this, please indicate so.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): If you have any medical conditions, please provide your Date of Birth.
* **Time of Entry** (Time): If you have any medical conditions, please provide your Time of Entry for easy reference.
* **Height** (Number): If you have any medical conditions, please enter your height.
* **Weight** (Number): If you have any medical conditions, please enter your weight.
* **Medical Condition 1** (Text): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Medical Condition 2** (Text): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Medical Condition 3** (Text): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Medical Conditions** (Select Multiple): If you have any medical devices, select the ones you have from the list.
* **Medical Device 1** (Text): If you have any medical devices, list them here.
* **Medical Device 2** (Text): If you have any medical devices, list them here.
* **Medical Device 3** (Text): If you have any medical devices, list them here.
* **Medical Device 1 Description** (Text): If you have any medical devices, provide a brief description of each.
* **Medical Device 2 Description** (Text): If you have any medical devices, provide a brief description of each.
* **Medical Device 3 Description** (Text): If you have any medical devices, provide a brief description of each.
* **Medical Device 1 Condition** (Text): If you have any medical devices, specify the condition of each.
* **Medical Device 2 Condition** (Text): If you have any medical devices, specify the condition of each.
* **Medical Device 3 Condition** (Text): If you have any medical devices, specify the condition of each.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure your safety and the safety of others.
* If you have any medical conditions, please be honest when listing them.
* If you have any medical devices, please provide accurate descriptions and conditions for each.
