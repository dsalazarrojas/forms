# Music Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Submission Form is used by artists and musicians to submit their music to our platform for review or consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Artist Name field with your real name as it appears on your official music credits.
2. Select your music genre from the drop-down menu.
3. Upload your music file (e.g. audio file, video file, etc.).
4. Enter your Email Address where you can be reached for further communication.
5. Enter your Phone Number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
6. Enter your Date of Birth (if applicable).
7. Enter the Event Name where your music was performed or recorded (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Name** (text, required): Enter your real name as it appears on your official music credits.
* **Music Genre** (select_one, optional): Select your music genre from the provided drop-down menu (e.g. Hip Hop, Pop, Electronic, Rock, Classical).
* **Music File** (text, optional): Upload your music file (e.g. audio file, video file, etc.). Make sure to check the file type and size limits before uploading.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth if applicable.
* **Event Name** (text, optional): Enter the event name where your music was performed or recorded (if applicable).
