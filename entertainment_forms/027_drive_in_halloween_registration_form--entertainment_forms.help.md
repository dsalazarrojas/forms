# drive_in_halloween_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from visitors planning to attend a Halloween event at a drive-in for the purpose of planning and organizing the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal details (first name, last name, email, phone number, and date of birth).
2. Enter the number of participants you are planning to bring.
3. Select the assigned tool for your visit.
4. If you are bringing multiple participants, fill out their information as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your visit.
* **Participants** (`participants`, number, required: false): Enter the number of participants you are planning to bring.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: true): Select the tool assigned to you for your visit. Options are `chatjimmy`, `drivein1`, `drivein2`.
* **Participant List** (`participant_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Select which participants you are bringing. Options are `participant1`, `participant2`, `participant3`.
* **Participant 1 Name** (`participant1_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the first participant.
* **Participant 1 Date of Birth** (`participant1_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the first participant.
* **Participant 1 Time of Visit** (`participant1_time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Enter the time of visit of the first participant.
* **Participant 1 Phone** (`participant1_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the first participant.
* **Participant 1 Email** (`participant1_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the first participant.
* **Participant 2 Name** (`participant2_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the second participant.
* **Participant 2 Date of Birth** (`participant2_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the second participant.
* **Participant 2 Time of Visit** (`participant2_time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Enter the time of visit of the second participant.
* **Participant 2 Phone** (`participant2_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the second participant.
* **Participant 2 Email** (`participant2_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the second participant.
* **Participant 3 Name** (`participant3_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the third participant.
* **Participant 3 Date of Birth** (`participant3_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the third participant.
* **Participant 3 Time of Visit** (`participant3_time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Enter the time of visit of the third participant.
* **Participant 3 Phone** (`participant3_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the third participant.
* **Participant 3 Email** (`participant3_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the third participant.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the event organizers.
