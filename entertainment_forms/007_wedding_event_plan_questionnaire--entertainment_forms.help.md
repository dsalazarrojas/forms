# Wedding Event Plan Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for planning a wedding event. It will help the wedding planner or coordinator to understand the event's specifics and ensure that all necessary details are covered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*) in the form.
2.  Select the appropriate options for each field from the provided choices.
3.  Provide as much detail as possible in the note fields, such as the "Ceremony Details" and "Reception Details" fields.
4.  Review and submit the form once you have completed all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the wedding event. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to schedule the event correctly.
    - Please select a date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` for the wedding date.
- **Location** (`wedding_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the wedding event. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to identify the venue.
    - Please enter the location's address or name, e.g., "City Hall" or "123 Main St."
- **Package** (`wedding_package`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the package or services required for the wedding event. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to determine the necessary resources and costs.
    - Select one of the three package options: Package A, Package B, or Package C.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number or email. This will ensure that the wedding planner or coordinator can reach you efficiently.
    - Please enter your name and contact information in the format "Name: John Doe, Phone: 123-456-7890, Email: [john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com).
- **People to Contact** (`people_to_contact`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List the people you want the wedding planner or coordinator to contact. This will help them to communicate with your team members effectively.
    - Select one or more of the three options: Wedding Planner, Wedding Coordinator, or Photographer.
- **Wedding Coordinator** (`wedding_coordinator`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the wedding coordinator or planner responsible for this event. This will help the planner or coordinator to understand the event's specifics.
    - Select one of the three coordinator options: Coordinator 1, Coordinator 2, or Coordinator 3.
- **Family Situation** (`family_situations`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate the family situation for the wedding event, e.g., parents, siblings, or grandparents. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to tailor the event according to your needs.
    - Select one or more of the three options: Parents, Siblings, or Grandparents.
- **Photography** (`photography`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the photography services or requirements. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to ensure that photography is covered.
    - Please enter a brief description of the photography services or requirements.
- **Logistics** (`logistics`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the logistics option that best suits your needs, e.g., transportation or accommodations. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to arrange the necessary services.
    - Select one of the three options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
- **Meal Plan** (`meal_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List the meal plan options you prefer for the wedding event. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to arrange the necessary catering services.
    - Select one or more of the three options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
- **Ceremony Details** (`ceremony_details`, `note`, required: false): Provide as much detail as possible about the ceremony. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to tailor the ceremony according to your needs.
    - Please provide a brief description of the ceremony, e.g., "Outdoor ceremony with a lake view" or "Traditional ceremony with a minister."
- **Reception Details** (`reception_details`, `note`, required: false): Provide as much detail as possible about the reception. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to tailor the reception according to your needs.
    - Please provide a brief description of the reception, e.g., "Reception will be held at the venue's ballroom" or "Reception will be held outdoors."
- **Vendors and Service Providers** (`vendor_info`, `text`, required: false): List the vendors or service providers you have chosen for the wedding event. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to track the services provided.
    - Please enter the names and contact information of the vendors or service providers.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the wedding event. This will help the wedding planner or coordinator to address any special requests or concerns.
    - Please enter any additional comments or notes about the event.
