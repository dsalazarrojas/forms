# Halloween Movie Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Movie Quiz form is designed to collect information about your favorite Halloween movie. This form is intended to be a fun and engaging way for users to participate in a trivia night or movie night event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions above: click on the form, fill out the movie name and year fields, and click "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Halloween Movie Quiz Form** (`halloween_movie_quiz_form`, 1, Required): This is the title of the form, but you don't need to fill it out.
* **Movie Name** (`movie_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your favorite Halloween movie.
* **Year** (`year`, number, Optional): Enter the year your favorite Halloween movie was released.
