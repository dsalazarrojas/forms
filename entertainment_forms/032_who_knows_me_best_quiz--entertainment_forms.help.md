# Who Knows Me Best Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you identify the people who know you best by asking a series of questions about your preferences, habits, and interests.

17. Share about where you first met your second-best friend in the "First met 2" field.
18. Name the place where you first met your third-best friend in the "First met 3" field.
