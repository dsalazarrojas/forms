# International Fan Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from F1 fans to help us better understand their engagement with the sport, their preferences, and their behaviors. We want to know about your interests, habits, and opinions to improve the F1 experience for you and your fellow fans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the required fields. Don't worry, we won't share your personal data, and you can stop at any time if you're not comfortable answering a question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. First Name
* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Last Name
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. Email
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you (if we need to).

### 4. Favorite Driver
* **Favorite Driver** (Select One, required): Choose the driver you enjoy watching the most.

### 5. Favorite Team
* **Favorite Team** (Select Multiple, required): Check the teams you're a fan of. (Please check all that apply)

### 6. Favorite Racing Track
* **Favorite Racing Track** (Select One, optional): Choose the track you like the most.

### 7. Age
* **Age** (Number, required): Enter your age as of today.

### 8. Favorite Race
* **Favorite Race** (Select One, required): Choose the race you enjoy watching the most.

### 9. How Long Have You Been a Fan?
* **How Long Have You Been a Fan?** (Date, optional): If you're a new fan, you can skip this question.

### 10. How Often Do You Watch F1?
* **How Often Do You Watch F1?** (Number, optional): Choose how often you watch F1.

### 11. Favorite Circuit
* **Favorite Circuit** (Text, optional): Enter the name of your favorite circuit.

### 12. Social Media
* **Social Media** (Select Multiple, required): Check the social media platforms you use to follow F1. (Please check all that apply)

### 13. Note
* **Note** (Note, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about F1.

### 14. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number (not required, but we might contact you).

### 15. Favorite Rider
* **Favorite Rider** (Select One, optional): Choose the rider you enjoy watching the most.

### 16. Favorite Event
* **Favorite Event** (Select One, optional): Choose the event you enjoy attending the most.

### 17. Favorite F1 Game
* **Favorite F1 Game** (Select Multiple, optional): Check the F1 games you enjoy playing. (Please check all that apply)

### 18. Time of Day
* **Time of Day** (Time, optional): Enter the time of day you usually watch F1. (If you're a night owl, just let us know)

### 19. Driver Standings
* **Driver Standings** (Select One, optional): Choose how you prefer to see driver standings displayed.

### 20. How Long Ago Became a Fan
* **How Long Ago Became a Fan** (Date, optional): If you're a new fan, you can skip this question.

### 21. Country
* **Country** (Text, optional): Enter your country of residence.

### 22. Language

### 23. How Do You Discover New Drivers?
* **How Do You Discover New Drivers?** (Select Multiple, optional): Check how you usually discover new drivers. (Please check all that apply)

### 25. How Do You Follow F1?
* **How Do You Follow F1?** (Select Multiple, optional): Check how you usually follow F1. (Please check all that apply)
