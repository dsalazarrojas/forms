# Podcast Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about a podcast topic. It helps the podcast team understand audience interest, engagement, and satisfaction, enabling them to create content that resonates with their audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the topic name in the field labeled **Topic name**.
2.  Provide a short description of the topic in the field labeled **Topic description**.
3.  Rate the topic's interestingness on a scale from 1 to 5 in the field labeled **Interestingness**.
4.  Assess the topic's engagement level on a scale from 1 to 5 in the field labeled **Engagement level**.
5.  Evaluate the topic's satisfaction level on a scale from 1 to 5 in the field labeled **Satisfaction level**.
6.  Select the relevance of the topic in the field labeled **Relevance**.
7.  Indicate whether you would recommend the topic in the field labeled **Recommendation**.
8.  Enter any additional comments about the topic in the field labeled **Comments**.
9.  Enter the **Podcast session ID** if you are participating in a specific podcast session.
10. Enter your email address in the field labeled **Email**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Topic name** (`topic_name`, text, required: false): Enter a brief name for the podcast topic.
*   **Topic description** (`topic_description`, text, required: false): Write a short description of the topic.
*   **Interestingness** (`topic_interestingness`, number, required: false): Rate the topic's interestingness on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Engagement level** (`topic_engagement`, select_one, required: false): Assess the topic's engagement level on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Satisfaction level** (`topic_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the topic's satisfaction level on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Relevance** (`topic_relevance`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the relevance of the topic (relevant, irrelevant, or neutral).
*   **Recommendation** (`topic_recommendation`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you would recommend the topic.
*   **Comments** (`topic_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the topic.
*   **Podcast session ID** (`podcast_session_id`, number, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the podcast session, if applicable.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.

## Tips
- Please answer each field honestly and accurately.
- If you're unsure about a field, you can always ask for clarification.
- This form is designed to help the podcast team create more enjoyable and relevant content for you.
- Your feedback is invaluable to us!
