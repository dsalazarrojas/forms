# Festival Performance Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Performance Release Form is used to gather information about a performer's release preferences for a festival performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you for any necessary follow-ups.
2. Choose the type of performance you would like to release (Video, Audio, or Audio & Video).
3. Select the locations where you would like to release your performance (Online, In Person, or Both).
4. Indicate the types of media you would like to release with your performance (Video, Audio, Photo, and/or Poster).
5. Provide the name of your performance, the date of the performance, and the time of the performance.
6. Agree or disagree with the release location agreement.
7. Enter your artist statement, manager statement, and additional release notes.
8. Confirm your manager and record label agreements.
9. Provide any additional performance media files.
10. Confirm your record label and manager agreements again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Performance ID** (`performance_id`, `text`, required): Enter a unique identifier for your performance.
* **Release Type** (`release_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to release your performance (Video, Audio, or Audio & Video).
* **Release Location** (`release_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Select where you would like to release your performance (Online, In Person, or Both).
* **Performance Media Types** (`performance_media_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the types of media you would like to release with your performance (Video, Audio, Photo, and/or Poster).
* **Performer Name** (`performer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a performer.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your performance.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your performance.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to release your performance.
* **Performer Agreement** (`performer_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree or disagree with the release location.
* **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`, `text`, required): Enter a statement about your performance.
* **Manager Statement** (`manager_statement`, `text`, required): Enter a statement from your manager.
* **Record Label** (`record_label`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your record label.
* **Manager Agreement** (`manager_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree or disagree with the release location agreement.
* **Release Notes** (`release_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional release notes.
* **Additional Release Notes** (`release_notes_additional`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional release notes.
* **Form Submission Note** (`form_submission_note`, `text`, optional): Enter any notes about this form submission.
* **Additional Performance Media Files** (`performance_media_additional_files`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional files related to your performance.
* **Record Label Agreement** (`record_label_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree or disagree with the release location agreement.
* **Manager Agreement 2** (`manager_agreement_2`, `select_one`, required): Agree or disagree with the release location agreement.
* **Artist Statement 2** (`artist_statement_2`, `text`, optional): Enter another statement about your performance.
* **Release Location Agreement** (`release_location_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree or disagree with the release location agreement.
* **Record Label Agreement 2** (`record_label_agreement_2`, `select_one`, required): Agree or disagree with the release location agreement.
