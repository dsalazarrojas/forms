# Festival People's Choice Voting - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form allows you to participate in a people's choice voting system for the festival. Your feedback is used to determine the most popular category and nominee.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow the on-screen instructions.
2. Please fill out all the required fields (if any).
3. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): This field allows you to select a category from the provided options: `Yes` and `No`. Selecting 'Yes' likely indicates you agree with the category or have a positive experience with it.
* **Nominee** (`nominee`, text, required: false): Enter the name or description of your chosen nominee. Please be specific and accurate for best results.
* **Vote** (`vote`, number, required: false): This field likely asks for a quantity or ranking of your choice, such as how many times you would vote for the nominee.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the festival or the nominated category.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to help us verify your submission and ensure it is not a duplicate. Please use a valid email address to receive any follow-up information.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you've already voted, please do not submit this form again.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the festival organizers for assistance.
