# Walkathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for the Walkathon event. It will help us collect the necessary information to ensure a seamless registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the provided **Event** options.
2. Fill in the **Contribution** field with the details about your contribution (if any).
3. Provide your **Schedule** information in the space provided.
4. Enter your fees for the selected event in the **Fees** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, text, not required): This field is a text input field for you to provide any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Event** (`Event`, select_one, not required): Choose the event you would like to participate in from the provided options (Walk 1, Run 1, Run 2).
* **Contribution** (`Contribution`, text, not required): Here's the space to describe your contribution to the Walkathon event.
* **Schedule** (`Schedule`, text, not required): In this field, please provide your schedule information.
* **Fees** (`Fees`, number, not required): Please enter your fees for the selected event.
