# Valentine Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for Valentine's Day orders, allowing customers to place orders for products or services with ease. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth order processing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if applicable.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
4. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
5. Select the date and time for your order in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
6. Provide detailed information about your order in the "Order Details" field.
7. Add any additional customer notes in the "Customer Note" field, if necessary.
8. Select your preferred shipping method in the "Shipping Method" field.
9. Choose your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
10. Enter the total value of your order in the "Total Value" field, if applicable.
11. Add any customer reference information in the "Customer Reference" field.
12. Add any additional comments or messages in the "Order Comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name or the name of the person you are placing the order for. This field is required.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you would like to receive updates about your order. This field is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you would like to receive calls from us about your order. This field is optional.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your order. This field is required.
* **Order Date**: Select the date you would like to place your order for. This field is optional.
* **Order Time**: Select the time you would like to place your order for. This field is optional.
* **Order Details**: Provide detailed information about the product or service you are ordering, including any custom requests. This field is required.
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order, if necessary. This field is optional.
* **Shipping Method**: Select your preferred shipping method from the available options (USPS, UPS, FedEX). This field is optional.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the available options (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer). This field is optional.
* **Total Value**: Enter the total value of your order, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Customer Reference**: Enter any relevant information about the customer you are placing the order for, such as a reference number or account name. This field is required.
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments or messages about your order, if necessary. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields accurately to avoid order issues.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
