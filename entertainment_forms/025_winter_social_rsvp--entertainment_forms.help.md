# Winter Social RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees to a winter social gathering. It will help us get a headcount, track attendance, and understand which products people are bringing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the gathering you're RSVPing for (step 1).
2. Choose your attendance status:
	* Attending: You're coming to the gathering.
	* Not Attending: You won't be attending the gathering.
	* Maybe: You're still unsure, but we're looking forward to hearing back from you.
3. If you're attending, you can also mention a product you'll be bringing (step 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **gathering name** (step 1, text, required: false): Enter the name of the gathering you're RSVPing for, such as "Holiday Party" or "Winter Gathering".
* **attendance status** (step 2, select_one, required: false): Select your attendance status to help us plan accordingly. If you're unsure, you can choose "Maybe".
* **product** (step 3, text, required: false): If you're attending, mention the product you'll be bringing, such as "cookies" or "craft beer".

## Tips
* Make sure to be honest about your attendance status so we can get an accurate headcount.
* If you're bringing a product, be specific about what it is so we can plan accordingly.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, feel free to include them in the "product" field.
