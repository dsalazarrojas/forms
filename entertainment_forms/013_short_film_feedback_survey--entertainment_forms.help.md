# Short Film Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about short films that they have watched in a specific cinema location. By completing this form, you will be able to share your thoughts on the quality of the film, the director, and your overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the "film_title" field with the title of the short film you have watched.
2. In the "director" field, enter the name of the director of the film.
3. Select your rating for the film by choosing one of the options in the "rating" field.
4. If you have a note about your experience, you can write it down in the "note" field.
5. If you have an email address where you can be contacted for further information, you can enter it in the "email" field.
6. Answer the question of whether you would "watch_again" by selecting "True" or "False".
7. If you have any additional comments, you can write them down in the "comments" field.
8. If you want to specify where you watched the film, you can enter the date in the "watched_at" field.
9. If you want to specify the time you spent watching the film, you can enter the time in the "watch time" field.
10. If you want to specify where you watched the film, you can enter the location in the "watch_location" field.
11. If you have watched the film in multiple locations, select all the relevant options in the "watched_at" field.
12. Finally, enter the festival in which you watched the film in the "festival" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **film_title** (Text, Required): Enter the title of the short film you have watched.
- **director** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the director of the film.
- **rating** (Select One, Required): Select your rating for the film: Very Good, Good, or Bad.
- **note** (Text, Optional): Write any additional comments or notes about your experience.
- **email** (Email, Optional): If you want to be contacted for further information, enter your email address.
- **watch_again** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if you would watch this film again: True or False.
- **comments** (Note, Optional): Write any additional comments about your experience.
- **watched_at** (Date, Optional): Specify the date on which you watched the film.
- **watch_time** (Time, Optional): Specify the time on which you spent watching the film.
- **watch_location** (Text, Optional): Specify the location where you watched the film.
- **watched_at** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the locations where you watched the film.
- **festival** (Text, Optional): Specify the festival in which you watched the film.
