# Summer Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for the Summer Competition. It is an original adaptation created using AI and is not affiliated with any form platform. You can use it freely, but please give credit to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select up to three events from the "Event 1" dropdown menu. You can choose from the available options.
2. Provide a response for "Event 2" in the text field. This field is optional.
3. If you agree to the terms and conditions, check the box for "Consent to terms and conditions". This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event 1**: Select up to three events from the dropdown menu. Choose the events you are eligible for.
* **Event 2**: Enter any information related to event 2. This field is for your convenience.
* **Consent to terms and conditions**: If you agree to the terms and conditions, check this box. This field is not required but is essential for participation.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose the correct events for which you are eligible to participate.
* You can only select up to three events in the "Event 1" dropdown menu.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
