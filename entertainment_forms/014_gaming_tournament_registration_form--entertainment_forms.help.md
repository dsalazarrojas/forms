# gaming_tournament_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from players or teams interested in joining a gaming tournament. The form is meant to capture essential details to facilitate smooth communication and organization within the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts and fill in the required fields. You can choose from the predefined options for select and select_multiple fields, and input your text responses in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to indicate whether you are registering as a team or an individual player. Select 'Yes' if you are registering as a team and 'No' if you are registering as an individual player.
* **Individual Player** (Select One): If you are registering as an individual player, select 'Yes' in this field. If you are registering as a team, this field will not apply to you.
* **Team Name** (Text): If you are registering as a team, enter your team name in this field.
* **Player Name** (Text): If you are registering as an individual player, enter your name in this field.
* **Discord** (Text): This field is for your Discord username, which will be used to contact you through the Discord platform.
* **Mobile** (Select One): This field is to indicate whether you have a mobile number that we can use to contact you. Select 'Yes' if you have a mobile number and 'No' if you do not.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address in this field if you want us to use it to contact you.
* **Phone** (Text): If you have a phone number that you want us to use to contact you, enter it in this field.
* **Description** (Text): This field is for any additional information you think is relevant to your registration, such as your skills or experience with the game.
* **Discord Channel** (Text): If you are registered through a specific Discord channel, enter its name in this field.
* **Discord Role** (Select One): This field is to indicate whether you want to receive messages from us through the Discord channel where you are registered. Select 'Yes' if you want to receive messages and 'No' if you do not.
* **Discord Message** (Text): This field is for any additional message you want to send to the tournament administrators through Discord.

## Tips

* Please make sure to select the correct options that apply to your registration, as this will help us organize the tournament more efficiently.
* If you are registering as a team, ensure that all team members are aware of this form's requirements and fill it out accordingly.
* If you are registering as an individual player, provide your team name if applicable, so we can place you in the correct group.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us through the email address or phone number you provided.
