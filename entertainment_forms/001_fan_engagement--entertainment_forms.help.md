# Fan Engagement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from fans of an entertainment entity, such as a music group, sports team, or celebrity, to understand their demographics, preferences, and engagement levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in your name in the first field.
2. Enter your email address in the second field.
3. For the third field, choose how you heard about the entertainment entity.
4. In the fourth field, tell us about your favorite song/movie/book within the entertainment entity.
5. Rate your engagement level (e.g., fan, enthusiast, superfan) in the fifth field.
6. Choose how often you engage with our content (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly) in the sixth field.
7. In the seventh field, explain what motivates you to engage with our content.
8. Select your preferred platform for consuming our content (e.g., social media, website, live events) in the eighth field.
9. If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the ninth field.
10. The remaining fields (10-25) are open text areas for additional comments, opinions, or preferences.
