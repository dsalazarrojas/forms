# thanksgiving_dinner_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about who is attending the Thanksgiving dinner and how many guests are expected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you are attending the dinner.
2. Enter the number of guests you are bringing with you, if any.
3. Confirm the number of guests that have declined the invitation.
4. Enter any additional guest information (e.g. their name, relationship, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **attending_guests** (yes, no): 
  This field is used to indicate if you are attending the dinner.
* **num_guests_attending** (Number): 
  Enter the number of guests you are bringing with you.
* **num_guests_declined** (Number): 
  Enter the number of guests who have declined the invitation.
* **guest_info** (Text): 
  Enter any additional guest information, such as their name, relationship, etc.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately to help with planning and preparation.
- If you are unable to attend, make sure to update the number of declined guests accordingly.
