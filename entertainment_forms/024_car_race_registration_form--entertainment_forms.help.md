# Car Race Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Race Registration Form is intended to collect personal and vehicle information from drivers for racing events. This form helps us to ensure that all necessary information is provided before the race, which in turn helps us to create a safe and enjoyable environment for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please select the correct options and fill in the required information for each field. Some fields have hints that will guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for race communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the city where you live.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you live.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your 5-digit ZIP code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Racing Experience** (`racing_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your experience level from First-time racer, Beginner, Intermediate, Experienced, or Professional.
* **Racing License** (`racing_license`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have a racing license, and if so, from which organization (SCCA, NASA, or other).
* **License Number** (`license_number`, `text`, required if racing_license is "Yes - SCCA" or "Yes - NASA"): Enter your racing license number if you have one.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_information`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional details about your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `text`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, `text`, required): Enter the primary color of your vehicle.
* **VIN** (`vehicle_vin`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's identification number.
* **Car Number** (`vehicle_number`, `text`, required): Enter your racing number.
* **Vehicle Class** (`vehicle_class`, `select_one`, required): Select the class of your vehicle (Stock, Modified, Super Modified, Prototype, Open class, or Undecided).
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, `text`, optional): Enter the specifications of your engine.
* **Engine Displacement** (`engine_displacement`, `text`, optional): Enter the CC or liters of your engine.
* **Horsepower** (`horsepower`, `text`, optional): Enter the estimated horsepower of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Weight** (`vehicle_weight`, `text`, optional): Enter the current weight of your vehicle.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, `note`, optional): Select any installed safety features.
* **Roll Cage** (`roll_cage`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have a roll cage, and if so, whether it's certified.
* **Racing Harness** (`racing_harness`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of harness you use.
* **Fire Suppression System** (`fire_suppression`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have a fire suppression system, and if so, whether it's onboard or handheld.
* **Racing Seat** (`racing_seat`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of seat you use.
* **Helmet Rating** (`helmet_rating`, `select_one`, required): Select your helmet certification level (SA2020, SA2015, M2020, M2015, Other, or None).
* **Racing Suit** (`racing_suit`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have a racing suit, and if so, its certification level (SFI 3.2A/5, SFI 3.2A/1, or Other).
* **Additional Safety Equipment** (`additional_safety`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional safety equipment you use.
* **Race Details** (`race_details`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your racing preferences.
* **Race Class Entry** (`race_class`, `select_one`, required): Select the class of racing you will be participating in (Novice, Amateur, Semi-Pro, Professional, or Exhibition).
* **Race Category** (`race_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of racing you will be participating in (Sprint, Endurance, Drag, Autocross, Time Attack, or Road Race).
* **Crew Members** (`crew_members`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of crew members you have.
* **Pit Space Needed** (`pit_space_needed`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you need pit space.
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the fuel type you use (Pump gas, Race fuel, E85, Diesel, or Electric).
* **Tire Type** (`tire_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of tires you use (Street tires, R-compound, Slicks, Rain tires, or Other).
* **Previous Racing Incidents** (`previous_incidents`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have had any previous racing incidents.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any details about previous racing incidents.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical conditions that may affect your racing.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Sponsorship** (`sponsorship`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you have any sponsors.
* **Sponsor Names** (`sponsor_names`, `text`, optional): Enter the names of your sponsors.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment status for the registration fee (Paid with registration, Will pay separately, Need invoice, or Sponsor paying).
* **Waiver Agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the waiver agreement.
* **Rules Agreement** (`rules_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to follow all race rules.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your racing preferences.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your registration.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the hints provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* We reserve the right to verify any information provided.
