# Audience Reaction Play Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data from audience members or production team members after a performance. It will help the production team understand what worked well and what did not during the performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a single sitting, one section at a time.
2. Answer each question truthfully, based on your experience.
3. If a question does not apply to your experience, select "Not Applicable" or leave it blank.
4. Once you have completed the form, please submit it by typing your name in the "Report Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`): This is the name of the person submitting this report.
* **Reporter Role** (`reporter_role`): Select your role in the production, such as Stage Manager, Front of House Manager, Marketing Team, Production Manager, Director, or Other.
* **Play Title** (`play_title`): Enter the name of the performance you attended or participated in.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`): Enter the date of the performance.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`): Enter the start time of the performance.
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`): Enter the name of the theater or venue where the performance took place.
* **Audience Size** (`audience_size`): Select the approximate number of attendees.
* **Capacity Percentage** (`capacity_percentage`): Select the percentage of the venue's capacity that was filled.
* **Audience Engagement Level** (`audience_engagement_level`): Select the overall engagement level of the audience, such as Very High, High, Moderate, Low, or Very Low.
* **Laughter Frequency** (`laughter_frequency`): Select how often you heard laughter during the performance.
* **Applause Moments** (`applause_moments`): Select the moments when the audience applauded.
* **Emotional Response** (`emotional_response`): Select the emotions you observed in the audience, such as Joy, Sadness, Tension, Excitement, Surprise, or Contemplation.
* **Audience Demographics** (`audience_demographics`): Select the general age range of the audience.
* **Notable Reactions** (`notable_reactions`): Describe any standout reactions from the audience, if any.
* **Technical Issues Impact** (`technical_issues_impact`): Select how significant any technical issues were on the audience's experience.
* **Overall Audience Satisfaction** (`overall_audience_satisfaction`): Rate the overall satisfaction of the audience, such as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Recommendations for Improvement** (`recommendations_for_improvement`): Suggest any improvements or ideas you have for future performances.
* **Report Submitted By** (`report_submitted_by`): Type your name to confirm submission.
