# Wedding Dance Floor Wrapping Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help our team understand your wedding event and dance floor wrapping requirements, allowing us to provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Details section with your name and contact information.
2. Provide information about your event in the Event Details section.
3. Specify any specific requirements for the dance floor wrapping in the Dance Floor Requirements section.
4. Select your preferred method of communication (Email, Phone, or SMS) in the Communication Preferences section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required): Please enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): Describe your event, including date, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, `text`, optional): If you are working with a specific vendor, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Dance Floor Requirements** (`dance_floor_requirements`, `text`, optional): Specify any specific requirements or requests you have for the dance floor wrapping.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how you would like us to communicate with you (Email, Phone, or SMS).

## Tips
* Please be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate.
* If you have any attachments or additional information you would like to provide, please attach them to this form or send them to us separately.
* Our team will be in touch with you shortly after submitting this form to discuss your event and dance floor wrapping requirements.
