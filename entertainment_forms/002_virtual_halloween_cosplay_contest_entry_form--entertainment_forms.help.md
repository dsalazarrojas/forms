# Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest Entry Form is designed to collect information about your character design, cosplay costume, and other relevant details for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Choose your character design and cosplay costume from the available options.
3. Upload a photo of your cosplay costume.
4. Enter a name and description for your entry.
5. Select the entry category and tags that best describe your entry.
6. Enter your entry date and time.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments about your entry.
8. Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: This field is optional and allows you to provide your name and other information about yourself.
* **Character Designs**: Please select "Yes" if your character is a custom-designed one, "No" if it's a stock character.
* **Cosplay Costume**: This is the main field where you describe your cosplay costume. Please be as detailed as possible about your costume's design, materials, and construction.
* **Cosplay Costume Description**: This field allows you to elaborate on your cosplay costume's description, including any relevant details or background information.
* **Photo Upload**: Upload a clear and high-quality photo of your cosplay costume.
* **Entry Name**: This is the name of your entry that will be displayed on our website and in our contest listing.
* **Entry Description**: Provide a brief description of your entry, including any relevant details or background information.
* **Entry Category**: Select the category that best fits your entry (e.g., "Horror", "Fantasy", "Sci-Fi", etc.).
* **Entry Tags**: Select any additional tags that best describe your entry (e.g., "Halloween", "Costume", "Character", etc.).
* **Entry Tags List**: This field allows you to enter any additional tags that are not listed in the "Entry Tags" field.
* **Entry Date**: This field is optional, but please enter the date when you completed your cosplay costume.
* **Entry Time**: This field is optional, but please enter the time when you completed your cosplay costume.
* **Entry Note**: This field is optional, and you can use it to provide any additional comments or notes about your entry.
* **Entry Email**: This is a required field and allows us to contact you for any further information. Please enter a valid email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and high-quality photos of your cosplay costume.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your character design, costume, and entry.
* Select the correct category and tags for your entry to increase its visibility in our contest listing.
* Double-check your entry for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting it.
