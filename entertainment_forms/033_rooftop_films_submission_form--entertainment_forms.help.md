# Rooftop Films Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rooftop Films Submission Form is a tool used to collect and manage film information for potential screening on the Rooftop Films platform. This form gathers essential details about each film and helps us to curate and select films for inclusion in our screenings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the film in the "Film Title" field.
2. Select the film's genre from the "Film Genre" dropdown menu.
3. Provide a brief description of the film in the "Film Description" field.
4. Enter the name of the film's director in the "Film Director" field.
5. Set the date and time for the screening in the "Screening Date" and "Screening Time" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the location where the film will be screened in the "Film Location" field.
7. Upload a poster image or enter the URL of the film's poster in the "Film Poster" field.
8. If applicable, enter the link to the film's online presence or webpage in the "Film Link" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the film in the "Film Notes" field.
10. Enter your email address for contact purposes in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Film Title** (`film_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the film you are submitting.
* **Film Genre** (`film_genre`, select_one, optional): Select the genre of the film from the dropdown menu.
* **Film Description** (`film_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the film.
* **Film Director** (`film_director`, text, optional): Enter the name of the film's director.
* **Screening Date** (`film_screening_date`, date, optional): Set the date for the screening.
* **Screening Time** (`film_screening_time`, time, optional): Set the time for the screening.
* **Film Location** (`film_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the film will be screened.
* **Film Poster** (`film_poster`, text, optional): Upload a poster image or enter the URL of the film's poster.
* **Film Link** (`film_link`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the link to the film's online presence or webpage.
* **Film Notes** (`film_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the film.
* **Email** (`film_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
