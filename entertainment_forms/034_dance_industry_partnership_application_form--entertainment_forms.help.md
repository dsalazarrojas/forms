# Dance Industry Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dance Industry Partnership Application Form is designed for dance professionals to submit their collaboration requests, providing essential information about their projects, events, and goals. This form helps facilitate partnerships between dancers, event organizers, and industry stakeholders to bring innovative and engaging dance projects to life.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the collaboration requests section on page 1.
2. Provide details about your collaboration requests, including the type of partnership you are seeking.
3. On page 2, provide a detailed profile of your dance company or organization.
4. Select the dance style(s) you specialize in on page 3.
5. Specify your goals and objectives for the partnership on page 4.
6. Enter your contact information, including name, email address, phone number, and date of birth on pages 5-8.
7. Choose the date and time of your event on page 9.
8. Enter the location of your event on page 10.
9. Describe your event in detail on page 11.
10. Outline your evaluation criteria on page 12.
11. Choose the evaluation location on page 13.
12. Enter any additional notes on page 14.
13. Provide evaluation comments on page 15.
14. Enter your evaluation score on page 16 (if applicable).
15. Select the evaluation status on page 17.
16. Enter your reviewer's name, email address, and phone number on pages 18-20.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collaboration Requests**: Describe your collaboration requests in this text field. This section helps us understand what type of partnership you are seeking.
* **Partner Profile**: Provide a detailed profile of your dance company or organization in this text field. This section helps us get to know you better.
* **Dance Style**: Select the dance style(s) you specialize in from the options provided. This section helps us understand your expertise.
* **Goals**: Specify your goals and objectives for the partnership. This section is crucial in aligning our partnership goals.
* **Goals (Other)**: If your goals do not fit into the provided options, enter them in this text field.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name, the person filling out the form.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number with the area code.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (optional).
* **Date of Event**: Choose the date of your event.
* **Time of Event**: Choose the time of your event.
* **Location of Event**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Description of Event**: Describe your event in detail.
* **Goals and Objectives**: Outline your goals and objectives for the partnership.
* **Evaluation Criteria**: Enter your evaluation criteria in this text field.
* **Evaluation Date**: Choose the date of evaluation (optional).
* **Evaluation Time**: Choose the time of evaluation (optional).
* **Evaluation Location**: Select the location of evaluation.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your event or partnership.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Provide evaluation comments about your event or partnership.
* **Evaluation Score**: Enter your evaluation score (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Status**: Select the evaluation status of your event or partnership.
* **Evaluation Review**: Provide a review of your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Recommendation**: Enter any recommendations for future events or partnerships.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the reviewer with the area code.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Dance Industry Partnership Application Form efficiently and effectively, ensuring that your collaboration requests are accurately represented and increasing the chances of successful partnerships.
