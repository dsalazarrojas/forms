# TV Show Idea Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TV Show Idea Submission Form is designed for individuals to submit original and innovative ideas for new TV shows. This form allows you to share your ideas with our team, who will review and potentially develop them into a full-fledged TV show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with as much detail as possible. Please make sure to review each field's requirements and provide accurate and clear information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **TV Show Idea Submission Form** (`TV Show Name`, required/optional): Enter a brief title of your TV show idea.
* **TV Show Name** (`tv_show_name`, optional): Enter the name of your TV show, if you have one.
* **Series Type** (`series_type`, optional): Select the type of series you are proposing (Drama, Comedy, Sci-Fi, etc.).
* **Genre** (`genre`, optional): Choose the genre of your TV show (Action, Adventure, Romance, Sci-Fi, Thriller, etc.).
* **Main Character** (`main_character`, optional): Enter a brief description of your main character.
* **Episode Count** (`episode_count`, optional): Enter the number of episodes you envision for your TV show.
* **Episode Length** (`episode_length`, optional): Enter the length of each episode in minutes.
* **Main Character Description** (`main_character_description`, optional): Enter a detailed description of your main character.
* **Supporting Character Description** (`supporting_character_description`, optional): Enter a detailed description of your supporting characters.
* **Supporting Character Count** (`supporting_character_count`, optional): Enter the number of supporting characters in your TV show.
* **Supporting Characters** (`supporting_character`, optional): Select the type of actors you are considering for your TV show (Actor, Actress, Comedian, Director, Writer, etc.).
* **Supporting Character Description List** (`supporting_character_description_list`, optional): Enter a detailed description of your supporting characters.
* **Production Team** (`production_team`, optional): Select the members of your production team (Producer, Production Assistant, Director, Writer, etc.).
* **Production Team Description** (`production_team_description`, optional): Enter a detailed description of your production team.
* **Production Budget** (`production_budget`, optional): Enter the production budget for your TV show.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, optional): Enter the date you are submitting your TV show idea.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, optional): Enter the time of day you are submitting your TV show idea.
* **File Upload 1** (`file_upload_1`, optional): Upload a file related to your TV show idea (e.g., script, concept art, etc.).
* **File Upload 2** (`file_upload_2`, optional): Upload another file related to your TV show idea (e.g., script, concept art, etc.).
* **File Upload 3** (`file_upload_3`, optional): Upload a third file related to your TV show idea (e.g., script, concept art, etc.).
* **File Upload 4** (`file_upload_4`, optional): Upload a fourth file related to your TV show idea (e.g., script, concept art, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations about your TV show idea.
* **Submission Email** (`submission_email`, optional): Enter your email address for our team to contact you about your TV show idea.
* **Submission Phone** (`submission_phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for our team to contact you about your TV show idea.
