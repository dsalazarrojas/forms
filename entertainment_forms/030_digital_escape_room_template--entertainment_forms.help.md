# Digital Escape Room Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for creating and managing digital escape rooms. It's designed to collect relevant information from users, including the room title and theme, puzzle descriptions and media, player status, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the room title and theme.
2. Add a brief description of your puzzle.
3. Choose a media type for your puzzle (image, video, or audio).
4. Indicate the player status (active, inactive, or solved).
5. Decide whether to submit the form immediately or save it for later.
6. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Choose the correct theme and player status for your room.
* Decide on the submission status wisely, as it will affect the form's behavior.
* Enter your actual puzzle image, video, or audio file for each puzzle.
* Use the escape code as a secret code for the room.
* Set a realistic date and time for the submission.
