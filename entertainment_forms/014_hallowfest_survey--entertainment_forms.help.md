# Hallowfest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hallowfest Survey is designed to gather information about attendees at an event. The survey is used to collect demographic information (name, email, phone number, date of birth), survey-related data (whether they attended an event, their answers to survey questions, time spent on the event, and comments), and a rating to help the event organizers understand the attendees' experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your attendee's name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2. Enter your email in the "email" field. Please use the format "yourname@email.com".
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
5. Answer "True" or "False" to the question of whether you attended the event in the "Event Attendee Survey" field.
6. Enter your answers to the "Survey Questions" in the text field.
7. Enter your responses to the "Survey Answers" in the text field.
8. Enter the number of hours you spent at the event in the "Time Spent" field.
9. Enter any additional comments about your experience in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter the time you visited the event in the "Time of Visit" field in the format "hh:mm A/PM".
11. Rate your overall experience by selecting a number from 1-10 in the "Survey Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name (attendee_name)**: Enter the name of the attendee you are representing.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter the date of birth of the attendee in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Event Attendee Survey (event_attendee_survey)**: Answer "True" or "False" to whether the attendee attended the event.
* **Survey Questions (survey_questions)**: Answer the survey questions in this text field.
* **Survey Answers (survey_answers)**: Enter any other comments or answers about the event.
* **Time Spent (time_spent)**: Enter the number of hours you spent at the event.
* **Comments (survey_comments)**: Enter any additional comments about your experience.
* **Time of Visit (time_of_visit)**: Enter the time you visited the event in the format "hh:mm A/PM".
* **Survey Rating (survey_rating)**: Rate your overall experience by selecting a number from 1-10.
