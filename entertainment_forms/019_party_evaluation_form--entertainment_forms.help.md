# Party Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive evaluation of a party or event, helping you to identify its strengths and weaknesses and providing valuable feedback to the organiser.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the instructions carefully before starting the form.
2. Answer every question to the best of your ability. If a question does not apply to you, please select "N/A" or "Not Applicable".
3. Use the dropdown menus to select your answer choices.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How well was the party?** (`party_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the party. Choose the rating that best describes your experience.
  * 1: The party was disappointing and did not meet my expectations.
  * 2: The party was average, it was just okay.
  * 3: The party was good and enjoyable.
  * 4: The party was great and exceeded my expectations.
  * 5: The party was excellent and one of the best I've ever attended.
* **What was the value of the party?** (`party_value`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the values that applied to the party.
  * 1: The party was too expensive.
  * 2: The party was fairly priced.
  * 3: The party was good value for money.
  * 4: The party was a bargain.
  * 5: The party was free.
* **How well was the food?** (`food_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the food. Choose the rating that best describes your experience.
  * 1: The food was poor and not good.
  * 2: The food was average and unimpressive.
  * 3: The food was good and enjoyable.
  * 4: The food was excellent and exceeded my expectations.
  * 5: The food was one of the best parts of the party.
* **How good was the atmosphere?** (`party_atmopshere`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the aspects that made the atmosphere at the party good.
  * 1: The party had a bad atmosphere.
  * 2: The party had an average atmosphere.
  * 3: The party had a good atmosphere.
  * 4: The party had a great atmosphere.
  * 5: The party had an excellent and unforgettable atmosphere.
* **How good was the music?** (`music_quality`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the aspects that made the music good.
  * 1: The music was bad and uninteresting.
  * 2: The music was average and not memorable.
  * 3: The music was good and enjoyable.
  * 4: The music was great and exceeded my expectations.
  * 5: The music was outstanding and a highlight of the party.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the party.
  * 1: I was very dissatisfied with the party.
  * 2: I was somewhat dissatisfied with the party.
  * 3: I was neutral, it was just okay.
  * 4: I was satisfied with the party.
  * 5: I was very satisfied with the party.
* **Event Coordinator** (`event_coordinator`, text, optional): If you want to provide feedback on the event coordinator, describe your experience.
* **Party Theme** (`party_theme`, select_one, optional): Choose the theme that best describes the party.
* **Party Venue** (`party_venue`, text, optional): If you want to provide feedback on the party venue, describe your experience.
* **Party Organizer** (`party_organizer`, select_one, optional): Choose the rating that best describes the party organizer's performance.
  * 1: The organizer did a poor job.
  * 2: The organizer did a fair job.
  * 3: The organizer did a good job.
  * 4: The organizer did a great job.
  * 5: The organizer did an excellent job.

## Tips
* Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If a question is not applicable to you, please select "N/A" or "Not Applicable".
* Please double-check your answers before submitting.
* If you have any additional feedback, feel free to include it in the "Event Coordinator" field.
* This form is intended to be completed by one person per party, so it's best to fill it out within 24 hours after the party.
