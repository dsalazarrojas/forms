# Zoom Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for a webinar hosted on the Zoom platform. Please fill out the form accurately to help us prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the required fields, marked as **Required** in the form.
2. Make sure to enter your name correctly, including both First Name and Last Name.
3. Enter your valid email address in the Email field.
4. Choose your preferred Time slot for the webinar from the dropdown menu.
5. Select one option from the Select One field.
6. Choose multiple options from the Select Multiple field. 
7. Enter any additional comments or questions in the Note field.
8. Select whether you will be attending the webinar (Yes/No) in the Yes/No field.
9. Enter any further comments or questions in the second Note field (only required if specified by the organizer).
10. Choose multiple options from the second Select Multiple field (only required if specified by the organizer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive important updates about the webinar.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select your preferred time slot for the webinar.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the webinar.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Yes/No** (`yes_no`, select_one, required): Select whether you will be attending the webinar.
* **Note 2** (`note2`, note, optional): If specified by the organizer, enter any further comments or questions here.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple2`, select_multiple, optional): If specified by the organizer, choose multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Time 2** (`time2`, time, optional): If specified by the organizer, select your second preferred time slot.
* **Email 2** (`email2`, email, optional): If specified by the organizer, enter a valid email address.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one2`, select_one, optional): If specified by the organizer, choose an option from the dropdown menu.
* **Note 3** (`note3`, note, optional): This field is optional and may be required depending on the organizer's requirements.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the organizer's specifications.
