# Rehearsal Studio Lighting Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Rehearsal Studio Lighting Booking Form" is designed for clients to book and customize lighting services for their events, rehearsals, and productions held at a rehearsal studio. This form will help you gather essential information to ensure that your event receives the perfect lighting setup.

## How To Complete This Form
To book your rehearsal studio lighting, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information for reference and communication purposes.
2. Select the date of your event.
3. Choose the time slot for your event.
4. Specify the number of people attending your event.
5. Describe your lighting requirements for the event.
6. Provide the name of the contact person responsible for your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rehearsal Studio Name** (`rehearsal_studio_name`, text, required): This is the name of the rehearsal studio where your event will take place. Please enter the name as it appears on our website or marketing materials.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your event. This can be in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Choose the time slot for your event in 24-hour format `HH:MM`.
* **Number of People** (`number_of_people`, number, optional): Enter the estimated number of people attending your event.
* **Lighting Requirements** (`lighting_requirements`, text, optional): Describe your lighting requirements for the event. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for your event. This will help us get in touch with you regarding any further information or questions.
