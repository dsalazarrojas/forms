# feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This feedback_form is designed to collect user feedback for the entertainment services provided. It is intended to help us understand your thoughts and suggestions on how we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information on each page.
2.  Please answer the rating question with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest rating and 5 is the highest.
3.  Enter your comment, name, and feedback in the provided text boxes.
4.  Provide your email address if you wish to be contacted back.
5.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your agreement with the provided statements.
6.  Finally, submit your feedback by selecting the 'Submit' button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 5.
*   **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts and suggestions on how we can improve our services.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted back, please enter your email address.
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for our records.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about our services.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' to submit your feedback and 'No' to cancel.
*   **Agree** (`agree`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate your agreement with the provided statements by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for our records, but this is optional. 

## Tips
- Please be honest and sincere in your feedback.
- We value your thoughts and suggestions and will use them to improve our services.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please describe them clearly in the 'Comment' field. 

Note: The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the entertainment services provided, but the provided fields are not specific enough to determine the exact services in question. The form is still useful for understanding general customer sentiments and experiences.
