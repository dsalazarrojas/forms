# New Year Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your interests, preferences, and behaviors related to entertainment and leisure activities. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us better understand your habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all that apply for each field. You can choose one or multiple options for the select_multiple type fields.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields marked as "Required".
3. Answer honestly and to the best of your knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your current goals?** (`current_goals`, select_multiple, Required): This field asks about your short-term and long-term goals, such as personal, career, financial, education, health and wellness, relationship, travel, and hobby goals.
* **Where do you spend most of your free time?** (`favorite_places`, select_multiple, Required): Please select all the places where you usually spend most of your free time, like home, work, school, park, beach, city, forest, mountain, or rural area.
* **What are your favorite activities or hobbies?** (`hobbies`, select_multiple, Required): Choose your favorite activities or hobbies, such as reading, writing, music, art, sports, cooking, traveling, playing video games, hiking, or dancing.
* **Where did you go on a vacation last year?** (`vacation_last_year`, select_multiple, Required): Select all the places you visited on your last vacation, like beach resort, city break, national park, theme park, cruise, cruise ship, road trip, or mountain retreat.
* **What is the best time of year for you?** (`best_time_of_year`, select_one, Required): Choose the time of year that you prefer, which could be spring, summer, autumn, or winter.
* **What is your favorite type of entertainment?** (`favorite_entertainment`, select_multiple, Required): Select all the types of entertainment you enjoy, such as TV shows, movies, music, video games, books, sports, theater, concerts, or festivals.
* **What is your favorite type of music?** (`favorite_music`, select_multiple, Required): Choose all the types of music you like, like pop, rock, classical, jazz, folk, hip-hop, country, electronic, or R&B.
* **How often do you go to the movies?** (`movie_watching_frequency`, select_one, Required): Answer how frequently you visit the movies, which could be daily, weekly, monthly, or rarely.
* **Do you have a favorite restaurant?** (`favorite_restaurant`, select_multiple, Optional): If you have a favorite restaurant, select "Yes".
