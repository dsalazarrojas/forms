# Children's Show Appearance Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the child's personal and contact details, as well as their participation in an upcoming show or production. This information will be used to ensure that the child's needs and boundaries are respected, and to obtain necessary permissions for media use and participation in the production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide the child's information on page 1, including their full name, nickname or stage name, date of birth, current age, and home address.
2. On page 2, provide the parent or guardian's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email.
3. On page 3, indicate your permission for media use and participation in the production, including granting permission for photographs, video recordings, and audio recordings.
4. On page 4, select the approved media usage for the production, including social media, website, print materials, press releases, internal archives, or educational purposes.
5. On page 5, indicate your understanding of compensation and liability, including payment or volunteer status and acceptance of liability.
6. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your agreement and understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child's Full Name**: Enter the child's full name as it appears on their birth certificate.
* **Child's Nickname or Stage Name**: If the child has a nickname or stage name, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Child's Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Child's Current Age**: Enter the child's current age.
* **Child's Home Address**: Enter the child's full street address.
* **Parent or Guardian Information**: Enter the parent or guardian's contact details, including name, phone number, and email.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's first name.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's last name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the parent or guardian's primary contact phone number.
* **Alternate Contact**: Provide an alternate contact phone number for the child, if applicable.
* **Show Information**: Provide details about the show or production the child will be participating in.
* **Name of Show or Production**: Enter the name of the show or production.
* **Production Company or Organization**: Enter the name of the production company or organization.
* **Performance Dates**: Enter the expected dates of participation.
* **Media Release Authorization**: Indicate whether you grant permission for media use. 
* **Photo Permission**: Indicate whether you grant permission for photographs of your child to be taken. 
* **Video Permission**: Indicate whether you grant permission for video recordings of your child. 
* **Audio Permission**: Indicate whether you grant permission for audio recordings of your child's voice. 
* **Approved Media Usage**: Select all the approved media usage for the production. 
* **Compensation Agreement**: Indicate your understanding of compensation and liability. 
* **Liability Waiver**: Indicate whether you accept liability and risk associated with participation. 
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of signature. 
* **Additional Comments or Concerns**: Provide any additional comments or concerns related to the child's participation.

**Note:** This form is intended for informational and entertainment purposes only. If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
