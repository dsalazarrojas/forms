# Television Pilot Episode Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and voting information for a television pilot episode. It is designed to help production teams and decision-makers gather valuable insights and opinions from relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and clear information.
2. Provide feedback and opinions on the series concept, episode name, target audience, and voting score.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Comments" field.
4. Ensure that the "Submit" button is clicked to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Series Concept** (`new_series_concept`, text, required): Describe the concept of the series, including its premise, genre, and expected target audience.
* **Episode Name** (`episode_name`, text, required): Provide the name of the episode or series episode.
* **Series Description** (`series_description`, text, required): Write a brief description of the series, including its main themes, tone, and style.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_multiple, required): Select the audience that the series is intended for, such as Young Adults, Adults, or Children.
* **Voting Score** (`voting_score`, select_one, required): Rate the series on a scale of High, Medium, or Low.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the series.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your completed form.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you are submitting your feedback.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time you are submitting your feedback.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, required): Select your time zone, such as Pacific or Eastern.
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, number, required): Enter the time offset in minutes.
* **Timezone Offset in Minutes** (`timezone_offset_minutes`, number, optional): Enter the time offset in minutes.
* **Timezone Offset in Hours** (`timezone_offset_hours`, number, optional): Enter the time offset in hours.
* **Timezone Offset in Seconds** (`timezone_offset_seconds`, number, optional): Enter the time offset in seconds.
* **Timezone Offset AM/PM** (`timezone_offset_am_pm`, select_one, optional): Select AM or PM.
* **Timezone Offset Description** (`timezone_offset_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the time offset.
* **Timezone Offset Notes** (`timezone_offset_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about the time offset.
* **Timezone Offset Additional Info** (`timezone_offset_additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the time offset.
* **Timezone Offset Reference** (`timezone_offset_reference`, text, optional): Provide a reference for the time offset.
* **Timezone Offset Reference URL** (`timezone_offset_reference_url`, text, optional): Provide a URL for the time offset reference.
* **Timezone Offset iCal** (`timezone_offset_ical`, text, optional): Provide the iCal feed for the time offset.
* **Timezone Offset iCal URL** (`timezone_offset_ical_url`, text, optional): Provide the iCal URL for the time offset.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and clear.
* Some fields are mandatory, please fill them out correctly.
