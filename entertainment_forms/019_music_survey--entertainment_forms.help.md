# music_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_survey form collects information on users' music habits, preferences, and behavior, helping us better understand your relationship with music.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please go through each page, starting with the first page "Introduction".
2.  Read each field label carefully and fill in the required information.
3.  You can choose from the provided options for select fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
*   **Music Genre** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Please choose a music genre that best describes your taste in music.
*   **Artists List** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the artists you like from the provided list (artist1, artist2, artist3).
*   **Frequency Of Use** (`4`, `select_one`, required: `false`): How often do you listen to music? Please select "often", "occasionally", "rarely", or "never".
*   **Age Of User** (`5`, `number`, required: `false`): Please enter your age.
*   **Favorite Band** (`6`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose your favorite band from the list (band1, band2, band3).
*   **Favorite Song** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of your favorite song.
*   **Music Format** (`8`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose your preferred music format (mp3, wav, flac).
*   **Social Media** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select your social media preferences from the list (social1, social2, social3).
*   **User Location** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter your location.
*   **Music Streaming** (`11`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Do you use music streaming services? Select "True" or "False".
*   **Music Purchase** (`12`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Have you ever purchased music? Select "True" or "False".
*   **Music Share** (`13`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Do you share your music with others? Select "True" or "False".
*   **Music Recommend** (`14`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Do you recommend music to others? Select "True" or "False".
*   **Music Survey Time** (`15`, `time`, required: `false`): Please enter the time you spent on this music survey.

**Tips**

*   If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the provided options or seek further clarification.
*   Please fill in the form accurately and honestly to help us better understand your music habits.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
