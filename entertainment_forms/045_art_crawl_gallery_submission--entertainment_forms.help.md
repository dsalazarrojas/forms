# Art Crawl Gallery Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect necessary information about your gallery for the art crawl event. By providing accurate and complete information, you'll help create a successful and enjoyable experience for participating artists and visitors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Gallery Information**: This section is for general gallery details.
	* **Gallery Name**: Enter the name of your gallery.
	* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your gallery's participation.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
	* **Gallery Address**: Enter the address of your gallery.
	* **Gallery Website**: Enter the website URL of your gallery (optional).
2. **Art Crawl Information**: This section is for event-specific details.
	* **Art Crawl Event Name**: Enter the name of the art crawl event.
	* **Art Crawl Date**: Enter the date of the art crawl event.
	* **Hours of Operation**: Enter the hours of operation for the event.
3. **Exhibit Details**: This section is for information about the exhibited art.
	* **Exhibit Title**: Enter the title of the exhibited artwork.
	* **Exhibit Description**: Enter a brief description of the exhibited artwork.
	* **Featured Artists**: List the names of artists participating in the event.
	* **Primary Medium or Art Form**: Select the primary medium or art form of the exhibited artwork.
4. **Logistics and Requirements**: This section is for logistical information about your gallery.
	* **Floor Space**: Enter the available square feet of floor space (optional).
	* **Wall Space**: Enter the available linear feet of wall space (optional).
	* **Special Requirements**: List any special requirements for displaying the artwork (e.g., lighting, climate control, security).
	* **Parking Availability**: Select how much parking is available for visitors.
5. **Promotion and Marketing**: This section is for promotional information.
	* **Promotional Materials**: List any promotional materials you'll provide (e.g., postcards, posters, social media).
	* **Gallery Social Media Handles**: Enter your gallery's social media handles for cross-promotion.

## Tips

- Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Use clear and concise language in your responses.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact event organizers for assistance.
