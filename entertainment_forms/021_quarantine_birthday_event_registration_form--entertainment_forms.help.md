# Quarantine Birthday Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quarantine Birthday Event Registration Form is a tool for users to register new events to be held under quarantine due to the COVID-19 pandemic. It allows users to provide details about the event, including name, date, time, location, and description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
5. Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
6. Choose the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
7. Choose the time zone of the event in the "Event Time Zone" field.
8. Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
9. Provide a description of the event in the "Event Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the event you are registering.
* **Event Date** (date, required: false): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (time, required: false): Select the time of the event.
* **Event Time Zone** (select_one, required: false): Choose the time zone of the event (UTC, EST, PST, or CST).
* **Event Location** (text, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (note, required: false): Provide a description of the event to give context.

## Tips
* Ensure you are registering future events only.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate event information.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
