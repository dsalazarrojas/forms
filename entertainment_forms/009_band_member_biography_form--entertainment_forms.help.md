# Band Member Biography Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Band Member Biography Form is used to collect information about each member of a band. This form is used to create a comprehensive profile of each band member, including their contact information, musical experience, and performance history. By filling out this form, you will be able to showcase your talents and experiences to potential collaborators, bookers, and other interested parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide your stage name (if different from your full name).
3. Enter your email address to contact you directly.
4. Enter your phone number for easy communication.
5. Enter your date of birth (in MM/DD/YYYY format).
6. Enter the city and state where you grew up.
7. Enter your current city and state of residence.
8. Select your primary instrument or role in the band.
9. Select any secondary instruments you are proficient in.
10. Enter the number of years you have been performing.
11. Select the musical genres you are familiar with.
12. Choose your role in the band.
13. Write a short biography about yourself.
14. List any notable performances or events you have played.
15. List any awards or recognition you have received.
16. List any previous bands or projects you have been part of.
17. List any artists who inspire your style.
18. Enter your social media links (e.g. Instagram, Twitter, Facebook).
19. Enter your personal website or portfolio URL (if applicable).
20. Enter your streaming profiles (e.g. Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud).
21. Upload a professional photo for press kit purposes.
22. Select your availability status for touring and gigging.
23. List any equipment or gear you use.
24. Specify any technical requirements for your performances.
25. Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
26. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
27. Provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your profile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`member_full_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Stage Name** (`stage_name`, text, optional): Enter your stage name if different from your full name.
* **Email Address** (`member_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`member_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Hometown** (`hometown`, text, optional): Enter the city and state where you grew up.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, text, required): Enter the city and state of your current residence.
* **Primary Instrument or Role** (`primary_instrument`, select_one, required): Select your primary instrument or role in the band.
* **Secondary Instruments** (`secondary_instruments`, select_multiple, optional): Select any other instruments you are proficient in.
* **Years of Musical Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been performing.
* **Musical Genres** (`musical_genres`, select_multiple, required): Select the musical genres you are familiar with.
* **Role in the Band** (`band_role`, select_one, required): Choose your role in the band.
* **Biography** (`biography_text`, text, required): Write a short bio about yourself.
* **Notable Performances** (`notable_performances`, text, optional): List any notable performances or events you have played.
* **Awards and Recognition** (`awards_recognition`, text, optional): List any awards or recognition you have received.
* **Previous Bands or Projects** (`previous_bands`, text, optional): List any previous bands or projects you have been part of.
* **Musical Influences** (`influences`, text, optional): List any artists who inspire your style.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, text, optional): Enter your social media links (e.g. Instagram, Twitter, Facebook).
* **Personal Website** (`website_url`, text, optional): Enter your personal website or portfolio URL.
* **Streaming Profiles** (`streaming_profiles`, text, optional): Enter your streaming profiles (e.g. Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud).
* **Headshot Photo** (`photo_upload`, text, optional): Upload a professional photo for press kit purposes.
* **Availability Status** (`availability`, select_one, required): Select your availability status for touring and gigging.
* **Equipment and Gear** (`gear_equipment`, text, optional): List any equipment or gear you use.
* **Technical Requirements** (`technical_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any technical requirements for your performances.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your profile.
