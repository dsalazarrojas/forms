# Choreography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Choreography Contract Form is used to document the details of a dance performance project, including client and choreographer information, project specifics, payment terms, and other relevant details. This form helps establish a clear understanding between the client and choreographer about the scope of work, payment expectations, and contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the client information fields (client's name, last name, phone number, email address, address).
2. Select the choreographer's information (name, company, phone number, email address).
3. Enter the project details (project name, type of performance, number of dancers, performance duration).
4. Choose the payment terms (fee, payment schedule, deposit due date, final payment due date).
5. Specify the cancellation policy and terms.
6. Choose the rights and ownership of the choreography.
7. Select the recording and video rights.
8. Add any additional terms or conditions.
9. Choose the travel expenses arrangement.
10. Specify the costume consultation and music selection.
11. Add any special requirements.
12. Enter any additional comments or notes.
13. Confirm that the client and choreographer agree to all terms.
14. Sign and date the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (1): Enter the client's name, last name, phone number, and email address.
* **Client First Name** (3): Enter the first name of the client.
* **Client Last Name** (4): Enter the last name of the client.
* **Organization or Company** (5): Enter the client's company or organization name, if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (6): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Email Address** (7): Enter the client's email address.
* **Address** (8): Enter the client's street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Choreographer Information** (9): Enter the choreographer's name, company, phone number, and email address.
* **Choreographer Name** (10): Enter the choreographer's name.
* **Choreography Company** (11): Enter the choreographer's company name, if applicable.
* **Choreographer Phone** (12): Enter the choreographer's phone number.
* **Choreographer Email** (13): Enter the choreographer's email address.
* **Project Details** (14): Enter a brief description of the project or performance.
* **Project or Performance Name** (15): Enter the name of the project or performance.
* **Type of Project** (16): Select the type of project or performance (e.g., solo, group, competition).
* **Dance Style** (17): Select the dance style (e.g., ballet, jazz, hip-hop).
* **Number of Dancers** (18): Enter the number of dancers in the performance.
* **Expected Performance Duration** (19): Enter the expected duration of the performance in minutes.
* **Performance Dates** (20): Enter all scheduled performance dates.
* **Performance Location** (21): Enter the venue name and address where the performance will take place.
* **Rehearsal Schedule** (22): Enter a brief description of the rehearsal schedule.
* **Rehearsals Start Date** (23): Enter the start date of the rehearsals.
* **Rehearsals End Date** (24): Enter the end date of the rehearsals.
* **Number of Scheduled Rehearsals** (25): Enter the number of scheduled rehearsals.
* **Rehearsal Location** (26): Enter the location of the rehearsals (e.g., studio, venue).
* **Rehearsal Times** (27): Enter the days and times of the rehearsals.
* **Compensation** (28): Enter the payment details, including the total fee and any other terms.
* **Total Choreography Fee** (29): Enter the total fee for the choreography services.
* **Fee Includes** (30): Select which services are included in the fee.
* **Payment Schedule** (31): Select the payment schedule (e.g., 50% deposit, 50% on completion).
* **Deposit Amount** (32): Enter the deposit amount, if applicable.
* **Deposit Due Date** (33): Enter the due date for the deposit.
* **Final Payment Due Date** (34): Enter the due date for the final payment.
* **Payment Method** (35): Select the payment method (e.g., cash, check, bank transfer).
* **Cancellation Policy** (36): Enter the cancellation policy and terms.
* **Cancellation Terms** (37): Select the cancellation terms (e.g., deposit non-refundable, 50% refund).
* **Cancellation Details** (38): Enter any additional cancellation terms or conditions.
* **Rights and Ownership** (39): Select the rights and ownership of the choreography.
* **Choreography Ownership** (40): Select who owns the choreography (e.g., choreographer, client).
* **Recording and Video Rights** (41): Select the recording and video rights.
* **Additional Terms** (42): Enter any additional terms or conditions.
* **Travel Expenses** (43): Select who pays for travel expenses (e.g., client, choreographer).
* **Costume Consultation** (44): Select whether the costume consultation is included.
* **Music Selection** (45): Select who selects the music (e.g., client, choreographer).
* **Special Requirements** (46): Enter any special requirements or conditions.
* **Additional Comments** (47): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Agreement** (48): Confirm that the client and choreographer agree to all terms.
* **Client Agrees** (49): Confirm that the client agrees to all terms.
* **Choreographer Agrees** (50): Confirm that the choreographer agrees to all terms.
* **Client Signature** (51): Enter the client's digital signature.
* **Client Signature Date** (52): Enter the date of the client's signature.
* **Choreographer Signature** (53): Enter the choreographer's digital signature.
* **Choreographer Signature Date** (54): Enter the date of the choreographer's signature.
