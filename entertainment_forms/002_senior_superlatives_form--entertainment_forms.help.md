# Senior Superlatives Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to nominate and vote for superlatives within a department or group of students. It helps in organizing and conducting the superlatives voting process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the "Name of the Senior Superlatives Form" field with the name of the superlatives event you are nominating for.
*   Select who from your department will be attending the superlatives event in the "Who will be attending from our department?" field.
*   Enter the name of the person who will be attending in the "Name" field.
*   Choose the category you will be voting for in the "What category will be voted for?" field.
*   Indicate whether the person you are nominating will be voting for category 1 in the "Will the person attending be voting for category 1?" field.
*   Indicate whether the person you are nominating will be voting for category 2 in the "Will the person attending be voting for category 2?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name of the Senior Superlatives Form** (text, Required):
    This field is used to enter the name of the superlatives event.
*   **Who will be attending from our department?** (select multiple, Required):
    Select all the people from your department who will be attending the superlatives event.
*   **Name** (text, Required):
    Enter the name of the person who will be attending the superlatives event.
*   **What category will be voted for?** (select multiple, Required):
    Choose the category you will be voting for.
*   **Will the person attending be voting for category 1?** (select one, Required):
    Indicate whether the person you are nominating will be voting for category 1.
*   **Will the person attending be voting for category 2?** (select one, Required):
    Indicate whether the person you are nominating will be voting for category 2.

## Tips

*   Fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure the correct data is collected.
*   Ensure that the form is submitted before the deadline to avoid last-minute rush and possible errors.
*   Only fill out the form if you are authorized to do so, as it is used for nominating and voting purposes.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's explanation above or contact the superlatives organizers for guidance.
