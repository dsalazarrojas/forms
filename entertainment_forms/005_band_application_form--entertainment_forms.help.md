# Band Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from bands and artists interested in performing at the HSGA Virtual Convention 2021. The information gathered will help us create an engaging and informative program for the convention attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields in each section, as denoted by the "required" flag in the form.
2. Be concise and clear in your answers, especially in the band bio and additional comments sections.
3. Use the provided options as a guide when selecting your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Band or Artist Name**: Enter the name you want listed in the program.
* **Musical Genre**: Select the genre of music you perform.
* **Primary Contact Person**: Name of the person handling the application.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your email address to be contacted for further information.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for scheduling and coordination.
* **Number of Band Members**: Total number of performers in your band.
* **List of Band Member Names**: Include a list of all band members, including instruments played.
* **Band Website or Social Media Link**: Provide a URL to your official website or social media profile.
* **Performance Video Link (YouTube/Vimeo)**: Share a link to a recent high-quality performance.
* **Short Band Biography (100-200 words)**: Write a brief introduction to your band, to be included in the convention program.
* **Technical Requirements for Virtual Recording**: Select your current audio/video recording setup.
* **Availability for Live Virtual Q&A Sessions**: Choose your preferred time zone for scheduling.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Share any additional information or requests you have.

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your answers for spelling and grammar before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please include them in the additional comments section.
* Our team will review and contact you with further instructions and details after receiving your application.
