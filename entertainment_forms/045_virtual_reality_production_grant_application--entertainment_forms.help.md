# virtual_reality_production_grant_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The virtual reality production grant application form is designed to collect information from organizations seeking funding for virtual reality projects. This guide will walk you through each field of the form to ensure that you fill it out correctly and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
Before you start filling out the form, make sure you have the necessary information and supporting documents ready. The form has multiple pages, each with its own set of fields. Please go through each page carefully and complete the fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Virtual Reality Production Grant Application** (Page 1, Field 1): This is the title of your project. Provide a brief and descriptive title that accurately represents your virtual reality project.
* **Category** (Page 1, Field 2): Select the category that best describes your project from the options provided: Arts, Entertainment, or Other. This will help the grant committee understand the nature of your project.
* **Description** (Page 1, Field 3): This is your opportunity to describe your virtual reality project in detail. Please provide a concise and clear summary of your project, including its goals, objectives, and key features.
* **Organization** (Page 1, Field 4): Enter the name of your organization, which will be associated with your grant application.
* **Contact Name** (Page 1, Field 5): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your organization.
* **Email** (Page 1, Field 6): Enter the email address of the contact person. This is how we will communicate with you about your application.
* **Phone** (Page 1, Field 7): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Budget** (Page 1, Field 8): Enter your estimated budget for the project. Please provide a realistic and detailed breakdown of your expenses.
* **Budget Justification** (Page 1, Field 9): In this field, explain how you arrived at your budget and justify any significant expenses.
* **Supporting Documents** (Page 1, Field 10): Upload any relevant documents that support your project, such as concept art, project plans, or previous successes.
* **Submission Date** (Page 1, Field 11): Date and time you are submitting your application.
