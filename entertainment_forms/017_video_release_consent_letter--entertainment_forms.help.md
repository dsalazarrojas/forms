# Video Release Consent Letter - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Release Consent Letter form is designed to collect information from individuals featured in videos or recordings. This form is used to obtain consent from the individual to release their likeness, voice, or other personal characteristics in a video or recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your home address in the "home_street_address" field.
4. Provide your city in the "city" field.
5. Provide your state in the "state" field.
6. Enter your zip code in the "zip" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
9. Enter the title of the video in which you appeared in the "video_title" field.
10. If you are aware, enter the date the video was released in the "video_release_date" field.
11. If you are aware, enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
12. If you are aware, enter the date you would like the video to be released in the "release_date" field.
13. If you are aware, enter the time you would like the video to be released in the "release_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text, required): This field is used to collect your first name.
* **last_name** (Text, required): This field is used to collect your last name.
* **home_street_address** (Text, required): This field is used to collect your home street address.
* **city** (Text, required): This field is used to collect your city.
* **state** (Text, required): This field is used to collect your state or province.
* **zip** (Text, required): This field is used to collect your zip or postal code.
* **email** (Email, required): This field is used to collect your email address.
* **phone** (Text, required): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **video_title** (Text, required): This field is used to collect the title of the video in which you appeared.
* **video_release_date** (Date, optional): This field is used to record the date the video was released.
* **date_of_birth** (Date, optional): This field is used to record your date of birth.
* **release_date** (Date, optional): This field is used to record the date you would like the video to be released.
* **release_time** (Time, optional): This field is used to record the time you would like the video to be released.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the form administrator for clarification.
* The information collected on this form will be used to obtain your consent to release your likeness, voice, or other personal characteristics in a video or recording.
* The form is designed to be completed by the individual featured in the video or recording.
