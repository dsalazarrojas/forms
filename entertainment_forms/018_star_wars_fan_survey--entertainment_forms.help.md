# Star Wars Fan Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Star Wars Fan Survey form is designed to gather information about your preferences and interests related to the Star Wars universe. This form will help us understand what makes you a Star Wars fan and how to better cater to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the form honestly, indicating your preferences and interests related to Star Wars.
2. Select the options that best match your feelings.
3. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please use the "Feedback" field.
4. If you have a favorite character, book, video, or other media related to Star Wars, please use the "Favorite" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Choose how you identify as a Star Wars fan (e.g., casual fan, die-hard fan, or other).
* **Name**: Please enter your full name (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional, for potential follow-up communication).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional, for additional contact or survey follow-up).
* **Date**: This field is not currently used, but it might be in the future.
* **Time**: This field is not currently used, but it might be in the future.
* **Preference 01**: Choose your preference for a particular aspect of Star Wars (e.g., character, movie, book).
* **Preference 02**: Choose your preference for another aspect of Star Wars (e.g., character, movie, book).
* **Feedback**: Share any thoughts, suggestions, or feedback you have about the form or the Star Wars universe.
* **Suggestion**: Suggest any changes or improvements to the form or the Star Wars universe.
* **Favorite Character**: Choose your favorite Star Wars character.
* **Favorite Movie**: Choose your favorite Star Wars movie.
* **Favorite Ship**: Choose your favorite Star Wars ship.
* **Favorite Planet**: Choose your favorite Star Wars planet.
* **Favorite Species**: Choose your favorite Star Wars species.
* **Favorite Quote**: Enter your favorite Star Wars quote.
* **Favorite Book**: Enter your favorite Star Wars book.
* **Favorite Game**: Enter your favorite Star Wars game.
* **Favorite Comic**: Enter your favorite Star Wars comic.
* **Favorite Video**: Enter your favorite Star Wars video.
* **Favorite TV Show**: Enter your favorite Star Wars TV show.
* **Favorite Podcast**: Enter your favorite Star Wars podcast.
* **Favorite Pledge**: Enter your favorite Star Wars pledge or charity.
* **Favorite Fanboy**: Enter your favorite Star Wars fanboy or fanboy-like personality.
