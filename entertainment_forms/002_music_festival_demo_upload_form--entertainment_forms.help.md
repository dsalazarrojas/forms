# music_festival_demo_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music festival demo upload form is a tool for artists and bands to submit their music for evaluation by festival organizers. This form gathers essential information about the artist/band, their music, and contact details to consider their participation in the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and band name in the respective fields.
2. Select the genre(s) that best describe your music.
3. Enter your contact email and phone number for festival organizers to get in touch with you.
4. Provide a brief description of your music.
5. Upload your music file (.mp3, .wav, or .flac).
6. Enter a name for your uploaded file.
7. Select the size of your uploaded file.
8. Click the "Upload" button to upload your music file.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your music for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist** (`artist`, text, optional): Enter your personal name as the artist.
* **Band** (`band`, text, optional): Enter your band name if you have one.
* **Music** (`music`, text, optional): Briefly describe your music genre or style.
* **Demo** (`demo`, text, optional): Enter a description of the music you're uploading.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where the festival organizers can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for festival organizers to get in touch with you.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide more details about yourself and your music.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, optional): Select and upload your music file (.mp3, .wav, or .flac).
* **File Name** (`file_name`, text, optional): Give a name to your uploaded music file.
* **File Size** (`file_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of your uploaded music file in MB.
* **Genre** (`genre`, select_multiple, required): Choose the genre(s) that best describe your music.
* **Upload** (`upload_button`, text, optional): Click to upload your music file.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit your music for evaluation by festival organizers.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload a high-quality music file that is not too large.
* Be specific with your genre(s) selection to help festival organizers understand your music style.
* Use a descriptive file name and size for easier identification.
* Double-check your contact details for accuracy.
* Click "Submit" to complete the submission process.
