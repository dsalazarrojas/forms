# Trading Card Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trading Card Submission Form is designed for users to submit information about trading cards they would like to trade or sell. This form collects details such as the card's condition, price, and description, as well as contact information and submission status updates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Submit your trading card details**: Enter the required and optional information about the trading card you would like to submit.
2. **Provide your contact information**: Enter your email address, phone number, and submission contact information.
3. **Upload an image**: If you have an image of the trading card, upload it to the form.
4. **Confirm your submission**: Review and confirm your submission details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Card Details** (`card_details`): Enter a brief description of the trading card you would like to submit.
* **Card Price** (`card_price`): Enter the price of the trading card you would like to submit.
* **Card Condition** (`card_condition`): Select the condition of the trading card, such as Near Mint, Like New, Used, or Damaged.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`): If you have a link to an image of the trading card, enter it here.
* **Image File** (`image_file`): If you have an image of the trading card, upload it to the form.
* **Image Description** (`image_description`): Enter a description of the image you uploaded.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`): Enter a brief description of your submission.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`): Review and confirm your submission details.
* **Submission Agreement** (`submission_agreement`): Check the box to confirm you have read and agree to the submission terms and conditions.
* **Submission Terms** (`submission_terms`): Review the submission terms and conditions.
* **Submission Privacy** (`submission_privacy`): Check the box to confirm you understand the submission privacy policy.
* **Submission Contact** (`submission_contact`): Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and submission contact information.
* **Submission Email** (`submission_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Submission Phone** (`submission_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Card ID** (`card_id`): Enter a unique ID for the trading card.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Enter the current status of your submission.
* **Submission Status Update** (`submission_status_update`): Enter any updates to the submission status.
* **Submission Update Date** (`submission_update_date`): Enter the date of the submission update.
* **Submission Update Time** (`submission_update_time`): Enter the time of the submission update.
* **Submission Update User** (`submission_update_user`): Enter the user who made the submission update.
* **Submission Updated By** (`submission_updated_by`): Enter the user who updated the submission.
* **Submission Last Updated** (`submission_last_updated`): Display the last updated date and time of the submission.
* **Submission Status History** (`submission_status_history`): Display the history of submission status updates.
* **Submission Status Last Updated** (`submission_status_last_updated`): Display the last updated date and time of the submission status history.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid errors.
* Use the image uploader to upload high-quality images of the trading card.
* Review and confirm your submission details carefully to avoid mistakes.
* Check the submission terms and conditions before submitting your form.
