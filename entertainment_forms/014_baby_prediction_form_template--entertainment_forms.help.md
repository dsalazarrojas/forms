# Baby Prediction Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Baby Prediction Form Template is designed for participants to make predictions about a baby's characteristics, such as gender, birth date, and name. The form is used to collect data for a fun prize draw and allows users to opt-in for a prize draw if they want to. The form's purpose is not only to collect demographic data but also to provide entertainment and engagement for participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Make your predictions about the baby's gender, birth date, and weight or length. Note that these fields are optional.
3. Suggest a name for the baby. This is also an optional field.
4. Decide if you want to participate in the prize draw by selecting "True" in the "Interest in prize" field.
5. If you choose to participate in the prize draw, provide your contact information in the "Contact info for prize winner" field.
6. Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the baby in the "Additional comments" field.
7. Finally, submit your entry and select a date for "Submission date."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name (Optional)**: Enter your name as a participant in this prediction game.
* **Guess for gender (Choose your prediction)**: Choose the gender of the baby from the options: Boy, Girl, Twins, No preference.
* **Guess birth date (Predicted due date)**: Enter your predicted birth date of the baby. If you know the date, you can enter it here. If not, leave it blank.
* **Guess birth weight in pounds (Estimated weight)**: Enter your predicted weight of the baby in pounds. This field is optional.
* **Guess birth length in inches (Optional)**: Enter your predicted length of the baby in inches. This field is optional.
* **Name guess (Your suggested baby name)**: Suggest a name for the baby. This can be any name you like.
* **Interest in prize (Select if you want to enter prize draw)**: Choose "True" if you want to participate in the prize draw. If you don't want to participate, select "False."
* **Contact info for prize winner (Only required if entering prize draw)**: If you want to participate in the prize draw, enter your contact information here. This field is only required if you choose to participate.
* **Additional comments (Anything else)**: Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the baby.
* **Submission date (Date of entry)**: Select the date you are submitting this entry. This field is required for tracking and logging purposes.

## Tips

* You can make predictions for any number of babies, so feel free to fill out the form multiple times.
* The prize draw will be announced at a later date.
* If you choose to participate in the prize draw, make sure to provide accurate contact information to receive the prize.
