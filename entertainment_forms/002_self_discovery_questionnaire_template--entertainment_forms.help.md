# Self Discovery Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you explore your personal goals, motivations, strengths, and reflections. It's a comprehensive guide to help you gain a deeper understanding of yourself and develop a plan to achieve your aspirations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each section carefully, and answer the questions that resonate with you the most.
2. Be honest and authentic in your answers.
3. Take your time to complete each section, and don't feel rushed.
4. Review your answers before moving on to the next section.
5. Don't hesitate to skip sections or questions that don't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, string, required): Enter the title of your self-discovery journey.
* **Introduction** (page_1, text, required): Briefly introduce yourself and explain why you're taking this journey.
* **Section 1 - Goals** (page_2, select_one, required): Are you clear about your goals? Answer "Yes" if you are, or "No" if not.
* **Section 2 - Motivations** (page_3, select_multiple, required): What motivates you? Select all that apply.
* **Section 3 - Strengths** (page_4, text, required): What are your strengths? Describe them in detail.
* **Section 4 - Personal Growth** (page_5, date, required): What date would you like to achieve personal growth by?
* **Section 5 - Challenges** (page_6, text, required): What are your biggest challenges? Describe them in detail.
* **Section 6 - Self-Care** (page_7, note, required): Take note of your self-care habits. What works for you, and what doesn't?
* **Section 7 - Conclusion** (page_8, text, required): Summarize your journey so far. What have you learned?
* **Section 8 - Future Goals** (page_9, date, required): What date would you like to achieve your future goals by?
* **Section 9 - Next Steps** (page_10, text, required): What's your next step? Describe it in detail.
* **Section 10 - Final Thoughts** (page_11, note, required): Reflect on your journey. What have you learned about yourself?
* **Section 11 - Reflections** (page_12, text, required): Reflect on your progress. What have you achieved so far?
* **Section 12 - Recommendations** (page_13, select_multiple, required): Who would you recommend for coaching or guidance?
* **Section 13 - Coaching Plan** (page_14, select_one, required): Do you think you need coaching or guidance? Answer "Yes" if you do, or "No" if not.
* **Section 14 - Final Questions** (page_15, select_multiple, required): Are there any final questions you'd like to ask yourself? Select all that apply.
* **Section 15 - Final Comments** (page_16, text, required): What final comments do you have about your journey?
* **Section 16 - Final Summary** (page_17, text, required): Summarize your journey. What have you achieved?
* **Section 17 - Final Actions** (page_18, select_one, required): Are you ready to take action on your goals? Answer "Yes" if you are, or "No" if not.
* **Section 18 - Final Review** (page_19, text, required): Review your journey. What did you learn about yourself?
* **Section 19 - Final Reflection** (page_19, note, required): Take note of your final reflections. What did you gain from this journey?
* **Section 20 - Final Feedback** (page_20, select_multiple, required): What feedback do you have about this form? Select all that apply.
* **Section 21 - Final Comments** (page_21, text, required): What final comments do you have about your journey?
* **Section 22 - Final Review** (page_22, select_one, required): Are you satisfied with your journey? Answer "Yes" if you are, or "No" if not.
* **Section 23 - Final Reflection** (page_23, text, required): Reflect on your final journey. What have you achieved?
* **Section 24 - Final Feedback** (page_24, select_multiple, required): What feedback do you have about this form and your journey? Select all that apply.

## Tips

* Be kind to yourself. This is a journey of self-discovery, not self-judgment.
* Take your time. Completing this form is not a race.
* Don't be afraid to revisit sections or questions that don't resonate with you. Your journey is unique, and that's okay.
