# Discord Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discord Report Form is designed for users to report incidents, issues, or concerns they have experienced within the Discord community or platform. This form helps the community moderators and staff to address and resolve problems efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Submit Report" button on the Discord server or platform.
2.  Enter your report in the designated text fields, providing as much detail as possible about the issue or incident you are reporting.
3.  If necessary, provide additional context or attachments to support your report.
4.  Click the "Submit" button to send your report to the moderators and staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Discord Report Form 1** (`discord_report_form_1`, text, not required): This field allows you to enter your name or a pseudonym if you prefer to remain anonymous. Please keep in mind that your identity will be verified by the moderators before taking further action.
* **Report Reason** (`report_reason`, text, not required): Describe the reason why you are reporting this incident or issue. Try to be as specific as possible and provide any relevant details.
* **Report Details** (`report_details`, text, not required): Enter any additional information you think might be helpful for the moderators and staff to understand the situation. This could include screenshots, URLs, or other relevant context.
* **Report Details 2** (`report_details_2`, text, not required): Another field for providing additional information or context.
* ... (This pattern repeats for **Report Details 3** to **Report Details 23**)

Note: The "Report Details" fields are designed to be flexible and allow you to provide any additional information you think is relevant to the report. If you're unsure what to enter, try to be as specific and provide any context you can.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when reporting incidents or issues.
* Avoid using profanity or derogatory language in your report.
* Do not submit frivolous or false reports.
* Keep your report clear and concise for the moderators and staff to efficiently address the issue.
