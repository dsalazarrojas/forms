# Event Announcer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential event announcers for collaboration and partnership purposes. It's a one-time form designed to provide the required details for event planners, production managers, and other stakeholders to find the best fit for their events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the event announcer details such as contact information and experience in the event industry.
2. Select your preferred demo files and work type.
3. Choose your availability date.
4. Select your work rate and contract length preferences.
5. Select your work locations and contract length units (e.g., weeks, months, or years).
6. Add any contract notes, such as specific requirements or expectations for your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Announcer Details** (text, optional): This field is where you can add any additional information about yourself as an event announcer, such as a brief description of your services or specialties.
* **Demo Files** (text, optional): If you have any demo files or video samples that showcase your work, please upload them here.
* **Preferences** (number, optional): Please select the level of preference for your work rate: 1 (1), 2 (2), 3 (3), and so on.
* **Availability** (date, optional): Indicate the earliest availability date for your event announcer services.
* **Work Type** (select_one, optional): Select which type of work you specialize in: Event Planners, Production Managers, or Other.
* **Experience** (number, optional): Please select your level of experience in the event industry: 1 (1), 2 (2), 3 (3), and so on.
* **Contact Information** (text, optional): This field is for your full contact information.
* **Email** (email, optional): Add your email address so that we can stay in touch.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Add your phone number for further communication.
* **Work Rate** (select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to be paid for your services: Hourly, Flat Rate, Negotiable, Package Deal, or Other.
* **Work Location** (select_multiple, optional): Select all the locations where you're able to work: Home Office, Studio, Remote, On Location, or Other.
* **Contract Length** (number, optional): Select the contract length in the number of weeks, months, or years.
* **Contract Length Unit** (select_one, optional): Choose the unit for your contract length: Weeks, Months, Years, etc.
* **Contract Notes** (text, optional): Add any special requirements or expectations for your work.

Note: This form is optional, but the more information you provide, the more successful we can be in collaborating.
