# Talent Payment Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Payment Submission Form is used to submit payment information for talent-related expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. For fields that are not required, you can still provide additional details if it's relevant to your situation.
3. Double-check your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. <img src="icon"> talent_name (text, optional)
* This field is used to enter the talent's name. Please provide the name of the talent you are submitting payment for.

### 2. <img src="icon"> payment_choice (select_multiple, required)
* This field allows you to select the payment option for the talent. Please choose one or more of the available options that apply to your situation.

### 3. <img src="icon"> payment_amount (number, optional)
* This field is used to enter the payment amount for the talent. Please provide the exact amount of payment made.

### 4. <img src="icon"> payment_date (date, optional)
* This field is used to select the payment date for the talent. Please choose the date the payment was made.

### 5. <img src="icon"> payment_time (time, optional)
* This field is used to select the payment time for the talent. Please choose the time the payment was made.

### 6. <img src="icon"> talent_note (note, optional)
* This field is used for any additional notes or comments about the talent. You can provide any relevant details here.

### 7. <img src="icon"> email (email, optional)
* This field is used to enter the talent's email address. Please provide the email address associated with the talent.

### 8. <img src="icon"> phone_number (text, optional)
* This field is used to enter the talent's phone number. Please provide the phone number associated with the talent.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any errors in processing your payment submission.
