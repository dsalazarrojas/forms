# Community Theater Performance Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain a release from participants in a community theater performance, ensuring their understanding and consent for the production team to use their name, image, or performance content in promotional materials.
## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps outlined in the Field-by-Field Explanation section to complete the form accurately.
