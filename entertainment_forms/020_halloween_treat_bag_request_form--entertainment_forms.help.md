# Halloween Treat Bag Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Halloween Treat Bag Request Form is used to request a treat bag for children visiting your doorstep on Halloween. This form is designed to gather information about the children who will be visiting your home, so you can provide them with a treat bag filled with goodies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Trick or Treat Bag Details field with your name and address, as you would like to be known.
2. Select the correct address for your home from the dropdown menu in the Address field.
3. If you have any additional information about the trick-or-treaters, such as their names or a special request, you can add it in the Trick or Treater Information field.
4. If you have a specific number of children visiting your home, you can enter that number in the Number of Children field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trick or Treat Bag Details** (`Trick or Treat Bag Details`, text, optional): In this field, enter your name and address as you would like to be known by the trick-or-treaters.
* **Address** (`Address`, select one, required): Select the correct address for your home from the dropdown menu.
* **Trick or Treater Information** (`Trick or Treater Information`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about the trick-or-treaters, you can add it here.
* **Number of Children** (`Number of Children`, number, optional): If you have a specific number of children visiting your home, you can enter that number here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate information so that you can be reached by the trick-or-treaters.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Note: This form is only for requesting a treat bag for children visiting your home on Halloween. If you are an adult or non-child visitor, please do not fill out this form.
