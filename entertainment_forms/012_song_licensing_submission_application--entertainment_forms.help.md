# Song Licensing Submission Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Song Licensing Submission Application is designed for music industry professionals to submit their songs for licensing and potential use in various media, such as films, television shows, commercials, and video games. This form helps to collect and store important information about the songs, artists, and submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the song information section (page 1) with details about the song, including title, artist name, and other relevant details.
2. Upload or link to any relevant files, such as audio files or lyrics sheets, in the files section (page 2).
3. Provide contact information and artist details in the artist information section (page 3).
4. Enter your contact information in the contact information section (page 4).
5. Write a brief submission message and attach any additional files in the submission section (page 5).
6. Review and approve the submission details in the review and approve section (page 6).
7. Provide supervisor information, if necessary, in the supervisor information section (page 7).
8. Read and agree to the license agreement in the license agreement section (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Song Information** (Song Information, text, required): This field is for entering the title of the song, artist name, and other relevant details about the song.
* **Files** (Files, text, optional): If you have any relevant files, such as audio files or lyrics sheets, you can upload or link to them here.
* **Artist Information** (Artist Information, text, optional): This field is for providing additional information about the artist, such as contact details or other relevant background information.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): This field is for entering your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Submission** (Submission, text, optional): Write a brief message to accompany your submission, explaining why you are submitting the song for licensing.
* **Review and Approve** (Review and Approve, text, optional): Review the submission details you entered and ensure everything is correct.
* **Supervisor Information** (Supervisor Information, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of a supervisor, enter their information here.
* **License Agreement** (License Agreement, text, required): Read and agree to the license agreement terms before submitting your song for licensing.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information about the song and your contact details.
* Use clear and concise language in the submission message.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and rights to submit the song for licensing.
* Review the license agreement carefully before accepting it.
