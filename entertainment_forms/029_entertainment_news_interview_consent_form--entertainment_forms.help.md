# entertainment_news_interview_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The entertainment news interview consent form is used to obtain the necessary information and agreements from the interviewee before an interview can take place. This form ensures that both parties are aware of the terms and conditions of the interview, including what will be recorded and shared publicly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your name and contact information.
3. Choose your response for each field based on your understanding of the questions asked.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Professional**: Please enter your name and contact information as a media professional.
* **Interview Details**: Describe the interview in detail, including the topic and purpose of the interview.
* **Interview Agreement**: Select whether or not you agree to be interviewed. Please note that by selecting "True", you acknowledge that the interview may be recorded or broadcasted.
* **Interview Date**: Enter the date of the interview, if applicable.
* **Start Time**: Enter the expected start time of the interview, if applicable.
* **End Time**: Enter the expected end time of the interview, if applicable.
* **Interviewer Name**: Please enter the name of the media professional conducting the interview.
* **Interviewee Name**: Please enter your name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to confirm the interview details.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to confirm the interview details.
