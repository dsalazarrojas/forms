# stand_up_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from attendees on the quality and enjoyment of a stand-up comedy performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions about the quality of the performance (question 1).
2. Indicate whether the performers were funny (question 2).
3. Enter the date of the event (question 3).
4. Enter the time of the event (question 4).
5. Enter your email address to allow us to follow up with you (question 5).
6. Enter your phone number (question 6).
7. Add any additional comments or feedback you would like to share about the performance (question 7).
8. Provide any further additional comments or feedback (question 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What was the quality of the performance?** (`stand_up_feedback_form_1`, text, required): Answer this question to let us know how much you enjoyed the performance. Please provide a brief description of your experience.
- **Were the performers funny?** (`stand_up_feedback_form_2`, select_one, optional): Choose 'True' if you found the performers funny, or 'False' if you didn't.
- **Date** (`stand_up_feedback_form_3`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you attended.
- **Time** (`stand_up_feedback_form_4`, time, required): Enter the time of the event you attended.
- **Your Email** (`stand_up_feedback_form_5`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
- **Phone Number** (`stand_up_feedback_form_6`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
- **Comments** (`stand_up_feedback_form_7`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback you'd like to share about the performance.
- **Additional Comments** (`stand_up_feedback_form_8`, note, optional): Provide any further additional comments or feedback.
