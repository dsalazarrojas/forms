# music_festival_backstage_access_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or groups requesting backstage access to a music festival. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the request, including event information, contact details, and emergency contact information. This data will be used to review and process the request for backstage access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the event you are requesting backstage access for.
2.  Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3.  Specify your role at the event (e.g., Media, VIP Guest, Crew).
4.  Select any relevant roles for the crew (e.g., Stagehand, Sound Engineer, Light Engineer).
5.  Share any relevant experience with events or the music industry.
6.  Enter any relevant event-specific details, such as event experience, emergency contact information, and medical clearance.
7.  Add any special instructions or requirements for accessing the festival grounds.
8.  Finally, provide an emergency contact name, phone number, and email for the festival organizers.
