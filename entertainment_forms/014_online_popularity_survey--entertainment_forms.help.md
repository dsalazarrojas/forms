# Online Popularity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their online popularity survey experience. Your feedback will help us understand how you engage with our website and what we can do better.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the on-screen instructions and fill in the required information. If you have any difficulty, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): This is the title of the survey you took. If you remember the title, please provide it here. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Audience Type** (`audience_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of audience you are part of. This will help us understand who our target audience is.
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the age range you fall under.
	+ 13-24
	+ 25-44
	+ 45-54
	+ 55-64
	+ 65 and above
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your favorite food from the options provided. 
	+ Pizza
	+ Sushi
	+ Burger
* **How do you find us?** (`how_do_you_find_us`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the platforms you find us on.
	+ Social media
	+ Friends
	+ Search engine
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Online advertisement
* **How often do you visit our website?** (`how_often_do_you_visit_website`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you visit our website.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please provide your email address if you would like to be contacted for any further surveys or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comment`, text, optional): If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Please rate your experience with us.
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5
* **How do you find us?** (`how_do_you_find_us_how`, select_multiple, optional): Please select how you find us. 
	+ Social media
	+ Friends
	+ Search engine
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Online advertisement
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): If you have a website, please provide it.
* **Social Links** (`social_links`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the social links you have.
	+ Instagram
	+ YouTube
	+ Snapchat
	+ LinkedIn
* **Email List** (`email_list`, email, optional): If you would like to be part of our email list, please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comment`, text, optional): If you have any other comments, please provide them here.
* **Why don't you like us?** (`why_dont_you_like_us`, text, optional): If you have any reasons why you don't like us, please provide them here.
* **How do you feel?** (`how_do_you_feel`, text, optional): If you have any feelings about us, please provide them here.
* **Why don't you feel us?** (`why_dont_you_feel_us`, text, optional): If you have any reasons why you don't feel us, please provide them here.

## Tips
* If you have any difficulty filling out this form, please refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
