# Tango Festival Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Tango Festival. Fill out the form with accurate information to ensure a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your valid email address to facilitate communication with the festival organizers.
3. Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
4. Choose your date of birth for age verification.
5. Select the dance categories you are interested in (you can choose multiple).
6. Choose your preferred payment method (bank transfer, PayPal, or Stripe).
7. Indicate your payment status (paid or unpaid).
8. Select your tango level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (Text, required): Enter your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last name** (Text, required): Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Date of birth** (Date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Dance category** (Select Multiple, optional): Choose the dance categories you are interested in (e.g., Argentine Tango, Milonga, Neoclassical, Tango nuevo, Tango Orquesta).
* **Payment method** (Select One, optional): Choose your preferred payment method (bank transfer, PayPal, or Stripe).
* **Payment status** (Select One, optional): Indicate your payment status (paid or unpaid).
* **Tango level** (Select One, optional): Choose your tango level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in accurate information to avoid any issues with the festival organizers.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact the festival organizers.
* Each participant should submit their own form to avoid duplication.
