# Dance Competition Registration Form UDO - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Competition Registration Form UDO is designed to collect essential information from contestants for the dance competition. This form ensures accurate and consistent data for efficient processing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Contestant Personal Details section with your information.
2.  Provide your contact details for smooth communication.
3.  Select your solo age category and division.
4.  Enter your and your contact person's name, contact number, email, address, city, state, zip, and country.
5.  Enter your school details such as name, address, city, state, zip, and country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contestant Personal Details (1)**: Please enter your personal details such as name, age, etc. This section is not required.
*   **Contact Details (2)**: Enter your name, contact number, email, address, city, state, zip, and country for smooth communication with us.
*   **Dance School (3)**: Enter the name of your dance school or institution.
*   **Solo Age Category (4)**: Select your age category from the provided options: 5-11, 12-17, 18-24, 25-34, 35+.
*   **Solo Division (5)**: Choose your solo division from the provided options: Junior, Senior, Professional.
*   **Name of Soloist (6)**: Enter the name of the soloist competing.
*   **Name of Contact Person (7)**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of emergencies.
*   **Contact Number (8)**: Enter the contact number for the contact person.
*   **Email (9)**: Enter the email of the contact person.
*   **Address (10)**: Enter the address of the contact person.
*   **City (11)**: Enter the city of the contact person's address.
*   **State (12)**: Enter the state of the contact person's address.
*   **Zip (13)**: Enter the zip of the contact person's address.
*   **Country (14)**: Enter the country of the contact person's address.
*   **Name of School (15)**: Enter the name of your school or institution.
*   **School Address (16)**: Enter the address of your school or institution.
*   **School City (17)**: Enter the city of your school or institution.
*   **School State (18)**: Enter the state of your school or institution.
*   **School Zip (19)**: Enter the zip of your school or institution.
