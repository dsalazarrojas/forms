# Community Theater RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Theater RSVP Form is a tool for guests to RSVP to a community theater event. Fill out this form to let us know if you can attend and make any necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Guest full name** in the "Guest full name" field. This will help us know who to seat you and provide tickets.
2. Enter your **Guest email address** in the "Guest email address" field. This will allow us to send you confirmation and tickets.
3. Choose the number of guests that will be attending, including yourself, in the "Number of guests" field.
4. If available, select your **Seating preference** from the dropdown menu. This will help us seat you in the best possible location.
5. If you have any **Accessibility needs**, describe them in the "Accessibility needs" field. This will help us prepare for your special requirements.
6. If you have any **Special requests**, such as dietary requests, enter them in the "Special requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest full name (Guest full name)**: 
  Enter the full name of the guest(s) attending.
* **Guest email address (Guest email address)**: 
  A valid email address that we can use to send confirmation and ticket information.
* **Number of guests (Number of guests)**: 
  Enter the total number of guests attending, including yourself.
* **Seating preference (Seating preference)**: 
  Select the seating preference from the dropdown menu, if available.
* **Accessibility needs (Accessibility needs)**: 
  Describe any accessibility needs or requirements.
* **Special requests (Special requests)**: 
  Enter any special requests, such as dietary requests.
