# Video Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Video Submission Form" is designed to collect information about comedy videos for entertainment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Submit A Comedy Video" field with a brief description of the video you want to submit.
2. If you'd like to, fill out any of the other fields: "Upload Video", "Video Title", "Author", and "Description" to provide additional information about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit A Comedy Video**: A brief description of the comedy video you want to submit.
	+ This field is required to be filled out.
* **Upload Video**: Optionally, attach a video file to support your submission.
	+ This field is not required but can be used to attach additional media.
* **Video Title**: Optionally, enter a title for your video submission.
	+ This field is not required but can be useful for organization purposes.
* **Author**: Optionally, identify yourself as the creator of the video submission.
	+ This field is not required but can be useful for attribution purposes.
* **Description**: Optionally, add a brief description of your video submission.
	+ This field is not required but can be useful for additional context.
* **Submit**: This is the final step to submit your video.
	+ This field is not required but is the last action to complete the submission process.
