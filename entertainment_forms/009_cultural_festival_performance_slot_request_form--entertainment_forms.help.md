# cultural_festival_performance_slot_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from artists for a cultural festival performance slot request. It helps festival organizers understand the artist's requirements and preferences for a successful performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your artist info in the "Artist Info Form" section.
2. Select your preferred schedule time slot in the "Schedule Request" section.
3. Specify your technical needs in the "Technical Needs" section.
4. Add any additional information about your performance in the "Additional Info" section.
5. Confirm your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
6. Confirm the accuracy of your submission in the "Confirm Info" section.
7. Select your preferred time slot in the "Preferred Time Slot" section.
8. Specify your technical requirements in the "Technical Requirements" section.
9. Finally, provide your complete contact details in the "Contact Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Info Form** (`artist_info_form`, `text`, optional):
	+ This section is for entering your artist's information, such as name, contact details, and any other relevant info.
* **Schedule Request** (`schedule_request`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select your preferred schedule time slot for your performance.
* **Technical Needs** (`technical_needs`, `text`, optional):
	+ Specify any technical requirements you need for your performance, such as sound or video equipment.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, optional):
	+ Add any additional information about your performance that you want to share with festival organizers.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your complete contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Confirm Info** (`confirm_info`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the information provided in this form.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time_slot`, `time`, optional):
	+ Select your preferred time slot for your performance.
* **Technical Requirements** (`technical_requirements`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Specify the technical requirements for your performance, such as sound or video equipment.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide your complete contact information for festival organizers to reach you.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth performance setup.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify them in the "Additional Info" section.
- Confirm your contact information to ensure that festival organizers can reach you.
- Select your preferred time slot carefully, considering the festival schedule and other performances.
