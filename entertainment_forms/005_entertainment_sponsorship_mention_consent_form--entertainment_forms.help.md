# Entertainment Sponsorship Mention Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent and details from entertainment sponsors for mentions in content such as events, streams, and videos. It ensures that the sponsor has given their consent to be mentioned and provides necessary information for follow-up contact and content creation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Sponsor Name**, which is the name of the sponsoring entity or individual.
2. Choose your **Preferred Mention Format**, which determines how you want to be mentioned in the content (Audio, Video, Print, or Social Media).
3. Enter your **Contact Info**, which is used by us to contact you with follow-up questions or concerns.
4. Provide your **Email**, which is used for communication and follow-up.
5. Enter your **Phone Number**, which is used for direct communication.
6. Choose your **Mention Format Preference**, which determines where and when you want to be mentioned in the content (In-stream, Pre-roll, Post-roll, End-screen, or In-video).
7. Check your **Formal Consent**, which indicates whether you consent to being mentioned in the content (True or False).
8. If applicable, fill in **Events Attended**, which lists any events you have participated in or attended.
9. Enter **Stream Details**, which provides additional information about the stream or event you are sponsoring.
10. Lastly, provide **Show Details**, which gives further context about the show or event you are sponsoring.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the entity or individual sponsoring the content.
* **Preferred Mention Format**: Choose how you want to be mentioned in the content, such as audio, video, print, or social media.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you with follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Email**: Provide your email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for direct communication.
* **Mention Format Preference**: Choose when and where you want to be mentioned in the content, such as in-stream or pre-roll.
* **Formal Consent**: Indicate whether you consent to being mentioned in the content (True or False).
* **Events Attended**: If you participated in or attended an event, list the event name and details.
* **Stream Details**: Enter any additional information about the stream you are sponsoring.
* **Show Details**: Provide any additional context about the show or event you are sponsoring.
