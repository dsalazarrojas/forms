# Daily Gratitude Journal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Gratitude Journal Form is a tool designed to help you cultivate a positive and mindful attitude by recording your daily experiences and feelings of gratitude. This form is meant to be completed on a daily basis, helping you reflect on the good things that have happened in your life and appreciate the moments that bring you joy.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the Daily Gratitude Journal Form, simply fill in the required fields and answer the questions to the best of your ability. Each section is designed to help you reflect on your day and express your gratitude in a meaningful way.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **daily_gratitude_form**: A short title for your entry, summarizing the good things that have happened in your life today. (Type any thoughts or experiences that come to mind, up to 255 characters)
* **grateful_moment**: Describe a moment from your day where you felt grateful or thankful. Be as specific as possible, sharing as much detail as you can about the experience.
* **moment_description**: Elaborate on the grateful moment you described above. What made it special? What did you learn from it?
* **feeling_level**: How you feel about the experience? Choose one of the following options: High, Low (or select "High" if the experience was overwhelmingly positive, and "Low" if it was a more minor positive experience or a negative one).
* **note**: If you'd like to add any further thoughts or comments about your day, feel free to write them here. This is a great place to reflect on any challenges you faced and how you overcame them.
* **email_address**: This is the email address to which your entry will be sent. If you'd like to share your gratitude journal with a friend or loved one, you can enter their email address here.
* **phone_number**: If you'd like to share your gratitude journal with a contact via phone, enter their phone number here.
* **submit_button**: Click this button to submit your entry and share your gratitude with others.
* **submit_note**: Add any final thoughts or comments about submitting your entry. This is a great place to reflect on your experience.
* **submit_status**: Check the status of your submission. If you've submitted successfully, this will say "Active". If you've not submitted, it will say "Inactive".
* **submission_date**: The date your entry was submitted.
* **submission_time**: The time your entry was submitted.
* **submitter**: Check if you're the one who submitted the entry.
* **submitter_name**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the entry (if you're sharing your journal with someone else).
