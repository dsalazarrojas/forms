# Zoo Visitor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from zoo visitors to help us improve your experience and services. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below to help us better understand your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (First Name, Email).
2. Select the animals you visited during your trip.
3. Choose how satisfied you are with your visit.
4. Let us know if you would visit again.
5. If you have any feedback or comments, use the text box at the bottom.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification.
* **Email**: Provide your email address so we can contact you with updates and surveys.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you for further information (optional).
* **Visit Date**: Select the date you visited the zoo.
* **Visit Time**: Choose the time you arrived at the zoo.
* **Animal Seen**: Tell us which animals you saw during your visit. Choose all that apply.
	+ Yes: I saw this animal during my visit.
	+ No: I did not see this animal during my visit.
* **Type of Animal**: Indicate which type of animal you saw during your visit. Choose all that apply.
	+ Yes: I saw this type of animal during my visit.
	+ No: I did not see this type of animal during my visit.
* **Comment**: Share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about your visit.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate how satisfied you are with your visit.
	+ Satisfied: I was very satisfied with my visit.
	+ Dissatisfied: I was not satisfied with my visit.
* **Would Visit Again**: Let us know if you would visit the zoo again.
	+ Yes: I would like to visit again.
	+ No: I do not plan to visit again.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the zoo.
	+ Satisfied: I was very satisfied with the zoo.
	+ Dissatisfied: I was not satisfied with the zoo.
* **Recommend**: Do you recommend the zoo to others?
	+ Yes: I would recommend the zoo.
	+ No: I would not recommend the zoo.
