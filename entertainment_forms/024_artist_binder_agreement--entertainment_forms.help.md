# Artist Binder Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Binder Agreement is a critical document that outlines the terms and conditions for artists or performers to agree on with their clients or venues. This form ensures a clear understanding between the parties involved, including performance details, payment terms, and cancellation policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Start" button to begin filling the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
3.  Review and confirm your agreement before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Artist Information
* **Artist or Performer Name**: Enter your professional name as it appears on your business cards, website, or promotional materials.
* **Email Address**: Provide your primary contact email for communication with clients or venues.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Website or Portfolio**: Enter the URL to your website or online portfolio for clients or venues to review your work.

### Section 2: Client or Venue Information
* **Client or Venue Name**: Enter the name of the client or venue requesting your performance.
* **Client Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person at the client or venue responsible for coordinating with you.
* **Client Email Address**: Enter the email for communication with the client or venue.
* **Client Phone Number**: Enter the contact number for easy communication.

### Section 3: Performance Details
* **Type of Performance**: Select the type of act or service you'll be providing (e.g., Live Music Performance, DJ Services, Comedy Show, etc.).
* **Requested Performance Date**: Enter the date of the performance as scheduled.
* **Performance Time**: Enter the start time of the performance (optional).
* **Duration of Performance**: Select the length of the performance (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, etc.).

### Section 4: Financial Agreement
* **Performance Fee (GBP)**: Enter the total amount for this performance, including any additional expenses.
* **Required Deposit Amount (GBP)**: Enter the deposit amount to secure the performance date.
* **Deposit Due Date**: Enter the date when the deposit should be received by the client or venue.
* **Final Payment Due Date**: Enter the date when the remaining balance is due.

### Section 5: Payment Terms
* **Final Payment Due Date**: Confirm the date when the final payment is due.
* **Accepted Payment Methods**: Select the accepted payment methods (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Credit Card, PayPal, etc.).

### Section 6: Cancellation and Terms
* **Cancellation Terms**: Confirm whether you agree to the binder terms and conditions.
* **Date of This Agreement**: Enter the date when this agreement is made.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting to ensure accurate information.
* Enter dates in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* Use the provided payment methods for payment processing.
* Review the cancellation terms before submitting to understand your obligations.
