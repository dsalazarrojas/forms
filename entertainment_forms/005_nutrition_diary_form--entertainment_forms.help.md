# Nutrition Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help users track and log their daily and weekly nutrition information, including intake and exercise data. It is meant to be completed for each day or period to be logged.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each page of the form as needed and relevant to your specific habits and activities.
2. Answer every question or field that applies to your daily or weekly activities.
3. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
4. Do not fill out fields that are not applicable or relevant to your activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the form, which is "Nutrition Diary Form".
* **First Page** (`first_page`, text, required): Enter your first day's details, including date and time of completion.
* **Daily Intake** (`daily_intake`, text, optional): Log your daily food and drink intake.
* **Water Intake** (`water_intake`, text, optional): Record the amount of water you consumed.
* **Exercise** (`exercise`, text, optional): Describe your physical exercise activities for the day.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your activities.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the logged activities.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the logged activities.
