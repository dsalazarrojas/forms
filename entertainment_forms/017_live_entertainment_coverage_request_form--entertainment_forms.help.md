# Live Entertainment Coverage Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Entertainment Coverage Request Form is used to request live coverage for events, allowing event promoters to share their event with a wider audience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Event Details section with the event's name, description, and location.
2. Select the required Media Requests and Media Outlets for the event's coverage.
3. Choose the correct Event Dates and Event Times for the event.
4. Enter the Contact Details, including the Contact Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone number.
5. Add an Event Image and Event Video if applicable.
6. Select the Event Status and Event Notes as necessary.
7. Review and submit the form to ensure all required information is filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you can provide a brief description of the event, including its name and location. 
* **Media Requests** (`media_requests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the media requests you are looking for, such as live streaming or social media coverage.
* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date of the event.
* **Event Times** (`event_times`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of the event.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact information for the event, including the contact name, email, and phone number.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Promoter** (`event_promoter`, `text`, required: false): Enter the event promoter's name.
* **Media Outlets** (`media_outlets`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the media outlet(s) you would like to cover your event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the event.
* **Event Image** (`event_image`, `text`, required: false): Upload an image of the event.
* **Event Video** (`event_video`, `text`, required: false): Upload a video of the event.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information or message.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the event, such as confirmed, pending, or declined.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional event information or notes.
