# hiphop_dance_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the registration of hip hop dance crews. It gathers information about the crew members, their manager's details, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth and organized event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the crew members' affiliation status.
2. Enter the manager's name and contact details.
3. Choose the terms and conditions and submit the form.
4. Provide information about the crew's size and venue.
5. Specify the crew's date of formation.
6. Enter the manager's title and contact information.
7. Add any additional information about the manager.
8. Provide a description of the crew, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Crew Members** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to indicate whether your crew members are affiliated with the event.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your crew members are affiliated with the event.
	+ Select 'No' if your crew members are not affiliated with the event.
* **Manager Name** (`text`, required: false): This field is for the name of the manager of your crew.
* **Manager Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`select_one`, required: false): This field is to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the event.
	+ Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
	+ Select 'No' to indicate that you have not read or agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit Form** (`select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' to submit the form.
* **Crew Size** (`number`, required: false): Enter the number of members in your crew.
* **Crew Owners** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is to indicate whether the crew is owned by an individual or a group of people.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your crew is owned by an individual.
	+ Select 'No' if your crew is owned by a group of people.
* **Crew Venue** (`select_one`, required: false): This field is to specify the venue where your crew performs.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your crew performs at a venue.
	+ Select 'No' if your crew does not perform at a venue.
* **Crew Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the crew's formation.
* **Manager Title** (`text`, required: false): Enter the manager's title.
* **Manager Role** (`text`, required: false): Enter the manager's role.
* **Manager Company** (`text`, required: false): Enter the manager's company.
* **Manager Contact** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information for the manager.
* **Manager Additional Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the manager.
* **Additional Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your crew.
* **Crew Description** (`select_one`, required: false): This field is to provide a description of your crew.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you want to provide a description of your crew.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not want to provide a description of your crew.
