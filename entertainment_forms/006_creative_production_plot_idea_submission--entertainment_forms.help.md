# Creative Production Plot Idea Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user-submitted plot idea submissions for creative production projects. Submissions are used to develop and produce new ideas in various industries and categories. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Submission Details" page with your plot idea submission.
2. Proceed to the "Contact" page to provide your contact information.
3. On the "Review and Approval" page, review the submission guidelines and agree to the terms.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Submission Details (Page 1)
* **Submission Title** (`title`, `text`, `optional`): Briefly describe your plot idea submission.
* **Submission Details** (`page_one`, `text`, `optional`): Provide a detailed description of your plot idea submission.

### Contact (Page 2)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_one`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your name, email, and/or phone number to be contacted for follow-up.

### Review and Approval (Page 3)
* **Review and Approval** (`review_one`, `text`, `optional`): Review and agree to the submission guidelines and terms of use.
 
## Tips
* Be original and unique when submitting your plot idea.
* Ensure your idea has not been previously submitted.
* Use this form only for submitting original work.
