# Football Trivia Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a collection of trivia questions and answers about football (soccer) to test your knowledge and interest in the sport.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options for each question by clicking on the desired answer.
2. Fill out the form fields as required, including providing a name of a football player, date, and score.
3. Select the multiple answer options for questions 6 and 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **About Football History**: This is a text field where you can enter your thoughts or opinions on the history of football.
* **Favorite Football Team**: This question requires you to select your favorite football team from the options provided.
* **Enter a Football Player's Name**: This field allows you to enter the name of a football player you admire or have interest in.
* **Football Trivia Date**: This question requires you to select a date related to football from the options provided.
* **Enter a Football Score**: This field is for you to enter a football score, which could be related to a match, tournament, or other football event.
* **Football Trivia Answer**: This question requires you to select multiple answer options related to football trivia.
* **Select Multiple Answers Related to Football Trivia**: This question is similar to question 6, requiring you to select multiple answer options related to football trivia.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
* Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate and relevant to the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
