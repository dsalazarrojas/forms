# graduation_party_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The graduation party registration form is designed to collect basic information from attendees about their plans to attend a graduation party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the necessary details about the party you plan to attend.
2. Provide information about yourself, including any relevant details about your attendance.
3. Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Details (Text, Required: False)**: Enter the name of the party, event date, and other relevant details about the party.
* **Attendee Information (Text, Required: False)**: Provide your name, email address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Info (Text, Required: False)**: Enter your email address, phone number, and any other contact details so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information about the party and yourself to ensure that we have the correct details.
* You can add any additional information about the party or yourself in the "Attendee Information" field if needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
