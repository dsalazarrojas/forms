# Invention Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invention Contest Entry Form is designed to collect information from users who want to submit their inventions for evaluation. This form helps us understand the details of their innovation, making it easier for us to assess and potentially support their idea.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide a description of your idea in the `Idea Description` field.
4. If applicable, give us information about your contact address.
5. Give a clear and concise description of your invention.
6. If your invention falls under a specific category, select one from the options provided.
7. If you have an image associated with your invention, enter its URL.
8. If you have a website associated with your invention, enter its URL.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Idea Description** (`idea_description`, text, required: false): Describe your idea briefly.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required: false): If you want to provide your contact address.
* **Invention Description** (`invention_description`, text, required: false): Give a clear and concise description of your invention.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category that best fits your invention (if applicable).
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the associated image.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of your associated website (if applicable).
