# Paranormal Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paranormal Giveaway Entry Form is designed to collect essential information from users who want to participate in the Paranormal Giveaway Contest. This form helps us determine the eligibility and tailor the experience to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter The Contest** - This is the title of the contest, which you are entering.
2. **First Name** - Please enter your first name as it is spelled on your government-issued ID or passport.
3. **Last Name** - Please enter your last name as it is spelled on your government-issued ID or passport.
4. **Email** - Enter a valid email address that you use and regularly check. This is how we will contact you if you win.
5. **Phone** - Optional - If you provide your phone number, we will contact you by phone if you win.
6. **Date Of Birth** - Please enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY. This is required for age verification.
7. **Address** - Optional - You may enter your address if you prefer to receive mail or prizes to be delivered to your home.
8. **Occupation** - Optional - You may enter your occupation or job title if you have one.
9. **Company** - Optional - You may enter your company name if you have one.
10. **Social Media** - Select all social media platforms where you are active. We will contact you on these platforms if you win.
11. **Do You Consent To Our Use Of Your Data** - Please select "True" to agree to our data use policy.
12. **I've Read And Agree To The Terms And Conditions** - Please select "I Agree" to confirm you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
13. **I've Read And Agree To The Privacy Policy** - Please select "I Agree" to confirm you have read and agree to the privacy policy.
14. **How Did You Hear About This Contest** - Select how you learned about this contest from the options provided.
15. **Other** - You may provide any other source if you cannot find your preferred option.
16. **What Type Of Content Do You Prefer** - Select all types of content you enjoy, such as horror, sci-fi, fantasy, etc.
17. **What's Your Age Of Interest** - Select your age range from the options provided.
18. **What's Your Favorite Hobbies** - Select all hobbies you enjoy, such as horror movies, reading, etc.
19. **What's Your Favorite Type Of Content** - Select all types of content you enjoy, such as horror, sci-fi, fantasy, etc.
20. **How Do You Prefer To Be Contacted** - Select all preferred contact methods, such as email, social media, etc.
21. **Would You Like To Be Followed Up On** - Select yes or no to follow up on future contests.
22. **Comments** - You may provide any comments or feedback you have.
23. **When Can We Use Your Data** - Select when we can use your data from the options provided.
24. **Do You Agree With Our Terms And Conditions** - Please select "True" to confirm you have read and agree to our terms and conditions.
25. **Do You Agree With Our Privacy Policy** - Please select "True" to confirm you have read and agree to our privacy policy.
