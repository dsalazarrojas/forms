# party_decoration_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The party decoration agreement form is a document used to collect information from clients or event organizers about party decoration services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the party name in the `party_name` field.
2. Select the date of the event in the `event_date` field.
3. Choose the time of the event in the `event_time` field.
4. Enter the location of the event in the `event_location` field.
5. Describe the decoration services required in the `decoration_description` field.
6. Specify if decoration services are required (`Yes` or `No`) in the `decoration_services` field.
7. If you have any additional information, enter it in the `additional_info` field.
8. If you have any other notes about the event, enter them in the `decoration_notes` field.
9. Enter your contact information in the `contact_info` field.
10. Enter any other details about the event in the `event_details` field.
11. Sign the form with your digital signature in the `signature` field.
12. Enter your printed name in the `printed_name` field.
13. Select the date of the printed form in the `printed_date` field.
14. Enter your printed name as it appears on your printed name in the `printed_signature` field.
15. Enter your company name as it appears on your printed name in the `printed_company` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party Name** (`party_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the party or event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Decoration Description** (`decoration_description`, `text`, required): Describe the decoration services required.
* **Decoration Services** (`decoration_services`, `select_one`, required): Specify if decoration services are required.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the event.
* **Decoration Notes** (`decoration_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any other notes about the event.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any other details about the event.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with your digital signature.
* **Printed Name** (`printed_name`, `text`, required): Enter your printed name.
* **Printed Date** (`printed_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the printed form.
* **Printed Signature** (`printed_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your printed name as it appears on your printed name.
* **Printed Company** (`printed_company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name as it appears on your printed name.
