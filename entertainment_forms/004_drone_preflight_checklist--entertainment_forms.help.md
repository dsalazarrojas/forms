# Drone Preflight Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drone Preflight Checklist is a comprehensive guide to help you ensure your drone is in good working condition and safe for flight. This form will walk you through a series of checks to identify potential issues that may affect your drone's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and select the options that apply to your drone.
2. For select_one fields, choose the single option that best describes your drone's status.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose all the options that apply to your drone.
4. Make sure to review each field carefully and take notes or actions as needed to ensure your drone is airworthy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Preflight Checklist** (`drone_preflight_checklist_page`)
* A general overview of the pre-flight checks, likely a table or list summarizing the results of the other checks.

### 2. **Engine Checklist** (`drone_preflight_checklist_engine_checklist`)
* This field allows you to choose one of the following options for the engine:
	+ Engine Air Filter
	+ Engine Propulsion System
	+ Engine Battery
	+ Engine Control System
	+ Engine Mounts

Choose the option(s) that apply to your drone's engine.

### 3. **Sensor Checklist** (`drone_preflight_checklist_sensors_checklist`)
* Select all the sensors that are functional on your drone:
	+ GPS
	+ Camera
	+ Obstacle Avoidance System
	+ Altitude
	+ Inertial Measurement Unit

### 4. **Control Checklist** (`drone_preflight_checklist_controls_checklist`)
* Choose all the control systems that are functional on your drone:
	+ Throttle
	+ Rudder
	+ Pitch Yaw
	+ Roll
	+ Elevon

### 5. **Propeller Checklist** (`drone_preflight_checklist_propeller_checklist`)
* Select all the propeller options that apply to your drone:
	+ Propeller Pitch
	+ Propeller Angle
	+ Propeller Spin

### 6. **Battery Checklist** (`drone_preflight_checklist_battery_checklist`)
* Choose the battery status:
	+ Battery Type
	+ Battery Condition
	+ Battery Power

### 7. **Safety Checklist** (`drone_preflight_checklist_safety_checklist`)
* Select all the safety features that are functional on your drone:
	+ Safety Net
	+ Safety Harness
	+ Safety Strap

### 8. **System Checklist** (`drone_preflight_checklist_system_checklist`)
* Choose all the system options that apply to your drone:
	+ System Software
	+ System Update
	+ System Version

Please review each field carefully and take note of any issues or concerns that may affect your drone's performance or safety.
