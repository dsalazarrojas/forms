# Easter Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Quiz is a set of questions designed to gather opinions and thoughts about Easter. It aims to explore different aspects of the holiday, from its meaning and traditions to its celebrations and activities. The goal is to collect data that will help users understand their own views on Easter and potentially provide insights for those who want to know what others think.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read through each question carefully and understand what is being asked.
2.  Choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
3.  If a question doesn't fit your thoughts or feelings, choose "Don't know" or "Other" if available.
4.  Answer all questions, even if you're not sure or disagree with some points.
5.  Submit your responses when you're ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What's the meaning of Easter?** (text) 
  This question is asking for a brief description of your understanding of Easter's meaning. Please answer in your own words, and feel free to be creative.
* **2. What's the date of Easter?** (date) 
  This question is asking for the actual date when Easter is celebrated. If you're unsure, you can choose "Don't know."
* **3. What's the main food eaten on Easter?** (select_one: Hot Cross Buns, Ham, Eggs, Chocolate) 
  This question is asking about a common food associated with Easter. Choose the one that best represents your favorite or the most traditional choice.
* **4. What's your favorite Easter activity?** (select_multiple: Hiding eggs, Dyeing eggs, Hopping like a bunny, Playing egg hunt) 
  This question is asking about activities you enjoy during Easter. Pick all that apply, and don't be afraid to get creative.
* **5. What's the best way to spend Easter?** (note) 
  This question is an open-ended field for you to describe your ideal way to spend Easter.
* **6. What's your favorite Easter memory?** (text) 
  Share a brief story or thought about a cherished Easter memory.
* **7. What's the Easter symbol?** (select_one: Rabbit, Egg, Lilac, Flower)
