# Sponsorship Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Donation Form is used to collect information from potential sponsors for funding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the sponsorship in the **Description** field.
2. Enter the **Sponsorship Amount** field with the total amount of the donation.
3. Select the **Frequency of Sponsorship** field to indicate how often the sponsorship will occur (e.g., once, quarterly, monthly, or yearly).
4. Enter the **Donation Date** field with the date of the donation.
5. If the sponsorship is archived, select **Archived Submissions** (True or False).
6. Enter the **Submitter's Information**:
	* **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
	* **Email**: Enter the submitter's email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter the submitter's phone number.
	* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization the submitter is representing.
7. Enter the **Sponsor's Information**:
	* **Sponsor**: Enter the name of the sponsor.
	* **Sponsor Organization**: Enter the name of the sponsor organization.
	* **Sponsor Email**: Enter the sponsor's email address.
	* **Sponsor Phone**: Enter the sponsor's phone number.
	* **Sponsor Organization URL**: Enter the website URL of the sponsor organization.
	* **Sponsor Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the sponsor.
