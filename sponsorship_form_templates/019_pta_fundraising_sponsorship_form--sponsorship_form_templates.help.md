# PTA Fundraising Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTA Fundraising Sponsorship Form is used to collect information from potential sponsors for the PTA's fundraising efforts. This form helps the PTA to understand the sponsor's intentions, contact information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the PTA contact information, including name, email, and group address.
2. Enter the sponsorship amount for the event.
3. Provide any additional event information that the sponsor should know.
4. Choose all applicable sponsors for the event.
5. Confirm if sponsors have submitted.
6. Choose the status of the sponsorship (e.g., Applied, Pending, Confirmed).
7. Select the payment method (e.g., credit card, online, or check).
8. Enter any additional payment information that the sponsor may need to know.
9. Enter any comments from the sponsor.
10. Enter contact information of the sponsor for easy communication.
11. Choose the relationship of the sponsor (e.g., parent or guardian).
12. Enter the name of the sponsor.
13. Choose the payment status of the sponsor.
14. Enter the amount paid by the sponsor.
15. Enter the amount sponsored.
16. Enter any comment from the sponsor.
17. Enter any additional notes about the sponsor.
18. Enter the commitment of the sponsor.
19. Enter the information about the PTA fundraising efforts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **PTA Contact Info (ptacommnt)** (`ptacommnt`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information of the PTA, including name, email, and group address.
* **Sponsorship Amount (sponsorshipamount)** (`sponsorshipamount`, `number`, required): Enter the monetary value of the sponsorship.
* **PTA E-mail (ptacommnt)** (`ptacommnt`, `email`, required): Enter the PTA email address to ensure communication is possible.
* **PTA Group Address (ptagpaddrss)** (`ptagpaddrss`, `text`, required): Enter the group address for the PTA.
* **PTA Event Info (ptaeveinfo)** (`ptaeveinfo`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about the event that the sponsor should know.
* **Sponsors (sponsors)** (`sponsors`, `select multiple`, required): Choose all applicable sponsors for the event.
* **Sponsors Submitted (sponsrsub)** (`sponsrsub`, `select multiple`, required): Confirm if sponsors have submitted.
* **Sponsorship Status (sponsorshipstatus)** (`sponsorshipstatus`, `select one`, required): Choose the status of the sponsorship.
* **Payment Method (paymentmethod)** (`paymentmethod`, `select one`, required): Choose how the sponsor will pay for the event.
* **Payment Info (paymentinfo)** (`paymentinfo`, `note`, required): Enter any additional payment information that the sponsor may need to know.
* **Sponsor Comments (sponsrcommnts)** (`sponsrcommnts`, `text`, required): Enter any comments or additional information from the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Contact Info (sponsrctinf)** (`sponsrctinf`, `text`, required): Enter contact information of the sponsor for easy communication.
* **Sponsor Relationship (sponrrelationship)** (`sponrrelationship`, `select one`, required): Choose how the sponsor is related to the student.
* **Sponsor Name (sponname)** (`sponname`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Payment Status (paymentstatus)** (`paymentstatus`, `select one`, required): Choose the payment status of the sponsor.
* **Payment Amount (paymentamount)** (`paymentamount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount paid by the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Amount (sponsoramount)** (`sponsoramount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount sponsored.
* **Sponsor Comment (sponcmnt)** (`sponcmnt`, `text`, required): Enter any comment from the sponsor.
* **Notes (notes)** (`notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional notes about the sponsor.
* **Sponsorship Commitment (sponsrcommit)** (`sponsrcommit`, `text`, required): Enter the commitment of the sponsor.
* **PTA Fundraising (ptafundraising)** (`ptafundraising`, `text`, required): Enter the information about the PTA fundraising efforts.
