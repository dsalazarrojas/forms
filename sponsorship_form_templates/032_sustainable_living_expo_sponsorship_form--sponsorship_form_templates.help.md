# Sustainable Living Expo Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential sponsors who want to participate in the Sustainable Living Expo. It gathers information about the sponsor, their sponsorship status, and the package they are applying for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Decide if you are applying for sponsorship or not. Select "Yes" or "No" in the Sponsor Applications field.
2. Choose your sponsor packages, select "Yes" or "No" in the Sponsor Packages field.
3. Confirm your branding details, select "Yes" or "No" in the Branding Details field.
4. Select if you want to participate in the event, choose "Yes" or "No" in the Event Info field.
5. Provide your contact information, select "Yes" or "No" in the Contact Info field.
6. Upload your logo, or provide text in the Logo field.
7. Enter the price of the package you are applying for in the Package Price field.
8. Select the status of your package, choose from Active or Inactive in the Package Status field.
9. Enter the date you applied for the sponsorship in the Application Date field.
10. Enter the date you want to contact the event organizers in the Contact Date field.
11. Provide your phone number and email address in the Phone and Email fields.
12. Select the type of package you are applying for, choose from "Yes" or "No" in the Package Type field.
13. Enter a brief description of the package you are applying for in the Package Description field.
14. Enter the name of the package you are applying for in the Package Name field.
15. Enter the quantity of the package you are applying for in the Package Quantity field.
16. Enter the price range of the package you are applying for in the Package Price Range field.
17. Select the status of your package, choose from Active or Inactive in the Package Status field.
18. Select the status of your application, choose from Active or Inactive in the Application Status field.
19. Select the status of your payment, choose from Active or Inactive in the Payment Status field.
20. Select the status of the event, choose from Active or Inactive in the Event Status field.
21. Enter any additional notes about your package in the Package Notes field.
22. Enter the name of the sponsor in the Sponsor Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Applications**: Select "Yes" if you are applying for sponsorship, "No" otherwise.
* **Sponsor Packages**: Select "Yes" if you have packages to offer, "No" otherwise.
* **Branding Details**: Select "Yes" if you have branding details, "No" otherwise. This field is currently not used in the form and is for future reference.
* **Event Info**: Select "Yes" if you want to participate in the event, "No" otherwise.
* **Contact Info**: Select "Yes" if you want to provide contact information, "No" otherwise.
* **Logo**: Upload your logo or enter text to represent it.
* **Package Price**: Enter the price of the package you are applying for.
* **Package Status**: Select the status of your package, choose from Active or Inactive.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you applied for the sponsorship.
* **Contact Date**: Enter the date you want to contact the event organizers.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Package Type**: Select "Yes" if you have a package to offer, "No" otherwise.
* **Package Description**: Enter a brief description of the package you are applying for.
* **Package Name**: Enter the name of the package you are applying for.
* **Package Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the package you are applying for.
* **Package Price Range**: Enter the price range of the package you are applying for.
* **Package Status**: Select the status of your package, choose from Active or Inactive.
* **Application Status**: Select the status of your application, choose from Active or Inactive.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment, choose from Active or Inactive.
* **Event Status**: Select the status of the event, choose from Active or Inactive.
* **Package Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your package.
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor.
