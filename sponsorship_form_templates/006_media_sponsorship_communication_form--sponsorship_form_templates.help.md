# Media Sponsorship Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document media sponsorships for record-keeping and communication purposes. It collects information from media sponsors, including the type of media, sponsor name, contact details, and agreement status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of media being sponsored (digital, print, or radio buttons).
2. Enter the name and contact details of the sponsor (email and phone number).
3. Provide additional comments or details about the sponsorship.
4. Check the agreement status (radio buttons or non-radio buttons).
5. Date the agreement if it has been signed (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title
* **Form Title** (`media_sponsorship_communication_form_1`, text, optional): The title of the form is used to identify the purpose and context of the form. You can leave this field blank if not required.

### Media Type
* **Media Type** (`form_media_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of media being sponsored. This field is used to categorize and filter media sponsorships. Select one of the following options:
	+ **Digital Media**
	+ **Print Media**
	+ **Radio Buttons**

### Sponsor Name
* **Sponsor Name** (`form_sponsor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the media sponsor.

### Sponsor Email
* **Sponsor Email** (`form_sponsor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the sponsor.

### Sponsor Phone
* **Sponsor Phone** (`form_sponsor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the sponsor.

### Sponsor Website
* **Sponsor Website** (`form_sponsor_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the sponsor (if available).

### Media Sponsorship Details
* **Media Sponsorship Details** (`form_media_sponsorship_details`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about the sponsorship.

### Agreement
* **Agreement** (`form_agreement`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the agreement. Select one of the following options:
	+ **Radio Buttons**
	+ **Non Radio Buttons**

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`form_additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the sponsorship.

### Agreement Signed
* **Agreement Signed** (`form_agreement_signed`, date, optional): Date the agreement if it has been signed.
