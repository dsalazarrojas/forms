# Sponsorship Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Communication Form is a collection of fields that gather information from sponsors to facilitate effective communication and management of sponsorship activities. It is an optional form used to capture details about sponsorship interests, amounts, types, periods, contact information, and communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, start by reviewing the sponsorship details provided, such as sponsorship interests and amounts.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field, ensuring you are accurate and truthful in your responses.
3. Complete the contact information section, providing the necessary details to facilitate communication.
4. Indicate your preference for sponsorship communication, such as email or phone calls.
5. Review and finalize your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsorship Details** (`sponsorship_communication_form_1`): This field captures information about the sponsorship details. Select the "True" or "False" option that best describes your sponsorship status.
* **Sponsorship Interest** (`sponsorship_communication_form_2`): This field is used to indicate your interest in sponsorship activities. Select the "True" or "False" option that best describes your interest level.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_communication_form_3`): This field captures the amount of sponsorship you are interested in or have already received. Enter a numerical value to represent the amount.
* **Sponsorship Type** (`sponsorship_communication_form_4`): This field is used to specify the type of sponsorship you are interested in or have already received. Select the "True" or "False" option that best describes the type of sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Period** (`sponsorship_communication_form_5`): This field captures the period of sponsorship you are interested in or have already received. Enter a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy to represent the period.
* **Sponsor Contact Info** (`sponsorship_communication_form_6`): This field captures the contact information of the sponsor. Enter the sponsor's email address in the format [sponsorname@example.com](mailto:sponsorname@example.com).
* **Sponsorship Communication Preference** (`sponsorship_communication_form_7`): This field captures your preference for sponsorship communication. Select the "True" or "False" option that best describes your preference.
* **Sponsorship Frequency** (`sponsorship_communication_form_8`): This field is used to indicate the frequency of sponsorship communication. Select the "True" or "False" option that best describes your preference.
* **Sponsorship Method** (`sponsorship_communication_form_9`): This field captures the method of sponsorship communication. Select the "True" or "False" option that best describes the method.
* **Sponsorship Notes** (`sponsorship_communication_form_10`): This field provides a notes section where you can add any additional comments or information about your sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Interest 2** (`sponsorship_communication_form_11`): This field is used to indicate your interest in additional sponsorship opportunities. Select the "True" or "False" option that best describes your interest level.
* **Sponsor Contact Info 2** (`sponsorship_communication_form_12`): This field captures the contact information of the sponsor for additional sponsorship activities. Enter the sponsor's email address in the format [sponsorname2@example.com](mailto:sponsorname2@example.com).
* **Sponsorship Communication** (`sponsorship_communication_form_13`): This field captures any additional comments or information about sponsorship communication.
* **Sponsor Contact Info 3** (`sponsorship_communication_form_14`): This field captures the contact information of the sponsor for additional sponsorship activities. Enter the sponsor's email address in the format [sponsorname3@example.com](mailto:sponsorname3@example.com).
* **Sponsorship Communication Form 15** (`sponsorship_communication_form_15`): This field is not applicable and can be ignored.
