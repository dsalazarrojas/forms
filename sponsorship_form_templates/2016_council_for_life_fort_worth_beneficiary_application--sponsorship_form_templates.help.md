# 2016 Council For Life Fort Worth Beneficiary Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2016 Council For Life Fort Worth Beneficiary Application form is designed to collect necessary information from organizations seeking to become beneficiaries of the 2016 Council For Life. The form is divided into sections that cover various aspects of the organization's profile, services, finances, and accreditation status. Completing this form will help the review committee assess the applicant organization's eligibility and readiness for beneficiary status.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before starting the application process.
2.  Review the form carefully and follow the instructions for each section.
3.  Provide accurate and truthful information for all fields.
4.  Use the available space to provide detailed responses for narrative questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Organization Information
*   **Official Organization Name** (`organization_name`): Enter the legal name as filed with the IRS.
*   **Federal Tax ID (EIN)** (`tax_id_ein`): 9-digit Employer Identification Number.
*   **Primary Facility Address** (`physical_address`): Street address where services are provided.
*   **Year Organization Established** (`year_established`): Four-digit year the organization was established (e.g., 1995).
*   **Executive Director / CEO Name** (`executive_director`): Current leadership.

### Primary Contact Information
*   **Primary Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Person responsible for this application.
*   **Contact Title/Role** (`contact_title`): e.g., Development Director, Board President.
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Primary email for correspondence regarding this application.
*   **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Direct line or main office number.

### Application Details
*   **Type of Application** (`application_type`): Select the category that applies to your organization (e.g., New Applicant, Renewal - 2015 Beneficiary).
*   **Application Submission Date** (`application_date`): Date the application is submitted.
*   **Fiscal Year End Date** (`fiscal_year_end`): Date the fiscal year ends (e.g., December 31 or June 30).

### Program Services & Statistics
*   **Primary Service Model** (`service_model`): Select your organization's main operational focus (e.g., Medical Clinic (with ultrasound), Non-Medical Peer Counseling Center).
*   **Annual Client Volume (2015)** (`annual_client_volume`): Total unduplicated clients served last calendar year.
*   **Services Provided** (`services_provided`): Check all services currently offered at your facility (e.g., Pregnancy Testing, Limited Ultrasound, STI/STD Testing & Treatment).
*   **Primary Service Area / Counties Served** (`primary_service_area`): Geographic area your center primarily serves.

### Financial Information
*   **Current Annual Operating Budget** (`operating_budget`): Select the range for your current fiscal year (e.g., Under $100,000, $100,000 - $249,999).
*   **Grant Amount Requested from Council For Life** (`grant_amount_requested`): Dollar amount requested for 2016 (numbers only, e.g., 15000).
*   **Other Grants Anticipated for 2016** (`grants_anticipated`): List major grants expected or pending (source and amount).
*   **Current Funding Sources** (`funding_sources`): Revenue streams currently supporting operations (e.g., Individual Donors, Churches/Religious Organizations).

### Accreditation & Compliance
*   **Current Accreditation/Affiliation** (`accreditation_status`): Select your primary network affiliation (e.g., Care Net Affiliate, Heartbeat International Affiliate).
*   **501(c)(3) Status Verification** (`nonprofit_verified`): Current tax-exempt status.

### Narrative Questions
*   **Purpose of Grant Request** (`grant_purpose`): How will Council For Life funds be used? Be specific about programs, staffing, or equipment needs.
*   **Unique Contribution to Pro-Life Movement** (`unique_contribution`): What distinguishes your center from others in the Fort Worth area? What specific gap do you fill?
*   **Anticipated Outcomes for 2016** (`anticipated_outcomes`): Measurable goals for clients served, lives saved, families helped, etc.
*   **Additional Comments or Information** (`additional_comments`): Any other relevant details for the review committee.
