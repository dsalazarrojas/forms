# Sponsorship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Survey form is used to collect general information about sponsors (or their organizations) for sponsorship purposes. This form is intended for sponsors or their representatives to provide details about their organization and contact information for potential collaborations or partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information in the **General Information** field.
2. Provide details about your sponsor information in the **Sponsor Info** field.
3. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address in the **Contact Information** field.
4. If applicable, enter the sponsorship details for the event or project in the **Sponsorship Details** field.
5. Review the **Terms and Conditions** field, which is a non-formatted text area for additional information or disclaimers.
6. Enter your sponsor's name and email address in the **Sponsor Name** and **Sponsor Email** fields, respectively.
7. Enter your sponsor's phone number and address in the **Sponsor Phone** and **Sponsor Address** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information:** 
	+ (General Information, text, not required): Enter your general information about your organization, event, or project.
* **Sponsor Info:**
	+ (Sponsor Info, text, not required): Enter details about your sponsor, including their name, logo, or other relevant information.
* **Contact Information:**
	+ (Contact Information, text, not required): Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Sponsorship Details:**
	+ (Sponsorship Details, text, not required): Enter specific details about the sponsorship, such as the event or project name, dates, and duration.
* **Terms and Conditions:**
	+ (Terms and Conditions, note, not required): Enter any additional information or disclaimers about the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Name:**
	+ (Sponsor Name, text, not required): Enter the name of your sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email:**
	+ (Sponsor Email, email, not required): Enter the email address of your sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone:**
	+ (Sponsor Phone, text, not required): Enter the phone number of your sponsor.
* **Sponsor Address:**
	+ (Sponsor Address, text, not required): Enter the address of your sponsor.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to the best of your ability.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact [insert contact info].
* Please note that this form is for internal use only and will not be shared publicly.
* If you're still unsure about what to fill out, feel free to contact us to clarify the requirements.
