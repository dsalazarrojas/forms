# mental_health_summit_sponsorship_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health summit sponsorship form is designed to collect sponsorship information from companies or individuals wishing to sponsor the event. This form should only be filled out once per sponsor, and any duplicate submissions will be disregarded.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your organization's sponsorship commitment and event preferences.
2. Select the desired sponsorship tier that suits your organization's level of support.
3. Provide details about the event you wish to sponsor, including date, time, location, and description.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
5. Confirm the submission by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Sponsor Commitment**: This field helps us understand your organization's level of commitment to the event. Please enter a number value to indicate the level of support you are willing to provide.
  - **Type:** number
  - **Required:** false

- **Tier Selection**: Choose the sponsorship tier that best fits your organization's level of support. This will help us understand your level of involvement in the event.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Required:** false

- **Promotional Coordination**: This field is for additional notes about how you would like to coordinate promotional activities with the event team.
  - **Type:** note
  - **Required:** false

- **Event Details**: Select all the event details you would like to provide, such as date, time, location, and description.
  - **Type:** select_multiple
  - **Required:** true

- **Company Information**: Enter a brief description of your company, such as its name and industry.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false

- **Contact Name**: Choose the name of the person responsible for this sponsorship.
  - **Type:** select_one
  - **Required:** true

- **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for this sponsorship.
  - **Type:** email
  - **Required:** true

- **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for this sponsorship.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
