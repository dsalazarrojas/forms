# Sponsorship Activation Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Activation Slot Booking Form is used to collect information from users about their sponsorship activation slots. This form is essential for ensuring that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your relevant details.
2. Provide Sponsor Info in the Sponsor Info section.
3. Enter Event Info in the corresponding field.
4. Select your Time Slots preference in the Time Slots section.
5. Choose your Time Selection and Time Slot.
6. Enter your Date & Time in the Date & Time field.
7. Select your preferred Time Of Day.
8. Specify the duration of your sponsorship activation slot in hours and seconds in the Duration (hours) and Duration (seconds) fields, respectively.
9. Add any additional notes in the Notes field.
10. Enter your Email and Phone number in the Email and Phone fields.
11. Finally, enter your Contact Name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (1)** (`User Info`, text, required: false): Enter your basic user information.
* **Sponsor Info (2)** (`Sponsor Info`, text, required: false): Provide information about the sponsor who will be activating the slot.
* **Event Info (3)** (`Event Info`, text, required: false): Enter details about the event being activated.
* **Time Slots (4)** (`Time Slots`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose which time slots you prefer for your sponsorship activation.
* **Time Selection (5)** (`Time Selection`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you want to be contacted during your preferred time slots.
* **Time Slot (6)** (`Time Slot`, select_one, required: false): Choose one time slot for your sponsorship activation.
* **Date & Time (7)** (`Date & Time`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of your sponsorship activation.
* **Time Of Day (8)** (`Time Of Day`, time, required: false): Select your preferred time of day for the activation.
* **Duration (hours) (9)** (`Duration (hours)`, number, required: false): Specify the duration of your sponsorship activation in hours.
* **Duration (seconds) (10)** (`Duration (seconds)`, number, required: false): Specify the duration of your sponsorship activation in seconds.
* **Notes (11)** (`Notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your sponsorship activation slot.
* **Email (12)** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (13)** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Name (14)** (`Contact Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
