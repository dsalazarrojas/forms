# Sponsor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Application Form is used to collect information from potential sponsors for events or projects. It helps determine the level of sponsorship, the type of sponsor, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to review and agree to the Sponsor Agreement Terms.
3. Provide a clear and concise sponsor message (if applicable).
4. Upload a sponsor logo (if required).
5. Review and review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Info**: Enter the name and details of the sponsor.
	+ (Required)
* **Event Info**: Enter a brief description of the event or project.
	+ (Optional)
* **Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of the sponsor.
	+ (Required)
* **Sponsor Relationship**: Enter the sponsor's relationship with the event or project (numerical value).
	+ (Optional)
* **Sponsor Level**: Select the level of sponsorship (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, Level 4, Level 5).
	+ (Required)
* **Sponsor Type**: Select all applicable sponsor types (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3).
	+ (Required)
* **Sponsor Message**: Enter a brief message to the sponsor (if applicable).
	+ (Optional)
* **Sponsor Email**: Enter the sponsor's email address.
	+ (Optional)
* **Sponsor Phone**: Enter the sponsor's phone number.
	+ (Optional)
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event or project.
	+ (Optional)
* **Event Venue**: Enter the location of the event or project.
	+ (Optional)
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event or project.
	+ (Optional)
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event or project.
	+ (Optional)
* **Event Address**: Enter the address of the event or project.
	+ (Required)
* **Event City**: Enter the city of the event or project.
	+ (Required)
* **Event Postal**: Enter the postal code of the event or project.
	+ (Required)
* **Event Country**: Enter the country of the event or project.
	+ (Required)
* **Sponsor Agreement**: Read and agree to the sponsor agreement terms.
	+ (Required)
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the agreement (if required).
	+ (Optional)
* **Agreement Version**: Enter the version of the agreement (if required).
	+ (Optional)
* **Sponsor Signature**: Upload the sponsor's signature.
	+ (Required)
* **Sponsor Logo**: Upload the sponsor's logo.
	+ (Required)
* **Sponsor Agreement Terms**: Enter the terms of the agreement.
	+ (Required)
* **Sponsor Terms Agreed**: Confirm that you agree to the terms.
	+ (Required)

Note: The fields marked as "Required" are mandatory and must be filled out before submitting the form.
