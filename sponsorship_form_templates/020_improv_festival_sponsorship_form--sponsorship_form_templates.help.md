# Improv Festival Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Improv Festival Sponsorship Form is designed to collect sponsorship information from potential sponsors to support the festival's activities and events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the sponsorship type that best fits your company's goals and interests.
2. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide branding details about your company, such as your company name and tagline.
4. Upload your logo, if applicable.
5. Sign the approval field to acknowledge your sponsorship agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsorship Selections** (`sponsorship_selections`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more sponsorship options that fit your company's goals and interests. Options include:
	+ Sponsorship Type: Select this option to support the festival's events or activities.
	+ Partnership: Partner with the festival to co-create and promote events.
	+ Brand Ambassador: Represent the festival and promote its activities.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, Text, Required): Enter your name, email, and phone number to facilitate communication with the festival organizers.
* **Branding Details** (`branding_details`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your company's branding, including its name and tagline.
* **Logo Upload** (`logo_upload`, Text, Optional): Upload your company's logo, if applicable.
* **Signed Approval** (`approval`, Note, Required): Sign this field to acknowledge your sponsorship agreement.
