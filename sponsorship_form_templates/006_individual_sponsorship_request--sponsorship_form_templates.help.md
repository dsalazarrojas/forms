# Individual Sponsorship Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential sponsors for individual sponsorships. Please provide accurate and clear information to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Contact Information" page with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. On the "Sponsorship Details" page, provide a brief description of the sponsorship you are seeking, including any relevant details or requirements.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure that all required information is included.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Individual Sponsorship Request Form** (`individual_sponsorship_request_form`, text, optional)
This is the main section of the form where you can describe the sponsorship you are seeking. Please keep it brief and concise.

### **Contact Information** (`contact_information_form`, text, optional)
Provide your name, email address, and phone number so that the project team can contact you to discuss your sponsorship request.

### **Sponsorship Details** (`sponsorship_details_form`, note, optional)
This section is intended for providing additional information about the sponsorship you are requesting. Please use the space provided to include any relevant details or requirements.

### Tips
* Be clear and concise in your answers to help the project team understand your sponsorship request.
* Double-check that you have not already submitted a sponsorship request for this project/initiative.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact the project team at form@gic.mx.
