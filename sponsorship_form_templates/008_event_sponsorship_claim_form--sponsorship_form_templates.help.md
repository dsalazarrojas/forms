# Event Sponsorship Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to claim sponsorship for events. It is intended for event organizers to document and track sponsorships for their events. This form will help to gather information about the event, including the event title, date, and details of the sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the event title in the "Event Title" field.
2. Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
3. Enter the name and email address of the sponsor in the "Sponsor Name" and "Sponsor E-mail" fields.
4. Enter the phone number of the sponsor in the "Sponsor Phone" field.
5. Enter the details of the sponsorship in the "Claim Details" field.
6. Enter the amount of the sponsorship in the "Sponsorship Amount" field.
7. Select the type of sponsorship in the "Event Sponsorship Type" field.
8. Select the frequency of the sponsorship in the "Event Sponsorship Frequency" field (this is a required field).
9. Select the status of the sponsorship in the "Event Sponsorship Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title**: Enter the title of the event for which you are claiming sponsorship.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor E-mail**: Enter the email address of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the sponsor.
* **Claim Details**: Enter a brief description of the sponsorship details.
* **Sponsorship Amount**: Enter the amount of the sponsorship.
* **Event Sponsorship Type**: Select the type of sponsorship (e.g. monetary, material, etc.).
* **Event Sponsorship Frequency**: Select the frequency of the sponsorship (e.g. one-time, recurring, etc.).
* **Event Sponsorship Status**: Select the status of the sponsorship (e.g. pending, confirmed, etc.).
