# Leadership St. Pete 2017 Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership St. Pete 2017 Sponsorship Form is designed to collect sponsorship information for the Leadership St. Pete 2017 event. This form will be used to gather contact and sponsorship details from individuals and organizations that are willing to sponsor the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your sponsorship.
2. Enter a brief description of your contribution.
3. Provide your name and contact information (email, phone number, and address).
4. Specify the amount of your contribution.
5. Choose the sponsorship committee that will handle your sponsorship.
6. Add any additional comments or information about your sponsorship.
7. Agree to the terms of the sponsorship.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (form_category)**: Select the category that best describes your sponsorship from the list provided. This will help us understand the type of sponsorship you are offering.
* **Description (form_description)**: Enter a brief description of your contribution. This will help us understand the details of your sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Name (form_name)**: Enter your name as the representative of your organization or individual.
* **Email (form_email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (form_phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Amount (form_amount)**: Enter the amount of your contribution.
* **Fax (form_fax)**: If you have a fax number, enter it here.
* **Address (form_address)**: Enter your physical address where checks or other materials can be sent.
* **Date (form_date)**: Enter the date you are willing to sponsor the event.
* **Time (form_time)**: Enter the time you are willing to sponsor the event.
* **Additional Information (form_additional_info)**: If you have any additional comments or information about your sponsorship, enter them here.
* **Why Sponsor (form_why_sponsor)**: Explain why you are willing to sponsor the event.
* **Sponsorship Committee (form_sponsorship_committee)**: Choose the sponsorship committee that will handle your sponsorship.
* **Additional Comments (form_additional)**: If you have any additional comments or information about your sponsorship, enter them here.
* **Agreement (form_agree)**: Select "Agree" if you are willing to sponsor the event.
* **Signature (form_signature)**: Sign your name as the representative of your organization or individual.
* **Date Signed (form_date_signed)**: Date and time of when you signed this form.
* **Time Signed (form_time_signed)**: Time when you signed this form.
