# interactive_exhibit_sponsorship_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interactive_exhibit_sponsorship_form is used to collect information about sponsorships for an interactive exhibit.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the exhibition date from the calendar picker.
2. Choose whether or not the sponsor is providing funding.
3. Enter the sponsor's name.
4. Enter the name of the organization (if applicable).
5. Enter the contact person's name.
6. Enter the sponsor's email address.
7. Enter the sponsor's phone number.
8. Enter the sponsorship amount.
9. Enter a note about the sponsorship (optional).
10. Choose the sponsorship level (if applicable).
11. Choose whether or not the sponsor has signed an agreement.
12. Enter any additional sponsor types or organizations if they are not listed in the options.
13. Enter the minimum and maximum sponsorship amount (if applicable).
14. Choose whether or not the sponsor is providing multiple sponsorships.
15. Enter any additional sponsorships (if applicable).
