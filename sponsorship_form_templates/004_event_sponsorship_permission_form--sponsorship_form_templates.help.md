# event_sponsorship_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event_sponsorship_permission_form is a tool used by event organizers to collect necessary information from potential sponsors for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the first page, "Event Sponsorship Permission Form", where you will find a brief introduction to the form.
2. Proceed to the "Sponsor Details" page, where you will choose the name of your sponsor from the options provided.
3. On the "Sponsorship Agreement" page, select the level of sponsorship you wish to commit to (if applicable).
4. On the "Sponsorship Type" page, choose the type of sponsorship you are offering.
5. If desired, add any additional comments about the sponsorship in the "event_sponsorship_permission_form_page_5" field.
6. Enter your email address on the "email" page.
7. Finally, provide your phone number on the "phone" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Details (select_one)**: Select the name of your sponsor from the provided options. This is a required field as it helps event organizers identify the sponsor and their level of involvement.
* **Sponsorship Agreement (time)**: If you are committing to a specific sponsorship level, choose the corresponding option from the available choices. This field is optional as not all sponsors may be committing to a specific level.
* **Sponsorship Type (select_one)**: Choose the type of sponsorship you are offering. This is also an optional field.
* **Additional Comments (note)**: Use this space to add any relevant information about your sponsorship commitment.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used as a contact point for communication and updates about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used to facilitate communication with event organizers about any event-related matters.
