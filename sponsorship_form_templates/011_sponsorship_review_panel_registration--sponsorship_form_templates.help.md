# Sponsorship Review Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a sponsorship review panel, which is a group of experts or non-experts who review and evaluate sponsorships.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the Sponsorship Review Panel Registration page with a brief description of the panel you are registering.
*   On the Sponsorship Review Panelist Details page, select the type of reviewer you would like to register (Expert, Non-expert, or Both).
*   On the Sponsorship Reviewer Availability page, enter the number of days you are available to review sponsorships.
*   On the Sponsorship Reviewer Expertise page, enter the number of years of experience you have as a reviewer.
*   On the Sponsorship Reviewer Application page, enter a brief statement of your application.
*   On the Sponsorship Reviewer Phone page, enter your phone number.
*   On the Sponsorship Reviewer Email page, enter your email address.
*   Finally, click the Submit button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Sponsorship Review Panel Registration**: A brief description of the panel you are registering.
*   **Sponsorship Review Panelist Details**: Select the type of reviewer you would like to register (Expert, Non-expert, or Both).
*   **Sponsorship Reviewer Availability**: Enter the number of days you are available to review sponsorships.
*   **Sponsorship Reviewer Expertise**: Enter the number of years of experience you have as a reviewer.
*   **Sponsorship Reviewer Application**: Enter a brief statement of your application.
*   **Sponsorship Reviewer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Sponsorship Reviewer Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Submit**: Click this button to submit your application.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is submitted successfully.
*   You can enter any other information you feel is relevant to your application in the Sponsorship Reviewer Application field.
*   If you have any issues submitting the form, please contact the form administrator.
*   This form is meant to be filled out by people who want to be part of the sponsorship review panel.
