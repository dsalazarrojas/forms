# Event Sponsorship Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from organizations and individuals interested in sponsoring events. It helps us understand their interests, agreement terms, and responsibilities involved in the sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's name in the Organization Name field.
2. Enter the name of the sponsor in the Sponsor Name field.
3. Enter your contact email in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. If applicable, select the Agreement Date for your sponsorship.
6. Provide a brief description of the rights and privileges you will receive as a sponsor in the Rights field.
7. Briefly describe your responsibilities as a sponsor in the Responsibilities field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's official name, as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the person authorized to represent your organization in this sponsorship agreement.
* **Email**: Enter your official contact email address. This will be used for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone**: Enter your official phone number. This will be used for communication and follow-up.
* **Agreement Date**: If you have an existing agreement or a preferred date for the sponsorship, select it. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Rights**: Describe the rights and privileges your organization will receive as a sponsor, such as logo placement or event promotions.
* **Responsibilities**: Describe your organization's responsibilities as a sponsor, such as promoting the event or participating in activities.
