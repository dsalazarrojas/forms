# golf_tournament_registration_sponsor_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering a golf tournament sponsorship, intended for participants to provide information about themselves and their sponsorship details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have received an invitation from the tournament organizers and are authorized to represent a sponsor.
2. Check with the tournament organizers if the sponsor you wish to represent is already registered or if there are specific requirements for sponsorship opportunities in the tournament.
3. Complete the form with the required information, making sure to fill in all the fields accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): This section is where you provide general information about yourself as a participant in the tournament.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant who will be representing the sponsorship.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the participant who will be representing the sponsorship.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the participant who will be representing the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Information** (`sponsor_info`, text, required: false): This section is where you provide general information about the sponsor you represent.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the sponsor you represent.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the sponsor you represent.
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the sponsor you represent.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the fields accurately to ensure that your sponsorship application is complete and valid.
* If you're unsure about any information, please contact the tournament organizers for clarification.
* Double-check with the tournament organizers if the sponsor you wish to represent is already registered or if there are specific requirements for sponsorship opportunities in the tournament.
