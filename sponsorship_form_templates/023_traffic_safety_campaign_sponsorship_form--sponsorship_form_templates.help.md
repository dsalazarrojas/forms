# traffic_safety_campaign_sponsorship_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The traffic safety campaign sponsorship form is for organizations or individuals to sponsor a traffic safety campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the sponsorship form, please fill out the following fields:
  1. Fill in your name in the required **name** field.
  2. Enter your email address in the required **email** field.
  3. Enter your phone number in the required **phone** field.
  4. Enter your address in the required **address** field.
  5. Select the **campaign_duration** for the sponsorship. This field is optional.
  6. Choose the type of **campaign_type** for the sponsorship. This field is required.
  7. Enter the name of your organization in the required **organization_name** field.
  8. Enter your contact person's name in the required **contact_person** field.
  9. Enter your contact email address in the required **contact_email** field.
  10. Enter your contact phone number in the required **contact_phone** field.
  11. You may optionally enter a message for the sponsorship in the **message** field.
  12. Enter your sponsorship details in the optional **sponsorship_details** field.
  13. Enter your sponsor's name in the optional **sponsor_name** field.
  14. Enter your sponsor's title in the optional **sponsor_title** field.
  15. Enter your sponsor's organization in the optional **sponsor_organization** field.
  16. Enter your sponsor's address in the optional **sponsor_address** field.
  17. Enter your sponsor's phone number in the optional **sponsor_phone** field.
  18. Enter your sponsor's email address in the required **sponsor_email** field.
  19. Select the **sponsorship_status** of the sponsorship. This field is required.
  20. Click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the organization or individual sponsoring the traffic safety campaign.
- **email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the contact person for the organization or individual.
- **phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the organization or individual.
- **address** (Text, Required): Enter the address of the organization or individual.
- **campaign_duration** (Text, Optional): The duration of the campaign. If left blank, a default duration will be applied.
- **campaign_type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of traffic safety campaign being sponsored.
- **organization_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the organization or individual sponsoring the campaign.
- **contact_person** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the contact person for the organization or individual.
- **contact_email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the contact person for the organization or individual.
- **contact_phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the organization or individual.
- **message** (Text, Optional): Enter a message for the sponsorship.
- **sponsorship_details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional details about the sponsorship.
- **sponsor_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the sponsor.
- **sponsor_title** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the sponsor.
- **sponsor_organization** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the sponsor's organization.
- **sponsor_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the sponsor's organization.
- **sponsor_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the sponsor's organization.
- **sponsor_email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the sponsor.
- **sponsorship_status** (Select One, Required): Choose the sponsorship status.
- **organization_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the organization or individual.
 
Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant contact person for clarification.
