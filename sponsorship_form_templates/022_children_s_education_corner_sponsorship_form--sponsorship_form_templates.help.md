The Children Education Corner Sponsorship Form is a form designed to capture sponsorship information from individuals or organizations interested in supporting Children Education Corner's activities. This form is meant for those who wish to provide a monetary contribution to the organization. It is essential that sponsors understand the purpose of this form to avoid duplication of efforts and ensure that their contributions are accurately recorded.

## Purpose
The Children Education Corner Sponsorship Form is used to collect sponsorship information from potential sponsors. This information will be used to communicate with them and to process their contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact email address in the **Sponsor Name** and **Contact Email** fields, respectively.
2. Select your level of support from the **Sponsorship Level** dropdown menu.
3. Enter the amount of your monetary commitment in the **Sponsorship Amount** field.
4. If you wish to be recognized for your sponsorship, describe how in the **Recognition Options** field. This field is optional.
5. If an invoice is required for payment, select **True** in the **Invoice Required** field. Otherwise, select **False**.
6. If applicable, provide your billing name and address in the **Billing Details** field.
7. Finally, provide your contact phone number for sponsorship coordination in the **Contact Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of your organization sponsoring Children Education Corner.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your or your organization's email address for correspondence.
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, select_one, required): Choose a level of support from the dropdown menu: Supporter, Partner, Sponsor, or Benefactor.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of your monetary commitment to Children Education Corner.
* **Recognition Options** (`recognition_options`, text, optional): Describe how you wish to be recognized for your sponsorship, if at all.
* **Invoice Required** (`invoice_required`, select_one, required): Select **True** if an invoice is required for payment; **False** otherwise.
* **Billing Details** (`billing_details`, text, optional): If an invoice is required, provide your billing name and address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for sponsorship coordination.
