# Brand Event Sponsorship Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential sponsors for a brand event. It collects their contact details and sponsorship preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide the required information for each field.
3. If any fields are not applicable to your situation, you can leave them blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sponsor name
**Sponsor name** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company or individual that is interested in sponsoring the event.
### Contact person
**Contact person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the person responsible for sponsorship correspondence at the company or individual.
### Contact email
**Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address for sponsorship communication.
### Sponsorship interest level
**Sponsorship interest level** (`sponsorship_interest_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of interest you have for the sponsorship:
	* Title sponsor: Interested in a premium sponsorship package.
	* Major sponsor: Interested in a standard sponsorship package.
	* Supporting sponsor: Interested in a minor sponsorship package.
	* In-kind sponsor: Interested in a non-monetary contribution.
### Budget capacity
**Budget capacity** (`budget_capacity`, `text`, optional): If you have an approximate budget in mind, please enter it here.
### Activation ideas
**Activation ideas** (`activation_ideas`, `text`, optional): Describe how you would like to be visible at the event.
### Timeline
**Timeline** (`timeline`, `text`, optional): Provide key dates or deadlines that are important for your sponsorship.
### Follow up preference
**Follow up preference** (`follow_up_preference`, `select_one`, optional): How would you like to be contacted after submitting this form?
	* Email: Send emails for further updates.
	* Phone: Send phone calls for further updates.
	* Meeting: Schedule a meeting to discuss further details.
	* No preference: Do not contact you again until you get in touch with us.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
