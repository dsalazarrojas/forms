> ## Sponsored Rider Application Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Sponsored Rider Application Form is a tool to help us understand your background, goals, and needs as a rider. This form will be reviewed carefully to ensure we can tailor our support to help you achieve your objectives.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide accurate and honest information about yourself. Each field is described below for your reference.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
#### 1. **First name**
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.

#### 2. **Last Name**
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.

#### 3. **Rider Type**
* **Rider Type** (`rider_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of riding you are interested in.
	+ **Bicycle**: Road, mountain, or other types of bicycles.
	+ **Road**: Road cycling events or activities.
	+ **Mountain**: Mountain biking events or activities.

#### 4. **Contact Email**
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address we can use to contact you for more information or to get in touch.

#### 5. **Contact Phone**
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Please enter a phone number we can use to contact you for more information or to get in touch. Use the format 123-123-1234 or +1 123 123 1234, for example.

#### 6. **Date of Birth**
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD (for example, 12/25/1990 or 1990-12-25).

#### 7. **Emergency Contact Name**
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the emergency contact person who we can reach out to in case of an emergency.

#### 8. **Relationship to Rider**
* **Relationship to Rider** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Please select the relationship you have with the emergency contact person.
	+ **Mother**: Biological or adoptive mother.
	+ **Father**: Biological or adoptive father.
	+ **Spouse**: Husband or wife.

#### 9. **Emergency Contact Phone**
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

#### 10. **Other (please specify)**
* **Other (please specify)** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, text, optional): If the relationship with the emergency contact person is not listed, please describe it here.

#### 11. **Emergency Contact Email**
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address of the emergency contact person.

#### 12. **How do you know us?**
* **How do you know us?** (`how_do_you_know_us`, text, optional): Please tell us how you heard about our organization or sponsorship program.

#### 13. **Years Active**
* **Years Active** (`years_active`, number, optional): Please enter the number of years you have been actively participating in cycling activities.

#### 14. **Experience**
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Please describe your relevant cycling experience.

#### 15. **Why are you asking for sponsorship?**
* **Why are you asking for sponsorship?** (`why_are_you_asking_for_sponsorship`, text, optional): Please explain why you are seeking sponsorship.

#### 16. **What do you hope to achieve?**
* **What do you hope to achieve?** (`what_do_you_hope_to_achieve`, text, optional): Describe the goals you aim to achieve with our sponsorship.

#### 17. **What do you expect from us?**
* **What do you expect from us?** (`what_do_you_expect_from_us`, text, optional): Please explain what you hope to get from our sponsorship.

#### 18. **What would you like to make up our sponsorship?**
* **What would you like to make up our sponsorship?** (`what_makeup_of_our_sponsorship`, text, optional): Describe how you plan to use our sponsorship support.

#### 19. **Signature**
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Please sign your application to confirm your submission.
