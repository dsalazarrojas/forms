# Hospice Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for hospices to submit their sponsorship information for review and approval. Please ensure that you have all necessary information ready before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Review and submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization, as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person who will be the primary contact for your organization.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for your organization.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the physical address of your organization.
- **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where your organization is located.
- **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where your organization is located.
- **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter the zip code where your organization is located.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for your organization.
- **Fax** (`fax`, text, optional): Enter a fax number for your organization, if applicable.
 

**Tips**: Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date to avoid any delays in the review process.
