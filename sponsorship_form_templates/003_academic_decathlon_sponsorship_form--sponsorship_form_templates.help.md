# Academic Decathlon Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Decathlon Sponsorship Form is designed to collect sponsorship information from individuals or organizations that support the Academic Decathlon program. This form will help us process and acknowledge sponsorships efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Each field is essential for proper processing and recognition of your sponsorship.
2. Select the sponsor level that represents your commitment to the program.
3. Enter the monetary amount or value of the sponsorship.
4. Choose how you would like to be acknowledged as a sponsor.
5. Provide your billing information for any invoices.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your sponsorship commitment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Sponsor Information --** (`sponsor_information`, `note`, not required): This is the introduction to the sponsorship information section.
* **Sponsor Organization or Individual Name** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization or yourself as a sponsor.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Provide the primary contact person's name for sponsorship coordination.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person for confirmation and receipt.
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred sponsorship tier:
	+ Platinum
	+ Gold
	+ Silver
	+ Bronze
* **Amount Committed** (`amount_committed`, `number`, required): Enter the monetary amount or value of the sponsorship.
* **Recognition Preferences** (`recognition_preferences`, `text`, optional): Choose how you would like to be acknowledged as a sponsor.
* **Billing Information** (`billing_information`, `text`, optional): Provide your billing information for any invoices.
* **Sponsor Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your sponsorship commitment.
* **Date of Sponsorship Commitment** (`date`, `date`, required): Date when the form is submitted.
