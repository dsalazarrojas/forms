# Ski Event Sponsorship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ski Event Sponsorship Application form is designed to gather information from ski event organizers to apply for sponsorship from our organization. This form helps us understand the event details, sponsor's information, and other relevant data points.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Ski Event Sponsorship Application form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information: Enter your first and last name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Provide event details: Enter the event name, date, type, and description.
3. Enter sponsor information: Enter the sponsor's name, title, organization, address, phone number, and email.
4. Confirm your application: Check the "Confirmation" box to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
5. Submit your application: Select all the submission options that apply to your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization or affiliation (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the ski event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the ski event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of ski event (Ski, Skiing, Snowboarding, Hiking, Running, or Other).
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Title** (`sponsor_title`, text, required): Enter the sponsor's title.
* **Sponsor Organization** (`sponsor_organization`, text, optional): Enter the sponsor's organization (if applicable).
* **Sponsor Address** (`sponsor_address`, text, optional): Enter the sponsor's address.
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the sponsor's phone number.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, optional): Enter the sponsor's email address.
* **Reason for Sponsorship** (`reason_for_sponsorship`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Budget** (`sponsor_budget`, number, required): Enter the budget for the sponsorship.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, select_one, required): Check the "Confirmation" box to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_multiple, required): Select all the submission options that apply to your event.
* **Confirmation Message** (`confirmation_message`, text, optional): Enter a confirmation message (if applicable).

Note: The confirmation field has been removed to avoid duplication.
