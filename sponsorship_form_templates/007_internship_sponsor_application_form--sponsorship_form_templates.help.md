# Internship Sponsor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Sponsor Application Form is used to collect information from sponsors who are supporting an internship program. This form will be used to track and manage sponsorships and their associated details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Sponsor Name field with the name of your organization or company.
2.  Enter a valid email address in the Email field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
4.  Provide the name of your company in the Company field.
5.  Specify your role in the Role field.
6.  Enter your address in the Address field.
7.  Enter your city in the City field.
8.  Specify your state in the State field.
9.  Enter your zip code in the Zip field.
10.  Select your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
11.  Enter the Start Date of your internship in the Start Date field.
12.  Enter the End Date of your internship in the End Date field.
13.  Choose the Type of Application from the dropdown menu.
14.  Select the Application Status from the dropdown menu.
15.  Enter any assigned tool or equipment in the Assigned Tool field.
16.  Provide any additional notes in the Note field.
17.  Specify the Category of your internship.
18.  Enter the Form ID.
19.  Enter the Form Title.
20.  Enter the Form Category.
21.  Enter the Form Version.
22.  Enter the Form Description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *Sponsor Name* (`sponsor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization or company.
*   *Email* (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
*   *Phone* (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   *Company* (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
*   *Role* (`role`, text, required): Specify your role in the company.
*   *Address* (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
*   *City* (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you're located.
*   *State* (`state`, text, required): Specify your state.
*   *Zip* (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
*   *Date of Birth* (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Select your date of birth.
*   *Start Date* (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your internship.
*   *End Date* (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your internship.
*   *Type of Application* (`application_type`, select_one, required): Choose from the options provided.
*   *Application Status* (`application_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose from the options provided.
*   *Assigned Tool* (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any assigned tool or equipment.
*   *Note* (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes.
*   *Category* (`category`, text, optional): Specify the category of your internship.
*   *Form ID* (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter the form ID.
*   *Form Title* (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the form title.
*   *Form Category* (`form_category`, text, optional): Enter the form category.
*   *Form Version* (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the form version.
*   *Form Description* (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter the form description.
*   *Form Assigned Tool* (`form_assignded_tool`, text, optional): Enter the form assigned tool.
