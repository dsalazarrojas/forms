# event_sponsorship_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about event sponsorships, allowing event organizers to provide accurate and complete data for sponsors and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Start by filling out the **Event Details** page with the necessary information about the event you're hosting or participating in.
2.  Move on to the **Sponsor Info** page and select the correct options for your sponsorship status and relationship.
3.  On the **Contact Info** page, provide details about your organization or team, including your sponsorship amount and other relevant information.
4.  Proceed to the **Event Start Date** and **Event End Date** pages to fill in the start and end dates of your event.
5.  Next, fill out the **Sponsorship Start Date** page with the start date of your sponsorship.
6.  On the **Event Sponsorship Details** page, provide any additional details about your event sponsorship.
7.  Finally, review the **Sponsorship Status** page to confirm your sponsorship status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, optional): This page is where you'll provide general information about the event you're hosting or participating in. You can add any relevant details that will help sponsors understand your event.
* **Sponsor Info** (`sponsor_info`, select_one, optional): Here, you'll select the correct options for your sponsorship status and relationship. Please choose 'Yes' if you're accepting sponsorship, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, optional): On this page, you'll provide details about your organization or team, including your sponsorship amount and other relevant information. You can choose 'Yes' if you have contact information, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of sponsorship you're seeking or providing.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, optional): Fill in the start date of your event.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your event.
* **Sponsorship Start Date** (`sponsorship_start_date`, date, optional): Provide the start date of your sponsorship.
* **Event Sponsorship Details** (`event_sponsorship_details`, note, optional): Add any extra details about your event sponsorship here.
* **Sponsor Relationship** (`sponsor_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a sponsor relationship, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Sponsorship Status** (`sponsorship_status`, select_multiple, optional): Here, you'll select your sponsorship status (Active or Inactive).
