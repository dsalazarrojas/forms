## Sponsorship Benefit Clarification Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you clarify the benefits of a sponsorship opportunity. By completing this form, you will be able to provide more information about your event and team, which will help us understand the benefits you are hoping to gain from our sponsorship. Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure your request is reviewed properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all fields are filled out correctly and completely.
2.  Review the form to ensure that you are providing the correct information.
3.  Submit the form to us for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Sponsorship Benefit Clarification Request Form** (`sponsorship_benefit_clarification_request_form`): This is the main form title.
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`): Please provide the name of the event you are seeking sponsorship for. This is a required field.
*   **Team Name** (`team_name`): Please provide the name of the team associated with the event. This is a required field.
*   **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`): Please indicate whether or not we are the sponsor. This field is optional.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`): Please provide the date of the event. This field is optional, but we require a date in a valid format.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Please provide a valid email address we can use to contact you about your request. This field is optional.
    Please note that we will not be able to reply to your request if this field is not correctly filled out.
