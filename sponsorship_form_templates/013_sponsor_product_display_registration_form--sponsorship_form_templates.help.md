# Sponsor Product Display Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for sponsors to register their products for display on a platform or website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your sponsor information:
	* Sponsor Product: Enter a brief description of your sponsor product.
	* Email: Enter a valid email address for the sponsor.
	* Phone Number: Enter the phone number of the sponsor.
2. Enter product details:
	* Product Name: Enter the name of the product.
	* Product Type: Enter the type of product (e.g., software, hardware, service).
	* Product URL: Enter a valid URL for the product (e.g., website or product page).
	* Social Media Links: Enter any social media links associated with the product.
3. Upload any relevant product images or videos:
	* Product Images: Upload any images of the product.
	* Product Video URL: Enter a valid YouTube URL for a product video.
4. Provide additional details about the product:
	* Product Description: Enter a brief description of the product.
	* Sponsor Details: Enter any additional details about the sponsor.
5. Review and submit the form:
	* Review all the information entered to ensure accuracy and completeness.
	* Click the "Submit" button to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sponsor Product** (`sponsor_product_display_registration_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your sponsor product.
- **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, required/optional): Enter details about the product, including name, type, URL, and social media links.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for the sponsor.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the sponsor.
- **Product Images** (`product_images`, text, required/optional): Upload any images of the product.
- **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the product.
- **Sponsor Details** (`sponsor_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the sponsor.
- **Product Video URL** (`product_video_url`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid YouTube URL for a product video.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the product.
- **Product Type** (`product_type`, text, required/optional): Enter the type of product (e.g., software, hardware, service).
- **Product URL** (`product_url`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid URL for the product.
- **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, text, required/optional): Enter any social media links associated with the product.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Review and click the "Submit" button to complete the registration.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use valid email addresses and URLs.
* Upload relevant and clear product images and videos.
* Enter any additional information that might be helpful for the sponsor and product.
