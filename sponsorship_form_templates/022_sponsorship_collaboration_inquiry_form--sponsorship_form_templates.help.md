# Sponsorship Collaboration Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for organizations and individuals interested in collaborating or sponsoring our events or initiatives. It helps us understand your needs, interests, and requirements, allowing us to tailor our collaboration or sponsorship proposal accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely with the required information (marked as "Required" in the field explanation).
- Ensure that your email address is valid and your phone number is correctly formatted.
- If you're unsure about a field, please leave it out or ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name or organization name.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company website URL (if applicable).
* **Collaboration Type** (`collaboration_type`, select_one, required): Choose from the two options:
	+ **Partnership Inquiry**: You're interested in exploring partnership opportunities with us.
	+ **Sponsorship Inquiry**: You're interested in sponsoring our events or initiatives.
* **Collaboration Message** (`collaboration_message`, text, optional): Provide a brief message about your collaboration or sponsorship interests.
* **Company Representative Name** (`company_representative_name`, text, optional): If you're representing a company, enter the name of the representative.
* **Company Representative Title** (`company_representative_title`, text, optional): If you're representing a company, enter the title of the representative.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise in your message.
- If you're representing a company, please provide all required information for your representative.
- We'll review your inquiry and get back to you as soon as possible.
