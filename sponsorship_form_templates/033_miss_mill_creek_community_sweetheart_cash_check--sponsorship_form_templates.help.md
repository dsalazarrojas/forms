# miss_mill_creek_community_sweetheart_cash_check - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "miss_mill_creek_community_sweetheart_cash_check" form is intended to collect information about cash checks received from sponsors within the Mill Creek community. This form helps the community to accurately track and manage the sponsorships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information about your cash check before filling out the form.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you're entering accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contest Name
* **Contest Name** (required): Enter the name of the contest or event associated with your cash check.

### Cash Amount
* **Cash Amount** (required): Enter the total cash amount of the check, in dollars and cents. This includes any additional fees or charges associated with the check.

### Check Number
* **Check Number** (required): Enter the number on the check that corresponds to your cash contribution. This helps track your check in case there are any issues or questions about payment.

### Check Date
* **Check Date** (required): Enter the date the check was received. This helps ensure accurate tracking of sponsorships and payments.

### Check Amount
* **Check Amount** (required): Enter the exact amount of the check, including any fees or charges. This ensures accurate accounting and distribution of funds.

### Sponsor Name
* **Sponsor Name** (required): Enter the name of the sponsor or organization making the cash contribution. This helps build relationships and recognize the sponsor's support.

### Sponsor Address
* **Sponsor Address** (required): Enter the address of the sponsor or organization making the contribution. This information will help with communication and follow-up.

### Sponsor Phone
* **Sponsor Phone** (required): Enter the phone number of the sponsor or organization making the contribution. This ensures that we can contact the sponsor if needed.

### Sponsor Email
* **Sponsor Email** (optional): Enter the email address of the sponsor or organization making the contribution. This helps with communication, but it's not required.

### Sponsor Check Number
* **Sponsor Check Number** (optional): Enter the number on the check associated with this sponsorship. This helps track any issues or questions about payment.

### Sponsor Check Date
* **Sponsor Check Date** (optional): Enter the date the sponsor's check was received. This helps with tracking and accounting.

### Sponsor Check Amount
* **Sponsor Check Amount** (optional): Enter the exact amount of the sponsor's check, including any fees or charges. This ensures accurate accounting and distribution of funds.

### Other Sponsorships
* **Other Sponsorships** (optional): If you have received multiple sponsorships for this contest, select all that apply from the list.
    - 1st place
    - 2nd place
    - 3rd place

### Notes
* **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about this sponsorship.

### Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (required): Select the person within the community who should be responsible for tracking and managing this sponsorship. This helps with distribution and follow-up.

### Form ID
* **Form ID** (required): Enter a unique identifier for this form. This helps with tracking and managing multiple forms.

### Form Version
* **Form Version** (required): Enter the version of this form. This ensures consistency and updates across different versions.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you're entering the correct information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the community for support.
