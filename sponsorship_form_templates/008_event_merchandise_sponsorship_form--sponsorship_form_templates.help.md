# Event Merchandise Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Merchandise Sponsorship Form is designed to collect information from potential event merchandise sponsors. This form is used to gather contact and sponsorship details from interested parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Sponsor Information" field with your name and any relevant identification.
2. Provide your "Contact Information" in the next field, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Describe your "Sponsorship Details" in the subsequent field, including the type of sponsorship you're interested in and any specific requirements.
4. If applicable, fill out the "Payment Terms" field to specify any payment conditions or requirements.
5. If necessary, review and agree to the "Review Terms" field to acknowledge any terms or conditions.
6. Finally, review your information and click "Review and Submit" to submit your sponsorship application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Information** (Sponsor Information, text, required/optional): Fill out your name and any relevant identification in this field.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required/optional): Provide your name, email, and phone number in this field. This information will be used for communication purposes only.
* **Sponsorship Details** (Sponsorship Details, text, required/optional): Describe the type of sponsorship you're interested in, including any specific requirements or conditions.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, text, required/optional): If applicable, specify any payment conditions or requirements for your sponsorship.
* **Review Terms** (Review Terms, text, required/optional): If necessary, review and agree to any terms or conditions related to your sponsorship.
* **Review and Submit** (Review and Submit, text, required/optional): Review your information carefully and submit your sponsorship application for review.
