# Sponsorship Kiosk Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Kiosk Booking Form is used to book a sponsorship kiosk at a preferred location, providing essential information about the organization and booking details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Organization Details section with your organization's name, address, and contact information.
2. Choose your preferred kiosk location from the options provided.
3. Select a date for your kiosk booking.
4. Select a time for your kiosk booking.
5. Agree to the terms of service.
6. Enter any additional contact information and organization details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Organization Details
* **Organization Name** (`org_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your organization's name.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, Optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, Optional): Enter the address of your organization.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, Optional): Enter the city of your organization.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, Optional): Enter the state of your organization.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, Optional): Enter the zip code of your organization.
* **Organization Name** (`org_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your organization's name.
* **Organization Website** (`org_website`, `text`, Optional): Enter your organization's website.
* **Organization Phone** (`org_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Organization Email** (`org_email`, `text`, Optional): Enter your organization's email.

### Preferred Kiosk Location and Details
* **Preferred Kiosk Location** (`preferred_kiosk_location`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose your preferred kiosk location from the options provided.
* **Preferred Kiosk Date** (`preferred_kiosk_date`, `date`, Optional): Select a date for your kiosk booking.
* **Preferred Kiosk Time** (`preferred_kiosk_time`, `time`, Optional): Select a time for your kiosk booking.

### Booking Details
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, `date`, Optional): Select a date for your kiosk booking.
* **Booking Time** (`booking_time`, `time`, Optional): Select a time for your kiosk booking.

### Terms Agreement
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, `note`, Optional): Agree to the terms of service.

### Additional Contact Information
* **Email 2** (`email2`, `email`, Optional): Enter an additional email for your organization.
* **Phone 2** (`phone2`, `text`, Optional): Enter an additional phone number for your organization.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- If you encounter any issues, contact the administration for assistance.
