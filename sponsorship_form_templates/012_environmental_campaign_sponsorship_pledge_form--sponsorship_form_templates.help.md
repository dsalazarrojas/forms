# Environmental Campaign Sponsorship Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who want to support an environmental campaign by making a pledge. The information collected will be used to contact you and confirm your pledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Provide your Email address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your Contact Phone number in the `Contact Phone` field.
5. Type in the name of your Organization (if applicable).
6. Enter the name of the environmental Campaign you wish to support.
7. Provide a brief description of the campaign you're supporting.
8. Specify the amount of money you're willing to pledge in the `Amount Pledges` field.
9. Select the currency of your pledge from the `Amount Currency` field.
10. Choose how often you'd like to make your pledge in the `Frequency Pledges` field.
11. Select the start date of your pledge in the `Pledge Start Date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the first name of the person filling out this form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the last name of the person filling out this form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is the email address of the person filling out this form.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): This is the contact phone number of the person filling out this form.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): This is the organization (if applicable) that the person filling out this form represents.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the name of the environmental campaign you wish to support.
* **Campaign Description** (`campaign_description`, `text`, required: false): This is a brief description of the environmental campaign you're supporting.
* **Amount Pledges** (`amount_pledges`, `number`, required: false): This is the amount of money you're willing to pledge for the campaign.
* **Amount Currency** (`amount_currency`, `text`, required: false): This is the currency of your pledge.
* **Frequency Pledges** (`frequency_pledges`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This option allows you to specify how often you'd like to make your pledge: Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly.
* **Pledge Start Date** (`pledge_start_date`, `date`, required: false): This is the start date of your pledge.

## Tips
* Please fill out one form per person.
* Ensure you've given the person filling out this form permission to do so if they're not the actual sponsor.
