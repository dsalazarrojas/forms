# Sponsor Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sponsor Information Form is a form designed to collect information from sponsors, which is crucial for various business and organizational purposes. This form helps in gathering essential details about the sponsor, including their name, title, organization, address, city, state, and zip code. The information collected will help in maintaining accurate records and facilitating communication with the sponsor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Sponsor First Name in the first field.
2. Enter the Sponsor Last Name in the second field.
3. Enter the Sponsor Title in the third field.
4. Enter the Sponsor Organization in the fourth field.
5. Enter the Sponsor Address in the fifth field.
6. Enter the Sponsor City in the sixth field.
7. Enter the Sponsor State in the seventh field.
8. Enter the Sponsor Zip in the eighth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor First Name** (`sponsor_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Last Name** (`sponsor_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Title** (`sponsor_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title or position of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Organization** (`sponsor_organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the sponsor's organization.
* **Sponsor Address** (`sponsor_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the sponsor's office or location.
* **Sponsor City** (`sponsor_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where the sponsor's office or location is situated.
* **Sponsor State** (`sponsor_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state where the sponsor's office or location is situated.
* **Sponsor Zip** (`sponsor_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the sponsor's office or location.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter for the sponsor.
- Ensure that the information entered is up-to-date and relevant.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can contact us for assistance.
- The information collected will be used for official purposes only.
