# Event Sponsorship Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from companies interested in sponsoring events organized by the event organizers. It ensures that the company's contact information is shared with the event sponsors only if the company agrees to do so.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (event sponsorship request form agreement, event sponsorship request form additional info).
2. The other fields are optional and can be filled in or left blank as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`): Enter your company's address.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`): Enter your company's email address.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event you are interested in sponsoring.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Sponsorship Request Form Description** (`event_sponsorship_request_form_description`): Provide a brief description of your request for sponsorship.
* **Event Sponsorship Request Form Agreement** (`event_sponsorship_request_form_agreement`): Select "I agree" if you allow the event organizers to share your contact information with the event sponsors.
* **Event Sponsorship Request Form Additional Info** (`event_sponsorship_request_form_additional_info`): Select "I am willing to provide additional information about my company" if you have more details to share with the event sponsors.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact the event organizers for clarification.
* The event organizers will review your request and follow up with you regarding the sponsorship status.
