# Debate Tournament Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Debate Tournament Sponsorship Form is used to collect sponsorship information from potential sponsors to support a debate tournament.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title to identify yourself as the sponsor.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about the sponsorship.
3. Provide your organization's name to show your affiliation.
4. Enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
5. Provide your address for communication and potential delivery purposes.
6. Specify the payment terms for the sponsorship.
7. Choose your preferred payment method.
8. Enter the amount of the sponsorship.
9. If necessary, add any additional terms or conditions for the sponsorship.
10. Sign and date the agreement to confirm the sponsorship.
11. Sign and date the signature field to confirm your commitment to the sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Name** (text): Enter your name as the sponsor of the debate tournament.
* **Sponsor Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the sponsorship.
* **Organization Name** (text): Enter your organization's name to show your affiliation.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Enter your contact phone number for us to reach out to you if needed.
* **Address** (note): Enter your address for communication and potential delivery purposes.
* **Payment Terms** (text): Specify the payment terms for the sponsorship.
* **Payment Method** (select_one): Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Check, Online Transfer).
* **Sponsorship Amount** (number): Enter the amount of the sponsorship.
* **Additional Terms** (text): If necessary, add any additional terms or conditions for the sponsorship.
* **Agreement Signed** (date): Sign and date the agreement to confirm the sponsorship.
* **Signature Date** (date): Sign and date the signature field to confirm your commitment to the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Signature** (note): Sign your name to confirm the sponsorship.

## Tips

* Be sure to clearly specify your sponsorship amount and terms.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure we can reach out to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
