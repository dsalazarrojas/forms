# Community Event Sponsorship Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for organizations that want to request sponsorship for a community event. You can use it to provide your organization's contact information, event details, and sponsorship requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out your name, email, and event details on page 1.
*   Choose your preferred sponsorship level and contribution type on page 1.
*   Enter any additional details about your event on page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Sponsor name**: This field is for your organization's name, company or individual name.
*   **Contact person full name**: This field is for the primary contact person's full name at your organization.
*   **Contact email**: This field is for your primary contact email address. Please use a valid email address as it will be used for sponsorship agreements.
*   **Preferred sponsorship level**: This field allows you to choose one of the available sponsorship packages: Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, or Custom.
*   **Contribution type**: This field lets you choose the type of contribution you are offering: Monetary, In kind, Services, or Combination.
*   **Amount or description of in kind support**: This field is for entering a dollar amount if you are requesting monetary support, or describing the item or service you are offering as in kind support.
*   **Recognition preferences**: This field is for specifying how you would like to be acknowledged as a sponsor, if at all.
*   **Agreement summary**: This field is for any special terms or conditions you would like to include in the sponsorship agreement.
*   **Authorized signature name**: This field is for the name of the person signing on behalf of your organization.
*   **Authorized signature**: This field is where you should sign to authenticate your request.
