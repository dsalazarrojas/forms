# vendor_sponsorship_options - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect sponsorship options from vendors, helping us to better understand the terms and conditions of our partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and requirements.
2. Enter the title of the sponsorship option.
3. Provide details about the sponsorship option on the "additional_details" page.
4. If any other relevant information is required, please enter it on the "example_field_3" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a concise and descriptive title of the sponsorship option.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the sponsorship option, including any specific terms, conditions, or requirements.
* **Example Field 3** (`example_field_3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any other relevant information regarding the sponsorship option.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your sponsorship agreement or contact the vendor for clarification.
* Double-check your submission before submitting it, as this form is used to inform important business decisions.
