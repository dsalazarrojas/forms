# Request For Student And School Resources Funding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a student's or school's financial situation and funding needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Request for Student and School Resources Funding Form` field with a brief description of your request.
2. Select the `Enrollment` field and enter the number of students or schools you are requesting funding for.
3. If you are requesting funding for students, select the number of students in the `Student Age Range` field. If you are requesting funding for teachers, select the number of teachers in the `Teacher Age Range` field.
4. Indicate the `Enrollment Status` and `Funding Status` of the students or schools you are requesting funding for.
5. Describe the `Financial Situation` in the `Financial Situation` field, including any relevant details.
6. Enter the total amount of funding you are requesting in the `Total Funding Requested` field.
7. If applicable, select any financial aid options you are requesting in the `Financial Aid` field.
8. Provide any additional information about your financial situation in the `Financial Situation Details` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request for Student and School Resources Funding Form**: A brief description of your request.
* **Enrollment**: The number of students or schools you are requesting funding for. 
* **Student Funding**: The amount of funding you are requesting for students.
* **School Funding**: A brief description of the funding you are requesting for schools.
* **Enrollment Type**: If you are a student, select "Student". If you are a teacher, select "Teacher".
* **Student Age Range**: The age range of the students you are requesting funding for.
* **Teacher Age Range**: The age range of the teachers you are requesting funding for, only applicable if you are a school requesting funding.
* **Enrollment Status**: Indicate if you are currently enrolled or not enrolled with the student/schools you are requesting funding for.
* **Funding Status**: Indicate if you are fully, partially, or not funded with the student/schools you are requesting funding for.
* **Financial Situation**: A description of your financial situation, including any relevant details.
* **Total Funding Requested**: The total amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Financial Aid**: If you are requesting financial aid, select "Full" or "Partial".
* **Financial Situation Details**: Any additional information about your financial situation.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing information, especially in the Financial Situation Details field.
* Ensure that you are filling this form only once per student or school.
* If you are a student, please provide your student ID number if available. 
* If you are a school, please provide your school ID number if available. 

Note: The following fields are not required but will help us better understand your situation: Financial Situation, Financial Situation Details. These fields are not required but highly recommended to be filled if possible.
