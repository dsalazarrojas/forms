# Baseball Fundraising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baseball Fundraising Form is a digital form designed to collect donations from individuals and businesses supporting a local baseball team. This form is used to capture essential information for processing and acknowledging sponsorships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Provide your sponsorship name (individual or business) in the "Sponsor Name" field.
* Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field for receipt and tax information purposes.
* If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Contact Phone" field (optional).
* Select the type of sponsorship you are providing in the "Sponsorship Type" dropdown menu.
* Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donation Amount" field.
* If you are sponsoring a specific player, enter their name in the "Sponsored Player Name" field; otherwise, leave it blank for a general team donation.
* Choose your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment Method" dropdown menu.
* If you have a message for the team or player, enter it in the "Message for the Team or Player" field (optional).
* Decide if you would like your donation to remain anonymous.
* Specify if you require a tax-deductible receipt.
* Finally, indicate the date of your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **-- Support Our Baseball Team --**
This section serves as an introduction to the form and its purpose.
### 2. **Sponsor Name (Individual or Business)**
Enter your name or business name to be associated with this sponsorship.
### 3. **Contact Email**
Enter your email address to receive receipts and tax information.
### 4. **Contact Phone**
Enter your phone number for additional contact information (optional).
### 5. **Sponsorship Type**
Select the type of sponsorship you are providing:
	* Fan (Individual)
	* Family Sponsor
	* Local Business
	* Corporate Partner
### 6. **Donation Amount ($)** 
Enter the amount you wish to contribute to the team.
### 7. **Sponsored Player Name**
If you are sponsoring a specific player, enter their name here; otherwise, leave blank for a general team donation.
### 8. **Preferred Payment Method**
Choose your preferred payment method:
	* Credit/Debit Card
	* PayPal
	* Venmo
	* Check
	* Cash
### 9. **Message for the Team or Player**
Enter a message for the team or player (optional).
### 10. **Would you like your donation to remain anonymous?**
Select 'True' if you prefer your donation to be anonymous.
### 11. **Do you require a tax-deductible receipt?**
Select 'True' if you require a tax-deductible receipt.
### 12. **Date of Donation**
Indicate the date of your donation.
