# Sponsorship And Resource Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from organizations that want to provide sponsorship and resources to a project. The form helps project managers and coordinators track and manage the sponsorships and resources received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's details, including your name and contact information.
2. Describe the sponsorship you're providing, including the type and any specific details.
3. Check the types of resources you're providing, such as food, shelter, medical supplies, or tools.
4. If necessary, provide any additional information about your sponsorship.
5. Review your answers carefully and submit the form to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Details** (page 1): Enter your organization's name and contact information to identify your sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Details** (page 1): Describe the sponsorship you're providing, including the type and any specific details.
* **In-Kind Resources** (page 2): Select the types of resources you're providing, such as food, shelter, medical supplies, or tools.
* **Additional Info** (page 2): If necessary, provide any additional information about your sponsorship.
* **Submit** (page 3): Review your answers carefully and submit the form to confirm your registration.
* **Confirm** (page 3): Confirm that you've reviewed and submitted your registration accurately.
