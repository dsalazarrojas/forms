## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization that is sponsoring the event.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person representing the organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person representing the organization.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person representing the organization.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of sponsorship requested.
* **Sponsorship Frequency** (`sponsorship_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of sponsorship (e.g., once for a specific event, ongoing support).
* **Sponsorship Type** (`sponsorship_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of sponsorship (e.g., general, in-kind donations, both).
* **Sponsorship Start Date** (`sponsorship_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship End Date** (`sponsorship_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Details** (`sponsor_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the sponsorship.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the sponsorship.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the assigned tool for the sponsorship.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the sponsorship.
* **Modified By** (`modified_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who modified the sponsorship.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, time, optional): Enter the date and time when the sponsorship was created.
* **Modified At** (`modified_at`, time, optional): Enter the date and time when the sponsorship was modified.
