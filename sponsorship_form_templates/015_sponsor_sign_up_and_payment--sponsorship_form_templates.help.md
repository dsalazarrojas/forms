# Sponsor Sign Up And Payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Sign Up And Payment form is designed to collect information from sponsors who wish to participate in sponsorship-related activities. The form is intended for sponsors to provide their details, including name, organization, role, email, phone number, payment amount, payment terms, sponsor type, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Sponsor Sign Up And Payment" form to begin.
- Fill in the required fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (text): Enter your name as the sponsor.
- **Organization** (text): Enter the name of the organization you are associated with.
- **Role** (select_one): Select "Yes" if you have a role in the organization, "No" otherwise.
- **Email** (email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Payment Amount** (number): Enter the amount you wish to pay for the sponsorship.
- **Payment Terms** (text): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
- **Sponsor Type** (select_multiple): Select multiple sponsor types as applicable.
- **Sponsor Details** (note): Enter any additional details about your sponsorship.
- **Payment Method** (select_one): Select "Yes" if you will be making a payment, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately to avoid errors.
- Review your information before submitting the form.
