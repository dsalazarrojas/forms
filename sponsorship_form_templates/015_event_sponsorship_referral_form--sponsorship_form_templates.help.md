# event_sponsorship_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event_sponsorship_referral_form is a tool used by event organizers to collect sponsorship information from potential sponsors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
2. Enter your name and contact details, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
3. Choose your interests from the list provided, ensuring they align with your sponsorship goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your organization or company. 
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `note`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your contact information, including any relevant details about your organization or company.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Sponsor Interests** (`sponsor_interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your interests from the provided options, which include Event, Cause, and Cause Related. Please choose all applicable interests that align with your sponsorship goals.
