# Event Sponsorship Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for event sponsors to renew their sponsorship agreement with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the purpose of this form.
2. Ensure that you are an authorized representative of the sponsor.
3. Carefully fill out the form with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Information**
Event Sponsorship Renewal Form
This field is for you to provide some general information about the event you are sponsoring.

* **Event Information**: Enter a brief description of the event you are sponsoring.
* **Required:** No

### 2. **Sponsor Information**
Sponsor Information
Please provide some general information about your organization as a sponsor.

* **Sponsor Information**: Enter a brief description of your organization.
* **Required:** No

### 3. **Contact Information**
Contact Information
This field is for you to provide contact information for your organization.

* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information (name, email, phone number, etc.) for your organization.
* **Required:** No

### 4. **Event Details**
Event Details
This field is for you to provide more detailed information about the event you are sponsoring.

* **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event you are sponsoring.
* **Required:** No

### 5. **Agreement**
Agreement
Please select one of the agreement options.

* **Agreement**: Please choose the agreement option that best suits your needs.
* **Required:** No

### 6. **Terms and Conditions**
Terms and Conditions
This field is a note field for you to read and acknowledge our terms and conditions.

* **Terms and Conditions**: Please read and acknowledge our terms and conditions by signing this field.
* **Required:** No

### 7. **Sponsor Details**
Sponsor Details
This field is for you to provide more detailed information about your organization as a sponsor.

* **Sponsor Details**: Enter a brief description of your organization.
* **Required:** No

### 8. **Event Sponsorship**
Event Sponsorship
This field is for you to provide more detailed information about the sponsorship.

* **Event Sponsorship**: Enter a brief description of the sponsorship.
* **Required:** No

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Make sure to read and understand the terms and conditions before proceeding.
