# Pediatric Research Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Research Sponsorship Form is used to collect information about a pediatric research project, including the organization sponsoring the project, the contact person, and details about the project such as its description, budget, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the organization sponsoring the project in the "Organization" field.
2. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the project in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Enter a brief description of the project in the "Project Description" field.
6. Enter the budget for the project in the "Project Budget" field.
7. Enter the amount of funding requested for the project in the "Funding Request" field.
8. Select the type of sponsorship being requested (Researcher, Institution, or Foundation) in the "Research Sponsorship" field.
9. Select the current status of the project (Completed, In Progress, or Pending) in the "Project Status" field.
10. Enter the date the project was created in the "Date Created" field.
11. Enter the date the project was last updated in the "Date Updated" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization (Organization)**: Enter the name of the organization sponsoring the project.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person)**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the project.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Project Description (Project Description)**: Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Project Budget (Project Budget)**: Enter the budget for the project.
* **Funding Request (Funding Request)**: Enter the amount of funding requested for the project.
* **Research Sponsorship (Research Sponsorship)**: Select the type of sponsorship being requested (Researcher, Institution, or Foundation).
* **Project Status (Project Status)**: Select the current status of the project (Completed, In Progress, or Pending).
* **Date Created (Date Created)**: Enter the date the project was created.
* **Date Updated (Date Updated)**: Enter the date the project was last updated.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the project is not a duplicate submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form, please contact us.
