# Event Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Event Sponsorship Application Form. Please read through each section carefully to ensure you provide the required information for your event sponsorship application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide a brief description of your event, including its name, date, time, and location.
3. If you have a credit card, please fill in the credit card number for payment purposes.
4. Once you've filled in all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details**: This section is where you'll provide your contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that the details are accurate and up-to-date.

### Credit Card
* **Credit Card**: If you have a credit card for payment purposes, please fill in the credit card number, but note that this field is not required. We encourage you to fill this in only if you're paying through a credit card.

### Event Info
* **Event Info**: This section is for describing your event, including its name, date, time, and location. Please be concise and provide as much information as you need to make your event stand out.

### Submit
* **Submit**: Once you've filled in all the required fields, click this button to submit your application. Please review your application before submitting to ensure it's accurate and complete. 

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your application before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
* If you're not sure about any part of the form, please don't hesitate to contact us at the provided email address.
* We appreciate your application, and we're looking forward to reviewing it.
