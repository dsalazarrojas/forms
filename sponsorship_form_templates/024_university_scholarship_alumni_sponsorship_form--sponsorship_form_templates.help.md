# university_scholarship_alumni_sponsorship_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Scholarship Alumni Sponsorship Form is designed for alumni or university representatives to provide financial support for scholarships to students. This form helps in collecting essential information required for sponsorship commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Enter your general information, including your name, and any other relevant details.
3. Provide your contact information, such as email and phone number.
4. Specify the commitment amount and frequency you're willing to sponsor.
5. Choose the payment method you'd like to use for the sponsorship.
6. Select the donation frequency that suits your schedule.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about your sponsorship.
8. Confirm your sponsor's name, title, and contact information.
9. Specify the university and department related to the sponsorship.
10. Choose the team or group you're representing.
11. Set a follow-up date and method for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (1) (`text`, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (2) (`text`, optional): Provide your contact details, such as email and phone number, to facilitate communication.
* **Commitment Amount** (3) (`number`, optional): Specify the amount you're willing to sponsor.
* **Commitment Frequency** (4) (`select_one`, optional): Choose how often you'd like to sponsor, e.g., monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Payment Method** (5) (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the payment method you'd like to use, e.g., cash, check, or credit/debit card.
* **Donation Frequency** (6) (`select_one`, optional): Choose how frequently you'd like to make donations, e.g., monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Email** (7) (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (8) (`text`, optional): Provide your phone number for additional contact.
* **Address** (9) (`text`, optional): Enter your address for any mail or package delivery.
* **Comments** (10) (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your sponsorship.
* **Scholarship Fund** (11) (`number`, optional): Enter the specific amount of the scholarship fund.
* **Scholarship Type** (12) (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of scholarship you'd like to sponsor, e.g., academic or financial.
* **Sponsor Name** (13) (`text`, optional): Enter your name as the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Title** (14) (`text`, optional): Enter your title or position.
* **Sponsor Company** (15) (`text`, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Sponsor Contact** (16) (`text`, optional): Enter your contact information as the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email** (17) (`email`, optional): Enter your email address as the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone** (18) (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number as the sponsor.
* **University** (19) (`text`, optional): Enter the university related to the sponsorship.
* **Department** (20) (`text`, optional): Enter the department related to the sponsorship.
* **Team** (21) (`text`, optional): Enter the team or group you're representing.
* **Follow-up** (22) (`date`, optional): Set a date for follow-up communication.
* **Follow-up Method** (23) (`select_one`, optional): Choose the method for follow-up communication, e.g., email, phone, or mail.
* **Additional Comments** (24) (`note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your sponsorship.
