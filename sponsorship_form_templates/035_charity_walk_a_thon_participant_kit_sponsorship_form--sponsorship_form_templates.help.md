# Charity Walk A Thon Participant Kit Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain sponsorship commitments and support for participants in a charity walk-a-thon event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the sponsor's contact information in the Sponsor Information section.
2. Provide the walk-a-thon event details in the Walk-a-Thon Details section.
3. Specify the participant's name and the type of kit sponsorship in the Kit Sponsorship section.
4. Choose the level of sponsorship commitment and the sponsorship amount.
5. Select the preferred recognition method and provide your logo for recognition materials.
6. Choose your communication preferences, such as receiving event updates or phone communication.
7. Confirm the payment terms and the expected payment due date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter your name or company name as the sponsor.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the primary contact person's name.
* **Email Address**: Enter the primary email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Organization**: Enter the sponsor organization's name (optional).
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the charity walk-a-thon event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the walk-a-thon event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the walk-a-thon event.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant you are sponsoring.
* **Type of Kit Sponsorship**: Select the type of kit sponsorship you are committing to (e.g., T-Shirt, Bib Number, etc.).
* **Number of Kits**: Specify how many participant kits you are sponsoring.
* **Sponsorship Tier**: Choose the level of sponsorship commitment (e.g., Platinum, Gold, etc.).
* **Sponsorship Amount**: Enter the dollar amount of your sponsorship commitment.
* **Recognition Preferences**: Select how you would like to be recognized (e.g., Logo on Kit, Announcement at Event, etc.).
* **Provide Your Logo**: Choose whether you will provide your logo for recognition materials.
* **Receive Event Updates**: Choose whether you would like to receive updates about the walk-a-thon event.
* **Prefer Phone Communication**: Choose whether you prefer phone communication.
* **Expected Payment Due Date**: Enter the date when payment is expected to be made.
* **Sponsorship Form Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date.

Note: Some fields are optional, but ensure you provide all required fields to complete the form.
