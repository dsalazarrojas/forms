# New Year's Celebration Sponsorship Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect sponsorship information and event details for the New Year's Celebration. It is used to determine if a sponsor is committed to sponsoring the event and what their expectations and contributions are. The form helps to ensure that all sponsors understand their role and responsibilities in the celebration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event details in the "Event Details" field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for sponsor information, payment details, and promotion preferences.
3. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
4. Select your payment method and terms.
5. Enter the payment amount and your sponsor level (High or Low).
6. Add any additional event details and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter the details of the event, including the date, time, and location. This information will help us plan and organize the celebration.
* **Sponsor Information**: Please select "Yes" if you are interested in sponsoring the event. This will help us determine if you are committed to sponsoring the event.
* **Payment Details**: Select "Yes" if you are able to make a payment for the event. This will help us understand your financial commitment.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment you would like to use (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Terms**: Select the terms of payment you are willing to accept (e.g., one-time payment, monthly payment, etc.).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Level**: Select your sponsor level (High or Low) to help us determine your level of commitment.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2024-01-01).
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Sponsor Contact**: Select "Yes" if you are willing to be contacted by the event organizers.
* **Promotion Preferences**: Select "Yes" for any promotion preferences you have (e.g., social media, email, etc.).
* **Event Budget**: Enter any additional event details or budget information.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Sponsor Image**: Upload an image or logo representing your sponsor.
* **Sponsor Logo**: Upload a logo representing your sponsor.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the sponsorship.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Website**: Enter your website so we can learn more about your sponsor.
* **Address**: Enter your address so we can send you information.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment you would like to use.
* **Payment Terms**: Select the terms of payment you are willing to accept.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Level**: Select your sponsor level (High or Low).
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Budget**: Enter any additional event details or budget information.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
