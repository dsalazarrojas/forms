# Event Sponsorship Budget Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to approve or reject a sponsorship budget proposal for an event. Before submitting this form, please ensure that you have a legitimate reason for sponsorship and that the proposal has not been previously submitted or approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field, indicated by \*.
2. For the "event_sponsorship_budget_proposal" field, select one or more proposals from the available options.
3. Enter the detailed description of the sponsorship budget in the "event_sponsorship_budget_details" field.
4. Select the approval status "approved" or "rejected" in the "event_sponsorship_budget_approval" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the proposal in the "event_sponsorship_budget_date" and "event_sponsorship_budget_time" fields.
6. Enter the contact information of the proposer in the "event_sponsorship_budget_email" and "event_sponsorship_budget_phone" fields.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "event_sponsorship_budget_comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **title** (`Event Sponsorship Budget Approval Form`, string): This is the title of the form.
- **event_sponsorship_budget_proposal** (`proposal 1, proposal 2`, select_multiple): Select one or more proposals for the event sponsorship.
- **event_sponsorship_budget_details** (``, string): Enter the detailed description of the sponsorship budget.
- **event_sponsorship_budget_approval** (`approved, rejected`, select_one): Select the approval status of the proposal.
- **event_sponsorship_budget_date** (``, date): Enter the date of the proposal.
- **event_sponsorship_budget_time** (``, time): Enter the time of the proposal.
- **event_sponsorship_budget_email** (``, email): Enter the email of the proposer.
- **event_sponsorship_budget_phone** (``, string): Enter the phone number of the proposer.
- **event_sponsorship_budget_notes** (``, string): Add any additional comments or notes about the proposal.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are not sure about the proposal, please add notes or comments to provide more information.
* This form is meant for internal use, please do not share it with external parties.
