# Warehouse Sponsorship Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their Warehouse Sponsorship Inquiry. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the user's business and specific sponsorship requirements. The form will help us understand and address their needs and provide more relevant information and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "User Input" page and provide your name, email, and other relevant contact information.
2. In the "Business Input" page, enter your business name, type, and location (city, state, or country).
3. On the "Inquiry Details" page, describe your sponsorship inquiry, including the type of sponsorship you are interested in and any specific details or requirements you have.
4. Finally, in the "Additional Details" page, provide any additional information you think would be helpful for us to understand your sponsorship inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name and email address so we can get in touch with you about your sponsorship inquiry.
* **Business Input** (`business name`, `text`, not required): Enter your business name and type (e.g., company, organization, etc.) and its location (city, state, or country).
* **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry description`, `text`, not required): Describe your sponsorship inquiry, including the type of sponsorship you are interested in and any specific requirements or details you have.
* **Additional Details** (`additional details`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional information you think would be helpful for us to understand your sponsorship inquiry.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can assist you better.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
