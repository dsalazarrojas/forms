# Business Conference Keynote Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Conference Keynote Sponsorship Form is designed to collect sponsorship information from potential sponsors, enabling us to evaluate their interest in sponsoring our conference and ensuring they meet the expectations of our event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your organization.
2. Select the sponsorship level that best suits your needs and budget.
3. Provide details about your keynote speaker, including their name, title, and brief bio.
4. Confirm the conference dates and preferred session date.
5. Select the target audience size you expect to attend.
6. Specify your marketing commitment level for the conference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`): Provide the URL of your company's website.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`): Specify your industry or market.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`): Enter the job title or position of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the business email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the business phone number of the contact person.
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`): Select the sponsorship package that suits your needs and budget.
* **Platinum Level Investment** (`platinum_benefits`): Enter the amount for platinum sponsorship (if applicable).
* **Gold Level Investment** (`gold_benefits`): Enter the amount for gold sponsorship (if applicable).
* **Silver Level Investment** (`silver_benefits`): Enter the amount for silver sponsorship (if applicable).
* **Bronze Level Investment** (`bronze_benefits`): Enter the amount for bronze sponsorship (if applicable).
* **Proposed Keynote Speaker** (`speaker_name`): Enter the name of your proposed keynote speaker.
* **Speaker Title and Organization** (`speaker_title`): Enter the speaker's position and company.
* **Speaker Bio and Credentials** (`speaker_bio`): Provide a brief biography of the speaker.
* **Presentation Topic** (`presentation_topic`): Enter the title or topic of the presentation.
* **Conference Dates** (`conference_dates`): Enter the event dates.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`session_date`): Choose a preferred date for the keynote presentation.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`): Select the length of the keynote presentation.
* **Target Audience Size** (`target_audience`): Enter the expected attendance size.
* **Marketing Commitment** (`marketing_commitment`): Specify your marketing commitment level for the conference.
