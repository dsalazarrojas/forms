# Comic Convention Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential sponsors for the comic convention. We use this information to understand their company information, sponsorship level interest, marketing goals, and payment details. This helps us tailor our sponsorship opportunities to their needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company name and contact person's name, title, and email address. This information will help us reach out to you.
2. Choose your company type and years in business. This will help us understand your company's background.
3. Describe your company in a brief paragraph. This should include any relevant details about your business.
4. Select your sponsorship tier and previous sponsorship experience.
5. Indicate if you need booth space and select your logo placement options.
6. Express your interest in speaking opportunities and VIP access.
7. Specify your marketing goals and select your preferred logo upload method.
8. Choose your payment method and schedule.
9. Provide your billing address.
10. If necessary, add any special requests or additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's legal name.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the title of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Email Address**: Enter your company's business email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your company's business phone number.
* **Company Website**: Enter the URL of your company's website.
* **Company Type**: Select the type of company you represent (e.g., Publisher, Retailer, Artist/Creator, etc.).
* **Years in Business**: Choose how long your company has been operating.
* **Company Description**: Briefly describe your company in a paragraph.
* **Sponsorship Tier**: Select the level of sponsorship you are interested in (e.g., Bronze, Silver, Gold, etc.).
* **Previous Sponsorship**: Indicate if you have sponsored with us before.
* **Booth Space Required**: Select if you need exhibition space.
* **Logo Placement**: Select where you would like your logo to appear (e.g., website, program book, banners, etc.).
* **Speaking Opportunity**: Indicate if you are interested in participating in a speaking opportunity.
* **VIP Access**: Select if you are interested in VIP event access for your representatives.
* **Marketing Goals**: Select your marketing goals for this sponsorship (e.g., brand awareness, lead generation, etc.).
* **Social Media Handles**: Enter your company's social media handles.
* **Logo Upload**: Upload your high-resolution logo.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer (e.g., credit card, wire transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Schedule**: Choose your preferred payment arrangement (e.g., full payment upfront, partial payment, etc.).
* **Billing Address**: Enter the address for invoices.
* **Special Requests**: Add any special requests or requirements.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Be specific about your company and sponsorship interests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your interest in sponsoring our comic convention and look forward to reviewing your application!
