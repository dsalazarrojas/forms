# Theater Show Sponsor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Show Sponsor Form is designed to gather information from prospective sponsors to support a theater show. This form helps event coordinators and producers collect all necessary details for a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the "Show Details" section with information about the show, including title, date, time, and venue.
- Move on to "Contact Details" to provide your personal or business information, including name, email, phone number, and title.
- In "Sponsorship Details" describe the specific sponsorship opportunities you're interested in and what you're offering.
- Choose your preferred "Payment Method" from the options available.
- In "Payment Terms" provide any conditions, deadlines, or other essential details about payment arrangements.
- Finally, "Sponsorship Agreement" is optional, but provide a description of the expected agreement and any terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Show Details** (Theater Show Sponsor Form - Page 1):
	+ **Title**: Enter the title of the theater show.
	+ **Date**: Enter the date of the show.
	+ **Time**: Enter the time of the show.
	+ **Venue**: Enter the venue where the show will take place.

* **Contact Details** (Theater Show Sponsor Form - Page 1):
	+ **Name**: Enter your name or your company's name.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ **Title**: Enter your title or position.

* **Sponsorship Details** (Theater Show Sponsor Form - Page 1):
	+ **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the sponsorship opportunities you're interested in.

* **Payment Method** (Theater Show Sponsor Form - Page 2):
	+ **Credit Card**: Select this option for credit card payment.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: Select this option for bank transfer payment.
	+ **Check**: Select this option for payment by check.

* **Payment Terms** (Theater Show Sponsor Form - Page 2):
	+ This field should contain any conditions, deadlines, or other essential details about payment arrangements.

* **Sponsorship Agreement** (Theater Show Sponsor Form - Page 3):
	+ Provide a description of the expected agreement and any terms.
