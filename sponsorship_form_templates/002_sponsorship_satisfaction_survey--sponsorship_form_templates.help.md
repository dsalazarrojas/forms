# sponsorship satisfaction survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from sponsors about their experience with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For the "Sponsor Feedback" section, select all the feedback options that apply to your experience.
3. If you have any comments or requests, fill in the "Comments" and "Requests" sections.
4. Fill in your "Contact Info" with the preferred method of communication.
5. Select the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields if you want to be tracked.
6. Fill in the "Submitted On" field with the date you are submitting the form.
7. Fill in the "Submitted By" field with your name or title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsorship Survey Questions**: This section is where you can provide your feedback on your experience with our organization.
	+ (Required: No)
* **Comments**: This is the space to write any comments or additional information you have about your experience.
	+ (Required: No)
* **Requests**: If there are any requests you have for our organization, please list them here.
	+ (Required: No)
* **Contact Info**: Please fill in your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
	+ (Required: No)
* **Sponsor Feedback**: Select all the feedback options that apply to your experience.
	+ (Required: No)
	+ Very Satisfied: I am extremely satisfied with our services.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: I am somewhat satisfied with our services.
	+ Not Satisfied: I am not satisfied with our services.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date you are submitting this form.
	+ (Required: No)
* **Submission Time**: Select the time you are submitting this form.
	+ (Required: No)
* **Submitted On**: Select the date you are submitting this form.
	+ (Required: No)
* **Submitted By**: Please fill in your name or title.
	+ (Required: No)

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "Required: Yes".
