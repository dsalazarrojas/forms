# cultural_event_sponsorship_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from attendees on the best sponsors at a cultural event. The form helps attendees vote for their preferred sponsors and provides organizers with valuable insights on the audience's perception of the event and its sponsors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide event details in the first section.
2. In the second section, select your preferred sponsors from the list of available sponsors.
3. In the remaining sections, provide your contact information and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. event_details (Page 1)
* **event_details** (`event_details`, text, required): This field is a text input for you to provide details about the event you have attended.

### 2. sponsor_details (Page 1)
* **sponsor_details** (`sponsor_details`, text, required): This field is a text input for you to provide details about the sponsors.

### 3. vote (Page 2)
* **vote** (`vote`, select_multiple, required): In this section, please select the sponsors you think performed the best. You can choose multiple sponsors from the list below:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 4. sponsor_info (Page 2)
* **sponsor_info** (`sponsor_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information about the sponsors you selected in the previous section.

### 5. review_sponsorship (Page 3)
* **review_sponsorships** (`review_sponsorship`, text, optional): This field is for your comments on the sponsors you did not select.

### 6. submit (Page 3)
* **submit** (`submit`, text, required): Please enter your name to submit your vote.

### 7. email_address (Page 4)
* **email_address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you if needed.

### 8. name (Page 4)
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for reference.

### 9. organization (Page 4)
* **organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization for reference.

Note: The form is designed to be filled out by attendees who have actually attended the event. If you have already voted, you will not be able to vote again.
