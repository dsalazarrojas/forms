# Equipment Upgrade Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from organizations interested in sponsoring an equipment upgrade for our team. Please fill out this form to provide us with necessary details about the upgrade, and we will review and contact you to discuss further.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
2. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Describe the equipment that you are interested in upgrading in the "Equipment Description" field.
6. Estimate the cost of the upgrade in the "Upgrade Cost" field.
7. Estimate the value of the upgrade in the "Upgrade Value" field.
8. Enter the sponsorship amount that you are willing to donate in the "Sponsorship Amount" field.
9. Select the sponsor(s) from the list in the "Sponsorships" field.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about the upgrade in the "Notes" field.
11. Enter the date for the upgrade in the "Date" field.
12. Enter the time for the upgrade in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Your organization's name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): The name of the contact person from your organization.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): The email of the contact person from your organization.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): The phone number of the contact person from your organization.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, required: false): A detailed description of the equipment that you are interested in upgrading.
* **Upgrade Cost** (`upgrade_cost`, number, required: false): An estimate of the cost of the upgrade.
* **Upgrade Value** (`upgrade_value`, number, required: false): An estimate of the value of the upgrade.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_amount`, number, required: false): The amount of the sponsorship that you are willing to offer.
* **Sponsorships** (`sponsorships`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the sponsor(s) from the list:
	+ Sponsor 1
	+ Sponsor 2
	+ Sponsor 3
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Any additional notes or comments about the upgrade.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): The date for the upgrade.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): The time for the upgrade.
