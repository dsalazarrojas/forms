# film-premiere-luxury-sponsorship-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors for a luxury film premiere event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Package Name
	* Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Select One
	* Decimal
	* Integer
	* Text
2. Fill in any relevant optional fields:
	* Email 2
	* Phone 2
	* Select Multiple
	* Decimal 2
	* Integer 2
	* Text 2
	* Date
	* Time
	* Note
3. Leave blank any fields that are not applicable or not relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Package Name** (1, text, required): Fill in the name of the package or product you are representing.
* **Name** (2, text, required): Fill in your name and surname.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Fill in your work email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Fill in your work phone number.
* **Select One** (5, select_one, required): Select one of the options provided.
* **Select Multiple** (6, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options separated by commas.
* **Note** (7, note, optional): Fill in any additional comments or notes if you are unable to fill in a field.
* **Date** (8, date, optional): Fill in the date of the event or any other relevant date.
* **Time** (9, time, optional): Fill in the time of the event or any other relevant time.
* **Decimal** (10, number, required): Fill in a decimal number (e.g., 10.5).
* **Integer** (11, number, required): Fill in an integer number (e.g., 10).
* **Text** (12, text, optional): Fill in any additional text.
* **Email 2** (13, email, optional): Fill in your work email address (if different from the first email field).
* **Phone 2** (14, text, optional): Fill in your work phone number (if different from the first phone field).
* **Select One 2** (15, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided.
* **Select Multiple 2** (16, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options separated by commas.
* **Decimal 2** (17, number, optional): Fill in a decimal number (e.g., 10.5).
* **Integer 2** (18, number, optional): Fill in an integer number (e.g., 10).
* **Text 2** (19, text, optional): Fill in any additional text.
* **Date 2** (20, date, optional): Fill in the date of the event or any other relevant date.
* **Time 2** (21, time, optional): Fill in the time of the event or any other relevant time.
* **Note 2** (22, note, optional): Fill in any additional comments or notes.
* **Email 3** (23, email, optional): Fill in your work email address (if different from the first and second email fields).
* **Phone 3** (24, text, optional): Fill in your work phone number (if different from the first and second phone fields).
* **Select Multiple 3** (25, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options separated by commas.

Please note that all fields marked as optional can be left blank if not applicable. If you're unsure about any field, please refer back to this guide for clarification.
