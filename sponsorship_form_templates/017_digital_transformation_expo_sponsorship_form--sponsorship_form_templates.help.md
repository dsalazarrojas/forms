# Digital Transformation Expo Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect sponsorship details from potential sponsors for the Digital Transformation Expo. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth partnership process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your sponsor name in the **Sponsor Name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Email** field (optional but recommended).
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Add your website URL in the **Website** field (optional).
5. Upload your logo in the **Logo** field (optional).
6. Select your sponsor type from the **Sponsor Type** field (multiple options available).
7. Describe the sponsorship opportunity in the **Sponsorship Details** field.
8. Choose a date for the **Agreement Date** field (optional).
9. Enter your name as it appears on the agreement in the **Sponsor Name on Agreement** field.
10. Provide your signature in the **Sponsor Signature** field.
11. Add any sponsor notes in the **Sponsor Notes** field (optional).
12. Enter your address in the **Sponsor Address** field (optional).
13. Enter your ZIP/Postal code in the **ZIP/Postal** field (optional).
14. Enter your city in the **City** field (optional).
15. Select your state from the list in the **State** field.
16. Select whether you have a sponsor representative in the **Representative** field.
17. Select whether you have a sponsor title in the **Title** field.
18. Select your relationship with the sponsor in the **Relationship** field (optional).
19. Enter the sponsorship amount (optional).
20. Accept the agreement terms in the **Acceptance** field.
21. Choose a date for the agreement to be signed in the **Agreement Signed Date** field (optional).
22. Enter your name as it appears on the agreement in the **Agreement Signed By** field.
23. Add any agreement notes in the **Agreement Notes** field (optional).
24. Choose a time for the agreement to be signed in the **Agreement Signed At** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Name** (1, text, required): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Email** (2, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Website** (4, text, optional): Enter your website URL for more information.
* **Logo** (5, text, optional): Upload your company logo (optional).
* **Sponsor Type** (6, select_multiple, required): Select one or more sponsor types from the options.
* **Sponsorship Details** (7, text, optional): Describe the sponsorship opportunity.
* **Agreement Date** (8, date, optional): Choose a date for the agreement (optional).
* **Sponsor Name on Agreement** (9, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on the agreement.
* **Sponsor Signature** (10, text, required): Provide your signature.
* **Sponsor Notes** (11, note, optional): Add any sponsor notes.
* **Sponsor Address** (12, text, optional): Enter your company address.
* **ZIP/Postal** (13, text, optional): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **City** (14, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (15, select_one, required): Select your state from the list.
* **Country** (16, select_one, optional): Select whether you have a sponsor representative.
* **Representative** (17, text, optional): Enter your representative's name (if you have one).
* **Title** (18, select_one, optional): Select whether you have a sponsor title.
* **Relationship** (19, select_one, optional): Select your relationship with the sponsor.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (20, number, optional): Enter the sponsorship amount.
* **Acceptance** (21, text, required): Accept the agreement terms.
* **Agreement Signed Date** (22, date, optional): Choose a date for the agreement (optional).
* **Agreement Signed By** (23, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on the agreement.
* **Agreement Notes** (24, note, optional): Add any agreement notes.
* **Agreement Signed At** (25, time, required): Choose a time for the agreement to be signed.
