# Event Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Sponsorship Form is used to collect information from potential sponsors about their event sponsorship. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth and successful sponsorship process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your event details:
	* **Event Title**: Briefly describe the title of your event.
	* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event.
	* **Event Time**: Enter the time of your event.
	* **Event Location**: Enter the location of your event.
2. Provide sponsor information:
	* **Sponsor Name**: Enter your name and contact information.
	* **Sponsor Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Sponsor Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Sponsor Address**: Enter your address.
	* **Sponsor Website**: Enter your website URL.
3. Confirm sponsorship details:
	* **Sponsor Confirmation**: Select the correct confirmation option for your sponsorship.
4. Add any additional information:
	* **Event Description**: Describe your event in detail.
	* **Event Category**: Choose a category for your event.
	* **Event Type**: Choose the type of your event.
	* **Event Notes**: Add any notes about your event.
	* **Sponsor Notes**: Add any notes about the sponsor.
	* **Sponsor Logo**: Upload or link to your sponsor's logo.
	* **Sponsor Agreement**: Upload or link to your sponsor's agreement.
	* **Sponsor Requirements**: Add any requirements for the sponsor.
	* **Sponsor Confirmation Message**: Leave this field for any additional confirmation message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_title`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of your event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): The date of your event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): The time of your event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required: false): The location of your event.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required: false): Your name and contact information.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, `email`, required: false): Your email address.
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Your phone number.
* **Sponsor Address** (`sponsor_address`, `text`, required: false): Your address.
* **Sponsor Website** (`sponsor_website`, `text`, required: false): Your website URL.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_amount`, `number`, required: false): The amount of sponsorship.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false): Any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `text`, required: false): The method of payment.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `text`, required: false): A detailed description of your event.
* **Event Category** (`event_category`, `text`, required: false): A category for your event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `text`, required: false): The type of your event.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, `text`, required: false): Any notes about your event.
* **Sponsor Notes** (`sponsor_notes`, `text`, required: false): Any notes about the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Logo** (`sponsor_logo`, `text`, required: false): Upload or link to your sponsor's logo.
* **Sponsor Agreement** (`sponsor_agreement`, `text`, required: false): Upload or link to your sponsor's agreement.
* **Sponsor Requirements** (`sponsor_requirements`, `text`, required: false): Any requirements for the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Confirmation** (`sponsor_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the correct confirmation option for your sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Confirmation Message** (`sponsor_confirmation_message`, `text`, required: false): Leave this field for any additional confirmation message.
* **Sponsor Terms** (`sponsor_terms`, `text`, required: false): Any terms or conditions of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Logo** (`sponsor_logo`, `text`, required: false): Upload or link to your sponsor's logo.

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's still essential to fill them out for accurate and complete sponsorship information.
