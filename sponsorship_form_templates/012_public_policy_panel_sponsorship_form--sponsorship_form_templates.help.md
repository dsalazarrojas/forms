# Public Policy Panel Sponsorship Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from potential sponsors for a public policy panel event. The information collected will be used to assign sponsorship and track payments.

## How To Complete This Form

- This form is designed to be filled out by a representative of the organization or individual applying for sponsorship.
- Please have the necessary information ready before filling out this form, such as contact details, sponsorship type and amount.
- Fill out the form in the required fields marked as such.
- If you are having trouble accessing the form, please contact our support staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): The name of the organization or individual applying for sponsorship.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): The name and contact information of the person responsible for this sponsorship application.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional): The role the organization or individual will play in the public policy panel event. This can be "Sponsor" or "Partner" or "Other".
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): The address of the organization or individual.
* **Sponsor Type** (`sponsor_type`, `select_one`, optional): The type of sponsorship being applied for. This can be "Individual", "Organization", or "Both".
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, optional): The amount of the sponsorship being applied for.
* **Currency** (`currency`, `select_one`, optional): The currency of the sponsorship. This can be "USD", "EUR", or "Other".
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): The date of the public policy panel event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): The time of the public policy panel event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, optional): The location of the public policy panel event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the public policy panel event.
* **Type of Sponsorship** (`sponsorship_type`, `select_one`, optional): The type of sponsorship being applied for. This can be "Monetary Support", "Product/Service", or "Both".
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, optional): The terms of payment for the sponsorship. This can be "One-time payment", "Ongoing payment", or "Other".
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required): The terms and conditions of the sponsorship, which must be accepted by the applicant.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Any additional information about the sponsorship application.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, optional): The person or department assigned to handle this sponsorship application. This can be "John Doe" or "Jane Doe".
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, `select_one`, optional): The person or department assigned to review this sponsorship application. This can be "John Doe" or "Jane Doe".

**Tips**

- Please make sure to proofread your submission before submitting it.
- If you are having trouble accessing the form, please contact our support staff for assistance.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
