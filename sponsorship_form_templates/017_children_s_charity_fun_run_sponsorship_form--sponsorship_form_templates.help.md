# Children's Charity Fun Run Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children's Charity Fun Run Sponsorship Form is a vital tool for our charity event organizers to collect necessary information from participants and sponsors. It helps us to track sponsorships, manage event logistics, and provide recognition to our donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill out your personal information as a participant: 
   - **Participant Name**: Please enter your child's name participating in the fun run.
   - **Age**: Please input your child's age.
   - **Parent/Guardian Name**: This is the name of the adult accompanying your child at the event.
   - **Parent Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
   - **Parent Phone Number**: Input the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
   - **Event Date**: Select the date of the fun run event.
   - **Event Location**: Specify the location of the event.
2.  Choose your sponsorship options:
   - **Sponsorship Type**: Select the type of sponsorship you're offering:
     - **Per mile Pledge**: A donation per each mile your child will run.
     - **Flat Donation**: A fixed amount pledged for your child's participation.
     - **Corporate Sponsorship**: A company supporting your child's participation.
3.  Provide your pledge details:
   - **Pledge Amount**: Input the amount you're willing to pledge.
   - **Estimated Miles to be Run**: If known, estimate the distance your child will run.
   - **Estimated Total Pledge**: If known, estimate the total amount you'll be pledging.
4.  Fill out sponsor information:
   - **Sponsor Name**: Enter your name or your company's name.
   - **Sponsor Address**: Input your address or your company's address.
   - **Sponsor Email**: Enter your contact email address.
5.  Select your payment method:
   - **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to make the payment:
     - **Cash**: In-person payment at the event.
     - **Check**: Payment via a check mailed to our organization.
     - **Credit Card**: Payment online or via phone.
     - **Bank Transfer**: Payment via bank transfer.
     - **Online Payment**: Payment through our website or platform.
6.  Specify when you'll pay:
   - **Payment Timing**: Select when you'd like to make the payment:
     - **On Event Day**: Payment on the day of the fun run.
     - **Within One Week**: Payment within a week of the event.
     - **End of Month**: Payment at the end of the month of the fun run.
7.  Choose whether you want recognition:
   - **Recognize My Sponsorship**: Check if you'd like to receive recognition for your sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: The name of your child participating in the fun run.
* **Age**: The age of your child.
* **Participant Name (Parent/Guardian)**: The name of the adult accompanying your child at the event.
* **Parent Email (Parent/Guardian)**: The contact email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone Number (Parent/Guardian)**: The phone contact number of the parent or guardian.
* **Event Date**: The date of the fun run event.
* **Event Location**: The location of the fun run event.
* **Sponsorship Type**: Choose the type of sponsorship you're offering.
* **Pledge Amount**: The amount you're willing to pledge for your child's participation.
* **Estimated Miles to be Run**: If known, estimate the distance your child will run.
* **Estimated Total Pledge**: If known, estimate the total amount you'll be pledging.
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter your name or your company's name.
* **Sponsor Address**: Input your address or your company's address.
* **Sponsor Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the preferred payment method for your sponsorship.
* **When will pledge be paid**: Select when you'd like to make the payment.
* **Recognize My Sponsorship**: Check if you'd like to receive public recognition for your sponsorship.

Please review the form carefully before submitting it, ensuring all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid duplication of efforts. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your earliest convenience.
