# Event Sponsorship Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for event organizers and coordinators to inquire about potential sponsorship opportunities for their events. It is used to gather information about the event, contact details, and sponsorship interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:

1. Enter a short description of your event in the "Event Sponsorship Information" field.
2. Select the type of sponsor you are interested in (e.g., event organizer, event coordinator, or other).
3. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
4. Provide details about your event sponsorship interest.
5. Choose the type of partnership or collaboration you are seeking (e.g., partnership, collaborative partnership, or other).
6. Specify the level of interest you have for the sponsorship (high, medium, or low).
7. Enter any additional information about the sponsor.
8. Check the boxes to indicate if any other sponsorships have been accepted or declined.
9. If necessary, add any additional notes about other sponsorships.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Sponsorship Information (1)
* **Event Sponsorship Inquiry Form (Text)**: This is a short description of your event.
* **Hint:** Enter a brief description of your event (e.g., "Music Festival, Charity Run, etc.").

### Event Name (2)
* **Event Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your event.

### Sponsor Type (3)
* **Sponsor Type (Select One)**: Select the type of sponsor you are interested in:
	+ Event Organizer
	+ Event Coordinator
	+ Other

### Contact Information (4)
* **Contact Information (Text)**: Enter your phone number.

### Event Sponsorship Interest (5)
* **Event Sponsorship Interest (Note)**: Provide a detailed explanation of your event sponsorship interest.

### Sponsorship Relationship (6)
* **Sponsorship Relationship (Select Multiple)**: Choose the type of partnership or collaboration you are seeking:
	+ Partnership
	+ Collaborative Partnership
	+ Other

### Event Sponsorship Interest Level (8)
* **Event Sponsorship Interest Level (Select One)**: Choose your level of interest for the sponsorship:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

### Sponsor Info (9)
* **Sponsor Info (Text)**: Enter any additional information about the sponsor.

### Contact Email (10)
* **Contact Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.

### Sponsor Phone (11)
* **Sponsor Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.

### Sponsor Address (12)
* **Sponsor Address (Text)**: Enter the sponsor's address.

### Event Sponsorship Request Details (13)
* **Event Sponsorship Request Details (Text)**: Provide any additional details about your event sponsorship request.

### Sponsor Terms (14)
* **Sponsor Terms (Text)**: Enter any terms or conditions you have for the sponsorship.

### Sponsors Accepted (15)
* **Sponsors Accepted (Select Multiple)**: Check the boxes for any sponsorships that have been accepted:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not Sure

### Sponsors Declined (16)
* **Sponsors Declined (Select Multiple)**: Check the boxes for any sponsorships that have been declined:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not Sure

### Other Sponsorships (17)
* **Other Sponsorships (Select Multiple)**: Check the boxes for any other sponsorships:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not Sure

### Other Sponsorships Notes (19)
* **Other Sponsorships Notes (Note)**: Enter any additional notes about other sponsorships.
