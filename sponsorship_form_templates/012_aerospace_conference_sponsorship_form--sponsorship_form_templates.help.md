# Aerospace Conference Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aerospace Conference Sponsorship Form is designed to collect information from companies interested in sponsoring the conference. This form helps us to understand your company's interests and preferences for the sponsorship packages we offer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review the options carefully and select the correct answers.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the conference organizers for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Company Name (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal name of your company.

* The company name is the official name of the company as it appears on your business registration documents.
* Please ensure that the name is spelled correctly and is up-to-date.

### 2. Industry Sector (`company_industry_sector`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary industry sector of your company.

* Choose the option that best describes your company's primary business activities.
* If your company operates in multiple sectors, please select the one that is most relevant to the aerospace industry.

### 3. Primary Contact Name (`primary_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for the sponsorship.

* This is the person who will be the main point of contact for the conference organizers.
* Please ensure that this is the correct person to receive emails and communication related to the sponsorship.

### 4. Primary Contact Title (`primary_contact_title`, `text`, required): Enter the job title of the primary contact person.

* This will help us to understand the contact person's role within the company.
* Please ensure that this is accurate and up-to-date.

### 5. Primary Contact Email (`primary_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.

* This is the email address that we will use to communicate with the primary contact person.
* Please ensure that it is a valid business email address.

### 6. Primary Contact Phone (`primary_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the business phone number of the primary contact person.

* This is the phone number that we will use to contact the primary contact person.
* Please ensure that it is a valid business phone number.

### 7. Selected Sponsorship Level (`selected_sponsorship_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the sponsorship level that you are interested in.

* Choose the level that best suits your company's goals and budget.
* Please review the benefits and details of each level carefully before making a selection.

### 8. Sponsorship Benefits Acknowledged (`sponsorship_benefits_acknowledged`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that you have reviewed and understand the benefits included in the selected sponsorship package.

* This ensures that you are aware of what you will receive in return for your sponsorship.
* Please review the benefits carefully before making a selection.

### 9. Logo File Upload (`logo_file_upload`, `text`, required): Upload your company logo in high resolution.

* This will be used for promotional materials.
* Please ensure that it is a high-quality, high-resolution image.

### 10. Exhibition Booth Preference (`booth_preference`, `text`, optional): Indicate your preference for booth location or size.

* If you have specific preferences, please provide details.
* Please note that availability and location subject to conference organizers' decision.

### 11. Marketing Materials Description (`marketing_materials_description`, `text`, optional): Describe any marketing materials you plan to bring.

* This helps us to understand your marketing plan and ensure that we can accommodate your requests.
* Please be as detailed as possible.

### 12. Special Requests or Accommodations (`special_requests_accommodations`, `text`, optional): Provide any special requests or accommodations.

* If you have specific requests or needs, please provide details.
* Please note that availability subject to conference organizers' decision.

### 13. Presentation and Speaking Opportunities (`presentation_opportunities_section`, `note`, optional): This section is for presenting or speaking opportunities.

* If you are interested in presenting a technical session or keynote, please select "True" and provide a proposed topic in the next field.
* Please review the presentation schedule and format carefully before making a decision.

### 14. Interest in Speaking Session (`speaking_session_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your interest in speaking or presenting.

* If you are interested, select "True".
* If unsure, please provide a brief explanation in the next field.

### 15. Proposed Presentation Topic (`presentation_topic`, `text`, optional): Enter a proposed presentation topic if you are interested in presenting.

* This helps us to understand your proposed session and ensure that we can accommodate your request.
* Please be as detailed as possible.

### 16. Networking Event Participation (`networking_event_participation`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your interest in participating or sponsoring a networking event.

* Select one of the options (Cocktail Reception, Breakfast Session, Lunch Session, or Gala Dinner).
* If you are unsure, please provide a brief explanation in the next field.

### 17. Payment Method (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method of payment for the sponsorship.

* Please select one of the options (Wire Transfer, Check, Credit Card, or Purchase Order).
* If unsure, please contact the conference organizers for clarification.

### 18. Billing Contact Name (`billing_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for billing.

* This is the person who will receive billing information and invoices.
* Please ensure that this is the correct person.

### 19. Billing Contact Email (`billing_contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the billing contact person.

* This is the email address that we will use for billing and communication.
* Please ensure that it is a valid business email address.

### 20. Billing Address (`billing_address`, `text`, required): Enter the complete address for sending invoices.

* This is the address that we will use to send invoices and billing information.
* Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.

### 21. Purchase Order Number (`purchase_order_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the purchase order number if paying by PO.

* This is the number that we will use to track your payment.
* If unsure, please contact the conference organizers for clarification.

### 22. Terms and Conditions Agreement (`terms_and_conditions_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that you agree to the conference sponsorship terms and conditions.

* This is a legal agreement that must be accepted before we can proceed with the sponsorship.
* Please review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing.

### 23. Cancellation Policy Acknowledged (`cancellation_policy_acknowledged`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that you understand and agree to the sponsorship cancellation policy.

* This is a critical part of the sponsorship agreement.
* Please review the cancellation policy carefully before agreeing.

### 24. Signature Authorization (`signature_authorization`, `text`, required): Provide the electronic signature of the authorized representative.

* This is a legal requirement for the sponsorship agreement.
* Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.

Please note that the fields marked as "required" must be filled out for the form to be considered complete. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the conference organizers for clarification.
