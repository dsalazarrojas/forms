# Conservation Fundraiser Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conservation Fundraiser Sponsorship Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your contribution to our conservation efforts. By completing this form, you will be able to provide us with the necessary information to acknowledge and recognize your support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you are making an in-kind donation, provide a detailed description of the goods or services being donated.
4. If you are making a matching gift, indicate if others will match your donation.
5. If you require tax documentation, indicate so and provide your tax ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsorship Date** (`sponsorship_date`): Date of sponsorship commitment.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`): Name of sponsor or organization.
* **Sponsor Type** (`sponsor_type`): Type of sponsoring entity (Individual, Family, Business, Corporation, Foundation, Nonprofit, Government Agency).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Primary contact name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Contact phone number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): Business or organization name (if applicable).
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`): Mailing address (if applicable).
* **Event or Program Name** (`event_name`): What conservation event or program are you sponsoring.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): When is the event or program.
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`): Select your sponsorship tier (Platinum - $10,000+, Gold - $5,000 to $9,999, Silver - $2,500 to $4,999, Bronze - $1,000 to $2,499, Friend - $500 to $999, Supporter - $100 to $499, Custom Amount).
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_amount`): Total commitment amount.
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`): How will you pay (Full payment now, 50 percent now, 50 percent later, Quarterly payments, Monthly payments, Custom schedule).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): How will you pay (Credit card, Debit card, Check, Bank transfer, In-kind donation, Stock transfer).
* **Area of Focus** (`sponsorship_focus`): Which conservation area to support (Where most needed, Land acquisition, Wildlife conservation, Water conservation, Forest restoration, Education programs, Research, Community outreach).
* **Recognition Preferences** (`recognition_preferences`): How would you like to be recognized (Event signage, Website listing, Social media mention, Press release, Program booklet, Plaque or certificate, Anonymous, No recognition needed).
* **Logo Submission** (`logo_submission`): Will you submit a logo (Yes - Will submit, False).
* **Logo Submission Email** (`logo_email`): Email for logo submission.
* **Booth Space Needed** (`booth_space_needed`): Do you need booth space for events with vendor booths (True, False, Maybe).
* **Volunteer Participation** (`volunteer_participation`): Will your team volunteer (True, False, Maybe).
* **Estimated Number of Volunteers** (`volunteer_count`): How many team members (if applicable).
* **In-Kind Donation** (`in_kind_donation`): Are you making an in-kind donation (True, False).
* **In-Kind Donation Details** (`in_kind_details`): Description of goods or services being donated (if in-kind donation is selected).
* **Estimated Value of In-Kind Donation** (`in_kind_value`): Dollar value of in-kind donation (if in-kind donation is selected).
* **Matching Gift** (`matching_gift`): Is this a matching gift (True, False, Unsure).
* **Tax Receipt Needed** (`tax_receipt_needed`): Do you need tax documentation (True, False).
* **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id_number`): EIN or tax ID number (if tax documentation is required).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Any other information.
* **Sponsor Signature** (`sponsor_signature`): Type your full name.
* **Sponsorship Status** (`sponsorship_status`): Sponsorship status (Pledged, Payment pending, Partially paid, Paid in full, Completed).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the conservation team for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are making an in-kind donation, consider providing a detailed description of the goods or services being donated.
* If you are making a matching gift, indicate so and provide the necessary information. 
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to proceed with your donation.
