# marathon_sponsorship_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a sponsorship application related to a marathon event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out the General Information page, providing basic details about your sponsorship request.
2. Move on to the Sponsor Information page, where you can provide more details about your organization or individual.
3. Fill out the Donation Information page, if applicable.
4. Sign the Acknowledgement page, confirming that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
5. Select the type of sponsorship you are applying for (Sponsorship, Support, or Other) on the Agreement page.
6. Provide a detailed description of your agreement on the Agreement Details page.
7. If you have additional information to provide, you can add it on the Sponsor Information Additional page.
8. Finally, fill out the Sponsor Details page with any relevant details about your organization or individual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (1)** (`General Information`, `text`, `false`): 
    This page is where you can provide basic details about your sponsorship request.
* **Sponsor Information (2)** (`Sponsor Information`, `text`, `false`): 
    This page is where you can provide more information about your organization or individual.
* **Donation Information (3)** (`Donation Information`, `text`, `false`): 
    This page is where you can provide more information about your donation, if applicable.
* **Acknowledgement (4)** (`Acknowledgement`, `text`, `true`): 
    This page is where you confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement (5)** (`Agreement`, `select_multiple`, `true`): 
    Select the type of sponsorship you are applying for (Sponsorship, Support, or Other).
* **Agreement Details (6)** (`Agreement Details`, `text`, `true`): 
    This page is where you can provide a detailed description of your agreement.
* **Sponsor Information Additional (7)** (`Sponsor Information Additional`, `text`, `false`): 
    This page is where you can add any additional information about your organization or individual.
* **Sponsor Details (8)** (`Sponsor Details`, `text`, `false`): 
    This page is where you can provide any relevant details about your organization or individual.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much information as possible, but only if it is relevant to your sponsorship request.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact us for clarification.
