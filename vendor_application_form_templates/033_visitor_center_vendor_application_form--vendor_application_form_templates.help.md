# Visitor Center Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help vendors apply to partner with the visitor center. It is a multi-page form that asks for various details about the vendor and their business. Please fill out the form carefully and completely to ensure a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Fill out each field according to the required and optional fields marked in the form.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business documents or ID.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business name as it appears on your business documents or ID.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose 'Yes' if your business is a vendor, 'No' otherwise.
* **Social Media URL** (`social_media_url`, `text`, optional): Enter your social media URL if applicable.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Enter your business address.
* **City** (`business_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city of your business address.
* **State** (`business_state`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose 'Yes' if your business is located in the state, 'No' otherwise.
* **Zip** (`business_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your business zip code.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your product.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your product.
* **Price** (`product_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of your product.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Choose 'Yes' to accept the terms and conditions.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are available to partner with the visitor center.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are available to partner with the visitor center.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about your product.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional terms and conditions you would like to add.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any personal details about yourself as the vendor.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional business information.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, `number`, optional): Enter any additional product price information.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* Review each page carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the visitor center staff.
