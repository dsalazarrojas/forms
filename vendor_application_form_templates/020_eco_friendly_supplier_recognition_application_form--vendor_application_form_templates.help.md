# Eco Friendly Supplier Recognition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Friendly Supplier Recognition Application Form is a template designed to help suppliers apply for eco-friendly recognition. This form gathers information about the supplier's sustainability practices, certifications, and other relevant details to ensure that only legitimate suppliers with a strong track record of eco-friendly practices are acknowledged and promoted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Supplier Details section with accurate and up-to-date information about your company.
2. Provide detailed information about your sustainability practices in the Sustainability Info section.
3. List any relevant certifications you hold in the Certifications section.
4. Upload any references that support your eco-friendly practices in the References section.
5. Enter your company details in the Company Details section.
6. Confirm your submission and provide any additional information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Details** (<label>Supplier Details</label>): Please fill in your company's name, address, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Sustainability Info** (<label>Sustainability Info</label>): Describe your company's sustainability practices, including any initiatives or programs you have in place to reduce your environmental impact.
* **Certifications** (<label>Certifications</label>): List any relevant certifications you hold, such as ISO 14001 or B Corp certifications.
* **References** (<label>References</label>): Upload any references that support your eco-friendly practices, such as certificates, reports, or articles.
* **Company Details** (<label>Company Details</label>): Enter your company's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Submission Confirmation** (<label>Submission Confirmation</label>): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted this form accurately.
* **Additional Details** (<label>Additional Details</label>): Provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application.
* **Review Page** (<label>Review Page</label>): This page is for internal review and confirmation; do not fill it in.
