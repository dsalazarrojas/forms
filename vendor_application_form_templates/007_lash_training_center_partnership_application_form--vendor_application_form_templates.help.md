# Lash Training Center Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by potential partners to apply for partnership with Lash Training Center. It is essential to complete the form accurately and honestly to facilitate a smooth review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first page with any relevant information.
2. On the second page, select the correct vendor details from the list.
3. On the third page, select all relevant partnership details that apply.
4. Add any additional notes about the partnership on the fourth page.
5. Finally, enter your email address in the fifth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page (First Page)**: A text field to fill in any information about yourself or your company. 
* **Vendor Details**: This field allows you to select which vendor you are partnering with. Please choose from the list: Lash Training Center, Jimmy Chen, example@example.com, 123-456-7890, 123 Street.
* **Partnership Details**: This field allows you to select multiple partnership details that apply to your partnership. Please choose from the list: Partnership Type, Partnership Location, Start Date.
* **Additional Notes**: This field is for any additional notes you may want to add to your application.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and honestly.
- Only fill out this form if you are a new partner to the Lash Training Center.
- If you need further assistance or have questions, contact the Lash Training Center administration.
