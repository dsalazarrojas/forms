# Global Sourcing Compliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Global Sourcing Compliance Registration Form is a template used to collect information about a vendor's business and contact details. This form ensures that all necessary data is collected to register a vendor in the system, ensuring compliance with global sourcing regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form title to describe the purpose of your registration.
2. Enter your company name as it appears on your business documents.
3. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this registration.
4. Enter your job title and job type.
5. Select your job level and categories as applicable.
6. Enter details about your job function.
7. Select your industry type.
8. Indicate whether you work in different locations or not.
9. Enter your job location (if applicable).
10. Enter details about your job type (if not working in a different location).
11. Enter your job description.
12. Select your vendor type.
13. Enter any other vendor type details (if necessary).
14. Enter your business address.
15. Enter your address line 1.
16. Enter your address line 2.
17. Enter your city.
18. Enter your state.
19. Enter your post code.
20. Enter your country.
21. Enter your contact number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: Enter a short description of the purpose of your registration.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business documents.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this registration.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Job Type**: Select your job type.
* **Job Level**: Select your job level. (Note: This field is optional but recommended for accurate categorization.)
* **Job Category**: Select your job category. (Note: This field is required for accurate categorization.)
* **Job Function**: Enter details about your job function.
* **Industry**: Select your industry type.
* **Work Location**: Indicate whether you work in different locations or not.
* **Job Location**: Enter your job location (if you work in different locations).
* **Job Type Details**: Enter details about your job type (if not working in a different location).
* **Job Description**: Enter a detailed description of your job.
* **Vendor Type**: Select your vendor type.
* **Vendor Type Other**: Enter any other vendor type details (if necessary).
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
* **Address 1**: Enter your address line 1.
* **Address 2**: Enter your address line 2.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **Post Code**: Enter your post code.
* **Country**: Enter your country.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.

**Tips**

* Ensure that you enter all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you work in different locations, please select "Yes" for "Work Location" and enter your job location in the corresponding field.
* If you have any other vendor type details, please enter them in the "Vendor Type Other" field.
* Please ensure that you enter your contact number accurately and completely.
