## # joint_venture_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The joint venture program application form is designed to collect information from potential partners interested in collaborating with our organization on a joint venture program. The form is used to gather necessary data to assess and review the proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form in a clear and concise manner, providing detailed information for each field.
2. Make sure to submit all required fields (indicated by *) and any additional optional fields relevant to your proposal.
3. Attach relevant supporting documents, if applicable, under the "Supporting Documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, *): This is the name of your organization. Please enter the official name of your company or institution.
* **Partner Details** (`partner_details`, text): Briefly describe your organization and its relevant experience, expertise, and qualifications.
* **Program Objectives** (`program_objectives`, text): Describe the main objectives of your proposed joint venture program, focusing on its goals and expected outcomes.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text): Upload any additional documents supporting your proposal, such as letters of introduction, resumes, or project proposals.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text): Please enter the name and title of the person primarily responsible for managing this joint venture.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter a direct line of communication for the contact person.
* **Partner Experience** (`partner_experience`, text): Briefly describe your organization's relevant experience in similar joint ventures or related projects.
## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are accurately and thoroughly completed before submission.
- Use a standard font and formatting to improve readability.
- If you need assistance or clarification, do not hesitate to contact the contact person listed above.
