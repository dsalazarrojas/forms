# Vendor Payment Portal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register vendors for payment processing. It gathers necessary information about the vendor, their contact details, and banking information to ensure seamless payment settlement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Choose the correct vendor type and payment type according to your business needs.
3. Enter your bank account details carefully.
4. Provide your contact and representative information.
5. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax ID**: Enter your tax identification number for tax purposes.
* **Name**: Enter your business name as it appears on your business cards or invoices.
* **Address**: Enter your business address for communication and payment processing.
* **Contact**: Enter your contact details for communication and follow-up.
* **Vendor Type**: Choose your vendor type from the provided options (Retail, Wholesale, Food).
* **Payment Type**: Choose your preferred payment method (Check, Wire Transfer, Online Payment).
* **Bank Account Number**: Enter your bank account number for payment settlement.
* **Bank Branch**: Enter your bank branch number or name.
* **Bank City**: Enter the city where your bank branch is located.
* **Bank State**: Enter the state where your bank branch is located.
* **Bank Country**: Enter the country where your bank branch is located.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any specific payment terms or conditions.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes for our reference.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and payment notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Fax**: Enter your fax number for urgent or confidential communication.
* **Primary Representative**: Enter the name of your primary representative for contact purposes.
* **Representative Email**: Enter the email address of your primary representative.
* **Vendor Email**: Enter your email address for payment notifications and updates.
* **Vendor Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
