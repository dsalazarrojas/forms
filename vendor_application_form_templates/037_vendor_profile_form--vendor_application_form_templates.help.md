# Vendor Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Vendor Profile Form is designed to collect essential information about vendors for our business purposes. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to help us better understand your company's details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start on the first page, where you will provide basic contact information.
2.  On the second page, you will provide more specific details about your business.
3.  Finally, on the last page, you will select your vendor type and category.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (First Page, text, required: false):** Please enter your preferred title for your profile.
* **Address (First Page, text, required: false):** Enter your business address to help us locate you easily.
* **Contact Email (First Page, email, required: false):** Enter your email address to ensure we can reach you for any inquiries or updates.
* **Contact Phone (First Page, text, required: false):** Provide your primary contact number for any communication.
* **Contact Phone Extension (First Page, text, required: false):** If you have an extension, please enter it to avoid any confusion.
* **Contact Alternate Email (Second Page, email, required: false):** Enter your alternate email address for any secondary contact or backup.
* **Contact Alternate Phone (Second Page, text, required: false):** Add another contact number for any alternate contact or backup.
* **Contact Fax (Second Page, text, required: false):** Provide your fax number for any urgent or official documents.
* **Contact Mobile (Second Page, text, required: false):** Enter your mobile number for any immediate contact.
* **Vendor Type (Third Page, select_one, required: true):** Please select whether you are an Individual or a Company.
* **Vendor Category (Third Page, select_multiple, required: true):** Choose from the available categories (Manufacturer, Retailer, Service, or Other) that best describe your business.
* **Business Name (Third Page, text, required: false):** Enter your business name to identify your company.
* **Business Title (Third Page, text, required: false):** Provide a title for your business (e.g., CEO, Marketing Manager, etc.).
* **Website (Third Page, text, required: false):** Enter your business website for any additional information.
* **Industry (Third Page, text, required: false):** Describe your industry to help us understand your business better.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to us for clarification.
* Double-check your entries for any typos or errors before submitting.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Vendor Profile Form efficiently and accurately, providing us with the necessary information to best serve your needs.
