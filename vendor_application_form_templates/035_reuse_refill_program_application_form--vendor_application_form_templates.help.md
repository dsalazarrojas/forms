# Reuse & Refill Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reuse & Refill Program Application Form is a document designed to collect necessary information from vendors who wish to participate in the Reuse & Refill Program. This form is used to facilitate the application process and ensure that all required details are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Reuse & Refill Program Application Form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Check that your email address is valid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reuse & Refill Program Application**: This field is the main section of the form where you can describe your interest in participating in the Reuse & Refill Program.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you regarding your application.
* **Product Details**: Enter a brief description of the product or service you would like to offer.
* **Payment Details**: Provide any necessary payment information, such as payment terms or methods.
* **Agreement**: Please confirm that you have read and understand the program's terms of service.
* **Agreement 2**: Choose one of the options regarding your agreement with the Reuse & Refill Program.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person handling the application process.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your business email address.
* **Date**: Select the desired date for the application process.
* **Time**: Select the desired time for the application process.
