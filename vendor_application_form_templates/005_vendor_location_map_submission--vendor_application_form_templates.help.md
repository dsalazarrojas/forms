# Vendor Location Map Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit information about your business or organization's location to be added to a mapping service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a title for your submission in the **form_title** field.
2. Select the type of vendor you are submitting as **vendor_type**. This could be an event organizer, local directory, or business association.
3. Fill in the **business_name** field with the name of your business or organization.
4. Enter the **vendor_name** field with your name or the name of the person submitting the information.
5. Provide the address of your business or organization in the **address** field.
6. Enter additional address details in the **address_line_2** field, if applicable.
7. Enter the city where your business or organization is located in the **city** field.
8. Select your state or province in the **state** field.
9. Enter your zip or postal code in the **zip** field.
10. Enter the country where your business or organization is located in the **country** field.
11. Enter your business or organization's phone number in the **phone** field.
12. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
13. Enter the latitude of your business or organization's location in the **latitude** field.
14. Enter the longitude of your business or organization's location in the **longitude** field.
15. If applicable, provide a URL to an image or map view of your location in the **image_url** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): A descriptive title for your submission.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of vendor you are submitting as (event organizer, local directory, or business association).
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): The name of your business or organization.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Your name or the name of the person submitting the information.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): The address of your business or organization.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, optional): Additional address details, if applicable.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): The city where your business or organization is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): The state or province where your business or organization is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): The zip or postal code of your business or organization.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): The country where your business or organization is located.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your business or organization's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address.
* **Latitude** (`latitude`, number, required): The latitude of your business or organization's location.
* **Longitude** (`longitude`, number, required): The longitude of your business or organization's location.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text, optional): A URL to an image or map view of your location, if applicable.
