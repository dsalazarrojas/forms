# delivery_partner_accreditation_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather the necessary information from delivery partners to review and verify their credentials for accreditation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information as accurately as possible.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Delivery Address**
* Label: Delivery Address
* Type: text
* Required: false
This field is used to collect your delivery address. Please enter your delivery address as you would like it to appear on any delivery receipts or documents.

### 2. **Delivery Experience**
* Label: Delivery Experience
* Type: text
* Required: false
This field is used to describe your delivery experience. Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any relevant details about your delivery services.

### 3. **Contact Email**
* Label: Contact Email
* Type: email
* Required: false
This field is used to collect your contact email address. Please enter your email address where you can be reached for any further communication or verification.

### 4. **Contact Phone**
* Label: Contact Phone
* Type: text
* Required: false
This field is used to collect your contact phone number. Please enter your phone number where you can be reached for any further communication or verification.

### 5. **Delivery Experience Date**
* Label: Delivery Experience Date
* Type: date
* Required: false
This field is used to select the date of your delivery experience. Please select the date when you started your delivery services.

### 6. **Partner Name**
* Label: Partner Name
* Type: text
* Required: false
This field is used to collect your partner's name. Please enter the name of your company or business as it should appear on any documents or receipts.

### 7. **Partner Address**
* Label: Partner Address
* Type: text
* Required: false
This field is used to collect your partner's address. Please enter your address as it should appear on any documents or receipts.

### 8. **Partner License Number**
* Label: Partner License Number
* Type: text
* Required: false
This field is used to collect your partner's license number. Please enter your license number as it appears on your license document.

### 9. **Partner License Date**
* Label: Partner License Date
* Type: date
* Required: false
This field is used to select the date of your partner's license. Please select the date when your license was issued.

### 10. **Partner License Status**
* Label: Partner License Status
* Type: text
* Required: false
This field is used to describe your partner's license status. Please select the current status of your license, such as active, suspended, or revoked.
