# event_vendor_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for vendors to register and provide information about their products or services for an event managed by our organization. The form will be used to gather necessary details about the vendor and their plans for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and complete information about your products or services and your contact details.
2. Select the correct options for "Contact Information" and "Type of Vendor" that best describe your business.
3. If you have any additional details about your products or services, you can provide them in the "Vendor Details" field.
4. Choose the date you plan to attend the event and the start date of your event (if applicable).
5. Review and agree to the vendor agreement by signing the "Vendor Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Information**: This field is for you to provide basic information about your company or business. Please enter a brief description of your company.
* **What kind of products do you plan to sell**: This field is for you to describe the types of products you plan to sell at the event. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Contact Information**: This field allows you to choose how you would like to be contacted by our team. Please select "Phone", "Email", or "Both" to indicate your preferred contact method.
* **Vendor Details**: This field is for you to provide any additional details about your products or services. Please use this space to describe what makes your products or services unique.
* **Type of Vendor**: This field is for you to select the type of vendor you are. Please choose "Food", "Service", or "Other" to indicate the type of vendor you are.
* **Event Attendance**: This field is for you to choose the date of the event you plan to attend. Please select a date from the calendar or enter a specific date (e.g., 2024-02-25).
* **Event Start Date**: This field is for you to provide the start date of your event (if applicable).
* **Vendor Agreement**: This field is for you to review and agree to the terms of our vendor agreement. Please read carefully before signing.
* **Vendor Agreement**: This field is not a field to fill in, it's a field to read, review and agree upon.
