# Vendor Application For Non Profit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Application For Non Profit form is designed to collect information about your non-profit business, including your details, services offered, and contact information. This form helps us evaluate your eligibility as a vendor and understand your capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether your organization is a non-profit by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the "Non Profit Information" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. In the "Vendor Details" section, provide detailed information about your business.
4. Select the services you offer from the "Services Offered" section.
5. In the "Goods Offered" section, select whether you offer goods.
6. Provide pricing details in the "Pricing Information" section.
7. Enter your payment terms in the "Payment Terms" section.
8. In the "Shipping Information" section, select your shipping options.
9. Provide tax identification number in the "Tax Information" section.
10. Enter any additional agreements or terms in the "Agreement Terms" section.
11. In the "Certification Information" section, select whether you have certification.
12. If applicable, enter your certification number.
13. Sign and date the form in the "Vendor's Signature" section.
14. Finally, provide any additional vendor reference information in the "Vendor Reference Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Profit Information (1)**: Select whether your organization is a non-profit business by choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Contact Information (2)**: Enter your contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Vendor Details (3)**: Provide detailed information about your business.
* **Services Offered (4)**: Select the services you offer.
* **Goods Offered (5)**: Select whether you offer goods.
* **Pricing Information (6)**: Enter your pricing details.
* **Payment Terms (7)**: Enter your payment terms.
* **Shipping Information (8)**: Select your shipping options.
* **Tax Information (9)**: Enter your tax identification number.
* **Agreement Terms (10)**: Enter any additional agreements or terms.
* **Certification Information (12)**: Select whether you have certification.
* **Certification Number (13)**: If applicable, enter your certification number.
* **Vendor's Signature (14)**: Sign and date the form.
* **Non Profit Reference Information (15)**: Provide any additional non-profit reference information.
* **Vendor Reference Information (16)**: Provide any additional vendor reference information.
* **Vendor Reference Contact (17)**: Enter vendor reference contact information.
* **Vendor Reference Phone (18)**: Enter vendor reference phone number.
* **Vendor Reference Email (19)**: Enter vendor reference email.
* **Vendor Reference Website (20)**: Enter vendor reference website.
* **Vendor Reference Fax (21)**: Enter vendor reference fax number.
* **Vendor Reference License (22)**: Enter vendor reference license number.

Note: Please review the form carefully and enter accurate information to ensure a smooth application process.
