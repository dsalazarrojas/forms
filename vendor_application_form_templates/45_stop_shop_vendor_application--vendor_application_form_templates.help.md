# 45 Stop & Shop Vendor Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential vendors who want to participate in the "45 Stop & Shop" vendor events. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Applicant Information" section with your personal details.
2. Select the correct business information in the "Vendor Details" section.
3. Choose your preferred booth size and location.
4. Indicate your experience as a vendor.
5. Provide your business information, including your estimated revenue per event and insurance details.
6. Ensure you comply with health and safety regulations.
7. Add any additional requirements or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name (id: 2)**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email address (id: 3)**: Use this email for us to send you your application status.
* **Phone number (id: 4)**: Include the country code (e.g., +1 555-123-4567).
* **Date of birth (id: 5)**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Home address (id: 6)**: Enter your street, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Application date (id: 7)**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Vendor or business name (id: 9)**: Enter the name under which you operate.
* **Business structure (id: 10)**: Select the legal form of your business (Individual, LLC, Corporation, Non-Profit, Cooperative).
* **Business license number (id: 11)**: Provide the official license ID.
* **Website URL (id: 12)**: Include the https:// prefix if applicable.
* **Primary social media handle (id: 13)**: Enter your social media handle (e.g., @yourbrand on Instagram or Facebook).
* **Product categories (id: 14)**: Select all the categories that apply to your products.
* **Preferred booth size (id: 15)**: Choose the size that best fits your display needs.
* **Preferred booth location (id: 16)**: Select where you would like to be positioned.
* **Have you sold at a vendor or craft show before? (id: 17)**: Indicate your experience status.
* **Years in business (id: 18)**: Enter the number of full years you have been operating.
* **Estimated revenue per event (id: 19)**: Provide an approximate amount in USD.
* **Do you carry liability insurance? (id: 20)**: Indicate your insurance status.
* **Liability insurance details (id: 21)**: Enter your policy number and provider.
* **Health & Safety compliance status (id: 22)**: Indicate your compliance level.
* **Food handling certification (id: 23)**: If you sell food, indicate your certification status.
* **Any special equipment or space requirements (id: 24)**: Enter any additional requirements.
* **Additional comments or notes (id: 25)**: Use this space to share anything else we should know.

## Tips

* Ensure you answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the event organizers for clarification.

Note: This help guide is provided as a general guide and may be subject to updates or changes. Please refer to the event rules and regulations for specific requirements and eligibility.
