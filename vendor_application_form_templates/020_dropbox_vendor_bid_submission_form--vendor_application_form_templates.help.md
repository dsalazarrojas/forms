# Dropbox Vendor Bid Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from vendors regarding their bid submissions for the Dropbox project. This form is used to gather data on vendor details, bid dates, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field to ensure you have provided the correct information.
3. If you have any supporting documents, please upload them using the "Upload File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Vendor Bidding Information**
* **Vendor Name**: This is the name of your company or organization that is submitting the bid.
* **Vendor Email**: This is the email address of the contact person for your company or organization.
* **Vendor Phone**: This is the phone number of the contact person for your company or organization.
*   **Bid Submission**: This is a brief description of your bid.
*   **Bid Date**: This is the date you expect the bid to be submitted.
*   **Bid Time**: This is the time you expect the bid to be submitted.
*   **Bid Note**: This is any additional note or comments about your bid.

### 2. **Additional Services**
*   Select all the additional services you are offering from the list. 

### 3. **Preferred Contact Method**
*   Choose your preferred contact method by selecting either Phone, Email, or Chat. 

### 4. **Vendor Service Details**
*   Provide a detailed description of your services offered.

### 5. **Upload File**
*   If you have any supporting documents, please upload them here. 

### 6. **Vendor Rating**
*   Choose your rating as either Good or Fair. 

### 7. **Vendor Status**
*   Choose your status as either Active or Inactive. 

### 8. **Vendor Price**
*   Enter the price of your bid. 

### 9. **Vendor Price Currency**
*   Enter the currency of your bid price. 

### 10. **Vendor Price Note**
*   Enter any additional notes about your bid price. 

### 11. **Submit**
*   Click this button to submit your bid. 

### 12. **Vendor Terms And Conditions**
*   Enter any terms and conditions of your bid. 

### 13. **Vendor Agreement**
*   Enter any agreement terms and conditions. 

### 14. **Vendor Payment Terms**
*   Enter any payment terms and conditions. 

### 15. **Vendor Payment Method**
*   Enter any payment method. 

### 16. **Vendor Payment Status**
*   Choose your payment status as either Active or Inactive. 

### 17. **Vendor Terms And Conditions Note**
*   Enter any additional notes about your terms and conditions. 

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled before submitting your bid.
