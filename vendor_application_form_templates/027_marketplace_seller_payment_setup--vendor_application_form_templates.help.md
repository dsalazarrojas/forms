# marketplace_seller_payment_setup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to set up payment settings for sellers on the marketplace. It collects necessary information for processing payments and ensures smooth payment processing experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "marketplace_seller_payment_setup" form.
- Fill in the required fields as requested:
  - **seller_id**: Enter your seller ID to identify you as a seller on the marketplace.
  - **seller_name**: Enter your name for the marketplace's records.
  - **seller_email**: Enter your email address for the marketplace's communication purposes.
  - **seller_phone**: Enter your phone number for the marketplace's communication.
  - **payout_method**: Select how you want to receive payments: 'Yes' or 'No'.
  - **payout_frequency**: Choose how often you want to be paid: 'Yes' or 'No'.
  - **payout_amount**: Enter the amount you want to be paid.
  - **bank_account_details**: Confirm if you want to provide bank account details: 'Yes' or 'No'.
  - **billing_address**: This field is optional, but if provided, it's for the marketplace's billing purposes. Enter your billing address if you want to be charged for any fees.
  - **tax_id**: This field is optional, but if provided, it's for tax purposes. Enter your tax ID or relevant tax details.
  - **tax_rate**: Enter the tax rate applicable to your sales.
  - **tax_excluded_amount**: Enter the amount excluded from tax.
  - **vat**: This field is optional, and it's for VAT or Value-Added Tax purposes. Enter your VAT number or relevant VAT details.
  - **shipping_address**: This field is optional, and it's for shipping purposes. Enter your shipping address if you want to track shipments.
  - **payment_method**: Confirm how you want to make payments: 'Yes' or 'No'.
  - **payment_schedule**: Choose your preferred payment schedule: 'Yes' or 'No'.
  - **payment_terms**: This field is optional and for payment terms. Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **seller_id**: Enter your unique seller ID.
* **seller_name**: Enter your name for the marketplace's records.
* **seller_email**: Enter your email address for the marketplace's communication purposes.
* **seller_phone**: Enter your phone number for the marketplace's communication.
* **payout_method**: Select 'Yes' if you want to receive payments, 'No' otherwise.
* **payout_frequency**: Choose 'Yes' if you want to be paid frequently, 'No' otherwise.
* **payout_amount**: Enter the payment amount you want to receive.
* **bank_account_details**: Confirm if you want to provide bank account details: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **billing_address**: If optional, enter your billing address for the marketplace's billing purposes.
* **tax_id**: If optional, enter your tax ID or relevant tax details.
* **tax_rate**: Enter the tax rate applicable to your sales.
* **tax_excluded_amount**: Enter the amount excluded from tax.
* **vat**: If optional, enter your VAT number or relevant VAT details.
* **shipping_address**: If optional, enter your shipping address for tracking purposes.
* **payment_method**: Confirm 'Yes' for payment, 'No' otherwise.
* **payment_schedule**: Choose 'Yes' for a preferred payment schedule, 'No' otherwise.
* **payment_terms**: If optional, enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
