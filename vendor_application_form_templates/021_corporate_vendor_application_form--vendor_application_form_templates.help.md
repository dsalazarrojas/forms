# Corporate Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from vendors interested in working with our organization. Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your company and its offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Title**: This is the title of your company. Keep it brief and to the point.
2. **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
3. **Email**: Enter your company's email address.
4. **Phone**: Enter your company's phone number.
5. **Address**: Enter your company's physical address.
6. **Description**: Briefly describe your company and its services or products.
7. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for contacting you.
8. **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
9. **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
10. **Website**: Enter your company's website URL.
11. **Logo**: (Optional) Upload your company's logo.
12. **Business Type**: Select the type of business you are (Service, Product, Both).
13. **Industry**: Enter the industry your business operates in.
14. **Products/Services Offered**: List the products or services your company offers.
15. **About Us**: Briefly describe your company and its mission.
16. **Management Structure**: Describe your company's management structure.
17. **Management Experience**: Enter the amount of time your management team has been in place.
18. **Management Location**: Enter the location of your management team.
19. **Contact Address**: Enter the address of the contact person.
20. **Financial Status**: Select the financial status of your company (Established, Starting, Growing).
21. **Business Years**: Enter the number of years your company has been in operation.
22. **Business Hours**: Enter the hours your business operates per day.
23. **Contact Person Title**: Enter the title of the contact person.
24. **Contact Person Role**: Select the role of the contact person (Owner, Director, Manager).
25. **Submission**: This is a note field for any additional information you would like to include with your submission.
