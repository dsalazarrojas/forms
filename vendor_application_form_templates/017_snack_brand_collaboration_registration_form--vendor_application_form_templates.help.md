# Snack Brand Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for snack brand vendors to register their interest in collaborating with us. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information about your brand and your collaboration request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your information as a user (Page 1: user_info)
	* Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
2. Share information about your snack brand (Page 2: snack_info)
	* Describe your brand, including its name and any other relevant details.
3. Make a collaboration request (Page 3: collaboration_request)
	* Outline your request for collaboration, including any specific products or initiatives you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (Name, Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **snack_info** (Name, Text, Required: False):
	+ Describe your snack brand, including its name.
* **collaboration_request** (Name, Text, Required: False):
	+ Outline your request for collaboration, including any specific products or initiatives you are interested in.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have reviewed the form carefully and answered all the questions accurately.
* If you need to make changes or updates to your registration, please contact our support team.
* We will review your registration and be in touch soon to discuss further.
