# Vendor Settlement Authorization Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Settlement Authorization Application Form is designed to collect information from vendors for the purpose of settlement authorization. This form is intended for vendors to provide their banking details, contact information, and other relevant details for the settlement process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your information is processed correctly.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department or organization for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Settlement Form** (`vendor_settlement_form`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is an introduction to the form and is not required to be filled out.
* **General Information** (`general_informatio`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide any additional information about yourself or your business.
* **Banking Details** (`banking_details`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide your banking information, including your account number and routing number.
* **Vendor Settlement Details** (`vendor_settlement_details`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide any additional details about your settlement requirements.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to specify any payment terms or conditions that need to be met for settlement.
* **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id_number`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide your tax ID number, if applicable.
* **Vendor Bank Name** (`vendor_bank_name`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide the name of your bank.
* **Bank Routing Number** (`bank_routing_number`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide the routing number of your bank.
* **Account Holder Name** (`account_holder_name`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide the name of the account holder.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, Select One, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to select the type of account you have (Checking, Savings, or Credit).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to select the payment method you would like to use (Wire Transfer or ACH).
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide your business address.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide your phone number.
* **Fax Number** (`fax_number`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide your fax number.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Fax** (`contact_fax`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide the fax number of the contact person.
* **Vendor Notes** (`vendor_notes`, Text, Not Required):
	+ This field is for you to provide any additional notes or information about your business.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any delays in the settlement process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department or organization for assistance.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
