This form is for vendors to create an application for a shop. Vendors can apply for a shop using this form. 

To be eligible for a shop, the vendor should have a valid license. This form will be used to collect the required information from the vendor. The form fields are categorized into different sections. The vendor is expected to fill in their contact information, order details, and license information. The vendor can add any other relevant details in the "Other Details" field. 

Please note that all fields are optional and are required only for the purpose of application.

## Purpose
The pop_up_shop_vendor_application_form is a form for vendors to apply for a shop. Vendors can fill in their details, order details, and license information to complete this form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the contact information: Enter your contact details such as name, phone number, and email in the contact information section.
2. Add your order details: Provide the start and end dates of your order, as well as the size of your order.
3. Add any other relevant details: If you have any other information that is relevant to your application, add it in the "Other Details" field.
