# Artisanal Brand Showcase Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for Artisanal Brand Showcase Registration, a platform to discover and connect artisanal vendors with their target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your brand's name and description in the first page.
2. Select the type of business you belong to from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your business registration status and primary contact information in the following fields.
4. Complete the product description, booth size requirements, and electricity needs.
5. Optionally provide more details about your production methods and sustainability practices, years in business, and previous markets or events you participated in.
6. Choose a booth size and select if you need electricity or utilities.
7. Upload your brand logo or product images and provide your social media handles.
8. Indicate if you are willing to pay an application or participation fee.
9. Agree to the vendor terms and conditions.
10. Add any additional notes or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand Name** (`brand_name`, text, required): Enter your brand's name as it appears on your products or marketing materials.
* **Type of Artisanal Business** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your business from the dropdown menu.
* **Business Registration Status** (`business_registered`, select_one, required): Select your business registration status from the options provided.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your brand.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your business address.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required): Describe your artisanal products in a few sentences.
* **Production Methods or Techniques** (`production_methods`, text, optional): Optionally, describe your production methods or techniques.
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, text, optional): If applicable, mention your sustainability practices.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of years you have been in business.
* **Previous Market or Event Participation** (`previous_markets`, text, optional): Mention any previous markets or events you have participated in.
* **Booth Size Required** (`booth_size_required`, select_one, required): Choose the booth size you require from the options provided.
* **Electricity or Utilities Required** (`electricity_needed`, select_one, optional): Select if you need electricity or utilities for your booth.
* **Brand Logo or Product Images** (`brand_images`, text, optional): Upload or provide URLs to your brand logo or product images.
* **Social Media Handles** (`social_media`, text, optional): Provide your social media handles (e.g., Instagram, Facebook).
* **Application or Participation Fee** (`participation_fee`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the application or participation fee.
* **I Agree to the Vendor Terms and Conditions** (`agreement_accepted`, select_one, required): Select if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Information or Questions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or questions you have.
