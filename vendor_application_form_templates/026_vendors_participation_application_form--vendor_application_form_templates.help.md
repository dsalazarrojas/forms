# Vendors Participation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your company's participation in our vendor program. It will help us to better understand your capabilities, experiences, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information for each field:

### Page 1: General Information
- **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your company and its products or services.

### Page 2
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please provide the contact information for your company.
- **Vendor Profile** (`vendor_profile`, text, optional): Please provide a detailed description of your company's profile and experience.
- **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): Please describe your products or services that you are offering.

### Page 3
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Please provide details about your payment methods and terms.
- **Business Info** (`business_info`, text, optional): Please provide information about your business operations.
- **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, optional): Please describe the events or activities that your company participates in.

### Page 4
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): Please provide any existing agreements between our company and your company.
- **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Please sign this document to confirm your application.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please provide the current date.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please provide the current time.

### Page 5
- **Vendor Rep** (`vendor_rep`, select_multiple, optional): Are you or your representative available to represent your company at events?
  - Yes
  - No

  - Yes
  - No
- **Vendor Assistant** (`vendor_assistant`, select_multiple, optional): Is your company's assistant available to help with tasks?
  - Yes
  - No
- **Event Manager** (`event_manager`, select_multiple, optional): Is your company's event manager available to handle event related tasks?
  - Yes

  - Option 1
  - Option 2

  - Option 1
  - Option 2
- **Vendor Equipment** (`vendor_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Do you have necessary equipment for events?
  - Yes
  - No
- **Vendor Certificate** (`vendor_certificate`, select_multiple, required): Do you have necessary certificates for events?
  - Yes

  - Yes

## Tips
- Please make sure that you have read and understood all the requirements and guidelines for participation in our vendor program.
- This form is a requirement for all vendor applications. Please complete all the fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before submitting your application.
