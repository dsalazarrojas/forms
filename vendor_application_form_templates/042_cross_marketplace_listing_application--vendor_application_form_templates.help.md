# Cross Marketplace Listing Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a listing for your product on our marketplace platform. Please provide all the necessary information to ensure your product appears correctly and accurately on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Vendor Name** to identify yourself and your business.
2. Enter your **Email** address to allow us to contact you for any further inquiries.
3. Provide your **Website** URL for further information about your business.
4. Choose a **Category** for your product from the list provided.
5. Write a detailed **Description** of your product to help customers understand what it is and what it does.
6. Enter the **Price** of your product, including the currency it is listed in.
7. If applicable, indicate whether your product offers **Shipping** and provide estimated **Shipping Cost**, **Shipping Weight**, and other relevant details.
8. Confirm if you offer **Return Policy** and provide details about it.
9. Enter any additional **Shipping Cost**, **Shipping Weight Value**, and other shipping details as applicable.
10. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`Vendor Name`, text, required): Enter your business name to be displayed on the marketplace platform.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you for further inquiries.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): Provide your website URL for customers to find more information about your business.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Choose a category for your product from the list provided to help customers find it easily.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Write a detailed description of your product to help customers understand it.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price of your product, including the currency it is listed in.
* **Currency** (`Currency`, select_one, required): Choose the currency for your product price from the list provided.
* **Product Images** (`Product Images`, select_multiple, required): Select up to three images of your product to display on the marketplace platform.
* **Shipping** (`Shipping`, select_one, required): Confirm if you offer shipping for your product.
* **Return Policy** (`Return Policy`, text, optional): If you offer a return policy, provide details about it.
* **Shipping Cost** (`Shipping Cost`, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost for your product.
* **Estimated Shipping Cost** (`Estimated Shipping Cost`, number, optional): Enter an estimated shipping cost for your product.
* **Shipping Weight** (`Shipping Weight`, number, optional): Enter the shipping weight of your product.
* **Shipping Height** (`Shipping Height`, number, optional): Enter the shipping height of your product.
* **Shipping Width** (`Shipping Width`, number, optional): Enter the shipping width of your product.
* **Shipping Length** (`Shipping Length`, number, optional): Enter the shipping length of your product.
* **Shipping Weight Unit** (`Shipping Weight Unit`, select_one, optional): Choose the shipping weight unit from the list provided.
* **Shipping Cost Currency** (`Shipping Cost Currency`, select_one, optional): Choose the currency for your shipping cost from the list provided.
* **Shipping Cost Value** (`Shipping Cost Value`, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost value.
* **Shipping Weight Value** (`Shipping Weight Value`, number, optional): Enter the shipping weight value.
* **Shipping Weight Unit 2** (`Shipping Weight Unit 2`, select_one, optional): Choose the shipping weight unit 2 from the list provided.
* **Shipping Price** (`Shipping Price`, number, optional): Enter the shipping price.
* **Shipping Price Currency** (`Shipping Price Currency`, select_one, optional): Choose the currency for your shipping price from the list provided.
* **Shipping Price Value** (`Shipping Price Value`, number, optional): Enter the shipping price value.
