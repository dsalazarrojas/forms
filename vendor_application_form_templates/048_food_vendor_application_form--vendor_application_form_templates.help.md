# Food Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Vendor Application Form is designed to collect information from food vendors to participate in an event or sell their products in a specific context. This form is used to assess the suitability of the vendor for the event or product sale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vendor's information on the first page.
2. Provide contact information on the second page.
3. Enter product details on the third page.
4. Select the event or context in which you would like to participate on the fourth page.
5. Indicate whether you have application documents available on the fifth page.
6. Choose your preferred payment method on the sixth page.
7. Specify your availability for events or product sales on the seventh page.
8. Add any additional information on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business name, address, and other identifying details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, `text`, required: false): Describe your products or services offered.
* **Event Selection** (`event_selection`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the event or context in which you would like to participate (Yes or No).
* **Application Documents** (`application_documents`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you have any documents to be included with your application (Yes or No).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred payment method (Yes or No).
* **Vendor Availability** (`vendor_availability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify your availability for events or product sales (Yes or No).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any other relevant information about your business or application.
