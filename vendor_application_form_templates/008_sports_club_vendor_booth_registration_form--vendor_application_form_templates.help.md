# Sports Club Vendor Booth Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Club Vendor Booth Registration Form is a registration form for vendors who want to participate in a sports-related event. This form is used to collect essential information from vendors, including their details, payment terms, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by filling out the "Vendor Details" field with your name and any other relevant details about your vendor.
2. In the "Booth Needs" field, describe the needs of your booth, such as equipment or supplies required.
3. In the "Payment Details" field, select whether you are able to pay on time (Yes/No) and what specific payment terms you agree to (Yes/No).
4. In the "Payment Amount" field, enter the amount you are willing to pay.
5. In the "Email" field, enter your email address so we can contact you with more information about the event.
6. In the "Phone" field, enter your phone number so we can contact you with more information about the event.
7. The "Note" field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information that's not captured in the previous fields.
8. The "Date" and "Time" fields are also optional and can be used to schedule any interviews or meetings with the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details about your vendor.
* **Booth Needs** (`booth_needs`, `text`, required): Describe the needs of your booth, such as equipment or supplies required.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are able to pay on time (Yes/No) and what specific payment terms you agree to (Yes/No).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you are willing to pay.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with more information about the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with more information about the event.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information that's not captured in the previous fields.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you are available for an interview or meeting.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you are available for an interview or meeting.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before completing this form.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
