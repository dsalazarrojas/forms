# Retail Tech Integration Partner Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential partners about their retail technology integration interests and details. It helps our team assess and collaborate with qualified partners.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your retail tech integration interest (yes or no).
2. Choose your retail tech integration interests (yes or no) for each category.
3. Enter a detailed description of your integration.
4. Provide your business email.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Select your integration type (yes or no).
7. Choose your integration types (yes or no) for each category.
8. Enter your expected integration date and time.
9. Add any additional notes about your integration.
10. Indicate if you have other integration interests (yes or no).
11. Select your retail tech integration status (yes or no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your retail tech integration interest?** (`retail_tech_integration_interest`, select_one, required): This question asks if you have an interest in integrating your retail technology with our system.
* **What are your retail tech integration interests?** (`retail_tech_integration_interests`, select_multiple, required): This question allows you to select multiple interests related to your integration.
* **Tell us more about your integration** (`integration_details`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of your retail technology integration.
* **What is your business email?** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone_number`, number, required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **What is your integration type?** (`integration_type`, select_one, required): This question asks if you have an integration type (yes or no).
* **What are your integration types?** (`integration_types`, select_multiple, required): This question allows you to select multiple integration types (yes or no).
* **What is your integration date?** (`integration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your expected integration.
* **What is your integration time?** (`integration_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your expected integration.
* **Do you have any notes about your integration?** (`integration_notes`, text, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your integration.
* **Do you have any other integration interests?** (`other_integration_interests`, select_multiple, required): This question allows you to select if you have other integration interests (yes or no).
* **What is your retail tech integration status?** (`retail_tech_integration_status`, select_one, required): This question asks if you have an active retail tech integration (yes or no).
