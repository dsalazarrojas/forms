# vendor_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vendor_inquiry_form" is a form used to gather information from vendors about their businesses, which helps us maintain accurate and up-to-date internal records and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "Required". These fields are essential for our records.
2. For optional fields, provide as much information as possible to help us better understand your business.
3. Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Information** (Required): This field is used to gather basic information about your business. Please provide a brief description of your business.
* **Email**: This field is for your company's or organization's email address. Please use the format "example@example.com".
* **Contact Number** (Optional): This is your company's or organization's contact number. This can be a phone number or other communication method.
* **Business Name** (Required): This is the name of your business or organization. Please make sure it's the same as your official business name.
* **Address**: This is your business's physical address, including the street address or suite number.
* **Zip** (Optional): This is your business's or organization's zip code. Please make sure it's accurate.
* **Business Description** (Optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of your business. Keep it concise and up-to-date.
* **Products Sold** (Optional): If your business sells products, list them here. This will help us understand your offerings better.
* **Contact Person** (Optional): This is the name of the person we should contact regarding your business.
* **Contact Email** (Optional): This is the email address of the contact person. Please use the format "example@example.com".
* **Contact Phone** (Optional): This is the contact person's phone number.
* **Business Hours** (Optional): If your business operates on a schedule, list your hours of operation here. This will help us plan our interactions better.
* **Website** (Optional): This is the URL of your business's website. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
- If you have any updates or changes to your business information, please let us know so we can update our records accordingly.
