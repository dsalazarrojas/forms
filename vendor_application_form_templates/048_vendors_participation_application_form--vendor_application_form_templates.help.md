# Vendors Participation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendors Participation Application Form is used to collect basic information from vendors participating in the application process. This information will be used to facilitate communication and ensure that vendors can complete the application process efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields to provide accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that your email address is correct and up-to-date.
3. Select all relevant options for the vendor type field.
4. Enter a clear and concise description of your services or products.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your application.
6. Enter the date and time you are available to start the application process.
7. Confirm your availability for the selected time.
8. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendors Participation Application Form** (`vendor_participation_application_form`, text, optional): This field is not required, and it's not necessary to fill it out. It's here to help identify the form type.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your name or the name of your company.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your correct and up-to-date email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you for further assistance.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant options from the list. This will help us understand your business type.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a clear and concise description of your services or products.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start the application process.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Confirm your availability for the selected time.
