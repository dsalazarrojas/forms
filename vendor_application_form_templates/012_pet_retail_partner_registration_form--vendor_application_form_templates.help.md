# Pet Retail Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Retail Partner Registration Form is designed to register pet retail partners with the company, allowing them to be listed on the platform and potentially receive benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields on the "partner_details" page.
2. Proceed to the next page to provide contact details.
3. Continue to the next page to provide store details.
4. Next, provide product details.
5. Finally, review and confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Details** (`partner_details`): This field is where you can provide information about your pet retail business, such as its name and address.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your company's contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Store Details** (`store_details`): Provide information about your store, including its name, address, and hours of operation.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`): Describe your products, including their names and descriptions.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`): Confirm that you have accurately filled out the form and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any issues, contact form@gic.mx.
