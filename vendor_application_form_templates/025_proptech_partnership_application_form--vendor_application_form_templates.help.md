# PropTech Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential partners for collaboration and partnership opportunities. It helps us to better understand your company's background, goals, and expectations for the potential partnership. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form sections and identify the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3.  For optional fields, provide relevant information if you have it. If you don't have it, you can leave it blank.
4.  Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Partner Information** (`partner_info`, text, required): This section is for you to describe your company, including its name, address, and any other relevant information.
    *   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): This section is for you to provide your contact details, including your name, title, and email address.
    *   **Collaboration Goals** (`collaboration_goals`, text, required): This section is for you to describe the potential partnership goals and objectives.
    *   **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, required): This section is for you to describe your company's background and history.
    *   **Partnership Terms** (`partnership_terms`, text, required): This section is for you to outline the terms and conditions of the potential partnership.
    *   **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required): This section is for you to provide any relevant payment information, including payment methods and schedules.
    *   **Partnership Agreement** (`agreement`, text, required): This section is for you to describe the terms of the potential partnership agreement.
    *   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): This section is for you to identify a contact person for the potential partnership.
    *   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This section is for you to provide a valid email address of the contact person.
    *   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): This section is for you to provide a valid phone number of the contact person.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
