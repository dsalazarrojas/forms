# easter_event_vendor_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for vendors to register themselves for Easter events. Vendors can provide their company name, contact information, and other details to be listed on the Easter event's vendor page.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure you are a valid vendor for the Easter event by checking with the event organizers.
2.  Fill out the form with your vendor information.
3.  Submit the form to submit your vendor listing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Company Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company name as it will be displayed on the vendor listing.
*   **Email** (`vendor_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your company's email address for communication purposes.
*   **Contact Name** (`vendor_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your company.
*   **Phone Number** (`vendor_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Category** (`vendor_category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if your company will be selling items at the Easter event, 'No' if you will not.
*   **Description** (`vendor_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your company and what you will be selling at the Easter event.
*   **Address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's address for shipping or delivery purposes.
*   **Website** (`vendor_website`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's website for more information.
*   **FTP Address** (`vendor_ftp_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address for your FTP server for file transfers.
*   **Logo URL** (`vendor_image_url`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the URL for your company logo.
*   **Status** (`vendor_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Active' if your company is currently active and participating in the Easter event, 'Inactive' if not.
*   **Language** (`vendor_language`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the language you will be using at the Easter event.
*   **Currency** (`vendor_currency`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the currency you will be using at the Easter event.
*   **Price** (`vendor_price`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your product prices in the currency you selected.
*   **Terms** (`vendor_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any special terms or conditions for your products.
*   **Notes** (`vendor_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about your company or products.
