# Heat Resistant Material Vendor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Heat Resistant Material Vendor Application Form is designed to collect information from vendors who supply heat-resistant materials. This form is used to gather essential details about the vendor, their contact information, and certification. By completing this form, vendors can demonstrate their qualifications and provide necessary documentation for our evaluation and consideration.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the vendor name field with your company or business name.
2. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your company.
3. Provide your company's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
4. Enter your company's address where we can send correspondence.
5. Enter the zip code for your company's location.
6. Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach out to you.
7. Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates.
8. Select the correct certification that applies to your company's expertise in heat-resistant materials.
9. Upload any relevant technical documentation or notes to support your application.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name (Text)**: Enter your company or business name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Contact Person (Text)**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your company.
* **Company Name (Text)**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Address (Text)**: Enter your company's address where we can send correspondence.
* **Zip (Text)**: Enter the zip code for your company's location.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach out to you.
* **Email (Text with email verification)**: Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates.
* **Certification (Select One)**: Select the correct certification that applies to your company's expertise in heat-resistant materials.
	+ Option 1: Level 1 Certification
	+ Option 2: Level 2 Certification
	+ Option 3: Level 3 Certification
* **Technical Document Upload (Note)**: Upload any relevant technical documentation or notes to support your application.
* **Notes (Note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with our team.
