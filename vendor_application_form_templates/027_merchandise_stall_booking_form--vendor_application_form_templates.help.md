## merchandise_stall_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The merchandise_stall_booking_form is used to book a merchandise stall for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the event details such as the event name, date, and venue.
* Select the details of the merchandise being sold such as the type and quantity.
* Provide the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
