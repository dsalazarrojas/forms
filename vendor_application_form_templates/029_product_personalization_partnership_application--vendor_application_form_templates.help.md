# Product Personalization Partnership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for companies interested in establishing a product personalization partnership with us. The information collected will be used to evaluate potential partners and initiate a partnership process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your company's information, such as your company name, email address, phone number, and website (if applicable).
2. Provide a brief description of your company and the products/services you offer.
3. Select the type of company you represent (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
4. List the products you offer (type in the text field).
5. Choose the products you are interested in offering through our platform (select multiple options).
6. Specify what you are interested in (open-ended text field).
7. If applicable, describe the opportunities you bring to the table (open-ended text field).
8. Please provide a brief overview of your review process (note field).
9. Outline the next steps you'd like to take to move forward (open-ended text field).
10. In the submission field, provide any additional information or files you'd like to submit (open-ended text field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company**: <name> (`company`, text, required: false): Enter your company's name, as this will be used to identify your business for our internal records.
* **Email**: <address> (`email`, email, required: false): Please provide a valid email address for us to contact you and your team.
* **Phone**: <number> (`phone`, text, required: false): If you have a phone number, please type it in. This will help us reach you more easily.
* **Website**: <address> (`website`, text, required: false): If your company has a website, please provide the URL for us to access more information about your business.
* **Description**: <text> (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your company and the products/services you offer.
* **Company Type**: <option> (`company_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of company you represent from the options provided.
* **Products**: <list> (`products`, text, required: false): List the products you offer.
* **Product Types**: <options> (`product_types`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the products you are interested in offering through our platform.
* **Interested In**: <text> (`interested_in`, text, required: false): Specify what you are interested in, and how it relates to our partnership.
* **Opportunities**: <text> (`opportunities`, text, required: false): If applicable, describe the opportunities you bring to the table.
* **Review Process**: <note> (`review_process`, note, required: false): Provide a brief overview of your review process for this opportunity.
* **Next Steps**: <text> (`next_steps`, text, required: false): Outline the next steps you'd like to take to move forward with our partnership.
* **Submission**: <text> (`submission`, text, required: false): In this field, provide any additional information or files you'd like to submit with your application.

Note: This form is intended to be a basic guide. If you're unsure about any of these fields or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
