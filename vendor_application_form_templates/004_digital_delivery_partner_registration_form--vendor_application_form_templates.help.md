# digital_delivery_partner_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a digital delivery partner registration form. It's designed for partners who want to be part of the platform's delivery network. Fill out this form to provide necessary information about yourself and your business.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields for your personal information:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Email: Enter your business email address.
	* Address: Enter your business address.
	* Business Phone: Enter your business phone number.
	* Business License: Enter your business license information.
2. Add your vehicle details:
	* Vehicle Registration Number: Enter the vehicle registration number.
	* Vehicle Year: Enter the year of manufacture for your vehicle.
	* Vehicle Make: Enter the make of your vehicle.
	* Vehicle Model: Enter the model of your vehicle.
	* Vehicle Color: Enter the color of your vehicle.
3. Select your driver and vehicle types:
	* Driver Type: Select the type of driver you are.
	* Vehicle Type: Select the type of vehicle you own.
4. Additional information:
	* Note: You can add any additional notes or information about yourself and your vehicle.
5. Select additional options:
	* Select One: Choose one from the options provided.
	* Select Multiple: Select multiple options from the provided options.
