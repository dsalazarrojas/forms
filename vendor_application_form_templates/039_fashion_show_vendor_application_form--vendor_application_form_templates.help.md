# Fashion Show Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Show Vendor Application Form is used to collect information from vendors who want to participate in the fashion show. The form helps our team review and verify the vendor's information to ensure they meet the requirements for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your business. You can submit the form multiple times, but please note that each submission will be reviewed and may be subject to approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business name or the name under which you will be participating in the fashion show.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the person we can reach out to with questions or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **Business License** (`business_license`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business license number or ID.
* **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter your tax ID number or ID.
* **About the Show** (`about_the_show`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the fashion show and why your business is interested in participating.
* **What We Will Sell** (`what_we_will_sell`, `text`, required: false): List the products or services you will be selling or offering at the fashion show.
* **How We Price** (`how_we_pricing`, `text`, required: false): Describe your pricing and payment policies.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, `text`, required: false): List your store hours and availability during the fashion show.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your business type (e.g. clothing store, boutique, online store).
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, `text`, required: false): Select the type of store you have (Boutique, Online, Brick, or Other).
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business website URL.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `text`, required: false): Enter your social media handles (e.g. Instagram, Facebook, Twitter).
* **Store Image URL** (`store_image_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter a URL to your store image or logo.
* **Logo Image URL** (`logo_image_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter a URL to your business logo.
* **Product Images URL** (`product_images_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter a URL to your product images.
* **Payment Methods** (`payment_methods`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the payment methods you accept (Cash, Credit, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Shipping Methods** (`shipping_methods`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the shipping methods you offer (In-store Pickup, Free shipping, Flat Rate, Other).
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter your shipping cost (if applicable).
* **Shipping Policy** (`shipping_policy`, `text`, required: false): Describe your shipping policy.
* **Return Policy** (`return_policy`, `text`, required: false): Describe your return policy.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter your store location.
* **Store Description** (`store_description`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your store and products/services offered.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you need help or clarification, contact us.
* We will review and verify the information provided.
* You will be notified if your application is approved or rejected.
* If approved, you will receive further instructions on how to prepare for the fashion show.
