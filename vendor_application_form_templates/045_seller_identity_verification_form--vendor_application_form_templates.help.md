# Seller Identity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seller Identity Verification Form is a document used to verify the identity of sellers who are looking to sell on our platform. It is designed to collect essential information about the seller, which will be used for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Seller Identity Verification Form** title in the text field provided.
2. Fill in your **First Name** in the corresponding text field.
3. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
4. Provide your **Email Address** in the format of `username@domain.com` or `username@domain`.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field.
6. Fill in your **Business Name** (if applicable) in the text field.
7. Enter your **ID Number** (if applicable) in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Identity Verification Form** (`seller_identity_verification_form`, text, required/optional: optional): This is the title of the form that you are providing your identity information on.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional: optional): Please fill in your first name to continue with the verification process.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional: optional): Fill in your last name to continue with the verification process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional: optional): Enter your email address in the format of `username@domain.com` or `username@domain`.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter your phone number in the format of `123-456-7890`.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional: optional): This field is currently not used in the verification process.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional: optional): If you are providing information for a business, please fill in the business name.
* **ID Number** (`id_number`, text, required/optional: optional): If you are providing information for a business, please fill in the ID number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid any delays in the verification process.
* If you are unsure of any information, please contact our support team for guidance.
