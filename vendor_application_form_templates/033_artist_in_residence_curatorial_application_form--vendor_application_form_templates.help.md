# Artist In Residence Curatorial Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for artists to apply for the Artist-in-Residence Curatorial Program. It is a curated selection process that aims to bring together local artists for a residency, providing them with a unique opportunity to grow and showcase their talents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much information as possible.
2. Proofread your application before submitting it to ensure accuracy and avoid duplication.
3. Follow the instructions carefully, and do not hesitate to ask if you need clarification.
4. Review the provided fields and ensure you understand the requirements and focus of the residency program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your professional name or pseudonym.
* **Legal Name** (`legal_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact number.
* **Website or Portfolio URL** (`artist_website`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a link to your website or portfolio.
* **Primary Artistic Medium** (`primary_medium`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose your main area of artistic practice (e.g., Painting, Sculpture, Photography, etc.).
* **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`, `text`, `required`): Describe your artistic vision and practice.
* **Years Active as an Artist** (`years_active`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the number of years you have been practicing as an artist.
* **Relevant Education or Training** (`education`, `text`, `optional`): List any relevant degrees, certifications, or training you have received.
* **Exhibition History** (`exhibition_history`, `text`, `required`): List your major exhibitions and shows.
* **Awards and Recognition** (`awards_recognition`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any grants, fellowships, or publications you have received.
* **Focus of Artist-in-Residence Program** (`residency_focus`, `text`, `required`): Describe what you would like to explore during the residency.
* **Project or Curatorial Proposal** (`project_description`, `text`, `required`): Describe your proposal in detail.
* **Proposed Outcomes or Deliverables** (`proposed_outcomes`, `text`, `required`): Enter what you plan to create or exhibit during the residency.
* **Proposed Residency Duration** (`residency_duration`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the duration of your proposed residency (1 month, 2-3 months, 4-6 months, 6-12 months, 12+ months, or Flexible).
* **Preferred Dates or Timeframe** (`preferred_dates`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your preferred dates or timeframe for the residency.
* **Technical or Space Requirements** (`technical_requirements`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any equipment, space, or resources you need for the residency.
* **Additional Information or Statement** (`additional_materials`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any other information for the selection committee.
* **I Confirm This Application is Accurate** (`apply_confirmation`, `select_one`, `required`): Confirm that your application is accurate (Yes, I Confirm or No, I Need to Make Changes).
