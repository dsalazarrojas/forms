# 2016 Downtown Dog Walk Vendor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2016 Downtown Dog Walk Vendor Registration form collects information about your business and services, enabling us to effectively plan and coordinate the event. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately and comprehensively.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each section header to expand or collapse the respective section.
- Read the hint for each field carefully to understand what information is required.
- Ensure all required fields are filled (* denotes required fields).
- Use the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (2, Full name, *): Enter the name that will appear on all event materials.
* **Email address** (3, Email address, *): Use a valid email for event communications.
* **Phone number** (4, Phone number, ): Provide a contact number for quick questions.
* **Mailing address** (5, Mailing address, ): Include city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Business name** (7, Business name, *): The official registered name of your company.
* **Business type** (8, Business type, *): Select the category that best describes your services.
* **Services offered** (11, Services offered, *): Select all that apply for your services.
* **Experience level** (12, Experience level, *): Choose the level that best describes your team.
* **How did you hear about us?** (13, How did you hear about us?, *): Select the source that led you to this event.
* **Logo URL** (15, Logo URL, ): Provide a link to a high-resolution PNG or JPG file.
* **Brochure URL** (16, Brochure URL, ): Optional. Link to a downloadable PDF.
* **Promotional video URL** (17, Promotional video URL, ): Optional. Link to a YouTube or Vimeo video.
* **Preferred walk dates** (19, Preferred walk dates, ): Choose the dates you are available for the event.
* **Preferred walk times** (20, Preferred walk times, ): Specify morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Number of walks per week** (21, Number of walks per week, ): Enter the maximum walks your team can handle.
* **Additional comments or notes** (22, Additional comments or notes, ): Provide any extra information that will help us plan.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled before submitting.
- Use accurate and up-to-date information to ensure effective event planning.
- In case you encounter any issues, contact us at form@gic.mx.
