# Retail Media Network Partner Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register a partner for the Retail Media Network, a collaboration between brands and partners for media-related services. By filling out this form, you'll be providing essential information to be considered as a potential partner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company details, including any relevant information about your business.
2. Provide contact information about your company, including address, phone number, and email.
3. Describe your partnership proposal, highlighting how you can contribute to the Retail Media Network.
4. Select your role in the partnership (Brand, Agency, or Tech Provider).
5. Enter your company's name, website, and other essential details.
6. Complete any additional required fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (`company_details`): This is the main section for your company's description.
	+ **Title:** This is your company's title or name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Provide contact information about your company.
	+ **Address:** Enter your company's physical address.
	+ **Phone:** Enter your company's phone number.
	+ **Email:** Enter your company's email address.
* **Partnership Proposal** (`partnership_proposal`): Describe how you can contribute to the Retail Media Network.
* **Partnership Proposal 2** (`partnership_proposal_2`): If applicable, provide additional partnership details.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Provide the name of the contact person or representative.
* **Role** (`role`): Select your role in the partnership (Brand, Agency, or Tech Provider).
	+ **Brand:** Partner as a brand.
	+ **Agency:** Partner as an agency.
	+ **Tech Provider:** Partner as a tech provider.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your company's name.
* **Website** (`website`): Enter your company's website URL.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your company's physical address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter your city or location.
* **State** (`state`): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description about your company.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill in all relevant information for a complete registration.
