# Beard Pact Act Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beard Pact Act Form is used for vendors to provide information about their business and products to ensure compliance with the PACT Act. It is a crucial step in the onboarding process and helps to verify vendor information and ensure they are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of doing business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Provide all required information.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (required): Enter your business name.
* **Contact Person** (required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Address** (required): Enter your full business address.
* **Business Type** (required): Select the type of entity your business is (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, Other).
* **Tax ID or EIN** (required): Enter your tax identification or EIN number.
* **Product Category** (required): Select the category of products your business supplies (e.g., Beard Oils, Beard Balms, Grooming Tools, Packaging, Raw Ingredients, Other).
* **Years in Business** (required): Select how long your business has been operating.
* **Compliance Certifications** (required): Select all the compliance certifications your business holds (e.g., FDA Registered, GMP Certified, ISO Certified, Organic Certified, None).
* **Product Liability Insurance** (required): Select whether you have product liability insurance.
* **Insurance Coverage Amount** (optional): Enter the insurance coverage amount.
* **Quality Assurance Process** (required): Describe your quality assurance process.
* **Delivery Capability** (required): Select your delivery options (e.g., Local Only, Regional, National, International).
* **Minimum Order Quantity** (required): Enter the minimum order quantity.
* **Payment Terms** (required): Select your payment terms (e.g., Net 30, Net 60, Due on Receipt, 50 Percent Deposit, Other).
* **References Available** (required): Select whether you can provide references.
* **PACT Act Compliance Acknowledged** (required): Select whether you acknowledge PACT Act compliance.
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted** (required): Select whether you have accepted the terms and conditions.
* **Authorized Signature** (required): Enter your full name.
* **Signature Date** (required): Enter the date you signed the form.

Please note that some fields are mandatory and required to be completed accurately and truthfully. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
