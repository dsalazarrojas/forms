# Lash Studio Franchise Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Studio Franchise Application Form is a standard application form for potential franchisees to apply for a Lash Studio franchise. This form gathers contact, business, and franchise-specific information to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all required fields filled in.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Ensure that your answers are clear and concise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Lash Studio Franchise Application Form
* **Lash Studio Franchise Application Form** (`franchise_application_form`, `note`, required: false): This field is a brief summary of your application. Please provide a concise description of your interest in joining the Lash Studio franchise program.

### Page 2 - Contact Info
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`Email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business email address here.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business phone number here.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name here.

### Page 3 - Business Info
* **Business Info** (`Business Info`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the following options:
	+ **Beauty Brand**
	+ **Lash Business**
	+ **Beauty Services**

### Page 4 - Franchise Info
* **Franchise Info** (`Franchise Info`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date you plan to open your Lash Studio franchise.

### Page 5 - Store Info
* **Store Info** (`Store Info`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your store location and layout.

### Page 6 - Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your expected payment method for the franchise fees (e.g., 1000, 2000, etc.).

### Page 7 - Franchise Fees
* **Franchise Fees** (`Franchise Fees`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your expected payment method for the franchise fees (e.g., 1000, 2000, etc.).
