# Black Excellence Expo Vendor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for vendors who want to participate in the Black Excellence Expo. It helps us understand your business and ensure a smooth experience for both you and our event team.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your business information, including your business name, contact person, and contact email.
- Select all the product categories that apply to your business.
- Indicate if you will require electricity at your booth and if you've provided proof of insurance.
- Provide your expected arrival date for setup.
- Choose your preferred payment method for the booth fee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business name** (vendor_business_name, text, required): Your legal or trade name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
* **Contact person** (contact_person, text, required): The primary representative of your business who will be responsible for communicating with us and our team.
* **Contact email** (contact_email, email, required): The email address we'll use to send you registration and update notifications.
* **Contact phone number** (contact_phone, text, required): The phone number we'll use to contact you for last-minute updates or questions.
* **Product categories** (product_categories, select_multiple, required): Select all the categories that apply to your business. This will help us determine your booth placement and promote your business to the right audience.
* **Booth size requested** (booth_size_requested, text, required): Please specify the approximate size of your booth in feet. This will help us reserve the correct space for you.
* **Electricity required** (electricity_required, select_one, required): If you'll need electricity for your booth, please select 'True'. If not, select 'False'.
* **Proof of insurance provided** (insurance_provided, select_one, required): If you have proof of insurance, select 'True'. If not, select 'False'.
* **Expected arrival date for setup** (arrival_date, date, required): Please provide the date you plan to arrive for setup and installation of your booth.
* **Payment method for booth fee** (payment_method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment option: Invoice, Credit card, or Bank transfer.
* **Additional requirements** (additional_requirements, text, required): If you have any further information or requests that we should be aware of, please include them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
- We'll follow up with you after submission to confirm your registration and provide further instructions.
