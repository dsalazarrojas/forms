# Government Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for government vendors to register their businesses and provide essential information for review. It helps the government to keep track of vendors and their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your supplier details, contact person, email, phone number, address, and other relevant qualifications.
2. Review your qualifications and ensure they are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Upload any supporting documents if required.
4. Assign a reviewer to the form if necessary.
5. Confirm that the form has been created by the correct person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Details**: Enter the name of your supplier and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for communication with the government.
* **Email**: Enter your company's email address where you can be reached for official purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your company's phone number where you can be reached for official purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your company's address where you can be reached for official purposes.
* **Qualification Details**: Describe your company's qualifications and experience in the field.
* **Review Status**: Choose the status of your review from "Active" or "Inactive" depending on your company's status with the government.
* **Upload Document**: Upload any supporting documents that you want to provide with your application.
* **Assigned To**: Assign a reviewer to the form if you have a specific contact person.
* **Created By**: Confirm that this form has been created by the correct person in your company.
* **Date Of Creation**: Enter the date when this form was created in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the correct format for the date of creation (YYYY-MM-DD).
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* If you need to make any changes to your application, please contact the government contact person.
