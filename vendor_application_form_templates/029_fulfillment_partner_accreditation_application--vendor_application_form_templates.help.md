# Fulfillment Partner Accreditation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fulfillment Partner Accreditation Application form is designed to collect information about your company's services, experience, and other details relevant to your accreditation status. Please answer the questions carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Please fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as you progress through the form.
2. Select the correct options for each field from the provided dropdown menus.
3. Make sure to check the "Accreditation Status" field to ensure that you are selecting the correct options.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`id: 1`): Enter your company's official name.
* **Company Type** (`id: 2`): Select the type of company you represent (Logistics, 3PL, etc.).
* **Contact Person** (`id: 3`): Enter the name of the contact person for your company.
* **Email** (`id: 4`): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 5`): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Services** (`id: 6`): Select the services your company provides (Warehousing, Transportation, Packaging, etc.).
* **Experience** (`id: 7`): Select your company's experience level (Less than 5 years, 5-10 years, etc.).
* **Warehouse** (`id: 8`): Enter the number of warehouses your company has.
* **Capacity** (`id: 9`): Enter the capacity of your company's warehouse.
* **Location** (`id: 10`): Select the location of your company's warehouse (Asia, Europe, North America, etc.).
* **Description** (`id: 11`): Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Accreditation Status** (`id: 12`): Select the current accreditation status of your company (Active, Inactive, Pending, etc.).
* **Date** (`id: 13`): Select the date of your company's accreditation.
* **Notes** (`id: 14`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your company.
* **Company Address** (`id: 15`): Enter your company's address.
* **Accreditation Date** (`id: 16`): Select the accreditation date of your company.
* **Accreditation ID** (`id: 17`): Enter your company's accreditation ID.
* **Accreditation Number** (`id: 18`): Enter your company's accreditation number.
* **Accreditation Notes** (`id: 19`): Enter any notes about your company's accreditation.
* **Contact Address** (`id: 20`): Enter the address of your contact person.
* **Accreditation Status Reason** (`id: 21`): Select the reason for your company's accreditation status.
* **Accreditation Note** (`id: 22`): Enter any notes about your company's accreditation.
* **Reason for Accreditation** (`id: 23`): Select the reason for your company's accreditation.
* **Reapply Date** (`id: 24`): Select the date to reapply for accreditation.
* **Reapply Reason** (`id: 25`): Select the reason to reapply for accreditation.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
