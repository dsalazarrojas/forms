# AI Based Event Security Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from vendors providing AI-based security services to help us evaluate and select suitable vendors for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field explanation to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Company Name** (`vendor_name`, required): Enter the legal name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required): Full name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Email address for correspondence.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required): Include country code and phone number.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, required): Title of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required): Date when the event will take place.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, required): City and venue (if applicable).
* **Attendance Type** (`attendance_type`, required): Choose the mode of attendance (In-person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
* **Expected Attendance** (`expected_attendance`, required): Estimated number of attendees.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, required): Select the primary AI-based security service you propose (Crowd Monitoring, Threat Detection, Access Control, Real-time Analytics, or Incident Response).
* **Deployment Timeframe** (`deployment_timeframe`, required): Estimated weeks needed for deployment.
* **Total Service Cost** (`service_cost`, required): Enter the total cost in US dollars.
* **Preferred Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, required): Choose your preferred payment schedule (30-day net, 60-day net, or 90-day net).
* **ISO 27001 Certification** (`iso_certification`, required): Select if ISO 27001 certified.
* **PCI DSS Compliance** (`pci_dss`, required): Select if PCI DSS compliant.
* **Reference Contact (Email/Phone)** (`reference_contact`, optional): Provide an email address or phone number for a past client (not required but recommended).
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, required): Rate your overall experience (1 = worst, 10 = best).
* **Recommend Partnership** (`recommend`, required): Select if you would recommend our partnership for future events.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Any other information you'd like to share (not required but recommended).
