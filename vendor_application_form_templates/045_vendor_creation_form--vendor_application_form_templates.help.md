# Vendor Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Creation Form is used to create a new vendor in the system. This form collects necessary information to identify and manage vendor relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the corresponding information.
2. Ensure you have filled out the required fields (marked as "required" in each field description).
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's address.
* **ZIP** (`zip`, number, optional): Enter the vendor's ZIP code.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your vendor's documentation or contact our support team.
* If you are trying to create multiple vendors at once, please fill out a new form for each vendor.
