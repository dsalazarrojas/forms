# Vendor Parking Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to a parking area for vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First name
2. Fill in your Last name
3. Enter your Email address
4. Enter the Vendor name you are representing
5. Enter the name of the contact person
6. Enter the contact number of the contact person
7. Enter the desired parking location
8. Select the date you plan to park
9. Select the time you plan to park
10. Provide the reason for your parking request

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Vendor name** (optional): Enter the name of the company or entity you are representing.
* **Contact name** (optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your company or entity.
* **Contact number** (optional): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Parking location** (required): Enter the specific parking location you are requesting access to.
* **Parking date** (optional): Select the date you plan to park. Note: This is not a required field, but it's best to provide a specific date to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* **Parking time** (optional): Select the time you plan to park. Note: This is not a required field, but it's best to provide a specific time to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* **Reason for requesting parking** (optional): Provide a brief explanation of why you need access to the parking area.
