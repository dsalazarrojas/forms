# pet microchip vendor application form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for pet microchip vendors to apply for certification, partnership, or registration. You will need to provide detailed information about your vendor, contact, shipping, and microchip products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor information, including your name and any relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information, such as your name and email.
3. Enter your shipping information, including your address and any other relevant details.
4. Add your payment details, such as payment method and payment date.
5. Describe your microchip products, including types and models.
6. Choose your vendor status and add any additional comments.
7. Select the types of microchips you offer.
8. Review your completed form to ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_information`, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a brief introduction about your company or business. Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, required/optional): Enter your name and email address so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_information`, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your shipping address and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, required/optional): Enter your payment method and payment date to complete this field.
* **Microchip Details** (`microchip_details`, required/optional): Describe your microchip products, including types and models.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information you think we should know.
* **Vendor Status** (`vendor_status`, required/optional): Choose one of the two options to indicate your vendor status.
* **Vendor Notes** (`vendor_notes`, required/optional): Add any notes you have about your business or products.
* **Microchip Types** (`microchip_types`, required/optional): Select all the types of microchips you offer.
* **Microchip Models** (`microchip_models`, required/optional): Enter any additional microchip models you offer.
