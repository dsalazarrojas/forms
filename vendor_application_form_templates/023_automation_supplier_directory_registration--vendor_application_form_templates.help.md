# Automation Supplier Directory Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automation Supplier Directory is a collection of information about suppliers of automation-related services and solutions. This form is used to register a supplier company in the directory.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your company's basic information (name, email, phone number, website, year established).
2. Choose your primary contact person and fill in their details.
3. Provide your business address.
4. Select the types of solutions and services your company offers.
5. Choose the industries you serve.
6. Select any relevant certifications your company holds.
7. (Optional) Mention any compliance standards your company supports.
8. Provide the date your company registered with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards and documents.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, `email`, required): Enter your company's email address. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your company's phone number. This can be a local or international number.
* **Year Established** (`established_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year your company was established.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Job Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, required): Enter the job title of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your primary contact person.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your company's street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city location.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province location.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code or postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter your country of operation.
* **Types of Solutions Offered** (`solution_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the types of solutions and services your company offers. These include RPA Software, Integration Platform, Testing Tools, Consulting Services, Implementation Services, Training and Support, and Hardware.
* **Describe Your Main Solutions** (`solution_description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your main solutions and services offered.
* **Target Industries** (`target_industries`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the industries your company serves. These include Manufacturing, Financial Services, Healthcare, Retail, Telecommunications, Energy, Government, and Education.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all relevant certifications your company holds. These include ISO 9001, ISO 27001, SOC 2, Industry-Specific Certs, and Partner Certified.
* **Compliance Standards Supported** (`compliance_standards`, `text`, optional): Optional field to mention any compliance standards your company supports.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your company registered with us.
