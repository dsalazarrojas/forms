# Vendor Application For Night Market - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Application For Night Market form is a tool for vendors to provide essential information about themselves and their products, allowing market organizers to review and approve their applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Vendor Details** (page 1) with your name, contact information, and product description.
2. Indicate your **Booth Preferences** (page 1) to help market organizers understand your needs and accommodate them accordingly.
3. Read and agree to the **Guidelines** (page 2) to ensure you are aware of the market's rules and regulations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Details** (`title`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself and your products. Please write your name, contact information, and a short description of your products.
* **Booth Preferences** (`type`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Use this field to specify any special requests you may have for your booth, such as location, power, or equipment needs.
* **Agree to Guidelines** (`type`, `note`, required: false):
	+ This field is a checkbox where you agree to abide by the market's guidelines, rules, and regulations. Please review the guidelines carefully before signing.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate and complete information to increase your chances of being approved.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure it is error-free and complete.
