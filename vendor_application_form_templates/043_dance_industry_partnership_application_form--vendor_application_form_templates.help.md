# Dance Industry Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a partnership application form for the dance industry. It aims to collect information from potential partners about their studio, festival, or other industry-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Studio Information** field with your studio's or event's name, address, and contact details.
2. Provide information about the **Festival Information** in the corresponding field.
3. Select at least one **Goal** from the list of options that best represents your partnership's objective.
4. Briefly describe your **Profile** in the text area, including your relevant experience and qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Studio Information** (`studio_info`, text, required: false): Fill out this field with your studio's or event's name, address, and contact details.
* **Festival Information** (`festival_info`, text, required: false): Provide information about the festival you are associated with, including dates and location.
* **Goals** (`goals`, select_multiple, required: true): Select at least one goal from the list that best represents your partnership's objective. This will help the review committee evaluate your application.
* **Profile** (`profile`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your relevant experience and qualifications in this text area.
