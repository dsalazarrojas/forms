# Local Host Partnership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for vendors and partners to register their interest in collaborating with us. It collects contact and partnership information to facilitate communication and potential collaborations. Please note that this form is not affiliated with any specific form platform and is a unique adaptation of publicly available information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
2. Enter your company's details in the "Company Information" page.
3. Describe your partnership proposal in the "Partnership Information" page.
4. Add any submission notes or attachments on the "Submission Information" page.
5. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details to enable us to reach out to you.
* **Company Information** (`company_info`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your company, including its name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Partnership Information** (`partnership_info`, text, required: false): Describe the purpose and scope of your partnership proposal, including what we can expect from each other.
* **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments, files, or attachments that support your partnership proposal.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we have a clear understanding of your partnership proposal.
- You can attach any supporting documents or files to the "Submission Information" page.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
