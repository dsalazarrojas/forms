# Craft Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors about themselves and their products, which will be used to evaluate their eligibility to be listed in our craft-related database or directory.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields accurately and thoroughly. Please make sure to answer all questions, including those marked as "required" to ensure that we receive the necessary information to evaluate your application.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Information**: This field is for you to introduce yourself and your business. Please provide a brief description of your business and your role within it.
* **Business**: In this field, provide a detailed description of your business, including its history, mission, and products or services offered.
* **Vendor's Products**: Describe your products or services offered, including their features, benefits, and pricing. Be as specific as possible.
* **Payment and Terms**: If you have any questions or concerns about payment terms, please provide us with your payment methods and any other relevant information.
* **Tips**:
	+ Be honest and accurate in your answers.
	+ Please answer all questions thoroughly and completely.
	+ Do not submit any confidential or sensitive information.
	+ If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
