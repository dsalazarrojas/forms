# Vendor Registration Form Craft Fair - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vendor Registration Form Craft Fair is a form designed for vendors to provide their contact information and other relevant details. This form helps us verify their identity and contact details for future communication and event organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please ensure that your email address is correct and active.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field. This will help us contact you in case of any queries or updates.
5. Enter your address in the **Address 1**, **Address 2**, and **City** fields. If you have a suite or apartment number, please enter it in **Address 1**.
6. Select your region from the **Region** dropdown menu. Please select **USA** or **Canada** from the available options.
7. Enter your postal code or zip code in the **Postal/Zip** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (email): Enter your active and correct email address here. This will be used for future communication.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number here. This will help us contact you in case of any queries or updates.
* **Address 1** (text): Enter your address line 1 here. If you have a suite or apartment number, enter it in this field.
* **Address 2** (text): Enter your address line 2 here (optional).
* **City** (text): Enter the name of your city here.
* **Region** (select_one): Select your region from the available options **USA** or **Canada**.
* **Postal/Zip** (number): Enter your postal code or zip code here.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review and verify your registration details before confirming your vendor status.
