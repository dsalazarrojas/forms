# Vendor Fee Review Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vendor Fee Review Consent Form is used to gather information from vendors for review and approval of fees charged by them to clients. This form is essential to ensure transparency in financial dealings and prevent potential conflicts of interest. You are required to fill out this form to review and approve fees charged by your vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Start by ensuring you have all the necessary information with you.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by a red star) with the requested details.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for further communication.
 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
* Review and submit the form to submit your consent for vendor fees.
