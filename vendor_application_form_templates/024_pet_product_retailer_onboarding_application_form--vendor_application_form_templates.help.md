# Pet Product Retailer Onboarding Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us learn more about your pet product retail business and get you set up with the necessary information to start selling on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below and fill in the required information about your business. Please note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Retailer Information
* **New Retailer Information** (`new_retailer_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about your retailer. It can be left blank if not necessary.

### Business Name
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Please enter your business name as it appears on your storefront or marketing materials.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is the email address where you can be reached. We'll use this to keep you updated on any platform notifications or issues.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number where we can reach you in case of any urgent matters.

### Location
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Please enter your business location. This can be a city, state, or street address.

### Product Categories
* **Product Categories** (`product_categories`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the categories of pet products you sell (e.g. Pet Food, Pet Toys, Pet Supplies).

### Store Information (Note)
* **Store Information (Note)** (`store_info`, note, optional): Use this space to provide any additional details about your store that will help us understand your business.

### Store Hours
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, time, optional): If your store has regular business hours, please enter them here (e.g. 9am-5pm, Mon-Fri).

### Store Address
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, optional): Enter your store's street address.

### Store Image
* **Store Image** (`store_image`, text, optional): If you have a logo or image of your store, please share it here.

### Store URL
* **Store URL** (`store_url`, text, optional): If you have a website for your store, please enter the URL here.

### Retailer Name
* **Retailer Name** (`retailer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name or the name of the person submitting the form.

### Store Contact Number
* **Store Contact Number** (`store_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for customers to contact your store.

### Store Contact Number (Note)
*   **Store Contact Number** (`store_contact_number`, text, optional): This field is intended to store the contact number of your store. 

Tips:
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
* Once you've submitted the form, we'll review your information and be in touch with you soon.
