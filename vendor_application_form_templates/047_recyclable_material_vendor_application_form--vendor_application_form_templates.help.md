This form is used for vendors to apply to become a supplier of recyclable materials. The form collects information about the vendor, their business, and their contact details. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your application is processed correctly. Please note that some fields are required, and you must fill them out to submit the form.

# Recyclable Material Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recyclable Material Vendor Application Form is a tool for vendors to apply to supply recyclable materials to our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your personal details:
	* First name: Enter your first name.
	* Last name: Enter your last name (required for verification purposes).
	* Email: Enter your work or personal email address.
2. Enter your business details:
	* Business name: Enter the name of your company or organization.
	* Address: Enter your business address.
	* City: Enter the city where your business is located.
	* State: Enter the state where your business is located.
	* Zip: Enter the zip code of your business.
	* Country: Enter the country where your business is located.
	* Contact person: Enter the person in charge of handling your application.
3. Select your material types and certifications:
	* Material types: Choose the types of recyclable materials you offer.
	* Material certifications: Choose the certifications related to the materials you offer.
4. Enter your application details:
	* Application date: Enter the date you are submitting your application.
	* Delivery terms: Enter any specific delivery terms you have.
	* Payment terms: Enter any specific payment terms you have.
5. Enter your contact details:
	* Contact number: Enter your work or personal phone number.
	* Contact email: Enter your work or personal email address.
6. Review and submit your application.
