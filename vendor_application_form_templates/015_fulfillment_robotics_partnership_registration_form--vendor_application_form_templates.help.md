# Fulfillment Robotics Partnership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential partners or collaborators for the fulfillment robotics industry. It helps us understand your partnership or collaboration interests and goals, ensuring a smooth and efficient partnership process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields. The required fields are marked with a star (*) in the field label.
2. Choose the correct role in the "Role" field, which will determine how you will be involved in the partnership.
3. Select the correct partnership type that best describes your partnership goals.
4. Provide a brief description of your company or organization.
5. Upload your submission agreement.
6. Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.
7. Provide contact details for the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partnership Details**: This field is used to provide a brief description of your partnership goals and interests.
* **Contact Person**: This field is used to provide the name and title of the person responsible for the partnership.
* **Role**: This field determines how you will be involved in the partnership:
	+ **Partnership**: You will be a key partner in the partnership.
	+ **Collaborator**: You will be a supporting partner in the partnership.
	+ **Affiliate**: You will have a non-partnership relationship with the Fulfillment Robotics.
* **Description**: This field is used to provide a brief description of your company or organization.
* **Partnership Type**: This field determines the type of partnership:
	+ **Strategic**: A long-term partnership for strategic interests.
	+ **Technical**: A partnership for technical collaborations.
	+ **Commercial**: A partnership for commercial interests.
* **Logo**: This field is used to upload your company or organization logo.
* **I have read and accepted the terms and conditions**: This field confirms that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions for the partnership.
* **Submission Agreement**: This field allows you to upload your submission agreement.
* **Submission Agreement Accepted**: This field is used to confirm that you have accepted the submission agreement.
* **Contact Details**: This field determines the type of contact information:
	+ **Company**: Contact information for a company.
	+ **Organization**: Contact information for an organization.
	+ **Personal**: Contact information for a personal contact.
* **Address**: This field is used to provide the address of the contact person.
* **City**: This field is used to provide the city of the contact person's address.
* **State**: This field is used to provide the state of the contact person's address.
* **Country**: This field is used to provide the country of the contact person's address.
* **Zip**: This field is used to provide the zip code of the contact person's address.
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email**: This field is used to provide the email address of the contact person.
* **Company**: This field is used to provide the name of the company or organization.
* **Job Title**: This field is used to provide the job title of the contact person.
* **Department**: This field is used to provide the department of the contact person.
* **Job Description**: This field is used to provide a brief description of the job role.
* **Job Status**: This field determines the status of the job:
	+ **Active**: The job is active.
	+ **Inactive**: The job is inactive.
* **Job Type**: This field determines the type of job:
	+ **Full Time**: The job is full-time.
	+ **Part Time**: The job is part-time.
	+ **Contract**: The job is contract-based.
* **Job Hours**: This field is used to provide the number of hours worked in the job.
* **Submission Agreement Uploads**: This field is used to upload any additional submission agreements required for the partnership.
