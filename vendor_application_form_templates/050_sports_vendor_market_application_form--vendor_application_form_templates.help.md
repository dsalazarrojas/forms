# Sports Vendor Market Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for vendors to submit their application to participate in a sports event market.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor name in the "Vendors Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Specify the documents you need for your application in the "Documents Needed" field.
5. Choose what you need for your booth from the options provided in "Booth Needs".
6. Write a brief message agreeing to the terms of service in the "Rule Agreement" field.
7. Add any additional event details relevant to your application in the "Event Details" field (optional).
8. If applicable, provide more information about yourself or your business in the "Vendor Details" field (optional).
9. Use the "Additional Info" field to provide any extra context about your application.
10. Finally, confirm you've read and agree to the "Terms of Service" by selecting "Agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendors Name**: Enter your name as it will be listed in the market.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your vendor account.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Documents Needed**: List the documents you will need for your application, such as business licenses or certifications.
* **Booth Needs**: Choose what you need for your booth from the available options.
	+ Booth with tables and chairs
	+ Table only
	+ Chair only
	+ Other
* **Rule Agreement**: Write a brief message agreeing to the terms of service.
* **Event Details**: If applicable, add any additional event details relevant to your application (optional).
* **Vendor Details**: If applicable, provide more information about yourself or your business (optional).
* **Additional Info**: Use this field to provide any extra context about your application.
* **Terms of Service**: Confirm you've read and agree to the terms of service by selecting "Agree".
