# Cold Storage Facility Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from potential partners in cold storage facilities to determine their capabilities and interests for potential partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Answer each field carefully and accurately, ensuring that your responses reflect your company's true capabilities and interests.
3. Select the correct options for each field, taking into account your company's characteristics and services offered.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Legal Name** (`company_legal_name`): Enter your company's registered business name.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`): Select the type of business structure your company operates under (e.g., Corporation, LLC, Partnership, etc.).
* **Year Established** (`year_established`): Enter the year your company was founded.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`): Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Title** (`primary_contact_title`): Enter the title of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_contact_email`): Enter your primary contact person's email address.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone`): Enter your primary contact person's direct phone number.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`): Enter your company's street address.
* **City** (`company_city`): Enter your city location.
* **State/Province** (`company_state`): Enter your state or province of operation.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`company_zip`): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`company_country`): Select the country where your company operates.
* **Cold Storage Facility Address** (`facility_address`): Enter the physical location of your cold storage facility.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`): Select the type of cold storage facility you operate (e.g., Public Cold Storage, Private Cold Storage, etc.).
* **Total Storage Capacity** (`total_storage_capacity`): Enter your facility's total storage capacity in square feet or cubic feet.
* **Temperature Zones Available** (`temperature_zones`): Select all the temperature zones available at your facility.
* **Number of Loading Docks** (`loading_docks`): Enter the number of loading docks available at your facility (if applicable).
* **Rail Access Available** (`rail_access`): Select whether your facility has rail access or not.
* **Industry Certifications** (`certifications`): Select all the industry certifications your facility holds (e.g., HACCP, FDA Registered, etc.).
* **Insurance Coverage Amount** (`insurance_coverage`): Select your facility's insurance coverage amount.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`): Enter your facility's insurance provider name.
* **Years in Cold Storage Business** (`years_in_business`): Select the number of years your company has been in the cold storage business.
* **Number of Current Clients** (`current_clients`): Enter the approximate number of clients your company currently serves.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`): Select all the services you offer (e.g., Cold Storage, Frozen Storage, etc.).
* **Operating Hours** (`operating_hours`): Select your facility's operating hours (e.g., 24/7, 24/5, etc.).
* **Technology Systems Used** (`technology_systems`): Select all the technology systems you use (e.g., WMS, TMS, RFID, etc.).
* **Desired Partnership Type** (`partnership_type`): Select the type of partnership you are interested in.
* **Geographic Coverage Area** (`geographic_coverage`): Select the geographic area your company serves (e.g., Local, Regional, National, etc.).
* **Can You Provide References** (`references_available`): Select whether you can provide client references.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`): Enter any other relevant details about your company or facility.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you answer each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Make sure to check the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
* Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
