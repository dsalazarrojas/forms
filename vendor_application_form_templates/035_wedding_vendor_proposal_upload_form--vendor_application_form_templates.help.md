# Wedding Vendor Proposal Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to upload and manage wedding vendor proposals. Vendors can provide their information, upload relevant documents, and agree to terms for their proposals to be reviewed and potentially selected for the wedding event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **User Input** section with your personal details.
2. Provide information about the **Vendor Information** in the next section.
3. Enter **Contact Details** for the vendor.
4. Add **Proposal Details** to describe your services and offerings.
5. **Upload File** any relevant documents or images.
6. Enter **Vendor Proposal** and **Vendor Quote** details.
7. **Upload Proof** of your work or certifications.
8. Choose **Submit** to indicate the form is ready for review.
9. The **Review Vendor** and **Review Date** fields are for internal use and will be populated automatically.
10. Add any **Review Note** if needed.
11. Enter **Contact Info** for communication.
12. **Upload Signature** any required document or image.
13. **Upload Document** and **Upload Agreement** for vendor contracts and terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: A brief description of the form and its purpose.
* **User Input**: Enter your personal details such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Vendor Information**: Provide information about the vendor, including name, address, and email.
* **Contact Details**: Enter contact details for the vendor, including email and phone number.
* **Proposal Details**: Describe the services and offerings of the proposal.
* **Upload File**: Upload any relevant documents or images.
* **Vendor Proposal**: A detailed description of the proposal.
* **Vendor Quote**: The price or quote for the services offered.
* **Upload Proof**: Upload any proof of work or certifications.
* **Submit**: Check this box to indicate the form is ready for review.
* **Review Vendor**: Internal use for tracking reviews.
* **Review Date**: Internal use for tracking review dates.
* **Review Note**: Add any notes or comments for the review process.
* **Contact Info**: Enter contact information for communication.
* **Upload Signature**: Upload any required document or image.
* **Upload Document**: Upload any documents related to the proposal.
* **Upload Agreement**: Upload any agreements related to the proposal.
* **Submit**: Check this box to finalize the submission.
* **Vendor Name**: Provide the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor Address**: Provide the address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Email**: Provide the email of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone**: Provide the phone number of the vendor.

Note: This form is designed for wedding vendors to provide their proposals and information. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before uploading any documents or submitting the form.
