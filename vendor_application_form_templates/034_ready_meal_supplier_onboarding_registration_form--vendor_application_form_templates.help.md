# Ready Meal Supplier Onboarding Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Ready Meal suppliers to onboard with the organization. It is used to gather information from suppliers and their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each section thoroughly and accurately.
2. Use the Next and Previous buttons to navigate through the pages.
3. Read each field explanation below for further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Submission (#1)** (`number`, `required: false`): This section is not required, but it is the starting point for the onboarding process. Simply click Next to proceed.
* **Supplier Details (#2)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter your supplier details, including your company name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Coverage Document (#3)** (`text`, `required: false`): Upload any relevant documents or information about your coverage. This can include certificates, licenses, or other proof of coverage.
* **Coverage Details (#4)** (`text`, `required: false`): Describe your coverage in detail, including any relevant dates, times, or locations.
* **Coverage Attachment (#5)** (`text`, `required: false`): Attach any relevant documents or information about your coverage. This can include scans, images, or videos.
* **Terms & Agreement (#6)** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Indicate whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the onboarding process.
* **Contact Info (#7)** (`select_one`, `required: false`): Select your address from the provided options.
* **Notes (#8)** (`note`, `required: false`): Add any additional notes or comments about your onboarding process.
* **Email (#9)** (`email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (#10)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email or Phone (#11)** (`select_one`, `required: false`): Indicate whether you want to be contacted via email or phone.
* **Submit (#12)** (`text`, `required: false`): Click this button to submit your application for review.
* **Cancel (#13)** (`text`, `required: false`): Click this button to cancel your application and start again.
* **Back (#14)** (`text`, `required: false`): Click this button to go back to the previous page.
* **Previous (#15)** (`text`, `required: false`): Click this button to go back to the previous page.
* **Reset (#16)** (`text`, `required: false`): Click this button to reset the current page.
* **Save and Preview (#17)** (`text`, `required: false`): Save your application and preview the information entered.
* **Save (#18)** (`text`, `required: false`): Save your application without previewing.
* **Delete (#19)** (`text`, `required: false`): Delete your application.
* **Upload (#20)** (`text`, `required: false`): Upload any relevant documents or attachments.
* **Upload Attachment (#21)** (`text`, `required: false`): Upload any relevant attachments or documents.
* **Select Multiple 1 (#22)** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select multiple options for this field.
* **Select Multiple 2 (#23)** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select multiple options for this field.
* **Select Multiple 3 (#24)** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select multiple options for this field.

Note: Please do not hesitate to ask for clarification or assistance if you are unsure about any part of the form. The organization has the right to review and verify the information provided before proceeding with the onboarding process.
