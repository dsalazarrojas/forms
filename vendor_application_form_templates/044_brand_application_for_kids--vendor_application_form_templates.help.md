# Brand Application For Kids - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents/guardians about their child's profile, interests, and collaboration preferences. It helps us understand who we're collaborating with and what kind of activities we can offer the child.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Read each section carefully**: Each section has a brief description to help you understand what we're looking for.
- **Fill in the child's basic information**: Start with the child's name, date of birth, and age.
- **Provide parent/guardian information**: Enter your full name, email, phone number, and relationship to the child.
- **Collaboration preferences**: Select the type of collaboration you're interested in.
- **Child's experience and background**: If applicable, mention any previous experience or collaborations.
- **Social media presence**: List the child's social media platforms and their follower count (if applicable).
- **Interests and talents**: Describe what the child enjoys and what makes them unique.
- **Consent**: Check the consent box to authorize participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Full Name**: First and last name of the child.
* **Date of Birth**: MM/DD/YYYY format, please enter the correct date.
* **Age**: Years old.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email Address**: Primary contact email.
* **Parent Phone Number**: Phone number to contact you.
* **Relationship to Child**: Describe your relationship to the child.
* **Type of Collaboration Interested In**: Select one of the provided options.
* **Previous Brand or Media Experience**: Choose from extensive, some, limited, or first-time experience.
* **Social Media Accounts**: List all platforms the child uses (e.g., TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, etc.).
* **Social Media Follower Count**: Combined follower count across all platforms (if applicable).
* **Child's Interests and Hobbies**: Describe what the child enjoys.
* **Special Talents or Skills**: Mention what makes this child unique.
* **I Give Consent for My Child to Participate**: Check to authorize participation.

Note: Please ensure you answer accurately and completely, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
