# Local Vendor Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Vendor Accelerator Application Form is designed to gather information from vendors about their business, including contact details, business type, and social media presence. This information will be used by internal stakeholders to review and validate vendor applications for the local vendor accelerator program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your User Name and Business Name, which should be a clear representation of your business identity.
2. Provide your Contact Email and Contact Phone number, which will be used for communication and verification purposes.
3. Select your business type from the options provided.
4. Complete your business address by filling out the Street Address, Postal Code, City, State/Province, and Country fields.
5. Add your business website, social media presence (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Youtube, LinkedIn, and Tiktok), and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Your personal name as the business owner or representative.
* **Business Name**: Your business name, which should be a clear representation of your identity.
* **Business Description**: A brief description of your business.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address that we can use for communication and verification purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number that we can use for communication and verification purposes.
* **Business Type**: Select your business type from the options provided (Restaurant, Store, Service, or Other).
* **Street Address**: Your business address.
* **Postal Code**: Your postal code (zip code or postcode).
* **City**: Your city or location.
* **State/Province**: Your state or province.
* **Country**: Your country of residence.
* **Business Website**: Your business website address.
* **Business Facebook**: Your business Facebook presence (if applicable).
* **Business Twitter**: Your business Twitter presence (if applicable).
* **Business Instagram**: Your business Instagram presence (if applicable).
* **Business Youtube**: Your business Youtube presence (if applicable).
* **Business LinkedIn**: Your business LinkedIn presence (if applicable).
* **Business Tiktok**: Your business Tiktok presence (if applicable).
* **Bank Account Number**: Your business bank account number (if applicable).
* **Business License Number**: Your business license number (if applicable).
* **Business Tax Federal**: Your business tax federal number (if applicable).
* **Business Tax State**: Your business tax state number (if applicable).
* **Business Tax City**: Your business tax city number (if applicable).
* **Business Tax Province**: Your business tax province number (if applicable).
* **Other Details**: Any other relevant information about your business.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your form is filled out completely and accurately, especially if you are missing information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask us and we'll be happy to guide you through the process.
* Remember that some fields are optional, but are still important for a complete understanding of your business.
* Review your form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
