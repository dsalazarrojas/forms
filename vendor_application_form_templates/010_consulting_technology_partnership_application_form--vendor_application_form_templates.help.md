# Consulting Technology Partnership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your company's background, technology stack, partnership goals, and contact information. We will use this information to evaluate potential partnerships and determine the feasibility of working together.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Company Information**: Start by filling out the **Company Name**, **Industry Focus**, **Company Size**, and **Company Website** fields, which will give us an idea about your company's background and scope.
2. **Technology and Services**: In this section, you will be asked to provide information about your **Primary Technology Stack**, **Primary Service Offerings**, and **Key Technology Certifications**. This will help us understand your company's technical capabilities and services offered.
3. **Partnership Goals**: Describe your **Primary Partnership Objectives** and how your company aligns with our vision. You will also be asked to indicate the level of **Target Market Overlap** between our companies.
4. **Contact Person**: Provide us with the **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Job Title** of the person filling out the form. This will ensure we know who to contact and how to reach you.
5. **Application Date**: Finally, please fill out the **Application Date** field with today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Your company's legal name.
* **Industry Focus**: Select all industries your company specializes in.
* **Company Size**: Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Company Website**: URL to your main website.
* **Primary Technology Stack**: List the key technologies your company uses.
* **Primary Service Offerings**: Select all services your company provides.
* **Key Technology Certifications**: Provide certifications relevant to the partnership.
* **Primary Partnership Objectives**: Describe what you hope to achieve by partnering with us.
* **Alignment with Our Vision**: Describe how your company aligns with our goals.
* **Target Market Overlap**: Indicate the level of overlap between our target markets.
* **Contact Name**: Full name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Primary email address for communication.
* **Job Title**: Position within the company.
* **Application Date**: Today's date.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or information you would like to share.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out for the form to be submitted. Please make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues.
