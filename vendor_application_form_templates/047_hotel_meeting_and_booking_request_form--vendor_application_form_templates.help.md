# Hotel Meeting And Booking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a meeting at a hotel. It will ask for event details, hotel information, and contact details to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Provide the required information for event details, hotel details, and contact details.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, marked as "required".
4. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (event_details, text, required: false): This section is used to provide general information about the event you are requesting.
* **Hotel Details** (hotel_details, text, required: false): This section is used to provide details about the hotel you are requesting for the meeting.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required: false): This section is used to provide your contact information for communication and follow-up purposes.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form title or the "Purpose" section for clarification.
