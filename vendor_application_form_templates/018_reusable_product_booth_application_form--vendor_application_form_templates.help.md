# Reusable Product Booth Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reusable Product Booth Application Form is a template designed for vendors to submit their products or services for approval to be part of a product booth at an event. The form is intended to collect relevant information about the vendor, their product, and the event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the vendor's details in the "Vendor Details" section.
2.  Enter the vendor's name in the "vendorName" field.
3.  Enter the vendor's email address in the "vendorEmail" field.
4.  Enter the vendor's phone number in the "vendorPhone" field.
5.  Describe the product in the "productDetails" field.
6.  Enter the product's name in the "productName" field.
7.  Provide a description of the product in the "productDescription" field.
8.  Enter the product price in the "productPrice" field.
9.  Upload a product image in the "productImage" field.
10.  Enter a link to the product in the "productLink" field.
11.  Enter the booth details in the "Booth Details" field.
12.  Enter the booth size in the "boothSize" field.
13.  Describe the booth layout in the "boothLayout" field.
14.  Enter any additional features of the booth in the "boothFeatures" field.
15.  Enter the submission in the "Submission" field.
16.  Sign and date the form in the "Signature" field.
17.  Agree to the submission terms in the "Submission Agreement" field.
18.  Upload any additional files in the "file" field.
19.  Enter any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.
20.  Enter the vendor's information in the "Vendor Info" field.
21.  Enter the event name in the "event_name" field.
22.  Enter the event dates in the "eventDates" field.
23.  Enter the event location in the "eventLocation" field.
24.  Describe the event requirements in the "eventRequirements" field.
25.  Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "termsAndConditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Details** (`Vendor Details`, text, required): This section is intended to collect the vendor's details.
* **vendorName** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter the vendor's name.
* **vendorEmail** (`vendor_email`, text, required): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **vendorPhone** (`vendor_phone`, text, required): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **productDetails** (`product_details`, text, required): Describe the product.
* **productName** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the product's name.
* **productDescription** (`product_description`, text, required): Provide a description of the product.
* **productPrice** (`product_price`, number, required): Enter the product price.
* **productImage** (`product_image`, text, required): Upload a product image.
* **productLink** (`product_link`, text, required): Enter a link to the product.
* **Booth Details** (`booth_details`, text, required): Enter the booth details.
* **boothSize** (`booth_size`, text, required): Enter the booth size.
* **boothLayout** (`booth_layout`, text, required): Describe the booth layout.
* **boothFeatures** (`booth_features`, text, required): Enter any additional features of the booth.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, required): Enter a submission.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form.
* **submissionAgreement** (`submission_agreement`, text, required): Agree to the submission terms.
* **file** (`file`, text, required): Upload any additional files.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information.
* **Vendor Info** (`vendor_info`, text, required): Enter the vendor's information.
* **event_name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the event name.
* **eventDates** (`event_dates`, text, required): Enter the event dates.
* **eventLocation** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the event location.
* **eventRequirements** (`event_requirements`, text, required): Describe the event requirements.
* **termsAndConditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
