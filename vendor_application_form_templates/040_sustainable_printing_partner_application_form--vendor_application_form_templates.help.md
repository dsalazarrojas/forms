# Sustainable Printing Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential sustainable printing partners to assess their suitability to partner with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name, company name, and address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your contact email and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
3. Provide your company website if applicable.
4. Describe the products and services you offer.
5. Indicate your current sustainability practices and certifications.
6. Select the status of your sustainability report.
7. Add any additional notes about your company.
8. Indicate whether you have already submitted a partnership application.
9. Select the assignee who has submitted this form.
10. Check the fields to indicate the assignee's role and the tool assigned to them.
11. Check the fields to indicate the status of the assigned tool and the user to whom it was assigned.
12. Check the field to indicate the location where the form was submitted.
13. Check the field to indicate the status of the assignee.
14. Check the field to indicate whether the partner has been assigned to a priority.
15. Add comments from the partner.
16. Check the field to indicate whether the partner's escalation status.
17. Check the field to indicate whether the partner has been assigned to for resolution.
18. Submit the form after filling out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it should appear on our records.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name as it should appear on our records.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your company address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number we can use to contact you.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): If you have a company website, provide the URL here.
* **What Products/Services** (`what_products_services`, `text`, required): Describe the products and services you offer.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate whether you have the following certifications: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Sustainability Report** (`sustainability_report`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate the status of your sustainability report: 'Certified', 'In Progress', or 'Not Evaluated'.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about your company.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate whether you are the submitter: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate the tool assigned to you: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate the user to whom you were assigned: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned Group** (`assigned_group`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate the group to which you were assigned: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which you were assigned.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time on which you were assigned.
* **Assigned Location** (`assigned_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate the location where you submitted this form: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned Status** (`assigned_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate the status of the assignee: 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Assigned Priority** (`assigned_priority`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate whether you were assigned to a priority: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned Comments** (`assigned_comments`, `text`, required): Add comments about your assigned status.
* **Assigned Escalation** (`assigned_escalation`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate whether your escalation status: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned Resolution** (`assigned_resolution`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to indicate whether you were assigned for resolution: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which you submitted this form.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, `text`, required): Add any comments about your submission.
