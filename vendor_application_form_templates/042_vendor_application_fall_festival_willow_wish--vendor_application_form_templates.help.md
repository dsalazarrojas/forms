# Vendor Application Fall Festival Willow Wish - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Application Fall Festival Willow Wish form is used by vendors to apply for participation in the Fall Festival Willow Wish event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Vendor Application** page with your basic information.
2. Enter your **Contact Person** and **Vendor Name** in the respective fields.
3. Provide your **Vendor Address** to help us contact you.
4. Enter your **Vendor Contact Number** so we can reach you.
5. Enter your **Email** address to receive updates and communications.
6. Add any **Notes** you'd like to share with us.
7. If you have a **Vendor License**, please enter the corresponding number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Application**: This is the main section of the form where you can provide a brief description of your application.
* **Contact Person**: Please enter the name and contact information of the person we should contact in case we need to reach you.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter your business name or the name under which you will be operating at the festival.
* **Vendor Address**: Provide your business address to help us with logistics and communication.
* **Vendor Contact Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive important updates and communications about the festival.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with us about your application.
* **Vendor License**: If you have a vendor license number, please enter it here to help us verify your credentials.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are submitting a new application and not a resubmission of a previous one.
