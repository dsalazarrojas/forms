# Call For Artists and Makers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Call For Artists and Makers" form is designed to collect information from individual artists and makers who are interested in showcasing their work with our organization. The form will be used to promote your art and connect you with potential clients and event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*)
2. Select all relevant categories of work for you or your artist/maker's work
3. Write a clear and concise description of your work and your artistic statement
4. Provide accurate and relevant information about your experience and background
5. Ensure that your website and social media handles are up-to-date and accurately represented

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Maker Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, \*): Please enter your name as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, \*): Please enter your professional email address that you use for business communications.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, \*): Please enter your phone number that you use for business communications.
* **Street Address** (`artist_address`, `text`, ): Please enter your street address if you are interested in receiving mail or shipping packages.
* **City** (`artist_city`, `text`, ): Please enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State** (`artist_state`, `text`, ): Please enter the state where you reside or work.
* **Zip Code** (`artist_zip`, `text`, ): Please enter your zip code if you are interested in receiving mail or shipping packages.
* **Categories of Work** (`art_categories`, `select_multiple`, \*): Select all categories that apply to your work, such as painting, sculpture, jewelry, etc.
* **Description of Your Work** (`art_description`, `text`, \*): Please describe your work in 1-2 paragraphs, highlighting your unique style and what makes your art distinct.
* **Inspiration or Artistic Statement** (`inspiration`, `text`, ): This is an optional field where you can describe your artistic inspiration or statement.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, \*): Please enter the number of years you have been creating art.
* **Previous Exhibitions or Shows** (`exhibitions`, `text`, ): Please list any previous exhibitions or shows you have participated in.
* **Awards or Recognition** (`awards`, `text`, ): Please list any awards or recognition you have received for your art.
* **Website or Portfolio URL** (`website`, `text`, ): Please enter your website or portfolio URL where people can see more of your work.
* **Social Media Handles** (`social_media`, `text`, ): Please enter your social media handles where people can connect with you.
* **Instagram Account** (`instagram`, `text`, ): Please enter your Instagram account.
* **Preferred Booth Size** (`booth_size`, `select_one`, \*): Please select the preferred booth size for your artwork.
* **Average Price Range of Items** (`price_range`, `select_one`, ): Please select the average price range of your artwork.
* **Artwork Sample Links** (`artwork_samples`, `text`, ): This is an optional field where you can share links to photos or images of your artwork.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, ): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about your work or experience.

Please ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and completely, as the information you provide will be used to promote your art and connect you with potential clients and event organizers.
