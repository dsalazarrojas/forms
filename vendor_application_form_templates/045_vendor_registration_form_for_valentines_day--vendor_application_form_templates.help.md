# vendor-registration-form-for-valentines-day - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for vendors to register themselves and their business information for Valentine's Day-related activities. Please fill out the form to be listed as a participating vendor for this occasion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Ensure all required fields (none are marked as required on this form) are filled out to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
3. Click "Submit" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendors Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or business name as it should be listed on the registration.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name who will be handling communication for your business.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your business email address for contact and communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number for contact and communication.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your business address for contact and shipping purposes.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name as it will be listed on the registration.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): Enter any product information you'd like to share about your business.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of product your business offers.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any tools or equipment assigned to your business for this event.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your business for the event.
