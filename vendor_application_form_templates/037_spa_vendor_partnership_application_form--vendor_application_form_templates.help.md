# Spa Vendor Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and business information from potential spa vendors interested in partnering with us. Please provide accurate and complete information to increase the chances of a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your contact and business information.
2. Select the services you offer from the list.
3. Provide your spa hours and contact person's information.
4. Enter your email address, phone number, and address.
5. Enter your zip code and website (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other contact person's name associated with your business.
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe your business and its services.
* **Spa Info** (`spa_info`, text, optional): Describe your spa, including its unique features and amenities.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the services your spa offers, such as Spa Massage, Hair Salon, or Wellness Center.
* **Spa Hours** (`spa_hours`, date, optional): Enter your spa's operating hours, including any special hours or closures.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the contact person associated with your business.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your business address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your business zip code.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your business website (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

Note: Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your application is reviewed promptly.
