# Theme Park Operator Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to onboard Theme Park operators, capturing essential details about them for efficient onboarding and access to Theme Park resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section with the required information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
3. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operator Details** (`operator_details`, text, required): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Documentation Details** (`documentation_details`, text, required): Provide your documentation details, such as your ID number, certification, or any other relevant documents.
* **Vendor Info** (`vendor_info`, text, required): Enter your vendor information, such as your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Person** (`operator_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name, email, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Operator Address** (`operator_address`, text, required): Enter your operator address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Contact Person Email** (`contact_person_email`, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Operator Phone** (`operator_phone`, text, optional): Enter your operator phone number.
* **Operator License** (`operator_license`, text, required): Enter your operator license details, such as your license number, expiration date, or any other relevant details.
* **Onboard Date** (`onboard_date`, date, required): Enter the date you were onboarded.
* **Onboard Time** (`onboard_time`, time, required): Enter the time you were onboarded.
* **Onboard Status** (`onboard_status`, select multiple, required): Select your onboarding status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Onboard Notes** (`onboard_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional onboarding notes.
* **Onboard Review** (`onboard_review`, select one, required): Select your onboarding review (Not Verified, Verified, or Pending).
* **Onboard Decision** (`onboard_decision`, select one, required): Select your onboarding decision (Approve, Decline, or Onhold).
