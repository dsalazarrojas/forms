# Third Party Vendor Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Third Party Vendor Access Form is used to grant third-party vendors access to certain systems, applications, or resources. This form is a crucial step in the vendor onboarding process, ensuring that only authorized vendors have access to sensitive information or resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal information, including your user info, email, phone number, and any other required fields.
2. Provide your vendor information, such as your name, address, and any other relevant details.
3. Select the access level you require (e.g., Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3) from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the duration of the access you require (optional).
5. Enter the start date and time you require access (optional).
6. Enter any additional notes or comments for the vendor access request (optional).
7. Answer the security question (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, required): Enter your personal contact information.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Vendor Info** (`vendor_info`, `text`, required): Enter your vendor information.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your vendor name.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required): Enter your vendor address.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of access you require:
	+ Level 1: Standard access.
	+ Level 2: Elevated access.
	+ Level 3: High-level access.
* **Access Duration** (`access_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the access you require.
* **Access Date** (`access_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date you require access.
* **Access Time** (`access_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time you require access.
* **Access Note** (`access_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the vendor access request.
* **Security Question** (`security_question`, `text`, required): Answer the security question to verify your identity.

Please note that this form is only accessible to authorized system administrators or security officers.
