# new_vendor_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_vendor_form is for new or existing vendors to create or update their company information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field with the requested information.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (*).
- Review each field for accuracy before submission.
- Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
- **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your company's address as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your company's phone number for contact.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your company's email address for correspondence.
- **Website** (Website, text, required): Enter your company's website URL (if applicable).
- **Vendor Type** (Vendor Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of business you are submitting your application as (e.g. type 1, type 2, type 3).
- **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact at your company.
- **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to use the correct format for email addresses (e.g. @example.com).
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the support team for assistance.
