# Eco Supply Vendor Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from eco-friendly suppliers who want to be listed on the website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your website in the "Website" field.
6. List any relevant eco-certifications in the "Eco certifications" field.
7. Describe your eco-friendly offerings in the "Offerings" field.
8. List any additional certifications you have in the "Certifications" field.
9. Enter your business location in the "Location" field.
10. Enter your contact name in the "Contact name" field.
11. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field.
12. Enter your contact email in the "Contact email" field.
13. Enter your contact website in the "Contact website" field.
14. Check the "Vendor type" field to select your type of business (e.g. supplier, manufacturer, etc.).
15. Check the "Eco focus" field to select the aspect of sustainability you are focused on (e.g. renewable energy, recycling, etc.).
16. Enter any additional comments about your business in the "Additional comments" field.
17. Finally, review the "Terms and Conditions" section and check the box to agree to its terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website.
* **Eco certifications** (`eco_certifications`, select multiple, optional): List any relevant eco-certifications.
* **Offerings** (`offerings`, text, optional): Describe your eco-friendly offerings.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select multiple, optional): List any additional certifications.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your business location.
* **Contact name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your contact name.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact website** (`contact_website`, text, optional): Enter your contact website.
* **Vendor type** (`vendor_type`, select one, optional): Select your type of business (e.g. supplier, manufacturer, etc.).
* **Eco focus** (`eco_focus`, select one, optional): Select the aspect of sustainability you are focused on (e.g. renewable energy, recycling, etc.).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your business.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the form.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is not a field to be filled, it's a confirmation button to submit the form.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's highly recommended to provide as much information as possible to ensure that your account is verified and listed correctly.
