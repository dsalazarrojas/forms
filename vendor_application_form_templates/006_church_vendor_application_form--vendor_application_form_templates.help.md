# Church Vendor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential vendors who wish to provide services to the church. This form helps the church administration understand the vendor's identity, contact information, services offered, and other relevant details. This form should only be used for vendors who have not already been vetted or approved by the church.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vendor's company name in the "Vendor or company name" field.
2. Enter the primary contact person's name in the "Contact person" field.
3. Provide the contact person's email address in the "Contact email" field.
4. Enter the contact person's business phone number in the "Contact phone" field.
5. Briefly describe the services or products offered by the vendor in the "Description of services or products" field.
6. Indicate the vendor's insurance and licensing status in the "Insurance and licensing status" field.
7. If not required, choose whether to agree or disagree with the church's vendor terms in the "Agreement to vendor terms" field.
8. Use the current date as the "Submission date" and click the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor or company name** (*): This is the legal business name of the vendor.
* **Contact person**: This is the name of the primary contact person for the vendor.
* **Contact email**: This is the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact phone**: This is the business phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Description of services or products**: This is a brief summary of the services or products offered by the vendor.
* **Insurance and licensing status**: This is where you indicate whether the vendor is licensed, insured, or both.
* **Professional references**: This is where you list two professional references with their contact information.
* **Agreement to vendor terms**: This is where you indicate whether you agree or disagree with the church's vendor terms.
* **Submission date**: This is the date the form is submitted. Please use the current date.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If a field is not required, it is not essential to fill it out. However, providing information in non-required fields can help the church administration better understand your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the church administration.
