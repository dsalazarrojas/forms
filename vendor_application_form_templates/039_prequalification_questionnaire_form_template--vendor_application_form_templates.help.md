# Prequalification Questionnaire Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from vendors or suppliers, assessing their eligibility and readiness to work with your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company information on the "Supplier Info" page.
2. Answer the "Company Details" question.
3. Provide your financial information on the "Financial Info" page.
4. Answer the "Compliance Info" question.
5. Share your operational practices and policies on the "Operational Info" page.
6. Submit your tax information on the "Tax Info" page.
7. Enter your contact information on the "Contact Info" page.
8. Complete your address information on the "Address Info" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Supplier Info** (id: 1, text, required: false): This is the first page of the form, where you can enter your company name, address, and other identifying information.
- **Company Details** (id: 2, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your company, including its products or services offered.
- **Financial Info** (id: 3, text, required: false): This is the section where you can provide information about your financial stability, such as your annual revenue or budget.
- **Compliance Info** (id: 4, text, required: false): Share any relevant certifications or compliance information that may be relevant to working with your organization.
- **Operational Info** (id: 5, text, required: false): Describe your operational practices, including your business hours, policies, or procedures.
- **Tax Info** (id: 6, text, required: false): Provide information about your tax identification numbers or rates.
- **Contact Info** (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter your contact name, email, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
- **Address Info** (id: 8, text, required: false): This is the last page of the form, where you can enter your business address and any other relevant location information.

## Tips
- Be accurate and truthful when filling out this form.
- Use clear and concise language.
- Attach any supporting documentation if requested by the organization.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.
