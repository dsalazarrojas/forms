# Predictive Inventory Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Predictive Inventory Partner Application Form is a tool for vendors and partners to apply to join the Predictive Inventory Partner program. This form collects information about the applicant's company and integration preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required".
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about your company and integration preferences.
3. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Application Details (Page 1)
* **application_details**: A brief introduction to your company and application.
* **submission_date**: The date you submitted your application.
* **submission_time**: The time you submitted your application.

### Company Information (Page 2)
* **company_info**: A description of your company.
* **business_info**: A description of your business model or operations.
* **company_name**: The name of your company.
* **company_type**: The type of your company (Tech Vendor, Retailer, Logistics Company).
* **contact_name**: The name of the contact person.
* **contact_email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone**: The phone number of the contact person.

### Integration Information (Page 3)
* **integration_info**: A description of your integration needs or preferences.
* **integration_type**: The type of integration you are seeking (API, MELI, Other).
* **integration_level**: The level of integration you require (Level 1 - Basic, Level 2 - Enhanced, Level 3 - Deep Integration).

### Agreement (Page 4)
* **agreement_read**: A confirmation that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **terms_read**: A confirmation that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **review_read**: A confirmation that you have reviewed and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **terms_accepted**: A confirmation that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **submitted**: A confirmation that your application has been submitted.

### Review and Submission (Page 5)
* **review_and_agreement**: A review of your application and agreement to the terms and conditions.

Please note that this form has 5 pages with various fields to fill in, each with its own description and instructions. Please fill in the required fields and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.
