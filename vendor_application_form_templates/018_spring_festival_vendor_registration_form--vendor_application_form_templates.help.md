# Spring Festival Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spring Festival Vendor Registration Form is a document for vendors to submit their application to participate in the Spring Festival. Completing this form allows the festival organizers to review and evaluate the vendor's application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the vendor_application field with a brief description of your application.
2. Check the e_signature box to indicate whether you, as a representative of your company, are willing to sign the contract.
3. Provide detailed vendor details in the vendor_details field.
4. Select the date for the vendor_application_form.
5. Enter your vendor_license number for verification purposes.
6. Enter your vendor_website URL for easy access.
7. Select the festival_date on which you wish to participate.
8. Enter the vendor_fee you are willing to pay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vendor_application** (text, optional): A brief description of your vendor application.
* **e_signature** (select_one, optional): Check the box to indicate your willingness to sign the contract.
* **vendor_details** (text, optional): Detailed information about your company or product.
* **vendor_application_form** (date, optional): Date of the vendor application form submission.
* **vendor_license** (text, optional): Your vendor license number for verification.
* **vendor_website** (text, optional): URL of your website for easy access.
* **festival_date** (date, optional): Date on which you wish to participate in the festival.
* **vendor_fee** (number, optional): Fee you are willing to pay for participating in the festival.
