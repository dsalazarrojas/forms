## Vendor Qualification Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Vendor Qualification Form is a tool used to gather essential information from vendors.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information required to complete the form.
3. Choose the correct business type that best represents your business.

* **Address** (10) - This is where you can enter the address of your business location.
* **City** (11) - This is where you can enter the city of your business location.
