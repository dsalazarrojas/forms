# Waste Optimization Platform Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waste Optimization Platform Application Form is a tool to help us get to know your organization better and assess your qualifications for partnership with our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the application form with accurate and detailed information about your organization.
2. Choose the type of waste you specialize in (Water, Food, or Other).
3. Select your primary goal for waste reduction (Reduce, Eliminate, or Optimize).
4. Enter any additional notes about your organization and services offered.
5. Provide your contact information, including email, phone number, and name.
6. Choose the status of your form submission (Submit, Save for later, or Cancel).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your organization and the services you offer.
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your organization, including its name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Waste Types** (`waste_types`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of waste your organization specializes in (Water, Food, or Other).
* **Waste Goals** (`waste_goals`, select_one, required): Select your primary goal for waste reduction (Reduce, Eliminate, or Optimize).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about your organization and services offered.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter your title or position within your organization.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of your form submission (Submit, Save for later, or Cancel).
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, required): Enter your organization's address.
* **Form Submission Status** (`form_submission_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current status of your form submission (In Progress, Completed, or Not Started).
* **Date Created** (`date_created`, date, required): This field will automatically populate with the date and time you started filling out the form.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): This field will automatically populate with the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required): This field will automatically populate with the time you submitted the form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, required): Enter the version number of the form you are using.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current status of your form (In Progress, Completed, or Not Started).
* **Form Type** (`form_type`, text, required): Enter the type of form you are filling out (Vendor Application, etc.).
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about your organization and services offered.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, text, required): Enter your organization type (e.g., government, private, non-profit, etc.).
* **Waste Reduction Goals** (`waste_reduction_goals`, text, required): Enter your goals for waste reduction.
* **Waste Reduction Targets** (`waste_reduction_targets`, text, required): Enter your targets for waste reduction.
* **Waste Reduction Tactics** (`waste_reduction_tactics`, text, required): Enter the tactics you use to achieve waste reduction.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): This field will automatically populate with the date and time you submitted the form.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
