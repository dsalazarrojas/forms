# Vendor Technology Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vendor Technology Grant Application Form is used by vendors to apply for grants for technology-related initiatives. The form helps us to understand the technology you are requesting and to assess your application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review the form carefully and ensure that all fields are filled in accurately.
2. Ensure you are not duplicating an existing application with the same vendor and technology requested.
3. Provide detailed and clear information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the technology you are applying for (e.g., software, hardware, data, etc.). This will help us to understand the nature of your request.
* **description** (`description`, text, required: false): Enter a clear and concise description of the technology you are requesting, including its features and benefits.
* **Vendor name** (`vendor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your company/organization.
* **Grant Requestor** (`grant_requestor`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact details of the person submitting the application.
* **Technology Requested** (`technology_requested`, text, required: false): Enter the specific technology you are requesting.
* **Vendor Tech Request** (`vendor_tech_request`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about your technology request.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of grant you are requesting.
* **Grant Start Date** (`grant_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like the grant to start from.
* **Grant End Date** (`grant_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like the grant to end on.
* **Grant Status** (`grant_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your grant application (e.g., Pending, Approved, Declined, Expired).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately.
- If you have any queries, contact the relevant department before submitting the application.
- Please do not duplicate an existing application with the same vendor and technology requested.
