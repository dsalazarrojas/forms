# Carpet Cleaning Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from vendors for a carpet cleaning project proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure that you have all necessary information and documentation before filling out this form.
- Fill out all required fields (marked as 'required' in the form).
- For any optional fields, only fill out the fields that are applicable to your company.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (id: 2): A required field where you need to enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (id: 3): A required field where you need to enter the contact person's name from your company.
* **Email Address** (id: 4): A required field where you need to enter your company's email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 5): A required field where you need to enter your company's phone number.
* **Business License and Registration Number** (id: 6): A required field where you need to enter your company's business license and registration number.
* **Years in Business** (id: 8): An optional field where you need to enter the number of years your company has been in business.
* **Service Locations** (id: 9): A required field where you need to select all locations your company provides service.
* **Type of Equipment Used** (id: 10): A required field where you need to select the type of equipment your company uses.
* **Cleaning Methods Offered** (id: 11): A required field where you need to select all cleaning methods your company offers.
* **Estimated Square Footage** (id: 13): A required field where you need to select the estimated square footage for the proposal.
* **Type of Facility** (id: 14): A required field where you need to select the type of facility your company is applying for.
* **Primary Carpet Type** (id: 15): A required field where you need to select the primary carpet type your company offers.
* **Included Special Treatments** (id: 16): An optional field where you need to select all special treatments your company includes in the proposal.
* **Total Estimated Proposal Cost** (id: 18): A required field where you need to enter the total estimated proposal cost.
* **Currency** (id: 19): A required field where you need to select the currency for the proposal cost.
* **Preferred Payment Terms** (id: 20): A required field where you need to select the preferred payment terms.
* **Proposed Start Date** (id: 21): An optional field where you need to select the proposed start date for the project.
* **Liability Insurance** (id: 23): A required field where you need to select whether you carry active general liability insurance.
* **Workers Comp** (id: 24): A required field where you need to select whether you carry workers' compensation insurance.
* **I certify the information provided is accurate and I am authorized to** (id: 25): A required field where you need to type your name as digital signature to confirm that the information provided is accurate and you have the authority to submit the proposal.

Note: The currency and payment terms fields have specific options to select from. Please ensure that you select the correct options that apply to your company.
