# Low Emission Transport Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors applying for low emission transport services and reviewers assessing these applications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Vendor Details**: Enter your vendor name, address, and any other relevant details about your business.
2. **Transport Services**: Select all transport services your company offers.
3. **Vendor Qualification**: Select your vendor qualification (yes/no).
4. **Vendor Documents**: Upload any required documents related to your business.
5. **Vendor Address**: Select whether your business has a physical address (yes/no).
6. **Vendor Email**: Enter your business email address.
7. **Vendor Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
8. **Reviewer**: Select whether a reviewer is assigned to your application (yes/no).
9. **Reviewer Email**: Enter reviewer's email address (if assigned).
10. **Reviewer Phone**: Enter reviewer's phone number (if assigned).
11. **Reviewer Comment**: Provide any comments for the reviewer.
12. **Vendor Status**: Select whether your application is active or inactive.
13. **Reviewer Status**: Select whether the reviewer is active or inactive.
14. **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
15. **Review Time**: Enter the time of the review.
16. **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the reviewer (if any).
17. **Review Note**: Enter any notes for the reviewer.
18. **Vendor License**: Select whether your business has a license (yes/no).
19. **Reviewer License**: Select whether the reviewer has a license (yes/no).
20. **Vendor License Number**: Select whether you have a license number (yes/no).
21. **Review License**: Select whether the review has a license (yes/no).
22. **Reviewer License**: Select whether the reviewer has a license (yes/no).
23. **Review Date/Time**: Enter the review date and time.
24. **Review Note**: Enter any notes for the reviewer.
25. **Submission Date**: Enter the date of the submission.
