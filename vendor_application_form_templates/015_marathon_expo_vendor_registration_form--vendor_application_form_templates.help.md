# marathon-expo-vendor-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The marathon-expo-vendor-registration-form is a vendor registration form designed for participating vendors to provide their details and preferences for the marathon expo.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form carefully and thoroughly with the required information.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accurate completion.
3. Double-check that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Vendor Information** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the vendor information.
* **2. Vendor Details** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your vendor.
* **3. Payment** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the provided payment options.
*	* Option 1
*	* Option 2
*	* Option 3
* **4. Payment Methods** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple payment methods you prefer (e.g., Bank Transfer, Cheque).
*	* Bank Transfer
*	* Cheque
* **5. Payment Info** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional payment information.
* **6. Payment Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the payment date.
* **7. Payment Amount** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **8. Booth Preferences** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any booth preferences, if needed.
* **9. Booth Location** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose a booth location from the provided options.
*	* Location A
*	* Location B
*	* Location C
*	* Location D
* **10. Booth Size** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the provided booth sizes.
*	* 10 x 10 ft
*	* 10 x 5 ft
*	* 5 x 5 ft
* **11. Vendor Notes** (`note`, required/optional): Enter any special notes or instructions for the vendor.
* **12. Vendor Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **13. Vendor Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **14. Vendor Address** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's street address.
* **15. Vendor City** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's city.
* **16. Vendor State** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's state.

## Tips

* Be sure to proofread your form before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.
* This form is subject to change; always check the latest version before proceeding. 

Please note that the fields marked as required/optional might have specific requirements or constraints that you need to follow.
