# Beauty Studio Vendor Renewal Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you, as a vendor, provide us with necessary information for the renewal of your partnership with Beauty Studio. Please ensure you have all required documents and details before filling out this form. If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact us for clarification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor's profile information, including your vendor's legal name, contact name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your current contract or vendor ID.
3. Select your primary product category.
4. Indicate if you plan to launch new products within the next 12 months.
5. Confirm that your current price list is up to date.
6. Confirm the status of your business license and insurance certificate.
7. Provide the expiration date of your insurance certificate.
8. Confirm that you agree to continue following the Beauty Studio Vendor Code of Conduct.
9. Select the date you're submitting this application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Legal Name** (`vendor_legal_name`, Text, Required): Your business's official legal name.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Required): The name of the person filling out this form.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): A valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Required): A valid phone number we can use to contact you.
* **Current Contract or Vendor ID** (`current_contract_id`, Text, Required): Your current contract or vendor ID number.
* **Primary Product/Service Category** (`primary_product_category`, Select One, Required): Select your primary product category from the options provided.
* **New Products Launch** (`new_products_launch`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you plan to launch new products within the next 12 months.
* **Price List Update** (`price_list_update`, Select One, Required): Confirm that your current price list is up to date.
* **Business License Status** (`business_license_status`, Select One, Required): Confirm the status of your business license.
* **Insurance Certificate** (`insurance_certificate_current`, Select One, Required): Confirm the status of your insurance certificate.
* **Insurance Expiration Date** (`insurance_expiration_date`, Date, Required): Provide the expiration date of your insurance certificate.
* **Compliance and Verification** (`section_compliance`, Note, Not Required): This is a note section for any additional comments or information.
* **Terms Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you agree to continue following the Beauty Studio Vendor Code of Conduct.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, Date, Required): Select the date you're submitting this application.

Note: Please ensure you have all necessary documents and information before submitting this form. If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact us for clarification.
