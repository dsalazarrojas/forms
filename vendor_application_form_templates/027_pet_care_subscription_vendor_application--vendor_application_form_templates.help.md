# Pet Care Subscription Vendor Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from vendors who offer pet care services. This information will help us evaluate your application and decide on your potential partnership with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your vendor information in the "Pet Care Vendor Info" section.
2. Select the services you provide in the "Services" section.
3. Enter your pricing information in the "Pricing Info" section.
4. Choose the services you offer in the "Services Offered" section.
5. Enter your pricing details in the "Pricing Details" section.
6. Specify your payment terms in the "Payment Terms" section.
7. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
8. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" section.
9. Choose your payment method in the "Payment Method" section.
10. Choose your payment frequency in the "Payment Frequency" section.
11. Choose your vendor status in the "Vendor Status" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Care Vendor Info** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your pet care business.
- **Services** (2, select_multiple, optional): Select all the services you provide. Choose from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
- **Pricing Info** (3, text, optional): Enter your pricing details.
- **Services Offered** (4, select_one, optional): Choose the services you offer. Choose from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
- **Pricing Details** (5, number, optional): Enter your pricing details. Please format as a number (e.g. 10.99).
- **Payment Terms** (6, text, optional): Specify your payment terms.
- **Contact Info** (7, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
- **Contact Email** (8, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
- **Payment Method** (9, select_one, optional): Choose your payment method. Choose from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
- **Payment Frequency** (10, select_one, optional): Choose your payment frequency. Choose from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
- **Vendor Status** (11, select_one, optional): Choose your vendor status. Choose from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
