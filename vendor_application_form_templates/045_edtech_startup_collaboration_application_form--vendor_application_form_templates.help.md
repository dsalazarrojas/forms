# Edtech Startup Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Edtech Startup Collaboration Application Form is designed to facilitate the application process for edtech startups. It gathers essential information about the applicant's team, partnership preferences, and other relevant details to help in making informed decisions for potential collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the **Contact Info** section.
2. Specify your partnership preferences in the **Partnership Preferences** section.
3. Provide details about your team in the **Team Info** section.
4. Describe your preferred collaboration style in the **Collaboration Preferences** section.
5. List your tech stack in the **Tech Stack** section.
6. Add any demo information in the **Demo Info** section.
7. Share your review information in the **Review Info** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info**
	+ **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please fill in your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Partnership Preferences**
	+ **Partnership Preferences** (`partnership_preferences`, text, optional): Specify your preferred partnership style, such as exclusive or non-exclusive.
* **Team Info**
	+ **Team Info** (`team_info`, text, optional): Introduce your team members and their roles.
* **Collaboration Preferences**
	+ **Collaboration Preferences** (`collaboration_preferences`, text, optional): Describe your preferred collaboration style, such as remote or in-person meetings.
* **Tech Stack**
	+ **Tech Stack** (`tech_stack`, text, optional): List your tech stack, including programming languages, frameworks, and tools used.
* **Demo Info**
	+ **Demo Info** (`demo_info`, text, optional): Share any demo information, such as links or videos.
* **Review Info**
	+ **Review Info** (`review_info`, text, optional): Share any review information, such as feedback or ratings.
