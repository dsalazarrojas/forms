# Supplier Ethics Declaration Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from suppliers about their business, contact details, and ethics declaration. It is meant to be a general template for suppliers to register themselves for various vendor application processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the accurate and truthful information about your supplier business.
2. Provide the contact details of the person filling out the form and the person responsible for ethics declaration.
3. Write a clear and concise ethics declaration statement.
4. Read and agree to the declaration terms.
5. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): Fill in a brief description of your supplier business.
* **Supplier Business Info** (`supplier_business_info`, text, optional): Provide detailed information about your supplier business.
* **Supplier Contact** (`supplier_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact details of the person filling out the form.
* **Ethics Declaration** (`ethics_declaration`, text, optional): Write a clear and concise statement of your ethics declaration.
* **Declaration Terms** (`declaration_terms`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the declaration.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Vendor Application Info** (`vendor_application_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the vendor application.
* **Vendor Agreement** (`vendor_agreement`, text, optional): This field is reserved for future use and is currently not required. 

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information about your supplier business.
* Ensure that the contact person and ethics declaration person are different individuals.
* Be clear and concise in your ethics declaration statement.
* Read and agree to the terms and conditions before signing the form.
* Do not fill in any field that is not required.
