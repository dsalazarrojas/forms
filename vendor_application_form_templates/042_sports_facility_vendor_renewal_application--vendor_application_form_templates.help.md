# Sports Facility Vendor Renewal Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from sports facility vendors for the purpose of renewing their contract or agreement with the facility. Please provide accurate and complete information in the required fields.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the vendor's information in the "Vendor Information" section, including their name, email, phone, address, city, state, zip code, and country.
2. Select the type of documents required for renewal from the dropdown menu in the "Documents Required" section.
3. Enter the IDs, URLs, and descriptions of the documents in the "Documents" section.
4. Select the current renewal status in the "Renewal Status" section.
5. Fill in the notes and review status in the respective sections.
6. If applicable, enter the renewal date, time, and due date in the respective fields.
7. Finally, add any additional notes or comments about the vendor in the "Vendor Notes" and "Renewal Note" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Information** (1, text, required: false): Enter the vendor's name, email, phone, address, city, state, zip code, and country.
* **Vendor Details** (2, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the vendor.
* **Renewal Period** (3, number, required: false): Select the renewal period for the vendor.
* **Documents Required** (4, select_one, required: false): Select the documents required for renewal, such as Tax ID, Insurance Card, License, or Certification.
* **Document IDs** (5, text, required: false): Enter the IDs of the selected documents.
* **Document URLs** (6, text, required: false): Enter the URLs of the selected documents.
* **Document Descriptions** (7, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the documents.
* **Document Dates** (8, date, required: false): Enter the dates of the selected documents.
* **Document Times** (9, time, required: false): Enter the times of the selected documents.
* **Renewal Status** (10, select_one, required: false): Select the current renewal status, which can be Approved, Rejected, or Under Review.
* **Notes** (11, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the vendor.
* **Vendor Name** (12, text, required: false): Enter the vendor's name.
* **Vendor Email** (13, email, required: false): Enter the vendor's email.
* **Vendor Phone** (14, text, required: false): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Vendor Address** (15, text, required: false): Enter the vendor's address.
* **Vendor City** (16, text, required: false): Enter the vendor's city.
* **Vendor State** (17, text, required: false): Enter the vendor's state.
* **Vendor Zip Code** (18, text, required: false): Enter the vendor's zip code.
* **Vendor Country** (19, text, required: false): Enter the vendor's country.
* **Vendor Notes** (20, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the vendor.
* **Review Status** (21, select_multiple, required: false): Select the review status, which can be Approved, Rejected, or Under Review.
* **Renewal Date** (22, date, required: false): Enter the renewal date.
* **Renewal Time** (23, time, required: false): Enter the renewal time.
* **Renewal Note** (24, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the renewal.
* **Renewal Due Date** (25, date, required: false): Enter the renewal due date.
