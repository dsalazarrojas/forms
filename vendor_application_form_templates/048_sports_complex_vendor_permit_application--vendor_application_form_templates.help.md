# Sports Complex Vendor Permit Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from vendors who wish to apply for a vendor permit at a sports complex. The form is intended for use by vendors who provide goods or services to the sports complex and need to obtain a permit to operate on the premises.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
    *   Fill out the "Vendor Details" page with your company's information.
    *   On the "Permit Details" page, provide the specific details about the permit you are applying for.
    *   On the "Insurance Status" page, indicate your insurance status as a vendor.
    *   Finally, on the "Terms Acceptance" page, confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions of the permit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vendor Details** (Page 1): Please fill out this page with your company's contact information and any other relevant details.
    *   This page is intended to collect your company's information such as name, address, and contact details.
    *   Be sure to fill out all required fields accurately to avoid any delays in processing your permit.
*   **Permit Details** (Page 2): Provide specific details about the permit you are applying for, including the type and duration of the permit.
    *   This page is intended to gather necessary information about the permit you are seeking to obtain.
    *   Ensure that you provide all required details to avoid any issues during the application process.
*   **Insurance Status** (Page 3): Indicate your insurance status as a vendor.
    *   This page is intended to determine your insurance status and ensure that you have adequate coverage for your operations at the sports complex.
*   **Terms Acceptance** (Page 4): Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions of the permit.
    *   This page is intended to ensure that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the permit.
    *   Read carefully and agree to all terms before submitting the form.
