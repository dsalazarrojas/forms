# Event Signage Printing Partner Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for event signage printing vendors to register their interest in partnering with us. By completing this form, you'll be providing us with essential contact and service information, which we'll use to communicate with you and discuss potential partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required for the form. This includes your vendor details, contact information, and service offerings.
2. Select the correct options for each field, as indicated in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, required): Check all the services your company offers. This will help us understand your capabilities and what we can offer to our customers. 
* **Pricing** (`pricing`, select_one, required): Indicate whether your company offers pricing for its services. This is crucial for us to understand your pricing model.
* **Vendor Contact** (`vendor_contact`, text, required): Enter your company's contact person's name and title. This will help us get in touch with you efficiently.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company's email address. This is how we'll communicate with you regarding partnership opportunities.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your company's phone number. This will help us contact you for further discussions.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): If your company has a website, please enter the URL. This will help us learn more about your company and its offerings.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, note, optional): If you have any supporting documents for your company, such as business cards or certificates, you can upload them here. This will help us better understand your company's offerings.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Please read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of our partnership program. This will ensure you're aware of your responsibilities as a partner.

## Tips

* Ensure you complete all required fields to avoid delays in the registration process.
* Double-check your submission before submitting to prevent errors.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact us before submitting the form.
