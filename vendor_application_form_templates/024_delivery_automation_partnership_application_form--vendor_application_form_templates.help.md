# delivery_automation_partnership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "delivery_automation_partnership_application_form" is used to collect information from companies interested in partnering with our company for delivery automation services. This guide walks you through the form fields and their corresponding labels for a smooth submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required and optional fields with accurate and relevant information about your company and partnership preferences.
2. Ensure you submit the correct type of email address for the email fields.
3. Select the correct options for select type fields, such as select_one and select_multiple.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address in the format `yourname@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): Enter your company website URL.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional): Select the role you are interested in for partnership (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of company you represent (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter your location.
* **Delivery Area** (`delivery_area`, `select_one`, optional): Select the delivery area you are interested in (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the delivery frequency (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Partner Since** (`partner_since`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you've been a partner since (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Partner Until** (`partner_until`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you will be a partner until (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your partnership request.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of your company (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Industry** (`company_industry`, `select_one`, optional): Select the industry your company represents (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Partner Agreement** (`partner_agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of partner agreement (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Partner Contact** (`partner_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Select the person in charge of partnership contact (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Partner Contact Email** (`partner_contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the partner contact email address.
* **Partner Contact Phone** (`partner_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the partner contact phone number.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your company address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, optional): Select your state (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

## Tips
* Ensure you submit accurate and relevant information to avoid any issues during the review process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your partnership request and look forward to reviewing your application.
