# Vendor Relations Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Relations Application Form is designed to collect and store information about vendors, including their contact details, business description, and relationship type. This form helps in standardizing the way vendor relations are managed within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required: true") with accurate and complete information.
3. Select the appropriate options for fields like "Vendor Type", "Vendor Category", and "Vendor Relationship Type" from the provided choices.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Vendor Notes" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Information**: Enter the name and any relevant details about the vendor, if applicable.
* **Contact Details**: Fill in the contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Address**: Enter the vendor's business address, if applicable.
* **Business Description**: Briefly describe the vendor's business or services offered.
* **Vendor Type**: Select the type of vendor that best fits the vendor's business or services offered.
* **Vendor Category**: Choose the category that best categorizes the vendor.
* **Business Size**: Select the size of the vendor's business or organization.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person for the vendor.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Business Website**: Enter the vendor's business website, if applicable.
* **Vendor Relationship Type**: Select the type of relationship you have with the vendor.
* **Vendor Manager**: Enter the name of the person managing the vendor relationship.
* **Vendor Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the vendor.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the selected options for fields like "Vendor Type", "Vendor Category", and "Vendor Relationship Type" to ensure they match the vendor's actual status.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, consult with the relevant department or contact person for clarification.
