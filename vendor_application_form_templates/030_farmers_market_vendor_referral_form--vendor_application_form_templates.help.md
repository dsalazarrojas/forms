# Farmers Market Vendor Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Farmers Market Vendor Referral Form is a tool used by market managers to collect information from potential vendors and refer them to the market. This form helps market managers verify the vendor's identity and contact them for further information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (vendor name, vendor email, and referral date) completely and accurately.
2. Provide a valid email address for the vendor.
3. Enter the date of the referral in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Referral** (vendor_referral, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the vendor referral. This can be a note to the market manager or a reason why the vendor is being referred to the market.
* **Vendor Name** (vendor_name, text, optional): This field is for the vendor's name.
* **Vendor Email** (vendor_email, email, optional): Enter the vendor's email address. This will be used to contact the vendor for further information.
* **Vendor Phone** (vendor_phone, text, optional): Enter the vendor's phone number. This can be used to contact the vendor for further information.
* **Referral Date** (referral_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the referral in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Market Manager Name** (market_manager_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the market manager who is referring the vendor to the market.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields completely and accurately.
* Use a valid email address for the vendor.
* Use the correct date format (MM/DD/YYYY) for the referral date.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the market manager for assistance.
