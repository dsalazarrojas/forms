# Vendor Vetting Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Vetting Initiative Registration Form is designed to collect information about vendors and businesses for vetting purposes. This form ensures that vendors provide accurate and up-to-date information about their contact and business details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
6. Enter your company's URL in the "Company URL" field.
7. Provide a brief description of your business in the "Business Description" field.
8. Select your business status from the "Vendor Status" field.
9. Select the type of business you operate in the "Business Type" field.
10. Select the contact person responsible for your business in the "Contact Person" field.
11. Enter your business's URL in the "Business URL" field.
12. Enter your business's address in the "Business Address" field.
13. Select whether your business operates in the "Industry" field.
14. Fill in your job title in the "Job Title" field.
15. Fill in your job role in the "Job Role" field.
16. Select whether you have a vendor ID in the "Vendor ID" field.
17. Select the status you require from the "Status Request" field.
18. Select the status of your business in the "Business Status" field.
19. Select the status of your contact in the "Contact Status" field.
20. Select the other status you require in the "Other Status" field.
21. Fill in the status description required in the "Status Description Req" field.
22. Select the status of your business in the "Business Status (2)" field.
23. Select the status of your contact in the "Contact Status (2)" field.
24. Select the other status you require in the "Other Status (2)" field.
