# Vendor Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Management Form is used to collect and manage information about vendors. It is a multi-page form designed to create, update, or edit vendor records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "New" or "Edit" button to access the form.
2.  Fill out the fields as prompted, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
3.  Review and submit the form once you have completed all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vendor Information** (`vendor_information`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the vendor.
    *   Enter a short text describing the vendor.
*   **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the vendor's name.
    *   Enter the name of the vendor.
*   **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the vendor's phone number.
    *   Enter the vendor's phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or just the number without dashes.
*   **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter the vendor's email address.
    *   Enter the vendor's email address in the format "username@domain.com".
*   **Vendor Note** (`vendor_note`, note, optional): This field is used to add any additional information about the vendor.
    *   Enter any relevant notes or comments about the vendor.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select which tool the vendor is assigned to.
    *   Select one or more tools from the provided options, including "chatjimmy" or "Other".
