# Digital Logistics Partnership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for vendors to submit their application for a digital logistics partnership with our company. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To start, ensure you have reviewed our company's existing partnerships to avoid duplication.
*   Fill out the form by providing the required information in each field.
*   Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Services** (`services`, text, required):
    Please describe your company's services offered. This is an opportunity to highlight your capabilities and what you can offer our company.
*   **Region** (`region`, select_one, required):
    Select the region you operate in from the available options: Logistics Provider 1, Logistics Provider 2, or Logistics Provider 3.
*   **Experience** (`experience`, text, required):
    Share your company's relevant experience in the digital logistics field. This includes any notable achievements, successful projects, or certifications relevant to the partnership.
*   **Documents** (`documents`, text, required):
    Upload or provide a link to any relevant documentation or certifications that support your application.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required):
    Enter your company's official email address for communication purposes. This will be used for any follow-up inquiries.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required):
    Provide your company's phone number for easy contact.
*   **Service Areas** (`service_areas`, select_multiple, required):
    Choose the specific service areas your company operates in from the available options: Area 1, Area 2, or Area 3.
*   **Company** (`company`, text, required):
    Enter your company's name as it appears on their logo or marketing materials.
*   **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional):
    If you have a designated contact person for this partnership application, enter their name and role in your company.

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
*   Double-check your input for any typos or errors before submission.
*   Attach relevant documents or links if asked for in the Documents field.

This guide will help ensure you provide the necessary information to support your partnership application. If you have any further questions, feel free to contact our support team.
