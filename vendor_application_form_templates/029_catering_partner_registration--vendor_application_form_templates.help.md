# Catering Partner Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Catering Partner Registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your business information, including the business name, contact person, and contact title.
2. Provide your email address and phone number.
3. Enter your business address, city, and state.
4. Specify your business type (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, etc.).
5. Choose your cuisine specialties (e.g., American, Italian, etc.).
6. List the services you offer (e.g., Full Service Catering, Drop-off Catering, etc.).
7. Specify the events you cater to (e.g., Weddings, Corporate Events, etc.).
8. Enter your maximum guest capacity.
9. Define the geographic area you serve.
10. Provide your business license and food service permit numbers.
11. Confirm your liability insurance details.
12. Indicate your food safety certification status.
13. Provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter your business name as it is officially registered.
* **Legal Business Name** (`legal_business_name`): Enter the official registered name of your business.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the primary contact for your business.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`): Enter the job title of the primary contact.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter the email address of your business.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`): Enter the physical address of your business.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`): Enter the ZIP code of your business location.
* **Website URL** (`website`): Enter the URL of your business website (optional).
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`): Enter how long your business has been operating.
* **Cuisine Specialties** (`cuisine_specialties`): Select the cuisines your business specializes in.
* **Service Types** (`service_types`): Select the services your business offers.
* **Event Types** (`event_types`): Select the types of events your business caters to.
* **Maximum Guest Capacity** (`capacity`): Enter the largest number of guests your business can handle.
* **Service Area** (`service_area`): Define the geographic area your business serves.
* **Business License Number** (`business_license`): Enter your business license number.
* **Food Service Permit Number** (`food_service_permit`): Enter your food service permit number.
* **Liability Insurance** (`liability_insurance`): Confirm your liability insurance status.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`): Enter the name of your insurance company (optional).
* **Food Safety Certified** (`food_safety_certified`): Confirm your food safety certification status.
* **Health Department Rating** (`health_department_rating`): Enter your latest health department rating (optional).
* **Professional References** (`references`): Provide 2-3 professional references.
* **Why Do You Want to Partner** (`why_partner`): Enter your motivation for wanting to partner with the platform.
* **Commission Rate Acceptable** (`commission_acceptable`): Confirm your commission rate status.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional information or comments about your business.
