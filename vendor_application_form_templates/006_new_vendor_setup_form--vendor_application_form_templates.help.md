# New Vendor Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Vendor Setup Form is used to create a new vendor account for participating in an event or exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your city in the "City" field.
7. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
8. Select your booth type (Yes/No) in the "Booth Type" field.
9. Enter the size of the table you will be using in the "Table Size" field.
10. Enter the number of vendors (if any) that will be representing your company in the "Number Of Vendors" field.
11. Select the date and time for your setup in the "Date" and "Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it should be displayed on any marketing materials.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it should be displayed on any marketing materials.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your business address or physical location.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code or postal code.
* **Booth Type** (`booth_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether you will have a booth or not. Select "Yes" if you will have a booth, "No" if you will not.
* **Table Size** (`table_size`, `text`, required): Enter the size of the table you will be using for your setup.
* **Number Of Vendors** (`number_of_vendors`, `number`, required): Enter the number of vendors that will be representing your company. Enter 0 if there will be only one vendor (you).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date for your setup and tear down.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time for your setup and tear down.
