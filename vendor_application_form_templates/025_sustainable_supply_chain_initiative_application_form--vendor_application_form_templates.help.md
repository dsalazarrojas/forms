# Sustainable Supply Chain Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Supply Chain Initiative Application Form is designed for vendors applying to participate in the Sustainable Supply Chain Initiative. This form collects essential information about the vendor, including contact details, company details, and sustainability practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth application process, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Address (5)**: Enter your physical address.
* **City (6)**: Enter your city's name.
* **State (7)**: Select your state from the provided list.
* **Zip (11)**: Enter your postal zip code.
* **Company (12)**: Enter your company name.
* **Contact Person (13)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for communication.
* **Job Title (14)**: Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Company Type (15)**: If not specified, leave blank.
* **Year Established (16)**: If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Number of Employees (17)**: If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Main Product (18)**: Enter your main product or service.
* **Certification (19)**: If applicable, enter any relevant certifications.
* **Website (20)**: Enter your company's website.
* **Email (21)**: This field is optional and should not be confused with the "Email (3)" field.
* **Contact Person Email (22)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone (23)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone Extension (24)**: Enter the contact person's phone extension (if applicable).
* **Sales Contact (25)**: Enter the name of the sales contact.
* **Purchase Contact (26)**: Enter the name of the purchase contact.
* **Marketing Contact (27)**: Enter the name of the marketing contact.
* **Sustainability Contact (28)**: Enter the name of the sustainability contact.

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in the application process.
* Review your responses for accuracy and completeness.
* If unsure about any field, ask your company's representative or refer to your company's documentation.
* For specific requirements or questions, contact your Sustainability Supply Chain Coordinator or the Sustainable Supply Chain Initiative Team.
