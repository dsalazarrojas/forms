# Fitness Event Vendor Space Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fitness Event Vendor Space Application" form is designed to collect information from vendors who wish to rent out their space for events in a fitness-related setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Share your contact information with us so we can get in touch with you.
3. Let us know about any special requests you may have for your event space rental.
4. Provide your insurance status to help us evaluate the suitability of your space for hosting events.
5. Enter any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know about your space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Contact Details (1, Main)**
• **Contact Details**: (`1`, `text`, required: false)
Contact details is a field where you can enter your name, email, and phone number.

### **Contact Details (2, Contact Details)**
• **Contact Details**: (`2`, `text`, required: false)
This field is similar to the previous field, but it seems to be a duplicate. Please only fill this field if you want to provide additional contact information.

### **Vendor Preferences (3, Vendor Preferences)**
• **Vendor Preferences**: (`3`, `text`, required: false)
In this field, you can mention any specific preferences you have for your event space rental, such as specific dates or hours you are available.

### **Insurance Status (4, Insurance Status)**
• **Insurance Status**: (`4`, `text`, required: false)
This field is for you to indicate your insurance status, which will help us evaluate the suitability of your space for hosting events.

### **Special Requests (5, Special Requests)**
• **Special Requests**: (`5`, `text`, required: false)
In this field, you can mention any special requests you have, such as equipment or services you need us to provide.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields to help us process your application.
- Make sure to be clear and concise in your answers to provide the best possible experience.
- If you have any doubts, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
- We will review your application and get back to you with feedback and instructions on how to proceed.
