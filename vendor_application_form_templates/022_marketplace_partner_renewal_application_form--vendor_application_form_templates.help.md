# Marketplace Partner Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help vendors renew their partnership with our marketplace. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill out the form with your accurate contact details, address, and other relevant information.
3. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the partnership.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional): Enter your partner name as it appears on your business card or website.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact name, title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your business address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth. (Note: This field is not typically required, but some partners may be required to provide this information.)
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your business email address.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, optional): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the partnership. (Note: Select "Option 1")
* **Partner Details** (`partner_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your business or partner.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your partnership.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to renew your partnership.
* **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current renewal status. (Note: This field is not typically required.)
* **Partner ID** (`partner_id`, text, optional): Enter your partner ID.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, optional): Enter your partner email address.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): Enter your partner phone number.
* **Partner Website** (`partner_website`, text, optional): Enter your partner website URL.
* **Partner Description** (`partner_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your partner.
* **Partner Logo** (`partner_logo`, text, optional): Upload a logo of your partner.
* **Partner Image** (`partner_image`, text, optional): Upload a image of your partner.
* **Vendor Status** (`vendor_status`, select_one, optional): Select your vendor status. (Note: Select "Option 1")
* **Agreement 2** (`agreement2`, select_one, optional): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the partnership. (Note: Select "Yes")
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and title.
* **Notes 2** (`notes2`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your partnership.
* **Notes 3** (`notes3`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your partnership.
