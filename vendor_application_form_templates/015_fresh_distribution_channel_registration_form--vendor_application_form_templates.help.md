# Fresh Distribution Channel Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fresh Distribution Channel Registration Form is designed to collect information from businesses or individuals who want to establish a relationship with the company for product distribution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user details in the **User Details** section.
2. Provide information about the channel you're registering in the **Channel Details** section.
3. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Info** section.
4. Add any sales information in the **Sales Info** section.
5. Enter shipping details in the **Shipping Info** section.
6. Provide information about your product in the **Product Info** section.
7. Specify your payment terms in the **Payment Terms** section.
8. Make a submission in the **Submission** section.
9. Enter the name and contact information of your sales representative in the **Sales Rep** and **Sales Rep Contact** sections (if applicable).
10. Enter the email of your sales representative in the **Sales Rep Email** section (if applicable).
