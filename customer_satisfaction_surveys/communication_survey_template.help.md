# communication_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their satisfaction with the communication they receive from us. The goal is to understand their perceptions and identify areas of improvement to enhance their overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Overall, how satisfied are you with the communication you receive from us?" by selecting one of the options provided.
2. Rate the clarity of our communication by choosing one of the five options.
3. Select all the channels you prefer to receive communication from us.
4. Indicate how timely our communication is by choosing one of the five options.
5. If you have any thoughts on specific aspects of our communication that could be improved, type them in the space provided.
6. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Single): Rate your overall satisfaction with the communication you receive from us.
	+ Please select one of the five options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Clarity** (Single): Rate the clarity of our communication.
	+ Choose one of the five options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Communication Channels** (Multiple): Select all the channels through which you prefer to receive communication from us.
	+ Choose from: Email, Phone calls, Text messages, Social media, Website/Portal.
* **Timeliness** (Single): Rate how timely our communication is.
	+ Choose one of the five options: Always timely, Usually timely, Sometimes timely, Rarely timely, Never timely.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (Text): Share specific aspects of our communication that could be improved.
* **Additional Comments** (Text): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about our communication.
