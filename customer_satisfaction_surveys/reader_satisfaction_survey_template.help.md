# reader_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from readers about their overall satisfaction with the content provided. The goal is to understand what readers think about the quality and relevance of the content and use this information to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question about your overall satisfaction with the content.
2. Choose the frequency with which you read the content (daily, weekly, monthly, occasionally, rarely).
3. Select all the content formats that you prefer (articles, blog posts, newsletters, videos, infographics, podcasts).
4. Choose how often you find the content relevant to your interests (always, often, sometimes, rarely, never).
5. In the text fields, provide your suggestions on topics you'd like us to cover more and what could improve your reading experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction**: (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`):
	+ This question asks how satisfied you are with the content you read.
* **Frequency of Reading**: (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Occasionally`, `Rarely`):
	+ This question asks how often you read our content.
* **Preferred Content Format**: (`Articles`, `Blog posts`, `Newsletters`, `Videos`, `Infographics`, `Podcasts`):
	+ This question asks which content formats you prefer.
* **Content Relevance**: (`Always`, `Often`, `Sometimes`, `Rarely`, `Never`):
	+ This question asks how often you find our content relevant to your interests.
* **Topic Suggestions**: (`text field`):
	+ This open-ended question asks for your suggestions on topics you'd like us to cover more.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: (`text field`):
	+ This open-ended question asks for your suggestions on what could improve your reading experience.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
* You can select multiple options for "Preferred Content Format".
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, please use the "Topic Suggestions" and "Improvement Suggestions" fields.
