# new_member_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "new_member_questionnaire_template" form gathers feedback from new members to help the organization assess its effectiveness in providing a positive experience for its newest team members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose a rating that best reflects your overall satisfaction with your role: 
2. Rate your experience with communication from management: 
3. Indicate whether you feel supported in your professional development: 
4. Select all the aspects that were helpful during your onboarding process: 
	* Orientation sessions
	* Mentorship program
	* Welcome kit
	* Access to resources
	* Team introductions
5. Provide any suggestions or comments about your initial experience: 
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?`): This question gauges your initial impression of your new role. Choose the best answer from the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Communication from Management** (`How would you rate communication from management?`): Evaluate your experience with how management keeps you informed or connected. Choose Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feel Supported** (`Do you feel supported in your professional development?`): Answer whether you feel you are receiving enough guidance or backing for your work. Choose true, false, or Somewhat.
* **Helpful Aspects of Onboarding** (`Which aspects of your onboarding experience were most helpful?`): Select all that apply from a list of common onboarding activities.
* **Additional Comments** (`What could be improved about your initial experience at work?`): Write any comments or suggestions you might have about your initial experience.
* **Open-Ended Comments** (`Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?`): Write any thoughts, ideas, or feedback you might have that didn't fit in the other fields.
