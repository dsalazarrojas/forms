# customer_effort_score_ces_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Effort Score (CES) Survey is a tool used to measure the effort customers exert when interacting with our service. It aims to identify areas of improvement and optimize the service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply answer the questions according to your genuine experience with our service. Be honest, and don't hesitate to select "None of the above" or "Other" if none of the provided options match your situation.
- Select one option for the "To what extent" question, and for "How much effort", choose one of the five levels of effort.
- Select all applicable issues for "What made your experience difficult?", and write your answer for "What could have made your experience easier?" and "Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?"

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **To what extent do you agree or disagree with the following statement?** (`strongly_disagree`, `disagree`, `neutral`, `agree`, `strongly_agree`): 
  This question assesses your level of agreement with the statement that our service made it easy for you to handle your issue or complete your task. Choose the option that best reflects your sentiment.
* **How much effort did you personally have to put forth to handle your request?** (`very_high_effort`, `high_effort`, `moderate_effort`, `low_effort`, `very_low_effort`): 
  This question measures the level of effort you personally had to invest to resolve your issue or complete your task. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Was your issue resolved or task completed?** (`true`, `false`, `partially`): 
  This question checks whether your issue was successfully resolved or your task was completed as expected.
* **What made your experience difficult?** (`unclear_instructions`, `long_wait_times`, `technical_issues`, `multiple_contacts`, `agent_knowledge`): 
  Select all that apply from the options provided to describe what made your experience challenging.
* **What could have made your experience easier?** (`text`): 
  Write a brief description of what could have improved your experience with our service.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`text`): 
  Provide any further thoughts or recommendations you may have to help us improve our service.
