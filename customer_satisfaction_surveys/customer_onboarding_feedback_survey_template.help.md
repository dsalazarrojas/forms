# Customer Onboarding Feedback Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our onboarding process. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and provide a better experience for new customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. If you're unsure about any of the questions, just let us know and we'll do our best to clarify. You can select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how satisfied were you with the onboarding process?**
  • Select one of the following options:
    + Very satisfied
    + Somewhat satisfied
    + Neutral
    + Somewhat dissatisfied
    + Very dissatisfied
* **How clear was the information provided during onboarding?**
  • Select one of the following options:
    + Very clear
    + Mostly clear
    + Neutral
    + Somewhat unclear
    + Very unclear
* **Which resources did you find most helpful during onboarding? (select all that apply)**
  • Choose all that apply:
    + Welcome email
    + Product tour
    + Help center
    + Support team
    + Documentation
* **Did you easily find the key features you needed after onboarding?**
  • Select one of the following options:
    + true
    + false
    + Somewhat
* **What was the most challenging part of your onboarding experience?**
  • Please provide a brief description of your experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend our product/service based on your onboarding?**
  • Select one of the following options:
    + Very likely
    + Likely
    + Neutral
    + Unlikely
    + Very unlikely
* **Do you have any further suggestions for improving the onboarding process?**
  • Please provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
