# salon_evaluation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience at our salon. This form helps us understand what they liked and disliked, and what we can do to improve their experience in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your overall satisfaction with the salon visit.
2. Rate the service provided by your stylist or technician using the provided scale.
3. Select all the services you received during this visit from the list.
4. Rate the salon's ambiance and cleanliness using the provided scale.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
6. Indicate how likely you are to recommend our salon to a friend or family member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): Please select one of the options to indicate your overall level of satisfaction with the salon visit.
* **Stylist/Technician Service** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Rate the service provided by your stylist or technician using the scale. A higher rating indicates a more positive experience.
* **Services Received** (`Haircut`, `Hair color`, `Hair styling`, `Manicure`, `Pedicure`, `Facial`, `Other`): Select all the services you received during this visit.
* **Ambiance and Cleanliness** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Acceptable`, `Needs improvement`): Rate the salon's ambiance and cleanliness using the scale.
* **Additional Comments** (`text`): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.
* **Recommendation** (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): Indicate how likely you are to recommend our salon to a friend or family member.
* **Additional Comments/Suggestions** (`text`): This field is a free text area where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
