# event_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event satisfaction survey is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their recent event experience. This feedback will help us improve future events and provide better experiences for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your genuine experience at the event.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in the open-ended questions.
3. Select all applicable activities from the list, even if it's more than one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall Satisfaction (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): 
    Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
- **Event Organization and Communication (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): 
    Assess how well the event was organized and communicated to you.
- **Event Content (`true`, `Mostly`, `Partially`, `Not really`, `false`): 
    Determine if the event content met your expectations.
- **Activities Participated In (Keynote sessions, Breakout sessions, Networking events, Workshops, Exhibition hall): 
    Select all the activities you participated in during the event.
- **Recommendation Likelihood (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): 
    Indicate how likely you are to recommend this event to a colleague or friend.
- **Suggestions for Improvement (`text`): 
    Provide feedback on what aspect of the event could be improved for future editions.
- **Additional Comments or Suggestions (`text`): 
    Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the event.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
- Select only the activities that you participated in.
- If an activity is not listed, it's okay to mention it in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field.
- Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and participation.
