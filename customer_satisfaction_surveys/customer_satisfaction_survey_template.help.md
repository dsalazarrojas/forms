# customer_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer satisfaction survey template is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with our product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question about your overall satisfaction with our product or service.
2. Rate the quality of our customer support using the options provided.
3. Select all the features that are most important to you from the list of options.
4. Choose an answer to the question about whether our product or service met your expectations.
5. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have in the text boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with our product or service?** (`Overall`, `single`, required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with our product or service using the options provided: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **How would you rate the quality of our customer support?** (`Customer support`, `single`, required): Rate the quality of our customer support using the options provided: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not applicable.
* **Which of the following features are most important to you?** (`Features`, `multiple`, required): Select all the features that are most important to you from the list of options: Ease of use, Performance, Reliability, Feature set, Integrations, Price.
* **Did our product or service meet your expectations?** (`Expectations`, `single`, required): Choose an answer to the question about whether our product or service met your expectations: Exceeded expectations, Met expectations, Partially met, or Did not meet.
* **What could we do to improve your experience with us?** (`Additional feedback`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have about how we can improve your experience with us.
* **Do you have any additional feedback or suggestions?** (`Additional feedback`, `text`, optional): This is a text box where you can provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have about our product or service.
