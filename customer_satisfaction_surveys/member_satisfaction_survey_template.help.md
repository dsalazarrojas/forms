# member_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from members on various aspects of their experience within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Choose the options that best match your current sentiment or feelings for each question.
2. Type in your answers for the open-ended questions (3, 4, and 6).
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Very Satisfied/ Somewhat Satisfied / Neutral / Somewhat Dissatisfied / Very Dissatisfied** (1): Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization? Please select the option that best describes your current level of satisfaction.
* **Excellent / Good / Fair / Poor** (2): How would you rate the communication from management? Choose the option that reflects your perception of the quality of communication.
* **Supported / Not Supported / Somewhat** (3): Do you feel supported in your professional development? Select "Supported," "Not Supported," or "Somewhat," depending on your experience.
* (4): What aspects of your experience at work could be improved? Please provide specific details on areas you think need improvement.
* (5): What three words best describe your experience as a member? Type in three words that capture your overall experience.
* (6): Do you have any additional comments or suggestions? Please share any other thoughts or ideas you have for improvement.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Provide specific examples to support your answers.
* Keep your answers concise and clear.
