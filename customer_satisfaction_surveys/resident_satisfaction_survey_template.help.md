# Resident Satisfaction Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey template is a tool for community management to understand resident satisfaction and opinions. It's meant to be a quick and easy way for residents to share their experiences, thoughts, and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select your answers carefully, and don't hesitate to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have. Make sure to choose all applicable community amenities that you regularly use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): Rate your overall satisfaction with living in this community.
* **Communication from Community Management** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`, `Not applicable`): Rate your experience with how community management communicates with you.
* **Upkeep and Cleanliness of Common Areas** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Rate your experience with the upkeep and cleanliness of community areas such as gyms, pools, community rooms, etc.
* **Community Amenities** (`Gym`, `Pool`, `Community Room`, `Laundry Facilities`, `Pet Park`, `Parking`, `None of the above`): Select all amenities you regularly use from the provided options.
* **Sense of Community** (`Strongly agree`, `Agree`, `Neutral`, `Disagree`, `Strongly disagree`): Rate how much you feel a sense of community where you live.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`text`): Share any specific suggestions that would enhance your living experience.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`text`): Add any additional thoughts or ideas you may have about the community.

## Tips
- Be honest when answering questions and providing suggestions.
- Select all applicable community amenities that you regularly use.
- Don't forget to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
