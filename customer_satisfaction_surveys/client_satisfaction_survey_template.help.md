# client_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather client feedback on their satisfaction with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Overall, how satisfied are you with our services?" by selecting one of the provided options.
2. Rate the communication you received from us by selecting one of the provided options.
3. Answer the question "Do you feel our services adequately met your needs?" by selecting one of the provided options.
4. If you used any of our services, select all that apply from the list of available options.
5. If you have any feedback on how we could improve your experience, type it in the "What could improve your overall experience with our services?" field.
6. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them in the "Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for us?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, how satisfied are you with our services?** (`satisfaction`, `single`, required)
  This question asks about your general feeling of satisfaction with our services. Please select one of the provided options to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **How would you rate the communication you received from us?** (`communication`, `single`, required)
  This question asks you to rate the quality of communication you received from us. Please select one of the provided options to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Do you feel our services adequately met your needs?** (`met_needs`, `single`, required)
  This question asks about whether our services met your expectations. Please select one of the provided options to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Which of our services did you utilize?** (`services_used`, `multiple`, required)
  This question asks about which of our services you used. Please select all that apply from the list of available options.
* **What could improve your overall experience with our services?** (`improve_experience`, `text`, optional)
  If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we could improve your experience, please type them in this field.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for us?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
  If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, type them in this field.
