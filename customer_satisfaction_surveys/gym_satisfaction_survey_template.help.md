# gym_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect member feedback on various aspects of the gym experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and answer the questions that apply to you.
2. Select one answer option for each multiple-choice question.
3. For the open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your gym experience?**
	+ A single question that asks about your overall satisfaction.
* **How would you rate the cleanliness and maintenance of the gym facilities?**
	+ This question asks about your opinion on the cleanliness and maintenance of the gym facilities.
* **How would you rate the availability and quality of exercise equipment?**
	+ This question assesses your opinion on the availability and quality of exercise equipment at the gym.
* **Which of the following gym services or areas do you use? (select all that apply)**
	+ This question asks you to select all the gym services or areas that you use.
* **How often do you typically visit the gym each week?**
	+ A question that asks about your frequency of visits to the gym.
* **What specific improvements would you suggest for our gym?**
	+ An open-ended question that invites you to provide specific suggestions for improvements.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?**
	+ A space for you to provide any additional thoughts or feedback about your gym experience.
