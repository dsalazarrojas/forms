# Student satisfaction survey template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from students on their overall satisfaction with their academic experience at your institution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, considering your personal experiences and perceptions.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you feel necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Overall, how satisfied are you with your academic experience at this institution?** 
  (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)

  - This question gauges your general satisfaction with your experience at this institution.
  - Please choose the option that best reflects your feelings.

- **How would you rate the clarity and timeliness of communication from the institution (e.g., faculty, administration)?**
  (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)

  - This question evaluates the quality and timeliness of communication you have received from the institution.
  - Choose the option that best describes your experience.

- **Do you feel adequately supported in your academic and career development?**
  (true, false, Somewhat)

  - This question assesses whether you feel that the institution provides sufficient support for your academic and career growth.
  - Choose the option that best reflects your feelings.

- **Which of the following aspects contribute most to your satisfaction? (Select all that apply)**
  (Quality of teaching, Course content, Campus facilities, Student support services, Social opportunities, Career services)

  - This question asks about the factors that contribute most to your satisfaction with your experience at this institution.
  - Choose all that apply.

- **What is the most significant area you believe could improve your student experience?**
  (Open-ended text field)

  - This question allows you to provide specific feedback on areas you feel need improvement.
  - Please provide as much detail as you can.

- **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?**
  (Open-ended text field)

  - This question allows you to provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
  - Please be as specific as possible.

## Tips

- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Take as much time as you need to complete the survey.
- Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the student experience.
