# Sponsorship Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback on your experience as a sponsor and identify areas for improvement in our sponsorship program. This survey aims to understand your perceptions on various aspects of the program, such as communication, value delivery, and support, to help us make informed decisions for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question about your overall satisfaction with the sponsorship program.
2. Rate the communication you received regarding your sponsorship.
3. Select all the aspects of the sponsorship that delivered the most value to you.
4. Evaluate the support and assistance provided by our team.
5. Indicate your likelihood of continuing your sponsorship with us in the future.
6. Provide any specific improvements you would suggest for the sponsorship program.
7. Add any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your experience as a sponsor?** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): Please select the answer that best describes your level of satisfaction with the sponsorship program.
* **How would you rate the communication you received regarding your sponsorship?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Rate the quality of the communication you received from our team, considering its relevance, timeliness, and effectiveness.
* **Which aspects of your sponsorship delivered the most value?** (Select all that apply):
	+ `Brand visibility`
	+ `Networking`
	+ `Lead generation`
	+ `Content integration`
	+ `Event access`
	+ `CSR alignment`
	Choose all the aspects that you consider were the most valuable to you.
* **How would you rate the support and assistance provided by our team?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Evaluate the level of support and assistance provided by our team, considering its timeliness, quality, and effectiveness.
* **How likely are you to continue your sponsorship with us in the future?** (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`): Indicate your likelihood of continuing your sponsorship with us based on your experience.
* **What specific improvements would you suggest for our sponsorship program?** (Free text): Provide any concrete ideas or suggestions you have for improving the sponsorship program.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (Free text): Add any additional thoughts, ideas, or suggestions you have for the sponsorship program.
