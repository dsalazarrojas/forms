# net_promoter_score_nps_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Net Promoter Score (NPS) survey designed to measure customer satisfaction and loyalty towards a product or service. It helps identify how likely customers are to recommend the product or service to others and gather feedback on areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly to provide accurate feedback.
2. Answer "How likely are you to recommend our product/service to a friend or colleague?" with a score that reflects your level of enthusiasm for the product or service.
3. Select the primary reason for your recommendation likelihood score.
4. Rate the overall satisfaction with the product or service.
5. Evaluate the quality of customer support received.
6. Indicate the aspects of the product or service that you value most (Select all that apply).
7. Provide one suggestion for improvement.
8. Offer any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How likely are you to recommend our product/service to a friend or colleague?** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `single`, required):
  This question helps measure the customer's willingness to recommend the product or service to others. Choose the score that best reflects your sentiment: Extremely likely, Very likely, Neutral, Unlikely, or Extremely unlikely.
* **What is the primary reason for your recommendation likelihood score?** (`primary_reason`, `text`, required):
  Provide a clear and concise explanation for the reasoning behind your score. This helps identify areas of strength and weakness in the product or service.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with our product/service?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `single`, required):
  Rate your satisfaction with the product or service: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **How would you rate the quality of our customer support?** (`customer_support_quality`, `single`, required):
  Evaluate the quality of the support you received: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Applicable.
* **Which of the following features or aspects do you value most?** (`valued_aspects`, `multiple`, optional):
  Select all the features or aspects that you value most about the product or service (Select all that apply): Ease of use, Performance, Feature set, Reliability, Price/Value, User interface.
* **What is one thing we could do to improve your experience with our product/service?** (`improvement_suggestion`, `text`, optional):
  Provide a specific and actionable suggestion for improving the experience with the product or service.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional):
  Offer any extra feedback or suggestions you may have for the product or service.

## Tips
- Be honest in your responses to provide valuable feedback.
- Select all relevant aspects that apply to your response in "Which of the following features or aspects do you value most?"
- Keep your answers clear and concise for ease of understanding.
