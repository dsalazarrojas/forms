# customer_feedback_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer feedback form is designed to collect valuable insights from customers on our product or service. Your feedback will help us improve our offerings and provide better services to you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer the questions to the best of your knowledge.
* For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
* For open-ended questions, provide detailed and specific comments.
* If you have any issues or problems, mention them in the comments sections.
* Please submit your feedback honestly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction**: 
  (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)
  Please rate your overall satisfaction with our product or service.
* **Customer Support**: 
  (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
  Rate your experience with our customer support.
* **Meets Needs**: 
  (true, false, Somewhat)
  Check if our product/service meets your current needs.
* **Most Valuable Aspects**: 
  (Ease of use, Performance, Feature set, Reliability, Customer support, Pricing)
  Select all that apply to identify the most valuable aspects of our product/service.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: 
  (Text input field)
  Write down any suggestions or improvements you have for our product/service.
* **Additional Comments**: 
  (Text input field)
  Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Provide specific examples or details when possible.
* If you have any issues or problems, mention them in the comments sections.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! Your input is valuable to us.
