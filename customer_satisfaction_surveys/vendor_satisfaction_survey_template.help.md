# vendor_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from vendors to improve our services and increase customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Overall, how satisfied are you with our services as a vendor?" with one of the five options provided.
2. Rate our communication regarding services and support by selecting one of the four options.
3. Choose the aspects of our service that you value most (if any) by selecting multiple options from the list.
4. Provide your feedback on what we could improve to better meet your needs as a client.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to include them in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with our services as a vendor?**
	+ This question asks about your overall satisfaction with our services. Please select the option that best represents your feelings.
* **How would you rate our communication regarding services and support?**
	+ This question assesses your experience with our communication and support. Choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
* **Which aspects of our service do you value most? (Select all that apply)**
	+ This question allows you to choose the aspects of our service that you value the most. Select as many options as apply to you.
* **What could we improve to better meet your needs as a client?**
	+ This question provides an opportunity for you to share your thoughts on areas we can improve to better serve you.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?**
	+ This text box is for any further comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
- Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve.
- You can select multiple options for the "Which aspects of our service do you value most?" question.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to share them in the text box.
