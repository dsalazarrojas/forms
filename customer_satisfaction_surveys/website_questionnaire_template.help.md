# website_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback on overall website satisfaction and user experience. It's meant to be completed by visitors who want to provide their input on what they liked or disliked about their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, and try to provide specific examples or reasons for your answers where possible.
2. For the "What was the primary reason for your visit today?" question, select all that apply if it pertains to your visit.
3. For the open-ended questions, be as detailed as you'd like. Your feedback will help us improve the website!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, `single`, `required`):
This question asks how satisfied you were with your overall experience on the website. Please select one of the five options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Ease of Finding Information** (`How easy was it to find the information you were looking for?`, `single`, `required`):
This question asks how easy or difficult it was to find the information you were looking for on the website. Select one of the five options: Very easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, or Very difficult.
* **Primary Reason for Visit** (`What was the primary reason for your visit today?`, `multiple`, `required`):
This question asks what motivated you to visit the website today. Select all that apply from the options listed:
	+ Find information
	+ Make a purchase
	+ Contact support
	+ Browse products/services
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Visual Design** (`How would you rate the visual design and aesthetics of the website?`, `single`, `required`):
This question rates the website's visual design and aesthetics on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Issues Encountered** (`Did you encounter any issues or difficulties during your visit?`, `single`, `required`):
This question asks if you experienced any problems or difficulties while browsing the website. Select True, False, or Not sure.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`What specific improvements would you suggest for our website?`):
This open-ended question invites you to provide specific ideas or suggestions on how to improve the website.
* **Additional Comments** (`Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?`):
This is an optional open-ended question where you can provide any additional feedback or suggestions you'd like to share.
