# Restaurant Customer Satisfaction Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The goal of this survey is to collect honest feedback from customers about their dining experience at our restaurant, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and make changes to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions honestly, choosing one response for each field.
2. For multiple choice questions, select all that apply from the options provided.
3. For open-ended questions, feel free to provide as much detail as you'd like in your response.
4. If you have any specific comments or suggestions, please include them in the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`):
	+ This question asks about your general impression of your dining experience at our restaurant. Choose the response that best reflects your feelings.
* **Food Quality** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`):
	+ Rate the quality of the food you ordered during your visit.
* **Staff Service** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`):
	+ Evaluate the service provided by our staff, including their friendliness and attentiveness.
* **Visit Type** (`Special occasion`, `Casual meal`, `Business`, `Try new place`, `Social gathering`):
	+ Choose all that apply from the options listed, or write in your own description if none are applicable.
* **Recommendation** (`Very likely`, `Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`, `Very unlikely`):
	+ Indicate how likely you are to recommend our restaurant to a friend or colleague.
* **Improvement Suggestions**:
	+ Share specific ideas on how we could improve your next visit. Be as detailed as you'd like!
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ If you have any additional thoughts or comments about your dining experience, feel free to share them here.

**Tips**

* Be honest and sincere in your responses - we value your feedback and want to make our restaurant the best it can be for you.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
* Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our service and create a more enjoyable experience for all our customers.
