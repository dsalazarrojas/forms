# customer_service_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to measure customer satisfaction with customer service interactions. The goal is to gather feedback that can help improve service quality and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Overall, how satisfied are you with the customer service you received?" by selecting one of the provided options.
2. Rate the clarity of communication from the service representative using the scale provided.
3. Indicate whether the service representative effectively resolved your issue.
4. Select all that apply from the list of aspects of the service that you found most helpful (e.g., politeness, knowledge, etc.).
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your recent customer service experience.
6. Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall, how satisfied are you with the customer service you received?`, `single`, **Required**): This question asks for your general feeling about the service experience. Select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **Communication Clarity** (`How would you rate the clarity of communication from the service representative?`, `single`, **Required**): Rate the service representative's communication style, from excellent to poor.
* **Issue Resolution** (`Did the service representative effectively resolve your issue?`, `single`, **Required**): Check whether the service representative successfully addressed your issue.
* **Helpful Aspects** (`What aspects of the service did you find most helpful?`, `multiple`, **Optional**): Select all the aspects of the service that you found helpful during your experience.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`What could be improved about your recent customer service experience?`, `text`, **Optional**): Share any ideas you have on how to improve the service.
* **Additional Comments** (`Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for us?`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide any extra feedback or suggestions you have about your customer service experience.
