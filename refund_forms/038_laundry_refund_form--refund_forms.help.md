# Laundry Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laundry Refund Form is a tool for customers to request a refund for a laundry service they may not have received or were not satisfied with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are submitting a new request for a refund. If you have already submitted a refund request, please do not fill out this form again.
2. Enter a clear and concise description of the reason you are requesting a refund in the "refund_reason" field.
3. If applicable, provide a brief description of the issue with your laundry service in the "laundry_service_issue" field.
4. Enter the date you received the laundry service you are requesting a refund for in the "date_of_service" field.
5. If applicable, enter the time you received the laundry service you are requesting a refund for in the "time_of_service" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field so we can contact you to discuss your refund request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, text, required): Describe the reason for your refund request in a clear and concise manner.
  * Please provide a specific reason for your refund request, such as "Did not receive service" or "Service not completed as promised".
  * Keep your description brief and to the point.

- **Laundry Service Issue** (`laundry_service_issue`, note, optional): If you experienced an issue with your laundry service, please describe it here.
  * This field is optional, but it can help us understand the root cause of your issue.
  * Keep your description brief and to the point.

- **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): Enter the date you received the laundry service you are requesting a refund for.
  * Please enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
  * If you are unsure, check your records or calendar for the correct date.

- **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time you received the laundry service you are requesting a refund for.
  * Please enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
  * If you are unsure, check your records or calendar for the correct time.

- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you to discuss your refund request.
  * Please ensure your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
  * We will use this email address to contact you regarding your refund request.
