# Overpayment Recovery Waiver Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for overpaid wages. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your overpayment is processed correctly and that you receive your refund promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the `employeeName` field to identify yourself as the overpayment requester.
2. Select your department in the `department` field to specify the department you belong to.
3. Enter your manager's name in the `managerName` field to provide their approval.
4. Enter the date you are making the request in the `dateOfRequest` field.
5. Provide a brief explanation for your request in the `reasonForRequest` field.
6. Enter the amount of overpayment in the `overpaymentAmount` field.
7. Enter the frequency of overpayment in the `overpaymentFrequency` field.
8. Enter the total amount of overpayment in the `overpaymentTotal` field.
9. Select the current status of the overpayment in the `overpaymentStatus` field.
10. Enter any comments or remarks about your request in the `comments` field.
11. Enter your employee ID number in the `employee_id` field.
12. Select who created the original overpayment in the `created_by` field.
13. Enter the date and time the overpayment was created in the `updated_at` field.
14. Enter the date of the overpayment in the `overpaymentDate` field.
15. Enter the person who submitted the request in the `submittedBy` field.
16. Enter the date and time the request was submitted in the `submittedAt` field.
17. Enter the date and time the overpayment was created in the `createdAt` field.
18. Enter the date and time the overpayment was updated in the `updated_at2` field.
19. Enter the date and time the overpayment was created in the `createdAt2` field.
20. Enter the date and time the request was submitted in the `submittedAt2` field.
21. Enter the amount recovered from the overpayment in the `overpaymentRecoveredAmount` field.
22. Enter the frequency of recovered amount in the `overpaymentRecoveredFrequency` field.
23. Enter the frequency of recovered amount 2 in the `overpaymentRecoveredFrequency2` field.
24. Enter the frequency of recovered amount 3 in the `overpaymentRecoveredFrequency3` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overpayment Recovery Waiver Request**: This field is the title of the form, which is a reminder of its purpose.
* **employeeName**: Enter your name as the overpayment requester.
* **department**: Select your department from the given options.
* **managerName**: Enter your manager's name for their approval.
* **dateOfRequest**: Enter the date you are making the request.
* **reasonForRequest**: Provide a brief explanation for your request.
* **overpaymentAmount**: Enter the amount of overpayment you want to recover.
* **overpaymentFrequency**: Enter the frequency of the overpayment.
* **overpaymentTotal**: Enter the total amount of overpayment.
* **overpaymentStatus**: Select the current status of the overpayment.
* **comments**: Enter any comments or remarks about your request.
* **employee_id**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **created_by**: Select who created the original overpayment.
* **updated_at**: Enter the date and time the overpayment was updated.
* **overpaymentDate**: Enter the date of the overpayment.
* **submittedBy**: Enter the person who submitted the request.
* **submittedAt**: Enter the date and time the request was submitted.
* **createdAt**: Enter the date and time the overpayment was created.
* **updated_at2**: Enter the date and time the overpayment was updated.
* **createdAt2**: Enter the date and time the overpayment was created.
* **submittedAt2**: Enter the date and time the request was submitted.
* **overpaymentRecoveredAmount**: Enter the amount recovered from the overpayment.
* **overpaymentRecoveredFrequency**: Enter the frequency of recovered amount.
* **overpaymentRecoveredFrequency2**: Enter the frequency of recovered amount 2.
* **overpaymentRecoveredFrequency3**: Enter the frequency of recovered amount 3.
