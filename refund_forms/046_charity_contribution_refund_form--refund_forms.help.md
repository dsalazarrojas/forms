# Charity Contribution Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for a charitable contribution made to our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start Submission" button to begin.
2. Fill in your name and email address to contact you about the refund.
3. Enter the date of the original donation and the amount you wish to be refunded.
4. Provide a clear reason for requesting the refund.
5. Select the original payment method used for the donation.
6. Optionally, you can provide supporting documents for internal review.
7. Type your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
8. Finally, select the date of the refund request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, required): Your name as used on the original contribution.
- **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, required): Your email address for contact and refund confirmation.
- **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, optional): The date of the original donation.
- **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, required): The amount to be refunded.
- **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, required): A clear explanation of why you are requesting a refund.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, optional): The original payment method used for the donation (e.g. Original payment method, Bank transfer, Check).
- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, optional): Any receipts or proof for internal review.
- **Consent Declaration** (`consent_declaration`, required): Type your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
- **Refund Request Date** (`request_date`, required): The date you are requesting the refund.
