# E Learning Subscription Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for an E-learning subscription. Please fill out the form below to initiate the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, as incomplete or incorrect information may lead to delays or rejections in the refund process.
2. Ensure you have a valid reason for the refund.
3. Review the form before submitting it, as you will not be able to edit it after submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submission Request** (`submission_request`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your refund request.
- **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed explanation for your refund request.
- **Refund for** (`reason_for_refund_select`, select_one, required/optional): Select the reason for your refund from the options provided.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student requesting the refund.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the student's E-learning subscription.
- **Student Phone Number** (`student_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number associated with the student's E-learning subscription.
- **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of the refund requested.
- **Course ID** (`course_id`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the course associated with the subscription.
- **Refund Date** (`refund_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for which the refund is being requested.
- **Refund Time** (`refund_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for which the refund is being requested.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information or context related to the refund request.
- **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the refund request was submitted by the student or a parent/guardian.
