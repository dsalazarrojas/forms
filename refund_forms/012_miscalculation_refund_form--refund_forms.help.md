## Purpose
The Miscalculation Refund Form is designed for customers to request a refund due to a billing error on their account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **billing_error** (`billing_error`, text, optional): Describe the billing error that occurred.
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name so we can address you properly.
* **refund_amount** (`refund_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you believe was incorrectly charged.
* **reason_for_refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the refund.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, please provide it in this space.
