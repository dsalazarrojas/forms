# Refund Timeframe Exception Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to request an exception to the timeframe for a refund. If you're experiencing difficulties returning your purchase within the originally specified timeframe, please use this form to provide additional information or context that would justify a refund outside of the standard window.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide a clear and concise reason for your refund request in the "Reason" field.
2. Enter the relevant details about your purchase in the "Purchase Details" field.
3. Attach or reference any relevant documentation related to your purchase in the "Document" field.
4. Enter your name as it appears on your account or order in the "Customer Name" field.
5. Enter the order number related to your purchase in the "Order Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reason** (Text, Optional): Provide a clear and concise explanation of why you're requesting a refund outside of the original timeframe.
* **Purchase Details** (Text, Optional): Share any relevant details about your purchase, such as the product description, purchase date, or any other information that can help us understand your situation.
* **Document** (Text, Optional): Attach or reference any relevant documentation related to your purchase, such as receipts, invoices, or contracts.
* **Customer Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your account or order.
* **Order Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the order number related to your purchase.
