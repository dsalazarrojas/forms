# Refund Reasons For Social Contributions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document reasons for social contributions refunds, allowing for transparency and accountability in the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant refund options for the contribution (reason_for_refund and reason_for_refund_2).
2. Enter the date of refund (if applicable).
3. Calculate the amount of refund for each contribution (amount_of_refund).
4. Calculate the total amount refunded (total_amount and total_refund).
5. Provide contact information (email and phone number, optional).
6. Add any additional note about the refund (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`): A field to select multiple refund reasons for social contributions. Select all relevant options from the provided list.
* **Reason for Refund 2** (`reason_for_refund_2`): A field to select an additional refund reason for social contributions. Select 'Yes' for the applicable reason.
* **Date of Refund** (`date_of_refund`): The date of the refund. If the refund is not date-dependent, you can leave this field empty.
* **Amount of Refund** (`amount_of_refund`): The amount of refund for each contribution. Enter a numeric value.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`): A field to calculate the total amount refunded. This value is not editable.
* **Reason for Refund 2** (`reason_for_refund_2`): A field to select an additional refund reason for social contributions. Select 'Yes' for the applicable reason.
* **Total Refund** (`total_refund`): A field to calculate the total amount refunded. This value is not editable.
* **Email** (`email`): A field to enter the contact email address. Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): A field to enter the contact phone number. Enter a valid phone number.
* **Note** (`note`): A field to add any additional information about the refund. This is optional.
