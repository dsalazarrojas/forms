# Test Prep Course Refund Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by students and test prep providers to request a refund for a test prep course. The form collects information about the reason for the refund request, the amount of the refund, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Make sure to provide all the required and optional fields, as indicated by the label.
2. Select a reason for the refund request in the "refund_reason" field.
3. Enter the refund amount in the "refund_amount" field.
4. Identify your user type in the "user_details" field. You can select "Student", "Provider", or "Other".
5. If you are a provider, enter your name in the "provider_name" field and your contact information in the "provider_contact" field.
6. Select a refund status in the "refund_status" field.
7. Choose a follow-up date and time for contacting the student in the "follow_up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **course_refund_request_form** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your refund request.
* **refund_reason** (Text, Optional): Please specify the reason for the refund request. This could be due to a cancellation, a course change, or a financial issue.
* **refund_amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the amount of the refund you are requesting.
* **user_details** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your user type. This could be "Student", "Provider", or "Other".
* **student_id** (Text, Optional): Enter your student ID number (if applicable).
* **refund_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you are requesting the refund for.
* **provider_name** (Text, Optional): If you are a test prep provider, enter your name here.
* **provider_contact** (Text, Optional): If you are a provider, enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number) here.
* **refund_status** (Select One, Optional): Select the status of your refund request. This could be "Pending", "Approved", or "Declined".
* **follow_up** (Time, Optional): Suggest a follow-up date and time to contact the student.
