# Cancellation And Refund Policy For Private Courses - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to handle cancellations and refunds for private courses. It is essential to ensure that students understand the process and implications of their decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the student information section with your full name, registration ID, and email address.
2. Select the course name and instructor name from the available options.
3. Choose the type of request you wish to make (full cancellation, partial cancellation, rescheduling request, or course transfer).
4. Select the reason for your request from the given options.
5. Provide additional details about your reason and the preferred method of refund.
6. Review and confirm that you have read the refund policy.
7. Confirm that you agree to non-refundable fees and understand the finality of this decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name**: Enter your full name as per your identity documents.
* **Student ID or Registration Number**: Enter the ID or registration number found in your enrollment confirmation email.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address associated with your course enrollment.
* **Course Name**: Select the full title of the course you are enrolled in.
* **Instructor Name**: Select the name of your instructor.
* **Type of Request**: Choose the type of request you wish to make:
  - Full Cancellation: Cancel the entire course
  - Partial Cancellation: Cancel only a portion of the course
  - Rescheduling Request: Change the schedule of the course
  - Course Transfer: Transfer to a different course
* **Reason for Request**: Select the most accurate reason for your request:
  - Scheduling Conflict
  - Medical Reason
  - Relocation
  - Dissatisfaction
  - Other
* **Elaborate on Reason**: Provide additional context for your request.
* **I have read the cancellation policy**: Confirm that you have reviewed the standard terms and conditions.
* **Percentage of refund expected**: Choose the percentage of refund based on the notice period rules:
  - 100% (7+ days notice)
  - 50% (3-6 days notice)
  - 0% (less than 3 days)
  - Other
* **Refund Method**: Select your preferred method of receiving the refund:
  - Credit Card (Back to original)
  - PayPal
  - Bank Transfer
  - Course Credit for Future
* **Agreement to non-refundable fees**: Confirm that you agree to administrative fees being deducted from the refund.
* **I understand the finality of this decision**: Confirm that you understand that cancellation may lead to loss of placement in the course.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date on which the form is submitted.
