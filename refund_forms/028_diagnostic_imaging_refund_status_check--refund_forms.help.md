# Diagnostic Imaging Refund Status Check - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Diagnostic Imaging Refund Status Check form is designed to help users track the status of diagnostic imaging refunds. This form is used to monitor and update the progress of imaging refunds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Enter the date the imaging refund was received in the `date_received` field.
- Enter the date the imaging refund was sent in the `date_sent` field.
- Select the status of the imaging refund from the options provided in the `status` field.
- Enter any follow-up information in the `follow_up` field (optional).
- Select the facility where the imaging refund was processed from the options provided in the `facility` field.
- Select the team responsible for processing the imaging refund from the options provided in the `team` field.
- Select the status of the patient's refund from the options provided in the `patient_refund_status` field.
- Provide any additional next steps or comments in the `next_steps` field. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Diagnostic Imaging Refund Status Check Form** (`form_1`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
- **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): Enter the date the imaging refund was received.
- **Date Sent** (`date_sent`, text, optional): Enter the date the imaging refund was sent.
- **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the imaging refund from the options provided:
  - Not Started
  - In Progress
  - Completed
- **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, note, optional): Enter any follow-up information related to the imaging refund.
- **Facility** (`facility`, select_one, optional): Select the facility where the imaging refund was processed:
  - Imaging
  - Radiology
  - Other
- **Team** (`team`, select_one, optional): Select the team responsible for processing the imaging refund:
  - Billing
  - Clinical
  - Other
- **Patient Refund Status** (`patient_refund_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the patient's refund from the options provided:
  - Not Started
  - In Progress
  - Completed
- **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Provide any additional next steps or comments related to the imaging refund.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate dates for date received and date sent.
- Select the correct status and facility for the imaging refund.
- Choose the correct team and patient refund status for the imaging refund.
- Enter any necessary follow-up information in the follow-up field.
- Provide clear and concise next steps to ensure the imaging refund is processed correctly.
