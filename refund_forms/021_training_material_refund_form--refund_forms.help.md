# Training Material Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Material Refund Form" is used to process refunds for training materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information. The form requires your:
	* User name
	* Reason for the refund
	* Email address
	* Phone number
	* Amount you are requesting to be refunded
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely. Make sure to provide valid information for each field.
3. Submit the form to process your refund.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Name
* **User name**: Enter your name as it appears on your training materials order confirmation.
* Required

### Reason
* **Reason**: Provide a valid reason for requesting a refund. This will help us process your request efficiently.
* Required

### Email Address
* **Email address**: Enter your active and valid email address. This is how we will communicate with you about your refund.
* Required

### Phone Number
* **Phone number**: Enter your active and valid phone number. This is optional but helps us contact you if needed.
* Optional

### Refund Reason
* **Refund reason**: Enter a valid reason for requesting a refund. This will help us process your request efficiently.
* Required

### Amount
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you are requesting to be refunded.
* Required

Note: The amount field is a number field, so ensure you enter a numerical value.
