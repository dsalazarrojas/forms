# Chargeback Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by customers to request a chargeback refund for a transaction that was not properly handled by the merchant. It helps to gather information about the transaction and the customer's reason for requesting a refund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your customer information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter the date of the transaction.
3. Enter the total amount charged for the transaction.
4. Provide the transaction ID or order number from your receipt.
5. Select the type of request you are making (e.g. full refund, partial refund, chargeback dispute, etc.).
6. Choose the primary reason for your request.
7. Enter a detailed explanation of what happened.
8. Indicate whether you have contacted the merchant and if you have any supporting documents.
9. Select your preferred resolution for the issue.
10. Indicate the level of urgency you are experiencing with this request.
11. Add any additional comments you may have about the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): This is your full name.
- **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): This is your contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required): This is your contact phone number.
- **Transaction Date** (`transaction_date`, date, required): This is the date of the transaction.
- **Transaction Amount** (`transaction_amount`, number, required): This is the total amount charged for the transaction.
- **Transaction ID or Order Number** (`transaction_id`, text, required): This is the reference number from your receipt.
- **Merchant Name** (`merchant_name`, text, required): This is the name of the store or business.
- **Type of Request** (`request_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of request you are making (e.g. full refund, partial refund, chargeback dispute, etc.).
- **Reason Category** (`reason_category`, select_one, required): Choose the primary reason for your request.
- **Detailed Explanation** (`reason_details`, text, required): Enter a detailed explanation of what happened.
- **Did you contact the merchant first?** (`attempted_resolution`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have contacted the merchant.
- **Do you have supporting documents?** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any supporting documents.
- **Preferred Resolution** (`preferred_resolution`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred resolution for the issue.
- **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of urgency you are experiencing with this request.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the issue.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for guidance.
- Please do not submit duplicate requests for the same transaction.
- If you have any supporting documents, please attach them to the form if possible.
- If you are experiencing a high level of urgency, please let us know so we can expedite the process.
