# Delayed Service Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a refund for a delayed service. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your input in the designated fields, making sure to provide all necessary information.
2. If you need to upload any attachments to support your request, click on the "Attachment Upload" field and select the relevant file type.
3. Fill in the "Service Provider" field with the name of the service provider.
4. Check the "Reason for Rejection" box if applicable.
5. Check the "Customer Agreement" box if you agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Check the "Business Agreement" box if you agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Once you have completed all fields, click on the "Submit" button to send your refund request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: This is the main input field for your refund request. Please enter a brief description of the delayed service and the reason for the delay.
* **Reason for Delay**: This field is used to specify the reason for the delay.
* **Service Date**: This field is used to enter the date of the service.
* **Refund Amount**: This field is used to specify the amount of refund requested.
* **Comments**: This field is used to add any additional comments about your refund request.
* **Email**: This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **Business Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the business providing the service.
* **Business Phone**: This field is used to enter the phone number of the business.
* **Business Email**: This field is used to enter the email address of the business.
* **Business Address**: This field is used to enter the address of the business.
* **Service Provider**: This field is used to enter the name of the service provider.
* **Reason for Rejection**: Check this box if the service provider is not accepting your refund request.
* **Customer Agreement**: Check this box if you agree to the terms and conditions of the refund request.
* **Business Agreement**: Check this box if the business agrees to the terms and conditions of the refund request.
* **Attachment Upload**: This field is used to upload any supporting attachments, such as PDFs, images, audio or video files, and documents.
* **Attachment Remove**: This field is used to select the type of attachment to be removed.
* **Attachment Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the uploaded attachment.
* **Attachment Data**: This field is used to enter additional data about the attachment.
* **Attachment Type**: This field is used to select the type of attachment to be uploaded.
* **Business Message**: This field is used to enter any additional message from the business.
* **Service Status**: This field is used to select the status of the service.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Make sure to upload any necessary attachments to support your refund request.
* Review your submission before clicking on the "Submit" button to ensure that all fields are filled in correctly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the relevant authority for assistance.
