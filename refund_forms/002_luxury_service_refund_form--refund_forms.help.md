# Luxury Service Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luxury Service Refund Form is a tool used to process refunds for luxury services that do not meet customer expectations. This form is meant to be filled out by customers who have experienced a problem with their luxury service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have tried to resolve the issue with the service provider before completing this form.
2. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
3. Select the reason for refund from the options provided.
4. Enter the correct refund amount.
5. Provide any additional information that may be helpful in processing the refund.
6. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
7. Sign and date the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`): Please enter your name and contact information so that we can contact you regarding your refund.
* **Reason for Refund** (`refund_reason`): Choose the reason that best describes why you are requesting a refund. Options include:
	+ Product issue
	+ Order not delivered
	+ Order not as described
	+ Other (please describe)
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`): Enter the exact amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **Refund Date** (`refund_date`): Enter the date you are requesting the refund for.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`): If you have any additional information that may be helpful in processing your refund, please enter it here.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`): If you are requesting a refund for a specific service provider, please select the one that corresponds to your issue.
* **Refund Status** (`refund_status`): Select the status of your refund request:
	+ Pending
	+ Approved
	+ Declined
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`): If you have any other information that may be helpful in processing your refund, please enter it here.
* **Confirm** (`confirmation`): Confirm that the information you have provided is accurate.
* **Review and Sign** (`review_and_sign`): Review the information provided and sign to confirm that it is accurate.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign your name to complete the form.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the current date.
