# Refund Appeal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refund Appeal Request Form is a tool for customers or users to submit a refund appeal in case of issues with their orders, shipments, or service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form accurately and completely with the required information.
2.  Select the reason for the refund appeal from the options provided in the 'reason' field.
3.  Provide a brief description of the issue in the 'description' field.
4.  If applicable, attach supporting documentation or relevant files to the 'attachments' field.
5.  Enter the date and time of the issue in the 'date' and 'time' fields.
6.  If necessary, include any additional comments in the 'customer_message' field.
7.  List any additional attachments or evidence supporting your refund appeal in the 'attachments' field.
8.  Sign and date the 'signature' field to confirm your identity and intent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This field is for your contact information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details. Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Reason**: Select the reason for your refund appeal from the options provided:
	+ Late: For delayed or missed deliveries.
	+ Non-delivered Goods: For items not delivered or received as expected.
	+ Non-received Goods: For items not received as expected.
	+ Non-delivered Service: For services not delivered or received as expected.
	+ Non-received Service: For services not received as expected.
	+ Other: For any other reasons not listed.
* **Description**: Please provide a brief description of the issue you're experiencing. This will help us understand the problem better and resolve it efficiently.
* **Attachment**: You can attach supporting documentation or files to this field if you need to. This can include photos, videos, or any other relevant evidence.
* **Date**: Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Time**: Enter the time when the issue occurred.
* **Customer Message**: If you have any additional comments or messages, please include them here. This will help us understand your concerns and resolve the issue faster.
* **Attachments**: If you have multiple attachments or evidence supporting your refund appeal, list them here.
* **Signature**: Please sign and date this field to confirm your identity and intent.
* **Attachments**: Please ensure that you've attached all relevant supporting documentation or files to this field.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and straightforward. If you're unsure about any part of the process, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
