# Vending Machine Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to initiate a refund request for a transaction made from a vending machine.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your refund.
3. Enter the transaction ID that corresponds with your purchase.
4. Describe the reason you are requesting a refund for.
5. Enter the date of the transaction.
6. Enter the ID of the vending machine involved in the transaction.
7. Describe the product you purchased.
8. Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
9. If you were assisted by a support representative, please enter their name.
10. If you have any additional comments or messages, please provide them here.
11. Finally, enter the date you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your refund.
* **Transaction ID** (`transaction_id`, text, required): Enter the transaction ID that corresponds with your purchase.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, required): Describe the reason you are requesting a refund for.
* **Date of Transaction** (`date_of_transaction`, date, required): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Vending Machine ID** (`machine_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the vending machine involved in the transaction.
* **Product Purchased** (`product_purchased`, text, required): Describe the product you purchased.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **Assigned Support** (`assigned_support`, text, optional): If you were assisted by a support representative, enter their name.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or messages, provide them here.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of who is submitting this form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.

**Tips**

- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid delays in processing your refund.
- If you need to attach any supporting documents or evidence, please do not attach them directly to this form. Instead, send them to our email or other specified contact methods.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with this form, please contact us at [support contact].
