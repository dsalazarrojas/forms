# Hotel Deposit Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Deposit Refund Form is designed to process refunds for customers who have made deposits to book hotel rooms. This form allows you to select a reason for the refund, enter the customer's contact information, and enter details about the deposit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for the refund from the "Refund" options.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
4. Enter the hotel's name, where the deposit was made.
5. Select the date of the deposit from the calendar.
6. Enter the refund amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Refund** (`refund`, `select_one`, not required): This field allows you to select a reason for the refund.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the customer's name here.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the customer's email address here.
* **Hotel Name** (`hotel_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the hotel where the deposit was made.
* **Deposit Date** (`deposit_date`, `date`, not required): Select the date of the deposit from the calendar.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, `number`, not required): Enter the refund amount.

## Tips
* Make sure to select a valid reason for the refund.
* Enter the customer's contact information accurately.
* Double-check the hotel name and deposit date to ensure accuracy.
* Enter the correct refund amount.
