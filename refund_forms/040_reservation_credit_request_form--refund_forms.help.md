# Reservation Credit Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reservation Credit Request Form is designed to facilitate the process of requesting a refund or credit for a reservation. This form is used to collect information from customers regarding their reservation details, reason for cancellation, and any additional comments or responses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your reservation details in the text field at the top of the form.
2. Provide your contact number so that we can reach out to you for further information.
3. Enter the date of your reservation in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
4. Provide any additional details about your reservation in the text field.
5. If you need to cancel your reservation, enter the reason for cancellation in the text field.
6. If you have any response to our cancellation, enter it in the text field.
7. Enter your customer email address so that we can contact you.
8. Enter your customer phone number so that we can reach out to you for further information.
9. Enter any additional message you would like to communicate to us in the text field.
10. Finally, our staff will review your request and respond to it in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Credit Request** (`reservation_credit_request_form`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your reservation request.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter your contact number so that we can reach out to you for further information.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your reservation in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Reservation Details** (`reservation_details`, text, not required): Enter any additional details about your reservation.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, not required): If you need to cancel your reservation, enter the reason here.
* **Response to Cancellation** (`response_to_cancellation`, text, not required): If you have any response to our cancellation, enter it here.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter your customer email address so that we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter your customer phone number so that we can reach out to you for further information.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, not required): Enter any additional message you would like to communicate to us.
* **Staff Response** (`staff_response`, text, not required): Our staff will respond to your request here.
* **Staff Comment** (`staff_comment`, text, not required): Our staff will add any additional comments here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field.
* If you need help with your reservation, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We will review your request and respond to you as soon as possible.
