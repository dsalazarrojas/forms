# Customer Refund Verification Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers seeking a refund for a purchase they made. The support team will review and process the information provided to ensure a smooth resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Determine if you are eligible for a refund by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Customer Refund Request" section.
2. Provide detailed information about the purchase, including the "Purchase Details", "Order Date", "Order ID", "Product Name", "Purchase Price", and "Purchase Location".
3. Specify the reason for the refund in the "Reason for Refund" section.
4. Provide supporting documentation for the refund request in the "Refund Documentation" section.
5. Indicate if you have already spoken with the support team about the refund in the "Support Team" section.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Refund Request**: Select "Yes" if you are eligible for a refund, "No" if you are not.
* **Purchase Details**: Enter a detailed description of the purchase you are requesting a refund for.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the purchase.
* **Order ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the purchase.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product purchased.
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the price you paid for the product.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your order.
* **Purchase Location**: Enter the location where the purchase was made.
* **Reason for Refund**: Select all reasons that apply for the refund.
* **Refund Documentation**: Upload any supporting documentation for the refund request.
* **Support Team**: Select "Yes" if you have already spoken with the support team about this refund.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and detailed information to facilitate a smooth resolution process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the support team.
* You can upload supporting documentation, such as receipts or proof of purchase, to support your refund request.
