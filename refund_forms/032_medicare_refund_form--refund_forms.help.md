# MedicaReFund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MedicaReFund Form is designed to collect information for a Medicare refund request. To complete this form correctly, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name as the **Provider Name** in the **Provider Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
4. Enter the claim number in the **Claim Number** field.
5. Enter the date of the claim in the **Claim Date** field in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
6. Enter the overpayment amount in the **Overpayment Amount** field, using the currency format `###.##` (e.g., `100.00`).
7. Choose the reason for the overpayment from the **Overpayment Reason** select field. Options include:
	* Overpayment Due to Insurance Company
	* Overpayment Due to Other
8. (Optional) If necessary, select the type of supporting documents you're attaching in the **Supporting Documents** select field. Options include:
	* Supporting Documentation
	* Other Documents
9. Upload or attach any relevant supporting documents in the **Supporting Documents** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Provider Name** (text): Enter your name as the provider.
- **Patient Name** (text): Enter the patient's name.
- **Date of Birth** (date): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- **Claim Number** (text): Enter the claim number.
- **Claim Date** (date): Enter the claim date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- **Overpayment Amount** (number): Enter the overpayment amount in the format `###.##` (e.g., `100.00`).
- **Overpayment Reason** (select one):
	+ Overpayment Due to Insurance Company
	+ Overpayment Due to Other
- **Supporting Documents** (select multiple):
	+ Supporting Documentation
	+ Other Documents

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are accurately filled out to ensure the refund process is completed correctly.
- Attach relevant supporting documents when necessary.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and instructions above.
