# Freight Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Refund Form is used to request a refund for freight charges associated with your order. This form helps our team process your refund request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the "Reason For Refund" dropdown menu to choose the correct reason for your refund request.
2.  Enter the date of the refund in the "Date Of Refund" field.
3.  Specify the amount you are requesting to be refunded in the "Amount Of Refund" field.
4.  If applicable, provide shipping information to ensure accurate refund processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (1, text, optional): Enter the customer information, such as name and contact details, as it appears on the original order.
* **Reason For Refund** (2, select_one, optional): Choose the correct reason for the refund from the dropdown menu. Possible options are Service Issue, Lost Package, and Billing Error.
* **Date Of Refund** (3, date, optional): Enter the date when the refund was processed or expected to be processed.
* **Amount Of Refund** (4, number, optional): Enter the amount you are requesting to be refunded.
* **Shipping Info** (5, text, optional): If applicable, provide shipping information for accurate refund processing.
* **Address** (6, text, optional): Enter the address associated with the shipping information.
* **City** (7, text, optional): Enter the city where the package was shipped from or to.
* **State** (8, text, optional): Enter the state where the package was shipped from or to.
* **Zip** (9, text, optional): Enter the zip code associated with the shipping information.
* **Country** (10, text, optional): Enter the country of origin or destination of the shipment.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information for the refund request.
* Ensure that you have reviewed the terms and conditions of your original order before submitting this form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact our support team for further assistance.
