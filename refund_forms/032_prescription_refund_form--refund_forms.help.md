# Prescription Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Refund Form is a medical document used to request a refund on a prescription medication. This form helps to gather essential information about the patient, the doctor who prescribed the medication, and the reason for the refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the patient's name.
* Enter the date of the visit when the patient was prescribed the medication.
* Enter the doctor's name who prescribed the medication.
* Specify the reason for the refund request.
* Enter the prescription number.
* Provide a description of the error in the prescription.
* Explain the outcome or result of the prescription.
* Enter the refund amount.
* Add any additional comments or details about the refund request.
* (Optional) Enter the patient's email address and phone number for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Visit** (date_of_visit, date, required): Enter the date of the visit when the patient was prescribed the medication.
* **Doctor Name** (doctor_name, text, required): Enter the name of the doctor who prescribed the medication.
* **Reason for Refund** (reason_for_refund, text, required): Specify the reason for the refund request. Please be detailed and provide as much information as possible.
* **Prescription Number** (prescription_number, number, required): Enter the number of the prescription.
* **Prescription Error Description** (prescription_error_description, text, required): Describe the error in the prescription.
* **Prescription Outcome Description** (prescription_outcome_description, text, required): Explain the outcome or result of the prescription.
* **Refund Amount** (refund_amount, number, required): Enter the amount to be refunded.
* **Comments** (minimalist_form_comments, note, required): Add any additional comments or details about the refund request.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number for further communication.
