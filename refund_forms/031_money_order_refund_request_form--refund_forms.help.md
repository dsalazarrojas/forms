# Money Order Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for a money order that has not been processed or was mistakenly charged.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
- Please note that some fields are not required, but are recommended to ensure the refund request is processed efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Money Order Number**: <page 1> The Money Order Number is the unique reference number assigned to your money order. If you do not know the money order number, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
* **Account Holder First Name**: <page 1> Please enter the first name of the account holder who is requesting the refund.
* **Account Holder Last Name**: <page 1> Please enter the last name of the account holder who is requesting the refund.
* **Account Holder Date of Birth**: <page 1> Enter the date of birth of the account holder, in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is not required for processing the refund, but it can help us to verify your identity.
* **Account Holder Email**: <page 1> Enter the email address of the account holder who is requesting the refund. This will help us to contact you regarding the status of your refund request.
* **Account Holder Phone**: <page 1> Enter the phone number of the account holder who is requesting the refund. This can be used to contact you in case we need further information.
* **Reason for Refund**: <page 1> Describe the reason for your refund request. Please be as detailed as possible, but do not include any sensitive or confidential information.
* **Date of Refund**: <page 1> Enter the date on which you would like to receive the refund. If you leave this blank, we will process the refund as soon as possible.
* **Amount to Refund**: <page 1> Enter the amount of the refund you are requesting.
* **Bank Name**: <page 1> Enter the name of your bank or financial institution where the refund will be received.
* **Branch Number**: <page 1> Enter the branch number of your bank or financial institution where the refund will be received.
* **Account Holder Signature**: <page 1> Please sign this page to confirm that you are the account holder and that the information provided is accurate. Your signature must be in ink and must be clear and legible.
* **Bank Signature**: <page 2> Please obtain the signature of the bank or financial institution representative who is processing the refund. This signature confirms that the refund is being processed correctly.
* **Note**: Please note that the signatures of both the account holder and the bank representative must be included. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our customer support team.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
- We will review your refund request and process it as soon as possible.
