## Purpose
The Factory Inspection Refund Form is intended to record and process refunds for factory inspection issues. It is a crucial tool for ensuring that customers receive the correct compensation for any problems related to the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Enter the Inspection Date, which is the date of the inspection that resulted in the refund request.
2.  Enter the Inspection Fee, which is the amount paid for the inspection that needs to be refunded.
3.  Enter the Refund Amount, which is the amount to be refunded.
4.  Select the Reason for Refund from the list provided, or enter a specific reason if it does not match any of the given options.
5.  If the reason for refund is "Other (please specify)", provide a brief explanation in the "Other Reason for Refund" field.
6.  Select the relevant Manufacturer of the inspection from the list provided.
7.  Select the relevant Contractor who performed the inspection from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Factory Inspection Refund Form** (`form_1`, text, Not required): This field is the title of the form.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, Required): Enter the date of the inspection that needs to be refunded.
* **Inspection Fee** (`inspection_fee`, number, Required): Enter the amount paid for the inspection that needs to be refunded.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, Required): Enter the amount to be refunded.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, select_one, Required): Select the reason for the refund from the list or enter a specific reason if it does not match any of the given options.
* **Other Reason for Refund** (`other_reason`, text, Not required): If the reason for refund is "Other (please specify)", provide a brief explanation in this field.
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`, select_multiple, Not required): Select the relevant Manufacturer of the inspection from the list provided.
* **Contractor** (`contractor`, select_one, Not required): Select the relevant Contractor who performed the inspection from the list provided.

## Tips
