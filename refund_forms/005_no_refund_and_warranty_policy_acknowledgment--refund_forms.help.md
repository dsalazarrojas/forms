# no_refund_and_warranty_policy_acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The no_refund_and_warranty_policy_acknowledgment form is a document that confirms a customer's understanding and acceptance of the company's refund and warranty policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the policy details provided.
2. Select the options that best describe your situation.
3. Check if you need to provide your contact details.
4. Sign and date the form if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **No Refund or Warranty** (`no_refund_no_warranty_policy`): Select the option that best applies to your situation. This field indicates whether you understand and accept the company's refund and warranty policies.
  - **I accept the terms of the refund policy**: This option is selected if you understand and accept the company's refund policy.
  - **I do not accept the terms of the refund policy**: This option is selected if you do not understand or do not accept the company's refund policy.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): If you need to contact us, please provide your contact details.
- **Contact Details Phone** (`contact_details_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Acceptance of No Refund and Warranty** (`acceptance_of_no_refund_and_warranty`): Select the option that best indicates your understanding and acceptance of the company's refund and warranty policies.
  - **I accept the terms of the refund policy**: This option is selected if you understand and accept the company's refund policy.
  - **I do not accept the terms of the refund policy**: This option is selected if you do not understand or do not accept the company's refund policy.
- **Sign** (`signature`): If your employer or company representative requires you to sign the form, please provide your signature.
- **Customer Sign** (`customer_signature`): If required, please provide your signature to confirm you have read and understand the policy.
