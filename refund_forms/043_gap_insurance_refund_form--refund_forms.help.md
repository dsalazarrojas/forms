# Gap Insurance Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gap Insurance Refund Form is used to request a refund on a gap insurance policy. This form helps policyholders provide necessary information for the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter your **Policy Number** in the "Policy Number" field.
3. Provide the **Reason for Refund** in the "Reason for Refund" field.
4. Select the **Date of Refund** using the date picker.
5. Enter a detailed **Description of Refund** in the "Description of Refund" field.
6. Enter the **Claim Amount** in the "Claim Amount" field.
7. Enter your **Claim Number** in the "Claim Number" field.
8. Upload any relevant **Documents** in the "Upload Documents" field.
9. Optionally, upload any additional **Other Documents** in the "Upload Other Documents" field.
10. Enter your **User Email** in the "User Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a policyholder.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required: false): Enter the number of your gap insurance policy.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, required: false): Describe the reason why you're requesting a refund.
* **Date of Refund** (`date_of_refund`, date, required: false): Select the date you are requesting the refund.
* **Description of Refund** (`description_of_refund`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the refund you are requesting.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of the claim you are making.
* **Claim Number** (`claim_number`, text, required: false): Enter the number of your claim.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required: false): Upload any supporting documents for your refund claim.
* **Upload Other Documents** (`upload_other_documents`, text, required: false): Upload any additional documents relevant to your refund claim.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact and updates.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Upload any relevant documents to support your refund claim.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email address provided.
