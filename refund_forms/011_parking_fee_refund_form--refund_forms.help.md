# parking_fee_refund_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers who wish to request a refund for parking fees. If you have paid for parking and are requesting a refund, please fill out this form to help us process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields below.
2. Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information.
3. Once you've completed the form, click "Submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **parking fee refund request** (`parking_fee_refund_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your parking fee refund request.
* **customer name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **customer email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates on your refund status.
* **parking fee date** (`parking_fee_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you paid the parking fee you're requesting a refund for.
* **parking fee time** (`parking_fee_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day you paid the parking fee you're requesting a refund for.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
- We'll be in touch with you regarding your refund request via the email address you provided.
