# Inflation Relief Refund Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are requesting inflation relief refunds. The form helps customers provide relevant details about their request, including their personal information, refund reasons, and supporting documents. It also allows customers to upload any necessary documents and track the review status of their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your title as a representation of your refund request.
2. Provide your customer details, if necessary.
3. Select the tax year for which you are requesting a refund.
4. Explain the reason for your refund request.
5. Upload any supporting documents related to your request.
6. Add any comments or notes about your request.
7. If necessary, provide comments from a program officer.
8. Select the review status of your request (pending, approved, or denied).
9. Enter the date of the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (Text, Optional)**: This field is a brief description of your refund request.
* **Customer Details (Text, Optional)**: If you are a business or organization, please provide your company or organization name, address, and contact information.
* **Tax Year (Select One, Optional)**: Select the tax year for which you are requesting a refund. Options: 2022, 2021, 2020.
* **Reason for Refund Request (Text, Optional)**: Provide a detailed explanation of why you are requesting a refund.
* **Documents (Text, Optional)**: List any relevant documents you have that support your refund request.
* **Upload Documents (Note, Optional)**: If necessary, you can upload documents related to your refund request.
* **Comments (Text, Optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Program Officer Comments (Text, Optional)**: If a program officer has made comments, please include them here.
* **Review Status (Select One, Optional)**: Select the current review status of your request. Options: pending, approved, denied.
* **Review Date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of the review.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any supporting documents, please upload them as a PDF or JPEG.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
