# Return Refund Method Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers select a refund method for their returned items. It gathers information necessary for a smooth return and refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you are selecting the correct options that apply to your situation.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information, making sure to select the correct refund method and reason for return.
3. Review your selections before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
1. **Return Request** (`return_request`): 
    Enter a brief description of the item you wish to return, including the item name, size, and any other relevant details.
2. **Preferred Refund Method** (`preferred_refund_method`): 
    Choose how you would like to receive your refund, whether it's in the form of store credit, exchange, or cash.
3. **Select a Refund Method** (`select_preferred_refund_method`): 
    Select all applicable methods from the list that you would like to receive your refund, in addition to your preferred method.
4. **Reason for Return** (`reason_for_request`): 
    Explain the reason for the return, such as "defective item" or "incorrect size".
5. **Refund Option** (`refund_option`): 
    Choose if you are eligible for a refund, taking into account the condition and status of the item.
6. **Select a Refund Option** (`select_refund_option`): 
    Select all applicable options from the list that you would like to receive your refund, in addition to the refund option you've chosen.
7. **Return Date** (`return_date`): 
    Enter the date you would like to return the item.
8. **Return Method** (`return_method`): 
    Choose how you would like to return the item, whether it's in-store, mail, or online.

## Tips
- Double-check your selections before submitting the form to avoid any potential issues.
- Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure a smooth return and refund process.
