# Celebrity Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a refund for a service or appearance that was not completed or met your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your customer or client name in the field labeled "Customer or Client Name".
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field to ensure that we can contact you regarding your refund request.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for further contact.
4. If applicable, enter your account or customer number in the "Account or Customer Number" field.
5. Select the date of the refund request in the "Refund Request Date" field.
6. Enter the date of the original transaction in the "Original Transaction Date" field.
7. Enter the original transaction amount in the "Original Transaction Amount" field.
8. Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund in the "Refund Amount Requested" field.
9. Select the reason for the refund request from the "Reason for Refund Request" dropdown menu (e.g., Service Not Provided, Service Quality Issues, Event Cancellation, etc.).
10. Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for the refund request in the "Detailed Explanation" text field.
11. Select the type of service or appearance that was booked from the "Type of Service or Appearance" dropdown menu (e.g., Personal Appearance, Meet and Greet, etc.).
12. Enter the name of the celebrity or talent involved in the service or appearance.
13. Select the date when the service was scheduled.
14. Confirm if the service was completed in the "Was Service Completed?" dropdown menu (e.g., Yes, No, Partially).
15. Select your preferred refund method (e.g., Original Payment Method, Bank Transfer, etc.).
16. If requesting a bank transfer, enter the bank account information.
17. If necessary, attach supporting documentation or evidence to support your refund claim.
18. Confirm that you authorize this refund request by selecting "Yes" from the "I authorize this refund request" dropdown menu.
