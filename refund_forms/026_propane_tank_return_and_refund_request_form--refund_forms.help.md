# propane_tank_return_and_refund_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a refund request on a propane tank return.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the "User Information" section.
2. Provide details about the propane tank in the "Propane Tank Info" section.
3. Enter your delivery team information in the "Delivery Info" section.
4. Enter the tank's serial number, size, and type in the "Tank Info" section.
5. Select the reason for return from the list.
6. Choose the correct date and time of return.
7. Enter your address, city, state, zip, and country in the "Contact Information" section.
8. Add any additional notes if needed.
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Enter your first and last name, email, and phone number to help us contact you regarding your refund request.
* **Propane Tank Info**: Provide details about the propane tank you are returning, including its serial number.
* **Delivery Info**: Enter the delivery team that delivered the tank.
* **Tank Serial Number**: Enter the tank's serial number.
* **Tank Size**: Choose the size of the tank from the options (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Tank Type**: Select the type of tank from the options (Above-Ground, Below-Ground).
* **Reason For Return**: Select the reason why you are returning the tank.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you returned the tank.
* **Return Time**: Enter the time you returned the tank.
* **Delivery Team**: Select the delivery team that delivered the tank.
* **Supplier Information**: Enter any information about the supplier of the tank.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the return.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Select the state from the options (California, New York, Florida, Texas).
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Signature**: Add your signature, if needed.
* **Submit**: Click submit to send the form.
* **Form ID**: Enter the form ID.
* **User ID**: Enter your user ID.
* **Timestamp**: Enter the timestamp of the form submission.
* **Signature**: Add your signature, if needed.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are necessary for the form to be submitted successfully.
