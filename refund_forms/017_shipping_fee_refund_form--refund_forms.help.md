# Shipping Fee Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to apply for a refund of shipping fees paid for their purchase. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all required fields (indicated as "Required" in the field explanation below)
2.  Be accurate and truthful in your answers.
3.  Make sure to attach any supporting documents as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, Optional): 
    Please provide your full name and any other relevant contact information.
*   **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, text, Optional): 
    Describe the reason for the refund.
*   **Date Shipped** (`date_shipped`, date, Optional): 
    Enter the date your item was shipped to you.
*   **Shipping Fee** (`shipping_fee`, number, Optional): 
    Enter the amount of shipping fee you are requesting a refund for.
*   **Amount Refunded** (`amount_refunded`, number, Optional): 
    Enter the amount of money that was refunded or expected to be refunded.
*   **Refund Method** (`refund_method`, select_one, Optional): 
    Choose how you would like to receive your refund (Cash, Check, Bank Transfer).
*   **Refund Status** (`refund_status`, select_multiple, Optional): 
    Choose the status of your refund (Pending, Approved, Denied).
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): 
    Enter any additional information you'd like us to know about your refund application.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): 
    Enter your valid email address. 

This form is for customer-initiated refund applications. If you have any questions, please contact us.
