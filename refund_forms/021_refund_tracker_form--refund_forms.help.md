# Refund Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to track refunds for customers. It allows you to collect and store customer information, refund details, and tracking information in one place.

## How To Complete This Form

To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Refund Tracker Form" as the form type.
2. Fill out each field with the relevant information for the refund request.
3. Make sure to enter the correct information for the customer, including their email address and phone number.
4. Enter the details of the refund request, including the amount requested and the current status.
5. Add any relevant notes or comments about the refund request.
6. Select the assigned tool or person responsible for the refund.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Refund Tracker Form** (`refund_tracker_form`): This field is the main title of the form. Please enter a brief description of the refund request.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`): Enter the customer's name, email address, and phone number. This is crucial for communication and follow-up.
* **Refund Request Details** (`refund_request_details`): Describe the reason for the refund request, including any relevant product or service details.
* **Tracking Information** (`tracking_info`): Enter any tracking numbers or other relevant tracking information for the refund shipment.
* **Refund Status** (`refund_status`): Select the current status of the refund request, such as "pending" or "approved".
* **Amount Requested** (`amount_requested`): Enter the amount the customer is requesting as a refund.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the customer's email address. This should match the email address you use for communication.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the customer's phone number. This is helpful for follow-up calls.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): Enter the customer's address. This is helpful for shipping and tracking purposes.
* **Refund Note** (`refund_note`): Add any additional comments or notes about the refund request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the tool or person responsible for processing the refund.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Double-check the customer's contact information.
* Keep the refund status up-to-date.
* Use the assigned tool or person for follow-up and communication.
