# Chargeback Withdrawal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Chargeback Withdrawal Request Form, which is used to request a refund or reversal of a previously submitted chargeback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as the account holder requesting the withdrawal.
2.  Enter the email associated with your account to facilitate communication and updates.
3.  Enter the reference number for the chargeback case you wish to withdraw.
4.  Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting a withdrawal.
5.  If a refund is required, select your preferred method of refund.
6.  Attach any supporting documents that support your request.
7.  Confirm your understanding of the consequences of withdrawal by selecting the corresponding option.
8.  Finally, enter the date of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requester name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the account holder requesting the withdrawal.
*   **Account email** (`account_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email associated with your account to facilitate communication and updates.
*   **Chargeback ID** (`chargeback_id`, `text`, required): Enter the reference number for the chargeback case you wish to withdraw.
*   **Original transaction ID** (`original_transaction_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the reference number for the original transaction related to the chargeback.
*   **Reason for withdrawal** (`reason_for_withdrawal`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting a withdrawal.
*   **Refund method preference** (`refund_method_preference`, `select_one`, optional): If a refund is required, select your preferred method of refund (Original payment method, Store credit, or Bank transfer).
*   **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant supporting documents that support your request.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the consequences of withdrawal by selecting the corresponding option.
*   **Submission date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your request.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
*   Attach supporting documents if available to strengthen your request.
*   Confirm your understanding of the withdrawal process before submitting the form.
