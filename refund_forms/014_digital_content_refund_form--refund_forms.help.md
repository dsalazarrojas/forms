# Digital Content Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to process refunds for digital content. It is essential to ensure that the refund process is accurate and thorough. When filling out this form, please make sure to provide all required information to help us process your refund efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information: Fill in the "Customer Name" field with the customer's full name.
2. Enter your contact details: Enter your email address in the "Email" field and your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Specify the digital product: Enter the "Product Name" of the digital product you are requesting a refund for.
4. Identify the product ID: Enter the "Product ID" of the digital product you are requesting a refund for.
5. Reason for Refund: Briefly explain the reason for your refund request in the "Reason" field.
6. Order Information: Enter the "Order ID" if applicable.
7. Refund Amount: Enter the exact amount you are requesting for a refund in the "Refund Amount" field.
8. Specify who submitted this form: Choose who submitted this form by selecting an option in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (text): The customer's full name. Please enter the name as it appears on their order.
- **Email** (email): The customer's email address. Please enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (text): The customer's phone number. Please enter a valid phone number.
- **Product Name** (text): The name of the digital product you are requesting a refund for.
- **Product ID** (text): The product ID associated with the digital product. Please refer to your order or product details for this information.
- **Reason** (text): Briefly explain the reason for your refund request. Please be as specific as possible.
- **Order ID** (text): If you are requesting a refund for an order, please enter the order ID. If not, please leave this field blank.
- **Refund Amount** (number): Enter the exact amount you are requesting for a refund. Please ensure this amount matches the original payment.
- **Submitted By** (select_one): Choose who submitted this form:

      - **Customer Support**: A member of your company's customer support team submitted this form.
      - **Customer**: The customer themselves submitted this form.
      - **Internal**: An internal team member submitted this form.
      - **External**: An external party submitted this form.
      - **Other**: Please select this option if you would like to specify another option that is not listed.
