# Cruise Ticket Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise Ticket Refund Form is used to submit a request for a refund on a cruise ticket. Please fill out this form with your details and reasons for the refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your request in the "Submit your request" field.
2. Provide a reason for the request in the "Reason for request" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you have any supporting documents, please attach them in the "Document 1" and "Document 2" fields.
6. Specify the amount you would like to be refunded in the "Refund amount" field.
7. Select the refund method(s) you would like to use in the "Select a refund method" and "Select multiple refund method" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit your request (cruise_tkt_req_refund)**: Enter a brief description of your refund request.
* **Reason for request (cruise_tkt_req_refund_reason)**: Explain the reason you are requesting a refund.
* **Email (cruise_tkt_email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your refund request.
* **Phone (cruise_tkt_phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your refund request.
* **Document 1 (cruise_tkt_doc1)**: Attach any supporting documents for your refund request.
* **Document 2 (cruise_tkt_doc2)**: Attach any additional supporting documents for your refund request.
* **Refund amount (cruise_tkt_refund_amt)**: Specify the amount you are requesting to be refunded.
* **Refund method (cruise_tkt_refund_method)**: Select the refund method you would like to use, e.g. "Yes" or "No".
* **Refund method options (cruise_tkt_refund_method_options)**: Select multiple refund methods, e.g. "Yes" or "No".
* **Confirmed (cruise_tkt_confirmed)**: This field is not relevant to the user, it's just a placeholder for the form submission status.

## Tips
Please make sure to submit a valid reason for your refund request. This will help us process your request efficiently. Also, ensure that you have attached all necessary supporting documents for your refund request.
