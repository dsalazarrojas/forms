# Security Deposit Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to initiate a refund of a security deposit for a tenant that has moved out of a rented property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary information as prompted.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
3. Upload a copy of the lease agreement if you have one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Id** (`tenant_id`, `number`, required): Enter the ID of the tenant who is moving out.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the tenant who is moving out. Please note that this field is not required but can be useful for your records.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, required): This field is not present on this form, it's a note to remember that you need to have the move-in date of the tenant.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the tenant actually moved out of the property.
* **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of security deposit that is being refunded.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of refund that the tenant is eligible for. This amount will be calculated automatically, but please double-check with the tenant before proceeding.
* **Landlord Id** (`landlord_id`, `number`, required): Enter your landlord ID number.
* **Lease Agreement Signed Date** (`lease_agreement_signed_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the lease agreement was signed. Please note that if you don't have a lease agreement, you might need to obtain one.
* **Lease Agreement Signed By** (`lease_agreement_signed_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who signed the lease agreement.
* **Lease Agreement Copy Attached** (`lease_agreement_copy_attached`, `text`, required): If you have a copy of the lease agreement, attach it to this form.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional notes about the refund, such as any outstanding issues or concerns.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, `text`, required): Enter the reason for the refund, e.g., the tenant moved out, etc.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who approved this refund.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the refund was approved.

Please fill in the form accurately and completely, and attach any required documents to initiate the security deposit refund process.
