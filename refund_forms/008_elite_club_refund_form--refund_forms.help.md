# elite_club_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Elite Club Refund Form" is a form designed for customers to request a refund for their elite club membership. It helps gather important information for processing the refund accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Request A Refund**: Enter a clear and concise explanation of the reason for the refund.
2. **Order ID**: Enter the unique identifier given to you at the time of purchase.
3. **Reason for Refund**: Enter the specific reason for the refund to facilitate the refund process.
4. **Refund Amount**: Enter the amount of the refund, matching the amount of the purchase or service rendered.
5. **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your account.
6. **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
7. **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
8. **Message**: Enter any additional message you want to include with the refund request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request A Refund**: 
  * (id: 1, text, required): A brief explanation of the reason for requesting a refund.
  * Enter a clear and concise reason for the refund.
- **Order ID**: 
  * (id: 2, text, required): Enter the unique identifier given to you at the time of purchase.
  * This helps our system identify the correct purchase to process the refund.
- **Reason for Refund**: 
  * (id: 3, text, required): Enter the specific reason for the refund.
  * Be as detailed as possible to facilitate the refund process.
- **Refund Amount**: 
  * (id: 4, number, required): Enter the amount of the refund, matching the amount of the purchase or service rendered.
  * Make sure the amount matches the original purchase or service cost.
- **Customer Name**: 
  * (id: 5, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your account.
  * Ensure it matches the name on your account for verification purposes.
- **Customer Email**: 
  * (id: 6, email, required): Enter your email address.
  * Ensure it matches the email address associated with your account.
- **Customer Phone**: 
  * (id: 7, text, required): Enter your phone number.
  * Ensure it matches the phone number associated with your account.
- **Message**: 
  * (id: 8, text, required): Enter any additional message you want to include with the refund request.
  * This can be used for any additional context or information about the refund.

## Tips
- Please be clear and concise in your responses to help us process your refund efficiently.
- Ensure that you have accurate information ready before submitting the form.
- Double-check your entries for any errors or discrepancies.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
