# Internal Training Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees on the impact of internal training programs. This survey aims to help us understand how training programs have affected your work and overall job satisfaction. Your input will be used to improve the effectiveness of our training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Provide the Training Program Title**: Enter the title of the training program you have completed.
2.  **Select Your Program Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you are with the training program using one of the options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, or Very Not Satisfied.
3.  **Rate the Program Usefulness**: Select all options that apply to how useful the training program has been to you:
    *   Very Useful
    *   Somewhat Useful
    *   Not Very Useful
    *   Not Useful At All
4.  **Skills Gained**: Choose all skills or knowledge areas that you have acquired from the training program:
    *   Very Much
    *   Somewhat
    *   Not Very Much
    *   Not At All
5.  **Satisfaction Score**: Enter a score between 1-5 that reflects your overall satisfaction with the training program.
6.  **Date Completed**: Enter the date you completed the training program.
7.  **Email Response**: Enter your email address so that we can reach you if needed.
8.  **Phone Number Response**: Enter a phone number where we can contact you if needed.
9.  **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Training Program Title (Page 1)**: Enter the title of the training program.
    *   This field allows you to specify which training program this survey is regarding.
*   **Training Program Satisfaction (Page 2)**: Select how satisfied you are with the training program.
    *   This helps us understand your level of satisfaction with the training program.
*   **Training Program Usefulness (Page 2)**: Select all options that apply to how useful the training program has been.
    *   This helps us understand the practical impact of the training program on your work.
*   **Skills Gained (Page 3)**: Select all skills or knowledge areas you have acquired from the training program.
    *   This helps us understand what skills you have gained from the training program.
*   **Satisfaction Score (Page 3)**: Enter a score that reflects your overall satisfaction with the training program.
    *   This helps us understand your overall satisfaction level.
*   **Date Completed (Page 4)**: Enter the date you completed the training program.
    *   This helps us understand when you completed the training program.
*   **Email Response (Page 4)**: Enter your email address so that we can reach you if needed.
    *   This helps us reach you with any follow-up questions or information.
*   **Phone Number Response (Page 5)**: Enter a phone number where we can contact you if needed.
    *   This helps us have a secure way to reach you with any further communication.
*   **Notes (Page 5)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the training program.
    *   This is an opportunity for you to share any feedback or suggestions about the training program.
