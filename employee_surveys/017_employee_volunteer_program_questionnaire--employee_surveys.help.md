# Employee Volunteer Program Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interests, preferences, and availability for volunteer work. It will help us tailor our volunteer programs to meet the needs of our employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. On the "Volunteer Interests" page, select all the options that apply to you.
3. On the "Volunteer Preferences" page, choose the volunteer opportunity that best suits your interests.
4. On the "How often do you volunteer" page, enter the number of times you are able to volunteer per month.
5. On the "How much time are you able to commit" page, enter the number of hours you are able to commit per volunteer opportunity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this survey about** (`intro_page`, text, required: false): This is a brief description of the survey. Please write a short paragraph explaining why you are completing this form.
* **Volunteer Interests** (`volunteer_interests_page`, select_multiple, required: false): This question allows you to select all the types of volunteer work that you are interested in. You can choose from:
	+ Community Events
	+ Disaster Response
	+ Fundraising
	+ Volunteer Management
* **Volunteer Preferences** (`volunteer_preferences_page`, select_one, required: false): This question allows you to choose a specific type of volunteer work that best suits your interests.
* **How often do you volunteer** (`volunteer_frequency_page`, number, required: false): This question asks about the frequency of your volunteer work. Please enter a number of times per month.
* **How much time are you able to commit** (`volunteer_commitment_page`, number, required: false): This question asks about the amount of time you can commit to a volunteer opportunity. Please enter a number of hours per opportunity.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when completing this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
- Completing this form is a one-time process. If you need to update your information, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
