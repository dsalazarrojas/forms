# Work Life Balance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Life Balance Assessment Form is a tool to help you assess your current work-life balance. It's a set of questions that will help you identify areas of improvement and provide insights for better managing your work and personal life.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Select an option for each question from the dropdown menus.
* For questions with text fields, type your response in the provided space.
* You may skip questions that are not applicable to your situation.
* Make sure to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your current work status?** (`work_status`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Active: I am currently employed and active in my role.
		- On-site: I am currently working on-site or in-person.
		- Off-site: I am currently working remotely or off-site.
* **How do you manage your work hours?** (`work_hours`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Work-life balance: I prioritize both work and personal life.
		- Work-only focus: I focus on work and put personal life on the backburner.
		- Other: Other (please specify).
		- Specify: Please describe your work hour management strategy.
* **What's your daily schedule like?** (`daily_schedule`, time, required: false)
	+ Please enter your typical daily schedule in 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **What are your main stressors?** (`stressors`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Work-related issues.
		- Home life issues.
		- Relationship issues.
		- Other.
* **How do you rate your current stress levels?** (`stress_levels`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose all that apply:
		- High.
		- Low.
* **What support networks do you have in your work or personal life?** (`support_networks`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose all that apply:
		- Family.
		- Friends.
		- Colleagues.
		- Other.
* **How do you manage your stress?** (`stress_management`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe your current stress management strategies.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to add or comment on?** (`email`, text, required: false)
	+ If you have any additional thoughts or comments, please share them here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false)
	+ If you have any additional comments, please share them here.
