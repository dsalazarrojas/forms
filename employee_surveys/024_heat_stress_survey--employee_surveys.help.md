# Heat Stress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heat Stress Survey is a form used to collect information about heat-related incidents in the workplace. The form is designed to gather details about the incident, including the location, date, time, and other relevant information. This data will be used to improve workplace safety and reduce the risk of heat-related incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct option from the "What happened" dropdown list.
2. Choose the location where the incident occurred.
3. Enter the date of the incident using the date picker or by typing it in.
4. Enter the time of the incident using the time picker or by typing it in.
5. Enter the temperature at which the incident occurred.
6. Enter the humidity level at which the incident occurred.
7. Select the wind speed at which the incident occurred.
8. Choose your level of sun exposure.
9. Provide any additional comments or notes about the incident (optional).
10. Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.
11. Enter your phone number so we can reach you in case of further questions.
12. Choose who you would like to be assigned to investigate and follow up on this incident.
13. Choose who submitted the report (if not yourself).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What happened** (Heat Stress, Heat Acclimatization, Heat Exhaustion, Heat Stroke, Heat Rash, Heat Cramps, Heat Syncope, Heat Fatigue, Dehydration, Other, None of the above): This field asks you to select the type of heat-related incident that occurred.
- **location** (Workplace, Break Area, Break Room, Outside, Other, None of the above): This field asks for the location where the incident occurred.
- **date** : Enter the date of the incident using the date picker or by typing it in.
- **time** : Enter the time of the incident using the time picker or by typing it in.
- **temperature** : Enter the temperature at which the incident occurred.
- **humidity** : Enter the humidity level at which the incident occurred.
- **wind speed** : Select the wind speed at which the incident occurred.
- **sun exposure** (High, Medium, Low, None): Select your level of sun exposure during the incident.
- **note** : Provide any additional comments or notes about the incident.
- **contact** : Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.
- **phone** : Enter your phone number so we can reach you in case of further questions.
- **assigned to** (chatjimmy, Other, None of the above): Choose who you would like to be assigned to investigate and follow up on this incident.
- **submitted by** (chatjimmy, Other, None of the above): Choose who submitted the report (if not yourself).
