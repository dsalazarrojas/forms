# Role Rotation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>**Purpose of this form:** This form is designed to collect feedback from employees regarding their experience and satisfaction with role rotation, a process where employees take on different roles within the organization. The purpose of this form is to gather insights on the effectiveness and challenges of role rotation, allowing for continuous improvement and better management of role rotation processes.

</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience and satisfaction with role rotation.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role Rotation Experience (1)** (`role_rotation_experience`, text, Required): Provide a brief description of your experience with role rotation.
* **Overall Satisfaction (2)** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, Not Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with role rotation, using a numeric scale.
* **Team Collaboration (3)** (`team_collaboration`, select_one, Required): Rate your experience with team collaboration during role rotation, selecting from the options: Team collaboration was excellent, Team collaboration was poor, Team collaboration was average.
* **Communication (4)** (`communication`, select_multiple, Not Required): Select the levels of clarity you experienced with communication regarding role rotation, from Clear, Unclear, Some, or None.
* **Feedback (5)** (`feedback`, note, Not Required): If you have any additional feedback or comments about your role rotation experience, please provide them here.
* **Satisfaction with Rotation (6)** (`satisfaction_with_rotation`, number, Required): Rate your satisfaction with rotation, using a numeric scale.
* **Satisfaction with Manager (7)** (`satisfaction_with_manager`, select_one, Required): Rate your satisfaction with your manager during role rotation, selecting from the options: Excellent, Average, Poor, None.
* **Manager Support (8)** (`manager_support`, select_one, Required): Rate your level of support from your manager during role rotation, selecting from the options: Excellent, Average, Poor, None.
* **Rotation Experience Satisfaction Scale (9)** (`rotation_experience_satisfaction_scale`, select_multiple, Required): Select the level of satisfaction with rotation, using the following scales: 5-6, 4, 2-3, Less than 2.
* **Manager Support Satisfaction Scale (10)** (`manager_support_satisfaction_scale`, select_multiple, Required): Select the level of satisfaction with manager support, using the following scales: 4-5, 3-4, 2-3, Less than 2.
* **Overall Satisfaction 5 Point Scale (11)** (`overall_satisfaction_5_point_scale`, select_multiple, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction, using the following scales: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1.
* **Manager Support 5 Point Scale (12)** (`manager_support_5_point_scale`, select_multiple, Required): Rate your satisfaction with manager support, using the following scales: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1.
* **Role Rotation Feedback Frequency (13)** (`role_rotation_feedback_frequency`, date, Required): Select the frequency of feedback provided during rotation.
* **Role Rotation Duration (14)** (`role_rotation_duration`, time, Required): Select the duration of role rotation.
* **Role Rotation Frequency (15)** (`role_rotation_frequency`, select_multiple, Required): Select the frequency of role rotation, from Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Rarely.
* **Role Rotation Experience (16)** (`role_rotation_experience`, text, Required): Provide a brief description of your experience with role rotation.
* **Rotation Length (17)** (`rotation_length`, select_multiple, Required): Select the length of rotation, from Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, More than 6 months.
* **Job Change Frequency (18)** (`job_change_frequency`, select_one, Required): Select the frequency of job changes, from More than once a year, 2-4 times a year, Rarely.
* **Rotation Satisfaction (19)** (`rotation_satisfaction`, select_multiple, Required): Rate your satisfaction with rotation, from Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied.
* **Rotation Satisfaction Scale (20)** (`rotation_satisfaction_scale`, select_multiple, Required): Rate your satisfaction with rotation, from 5-6, 4, 2-3, Less than 2.
* **Manager Satisfaction (21)** (`manager_satisfaction`, select_multiple, Required): Rate your satisfaction with your manager, from Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied.
* **Manager Satisfaction Scale (22)** (`manager_satisfaction_scale`, select_multiple, Required): Rate your satisfaction with your manager, from 5-6, 4, 2-3, Less than 2.
* **Team Satisfaction (23)** (`team_satisfaction`, select_multiple, Required): Rate your satisfaction with your team, from Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied.
* **Team Satisfaction Scale (24)** (`team_satisfaction_scale`, select_multiple, Required): Rate your satisfaction with your team, from 5-6, 4, 2-3, Less than 2.
* **Employee Satisfaction (25)** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_multiple, Required): Rate your satisfaction as an employee, from Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied.

Note: Make sure to complete all required fields (asterisked) to ensure accurate and comprehensive feedback.
