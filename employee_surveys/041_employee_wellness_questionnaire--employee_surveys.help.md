# Employee Wellness Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Employee Wellness Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your current level of well-being and satisfaction in the workplace. It will help us understand your needs and experiences, allowing us to provide support and resources that enhance your overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. You can choose one or multiple options for select fields, and provide detailed responses for text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey** (1, employee_survey, optional)
	+ This is a brief introduction to the questionnaire.
* **Wellness Programs** (2, wellness_programs, optional)
	+ Check which wellness programs you are participating in or interested in:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Number of Participants** (3, number_of_participants, optional)
	+ Enter the number of participants you work with or lead.
* **Participant Gender** (4, participant_gender, optional)
	+ Select your gender:
	+ Male
	+ Female
* **Participant Age** (5, participant_age, optional)
	+ Enter your age.
* **Participant Education** (6, participant_education, optional)
	+ Select your level of education:
	+ High School or Lower
	+ Some College
	+ Bachelors
	+ Masters
* **Other Education** (7, participant_education_other, optional)
	+ If you have other education levels, please specify.
* **Participant Occupation** (8, participant_occupation, optional)
	+ Select your current occupation:
	+ Manager
	+ Non-Manager
* **Other Occupation** (9, participant_occupation_other, optional)
	+ If you have an other occupation, please specify.
* **Participant Department** (10, participant_department, optional)
	+ Select your department:
	+ Marketing
	+ Sales
	+ Finance
* **Other Department** (11, participant_department_other, optional)
	+ If you have an other department, please specify.
* **Job Title** (12, participant_job_title, optional)
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Other Job Title** (13, participant_job_title_other, optional)
	+ If you have an other job title, please specify.
* **Job Level** (14, participant_job_level, optional)
	+ Select your job level:
	+ Entry-Level
	+ Mid-Level
	+ Senior-Level
* **Other Job Level** (15, participant_job_level_other, optional)
	+ If you have an other job level, please specify.
* **Job Satisfaction** (16, participant_job_satisfaction, optional)
	+ Select your level of satisfaction with your job:
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Other Job Satisfaction** (17, participant_job_satisfaction_other, optional)
	+ If you have other feelings about your job satisfaction, please specify.
* **Job Security** (18, participant_job_security, optional)
	+ Select how important your job security is:
	+ Very Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
* **Other Job Security** (19, participant_job_security_other, optional)
	+ If you have other feelings about your job security, please specify.
* **Leadership Support** (20, participant_leadership_support, optional)
	+ Check which leadership support you have:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Other Leadership Support** (21, participant_leadership_support_other, optional)
	+ If you have other leadership support, please specify.
* **Leadership Development** (22, participant_leadership_development, optional)
	+ Select your level of leadership development:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **Other Leadership Development** (23, participant_leadership_development_other, optional)
	+ If you have other feelings about leadership development, please specify.
* **Health Climate** (24, participant_health_climate, optional)
	+ Select your perception of the health climate:
	+ Very Positive
	+ Somewhat Positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Negative
* **Other Health Climate** (25, participant_health_climate_other, optional)
	+ If you have other feelings about the health climate, please specify.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative.
- Your participation is optional, but it will help us understand and support your well-being.
