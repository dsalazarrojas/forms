# long_service_award_recipient_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The long service award recipient survey is a form used to collect information about an employee's long service award. This survey aims to gather insights on employee engagement, retention, and recognition programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your knowledge or available records.
3. Enter accurate and complete information for all required fields.
4. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Award Issuer** (`award_issuer`, select_one, required): Choose whether the employee received the award from an internal or external source.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Award Year** (`award_year`, number, required): Enter the year the employee received the award.
* **Award Length** (`award_length`, select_multiple, required): Choose the length of the award.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **Recognition Date** (`recognition_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee received recognition for the award.
* **Milestone Celebration Type** (`milestone_celebration_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of milestone celebration the employee received.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Choose the level of employee satisfaction for the award.
	+ Options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied
* **Recognition Type** (`recognition_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of recognition the employee received.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Enter feedback or comments about the award.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required): Enter suggestions or ideas for future awards or recognition programs.
* **Milestone Celebration Frequency** (`milestone_celebration_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose the frequency of milestone celebrations the employee received.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Milestone Celebration Budget** (`milestone_celebration_budget`, number, required): Enter the budget for milestone celebrations.
* **Employee Satisfaction Level** (`employee_satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of employee satisfaction.
	+ Options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied
* **Award Frequency** (`award_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose the frequency of awards the employee received.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Milestone Celebration Budget Amount** (`milestone_celebration_budget_amount`, number, required): Enter the budget amount for milestone celebrations.
* **Milestone Celebration Frequency Employees** (`milestone_celebration_frequency_employees`, number, required): Enter the number of employees participating in milestone celebrations.
* **Milestone Celebration Budget Employees** (`milestone_celebration_budget_employees`, number, required): Enter the budget allocated for milestone celebrations per employee.
* **Award Issuer Satisfaction** (`award_issuer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Choose the level of satisfaction with the award issuer.
	+ Options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied
*   **Award Frequency Employees** (`award_frequency_employees`, number, required): Enter the number of employees receiving awards.
*   **Milestone Celebration Frequency Other** (`milestone_celebration_frequency_other`, text, required): Enter any additional information about milestone celebration frequency.
*   **Milestone Celebration Budget Other** (`milestone_celebration_budget_other`, text, required): Enter any additional information about milestone celebration budget.
*   **Award Issuer Satisfaction Other** (`award_issuer_satisfaction_other`, text, required): Enter any additional information about award issuer satisfaction.
*   **Milestone Celebration Frequency Employees Other** (`milestone_celebration_frequency_employees_other`, text, required): Enter any additional information about milestone celebration frequency employees.
*   **Milestone Celebration Budget Employees Other** (`milestone_celebration_budget_employees_other`, text, required): Enter any additional information about milestone celebration budget employees.
*   **Award Issuer Satisfaction Other** (`award_issuer_satisfaction_other`, text, required): Enter any additional information about award issuer satisfaction.
