## Purpose
The HR Service Quality Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees about their overall experience with the HR department. This survey aims to collect data on various aspects of HR services, such as satisfaction, areas for improvement, and suggestions for enhancements. The survey is used to help HR services teams identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about service quality and employee satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. For the `service_satisfaction` field, select a numerical value from 1-5 to indicate your satisfaction level.
3. Select one or multiple options in the `areas_of_improvement` field, depending on your experience with the HR department.
4. Choose one option from the `department_satisfaction` and `hr_services` fields, as they are both required fields.
5. Enter any open-ended comments in the `open_comments` and `open_ended_comments` fields.
6. Rate your employee satisfaction and rating for the overall survey in the `employee_satisfaction` and `employee_survey_rating` fields.
7. Select the completion time in the `employee_survey_completion_time` field.
8. Select the submission time in the `submission_time` field.
9. For the `action_items` field, select one or multiple options.
10. Enter any employee suggestions in the `employee_suggestions` field.

* **HR Service Feedback** (`employee_feedback`): Provide a brief description of your experience with the HR department.
