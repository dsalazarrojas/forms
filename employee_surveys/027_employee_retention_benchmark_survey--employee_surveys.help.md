# Employee Retention Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to help us better understand employee satisfaction, intentions to leave, and factors that influence your decision to stay or leave the company. Your responses will help shape company policies and decisions that promote employee satisfaction and retention.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You may select one or multiple options for select_one and select_multiple questions.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
