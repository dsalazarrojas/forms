# Employee Handbook Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on the clarity and effectiveness of the company's employee handbook.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Read each page carefully and answer the questions according to your experience with the employee handbook.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the questions as applicable.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text box at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback on Handbook (Page 1)
* **Feedback on Handbooks** (`feedback_on_handbooks`, `text`, required): Please provide your thoughts on the clarity and effectiveness of the employee handbook.

### Have You Read the Employee Handbook (Page 2)
* **Have you read the employee handbook** (`employee_handbooks_read`, `select_one`, required): Have you read the employee handbook? Answer "Yes" if you have read it, "No" otherwise.

### Have You Understood the Employee Handbook (Page 2)
* **Have you understood the employee handbook** (`employee_handbooks_understood`, `select_one`, required): Have you understood the employee handbook after reading it? Answer "Yes" if you have, "No" otherwise.

### Clearness of Communication (Page 3)
* **How clear is the communication in the employee handbook** (`communication_clearness`, `number`, required): Rate the clearness of the communication in the employee handbook on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Effectiveness of Communication (Page 3)
* **How effective is the communication in the employee handbook** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Have you found the communication in the employee handbook effective? Answer "Yes" if you have, "No" otherwise.

### Additional Comments (Page 4)
* **Do you have any feedback or suggestions** (`feedback_suggestions`, `text`, required): Please provide any feedback or suggestions you have for improving the employee handbook.

### Contact Information (Page 5)
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you for further feedback.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact number so that we can contact you for further feedback.
