This form is for outgoing athletic trainers to provide feedback on their experience and reason for leaving the organization. It's essential to complete this form honestly and accurately to help us improve our workplace culture and provide better support for future staff members. Please note that this form should only be completed once you have officially left the organization or are in the process of leaving.

## Purpose

The Athletic Trainer Exit Offboarding Form is designed to gather information about your experience and reasons for leaving the organization. This form will help us understand your perspective and provide valuable insights to improve our workplace culture and support for future staff members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your honest feedback and opinions.
3. Use the hints provided to guide you through each question.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athletic Trainer Full Name** (`trainer_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel file.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`): If you have an employee ID number, please enter it. This is an optional field and you may leave it blank if you don't have one.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`): Enter your job title as it appears on your ID badge or personnel file.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department you belong to (e.g., Athletics, Sports Medicine, Student Health, etc.).
* **Work Location** (`location`): Enter your primary work location.
* **Last Day of Employment** (`last_day`): Enter the date you last worked or the date you officially left the organization.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`): Select the primary reason why you are leaving the organization.
* **Reason Details** (`reason_details`): If you feel comfortable, please provide more details about your reason for leaving.
* **Length of Employment** (`employment_duration`): Select how long you worked for the organization.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience`): Rate your overall experience at the organization.
* **Management Support Rating** (`management_support`): Rate the support you received from management.
* **Resources and Equipment Availability** (`resources_availability`): Rate the availability of resources and equipment.
* **Work-Life Balance Rating** (`work_life_balance`): Rate your work-life balance while working at the organization.
* **Professional Development Opportunities** (`professional_development`): Rate opportunities for growth and development within the organization.
* **Compensation and Benefits Rating** (`compensation_benefits`): Rate your compensation and benefits package.
* **Workplace Culture Rating** (`workplace_culture`): Rate the overall culture of the organization.
* **Best Part of the Job** (`best_part_of_job`): What did you enjoy most about your job?
* **Most Challenging Aspect** (`most_challenging`): What was the most challenging part of your job?
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them.
* **Staffing Adequacy** (`staffing_adequacy`): Rate the adequacy of staffing for your role.
* **Athlete Support Quality** (`athlete_support`): Rate the quality of support provided to athletes.
* **Would You Recommend Working Here** (`would_recommend`): Would you recommend working here to other athletic trainers?
* **Would You Consider Returning** (`would_return`): Would you consider returning to work here in the future?
* **Equipment Return Status** (`equipment_returned`): Have you returned all equipment?
* **Access and Credentials Return Status** (`access_credentials_returned`): Have you returned all access and credentials?
* **Transition Plan Status** (`transition_plan`): Is there a transition plan for your replacement?
* **Outstanding Projects or Tasks** (`outstanding_projects`): If you have any outstanding projects or tasks, please list them.
* **May We Contact You for Follow-Up** (`contact_permission`): May we contact you for follow-up questions?
* **May We Contact You for Future Opportunities** (`future_contact`): May we contact you for future opportunities or networking purposes?
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Any other feedback or comments?
* **Exit Interview Requested** (`exit_interview_requested`): Would you like an exit interview?
* **Signature** (`signature`): Please type your full name as a signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Enter the date of your signature.

Note: Please be honest and thorough when completing this form. Your feedback is valuable to us, and it will help us improve our workplace culture and support for future staff members.
