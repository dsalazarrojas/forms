# Warehouse Supervisor Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Supervisor Performance Survey is designed to collect feedback on the performance of warehouse supervisors. It helps in evaluating their leadership, communication, and safety compliance skills, as well as overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit. You can choose from the available options for leadership, communication, and safety compliance questions. Rate your overall satisfaction and provide comments and suggestions if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leadership** (1): Please select one or more options that apply to your supervisor's leadership skills.
* **Communication** (2): Describe your supervisor's communication skills in this text field.
* **Safety Compliance** (3): Select the option that applies to your supervisor's safety compliance.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (4): Choose the option that describes your overall satisfaction with your supervisor.
* **Leadership Rating** (5): Rate your supervisor's leadership on a 5-point scale.
* **Communication Rating** (6): Rate your supervisor's communication on a 5-point scale.
* **Safety Compliance Rating** (7): Rate your supervisor's safety compliance on a 5-point scale.
* **Comments** (8): Share any comments or feedback you have about your supervisor.
* **Suggestions** (9): Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Email** (10): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (11): Enter your phone number.
* **Submission Date** (12): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (13): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Date and Time** (14): Enter the date and time you are submitting this form.
* **Assigned Tool** (15): Enter the tool assigned to you for this form.
* **Form ID** (16): This is a unique identifier for this form.
* **Category** (17): Enter the category of this form.
* **Description** (18): Enter a description of this form.
* **Form Title** (19): Enter the title of this form.
* **Form Category** (20): Enter the category of this form.
* **Form Description** (21): Enter a description of this form.
* **Form Issuer** (22): Enter the issuer of this form.
* **Form Version** (23): Enter the version of this form.
* **Form Created At** (24): Enter the date this form was created.
* **Form Updated At** (25): Enter the date this form was updated.

Note: All fields are optional except for Email and Phone which are required.
