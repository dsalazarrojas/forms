# Employee Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees about their nominations for outstanding performance, dedication, or other notable achievements. This form helps to recognize and reward employees who have made significant contributions to the team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing the employee's name and your own name and contact information in the corresponding fields.
*   Choose the type of nomination (Employee of the Month, Employee of the Quarter, or Employee of the Year) from the dropdown menu.
*   Enter a brief reason for the nomination.
*   Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nominate An Employee** (`employee_nomination_form`): Enter a brief description of the employee you are nominating. This is not a required field, but it helps to provide more context about the nominee.
*   **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`): Please enter a brief explanation of why this employee is being nominated. This could include details about their outstanding performance, dedication, or other notable achievements.
*   **Nominator Information** (`nominator_info`): This field is not required, but it would be helpful to include any additional information about yourself that would aid in the nomination process (e.g. your name, email address, or department).
*   **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`): Enter your name so that the HR department can contact you for more information.
*   **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`): Enter your email address so that the HR department can contact you for more information.
*   **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`): Enter your phone number so that the HR department can contact you for more information.
*   **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`): Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
*   **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`): Enter the title or position of the employee being nominated.
*   **Nomination Type** (`nomination_type`): Select the type of nomination from the dropdown menu to specify whether the nomination is for Employee of the Month, Employee of the Quarter, or Employee of the Year.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Be specific and detailed in your explanations when entering your reasons for the nomination.
*   Proofread your entries carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
