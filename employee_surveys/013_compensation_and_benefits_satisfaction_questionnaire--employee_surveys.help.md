# Compensation And Benefits Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your current satisfaction with compensation and benefits provided by the organization. Your feedback will help us understand your perceptions and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct answer for each question, based on your current experience and understanding.
2. For questions with a "text" type, type in your thoughts and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How long have you been with the organization?** (`current_role_tenure`, select_one, required)
	+ This question helps us understand your tenure and experience with the company.
	+ Select the answer that best describes your time at the organization.
* **How satisfied are you with your current base salary?** (`salary_satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required)
	+ This question assesses your satisfaction with your base salary.
	+ Choose how satisfied or dissatisfied you are, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **Overall satisfaction with the health and wellness benefits** (`benefits_package_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
	+ This question measures your satisfaction with your health and wellness benefits.
	+ Choose the answer that best represents your overall satisfaction.
* **Satisfaction with 401k or retirement matching contributions** (`retirement_plan_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks about your satisfaction with the retirement plan.
	+ Choose the answer that best represents your satisfaction, if applicable.
* **How satisfied are you with the Paid Time Off (PTO) policy?** (`pto_policy_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
	+ This question evaluates your satisfaction with the PTO policy.
	+ Choose how satisfied or dissatisfied you are with the ease of taking leave and the amount of time provided.
* **Do you feel your compensation is fair compared to similar roles in the industry?** (`fairness_perception`, select_one, required)
	+ This question inquires about your perception of fairness in compensation compared to similar roles.
	+ Choose the answer that best represents your understanding based on your market research or experience.
* **Is the bonus or incentive structure clearly communicated?** (`bonus_incentive_clarity`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks about the clarity of the bonus or incentive structure.
	+ Choose the answer that best represents your understanding of how your performance affects your compensation.
* **Which current benefit do you value the most?** (`most_valued_benefit`, select_one, required)
	+ This question identifies the most valued benefit among the provided options.
	+ Choose the one benefit that you value the most.
* **What is one new benefit you would like the organization to consider?** (`desired_new_benefit`, text, optional)
	+ This question invites you to suggest a new benefit that you would like the organization to consider.
	+ Type in your thoughts and comments.
* **Additional comments or concerns regarding compensation and benefits** (`additional_comments_compensation`, text, optional)
	+ This question provides an opportunity for you to express any additional thoughts, comments, or concerns about compensation and benefits.
	+ Type in your comments.
