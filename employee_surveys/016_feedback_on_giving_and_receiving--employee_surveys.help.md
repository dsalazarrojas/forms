# Feedback On Giving And Receiving - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on giving and receiving feedback in the workplace, helping to identify areas of strength and opportunity for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. You can refer to your personal experience with giving or receiving feedback.
3. You can use the options provided to select from or add additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Giving Feedback** (giving_feedback): 
  • Effective: Feedback that clearly communicates your message and helps the recipient understand their strengths and areas for improvement.
  • Ineffective: Feedback that doesn't convey its message effectively, leading to confusion or miscommunication.
  • Neutral: Feedback that doesn't have an impact on the recipient, often lacking a clear message or context.
* **Receiving Feedback** (receiving_feedback): 
  • Positive: Feedback that is well-received, understood, and actioned upon.
  • Negative: Feedback that is not well-received, creating tension or conflict.
  • Neutral: Feedback that is acknowledged but not taken into consideration.
* **Challenges Giving Feedback** (challenges_giving_feedback): 
  • Please describe any challenges you faced while giving feedback.
* **Challenges Receiving Feedback** (challenges_receiving_feedback): 
  • Please describe any challenges you faced while receiving feedback.
* **Suggestions For Improving Feedback** (suggestions_improving_feedback): 
  • Please provide any suggestions you have for improving the feedback process.
