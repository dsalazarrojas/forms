# job_importance_rating_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job importance rating survey is used to gather information from employees about their job importance ratings and comments. This data is collected for employee and manager evaluations to help identify areas of improvement and provide feedback for growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all required questions, marked as required in the form.
2. Select all applicable options for the "Employee Priorities" field.
3. Type in your comments in the "Employee Comments" and "Manager Comments" fields.
4. Ensure that all fields are accurately filled and free of any typos.
5. Submit the form once all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Importance Rating** (`<name>`, `number`, **required**): Please rate the importance of your current job on a scale. This rating is used to help identify areas of improvement and provide feedback for growth opportunities.
* **Employee Job Satisfaction** (`<name>`, `text`, **optional**): Describe your overall satisfaction with your current job. This field is used to gather information about employee morale and job fulfillment.
* **Employee Priorities** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all applicable priorities that are important for your current job. This field helps identify key areas of focus for employee and manager evaluations.
* **Employee Comments** (`<name>`, `note`, **optional**): Type in any additional comments or concerns you have about your current job. This field is used to gather more detailed information from employees.
* **Manager Comments** (`<name>`, `note`, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or concerns for your manager, please type them in this field. This helps managers understand individual perspectives and provide tailored feedback.
