# Workplace Cycling Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their participation, satisfaction, and ideas for improvement of the workplace cycling program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and understand what is being asked.
2. Select the best option that applies to your experience with the cycling program.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Welcome to the form!
This is an introduction page and a note field.

### 2. How do you participate in the cycling program?
This question asks about your level of participation in the cycling program.

* **How do you participate in the cycling program?** (`participation`, select_multiple, **Not required**): How often do you participate in the cycling program?
* Select one or more of the options that apply to you.

### 3. What do you like about the cycling program?
This question asks about your positive experiences with the cycling program.

* **What do you like about the cycling program?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, **Not required**): What do you like about the cycling program?
* Select the option that best describes your experience.

### 4. Do you have any improvement ideas for the cycling program?
This question invites you to share your suggestions for improvement.

* **Do you have any improvement ideas for the cycling program?** (`improvement_ideas`, text, **Not required**): Share any ideas you have for the cycling program.
* Type your ideas in the text field.

### 5. How often do you participate in the cycling program?
This question asks about your participation frequency.

* **How often do you participate in the cycling program?** (`participation_frequency`, select_one, **Not required**): How often do you participate in the cycling program?
* Select the option that best describes your participation frequency.

### 6. How satisfied are you with the cycling program?
This question asks about your level of satisfaction.

* **How satisfied are you with the cycling program?** (`participation_satisfaction`, select_one, **Not required**): How satisfied are you with the cycling program?
* Select the option that best describes your satisfaction level.

### 7. How do you think the program could be improved?
This question invites you to share your suggestions for improvement.

* **How do you think the program could be improved?** (`participation_improvement`, text, **Not required**): Share any ideas you have for the cycling program.
* Type your ideas in the text field.

### 8. Would you recommend the program to colleagues?
This question asks about your recommendation.

* **Would you recommend the program to colleagues?** (`participation_recommend`, select_one, **Not required**): Would you recommend the program to colleagues?
* Select the option that best describes your recommendation.

### 9. Can we contact you?
This question asks if you're willing to be contacted for further discussion.

* **Can we contact you?** (`contact_info`, select_one, **Not required**): Are you willing to be contacted for further discussion?
* Select the option that applies to you.

### Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the form creators.
