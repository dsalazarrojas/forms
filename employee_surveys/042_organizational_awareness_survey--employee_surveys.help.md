This guide is intended to provide users with a clear understanding of the purpose and functionality of the "Organizational Awareness Survey" form. The form is designed to gather information from employees about their current and expected levels of awareness of the organization's values and objectives, as well as their perceptions of communication channels used to stay informed and feedback from managers. This information will help the organization better understand employee perspectives and make informed decisions about communication and awareness initiatives.

# Organizational Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about employees' current and expected levels of awareness of the organization's values and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
*   Select the options that best match your current situation.
*   Complete each page before moving on to the next one.
*   Use a separate page for each question type (e.g., number, select multiple, select one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: no): This is a brief introduction to the survey and provides background information about the purpose and importance of completing this survey.
*   **What are the core values of our organization?** (`organizational_values`, `text`, required: no): In this question, please provide a list of the main values that you believe are essential to our organization's mission and vision.
*   **What are our organizational objectives?** (`organizational_objectives`, `text`, required: no): In this question, please provide a list of the main objectives that guide our organization's decision-making and actions.
*   **What is the current level of awareness of our organizational values among employees?** (`current_level_of_awareness`, `number`, required: no): Rate the current level of awareness of our organization's values on a scale of 1-10, where 1 means very low and 10 means very high.
*   **What is the expected level of awareness of our organizational values among employees?** (`expected_level_of_awareness`, `number`, required: no): Rate the expected level of awareness of our organization's values in the future on a scale of 1-10, where 1 means very low and 10 means very high.
*   **What communication channels do you currently use to stay informed about the organization's values and objectives?** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: no): Select all the communication channels that you use to stay informed about the organization's values and objectives, such as email, intranet, manager, social media, team meeting, or newsletters.
*   **What kind of feedback do you get from your manager regarding our organizational values?** (`feedback_on_awareness`, `select_one`, required: no): Choose one of the options, always, sometimes, rarely, or never, to describe the kind of feedback you get from your manager regarding our organizational values.
*   **How would you rate the level of awareness of our organizational values among employees?** (`organizational_awareness_level`, `number`, required: no): Rate the level of awareness of our organization's values among employees on a scale of 1-10, where 1 means very low and 10 means very high.
