# Orientation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orientation Quiz is a form designed to collect information from new employees about their understanding of the organization and their initial feedback on their onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully to ensure you provide accurate answers.
2. Select the appropriate response for each question.
3. Enter any comments or feedback in the "feedback" field.
4. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What Is An Orientation Quiz**: This is a text-based section where you can describe your understanding of what an Orientation Quiz is. Keep it concise and to the point.
* **Orientation Quiz Questions**: This section consists of multiple-choice questions designed to gauge your knowledge of the organization's policies, procedures, and culture.
	+ Select the correct option that best describes your answer to each question.
	+ If none of the options match your response, you can select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* **Quiz Score**: This field is for you to enter a score out of 10 for how well you think you understand the orientation process.
* **Feedback**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your onboarding experience.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Review your submission before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
* Ensure to complete the form in a way that accurately reflects your thoughts and experiences.
