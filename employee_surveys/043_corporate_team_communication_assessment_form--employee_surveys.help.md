# corporate_team_communication_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the effectiveness of communication within a corporate team. It helps to gauge the frequency and quality of communication among team members. The results will help the management to identify areas of improvement in team communication and make data-driven decisions to enhance collaboration and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and make sure you have understood the questions.
2. Answer each question honestly, and provide ratings on a scale of 1-5.
3. Select the most accurate options for each question.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about how often you communicate with your team. Please select all the frequencies that apply to you:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Communication Rating** (`frequency_rating`, number, required/optional): This question asks for your rating of the team's communication on a scale of 1-5. Rate how often you feel the team communicates with each other.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks which communication channels you use most often. Please select the channel you use most:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In Person
	+ Chat
	+ Video Conference
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required/optional): This question asks about the size of your team. Please enter the number of people in your team.
* **Team Size Rating** (`team_size_rating`, number, required/optional): This question asks for your rating of the team size on a scale of 1-5. Rate how effective you think the team size is for communication.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, number, required/optional): This question asks for your rating of the team's communication effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Introduction** (`page_1`, note, not required): This is a space for you to write any additional comments or thoughts about your team's communication.
* **Page 2** (`page_2`, note, not required): This is a space for any additional comments or thoughts about your team's communication.
* **Page 3** (`page_3`, note, not required): This is a space for any additional comments or thoughts about your team's communication.
