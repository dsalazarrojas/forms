# grievance_procedure_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The grievance procedure assessment form is used to evaluate the effectiveness and adherence to the employee grievance procedure in place within the organization. It is designed to gather information from employees about their experiences with the grievance procedure, including whether it is being followed correctly, and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, choose the most accurate response from the provided options.
3. If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use the "grievance_notes" field to provide additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grievance Procedure** (`grievance_procedure`, text, required: false): This field is where you can describe the grievance procedure that was in place during the incident.
* **Grievance Category** (`grievance_category`, select_one, required: false): Please choose whether the grievance was related to a specific category (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Grievance Description** (`grievance_description`, text, required: false): This is the description of the grievance that occurred.
* **Grievance Resolution Process** (`grievance_resolution_process`, select_multiple, required: false): Please choose whether the resolution process was effective or not.
* **Grievance Action Taken** (`grievance_action_taken`, select_one, required: false): Please choose whether an action was taken to address the grievance.
* **Grievance Outcome** (`grievance_outcome`, select_one, required: false): Please choose the outcome of the grievance procedure.
* **Grievance Next Steps** (`grievance_next_steps`, text, required: false): If there are any next steps or actions that you would like to suggest, please describe them here.
* **Grievance Notes** (`grievance_notes`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use this field to provide them.
