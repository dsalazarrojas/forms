# Remote Work Conditions Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and experiences about your remote work conditions. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and make data-driven decisions to improve your work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each field with the best possible information.
2. Be as honest and detailed as you can be in your answers.
3. Do not worry about the length or complexity of your answers; we want to hear from you.
4. If you have any questions or need help, use the "Feedback" field at the end of this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Remote Work Conditions** (Remote Work Conditions, optional): This field is a general note field where you can provide any thoughts or comments about your remote work conditions. If you have specific experiences or challenges that you'd like to share, this is the right place for them.
- **Collaboration** (Collaboration, required if selected): Choose one option for how you currently feel about your ability to collaborate with colleagues while working remotely.
  • Option 1: I am happy with the level of collaboration I have with my colleagues.
  • Isolation: I feel isolated and disconnected from my colleagues.
- **Remote Setup** (Remote Setup, optional): If you're currently working remotely, please select the number of days you work remotely per week.
- **Productivity** (Productivity, optional): Describe your level of productivity when working remotely. This will help us understand how we can better support you in this area.
- **Well-being** (Well-being, optional): Select all the options that apply to describe your well-being while working remotely.
  • Good: I feel good about my well-being while working remotely.
  • Fair: I have mixed feelings about my well-being while working remotely.
  • Bad: I have poor well-being while working remotely.
- **Support** (Support, optional): Choose the option that best describes the level of support you receive while working remotely.
  • HR Support: I have received support from HR.
  • Line Manager Support: I have received support from my line manager.
  • Team Lead Support: I have received support from my team lead or colleagues.
  • No Support: I receive little to no support while working remotely.
- **Feedback** (Feedback, optional): If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions about this form, please share them here. This is a great opportunity to tell us about any issues or challenges you're facing.

## Tips
* Please take your time and think carefully about each question.
* If you're not sure about something, just answer the best you can.
* We value your honesty and appreciate your input, and your feedback will help us make improvements to our remote work conditions.
