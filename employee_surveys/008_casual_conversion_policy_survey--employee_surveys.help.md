# Casual Conversion Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casual Conversion Policy Survey is designed to gather information and feedback from employees on the casual conversion policy. Your responses will help us refine the conversion process and make it fair and efficient for all employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct response for each question based on your experience and understanding.
2. Answer questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you select the "Submit anonymously" option, your responses will remain anonymous.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role** (`respondent_role`, text, optional): Please enter your job title or position.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your current employment type:
	+ Casual
	+ Fixed term
	+ Permanent
* **Awareness of conversion policy** (`awareness_of_policy`, select_one, required): Please indicate your level of awareness:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
	+ Unsure
* **Clarity of eligibility criteria** (`clarity_of_criteria`, select_one, required): How clear were the conversion criteria to you?
	+ Very clear

	+ Not clear

	+ Very positive

	+ Negative
