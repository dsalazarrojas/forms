# Digital Tools For Work Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience with digital tools for work management. It will help the organization understand what digital tools are being used, how they are being used, and what areas for improvement are needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Title** (`form_title`): Enter a title for the survey.
	* **Category** (`category`): Select the category for the survey (e.g., Employee Surveys, Team Surveys, etc.).
3. Answer the questions in the remaining fields, providing as much detail as possible.
4. Click on the "Submit" button again to send the survey.
