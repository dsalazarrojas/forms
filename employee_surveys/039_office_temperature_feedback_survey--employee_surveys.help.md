# Office Temperature Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Temperature Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from employees about the comfort and working conditions of the office. This survey aims to gather information about the temperature, air quality, humidity, and other factors that affect employees' work comfort. The feedback collected will help management make informed decisions to create a more comfortable and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer as accurately as possible.
2. Fill out the required fields (if any).
3. Choose your answers from the provided options, if applicable.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit Feedback" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Feedback** (`general_feedback`, text, required: false): This field is for any general comments or observations you may have about the office environment.
* **Temperature Comfort** (`comfort_temperature`, number, required: false): Please rate your comfort level with the office temperature, with 1 being "very uncomfortable" and 5 being "very comfortable".
* **Air Quality** (`air_quality`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with the air quality in the office.
* **Humidity** (`humidity`, number, required: false): Rate your comfort level with the office humidity, with 1 being "very dry" and 5 being "very humid".
* **Light Level** (`light_level`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with the light level in the office.
* **Noise Level** (`noise_level`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with the noise level in the office.
* **Workspace** (`work_space`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you find your workspace comfortable, "No" if you don't.
* **Submit Feedback** (`submit_feedback`, text, required: false): Enter your feedback or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for feedback.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Team Member** (`team_member`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you have team members who share the same experience as you.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your office.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" to submit your feedback.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false): This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required: false): This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, note, required: false): This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
* **Office ID** (`office_id`, number, required: false): This field is automatically generated when you submit the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required: false): This field is automatically generated when you submit the form.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required: false): This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, required: false): This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, time, required: false): This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, time, required: false): This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, text, required: false): This field is automatically filled in when you submit the form.

Note: The fields marked as required: false are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
