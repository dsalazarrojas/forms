# Initial Team Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teams assess their performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Use the provided text fields to provide detailed responses to each question.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by the "required" label).
5. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, text, optional): Describe your team's leadership style and how it contributes to the team's overall performance.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional): How do you rate your team's communication style? (e.g., effective, poor, neutral)
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, text, optional): Describe your team's approach to problem-solving and how it has contributed to your team's success.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): What kind of feedback do you receive from team members, and how do you use it to improve?
* **Team Dynamics** (`team_dynamics`, text, optional): Describe the overall dynamic of your team, including how members interact with each other.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, text, optional): How does your team collaborate on tasks and projects?
* **Decision Making** (`decision_making`, text, optional): Describe your team's approach to decision-making and how it has contributed to your team's success.
* **Team Workstyle** (`team_workstyle`, text, optional): Describe your team's work style, including work hours, work pace, and workload.
* **Team Morale** (`team_morale`, text, optional): How does your team's morale affect its overall performance?
* **Leadership Style** (`leadership_style`, text, optional): Describe your team's leadership style and how it contributes to the team's overall performance.

Note: All fields are optional, but please answer each question thoroughly for the best results.
