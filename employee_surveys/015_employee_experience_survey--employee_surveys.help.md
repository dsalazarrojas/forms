# Employee Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees provide feedback and insights about their experience within our organization. The purpose of this form is to collect data that will help us better understand your needs, preferences, and suggestions for improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the type of feedback you are providing.
2. Enter a brief description of your feedback, if desired.
3. Choose the specific category for your feedback from the select options.
4. Answer the four questions listed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: (Select One, Required: false)
	+ This field allows you to select the type of feedback you are providing. Please choose the category that best describes your feedback.
* **Description**: (Text, Required: false)
	+ This field is optional and allows you to provide additional context or details about your feedback.
* **Category_id**: (Select One, Required: false)
	+ This field is similar to the "Category" field, but allows you to select a specific category for your feedback. Please choose the category that best describes your feedback.
* **Description_id**: (Text, Required: false)
	+ This field is similar to the "Description" field, but allows you to provide additional context or details about your feedback.
* **question1**: (Text, Required: false)
	+ This question asks about your overall satisfaction with our organization. Please provide your honest feedback.
* **question2**: (Text, Required: false)
	+ This question asks about your suggestions for improvements or changes that would benefit our organization.
* **question3**: (Text, Required: false)
	+ This question asks about any challenges or issues you have faced while working with our organization.
* **question4**: (Text, Required: false)
	+ This question asks about any positive experiences or accomplishments you have had while working with our organization.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field.
* If a field is not required, you can still provide information if you feel it is relevant to your feedback.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please reach out to us directly.
