# Cross Team Collaboration Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on the cross-team collaboration within their team, communication channels used, and the frequency of collaboration with other teams. This information will help us understand the team's performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your team name in the field titled "Team Name".
2. Rate the collaboration within your team by selecting one of the options provided under "How do you rate the level of collaboration within your team?".
3. Indicate how often you collaborate with other teams by selecting one or more options under "How often do you collaborate with other teams?".
4. Choose your primary communication channel with your team under "What is your primary communication channel with your team?".
5. If you have any additional feedback, you can provide it in the "Do you have any additional feedback to provide?" text box.
6. If you have been assigned to use a specific tool, please enter it in the "Assigned Tool" field.
7. Select the type of submission you are providing under "Submission Type" (this field is not relevant to end users but is included for administrative purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter your team name as it appears in the project or department.
* **How do you rate the level of collaboration within your team?**: Select one of the options provided:
	+ Team collaboration is excellent
	+ Team collaboration is good
	+ Team collaboration is average
	+ Team collaboration is bad
	+ Team collaboration is very bad
* **How often do you collaborate with other teams?**: Select one or more options that apply to you:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **What is your primary communication channel with your team?**: Select one of the options provided:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-person
	+ Chat
	+ Video call
* **Do you have any additional feedback to provide?**: If you have any comments or suggestions, you can provide them here.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you have been assigned to use a specific tool, enter it here.
* **Submission Type**: This field is not relevant to end users, but is intended for administrative purposes. 

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can ask your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
- Your feedback is valuable to us and will help improve team performance.
