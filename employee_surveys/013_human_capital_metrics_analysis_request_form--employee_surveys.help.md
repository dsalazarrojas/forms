# Human Capital Metrics Analysis Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant metrics and feedback from employees and managers within the organization to help analyze and improve human capital metrics. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. For the employee fields, please ensure that you are filling in your own details accurately.
3. For the manager fields, please ensure that you are filling in the details for the manager you are reporting on.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **employee_name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID card or personnel file.
- **employee_department** (`employee_department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your department as it appears on your ID card or personnel file.
- **employee_job_title** (`employee_job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title as it appears on your ID card or personnel file.
- **metrics_1** (`metrics_1`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe your skills and experience in your job.
- **metrics_2** (`metrics_2`, `number`, required/optional): If applicable, enter a numerical value representing your level of expertise in your job (e.g., 0-10).
- **metrics_3** (`metrics_3`, `number`, required/optional): If applicable, enter a numerical value representing your performance rating in your job (e.g., 0-10).
- **metrics_4** (`metrics_4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the options to indicate if you have received any training or certification relevant to your job.
  - 'Yes'
  - 'No'
- **metrics_5** (`metrics_5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all the options that apply to indicate your areas of expertise.
  - 'Yes'
  - 'No'
- **metrics_6** (`metrics_6`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when you last updated your skills or training relevant to your job.
- **metrics_7** (`metrics_7`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you spend on your job (e.g., part-time or full-time).
- **metrics_8** (`metrics_8`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your skills and performance.
- **email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your work email address.
- **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.
- **manager_comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback from your manager.
- **employee_comments** (`employee_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback from you about your skills and performance.
- **manager_name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your manager.
- **manager_department** (`manager_department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department of your manager.
- **manager_job_title** (`manager_job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title of your manager.
- **manager_email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of your manager.
- **manager_phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your manager.
- **manager_comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback from your manager.
- **approval** (`approval`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the options to indicate if you approve of your skills and performance.
  - 'Yes'
  - 'No'
- **approval_reasons** (`approval_reasons`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reasons for your approval.
- **manager_approval** (`manager_approval`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all the options that apply to indicate if you approve of your skills and performance.
  - 'Yes'
  - 'No'

## Tips
1. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
2. If you're unsure about any field, please ask your manager for guidance.
3. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the support team.
