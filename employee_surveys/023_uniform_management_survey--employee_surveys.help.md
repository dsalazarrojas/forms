# Uniform Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the uniform management system used in the organization. The survey aims to gather information on various aspects of the uniform, including employee satisfaction, wearability, size selection, material quality, price range, and other relevant factors. The collected data will be used to improve the uniform management system and make informed decisions for future uniform procurement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select an option from the dropdown menu for each field. If a field is not relevant to your uniform, select "No" or leave it blank.
2.  Enter the necessary information in the text fields, such as your manager's name, title, phone number, and email.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
