# Corporate Mission Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Mission Understanding Survey is designed to gauge the understanding of employees about the company's mission and their role within it. This survey aims to collect feedback on the level of understanding, internal communication, and alignment of employees with the company's mission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand each question before starting.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Select the corresponding answer options for each question.
4. Submit your responses to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Survey 1 (employee_survey_1)** (`select_one`, **Optional**): 
    This question asks whether you understand the company's mission. Please select 'Yes' if you have a clear understanding of the company's mission, or 'No' if you don't have a good understanding.
- **Mission Understanding (mission_understanding)** (`text`, **Optional**): 
    Please provide a brief description of your understanding of the company's mission.
- **Company Mission (company_mission)** (`text`, **Required**): 
    Describe the company's mission in your own words.
- **Internal Communication (internal_communication)** (`select_multiple`, **Required**): 
    Please select all the ways you receive internal communication (e.g., email, phone, meetings).
- **Feedback (feedback)** (`text`, **Optional**): 
    If you have any feedback or suggestions about the company's mission, please provide them here.
- **Opportunities (opportunities)** (`text`, **Required**): 
    Describe how you contribute to and are aligned with the company's mission.
- **Alignment (alignment)** (`number`, **Required**):
    This question asks for your level of alignment with the company's mission on a scale of 1-5.
