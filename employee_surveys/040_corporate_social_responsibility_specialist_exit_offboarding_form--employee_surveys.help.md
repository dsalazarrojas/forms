# corporate_social_responsibility_specialist_exit_offboarding_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for corporate social responsibility specialists to gather feedback about their offboarding experience. The goal is to improve the process and ensure a smooth transition for future corporate social responsibility specialists.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Read through each question carefully and select the correct response for each one.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to include them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief welcome message explaining the purpose of the form.
- **Questions** (`Questions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all the questions that you were able to ask during the offboarding process. This helps us understand what worked well and what could be improved.
  - "I was able to ask questions during the offboarding process" 
  - "My manager was able to answer questions during the offboarding process" 
  - "The offboarding process was smooth" 
  - "I was able to get feedback on my offboarding process"
- **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the offboarding process, please share them here.
- **How would you rate the offboarding process?** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Please rate the offboarding process on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
- **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the offboarding process, please share them here.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you for follow-up questions or discussions.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Please add any additional comments you may have about the offboarding process.
- **Questions Asked** (`Questions Asked`, `select_one`, optional): Were you able to ask questions during the offboarding process? Please select "Yes" or "No".
  - "Yes"
  - "No"
  - "Don't know"
- **Questions Answered** (`Questions Answered`, `select_one`, optional): Were questions answered during the offboarding process? Please select "Yes" or "No".
  - "Yes"
  - "No"
  - "Don't know"
