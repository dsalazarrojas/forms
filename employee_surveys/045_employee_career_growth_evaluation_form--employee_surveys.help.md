# Employee Career Growth Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Career Growth Evaluation Form is a self-assessment tool designed to help employees evaluate their career growth and satisfaction within their current role. This form provides a framework for employees to reflect on their career development and identify areas of improvement and growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form title section with a brief title that summarizes your current evaluation.
2. Read through each field carefully and answer the questions honestly.
3. Select the options that best fit your current situation for each question.
4. Provide as much detail as possible in the text boxes (e.g., current job title, job growth opportunities).
5. Once you have completed the form, review your answers carefully and consider how you can use this information to inform your career development plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title of the Evaluation** (`form_title`, text, optional): A brief title that summarizes your current evaluation. This can be as simple as "Career Growth Evaluation" or something more specific, such as "Career Growth Evaluation for Marketing Manager Position".
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, optional): A brief introduction to your evaluation. You can provide any additional context or information that may be helpful for understanding your answers.
* **Current Job Title** (`current_job_title`, text, optional): Your current job title.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): The number of years you have been in your current role.
* **What are your top career growth goals?** (`career_growth_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Professional Development and Growth
	+ Career Advancement and Growth
	+ Leadership Development and Growth
* **How satisfied are you with your current job?** (`current_job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your current job? Select one of the following:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **How satisfied are you with your career overall?** (`career_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your career overall? Select one of the following:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **How well does your current job description match your career aspirations?** (`job_description_match`, select_one, optional): How well does your current job description match your career aspirations? Select one of the following:
	+ Very Well
	+ Somewhat Well
	+ Not Well
* **How well does your current job fit with your skills and experience?** (`job_fit`, select_one, optional): How well does your current job fit with your skills and experience? Select one of the following:
	+ Very Well
	+ Somewhat Well
	+ Not Well
* **What growth opportunities do you have within your current role?** (`job_growth_opportunities`, text, optional): Describe any growth opportunities you have within your current role.
* **Which areas of career development would you like to see more opportunities for?** (`career_development_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Leadership Development
	+ Professional Development
	+ Technical Development
* **Is there anything else you'd like to add or comment on?** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments you'd like to share.

Note: This form is meant to be a helpful guide for employees to reflect on their career development and make informed decisions about their future career aspirations.
