# exit_interview_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exit interview form is used to gather information from employees who are leaving the company. This helps the HR department to understand the reasons for their departure and to identify areas for improvement to reduce turnover in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the HR department for assistance.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`note`, not required): This is a brief description of the form and its purpose. Please read through it carefully before proceeding.
### Exit Reason
* **Exit Reason** (select_multiple, `Yes`/`No`): Please select the reasons why you are leaving the company. You can choose more than one reason.
### Expected Date of Departure
* **Expected Date of Departure** (date, `YYYY-MM-DD`): Enter the date you expect to leave the company. The format should be in the `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
### Reason for Departure
* **Reason for Departure** (`text`, not required): Please provide a detailed reason for your departure.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`text`, not required): If you have any additional comments or information you would like to share, please enter them here.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`text`, not required): If you would like to provide your contact information for follow-up, please enter it here.
### Preferred Contact Method
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`select_one`, `Email/Phone/In-person/Other`): Please select how you would prefer to be contacted for follow-up.
### Available Timeframes
* **Available Timeframes** (`time`, not required): If you have a specific time frame when you are available to be contacted, please enter it here.
### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`text`, not required): If you have an assigned tool or equipment that needs to be returned, please enter it here.

Note: This form requires a specific format for the `Expected Date of Departure` field.
