This form is designed to help managers and HR personnel assess the performance of employees in a concise and structured manner. It is typically used during employee evaluations and reviews. The form asks the assessor to provide their evaluation on various aspects of the employee's performance, including skills, areas for improvement, and comments. The assessment is usually completed by a manager or HR representative, but can be filled out by the employee themselves as well, providing a self-assessment of their performance.

Before starting, ensure you have a clear understanding of the performance areas to be assessed and the specific goals and objectives of the employee. This form is not a one-size-fits-all solution, and answers may vary depending on individual circumstances.

# Employee Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Performance Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate an employee's performance in a structured and comprehensive manner. It helps identify areas of strength and weakness, providing valuable insights for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose the performance areas to improve from the list provided.
2.  Select the relevant skills that the employee has demonstrated.
3.  Provide any areas of improvement in the text fields.
4.  Add any comments or feedback from the employee or manager.
5.  Enter the date and time of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Performance Assessment Form** (Text, Optional): This is the header of the form, and you are not required to fill out this field.
* **Performance areas to improve**: 
  + Select all areas that you feel the employee needs to improve (Communication, Teamwork, Problem solving, Leadership, Adaptability, Time management).
  + Choose from the list of options, or add new areas not listed.
* **Employee skills**: 
  + Select only one skill that the employee has demonstrated (Leadership, Communication, Time management, Teamwork, Adaptability, Problem solving).
  + This skill should be relevant to their job function and responsibilities.
* **Areas of improvement**: 
  + Describe the specific areas that the employee needs to improve in.
  + This can include feedback or suggestions from the manager or HR representative.
* **Employee Comments**: 
  + The employee is asked to provide any comments or feedback they may have.
  + This can be a great opportunity to express their own thoughts and perspectives on their performance.
* **Manager Comments**: 
  + The manager or HR representative is asked to provide comments or feedback.
  + This can help to provide a more objective evaluation and support growth.
* **Date**: 
  + Enter the date of the assessment.
  + This helps to track the timing and frequency of evaluations.
* **Time**: 
  + Enter the time of the assessment.
  + This helps to track the timing and duration of the evaluation.
