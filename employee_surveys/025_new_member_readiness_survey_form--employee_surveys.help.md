# New Member Readiness Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your readiness to start your new role as a member of our team. This will help us better understand your current skills and experience, and provide a more effective onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Answer the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* Provide detailed and specific information in the text fields.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, Optional):
This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, Optional):
This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Team Name** (team_name, text, Optional):
This field is for the name of the team you will be working with. If you are not sure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Department** (department, text, Optional):
This field is for the department you will be working in. If you are not sure, you can leave this field blank.

Note: All fields are optional, but it is recommended to fill in as much information as possible to help us better understand your situation.
