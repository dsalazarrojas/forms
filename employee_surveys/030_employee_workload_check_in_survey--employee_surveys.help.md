# Employee Workload Check In Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help employees provide accurate and timely information about their workload and stress levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all workloads that apply to you for this week.
2. Indicate your current stress level.
3. Estimate your work capacity.
4. (Optional) Enter the total number of hours you work on tasks selected for the week.
5. (Optional) List any team leads you report to directly.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief introduction or any specific information related to your current week.
*  **Workloads** (`workloads`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all workloads that apply to you this week. You can choose multiple options.
* **Stress Levels** (`stress_levels`, `select_one`, **required**): Indicate your current stress level. Please note that "stressed" and "high" indicate significant work-related stress or pressure.
* **Capacity** (`capacity`, `number`, **optional**): Estimate your total work capacity in hours for the week.
* **Workload Hours Per Week** (`workload_hours_per_week`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the total number of hours you work on tasks selected for the week.
* **Team Leads** (`team_leads`, `email`, **optional**): List any team leads you report to directly. Please separate email addresses with commas.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any additional information or feedback you'd like to share.
