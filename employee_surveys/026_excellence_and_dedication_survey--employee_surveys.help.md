# Excellence And Dedication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their experience working with our organization. It aims to identify areas of improvement, areas of strength, and suggestions for growth. Your honest opinions and suggestions are essential in helping us enhance our work environment and employee experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate and useful feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the most relevant option from the select_one and select_multiple fields.
2. Select all applicable options for the select_multiple fields.
3. Be as descriptive as possible in the open-ended fields.
4. Do not repeat similar responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Surveys (1)**: Enter a brief description of your current experience as an employee.
* **What matters most (2)**: Select one option that best describes what matters most to you in our organization.
	+ Employee engagement: How much time and effort you put into your work and how motivated you are.
	+ Company culture: How our culture and values align with your personal values.
	+ Job fit: How well your job aligns with your skills and experience.
	+ Benefits: What benefits we offer that support you.
	+ Professional development: How we support your growth and development.
	+ Work-life balance: How we balance your work and personal life.
	+ Management: How we manage and lead our teams.
* **How do you feel (3)**: Select all options that apply to how you feel about your experience in our organization.
	+ I feel valued: I feel recognized and appreciated for my contributions.
	+ I feel respected: I feel respected in my interactions with colleagues and management.
	+ I feel supported: I feel supported in my work and personal growth.
	+ I feel engaged: I feel motivated and committed to my work.
	+ I feel happy: I feel satisfied with my overall experience.
* **What do you suggest (4)**: Write any suggestions or ideas for improvement that you have.
* **Contact Information (5)**: Select one or more methods of contact for us to reach out to you with updates or follow-ups.
	+ Email: Your email address.
	+ Phone: Your phone number.
	+ Address: Your physical address.
* **Open-ended (6)**: Share any additional thoughts or comments that you'd like to add.
* **Additional Comments (7)**: Share any additional comments or suggestions that you'd like to make. 
* **What are some things we could improve on? (8)**: Select one or more options that you think we could improve on.
	+ Something we do not have: We could add a new feature or benefit.
	+ Something we are doing poorly: We could improve on something that's not working well.
	+ Something we are doing well: We're already doing something correctly, let's keep it up.
