# Workforce Voice Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from employees about their experience with the company, focusing on engagement, communication, and overall job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Select your role at the company from the dropdown menu.
2. For questions 2 and 3, choose one or more options that best describe your feelings about company engagement and communication within your team.
3. Enter your email address and work phone number, if you have one.
4. Enter your expected return date, if you have one.
5. Choose a 24-hour format for your availability.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
7. Enter your name.
8. Enter your job title.
9. Select your department from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What's your role at the company?
* **What's your role at the company?** (Manager, Individual Contributor, Team Lead): Please select your role at the company.
### 2. How do you feel about company engagement and culture?
* **How do you feel about company engagement and culture?** (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither Agree Nor Disagree, Somewhat Disagree, Strongly Disagree): Select one or more options that best describe your feelings about company engagement and culture.
### 3. How would you rate the quality of communication within your team?
* **How would you rate the quality of communication within your team?** (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor): Select one option that best describes your feelings about the communication within your team.
### 4. Do you have any feedback or suggestions for improvements?
* **Do you have any feedback or suggestions for improvements?**: This is a text box for you to type in any comments or suggestions you may have.
### 5. What is your work email address?
* **What is your work email address?**: Please enter your work email address.
### 6. What is your work phone number?
* **What is your work phone number?**: This is a text field for you to enter your work phone number.
### 7. What is your expected return date?
* **What is your expected return date?** (YYYY-MM-DD): Enter your expected return date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
### 8. What is your availability in 24-hour format?
* **What is your availability in 24-hour format (HH-MM)?**: Enter your availability in 24-hour format (HH-MM).
### 9. Do you have any additional comments or feedback?
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?**: This is a text box for you to type in any additional comments or feedback.
### 10. What is your name?
* **What is your name?**: Please enter your name.
### 11. What is your job title?
* **What is your job title?**: Please enter your job title.
### 12. What department do you work in?
* **What department do you work in?** (HR, IT, Marketing, Finance, Operations, Management, Sales, Customer Support, Development): Select the department you work in.

Note: The questions are designed to be completed at your own pace and comfort level. Your responses will be kept confidential and will help us understand your experiences and perspectives. Thank you for taking the time to participate!
