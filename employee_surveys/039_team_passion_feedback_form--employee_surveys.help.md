# team_passion_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team passion feedback form is designed to gather information about team members' experiences and perceptions of their work environment. This information will help the team and leadership understand areas of strength and areas for improvement, leading to a more positive and productive team culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each question and its purpose.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide specific examples or explanations where possible.
4. Ensure you complete the form in its entirety, as some questions are required for accurate analysis.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team_passion_feedback_form** (`title`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is currently set to "team_passion_feedback_form". You can leave this field as is, unless you have a specific request or suggestion.
* **job_satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction_label`, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Very dissatisfied" and 5 being "Very satisfied".
* **job_satisfaction_level** (`job_satisfaction_level_label`, optional): This question is a follow-up to the previous question, asking you to select a specific level of job satisfaction. Please choose the corresponding level that best describes your experience.
* **team_dynamics** (`team_dynamics_label`, optional): This question asks you to select whether the team dynamics are "Yes" or "No". Please choose "Yes" if you feel the team has a positive, supportive, and collaborative environment.
* **team_dynamics_options** (`team_dynamics_options_label`, optional): This question is a follow-up to the previous question, asking you to select all that apply regarding team dynamics. Please choose all the options that best describe your experience with the team.
* **team_dynamics_options_label** (`team_dynamics_options_label`, optional): This is not a field for user input, please ignore.

Note: The required/optional labels for each field are based on the provided YAML, where required fields are not present.
