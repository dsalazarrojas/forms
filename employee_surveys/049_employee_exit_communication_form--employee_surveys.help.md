# Employee Exit Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees who are leaving the company. It helps in documenting the reasons for departure, any outstanding issues, and the employee's next steps. This form should be used by HR or management to understand the employee's perspective and to facilitate a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For the "what_will_you_miss_the_most" question, think about the aspects of the company that you will miss the most, such as colleagues, work environment, or benefits.
3. For the "what_will_you_miss" question, list the things you will miss about the company, such as people, work culture, or facilities.
4. In the "why_are_you_leaving" question, provide a clear explanation of your reasons for leaving, including any challenges or difficulties you faced.
5. For the "what_can_we_improve" question, suggest areas where the company can improve to prevent similar departures in the future.
6. In the "who_else_has_you_worked_for" question, list any previous employers or work experiences that may be relevant to your current departure.
7. The "how_do_you_feel" question is an opportunity to express your overall sentiment about your experience at the company.
8. In the "suggestions" field, provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the company.
9. For the "exit_comments" field, include any final thoughts or messages you want to communicate to your colleagues or management.
10. In the "additional_comments" field, provide any other information you think is relevant to your departure.
11. If you have a preferred method of contact, please provide it in the "exit_dated" field.
12. If you have a specific date for your exit, please provide it in the "exit_dated" field.
13-20. If you have a preferred method of contact, please provide it in the "contact_method_1" to "contact_method_10" fields.
14. If you have a specific date for your exit, please provide it in the "exit_date_1" to "exit_date_3" fields.
