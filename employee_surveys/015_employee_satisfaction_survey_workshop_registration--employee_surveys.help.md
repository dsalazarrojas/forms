# Employee Satisfaction Survey Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees to plan and execute a successful workshop that caters to their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on "Attendee Details" page to provide your name and other required information.
2. On the "Preferred Dates" page, select the date you are available to attend the workshop.
3. In the "Dietary Requirements" page, choose your dietary requirements if you have any.
4. On the "Discussion Topics" page, provide any additional topics you'd like to be discussed during the workshop.
5. Fill in the "Dates Available" field with your available dates to attend the workshop.
6. Select the start and end time you'd like the workshop to begin and end.
7. Choose the duration of the workshop you'd like to attend.
8. Provide the location where you'd like the workshop to be held.
9. Add any comments or feedback you have about the workshop.
10. Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
11. Provide any additional notes about your registration.
12. Finally, review the form to ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details** (Text): Please provide your name and any other relevant details.
* **Preferred Dates** (Select One): Choose the date you are available to attend the workshop.
* **Dietary Requirements** (Select One): If you have any dietary requirements, please select them here.
* **Discussion Topics** (Text): Add any additional topics you'd like to be discussed during the workshop.
* **Dates Available** (Date): Provide your available dates to attend the workshop.
* **Start Time** (Time): Select the time you'd like the workshop to begin.
* **End Time** (Time): Select the time you'd like the workshop to end.
* **Duration** (Number): Choose the duration of the workshop you'd like to attend.
* **Location** (Text): Provide the location where you'd like the workshop to be held.
* **Comments** (Note): Add any comments or feedback you have about the workshop.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Required Fields** (Text): This field is not applicable, please ignore.
* **Additional Notes** (Text): Provide any additional notes about your registration.
* **Submission Date** (Date): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **Created By** (Text): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **Submitted By** (Text): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **Updated By** (Text): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **Updated Date** (Date): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **Form ID** (Text): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **User ID** (Text): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **Form Title** (Text): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **Form Description** (Text): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **Form Category** (Text): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.
* **Form Status** (Text): This field is not editable, it's automatically populated.

Note: Some fields are not editable and are automatically populated by the system, please do not fill in these fields.
