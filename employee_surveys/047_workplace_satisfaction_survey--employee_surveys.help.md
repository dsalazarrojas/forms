# Workplace Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their overall satisfaction with various aspects of their workplace experience. By providing honest and constructive feedback, employees can help identify areas of improvement for a better work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page according to your genuine feelings and experiences.
2. Select one or multiple options for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Feedback" and "Suggestions" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Workplace Satisfaction** (`workplace_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select one or multiple answers that best describe your current state of satisfaction with the workplace.

* Satisfied: I am happy and content with my workplace experience.
* Dissatisfied: I am unhappy and dissatisfied with my workplace experience.

### **Work hours** (`work_hours`, number, required: false)
Please enter the number of hours you work per week.

### **Work-life balance** (`work_life`, number, required: false)
Rate how well your work-life balance is managed.

### **Workload** (`workload`, number, required: false)
Rate how manageable your current workload is.

### **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)
Select one answer that best describes your job satisfaction.

* Satisfied: I am happy and satisfied with my job.
* Dissatisfied: I am unhappy and dissatisfied with my job.

### **Work-life satisfaction** (`work_life_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false)
Select one answer that best describes your work-life satisfaction.

* Satisfied: I am happy and satisfied with my work-life balance.
* Dissatisfied: I am unhappy and dissatisfied with my work-life balance.

### **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, required: false)
Select one answer that best describes your experience with communication in the workplace.

* Yes: I have a good experience with communication in the workplace.
* No: I do not have a good experience with communication in the workplace.

### **Leadership** (`leadership`, select_one, required: false)
Select one answer that best describes your experience with leadership in the workplace.

* Yes: I have a good experience with leadership in the workplace.
* No: I do not have a good experience with leadership in the workplace.

### **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false)
Please provide any feedback or suggestions you have for improving the workplace experience.

### **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false)
Please provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the workplace experience.

### **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false)
Please provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about your workplace experience.

### **Work environment** (`work_environment`, select_one, required: false)
Select one answer that best describes your experience with the work environment.

* Yes: I have a good experience with the work environment.
* No: I do not have a good experience with the work environment.

### **Job security** (`job_security`, select_one, required: false)
