# feedback_process_effectiveness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from employees on the effectiveness of the feedback process within the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page. Each question is labeled and explained below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`feedback_process_effectiveness_survey_page_1`, `note`, required: false):
  This page is an introduction to the survey, explaining the purpose and importance of the feedback process.

* **What do you think about the feedback process?** (`feedback_process_effectiveness_survey_page_2`, `text`, required: false):
  Please share your thoughts about the feedback process. Be as detailed as possible.

* **How would you rate the usefulness of feedback in improving workplace culture?** (`feedback_process_effectiveness_survey_page_3`, `select_one`, required: false):
  Select one of the following options:
  * Strongly Agree
  * Somewhat Agree
  * Neither Agree nor Disagree
  * Somewhat Disagree
  * Strongly Disagree

* **What can be improved in the feedback process?** (`feedback_process_effectiveness_survey_page_4`, `text`, required: false):
  If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them here.

* **Have you ever received any negative feedback that you feel was unfair or unjust?** (`feedback_process_effectiveness_survey_page_5`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  Select all that apply:
  * Yes
  * No

* **How would you rate the effectiveness of the feedback process?** (`feedback_process_effectiveness_survey_page_6`, `number`, required: false):
  Please rate the effectiveness of the feedback process on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very ineffective and 5 is very effective.

* **Have you ever received feedback on how to improve your skills or job performance?** (`feedback_process_effectiveness_survey_page_7`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  Select all that apply:
  * Yes
  * No

* **What do you think about the feedback process?** (`feedback_process_effectiveness_survey_page_8`, `text`, required: false):
  Please share your thoughts about the feedback process.

* **Have you ever felt that the feedback process was too long?** (`feedback_process_effectiveness_survey_page_9`, `select_one`, required: false):
  Select one of the following options:
  * Yes
  * Somewhat
  * Rarely
  * Never

  * Yes
  * Somewhat
  * Rarely
  * Never
