# Talent Optimization Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about the impact of talent optimization initiatives on employees within the organization. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and insights from employees to help inform and improve talent development strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete the form in one of the following roles:
	* CEO
	* Director
	* Manager
	* Team Member
	* Other (Specify in the "Note" field)
2.  Select your department(s) that you belong to from the list:
	* Sales
	* Marketing
	* Product
	* Development
	* Finance
3.  Indicate whether your segment results are impacted by talent optimization initiatives:
	* Yes
	* No
4.  Indicate whether talent initiatives are being implemented in your area:
	* Yes
	* No
5.  Provide feedback about your experience with talent optimization initiatives:
	* Enter your feedback here
6.  Enter your email address:
	* This will be used to contact you if needed
7.  Enter your phone number:
	* This will be used to contact you if needed
8.  Leave a note for additional comments:
	* Enter any additional information you think would be helpful
9.  Select who submitted this form:
	* Jimmy
	* John
	* Jane

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role** (`role_input`):
	+ Select the role that best describes your position within the organization.
* **Department** (`department_input`):
	+ Select all departments that you belong to (e.g., Sales and Marketing).
* **Segment Results** (`segment_results_input`):
	+ Indicate whether your segment results are impacted by talent optimization initiatives.
* **Talent Initiatives** (`talent_initiatives_input`):
	+ Indicate whether talent initiatives are being implemented in your area.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_input`):
	+ Provide feedback about your experience with talent optimization initiatives.
* **Email** (`email_input`):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Note** (`note_input`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information you think would be helpful.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`):
	+ Select who submitted this form.
