# Employee Work Life Balance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate an employee's perception and experience with work-life balance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This section is a brief introduction to the form. You can provide any additional context or information about your work-life balance experience in this field.
2. **On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate your perception of your work-life balance?**: Rate your perception of your work-life balance on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
3. **Description of your work-life balance**: Provide a brief description of your current work-life balance in 100 words or less. Try to describe what you experience is working for work and personal life.
4. **How many hours do you work per week?**: Please enter the number of hours you work per week.
5. **Do you feel you have sufficient flexibility in your work schedule?**: Select all that apply if you feel you have sufficient flexibility in your work schedule.
	* Yes
	* No
	* Other (please specify)
6. **How would you rate the communication with your manager about your work-life balance?**: Select one of the provided options.
7. **Has your manager supported you in achieving a better work-life balance?**: Select one of the provided options.
8. **What self-care activities do you regularly engage in to maintain your well-being?**: Select all that apply.
	* Yoga
	* Meditation
	* Exercise
	* Hobbies
	* Other (please specify)
9. **Do you have a personal wellness plan in place?**: Select one of the provided options.
10. **Any additional feedback or comments?**: Please provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your work-life balance.
11. **Do you have any suggestions for improving our work-life balance policies?**: Please provide any suggestions you may have.
12. **Do you have any contact information you'd like to provide?**: If you wish to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your contact information.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any suggestions or feedback, please provide it in the designated fields.
* If you have any contact information you'd like to provide, please do so in the designated field.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask your manager or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed confidentially, so please feel free to answer without fear of judgment.
