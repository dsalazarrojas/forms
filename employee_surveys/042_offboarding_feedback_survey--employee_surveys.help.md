# offboarding_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The offboarding_feedback_survey form is used to gather feedback and comments on the offboarding process of an employee. This information helps the offboarding team improve the process and make it more efficient for future offboardings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction section, which explains the purpose and importance of this form.
2. Provide feedback on what the offboarding team can do to improve the process by answering the "What can we do to improve" question.
3. Answer the "Manager Feedback" question with either "Yes" or "No", indicating whether the manager provided feedback during the offboarding process.
4. Rate the manager's feedback by selecting either "Good" or "Fair" from the dropdown options.
5. Provide any comments or feedback you have as the employee by answering the "Employee Feedback" question.
6. Enter the date of the last interaction with the employee.
7. Indicate whether the reason for offboarding was "Yes" or "No" by answering the "Reason for Offboarding" question.
8. Answer the "Last Interaction Method" question with either "Yes" or "No", indicating whether the last interaction was done in-person, phone call, email, or other.
9. Provide any comments or concerns about the offboarding process in the "Offboarding Comments" text field.
10. Enter the employee's name for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: An introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **What can we do to improve**: Provide feedback on what the offboarding team can do to improve the process. This is an optional question.
* **Manager Feedback**: Indicate whether the manager provided feedback during the offboarding process. Select either "Yes" or "No".
* **Manager Rating**: Rate the manager's feedback by selecting either "Good" or "Fair".
* **Employee Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have as the employee.
* **Date of Last Interaction**: Enter the date of the last interaction with the employee.
* **Reason for Offboarding**: Indicate whether the reason for offboarding was "Yes" or "No". This helps the team understand the circumstances.
* **Last Interaction Method**: Indicate whether the last interaction was done in-person, phone call, email, or other. Select either "Yes" or "No".
* **Offboarding Comments**: Provide any comments or concerns about the offboarding process.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name for reference.
