# Employee Wellness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your well-being and work experience within our organization. Your responses will help us understand the overall health and happiness of our employees, and we use this information to make data-driven decisions for our organization's well-being initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select all that apply to each question.
2. Be honest and complete all the required fields.
3. Enter your contact information at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Wellness Survey** (1, note, required: false): This is the title of the survey.
* **Health** (2, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all of the options that apply to your current health status.
	+ 'Yes': You feel healthy and well.
	+ 'No': You do not feel healthy and well.
* **How are you feeling?** (3, select_one, required: false): Please select your current emotional state.
	+ Option 1: Feeling positive and energetic.
	+ Option 2: Feeling neutral.
	+ Option 3: Feeling negative and stressed.
* **Wellbeing** (4, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all of the options that apply to your current well-being status.
	+ 'Yes': You feel well and supported.
	+ 'No': You do not feel well and supported.
* **Do you get enough sleep?** (5, select_one, required: true): How many hours of sleep do you get each night?
	+ Option 1: Less than 5 hours.
	+ Option 2: 5-7 hours.
	+ Option 3: 8-10 hours.
	+ Option 4: More than 10 hours.
* **How stressed do you feel?** (6, select_one, required: false): How stressed do you feel at work?
	+ Option 1: Very stressed.
	+ Option 2: Somewhat stressed.
	+ Option 3: Not stressed at all.
* **How's your mental health?** (7, select_one, required: false): How would you describe your mental health?
	+ Option 1: Excellent.
	+ Option 2: Satisfactory.
	+ Option 3: Unhealthy.
* **How's your physical health?** (8, select_one, required: false): How would you describe your physical health?
	+ Option 1: Excellent.
	+ Option 2: Satisfactory.
	+ Option 3: Unhealthy.
* **Do you get enough exercise?** (9, select_one, required: false): How often do you exercise?
	+ Option 1: Rarely.
	+ Option 2: Occasionally.
	+ Option 3: Frequently.
* **Work Environment** (10, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all of the options that apply to your work environment.
	+ 'Yes': You feel safe and supported in your work environment.
	+ 'No': You do not feel safe and supported in your work environment.
* **Do you have a supportive manager?** (11, select_one, required: false): How would you describe your manager's support?
	+ Option 1: Very supportive.
	+ Option 2: Somewhat supportive.
	+ Option 3: Not supportive at all.
* **Do you feel your work life balance?** (12, select_one, required: false): How do you balance your work and personal life?
	+ Option 1: Excellent balance.
	+ Option 2: Satisfactory balance.
	+ Option 3: Poor balance.
* **Workplace Safety** (13, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all of the options that apply to your workplace safety.
	+ 'Yes': You feel safe at work.
	+ 'No': You do not feel safe at work.
* **Do you feel safe at work?** (14, select_one, required: false): How safe do you feel at work?
	+ Option 1: Very safe.
	+ Option 2: Somewhat safe.
	+ Option 3: Not safe at all.
* **Additional Comments** (15, note, required: false): Any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share.
* **Comments** (16, text, required: false): Any other comments or feedback.
* **Contact Name** (17, text, required: true): Your name.
* **Contact Email** (18, email, required: true): Your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (19, text, required: true): Your phone number.
* **Comments** (20, text, required: false): Any other comments or feedback for us.
* **Which department do you work in?** (21, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all of the options that apply to your department.
	+ 'Yes': You work in this department.
	+ 'No': You do not work in this department.
* **What is your job title?** (22, text, required: false): Your job title.
* **How many years have you been with our organization?** (23, select_one, required: false): How long have you been with our organization?
	+ Option 1: Less than 1 year.
	+ Option 2: 1-3 years.
	+ Option 3: 3-5 years.
	+ Option 4: More than 5 years.
* **Closing Comments** (24, note, required: false): Any final comments or thoughts.
