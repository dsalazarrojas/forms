# Return To Office Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your return to the office plans, which will help us assess the feasibility of your return and make necessary arrangements to support a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose the type of work you plan on doing while returning to the office from the options provided.
2. Estimate the number of hours you expect to work from home.
3. Select how you usually travel to work.
4. Select how you expect to transport yourself to the office.
5. (Optional) Choose how concerned you are about parking concerns.
6. (Optional) Select the type of office environment you prefer.
7. Choose the date you expect to return to the office.
8. Add any additional comments about your return to the office.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What kind of work do you plan on doing while returning to the office?** (`return_to_office_survey_questions`, `select_multiple`, **required**): This field asks you to select all the types of work you plan on doing while returning to the office. Please choose from the options: In office, Remotely, In hybrid.
* **How many hours do you expect to work from home?** (`work_hours`, `number`, **required**): This field asks you to estimate the number of hours you plan on working from home. Please provide a number that reflects your expected work hours.
* **How do you usually travel to work?** (`transportation_to_work`, `select_one`, **required**): This field asks you how you usually travel to work. Please select from the options: Drive, Public transportation, Walk, Other.
* **How do you expect to transport yourself to the office?** (`transportation_to_office`, `select_multiple`, **required**): This field asks you how you plan to transport yourself to the office. Please select all the options that apply.
* **How concerned are you about parking?** (`parking_concerns`, `select_one`, **optional**): This field asks you to rate your concern level about parking. Please select from the options: Very concerned, Somewhat concerned, Not concerned.
* **What kind of office environment do you prefer?** (`office_environment`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): This field asks you to select your preferred office environment. Please choose from the options: Quiet, Open, Collaborative.
* **When do you expect to return to the office?** (`return_date`, `date`, **required**): This field asks you to choose the date you expect to return to the office. Please provide a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Comments?** (`comments`, `note`, **optional**): This field is a free-text field where you can add any additional comments about your return to the office.
