# Job Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form aims to help employees assess their current job satisfaction. It will assist management and HR in understanding employee opinions and identify areas where the organization can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely.
2. Answer honestly, as your responses will remain confidential.
3. If you're unsure about a question, try to answer based on your best judgment.
4. You can complete this form on your own time, but please save your progress and submit it when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Please enter your current company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Type in your current job title or position.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your current department or team name.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of team members you work with regularly.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your current job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Job Satisfaction Importance** (`job_satisfaction_important`, text, optional): Briefly explain why this job satisfaction is important to you.
* **Job Satisfaction Importance Scale** (`job_satisfaction_important_scale`, number, optional): If you answered "very satisfied" or "very dissatisfied" for the previous question, select the corresponding scale value (1-5). If not, leave this field blank.
* **Job Satisfaction Importance Max** (`job_satisfaction_important_scale_max`, text, optional): Enter the maximum scale value you'd be willing to accept for job satisfaction. This will help us understand your expectations.
* **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, number, optional): Rate your current work-life balance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "very good".
* **Career Growth** (`career_growth`, number, optional): Rate your current career growth on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "slow" and 5 is "fast".
* **Job Satisfaction Score** (`job_satisfaction_score`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the two options: "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Job Satisfaction Scale** (`job_satisfaction_scale`, text, optional): Please ignore this field as it's not currently used.
