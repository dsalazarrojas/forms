# Worker Information Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Worker Information Survey form is designed to collect information about an employee's profile, including their first name, last name, contact details, and job title.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided. This is a required field to help us identify you.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field provided. This is a required field to help us identify you.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the text field, formatted as a standard email address (e.g., `user@example.com`). This is a required field to ensure we can contact you.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field. This is an optional field, but it helps us to get in touch with you.
5. Enter your **Job Title** in the text field provided. This is an optional field to provide additional context about your role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID card, passport, or driver's license).
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a standard email address (e.g., `user@example.com`) that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional), which will only be used to contact you for official purposes.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within the company (optional), to provide context about your role.
