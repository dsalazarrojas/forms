# Veterinary Staff Retention Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Staff Retention Insight Survey is a tool to gather information from veterinary staff on their job satisfaction, reasons for leaving or staying, and ideas for improving staff retention. This survey aims to provide valuable insights to support the organization's efforts to maintain a stable and engaged workforce.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please navigate through the following pages:
	* Page 1: Satisfaction - How satisfied are you with your job?
	* Page 2: Reasons for Leaving - Why are you leaving the organization?
	* Page 3: Reasons for Staying - Why do you feel invested in your job role?
	* Page 4: Ideas for Improvement - What ideas do you have to improve staff retention?
	* Page 5: Feedback - Do you have any additional feedback for the clinic or hospital?
	* Page 6: Additional Comments - Would you like to share any additional comments?
	* Page 7: Contact Information - Please provide your email and phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with your job? (Page 1)** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".
* **Why are you leaving? (Page 2)** (`reasons_for_leaving`, `text`, required: false): Select all the reasons why you are leaving the organization.
* **Why are you staying? (Page 3)** (`reasons_for_staying`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all that apply for the reasons why you feel invested in your job role.
	+ Option 1: Opportunity for growth and development
	+ Option 2: Positive work environment
	+ Option 3: Supportive colleagues and management
* **What ideas do you have for improving staff retention? (Page 4)** (`ideas_for_retention`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one idea for improving staff retention.
	+ Option 1: Better compensation and benefits
	+ Option 2: More opportunities for advancement
	+ Option 3: Enhanced work-life balance
* **What feedback do you have for the clinic or hospital? (Page 5)** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share any feedback or comments you have regarding the clinic or hospital.
* **Additional comments (Page 6)** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Write any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Email (Page 7)** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone (Page 7)** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Tips
* Answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
* Please ensure all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
