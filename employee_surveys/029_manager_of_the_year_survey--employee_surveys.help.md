# Manager Of The Year Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Of The Year Survey is a platform for employees to express their opinions on who they believe deserves the title of "Manager of the Year" based on the individual's managerial performance, leadership skills, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Select the managerial performance option that best describes your manager's performance.
2. Choose the leadership skills that you think your manager possesses.
3. Rate your manager's performance on the specified criteria.
4. Select the title of "Manager of the Year" that you believe best suits your manager.
5. Choose the department where your manager works.
6. Enter your manager's email address.
7. Provide your manager's phone number.
8. Add any additional comments about your manager's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey (1)** (`employee_survey`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your manager's performance in their role as an employee.
* **Managerial Performance (2)** (`managerial_performance`, select_one, optional): Please select the option that best describes your manager's performance.
	+ Managerial Leadership: This option is best suited for managers who have demonstrated exceptional leadership skills.
	+ Strategic Planning: This option is ideal for managers who have shown the ability to create and execute strategic plans.
	+ Communication: This option is suitable for managers who are skilled in effectively communicating with their team members.
	+ Problem Solving: This option is best suited for managers who have demonstrated exceptional problem-solving skills.
	+ Adaptability: This option is ideal for managers who can adapt to changing circumstances.
	+ Results Orientation: This option is suitable for managers who focus on achieving results and meeting goals.
* **Leadership Skills (3)** (`leadership_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the leadership skills that you believe your manager possesses.
	+ goal1: This skill is essential for setting and achieving goals.
	+ goal2: This skill is critical for maintaining a positive work environment.
	+ goal3: This skill is vital for developing and implementing plans.
	+ goal4: This skill is necessary for resolving conflicts.
	+ goal5: This skill is essential for managing resources.
* **Performance (4)** (`performance_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Please rate your manager's performance on the specified criteria.
	+ rating1: This rating is suitable for managers with exceptional performance.
	+ rating2: This rating is ideal for managers with above-average performance.
	+ rating3: This rating is suitable for managers with average performance.
	+ rating4: This rating is suitable for managers with below-average performance.
	+ rating5: This rating is suitable for managers with very poor performance.
* **Manager Of The Year Title (5)** (`manager_of_the_year_title`, select_one, optional): Please select the title of "Manager of the Year" that you believe best suits your manager.
	+ Manager: This title is suitable for managers who have consistently demonstrated exceptional leadership and performance.
	+ Assistant Manager: This title is ideal for managers who have shown potential for growth and development.
	+ Department Head: This title is suitable for managers who have consistently demonstrated exceptional leadership and performance in their department.
* **Department (6)** (`department`, select_one, optional): Please select the department where your manager works.
	+ Engineering: This option is ideal for managers who work in the Engineering department.
	+ Finance: This option is suitable for managers who work in the Finance department.
	+ Marketing: This option is suitable for managers who work in the Marketing department.
	+ Sales: This option is suitable for managers who work in the Sales department.
* **Email (7)** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your manager's email address.
* **Phone (8)** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Of The Year Comments (9)** (`manager_of_the_year_comments`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments about your manager's performance.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions based on your personal experience and opinion.
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
