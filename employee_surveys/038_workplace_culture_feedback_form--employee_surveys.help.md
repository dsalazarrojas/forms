# Workplace Culture Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Workplace Culture Feedback Form" is designed to help you provide feedback on your experiences at work. Your responses will help us understand how you feel about your job, team, and work environment. Please answer each question honestly, and remember that your responses are anonymous.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, considering your experiences and feelings.
2. For select_multiple, select all applicable options.
3. For select_one, select only one option that best describes your situation.
4. For number and time fields, enter a numerical value or a time duration (e.g., 1-2 hours).
5. For text fields, provide a brief answer (max 100 characters).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, text, optional): Describe how satisfied you are with your job and work environment.
