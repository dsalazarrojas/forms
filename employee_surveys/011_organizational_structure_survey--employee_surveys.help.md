# Organizational Structure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Structure Survey is designed to gather information from employees about their perceptions of the organizational structure within the company. The survey will help inform decisions on future changes to the company's internal policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, select all the options that apply to you from each section and provide any additional feedback or suggestions in the "Employee Satisfactions 1" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Surveys**: 
  • Select all the options that apply to you, indicating your level of satisfaction with your role and team within the company. This will help us understand your overall level of satisfaction.
* **Employee Surveys 1**: 
  • Enter any additional feedback or suggestions you have for improving the organizational structure. This is not a multiple-choice question, but rather a free text field for any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Organizational Structure**: 
  • Choose whether you are satisfied or dissatisfied with your current "Organizational Structure". This will help us understand whether the current structure is meeting your needs.
* **Employee Satisfactions**: 
  • Select all the options that apply to you, indicating your level of satisfaction with your current role and team. This will help us understand your overall level of satisfaction.
* **Organizational Structure 1**: 
  • Choose whether you are satisfied or dissatisfied with this part of the "Organizational Structure". This will help us understand whether this structure is meeting your needs.
* **Organizational Structure 2**: 
  • Choose whether you are satisfied or dissatisfied with this part of the "Organizational Structure". This will help us understand whether this structure is meeting your needs.

## Tips
* Please complete all the fields, but note that some fields are not required.
* If you are not sure about any of the options, please select "No" or "Not Applicable" and we will take that into consideration when reviewing your response.
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please use the "Employee Satisfactions 1" field to provide them.
