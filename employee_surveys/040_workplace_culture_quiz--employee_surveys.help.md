# Workplace Culture Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather employees' opinions on various aspects of their workplace culture to help identify areas of improvement or areas of strength.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions based on your genuine experience and feelings about your workplace culture.
2. Please provide honest and constructive feedback for each question.
3. You can add any additional comments or thoughts in the "feedback and comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with your overall job satisfaction?
* **Company Values**: How well do you think your company's values align with your personal values?
* **Mission Alignment**: How well does your company's mission align with your understanding of the company's purpose?
* **Culture Fidelity**: How well does your company's culture align with its stated values and mission?
* **Company Mission**: What is your understanding of the company's mission and vision?
* **Team Spirit**: How strong is the sense of teamwork and collaboration in your department or team?
* **Leadership Support**: How supportive is your manager or supervisor in helping you grow and develop in your role?
* **Collaboration**: How well do you think your team members collaborate with each other to achieve common goals?
* **Innovation**: How often do you feel encouraged to think innovatively and try new ideas in your work?
* **Communication**: How clear and effective is communication between team members and management?
* **Trust and Teamwork**: How strong is the trust and cooperation among team members?
* **Job Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with your current job and role?
* **Diversity and Equity**: How well does your company promote diversity and inclusion in the workplace?
* **Inclusion**: How inclusive do you feel in the workplace, considering your background and identity?
* **Growth and Development**: How much opportunities do you have for growth and development in your role and career?
* **Recognition**: How often do you feel recognized and appreciated for your work and contributions?
* **Feedback and Comments**: Any additional comments or thoughts about your workplace culture?

Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest feedback. Your input will help us improve our workplace culture and better serve you and your colleagues.
