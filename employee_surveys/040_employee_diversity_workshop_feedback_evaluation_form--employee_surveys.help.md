# Employee Diversity Workshop Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey for employees who have attended the Employee Diversity Workshop. Your feedback will help us improve the workshop and make it more beneficial for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If a field is required, please make sure to fill it in completely.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field.
4. If you have any contact information, please enter it in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: This is an optional note field for any additional comments or information about the workshop.
- **How effective was the workshop?** (`workshop_effectiveness`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, how effective do you think the workshop was in addressing diversity and inclusion?
- **What did you think of the workshop content?** (`workshop_content`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all that apply from the options provided. This will help us understand what topics were covered and how well they were received.

- **How well was the workshop organized?** (`workshop_organization`, `text`, required: true): Please describe how well the workshop was organized in terms of time management, communication, and overall flow.
- **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-5, please rate the overall quality of the workshop. Note that this is optional and not required to submit the form.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the workshop.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false): If you have any feedback or questions, please include your contact information so we can follow up with you.
- **Follow up** (`follow_up`, `text`, required: false): Please use this field to suggest any follow-up activities or resources that we could use to continue the conversation about diversity and inclusion in the workplace.
