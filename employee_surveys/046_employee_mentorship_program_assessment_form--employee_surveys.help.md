# Employee Mentorship Program Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from employees who have participated in the mentorship program. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions for future program development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
3. Use the select_one fields to choose the option that best represents your response.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Other comments about your mentorship experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you enjoy most about the mentorship program?**
	+ This field is asking for a brief description of the aspects of the mentorship program that you enjoyed the most. Please describe the aspects that you found most valuable or satisfying.
* **What did you enjoy least about the mentorship program?**
	+ This field is asking for a brief description of the aspects of the mentorship program that you found least enjoyable or challenging. Please describe the specific issues or problems you encountered.
* **What could be improved about the mentorship program?**
	+ This field is asking for suggestions on how the program could be improved. Please provide specific ideas or recommendations for enhancing the program.
* **How often do you think you could use your mentor's guidance?**
	+ This field is asking about your perceived need for guidance from your mentor. Please select the option that best represents how often you think you could use your mentor's guidance.
* **How often do you think you could give guidance to your mentor?**
	+ This field is asking about your perceived ability to give guidance to your mentor. Please select the option that best represents how often you think you could give guidance to your mentor.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with your mentorship experience overall?**
	+ This field is asking for your overall satisfaction level with the mentorship program. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Other comments about your mentorship experience**
	+ This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your mentorship experience. Please use this space to provide any further feedback or insights that you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **How likely are you to recommend the mentorship program to others?**
	+ This field is asking about your willingness to recommend the mentorship program to others. Please select the option that best represents how likely you are to recommend the program.
