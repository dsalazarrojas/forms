# Performance Goals Alignment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Goals Alignment Survey is a tool for evaluating the alignment between employees' performance goals and the organization's objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the answers to the questions according to your experience and understanding.
2. Select all applicable options for each question.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.
