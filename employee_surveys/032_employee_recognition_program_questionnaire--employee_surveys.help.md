# Employee Recognition Program Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form gathers insights into the recognition program's effectiveness, practices, and areas for improvement, helping your employers to better understand your thoughts and shape the future of the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question in the best way that reflects your true feelings and experiences with the recognition program.
2. You can choose one or multiple answers for most questions, as indicated by the question type (e.g., "select multiple" or "select one").
3. Be as specific as possible when answering questions that allow text responses (e.g., "recognition_suggestions" fields).
4. You can skip any questions that are not relevant to your personal experience or don't make sense to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recognition Practices** (2): This question asks about the types of recognition you receive in your workplace. You can choose from "Public Acknowledgement", "Private Acknowledgement", "Both", or "None". Please select all that apply.
* **Recognition Types** (3): This question asks about the types of recognition you receive. You can choose from "Monetary", "Tangible", "Intangible", or "All of the Above". Please select all that apply.
* **Program Areas** (4): This question asks about the areas where recognition is most commonly practiced. You can choose from "All Employees", "Leadership", "Team Members", or "Both".
* **Recognition Frequency** (5): This question asks about how frequently you are recognized. You can choose from "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", or "Quarterly".
* **Employee Satisfaction** (6): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the recognition program. You can choose from "Very Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat Dissatisfied", or "Very Dissatisfied".
