# Logistics Delivery Employment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees about their delivery experience, improvement areas, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Please note that some fields are optional, but providing as much detail as possible will help us better understand your experience.
3. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Delivery Experience
* **Delivery Experience** (`delivery_experience`, `text`, not required): Describe your experience with our delivery process. Please provide as much detail as possible about what happened during the delivery.

### Improvement Areas
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, `select_multiple`, not required): Identify any areas where we could improve our delivery process. You can select multiple options from the dropdown menu.
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Suggestions
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, not required): Provide any suggestions you think could help us improve our delivery process.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Share any additional comments or observations about your experience.

### Employee Experience
* **Employee Experience** (`employee_experience`, `number`, not required): Rate your overall experience with our delivery process on a scale from 1 to 100.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, not required): If applicable, provide your job title to help us better understand your role.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, not required): If applicable, provide your department to help us better understand your role.

### Feedback Score
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, `number`, not required): Rate your satisfaction with our delivery process on a scale from 1 to 100.

### Job Performance
* **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, `select_one`, not required): Rate whether or not you were satisfied with our job performance during the delivery process.
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Feedback Type
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, `select_one`, not required): Rate whether or not this feedback is a complaint or a compliment.
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Contact Method
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, not required): Rate whether or not you would like to be contacted regarding this feedback.
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, not required): If you would like to be contacted, provide your name.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, not required): If you would like to be contacted, provide your email address.

### Tips
* This form is intended to be completed by employees who have had a recent delivery experience.
* Please provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
