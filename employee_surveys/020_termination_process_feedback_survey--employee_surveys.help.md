# Termination Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Termination Process Feedback Survey is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about our termination process. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us identify areas for improvement and enhance the experience for all employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey and provide your feedback.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, and if you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
3. For select questions, choose one or multiple options that best describe your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What happened with this termination?** (Text field)
	+ Please share a brief description of what happened with your termination process. This will help us understand the context of your experience.
* **How satisfied are you with the termination process?** (Number field)
	+ Please rate your satisfaction level on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is "not satisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".
* **Why do you not agree with the termination?** (Text field)
	+ If you have any concerns or disagreements with the termination process, please explain why.
* **Why do you agree with the termination?** (Text field)
	+ If you have any positive experiences or reasons for agreeing with the termination process, please share them.
* **How can we improve the termination process?** (Text field)
	+ Share your suggestions for improvement, no matter how small they may seem.
* **What would you like to see improved in our termination process?** (Text field)
	+ Similar to the previous question, share your suggestions for improvement.
* **What is the most surprising thing you learned from this termination experience?** (Text field)
	+ Share any unexpected insights or learnings you gained from the process.
