# Workstation Setup Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workstation Setup Feedback Questionnaire is a form used to gather feedback from employees on their workstation setup, allowing the organization to understand their comfort and productivity level. This form will help in identifying areas that need improvement and enable the organization to make necessary adjustments to create a more comfortable and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your workstation setup.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **workstation_setup** (Select Multiple, Required: False): 
    This field allows you to specify if your workstation is set up for your needs, such as having a comfortable chair, proper lighting, and a suitable monitor resolution.
* **workstation_configuration** (Select One, Required: False): 
    Please select the option that best describes your workstation configuration.
* **comfort** (Number, Required: False): 
    Rate your level of comfort in the workplace, with higher numbers indicating higher levels of comfort.
* **noise_level** (Number, Required: False): 
    Rate the noise level in your workstation, with higher numbers indicating higher levels of noise.
* **temperature** (Number, Required: False): 
    Rate the temperature in your workstation, with higher numbers indicating higher levels of temperature.
* **chair_height** (Number, Required: False): 
    Please specify the height of your chair, in inches.
* **monitor_resolution** (Select One, Required: False): 
    Please select the resolution of your monitor that best describes your current setup.
* **employee_id** (Number, Required: False): 
    This field is not used in the feedback process and is likely for administrative purposes.
* **workstation_type** (Select Multiple, Required: False): 
    Please select the options that best describe your workstation type, such as having a keyboard, mouse, and monitor.
* **keyboard_layout** (Select One, Required: False): 
    Please select the layout of your keyboard that best describes your current setup.
* **mouse_layout** (Select One, Required: False): 
    Please select the layout of your mouse that best describes your current setup.
* **feedback** (Note, Required: False): 
    Provide any additional feedback about your workstation setup.
* **workstation_location** (Select Multiple, Required: False): 
    Please select the location of your workstation that best describes your current setup.
* **monitor_orientation** (Select One, Required: False): 
    Please select the orientation of your monitor that best describes your current setup.
* **employee_survey_id** (Number, Required: False): 
    This field is not used in the feedback process and is likely for administrative purposes.
* **workstation_model** (Select One, Required: False): 
    Please select the model of your workstation that best describes your current setup.
* **employee_survey** (Select Multiple, Required: False): 
    Please select the options that best describe the employee surveys you participated in.
* **keyboard_type** (Select One, Required: False): 
    Please select the type of keyboard you use for work.
* **employee_survey_id_2** (Number, Required: False): 
    This field is not used in the feedback process and is likely for administrative purposes.
* **mouse_type** (Select One, Required: False): 
    Please select the type of mouse you use for work.
* **monitor_type** (Select One, Required: False): 
    Please select the type of monitor you use for work.
* **keyboard_height** (Number, Required: False): 
    Please specify the height of your keyboard in inches.
* **mouse_height** (Number, Required: False): 
    Please specify the height of your mouse in inches.
* **monitor_width** (Number, Required: False): 
    Please specify the width of your monitor in inches.
* **monitor_depth** (Number, Required: False): 
    Please specify the depth of your monitor in inches.
