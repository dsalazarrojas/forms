# Employee Benefits Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **Employee Benefits Preference Survey** is designed to understand your preferences for the company's employee benefits package. This survey will help us tailor the benefits to better meet your individual needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each question and select your preferences for each field. If you have any questions or concerns about a particular field, feel free to reach out to your HR representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of benefits package do you prefer?** (`benefits_package_preferred`, select_one): Choose from options: Basic, Premium, Custom. This field allows you to select the level of benefits you prefer: Basic covers the essentials, Premium includes additional perks and services, and Custom allows you to tailor your benefits package to your specific needs.
* **What benefits options are you interested in?** (`benefits_options_preferred`, select_multiple): Select all benefits options that interest you from the options: Gym Membership, Flexible Work Arrangement, Professional Development. This field helps us understand which benefits you value most and are willing to take advantage of.
* **How often do you take breaks during the workday?** (`break_frequency`, select_one): Choose how frequently you take breaks: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely. This field helps us understand your work style and how often you need breaks.
* **Do you prefer morning, afternoon, or evening work shifts?** (`shift_preference`, select_one): Choose from options: Morning, Afternoon, Evening. This field helps us understand your schedule preferences.
* **What are your preferred work hours?** (`work_hours`, text): Enter your preferred work hours in a 24-hour format. For example: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday. This field helps us tailor your schedule to your needs.
* **Do you prefer working from home or in the office?** (`work_location`, select_one): Choose from options: Home, Office. This field helps us understand your work style and preferences for remote work or on-site work.
* **How many vacation days do you prefer?** (`vacation_days`, number): Enter a whole number, e.g., 10, 20, 30. This field helps us understand your vacation needs.
* **What type of health insurance do you prefer?** (`health_insurance_preferred`, select_multiple): Select all health insurance options that interest you from the options: Traditional, Premium, Custom. This field helps us understand your health insurance needs.
* **Do you have any health conditions that require special accommodations?** (`health_conditions`, select_one): Choose from options: True, False. This field helps us understand your health needs and potential requirements for accommodations.
* **Do you have any family members or dependents that may need to be included in our benefits package?** (`dependents_included`, select_one): Choose from options: True, False. This field helps us understand your family or dependent needs.
* **How many dependents do you have that may need benefits?** (`dependents_number`, number): Enter a whole number, e.g., 1, 2, 3. This field helps us understand how many dependents you have.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share about your benefits preferences?** (`manager_comments`, note): This field is optional and allows you to add any additional comments or feedback about your benefits preferences.
