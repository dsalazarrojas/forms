# Chronic Illness Employment Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand how chronic illnesses affect employment. Please answer the questions honestly, and your responses will help us identify areas where support and accommodations can be improved to better support individuals with chronic illnesses in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your experiences.
2. Select one option from the provided choices for each question.
3. If you have a unique situation or experience, you can write it down in the "Additional Comments" section at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This is the first question on the form. Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the survey results.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates on the survey results.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Please select the age group that best represents your age. This will help us understand how different age groups may be affected by chronic illnesses.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Please select your gender from the provided options. This will help us understand how different genders may be affected by chronic illnesses.
* **Chronic Condition** (`chronic_condition`, select_one, required): Please select the chronic condition that best describes your primary condition. This will help us understand which conditions are most prevalent and their impact on employment.
* **How Long Diagnosed** (`condition_duration`, select_one, required): Please select the time since your diagnosis. This will help us understand how long-term effects of chronic illnesses may impact employment.
* **Symptom Severity** (`symptom_severity`, select_one, required): Please rate your typical symptom severity. This will help us understand the level of impairment caused by your symptoms.
* **Current Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Please select your current employment status. This will help us understand how chronic illnesses affect employment status.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of work you do. This will help us understand how different types of work may be affected by chronic illnesses.
* **Years in Current Position** (`years_employed`, select_one, optional): Please select how long you have been in your current position. This will help us understand how long-term employment affects chronic illnesses.
* **Work Limitations** (`work_limitations`, select_one, required): Please select how your chronic condition limits your work. This will help us understand how much work is lost or reduced due to chronic illnesses.
* **Missed Work Days** (`missed_work_days`, select_one, required): Please select the number of work days you have missed in the past 3 months. This will help us understand how much work time is lost due to chronic illnesses.
* **Reduced Productivity** (`reduced_productivity`, select_one, required): Please select how often your productivity is affected by your chronic condition. This will help us understand how much productivity is lost due to chronic illnesses.
* **Accommodations Needed** (`accommodations_needed`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the accommodations you need or have needed in the workplace. This will help us understand what types of accommodations are most necessary.
* **Accommodations Requested** (`accommodations_requested`, select_one, required): Please select whether you have requested accommodations, and if so, what type(s) they were.
* **Employer Support** (`employer_support`, select_one, required): Please select how supportive your employer is. This will help us understand how much support is available from employers.
* **Career Advancement Impact** (`career_advancement_impact`, select_one, required): Please select how your chronic condition has affected your career growth. This will help us understand how much career advancement is hindered by chronic illnesses.
* **Income Impact** (`income_impact`, select_one, required): Please select how your chronic condition has affected your income. This will help us understand how much income is lost due to chronic illnesses.
* **Discrimination Experience** (`discrimination_experience`, select_one, required): Please select if you have experienced discrimination due to your chronic condition. This will help us understand how often discrimination occurs.
* **Job Security Concern** (`job_security_concern`, select_one, required): Please select how concerned you are about job security. This will help us understand how much concern there is about job security.
* **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, required): Please select how your work-life balance is affected by your chronic condition. This will help us understand how much work-life balance is affected by chronic illnesses.
* **Biggest Challenge** (`biggest_challenge`, text, optional): If you have a unique situation or experience that you would like to share, please write it down here.
* **Support Needed** (`support_needed`, text, optional): If you have a specific type of support that you would like to receive, please write it down here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any other experiences or thoughts that you would like to share, please write them down here.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and according to your experiences.
* If you have any difficulty answering a question, please contact us for assistance.
* Your responses will help us improve support and accommodations for individuals with chronic illnesses in the workplace.
