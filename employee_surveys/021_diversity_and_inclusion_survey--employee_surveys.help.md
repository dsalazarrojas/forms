# Diversity And Inclusion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Diversity And Inclusion Survey" is a form designed to gather information about employee experiences, perceptions, and preferences related to diversity and inclusion in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and questions asked.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the correct options for each question, based on your personal experiences and preferences.
4. Ensure that you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Survey** (`employee_survey`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have taken part in a diversity and inclusion survey before.
	+ This question helps us understand if you have experience with diversity and inclusion surveys.
- **Demographic Data** (`demographic_data`, `text`, required: false): Provide your demographic information, including your job title, department, and years of service.
	+ This information helps us understand your role and position within the organization.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address and phone number to allow us to follow up with you.
	+ This information enables us to contact you for further discussion or clarification.
- **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you experience a positive and inclusive work environment.
	+ This question helps us understand your perception of the work environment.
- **Diversity Practices** (`diversity_practices`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts on diversity practices within the organization.
	+ This information helps us understand your experiences and suggestions for diversity practices.
- **Inclusion Practices** (`inclusion_practices`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experiences with inclusion practices in the organization.
	+ This information helps us understand how we can improve inclusion initiatives.
- **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have participated in diversity and inclusion training programs.
	+ This question helps us understand the effectiveness of our training programs.
- **Communication** (`communication`, `note`, required: false): Share any comments or thoughts on communication within the organization.
	+ This information helps us understand how to improve communication on diversity and inclusion initiatives.
- **Reporting** (`reporting`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you last reported a diversity and inclusion incident or issue.
	+ This helps us understand your reporting behavior and preferences.
- **Action Items** (`action_items`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" for each action item you have taken to promote diversity and inclusion.
	+ This helps us understand your actions and behaviors related to diversity and inclusion.
- **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `time`, required: false): Schedule a follow-up meeting or call to discuss diversity and inclusion initiatives.
	+ This information helps us plan and schedule follow-up meetings or calls.
- **Survey Completion** (`survey_completion`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the survey.
	+ This helps us understand your thoughts on the survey completion process.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive follow-up communication.
	+ This information enables us to contact you for further discussion or clarification.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to receive follow-up calls.
	+ This information enables us to contact you for further discussion or clarification.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback on diversity and inclusion initiatives.
	+ This information helps us understand your thoughts and suggestions.
- **Follow Up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any follow-up comments or feedback on diversity and inclusion initiatives.
	+ This information helps us understand your ongoing thoughts and suggestions.
- **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have received email confirmation on diversity and inclusion initiatives.
	+ This question helps us understand your experience with email communication.
- **Follow Up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to receive follow-up calls on diversity and inclusion initiatives.
	+ This information enables us to contact you for further discussion or clarification.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
- Be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the diversity and inclusion team.
- Your feedback is valuable and helps shape the organization's diversity and inclusion initiatives.
