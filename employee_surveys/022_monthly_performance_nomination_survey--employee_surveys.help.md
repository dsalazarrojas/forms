# Monthly Performance Nomination Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about nominations for employee performance and review cycles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the nomination type from the options provided on page 1.
2. Choose the nominee from the list of options on page 2.
3. Select the nomination reason and provide any additional details on page 3.
4. Fill in the review information and nomination frequencies on pages 4-9.
5. Provide any additional review information on pages 10-11.
6. Indicate if the manager has been nominated on page 12.
7. Check if the team lead has been nominated on page 13.
8. Provide a peer review on page 14.
9. Select the nomination frequencies for the additional frequencies on pages 15-21.
10. Fill in any additional review information on pages 22-23.
11. Submit the form when finished.
