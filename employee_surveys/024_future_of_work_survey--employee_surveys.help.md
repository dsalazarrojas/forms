# future-of-work-survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This is a future-of-work survey designed to gather insights on employee preferences, work arrangements, and collaboration tools. The survey is meant to understand the current state of employee satisfaction, communication channels, work-life balance, remote work capabilities, and employee engagement. It also assesses the use of collaboration platforms, work from home tools, and feedback received from employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions based on your experience and perception.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the one that best reflects your current situation.
3. If you have trouble determining the best option for a particular question, use your best judgment and provide a brief explanation in the "Feedback" field at the end of the survey.
4. Please answer all questions honestly, and if you need help, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work From Home Setups** (`1`, `Work From Home Setups`, `Note`, not required): This section is meant to understand your current work from home setup, including any equipment or tools you use.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`2`, `Employee Satisfaction`, `Number`, not required): This question aims to gauge your current level of satisfaction with your work environment. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Communication Channels** (`3`, `Communication Channels`, `Select One`, `Select One`, required): This question asks about your preferred communication method. Choose the channel you most frequently use for work-related communications.
* **Work Life Balance** (`4`, `Work Life Balance`, `Number`, not required): This question assesses your balance between work and personal life. Select the option that reflects your current state.
* **Collaboration Platforms** (`5`, `Collaboration Platforms`, `Select Multiple`, not required): This section is meant to understand your experience with collaboration platforms. Choose the ones you've used or currently use for work.
* **Remote Work Capabilities** (`6`, `Remote Work Capabilities`, `Select One`, not required): This question explores your ability to work remotely. Choose the option that best describes your situation.
* **Work From Home Frequency** (`7`, `Work From Home Frequency`, `Time`, not required): This field allows you to indicate how frequently you work from home. Be as specific as possible.
* **Employee Engagement** (`8`, `Employee Engagement`, `Select One`, not required): This question gauges your level of engagement at work. Choose the option that best reflects your feelings.
* **Remote Work Environment** (`9`, `Remote Work Environment`, `Text`, not required): This field is for you to describe the work environment you've experienced while working remotely.
* **Work From Home Tools** (`10`, `Work From Home Tools`, `Select Multiple`, not required): This section is meant to understand the tools you use for remote work. Choose the ones you've used or currently use.
* **Employee Satisfaction 2** (`11`, `Employee Satisfaction 2`, `Select One`, not required): This question is similar to the first one, but more specific to your current situation.
* **Collaboration Platforms 2** (`12`, `Collaboration Platforms 2`, `Select Multiple`, not required): This section is a repetition of the previous one, to understand your experience with collaboration platforms.
* **Work From Home Tools 2** (`13`, `Work From Home Tools 2`, `Select Multiple`, not required): Similar to the previous one, this section is meant to understand the tools you use for remote work.
* **Employee Engagement 2** (`14`, `Employee Engagement 2`, `Select One`, not required): This question assesses your level of engagement at work, similar to the previous one.
* **Work From Home Frequency 2** (`15`, `Work From Home Frequency 2`, `Time`, not required): This field is a repetition of the one before, allowing you to indicate your work from home frequency.
* **Work From Home Setups 2** (`16`, `Work From Home Setups 2`, `Text`, not required): This field is meant to understand any additional setup you have for remote work.
* **Feedback** (`17`, `Feedback`, `Select Multiple`, not required): This is the last section, where you can provide any additional feedback or thoughts about your experience with remote work.

## Tips
- Take your time to answer each question carefully, as they might have a significant impact on the results.
- If you're unsure about any question, use your best judgment and explain your reasoning in the "Feedback" field.
- Remember, this survey is meant to help us understand your experiences and preferences, not to judge or evaluate you.
- Your responses will remain confidential and anonymous.
