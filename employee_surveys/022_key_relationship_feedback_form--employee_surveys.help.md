> The purpose of the "Key Relationship Feedback Form" is to gather feedback on the key relationships within an organization, specifically on whether an employee has a relationship manager and if they are performing well in their role. This form is intended to be completed by employees to provide an honest and constructive evaluation of their relationships with others in the company. Before completing this form, please note that it is intended for internal use only and will not be shared externally. If you are not sure if you should complete this form, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Please complete the form in a thorough and honest manner**. Take the time to carefully consider each question and provide detailed responses.
2. **Choose the correct options for each field**. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that have only two options, and choose a number between 1-5 or 6-20 for rating questions.
3. **Save your progress and review before submitting**. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Performance Evaluation Form (ID: 1)**: This field is intended for you to describe your overall performance and any notable achievements or areas for improvement.
* **Relationship Manager (ID: 2)**: Select "Yes" if you have a designated relationship manager, and "No" otherwise.
* **Employee Name (ID: 3)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official company documents.
* **Employee Title (ID: 4)**: Select all applicable titles that describe your job function. For example, if you are a Marketing Manager, select "Marketing Manager".
* **Feedback Score 1-5 (ID: 5)**: Rate your relationship manager's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Feedback Score 6-10 (ID: 6)**: Rate your relationship manager's performance on a scale of 6-10, where 6 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Feedback Score 11-15 (ID: 7)**: Rate your relationship manager's performance on a scale of 11-15, where 11 is the lowest and 15 is the highest.
* **Feedback Score 16-20 (ID: 8)**: Rate your relationship manager's performance on a scale of 16-20, where 16 is the lowest and 20 is the highest.
* **Feedback Score 21-25 (ID: 9)**: Rate your relationship manager's performance on a scale of 21-25, where 21 is the lowest and 25 is the highest.

### Tips

* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* If you are unsure about any question, reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.

Please note that the fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the form. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
