# Safety Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Perception Survey is designed to collect your opinions on how safe you feel in different situations, such as walking alone at night or on campus. Your input will help us better understand your perceptions and identify areas where we can improve safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly, using the provided response options.
2. Choose one response option per question.
3. Provide any additional comments or thoughts you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How safe do you feel when walking alone at night?** (`safety_perception`, select_one): Select one of the five options: Very safe, Somewhat safe, Not very safe, Not at all safe, or Do not answer.
* **Do you feel safe when walking alone on campus?** (`safety_perception_2`, select_one): Choose one of the five options: Strongly agree, Somewhat agree, Neutral, Somewhat disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **How safe do you feel when you're walking on a parking lot or garage?** (`safety_perception_3`, select_one): Select one of the four options: Very safe, Somewhat safe, Not very safe, or Not at all safe.
* **Do you feel safe when you are walking in a poorly lit area?** (`safety_perception_4`, select_one): Choose one of the four options: Very safe, Somewhat safe, Not very safe, or Not at all safe.
* **Do you feel safe when you are alone in a parking garage?** (`safety_perception_5`, select_one): Select one of the five options: Strongly agree, Somewhat agree, Neutral, Somewhat disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **How would you rate your sense of safety on a scale of 1 to 5?** (`safety_score`, select_one): Rate your sense of safety on a scale of 1 (Strongly disagree) to 5 (Very safe).
* **Would you rate your sense of safety as 1-5 when walking in a poorly lit area?** (`safety_score_2`, select_multiple): Choose one or multiple options that apply to you: Very safe, Somewhat safe, Not very safe, or Not at all safe.
* **How would you rate your sense of safety when walking alone in a parking garage?** (`safety_score_3`, select_multiple): Rate your sense of safety on a scale of 1-5: Strongly agree, Somewhat agree, Neutral, Somewhat disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **Are there any areas around the parking garage that are considered unsafe?** (`safety_perception_6`, select_multiple): Choose yes or no.
* **Any additional comments?** (`comments`, text): Share any thoughts or comments you may have about your safety experiences.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and accurately to help us better understand your perceptions.
* If you need help or have questions, feel free to ask.
* You can skip any question that doesn't apply to you.
* Remember, your responses will help us improve safety on campus or in areas where you feel vulnerable.
