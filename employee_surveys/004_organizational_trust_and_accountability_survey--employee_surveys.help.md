# Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their perceptions of the organizational culture, their relationship with their supervisor or manager, and their views on transparency and accountability within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and in as much detail as possible.
2. Please provide your email address so that we can get back to you with your results and any follow-up questions.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about the company culture?** (`question_1`): This question is about your overall feelings towards the company culture. Please describe your thoughts and experiences with the company culture.
* **How do you feel about your supervisor or manager?** (`question_2`): This question is about your relationship with your supervisor or manager. Please describe your experiences and feelings with your supervisor or manager.
* **How do you feel about transparency and honesty in decision-making processes?** (`question_3`): This question is about the level of transparency and honesty you experience in decision-making processes within the organization.
* **How do you feel about accountability within the organization?** (`question_4`): This question is about how accountable you feel the organization is in terms of its actions and decisions.
* **What do you think is the most important factor when it comes to trust?** (`question_5`): This question is about what you think is most important for building trust within the organization.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_section`): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Email** (`email`): Please enter your email address so that we can get back to you with your results and any follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This field is not required, but please enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you.
* **Date** (`date`): Please enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-dd`.
* **Time** (`time`): This field is not required, but please enter the time if you would like.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`), **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`), **Manager Name** (`manager_name`), **Manager Title** (`manager_title`): These fields are not required, but please enter your manager's contact information if you would like us to contact them.
* **This is question 1** (`question_1_1`), **This is question 2** (`question_2_1`), **This is question 3** (`question_3_1`): These questions are duplicates of the previous ones, and you can skip them.

Note that some fields are not required, but please answer as many questions as possible to provide us with valuable feedback.
