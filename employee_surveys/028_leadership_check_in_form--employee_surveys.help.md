# leadership_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from team members about their leaders. The information collected will help leaders develop and improve their skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the team member being evaluated.
2. Enter the email of the team member being evaluated.
3. Enter the name of the leader being evaluated.
4. Enter the department of the leader being evaluated.
5. Enter the date the evaluation was submitted.
6. Provide ratings and feedback for each of the 5 evaluation criteria.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team member being evaluated.
* **Team Member Email** (`team_member_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the team member being evaluated.
* **Leader Name** (`leader_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the leader being evaluated.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department of the leader being evaluated.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date the evaluation was submitted.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, number, required): Rate the leader's performance on the first evaluation criteria (1-5 scale).
* **Feedback 1** (`feedback_1`, text, required): Provide feedback on the leader's performance on the first evaluation criteria.
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, number, required): Rate the leader's performance on the second evaluation criteria (1-5 scale).
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, text, required): Provide feedback on the leader's performance on the second evaluation criteria.
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, number, required): Rate the leader's performance on the third evaluation criteria (1-5 scale).
* **Feedback 3** (`feedback_3`, text, required): Provide feedback on the leader's performance on the third evaluation criteria.
* **Rating 4** (`rating_4`, number, required): Rate the leader's performance on the fourth evaluation criteria (1-5 scale).
* **Feedback 4** (`feedback_4`, text, required): Provide feedback on the leader's performance on the fourth evaluation criteria.
* **Rating 5** (`rating_5`, number, required): Rate the leader's performance on the fifth evaluation criteria (1-5 scale).
* **Feedback 5** (`feedback_5`, text, required): Provide feedback on the leader's performance on the fifth evaluation criteria.
