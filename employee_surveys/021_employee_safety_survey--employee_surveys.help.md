# Employee Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Safety Survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from employees regarding the company's safety practices and culture. This form helps the company understand employee concerns and perceptions, and gather suggestions for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:
1. Review each page and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Be honest and thorough in your answers.
3. You can select multiple options for questions that allow it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Safety Concerns** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all concerns that apply from the list.
* **Which of the following concerns do you have?** (Select One, Optional): Choose one concern from the list.
* **How do you rate safety practices?** (Select Multiple, Optional): Rate safety practices as Very good, Average, or Poor.
* **Describe your perceptions of our company safety culture** (Text, Required): Share your honest thoughts about the company safety culture.
* **Any additional comments** (Text, Optional): Share any other thoughts or suggestions you may have.
* **How often do you think this survey should be done in the future?** (Select One, Optional): Choose how often you think the survey should be done.
* **Would you like to provide any additional feedback on your experience with this survey?** (Select One, Optional): Answer yes or no.
* **How satisfied are you with this survey?** (Select One, Optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the survey.
* **Would you like to suggest any changes to improve this survey?** (Select One, Optional): Answer yes or no.

Note: Some pages in this form are not actually questions, but rather informational sections. These will not require answers.
