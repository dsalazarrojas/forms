# Online Collaboration Fatigue Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about your experiences and feelings related to online collaboration and virtual meetings. Your responses will help us identify areas for improvement and develop strategies to increase productivity and reduce fatigue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully, considering your personal experiences and feelings.
2. Respond with the corresponding options for each question:
	* Select a single option from the dropdown menu for questions with multiple options.
	* Enter a numerical value for questions asking for a specific number.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
