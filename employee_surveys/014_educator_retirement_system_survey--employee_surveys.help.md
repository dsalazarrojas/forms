# Educator Retirement System Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from educators about the overall experience of the current educator retirement system. We would like to understand how you feel about the system, what benefits you think are missing, and how the system could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoughtfully.
2. Rate or select your level of agreement with each question based on your personal experience.
3. Provide specific examples or details whenever possible.
4. If you have any other comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your overall experience with the current educator retirement system?** (`retirement_system`, select_one): This question asks for your overall impression of the retirement system. Please select one of the five options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **What benefits do you feel are missing from the current retirement system?** (`retirement_benefits`, select_multiple): This question asks about the benefits that you feel are missing from the current system. Please select all that apply from the options: Retirement benefits (e.g., medical, dental), Financial support (e.g., loans, grants), or Other benefits (please specify).
* **How would you rate the communication from the university regarding retirement benefits?** (`communication`, select_one): This question asks for your rating of the communication from the university regarding retirement benefits. Please select one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Do you feel that the current retirement system is fair and equitable?** (`fairness`, checkbox): This question asks whether you feel that the current system is fair and equitable. Please select one of the two options: Option A or Option B.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the retirement system?** (`suggestions`, text): This question is for any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving the retirement system. Please provide specific details about your suggestions.
* **What benefits do you feel are essential for educators to have in the current retirement system?** (`essential_benefits`, select_multiple): This question asks about the benefits that you feel are essential for educators. Please select all that apply from the options: Retirement benefits (e.g., medical, dental), Financial support (e.g., loans, grants), or Other benefits (please specify).
* **How would you rate the timeliness of communication regarding retirement benefits?** (`timeliness`, select_one): This question asks for your rating of the timeliness of communication regarding retirement benefits. Please select one of the four options: Very Timely, Somewhat Timely, Not Very Timely, or Never.
* **Do you feel that the current retirement system is easy to navigate?** (`navigability`, checkbox): This question asks whether you feel that the current system is easy to navigate. Please select one of the two options: Option A or Option B.
* **How would you rate the clarity of information regarding retirement benefits?** (`clarity`, select_one): This question asks for your rating of the clarity of information regarding retirement benefits. Please select one of the four options: Very Clear, Somewhat Clear, Not Very Clear, or Not Clear At All.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions about the retirement system?** (`comments`, text): This question is for any additional comments or suggestions you have. Please provide specific details about your comments.
* **What would make you feel confident that the current retirement system is fair?** (`confidence`, text): This question is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about what would make you feel confident that the current system is fair. Please provide specific details about your comments.
* **Are there any other comments or suggestions you'd like to make?** (`comments2`, text): This question is for any additional comments or suggestions you have. Please provide specific details about your comments.
