# staff_longevity_and_attrition_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on employee satisfaction, retention, and attrition to help the organization improve its workplace policies and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete each page thoroughly, answering all required questions.
2.  Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they will contribute to improving the work environment.
3.  If a page has multiple questions, answer each one before moving on to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **How Long Employees Stay (page 1)** (`how_long_employees_stay`, `number`, **optional**):
    Enter the number of years an employee has been with the company.
*  **Job Satisfaction (page 2)** (`job_satisfaction`, `select_one`, **optional**):
    Select your level of satisfaction with your current job.
    *   -   **Satisfied**
        *   -   A measure of how satisfied you are with your job.
*  **Reasons for Employees Leaving (page 2)** (`turnover_reasons`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):
    Check the box(es) that apply to why you're thinking about leaving or have left the company.
    *   -   **Yes**
        *   -   You are considering or have considered leaving.
        *   -   You are not considering leaving.
*  **Manager Satisfaction (page 4)** (`manager_satisfaction`, `number`, **required**):
    Enter a value representing your level of satisfaction with your manager.
*  **Department Satisfaction (page 4)** (`department_satisfaction`, `number`, **required**):
    Enter a value representing your level of satisfaction with your department.
*  **Work-Life Balance (page 5)** (`work_life_balance`, `number`, **required**):
    Enter a value representing your level of satisfaction with your work-life balance.
*  **Company Culture (page 6)** (`company_culture`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):
    Check the box(es) that apply to your level of satisfaction with the company culture.
    *   -   **Yes**
        *   -   You are satisfied with the company culture.
        *   -   You are not satisfied with the company culture.
*  **Communication (page 6)** (`communication`, `select_one`, **optional**):
    Select your level of satisfaction with communication within the company.
    *   -   **Yes**
        *   -   You are satisfied with communication.
        *   -   You are not satisfied with communication.
*  **Team Support (page 6)** (`team_support`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):
    Check the box(es) that apply to your level of satisfaction with your team support.
    *   -   **Yes**
        *   -   You are satisfied with team support.
        *   -   You are not satisfied with team support.
*  **Benefits (page 7)** (`benefits`, `select_one`, **optional**):
    Select your level of satisfaction with company benefits.
    *   -   **Yes**
        *   -   You are satisfied with benefits.
        *   -   You are not satisfied with benefits.
*  **Feedback (page 7)** (`feedback`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):
    Check the box(es) that apply to your level of satisfaction with feedback opportunities within the company.
    *   -   **Yes**
        *   -   You are satisfied with feedback opportunities.
        *   -   You are not satisfied with feedback opportunities.
*  **Manager Satisfaction 2 (page 7)** (`manager_satisfaction_2`, `number`, **required**):
    Enter a value representing your level of satisfaction with your manager 2.
## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Answer all required questions thoroughly.
- If a field is not required, answer it only if you feel it's relevant to your experience.
