# Organizational Behavior Dynamics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the organizational behavior dynamics in the workplace, focusing on company culture, leadership effectiveness, communication, collaboration, and overall satisfaction with various aspects of the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the options that best describe your perceptions and experiences in the workplace. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose only one or multiple options, respectively. For number fields (e.g., collaboration_frequency_with_manager), enter a numerical value between 1 and 10, where 1 represents low and 10 represents high.

## Tips
*   Please answer all questions honestly and sincerely.
*   Select only one option for select_one fields or multiple options for select_multiple fields that best describe your perception or experience.
*   For number fields, enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.
*   Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
*   If you're unsure or have no opinion, leave the field blank or select "Don't know."
*   This form is intended to gather feedback from employees and is not a performance evaluation.
