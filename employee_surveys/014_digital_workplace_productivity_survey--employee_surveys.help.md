# Digital Workplace Productivity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand how employees use digital tools and technologies in their daily work, and how they perceive their work environment. The information gathered will help us identify areas for improvement and inform our digital transformation strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
2. You can select one or multiple options for select fields.
3. If you are unsure about any question, you can select "Not Applicable" or "Don't know".
4. Please complete all questions on each page before moving on to the next.
5. If you need help or have questions, please ask your manager or HR representative.
