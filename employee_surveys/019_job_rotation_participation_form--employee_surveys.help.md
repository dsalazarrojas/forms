# Job Rotation Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from employees about their interest in participating in a job rotation program. The program allows employees to take on different roles and responsibilities within the company, promoting growth, development, and diversity in work experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out this form completely and accurately.**
2. **Select the correct option for each field.**
3. **Submit the form once you have finished filling out all the fields.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Surveys** (`employee_surveys_form`, Select Multiple, **Required**: No)
    * This field allows you to choose multiple options for employee surveys that will be used to gauge employee interest in the job rotation program.
* **Job Rotation Form** (`job_rotation_form`, Select One, **Required**: No)
    * This field asks whether you are interested in participating in the job rotation program. You can choose either "Yes" or "No" depending on your preference.
* **Job Rotation Program** (`job_rotation_program`, Text, **Required**: No)
    * This field is for any additional comments or information about the job rotation program that you would like to share.
* **Job Rotation Participation Rules** (`job_rotation_participation_rules`, Text, **Required**: No)
    * In this field, please describe any specific rules or conditions that you have about participating in the job rotation program.
* **Job Rotation Participation Benefits** (`job_rotation_participation_benefits`, Text, **Required**: No)
    * Describe any benefits you expect to gain from participating in the job rotation program.

## Tips

* **Answer honestly and accurately.**
* **If you need help or clarification, please don't hesitate to contact your supervisor or HR representative.**
* **Please submit the form once you have finished filling out all the fields.**
