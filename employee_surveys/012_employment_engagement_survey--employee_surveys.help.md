# Employment Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Employment Engagement Survey is a tool used to gauge the overall happiness and engagement of employees within the organization. This survey helps to gather feedback on various aspects of work, including overall happiness, satisfaction level, work perception, employee survey, and work-life balance. The goal is to collect accurate and honest feedback from employees to improve their work experience and overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of employment engagement.
2. Answer each question honestly, taking into account your personal experiences and feelings about your job.
3. For the "Employee Survey" section, select all the options that apply to you.
4. You can answer all questions or only those that are relevant to you.
5. Submit your completed form to provide valuable feedback to the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Happiness**: This question asks about your overall happiness with your job. Please rate your happiness on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Satisfaction Level**: This question assesses your level of satisfaction with your job. Rate your satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Work Perception**: This question explores your perception of your work environment. Rate your perception on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Employee Survey**: This question allows you to express your satisfaction with various aspects of your job. Select all the options that apply to you, including Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, and Very dissatisfied.
* **Work-Life Balance**: This question asks about your balance between work and personal life. Provide a written response, sharing your thoughts and experiences.

## Tips

* Be honest and truthful in your responses.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you're unsure about any question, skip it or provide a brief explanation.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help improve the organization's work environment.
