# supervisor_readiness_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The supervisor_readiness_assessment form is used to evaluate the readiness and skills of supervisors in leading their teams. The assessment aims to provide an objective evaluation of a supervisor's strengths and areas of improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by reviewing each question carefully and providing your honest opinion about your supervisor's skills and readiness.
- Answer each question with either 'Yes' or 'No' or 'text' as required.
- You can provide additional comments in the 'next_steps' field if you have any suggestions or ideas on how to improve the supervisor's performance.
- If you are unsure about any question, you can always consult with your supervisor or your manager for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **confidence_in_leadership** (Number)
  A measure of how much confidence you have in your supervisor's leadership abilities.
* **development_planning** (Select Multiple)
  Check the areas where you would like to see more development planning from your supervisor.
* **performance_reviews** (Text)
  Briefly describe your thoughts about your supervisor's performance reviews.
* **strengths_identification** (Select One)
  Identify one area where you think your supervisor excels.
* **development_needs** (Text)
  Specify any areas that you believe your supervisor needs further development in.
* **next_steps** (Select Multiple)
  Check the actions you would like to see your supervisor take to improve their performance.
* **communication_skills** (Select Multiple)
  Evaluate your supervisor's communication skills, such as listening, verbal, or written communication.
* **leadership_skills** (Select Multiple)
  Assess your supervisor's leadership skills, such as conflict resolution, time management, or decision making.
* **problem_solving** (Select Multiple)
  Rate your supervisor's problem-solving skills, such as analyzing data, resolving conflicts, or critical thinking.
* **next_steps** (Text)
  Add any additional suggestions or comments on how your supervisor can improve their performance.

## Tips

- Be honest and provide your best judgment when answering the questions.
- Remember that the purpose of this form is to help your supervisor grow and improve, not to evaluate or criticize them.
- If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to discuss them with your supervisor or manager.
