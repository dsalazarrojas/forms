# Retail Employee Burnout Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to document and track employee burnout leave of absence (LOA) in retail stores. A burnout LOA is an employee's formal request for leave of absence due to burnout, which is typically caused by prolonged exposure to stress, excessive workload, or other factors. The form is completed by the employee and reviewed/ approved by the store manager.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the store location in the "Store Location" field.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the burnout leave of absence in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
3. Select a reason for the leave of absence in the "Reason" field.
4. Enter the number of hours requested in the "Hours Requested" field.
5. Select the current status of the employee as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Status" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Manager Note", "Employee Note", and "People Team Note" fields.
7. Select the review status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Review Status" field.
8. Enter the review date in the "Review Date" field.
9. Enter the store manager's note in the "Store Manager Note" field.
10. Enter the people team's note in the "People Team Note" field.
11. Enter the manager's signature in the "Manager Signature" field.
12. Enter the people team signature in the "People Team Signature" field.
13. Enter the submitted by field, which is typically the employee's initials.
14. Enter the submitted to field, which is typically the store manager's initials.
15. Enter the approved by field, which is typically the store manager's initials.
16. Enter the approved date in the "Approved Date" field.
17. Enter the store ID in the "Store ID" field.
18. Enter the employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
19. Enter the store manager ID in the "Store Manager ID" field.
20. Enter the people team ID in the "People Team ID" field.
21. Enter the store location 2 in the "Store Location 2" field.
22. Enter the reason 2 in the "Reason 2" field.
23. Enter any additional comments from the employee in the "Employee Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, required): Enter the retail store location where you work.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your burnout leave of absence.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your burnout leave of absence.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Select a reason for your burnout leave of absence from the available options.
* **Hours Requested** (`hours_requested`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you are requesting for your burnout leave of absence.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the employee as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the store manager.
* **Employee Note** (`employee_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the employee.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): Select the review status as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the review date.
* **Store Manager Note** (`store_manager_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the store manager.
* **People Team Note** (`people_team_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the people team.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): Enter the store manager's signature.
* **People Team Signature** (`people_team_signature`, text, required): Enter the people team's signature.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your initials.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, required): Enter the store manager's initials.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, required): Enter the store manager's initials.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, required): Enter the approved date.
* **Store ID** (`store_id`, text, required): Enter the store ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID.
* **Store Manager ID** (`store_manager_id`, text, required): Enter the store manager's ID.
* **People Team ID** (`people_team_id`, text, required): Enter the people team ID.
* **Store Location 2** (`store_location_2`, text, required): Enter the store location 2.
* **Reason 2** (`reason_2`, text, required): Enter the reason 2.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments from the employee.
