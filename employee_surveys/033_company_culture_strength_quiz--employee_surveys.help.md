# Company Culture Strength Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Culture Strength Quiz is designed to help assess the strength of a company culture from the perspective of its employees. It's a self-assessment tool that gauges how well a company's communication, leadership, and values are aligned. By answering a few simple questions, you can gain insights into your company's culture and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the role that best describes your position within the company from the options provided in the "Role in Company" field.
2. Select the response that best describes your experience with communication across teams in the "Communication Effectiveness" field.
3. Rate your level of trust in the leadership team in the "Trust in Leadership" field.
4. Indicate how often teams collaborate effectively in the "Collaboration Frequency" field.
5. Choose the response that best describes how well your actions align with the company's stated values in the "Alignment with Company Values" field.
6. (Optional) Provide any suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Role in Company** (`respondent_role`, `text`, required/optional): Select the role that best describes your position within the company. This could be "Employee", "Manager", "Executive", or "Contractor". This field helps us understand your perspective on the company culture.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required/true): This question assesses how well communication functions across teams. Choose from the options: "Very effective", "Effective", "Neutral", "Ineffective", or "Very ineffective".
* **Trust in Leadership** (`leadership_trust`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate your level of trust in the leadership team. Choose from the options: "Very high", "High", "Neutral", "Low", or "Very low".
* **Collaboration Frequency** (`collaboration_frequency`, `select_one`, optional/true): Indicate how often teams collaborate effectively. Select from the options: "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", or "Rarely".
* **Alignment with Company Values** (`values_alignment`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose how well your actions align with the company's stated values. Select from the options: "Strongly aligned", "Aligned", "Neutral", "Misaligned".
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, optional/true): Optional field to provide any ideas you have to strengthen the company culture.

## Tips
- Be honest with your responses, as your input will help us gain a more accurate picture of the company culture.
- If you're unsure about any question, choose the response that best fits your experience.
