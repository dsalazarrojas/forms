# Employee Leave Management Software Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on employee satisfaction and experience with the company's leave management software.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer each question based on your personal experience with the software. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best reflects your opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Satisfaction with Leave Management Software**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the leave management software on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not satisfied and 5 is satisfied.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Leave Management Software**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the leave management software on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not satisfied and 5 is satisfied.
* **Leave Management Software Adoption**: Select how satisfied you are with the leave management software, from highly satisfied to not at all satisfied.
* **Improvement Priorities**: Select all areas where you would like to see improvements, such as user interface, user experience, system improvement, data integration, or other.
* **Leave Request Process**: Select how you would describe the leave request process, from simple and straightforward to complex and time consuming.
* **Leave Request Process Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the leave request process, from satisfied to not satisfied.
* **Leave Request Form Clarity**: Rate how clear the leave request form is to you, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not clear and 5 is clear.
* **Leave Request Process Clarity**: Rate how clear the leave request process is to you, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not clear and 5 is clear.
* **Overall Experience with Leave Management Software**: Rate your overall experience with the leave management software, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not good and 5 is good.
* **User Experience with Leave Request Form**: Rate your experience with the leave request form, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not good and 5 is good.
* **Employee Satisfaction with Leave Management Software**: Select how you feel about the leave management software, from satisfied, neutral, or not satisfied.
* **Leave Request Process Satisfaction Score**: Rate your satisfaction with the leave request process, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not satisfied and 5 is satisfied.
* **Overall Satisfaction Score**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the leave management software, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not satisfied and 5 is satisfied.
* **Improvement Priorities 2**: Select all areas where you would like to see improvements, such as user interface, user experience, system improvement, data integration, or other.
* **Leave Request Process Difficulty**: Select how difficult you find the leave request process, from easy, moderately difficult, difficult, or not difficult.
* **Leave Request Process Difficulty Score**: Rate how difficult you find the leave request process, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not difficult and 5 is difficult.
* **Leave Request Form Accuracy**: Select how accurate you think the leave request form is, from accurate, not accurate, or not relevant.
* **Leave Request Form Accuracy Score**: Rate how accurate you think the leave request form is, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not accurate and 5 is accurate.
* **Overall Form Accuracy**: Select how accurate you think the overall leave request form is, from accurate, not accurate, or not relevant.
