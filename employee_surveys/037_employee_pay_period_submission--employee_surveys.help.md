## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employee work hours and pay periods. It is meant to be submitted by employees to report their regular and overtime hours worked during a pay period, as well as other relevant details such as department, manager, and submitter information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Ensure that you have the correct pay period and fiscal period selected.
3. Fill in the submitter and manager fields with the relevant information.
4. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Regular Hours** (`1`, `number`, required: false): Enter your regular hours worked during the pay period.
* **Worked Hours** (`2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total hours you worked during the pay period, including regular and overtime hours.
* **Pay Period Start Date** (`3`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the pay period.
* **Pay Period End Date** (`4`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the pay period.
* **Regular and Overtime Hours** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Enter your regular and overtime hours worked during the pay period.
* **Worked Overtime Hours** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total overtime hours worked during the pay period.
* **Total Hours** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total hours worked during the pay period.
* **Submitter** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the person submitting the form. Options: None, Other.
* **Department** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the department you belong to. Options: None, Other.
* **Manager** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your manager. Options: None, Other.
* **Employee ID** (`11`, `number`, required: false): Enter your employee ID.
* **Employee Name** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Department Name** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department name.
* **Manager Name** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Enter your manager's name.
* **Pay Cycle** (`16`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the pay cycle. Options: None, Other.
* **Pay Period** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Enter the pay period.
* **Fiscal Period** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Enter the fiscal period.
* **Pay Rate** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Enter your pay rate.
* **Regular and Overtime Rate** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Enter your regular and overtime rate.
* **Worked Overtime Rate** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Enter the worked overtime rate.
* **Total Hours Rate** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Enter the total hours rate.
* **Submitted** (`23`, `note`, required: false): This field is for internal notes about the submission.
* **Submission Date** (`24`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter Name** (`25`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

Note: Some fields are optional, so only fill in the information that is required for your specific situation.
