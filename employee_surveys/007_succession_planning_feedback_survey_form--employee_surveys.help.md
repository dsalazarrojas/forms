# Succession Planning Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Succession Planning Feedback Survey Form is a tool used to gather opinions and feedback from employees on the effectiveness of succession planning and talent management strategies within the organization. The feedback provided will help management identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the succession planning process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2. Select one or multiple options for each question, depending on your level of agreement or rating.
3. Fill out the form at your own pace, and save your progress as you go.
4. When finished, review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Succession Planning Effectiveness** (`succession_planning_effectiveness`, Select Multiple, Optional): Answer whether you think the current succession planning process is effective or not.
  * Select one or both of the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Talent Management Strategies** (`talent_management_strategies`, Text, Optional): Please provide any insights or feedback on the talent management strategies within the organization.
* **Succession Planning Effectiveness Rating** (`succession_planning_effectiveness_rating`, Number, Optional): Rate the effectiveness of the succession planning process on a scale of 1-5.
* **Talent Management Strategies Rating** (`talent_management_strategies_rating`, Number, Optional): Rate the effectiveness of the talent management strategies within the organization on a scale of 1-5.
* **Employee Insights** (`employee_insights`, Text, Optional): Share any personal experiences or observations related to the succession planning process.
* **Organizational Improvement** (`organizational_improvement`, Text, Optional): Suggest any potential improvements or changes you would like to see in the succession planning process.
* **Succession Planning Process** (`succession_planning_process`, Text, Optional): Describe your understanding of the current succession planning process.
* **Succession Planning Effectiveness Roadmap** (`succession_planning_effectiveness_roadmap`, Select One, Optional): Answer whether the succession planning effectiveness roadmap is active or not.
  * Select one of the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Talent Management Strategies Roadmap** (`talent_management_strategies_roadmap`, Select One, Optional): Rate the effectiveness of the talent management strategies roadmap using the options 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **Succession Planning Effectiveness Roadmap Implementation** (`succession_planning_effectiveness_roadmap_implementation`, Text, Optional): Describe the progress of the succession planning effectiveness roadmap implementation.
* **Talent Management Strategies Roadmap Implementation** (`talent_management_strategies_roadmap_implementation`, Text, Optional): Describe the progress of the talent management strategies roadmap implementation.
* **Succession Planning Process Rating** (`succession_planning_process_rating`, Number, Optional): Rate the effectiveness of the succession planning process on a scale of 1-5.
* **Talent Management Strategies Rating** (`talent_management_strategies_rating`, Number, Optional): Rate the effectiveness of the talent management strategies within the organization on a scale of 1-5.
* **Succession Planning Effectiveness Roadmap Status** (`succession_planning_effectiveness_roadmap_status`, Select One, Optional): Answer whether the succession planning effectiveness roadmap is active or inactive.
  * Select one of the options 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Talent Management Strategies Roadmap Status** (`talent_management_strategies_roadmap_status`, Select One, Optional): Rate the effectiveness of the talent management strategies roadmap using the options 'Good' or 'Fair'.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your input.
