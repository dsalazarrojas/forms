# Affirmation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees about their experience with affirmations or recognition in the workplace. Your input will help us understand how often you receive positive feedback, what impact it has on your performance, and what we can do to improve our recognition practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Fill out your personal information, including your name and email address. Please use your company email address to ensure we can follow up with you if needed.
*   Select your department or team from the options provided.
*   Choose how often you receive affirmations or recognition (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never).
*   If you've received a recent affirmation or recognition, describe it in the box provided. This will help us understand your experience.
*   Rate your satisfaction with the quality of feedback you receive (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
*   Provide any suggestions you have for improving recognition practices (e.g. what we can do better, what we're doing well).
*   Let us know if you would like a follow-up about your feedback (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **-- Personal Information --**
    *   **Employee name** (`Employee name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your company ID or benefits documents.
*   **Employee email** (`Employee email`, `email`, required): Enter your company email address to ensure we can follow up with you if needed.
*   **Department** (`Department`, `select_one`, required): Choose your department or team from the options provided (Sales, Engineering, Marketing, HR, Operations, Other).
*   **How often do you receive affirmations or recognition** (`How often do you receive affirmations or recognition`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you receive positive feedback (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, Never).
*   **Recent affirmation example** (`Recent affirmation example`, `text`, optional): If you've received a recent affirmation or recognition, describe it briefly in this box. This will help us understand your experience.
*   **Impact of affirmation on performance** (`Impact of affirmation on performance`, `text`, optional): Describe how recognition has affected your motivation or performance.
*   **Satisfaction with feedback** (`Satisfaction with feedback quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the feedback you receive (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
*   **Suggestions for improvement** (`Suggestions for improving recognition practices`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions you have for improving recognition practices. This is an opportunity to give us feedback on what we're doing well and what we can improve.
*   **Would you like a follow up from HR** (`Would you like a follow up from HR`, `select_one`, required): Choose yes if you would like a follow up about your feedback.
