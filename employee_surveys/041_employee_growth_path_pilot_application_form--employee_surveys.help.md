# Employee Growth Path Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Growth Path Pilot Application Form is designed to help employees express their interest in career development opportunities within their organization. This form collects relevant information from employees, including their background, motivation, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields below and ensure you have the required information ready.
2.  Enter your Key Role (a brief description of your desired role or position).
3.  Enter your current tenure in the organization (number of months or years).
4.  Write a brief Motivation Statement, explaining why you are interested in this opportunity.
5.  Enter your Name for contact purposes.
6.  Enter your valid Email address for communication.
7.  Enter your Phone Number for quick contact.
8.  If you are available to start immediately, select the available Date. If not, enter the date you are available to start.
9.  Finally, enter your Preferred Availability, which may include specific days and hours you are available to work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Key Role** (`Key Role`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your desired role or position.
*   **Current Tenure** (`Current Tenure`, text, required: false): Enter the number of months or years you have been with the organization.
*   **Motivation Statement** (`Motivation Statement`, text, required: false): Write a brief explanation of why you are interested in this opportunity.
*   **Name** (`Name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for contact purposes.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for communication.
*   **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for quick contact.
*   **Available Date** (`Available Date`, date, required: false): If you are available to start immediately, select the current date. If not, enter the date you are available to start.
*   **Preferred Availability** (`Preferred Availability`, text, required: false): Enter specific days and hours you are available to work.

## Tips
*   Ensure the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Be as specific as possible when describing your desired role or position.
*   Use a valid email address and phone number for contact.
*   If you are not available to start immediately, select the date you are available to start to avoid delays in the hiring process.
