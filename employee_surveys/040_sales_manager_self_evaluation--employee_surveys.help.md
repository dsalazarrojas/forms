# Sales Manager Self Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Manager Self Evaluation form is designed to help sales managers reflect on their performance, goals, and areas of improvement. The form is used to assess the manager's leadership skills, areas of challenge and opportunity, and development plan. This self evaluation is essential for continuous professional growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you agree or disagree with the statements about your leadership score.
2. Enter a numerical score out of 10 for the given leadership scores, with 10 being excellent and 0 being low.
3. Choose the areas that you need improvement in from the dropdown menu, selecting only one option.
4. Enter your score out of 10 for areas of focus.
5. Enter your leadership development plan, including any relevant notes.
6. Choose the status of your leadership development plan from the dropdown menu.
7. Select the next review date and time for your leadership development plan.
8. Enter any additional notes for your leadership development plan.
9. Enter any additional notes for your leadership development plan.
