# IT Tech Resources Satisfaction Employment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees on their satisfaction with IT resources and support. Your input will help us understand areas for improvement and inform decisions on resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email (format: `username@companydomain.com`)
	* Phone Number (format: `xxxx-xxxx-xxxx`)
	* Manager Name (if applicable)
2. Answer the following questions:
	* How often do you encounter technical issues? (e.g., daily, weekly, occasionally)
	* How often do technical issues impact productivity? (e.g., significantly, occasionally, never)
	* How would you rate the level of IT support you receive? (e.g., Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied)
	* How often do you contact IT for support? (e.g., daily, weekly, occasionally, never)
	* How would you rate the level of IT funding for technology and training? (e.g., Very underfunded, Somewhat underfunded, Neutral, Somewhat overfunded, Very overfunded)
	* How often does your manager support your use of technology? (e.g., Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Often, Almost always)
3. Rate your satisfaction with IT resources and support:
	* How would you rate your satisfaction with IT resources? (e.g., Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied)
	* How would you rate your satisfaction with IT support? (e.g., Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, Very satisfied)
