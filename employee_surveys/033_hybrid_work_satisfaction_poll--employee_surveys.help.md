# Hybrid Work Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gauge employee satisfaction with various aspects of their work environment and experience. The Hybrid Work Satisfaction Poll is used to collect feedback from employees on their feelings about the work environment, job satisfaction, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the available options for select fields to choose your response.
4. Enter your response in the text fields for longer answers.
5. Submit your completed survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Experience (id: 1)**: Please enter a brief description of your work experience in the past year.
* **Hybrid Work Environment (id: 2)**: Choose how you typically work, from Office, Remote, or Hybrid (a combination of both).
* **Satisfaction Level (id: 3)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Very Satisfied and 5 is Very Dissatisfied.
* **Communication with Manager (id: 4)**: Choose how often you communicate with your manager, from Often to Never.
* **Team Collaboration (id: 5)**: Enter a brief description of your collaboration with your team members.
* **Work-life Balance (id: 6)**: Rate your balance between work and personal life on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Very Well Balanced and 5 is Not Well Balanced at All.
* **Job Satisfaction (id: 7)**: Choose how satisfied you are with your job, from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Company Culture (id: 8)**: Choose how you perceive the company culture, from Strong to Somewhat Negative.
* **Job Performance (id: 9)**: Enter a brief description of your job performance and achievements.
* **Work Safety (id: 10)**: Rate your perception of safety at work, from Very Safe to Not At All Safe.
* **Work Equipment (id: 11)**: Choose how you rate the quality of work equipment, from Very Good to Very Bad.
* **Work Space (id: 12)**: Rate how comfortable your work space is, from Very Comfortable to Very Uncomfortable.
* **Job Title (id: 13)**: Enter your job title.
* **Department (id: 14)**: Enter your department name.
* **Location (id: 15)**: Enter your work location.
* **Shift (id: 16)**: Enter your work shift or schedule.
* **Job Role (id: 17)**: Enter a brief description of your job role.
* **Manager (id: 18)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Job Classification (id: 19)**: Enter your job classification or category.
* **Work Status (id: 20)**: Choose how you are classified as an employee, from Regular to Temporary.
* **Job Type (id: 21)**: Enter your job type or category.
* **Job Function (id: 22)**: Enter a brief description of your job function or responsibilities.
* **Work Scheduling (id: 23)**: Enter your work scheduling details.
* **Work Location (id: 24)**: Enter your work location.
* **Employee Survey (id: 25)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your work experience.
