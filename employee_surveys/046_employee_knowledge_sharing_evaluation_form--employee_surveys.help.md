# employee_knowledge_sharing_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_knowledge_sharing_evaluation_form" is a survey used to evaluate the effectiveness of the knowledge sharing initiative. It assesses employees' participation and feedback on sharing knowledge within the organization. Your input will help us understand the impact of this initiative and make informed decisions for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the knowledge areas you are involved in or have experience with.
2. Rate the knowledge sharing initiative based on your experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the initiative.
4. Indicate how often you participate in knowledge sharing.
5. Enter your email address (optional, but helps us get back to you with any follow-up questions).
6. Enter your phone number (optional, but helps us reach you quickly if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Knowledge Areas** (`knowledge_areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the knowledge areas you are involved in or have experience with (e.g., project management, marketing, sales). This will help us understand your expertise and how knowledge sharing applies to your work.
- **Rate the Knowledge Sharing Initiative** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience with the knowledge sharing initiative on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is 'poor' and 10 is 'excellent'. This will give us your overall impression of the initiative's effectiveness.
- **Any Other Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Share any thoughts, ideas, or suggestions you have about the knowledge sharing initiative. This is your chance to provide more details about what worked well and what didn't.
- **How Often Do You Participate in Knowledge Sharing?** (`frequency_of_knowledge_sharing`, `select_one`, required: true): Indicate how frequently you participate in knowledge sharing activities, such as never, less than monthly, monthly, etc. This will help us understand your level of engagement.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you with any follow-up questions or to discuss your feedback.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to reach you quickly if needed.
