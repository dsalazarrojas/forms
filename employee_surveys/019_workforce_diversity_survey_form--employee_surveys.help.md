# Workforce Diversity Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about employee demographics, work status, and work arrangements to help us better understand and promote a diverse and inclusive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each field and provide the required information. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Demographics**: Please provide a brief description of your employee demographics.
* **Ethnicity**: Select the most accurate representation of your ethnic identity:
	+ African American or Black
	+ Asian or Asian American or Pacific Islander
	+ White
	+ Hispanic or Latino
	+ Native American or Alaskan Native
	+ Other
	+ Prefer not to answer
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Years of Service**: Enter the number of years you have been working with our organization.
* **Work Status**: Select your current work status:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Contract
	+ Other
* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
* **Shift**: Select the shift you work on:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Night
	+ Other
* **Work Arrangement**: Select the arrangement that best describes your work setup:
	+ Remote work
	+ On-site
	+ Hybrid
	+ Other
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Job Performance**: Select the level of job performance you believe you have achieved:
	+ Excellent
	+ Needs improvement
	+ Average
	+ Unsatisfactory
* **Department Headquarters**: Select the location of your department's headquarters:
	+ HQ
	+ Remote office
	+ Both
	+ Other

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your HR representative for guidance.
* Please note that all answers are anonymous and will be kept confidential.
* This survey is meant to promote diversity, equity, and inclusion in our organization, and all answers will be used to help us better understand and address the needs of our diverse workforce.
