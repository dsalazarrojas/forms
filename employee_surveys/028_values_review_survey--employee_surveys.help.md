# Values_Review_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Values_Review_Survey is a form designed to review and evaluate employee values, company values, and various other relevant details. This form is intended to help employees and managers provide a comprehensive assessment of an employee's performance and development needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully, ensuring you have all relevant information.
2. Select multiple values that apply to the employee from the "Employee_values" and "Company_values" dropdown menus.
3. Enter comments, ratings, and other relevant details in the "Comments" and other text fields as needed.
4. Enter dates and times for the employee's performance review period.
5. Provide the employee's email and phone number (if applicable).
6. Select the employee's values from the "Values" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the employee's name, department, team, manager, job title, job description, job role, job function, job level, job family, job group, job title 2, job level 2, job family 2, and job group 2 (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Note, not required): A brief introduction to the employee's performance review.
* **Employee_values** (Select Multiple): Select multiple values that apply to the employee from the dropdown menu.
* **Company_values** (Select Multiple): Select multiple values that apply to the company from the dropdown menu.
* **Comments** (Text): Enter any comments or feedback regarding the employee's performance.
* **Ratings** (Number): Enter a numerical rating for the employee's performance.
* **Dates** (Date): Enter the dates of the performance review period.
* **Times** (Time): Enter the times of the performance review period.
* **Emails** (Email): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phones** (Text): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Values** (Select One): Select the employee's values from the dropdown menu.
* **Employee Name** (Text): Enter the employee's name.
* **Department** (Text): Enter the employee's department.
* **Team** (Text): Enter the team the employee belongs to.
* **Manager** (Text): Enter the employee's manager's name.
* **Job Title** (Text): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Job Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the employee's job.
* **Job Role** (Text): Enter the employee's job role.
* **Job Function** (Text): Enter the employee's job function.
* **Job Level** (Text): Enter the employee's job level.
* **Job Family** (Text): Enter the employee's job family.
* **Job Group** (Text): Enter the employee's job group.
* **Job Title 2** (Text): Enter the employee's second job title.
* **Job Level 2** (Text): Enter the employee's job level 2.
* **Job Family 2** (Text): Enter the employee's job family 2.
* **Job Group 2** (Text): Enter the employee's job group 2.

Note: The fields with a type of "text" are intended for free-form input. The fields with a type of "date" are intended for dates in the format of mm/dd/yyyy. The fields with a type of "time" are intended for times in the format of hh:mm AM/PM. The fields with a type of "select_multiple" and "select_one" are dropdown menus with the provided options. 

This guide assumes familiarity with the employee's performance data and relevant business terminology. If you're unsure of any field's purpose or requirements, please contact a supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
