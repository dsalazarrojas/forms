# Creative Break Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Break Time Off Form is designed to help employees request and track creative breaks. This form allows you to submit requests for a specific period off, providing essential details about the project you are working on and the reason for your request.
## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill in your project name and type**: Enter the name of the project you are currently working on and select whether it is an active or inactive project.
2.  **Enter start and end dates for your creative break**: Select the start and end dates for your requested creative break.
3.  **Provide a brief description of your request**: Describe your reason for requesting creative break time off.
4.  **Add any additional comments**: If you need to include any further details, add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are currently working on.
*   **Project Type**: Indicate whether your project is active or inactive to help the review team understand its status.
*   **Reason for Requesting Time Off**: Describe why you are requesting creative break time off.
*   **Creative Break Start Date**: Select the start date of your creative break.
*   **Creative Break End Date**: Select the end date of your creative break.
*   **Additional Comments**: Add any extra details about your request, if necessary.
*   **Project Status**: Select whether your project is active or inactive to provide context.
*   **Time Off Start Date**: Enter the date you wish to begin your creative break.
*   **Time Off End Date**: Enter the date you wish to end your creative break.

## Tips

*   Make sure to discuss your request with your manager or HR representative if required.
*   Check that you have all necessary information and permissions before submitting your request.
*   Be clear and concise in your answers to facilitate the review process.
