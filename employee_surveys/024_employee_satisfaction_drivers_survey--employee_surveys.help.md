# Employee Satisfaction Drivers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to help management understand what drives employees' motivation and satisfaction at work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
3. Ensure that each question is answered truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What drives your motivation at work?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about the primary factor that motivates you at work. Choose one of the options: High level of autonomy, High level of recognition, High level of growth, or High level of challenge.
- **How often do you experience a high level of challenge?** (`question_2`, `number`, not required): This question asks about your experience of challenge in your daily work. Please answer with a number between 1 (never) and 5 (often).
- **What motivates you?** (`question_3`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about the factors that motivate you at work. Choose one or more of the options: High level of autonomy, High level of recognition, High level of growth, or High level of challenge.
- **Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey** (`question_4`, `note`, not required): This is a thank-you note for your participation in the survey.
- **Do you have any additional comments or concerns?** (`question_5`, `text`, not required): This question allows you to share any additional comments or concerns you may have about your work or working conditions.
