# Performance Review Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for performance reviews. It's used to gather feedback and ratings from team leads, employees, and managers for the reviewed period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select your team lead**: Choose the team lead you are working with during the review period from the provided options.
2. Enter your employee's name and title.
3. Select the performance period you are being reviewed for.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the review period.
5. Write any comments about the review.
6. Rate the employee's overall performance.
7. (Optional) Check areas for improvement and highlight specific accomplishments.
8. Finally, select the overall performance rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, required): Select the team lead you are working with during this review period.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's title.
* **Performance Period** (`performance_period`, date, required): Select the performance period you are being reviewed for.
* **Review Start Date** (`review_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the review period.
* **Review End Date** (`review_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the review period.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, note, required): Write any comments about the review.
* **Overall Rating 1** (`overall_rating_1`, number, required): Rate the employee's overall performance.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`overall_rating_2`, checkbox, optional): Check the areas for improvement you'd like to focus on.
* **Accomplishments** (`overall_rating_3`, text, optional): Write specific accomplishments or successes.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_rating_4`, select_one, required): Select the overall performance rating.
