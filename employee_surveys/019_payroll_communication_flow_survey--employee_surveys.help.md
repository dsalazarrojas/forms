# Payroll Communication Flow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on the communication flow between employees and the payroll team. The goal is to understand how employees currently perceive and experience the payroll process, and to identify potential areas for improvement.

> <thinking>Before answering this form, please note that this survey is meant to gather general feedback and is not a formal assessment of individual performance. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our processes and services. Please answer these questions based on your current experience with our payroll team.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. For select_multiple, select all that apply, and for select_one, select only one option that best describes your experience. You can skip questions that are not relevant or do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all the channels you use to stay informed about your payroll (e.g., email, intranet, phone call).
* **Payroll Clarity** (`payroll_clarity`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all the factors that affect your understanding of your payroll (e.g., pay stubs, benefits, time-off policies).
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select how satisfied you are with your current experience.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve our communication flow.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false):
	+ If you have any further comments or insights that are not captured in the previous questions, please share them here.
* **Payroll Communication Flow** (`payroll_communication_flow`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select how clear you think our communication flow is.
* **Communication Flow Satisfaction** (`communication_flow_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select how satisfied you are with our communication flow.
* **Payroll Clarity Satisfaction** (`payroll_clarity_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select how satisfied you are with your understanding of our payroll processes.
* **Improvement Idea Categories** (`improvement_idea_categories`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all the categories for improvement ideas you think we should consider.
* **Communication Channels How** (`communication_channels_how`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all the ways in which you receive information about your payroll (e.g., email, phone call, in-person meetings).
* **Payroll Clarity How** (`payroll_clarity_how`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all the factors that affect your understanding of your payroll (e.g., pay stubs, benefits, time-off policies).
* **Communication Channels Why** (`communication_channels_why`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all the reasons why you use or do not use certain communication channels.
* **Payroll Clarity Why** (`payroll_clarity_why`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all the reasons why you are or are not satisfied with your understanding of our payroll processes.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please feel free to ask your HR representative for clarification.
- Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our services and make your experience more efficient.
