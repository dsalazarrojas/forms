# Government Workforce Diversity Employment Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Workforce Diversity Employment Analysis form is used to collect information and insights on employee demographics, diversity, and inclusion in the workplace. This form is essential for understanding the current state of diversity and inclusion in the organization and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button when you are ready to complete the form.
2. Fill out each page of the form, providing information on the requested topics.
3. Use the provided options for select_multiple and select_one fields to choose relevant responses.
4. Use the text fields to provide additional comments or information as needed.
5. Once you have completed all the pages, click "Submit" to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **demographic_background** (id: 2, name: demographic_background, type: text, required: false)
  This field is for providing demographic information about yourself, such as your age, gender, ethnicity, etc.
* **work_life_balance** (id: 3, name: work_life_balance, type: text, required: false)
  This field is for describing your work-life balance, including your work hours, work-life conflict, and work-life satisfaction.
* **diversity_and_inclusion** (id: 4, name: diversity_and_inclusion, type: select_multiple, required: false)
  This field is for indicating whether you experience or have experienced diversity and inclusion in the workplace, such as feeling included, heard, and valued.
* **training_and_development** (id: 5, name: training_and_development, type: select_one, required: false)
  This field is for indicating whether you have received training and development opportunities in the workplace, such as diversity and inclusion training, etc.
* **diversity_and_inclusion_programs** (id: 6, name: diversity_and_inclusion_programs, type: select_one, required: false)
  This field is for indicating whether your organization has diversity and inclusion programs in place, such as mentorship programs, etc.
* **employee_resources** (id: 7, name: employee_resources, type: select_one, required: false)
  This field is for indicating whether you have access to employee resources, such as diversity and inclusion training, etc.
* **reporting_and_compliance** (id: 8, name: reporting_and_compliance, type: select_multiple, required: false)

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* You can choose "None of the above" or "Not Applicable" for select_multiple or select_one fields if none of the options apply.
* You can provide additional comments or information in the text fields if needed.
* You can submit this form at any time, but you can also cancel it and come back later.
