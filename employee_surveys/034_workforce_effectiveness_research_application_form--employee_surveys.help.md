# Workforce Effectiveness Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employees for workforce effectiveness research purposes. Please ensure that you are an employee of the organization before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked as "required".
2. Be honest about your employment status and education level.
3. If you have multiple job titles, select all that apply.
4. Leave the date of submission and work hours fields blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Work Experience** (4, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
* **Job Title** (5, text, optional): Enter your current or previous job title.
* **Company** (6, text, optional): Enter your current or previous company name.
* **Department** (7, text, optional): Enter your current or previous department name.
* **Role** (8, text, optional): Enter your current or previous role name.
* **Job Function** (9, text, optional): Enter your current or previous job function name.
* **Work Location** (10, text, optional): Enter your current or previous work location.
* **Years of Service** (11, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Employment Status** (12, select_one, required): Select your current employment status.
* **Education** (13, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable education levels.
* **Date of Birth** (14, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Job Title 1** (15, select_one, optional): Select your current or previous job title.
* **Job Title 2** (16, select_one, optional): Select your current or previous job title.
* **Job Title 3** (17, select_one, optional): Select your current or previous job title.
* **Job Title 4** (18, select_one, optional): Select your current or previous job title.
* **Job Title 5** (19, select_one, optional): Select your current or previous job title.
* **Date of Submission** (20, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Work Hours** (21, time, optional): Enter your current or regular work hours.
* **Work Hours 1** (22, time, optional): Enter your alternate or flexible work hours.
* **Note** (23, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments.
* **Email 1** (24, email, optional): Enter an email address.
* **Phone** (25, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
1. Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
2. If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
3. The collected data will be used for workforce effectiveness research purposes only.
