# Accounting Team Collaboration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Team Collaboration Survey is designed to evaluate the current state of teamwork and collaboration within the accounting team. This survey aims to gather feedback on communication, task distribution, and the tools used for collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select your role within the team and provide a brief description of your job function.
2. Estimate the size of your team.
3. Rate how effectively you feel the team communicates (e.g., very effective, effective, neutral, ineffective, very ineffective).
4. Choose whether you believe work is distributed fairly across the team (e.g., strongly agree, agree, neutral, disagree, strongly disagree).
5. List up to three collaboration tools your team uses for communication and task tracking (e.g., email, Slack or Teams, project management tool, shared drives).
6. Describe any obstacles that hinder teamwork (e.g., technical issues, scheduling conflicts, etc.).
7. If you have any practical suggestions to improve collaboration, please provide them.
8. Indicate your willingness to participate in workshops or activities to enhance teamwork.
9. Share any additional comments or feedback about team collaboration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role within the team** (`respondent_role`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe your position within the team, such as accountant, team leader, or team member.
* **Team size** (`team_size`, `number`, **required: false**): Estimate the number of people in your accounting team.
* **Communication effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, **required: true**): Choose how well the team communicates, with options ranging from very effective to very ineffective.
* **Task distribution fairness** (`task_distribution_fairness`, `select_one`, **required: true**): Decide if you think work is distributed fairly across the team, selecting from strongly agree to strongly disagree.
* **Collaboration tools used** (`collaboration_tools_used`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**): List up to three tools used for collaboration, such as email, project management tools, or shared drives.
* **Obstacles to collaboration** (`obstacles_to_collaboration`, `text`, **required: false**): Describe up to three obstacles that affect teamwork.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, **required: false**): Offer practical ideas on how to enhance collaboration.
* **Willingness to participate** (`willingness_to_participate`, `select_one`, **required: false**): Choose if you are willing to participate in workshops or activities to improve teamwork.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **required: false**): Share any other feedback about team collaboration.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing the form, as this will provide valuable insights into team collaboration.
* Ensure you are aware of your role and the team size to provide accurate information.
* When rating communication effectiveness, consider the team's overall communication style.
* When listing collaboration tools, only select up to three tools to avoid overwhelming the response.
* Be constructive when suggesting improvements, and provide practical ideas.
* If you have any additional feedback, share it in the "Additional comments" section.
