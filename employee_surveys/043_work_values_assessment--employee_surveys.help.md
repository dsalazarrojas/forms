# Work Values Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess employees' values and job satisfaction. It helps managers and HR teams to better understand individual employees' priorities, motivation, and job-related concerns. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your job title in the **Job Title** field.
2. For **Which of the following values do you most identify with?**, select all the values that best describe you. For example, if you value achievement and helping others, select both.
3. Enter your years of experience in your current job in the **Years of Experience in Current Job** field.
4. In **Job Satisfaction**, describe your overall satisfaction with your job.
5. Answer the question **On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with your job?** by selecting a number between 1 and 5.
6. Answer the question **On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with your work-life balance?** by selecting a number between 1 and 5.
7. In **Motivation**, describe what motivates you.
8. Answer the question **On a scale of 1-5, how motivated are you?** by selecting a number between 1 and 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title, e.g., Software Engineer, Data Scientist, etc.
* **Which of the following values do you most identify with?**:
	+ **Achievement**: Select if you value achieving goals and being successful in your work.
	+ **Competition**: Select if you enjoy competing with others or setting high standards.
	+ **Creativity**: Select if you value innovative thinking and problem-solving.
	+ **Independence**: Select if you prefer to work independently without close supervision.
	+ **Helping Others**: Select if you value supporting and assisting colleagues and others.
	+ **Recognition**: Select if you value praise, awards, or public recognition.
	+ **Social Status**: Select if you value the perception of being successful or influential.
	+ **Work-Life Balance**: Select if you value balancing work and personal life.
	+ **Altruism**: Select if you value helping others for the greater good.
	+ **Loyalty**: Select if you value long-term commitment and loyalty to your organization.
	+ **Stability**: Select if you value stability and predictability in your work.
* **Years of Experience in Current Job**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your current job.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Describe your overall satisfaction with your job, e.g., "I enjoy my job and feel engaged."
* **On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with your job?**: Answer by selecting a number between 1 and 5, with 1 being the lowest satisfaction and 5 the highest.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with your work-life balance?**: Answer by selecting a number between 1 and 5, with 1 being the lowest satisfaction and 5 the highest.
* **Motivation**: Describe what motivates you, e.g., "I am motivated by learning and development opportunities."
* **On a scale of 1-5, how motivated are you?**: Answer by selecting a number between 1 and 5, with 1 being the lowest motivation and 5 the highest.
* **Work-Life Balance**: Describe your balance between work and personal life, e.g., "I work from home and have flexible hours."
