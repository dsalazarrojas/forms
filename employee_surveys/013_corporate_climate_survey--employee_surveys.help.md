# Corporate Climate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Climate Survey is designed to gather anonymous feedback from employees on various aspects of their corporate climate. This form aims to understand employees' perceptions of their overall experience and feelings about their workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Select the options that best match your perceptions.
3. Make sure to answer all questions marked as "required".
4. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Health**: Please describe your overall health and well-being in the workplace.
* **Company Culture**: Describe your experience with the company culture.
* **Leadership Support**: Rate your level of agreement with the statement "Leadership support is strong in this company."
* **Employee Engagement**: Select the level of agreement with the statement "I feel engaged and motivated at work."
* **Communication**: Rate your level of agreement with the statement "Communication within the team is open and clear."
* **Job Security**: Describe your experience with job security.
* **Company Values**: Describe your experience with company values.
* **Team Dynamics**: Describe your experience with team dynamics.
* **Overall Health 2**: Please describe your overall health and well-being in the workplace.
* **Leadership Support 2**: Rate your level of agreement with the statement "Leadership support is strong in this company."
* **Company Culture 2**: Describe your experience with company culture.
* **Employee Survey**: Describe your experience with employee survey.
* **Team Dynamics 2**: Describe your experience with team dynamics.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* Make sure to answer all required questions.
* You can select multiple options for questions that allow multiple selections.
* Review your responses before submitting the form.
* The form should only take a few minutes to complete.
* You can use this form for anonymous feedback.
