# employee_performance_feedback_receipt_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for providing feedback to employees on their performance and progress within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the appropriate category for your feedback, such as "Employee Surveys" or "Human Resources Forms".
2. Provide a brief description of the feedback you are giving, if necessary.
3. Fill in the description field if you have any comments or thoughts on the employee's performance.
4. Finally, sign the acknowledgement section to confirm that you have reviewed and provided feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category** (Select One, optional): Choose the relevant category for the feedback, e.g., "Employee Surveys" or "Human Resources Forms".
* **description** (Text, optional): Provide a brief description of the feedback, if necessary.
* **description** (Text, optional): This field is for any comments or thoughts on the employee's performance.
* **acknowledgement** (Text, optional): Sign here to confirm that you have reviewed and provided feedback.
