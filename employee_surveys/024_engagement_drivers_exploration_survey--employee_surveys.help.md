# Engagement Drivers Exploration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about employee engagement and satisfaction with their job, manager, and company. This information will be used to identify factors that drive engagement and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, starting from page 1 (What is your job title).
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible.
3. If a question does not apply to you, you can select "N/A" or "Not Applicable" in the answer field.
4. Once you have completed all questions, click "Submit" to save your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your job title? (<name: first_page>)
* **Job Title**: This field asks for your current job title. Please enter your title as it is currently listed on your job description or business card.

### 2. What is your job role? (<name: second_page>)
* **Job Role**: This field asks for the primary function or responsibilities of your job. Please choose the option that best describes your role.

### 3. What is your current salary? (<name: what_is_your_current_salary>)
* **Current Salary**: This field asks for your current annual salary. Please enter a whole number (e.g., 50000, 60000).

### 4. What is your annual salary? (<name: what_is_your_annual_salary>)
* **Annual Salary**: This field asks for your expected or desired annual salary. Please enter a whole number (e.g., 50000, 60000).

### 5. What is your department? (<name: what_is_your_department>)
* **Department**: This field asks for the department you currently work in. Please select the option that best describes your department.

### 6. What is your job function? (<name: what_is_your_job_function>)
* **Job Function**: This field asks for the primary functions or responsibilities of your job. Please select all options that apply.

### 7. What is your employment status? (<name: what_is_your_employment_status>)
* **Employment Status**: This field asks for your current employment status. Please select the option that best describes your status.

### 8. What is your job title? (<name: what_is_your_job_title>)
* **Job Title**: This field asks for your current job title. Please enter your title as it is currently listed on your job description or business card.

### 9. What is your manager's name? (<name: what_is_your_manager_name>)
* **Manager's Name**: This field asks for the name of your direct manager. Please enter their name as it is currently listed on their business card or job description.

### 10. Do you report to a manager? (<name: what_is_your_report_to_manager>)
* **Report to Manager**: This field asks if you report directly to a manager. Please select the option that best describes your situation.

### 11. What is your annual leave balance? (<name: what_is_your_annual_leave_balance>)
* **Annual Leave Balance**: This field asks for your current annual leave balance. Please enter a whole number (e.g., 10, 20).

### 12. What is your job level? (<name: what_is_your_job_level>)
* **Job Level**: This field asks for your current job level. Please select the option that best describes your level.

### 13. What is your work status? (<name: what_is_your_work_status>)
* **Work Status**: This field asks for your current work status. Please select the option that best describes your status.
