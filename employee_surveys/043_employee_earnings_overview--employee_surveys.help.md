# employee_earnings_overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_earnings_overview form is used to collect and report on employee earnings and benefits data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the company from the options provided in the "company" field.
2. If applicable, enter the employee's salary in the "salary" field.
3. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of the pay period in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields, respectively.
4. In the "gross_pay", "net_pay", and "gross_pay_2" fields, enter the corresponding pay values.
5. In the "benefits" fields (e.g., benefits_1 to benefits_13), select the types of benefits received by the employee. You can also select "Other" if the benefit is not listed.
6. In the "deductions" fields (id: 8), select the types of deductions applied to the employee's pay.
7. In the "pay_period" and "pay_frequency" fields, select the applicable pay period and frequency, respectively.
8. Ensure that all fields that are applicable to the employee's situation are filled out accurately and completely.
