# Employee Benefits Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Benefits Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather information from employees on their satisfaction with the company's benefits package. Your feedback is essential in helping us understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to make informed decisions about future benefits offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, simply go through each page and select the options that best match your responses.
2.  For select fields, choose the options from the dropdown menus that best suit your answers.
3.  For open-ended fields, type your comments in the provided text boxes.
4.  Please answer all questions truthfully to ensure the accuracy of the data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Benefits Package (Employee Benefits Satisfaction)**: This question asks you to select which of the following benefits you currently receive from your company (Yes, No).
* **What benefits do you currently receive from your company? (Company Benefits)**: This question asks you to select the benefits currently offered by your company.
* **Satisfaction with current benefits**: This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the benefits package.
* **What benefits would you recommend to your company provide? (Recommended Benefits)**: This question asks you to select which benefits you would recommend to your company.
* **Which of the following benefits are important to you? (Employee Benefits Important)**: This question asks you to select which benefits are important to you.
* **Satisfaction with important benefits**: This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the benefits that are important to you.
* **Would you like to see any of the following benefits improved? (Benefits Improved)**: This question asks you to select which benefits you would like to see improved.
* **None**: This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Satisfaction with important benefits improvement**: This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the improvement of important benefits.
* **Do you feel the current benefits package has improved over the past year? (Employee Benefits Satisfaction Improved)**: This question asks you to rate your perception of the benefits package over the past year.
* **What future benefits would you like to see offered by the company? (Future Benefits)**: This question asks you to select which benefits you would like to see offered by the company in the future.
* **Do you feel that the benefits package has improved over the past year? (Employee Benefits Improvement)**: This question asks you to rate your perception of the benefits package over the past year.
* **Are the benefits that will be offered in the future important to you? (Benefits Future Important)**: This question asks you to select which benefits that will be offered in the future are important to you.
* **Satisfaction with future important benefits**: This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the benefits offered in the future that are important to you.
* **Open Ended Comments**: This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Overall satisfaction with company benefits**: This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the company's benefits package.
* **Would you like to see the benefits package improved? (Benefit Satisfaction Improvement)**: This question asks you to rate your desire to see the benefits package improved.
* **Would you like to see benefits that are important to you improved? (Benefit Important Improvement)**: This question asks you to rate your desire to see benefits that are important to you improved.
* **Would you like to see your benefits package improved? (Employee Benefits Satisfaction Improvement)**: This question asks you to rate your desire to see your benefits package improved.
* **Would you like to see important benefits improved? (Employee Benefits Important Improvement)**: This question asks you to rate your desire to see important benefits improved.
* **What benefits would you like to see added to the package? (Employee Benefits)**: This question asks you to select which benefits you would like to see added to the package.
* **Satisfaction with benefits**: This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the benefits package.
* **Employee Benefits Improvement 2**: This question asks you to rate your desire to see your benefits package improved.
* **Open Ended Comments 2**: This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Satisfaction with benefits important**: This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the benefits important to you.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully to ensure accurate data.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is essential in helping us improve the benefits package.
