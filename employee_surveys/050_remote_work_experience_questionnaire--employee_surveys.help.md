# Remote Work Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your remote work experience. Please answer the questions honestly to help us understand your needs and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your current remote work experience.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Enter numbers or text as required.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them in the "Any other comments?" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current job role?**
	+ Select your current job role from the dropdown menu.
	+ For example, Software Engineer, Project Manager, or Product Owner.
* **How many hours do you work from home?**
	+ Enter the number of hours you work from home per day.
	+ For example, 4-6 hours or 8 hours.
* **What type of work environment do you prefer?**
	+ Select all that apply from Home office, Co-working space, or Coffee shop.
* **How often do you feel productive while working from home?**
	+ Select High, Medium, or Low based on your productivity levels.
* **What tools do you use to communicate with your team?**
	+ Select all that apply from Email, Phone, or Video conferencing.
* **How long have you been working from home?**
	+ Select Less than 6 months, 6-11 months, or Over a year based on your remote work experience.
* **How many people do you live with while working from home?**
	+ Enter the number of people you live with.
* **What is the most challenging part of working from home?**
	+ Select all that apply from Lack of separation between work and personal life, Difficulty concentrating, or Distractions from family or roommates.
* **Have you experienced any burnout while working from home?**
	+ Select True or False based on your experience.
* **How has your productivity changed while working from home?**
	+ Select Increased, Decreased, or No change based on your experience.
* **Have you experienced any technical difficulties while working from home?**
	+ Select True or False based on your experience.
* **How do you handle work-related stress while working from home?**
	+ Select Regular exercise, Meditation, or None based on your coping mechanisms.
* **What benefits do you value most while working from home?**
	+ Select all that apply from Autonomy, Flexibility, or Better work-life balance.
* **How has your physical and mental health changed while working from home?**
	+ Select Improved, No change, or Deteriorated based on your experience.
* **How has your relationship with your family changed while working from home?**
	+ Select Improved, No change, or Deteriorated based on your experience.
* **Have you experienced any negative impacts on your career while working from home?**
	+ Select True or False based on your experience.
* **How has your connection with your team changed while working from home?**
	+ Select Improved, No change, or Deteriorated based on your experience.
* **Have you experienced any negative impacts on your work performance while working from home?**
	+ Select True or False based on your experience.
* **What changes do you propose to improve your remote work experience?**
	+ Provide any suggestions or comments you have for improving your remote work experience.
* **Any other comments?**
	+ Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have.
* **What time do you work most often?**
	+ Enter the time you work most often, e.g., 9-5 PM.
* **How many hours do you work most often?**
	+ Enter the number of hours you work most often.
* **What is your work schedule like?**
	+ Select Full-time, Part-time, or Variable based on your schedule.
* **How many days do you work most often?**
	+ Enter the number of days you work most often.
* **Do you have a dedicated workspace at home?**
	+ Select True or False based on whether you have a dedicated workspace at home.
