# employee_reward_preference_poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store employees' individual preferences regarding rewards and communication channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out all relevant fields with your information. Fields marked as required should be completed, while non-required fields can be left blank if you do not have a preference.
- Select your preferred department from the options provided.
- For preferred rewards, select all that apply.
- For preferred communication channels, select one method that you prefer to receive information from the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Department** (Department, select_one, required): Choose your department from the options provided.
* **Preferred Reward** (Preferred Reward, select_multiple, optional): Select all the rewards you prefer. Options include Gift Card, Extra Vacation Time, and Monetary Bonus.
* **Preferred Channel** (Preferred Channel, select_one, optional): Choose one preferred way to receive information from the company. Options include Email, Text Message, In-app, and Push Notification.
* **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time, time, optional): This field is currently not available.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or notes about your preferences.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you are unsure about a field, you can leave it blank, but be aware that it may affect the form's functionality.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
