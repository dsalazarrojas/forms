# Salary Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Salary Survey" form is designed to collect employee responses to a set of questions. The form helps to gather information about employee attitudes and experiences related to their compensation and benefits. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand your opinions and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select all relevant options from the list below by checking the boxes next to the options that apply to you.
2. Fill out the "description" field with any additional comments or insights you would like to share.
3. Enter your "title" if you would like to provide a specific title for your response.
4. Optionally, you can provide a unique identifier for the form by entering a number in the "form-ids" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Surveys** (`employee_surveys`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all options that apply to you:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options that best describes your situation:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or insights related to your response.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, number, required/optional): Optionally, enter a unique identifier for the form to help us track your response.
* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for your response if you would like to provide one.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions truthfully and accurately based on your experiences and opinions.
* If you have any additional concerns or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "description" field.
* If you have any technical issues with the form, contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
