# 30 60 90 Day Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The 30 60 90 Day Review Form is a tool used to evaluate an employee's performance at regular intervals during their tenure. It is designed to gather information about their progress, strengths, and areas for improvement, and to provide a baseline for future evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your personal details**: Fill in your full name, email address, department, job title, and date of hire. These fields are required to ensure that the review is accurate and specific to your role.
2. **Select the review milestone**: Choose the review point you are at (30, 60, or 90 days) to ensure that the review is tailored to your current progress.
3. **Rate your performance**: Answer the performance score and rating questions, which will help us understand your current level of performance and identify areas for improvement.
4. **Provide feedback**: Share your strengths, areas for improvement, and any suggestions for future growth and development.
5. **Rate the review process**: Share your thoughts on how you feel about the review process itself, including communication from management and overall satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your legal name as recorded in our HR system.
* **Email Address**: Your work email address.
* **Department**: Your department or team.
* **Job Title**: Your official title within the organization.
* **Date of Hire**: The date you started with the organization (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Years at Organization**: The number of full years you have been with us.
* **Review Milestone**: Select the review point you are at (30, 60, or 90 days).
* **Review Date**: The date this review is being conducted (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Manager Name**: The name of your supervising manager.
* **Performance Score (0-100)**: Rate your current performance level out of 100.
* **Performance Rating**: Choose an overall rating of your performance (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory).
* **Key Strengths**: List your main strengths and accomplishments.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas where you'd like to see growth and development.
* **Training Completed**: Select all training programs you have completed (Onboarding, Technical, Soft Skills, Compliance, Leadership, None).
* **Growth Opportunities Support**: Rate how supported you feel in terms of growth opportunities.
* **Work-Life Balance**: Rate how you feel about your work-life balance (1-10, 1 = very poor, 10 = excellent).
* **Satisfaction with Review Process**: Rate your satisfaction with the review process.
* **Communication from Management**: Rate the quality of communication from management.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Share any ideas to improve the review process.
* **Upcoming Goals**: Set targets for the next 30 days.
* **Overall Feedback**: Share general comments about your experience.
* **Additional Comments or Notes**: Any other remarks you'd like to share.
