# hybrid_work_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Hybrid Work Experience Survey" is a form designed to collect feedback from employees on various aspects of their work experience, including work location, job satisfaction, communication, collaboration, and other related topics. This survey aims to gather insights from employees to improve their work environment and overall job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Hybrid Work Experience Survey" form link to access the survey.
2. Review each question carefully and answer them honestly to the best of your ability.
3. Select the relevant options for each question, and provide any additional comments if needed.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hybrid Work Experience** (`1`): This is a text field where you can describe your experience working in a hybrid work environment (e.g., working from home and in the office).
* **Work Location** (`2`): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Remote: I work entirely remotely.
	+ Office: I work entirely in the office.
	+ Both: I work from both home and the office.
* **Job Title** (`3`): This is a text field where you can describe your job title and responsibilities.
* **Work Experience** (`4`): Enter a numerical value to indicate your years of work experience.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`5`): This is a text field where you can describe your overall job satisfaction.
* **Work Life Balance** (`6`): This is a text field where you can describe your work-life balance.
* **Communication** (`7`): This is a text field where you can describe your communication with colleagues and management.
* **Collaboration** (`8`): This is a text field where you can describe your collaboration with colleagues.
* **Work Hours** (`9`): This is a text field where you can describe your work hours and schedule.
* **Benefits** (`10`): This is a text field where you can describe your benefits and compensation.
* **Work Environment** (`11`): This is a text field where you can describe your work environment.
* **Company Resources** (`12`): This is a text field where you can describe the company resources available to you.
* **Manager Support** (`13`): This is a text field where you can describe your manager's support and guidance.
* **Team Collaboration** (`14`): This is a text field where you can describe your collaboration with your team.
* **Job Security** (`15`): This is a text field where you can describe your job security and stability.
* **Job Satisfaction Level** (`16`): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Unsatisfied
	+ Neutral
* **Job Satisfaction Important** (`17`): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
* **Job Satisfaction Reasons** (`18`): Select all that apply:
	+ Job Title
	+ Work Environment
	+ Work Hours
	+ Team
	+ Manager Support
	+ Benefits
* **Job Satisfaction Comments** (`19`): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments about your job satisfaction.
* **Work Satisfaction** (`20`): This is a text field where you can describe your overall work satisfaction.
* **Time Management** (`21`): This is a text field where you can describe your time management skills and strategies.
* **Work Satisfaction Level** (`22`): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Unsatisfied
	+ Very Unsatisfied
* **Time Management Importance** (`23`): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not Important
* **Time Management Reasons** (`24`): Select all that apply:
	+ Work Hours
	+ Job Title
	+ Work Environment
	+ Team
	+ Manager Support
	+ Benefits
* **Time Management Comments** (`25`): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments about your time management.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Answer all required fields before submitting the form.
* Provide any additional comments or explanations where possible.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
