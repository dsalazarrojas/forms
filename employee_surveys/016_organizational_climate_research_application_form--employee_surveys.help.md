# Organizational Climate Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Climate Research Application Form is designed to collect information from organizations regarding their climate and research needs. It will help us understand more about the organizations that are interested in our research and tailor our services to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's name in the "organization" field.
2. Enter the contact person's name and job title in the "contact person" and "contact title" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your organization's email address and contact phone number.
4. Describe your organization's description in the brief description field.
5. Select the relevant category and organization type for your organization.
6. Provide any additional notes if needed.
7. Finally, enter the date and time you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **organization** (Text): Enter your organization's name.
* **contact person** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **email** (Email): Enter your organization's email address.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your organization's contact phone number.
* **description** (Text): Provide a brief description of your organization.
* **category** (Select Multiple): Select the category that best describes your organization (Employee Surveys).
* **organization type** (Select One): Select the type of your organization (University, School, Hospital, Other).
* **contact_title** (Select One): Select the title of your contact person (Director, Manager, Admin, Other).
* **contact_department** (Text): Enter the contact department.
* **contact_position** (Text): Enter the contact position.
* **contact_phone** (Text): Enter the contact phone number.
* **contact_email** (Email): Enter the contact email.
* **notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes.
* **submitted_by** (Text): Enter the name of person who submitted this form.
* **submitted_on** (Date): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **submitted_at** (Time): Enter the time this form was submitted.

Note: This help guide assumes the user has the necessary permissions to submit the form, and the form fields are filled out according to their actual requirements.
