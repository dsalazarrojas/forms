# Employee Training Needs Identification Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect employee training needs information for effective evaluation and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Form Data field with any additional details about the employee (Page 1).
2. Enter the employee's name (Page 2).
3. Select the employee's training needs from the options provided (Page 3).
4. Provide any additional training needs that are not listed (Page 4).
5. Enter the employee's manager's name (Page 5).
6. Fill out the department and job title of the employee (Page 6).
7. Select whether the employee has completed training or not, and if not, specify the reason (Page 7).
8. Evaluate the status of the employee's training needs as per the company's policy (Page 8).
9. Enter the employee's date of birth, job start date, and job finish date (Page 9).
10. Enter the employee's email and phone number (Page 10).
11. Specify the reason for job finish, if applicable (Page 11).
12. Enter the employee's finish date (Page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data (ID 1)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
	+ A text field for additional details about the employee's training needs.
* **Employee Name (ID 2)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's name.
* **Training Needs (ID 3)** (`<name>: select_multiple, required: false`)
	+ A multiple-select field where you can select the training needs of the employee from the options provided.
* **Other Training Needs (ID 4)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
	+ This field is for any additional training needs that are not listed.
* **Manager Name (ID 6)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's manager's name.
* **Department (ID 7)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's department.
* **Job Title (ID 8)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's job title.
* **Training Status (ID 9)** (`<name>: select_one, required: false`)
	+ This field is for whether the employee has completed training or not.
* **Other (Training Status) (ID 10)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
	+ This field is for specifying the reason if the employee has not completed training.
* **Training Needs Status (ID 11)** (`<name>: select_one, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's training needs status as per the company's policy.
* **Other (Training Needs Status) (ID 12)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
	+ This field is for specifying additional training needs status that are not listed.
* **Date of Birth (ID 13)** (`<name>: date, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's date of birth.
* **Job Start Date (ID 14)** (`<name>: date, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's job start date.
* **Job Finish Date (ID 15)** (`<name>: date, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's job finish date.
* **Email (ID 16)** (`<name>: email, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's email address.
* **Phone (ID 17)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
	+ This field is for the employee's phone number.
* **Job Finish Reason (ID 18)** (`<name>: text, required: false`)
