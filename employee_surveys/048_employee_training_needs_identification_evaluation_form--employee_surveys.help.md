# Employee Training Needs Identification Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect employee training needs information for effective evaluation and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Form Data field with any additional details about the employee (Page 1).
2. Enter the employee's name (Page 2).
3. Select the employee's training needs from the options provided (Page 3).
4. Provide any additional training needs that are not listed (Page 4).
5. Enter the employee's manager's name (Page 5).
6. Fill out the department and job title of the employee (Page 6).
7. Select whether the employee has completed training or not, and if not, specify the reason (Page 7).
8. Evaluate the status of the employee's training needs as per the company's policy (Page 8).
9. Enter the employee's date of birth, job start date, and job finish date (Page 9).
10. Enter the employee's email and phone number (Page 10).
11. Specify the reason for job finish, if applicable (Page 11).
12. Enter the employee's finish date (Page 12).
