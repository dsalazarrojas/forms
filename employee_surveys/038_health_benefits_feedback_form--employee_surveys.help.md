# Health Benefits Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Benefits Feedback Form is designed to collect employee feedback on the current health benefits program. This form helps us understand the program's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Make sure you are filling out this form as the correct employee, using the correct employee ID and name.
* Review each question carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall satisfaction with our health benefits program** (Required): Select whether you are satisfied or dissatisfied with our health benefits program.
	+ If you select "Satisfied", we'll take it as a positive feedback.
	+ If you select "Dissatisfied", we'll investigate areas for improvement.
* **What opportunities for improvement do you see?** (Optional): Share your thoughts on how we can improve the health benefits program.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (Optional): Provide any other thoughts, ideas, or suggestions you have about the program.
* **Is this email address valid?** (Optional): Confirm that the email address you provide is accurate and active.
* **Is this phone number valid?** (Optional): Confirm that the phone number you provide is accurate and active.
* **Date of feedback** (Required): Select the date when you are providing this feedback.
* **Time of feedback** (Required): Select the time when you are providing this feedback.
* **Employee ID** (Required): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Employee name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Department** (Required): Enter the department where you work.
* **Job title** (Required): Enter your job title as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Company** (Required): Enter the name of the company you work for.
* **Team** (Required): Enter the name of the team you belong to.
* **Manager** (Required): Enter the name of your direct manager.
* **Location** (Required): Enter the location where you work.
* **Comments** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Overall rating** (Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the health benefits program on a scale of 1-10.
* **Do you have health benefits?** (Required): Select whether you have health benefits or not.
* **What do you think about our current health benefits program?** (Optional): Share your thoughts on our current health benefits program.
