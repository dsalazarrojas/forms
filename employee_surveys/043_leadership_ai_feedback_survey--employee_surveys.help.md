# Leadership AI Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership AI Feedback Survey is a tool to help gather feedback from employees about their leadership. This form is meant to encourage open and honest communication within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and department.
2. Provide your email address if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
3. Answer the questions regarding feedback on leadership, including what you think is valuable, how often you receive feedback, and how often you receive feedback from your leader.
4. Add any additional notes if you feel it is necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A text field to fill in your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to fill in your last name.
* **Email**: An email field to provide your contact information.
* **Department**: A dropdown menu with the following options:
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ IT
	+ HR
* **Feedback on Leadership**: A numerical field to rate how valuable you think feedback from leadership is to you.
* **Feedback on Leadership Frequency**: A dropdown menu to rate how often you receive feedback from your leader(s).
	+ Always
	+ Often
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
* **Feedback on Leadership Value**: A dropdown menu to rate how valuable you find feedback from your leader(s) to be.
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat Important
	+ Not at all
* **Date**: A date field to fill in the date you are filling out this form.
* **Time**: A time field to fill in the time you are filling out this form.
* **Note**: A text area for any additional comments or notes you would like to add. 

## Tips
- Be honest and sincere when answering the questions.
- Please answer all questions as best you can.
- Your feedback will help create a more open and honest culture within the company. 
- Please do not repeat this form if you have already done so recently.
