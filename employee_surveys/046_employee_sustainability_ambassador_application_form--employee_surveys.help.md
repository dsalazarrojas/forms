# Employee Sustainability Ambassador Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees who are interested in becoming a Sustainability Ambassador within the company. A Sustainability Ambassador is a role that promotes and champions sustainability practices and initiatives within their department or team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal contact information in the **Employee** section.
2. Select your department from the **Department** dropdown menu.
3. Provide your job title in the **Job Title** field.
4. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
5. Select your role within the company in the **Role** field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the **Contact Email** field.
7. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
8. List your sustainability interests in the **Sustainability Interests** field (select all that apply).
9. Provide a brief explanation of why you are interested in becoming a Sustainability Ambassador in the **Why** field.
10. Explain how you plan to contribute to the Sustainability Mission in the **How** field.
11. Describe your relevant skills and experience related to sustainability in the **Sustainability** field.
12. Indicate your availability for the Sustainability Ambassador role, including any specific dates or periods you are available.
13. Specify when you can start the Sustainability Ambassador role in the **Start Date** field.
14. Specify when you will end the Sustainability Ambassador role in the **End Date** field.
15. Provide any additional availability notes in the **Availability Note** field.
16. Select how often you can commit to the Sustainability Ambassador role in the **Commitments** field.
17. Choose the type of job you think you would be best suited for as a Sustainability Ambassador in the **Job Description** field.
18. Enter the name of your manager in the **Manager** field.
19. Enter your manager's contact email address in the **Manager Email** field.
20. Enter your manager's contact phone number in the **Manager Contact Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (1): This is the section where you can enter your personal contact information.
  * Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID or badge.
  * Email: Enter your company email address.
  * Phone: Enter your company phone number.
* **Department** (2): Select the department you work in from the list provided.
* **Job Title** (3): Enter your current job title within the company.
* **Name** (4): Enter your full name.
* **Role** (5): Select your role within the company from the list provided.
* **Contact Email** (6): Enter your email address that you would like to be reached at.
* **Contact Phone** (7): Enter your phone number that you would like to be reached at.
* **Sustainability Interests** (8): Select all the areas related to sustainability that you are interested in.
	+ Climate Change: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to reducing greenhouse gas emissions and mitigating the effects of climate change.
	+ Diversity and Inclusion: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives that promote diversity, equity, and inclusion in the workplace and beyond.
	+ Green Infrastructure: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to building sustainable infrastructure.
	+ Social Responsibility: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives that promote social responsibility and community engagement.
	+ Sustainability Education: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives that promote sustainability education and awareness.
	+ Supply Chain Management: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to sustainable supply chain management.
	+ Energy Efficiency: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to reducing energy consumption.
	+ Green Building: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to green building and sustainable design.
	+ Sustainable Materials: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to sustainable materials and waste management.
	+ Sustainable Transportation: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to alternative transportation options.
	+ Green Communication: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to green communication and awareness raising.
	+ Circular Economy: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to circular economy principles.
	+ Resilience: Select this option if you are interested in initiatives related to building resilience in the face of climate change.
* **Why** (9): Provide a brief explanation of why you are interested in becoming a Sustainability Ambassador.
* **How** (10): Explain how you plan to contribute to the Sustainability Mission.
* **Sustainability** (11): Describe your relevant skills and experience related to sustainability.
* **Availability** (12): Indicate your availability for the Sustainability Ambassador role, including any specific dates or periods you are available.
* **Start Date** (13): Specify when you can start the Sustainability Ambassador role.
* **End Date** (14): Specify when you will end the Sustainability Ambassador role.
* **Availability Note** (15): Provide any additional availability notes.
* **Commitments** (16): Select how often you can commit to the Sustainability Ambassador role.
* **Job Description** (17): Choose the type of job you think you would be best suited for as a Sustainability Ambassador.
* **Manager** (18): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (19): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Contact Phone** (20): Enter your manager's contact phone number.

## Tips

* Please be concise and clear in your answers.
* Be specific about your experiences and skills related to sustainability.
* Proofread your answers for spelling and grammar errors.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the Sustainability Committee.
