# New Hire Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Monitoring Form is a document used to track new hires' progress, status, and performance. It helps managers and HR representatives to keep track of new employees' onboarding journey, from the beginning to the end.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including the company, job title, job grade, job status, and other relevant details.
2. Select the relevant teams, training hours, and termination status (if applicable).
3. Enter the onboarding date, termination date (if applicable), and termination reason (if applicable).
4. Provide any additional comments or notes about the new hire's progress.
5. Enter the email, phone number, job location, and other contact details for the new hire.
6. Select the reporting manager's name, job title, and contact information.
7. Indicate the onboarding status, last reviewed by, and last reviewed date (if applicable).
8. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company**: Enter the name of the new hire's company. This field is used to identify the company where the new hire works.
* **Job Title**: Enter the new hire's job title. This field helps to categorize the new hire's role within the company.
* **Job Grade**: Select the new hire's job grade. This field determines their level of responsibility and authority within the company.
* **Job Status**: Select the new hire's job status. This field indicates whether the new hire is active, inactive, or on leave.
* **Team**: Select the teams the new hire belongs to. This field helps to identify the new hire's departments and teams.
* **Onboarding Date**: Enter the date the new hire started working. This field keeps track of the onboarding progress.
* **Training Hours**: Enter the number of hours the new hire has completed training. This field monitors the new hire's progress in training.
* **Training Status**: Select the new hire's training status. This field indicates whether the training is complete or incomplete.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date the new hire left the company (if applicable). This field keeps track of the termination process.
* **Termination Reason**: Enter the reason for termination (if applicable). This field provides clarity on the reasons for termination.
* **Termination Status**: Select the new hire's termination status. This field indicates whether the new hire is active, inactive, or on leave.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the new hire's progress.
* **Email**: Enter the new hire's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the new hire's phone number.
* **Job Location**: Enter the new hire's work location.
* **Reporting Manager**: Enter the name of the new hire's reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Job Title**: Enter the reporting manager's job title.
* **Reporting Manager Email**: Enter the reporting manager's email address.
* **Reporting Manager Phone**: Enter the reporting manager's phone number.
* **Onboarding Status**: Select the new hire's onboarding status. This field indicates whether the onboarding process is complete or incomplete.
* **Onboarding Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the new hire's progress.
* **Last Reviewed By**: Select who reviewed the new hire's form last (Manager or HR).
* **Last Reviewed Date**: Enter the date the form was last reviewed.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Double-check the information for errors and inconsistencies.
* Update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the new hire's status.
* Use this form as a tool to facilitate communication and ensure smooth onboarding processes.
