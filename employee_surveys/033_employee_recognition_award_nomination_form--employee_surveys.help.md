# Employee Recognition Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Award Nomination Form is used to identify and recognize employees who have made significant contributions to the team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee(s) you would like to nominate by choosing 'Yes' for the "Select Employee To Nominate" field.
2. Choose the manager who will be nominating the employee(s) by selecting 'Manager 1', 'Manager 2', or 'Manager 3' from the "Select Nominating Manager" field.
3. Enter the date of the nomination by selecting a date in the "Nomination Date" field.
4. Provide a clear and specific reason for the nomination in the "Nomination Reason" field.
5. Select the employee(s) you would like to recognize by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' for the "Select Employee To Recognize" field.
6. Enter the date of the recognition event by selecting a date in the "Recognition Date" field.
7. Provide a clear and specific reason for the recognition in the "Recognition Reason" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Employee To Nominate** (`employee_nominated`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select the employee(s) you would like to nominate for recognition. Please choose 'Yes' next to the names of the employees who have demonstrated outstanding performance or have made significant contributions to the team or organization.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you are nominating the employee(s).
* **Select Nominating Manager** (`nominating_manager`, select_one, required): Select the manager who will be nominating the employee(s). This is usually your direct supervisor or team leader.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Provide a clear and specific reason for the nomination, explaining why the employee(s) deserve recognition.
* **Select Employee To Recognize** (`employee_recognized`, select_multiple, required): Choose 'Yes' next to the names of the employees you would like to recognize for their outstanding work or achievements.
* **Recognition Date** (`recognition_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the recognition event will take place.
* **Recognition Reason** (`recognition_reason`, text, required): Provide a clear and specific reason for the recognition event, explaining why the employee(s) deserve to be recognized.
* **Note**: This form is intended to be completed by managers or team leaders who are familiar with the employees and their work. If you're unsure about any part of the form, please consult your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
