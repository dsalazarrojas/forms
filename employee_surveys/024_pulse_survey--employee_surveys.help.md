# Pulse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pulse Survey is a form designed to collect employee feedback about their work environment, engagement, and overall satisfaction. This form helps managers and HR understand employee concerns and preferences, allowing them to make data-driven decisions to improve the work environment and employee experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Pulse Survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Choose the options that best describe your feelings and opinions.
3. Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
4. Submit your survey once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think of your work environment** (`intro_page`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-form text field where you can describe your overall impression of your work environment. Please be as brief or detailed as you'd like.
* **How would you rate your work-life balance** (`work_life_balance`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your current level of work-life balance using the provided options (very unhappy, unhappy, neutral, happy, very happy).
* **Work-life balance score** (`work_life_balance_score`, `number`, required: false): Provide a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate your work-life balance.
* **Engagement level** (`engagement_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the options that describe your current engagement level (unengaged, somewhat engaged, engaged, somewhat disengaged, disengaged).
* **Do you have any suggestions to improve the work environment** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Type any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the work environment.
* **What do you like or dislike about our company culture** (`company_culture`, `text`, required: false): Describe what you like or dislike about your company culture.
* **How would you rate your direct manager** (`management`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your direct manager's fairness using the provided options (unfair, fair, very fair, extremely fair, very unfair).
* **Team spirit** (`team_spirit`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-form text field where you can describe your team members' spirit.
* **What type of feedback are you looking for from your manager** (`feedback_important`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the options that describe the type of feedback you're looking for (regular, constructive, critical, positive).
* **How would you rate your manager's response to your feedback** (`manager_response`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your manager's response to your feedback using the provided options (unfair, fair, very fair, extremely fair, very unfair).
* **What is your preferred way to communicate with your manager** (`communication_channel`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred way to communicate with your manager (email, phone, chat, inperson, other).
* **What aspects of work-life balance are important to you** (`work_life_balance_important`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the options that describe what aspects of work-life balance are important to you (work life balance, work life balance score, health and wellness, time off, money, other).

## Tips
* Be honest and open when answering the questions.
* Choose the options that best describe your feelings and opinions.
* Use the free-form text fields to provide any additional context or suggestions.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to choose the option that best represents your feelings.
* Remember, your feedback is crucial in helping your manager and HR understand your concerns and preferences.
