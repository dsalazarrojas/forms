# Work Life Balance Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees communicate their work-life balance needs and preferences. It is used to request time off or adjustments to your work schedule, ensuring that your needs are considered in a fair and compassionate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best represent your current situation.
2. Answer the questions as accurately as possible.
3. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_section_1** (select_multiple, not required): 
  This field asks you to choose how often you work from home. Please select the options that apply to you, such as 'Yes, frequently', 'Yes, occasionally', or 'No'.
* **form_section_2** (select_one, required): 
  This field asks you to choose how you currently balance work and personal life. Please select the option that best describes your current situation.
* **form_section_3** (text, not required): 
  If you have any additional comments or concerns that you'd like to share about your work-life balance, please type them here.
* **form_section_4** (select_one, not required): 
  This field asks you to choose how often you work on weekends. Please select the option that applies to you.
* **form_section_5** (note, not required): 
  This field provides you with space to write any notes or thoughts you have about your work-life balance.
* **form_section_6** (select_multiple, not required): 
  This field asks you to choose how you usually spend your time off. Please select the options that apply to you.
* **form_section_7** (number, not required): 
  If you have a preferred number of hours to work in a day, please enter it here.
* **form_section_8** (number, not required): 
  If you have a preferred number of hours to work in a week, please enter it here.
