# Negative Feedback Mechanism Explanation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and concise explanation for the negative feedback mechanism in place within the organization. This includes details on who collected the feedback, for whom it was collected, and the frequency and type of feedback collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct options for "Collected By" and "Collected For" to indicate who and whom the feedback was collected from.
2. Choose the frequency and type of feedback collected in "Feedback Frequency" and "Feedback Type" fields respectively.
3. Provide the necessary details in the "Negative Feedback Frequencies" field, if applicable.
4. Fill in the "Collected At" field with the date the feedback was collected.
5. Enter the submitted by details in the "Submitted By" field, including department and position.
6. If applicable, fill in the "Collected By Who" fields with the relevant details.
7. Select the review cycle details in the "Review Cycle" field and enter the start and end dates in the "Review Cycle Start Date" and "Review Cycle End Date" fields respectively.
8. Enter the submitted at date in the "Submitted At" field.
