# manager_feedback_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "manager_feedback_request" form is designed to collect feedback from managers, helping them to identify their strengths and areas for improvement. This form is intended for internal use and should only be used by managers to request feedback from their direct reports or team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by answering the first question on the form, which asks for the feedback itself.
2. Next, describe your strengths, which are the things you do well in your role.
3. Identify areas where you'd like to improve or receive feedback on.
4. If you'd like to remain anonymous, select "Yes" in the "Can the feedback be left anonymous" section.
5. Finally, enter your email address in the "Email" field so that your feedback can be shared with your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): This is the question where you can provide your manager with any feedback you'd like to share.
* **Strengths** (`what_are_the_managers_strengths`, text, required/optional): Describe your strengths, including your accomplishments, skills, and qualities that make you a great manager.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`what_are_the_areas_for_improvement`, text, required/optional): Identify areas where you'd like to receive feedback or improve in your role.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): This is your name, which will help your manager associate the feedback with you.
* **Can the feedback be left anonymous** (`can_the_feedback_be_left_anonymous`, select_one, required/optional): If you'd like to remain anonymous, select "Yes" here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that your feedback can be shared with your manager.

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Focus on specific examples or experiences when providing feedback.
* Remember that feedback is a two-way conversation; be open to receiving feedback as well.
* If you're unsure about anything, reach out to your manager or HR representative for guidance.
