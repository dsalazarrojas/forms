## Workplace Accessibility Feedback Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Workplace Accessibility Feedback Form is a valuable tool that allows employees to provide valuable insights into the accessibility of their work environment. The feedback collected through this form will help us identify areas that need improvement, ensuring that our workplace is inclusive and accessible for everyone. Your input is crucial in helping us create a better workplace for everyone.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Answer every question that applies to you, even if it might seem unrelated at first glance.
3. Be as specific as you can when providing feedback or suggestions.
4. If you're unsure about a field or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note): This is a brief welcome message that appears at the beginning of the form. Please don't worry about filling this out; it's just a placeholder.
* **Workplace Accessibility** (Number): This field asks you to rate how accessible you feel your work environment is. Please select a number that best reflects your experience.
* **Suggest Improvements** (Select Multiple): Here, you can suggest specific improvements to make your work environment more accessible. Choose from the available options or add your own ideas.
* **Contact Information** (Email): This field is for you to provide your contact information so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Employee Comments** (Text): Share any thoughts, feelings, or experiences you'd like to discuss about your work environment. Be as detailed as you'd like.
* **Manager Comments** (Text): If you have feedback or concerns that you'd like to address with your manager directly, this is the place to do so.
* **Accessibility Features** (Select One): Please indicate if you have access to the following features: [Feature 1, Feature 2].
* **Suggestions** (Select One): Here, you can suggest additional ideas for improving the work environment.

* **Employee Suggestions** (Text): Share any additional suggestions you have for improving the workplace.
* **Manager Suggestions** (Text): If you have feedback or ideas for your manager, this is the place to share them.
* **Manager Comments 2** (Text): If you'd like to address any concerns or questions with your manager directly, please use this field.

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback or suggestions.
