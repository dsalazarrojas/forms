** industrial-engagement-survey - Help Guide **

## Purpose
The **Industrial Engagement Survey** is designed to measure employee engagement, satisfaction, and feedback within the organization. This survey is meant to provide a comprehensive understanding of the employees' experiences and perceptions of their work environment, supervisor, and overall job satisfaction. The results of this survey will help identify areas of improvement and opportunities for growth within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please select the corresponding page for each section (e.g., "employee_survey_questions", "employee_survey_answers", etc.).
2. For each page, fill in the required fields and select from the available options (if any).
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, as marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field labels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_survey_questions**: A text field for employees to provide answers to the survey questions. Please provide detailed and honest answers to help us better understand your experiences.
* **employee_survey_answers**: A text field for employees to provide feedback and answers to the survey questions. Please provide detailed and honest answers to help us better understand your experiences.
* **engagement_level**: Select one of the options to indicate your level of engagement at work. This will help us understand your level of commitment and motivation.
* **feedback**: A text field for employees to provide feedback and suggestions for improvement. Please be specific and provide actionable suggestions.
* **contact_information**: A field to input your contact information (if required). This will help us get in touch with you for further discussion or clarification.
* **phone_number**: A field to input your phone number (if required). Please be aware that your phone number will be used for further discussion or clarification.
* **email_address**: An email field to input your email address (if required). This will be used for further discussion or clarification.
* **employee_satisfactory_experience**: Select one of the options to indicate your satisfaction with your overall work experience. This will help us understand your level of job satisfaction.
* **workplace_climate**: Select multiple options to indicate the climate of your workplace. This will help us understand the overall work environment.
* **organizational_change**: Select multiple options to indicate whether you have noticed any changes in the organization. This will help us understand the impact of organizational changes on employee engagement.
* **department**: Select one of the options to indicate your department. This will help us understand your team's dynamics and performance.
* **supervisor_name**: A field to input your supervisor's name (if required). Please be aware that your supervisor's name will be used to get in touch with them for further discussion or clarification.
* **supervisor_email**: A field to input your supervisor's email address (if required). This will be used for further discussion or clarification.
* **feedback_frequency**: Select one of the options to indicate how frequently you provide feedback. This will help us understand your feedback behavior.
* **feedback_format**: Select one of the options to indicate your preferred format for feedback. This will help us understand your communication style.
* **feedback_channel**: Select one of the options to indicate where you prefer to provide feedback. This will help us understand your communication preferences.
* **supervisor_response_time**: Select one of the options to indicate how quickly your supervisor responds to your feedback. This will help us understand their response time.
* **communication**: Select one of the options to indicate how effective you find the communication within your team. This will help us understand your communication experience.
* **manager_survey_questions**: A text field for your supervisor to provide answers to their survey questions. Please have them provide detailed and honest answers to help us better understand their experiences.
* **manager_survey_answers**: A text field for your supervisor to provide feedback and answers to their survey questions. Please have them provide detailed and honest answers to help us better understand their experiences.
* **employee_satisfaction**: Select one of the options to indicate your overall satisfaction with your job. This will help us understand your job satisfaction.
* **overall_experience**: Select one of the options to indicate your overall experience with your job. This will help us understand your job performance.
* **engagement_score**: Select one of the options to indicate your level of engagement with your work. This will help us understand your level of commitment and motivation.
* **suggestions_for_improvement**: A field to input your suggestions for improvement. Please be specific and provide actionable suggestions.
* **action_planning**: A field to input your action planning for suggestions. Please be specific and provide actionable suggestions.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be as specific as possible in your answers and suggestions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey and for your feedback!
