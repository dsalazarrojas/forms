# Business Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Culture Survey is a tool used to gather insights from employees about their experiences and perceptions of the company's culture. It helps to identify areas of strength and opportunities for improvement in company policies and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Select the most accurate answer for each question from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information** (`respondent_information`): Please enter your name, title, and department. This information will help us identify who responded to the survey and which department is represented.
* **Department** (`department`): Please select the department you work in.
* **How Long Have You Worked Here** (`tenure`): Please select the length of time you have worked for the company.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`): Please select your current job level within the company.
* **Culture Assessment** (`culture_assessment`): This is a note field, so you can add any additional comments or thoughts about the company culture.
* **Company Values Alignment** (`values_alignment`): Please select how well you align with the company's values.
* **Leadership Effectiveness** (`leadership_effectiveness`): Please rate the quality of leadership in the company.
* **Cross-Team Collaboration** (`collaboration_level`): Please rate how well teams collaborate with each other.
* **Internal Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`): Please rate the quality of internal communication in the company.
* **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`): Please select how satisfied you are with your work-life balance.
* **Innovation is Encouraged** (`innovation_encouraged`): Please select how welcome new ideas are in the company.
* **Diversity and Inclusion** (`diversity_inclusion`): Please rate how inclusive you find the work environment.
* **Career Development Opportunities** (`career_development`): Please select how available career development opportunities are.
* **Culture Strengths** (`culture_strengths`): Please select up to three areas where you think the company excels.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`culture_improvements`): Please select up to three areas where you think the company could improve.
* **Overall Job Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Please select how satisfied you are with your job overall.
* **Likelihood to Stay** (`retention_likelihood`): Please select how likely you are to stay with the company.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Please provide any additional comments or feedback for leadership.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Select the most accurate answer for each question from the available options.
* If you have any suggestions or feedback for improving the survey, please let us know.
