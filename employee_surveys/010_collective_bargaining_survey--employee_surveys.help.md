# Collective Bargaining Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Collective Bargaining Survey is designed to collect feedback from employees about their experiences and perceptions of the collective bargaining agreement. The information gathered will help us inform future negotiations and make improvements to the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your Employee ID Number in the first field.
2. Provide your Employee Name.
3. Select your Department or Unit.
4. Enter the number of years you have worked here.
5. Enter your current Job Title.
6. Choose whether you are a union member.
7. Choose how familiar you are with the collective bargaining agreement.
8. Select your level of satisfaction with current wage.
9. Select your level of satisfaction with health insurance benefits.
10. Select your level of satisfaction with retirement benefits.
11. Select your level of satisfaction with your work schedule and hours.
12. Select your level of satisfaction with job security under the current agreement.
13. Select your level of satisfaction with the grievance procedure.
14. Select your level of agreement that the agreement is fairly administered.
15. Select up to 3 top priorities for the next agreement.
16. Rate your working conditions.
17. Describe your experience with management-employee relations.
18. Select how well the union communicates with members.
19. (Optional) Provide any concerns or suggestions for future negotiations.
20. Choose whether you would be willing to participate in negotiations or advocacy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your unique Employee ID Number.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Provide your name for reference purposes.
* **Department or Unit** (`department`, text, required): Choose your Department or Unit where you work.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, required): Enter how long you have worked here.
* **Job Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your current Job Title.
* **Are you a union member?** (`union_member`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are a member of the union.
* **Familiarity with Collective Bargaining Agreement** (`agreement_awareness`, select_one, required): Choose how familiar you are with the collective bargaining agreement.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Current Wage** (`wage_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with your current wage.
* **Satisfaction with Health Insurance Benefits** (`benefit_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with health insurance benefits.
* **Satisfaction with Retirement Benefits - Pension** (`retirement_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with retirement benefits.
* **Satisfaction with Work Schedule and Hours** (`work_schedule_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with your work schedule and hours.
* **Satisfaction with Job Security under Current Agreement** (`job_security`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with job security under the current agreement.
* **Satisfaction with Grievance Procedure** (`grievance_process`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with the grievance procedure.
* **Belief that Agreement is Fairly Administered** (`fair_treatment`, select_one, required): Select your level of agreement that the agreement is fairly administered.
* **Top Priorities for Next Agreement** (`priority_improvements`, select_multiple, required): Select up to 3 of your top priorities for the next agreement.
* **Working Conditions** (`work_conditions`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with working conditions.
* **Management-Employee Relations** (`management_relations`, select_one, required): Describe your experience with management-employee relations.
* **Union Communication** (`union_communication`, select_one, required): Select how well you believe the union communicates with members.
* **Concerns or Suggestions for Future Negotiations** (`concerns_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any concerns or suggestions for future negotiations.
* **Willingness to Participate in Negotiations or Advocacy** (`willingness_to_advocate`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would be willing to participate in negotiations or advocacy.
