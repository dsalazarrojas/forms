# Employer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employer Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from employers about their experiences working with our company. This feedback will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better support to our employers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  First, fill out the first name and last name fields with your actual name.
2.  Next, enter your company and job title as it appears on your business card or resume.
3.  Provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you for any follow-up questions.
4.  Choose your working hours (40, 30, or 20) and select your level of working experience (select one of the options).
5.  Indicate your level of satisfaction with your job, selecting multiple options if applicable. You can choose very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
6.  Describe your experience in your job, including any positive or negative aspects.
7.  Answer the question if you would rehire us. If yes, explain why.
8.  If you would not rehire us, explain why.
9.  Choose your job title position (Team Lead, Manager, or Lead).
10.  Enter your job description in the space provided.
11.  Enter your date of joining.
12.  Finally, choose your job title of the employer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name.
*   **Company (3)**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business card or resume.
*   **Job Title (4)**: Enter your job title as it appears on your business card or resume.
*   **Email (5)**: Enter your work email address.
*   **Phone (6)**: Enter your work phone number.
*   **Working Hours (7)**: Select your working hours per week (40, 30, or 20).
*   **Working Experience (8)**: Select your level of working experience.
*   **Satisfaction (9)**: Indicate your level of satisfaction with your job by selecting multiple options.
*   **Experience Description (10)**: Describe your experience in your job, including any positive or negative aspects.
*   **Would Rehire (11)**: Indicate whether you would rehire us.
*   **Rehire Reason (12)**: If you would rehire us, explain why.
*   **Job Title Position (13)**: Choose your job title position (Team Lead, Manager, or Lead).
*   **Job Title Description (14)**: Enter your job description.
*   **Date of Joining (15)**: Enter your date of joining.
*   **Employer (16)**: Enter your employer's name.
*   **Job Title of the Employer (17)**: Choose your job title of the employer.
*   **Job Title of Department (18)**: Select your job title of department (Accounting, Engineering, or IT).
*   **Experience Description (19)**: Describe your experience in your job, including any positive or negative aspects.
*   **Rehire Reason (20)**: If you would not rehire us, explain why.
*   **Rehire (21)**: Indicate whether you would rehire us.
*   **Rehire Reason (22)**: If you would rehire us, explain why.
*   **Job Title Position (23)**: Choose your job title position (Team Lead, Manager, or Lead).
*   **Job Title Description (24)**: Enter your job description.
*   **Date of Joining (25)**: Enter your date of joining.

## Tips
*   Make sure to answer all required fields to provide us with accurate and complete feedback.
*   If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them in the comments section.
*   If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact us for clarification.
*   We appreciate your honest feedback and look forward to improving our services based on your input.
