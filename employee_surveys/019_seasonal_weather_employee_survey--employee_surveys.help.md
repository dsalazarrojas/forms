# Seasonal Weather Employee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seasonal Weather Employee Survey is a tool to collect data about employee exposure to seasonal weather conditions over the last 7 days.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question with your estimated hours spent outside during the last 7 days.
3. Enter your responses in the corresponding fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How Many Days Outside in Last 7 Days**: This question asks about the total number of days you spent outside during the last 7 days.
* **How Many Hours Outside in Last 7 Days**: This question asks about the total number of hours you spent outside during the last 7 days.
* **How Many Hours Outside Last 7 Days in Sunny Weather**: This question asks about the number of hours you spent outside when it was sunny.
* **How Many Hours Outside Last 7 Days in Rainy Weather**: This question asks about the number of hours you spent outside when it was rainy.
* **How Many Hours Outside Last 7 Days in Windy Weather**: This question asks about the number of hours you spent outside when it was windy.
* **How Many Hours Outside Last 7 Days in Stormy Weather**: This question asks about the number of hours you spent outside when it was stormy.
* **How Many Hours Outside Last 7 Days in Extremely Cold Weather**: This question asks about the number of hours you spent outside when it was extremely cold.
* **How Many Hours Outside Last 7 Days in Extremely Hot Weather**: This question asks about the number of hours you spent outside when it was extremely hot.
* **How Many Hours Outside Last 7 Days in Extremely Windy Weather**: This question asks about the number of hours you spent outside when it was extremely windy.

## Tips
* Please answer based on your best estimates.
* If you're unsure about a weather condition, please answer as accurately as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
