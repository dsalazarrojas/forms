# Employee Productivity Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Productivity Evaluation Form is a self-assessment tool designed to help employees evaluate their own performance, quality of work, collaboration skills, and time management. This form aims to gather data that can be used for performance evaluations and professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For rating fields, choose the corresponding number or option that best reflects your opinion.
3. For open-ended fields, provide your thoughts and comments in as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Excellent / Good / Fair / Poor** (employee_performance, 1): Please choose how you would rate your overall performance.
* **Quality of work** (quality_of_work, 2): Enter the numerical rating for the quality of your work. A higher rating indicates higher quality work.
* **Collaboration skills** (collaboration_skills, 3): Choose all collaboration skills that you possess, such as Strong, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback and Comments** (feedback_and_comments, 4): Enter any feedback or comments about your performance, collaboration, or work quality.
* **How do you like your job?** (employee_feedback_1, 5): Please enter your thoughts about your job, such as what you enjoy or dislike.
* **How would you rate the quality of your work?** (work_quality_scale, 6): Enter a numerical rating for the quality of your work.
* **How would you rate your collaboration skills?** (collaboration_skills_scale, 7): Enter a numerical rating for your collaboration skills.
* **How would you rate your performance?** (performance_score, 8): Enter a numerical rating for your performance.
* **What do you think can be improved?** (feedback_and_comments_1, 9): Enter any areas you think need improvement.
* **What do you think is your strongest area?** (work_quality_score, 10): Choose all areas where you excel, such as Quality of work, Time management, Collaboration skills, Adaptability, or Communication skills.
* **What skills do you use to collaborate with your team?** (collaboration_skills_1, 11): Enter any skills you use for collaboration, such as communication or problem-solving.
* **How do you rate yourself on collaboration skills?** (collaboration_scale_1, 12): Enter a numerical rating for your collaboration skills.
* **What do you expect to improve in your performance?** (performance_score_1, 13): Choose all areas you expect to improve, such as Communication, Adaptability, Time management, Quality of work, or any other relevant area.
* **How do you rate yourself on collaboration skills?** (collaboration_scale_1, 14): Enter a numerical rating for your collaboration skills.
* **What would you do if your supervisor told you to improve?** (feedback_and_comments_2, 15): Enter your thoughts on what you would do if your supervisor suggested improvements.
* **How much time do you spend on your task?** (time_management_scale, 16): Enter the average hours you spend on a task.
* **How many hours do you spend on your task?** (work_hours_scale, 17): Enter the average hours you spend on a task.
* **How do you rate yourself on time management?** (time_management_1, 18): Enter a numerical rating for your time management.
* **Any other comments?** (feedback_and_comments_3, 19): Enter any additional thoughts or comments.
* **How many hours do you spend on your task?** (work_hours_1, 20): Enter the average hours you spend on a task.
* **How do you rate yourself on performance?** (performance_scale_1, 21): Enter a numerical rating for your performance.
* **What do you expect from your colleagues?** (employee_feedback_2, 22): Enter what you expect from your colleagues.
* **Any other comments?** (feedback_and_comments_4, 23): Enter any additional thoughts or comments.
* **How do you feel about your job?** (employee_feedback_3, 24): Enter your thoughts about your job, such as what you enjoy or dislike.
* **How do you rate yourself on collaboration skills?** (collaboration_scale_2, 25): Enter a numerical rating for your collaboration skills.
