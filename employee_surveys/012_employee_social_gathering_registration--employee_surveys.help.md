The form is used to register for a social gathering event organized by the company, and it captures relevant information about the event and the employee's participation. Before starting, please note that this form is for registering a single event. If you're registering for multiple events or have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.

## Purpose
The Employee Social Gathering Registration form is designed to gather information about an employee's registration for a social gathering event. This includes event details and employee participation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event name, date, and time in the respective fields.
2. Provide your employee name and job title.
3. Choose the department you belong to.
4. Indicate the number of people you're registering for the event.
5. If needed, add any comments or additional information about your registration.
6. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Select the tool assigned to you for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name**: Enter the name of the social gathering event you're registering for. This will help us identify the event and track attendance.
- **Event Date**: Enter the date of the social gathering event.
- **Event Time**: Enter the time of the social gathering event.
- **Event Location**: Enter the location of the social gathering event.
- **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or official records.
- **Job Title**: Enter your official job title within the company.
- **Department**: Enter the department you belong to within the company.
- **Registration Count**: Indicate the number of people you're registering for the event, including yourself.
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your registration, if needed.
- **Employee Email**: Enter your official company email address.
- **Employee Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool assigned to you for the event from the options provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter accurate and correct information for all fields to avoid any confusion or errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your HR representative for assistance.
- Registration is subject to availability and event capacity; please check with your HR representative if you have any questions.
