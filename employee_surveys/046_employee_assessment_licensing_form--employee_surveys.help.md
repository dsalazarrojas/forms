# Employee Assessment Licensing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Assessment Licensing Form is a tool used to track and assess the licensing status of employees, including survey completion and renewal dates. It helps ensure that employee information is up-to-date and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for the employee survey from the options provided in the "Employee Survey Category" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the survey in the "Description" field, if necessary.
3. Select the survey date and time in the "Survey Date" and "Survey Time" fields, if applicable.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field, if necessary.
5. Enter the employee's email address in the "Employee Email" field, if applicable.
6. Enter the employee's phone number in the "Employee Phone" field, if applicable.
7. Select the survey status from the options provided in the "Survey Status" field.
8. Enter any comments about the survey in the "Survey Comments" field, if necessary.
9. Upload any relevant files related to the survey in the "Survey File" field, if necessary.
10. Enter the license expiration date and time in the "License Expiration Date" and "License Expiration Time" fields, if applicable.
11. Enter any notes or comments about the license expiration in the "License Expiration Note" field, if necessary.
12. Select the licensing status from the options provided in the "Survey Licensing Status" field.
13. Enter the survey renewal date and time in the "Survey Renewal Date" and "Survey Renewal Time" fields, if applicable.
14. Enter any notes or comments about the survey renewal in the "Survey Renewal Note" field, if necessary.
15. Enter the date and time when the survey was last updated in the "Survey Last Updated" and "Survey Last Updated Time" fields, if applicable.
16. Enter the user who last updated the survey in the "Survey Last Updated By" field, if necessary.
17. Enter the date when the survey was last updated by the user in the "Survey Last Updated On" field, if necessary.
18. Enter any additional information about the user who last updated the survey in the "Survey Last Updated By User" field, if necessary.
19. Enter the date when the survey was last updated by the user in the "Survey Last Updated On User" field, if necessary.
20. Enter the user who created the survey in the "Survey Created By" field, if necessary.

* **Employee Survey Category** (Required): Select the relevant category for the employee survey from the options provided.
* **Description** (Optional): Enter a brief description of the survey, if necessary.

* **License Expiration Time** (Optional): Enter the license expiration time, if applicable.
* **License Expiration Note** (Optional): Enter any notes or comments about the license expiration.
* **Survey Licensing Status** (Required): Select the licensing status from the options provided.
* **Survey Renewal Date** (Optional): Enter the survey renewal date, if applicable.
* **Survey Renewal Time** (Optional): Enter the survey renewal time, if applicable.
* **Survey Renewal Note** (Optional): Enter any notes or comments about the survey renewal.
* **Survey Last Updated** (Optional): Enter the date and time when the survey was last updated.
* **Survey Last Updated By** (Optional): Enter the user who last updated the survey, if necessary.
* **Survey Last Updated On** (Optional): Enter the date when the survey was last updated.
* **Survey Last Updated By User** (Optional): Enter any additional information about the user who last updated the survey.
