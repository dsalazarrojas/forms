# Weekly Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Reflection Survey is a tool designed to help employees reflect on their progress, challenges, and goals on a weekly basis. It aims to gather information about employee performance and provide feedback and support to employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID in the first field. This is a unique identifier that helps us track your progress and goals.
2. Choose the date you submitted this form.
3. In the "Wins" field, describe your accomplishments and successes from the past week.
4. In the "Challenges" field, describe any difficulties or obstacles you faced in the past week.
5. In the "Goals" field, describe your short-term and long-term goals.
6. In the "Progress" field, describe your progress towards your goals.
7. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional information or comments about your performance.
8. The "Submitted By" field will automatically display the employee who submitted this form.
9. The "Created By" field will automatically display the user who created this form.
10. The "Created At" field will automatically display the timestamp when this form was created.
11. The "Updated At" field will automatically display the timestamp when this form was last updated.
12. The "Submitted At" field will automatically display the timestamp when this form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, required: false): Enter your unique Employee ID to associate your reflections with your employee profile.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false): Choose the date you submitted this form.
* **Wins** (`wins`, text, required: false): Describe your accomplishments and successes from the past week.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, required: false): Describe any difficulties or obstacles you faced in the past week.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required: false): Describe your short-term and long-term goals.
* **Progress** (`progress`, text, required: false): Describe your progress towards your goals.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information or comments about your performance.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): This field will automatically display the employee who submitted this form.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required: false): This field will automatically display the user who created this form.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, required: false): This field will automatically display the timestamp when this form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, required: false): This field will automatically display the timestamp when this form was last updated.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, required: false): This field will automatically display the timestamp when this form was submitted.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* Use bullet points or numbered lists to break up your answers if needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or manager.
