> # corporate_ethics_perception_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on employees' perceptions of corporate ethics. It aims to understand their views on reporting, ethics committees, feedback, and incentives in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions carefully.
2. Answer each question in the space provided.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This is an optional field where you can briefly introduce yourself and your role in the company.
* **Reporting** (2): Please describe how you perceive reporting in your company. For example, do you feel comfortable reporting concerns or incidents?
* **Ethics Committees** (3): Share your views on the effectiveness and impact of ethics committees in your organization.
* **Feedback** (4): Describe how you receive and perceive feedback in your workplace. Do you feel encouraged to provide feedback, and do you receive constructive feedback in return?
* **Ethics Survey** (5): This field is intended for you to elaborate on your views on corporate ethics. Please share your understanding of what ethics means in our company culture.
* **Ethics Leadership** (6): Share your views on the company's leadership and their commitment to ethics.
* **Incentives** (7): Describe the incentives or rewards that motivate you to act ethically in your work.
* **Discipline** (8): Please elaborate on how discipline is perceived in your company.
* **Reporting Example** (9): This field is optional and intended for you to provide an example of reporting in your company. You can describe a specific situation where you reported something.
* **Ethics Committees Example** (10): Share an example of ethics committees in action in your company.
* **Feedback Example** (11): This field is optional and intended for you to provide an example of feedback you have received or given.
* **Ethics Survey Example** (12): Provide an example of an ethics survey you have taken part in or seen in your company.
* **Ethics Leadership Example** (13): Share an example of ethics leadership in action in your company.
* **Ethics Incentives Example** (14): Describe an example of incentives that motivate you to act ethically.
* **Ethics Discipline Example** (15): This field is optional and intended for you to provide an example of discipline in your company.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you need more space to elaborate, please provide specific examples.
* If you have any feedback or concerns, please contact us.
