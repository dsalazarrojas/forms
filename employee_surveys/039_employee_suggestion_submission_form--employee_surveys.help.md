# Employee Suggestion Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Suggestion Submission Form is a tool for employees to submit their suggestions and ideas for improvement or change within the organization. This form helps gather feedback from employees, provides a platform for them to share their thoughts, and ensures that their ideas are evaluated and responded to.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Employee Suggestion" field, providing your full name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
2. Select the category that best fits your suggestion from the list provided.
3. Type a brief description of your suggestion in the "Suggestion Description" field.
4. Provide additional details about your suggestion in the "Suggestion Details" field.
5. You can also include a category ID if relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Suggestion** (`employee_suggestion_name`, text, optional):
    Your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
- **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional):
    Select the category that best fits your suggestion.
    - Category 1
    - Category 2
    - Category 3
- **Suggestion Description** (`suggestion_description`, text, optional):
    Provide a brief description of your suggestion.
- **Suggestion Details** (`suggestion_details`, text, optional):
    Provide additional details about your suggestion.
- **Category ID** (`category_id`, number, optional):
    Provide the ID of the category for your suggestion.
    Note: This field is optional, but if you have the category ID, please enter it to help us track and evaluate your suggestion.

## Tips

*   Please be honest and clear when submitting your suggestions.
*   If you are submitting a suggestion that has already been made, add your thoughts to the existing suggestion instead of resubmitting.
*   All suggestions will be reviewed and responded to once evaluated.
