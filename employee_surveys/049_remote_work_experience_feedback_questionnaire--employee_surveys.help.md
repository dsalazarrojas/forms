# Remote Work Experience Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees who have participated in remote work. Your input will help us to understand your experiences, challenges, and suggestions for improving our remote work policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
- Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
- Select the option that best describes your experience for each question.
- Make sure to complete all questions, even if you are unsure of the answer.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your experience like?** (`remote_work_experience_feedback_questionnaire_form_question_1`, text, required: false)
	+ This question is designed to get a general idea of your experience with remote work. Please describe your experience in a few sentences, mentioning any challenges you faced, benefits you enjoyed, and any notable events that occurred.
* **How often did you participate in video meetings?** (`remote_work_experience_feedback_questionnaire_form_question_2`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your level of participation in video meetings while working remotely. Please select the option that best describes your experience:
		+ Never
		+ Rarely
		+ Occasionally
		+ Frequently
		+ Almost always
* **How would you rate the quality of your work?** (`remote_work_experience_feedback_questionnaire_form_question_3`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks you to rate the quality of your work while working remotely. Please rate your experience on a scale from 0 (low) to 10 (high).
* **How many hours did you work outside of office hours?** (`remote_work_experience_feedback_questionnaire_form_question_4`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks you to report the number of hours you worked outside of traditional office hours while working remotely.

		+ Very satisfied
* **How many days were you able to work from home?** (`remote_work_experience_feedback_questionnaire_form_question_6`, select_multiple, required: false)

	+ This question asks about your level of connection to your team while working remotely. Please select the option that best describes your experience:
		+ Strongly disagree

		+ Strongly agree

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section.
- If you are unsure of any question, please don't hesitate to ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
