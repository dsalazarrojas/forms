# Job Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from employees about their job satisfaction. It is designed to help the organization improve the workplace environment and overall employee experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Enter your manager's name in the "manager_name" field.
3. Enter your job title in the "job_title" field.
4. Rate your satisfaction level on a scale of 1-5 in the "satisfaction_level" field.
5. Select the reason for your current satisfaction level in the "satisfaction_reason" field.
6. Identify areas for improvement in the "improvement_areas" field.
7. Rate your morale and retention on a scale of 1-5 in the "morale_retention" field.
8. Provide any additional comments in the "comments" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name as it appears on their employee ID badge.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5 where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Satisfaction Reason** (`satisfaction_reason`, select_one, optional): Select the reason for your current satisfaction level. Options include:
	+ 1. "Not Satisfied"
	+ 2. "Somewhat Satisfied"
	+ 3. "Neutral"
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select areas for improvement. Options include:
	+ 1. "Not Satisfied"
	+ 2. "Somewhat Satisfied"
	+ 3. "Neutral"
* **Morale and Retention** (`morale_retention`, number, optional): Rate your morale and retention on a scale of 1-5 where 1 is "Very Low" and 5 is "Very High".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
