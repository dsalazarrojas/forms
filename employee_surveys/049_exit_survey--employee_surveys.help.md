# Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exit Survey is designed to collect feedback from departing employees about their reasons for leaving the company. This survey helps the organization understand the reasons behind employee departures, which can inform decision-making and improve future employee retention and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the most accurate option that best represents your feelings and experiences.
3. For text fields, provide a brief but clear explanation for each question.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Why Are You Leaving** (`why_leaving`, text, required/optional): A brief explanation of your reasons for leaving the company.
* **Reasons For Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required/optional): A list of specific reasons why you're leaving, if applicable.
* **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, select_one, required/optional): How would you rate your job performance during your tenure? (Excellent, Average, Below Average, Very Poor)
* **Job Environment** (`job_environment`, text, required/optional): A description of your experiences with the job environment, including work spaces, communication, and team dynamics.
* **Work Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_multiple, required/optional): What aspects of your personal life were most affected by your work schedule? (Family Time, Personal Time, Relaxation, Exercise, Social Life, Career Growth, Travel)
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, text, required/optional): A description of your overall job satisfaction during your tenure.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required/optional): A description of your experiences with communication within the company, including feedback, meetings, and team collaboration.
* **Career Growth** (`career_growth`, select_one, required/optional): How satisfied are you with your career growth and development opportunities during your time at the company? (Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied)
* **Team Environment** (`team_environment`, text, required/optional): A description of your experiences with your team, including collaboration, support, and communication.
* **Company Image** (`company_image`, text, required/optional): A description of your perception of the company's values, mission, and reputation.
* **Management** (`management`, text, required/optional): A description of your experiences with your direct manager, including leadership, communication, and support.
* **Job Security** (`job_security`, text, required/optional): A description of your confidence in job security, including job stability, benefits, and career advancement opportunities.
* **Job Satisfaction 2** (`job_satisfaction_2`, select_one, required/optional): How satisfied are you with your job overall? (Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)
* **Salary** (`salary`, text, required/optional): A description of your salary and compensation during your tenure.
* **Job Performance 2** (`job_performance_2`, select_one, required/optional): How would you rate your job performance during your time at the company? (Excellent, Average, Below Average, Very Poor)
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, required/optional): What aspects of the company's benefits were most satisfactory or unsatisfactory? (Excellent, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)
* **Communication 2** (`communication_2`, text, required/optional): A description of your experiences with internal and external communication, including email, phone, and meetings.
* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, text, required/optional): A description of your experiences with your team's performance and collaboration.
* **Company Image 2** (`company_image_2`, text, required/optional): A description of your perception of the company's values, mission, and reputation.
* **Management 2** (`management_2`, text, required/optional): A description of your experiences with your direct manager, including leadership, communication, and support.
* **Job Security 2** (`job_security_2`, text, required/optional): A description of your confidence in job security, including job stability, benefits, and career advancement opportunities.
* **Work Life Balance 2** (`work_life_balance_2`, text, required/optional): A description of your experiences with work-life balance, including flexibility, workload, and personal time.
* **Job Satisfaction 3** (`job_satisfaction_3`, text, required/optional): A description of your overall job satisfaction during your tenure.
* **Reasons For Leaving** (`reasons_for_leaving`, text, required/optional): A list of specific reasons why you're leaving, if applicable.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share with the organization.
