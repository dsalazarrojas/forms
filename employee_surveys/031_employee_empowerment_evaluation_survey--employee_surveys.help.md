# Employee Empowerment Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to evaluate how empowered employees feel in the workplace and identify areas for improvement. The survey is designed to gather feedback on various aspects of employee experience, including decision-making frequency, job satisfaction, and communication with management. This information will help the organization understand employees' perceptions and make informed decisions to enhance their well-being and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most appropriate option for each question.
3. If you have trouble with a question, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How often do you feel empowered to make decisions? (empowerment_frequency)
• **How often do you feel empowered to make decisions?** (`empowerment_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false)
  This question assesses your level of autonomy and decision-making authority in the workplace. Choose the option that best describes how often you feel empowered to make decisions.

### 2. What are your biggest challenges in the workplace? (workplace_challenges)
• **What are your biggest challenges in the workplace?** (`workplace_challenges`, `text`, required: false)
  In this open-ended question, please describe the most significant obstacles you face in your job. This will help us understand the areas where we can provide support and resources.

### 3. How would you rate your overall job satisfaction? (job_satisfaction)
• **How would you rate your overall job satisfaction?** (`job_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false)
  Use a number from 1 to 5 to rate your overall job satisfaction, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

### 4. What are your favorite tools and resources in the workplace? (favorite_tools)
• **What are your favorite tools and resources in the workplace?** (`favorite_tools`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  Choose one or more of the options that represent your favorite tools and resources in the workplace.

### 5. Do you have the necessary autonomy to make decisions? (autonomy)
• **Do you have the necessary autonomy to make decisions?** (`autonomy`, `select_one`, required: false)
  Choose whether you have the autonomy to make decisions or not.

### 6. What are your biggest suggestions for improvement? (improvement_suggestions)
• **What are your biggest suggestions for improvement?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, required: false)
  Share your thoughts on how we can improve the workplace and make it more enjoyable for everyone.

### 7. How would you rate your communication with your manager? (communication)
• **How would you rate your communication with your manager?** (`communication`, `number`, required: false)
  Rate your communication with your manager using a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.

### 8. Do you feel valued and heard in the workplace? (felt_valued)
• **Do you feel valued and heard in the workplace?** (`felt_valued`, `select_one`, required: false)
  Choose whether you feel valued and heard or not.

### 9. How would you rate your work-life balance? (work_life_balance)
• **How would you rate your work-life balance?** (`work_life_balance`, `number`, required: false)
  Rate your work-life balance using a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being the most unbalanced and 5 being the best.

### 10. Do you feel supported in your role? (role_support)
• **Do you feel supported in your role?** (`role_support`, `select_one`, required: false)
  Choose whether you feel supported or not.

### 11. What are some areas where you would like to see more support? (areas_for_improvement)
• **What are some areas where you would like to see more support?** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false)
  In this open-ended question, describe the areas where you would like to see more support and resources.

### 12. How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the survey? (overall_satisfaction)
• **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the survey?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false)
  Rate your overall satisfaction with the survey using a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being the least satisfied and 5 being the most satisfied.
