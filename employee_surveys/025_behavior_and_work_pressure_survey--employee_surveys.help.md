# Behavior And Work Pressure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavior And Work Pressure Survey aims to collect information about employees' experiences with work pressure and job satisfaction. This survey is designed to help management identify areas for improvement and create a better work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer the questions honestly, and remember that responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.
2.  Fill in your Full Name, even though it's optional, as it helps us maintain confidentiality.
3.  Select your Department from the provided options.
4.  Answer questions about your work experience, such as your Position, Tenure, and Typical Work Hours.
5.  Evaluate your workload, communication, and support from management and colleagues.
6.  Rate your stress level and primary sources of stress.
7.  Indicate how often you receive recognition and feel supported by management and colleagues.
8.  Rate your job satisfaction, role clarity, and resources adequacy.
9.  Answer questions about burnout symptoms and how you cope with work pressure.
10.  Provide suggestions for improvement and additional comments about your work experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your full name, which is optional for confidentiality purposes.
* **Employee ID** (2, text, required: false): If you have an Employee ID, please enter it here, otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Department** (3, select_one, required: true): Select your department from the options provided.
* **Position or Job Title** (4, text, required: false): Enter your current job title or role.
* **How Long Have You Worked Here** (5, select_one, required: true): Choose the length of your employment.
* **Typical Work Hours** (6, select_one, required: true): Select your typical work hours per week.
* **Workload Manageability** (7, select_one, required: true): Rate how manageable your workload is.
* **Deadline Pressure** (8, select_one, required: true): Evaluate how often you feel pressured by deadlines.
* **Work-Life Balance** (9, select_one, required: true): Rate your work-life balance.
* **Overtime Frequency** (10, select_one, required: true): Indicate how often you work overtime.
* **Stress Level** (11, select_one, required: true): Rate your stress level at work.
* **Primary Source of Stress** (12, select_multiple, required: true): Select all that apply for your primary source of stress.
* **Support from Management** (13, select_one, required: true): Evaluate how supported you feel by management.
* **Support from Colleagues** (14, select_one, required: true): Evaluate how supported you feel by colleagues.
* **Communication Quality** (15, select_one, required: true): Rate the quality of communication in your team.
* **Role Clarity** (16, select_one, required: true): Rate how clear your role and responsibilities are.
* **Resources Adequacy** (17, select_one, required: true): Indicate if you have adequate resources to do your job.
* **Recognition** (18, select_one, required: true): Indicate how often you receive recognition.
* **Career Growth Opportunities** (19, select_one, required: true): Evaluate the opportunities for career growth.
* **Job Satisfaction** (20, select_one, required: true): Rate your job satisfaction.
* **Burnout Symptoms** (21, select_multiple, required: true): Select all that apply for burnout symptoms you experience.
* **Coping Mechanisms** (22, select_multiple, required: true): Indicate how you cope with work pressure.
* **Would Recommend as Workplace** (23, select_one, required: true): Indicate if you would recommend this company to friends.
* **Plan to Stay** (24, select_one, required: true): Indicate if you plan to stay with this company.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (25, text, required: false): Provide suggestions for improvement to reduce work pressure.
* **Additional Comments** (26, text, required: false): Add any other feedback about your work experience.

## Tips

* Answer questions truthfully and with honesty.
* Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
* Remember that responses are kept confidential and anonymous.
* If you have suggestions or additional comments, please provide them in the provided text fields.
* If you're unsure about any question, please consult with your HR representative for guidance.
