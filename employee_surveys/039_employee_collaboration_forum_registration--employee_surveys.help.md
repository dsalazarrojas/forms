# Employee Collaboration Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Collaboration Forum Registration form is a tool for teams to register and connect with each other, fostering better communication and collaboration within the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title in the **Employee Collaboration Forum Registration** field. This field is a text box where you can enter a brief name or title for your team.
2. Indicate if you are part of a **Department**. If you are part of a department, select "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your **Team Name**. This field is for your team's name or ID.
4. Enter your **Role**. In this field, describe your role within the team, such as project manager, team lead, or team member.
5. List your **Interests** or **Areas of Expertise**. This field is for you to describe your areas of interest or expertise within the team.
6. Describe your **Expectations**. This field is for you to share any expectations you have from the team or its members.
7. Select your **Communication Preferences**. You can choose to be reached through Email, Phone, or Instant Message.
8. Indicate if this is a **Participants**-only event. If you want to participate, select "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter the **Date** for your registration or the event you are interested in. You can choose a date from the calendar or enter a custom date.
10. Enter the **Time** for your registration or the event you are interested in. You can choose a time from the available options or enter a custom time.
11. Enter your **Email**. This field is for your email address where we will communicate with you.
12. Enter your **Phone Number**. This field is for your phone number where you can be reached.
13. Click the **Submit** button to save your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Collaboration Forum Registration**: A text field where you can enter a brief name or title for your team.
* **Department**: A dropdown menu that indicates if you are part of a department. Select "Yes" if you are part of a department.
* **Team Name**: A text field where you can enter your team's name or ID.
* **Role**: A text field where you can describe your role within the team.
* **Interests**: A text field where you can list your areas of interest or expertise within the team.
* **Expectations**: A text field where you can share any expectations you have from the team or its members.
* **Communication Preferences**: A dropdown menu that allows you to choose how you want to be reached (Email, Phone, Instant Message).
* **Participants**: A dropdown menu that indicates if this is a participants-only event. Select "Yes" if you want to participate.
* **Date**: A date field where you can choose a date from the calendar or enter a custom date.
* **Time**: A time field where you can choose a time from the available options or enter a custom time.
* **Email**: A text field where you can enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone**: A text field where you can enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out as much information as possible to help your team understand who you are and what you do.
* If unsure about any of the fields, contact your team lead or manager for guidance.
