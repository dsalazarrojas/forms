# staff_burnout_prevention_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about burnout prevention strategies and resources available to employees. It aims to help identify potential risks and areas for improvement in staff burnout prevention.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Select the relevant options for each question that best describe your current situation.
3. Answer all questions that are required.
4. If a question is not required, you can skip it.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Burnout Risk Level** (`burnout_risk_level`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This question asks how likely you are to experience burnout. Select the level of burnout risk you think you face.
  - `low`: I do not expect to experience burnout.
  - `moderate`: I may experience burnout if I don't take care of myself.
  - `high`: I am likely to experience burnout.
  - `critical`: I am at risk of severe burnout.
- **Burnout Risks Known** (`burnout_risks_known`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Select the burnout risks that are known to you.
  - `work life balance`: I am worried about balancing work and personal life.
  - `workload`: I feel overwhelmed with my workload.
  - `stress`: I am experiencing high levels of stress.
  - `lack of support`: I feel I lack the support I need.
- **Needed Support** (`support_needed`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This question asks whether you feel you need support.
  - `True`: I feel I need support.
  - `False`: I do not feel I need support.
- **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This question asks how committed your manager is to your well-being.
  - `high`: My manager is very committed to supporting me.
  - `low`: My manager is not very committed to supporting me.
- **Training Support** (`training_support`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This question asks whether you receive regular training and support.
  - `True`: I receive regular training and support.
  - `False`: I do not receive regular training and support.
- **Employee Resources** (`employee_resources`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This question asks whether you have access to adequate resources.
  - `adequate`: I have access to adequate resources.
  - `inadequate`: I do not have access to adequate resources.
- **Burnout Prevention Strategies** (`burnout_prevention_strategies`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This question asks whether you are aware of burnout prevention strategies.
  - `True`: I am aware of burnout prevention strategies.
  - `False`: I am not aware of burnout prevention strategies.
- **Burnout Prevention Plan** (`burnout_prevention_plan`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This question asks whether you are aware of a burnout prevention plan.
  - `True`: I am aware of a burnout prevention plan.
  - `False`: I am not aware of a burnout prevention plan.
- **Manager Commitment** (`manager_commitment`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This question asks how committed your manager is to preventing burnout.
  - `high`: My manager is very committed to preventing burnout.
  - `low`: My manager is not very committed to preventing burnout.
- **Feedback Mechanism** (`feedback_mechanism`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  This question asks whether you have a feedback mechanism in place.
  - `True`: I have a feedback mechanism in place.
  - `False`: I do not have a feedback mechanism in place.
## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required questions.
- Take your time to review each page carefully.
- If you are not sure about a question, please consult with your manager or HR representative.
