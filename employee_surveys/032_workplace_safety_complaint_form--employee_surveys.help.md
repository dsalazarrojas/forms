By filling out this form, employees can help identify and address potential safety concerns in the workplace.

2. Enter your department name in the "Department Name" field.
3. Select the date of the incident using the "Incident Date" field.
4. Enter the time of the incident using the "Incident Time" field.
5. Enter the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
6. Enter the name of the safety officer you are reporting to in the "Safety Officer Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your company ID or badge.
* **Department Name**: Enter the department where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date**: Select the date of the incident using the calendar or date picker.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident using the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 9:00 AM).
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the building, floor, or room number.
* **Safety Officer Name**: Enter the name of the safety officer you are reporting to. If you are unsure who to report to, enter "unknown".
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including what happened and any relevant details.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when providing incident descriptions.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your safety officer for guidance.
* Remember to keep the form confidential and only share with authorized personnel.
