# Fashion Seasonal Employment Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your experiences and challenges during the peak season. Your responses will help management make informed decisions to address any potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form consists of several sections with different types of questions. Please complete each section carefully, and make sure to answer all the required questions.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
3. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it to ensure that all the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Peak Season** (`peak_season`, select_one, optional):
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you had a peak season in your role.
* **Hiring Needs** (`hiring_needs`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the seasons during which you had peak hiring needs. You can select multiple options.
	+ Options: Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall
* **Operational Impact** (`operational_impact`, text, optional):
	+ Describe how the peak season impacted your operations, if at all.
* **Seasonal Staffing** (`seasonal_staffing`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you used agency staff during the peak season.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Season Duration** (`season_duration`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of months you worked during the peak season.
* **Peak Season Start** (`peak_season_start`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the start date of your peak season, if applicable.
* **Peak Season End** (`peak_season_end`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the end date of your peak season, if applicable.
* **Peak Period** (`peak_period`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time period during which your peak season occurred, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when describing your experiences and challenges during the peak season.
* If you have any supporting documents or evidence, such as performance reports or emails, that can help support your responses, please attach them to your form submission.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
