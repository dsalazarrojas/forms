# Construction Job Site Safety Employment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employees' perceptions and experiences with safety measures on construction job sites.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit your response.
*   Ensure you answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee name (Optional for anonymous responses)**: This is a text field where you can enter your name, or if you prefer to remain anonymous, you can leave it blank.
*   **Role on site (Job title or trade)**: This is a text field where you can enter your job title or the trade you're working with.
*   **Site location (Project or site name)**: Enter the name of the project or site you're working on.
*   **Safety training received**: Select all the safety trainings you've completed from the list provided.
*   **Confidence in safety procedures**: Rate your confidence level in the site's safety measures. The options range from "Very confident" to "Not confident".
*   **Near miss reporting frequency**: Select how often you report near misses on the site. The options range from "Regularly" to "Never".
*   **Hazards observed**: If you've observed any recurring safety hazards, describe them in this text field.
*   **Suggestions for improvement**: If you have any recommended safety improvements or training suggestions, enter them in this text field.

## Tips

*   Please answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
*   Ensure you've only completed this form once and for your own work site.
