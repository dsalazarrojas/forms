# Marketing Campaign Management Employment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on the effectiveness of marketing campaign management, focusing on the employment aspects of the campaign. It aims to collect information on the campaign's performance in meeting employee resource needs and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Select your favorite prophet from the **Prophets** field. (Please note that this field is not required.)
3. Rate the prophet's performance using the **Prophets rating** field, with a number between 1 and 5.
4. Provide any additional comments in the **Notes** field.
5. Enter the name of the campaign you are providing feedback on in the **Campaign Name** field.
6. Enter the duration of the campaign in the **Campaign Duration** field.
7. Enter the start date of the campaign in the **Start Date** field.
8. Enter the end date of the campaign in the **End Date** field.
9. Evaluate the process of the campaign using the **Process Evaluation** field.
10. Enter the goals of the campaign in the **Resourcing Goals** field.
11. Enter the outcomes of the campaign in the **Resourcing Outcomes** field.
12. Evaluate the outcome of the campaign using the **Outcome Evaluation** field.
13. Enter any additional comments on the process in the **Process** field.
14. Enter any additional goals of the campaign in the **Process Goals** field.
15. Enter any additional outcomes of the campaign in the **Process Outcomes** field.
16. Enter any additional comments on the campaign outcomes in the **Campaign Outcomes** field.
17. Enter any additional goals of the campaign in the **Campaign Goals** field.
18. Enter any additional process goals of the campaign in the **Campaign Process Goals** field.
19. Evaluate the process of the campaign using the **Process Evaluation 2** field.
20. Enter any additional comments on the process in the **Process Outcomes 2** field.
21. Enter any additional goals of the campaign in the **Campaign Process Goals 2** field.
22. Evaluate the campaign process using the **Campaign Process Evaluation** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or other official documents.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, select_one, optional): Select your favorite prophet from the provided options.
* **Prophets rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the prophet's performance with a number between 1 and 5.
* **Rating** (`rating_2`, number, optional): This field is not clearly labeled, but it is likely meant to be another rating field for the same campaign.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the campaign.
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the campaign you are providing feedback on.
* **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the campaign in days or hours.
* **Start Date** (`campaign_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the campaign.
* **End Date** (`campaign_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the campaign.
* **Process Evaluation** (`process_evaluation`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the process of the campaign using the provided options.
* **Resourcing Goals** (`resourcing_goals`, text, optional): Enter any additional goals of the campaign.
* **Resourcing Outcomes** (`resourcing_outcomes`, text, optional): Enter any additional outcomes of the campaign.
* **Outcome Evaluation** (`outcome_evaluation`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the outcome of the campaign using the provided options.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments on the outcome of the campaign.
* **Process** (`process`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments on the process of the campaign.
* **Process Goals** (`process_goals`, text, optional): Enter any additional goals of the campaign.
* **Process Outcomes** (`process_outcomes`, text, optional): Enter any additional outcomes of the campaign.
* **Campaign Outcomes** (`campaign_outcomes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments on the outcomes of the campaign.
* **Campaign Goals** (`campaign_goals`, text, optional): Enter any additional goals of the campaign.
* **Campaign Process Goals** (`campaign_process_goals`, text, optional): Enter any additional process goals of the campaign.
* **Process Evaluation 2** (`process_evaluation_2`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the process of the campaign using the provided options.
* **Process Outcomes 2** (`process_outcomes_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments on the process of the campaign.
* **Campaign Process Goals 2** (`campaign_process_goals_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional goals of the campaign.
* **Campaign Process Evaluation** (`campaign_process_evaluation`, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the campaign process using the provided options.
