# Employee Retention Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Retention Program Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from employees and managers to evaluate the effectiveness of the Employee Retention Program. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to help improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the most appropriate response for each question, taking into account the employee's experience and feedback.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide specific examples and detailed information whenever possible.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Survey** (`employee_retention_program`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your experience with the Employee Retention Program.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the Employee Retention Program on a scale from Very Satisfied to Not Satisfied.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any thoughts, concerns, or ideas you have about the Employee Retention Program.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Offer suggestions for improving the Employee Retention Program.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Contact** (`manager_contact`, text, optional): Provide the contact information for your manager.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Specify the department you belong to.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, required): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Retention Initiatives** (`retention_initiatives`, text, optional): Describe any retention initiatives you've implemented or would like to implement.
* **Retention Effectiveness** (`retention_effectiveness`, number, optional): Evaluate the effectiveness of the retention initiatives on a scale.
* **Retention Initiatives Satisfaction** (`retention_initiatives_satisfaction`, number, optional): Evaluate your satisfaction with the retention initiatives on a scale.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions for improving the Employee Retention Program.
* **Retention Plan** (`retention_plan`, text, optional): Describe the retention plan you'd like to implement or are currently using.
* **Retention Goal** (`retention_goal`, text, optional): Specify your retention goal.
* **Retention Objective** (`retention_objective`, text, optional): Describe your retention objective.
* **Manager Retention Initiatives** (`manager_retention_initiatives`, text, optional): Describe any retention initiatives your manager has implemented or would like to implement.
* **Employee Satisfaction Level** (`employee_satisfaction_level`, text, optional): Evaluate your satisfaction level.
* **Retention Commitment** (`retention_commitment`, text, optional): Evaluate your commitment to the retention program.
* **Retention Involvement** (`retention_involvement`, text, optional): Evaluate your involvement with the retention program.
* **Retention Satisfaction** (`retention_satisfaction`, text, optional): Evaluate your satisfaction with the retention program.
* **Manager Satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, text, optional): Evaluate your manager's satisfaction with the retention program.
* **Employee Retention Program** (`employee_retention_program`, text, optional): Describe your understanding and experience with the Employee Retention Program.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the Employee Retention Program on a scale from Very Satisfied to Not Satisfied.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
* Provide detailed and specific feedback to help improve the Employee Retention Program.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to the HR department.
