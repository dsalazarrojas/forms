# Workplace Efficiency Barriers Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the workplace efficiency barriers faced by employees to identify areas for process improvement and increase productivity and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form carefully, answering all the questions truthfully. You can save your progress and come back later if needed. Each page is designed to capture specific information about your current work situation. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Process** (`work_process`, text, required: false): This field is for you to describe your current work process. Please explain your day-to-day tasks and activities in a few sentences.
* **Challenge Faced** (`challenge_faced`, text, required: false): Describe the challenge you face in your current work process.
* **Impact** (`impact`, select_one, required: false): Please choose one of the options to describe the impact of this challenge on your work.
*   • Yes
*   • No
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): Offer any suggestions you have to overcome this challenge.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): If you have any feedback, comments, or suggestions, please type them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address for contact purposes only. This information will not be used for any other purpose.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number for contact purposes only. This information will not be used for any other purpose.

## Tips
Save your progress if you want to return later. Answer all questions truthfully and honestly to provide a clear understanding of your current work situation. If you need help or clarification, contact the support team.
