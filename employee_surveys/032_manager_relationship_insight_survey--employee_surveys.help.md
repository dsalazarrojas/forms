# Manager Relationship Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Relationship Insight Survey is designed to gather information on the relationships between employees and their managers, focusing on various aspects such as job performance, communication, leadership, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, and Work Email.
2. For the remaining fields, select one or multiple options from the dropdown menus, based on your honest feedback about your manager relationship.
3. Make sure to answer all questions honestly, as this information will help improve the work environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (`first_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter your first name to identify yourself in the survey.

### Last Name (`last_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter your last name to identify yourself in the survey.

### Manager First Name (`manager_first_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter your manager's first name if you have one.

### Manager Last Name (`manager_last_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter your manager's last name if you have one.

### Work Email (`work_email`, `email`, optional)
Enter your work email address for contact purposes.

### Work Phone (`work_phone`, `text`, optional)
Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.

### Department (`department`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the department you belong to from the dropdown menu.

### Job Title (`job_title`, `text`, optional)
Enter your job title to provide more context about your role.

### Years Worked (`years_worked`, `number`, optional)
Enter the number of years you have worked in your current department.

### Manager Relationship (`manager_relationship_rating`, `number`, optional)
Rate your relationship with your manager on a scale of 1-5.

### Feedback (`feedback`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding your feedback about your manager.

### Manager Survey (`manager_survey_rating`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding your manager's performance.

### Job Performance (`job_performance_rating`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding your job performance.

### Communication (`communication_rating`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding your manager's communication.

### Leadership (`leadership_rating`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding your manager's leadership.

### Feedback Frequency (`feedback_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding how often you receive feedback from your manager.

### Support (`support_rating`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding the support you receive from your manager.

### Recognition (`recognition_rating`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding the recognition you receive from your manager.

### Feedback Availability (`feedback_availability`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding how often you have access to feedback.

### Training and Development (`training_and_development`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding the importance of training and development opportunities from your manager.

### Opportunities for Advancement (`opportunities_for_advancement`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding the opportunities for advancement you have with your manager.

### Work-Life Balance (`worklife_balance`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding the balance between your work and personal life.

### Overall Satisfaction (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the dropdown menu regarding your overall satisfaction with your manager.

Note: Please make sure to select all questions honestly to help improve the work environment!
