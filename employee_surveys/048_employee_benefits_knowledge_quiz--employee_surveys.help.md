# Employee Benefits Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your knowledge of employee benefits. This quiz will ask you a series of questions about different types of employee benefits, such as health insurance, retirement plans, and life insurance. By completing this quiz, you will be able to demonstrate your understanding of these important benefits and how they can support your well-being and financial security.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction page, which provides some background information about the benefits offered by your company.
2. Answer the questions on each page, making sure to select the correct options for each benefit type.
3. For each question, select only one option for single-answer questions (e.g., "Benefits 1") and multiple options for multiple-choice questions (e.g., "Benefits 2").
4. Enter your demographic information (e.g., name, email) when prompted.
5. Complete the time and date fields as needed.
6. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (Page 1, Required: False)
    The introduction page provides background information about the benefits offered by your company.

### Benefits
* **Benefits** (Page 2, Required: True)
    This question asks you to select one benefit type from the options provided. Select the correct option that best fits your benefits knowledge.

### Employee Information
* **Employee Information** (Page 3, Required: False)
    This is a numeric field where you can enter your demographic information, such as your age or years of service.

### Benefits 2
* **Benefits 2** (Page 4, Required: False)
    This question is multiple-choice, allowing you to select multiple benefit types. Select all applicable options from the list.

### Benefits 3
* **Benefits 3** (Page 5, Required: False)
    Similar to "Benefits 1", this question is single-choice, selecting only one benefit type from the options provided.

### Time
* **Time** (Page 6, Required: False)
    This field is not relevant for most users; it seems to be a generic "time" field without any further specification.

### Date
* **Date** (Page 7, Required: False)
    This field is not used in this form, but it might be related to the date of birth or other date-related benefits.

### Email
* **Email** (Page 8, Required: False)
    This is an email field where you can enter your company email address or other relevant contact information.
