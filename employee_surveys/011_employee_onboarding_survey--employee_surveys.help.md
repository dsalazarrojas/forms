# Employee Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Onboarding Survey is designed to measure the effectiveness of our onboarding process and gather feedback from new employees. This helps us identify areas for improvement and ensure that future employees have a smooth transition into their new role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide basic information about yourself**: On page 1, fill in the "Employee Information" section with your name, position, and department (if applicable).
2. **Share your onboarding experience**: On page 2, briefly describe your overall onboarding experience. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand what worked well and what could be improved.
3. **Select your job role**: On page 3, choose the job role you were assigned, such as Data Analyst, IT Support, or Marketing Manager.
4. **Provide training hours and completion time**: On page 4, report the number of hours you spent on training and the number of days it took you to complete your onboarding process.
5. **Access to equipment**: On page 5, select which equipment you have access to in your new role.
6. **Choose your onboarding buddy**: On page 6, select whether you have an onboarding buddy assigned to you.
7. **Evaluate supervisor communication**: On page 7, rate how well you feel your supervisor communicated with you during your onboarding process.
8. **Select your onboarding plan**: On page 8, choose the level of onboarding plan you received, such as Comprehensive, Basic, or None.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information**: This section is used to gather basic contact information about you, which will help us keep you informed about company news and updates.
  * **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID badge.
  * **Position**: Select your job title or position.
  * **Department**: If you're part of a specific department, select it here.
* **Onboarding Experience**: This section is used to collect your feedback about your onboarding experience. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us improve the process.
* **Job Role**: This field asks you to select the job role you were assigned, such as Data Analyst, IT Support, or Marketing Manager.
* **Training Hours**: Report the number of hours you spent on training to help us assess the effectiveness of our training program.
* **Days to Complete Onboarding**: Report the number of days it took you to complete your onboarding process.
* **Access to Equipment**: Select which equipment you have access to in your new role, such as computer, internet, or software tools.
* **Onboarding Buddy**: Check if you have an onboarding buddy assigned to you to assist with your transition.
* **Supervisor Communication**: Rate how well your supervisor communicated with you during your onboarding process. Choose one of the following options: Good, Needs Improvement, or Poor.
* **Onboarding Plan**: Select the level of onboarding plan you received, such as Comprehensive, Basic, or None.
