# Workplace Culture Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Culture Monitoring Form is a series of questions designed to gauge employees' perceptions of their work environment, manager support, and other key aspects of their work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as necessary to help us understand your perspective.
2. Please answer all required questions, denoted by the asterisk (*).
3. For text fields, you can type in as much or as little information as you like, but please be concise and clear in your answers.
4. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or more of the provided options that best fit your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, text, optional): Describe your current work environment. What is it like to come to work every day? Do you feel supported and valued?
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, select_one, required*): How satisfied are you with the level of support you receive from your manager?
	+ Highly Satisfied: You feel fully supported and valued by your manager.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: Your manager is somewhat supportive, but there is room for improvement.
	+ Not Satisfied: You feel unsupported or undervalued by your manager.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required*): Please provide details about the communication in your team. How do team members interact with each other? Are there any issues or challenges you'd like to mention?
* **Team Collaboration** (`team_collaboration`, select_multiple, optional): How would you rate the level of collaboration within your team?
	+ High: You feel like you're working as a cohesive unit, with everyone supporting each other.
	+ Medium: You're working somewhat collaboratively, but there's room for improvement.
	+ Low: You feel like you're working alone, with limited collaboration from others.
* **Diversity and Inclusion** (`diversity_inclusion`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about diversity and inclusion within your team, please share them here.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_one, required*): How satisfied are you with your overall work experience?
	+ High: You feel fully satisfied with your job and your work environment.
	+ Medium: You're somewhat satisfied, but there are some areas for improvement.
	+ Low: You're not satisfied with your job or work environment.
