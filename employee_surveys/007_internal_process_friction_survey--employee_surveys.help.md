# Internal Process Friction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help identify and address internal process friction points that affect your daily work. Your responses will help us understand where and how we can improve our internal processes to make your work easier and more efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the best options for each question.
3. For free text fields, describe your answers in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are the biggest workflow bottlenecks in your workday?** (`workflow_bottlenecks`, text, optional): Describe the biggest obstacles you face in your workflow.
* **How many work hours are lost due to bottlenecks?** (`work_hours`, number, required): Estimate the number of hours you lose due to workflow bottlenecks per week.
* **What are the biggest pain points in the current process?** (`pain_points`, text, required): Describe the areas where you experience the most difficulties or frustrations.
* **What solutions are needed to eliminate bottlenecks?** (`solutions_needed`, select_multiple, required): Choose the solutions you think would help resolve the bottlenecks.
* **Do you want to receive emails when new submissions are made?** (`email_needed`, text, optional): Select one of the available options.
* **Comments** (`submit_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to add.
* **Submission Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submit this survey.
* **Submission Time** (`submit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submit this survey.
* **Who are the team members involved?** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required): Choose the team members affected by the process.
* **Who is the team lead?** (`team_lead`, select_one, required): Select the team lead who should be informed about your feedback.
* **Who is your manager?** (`manager`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select your manager.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, number, optional): Enter your employee number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your location.
* **How many work hours do you work per week?** (`work_hours_per_week`, number, optional): Estimate the number of hours you work per week.
* **How many overtime hours do you work per week?** (`overtime_hours`, number, optional): Estimate the number of overtime hours you work per week.
* **How many work hours are lost to workflow bottlenecks?** (`work_hours_lost`, number, required): Estimate the number of hours lost due to workflow bottlenecks.
* **What percentage of your work hours are wasted?** (`work_hours_wasted`, number, required): Estimate the percentage of your work hours that are wasted.
* **What causes the most time waste in your workflow?** (`time_waste`, select_multiple, required): Choose the reasons for the time waste.
* **What are the reasons for the time waste?** (`time_waste_reasons`, text, required): Describe the reasons for the time waste.
* **What solutions have been implemented to address bottlenecks?** (`solutions_implemented`, text, required): Describe the solutions implemented.
* **What are the reasons for the solutions implemented?** (`solutions_implemented_reasons`, text, required): Describe the reasons for the solutions implemented.
* **Success of solutions implemented** (`solutions_implemented_success`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the success level of the solutions implemented.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to add.
