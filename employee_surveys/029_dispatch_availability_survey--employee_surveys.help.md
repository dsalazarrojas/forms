# Dispatch Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your availability and schedule preferences for dispatching work. This information will help us better understand your work schedule and make more efficient dispatching decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Select the options that best fit your schedule and preferences.
3. Add any additional notes or comments if needed.
4. Double-check your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Availability
* **Availability** (`dispatch_availability`, text): Enter a brief description of your current availability.

### Shift Preferences
* **Shift Preferences** (`shift_preferences`, select_multiple): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Early Morning
	+ Late Night
	+ Weekends

### Scheduling Constraints
* **Scheduling Constraints** (`scheduling_constraints`, text): Enter any scheduling constraints or limitations you have.

### Shift Start Time
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, date): Enter the start time of your shift.

### Shift End Time
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, date): Enter the end time of your shift.

### Days Available
* **Days Available** (`days_available`, select_one): Select one or more of the following:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday

### Shift Length
* **Shift Length** (`shift_length`, number): Enter the length of your shift (e.g., 8 hours, 10 hours, 12 hours).

### Break Time
* **Break Time** (`break_time`, number): Enter the length of your break time.

### Available Shifts
* **Available Shifts** (`available_shifts`, select_multiple): Select one or more of the following:
	+ 8 hours
	+ 10 hours
	+ 12 hours

### Available Days
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple): Select one or more of the following:
	+ Monday to Friday
	+ Tuesday to Thursday
	+ Wednesday to Friday

### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time): Enter the start time of your shift.

### End Time
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Enter the end time of your shift.

### Shift Start
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start`, date): Enter the start date of your shift.

### Shift End
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end`, date): Enter the end date of your shift.

### Break
* **Break** (`break`, note): Add any additional notes about your break time.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note): Add any additional notes or comments about your availability and schedule.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact`, text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text): Enter any additional contact information.

### Supervisor Name
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor`, text): Enter the name of your supervisor.

### Team Name
* **Team Name** (`team`, text): Enter the name of your team.

### Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (`manager`, text): Enter the name of your manager.

### Location
* **Location** (`location`, text): Enter your location.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.

# Tips
* Make sure to double-check your submissions for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your participation in this survey, which will help us better understand your work schedule and preferences.
