# humble_hungry_smart_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and performance metrics from team members and possibly from managers. It's a mix of dropdowns, date/time fields, and text areas that aim to collect data on team development, goals, submission status, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the introduction page.
2. Select your team members from the dropdown list.
3. Choose your feedback questions from the available options.
4. Rate the team's performance using the number field.
5. Provide feedback in the text area.
6. Add any additional comments.
7. Select the submission status.
8. Choose the submission date and time.
9. Select the team development areas.
10. Choose the submission status again.
11. Add any submission comments.
12. Enter the manager's name.
13. Enter the team lead's name.
14. Select if team members are aware of the survey.
15. Choose the survey completion date and time.
16. Select the survey submission status.
17. Enter the team development goals.
18. Add feedback questions comments.
19. Add submission notes.
20. Add survey completion comments.
21. Enter the submission date.
22. Enter team development areas comments.
23. Add manager notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This is a page with a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the team members you're providing feedback on.
* **Feedback Questions** (`feedback_questions`, select_one, required: false): Choose the feedback questions you'd like to answer.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the team's performance using a number.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Provide your feedback on the team's performance.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments you'd like to make.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Select the submission date.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Select the submission time.
* **Team Development Areas** (`team_development_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the team development areas.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required: true): Choose the submission status.
* **Submission Date and Time** (`submission_date_time`, date, required: false): Select the submission date and time.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, text, required: false): Add any submission comments.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the manager's name.
* **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, text, required: false): Enter the team lead's name.
* **Team Members Aware of Survey** (`team_members_aware_of_survey`, select_one, required: false): Select if team members are aware of the survey.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_completion_date`, date, required: false): Select the survey completion date.
* **Survey Completion Time** (`survey_completion_time`, time, required: false): Select the survey completion time.
* **Survey Submission Status** (`survey_submission_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the survey submission status.
* **Team Development Goals** (`team_development_goals`, text, required: false): Enter the team development goals.
* **Feedback Questions Comments** (`feedback_questions_comments`, text, required: false): Add feedback questions comments.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, text, required: false): Add submission notes.
* **Survey Completion Comments** (`survey_completion_comments`, text, required: false): Add survey completion comments.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Select the submission date.
* **Team Development Areas Comments** (`team_development_areas_comments`, text, required: false): Add team development areas comments.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, text, required: false): Enter the manager's notes.
* **Survey Submission Date** (`survey_submission_date`, date, required: false): Select the survey submission date.

## Tips

* Remember to save your progress and review your completed form before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the team.
