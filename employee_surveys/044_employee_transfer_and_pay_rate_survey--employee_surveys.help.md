# Employee Transfer And Pay Rate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Transfer And Pay Rate Survey is used to document employee transfers and changes in pay rates. This information is used to keep track of employee movements and compensation adjustments within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Enter the start date of the employee's transfer in the "Transfer Start Date" field.
4. Enter the end date of the employee's transfer in the "Transfer End Date" field.
5. Enter the amount of pay change in the "Pay Change" field.
6. Provide a reason for the pay change in the "Reasons For Pay Change" field.
7. Enter the name of the person reviewing the transfer in the "Reviewer Name" field.
8. Enter the date the transfer was reviewed in the "Review Date" field.
9. (Optional) Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
10. (Optional) Enter any comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the employee being transferred.
  - **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the employee ID number associated with the employee.
  - **Transfer Start Date** (`transfer_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee started their transfer.
  - **Transfer End Date** (`transfer_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee ended their transfer.
  - **Pay Change** (`pay_change`, number, required): Enter the amount of pay change, if any.
  - **Reasons For Pay Change** (`reasons_for_pay_change`, text, required): Provide a reason for the pay change, if any.
  - **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person reviewing the transfer.
  - **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date the transfer was reviewed.
  - **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the transfer.
  - **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the transfer.
