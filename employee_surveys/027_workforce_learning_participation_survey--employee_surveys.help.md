# Workforce Learning Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the participation of employees in workforce learning programs, assessing their effectiveness and impact on employees' skills and knowledge development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to provide detailed and specific feedback where possible.
3. Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the learning program?** (`what_is_the_learning_program`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the learning program you have participated in or have knowledge about.
* **Have you completed the program?** (`which_program_was_completed`, select one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have completed the program, 'No' otherwise.
* **How useful is the program?** (`how_useful_is_the_program`, number, optional): Rate the program's usefulness on a scale from 1 (not useful) to 5 (very useful).
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the learning program.
* **Email feedback** (`email_feedback`, email, optional): Provide your email address for any further contact or discussion.
* **Phone number feedback** (`phone_number_feedback`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any further contact or discussion.
* **Date feedback** (`date_feedback`, date, optional): Enter the date you provided feedback about the program.
* **Time feedback** (`time_feedback`, time, optional): Enter the time you provided feedback about the program.
