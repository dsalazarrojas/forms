# Employee Relationship Dynamics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Relationship Dynamics Survey is a tool used to collect data about employee relationships within an organization, including reporting hierarchies, team structures, and supervisor relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your current role and relationships within the organization.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Review your answers for consistency and clarity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Employee Survey** (`employee_survey`, `text`, optional)
This field is a text box where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your relationship dynamics.

### 2. **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose the name of your reporting manager from the options provided.

### 3. **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `select_one`, optional)
Indicate if you have a team lead or not.

### 4. **Direct Supervisor** (`direct_supervisor`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the people who are your direct supervisors.

### 5. **Manager** (`manager`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the managers who report to you.

### 6. **Direct Report** (`direct_report`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the people who directly report to you.

### 7. **Indirect Report** (`indirect_report`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the people who indirectly report to you.

### 8. **Reporting Level** (`reporting_level`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose the level of your reporting relationship.

### 9. **Direct Reporting** (`direct_reporting`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the people you directly report to.

### 10. **Indirect Reporting** (`indirect_reporting`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the people you indirectly report to.

### 11. **Reporting Level 1** (`reporting_level_1`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Choose the level of your first-level reporting relationship.

### 12. **Reporting Level 2** (`reporting_level_2`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Choose the level of your second-level reporting relationship.

### 13. **Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, optional)
Enter the size of your team.

### 14. **Team Size Range** (`team_size_range`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the range of your team size.

### 15. **Team Size Range 1** (`team_size_range_1`, `select_one`, optional)
Select whether your team size is within the range.

### 16. **Direct Supervisor Name** (`direct_supervisor_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter the name of your direct supervisor.

### 17. **Direct Supervisor Title** (`direct_supervisor_title`, `text`, optional)
Enter the title of your direct supervisor.

### 18. **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter the name of your manager.

### 19. **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, `text`, optional)
Enter the title of your manager.

### 20. **Direct Report Name** (`direct_report_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter the name of your direct report.

### 21. **Direct Report Title** (`direct_report_title`, `text`, optional)
Enter the title of your direct report.

### 22. **Direct Supervisor Email** (`direct_supervisor_email`, `email`, optional)
Enter the email of your direct supervisor.

### 23. **Direct Supervisor Phone** (`direct_supervisor_phone`, `text`, optional)
Enter the phone number of your direct supervisor.

### 24. **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional)
Enter the email of your manager.

### 25. **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional)
Enter the phone number of your manager.

## Tips

* Please answer each question accurately and consistently.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, ask your HR representative or supervisor for guidance.
* This form is for organizational development and performance evaluation purposes only.
* The information collected will be kept confidential and used only for the intended purposes.
