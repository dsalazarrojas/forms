# Organizational Culture Alignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Culture Alignment Survey aims to understand the alignment between individual employees and their team or department's culture. This survey gathers information about employee perceptions of their departmentalization, cultural identity, manager and team support, and leadership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Surname.
2. Enter your Employee Given Name.
3. Provide your Employee Family Name.
4. Select your department from the provided options.
5. Choose your Team Leadership level (High, Medium, or Low).
6. Choose your Employee Job Title from the provided options.
7. Enter your Employee Manager's name.
8. Choose your Manager's Job Title.
9. Provide a score for your Employee Cultural Identity Score.
10. Provide a score for your Team Cultural Identity Score.
11. Choose your Departmentalization level (High, Medium, or Low).
12. Choose your Manager's Support level (High, Medium, or Low).
13. Choose your Team's Support level (High, Medium, or Low).
14. Choose your Manager's Leadership level (High, Medium, or Low).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Surname** (`employee_surname`, text, optional): Your last name as an employee.
* **Employee Given Name** (`employee_given_name`, text, optional): Your first name as an employee.
* **Employee Family Name** (`employee_family_name`, text, optional): Your middle or other family names as an employee.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, select_multiple, optional): The department you belong to.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, time, optional): Your perception of your team's leadership level (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, select_one, optional): Your job title within the organization.
* **Employee Manager Name** (`employee_manager_name`, text, optional): Your manager's name.
* **Manager Job Title** (`manager_job_title`, select_one, optional): Your manager's job title.
* **Employee Cultural Identity Score** (`employee_cultural_identity_score`, number, optional): A score indicating how well you align with the organization's culture (1-10).
* **Team Cultural Identity Score** (`team_cultural_identity_score`, number, optional): A score indicating how well your team aligns with the organization's culture (1-10).
* **Departmentalization** (`departmentalization`, select_one, optional): Your perception of your department's level of departmentalization (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, select_one, optional): Your perception of your manager's support level (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Team Support** (`team_support`, select_one, optional): Your perception of your team's support level (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Manager Leadership** (`manager_leadership`, select_one, optional): Your perception of your manager's leadership level (High, Medium, or Low).

Note: All fields are optional, but your feedback is valuable to help create a more inclusive and supportive work environment. Please be honest and fill in the fields that are relevant to you.
