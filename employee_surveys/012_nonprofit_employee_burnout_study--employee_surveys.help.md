# Nonprofit Employee Burnout Study - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about nonprofit employees' burnout prevalence and other related aspects, aiming to provide valuable insights for organizational and individual well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the most appropriate answer for each question based on your current situation and understanding.
2. Please note that some questions have multiple select options, choose the one that best suits your experience and perception.
3. Ensure that you have enough information about the topic before answering.
4. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nonprofit Employee Burnout Study**: This is the main category you will select to identify which type of study you would like to answer: Burnout, Exhaustion, Cynicism, Lack of Engagement, etc.
* **Employee Demographics**: Select all applicable demographics that apply to you from the provided options (Age, Job Title, Department, Job Level, Length of Service).
* **Job Characteristics**: Choose all relevant job characteristics (Job Involvement, Job Autonomy, Job Feedback, Job Security, Job Insecurity, Job Overload, Job Underload, Job Variety, Job Complexity, Job Change, Job Pressure, Job Conflict).
* **Burnout Factors**: Select all applicable burnout factors (Overwork, Underwork, Time Pressure, Lack of Autonomy, Lack of Control, Lack of Feedback, Lack of Support, Lack of Resources).
* **Wellbeing Scale**: Choose how high or low you rate your wellbeing, from High to Extremely Low.
* **Work Life Balance**: Select how you rate your work-life balance from High to Extremely Low.
* **Burnout Prevalence**: Indicate if burnout is or is not prevalent in your workplace.
* **Job Stress**: Choose your level of job stress from High to Extremely Low.
* **Physical Health**: Select how you rate your physical health from Excellent to Poor.
* **Mental Health**: Choose how you rate your mental health from Excellent to Poor.
* **Sleep Quality**: Rate your sleep quality from Excellent to Poor.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you are with your job, from High to Extremely Low.
