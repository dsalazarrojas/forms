# Remote Work Ergonomic Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to identify and assess potential ergonomic risks for employees working remotely. It aims to gather information on the employee's workstation setup, ergonomic risks, and solutions to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the corresponding options for each field based on your current working situation. If you're unsure about any of the questions, refer to the company's policies on remote work or consult with your supervisor or HR department.
2. Answer the questions accurately and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to fill out the form once for each employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote Work Ergonomic Assessment 1** (`remote_work_ergonomic_assessment_1`, Text, Not Required): This field is for you to describe your current working situation, such as working from home, coffee shop, or co-working space.
* **Ergonomic Assessment** (`ergonomic_assessment`, Text, Not Required): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your workstation and ergonomic risks.
* **Ergonomic Risks** (`ergonomic_risks`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose from the provided options the ergonomic risks you're currently facing, such as sitting for long periods, poor lighting, or eye strain.
* **Ergonomic Solutions** (`ergonomic_solutions`, Select One, Not Required): Select one solution from the provided options that you believe can help mitigate the ergonomic risks you've selected, such as using a standing desk or taking breaks.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Not Required): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel file.
* **Employee Department** (`employee_department`, Text, Not Required): Select the department you belong to from the provided options, if applicable.
* **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, Text, Not Required): Enter your job title as it appears in the company's HR system.
* **Employee Workstation Setup** (`employee_workstation_setup`, Text, Not Required): Describe your current workstation setup, including any specific equipment or tools you use.
* **Ergonomic Issues** (`ergonomic_issues`, Text, Not Required): List any ergonomic issues you're currently facing, such as back pain or neck strain.
* **Ergonomic Comments** (`ergonomic_comments`, Text, Not Required): Add any additional comments or concerns about your ergonomic situation.
* **Date Of Survey** (`date_of_survey`, Date, Not Required): Select the date when this survey was completed.
* **Time Of Survey** (`time_of_survey`, Time, Not Required): Select the time when this survey was completed.
* **Location Of Survey** (`location_of_survey`, Text, Not Required): Enter the location where this survey was completed.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, Text, Not Required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, Email, Not Required): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, Text, Not Required): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, Text, Not Required): Add any comments or concerns from your supervisor about your ergonomic situation.
* **Remote Work Setup** (`remote_work_setup`, Text, Not Required): Describe your current remote work setup, including any specific equipment or tools you use.
* **Ergonomic Setup** (`ergonomic_setup`, Text, Not Required): Describe your current ergonomic setup, including any specific equipment or tools you use.
* **Workstation Setup** (`workstation_setup`, Text, Not Required): Describe your current workstation setup, including any specific equipment or tools you use.
* **Ergonomic Risk Factors** (`ergonomic_risk_factors`, Text, Not Required): List any ergonomic risk factors you're facing, such as sitting for long periods or poor posture.
* **Ergonomic Risk Control** (`ergonomic_risk_control`, Text, Not Required): Describe any measures you're taking or have taken to control the ergonomic risks you've selected.
* **Ergonomic Risk Frequency** (`ergonomic_risk_frequency`, Text, Not Required): Indicate how often you're facing the ergonomic risks you've selected, such as daily or occasional.
* **Ergonomic Risk Severity** (`ergonomic_risk_severity`, Text, Not Required): Describe the severity of the ergonomic risks you're facing, such as mild, moderate, or severe.
