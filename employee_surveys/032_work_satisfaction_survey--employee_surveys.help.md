# Work Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees about their job satisfaction, providing valuable insights to help managers and HR teams make informed decisions to improve the work environment and employee well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and remember that your responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.
2. Select your answers from the provided options.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments about your work experience, feel free to share them in the "suggestions" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can type them in the "comments" field.
5. Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
6. Finally, provide your phone number (optional) and indicate if you are the team leader or manager of your department (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **work_satisfaction**: This question is meant to assess your overall satisfaction with your current job. Answer based on your feelings and experiences. 
    * **job**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are satisfied with your current job.
    * **pay**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you feel your pay is satisfactory.
    * **benefits**: Choose "Yes" or "No" for each benefit that you feel is satisfactory.
    * **people**: Select "Yes" or "No" for each group of people mentioned.
    * **pay_scale**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you feel your pay scale is satisfactory.
    * **satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your current job, on a scale from 1-10.
    * **suggestions**: If you have any suggestions or feedback, please share them in this field.
    * **comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in this field.
    * **email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
    * **phone**: Provide your phone number (optional).
    * **manager**: Indicate whether you are the manager of your team (optional).
    * **team_leader**: Indicate whether you are the leader of your team (optional).
    * **team_members**: Select "Yes" or "No" for each group of team members.
    * **department**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are satisfied with your department.
    * **division**: Select "Yes" or "No" for each division.
    * **work_satisfaction_1**-**work_satisfaction_9**: Rate your satisfaction with specific aspects of your job on a scale from 1-10.
