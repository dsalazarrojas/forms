# Diversity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect diversity data from employees to help the company create a more inclusive work environment. Please answer the questions honestly, and make sure to fill out the form only once per employee.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the link to access the form.
2. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions.
3. Make sure to answer truthfully and accurately.
4. Click "Submit" to submit your completed form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Please enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time Of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select your department.
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the teams you belong to.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ethnicities that apply to you.
* **Race** (`race`, select_one, optional): Select your race.
* **Religion** (`religion`, select_one, optional): Select your religion.
* **Disability Status** (`disability_status`, select_one, optional): Select your disability status.
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, optional): Select your native language.
* **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, select_one, optional): Select how you perform your job.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): Select your education level.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Work Days** (`work_days`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you work per week.
* **Vacation Days** (`vacation_days`, number, optional): Enter the number of vacation days you have taken this year.
* **Sick Leave** (`sick_leave`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the sick leave options that apply to you.
* **Flexible Work Arrangements** (`flexible_work_arrangements`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the flexible work arrangements that apply to you.
* **Work From Home** (`work_from_home`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options for working from home that apply to you.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select how satisfied you are with your job.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, select_one, optional): Select your employee type.
