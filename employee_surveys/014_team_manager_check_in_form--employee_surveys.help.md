# team_manager_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "team_manager_check_in_form" is a form designed for team managers to check in team members and gather important information for team updates and feedback. This form is intended for use by team managers to facilitate efficient and organized communication within the team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select team members by clicking on the "team_member" field.
2. Enter relevant information in the "team_status" field, including updates or comments about each selected team member.
3. In the "team_update" field, provide any additional updates or notes about the team.
4. In the "team_feedback" field, offer feedback or suggestions for the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team_member**: Select one or multiple team members to include in the team update. This field allows you to choose from a list of options. Please select the team members that are relevant to the current update.
* **team_status**: Enter any relevant information or updates about each selected team member. This field is for providing detailed updates or comments about each team member's status.
* **team_update**: Enter any additional updates or notes about the team as a whole. This field is for providing general information about the team's progress or changes.
* **team_feedback**: Offer feedback or suggestions for the team. This field is for providing constructive feedback or ideas for improvement.
