# Corporate Employee Benefits Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to vote on the top corporate benefits that they value the most. It will help us understand the preferences and priorities of our team members, which in turn will inform our company's benefit offerings and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select up to 3 options from the "Employee Surveys" page that best represent your preferences for corporate benefits.
2. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them in the "Add any comments" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, so we can contact you with the results of the survey.
4. If you'd like, you can also provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Lastly, please select your preferred time slot for the voting period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Surveys (Select up to 3)** (`Employee Surveys`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This is where you'll select up to 3 options from the list that best represent the corporate benefits that you value the most. Please choose the ones that resonate with you the most.
* **What are the top 3 benefits that you value the most? (Select up to 3)** (`benefit_priorities`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Similar to the previous question, please select up to 3 options from the list that best represent the top benefits you value the most.
* **Add any comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions for the corporate benefits, please type them in here.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with the results of the survey.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): If you'd like, you can provide your phone number to enable us to contact you.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Please select your preferred time slot for the voting period.

## Tips

* Please select only the options that truly represent your preferences and priorities.
* Feel free to add comments or suggestions if you have any.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
