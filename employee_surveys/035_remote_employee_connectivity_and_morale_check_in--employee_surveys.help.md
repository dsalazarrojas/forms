# Remote Employee Connectivity And Morale Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect employee feedback on their experiences and perceptions related to remote work and connectivity. The goal is to gather information on how employees feel connected to their team, manager, and department, as well as their satisfaction with working remotely. The feedback collected will help management and HR to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance remote work experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best reflect your experiences and perceptions.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Please provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
