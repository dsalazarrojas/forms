# DEI Perception Employee Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The DEI Perception Employee Survey Form is designed to collect employees' perceptions on Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion topics. This form is meant to be completed by employees to provide feedback and insights on their workplace environment and how DEI initiatives can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

*   Start by selecting one of the three options provided in the "Employee Survey" section.
*   Read the "Introduction" section carefully, which explains the purpose and instructions for the survey.
*   For "Section 1", select all that apply from the options provided.
*   For "Section 2", select all that apply from the options provided.
*   Finally, select one of the three options provided in the "Section 3" section.
