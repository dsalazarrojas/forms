# Startup Workplace Culture Employment Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather employee feedback on various aspects of the startup culture, leadership, and work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question thoughtfully and honestly. You can choose one or more of the options provided for each question, depending on your personal experience and opinion. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments or Suggestions" section at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think of our startup culture?** (Page 1, Question 1)
	+ This question asks for your overall impression of the startup culture. Please write a brief description of your thoughts and feelings about the company culture.
* **How would you rate the leadership team?** (Page 2, Question 2)
	+ This question asks you to rate the leadership team's performance. Choose one of the options from the list provided, based on your experience working with the leadership team.
* **How would you describe your work-life balance at our startup?** (Page 3, Question 3)
	+ This question asks you to rate your work-life balance. Select all the options that apply to you, as you may have experienced different aspects of work-life balance during your time at the startup.
* **How satisfied are you with your job?** (Page 4, Question 4)
	+ This question asks for your level of satisfaction with your job. Choose one of the options from the list provided, based on your experience and feelings about your job.
* **How would you rate the communication between teams and departments?** (Page 5, Question 5)
	+ This question asks you to rate the effectiveness of communication between teams and departments. Choose one of the options from the list provided, based on your experience and observations.
* **How important is it for you to have growth opportunities?** (Page 6, Question 6)
	+ This question asks you to indicate how important it is for you to have growth opportunities at the startup. Choose one of the options from the list provided, based on your personal priorities.
* **How would you rate the team dynamics?** (Page 7, Question 7)
	+ This question asks you to rate the team's performance and relationships. Choose one of the options from the list provided, based on your experience working with the team.
* **How well does the company align with your personal values?** (Page 8, Question 8)
	+ This question asks you to rate how well the company's values align with your personal values. Choose one of the options from the list provided, based on your experience and feelings.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to feedback on?** (Page 9, Question 9)
	+ This question asks you to provide any additional feedback or comments you may have about the startup culture, leadership, or work environment.
* **Comments or suggestions?** (Page 10, Question 10)
	+ This is an open-ended question for you to provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the startup culture, leadership, or work environment.
