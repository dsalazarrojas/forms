# HR Responsiveness Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from employees on the responsiveness and effectiveness of the HR department. Your input will help identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for HR to enhance their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your personal experience and interaction with HR.
2. Provide clear and concise answers in the required fields.
3. Use the "Any comments or suggestions" fields to add any additional information you deem relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (`id: 12`, `type: note`): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you have on the HR department's performance.
* **Any other comments or suggestions** (`id: 12`, `type: note`): Similar to the previous field, use this to add any other comments or suggestions you have for the HR department.
* **How much time do you have to respond to HR** (`id: 9`, `type: time`): Estimate the average time it takes you to respond to HR-related requests or inquiries.
* **Any comments on time taken** (`id: 11`, `type: note`): Provide any comments or suggestions on the time it takes HR to respond to your requests or inquiries.
* **Professionalism of HR** (`id: 3`, `type: select_one`): Rate your experience with HR's professionalism on a scale from 1 to 4.
* **Effectiveness of HR** (`id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`): Select all that apply from the options provided to rate your experience with HR's effectiveness.
* **First Name** (`id: 5`, `type: text`): Enter your first name (if required).
* **Last Name** (`id: 6`, `type: text`): Enter your last name (if required).
* **Email** (`id: 7`, `type: email`): Enter your email (if required).
* **Contact Info** (`id: 8`, `type: select_one`): Select the most relevant option for your preferred contact method with HR.
* **How fast do you typically receive responses from HR** (`id: 2`, `type: number`): Estimate the average time it takes HR to respond to your requests or inquiries.
* **Any comments on professionalism** (`id: 10`, `type: note`): Provide any comments or suggestions on HR's professionalism.
