# leadership_communication_flow_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about leadership communication flow within an organization, helping teams and leaders understand how effectively they are communicating with their team members and identify potential barriers to effective leadership communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the introduction to understand the context and purpose of the form.
2. Answer the "Frequency of Leadership Communication" field by selecting how frequently you communicate with your leader(s).
3. Choose the appropriate channel(s) you use for communication with your leader(s) from the list of options provided.
4. Rate the effectiveness of your leader's communication with you from the options provided.
5. Select any barriers that impede effective communication with your leader(s).
6. Repeat steps 2-5 for communication with your team.
7. Finally, rate the effectiveness of your team's communication with your leader(s).

- **Frequency of Leadership Communication (Select Multiple)** (`id: 2`): How often do you communicate with your leader(s)? Select one or more of the options provided.
	+ Frequently

	+ Rarely

## Tips
- Answer honestly and accurately, as this form is designed to help improve leadership communication flow within your organization.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your leader or team for clarification.
- This form is not meant to be completed by leaders, but by team members, as it's focused on communication flow from team members' perspectives.
