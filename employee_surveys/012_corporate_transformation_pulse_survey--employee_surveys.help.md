# corporate_transformation_pulse_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on the company's transformation progress, their role, department, and overall sentiment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and honest answers to each question.
2. Ensure you have a clear understanding of your role and department affiliation.
3. Select your role in the company from the provided options.
4. Choose the departments you're affiliated with from the provided options.
5. Indicate your current sentiment about the company's transformation using the scale provided.
6. Rate your confidence in the company's change initiatives using the scale provided.
7. Write down any concerns you have about the transformation process.
8. Provide any recommendations for improvement.
9. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
10. Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
11. Provide the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
12. Provide the current time in the format HH:MM (24-hour format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Corporate Transformation** (`company_name`, `text`, required/optional): 
    Enter your understanding of the company's current transformation progress and initiatives.
- **Role in the Company** (`role`, `select_one`, required/optional): 
    Please select your role in the company from the options provided.
- **Department(s) Affiliated** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): 
    Choose the departments you're affiliated with from the provided options.
- **Current Sentiment** (`sentiment`, `select_one`, required/optional): 
    Indicate your current sentiment about the company's transformation using the scale provided.
- **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, `number`, required/optional): 
    Rate your experience with communication from the company's transformation team.
- **Confidence in Change Initiatives** (`confidence`, `select_one`, required/optional): 
    Rate your confidence in the company's change initiatives using the scale provided.
- **Concerns** (`concerns`, `text`, required/optional): 
    Write down any concerns you have about the transformation process.
- **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required/optional): 
    Provide any recommendations for improvement.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): 
    Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): 
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): 
    Provide the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): 
    Provide the current time in the format HH:MM (24-hour format).
