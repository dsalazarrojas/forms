# Creative Direction Self Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Creative Direction Self Assessment form helps employees evaluate their creative direction and self-awareness in various aspects of their work. This form is designed to be a self-assessment tool, helping employees identify areas of improvement and growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For questions with options, select the one that best represents your current skill level or approach.
3. For questions that ask for a text response, provide a clear and concise answer.
4. If you're unsure about a question or option, try to make an educated guess or provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you do here?** (`creative_direction_self_assessment`, text, required): This question is designed to help you reflect on your current role and responsibilities.
* **Goals and Objectives** (`goals_and_objectives`, text, optional): Describe your current goals and objectives, or what you aim to achieve in your role.
* **Team leadership** (`team_leadership`, text, required): Share your experience and approach to team leadership, including any successes or challenges you've faced.
* **Strategic Planning** (`strategic_planning`, select_one, required): Select the level of strategic planning you use in your work:
	+ Strategic: You often think ahead and plan for the future.
	+ Tactical: You focus on short-term tasks and goals.
	+ Both: You balance both strategic and tactical planning.
* **Communication skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Choose the level of communication skills you use in your work:
	+ Verbal: You prefer speaking and listening to others.
	+ Non-Verbal: You rely on writing and reading to communicate.
	+ Both: You use a combination of both verbal and non-verbal communication.
* **Project Management** (`project_management`, select_one, required): Select your approach to project management:
	+ Agile: You use an iterative and adaptive approach.
	+ Waterfall: You follow a linear and sequential approach.
	+ Both: You balance both Agile and Waterfall methodologies.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_one, required): Choose your approach to time management:
	+ Time: You focus on meeting deadlines and schedules.
	+ Focus: You prioritize tasks based on importance.
	+ Both: You balance both time and focus.
* **Leadership and Management** (`leadership_and_management`, text, required): Share your experience and approach to leadership and management.
* **Communication skills awareness** (`communication_skills_awareness`, select_one, required): Select your level of awareness about communication skills:
	+ Verbal: You recognize the importance of verbal communication.
	+ Non-Verbal: You recognize the importance of non-verbal communication.
	+ Both: You understand the value of both verbal and non-verbal communication.
* **What did you learn?** (`self_assessment`, note, optional): Use this space to reflect on what you've learned throughout this form.
* **What's the impact?** (`performance_conversations`, text, required): Describe the impact of your actions and decisions.
* **Next steps** (`action_planning`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the next steps you'll take based on your self-assessment:
	+ Plan: You'll create a plan to address areas of improvement.
	+ Do: You'll take action on your plan.
	+ Check: You'll review and evaluate your progress.
	+ Act: You'll adjust your approach as needed.
* **What's the evaluation?** (`evaluation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the evaluation of your performance:
	+ Positive: You've had a positive evaluation.
	+ Negative: You've had a negative evaluation.
	+ Neutral: You've had a neutral evaluation.
	+ Mixed: You've had a mixed evaluation.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Use specific examples and anecdotes to support your answers.
* Take your time to reflect on your answers before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you've covered all the required and optional questions.
