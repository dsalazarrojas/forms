# Team Role Ownership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Role Ownership Survey form is used to gather information on team role ownership within the team. It helps ensure that everyone has a clear understanding of their role and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your role and team responsibilities.
3. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager**: Select your role as a manager within the team.
* **Team Member**: Select your role as a team member, team lead, or supervisor.
* **Team Ownership**: Select the level of ownership you feel you have for your role in the team (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Comfort Level**: Select the level of comfort you have in performing your role (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Interest Level**: Select the level of interest you have in performing your role (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Support Level**: Enter a numerical value indicating the level of support you receive for your role.
* **New Roles**: Select any new roles you have taken on or are interested in taking on.
* **Other Roles**: Enter any other roles you have or are interested in taking on.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have on your role or team dynamics.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from your manager on your role or performance.
* **HR Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from HR on your role or performance.
* **Form Completed**: Select whether you have completed the form accurately and truthfully.
* **Completion Date**: Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Completion Time**: Enter the time it took you to complete the form.
* **Form Submitted**: Select whether you have submitted the form for review.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form (Manager, HR, or Team Member).
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Form Status**: Select the current status of the form (In Progress, Completed, or Not Started).
