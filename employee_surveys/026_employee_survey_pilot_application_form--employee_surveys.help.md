# employee_survey_pilot_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_survey_pilot_application_form" is used to gather information from employees about their participation in the pilot program. This form helps to evaluate employee suitability and assess their interest in joining the pilot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the required questions, marked as "required".
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_survey_pilot_application_form (Page 1, Text)** 
  - Label: This is a brief introduction or summary of the form.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
* **participant_selection (Page 2, Select One)** 
  - Label: Select "Yes" if you are interested in participating in the pilot program, or "No" if you are not.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
* **program_evaluation (Page 3, Text)** 
  - Label: This is a free-text field for you to provide a detailed evaluation of the program.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
* **participant_id (Page 4, Number)** 
  - Label: Enter your participant ID.
  - Type: Number
  - Required: Yes
* **employee_id (Page 5, Number)** 
  - Label: Enter your employee ID.
  - Type: Number
  - Required: Yes
* **consent_date (Page 6, Date)** 
  - Label: Enter the date you give consent to participate in the pilot program.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: Yes
* **consent_time (Page 7, Time)** 
  - Label: Enter the time you give consent to participate in the pilot program.
  - Type: Time
  - Required: Yes
* **email (Page 8, Email)** 
  - Label: Enter your email address.
  - Type: Email
  - Required: Yes
* **phone (Page 9, Text)** 
  - Label: Enter your phone number.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
* **note (Page 10, Note)** 
  - Label: Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
  - Type: Note
  - Required: No
