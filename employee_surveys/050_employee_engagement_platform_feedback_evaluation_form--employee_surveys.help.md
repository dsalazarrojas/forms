# Employee Engagement Platform Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from employees on their experience with the Employee Engagement Platform. It's essential to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the platform and provide better support to all users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each field, and submit your feedback when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Platform Feedback** (`employee_engagement_platform_feedback`, text, optional): Please share your thoughts about your overall experience with the Employee Engagement Platform. What do you like or dislike about it? Any suggestions for improvement?
- **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the platform on a scale from 1 (least satisfied) to 3 (most satisfied). This will help us understand your level of satisfaction with the platform.
- **Frequency of Use** (`frequency_of_use`, select multiple, optional): Select all the days you use the platform:
  * Option 1: Daily
  * Option 2: Several times a week
  * Option 3: Rarely
  * Option 4: Never (not yet)
- **Communication with Admin** (`communication_with_admin`, select one, optional): How would you rate your interactions with the admin? Select one:
  * Option 1: Excellent
  * Option 2: Good
  * Option 3: Fair
  * Option 4: Poor
- **Support Experience** (`support_experience`, number, optional): Rate your experience with our support team on a scale from 1 (least satisfied) to 3 (most satisfied). This will help us understand how well we're supporting you.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback, questions, or concerns you may have about the platform. This is an opportunity for you to provide more detailed feedback that may not have been covered in other fields.
