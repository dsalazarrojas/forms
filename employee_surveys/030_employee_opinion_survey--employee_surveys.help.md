# employee_opinion_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_opinion_survey form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience and opinions about the company's management, job satisfaction, company culture, and other aspects of their work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and respond thoughtfully.
2. Choose the options that best fit your opinions and experiences.
3. Provide clear and concise answers in the text fields.
4. Ensure you have answered all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you rate our company's management** (`employee_feedback`, select_one): Rate our company's management from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What do you feel about your job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_multiple): Choose between High, Neutral, and Low to express your opinion on your job satisfaction.
* **How would you rate our company culture?** (`company_culture`, number): Select a number from 1 to 10 to rate our company culture.
* **Communication with team members** (`communication`, select_one): Rate our communication with team members from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How secure do you feel about your job?** (`job_security`, select_multiple): Choose between High and Low to express your opinion on your job security.
* **How do you balance work and personal life?** (`work_life_balance`, text): Describe how you balance work and personal life, considering any challenges or successes you have faced.
* **What do you like about our management?** (`feedback_on_management`, select_one): Rate your positive opinions on our management from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What do you dislike about our management?** (`feedback_on_management`, select_multiple): Choose between High, Low to express your negative opinions on our management.
* **What do you like about our company culture?** (`feedback_on_company`, select_multiple): Choose between High, Low to express your positive opinions on our company culture.
* **What do you feel about your job satisfaction?** (`job_satisfaction`, text): Describe your feelings on your job satisfaction, considering any challenges or successes you have faced.
* **How secure do you feel about your job?** (`job_security`, select_multiple): Choose between High and Low to express your opinions on your job security.
* **How do you balance work and personal life?** (`work_life_balance`, text): Describe how you balance work and personal life, considering any challenges or successes you have faced.

## Tips
* Be honest and thoughtful in your responses.
* Ensure you have answered all required fields.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, feel free to share them with us.
