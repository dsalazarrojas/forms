# Year End Business Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Year End Business Review form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the company's performance, objectives, and overall experience. This feedback will help the organization identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions for the next year.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the most appropriate option from the provided choices.
3. For text fields, provide a brief but detailed description of your thoughts and experiences.
4. Make sure to submit your answers to ensure they are included in the year-end review.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Keep your answers brief but descriptive.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure you've answered all required questions. 

This guide should help employees complete the Year End Business Review form effectively and provide valuable feedback to support the company's growth and improvement.
