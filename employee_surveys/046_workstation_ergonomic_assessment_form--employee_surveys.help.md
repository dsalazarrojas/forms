# Workstation Ergonomic Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workstation Ergonomic Assessment Form is used to collect information about an employee's workstation and work environment to assess its ergonomic quality. The form helps to identify potential ergonomic issues and provide recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee information: Enter your employee name, job title, department, and supervisor name, job title, and department.
2. Enter workstation information: Describe your workstation location, status (Active, Pending, or Inactive), and comments.
3. Assess the workstation ergonomic score: Select an ergonomic score from 1 to 5 for your workstation, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
4. Provide additional comments and recommendations: Use the free-text fields for any additional comments or recommendations regarding workstation ergonomics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (Employee Name, text, **Required**): Enter your name.
* **employee_jobtitle** (Employee Job Title, text, **Optional**): Enter your job title.
* **employee_department** (Employee Department, text, **Optional**): Enter your department.
* **workstation** (Workstation, select_one, **Optional**): Select your workstation type (Ergo Chair, Adjustable Desk, Task Light, Monitor Arm).
* **ergo_rating** (Ergonomic Rating, number, **Optional**): Select an ergonomic score from 1 to 5 for your workstation.
* **worktime** (Work Time, time, **Optional**): Enter your work hours or work time.
* **workhours** (Work Hours, number, **Optional**): Enter your work hours or work time.
* **ergonomic_rating** (Ergonomic Rating, text, **Optional**): Provide your ergonomic assessment of your workstation.
* **ergonomic_comments** (Ergonomic Comments, note, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments or recommendations regarding workstation ergonomics.
* **ergonomic_score** (Ergonomic Score, number, **Optional**): Enter an ergonomic score from 1 to 5 for your workstation.
* **workstation_ergonomic_score** (Workstation Ergonomic Score, number, **Optional**): Enter an ergonomic score from 1 to 5 for your workstation.
* **employee_jobtitle** (Employee Job Title, text, **Optional**): Enter your job title.
* **employee_department** (Employee Department, text, **Optional**): Enter your department.
* **workstation_location** (Workstation Location, text, **Optional**): Enter your workstation location.
* **supervisor_name** (Supervisor Name, text, **Optional**): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **supervisor_jobtitle** (Supervisor Job Title, text, **Optional**): Enter your supervisor's job title.
* **supervisor_department** (Supervisor Department, text, **Optional**): Enter your supervisor's department.
* **supervisor_phone** (Supervisor Phone, text, **Optional**): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **supervisor_email** (Supervisor Email, email, **Optional**): Enter your supervisor's email.
* **ergo_comments** (Ergonomic Comments, note, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments or recommendations regarding workstation ergonomics.
* **workstation_comments** (Workstation Comments, note, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments or recommendations regarding workstation ergonomics.
* **workstation_status** (Workstation Status, select_one, **Optional**): Select your workstation status (Active, Pending, or Inactive).
* **workstation_status_comments** (Workstation Status Comments, note, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments or recommendations regarding workstation status.
* **ergonomic_recommendations** (Ergonomic Recommendations, note, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments or recommendations regarding workstation ergonomics.
* **submit** (Submit, text, **Optional**): Click submit to finalize the form.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and honest information regarding your workstation ergonomics.
* Use the free-text fields to provide additional comments or recommendations.
* If you are unsure about any questions, please reach out to your supervisor or ergonomics team.
