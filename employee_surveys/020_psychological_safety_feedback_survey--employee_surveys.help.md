# psychological_safety_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on psychological safety in the workplace. It helps employees provide their honest and confidential feedback to their managers, ensuring a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Fill in all the required fields.
3. You can skip optional fields if you don't have an opinion or answer.
4. For numerical fields, ensure you enter a valid number.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1, note, required): This section is a brief note to introduce the form and explain its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Feedback Scale** (2, number, required): This question asks you to rate your current psychological safety in the workplace. Please select a rating from 0 to 10, where 10 is the safest.
* **Comments** (3, text, optional): If you have any specific comments or concerns, please write them here. This is an opportunity to provide detailed feedback.
* **Suggestions** (4, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving psychological safety in the workplace, please list them here.
* **Manager Name** (5, text, required): Please enter the name of your manager or team lead.
* **Manager Title** (6, select_one, required): Please select whether your manager is aware of your concerns about psychological safety.
* **Department** (7, select_one, required): Please select whether your department has a strong focus on psychological safety.
* **Team** (8, select_multiple, optional): If you are part of a team, please select whether they also focus on psychological safety.
* **Manager** (9, text, required): Please enter your manager's name as it appears on their business card or email signature.
* **Rating Date** (10, date, required): Please select the date you last rated your psychological safety in the workplace.
* **Rating Average** (11, number, optional): This question is not applicable as it's intended for managers to calculate the average rating.
* **Prophets Rating** (12, number, required): Please rate the overall psychological safety of your workplace on a scale from 1 to 100.
* **Comments Count** (13, number, optional): This question is not applicable as it's intended for managers to count the number of comments.

Note: The questions marked as "required" must be filled in. Optional questions can be skipped, but keep in mind that they might provide valuable context for your feedback.
