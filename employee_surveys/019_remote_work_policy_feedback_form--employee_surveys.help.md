# Remote Work Policy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Work Policy Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their remote work experience. This form helps us understand their satisfaction with the remote work policy, challenges faced, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
- Choose the options that best fit your experience.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Suggestions for improvement" text field.
- Make sure to select all that apply for the resources you feel are lacking while working remotely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Remote Work Policy Satisfaction** (`remote_work_policy_satisfaction`): Select how satisfied you are with our remote work policy.
  - Satisfied: You are satisfied with the policy.
  - Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with the policy.
  - Neutral: You are neutral about the policy.
  - Dissatisfied: You are dissatisfied with the policy.
  - Very Dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with the policy.
- **Work Location** (`work_location`): Select all the locations where you work remotely.
  - Home: You work from home.
  - Office: You work from the office.
  - Coffee Shop: You work from a coffee shop or co-working space.
  - Co-working Space: You work from a co-working space.
  - Other: Select "Other" if your work location is not listed.
- **How often do you communicate with your team while working remotely?** (`communication`): Select how often you communicate with your team.
  - Daily: You communicate with your team daily.
  - Weekly: You communicate with your team weekly.
  - Rarely: You rarely communicate with your team.
  - Never: You never communicate with your team.
- **Time Zone** (`time_zone`): Select your time zone.
  - Pacific: You are in the Pacific time zone.
  - Mountain: You are in the Mountain time zone.
  - Central: You are in the Central time zone.
  - Eastern: You are in the Eastern time zone.
  - Other: Select "Other" if your time zone is not listed.
- **What are the biggest challenges you've faced while working remotely?** (`challenges_faced`): Describe the biggest challenges you've faced while working remotely.
- **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`): Provide any suggestions you have for improving our remote work policy.
- **Manager Support** (`manager_support`): Select if your manager is available to support you while working remotely.
  - Available: Your manager is available to support you.
  - Not Available: Your manager is not available to support you.
  - Neutral: Your manager is neutral, but you still have access to resources.
- **Resources** (`company_resources`): Select all the resources you feel are lacking while working remotely.
  - Computer: You need a computer.
  - Internet Connection: You need a stable internet connection.
  - Software: You need software for your work.
  - Collaboration Tools: You need collaboration tools for teamwork.
  - Other: Select "Other" if you need something else.
- **How does your work-life balance change while working remotely?** (`work_life_balance`): Select how your work-life balance changes while working remotely.
  - Better: Your work-life balance is better.
  - Worse: Your work-life balance is worse.
  - Neutral: Your work-life balance is neutral.
- **Job satisfaction level** (`job_satisfaction`): Select your job satisfaction level.
  - (Note: This field is required)
- **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction level (1-10).
  - (Note: This field is not required, but it provides valuable feedback)
