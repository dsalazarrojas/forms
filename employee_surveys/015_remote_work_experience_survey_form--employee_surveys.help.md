# Remote Work Experience Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees about their experiences with remote work. It is intended to help the company understand the benefits, challenges, and best practices related to remote work arrangements, ultimately informing decisions on implementing or optimizing remote work policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction to understand the purpose of the form.
2.  Fill out the required and optional fields with your relevant information. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  Be as detailed as possible when providing information in the text fields.
4.  Select the correct options for the dropdown fields.
5.  Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your job title during your remote work experience. Please enter the actual title of your job.
* **Remote Work Experience** (`remote_work_experience`, text, required/optional): Share your experience with remote work, including any notable achievements or challenges faced.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company where you worked remotely.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select how you were employed during your remote work experience (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Remote Status** (`remote_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check which types of remote work you experienced (True/False).
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you started your remote work experience.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you ended your remote work experience.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required/optional): If you left your remote work experience, provide the reason for your departure.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required/optional): Describe your work experience, including any notable projects or responsibilities.
* **Work Experience Description** (`work_experience_description`, text, required/optional): Share a detailed description of your work experience, if you have one.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of environment you worked in (Office, Home, Hybrid).
* **Other (please specify)** (`work_environment_other`, text, required/optional): If you chose "Other" in the previous field, provide a detailed description of your work environment.
