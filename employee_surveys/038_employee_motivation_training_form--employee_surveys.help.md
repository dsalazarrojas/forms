# Employee Motivation Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees assess their motivation level, identify areas for improvement, and plan for training initiatives. It is intended for employees who are interested in receiving coaching or development programs to enhance their performance.

Before completing this form, please ensure that you have not previously submitted a similar form to avoid duplication of effort. If you are unsure about your motivation level or training needs, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your employee name and ID on pages 1-2.
2. Select your current motivation level on page 3.
3. Choose the training initiatives you are interested in on page 4.
4. Provide a brief description of your training needs on page 6.
5. Fill in your available time on page 7, if applicable.
6. Enter your email address on page 8.
7. Provide a brief description of your training initiatives on page 10.
8. Choose the date and time you are available for training on page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. Employee ID
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Enter your employee ID number as per your company's HR records.

### 3. Motivation Level
* **Motivation Level** (`motivation_level`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

### 4. Training Initiatives
* **Training Initiatives** (`training_initiatives`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more initiatives you are interested in:
	+ Collaboration
	+ Communication
	+ Time Management

### 5. Training Initiatives Description
* **Training Initiatives Description** (`training_initiatives_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the training initiatives you chose on page 4.

### 6. Training Needs
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more areas of improvement:
	+ Motivation
	+ Engagement
	+ Leadership
	+ Time Management

### 7. Available Time
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, `time`, optional): If you are available for training, please enter your available time. If not, leave this field blank.

### 8. Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your company email address.

### 9. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.

### 10. Training Initiatives Description 2
* **Training Initiatives Description 2** (`training_initiatives_description_2`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the training initiatives you chose on page 4.

### 11. Date and Time
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, `date`, required): Choose a date and time you are available for training.

### Tips
Please answer all questions honestly and accurately. Your feedback will be reviewed by HR representatives to plan for your training and development needs. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
