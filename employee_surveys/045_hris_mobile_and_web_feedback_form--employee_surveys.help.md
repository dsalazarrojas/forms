# HRIS Mobile And Web Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HRIS Mobile And Web Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience with the HRIS system, mobile app, and web application. This form is crucial for identifying areas of improvement and making data-driven decisions to enhance the user experience.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Open the form in your browser or mobile app.
2. Select the correct option for the 'System Use' field, indicating whether you used the system recently.
3. Provide detailed feedback in the 'Feedback' field, describing your experience, suggestions, or concerns.
4. Click the 'Submit' button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **System Use** (`system_use`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you've recently used the system, mobile app, or both to help us understand your experience and make data-driven decisions.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts, ideas, or concerns about the system, mobile app, or web application. This field is where you can provide specific suggestions or comments on how to improve the system.
  Note: You can type as much or as little as you need to share your feedback. Your input is valued, and we'll review each submission carefully.
*   **Additional Tips**:
  + Be specific with your feedback to help us understand your concerns or suggestions.
  + Keep your feedback professional and respectful.
