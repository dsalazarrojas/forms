# Employee Feedback Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Feedback Release Form is designed to gather feedback from employees on various aspects of their work experience. This form is intended to be completed by employees to provide their suggestions, thoughts, and opinions on their work environment, management, and overall job satisfaction. Please complete this form only once, as it will help us understand your feedback and improve our services for you and your colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please make sure you have read the form carefully and understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question in as much detail as you feel necessary to provide your honest feedback.
3. For text fields (employee_feedback, suggestions_for_improvement, and feedback_frequency), type your response in the provided text area.
4. For number fields (employee_satisfaction and employee_deductions), enter a numerical value between 1 and 100.
5. For employee_deductions, use the following scale: 
   * 0-20: Very low (unsatisfied with my job)
   * 21-40: Low (somewhat dissatisfied with my job)
   * 41-60: Neutral (neither satisfied nor dissatisfied)
   * 61-80: High (somewhat satisfied with my job)
   * 81-100: Very high (satisfied with my job)
6. For employee_deductions_amount, enter the exact amount of deductions you are eligible for.
7. Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback (employee_feedback)**: A text field where you can provide any feedback or suggestions you have about your work experience. Please be as detailed as you like.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (suggestions_for_improvement)**: A text field where you can suggest improvements to your work environment, processes, or management. Please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Employee Satisfaction (employee_satisfaction)**: A numerical value between 1 and 100 representing how satisfied or dissatisfied you are with your job. Please follow the provided scale.
* **Feedback Frequency (feedback_frequency)**: A text field where you can indicate how frequently you provide feedback (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly). Please be specific and provide examples if possible.
* **Employee Deductions (employee_deductions)**: A numerical value between 1 and 100 representing how satisfied or dissatisfied you are with deductions.
* **Employee Deductions Amount (employee_deductions_amount)**: The exact amount of deductions you are eligible for. Please enter a numerical value.
