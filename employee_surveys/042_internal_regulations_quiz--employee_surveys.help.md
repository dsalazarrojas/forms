# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge employee understanding of the policy and capture various employee information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct answers for the quiz questions in the "Policy Understanding" section.
2. Answer the questions about the employee's department, collected by, date, time, email, phone, comments, company, and manager in the "Quiz Questions" section.
3. Select relevant sections in the "Section 1" to "Section 12" sections.

* **Title**: This is the title of the form.
