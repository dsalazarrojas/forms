# Team Culture Lab Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Culture Lab Application Form is a crucial tool in assessing team dynamics and cultural fit. It's essential to understand that this form serves as a starting point for the Team Culture Lab process, and the information collected will help facilitate positive changes within your team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Team Culture Lab Application Form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Job Title, Department, Shift, and Phone Number accurately.
2. Enter your Date of Birth and Date of Joining in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. In the Work Experience field, provide a brief description of your job role and responsibilities.
4. If applicable, indicate the name and contact information of your Team Lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address for official communication purposes.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title or role within the organization.
* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to or work in.
* **Shift**: Enter your working shift or schedule (e.g., morning, afternoon, night, rotating).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Date of Joining**: Enter the date you joined the organization in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/15/2015).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number or contact number for external communication.
* **Work Experience**: Briefly describe your job role, responsibilities, and any specific accomplishments or achievements in your work experience.
* **Team Lead**: If you have a Team Lead or someone who can vouch for you, provide their name and contact information.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and honestly to ensure that the assessment results reflect your true team dynamics.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with your Team Lead or HR representative.
- Do not repeat this form, as it may affect the assessment results.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your Team Lead or HR representative.
