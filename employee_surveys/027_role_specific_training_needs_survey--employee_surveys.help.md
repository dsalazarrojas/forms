# role_specific_training_needs_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your role-specific training needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply click on each field and enter the required information. For multiple-choice fields, select the relevant options.
- Fill out all the required fields, marked as required.
- For fields with multiple-choice options, select all that apply.
- Once you've completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title as it appears in our organizational hierarchy.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you are currently working (e.g., office, home, etc.).
* **What are your training needs for this role?** (`training_needs`, text, optional): Please enter any specific training needs you have for your current role.
* **What skills are being assessed in this role?** (`skills_assessed`, select_multiple, required): Select all skills that are being assessed in your current role.
	+ Leadership/Management
	+ Communication
	+ Data Analysis
	+ Project Management
	+ Time Management
	+ Problem Solving
	+ Team Collaboration
	+ Adaptability
	+ Creativity
* **What training topics would you like to see in the future?** (`training_topics`, select_multiple, required): Select all training topics you would like to see in future training programs.
	+ Communication
	+ Leadership/Management
	+ Presentation
	+ Time Management
	+ Project Management
	+ Data Analysis
	+ Problem Solving
	+ Teamwork
	+ Goal Setting
* **How often do you want training to be offered?** (`training_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you would like to see training offered.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Semi-Annual
	+ Annually
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when describing your training needs and preferences.
- If you're unsure about any of the options, it's okay to leave them blank or select "Other" (if available).
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "What are your training needs for this role?" field.
- Once you've completed the form, you'll be able to review and submit your responses.
