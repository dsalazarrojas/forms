# Workplace-Wellness-Feedback-Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on our workplace wellness program. Your input is crucial in helping us identify areas for improvement and making our program more effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and provide your honest feedback.
2. Answer each question in the space provided.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Notes" section.
4. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Employee Survey** (`employee_survey_section`, text, required: false)
*This section is a general feedback area where you can express your thoughts and feelings about the wellness program.*

*   **Employee Survey** (text): Share your general feedback about the wellness program.

### 2. **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, number, required: false)
*This section measures your satisfaction with the program. Please rate on a scale from 1-5.*

*   **Program Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the program (1-5).

### 3. **Feedback on Services** (`feedback_on_services`, text, required: false)
*This section is an opportunity to provide more specific feedback on the services offered by the program.*

*   **Feedback on Services**: Share your feedback about the services offered.

### 4. **Program Evaluation** (`program_evaluation`, select_one, required: false)
*Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your evaluation of the program.*

*   **Program Evaluation**: Evaluate the program as "Yes" or "No".

### 5. **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required: false)
*This section is your chance to suggest changes or ideas for the program.*

*   **Suggestions for Improvement**: Share your ideas for improvement.

### 6. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required: false)
*Please provide a way for us to contact you, if needed.*

*   **Contact Info**: Provide your email address or other contact information.

### 7. **Availability** (`availability`, time, required: false)
*This section is used to schedule a call to discuss your feedback.*

*   **Availability**: Choose a time when we can schedule a call to discuss your feedback.

### 8. **Schedule a Call** (`schedule_a_call`, select_multiple, required: false)
*Choose "Yes" if you'd like to schedule a call with us.*

*   **Schedule a Call**: Select "Yes" to schedule a call.

### 9. **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false)
*This is an optional field for any additional comments or information.*

*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information here.
