# internal_communication_strategy_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internal communication strategy evaluation form is a tool used to assess the current state of internal communication within an organization. It aims to gather feedback on the effectiveness and frequency of internal communication channels, as well as employee satisfaction with the current strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and options provided, ensuring you understand the context and purpose of each question.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability, using the provided options.
3. If you are unsure about any question or option, use the provided notes field to clarify your thoughts.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed, indicated by an asterisk \* in the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **update_frequency** (number, *): How often do you receive updates on company information?
* This question aims to understand how frequently employees receive updates on company information. Please select the number of times per week you receive updates.

### 2. **update_channel** (select one, *): Which channel do you primarily use to receive updates?
* This question asks which channel you use most often to receive updates on company information (e.g., Communication, Chat, Email).

### 3. **satisfaction_level** (select multiple, *): How would you rate your level of satisfaction with the current internal communication strategy?
* This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the current strategy, selecting all applicable levels of satisfaction.

### 4. **communication_channels_effectiveness** (number, ): How would you rate the effectiveness of internal communication channels?
* This question asks you to rate the effectiveness of internal communication channels, on a scale of 0-10.

### 5. **note** (note, ): If you have any comments or suggestions for improving internal communication, please share them here.
* This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving internal communication.

### 6. **email** (email, ): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* This field is for your email address, which will be used for follow-up or if any further information is needed.

### 7. **phone** (text, ): If you have a phone number where you can be reached for further information, please enter it here.
* This field is for your phone number, which will be used for follow-up or if any further information is needed.

### 8. **output_frequency** (number, ): How often do you think updates should be sent out?
* This question asks you to suggest the ideal frequency of updates.

### 9. **centralize_data_collection** (number, ): On a scale of 0-10, how well do you think data collection should be centralized?
* This question asks you to rate the ideal level of centralization for data collection.

Note: The asterisk \* indicates a required field.
