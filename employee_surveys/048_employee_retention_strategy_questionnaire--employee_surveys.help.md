# Employee Retention Strategy Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Retention Strategy Questionnaire is designed to collect employee feedback on their experience and satisfaction level at work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question truthfully and concisely.
2. Use the provided options to select your response for questions 2 and 3.
3. Keep your feedback in question 4 brief, as it's a free-text field.
4. Enter your date of response for question 5.
5. Enter your response time for question 6.
6. Enter your email address for question 7 (this will be used for follow-up communication).
7. Enter your phone number for question 8 (this will be used for follow-up communication).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Retention Strategy Questionnaire** (1, text, required): This field is the title of the form, and is required to be filled out as it's a mandatory label.
* **Experience Level** (2, select_one, false): This question asks about your experience level in the company. Please select one of the provided options:
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **Satisfaction Level** (3, select_multiple, false): This question asks about your overall satisfaction level with your experience. Please select one or more of the provided options:
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **Feedback** (4, text, false): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience. Please keep it brief.
* **Date** (5, date, false): This field asks for the date when you responded to this form. Please enter it in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (6, time, false): This field asks for the time when you responded to this form. Please enter it in the correct format (e.g., 12:34 PM).
* **Email** (7, email, false): This field asks for your email address. Please enter it so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (8, text, false): This field asks for your phone number. Please enter it so that we can follow up with you if needed.
