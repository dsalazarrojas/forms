# Team Member Of The Month Vote - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Of The Month Vote form is used to recognize and reward team members for their outstanding contributions and efforts. By completing this form, you can nominate team members and provide feedback on their strengths and achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the team member you would like to nominate by choosing an option from the first dropdown menu.
2. For the second dropdown menu, select additional team members if you would like (optional).
3. In the text field, provide a brief explanation of why you think this team member should be recognized.
4. Describe what skills or abilities make this team member stand out.
5. Share when you first noticed this team member's outstanding contribution.
6. Select the frequency of interaction with this team member.
7. Provide any additional feedback you have about this team member.
8. Finally, select the team members you would like to recognize in the last dropdown menu (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member Of The Month Vote** (`team_member_of_the_month_1`): Select the team member you would like to nominate from the dropdown menu. 
* **Select Team Member 2** (`team_member_of_the_month_2`): Select additional team members you would like to recognize (optional). 
* **Why Vote For This Team Member?** (`team_member_of_the_month_3`): Provide a brief explanation of why you think this team member should be recognized. 
* **What Makes This Team Member Stand Out?** (`team_member_of_the_month_4`): Describe the skills or abilities that make this team member stand out. 
* **When Did You First Notice This Team Member's Outstanding Contribution?** (`team_member_of_the_month_5`): Share when you first noticed this team member's outstanding contribution. 
* **How Often Do I Interact With This Team Member?** (`team_member_of_the_month_11`): Select the frequency of interaction with this team member. 
* **Other Feedback** (`team_member_of_the_month_16`): Provide any additional feedback you have about this team member. 
* **Team Member Of The Month 15** (`team_member_of_the_month_15`): Select additional team members you would like to recognize (optional).
