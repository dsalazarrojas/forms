# job_training_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for referring employees for job training programs to help them develop new skills and enhance their job performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please select the relevant fields to provide information about the employee you are referring for training.
2. Choose the category from the dropdown menu to categorize the referral.
3. In the description field, provide a brief explanation about the referral and the reason for referring the employee.
4. Select one or multiple job training programs that the employee would benefit from.
5. Fill in the job title and job description of the employee.
6. Choose the status of the job training, whether it's In Progress or Complete.
7. Select one or multiple status options for the job training.
8. Choose the tool assigned to the employee for the training.
9. Finally, provide the output file name for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category** (Select One): Choose the category of the referral from the dropdown menu.
* **description** (Text): Provide a brief explanation about the referral and the reason for referring the employee.
* **job_training_programs** (Select Multiple): Choose one or multiple job training programs that the employee would benefit from.
* **job_title** (Text): Fill in the job title of the employee.
* **job_description** (Text): Fill in the job description of the employee.
* **job_status** (Select One): Choose the status of the job training whether it's In Progress or Complete.
* **job_status_1** (Select Multiple): Choose one or multiple status options for the job training.
* **assigned_tool** (Select One): Choose the tool assigned to the employee for the training.
* **output_file** (Text): Provide the output file name for the form.
