# gym_staff_training_effectiveness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gym staff training effectiveness survey is designed to gather feedback from staff members on the effectiveness of the training sessions, enabling trainers and management to evaluate and improve the training program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have the necessary authority to access and complete this form.
2. Review the pages carefully and answer all mandatory fields (* indicates required field).
3. Provide thorough and honest feedback in all fields.
4. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**survey_questions** (`1`, `text`, optional)
- This page allows you to provide any questions or comments you have about the training session. Please use this space to ask any questions or clarify points that are unclear.

**survey_results** (`2`, `select_one`, required)
- This question is about whether you found the training session effective. Choose between "Yes" and "No" based on your experience.

**comments** (`3`, `note`, optional)
- If you have any comments or suggestions for the training, please provide them in this text box. This will help us to identify areas for improvement.

**email_address** (`4`, `email`, required)
- Please enter your email address. This will enable the training team to contact you directly for further discussion or to provide you with follow-up information.

**phone_number** (`5`, `text`, optional)
- If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number. This will help us to reach out to you in case we need to discuss further.

**survey_comments** (`6`, `text`, optional)
- If you have any further comments or suggestions for the training, please provide them in this text box. This will help us to improve the program.

**additional_comments** (`7`, `text`, optional)
- If you have any additional information or suggestions that didn't fit in any other field, please provide them here.

**survey_completion_date** (`8`, `date`, required)
- Please select the date on which the training session was completed. This will help us to evaluate the progression of the training program over time.

**survey_completion_time** (`9`, `time`, required)
- Please select the time at which the training session was completed. This will help us to evaluate the duration and timing of the training sessions.

## Tips
- Please be concise and clear in your responses, providing actionable feedback.
- If you have any difficulty accessing or completing this form, please contact the training team for assistance.
- Your feedback is confidential and will only be used to improve the training program.
