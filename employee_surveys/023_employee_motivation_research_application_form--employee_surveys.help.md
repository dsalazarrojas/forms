# Employee Motivation Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Motivation Research Application Form is a tool used to gather information about an employee's job satisfaction, engagement factors, and other relevant metrics. This form helps us identify areas for improvement within the organization and is designed to provide an honest assessment of your work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Start by filling out the form header with your name and employee ID.
2. Answer the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the relevant options for each question, based on your current situation.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (Page 1):
	+ This is the header section where you need to enter your name and employee ID.
* **Employee Name** (Page 2):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
	+ This field is used to identify you as an employee.
* **Employee Email** (Page 3):
	+ Enter your official email address associated with your employee ID.
* **Manager Name** (Page 4):
	+ Enter the name of your direct manager.
	+ This field is used to identify your line of reporting.
* **Manager Email** (Page 5):
	+ Enter the email address of your direct manager.
* **Department** (Page 6):
	+ Select 'Yes' if your department has a clear mission and goals.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.
* **Department ID** (Page 7):
	+ Select all departments that you are directly or indirectly responsible for.
* **Motivation Level** (Page 8):
	+ Select 'High' if you are highly motivated and engaged at work.
	+ Select 'Low' otherwise.
* **Engagement Factors** (Page 9):
	+ Select all factors that motivate you to be engaged at work.
* **Feedback** (Page 10):
	+ Select all areas where you would like to receive feedback.
* **Job Performance** (Page 11):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel you are performing well in your role.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.
* **Job Satisfaction** (Page 12):
	+ Select 'Satisfied' if you are satisfied with your job.
	+ Select 'Dissatisfied' otherwise.
* **Work Life Balance** (Page 13):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have a good balance between work and life.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.
* **Team Performance** (Page 14):
	+ Select 'Yes' if your team performs well.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.
* **Job Security** (Page 15):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel secure in your job.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, please leave it blank.
- You can skip this form if you have already completed a similar form in the past.
