# Employee Benefits Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about employee benefits satisfaction and priorities. Your feedback is valuable to us, as it helps us to make informed decisions about our company's benefits package.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Take a few minutes to read through each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about a particular question, feel free to ask your HR representative.
4. Once you've completed the form, please review your responses carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Benefits Package** (`employee_benefits_package`, text, optional): Please describe your current employee benefits package that you receive from the company.
* **Benefits Satisfaction** (`benefits_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your current benefits package? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Benefits Important** (`benefits_important`, select_multiple, optional): What benefits are most important to you? Choose all that apply:
	+ Health Insurance
	+ Retirement Plan
	+ Paid Time Off
	+ Other
* **Benefits Satisfaction 2** (`benefits_satisfaction_2`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with other benefits offered by the company? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Other Benefits** (`other_benefits`, text, optional): Please describe any other benefits not listed that you feel are important to you.
* **Benefits Satisfaction 3** (`benefits_satisfaction_3`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with other benefits not listed? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Benefits Satisfaction 4** (`benefits_satisfaction_4`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with other benefits not listed? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied

## Tips
* Please answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your HR representative.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your honesty.
