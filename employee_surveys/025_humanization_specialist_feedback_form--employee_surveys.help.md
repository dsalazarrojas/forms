# Humanization Specialist Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Humanization Specialist Feedback Form is used to collect feedback on the performance of Humanization Specialists. This form is used by managers to evaluate and improve the performance of these critical team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Employee ID of the Humanization Specialist you are providing feedback on.
2. Select the date when the feedback was given.
3. Enter the name of the Humanization Specialist.
4. Choose the department the Humanization Specialist belongs to (Sales, Marketing, or Customer Support).
5. Select the type of feedback you are providing (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
6. Enter the details of the feedback.
7. Describe the action taken as a result of the feedback.
8. Enter the score of the employee (out of 100).
9. Rate the overall performance of the employee (out of 5).
10. Indicate whether you would recommend this employee.
11. Enter any additional comments.
12. Enter the date when the feedback was submitted.
13. Enter your name as the feedback submitter.
14. Enter your title as the feedback submitter.
15. Enter the name of the reviewer who reviewed the feedback.
16. Enter the title of the reviewer who reviewed the feedback.
17. Select the date when the review was conducted.
18. Enter the time when the review was conducted.
19. Choose the review status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
20. Enter any comments about the review.
21. Describe the actions taken as a result of the review.
22. Enter the date when the action was taken.
23. Enter your signature as the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (1)**: The ID of the Humanization Specialist being reviewed.
	+ Enter the unique ID of the employee.
* **Feedback Date (2)**: The date when the feedback was given.
	+ Select the date using the date picker.
* **Employee Name (3)**: The name of the Humanization Specialist.
	+ Enter the name of the employee.
* **Department (4)**: The department the Humanization Specialist belongs to.
	+ Choose from the options: Sales, Marketing, or Customer Support.
* **Feedback Type (5)**: The type of feedback you are providing.
	+ Choose from the options: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Feedback Details (6)**: A description of the feedback.
	+ Enter the details of the feedback.
* **Action Taken (7)**: An action taken as a result of the feedback.
	+ Describe the action taken.
* **Employee Survey Score (8)**: A score given to the employee.
	+ Enter a score out of 100.
* **Overall Rating (9)**: A rating of the employee's performance.
	+ Rate the performance out of 5.
* **Would you recommend this employee? (10)**: Indicate whether you would recommend this employee.
	+ Choose from the options: True, False, or Maybe.
* **Comments (11)**: Any additional comments about the employee.
	+ Enter any comments about the employee.
* **Rating Comments (12)**: Comments about the rating given.
	+ Enter comments about the rating.
* **Additional Comments (13)**: Any additional comments about the feedback process.
	+ Enter any additional comments.
* **Feedback Submitted On (14)**: The date when the feedback was submitted.
	+ Select the date using the date picker.
* **Feedback Submitter (15)**: The name of the person who submitted the feedback.
	+ Enter the name of the submitter.
* **Submitter Title (16)**: The title of the person who submitted the feedback.
	+ Enter the title of the submitter.
* **Feedback Reviewer (17)**: The name of the person who reviewed the feedback.
	+ Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title (18)**: The title of the person who reviewed the feedback.
	+ Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Review Date (19)**: The date when the review was conducted.
	+ Select the date using the date picker.
* **Review Time (20)**: The time when the review was conducted.
	+ Enter the time using the time picker.
* **Review Status (21)**: The status of the review.
	+ Choose from the options: Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
* **Review Comments (22)**: Any comments about the review.
	+ Enter any comments about the review.
* **Action Taken by Reviewer (23)**: An action taken as a result of the review.
	+ Describe the action taken.
* **Action Taken by Reviewer Date (24)**: The date when the action was taken.
	+ Enter the date using the date picker.
* **Reviewer Signature (25)**: The signature of the reviewer.
	+ Enter the signature of the reviewer.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and honest feedback.
* Provide feedback only when there are new issues or updates to report.
* Fill out a new form only for new feedback.
