# Compensation And Rewards Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your opinions and suggestions on our compensation and rewards programs. Your feedback will help us make informed decisions to improve our rewards structure and make your work experience more valuable and satisfying.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, answering each question to the best of your ability.
2. Most questions are multiple-choice, and you'll have the option to select one of the provided options.
3. Some questions allow you to provide a free-text response, like "Suggestions for improvement" and "Additional Comments."
4. Make sure to select all options that apply in the "Reward Improvements" question.
5. For all free-text questions, please keep your responses concise and clear, so we can easily understand your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional field for us to keep track of your identity. You can choose to keep it anonymous if you prefer.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: false): Similar to the Employee Name field, this is an internal tracking field.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select your department to help us understand your perspective.
* **Work Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose your primary work location to help us know where you're based.
* **Years with Company** (`tenure_years`, `select_one`, required: true): Select how long you've been with the company to help us understand your experience.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your current job level to help us understand your responsibilities.
* **My salary is competitive** (`salary_competitiveness`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how competitive your salary is compared to similar roles in the market.
* **My salary is fair compared to peers** (`salary_internal_equity`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how you feel about internal equity in salary compared to your peers.
* **I understand how bonuses are calculated** (`annual_bonus_clarity`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how clear you are about the bonus structure.
* **Bonus structure motivates me** (`bonus_motivation`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how motivating the current bonus structure is for you.
* **Recognition programs are meaningful** (`recognition_programs`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the effectiveness of recognition programs.
* **I receive recognition often enough** (`recognition_frequency`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how often you receive recognition.
* **Non-monetary rewards are valued** (`non_monetary_rewards`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how valuable you find non-monetary rewards.
* **Rewards are linked to career growth** (`career_growth_link`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how connected you feel the rewards are to career growth.
* **High performance is well rewarded** (`performance_reward_link`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how well you feel high performance is rewarded.
* **Rewards are well communicated** (`reward_communication`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how clear you are about rewards.
* **Recognition is given in a timely manner** (`timely_recognition`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how timely recognition is received.
* **Peer-to-peer recognition is encouraged** (`peer_recognition`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the culture of peer recognition.
* **My manager recognizes my contributions** (`manager_recognition`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how well your manager recognizes your contributions.
* **Rewards impact my desire to stay** (`rewards_impact_retention`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how much rewards impact your desire to stay with the company.
* **What reward do you value most** (`most_valued_reward`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your top choice of reward.
* **Reward improvements** (`reward_improvements`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all areas for improvement you'd like to see in our rewards program.
* **Overall satisfaction with rewards** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your overall satisfaction with the current rewards structure.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any other ideas or suggestions you may have.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any other thoughts or comments you may have about the rewards program.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and thoughtfully, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* Take as much time as you need to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions.
* We appreciate your input and look forward to making positive changes based on your feedback.
