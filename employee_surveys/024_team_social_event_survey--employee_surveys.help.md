## Purpose
The Team Social Event Survey is a tool to gather feedback from team members on past team social events. This form helps the team organizers understand what worked and what didn't, so they can plan future events more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, navigate through its pages, and fill in the required and optional fields. Each page corresponds to a specific section of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Date (`event_date`)
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Please select the date of the event you are providing feedback on.

### Event Location (`event_location`)
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the event, if you wish to provide more details about it.

### Attended Event (`attended_event`)
* **Attended Event** (`attended_event`, select_one, required: false): Please select if you attended the event. Options: Yes, No, Maybe

### Number of Attendees (`attendees`)
* **Number of attendees** (`attendees`, number, required: false): If you attended the event, please enter the total number of people who attended.

### Food Order (`food_order`)
* **Food order** (`food_order`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of food you ordered during the event. Options: None, Snacks, Dinner

### Team Outing (`team_outing`)
* **Team Outing** (`team_outing`, select_one, required: false): Please select if the event was a team outing. Options: Yes, No

### Team Attendance (`team_attendance`)
* **Team Attendance** (`team_attendance`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the attendance rate of the team members during the event. Options: Everyone, Some, None

### Comments (`comments`)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions about the event, please enter them here.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill in the required fields, but you can skip the optional ones if you're not sure or don't have enough information to answer them.
* If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the team organizers.
