# Employee Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and experiences from employees to help the company understand the overall work environment, satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please select one or multiple options from each page.
2. Please fill in the text fields as much as possible, providing specific examples or details if possible.
3. Do not leave any fields blank unless the question doesn't apply to your experience or you're unsure of the answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Experience**: Select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes': You are satisfied with your overall experience.
	+ 'No': You are not satisfied with your overall experience.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name as it appears on your paycheck stub or company ID badge.
* **Department**: Select your department name. This will help us understand which team you're a part of.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your paycheck stub or job description.
* **Work Start Date**: Enter the date you started your job in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Team Members**: Select all that apply.
	+ 'Yes': You have a good working relationship with your team members.
	+ 'No': You do not have a good working relationship with your team members.
* **Job Function**: Enter a brief description of your job function or role.
* **Job Classification**: Enter a brief description of your job classification or category.
* **Work Location**: Enter your work location (e.g., office, home, remote).
* **Job Title Description**: Enter a brief description of your job title.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Performance Goals**: Enter your performance goals or objectives.
* **Work Hours**: Enter your regular work hours or schedule.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Select one of the following.
	+ Satisfied: You are satisfied with your job.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are not satisfied with your job.
* **Communication**: Enter your feedback on communication with your team and management.
* **Feedback**: Enter any additional feedback or suggestions you have for the company.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have for the company.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure of the answer to a question, please provide the best answer you can.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them in the "Comments" field.
