# Workplace Diversity Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their experiences with diversity and inclusion in the workplace. Your input will help management understand the current state of diversity and inclusion within the organization and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Use one of the provided options for each question, even if you don't have a strong opinion.
3. If you have experienced discrimination or bias, please describe the specific type(s) of discrimination you experienced.
4. Take your time, but try to complete the form in one sitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey Form** (`employee_survey_form`): This is the introduction to the form. Please fill out this section to provide some background information.
* **How would you describe your experience with diversity and inclusion at work?** (`workplace_diversity_perception`): This question asks you to rate your experience with diversity and inclusion at work. Please answer on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Rarely" and 5 is "Every day".
* **How often do you see or experience people of different races, cultures, or sexual orientations?** (`work_life_beyond_our_company`): This question asks you to rate how often you see or experience people of different backgrounds at work or in your daily life. Please select one of the options: "Every day", "Several days a week", "Rarely", or "Never".
* **How often do you see or experience people of different races, cultures, or sexual orientations at work?** (`work_life_at_workplace`): This question is similar to the previous one, but specifically asks about your experience at work. Please select multiple options that apply to you.
* **Have you experienced discrimination, bias or harassment in the workplace?** (`discrimination_experience`): This question asks if you have experienced any form of discrimination, bias, or harassment in the workplace. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **Types of Discrimination** (`discrimination_type`): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, this section asks you to specify the type(s) of discrimination you experienced. Please select all that apply.
* **Do you feel comfortable reporting discrimination or bias experiences to your manager or HR?** (`support_system`): This question asks if you feel comfortable reporting discrimination or bias experiences to your manager or HR. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **How do you feel about the response to your reporting of bias or discrimination?** (`support_response`): This question asks how you feel about the response to your reporting of bias or discrimination. Please select one of the options: "Very satisfied", "Somewhat satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat dissatisfied", or "Very dissatisfied".
