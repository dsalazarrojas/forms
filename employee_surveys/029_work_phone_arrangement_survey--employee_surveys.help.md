# Work Phone Arrangement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help management understand employees' work phone arrangements, including usage frequency, support needs, and experiences with company phones. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our phone-related services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your actual experience with work phones.
2. Select the corresponding options for each multiple-choice question.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the "comments" field to share your thoughts.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **work_phone_arrangement**:
  Enter your current work phone arrangement (e.g., Mobile Phone, Desk Phone, Laptop).
- **category**:
  Select all categories that apply to your work phone arrangement (e.g., Mobile Phone, Desk Phone, Laptop).
- **do_you_use_your_work_phone_at_home**:
  Indicate how often you use your work phone at home (e.g., 'True' if you use it often).
- **do_you_own_a_companies_phone**:
  Tell us if you own a company-provided phone (e.g., 'True' if you do).
- **do_you_use_your_companies_phone**:
  Indicate how often you use your company-provided phone (e.g., 'True' if you use it often).
- **how_much_do_you_use_the_phone_support**:
  Rate how much you use phone support (e.g., Not Needed, Rarely, Occasionally, Often, Always).
- **how_much_time_do_you_spend_with_phone_support**:
  (Optional) If applicable, report the amount of time you spend with phone support.
- **how_would_you_rate_the_quality_of_phone_support**:
  Rate the quality of phone support (e.g., Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
- **how_friendly_are_phone_supports**:
  Rate how friendly phone support personnel are (e.g., Not Friendly, Neutral, Friendly).
- **how_helpful_are_phone_supports**:
  Rate how helpful phone support is (e.g., Not Helpful, A Little Helpful, Helpful).
- **comments**:
  Share any additional comments or concerns about your work phone arrangement.
- **category_2**:
  (Optional) Select additional categories related to your work phone arrangement (e.g., Mobile Phone, Desk Phone, Laptop).
- **email**:
  (Optional) Enter your work email address (e.g., [your email]).
- **phone_number**:
  (Optional) Enter your work phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
