# Compensation Benchmark Data Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compensation Benchmark Data Request Form is designed to gather compensation data from an organization for the purpose of creating a comprehensive report on salary standards within a specific industry and geographic region. This form is intended to ensure that the data collected is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the requester information section, which includes your name, department, and contact email.
2. Provide details on the benchmark requirements, including the target job title, target industry, geographic scope, and specific data points requested.
3. Explain the business justification for the request, and provide a deadline for completion.
4. Choose your preferred data source, and provide a date of request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name of Requester**: Enter the name of the person or department requesting the data.
  - This information will be used to contact you for any further questions or clarifications.
- **Department**: Enter the department or organization unit requesting the benchmark.
  - This will help us to provide more accurate results within the context of your organization.
- **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address for delivery of the benchmark report.
  - This is the contact email address we will use to send you the completed report.
- **Target Job Title**: Enter the specific job title to be analyzed.
  - This information will help us to provide accurate benchmarking data within your organization.
- **Target Industry**: Select the primary industry to benchmark against.
  - Examples include Technology / Software, Healthcare / Medical, Finance / Banking, etc.
- **Geographic Scope**: Select the region for salary comparison.
  - Examples include Local (City-wide), Regional (State/Province), National, International.
- **Specific Data Points Required**: Select all data points you want to be included in the report.
  - Examples include Base Salary (25th/50th/75th percentiles), Annual Bonus / STI, etc.
- **Business Justification for Request**: Explain why this benchmark is needed now.
  - This will help us to understand the context and provide more accurate results.
- **Required Completion Date**: Enter the date you need the data by.
  - This is the deadline for the report delivery.
- **Preferred Data Source**: Enter your preferred data source (if any).
  - This information will help us to provide more accurate data.
- **Date of Request**: This is the current date.
  - Please note that this date will automatically be filled out.
