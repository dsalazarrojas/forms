# Job Flexibility Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey aims to gather insights from employees about their experiences with job flexibility in the organization. It covers various aspects of job flexibility, including work-life balance, career development opportunities, and flexible work arrangements. The survey will help management understand employees' perspectives and make informed decisions to improve job flexibility policies and practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer the questions honestly, and only answer the questions that apply to you.
2.  For questions with multiple options (e.g., "How satisfied are you with your work-life balance?", "How often do you have flexible work arrangements?"), choose the option that best represents your current experience.
3.  For the "Any additional comments or feedback" section, you can provide any thoughts or concerns not covered by the previous questions.
4.  Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
5.  If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your manager or HR representative.
