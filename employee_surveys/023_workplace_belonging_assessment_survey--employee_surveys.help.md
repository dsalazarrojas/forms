# Workplace Belonging Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Belonging Assessment Survey is designed to gather information about employee experiences and perceptions regarding their sense of belonging in the workplace. This survey aims to help organizations understand how well their employees feel connected to their team and the workplace as a whole.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please select the options that best describe your experiences and opinions.**
2. **For text fields, please provide detailed answers.**
3. **For select options, choose the one that best matches your answer.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you enjoy most about working here**: This question helps us understand what aspects of the workplace make you feel the most valued and appreciated. Please provide a brief description of your favorite part of your job or the workplace environment.
* **Which team do you feel the most connected with**: Select all teams that you feel a strong connection with. This will help us understand which teams you are most closely tied to and how we can improve communication and collaboration within those teams.
* **What level of inclusion do you feel in the workplace**: Choose the option that best describes your sense of belonging and inclusion in the workplace. This will help us understand how we can create a more inclusive environment for everyone.
	+ **High**: You feel very included and connected to your team and the workplace.
	+ **Medium**: You feel somewhat included, but there is room for improvement.
	+ **Low**: You feel relatively disconnected and less included.
* **What level of satisfaction do you experience in the workplace**: Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with your job and the workplace.
	+ **High**: You are very satisfied with your job and the workplace.
	+ **Medium**: You are somewhat satisfied, but there is room for improvement.
	+ **Low**: You are not very satisfied.
* **Is there an action plan in place for inclusion and diversity**: Choose the option that best answers this question.
	+ **True**: There is a clear action plan to promote inclusion and diversity.
	+ **False**: No clear action plan is in place.
* **Have you seen actions taken to address inclusion and diversity issues**: Choose the option that best describes your experiences.
	+ **True**: Yes, actions have been taken.
	+ **False**: No, no actions have been taken.
* **What do you suggest we do to improve our workplace's sense of community**: This question allows you to provide suggestions on how we can improve the workplace's sense of community.
* **What kind of action plan would you recommend for implementing improvements**: Choose the option that best describes your suggested action plan.
	+ **Training**: Provide training on inclusion and diversity.
	+ **Meetings**: Organize meetings to discuss inclusion and diversity.
	+ **Policy changes**: Introduce new policies to promote inclusion and diversity.
* **How important is team leadership in the workplace**: Choose the option that best describes your opinion on the importance of team leadership.
	+ **Very important**: Team leadership is very essential.
	+ **Somewhat important**: Team leadership is somewhat important.
	+ **Not very important**: Team leadership is relatively unimportant.
* **Which style of team leadership do you prefer**: Select all styles of team leadership that you prefer.
	+ **Autocratic**: Autocratic leadership.
	+ **Democratic**: Democratic leadership.
	+ **Laissez-faire**: Laissez-faire leadership.
* **Which communication channel do you use most often for team interactions**: Select all communication channels you frequently use for team interactions.
	+ **Email**: Email.
	+ **Phone**: Phone.
	+ **Video conferencing**: Video conferencing.
* **What time of day do you usually take your break**: This question is not relevant to assessing workplace belonging.
* **What kind of work life balance do you experience in the workplace**: This question is not relevant to assessing workplace belonging.
* **Do you feel secure in your job**: Choose the option that best describes your feelings on job security.
	+ **True**: You feel very secure.
	+ **False**: You do not feel secure.
* **If No, what are the reasons**: Only answer if you chose False for the previous question.
* **How many team members do you have direct interactions with**: This question is not directly relevant to assessing workplace belonging, but it can provide useful context.

## Tips
* Please answer questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the text fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [insert contact information].
