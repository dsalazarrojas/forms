# Employee Wellbeing Audit Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Wellbeing Audit Initiative Application Form is a tool used to gather information about employees' work status, job title, location, and any wellbeing issues they may be experiencing. The information collected will be used to provide support and guidance to employees who may be struggling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and select your contact method from the options.
2. Enter your email address as prompted.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter your job title.
5. Specify your location.
6. Choose how you work (select one or more options).
7. If you're experiencing any wellbeing issues, select the relevant option(s).
8. Provide a brief description of your wellbeing issue.
9. Finally, choose your submission option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: Select one of the two options to indicate how you would like to be contacted.
	+ This field is required as it is used to determine how to reach out to you for support.
* **Email**: Enter your email address as prompted.
	+ This field is required as it is used to contact you with further support and guidance.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is required as it is used to contact you for immediate support.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
	+ This field is required as it helps us understand your role and position.
* **Location**: Enter your location.
	+ This field is required as it helps us understand your work environment.
* **Work Status**: Choose how you work from the options provided.
	+ This field is required as it helps us understand your work situation.
	+ Options include:
		- Working from the office
		- Working from home
		- Other (please specify)
* **Wellbeing Issue**: Select the wellbeing issue you are experiencing (if any).
	+ This field is not required, as it is only for those who need support.
	+ Options include:
		- Physical Health
		- Mental Health
		- Other (please specify)
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your wellbeing issue (if you selected one).
	+ This field is required if you selected a wellbeing issue.
* **Submission**: Choose how you'd like to proceed with your submission.
	+ Options include:
		- Submit and Get Help
		- Submit and Review
		- Submit and Review and Get Help
