# Employee Engagement Experiment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees on their engagement and participation in an experiment. Please complete the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible to help us understand your experiences and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the four sections and fill out each field as instructed. You can move back and forth between sections using the navigation controls or by clicking on the next page button at the end of each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Details (Page 1)
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, required): This field is for your official name as an employee of the company.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, text, required): This is your unique employee identification number.
* **Employee Role** (`Employee Role`, text, required): This is your job title or position within the company.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, required): This is the department you belong to within the company.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, optional): This is the location of your workplace.

### Team Information (Page 2)
* **Team Name** (`Team Name`, text, required): This is the name of the team you belong to.
* **Team Lead/Manager** (`Team Lead/Manager`, text, required): This is the name of your team lead or manager.

### Pilot Information (Page 3)
* **Pilot Name** (`Pilot Name`, text, required): This is the name of the pilot project you are participating in.
* **Pilot Number** (`Pilot Number`, text, optional): This is the unique pilot number assigned to you.

### Additional Information (Page 4)
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): This is a free text field for you to provide any additional information or comments about your experiences.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for clarification.
* You can move back and forth between sections using the navigation controls or by clicking on the next page button at the end of each section.
