# manager_feedback_coaching_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register feedback and coaching sessions between managers and their employees. This form helps to schedule and track coaching sessions, ensuring that both parties are on the same page and that the coaching goals and topics are well-defined.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with a red asterisk (*).
2. Select the options that best fit your needs.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the Notes field.
4. Review your completed form for accuracy before submitting.
5. Click the Submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Manager Information
* **Manager Name**: Enter your name as the manager responsible for the coaching session.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Feedback Topics**: List the topics to be covered in the coaching sessions.
* **Coaching Goals**: List the goals of the coaching sessions.
* **Frequency of Coaching**: Select how often you would like to meet for coaching sessions (Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly).
* **Meeting Time**: Select your preferred meeting time.
* **Duration of Coaching**: Enter the number of sessions you would like to have.
* **Meeting Location**: Select where you would like to have the coaching sessions (On-site, Off-site, or Remote).
* **Coaching Location**: Select where you would like to have the coaching sessions (Office, Home, or Park).
* **Manager Name 2**: Enter the name of the second manager (if applicable).
* **Employee Name 2**: Enter the name of the second employee (if applicable).

### Page 2: Second Manager and Employee Information
* **Manager Name 2**: Enter the name of the second manager (if applicable).
* **Employee Name 2**: Enter the name of the second employee (if applicable).
* **Feedback Topics 2**: List the topics to be covered in the coaching sessions with the second employee.
* **Coaching Goals 2**: List the goals of the coaching sessions with the second employee.
* **Frequency of Coaching 2**: Select how often you would like to meet for coaching sessions with the second employee (Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly).
* **Meeting Time 2**: Select your preferred meeting time with the second employee.
* **Duration of Coaching 2**: Enter the number of sessions you would like to have with the second employee.
* **Meeting Location 2**: Select where you would like to have the coaching sessions with the second employee (On-site, Off-site, or Remote).
* **Coaching Location 2**: Select where you would like to have the coaching sessions with the second employee (Office, Home, or Park).

### Additional Information
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the coaching sessions.
