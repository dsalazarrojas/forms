# HR Professional Development Fund Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Professional Development Fund Application form is a tool for employees to request funding for professional development initiatives, such as courses, training, or certifications. This form helps employees to submit their requests and track the status of their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, and employee ID.
2. Enter the amount you are requesting for professional development funds.
3. Specify the source of the funding request (e.g., department, company).
4. Provide the title and description of the course, training, or certification you are requesting.
5. Enter the date you need the funds by.
6. (Optional) Select the status of your request (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
7. (Optional) Enter a brief explanation for why your request has been denied (if applicable).
8. Finally, enter any additional notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required)
First name

### 2. **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required)
Last name

### 3. **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required)
Employee ID number

### 4. **Request Amount** (`request_amount`, number, required)
The amount of money you are requesting for professional development funds.

### 5. **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, text, required)
The department or company funding your request.

### 6. **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required)
The title of the course, training, or certification you are requesting.

### 7. **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required)
The date by which you need the funds.

### 8. **Funding Amount** (`funding_amount`, number, required)
The total amount of funds you are requesting.

### 9. **Course Description** (`course_description`, text, required)
A brief description of the course, training, or certification you are requesting.

### 10. **Request Status** (`request_status`, select_one, required)
The current status of your request (Pending, Approved, or Denied).

### 11. **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, required)
Your job title.

### 12. **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required)
The name of your manager.

### 13. **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required)
The email address of your manager.

### 14. **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required)
The status of your request for approval (Pending, Approved, or Denied).

### 15. **Reason for Denial** (`reason_for_denial`, text, required)
A brief explanation for why your request has been denied (if applicable).

### 16. **Notes** (`notes`, text, required)
Any additional comments or information about your request.
