# Employee Feedback On Work Hours Policy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the company's work hours policy. It will help the management understand the experiences, likes, and dislikes of employees regarding the policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions provided on the first page to understand what the form is about.
2. Answer the questions on the following pages, which cover different aspects of the work hours policy.
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Instructions** (`form_instructions`, `note`, optional): This section provides an overview of the form and its purpose.
* **What are your work hours?** (`work_hours`, `text`, optional): Please describe your typical work hours, including the number of hours you work per week and your availability during that time.
* **Start hour** (`hours_start`, `number`, optional): If you have a regular start time, please enter the number of hours you start work.
* **End hour** (`hours_end`, `number`, optional): If you have a regular end time, please enter the number of hours you end work.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments on the work hours policy.
* **How many days do you work?** (`work_days`, `select_one`, optional): Please select which days of the week you typically work.
* **How often do you work?** (`work_hours_often`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select how many hours you work per week (e.g. less than 5 hours, 5-10 hours, more than 10 hours).
* **How do you feel about working long hours?** (`work_hours_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Please select how you feel about working long hours (e.g. positive, negative, neutral).
* **Satisfaction level** (`work_hours_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select your level of satisfaction with your current work hours (e.g. satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, dissatisfied).
* **Adequacy** (`work_hours_adequacy`, `number`, optional): If you feel that your current work hours are adequate, please enter a number (e.g. 5, 7, etc.).
* **Flexibility** (`work_hours_flexibility`, `select_one`, optional): Please select how flexible you feel about your current work hours (e.g. high, medium, low).
* **Please add your feedback here** (`please_add_your_feedback_here`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments you may have.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Your feedback is anonymous, and your responses will be kept confidential.
