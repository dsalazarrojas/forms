## Employee Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Employee Feedback Form" is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from employees on various aspects of their work experience. This form helps management identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance employee engagement, satisfaction, and overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Provide as much detail as you feel is necessary to answer each question.
3. Make sure to submit your feedback and suggestions at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (text, optional): Share your thoughts and experiences about your work environment, colleagues, or any other related topics.
* **Name** (text, optional): Provide your name or a pseudonym for your feedback to be attributed to.
* **Satisfaction** (select one): Indicate your level of satisfaction with your current role, team, or company using the following options:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Suggestion** (text, optional): Share any specific suggestions or ideas you have to improve the work environment, processes, or policies.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up discussions or further clarification).
* **Phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number (if you want to be contacted via phone for further discussion or clarification).
* **Note** (note, optional): Write any additional comments or notes that you feel are relevant to your feedback.
* **Category** (text, optional): Choose a relevant category or topic related to your feedback (e.g., "Work Environment", "Team Collaboration", etc.).
* **Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your feedback or suggestion, making it easier for reviewers to understand your comments.

## Tips

* Please keep your feedback and suggestions constructive and respectful.
* You can submit your feedback anonymously if you prefer, but please note that it may affect the ability to follow up or discuss your points.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to your management team or HR department.
