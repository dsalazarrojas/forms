# Hospitality Seasonal Employment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from seasonal employees on their experiences with the hospital's employment practices. The goal of this form is to help the hospital's management improve the overall experience and satisfaction of seasonal employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Seasonal Employment" page.
2. Enter your feedback in the "Feedback" text field. This is your opportunity to provide any comments, suggestions, or concerns about your experience as a seasonal employee.
3. Select your level of satisfaction with your experience as a seasonal employee. Choose from "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
4. If applicable, select all the departments you worked with during your seasonal employment. This will help us understand which areas of the hospital may need improvement.
5. If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvements, enter them in the "Improvements" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Employment** (`seasonal_employment`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your experience as a seasonal employee, including any notable events or interactions that stand out during your time with us.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): This is your chance to provide any comments, suggestions, or concerns about your experience as a seasonal employee. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of satisfaction with your experience as a seasonal employee. If you are unsure, or have mixed feelings, please select "Dissatisfied" and explain in the feedback field.
* **Departments** (`departments`, select_multiple, required: false): If you worked with multiple departments, please select all that apply. This will help us understand which areas of the hospital may need improvement.
* **Improvements** (`improvements`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvements, please enter them here. This is your chance to make a positive impact on our seasonal employment practices.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide concrete examples or details.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a member of our HR team for clarification.
