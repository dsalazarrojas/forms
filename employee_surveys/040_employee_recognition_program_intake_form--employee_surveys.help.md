# Employee Recognition Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Recognition Program Intake Form" is designed to help employees nominate their colleagues for recognition within the company. This form captures essential details about the nominee, their achievements, and the recognition type. Please complete this form accurately to ensure a fair evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by selecting the employee you wish to nominate** by filling in the "Employee Name" field.
2. **Provide a brief reason for the nomination** in the "Reason for Nomination" field.
3. **Choose the recognition type** that best fits the nominee's achievement (e.g., Award, Bonus, Gift).
4. **Specify the date of the achievement** you're nominating for, if applicable.
5. **Indicate the period of the achievement** (e.g., regular, occasional, or one-time).
6. **Add any additional comments** about the nominee's performance or achievement.
7. **Select the department and team** the nominee belongs to, if applicable.
8. **Provide the names of the team members** involved in the nomination, if applicable.
9. **Add any further comments or details** to support the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Nominations**: Provide a brief description of the employee you wish to nominate.
* **Reason for Nomination**: Give a clear and concise reason for nominating the employee, e.g., "For their exceptional performance on project XYZ".
* **Recognition Type**: Choose one of the provided recognition types (Award, Bonus, Gift) that best fits the nominee's achievement.
* **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the achievement, if applicable.
* **Nomination Period**: Indicate the period of the achievement (e.g., Regular, Occasional, One-time).
* **Nomination Description**: Add any additional comments or details about the nominee's performance or achievement.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the full name of the employee you're nominating.
* **Department**: Indicate the department the nominee belongs to, if applicable.
* **Team Name**: Indicate the team the nominee belongs to, if applicable.
* **Team Members**: List the team members involved in the nomination, if applicable.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any further comments or details to support the nomination.

## Tips
* Ensure that the nomination is accurate and complete to facilitate a fair evaluation process.
* Only submit one nomination per employee per month.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR department.
