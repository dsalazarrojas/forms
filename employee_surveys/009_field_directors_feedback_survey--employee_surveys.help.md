# field_directors_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from directors on their experiences and perceptions about their manager, organization, and overall work environment. The data collected will help the organization identify areas for improvement and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field to express your feedback and suggestions.
2. Provide detailed responses in the text fields (e.g., "Feedback about Manager", "Suggestions for Improvement").
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback about Manager** (`Feedback about Manager`, text, optional): This field is for providing feedback about your manager. Please be specific and provide concrete examples or reasons for your feedback.
* **Challenges faced** (`Challenges faced`, select multiple, optional): Select all the challenges you have faced in your role as a director.
* **Satisfaction with Management** (`Satisfaction with Management`, select one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the management in your organization.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`Areas for Improvement`, text, optional): Suggest areas for improvement in your organization.
* **Feedback about Organization** (`Feedback about Organization`, text, optional): Provide feedback about your organization.
* **Satisfaction with Resources** (`Satisfaction with Resources`, select one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the resources provided by your organization.
* **Communication with Team** (`Communication with Team`, select multiple, optional): Select all the types of communication you have with your team.
* **Support from Organizers** (`Support from Organizers`, select one, optional): Choose how supported you feel by your organizers.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, select one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with your overall experience in the organization.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`Suggestions for Improvement`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for improving your experience and the organization.
