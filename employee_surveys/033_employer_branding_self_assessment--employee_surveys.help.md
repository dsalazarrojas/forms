## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool designed to gather feedback from employees on their perception of the company's branding. Their responses will help the organization understand its image and culture, enabling it to make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
This form has several sections that you will need to fill out, each addressing a different aspect of the company's branding. Take a few minutes to answer each question honestly, providing the best information you can. You can choose from the options provided for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is the first page of the form, where you can write any general thoughts or comments about the company's branding. This section is optional, but it's a good place to start discussing your overall impression.
* **Company Image** (`2`, `number`, required: false): Imagine you are representing the company to a potential client or customer. On a scale of 1-10, how strong is the company's image? Think of how well the company's name and reputation are seen in the public eye.
* **Employee Engagement** (`3`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-10, how engaged do you feel in the company? This includes your motivation, commitment, and sense of purpose.
* **Employer Value Propositions** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option that best describes the company's value proposition. Options:

* **Work-Life Balance** (`5`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-10, how important do you feel work-life balance is for the company? This includes factors like work hours, vacation time, and employee benefits.
* **Company Culture** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that best describe the company's culture. Options:
  * Option 1
  * Option 2

* **Leadership** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option that best describes the company's leadership style. Options:
  * Option 1

* **Employee Satisfaction** (`10`, `number`, required: false): On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with your current level of satisfaction with the company? This includes your overall happiness and fulfillment with your job and work environment.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide thoughtful responses to each question.
* Choose the option that best reflects your personal opinion.
* If you're unsure about an option, try to select the one that feels most true.
* Remember, this is a self-assessment tool. Take a few minutes to answer each question, and provide as much detail as you can.
