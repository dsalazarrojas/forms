# Employee Communication Tools Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Communication Tools Satisfaction Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience with communication tools used within the company. This form aims to measure the effectiveness of these tools in facilitating communication and collaboration among team members. The purpose of this form is to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the communication landscape within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction and understanding the purpose of the questionnaire.
2. Answer the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For each question, select the correct option from the provided choices or type in your answer in the text fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prophets introduction**: This is a note field where you can read the introduction and understand the purpose of the questionnaire.
* **Communication tools used**: Select all the tools that you use for communication, such as email, instant messaging apps, or project management software.
* **Prophets rate communication**: Rate your experience with the communication tools used, on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Feedback about prophets**: Provide any feedback you have about the communication tools used, such as what works well and what doesn't.
* **Collaboration with team**: Select whether you collaborate with your team members during meetings, phone calls, or online discussions.
* **Communication channels used**: Select all the channels you use for communication, such as email, phone calls, or video conferencing tools.
* **Communication satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the communication tools used, on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Suggestions for improvement**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving the communication tools used, such as new features or functionalities.
* **Prophets suggestions**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions that are specific to the prophets.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the communication tools used.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* Provide specific examples and details to support your answers.
* Don't forget to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any technical issues while completing the form, contact your administrator for assistance.
