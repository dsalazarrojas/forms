# Employee Feedback On Leadership Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee feedback on leadership evaluation, helping to assess how well leaders are doing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on the form honestly, providing detailed feedback on your leadership experiences.
2. Choose the relevant categories that apply to your feedback on the "Category" field.
3. Provide additional comments on the "Evaluation Form Comments" fields if you have more to say.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`): Share your thoughts on your leadership experience. This field is for providing an open-ended feedback on your leader's performance.
* **Feedback On Leadership** (`feedback_on_leadership`): Write a brief statement or sentence about your feedback on the leader's performance.
* **Employee Suggestions** (`employee_suggestions`): Suggest areas where the leader could improve or new ideas for their growth.
* **Category** (`employee_suggestions_category`): Select one of the options (Yes, No) to indicate if the suggestion is relevant.
* **Category** (`category`): Select one or more categories that apply to your feedback, such as Communication, Leadership, or Teamwork.
* **Evaluation Form Comments** (`evaluation_form_comments`, `evaluation_form_comments_2`, `evaluation_form_comments_3`): Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about your leadership evaluation experience. These fields are for providing more detailed feedback. 

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- Keep your feedback focused on your own experiences and perceptions.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask a colleague or supervisor for guidance.
