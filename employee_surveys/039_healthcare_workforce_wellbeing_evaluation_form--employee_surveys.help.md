# Healthcare Workforce Wellbeing Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Workforce Wellbeing Evaluation Form is designed to help healthcare workers assess their current state of well-being, burnout, compassion, compassion fatigue, self-care, and workload. This form is meant to be a self-assessment tool to help workers identify areas that need improvement and provide insights for their organizations to address.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each page to access the corresponding sections of the form.
- Answer each question truthfully, taking as much time as you need.
- Select the options that best describe your current state of well-being, burnout, compassion, compassion fatigue, self-care, and workload.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1**: This page is the introduction to the form and contains a hint to review the form's purpose and questions.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Section A**: Choose up to three options that best describe your current state of well-being, burnout, compassion, compassion fatigue, self-care, and workload. Choose as many or as few options as you need to accurately represent your state.
	+ Wellbeing: Feeling well, rested, and energized.
	+ Burnout: Feeling exhausted, depleted, and unable to cope.
	+ Compassion: Showing empathy and care for patients.
	+ Compassion Fatigue: Feeling emotionally exhausted due to excessive empathy.
	+ Self-Care: Prioritizing activities that promote physical and emotional well-being.
	+ Workload: Feeling overwhelmed by tasks and responsibilities.
* **Section B**, **Section C**, **Section D**, and **Section E**: Choose one option that best describes your current state of well-being, burnout, compassion, compassion fatigue, self-care, and workload.
* **Section F**, **Section G**, and **Section H**: Write a brief description of your current state of well-being, burnout, compassion, compassion fatigue, self-care, and workload.
* **Evaluation Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for this evaluation form.

Please note that this form is a self-assessment tool, and the answers are meant to provide insights for your organization to improve your well-being.
