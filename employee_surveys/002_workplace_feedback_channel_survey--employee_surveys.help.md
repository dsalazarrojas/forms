# Workplace Feedback Channel Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience with the workplace feedback channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your employee ID number and manager's email address in the respective fields.
2. Choose the feedback channels you have used in the past month.
3. Select the feedback channels you would like to use in the future.
4. Provide a brief feedback message on your experience with the feedback channels.
5. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please include them in the "Feedback" field.
6. Finally, select the date and time when you usually use the feedback channels.
7. Click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, required): Please enter your manager's email address.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Please enter your employee ID number.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Please enter your manager's email address if you have it.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Please enter your department name if you have one.
* **Feedback Channel** (`feedback_channel`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the feedback channels you have used in the past month.
* **Feedback Channels** (`feedback_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the feedback channels you would like to use in the future.
* **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, text, optional): Please provide a brief feedback message on your experience with the feedback channels.
* **Feedback Message 2** (`feedback_message_2`, text, optional): Please provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please select the date when you usually use the feedback channels.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please select the time when you usually use the feedback channels.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Please provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, note, optional): Please provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is the final step to submit your feedback.
