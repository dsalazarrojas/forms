# Seasonal Staff Employment Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from seasonal staff members on their experience, satisfaction, and engagement with their job, manager, and colleagues. Your input will help us understand your needs and make improvements to enhance your working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the job you are currently or previously employed with us from the dropdown menu or enter the job name if not listed.
2. Answer the questions regarding your satisfaction with your job, manager, colleagues, and other aspects of your employment. Choose one of the options provided for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to share them in the comments section.
4. Once you have completed the form, click "Submit" to send it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, note, required): Enter your title for this survey, e.g., "Satisfaction with Seasonal Job at XYZ".
* **Job Name** (`job_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or previous job with us, if not already listed in the dropdown menu.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Describe your job title or position.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the department where you worked.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you worked.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Value 1: Temporary/Hourly
	+ Value 2: Seasonal
	+ Value 3: Permanent
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Option 1: Current Employee
	+ Option 2: Former Employee
	+ Option 3: Both
* **Satisfaction with Job** (`satisfaction_with_job`, select_one, optional):
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Satisfaction with Manager** (`satisfaction_with_manager`, select_one, optional):
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Satisfaction with Colleagues** (`satisfaction_with_colleagues`, select_one, optional):
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Communication with Management** (`communication_with_management`, select_one, optional):
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Enter your overall satisfaction score on a scale of 1-5.
* **Would you recommend this job to a friend or family member?** (`recommendation`, select_one, optional):
	+ True
	+ False
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Season** (`season`, select_one, optional):
	+ Spring
	+ Summer
	+ Autumn
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your employment.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your employment.
* **Total Hours Worked** (`total_hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the total hours you worked.
* **Pay** (`pay`, text, optional): Describe your compensation or pay rate.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, text, optional): List any benefits you received.
* **Is feedback important?** (`feedback_important`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ True
	+ False
* **How often is feedback given?** (`feedback_frequency`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Often
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
* **What type of feedback do you get?** (`feedback_content`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Constructive
	+ Negative
	+ Positive
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is the final step to submit your feedback.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* Your input is invaluable, and we appreciate your time and feedback.
