# Workplace Culture Trends Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the workplace culture in your company. It will help us understand the current trends and sentiments within the organization, allowing us to identify areas of improvement and create a more positive work environment. Please fill out this form to contribute to our understanding of your workplace culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information about your workplace. You will need to provide your company name, your name, your department, your job position, your manager's name, email, phone number, and address. You will also need to provide information about your team size and your manager's team size. Additionally, you will be asked about your level of employee satisfaction and your team lead's information. Please make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or business cards.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter the department you work in (e.g. Sales, Marketing, Customer Service, etc.).
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your direct supervisor's or manager's name.
* **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager's Phone Number** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager's Address** (`manager_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's address.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of people in your team.
* **Manager's Manager's Name** (`manager_manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's direct supervisor's name.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, required/optional): Enter your team lead's name.
* **Team Lead's Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your team lead's email address.
* **Team Lead's Phone Number** (`team_lead_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your team lead's phone number.
* **Team Lead's Address** (`team_lead_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your team lead's address.
* **Manager's Team Size** (`manager_team_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of people in your team lead's team.
* **Manager's Manager's Email** (`manager_manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your manager's direct supervisor's email address.
* **Manager's Manager's Phone Number** (`manager_manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's direct supervisor's phone number.
* **Manager's Manager's Address** (`manager_manager_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's direct supervisor's address.
* **Manager's Manager's Team Size** (`manager_manager_team_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of people in your manager's team.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction with your workplace culture. The options are:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
