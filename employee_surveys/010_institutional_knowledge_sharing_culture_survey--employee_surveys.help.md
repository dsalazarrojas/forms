# Institutional Knowledge Sharing Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather information about the culture of knowledge sharing within your institution. It aims to understand how well the institution supports collaboration, communication, and information sharing among its employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please take a few minutes to read through each field carefully.
2. Answer each question truthfully, based on your personal experience within the institution.
3. You can select multiple options for select_multiple type fields.
4. For the collaboration_score field (id: 9), please type your answer in the text box.

* **Survey Introduction** (survey_introduction, text, not required): This field is an optional space for you to provide any additional information or context about your institution that may be helpful for the survey.
