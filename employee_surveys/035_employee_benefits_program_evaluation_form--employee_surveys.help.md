# Employee Benefits Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees evaluate the effectiveness of our company's employee benefits program. It will collect information about employee satisfaction with the program, their participation in it, and any comments they may have. The goal is to gather feedback that will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name and job title in the "Employee Name" and "Job Title" fields, respectively.
2. Choose your department from the list provided.
3. Indicate whether you are currently participating in the employee benefits program by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Benefits Program" field.
4. Rate your program satisfaction on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being "Very dissatisfied" and 10 being "Very satisfied" in the "Program Satisfaction" field.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your employee name as it is written on your ID badge or in our HR system.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, **optional**): Enter your current job title as it is listed in our HR system.
* **Department** (`department`, text, **optional**): Choose the department you belong to from the list provided.
* **Benefits Program** (`benefits_program`, select_one, **optional**): Check the box next to "Yes" if you are currently participating in the employee benefits program.
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, number, **optional**): Rate your satisfaction with the employee benefits program on a scale of 1-10. 
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, **optional**): Provide any comments or feedback you may have about the employee benefits program.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and honestly to ensure accurate feedback.
- If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide specific examples or reasons to support your feedback.
- If you are not participating in the program, please indicate why not.
- If you have any questions or need help completing the form, contact your HR representative.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback, it is greatly appreciated!
