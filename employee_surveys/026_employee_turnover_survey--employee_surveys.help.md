# Employee Turnover Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Turnover Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from employees who are leaving the company. It's a way for us to understand the reasons behind their departure and gather insights on how to improve our work environment and processes for future employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions, especially in the "Reason for Leaving" section.
3. Don't worry if you're not sure about some questions; just choose the best option that applies to your situation.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section 2: Reason for Leaving** (`Reason for Leaving`, `text`, required/optional): This section asks you to describe the reason you are leaving the company, including the date. Please be as detailed as possible, and if you have a specific date, please provide it.
* **Section 3: What was the main reason you are leaving this company?** (`What was the main reason you are leaving this company?`, `select_one`, required): This question is asking you to choose the most significant reason for your departure. Please select the option that best represents your reason.
* **Section 5: Reason for Leaving (Other)** (`Reason for Leaving (Other)`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide a more detailed explanation if your reason for leaving is not listed in the previous question.
* **Section 7: How many years did you work at the organization?** (`How many years did you work at the organization?`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question is asking you to choose all the years you worked at the company that apply to your situation.
* **Section 11: Did you experience any of the following benefits while working at this organization?** (`Did you experience any of the following benefits while working at this organization?`, `select_one`, optional): This question is asking you to choose all the benefits that you experienced while working at the company that apply to your situation. Please choose the options that best describe your experience.

**Tips**

* Please be honest and as detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
* If you're unsure about some questions, just choose the best option that applies to your situation.
* Remember, this form is not a performance evaluation or disciplinary action, but rather a tool to help us improve our work environment and processes for future employees.
