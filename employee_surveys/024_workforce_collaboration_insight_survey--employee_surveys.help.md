# Workforce Collaboration Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Collaboration Insight Survey is designed to gather information about your team's collaboration, communication, and dynamics. This survey aims to help your team understand how you work together, identify areas of improvement, and develop strategies to enhance team productivity and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and honestly.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General information** (`general_info`, text, optional):
	+ This section is a brief introduction to your team.
	+ Please provide a short description of your team, including their size, composition, and work environment.
* **Team information** (`team_info`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for you to describe your team's structure, roles, and responsibilities.
	+ Provide information about your team's goals, objectives, and expectations.
* **Contact information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for you to provide your contact information and any other relevant details.
* **Team leadership** (`team_leadership`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for you to describe your team's leadership structure and communication style.
* **Team members** (`team_members`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for you to list the names and positions of team members.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for you to describe how your team collaborates and communicates with each other.
	+ Provide examples of successful collaborations and areas for improvement.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for you to describe your team's communication channels and frequency.
* **Collaboration insights** (`collaboration_insights`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for you to provide any additional information that you think would be helpful to understand your team dynamics.
* **Team dynamics** (`team_dynamics`, text, optional):
	+ This section is for you to describe your team's work environment, culture, and social dynamics.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask your team lead or HR representative for guidance.
* Remember that this survey is a tool to help your team improve, so don't worry about sharing your honest opinions.
