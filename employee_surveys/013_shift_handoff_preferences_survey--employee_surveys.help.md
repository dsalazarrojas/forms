# Shift Handoff Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees on their preferences for shift handoffs, including communication methods, frequency, and priorities. The purpose of this form is to understand each employee's individual needs and provide a more effective handoff process. Please fill out this form thoroughly and honestly to help us better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your preferred answers from the provided fields and submit. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of shift handoffs. You can navigate through the sections by scrolling down or using the "Next" and "Previous" buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Shift Preferences (id: 1)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the shifts you prefer for handoff. Options: Yes, No.
  - This field determines which shifts you would like to have handoff support for. Select all the shifts that apply to you.

- **Communication Method (id: 2)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred communication method. Options: Phone, Text, Email, In-app.
  - This field allows you to choose how you would like to receive handoff communications. Select the method that works best for you.

- **Communication Frequency (id: 3)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Check the frequencies at which you would like to receive handoff communications. Options: Yes, No.
  - This field determines how often you would like to receive handoff communications. Check the frequencies that apply to you.

- **Team Lead Contact (id: 4)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred contact method for the team lead. Options: Email, Phone, Slack, In-app.
  - This field allows you to choose how you would like to contact the team lead for handoff support. Select the method that works best for you.

- **Communication Priority (id: 5)** (`number`, required: false): Enter a numerical value to indicate your preferred level of communication priority. (Note: This field is not explicitly defined in the options, so it seems to be an open-ended field, but based on the label, it likely refers to the level of priority you would like for handoff communications.)
  - This field is intended to allow you to specify a numerical value for your preferred level of communication priority. However, as there are no options provided, it's best to use your best judgment when filling out this field.

- **Time Off (id: 6)** (`number`, required: false): Enter a numerical value to indicate your preferred time off for handoff communications. (Note: Like the previous field, there are no options provided, so use your best judgment.)
  - Similar to the previous field, this one asks for a numerical value for your preferred time off for handoff communications.

- **Communication Method Improvements (id: 7)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Check if you have any method improvements for handoff communications. Options: Yes, No.
  - This field is for suggesting improvements to the handoff communication methods. Check the options that apply to you.

- **Improvement Areas (id: 8)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Check the areas where you would like to see improvements. Options: Yes, No.
  - This field is for suggesting areas where improvements could be made to the handoff process. Check the options that apply to you.

- **Action Items (id: 9, 10, 11)** (`note`, required: false): Enter any specific action items you would like to address during the handoff process. (Note: There are multiple action item fields, but they are identical, so I've only included one example here.)
  - This field is for entering specific action items you would like to be addressed during the handoff process.

- **Shift Handoff Process (id: 12)** (`note`, required: false): Describe your ideal shift handoff process. 
  - This field is for describing your preferred shift handoff process in as much detail as possible.

- **Team Handoff Process (id: 13)** (`note`, required: false): Describe your team handoff process. 
  - This field is for describing your team handoff process in as much detail as possible.

Please submit your answers and help us improve our shift handoff process!
