# Daily Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Daily Experience Feedback" form is designed to gather employee feedback about their daily work experience. This form helps management understand how employees perceive their daily work experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form once for each day you want to provide feedback.
2. Rate your experience using the rating scale (Very Poor, Poor, Average, Good, Great).
3. Provide comments about your experience in the text fields.
4. Optionally, you can provide your date and time of the feedback.
