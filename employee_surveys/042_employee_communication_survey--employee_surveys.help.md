# Employee Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the current state of communication within your team, including how often team members communicate with each other, their preferred method of communication, and challenges they face in communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your communication frequency with your team members:
	* How often do you communicate with your team members?
	* How often do you think you should be communicating with team members?
	* How often do you think you should be having team dynamics?
2. Choose your preferred method of communication:
	* What is your preferred method of communication?
3. Select the methods of communication you use most often:
	* What method of communication do you use most often?
4. Answer the multiple-choice questions about team dynamics:
	* What team dynamics would help communication?
	* How satisfied are you with the current state of team dynamics?
5. Provide any comments or suggestions on how to improve communication:
	* Do you have any comments on how to improve communication in your team?
	* What questions would you like to see on the employee communication survey?
6. Answer the multiple-choice questions about challenges you face in communication:
	* What are some common challenges you face in communication in your team?
7. Provide any comments or suggestions on how to improve communication:
	* Do you have any suggestions on how to improve communication in your team?
	* What action will you take to improve communication in your team?

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you communicate with your team members?**
	+ This question asks how often you interact with your team members.
	+ Choose the answer that best reflects your current communication frequency.
* **What is your preferred method of communication?**
	+ This question asks how you prefer to communicate with your team members.
	+ Choose the method that you find most effective for you.
* **What method of communication do you use most often?**
	+ This question asks about the methods you commonly use to communicate with your team members.
	+ Choose all the methods that apply to you.
* **What team dynamics would help communication?**
	+ This question asks about the team dynamics that would improve communication.
	+ Choose all the team dynamics that apply to you.
* **What are some common challenges you face in communication in your team?**
	+ This question asks about the challenges you face in communication.
	+ Choose all the challenges that apply to you.
* **Do you have any comments on how to improve communication in your team?**
	+ This question asks for any additional comments or suggestions you have to improve communication.
	+ Provide any additional information you think would be helpful.
* **What questions would you like to see on the employee communication survey?**
	+ This question asks for any additional questions you think should be included on the survey.
	+ Provide any additional questions you think would be relevant.
* **What are you satisfied with the current state of team dynamics?**
	+ This question asks about your satisfaction with the current state of team dynamics.
	+ Choose your answer based on how you feel about the current state of team dynamics.
* **What is your improvement plan for team dynamics?**
	+ This question asks about your plan to improve team dynamics.
	+ Provide any additional information you think would be helpful.
* **What action will you take to improve team dynamics?**
	+ This question asks about the specific action you will take to improve team dynamics.
	+ Provide any additional information you think would be helpful.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Provide any additional comments or suggestions you think would be helpful.
* Choose your answers based on your personal experience and perspective.
* Remember, this survey is designed to help improve communication within your team.
