# Performance Bonus Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Bonus Request Form is designed to allow employees to request a bonus for a specific performance-related achievement or milestone. This form should only be used when an employee wants to request a bonus for a particular event or achievement.

## How To Complete This Form

To request a performance bonus, please follow these steps:

1. **Submit Bonus Request**: Select 'Yes' if you want to submit a bonus request. This will trigger the form to display the relevant fields.
2. Fill out the relevant fields according to your bonus request:
   - **Reason for Bonus Request**: Provide a clear and concise reason for your bonus request. This is your opportunity to explain why you deserve a bonus for this performance.
   - **Performance Level**: Select the performance level that reflects your accomplishment. This will help assess your bonus eligibility.
   - **Bonus Amount**: Enter the specific bonus amount you are requesting.
   - **Date of Request**: Enter the date of the event or achievement for which you are requesting a bonus.
   - **Bonus Type**: Select 'Yes' if the bonus type you are requesting is specific (e.g., one-time or ongoing).
   - **Bonus Frequency**: Select the bonus frequency you are requesting (e.g., one-time, quarterly, or yearly).
   - **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID, which can be found in your employee information page.
   - **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee information page.
   - **Department ID**: Enter your department ID, which can be found in your employee information page.
   - **Submitted By**: Select 'Yes' if the bonus request is being submitted by you.

* **Submit Bonus Request**: This field is used to trigger the form to display the relevant fields for a bonus request.
*  **Reason for Bonus Request**: A clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting a bonus for this performance. This field should be filled out to help assess your bonus eligibility.
*   **Performance Level**: Select the performance level that best reflects your accomplishment.
*   **Bonus Amount**: Enter the specific bonus amount you are requesting.
*   **Date of Request**: Enter the date of the event or achievement for which you are requesting a bonus.
*   **Bonus Type**: Select 'Yes' if the bonus type you are requesting is specific (e.g., one-time or ongoing).
*   **Bonus Frequency**: Select the bonus frequency you are requesting (e.g., one-time, quarterly, or yearly).
*   **Employee ID**: Your employee ID, which can be found in your employee information page.
*   **Employee Name**: Your name as it appears on your employee information page.
*   **Department ID**: Your department ID, which can be found in your employee information page.
*   **Submitted By**: Select 'Yes' if the bonus request is being submitted by you.

## Tips
Please ensure that you only request a bonus for specific performance-related achievements. Bonus requests should be based on clear and verifiable evidence. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department for guidance.
