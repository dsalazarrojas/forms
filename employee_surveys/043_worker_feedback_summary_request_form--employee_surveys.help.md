# Worker Feedback Summary Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from employees about their work experiences and interactions with their managers or colleagues. The feedback will be reviewed by HR or management to help improve work environments and relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Make sure to keep your feedback constructive and respectful.
3. Check the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your company records.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, text): Enter the department you work in.
* **Manager Name** (`employee_manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager or the person you are providing feedback on.
* **Survey Date** (`employee_survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date you received the feedback or the survey was taken.
* **Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, required): Provide a detailed and honest feedback about your work experience and interaction with your manager or colleague.
* **Feedback Date** (`employee_feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this feedback form.

## Tips
* Be sure to keep your feedback concise and focused on work-related experiences.
* Avoid using profanity or making personal attacks.
* Make sure to review and edit your feedback before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your HR or management for clarification.
