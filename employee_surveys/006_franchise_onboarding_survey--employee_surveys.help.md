# Franchise Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about new franchisees, helping the franchise management team create a tailored onboarding process and build a strong relationship with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required fields as follows:
- Page 1: Enter your first name.
- Page 2: Enter your last name.
- Page 3: Enter your email address (in the format: `example@email.com`).
- Page 4: Enter your phone number.
- Page 5: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- Page 6: Enter the date you joined the franchise in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
- Page 7: Enter the number of years you have experience in the franchise.
- Page 8: Enter the number of months you have experience in the franchise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the format `example@email.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Date Joined** (`date_of_joined`, date, optional): Enter the date you joined the franchise in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Experience in Years** (`experience_in_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have experience in the franchise.
* **Experience in Months** (`experience_in_months`, number, optional): Enter the number of months you have experience in the franchise.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the date formats are correct for the `Date of Birth` and `Date Joined` fields.
- Enter your information accurately to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
