# Mental Wellness Among Staff Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about the mental wellness of staff members, aiming to identify areas where employees may be struggling and provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the options that best fit your response to each question.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions, indicated by a (*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Work Environment** (text)
* **Label:** Work Environment
* **Type:** Text
* **Purpose:** Describe your current work environment. Please provide a brief description of your work setting, such as your office or workspace.

### 2. **Stress Levels** (number)
* **Label:** Stress Levels
* **Type:** Number
* **Purpose:** Indicate your current level of stress, with higher numbers indicating higher stress levels.

### 3. **Support Needs** (select multiple)
* **Label:** Support Needs
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Purpose:** Check all the support options that you feel you need or have access to, such as mental health services, wellness programs, or manager support.

### 4. **Feedback** (text)
* **Label:** Feedback
* **Type:** Text
* **Purpose:** Share any additional feedback or comments about your mental wellness or work environment.

### 5. **Well Being** (note)
* **Label:** Well Being
* **Type:** Note
* **Purpose:** Provide any relevant comments or thoughts about your current well-being, such as feelings of burnout or lack of motivation.

### 6. **Employee Support** (select one)
* **Label:** Employee Support
* **Type:** Select One
* **Purpose:** Indicate whether you receive support from your manager or HR department.

### 7. **Team Support** (select multiple)
* **Label:** Team Support
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Purpose:** Check all the team support options that you feel you have access to, such as peer support or team events.

### 8. **Work Life Balance** (select multiple)
* **Label:** Work Life Balance
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Purpose:** Indicate your current level of balance between work and personal life, with options for work-life balance, work-life integration, or no balance.

### 9. **Work Life Balance Score** (number)
* **Label:** Work Life Balance Score
* **Type:** Number
* **Purpose:** Provide a score indicating your current level of work-life balance, with higher numbers indicating better balance.

### 10. **Mental Health Services** (select multiple)
* **Label:** Mental Health Services
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Purpose:** Check all the mental health services that you have access to, such as counseling or employee assistance programs.

### 11. **Employee Wellness Programs** (select multiple)
* **Label:** Employee Wellness Programs
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Purpose:** Indicate which wellness programs you participate in or have access to, such as fitness classes or meditation sessions.

### 12. **Manager Support** (select one)
* **Label:** Manager Support
* **Type:** Select One
* **Purpose:** Indicate whether you receive support from your manager.

### 13. **Manager Wellness Programs** (select multiple)
* **Label:** Manager Wellness Programs
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Purpose:** Check all the wellness programs that your manager participates in or has access to, such as stress management or mindfulness training.

### 14. **Department** (select one)
* **Label:** Department
* **Type:** Select One
* **Purpose:** Indicate which department you work in.

Note: Please answer all required questions.
