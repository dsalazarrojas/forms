# Holiday Office Hours Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their holiday office hours experience. Your input will help us better understand your needs and improve our office hours schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each field honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For the "How satisfied are you with holiday office hours?" field, select one of the options that best reflects your level of satisfaction.
3. For the "How well are your holiday office hour needs met?" field, enter a number that represents how well your needs are met out of a scale of 0-10.
4. In the "What would you prefer for future office hours?" field, select all the days you would like to see office hours held.
5. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have.
6. For the "Email" field, enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on future office hours changes.
7. For the "Phone" field, enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on future office hours changes.
8. Finally, review your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Office Hours Awareness** (`holiday_office_hours_awareness`, number, required: false): This field is not required but will help us understand if you are aware of our current office hours schedule.
* **How satisfied are you with holiday office hours?** (`office_hours_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with our current office hours schedule.
	+ Highly Satisfied: You are extremely satisfied with our current office hours schedule.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with our current office hours schedule.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral or have no strong feelings about our current office hours schedule.
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with our current office hours schedule.
	+ Highly Dissatisfied: You are extremely dissatisfied with our current office hours schedule.
* **How well are your holiday office hour needs met?** (`holiday_office_hours_fulfillment`, number, required: false): Enter a number between 0 and 10 that indicates how well your needs are met by our current office hours schedule.
* **What would you prefer for future office hours?** (`future_office_hours_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more days of the week that you would like to see office hours held.
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have for improving our office hours schedule.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on future office hours changes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on future office hours changes.
* **Submitted** (`submitted_date`, date, required: false): This field will be automatically populated with the date and time of your submission.
