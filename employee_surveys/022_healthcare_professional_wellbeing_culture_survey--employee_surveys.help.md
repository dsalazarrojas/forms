# Healthcare Professional Wellbeing Culture Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to measure the wellbeing culture in a healthcare setting. It will help you provide insights into areas of strengths and weaknesses to support the wellbeing of our staff.

## How To Complete This Form
- First, please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
- You can choose only one option for the "culture_climate" question.
- For the "team_climate" and "communication_climate" questions, select all the options that apply.
- You can provide any additional feedback in the "feedback" field if you would like.
- If you have any comments about the wellbeing resources available, please provide them in the "wellbeing_resources" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Form**: This is the main survey form where you can provide your answers to all the questions.
* **Demographic Info**: Please provide your demographic information, such as your employee ID, role, and department, if you are willing to do so.
* **Manager Name**: Please enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Please enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Please enter your manager's phone number.
* **Wellbeing Score**: Please provide your score for the current wellbeing culture.
* **Culture Climate**: Please select one of the options that best describes the current culture climate.
	+ Option 1: Positive
	+ Option 2: Negative
	+ Option 3: Neutral
* **Team Climate**: Please select all the options that apply to describe the current team climate.
	+ Option 1: Positive
	+ Option 2: Negative
	+ Option 3: Neutral
* **Communication Climate**: Please select all the options that apply to describe the current communication climate.
	+ Option 1: Positive
	+ Option 2: Negative
	+ Option 3: Neutral
* **Feedback**: Please provide any additional feedback or comments you have.
* **Wellbeing Resources**: Please provide any comments on the wellbeing resources available in your organization.
