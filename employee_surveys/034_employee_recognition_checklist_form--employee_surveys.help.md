# Employee Recognition Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Checklist Form is used to track and document employee recognition and appreciation within the organization. This form helps to identify and acknowledge employees' contributions, efforts, and achievements, fostering a positive and motivating work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as prompted, ensuring you answer all required fields (*).
2. Select the recognition type that best suits the employee's achievement.
3. Enter a brief description of the recognition reason.
4. Add any relevant recognition message or note.
5. Verify that the employee's details (name, email, and phone number) are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Name (Field 2)
* **Employee Name**: This field captures the name of the employee being recognized. Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their ID card or official documents.

### Employee Recognition Checklist (Field 1)
* **Employee Recognition Checklist**: This is the main description field where you can type out the employee's achievements, contributions, and efforts.

### Recognition Date (Field 3)
* **Recognition Date**: Enter the date when the recognition took place or when it was decided to be given. Use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### Recognition Time (Field 4)
* **Recognition Time**: Choose the time when the recognition took place or when it was decided to be given. This field is optional, but it helps to provide a more accurate timestamp.

### Recognition Type (Field 5)
* **Recognition Type**: Select the type of recognition that best suits the employee's achievement. Options include:
	+ Employee Award
	+ Team Award
	+ Company Award

### Recognition Reason (Field 6)
* **Recognition Reason**: Briefly describe the reason why the employee is being recognized. This helps to provide context and justification for the recognition.

### Recognition Message (Field 7)
* **Recognition Message**: Add any relevant message or note about the recognition, such as a brief commendation or appreciation message.

### Employee ID (Field 8)
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number for accurate identification purposes.

### Employee Email (Field 9)
* **Employee Email**: Verify the employee's email address for correspondence and notifications.

### Employee Phone (Field 10)
* **Employee Phone**: Enter the employee's phone number for direct contact and communication.

Note: All fields marked with (*) are required.
