# New Hire Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Onboarding Questionnaire is designed to collect essential information from new hires during their onboarding process. This form helps the HR department gather necessary data to create a smooth and efficient transition for the employee, ensuring a quick adaptation to the company culture and work environment. It's not intended to be a lengthy or complicated process, and it's meant to be completed by the new hire during their onboarding session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the most relevant Option from the "New Hire Onboarding" dropdown list.
2. Enter your Job Title, as it appears on your company ID or in your email signature.
3. Select the Hire Date from the calendar or enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your Employee Name, as it appears on your company ID or in your email signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Onboarding** (`new_hire_onboarding`): This field allows you to select the most relevant onboarding option for your role. Please choose one of the available options that best suit your position.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): This field is for entering your job title, as it appears on your company ID or in your email signature. This helps us to identify you correctly within the company.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`): This field is for selecting or entering the date you were hired. You can either use the calendar icon to select a date or enter it manually in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): This field is for entering your full name as it appears on your company ID or in your email signature. This ensures we have the correct spelling and formatting of your name.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete each field accurately and consistently with company documents and your personal records.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your HR representative or ask your manager for guidance.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the form to ensure they are correct and complete.
