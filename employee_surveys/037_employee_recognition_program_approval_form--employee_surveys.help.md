# employee_recognition_program_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee recognition program approval form is used to request approval for employee recognition. Managers use this form to review and approve recognition requests submitted by employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary information ready, including your employee ID, your manager's ID, and the date you would like to receive recognition.
2. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
4. Enter your manager's ID in the "Manager ID" field.
5. Select a date for the "Approval Date" field.
6. Choose the recognition amount to be approved in the "Recognition Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter your unique employee ID to associate this approval request with your personnel record.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Provide your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Manager ID (3)**: Enter your manager's unique ID to ensure they receive notification of this approval request.
* **Approval Date (4)**: Select a date for when you would like to receive recognition.
* **Recognition Amount (5)**: Choose the amount of recognition you would like to receive.
 
## Tips
* Ensure that you have all necessary information and approvals before submitting this form.
* Double-check that your manager's ID is correct to ensure they receive the request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
