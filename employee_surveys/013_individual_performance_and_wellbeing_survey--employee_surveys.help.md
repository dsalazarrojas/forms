# individual_performance_and_wellbeing_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their individual performance and wellbeing, including job satisfaction, motivation, stress levels, and more. The information gathered will help managers or HR understand employee sentiments and make informed decisions to improve work environments and employee experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a response for each question, choosing from the provided options or typing in the text field as required.
2. For text fields, type in your thoughts or answers in the provided space.
3. Ensure you answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your overall satisfaction with your job** (employee_satisfaction): This question asks how satisfied you are with your current job. Choose from very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **How motivated are you at work** (motivation): This question asks how motivated you feel in your role. Select all that apply from very motivated, somewhat motivated, somewhat ambitious, not motivated, or unsure.
* **What is your current stress level** (stress_level): This question inquires about your current stress levels. Choose from very little stress, somewhat little stress, neutral, somewhat high stress, or very high stress.
* **What abilities do you feel are your strengths at work** (job_abilities): This question invites you to type in your strengths or abilities you feel confident about in your current role.
* **How would you describe your current wellbeing** (wellbeing): This question asks how you perceive your current wellbeing. Select from excellent, good, neutral, poor, or very poor.
* **How satisfied are you with your work-life balance** (work_life_balance): This question assesses your satisfaction with work-life balance. Choose from very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **Do you feel you are able to grow in your career** (career_growth): This question asks if you feel you have opportunities to grow or develop in your role. Select True or False.
* **Is there anything you would like to share with your manager or HR** (feedback): This question allows you to type in any thoughts or concerns you'd like to share.
* **Is there anything you would like your manager to know about you** (manager_feedback): Similar to the previous question, type in any thoughts or concerns you'd like your manager to be aware of.
* **What is your manager's name** (manager_name): Type in your manager's name.
* **What is your manager's email** (manager_email): Type in your manager's email address.
* **What is your manager's phone number** (manager_phone): Type in your manager's phone number.
* **Which department do you work in** (department): Type in the name of your department.
* **What is your current job title** (job_title): Type in your current job title.
* **What type of employee are you** (job_type): Type in your employee type (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract).
* **What level are you at in your job** (job_level): Type in your current level of your job (e.g., junior, middle, senior).
* **Do you own your current role or job** (job_ownership): This question asks if you own or have control over your role or job. Select True, False, or unsure.
* **What is your satisfaction level with your current job** (job_satisfaction): This question assesses your satisfaction with your job. Choose from very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **How satisfied are you with your team** (team_collaboration): This question asks how satisfied you are with your team's collaboration. Choose from very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **How satisfied are you with communication in your team** (communication): This question inquires about your satisfaction with team communication. Choose from very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **How much autonomy do you have in your work** (autonomy): This question asks about your level of autonomy. Choose from very high autonomy, high autonomy, moderate autonomy, low autonomy, or very low autonomy.
* **How satisfied are you with your performance** (performance): This question asks about your satisfaction with your performance. Choose from very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **How engaged do you feel at work** (engagement): This question inquires about your engagement levels. Choose from very engaged, somewhat engaged, somewhat disengaged, neutral, or very disengaged.
* **Is your job description up-to-date with current needs** (job_description): This question asks if your current job description matches your needs. Select True or False.
* **Is there anything else you would like to comment on** (survey_comments): This question invites you to type in any additional thoughts or concerns.
