3. Choose how often you work from home (Never, Occasionally, 1-2 times a week, 3-4 times a week, Every day).

5. Choose what affects your work-life balance (Work-life balance, Work, Life).
6. Select where you do most of your work (Office, Home, Coffee shop/Library, Park/Bike path, Other).
7. Select how hybrid work impacts you (Positive, Negative, Neutral).
8. Add any additional feedback you have.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
11. Enter your manager's name and title.
12. Enter your team lead's name and title.
13. Enter your department.

15. Enter your manager's job title.
16. Enter your team lead's department.
17. Enter your team lead's job title.
18. Select your team size.
19. Enter the range of your team size.
