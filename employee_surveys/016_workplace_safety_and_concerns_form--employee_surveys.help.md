# Workplace Safety And Concerns Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting concerns or challenges that impact your workplace safety or overall well-being. It helps management and HR address these concerns and take steps to improve the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:
1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions that apply to your situation.
2. Select the correct options for each question and provide clear, concise answers in the text fields.
3. Make sure to submit your response for review by management and HR.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Have you faced any challenges or concerns?** (`have_you_faced`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the concerns that apply to you, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Work from home** (`work_from_home`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you work, such as "Remote", "On-site", or "Hybrid".
* **What type of concerns?** (`what_type_of_concerns`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the concerns you faced, e.g., "Physical safety", "Bullying", or "Discrimination".
* **Concerns description** (`concerns_description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the concerns or challenges you faced in more detail, including specific incidents or situations.
* **Impact on work** (`concerns_impact`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of impact, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Number of employees affected** (`how_many_employees`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, specify the number of employees impacted by the concern.
* **Have any employees expressed concerns?** (`have_any_employees`, `text`, required: false): If employees have expressed concerns, describe them in detail.
* **Action to address concerns** (`what_will_be`, `text`, required: false): Suggest potential actions to address the concerns, such as "Training", "Policy update", or "Mandatory reporting".
* **Who will be handling concerns?** (`who_will_be`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose who should handle the concerns, such as "HR", "Management", or "Team".
* **When concerns will be addressed** (`when_concerns_will_be_addressed`, `date`, required: false): Provide a date or timeframe for addressing the concerns, if applicable.
* **Other comments** (`other_comments`, text, required: false): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
