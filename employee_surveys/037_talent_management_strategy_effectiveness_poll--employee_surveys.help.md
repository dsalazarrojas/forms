# Talent Management Strategy Effectiveness Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of your talent management strategy across three key areas: Recruiting, Onboarding, and Development. The purpose of this form is to help you understand your team's perceptions and experiences with these processes, identifying areas of improvement and opportunities for growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the sections listed below and answer the questions in each. You can type your responses in the text fields provided. Each section represents a different area of talent management, so be sure to answer in relation to your experience within that context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recruiting (section_1)**: This section asks about your experiences and perceptions of the recruiting process. Please describe any challenges or opportunities you've encountered while being recruited or participating in the recruiting process.
* **Onboarding (section_2)**: In this section, think about the onboarding process for new hires or team members. Describe any positive or negative aspects of the onboarding experience, including any difficulties you've faced while adapting to the team or role.
* **Development (section_3)**: This section is about your experiences and perceptions of the opportunities for growth and development within the team. Discuss any successes or areas for improvement you've seen, including any support or resources you've received or lacked during your time in the team.
* **Submit**: Once you've completed all sections, click "Submit" to send your feedback. Be sure to review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
