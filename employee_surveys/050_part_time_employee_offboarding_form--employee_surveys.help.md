# part-time-employee-offboarding-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help part-time employees complete the "part-time-employee-offboarding-form" correctly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information about your last day of work and reasons for leaving the company.
2. Ensure you have the necessary details about your last day of work, including date, time, and other relevant information.
3. If you have any manager comments or employee comments, please provide them in the "manager comments" and "employee comments" sections.
4. Check the "reason for leaving" section and select the appropriate reason from the provided options. If your reason is not listed, you can select "Other" and provide a brief description in the "reason for leaving other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **part time employee offboarding form 1** (`part_time_employee_offboarding_form_1`, text, required: false): This field is intended for any additional comments or information about the form.
- **date of last day worked** (`date_of_last_day_worked`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your last day of work.
- **reason for leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the reason(s) for your departure from the company. You can choose more than one option if applicable.
- **last day worked date** (`last_day_worked_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your last day of work.
- **last day worked time** (`last_day_worked_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your last day of work.
- **manager comments** (`manager_comments`, text, required: false): This field is for your manager's comments or any additional information they may want to include.
- **manager name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's name.
- **manager contact info** (`manager_contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
- **employee comments** (`employee_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback you may have.
- **outplacement assistance requested** (`outplacement_assistance_requested`, select_one, required: false): Check if you are interested in receiving outplacement assistance. 
- **outplacement contact info** (`outplacement_contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter contact information if you are interested in outplacement assistance.
- **outplacement contact name** (`outplacement_contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person for outplacement assistance.
- **outplacement contact email** (`outplacement_contact_email`, text, required: false): Enter the email of the contact person for outplacement assistance.
- **outplacement contact phone** (`outplacement_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person for outplacement assistance.
- **outplacement notes** (`outplacement_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes about outplacement assistance.
- **last day worked week of** (`last_day_worked_week_of`, number, required: false): Enter the week of the month your last day of work falls in.
- **last day worked day of** (`last_day_worked_day_of`, number, required: false): Enter the day of the week your last day of work falls on.
- **last day worked year** (`last_day_worked_year`, number, required: false): Enter the year of your last day of work.
- **last day worked month** (`last_day_worked_month`, number, required: false): Enter the month of your last day of work.
- **last day worked year of employment** (`last_day_worked_year_of_employment`, number, required: false): Enter the year you started working.
- **reason for leaving other** (`reason_for_leaving_other`, text, required: false): If your reason for leaving is not listed, you can select "Other" and provide a brief description here.
- **last day worked hour** (`last_day_worked_hour`, number, required: false): Enter the hour of the day your last day of work was.
- **last day worked minute** (`last_day_worked_minute`, number, required: false): Enter the minute of the hour your last day of work was.
- **last day worked second** (`last_day_worked_second`, number, required: false): Enter the second of the minute your last day of work was.
- **last day worked millisecond** (`last_day_worked_millisecond`, number, required: false): This field is not clearly explained, but it appears to be intended for millisecond precision time.
