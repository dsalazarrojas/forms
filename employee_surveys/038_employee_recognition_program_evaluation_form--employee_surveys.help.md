# Employee Recognition Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from employees on the Employee Recognition Program. It helps the organization understand how the program is performing and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each field, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2.  Ensure that you answer all the required fields, and if any field is optional, please feel free to provide additional comments if you wish.
3.  For the "Recognition Frequency" field, please enter the frequency of recognition you have received or would like to receive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Survey Form**: A text box where you can provide any relevant information about your experiences with the Employee Recognition Program.
*   **Recognition Programs**: Select one or more of the options to indicate the type of recognition programs you have participated in or would like to participate in. The options are:
    +   **Monetary**: Programs that involve financial rewards.
    +   **Non-monetary**: Programs that involve non-financial rewards, such as public recognition or bonuses.
    +   **Other**: Select this option if you would like to suggest a different type of program.
    +   **Unknown**: If you are unsure or do not have any information about the program.
*   **Program Description**: In this text field, provide a brief description of the recognition program you are commenting on.
*   **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date you evaluated the recognition program. This can be the current date or the date you last evaluated the program.
*   **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time you spent evaluating the recognition program.
*   **Recognition Frequency**: Enter the frequency of recognition you have received or would like to receive. For example, "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", or a specific number of times per year.
*   **Acknowledgement Form**: This field is a text box where you can provide any comments or feedback on the program.
*   **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the program.
*   **Manager Comments**: If you have received feedback from your manager on the recognition program, please enter it here.
*   **Employee Feedback**: Select one or more of the options to indicate your feedback on the program. The options are:
    +   **Strongly Agree**: You strongly agree with the program's effectiveness.
    +   **Somewhat Agree**: You somewhat agree with the program's effectiveness.
    +   **Neutral**: You are neutral about the program's effectiveness.
    +   **Somewhat Disagree**: You somewhat disagree with the program's effectiveness.
    +   **Strongly Disagree**: You strongly disagree with the program's effectiveness.
    +   **Unknown**: If you are unsure or do not have any feedback on the program.

**Tips:**

*   Be honest and as accurate as possible when providing feedback and comments.
*   If unsure, it's okay to select "Unknown" or provide a brief explanation.
*   Your feedback will help the organization improve the Employee Recognition Program.
*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
