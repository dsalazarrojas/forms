# NGO Internal Culture Diversity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gauge the level of inclusivity and diversity within the NGO's internal culture. It will help the management and HR team to identify areas of improvement and create a more inclusive and supportive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question based on your honest opinions and feelings.
2. Use the scale provided for each question, where 1 is "Strongly Disagree" and 4 is "Strongly Agree".
3. Take your time to answer each question carefully, and if you need help, contact the HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feel at Work** (`2`, number, required: false): Please rate how you feel when you are at work, considering your overall feelings towards your job and workplace environment.
* **Feel at Home** (`3`, number, required: false): Rate how you feel when you are at home, considering your overall feelings towards your work and personal life.
* **Feel Included** (`4`, number, required: false): Rate how included you feel in the work environment, considering how valued and respected you are.
* **Feel Valued** (`5`, number, required: false): Rate how valued you feel in the work environment, considering how much you contribute and are appreciated.
* **Feel Respected** (`6`, number, required: false): Rate how respected you feel in the work environment, considering how your opinions and feelings are valued.
* **Inclusion in Policy** (`7`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose all the reasons why you feel included in the work environment, based on the following options:
	+ 1: Diversity and Inclusion Policy is clear and effective.

	+ 4: Employees feel confident in their abilities and contributions.
* **Inclusion in Practice** (`8`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose all the reasons why you feel included in the work environment, based on the following options:

* Please answer honestly and accurately to provide meaningful feedback.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully, and if you need help, contact the HR department.
