# Staff Examination Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, "Staff Examination Survey," is used to gather feedback and opinions from employees to help management assess their performance, strengths, and potential areas for improvement. It is an internal assessment tool used to help employees understand their standing within the team and organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Survey Form section with any relevant feedback you have about the staff member in question.
2. Select the correct job title and performance level to accurately reflect the employee's status.
3. List the employee's strengths and areas for improvement in the Strengths and Improvement Areas sections.
4. Provide any promotion recommendations, specifying whether you suggest promoting the employee to a manager, senior, or other position.
5. Evaluate the employee's performance using the dropdown menu in the Evaluation section.
6. If applicable, select the date and time the survey was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Survey Form** (`survey_form`, text, optional)
This section is for providing any relevant feedback or comments about the staff member.

### 2. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional)
Select the job title that best matches the staff member's current position.

### 3. **Performance** (`performance`, number, optional)
Enter a numerical value to represent the staff member's performance level.

### 4. **Promotion Recommendations** (`promotion_recommendations`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose all applicable promotion options for the staff member:
	* Promote to Manager
	* Promote to Senior
	* Promote to Other (please specify)

### 5. **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, optional)
List the employee's notable strengths or positive attributes.

### 6. **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional)
Identify any areas where the employee could improve or develop further.

### 7. **Responsibility Recommendations** (`responsibility_recommendations`, text, optional)
If you have any specific suggestions for the staff member to take on more responsibilities, list them here.

### 8. **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, select_one, optional)
Select the performance level of the staff member:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor

### 9. **Date** (`date`, date, optional)
If necessary, enter the date the survey was completed.

### 10. **Time** (`time`, time, optional)
If necessary, enter the time the survey was completed.
