# Menopause Workplace Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menopause Workplace Audit Form is designed to gather information from employees about their experiences and perceptions of the workplace culture, policies, and practices regarding menopause.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions to the best of your ability, providing honest and thoughtful responses. Please fill out each section carefully, as it will help us better understand your experiences and provide valuable insights for improving our workplace culture and support for employees going through menopause.
