# Organizational Culture Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from employees on various aspects of the workplace culture, including communication, collaboration, job satisfaction, and overall job security. The information gathered will help us understand the strengths and weaknesses of our current culture and inform decision-making to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into two sections: Introduction and Questions.
2. Please read each question carefully and answer based on your honest opinions.
3. Select the options that best fit your answers or type in your answer in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: This is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **How do you communicate with your team and manager? (2)**: Please select how often you communicate with your team and manager, or type in a brief answer if you prefer text.
* **What do you value most in our culture? (3)**: Choose one of the options that best describes what you value most in our culture.
* **What do you like most about our workplace? (4)**: Rate your satisfaction with our workplace on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Do you get regular feedback from your manager and colleagues? (5)**: Select all that apply if you receive feedback from both your manager and colleagues.
* **Face-to-face meetings with your manager (6)**: Please select how often you have face-to-face meetings with your manager.
* **Team meetings (7)**: Select how often you attend team meetings.
* **How open-minded are your colleagues? (8)**: Rate your colleagues' open-mindedness on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **How often do you collaborate with colleagues? (9)**: Select how often you collaborate with colleagues.
* **How much do you trust your colleagues? (10)**: Rate your trust in your colleagues on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Do you get regular feedback from your colleagues? (11)**: Select all that apply if you receive feedback from your colleagues.
* **How much value do you place on our organizational culture? (12)**: Rate your value for our culture on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **How secure do you feel in your job? (13)**: Select how often you feel secure in your job.
* **Satisfaction with work-life balance (14)**: Rate your satisfaction with work-life balance on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Job satisfaction (15)**: Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Culture Value 2 (16)**: Select one of the options that best describes your answer.
* **Introduction 2 (17)**: This is a brief introduction to the next section of the form.
* **Conclusion (18)**: This is a final note section where you can provide any additional comments or insights.

Note: Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, and provide any additional comments or insights in the conclusion section.
