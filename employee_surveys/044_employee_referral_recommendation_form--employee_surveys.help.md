# employee_referral_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect employee referrals for potential job openings within the company. It helps in identifying and tracking potential candidates for job openings, ensuring that referrals are submitted and managed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that all fields are correctly formatted (e.g., email addresses should be in the correct format).
3. Click submit once you have filled in all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (text)**: Enter the name of the company that the job opening is located at.
* **Job Title (text)**: Enter the job title that is currently open or vacant.
* **Employee Name (text)**: Enter the name of the referring employee.
* **Referral Name (text)**: Enter the name of the person being referred for the job.
* **Contact Email (text)**: Enter the email address of the referrer for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter the phone number of the referrer for contact purposes.
* **Department (text)**: Enter the department within the company where the job opening is located.
* **Job Description (text)**: Enter a brief description of the job duties and requirements.
* **Job Requirements (text)**: List the key skills or qualifications required for the job.
* **Recommended By (text)**: Enter the name of the person who recommends the candidate.
* **Recommended Date (date)**: Enter the date when the referral was recommended.
* **Recommended Time (time)**: Enter the time of day when the referral was recommended. 

Please note that some fields may not be required to fill in, but it is recommended to provide as much information as possible to aid in the referral process.
