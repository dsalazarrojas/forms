# Team Productivity Barriers Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Team Productivity Barriers Survey is a tool designed to help identify and address obstacles that hinder team productivity. By completing this survey, you will contribute to a better understanding of the challenges faced by the team and help improve work processes and workflows.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introductory note on the first page to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
2. Select your department from the options provided in the second page.
3. Identify your manager(s) in charge of your work in the third page (you may select multiple managers).
4. In the fourth page, list the obstacles that have hindered your productivity.
5. In the fifth page, provide actionable suggestions to improve productivity.
6. Enter your email address in the sixth page to ensure we can contact you about your survey responses.
7. In the seventh page, enter your work phone number (if applicable).
8. In the eighth page, select the date you completed this survey.
9. In the ninth page, select your available time in hours and minutes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro`, `note`, required: false): This is the introductory note that explains the purpose and context of the survey. Read it before proceeding.
* **department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your department from the options provided:
	+ Department
	+ Team
	+ Project
	+ Other
* **manager** (`manager`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Identify your manager(s) in charge of your work. You may select multiple managers:
	+ Manager
	+ Team Lead
	+ Department Lead
	+ Other
* **barriers** (`obstacle`, `text`, required: false): List the obstacles that have hindered your productivity. Try to be as specific as possible.
* **suggestion** (`suggestion`, `text`, required: false): Provide actionable suggestions to improve productivity. Try to be as concrete as possible.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your survey responses.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work phone number (if applicable). This will help us contact you about your survey responses.
* **date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you completed this survey.
* **time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select your available time in hours and minutes. 

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when listing obstacles and providing suggestions to ensure accurate and actionable insights.
* If you have multiple managers, separate them with commas (e.g., Manager, Team Lead, Department Lead).
* Ensure your email and phone number are accurate to receive updates about your survey responses.
