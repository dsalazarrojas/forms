# Manager Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Offboarding Form is a necessary step in the offboarding process for employees leaving the company. It helps managers to gather information and input required for a smooth transition of the employee's role and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed and understand the purpose and instructions of this form.
2. Fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
3. Select the correct options for each field as needed.
4. Review and submit the form to initiate the offboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the manager of the employee being offboarded.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, text, required): Enter the employee's company ID or number.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, select_one, required): Select your reporting manager from the options provided.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project the employee is currently working on.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Reporting Level** (`reporting_level`, select_one, optional): Select the reporting level of the employee.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the employee's team members will be affected by the offboarding process.
* **Date of Departure** (`date_of_departure`, date, required): Enter the date of the employee's last working day.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required): Enter the reason for the employee's departure.
* **Next Step** (`next_step`, text, required): Enter the next step or action to be taken after the employee's departure.
* **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date you approve the offboarding process.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes for the offboarding process.
* **Team Approval Date** (`team_approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of approval from the team.
* **Team Comments** (`team_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the team.
* **IT Support** (`it_support`, text, required): Enter information about IT support for the offboarding process.
* **IT Support Comments** (`it_support_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from IT support.
* **Finance** (`finance`, text, required): Enter information about finance-related tasks for the offboarding process.
* **Finance Comments** (`finance_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from finance.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, text, required): Enter information about benefits for the offboarding process.
* **Benefits Comments** (`benefits_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from benefits.
* **HR** (`hr`, text, required): Enter information about HR-related tasks for the offboarding process.
* **HR Comments** (`hr_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from HR.
* **Other** (`other`, text, optional): Enter any other information or comments not covered by the above fields.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other comments or notes not covered by the above fields.

## Tips
When filling out this form, make sure to provide accurate and thorough information for each field. If you are unsure about any information, please contact the HR or IT department for assistance.
