# Employee Attitude Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Attitude Survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from employees about their work experience, work environment, and overall job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by answering "How was your day today?" with a brief description of your workday.
2.  For each rating question (Manager, Team, Work Environment, and Work-Life Balance), select the appropriate score from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
3.  In the "Feedback and suggestions" section, provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
4.  In the "Contact Information" section, enter your email address and phone number (optional).
5.  In the "Comments" section, provide any additional comments or feedback.
