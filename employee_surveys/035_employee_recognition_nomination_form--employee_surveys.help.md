# Employee Recognition Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Nomination Form is designed to help colleagues nominate their peers for outstanding performance, teamwork, leadership, or other notable achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name you would like to nominate in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Provide the employee's email address in the "Employee Email" field.
3. Select the reason for the nomination in the "Award Nomination" dropdown menu.
4. Write a brief message explaining the employee's outstanding performance or achievement in the "Nomination Message" field.
5. Enter your name and email address in the "Nominator Name" and "Nominator Email" fields respectively.
6. Optionally, provide your phone number in the "Nominator Phone" field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee you would like to nominate.
### Nomination Reason
* **Award Nomination**: Select the reason for the nomination from the dropdown menu:
	+ Nomination for Outstanding Employee of the Month
	+ Nomination for Outstanding Team Member of the Month
	+ Nomination for Outstanding Leadership Award
	+ Nomination for Outstanding Service Award
	+ Nomination for Outstanding Innovation Award
	+ Nomination for Outstanding Customer Service Award
	+ Nomination for Outstanding Employee of the Year
### Nomination Message
* **Nomination Message**: Write a brief message explaining the employee's outstanding performance or achievement.
### Nominator Information
* **Nominator Name**: Enter your name so we know who is nominating the employee.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Nominator Phone**: (Optional) Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send the nomination.

## Tips
Please make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form. If you are unsure about the nomination reason, refer to the award description for guidance.
