# In Person Meetings Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Person Meetings Survey form is designed to collect information about in-person meetings, such as dates, times, and feedback. This form is intended for employees to report on meetings they have attended or plan to attend. The information collected will help the organization track and analyze meeting activity to improve communication and collaboration among employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required information. Fields marked as "required" in the form are necessary to complete the survey.
2. Fill in all optional information that is relevant to the meeting.
3. Enter the correct date for the meeting using the date field. If you're unsure of the format, use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `2022-06-01`).
4. Select the correct time for the meeting.
5. Leave feedback about the meeting in the "Comments" field.
6. Enter your email address if you have any comments or questions.
7. Enter your phone number if you have any comments or questions.
8. Enter the email address of anyone you would like to receive comments or questions about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **In Person Meetings** (`in_person_meetings`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the meeting, such as the meeting topic, location, or purpose.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments or feedback about the meeting.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting using the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of the meeting.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share any feedback or comments about the meeting.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that others can contact you about the meeting.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that others can contact you about the meeting.
* **In Person Meetings Comments Email** (`comments_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of anyone you would like to receive comments or questions about the meeting.
