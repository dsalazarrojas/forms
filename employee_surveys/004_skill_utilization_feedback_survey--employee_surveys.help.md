# Skill Utilization Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on the skills and abilities of individual employees and track their utilization across the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your name and date for submission.
2. Choose the skills you have utilized in your last project or task.
3. Select the level of skill you have used in your last project or task.
4. Choose how often you used the skills in your last project or task.
5. Select how often you asked or were asked to use the skills in your last project or task.
6. Select how often you developed or improved the skills in your last project or task.
7. Select how often you shared your skills with others in your last project or task.
8. Enter any additional comments about your skill utilization.
9. Click "Submit" to save your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Survey** (Employee Survey, text, optional): This is a text field for entering your name.
* **Skills Used** (Skills Used, select one, optional): Choose the skills you utilized in your last project or task. This will help us understand which skills are being used across the company.
* **Skill Level** (Skill Level, select one, optional): Select the level of skill you used in your last project or task. This will help us understand the complexity of the skills utilized.
* **How Often Used** (How Often Used, select multiple, optional): This is a select multiple question where you can choose how often you used the skills in your last project or task. For example, "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly".
* **How Often Asked** (How Often Asked, select one, optional): This is a question about how often you were asked to use the skills in your last project or task.
* **How Often Developed** (How Often Developed, select one, optional): This is a question about how often you developed or improved the skills in your last project or task.
* **How Often Shared** (How Often Shared, select one, optional): This is a question about how often you shared your skills with others in your last project or task.
* **Skill Utilization** (Skill Utilization, number, optional): This is a numerical field for entering a number, but it seems to be an optional field, so it's likely not required for submission.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, optional): This is a free text field for any additional comments you want to provide about your skill utilization.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, text, optional): This is a text field for entering your name.
* **Submitted On** (Submitted On, date, optional): This is a date field for submitting the form.
