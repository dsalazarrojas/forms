# Manager Accessibility Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to assess how employees perceive their manager's approachability, responsiveness, communication, and overall accessibility. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand areas for improvement and provide better support for our team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus for each question.
2. If a question allows multiple responses, select all the options that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, type your response in the text field.
4. Make sure to review all the questions carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback (page1)**:
	+ This question allows you to provide feedback on your manager's approachability from the perspective of "High", "Low", and "Medium" options.
* **Manager Approachability (question1)**:
	+ This question is about how approachable you feel your manager is, with options ranging from "Very approachable" to "Not approachable at all".
* **Responsiveness (question2)**:
	+ Choose how responsive you feel your manager is, with options being "Responsive", "Not very responsive", or "Neither Responsive nor Not responsive".
* **Communication (question3)**:
	+ Select how clear you feel the communication from your manager is, with options being "Clear", "Somewhat clear", "Somewhat unclear", or "Completely unclear".
* **Approachability (question4)**:
	+ This question is similar to question1, asking about how approachable you feel your manager is, with the same options.
* **Open Door (question5)**:
	+ Choose how often you feel your manager is open to feedback and questions, with options being "Always open", "Sometimes open", or "Rarely open".
* **Meeting Time (question6)**:
	+ This question is for selecting a date, ideally the first available date for a meeting with your manager.
* **Manager Time (question7)**:
	+ This question is for selecting a time, ideally the first available time for a meeting with your manager.
* **Email Response (question8)**:
	+ This is an open-ended question for providing an email address for your manager's response.
* **Manager Phone (question9)**:
	+ This is an open-ended question for providing a phone number for your manager's response.
* **Manager Availability (question10)**:
	+ Choose how available you feel your manager is to work, with options being "Available to work", "Somewhat available to work", or "Not available to work".
