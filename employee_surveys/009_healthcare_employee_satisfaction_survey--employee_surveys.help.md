# Healthcare Employee Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from healthcare employees on their job satisfaction and other aspects of their work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each page carefully.
2. Answer the questions as honestly as possible.
3. For select_multiple and select_one questions, choose the options that best fit your experience.
4. For text fields, provide a brief explanation.
5. If you have any further comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "What recommendations do you have for our hospital?" field.
