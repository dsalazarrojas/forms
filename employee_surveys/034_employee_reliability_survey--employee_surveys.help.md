# Employee Reliability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reliability Survey is a tool used to gather feedback from employees on the reliability of their colleagues. This information will help us better understand individual and group dynamics, enabling us to make informed decisions about team building and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply select the corresponding options for each of the following questions:

- **High, Medium, or Low** (`Employee Survey`, select_multiple): Rate your overall reliability of your colleagues.
- **Good, Fair, or Poor** (`reliability_character`, select_one): Rate your trust in your colleagues.
- **Consignment** (`merchandise_handling`, select_one): Indicate whether or not your colleagues handle merchandise.
- **Handle** (`equipment_handling`, select_one): Indicate whether or not your colleagues handle equipment.
- **Handle** (`money_handling`, select_one): Indicate whether or not your colleagues handle money.
- **Yes** or **No** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Indicate whether or not your colleagues are assigned to handle a specific tool.
- **Yes** or **No** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Indicate whether or not you are assigned to handle a specific tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Survey** (`High, Medium, Low`): This question asks for your overall assessment of the reliability of your colleagues. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
  - Selecting "High" means you generally trust and rely on your colleagues.
  - Selecting "Medium" means you trust some colleagues but not all.
  - Selecting "Low" means you don't trust your colleagues and may experience issues with reliability.
- **Character** (`Good, Fair, Poor`): This question measures your trust in your colleagues. Choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
  - Selecting "Good" means you trust your colleagues entirely.
  - Selecting "Fair" means you're neutral and don't have strong feelings about it.
  - Selecting "Poor" means you distrust your colleagues and may experience issues with reliability.
- **Trustworthiness** (`Trustworthy, Somewhat Trustworthy, Not very trustworthy`): This question also measures your trust in your colleagues. Choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
  - Selecting "Trustworthy" means you trust your colleagues entirely.
  - Selecting "Somewhat Trustworthy" means you have some degree of trust.
  - Selecting "Not very trustworthy" means you don't trust your colleagues.
- **Consignment** (`Consignment, No Consignment`): This question asks whether or not your colleagues handle merchandise.
  - Selecting "Consignment" means your colleagues are responsible for handling merchandise.
  - Selecting "No Consignment" means your colleagues are not responsible for handling merchandise.
- **Handle Equipment** (`Handle, Not Handle`): This question asks whether or not your colleagues handle equipment.
  - Selecting "Handle" means your colleagues are responsible for handling equipment.
  - Selecting "Not Handle" means your colleagues are not responsible for handling equipment.
- **Handle Money** (`Handle, Not Handle`): This question asks whether or not your colleagues handle money.
  - Selecting "Handle" means your colleagues are responsible for handling money.
  - Selecting "Not Handle" means your colleagues are not responsible for handling money.
- **Tool** (`chatjimmy, Other`): This question asks whether or not you're assigned to handle a specific tool.
  - Selecting "chatjimmy" means you're assigned to handle this specific tool.
  - Selecting "Other" means you're not assigned to handle this specific tool.
- **Other Tool** (`Yes, No`): This question asks whether or not you're assigned to handle another tool.
  - Selecting "Yes" means you're assigned to handle this specific tool.
  - Selecting "No" means you're not assigned to handle this specific tool.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately based on your experience.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
- If you need to provide more information or clarification, please use the "Other" option.
