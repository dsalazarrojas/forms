# Workforce Capability Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the performance and capability of employees within your organization. It's used to gauge the current state of your workforce's skills and knowledge and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill out each field with the requested information. Each page is designed to ask a specific set of questions, which will help us better understand your workforce's capabilities. You can submit your ratings and comments for each department, and we'll use this data to create a comprehensive report on your organization's workforce capabilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide a brief overview of the form's purpose and any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Manager Rating**: Rate the overall performance of your team or department on a scale of Good or Fair. This rating is intended to help us understand your manager's perspective on the team's capabilities.
* **Manager Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your team or department's performance.
* **Employee Rating**: Rate the overall performance of your team or department on a scale of Good or Fair, from your perspective as an employee.
* **Department 1 Rating**: Rate the performance of your team or department in your primary area of responsibility on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Department 1 Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your team or department's performance in your primary area of responsibility.
* **Department 2 Rating**: Rate the performance of your team or department in your secondary area of responsibility on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Department 2 Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your team or department's performance in your secondary area of responsibility.
* **Department 3 Rating**: Rate the performance of your team or department in your third area of responsibility on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Department 3 Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your team or department's performance in your third area of responsibility.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form, as your input will help us create a comprehensive report on your organization's workforce capabilities.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the questions, feel free to reach out to us.
* You can submit this form as many times as you need, but please note that each submission will be recorded and used in the report.
* Make sure to fill out each page completely and thoroughly, as this will help us get a full picture of your department's capabilities.
