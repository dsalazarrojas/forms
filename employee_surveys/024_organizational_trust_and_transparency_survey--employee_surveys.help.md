# Organizational Trust And Transparency Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Organizational Trust And Transparency Survey" is a self-administered survey designed to help the organization understand the level of trust and confidence its employees have in it. The survey collects data on various aspects of trust, including employee openness, decision-making, and overall feelings towards the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please read each question carefully and answer them honestly to the best of your ability.
2.  You can select more than one option for multiple-choice questions.
3.  For questions with text boxes, provide as much detail as possible.
4.  Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
5.  Once you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.
