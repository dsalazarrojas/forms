# Weekly Team Reflection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a platform for team members to reflect on their experiences, challenges, and feedback on a weekly basis. It helps the manager to track team performance and provide guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Team Name** of the team you are part of.
2. Select the **Week Start Date** and **Week End Date** of the period you are reflecting on.
3. In the **Experiences** field, type a brief summary of your positive experiences during the week.
4. In the **Challenges** field, type a brief summary of any challenges you faced during the week.
5. In the **Suggestions** field, type any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
6. For **Team Members**, select the team members who were involved in your work during the week.
7. If there are any other team members who were not listed, select **Other Team Members**.
8. Indicate if your **Manager's Name** was involved in your work during the week.
9. If your **Manager's Name** was not involved, enter their **Manager's Phone** number so they can be reached.
10. In the **Feedback** field, provide any additional feedback or comments you have.
11. In the **Comments** field, provide any comments from your manager or team members.
12. Finally, in the **Manager Comments** field, enter any comments or feedback from your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team, e.g., "Marketing Team" or "Sales Team".
* **Week Start Date**: Select the date when your week started. This could be Monday or the first day of your workweek.
* **Week End Date**: Select the date when your week ended. This could be Friday or the last day of your workweek.
* **Experiences**: Describe your positive experiences during the week, e.g., "I completed a challenging project," or "I learned a new skill."
* **Challenges**: Describe any challenges you faced during the week, e.g., "I struggled with a difficult client," or "I had to deal with a tight deadline."
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement, e.g., "We could have more training on new software," or "We need more resources."
* **Team Members**: Select the team members who were involved in your work during the week. This helps your manager track collaboration and communication within the team.
* **Other Team Members**: If there are any team members not listed, select this option to indicate their involvement.
* **Manager's Name**: Indicate if your manager was involved in your work during the week.
* **Manager's Phone**: If your manager was not involved, enter their phone number so they can be reached.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments you have.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments from your manager or team members.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from your manager.
