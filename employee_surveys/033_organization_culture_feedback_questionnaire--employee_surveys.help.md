# organization_culture_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on the overall culture and values of their organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and respond based on your personal experience and perception.
2. For numeric questions, rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
3. Provide honest and constructive feedback to help shape the company's culture and values.
4. Submit your responses when you are ready.
