# Change Coordinator Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experiences working with change coordinators in your organization. The purpose of this form is to help identify areas of improvement and recognize strengths in change coordinator performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date you completed this feedback form.
2. Enter your full name as an employee.
3. Specify the department you belong to.
4. Enter the name of the change coordinator you are evaluating.
5. Choose the frequency of your interaction with the coordinator.
6. Evaluate the communication effectiveness of the coordinator.
7. Assess the clarity of change information provided.
8. Rate the coordinator's responsiveness to questions and concerns.
9. Rate the coordinator's ability to resolve problems.
10. Rate the coordinator's organizational and planning skills.
11. Evaluate the coordinator's ability to build relationships and establish trust.
12. Rate the coordinator's technical knowledge and expertise.
13. (Optional) Enter specific strengths and weaknesses about the coordinator's performance.
14. (Optional) Enter specific examples or incidents where the coordinator excelled or struggled.
15. (Optional) Enter areas for improvement for the coordinator.
16. Rate whether you would work with this coordinator again on future change initiatives.
17. Rate the overall performance of the change coordinator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): 
    This is the date you are providing feedback.
* **Your Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): 
    Please enter your full name as an employee for identification purposes.
* **Your Department** (`employee_department`, text, optional): 
    This field is optional but helps us understand your role within the organization.
* **Change Coordinator Name** (`coordinator_name`, text, required): 
    Please enter the name of the change coordinator you are evaluating.
* **Change Initiative Name** (`change_initiative`, text, required): 
    This field is for the specific change initiative you are providing feedback on.
* **Frequency of Interaction** (`interaction_frequency`, select_one, optional): 
    This question helps us understand how frequently you interacted with the coordinator. Possible answers are Daily, Several Times Per Week, Weekly, Few Times During Initiative, and One-time.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): 
    Rate how clear and timely the coordinator's communication was. Possible answers are Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, and Poor.
* **Clarity of Change Information Provided** (`clarity_of_information`, select_one, required): 
    Evaluate how understandable the change information was. Possible answers are Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear, and Very Unclear.
* **Responsiveness to Questions and Concerns** (`responsiveness`, select_one, required): 
    Rate how responsive the coordinator was to questions and concerns. Possible answers are Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, and Poor.
* **Problem Resolution and Support** (`problem_resolution`, select_one, required): 
    Evaluate how well the coordinator handled problems. Possible answers are Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, and Poor.
* **Organization and Planning Skills** (`organization_and_planning`, select_one, optional): 
    Rate the coordinator's organizational and planning skills. Possible answers are Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, and Poor.
* **Relationship Building and Trust** (`relationship_building`, select_one, required): 
    Evaluate how well the coordinator built relationships and established trust. Possible answers are Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, and Poor.
* **Technical Knowledge and Expertise** (`technical_knowledge`, select_one, optional): 
    Rate the coordinator's technical knowledge and expertise. Possible answers are Highly Knowledgeable, Knowledgeable, Somewhat Knowledgeable, Limited Knowledge, and Not Knowledgeable.
* **Morale Impact** (`morale_impact`, select_one, optional): 
    This question helps us understand how the coordinator's performance affected team morale. Possible answers are Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, and Very Negative.
* **Support for Change Adoption** (`adoption_support`, select_one, required): 
    Rate the coordinator's support for change adoption. Possible answers are Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, and Poor.
* **Coordinator's Key Strengths** (`strengths_feedback`, text, optional): 
    Optional: if you want to highlight the coordinator's positive aspects.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): 
    Optional: if you want to provide feedback on areas the coordinator could improve.
* **Specific Examples or Incidents** (`specific_examples`, text, optional): 
    Optional: if you want to provide specific examples or incidents where the coordinator excelled or struggled.
* **Would You Work With This Coordinator Again** (`would_work_again`, select_one, optional): 
    This question helps us understand if you would work with this coordinator again on future change initiatives. Possible answers are Yes, Definitely, Probably Yes, Unsure, Probably No, and No.
* **Overall Rating of Change Coordinator** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): 
    Rate the coordinator's overall performance. Possible answers are Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, and Poor.
