# Organizational Fairness Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure employee perception of fairness within the organization. The results will help management understand what employees value most in terms of work-life balance, compensation, and opportunities for growth and advancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the following questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fairness Scale** (`fairness_scale`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall perception of fairness within the organization, with a numeric value ranging from 1 to 10.
* **Leadership** (`leadership`, text, optional): Describe your perception of the leadership within the organization. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Diversity and Inclusion** (`diversity-and-inclusion`, select, optional): Choose one of the two options:
  * Diversity and Inclusion
  * Other (please specify)
* **Internal Communication** (`communication`, select multiple, optional): Choose all that apply:
  * Internal communication
  * External communication
  * Communication with leadership
* **Work-Life** (`work-life`, select, optional): Choose one of the three options:
  * Work-life balance
  * Family life
  * Wellness
* **Fair Compensation** (`fair-compensation`, select multiple, optional): Choose all that apply:
  * Salary and wages
  * Bennies
  * Stock options
* **Promotion Opportunities** (`promotion-opportunities`, select, optional): Choose one of the three options:
  * Opportunities for advancement
  * Opportunities for growth
  * Opportunities for development

## Tips
* Please answer these questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any suggestions or concerns that are not listed here, feel free to mention them in the "Leadership" field.
* If you are unsure of any of the options, choose "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
