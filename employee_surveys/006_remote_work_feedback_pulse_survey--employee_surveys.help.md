# Remote Work Feedback Pulse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Work Feedback Pulse Survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from employees on their experiences with remote work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Rate your satisfaction and productivity scores on a scale of 1-5.
3. List any challenges you faced during remote work.
4. Provide any comments or feedback on your remote work experience.
5. Select the category that best describes your experience (e.g. Remote Work, Flexible Work Arrangements, Work-Life Balance, etc.).
6. Choose the duration of your remote work experience.
7. Choose the duration of your remote work setup.
8. Provide the date of your remote work experience.
9. Select any other options that describe your experience (e.g. Yes, No, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (employee_name): Please provide your name.
* **Manager's Name** (manager_name): Please provide your manager's name.
* **Manager's Email** (manager_email): Please provide your manager's email address.
* **Manager's Phone** (manager_phone): Please provide your manager's phone number.
* **Satisfaction Score** (satisfaction_score): Please rate your satisfaction with remote work on a scale of 1-5.
* **Productivity Score** (productivity_score): Please rate your productivity with remote work on a scale of 1-5.
* **Challenges Faced** (challenges): Please list any challenges you faced during remote work.
* **Comments and Feedback** (comments): Please provide any comments or feedback on your remote work experience.
* **Category** (category): Please select the category that best describes your experience (e.g. Remote Work, Flexible Work Arrangements, Work-Life Balance, etc.).
* **How Long Have You Worked Remotely?** (how_long_have): Please select the duration of your remote work experience.
* **Duration of Remote Setup** (remote_duration): Please select the duration of your remote work setup.
* **Other Category** (category_other): Please provide any other category that best describes your experience.
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple): Please select multiple options that best describe your experience.
* **Date of Experience** (date_experience): Please provide the date of your remote work experience.
* **End Screen** (end_screen): Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback.
