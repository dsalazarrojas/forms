# Compensation Fairness Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback on how employees perceive fairness in their compensation. The survey aims to understand how employees feel about their pay, the communication process, and the overall fairness of the compensation system. This will help leadership make informed decisions to improve the compensation structure and ensure a fair and motivating work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Assess your pay fairness compared to your colleagues in similar roles (internal equity perception).
2.  Evaluate your pay fairness compared to people outside the company (external equity perception).
3.  Indicate how clear you understand the criteria used to determine your pay (pay criteria clarity).
4.  Rate the fairness of the annual compensation review process (review process fairness).
5.  Express your level of trust in leadership when it comes to fair decisions (trust in leadership comp).
6.  Select your level of satisfaction with the communication of compensation changes (communication satisfaction).
7.  State how your current compensation level motivates you to perform at your best (motivation impact pay).
8.  Choose how often you have discussions with your manager about compensation (frequency of pay talks).
9.  Optionally provide one thing your organization could do to improve pay fairness (fairness improvement suggestion).
10.  Select today's date for completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Internal Equity Perception**: How fairly do you think your pay is compared to your colleagues in similar roles? Please select one of the following options:
    *   Strongly Agree: You strongly believe your pay is fair compared to your colleagues.
    *   Agree: You slightly believe your pay is fair compared to your colleagues.
    *   Neutral: You are unsure or have no strong opinion about your pay fairness compared to your colleagues.
    *   Disagree: You slightly believe your pay is unfair compared to your colleagues.
    *   Strongly Disagree: You strongly believe your pay is unfair compared to your colleagues.
*   **External Equity Perception**: How fairly do you think your pay is compared to people in similar roles outside the company? Please select one of the following options:
    *   Strongly Agree: You strongly believe your pay is fair compared to people in similar roles outside the company.
    *   Agree: You slightly believe your pay is fair compared to people in similar roles outside the company.
    *   Neutral: You are unsure or have no strong opinion about your pay fairness compared to people in similar roles outside the company.
    *   Disagree: You slightly believe your pay is unfair compared to people in similar roles outside the company.
    *   Strongly Disagree: You strongly believe your pay is unfair compared to people in similar roles outside the company.
*   **Pay Criteria Clarity**: How clear do you understand the criteria used to determine your pay? Please select one of the following options:
    *   Very Clear: You completely understand the pay criteria.
    *   Somewhat Clear: You somewhat understand the pay criteria.
    *   Vague: You are somewhat unsure about the pay criteria.
    *   Very Vague: You don't understand the pay criteria.
*   **Review Process Fairness**: How fair do you find the annual compensation review process? Please select one of the following options:
    *   Very Fair: You think the review process is completely fair.
    *   Fair: You think the review process is slightly fair.
    *   Neutral: You are unsure or have no strong opinion about the review process fairness.
    *   Unfair: You think the review process is unfair.
    *   Very Unfair: You strongly believe the review process is unfair.
*   **Trust in Leadership**: How much do you trust that leadership makes fair decisions regarding compensation? Please select one of the following options:
    *   Complete Trust: You completely trust leadership.
    *   Some Trust: You somewhat trust leadership.
    *   Neutral: You are unsure or have no strong opinion about leadership trust.
    *   Little Trust: You somewhat distrust leadership.
    *   No Trust: You don't trust leadership at all.
*   **Communication Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with the way compensation changes are communicated? Please select one of the following options:
    *   Very Satisfied: You are completely satisfied with compensation communication.
    *   Satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with compensation communication.
    *   Neutral: You are unsure or have no strong opinion about compensation communication.
    *   Dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with compensation communication.
    *   Very Dissatisfied: You are completely dissatisfied with compensation communication.
*   **Motivation Impact Pay**: How much does your current compensation level motivate you to perform at your best? Please select one of the following options:
    *   Strongly Agree: Your compensation level strongly motivates you to perform at your best.
    *   Agree: Your compensation level somewhat motivates you to perform at your best.
    *   Neutral: You are unsure or have no strong opinion about the impact of your compensation on motivation.
    *   Disagree: Your compensation level somewhat demotivates you from performing at your best.
    *   Strongly Disagree: Your compensation level strongly demotivates you from performing at your best.
*   **Frequency of Pay Talks**: How often do you discuss your compensation with your manager? Please select one of the following options:
    *   At every review (Annually): You discuss your compensation at every review.
    *   Twice a year: You discuss your compensation twice a year.
    *   Quarterly: You discuss your compensation quarterly.
    *   Rarely or Never: You rarely or never discuss your compensation with your manager.
*   **Fairness Improvement Suggestion**: Please provide one thing your organization could do to improve the fairness of pay. This is an optional field for open feedback.
*   **Survey Completion Date**: Select today's date for completion. This is for administrative purposes only.
