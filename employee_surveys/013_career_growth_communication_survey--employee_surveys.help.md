# Career Growth Communication Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experiences and perceptions regarding career development and communication within the company. Your input will help us understand what's working and what areas need improvement. Please be honest and provide your thoughts, and remember that all responses are anonymous.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name as we have for you in our system.
2. If applicable, enter your employee ID as we have for you in our system.
3. Provide your current job title and department.
4. Enter the number of years you've been with the company.
5. Answer the questions regarding your career path clarity, communication frequency, and manager effectiveness.
6. Select your preferred communication method for career discussions.
7. Choose the barriers that prevent career development communication.
8. Provide suggestions for improving career growth communication.
9. Rate your manager's effectiveness and how motivating career development conversations are to you.
10. Enter any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as we have for you in our system.
* **Employee ID**: If you have an employee ID, enter it as we have for you in our system. If not, just leave this field blank.
* **Department**: Type your current department or team.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Years with Company**: Enter the number of years you've been with the company.
* **Manager's Name**: Enter your manager's name, if you want to provide feedback about your manager.
* **Career Path Clarity**: Select how clear your career path is to you:
	+ Very Clear - I know my progression
	+ Somewhat Clear - I have an idea
	+ Unclear - I'm not sure
	+ Not Clear - I don't know what's available
* **How Often Does Management Discuss Career Development With You?**: Select how often you receive career development discussions:
	+ Regularly - at least monthly
	+ Occasionally - a few times a year
	+ Rarely - once or twice a year
	+ Never - no career discussions
* **Does Your Manager Discuss Career Growth Opportunities?**: Select how often your manager discusses career growth opportunities with you:
	+ Yes - frequently and proactively
	+ Somewhat - occasionally when asked
	+ Rarely - very limited discussion
	+ No - never discusses it
* **How Well Does Company Communicate Career Opportunities?**: Select how well the company communicates career opportunities:
	+ Excellent - very clear and accessible
	+ Good - adequate information available
	+ Fair - some information available
	+ Poor - limited communication
* **Have You Received Information About Career Development Programs?**: Select the level of information you've received about career development programs:
	+ Yes - comprehensive information
	+ Yes - some information
	+ No - very limited information
	+ No - not aware of any programs
* **Support for Career Planning**: Select how much support you received for career planning:
	+ Excellent support - have clear plan
	+ Good support - working on plan
	+ Minimal support - struggling to plan
	+ No support - no help available
* **Are You Aware of Promotion Criteria and Paths?**: Select how aware you are of promotion criteria and paths:
	+ Yes - very aware
	+ Somewhat - have general idea
	+ Vague - uncertain about process
	+ No - don't know how promotions work
* **Communication About Required Skills for Advancement**: Select how clear guidance you've received on required skills for advancement:
	+ Clear guidance - know exactly what's needed
	+ Somewhat clear - general guidance given
	+ Unclear - vague information
	+ No guidance - haven't discussed it
* **Available Training and Development Programs**: Select how aware you are of available training and development programs:
	+ Yes - very aware
	+ Somewhat - aware of some
	+ Limited - know of a few
	+ No - not aware of programs
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Select your preferred method for receiving career development communications:
	+ One-on-one meetings with manager
	+ Group meetings or workshops
	+ Email or written communication
	+ Company intranet or online portal
	+ Career fairs or networking events
	+ Mentoring relationships
* **Barriers to Career Development Communication**: Select all that apply:
	+ Lack of manager availability
	+ Unclear company policies
	+ Limited resources or programs
	+ Insufficient feedback on performance
	+ Unclear job expectations
	+ Lack of visibility to leadership
	+ Other barriers (please specify)
* **How Could Career Growth Communication Be Improved?**: Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Manager Effectiveness**: Select how effective your manager is at career development discussions:
	+ Very effective - helpful and supportive
	+ Effective - generally helpful
	+ Somewhat effective - could improve
	+ Not very effective - needs improvement
	+ Not at all effective - unhelpful
* **Do Career Development Communications Motivate You?**: Select the impact of career development communications on your engagement:
	+ Very motivating - energizes me
	+ Motivating - encourages growth
	+ Neutral - no real impact
	+ Discouraging - makes me less motivated
	+ Very discouraging - negative impact
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional feedback or comments about career communication.
