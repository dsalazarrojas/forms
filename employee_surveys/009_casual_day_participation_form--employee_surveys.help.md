# Casual Day Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casual Day Participation Form is used to collect information from employees about their participation in Casual Days, which are optional paid days off from work. This form is required to be completed by employees who wish to participate in a Casual Day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name in the "Participant name" field. This is your full name for records.
2. If you are participating in a team or department, fill in the "Department or team" field with your relevant team name.
3. Enter the date of the event in the "Date of event" field. This is the date on which the Casual Day will take place.
4. Confirm that you have read and understood the guidelines and theme of the event by selecting "True" in the "Understanding of theme and guidelines" field.
5. If you have any dietary or allergy notes for the event, fill in the "Dietary or allergy notes" field. This is optional.
6. If you wish to make contributions or donations for the event, type them in the "Contributions or donations" field. This is optional.
7. If you want to be contacted on the day of the event, enter your phone number in the "Contact phone" field. This is optional.
8. If you consent to be photographed during the event for internal use, select "True" in the "Consent to be photographed" field. This is optional.
9. If you need to provide an emergency contact name and phone number, fill in the "Emergency contact name and phone" field. This is optional.
10. If you have any special needs or accessibility requirements, describe them in the "Special needs or accessibility requirements" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (Participant name): This is your full name for records.
* **Department or team** (Department or team): If you are participating in a team or department, select your relevant team name.
* **Date of event** (Date of event): Enter the date of the event.
* **Understanding of theme and guidelines** (Understanding of theme and guidelines): Confirm that you have read and understood the guidelines and theme of the event.
* **Dietary or allergy notes** (Dietary or allergy notes): If you have any dietary or allergy notes, provide them here.
* **Contributions or donations** (Contributions or donations): If you wish to make contributions or donations, type them in here.
* **Contact phone** (Contact phone): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted on the day of the event.
* **Consent to be photographed** (Consent to be photographed): If you consent to be photographed for internal use, select "True".
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (Emergency contact name and phone): If you need to provide an emergency contact name and phone number, fill in here.
* **Special needs or accessibility requirements** (Special needs or accessibility requirements): If you have any special needs or accessibility requirements, describe them here.
