# Commission Structure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from employees on the current commission structure within the company. It aims to gather data on employee satisfaction, understanding, and perception of the commission structure.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out this form with as much detail as possible.
2.  Answer all questions honestly, and select the options that best reflect your thoughts.
3.  If you have additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Feedback" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Optional): Your full name, if you choose to provide it.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Text, Optional): Your employee identification number, if you have one.
* **Department** (`department`, Select One, Required): Choose the department you work in.
*   * Sales
*   * Business Development
*   * Account Management
*   * Real Estate
*   * Insurance
*   * Financial Services
*   * Other
* **Tenure** (`tenure`, Select One, Required): Select how long you've been with the company.
*   * Less than 6 months
*   * 6 months - 1 year
*   * 1-2 years
*   * 2-5 years
*   * 5+ years
* **Current Structure Understanding** (`current_structure_understanding`, Select One, Required): Rate how well you understand the current commission structure.
*   * Very Well
*   * Somewhat Well
*   * Neutral
*   * Not Very Well
*   * Not At All
* **Commission Satisfaction** (`commission_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): How satisfied are you with the current commission structure.
*   * Very Satisfied
*   * Satisfied
*   * Neutral
*   * Dissatisfied
*   * Very Dissatisfied
* **Fairness Perception** (`fairness_perception`, Select One, Required): Do you feel the commission structure is fair.
*   * Very Fair
*   * Somewhat Fair
*   * Neutral
*   * Somewhat Unfair
*   * Very Unfair
* **Motivation Level** (`motivation_level`, Select One, Required): How motivated does the commission structure make you feel.
*   * Very Motivated
*   * Motivated
*   * Neutral
*   * Demotivated
*   * Very Demotivated
* **Rate Aspects** (`rate_aspects`, Note, Optional): Please rate the aspects of the commission structure (not visible in the provided YAML).
* **Commission Rate Satisfaction** (`commission_rate_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Satisfaction with current commission rates.
*   * Very Satisfied
*   * Satisfied
*   * Neutral
*   * Dissatisfied
*   * Very Dissatisfied
* **Payment Frequency Satisfaction** (`payment_frequency_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Satisfaction with how often commissions are paid.
*   * Very Satisfied
*   * Satisfied
*   * Neutral
*   * Dissatisfied
*   * Very Dissatisfied
* **Bonus Structure Satisfaction** (`bonus_structure_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Satisfaction with bonus opportunities.
*   * Very Satisfied
*   * Satisfied
*   * Neutral
*   * Dissatisfied
*   * Very Dissatisfied
*   * Not Applicable
* **Transparency Satisfaction** (`transparency_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Satisfaction with commission calculation transparency.
*   * Very Satisfied
*   * Satisfied
*   * Neutral
*   * Dissatisfied
*   * Very Dissatisfied
* **Preferred Changes** (`preferred_changes`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the changes you would like to see in the commission structure.
*   * Higher Base Commission Rate
*   * More Frequent Payments
*   * Better Bonus Structure
*   * Clearer Calculation Methods
*   * Tiered Commission Levels
*   * Team-Based Incentives
*   * Performance Accelerators
*   * Guaranteed Minimum Commission
*   * No Changes Needed
*   * Other (not listed, provide additional comments in "Additional Feedback")
* **Commission Impact** (`commission_impact`, Text, Optional): Describe how the commission structure impacts your work.
* **Competitor Comparison** (`competitor_comparison`, Select One, Optional): Compare our commission structure with competitors.
*   * Much Better
*   * Somewhat Better
*   * About the Same
*   * Somewhat Worse
*   * Much Worse
*   * Do Not Know
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Willing to Discuss** (`willing_to_discuss`, Select One, Optional): Are you willing to discuss your feedback further.
*   * Yes
*   * No
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, Date, Required): Date you completed this survey.
