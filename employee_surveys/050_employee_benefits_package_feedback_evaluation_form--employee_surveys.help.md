# Employee Benefits Package Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience with the employee benefits package provided by the company. The goal is to understand overall satisfaction and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer the questions that apply to you.
2. Be honest and provide detailed feedback where possible.
3. Fill in the required fields only, as marked.
4. Once you have finished, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback** (employee_benefits_package_feedback, note, required: false): This field is a place for you to provide general feedback on your experience with the benefits package. You can write down any thoughts, observations, or concerns you have.

- **Overall Satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the benefits package. Choose one of the options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied.

- **Benefits Package** (benefits_package, text, required: false): This field is for you to describe the benefits package you are currently using or have experience with.

- **Suggestions for Improvement** (suggestions_for_improvement, text, required: false): In this field, you can suggest ideas on how to improve the benefits package. Be as specific as possible with your suggestions.

- **Feedback on Benefits** (feedback_on_benefits, text, required: false): This field is a place to provide general feedback on the benefits package. You can write about any aspect of the package that you would like to see changed or improved.

- **Employee Benefits Package Suggestions** (employee_benefits_package_suggestions, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide feedback on the benefits package. You can write down your thoughts, observations, or concerns you have.

- **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, required: false): In this field, you can provide any additional comments or suggestions that are not specifically mentioned in the previous fields.

- **Email** (email, email, required: false): This field is for you to provide your work email address, if you would like to be contacted for further feedback.

- **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): In this field, you can provide your work phone number, if you would like to be contacted for further feedback.
