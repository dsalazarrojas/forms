# Workplace Productivity Initiative Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Productivity Initiative Feedback Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from employees on the overall effectiveness and areas for improvement of the Workplace Productivity Initiative. This form is an opportunity for employees to share their thoughts and suggestions on how to enhance the initiative, ensuring it meets their needs and improves productivity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately and honestly.
2. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" and "Additional Comments" fields.
3. Rate the initiative's effectiveness and overall performance using the "Overall Rating" and "How would you rate the overall effectiveness of the Workplace Productivity Initiative" fields.
4. Select the areas that need improvement using the "Areas for Improvement" field.
5. Provide any additional feedback or recommendations in the "Recommendations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Productivity Initiative Feedback** (1) (`text`, required/optional): This is the main feedback field where you can provide your thoughts and comments on the Workplace Productivity Initiative.
* **What do you think are the top 3 areas that need improvement?** (2) (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select up to 3 areas that you think need improvement in the initiative.
* **What benefits do you think you get from the Workplace Productivity Initiative?** (3) (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose one benefit that you perceive from the initiative.
* **Benefits you get from the Workplace Productivity Initiative** (4) (`select_one`, required/optional): Another option to choose one benefit that you perceive from the initiative.
* **Comments** (5) (`note`, required/optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Overall Rating** (6) (`number`, required/optional): Rate the initiative's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the overall effectiveness of the Workplace Productivity Initiative** (7) (`number`, required/optional): Rate the initiative's effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Areas for Improvement** (8) (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select up to 3 areas that need improvement in the initiative.
* **Recommendations** (9) (`note`, required/optional): Use this field to provide any recommendations you may have for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (10) (`note`, required/optional): Use this field to provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.
