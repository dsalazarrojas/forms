# Employee Team Collaboration Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the employee team collaboration, communication, and overall satisfaction in a team setting. It will help team leaders and managers identify areas of improvement and gather insights for future team development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your team members who you wish to evaluate from the "Team Members" dropdown list.
2. Answer the questions about the collaboration level, communication, leadership, initiative, and overall satisfaction using the corresponding scale options.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" text field.
4. Fill in your submission date and time, and select your time zone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Members**: Select one or more team members you wish to evaluate. Use the dropdown list to choose the team members you wish to provide feedback on.
* **Collaboration Level** (`collaboration_level`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks how well the team collaborates with each other. Choose from the options: Not at all, A little, Somewhat, Very, Extremely.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about the level of communication within the team. Please provide your thoughts on how well the team communicates with each other.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about the level of teamwork within the team. Rate the team's ability to work together on a scale of 1-5.
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about the team's problem-solving skills. Rate the team's ability to solve problems on a scale of 1-5.
* **Leadership** (`leadership`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the leadership within the team. Choose from the options: Not at all, A little, Somewhat, Very, Extremely.
* **Initiative** (`initiative`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the level of initiative within the team. Choose from the options: Not at all, A little, Somewhat, Very, Extremely.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Please share any feedback you have for the team, either positive or constructive.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions for the team, please share them here.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the team on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments, please share them here.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about who assigned the task or project to the team. Choose from the options: Not at all, A little, Somewhat, Very, Extremely.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Please fill in the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Please fill in the time you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time Zone** (`submission_timezone`, `text`, required: false): Please select your time zone.
* **Submission IP** (`submission_ip`, `text`, required: false): This is a read-only field displaying the IP address of the submitter.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is not intended for external sharing.
