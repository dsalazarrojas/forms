# Employee Motivation & Reward Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees to improve employee motivation and reward them accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you provide your correct contact information.
3. Select your preferred rewards options.
4. Sign the consent form, acknowledging that you are participating in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey (Section 1)**: This section is to gather your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about your work experience and what motivates you. Please type your answers in the text box provided.
* **Contact Details (Section 2)**: This section is to collect your contact information, including your job title, department, manager's name, and email. Please ensure that your manager's email is accurate.
* **Preferences (Section 3)**: This section is a multiple-choice question where you can select one or more reward options that interest you. Please choose from the list provided.
* **Consent (Section 4)**: This is a note field where you can type your consent to participate in the program.
* **Employee Survey 2 (Section 5)**: This is a duplicate of Section 1, for your second set of thoughts, opinions, and suggestions. Please type your answers in the text box provided.
* **Contact Details 2 (Section 6)**: This is a duplicate of Section 2, where you can provide your contact information for your second set of answers. Please ensure that your manager's email is accurate.
* **Preferences 2 (Section 7)**: This is a duplicate of Section 3, where you can select one or more reward options that interest you for your second set of answers. Please choose from the list provided.
* **Consent 2 (Section 8)**: This is a duplicate of Section 4, where you can type your consent to participate in the program.
* **Submission (Section 9)**: This is the final section where you will provide your submitted information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email addresses are accurate and up-to-date.
* Choose your preferred rewards options carefully.
* Remember to sign the consent form for each set of answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administration team.
