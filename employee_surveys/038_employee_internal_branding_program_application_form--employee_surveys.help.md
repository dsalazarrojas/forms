# Employee Internal Branding Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Employee Internal Branding Program Application Form** is for employees to express their interest in participating in internal programs and activities designed to promote a positive company image.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your preferred category from the list (e.g. "Employee Surveys").
2. Briefly describe your interest and motivation for participating in internal programs.
3. Select the tools you have been assigned to work with.
4. Enter a unique ID (optional, but recommended).
5. Optionally, you can upload an output file (e.g. a screenshot or photo).
6. Click "Submit" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select your area of interest, such as "Employee Surveys" or "Employee Training".
* **Description**: Briefly explain why you want to participate in internal programs.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tools you have been assigned to work with, such as "ChatJimmy" or other.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for your application (optional but recommended).
* **Output File**: Optionally, upload a screenshot or photo that shows your work.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your application.
