# Payroll System Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll System Performance Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on the company's payroll system, its performance, functionality, user satisfaction, and efficiency. By completing this survey, you will help the company to better understand your needs and make informed decisions to optimize the payroll system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your role in the company from the options provided.
2. Choose how you rate the payroll process performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3. Rate the payroll system functionality (Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Indicate your level of satisfaction with the payroll system (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
5. Choose how you rate the payroll process efficiency (Very efficient, Efficient, Neutral, Somewhat inefficient, Not efficient).
6. Choose how you rate the payroll process accuracy (Very accurate, Accurate, Neutral, Somewhat inaccurate, Not accurate).
7. Choose how you rate the payroll process timeliness (Very timely, Timely, Neutral, Somewhat untimely, Not timely).
8. If you have any additional feedback, you can provide it in the text box.
9. If you are an employee, provide your name and email address. This information will help us to contact you for any clarification or follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your role in the company?** (`Employee`, `HR`, `Finance`, `Admin`): Select your position within the company.
* **How would you rate the payroll process performance?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Rate the performance of the payroll process based on your experience.
* **How do you rate the payroll system functionality?** (`Very good`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Rate the user experience and features of the payroll system.
* **How satisfied are you with the payroll system?** (`Very satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the payroll system.
* **How efficient is the payroll process?** (`Very efficient`, `Efficient`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat inefficient`, `Not efficient`): Rate the speed and ease of use of the payroll process.
* **How accurate is the payroll process?** (`Very accurate`, `Accurate`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat inaccurate`, `Not accurate`): Rate the accuracy of the payroll process results.
* **How timely is the payroll process?** (`Very timely`, `Timely`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat untimely`, `Not timely`): Rate the punctuality of the payroll process.
* **Additional feedback**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the payroll system.
* **Employee name** (`Employee name`): If you are an employee, please provide your name.
* **Employee email** (`Employee email address`): If you are an employee, please provide your email address.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can choose one option from the available choices.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your email address].
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey! Your feedback is invaluable to us.
