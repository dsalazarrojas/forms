# employee_retention_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees on their perceptions of their work environment, job satisfaction, and other relevant factors that affect job retention.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your honest answers. Please answer as concisely as possible, and try to be as accurate as you can. You can type in your answers in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Employee Retention** (`employee_retention`, text, required): A brief description of your overall feelings about your job and workplace.
  - **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, text, required): How satisfied are you with your current job and role?
  - **Workplace Culture** (`workplace_culture`, text, required): How would you describe the culture of your workplace?
  - **Engagement** (`engagement`, text, required): How engaged do you feel in your work and job?
  - **Communication** (`communication`, text, required): How would you rate the communication within your workplace?
  - **Job Security** (`job_security`, text, required): How secure do you feel about your job and future prospects?
  - **Growth Opportunities** (`growth_opportunities`, text, required): Are there opportunities for growth and development within your current role or company?
  - **Job Satisfaction 2** (`job_satisfaction_2`, text, required): A follow-up question to gauge your ongoing job satisfaction.
  - **Work Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, text, required): How balanced do you feel your work and personal life are?
  - **Job Security 2** (`job_security_2`, text, required): A follow-up question to gauge your ongoing job security.

## Tips

- Be as honest as possible when answering the questions.
- Try to be specific and provide concrete examples when answering the questions.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, feel free to share them in the comments section.
