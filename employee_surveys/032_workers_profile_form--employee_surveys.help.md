# workers_profile_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workers_profile_form is a collection of fields designed to gather detailed information about an employee's personal and professional profile. This form is intended to help you understand an employee's work history, job status, and other relevant details. Please ensure that you have a valid reason for filling out this form, such as a change in job status or an update to personal contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your Basic Info, including your first name, job title, and manager's name.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email, work phone, and personal phone.
3. Enter your address details, including your street, city, and state.
4. Specify your work schedule, including your work start and end dates, hours worked per week, and job status.
5. Select your job type (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Temporary).
6. Choose your job category (e.g., Marketing, Operations, Sales).
7. Finally, confirm your manager's first and last name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Info (id: 1)**: Enter your basic contact information, including your first name, job title, and manager's name.
* **First name (id: 2)**: Enter your first name.
* **Job title (id: 3)**: Enter your job title.
* **Manager name (id: 4)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Email (id: 5)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Contact info (id: 6)**: Enter any additional contact information you'd like to share.
* **Work phone (id: 7)**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Personal phone (id: 8)**: Enter your personal phone number.
* **Street (id: 9)**: Enter your street address.
* **City (id: 10)**: Enter the city where you work.
* **State (id: 11)**: Enter the state where you work.
* **Zip (id: 12)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Location (id: 13)**: Enter a brief description of your work location.
* **Work start date (id: 14)**: Enter your start date of work.
* **Work end date (id: 15)**: Enter your end date of work.
* **Hours worked (id: 16)**: Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Job status (id: 17)**: Select your current job status (Active, Inactive, Retired).
* **Job type (id: 18)**: Choose your job type (Full-time, Part-time, Temporary).
* **Job category (id: 19)**: Select your job category (Marketing, Operations, Sales).
* **Manager first name (id: 20)**: Enter your manager's first name.
* **Manager last name (id: 21)**: Enter your manager's last name.
