# Employee Grievance Handling Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate and track the handling of employee grievances in the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee ID
2. Briefly describe the issue (Grievance Description)
3. Describe how the issue was resolved (Resolution)
4. Add any manager comments (Manager Comments)
5. Add any employee comments (Employee Comments)
6. Select the outcome of the resolution (Resolution Outcome)
7. Rate your satisfaction with the resolution (Employee Satisfaction)
8. Provide any additional feedback (Feedback)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This section is a note section for the form header.
* **Employee ID**: This field is where you enter your employee ID, a unique identifier assigned to you by the company.
* **Grievance Description**: This field is where you provide a brief description of the issue that was reported to the manager.
* **Resolution**: This field is where you describe how the issue was resolved, including any actions taken by the management team.
* **Manager Comments**: This field is where the manager who resolved the issue can add any comments or notes about the resolution.
* **Employee Comments**: This field is where you can add any additional comments or feedback about the resolution.
* **Resolution Outcome**: This field asks you to select the outcome of the resolution: Not Resolved, Partially Resolved, or Fully Resolved.
* **Employee Satisfaction**: This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the resolution, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback**: This field is where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the resolution, the manager's handling, or any other aspects of the grievance process.

## Tips
* Be thorough when describing the issue and the resolution to help the manager track progress and improve the process.
* Rate your satisfaction honestly, based on your experience with the grievance process.
* Provide feedback on any areas of improvement for the manager or the company to address.
