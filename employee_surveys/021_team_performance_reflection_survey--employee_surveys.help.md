# Team Performance Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "Team Performance Reflection Survey", is designed to provide an opportunity for team members to reflect on their current team performance. The survey will help the team evaluate their strengths and areas of improvement, identify key areas that need attention, and provide a basis for future team development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the provided questions and options carefully.
2. Respond to each question based on your honest and sincere opinion.
3. Select all relevant options that apply to your answer.
4. Submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Performance** (`team_performance_1` and `team_performance_2`): 
    Rate your perception of the team's performance in these areas on a scale from 1-3.
* **Team Work** (`team_work_1` and `team_work_2`): 
    Select all that apply to describe your team dynamics, such as Collaborative, Individual, or Hybrid.
* **Communication** (`communication_1`): 
    Select all methods of communication you use in the team, such as In-person Meetings, Email, Phone, or Chat.
* **Task Workload** (`task_workload_1`): 
    Rate your perception of the workload assigned to the team on a scale from 1-3.
* **Time Management** (`time_management_1`): 
    Rate your perception of the team's time management on a scale from 1-3.
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving_1`): 
    Rate your perception of the team's ability to solve problems on a scale from 1-3.
* **Continuous Learning** (`continuous_learning_1` and `continuous_learning_2`): 
    Select all that apply to describe the team's approach to continuous learning, such as Mandatory, Optional, or Self-Driven.
* **Innovation** (`innovation_1`): 
    Select one or both of the options, Team Members or Team Leaders, that you think is most responsible for the team's innovation.
* **Team Roles** (`team_roles_1`): 
    Select all roles that apply to the team, such as Team Member, Team Leader, or Other (please specify).
* **Team Communication** (`team_communication_1`): 
    Select all methods of communication you use in the team, such as In-person Meetings, Email, Phone, or Other (please specify).
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership_1`): 
    Select one of the options, Autocratic, Democratic, or Laissez-faire, that best describes the team's leadership style.
* **Team Management** (`team_management_1`): 
    Select all that apply to describe the team's management style, such as Centralized, Decentralized, or Hybrid.
* **Team Cohesion** (`team_cohesion_1`): 
    Rate your perception of the team's cohesion on a scale from 1-3.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment_1`): 
    Select all that apply to describe your perception of the work environment, such as Good, Bad, or Neutral.
* **Team Engagement** (`team_engagement_1`): 
    Select all that apply to describe your perception of team engagement, such as High, Low, or Neutral.
* **Team Satisfaction** (`team_satisfaction_1` and `team_satisfaction_2`): 
    Select all that apply to describe your team satisfaction, such as High, Low, or Neutral.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction_1`): 
    Select all that apply to describe your overall satisfaction with the team, such as High, Low, or Neutral.
* **Team Effectiveness** (`team_effectiveness_1`): 
    Select all that apply to describe your perception of the team's effectiveness, such as High, Low, or Neutral.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_1`): 
    Select all that apply to describe the type of feedback you give and receive in the team, such as Constructive, Unconstructive, or Neutral.
* **Submission** (`submission_1`): 
    Select all that apply to describe the way you provide feedback, such as Anonymous, Pseudonymously, or Openly.
