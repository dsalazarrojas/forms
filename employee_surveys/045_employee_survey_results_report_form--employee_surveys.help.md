# Employee Survey Results Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and report results from employee surveys, providing a structured way to gather feedback on various aspects of employee satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for each section.
2. Provide feedback and comments where required.
3. Ensure that your responses are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Survey Results (`survey_results`)
* **Survey Results** (`select_one`, required: false): Select whether the employee survey results are 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 2. Employee Feedback (`employee_feedback`)
* **Employee Feedback** (`text`, required: false): Provide your feedback on your work experience.

### 3. Suggestion Areas (`suggestion_areas`)
* **Suggestion Areas** (`text`, required: false): Suggest areas where improvement is needed.

### 4. Suggested Improvements (`suggested_improvements`)
* **Suggested Improvements** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether suggested improvements are 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 5. Action Points (`action_points`)
* **Action Points** (`select_one`, required: false): Select whether action points are 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 6. Action Points Comments (`action_points_comments`)
* **Action Points Comments** (`text`, required: false): Provide comments on action points.

### 7. Manager Comments (`manager_comments`)
* **Manager Comments** (`text`, required: false): Provide comments from your manager.

### 8. Employee Satisfaction (`employee_satisfaction`)
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`select_one`, required: false): Select whether you are Satisfied or Dissatisfied.

### 9. Suggested Improvements Comments (`suggested_improvements_comments`)
* **Suggested Improvements Comments** (`text`, required: false): Provide comments on suggested improvements.

### 10. Action Plan (`action_plan`)
* **Action Plan** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether action plans are 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 11. Action Plan Comments (`action_plan_comments`)
* **Action Plan Comments** (`text`, required: false): Provide comments on action plans.

Please note that some fields are optional, and you should only fill them out if required or if you have something to add.
