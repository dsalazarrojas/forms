# Employee Motivation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The employee motivation survey is designed to help us understand your level of motivation and engagement in the workplace. This survey will help us to identify areas of improvement and create a more motivating work environment for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Begin" button to start the survey.
2. Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
3. Select all the options that apply to your motivation level and factors that motivate you.
4. Enter your email and phone number (if applicable) for further communication.
5. Click on "Submit" to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Employee Motivation Survey** (Page 1): This page serves as an introduction to the survey.
	+ This page is not a field that requires filling out.
* **Motivation Level** (id: 2, Number): Rate your level of motivation on a scale of 1-5.
	+ This scale will help us understand how motivated you are to come to work.
* **What motivates you to come to work?** (id: 3, Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to you.
	+ Select all the options that you think motivate you to come to work.
	+ Options are: Motivation, Engagement, Personal growth.
* **Which factors motivate you at work?** (id: 4, Select One): Choose one option that best describes your motivation.
	+ Select one option from: Autonomy, Feedback, Recognition, Challenge, Fit, Growth.
* **Do you have any comments?** (id: 5, Note): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have.
	+ This is an open-ended field for you to share your thoughts.
* **Email** (id: 6, Email): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for further communication).
	+ Please enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your survey results.
* **Phone** (id: 7, Text): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for further communication).
	+ Please enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your survey results.
* **Motivation Scale** (id: 8, Number): Rate your motivation on a scale of 1-5.
	+ This scale will help us understand your motivation level.
* **Comments** (id: 9, Text): Add any additional comments.
	+ This is an open-ended field for you to share any additional thoughts.
* **Motivation Score** (id: 10, Number): This field will show your motivation score.
	+ This score will be calculated based on your answers.
* **Results** (id: 11, Date): This field will show the date and time of your survey completion.
	+ This field is for reference purposes only.

## Tips

* Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* Select all the options that apply to you.
* Enter your email and phone number (if applicable) for further communication.
* Click on "Submit" to complete the survey.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your manager or HR representative.
