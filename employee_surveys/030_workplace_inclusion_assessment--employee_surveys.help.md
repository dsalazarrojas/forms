# Workplace Inclusion Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Inclusion Assessment is designed to gauge how well employees feel included in the team and identify areas for improvement. This form will help the management understand individual perspectives and create a more inclusive and welcoming work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions truthfully, using the options provided.
2. Select the options that best describe your feelings or experiences for each question.
3. If you want to elaborate on any of your answers, use the "Can you share any additional comments about inclusion?" field.
4. For questions with a hint, please refer to the hints for guidance on how to answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What do you value most in our team? (select_one, required: false)
* This question is asking about your priorities and what matters most to you in the team's dynamics.

### How would you rate our team's communication style? (number, required: false)
* Rate your experience with the team's communication style, using a scale (e.g., 1-10).

### What's preventing you from feeling fully included in our team? (text, required: false)
* This is an open-ended question where you can share any reasons or concerns you might have about feeling included.

### Do you feel like you belong in our team? (select_multiple, required: false)
* Select all that apply if you feel like you belong in the team (True) or don't feel like you belong (False).

### What would help you feel more included? (text, required: false)
* Think about any suggestions or ideas you have for improving inclusion and share them here.

### How long have you been working with our team? (time, required: false)
* Please provide the length of time you've been working with the team (e.g., weeks, months, years).

### Can you share any additional comments about inclusion? (note, required: false)
* Share any further thoughts or insights about inclusion that weren't covered by previous questions.

### What's your email address? (email, required: false)
* Please enter your work email address.

### What's your phone number? (text, required: false)
* Please enter your work phone number.
