# PC Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and information from employees about the management of personal computers in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as they appear on each page. Make sure to provide your honest feedback and input for each question.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields. Some fields may be optional, but required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Workplace Computers
* **Workplace Computers** (`workplace_computers`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks how you rate the quality of workplace computers. Choose from the options "Computers are fast", "Computers are slow", or "Computers are average".

### 2. IT Support
* **IT Support** (`it_support`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks how you rate the quality of IT support. Choose from the options "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".

### 3. Date Last Updated
* **Date Last Updated** (`date_last_updated`, `date`, required: true): This question asks for the date the form was last updated. Please enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### 4. IT Support Team
* **IT Support Team** (`it_support_team`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for your feedback about the IT support team. Please enter a brief description of your experience with the team.

### 5. Issues
* **Issues** (`issues`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for listing any issues you have encountered with your computer or IT support. Be as detailed as possible.

### 6. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for any additional comments you would like to make about your experience.

### 7. Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): This is a field for entering your work email address.

### 8. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering your work phone number.

### 9. Date Submitted
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required: true): This question asks for the date and time the form was submitted. Please enter in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

### 10. Time Submitted
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, `time`, required: true): This question asks for the time the form was submitted. Please enter in the format HH:MM:SS.

### 11. Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks for your feedback on your overall experience. Choose from the options "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".

### 12. Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: true): This question asks which tool you were assigned to use for IT support. Choose from the options "chatjimmy", "chatbot", or "chatbot2".

### 13. Employee ID
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering your employee ID number.

### 14. Supervisor ID
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering your supervisor's ID number.

### 15. Department
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering your department name.

### 16. Location
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering your location.

### 17. Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering your job title.

### 18. Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering your manager's name.

### 19. Note
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: true): This is a text field for any additional notes about your experience.

### 20. Issues Description
* **Issues Description** (`issues_description`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for describing any issues you have encountered with the computer or IT support.

### 21. Email Description
* **Email Description** (`email_description`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering your email address description.

### 22. Feedback Description
* **Feedback Description** (`feedback_description`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for providing a detailed description of your feedback.

### 23. Department Description
* **Department Description** (`department_description`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering a description of your department.

### 24. Time Submitted Description
* **Time Submitted Description** (`time_submitted_description`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering a description of the time the form was submitted.

### 25. Submitted Description
* **Submitted Description** (`submitted_description`, `text`, required: true): This is a text field for entering a description of when the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your supervisor or IT support staff.
