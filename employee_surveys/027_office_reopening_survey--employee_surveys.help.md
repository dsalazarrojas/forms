# Office Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees about their expectations and concerns regarding the reopening of the office.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the frequency of visits you expect to make to the office:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Biweekly
	* Monthly
2. Select the reason(s) why you want to reopen the office:
	* For work
	* For socializing
	* For meetings
	* For events
	* For personal reasons
3. If you selected "other" for the reason, please specify why you want to reopen the office.
4. Describe any concerns you have about reopening the office.
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please list them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office Reopening** (`Select Multiple`, 1): This is where you will choose the frequency of visits you expect to make to the office. Please select all that apply.
* **Reason for Reopening** (`Text`, 2): Here, you can describe why you want to reopen the office. If you have selected "other" as your reason, please specify in this field.
* **Proposed Reopening Date** (`Date`, 3): If you have a specific date in mind for the office to reopen, enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Proposed Reopening Time** (`Time`, 4): If you have a specific time in mind for the office to reopen, enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Frequency of Visits** (`Select One`, 5, 16): This is where you will choose the frequency of visits you expect to make to the office.
* **Reasons for Reopening** (`Select Multiple`, 7): Here, you can select the reason(s) why you want to reopen the office.
* **Additional Comments** (`Text`, 6, 21, 24): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please list them here.
* **Office Reopening Concerns** (`Text`, 8, 25): Describe any concerns you have about reopening the office.
* **Expectations about Reopening** (`Text`, 9): Describe your expectations about reopening the office.
* **Reopen Frequency Other** (`Text`, 10, 17): If you selected "other" for the frequency of visits, please specify.
* **Concerns Other** (`Text`, 11): If you have selected "other" for the reason for reopening, please specify.
* **Reopen Reasons Other** (`Text`, 13): If you selected "other" for the reason for reopening, please specify.
* **Reopen Date Other** (`Date`, 15): If you have a specific date in mind for the office to reopen, enter it here.
* **Reopen Frequency Other** (`Text`, 20): If you selected "other" for the frequency of visits, please specify.
* **Reopen Frequency Details** (`Text`, 21): If you selected "other" for the frequency of visits, please provide details.
* **Concerns about Reopen Concerns** (`Text`, 14): If you have any concerns about your concerns, please list them here.
* **Reopen Concerns Other** (`Text`, 14): If you have concerns about reopening, please describe them here.
* **Reopen Expectations** (`Text`, 19): Describe your expectations about reopening the office.
* **Office Reopening Frequency** (`Select One`, 23): This is where you will choose the frequency of visits you expect to make to the office.
* **Any Other Comments** (`Text`, 24): If you have any other comments or concerns, please list them here.
