# Sick Leave Donation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge your opinions and attitudes about the sick leave donation program. It will help us identify areas of concern and overall support for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the option that best reflects your attitude or opinion for each question.
3. You may select multiple departments if you're in a hybrid or shared role.
4. Take your time, and make sure to read each question carefully before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sick-Leave-Donation-Survey** (`sick_leave_donation_survey`, text, required): This is the title of the survey. Please write your name or ID to identify yourself.
* **How do you feel about the sick leave donation program?** (`sentiment`, select_one, required): This question will gauge your overall attitude towards the program. Select one of the options:
	+ Very negative
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
	+ Positive
	+ Very positive
* **What concerns do you have about the sick leave donation program?** (`concerns`, text, optional): This question is an opportunity for you to express any concerns or issues you have with the program.
* **How much overall support do you give to the sick leave donation program?** (`overall_support`, select_one, optional): This question will help us understand your level of support for the program. Select one of the options:
	+ Strongly disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Not sure
	+ Neutral
	+ Agree
	+ Strongly agree
* **Which department do you work in?** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the department(s) you work in. You may select multiple departments if you're in a hybrid or shared role.
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ Customer Service
	+ IT and Software
	+ Finance
	+ Human Resources
	+ Executive and Management
	+ Operations
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, select_one, optional): This question will ask you to select one of the options provided.
* **Question 2** (`question_2`, select_multiple, optional): This question will ask you to select multiple options.
* **Question 3** (`question_3`, note, optional): This question is a text area for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Question 4** (`question_4`, select_one, optional): This question will ask you to select one of the options provided.

## Tips
* Take your time, and make sure to read each question carefully before proceeding.
* Answer honestly, and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
