# Employee Recognition Program Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Program Feedback Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the Employee Recognition Program. The form aims to collect information from employees about their experiences, suggestions, and ratings regarding the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your answer for "Recognize prophets" by choosing one or more options from the list.
2. For "How often" and "Quality", select one option from the list.
3. In "Suggestions", type any ideas or comments you have about the program.
4. If you have feedback, rate the program from 1 to 5.
5. Answer the demographic questions by providing your employee ID, manager's name, department, team member, and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Recognize Prophets**
* (Yes, No, Other): This question asks about your experience with recognizing team members or team leaders who are doing something exceptionally well. 
### **How often**
* (Yes, No, Other): Please select how often you feel you receive feedback or rewards from the recognition program.
### **Quality**
* (Yes, No, Other): Select whether the quality of the recognition program is meeting your expectations.
### **Suggestions**
* (Text): Share any suggestions or ideas you have about the program. This could be about improvements or changes that would make the program more effective.
### **Rating**
* (Number): Rate the program from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) based on your overall experience.
### **Feedback Frequency 2**
* (Yes, No, Other): This question is similar to "How often", but with a slightly different perspective.
### **Employee ID**
* (Number): Enter your employee ID number.
### **Manager**
* (Text): Type in the name of your manager.
### **Department**
* (Text): Enter the name of your department.
### **Team member**
* (Text): Identify who you are a team member of.
### **Manager (2)**
* (Text): Type in the name of your manager.
### **Team**
* (Text): Enter the name of your team.
### **Employee**
* (Text): Identify who the employee is that you are giving feedback for.
### **Department 2**
* (Text): Type in the name of your department.
### **Manager (3)**
* (Text): Type in the name of your manager.
