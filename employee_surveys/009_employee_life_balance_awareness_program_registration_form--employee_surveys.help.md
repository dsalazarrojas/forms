# Employee Life Balance Awareness Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Life Balance Awareness Program is designed to promote employee well-being and work-life balance. This form is used to register employees for the program and gather relevant information to tailor the program to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your Job Title from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose your Department(s) from the list.
3. Enter your Start Date and End Date (if applicable).
4. Select your Time Off Policy.
5. Enter your Manager's Name and Email (if applicable).
6. Enter any Employee Notes and Manager Notes (if applicable).
7. Select your Employee Type (Full Time, Part Time, or Contract).
8. Choose your Work Location (Office, Remote, or Hybrid).
9. Select your Job Category, Job Family, and Job Grade (if applicable).
10. Select your Employee Status and Work Status (if applicable).
11. Select whether you are registering as a Team Lead or Team Member.
12. Select your Team Location (if applicable).
13. Select whether you are registering as an Individual or Team Lead/Member.
14. Click Submit to complete your registration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are registering accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your manager or HR representative.
* You can register for the program as a team lead or team member.
* Please note that only one registration per employee is permitted.
* Please ensure that your team members are also registered for the program (if applicable).
