# Employee Competency Mapping Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate an employee's competencies and create a performance plan, helping identify areas for development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information in the **Employee Survey Form** section, including any relevant comments or observations.
2. In the **Evaluation Form** section, provide a brief evaluation of the employee's performance, including strengths and areas for development.
3. The **Skills Assessment Form** section allows you to select up to 10 skills that the employee has demonstrated proficiency in.
4. The **Performance Plan** section is for outlining specific goals and objectives for the employee's development.
5. In the **Development Plan** section, describe specific actions and steps the employee should take to improve in the selected skills.
6. List any **Action Items** that the employee needs to complete to achieve the goals outlined in the **Performance Plan**.
7. Provide any **Evaluation Comments** or observations about the employee's performance.
8. In the **Skills Selection Form**, choose up to 10 skills that the employee has demonstrated proficiency in.
9. Enter the **Review Date** on which this form was reviewed.
10. Enter your name and title in the **Reviewed By** section.
11. In the **Reviewer Comments** section, provide any additional comments or feedback.
12. Finally, enter the **Final Rating** of the employee's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Survey Form (<id: 1>)
* **Employee Survey Form**: A brief summary of the employee's performance, including strengths and weaknesses.

### Evaluation Form (<id: 2>)
* **Evaluation Form**: A detailed evaluation of the employee's performance, including areas for development.

### Skills Assessment Form (<id: 3>)
* **Skills Assessment Form**: Select up to 10 skills the employee has demonstrated proficiency in.

### Performance Plan (<id: 4>)
* **Performance Plan**: Outlines specific goals and objectives for the employee's development.

### Development Plan (<id: 5>)
* **Development Plan**: Describes specific actions and steps the employee should take to improve in the selected skills.

### Action Items (<id: 6>)
* **Action Items**: A list of specific tasks the employee needs to complete to achieve the goals outlined in the **Performance Plan**.

### Evaluation Comments (<id: 7>)
* **Evaluation Comments**: Additional comments or feedback on the employee's performance.

### Skills Selection Form (<id: 8>)
* **Skills Selection Form**: Select up to 10 skills the employee has demonstrated proficiency in. The skills listed include:
	+ Leadership
	+ Communication
	+ Time Management
	+ Problem-solving
	+ Adaptability
	+ Team Management
	+ Interpersonal Skills
	+ Results-driven
	+ Continuous Learning
	+ Strategic Thinking
	+ Collaboration
	+ Innovation

### Review Date (<id: 9>)
* **Review Date**: The date on which this form was reviewed.

### Reviewed By (<id: 10>)
* **Reviewed By**: Enter your name and title.

### Reviewer Comments (<id: 11>)
* **Reviewer Comments**: Additional comments or feedback on the employee's performance.

### Final Rating (<id: 12>)
* **Final Rating**: A final evaluation of the employee's performance.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all necessary information before completing this form.
* Use concrete and specific language when evaluating an employee's performance.
* Consider the employee's developmental goals and objectives when creating the **Performance Plan** and **Development Plan** sections.
* Make sure to include all required fields, as indicated by the **Required** label.
