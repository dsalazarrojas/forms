# representative_compensation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
Fill out this form to provide feedback on your compensation and benefits package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (Text):** Enter the name of your company.
* **job_title (Text):** Enter your job title.
* **Base Salary (Number):** Enter your current base salary.
* **bonus_structure (Select One):** Select the bonus structure you currently receive.
* **Annual Bonus (Number):** Enter your annual bonus amount.
* **health_insurance (Select Multiple):** Select the health insurance options you currently receive.
* **Benefits Package (Text):** Enter your benefits package.
* **Retirement Plan (Select One):** Select the retirement plan you currently have.
* **Job Satisfaction (Select One):** Select how satisfied you are with your job.
* **compensation_equity (Select Multiple):** Select whether or not you have compensation equity.
* **pay_forperformance (Select Multiple):** Select whether or not you have pay for performance.
* **paid_time_off (Select Multiple):** Select the paid time off options you currently have.
* **paid_holidays (Select Multiple):** Select the paid holidays options you currently have.
* **paid_vacation (Select Multiple):** Select the paid vacation options you currently have.
* **Job Security (Select One):** Select how secure you feel in your job.
* **work_life_balance (Select One):** Select how balanced you feel in your work and personal life.
* **Employee Recognition (Select One):** Select how recognized you feel as an employee.
* **Training and Development (Select Multiple):** Select whether or not you have access to training and development opportunities.
* **Career Growth Opportunities (Select One):** Select how many opportunities you have for career growth.
* **Job Stability (Select One):** Select how stable you feel in your job.
* **Communication (Select Multiple):** Select whether or not you feel communication is effective.
* **Work Life Quality (Select Multiple):** Select how good or poor you feel your work life balance is.
* **Team Dynamics (Select One):** Select how dynamic you feel your team is.
* **Company Culture (Select One):** Select how aligned you feel with the company culture.
* **Diversity and Inclusion (Select One):** Select how inclusive you feel the company culture is.

Note: Please select all that apply for Select Multiple fields.
