## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique Employee ID to identify yourself and ensure your feedback is linked to your personnel file.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, number, required): Enter the Employee ID of your supervisor, which is necessary for accurate tracking and feedback.
* **HR Manager ID** (`hr_manager_id`, number, required): Enter the Employee ID of your HR representative or manager.
* **Overall Tone of HR Communication** (`hr_employee_survey_tone_1`, select_one, required): Rate on a scale of 1-10 how you feel about the overall tone of HR communication. A higher score indicates a more positive tone.
* **Tone of HR Communication** (`hr_employee_survey_tone_2`, select_one, required): Answer how often you feel HR communication is clear and understandable.
* **Hr Employee Survey Tone 3** (`hr_employee_survey_tone_3`, select_one, required): Answer how often you feel HR communication is respectful.
* **Hr Employee Survey Tone 4** (`hr_employee_survey_tone_4`, select_one, required): Answer how often you feel HR communication is informative.
* **Hr Employee Survey Tone 5** (`hr_employee_survey_tone_5`, select_one, required): Answer how often you feel HR communication is helpful.
* **Hr Employee Survey Tone 6** (`hr_employee_survey_tone_6`, select_one, required): Answer how often you feel HR communication is supportive.
* **Overall Satisfaction with HR Communication** (`hr_satisfaction_levels_1`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-10 with HR communication.
* **Suggestion for HR Communication** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Type any suggestions you have for improving HR communication.
* **Additional Comments** (`hr_survey_tone_comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about HR communication.
