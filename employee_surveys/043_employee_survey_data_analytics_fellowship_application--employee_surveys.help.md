# Employee Survey Data Analytics Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to apply to the Data Analytics Fellowship program. The program aims to provide a culture of data-driven decision-making within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant category that best describes your application.
2. Fill out the application title.
3. Provide a short description of your background and motivations for joining the fellowship.
4. Select the training teams you are interested in joining.
5. Choose how you would like your reviews to be handled.
6. Enter your application form ID (if known).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Title** (`application_title`, text, **optional**): Enter a title that best describes your application.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, **optional**): Select the category that best describes your application:
	+ Employee Surveys: For employees who are interested in data surveys.
	+ Other: For employees with other data-related interests.
* **Description** (`description`, text, **optional**): Provide a short description of your background and motivations for joining the fellowship. This will help the selection committee understand your fit for the program.
* **Training Teams** (`training_teams`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select the training teams you are interested in joining:
	+ HR: For employees interested in Human Resources.
	+ People Analytics: For employees interested in people analytics.
	+ Training: For employees interested in data-related training.
* **Compare Applications** (`compare_applications`, text, **optional**): Not applicable.
* **Consistent Data Collection** (`consistent_data_collection`, text, **optional**): Not applicable.
* **Organized Review** (`organized_review`, text, **optional**): Not applicable.
* **Review from Each Form Submission** (`review_from_each_form_submission`, select_one, **optional**): Choose how you want your reviews to be handled:
	+ Individual: Each submission will be reviewed separately.
	+ Batch: Multiple submissions will be reviewed together.
	+ All at Once: All submissions will be reviewed as one.
* **Application Form ID** (`application_form_id`, text, **optional**): Enter your application form ID if you know it, otherwise, it's not necessary.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much information as possible for the selection committee.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact the program administrators for help.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
