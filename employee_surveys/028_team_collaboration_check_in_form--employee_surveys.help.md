# team_collaboration_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team collaboration check-in form is designed to help teams assess and improve their collaboration quality, dynamics, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and answer each question based on your recent or current project experience.
2. Answer every question to the best of your abilities, providing concrete examples or anecdotes when possible.
3. If a question doesn't apply to your experience, indicate so in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the project you're evaluating. This will help in identifying the project-specific context of your answers.
* **Collaboration Dynamics** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Describe the collaboration dynamics within your team during this project. For example, were there any conflicts, and how did you resolve them?
* **Team Dynamics** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Reflect on the team's overall dynamics, including communication, problem-solving, and decision-making processes.
* **Communication Channels** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Think about how team members communicated with each other during this project, including the tools and methods used.
* **Feedback** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Describe any feedback you received from teammates or gave to them, including constructive feedback or suggestions.
* **Project Leadership** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Assess the leadership style of the project lead or team lead, including decision-making processes and conflict resolution.
* **Feedback Leadership** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Reflect on how feedback was given and received from team leaders, including the quality and timeliness of feedback.
* **Team Leadership** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Evaluate the team's leadership structure and performance during this project.
* **Overall Rating** (`9`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the team, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest). This will help in identifying areas that need improvement and provide a quick snapshot of your experience.
