# Employee Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by an employee who is leaving the company, providing important information about their employment history, reason for departure, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
3. Enter your current job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Provide a brief reason for your departure in the "Reason for Departure" field.
5. Enter the notice period you will be providing, if applicable, in the "Notice Period" field.
6. Enter the date you will be leaving the company in the "Exit Date" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Exit Comments" field, if necessary.
8. Provide any comments or feedback from your manager in the "Manager Comments" field, if necessary.
9. Enter the name of your department or team in the "Department" field.
10. Enter any details about company property that you are responsible for, or any items you need to return or dispose of, in the "Company Property" field.
11. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field, in case the company needs to contact you after you leave.
12. Answer any exit interview questions in the "Exit Interview Questions" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Reason for Departure** (`reason_for_departure`, text, required: false): Provide a brief reason for your departure from the company.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, text, required: false): Enter the notice period you will be providing, if applicable.
* **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you will be leaving the company.
* **Exit Comments** (`exit_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your departure.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback from your manager about your departure.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your department or team.
* **Company Property** (`company_property`, text, required: false): Enter details about company property that you are responsible for, or any items you need to return or dispose of.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, in case the company needs to contact you after you leave.
* **Exit Interview Questions** (`exit_interview_questions`, text, required: false): Answer any exit interview questions, if applicable.
