# Procurement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Procurement Survey is a tool to gather feedback on our procurement services, allowing us to understand our employees' experiences and preferences when it comes to these services. This information will help us improve our procurement processes and better serve our employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions to the best of your ability. You can select multiple answers for some questions if applicable. Make sure to answer all questions that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What services do you buy most frequently?** (`procurement_service_1`, select_multiple): Answer with all services you purchase frequently from our procurement team. This could be items like office supplies, equipment, software, etc.
* **Procurement service 1** (`procurement_service_1`, select_one): Answer with your level of satisfaction with this service.
* **Procurement service 2** (`procurement_service_2`, select_multiple): Answer with all services you purchase from our procurement team.
* **Procurement service 3** (`procurement_service_3`, select_one): Answer with your level of satisfaction with this service.
* **What frequency do you buy the services?** (`procurement_frequency`, select_one): Answer with the frequency you purchase these services.
* **Procurement service 4** (`procurement_service_4`, select_one): Answer with your rating of the quality of this service.
* **Procurement service 5** (`procurement_service_5`, select_one): Answer with your rating of the quality of this product.
* **Is there anything you'd like to add?** (`procurement_service_8`, note): Use this field to add any additional comments or suggestions about our procurement services.
* **Have you ever used the service?** (`procurement_service_7`, select_one): Answer with your experience using our procurement services.
* **What do you think about this service?** (`procurement_service_10`, select_multiple): Answer with your thoughts about our procurement services.
* **Have you been satisfied with the service?** (`procurement_service_9`, select_one): Answer with your level of satisfaction with this service.
* **What do you think we could improve?** (`procurement_service_12`, select_multiple): Answer with any areas where we could improve our procurement services.
* **Would you like to use our service again?** (`procurement_service_13`, select_one): Answer with your willingness to use our procurement services again.
* **Rating (out of 5)** (`procurement_survey_rating_1`, number): Rate your overall satisfaction with our procurement services.
* **Rating (out of 10)** (`procurement_survey_rating_2`, number): Rate the quality of our procurement services.
* **Rating (out of 5)** (`procurement_survey_rating_3`, number): Rate your satisfaction with our procurement services.
* **Procurement service 12** (`procurement_service_12`, select_one): Answer with your level of satisfaction with this service.
* **Procurement service 13** (`procurement_service_13`, select_one): Answer with your level of satisfaction with this service.
* **Date** (`procurement_survey_date`, date): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`procurement_survey_time`, time): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Email** (`procurement_survey_email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Procurement service 16** (`procurement_service_16`, select_one): Answer with your level of satisfaction with this service.
* **Procurement phone number** (`procurement_survey_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Procurement service 17** (`procurement_service_17`, select_one): Answer with your level of satisfaction with this service.
