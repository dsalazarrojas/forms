# Psychological Safety At Work Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychological Safety At Work Survey is designed to help measure the level of psychological safety within the workplace. It's meant to gauge whether you, as an employee, feel safe and supported within the work environment. This survey will help management and HR understand where improvements are needed to create a healthier work atmosphere. Answer the questions honestly to ensure accurate results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully, as they are self-explanatory and designed to cover different aspects of psychological safety.
2. Select the options that best describe your feelings on each page.
3. Be honest and thorough in your answers.
4. Ensure you complete all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Psychological Safety** (`page_1`, `select_multiple`, **required:** false): This field assesses how you feel about psychological safety in the workplace. Choose 'Yes' if you feel safe in your work environment, or 'No' if not.
* **Trust** (`page_2`, `select_multiple`, **required:** false): Here you will answer how much trust you feel towards your employer, colleagues, or manager. Choose 'Yes' if you trust them and 'No' if you don't.
* **Openness** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`, **required:** false): Select 'Yes' if you feel open to share your thoughts, opinions, and feelings within the team and workplace, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Team Safety** (`page_4`, `select_one`, **required:** false): This question asks about your perception of team safety. Choose 'Yes' if you feel safe within your team and 'No' if not.
* **Team Trust** (`page_5`, `select_one`, **required:** false): Select 'Yes' if you trust your team members and 'No' if you do not.
* **Team Openness** (`page_6`, `select_one`, **required:** false): Choose 'Yes' if you feel comfortable sharing your thoughts and opinions with your team and 'No' otherwise.
* **Team Support** (`page_7`, `select_multiple`, **required:** false): Select 'Yes' if you receive support from your team and 'No' if not.
* **Page 8** (`page_8`, `select_multiple`, **required:** false): Similar to previous questions, choose the options that best describe your feelings.
* **Page 9** (`page_9`, `select_one`, **required:** false): This question is also similar to previous ones.
* **Page 10** (`page_10`, `select_one`, **required:** false): Same as Page 9.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure accurate results.
- Review each page carefully before submitting.
- If you need help or have questions, contact your HR or supervisor.
