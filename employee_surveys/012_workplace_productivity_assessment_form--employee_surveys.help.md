# Workplace Productivity Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees assess their productivity and work environment. It aims to gather information on various aspects of work performance, such as work hours, breaks, time management, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the options that best describe your current work situation.

* **Employee Performance** (`employee_performance`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to rate your current performance, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Collaboration Tools** (`collaboration_tools`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to indicate the collaboration tools you use during work, such as email or phone. Select all that apply.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to indicate your primary method of communication, such as email, phone, or in-person.
* **Task Management** (`task_management`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to report the tools you use for task management, such as Task 1 or Task 2. Select all that apply.
* **Team Collaboration** (`team_collaboration`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your collaboration with your team members, such as how often you meet or communicate.
* **Productivity Tools** (`productivity_tools`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to indicate the tools you use to increase productivity, such as Tool 1 or Tool 2.
* **Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to indicate how often you report your progress, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Reporting Method** (`reporting_method`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to report the methods you use to report your progress, such as Method 1 or Method 2. Select all that apply.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your work environment, such as your workspace or office.

* **Work Space** (`work_space`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your work space, such as your desk or office.
* **Work Ergonomics** (`work_ergonomics`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your work ergonomics, such as chair or keyboard height.
* **Work Safety** (`work_safety`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to indicate whether your work environment is safe, with options Safe or Not Safe.
* **Work Flexibility** (`work_flexibility`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to report your flexibility in work, such as Flexible, Not Flexible, or Neutral. Select all that apply.

* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to rate your job satisfaction, with options High, Medium, or Low.
* **Team Satisfaction** (`team_satisfaction`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your satisfaction with your team, such as how well you work together.
