# Team Member Recognition Vote - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow team members to cast their vote for team recognition, providing details about the nominee and managing the submission process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select "Yes" or "No" from the "Nomination" section to determine if you wish to nominate a team member.
*   If you select "Yes", fill in the "Name" field with the name of the team member you wish to nominate.
*   In the "Nomination Reason" field, provide a brief explanation for your nomination.
*   In the "Nominee Name", "Nominee Job Title", "Nominee Department", "Nominee Manager", and "Nominee Manager Email" fields, enter the relevant information for the nominee.
*   In the "Submitter Name", "Submitter Email", "Submitter Phone", and "Submitter Note" fields, enter your own information as the person submitting the nomination.
*   If you wish to indicate the nominee's position, select "Yes" or "No" from the "Nominee Position" dropdown menu.
*   Optionally, select the "Impact" level as "Yes" or "No" to describe the impact of the nominee's work.
*   Select "Active" or "Inactive" from the "Nomination Status" dropdown menu to determine the current status of the nomination.
*   If you wish to add a "Date of Nomination", select a date from the calendar or enter it manually.
*   In the "Manager Note" field, you may add any additional comments or notes from the manager.
*   Finally, click "Submit" to complete the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nomination** (`Select Nomination`, `select_multiple`, **Required**: Yes):
    Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you wish to nominate a team member.
*   **Name** (`Name`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the name of the team member you wish to nominate.
*   **Nomination Reason** (`Nomination Reason`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Provide a brief explanation for your nomination.
*   **Name of Nominated** (`Name of Nominated`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the name of the team member you are nominating.
*   **Impact** (`Impact`, `select_one`, **Required**: No):
    Optionally, select "Yes" or "No" to describe the impact of the nominee's work.
*   **Nomination Type** (`Nomination Type`, `select_one`, **Required**: No):
    Select "Yes" or "No" to determine the type of nomination.
*   **Nomination Details** (`Nomination Details`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Provide additional details about the nomination.
*   **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the name of the manager of the nominated team member.
*   **Manager Email** (`Manager Email`, `email`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the email address of the nominee's manager.
*   **Manager Phone** (`Manager Phone`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the phone number of the nominee's manager.
*   **Rating** (`Rating`, `select_one`, **Required**: No):
    Select "Good" or "Fair" to rate the nominee's performance.
*   **Nomination Status** (`Nomination Status`, `select_one`, **Required**: No):
    Select "Active" or "Inactive" to determine the current status of the nomination.
*   **Submitter Name** (`Submitter Name`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter your own name as the person submitting the nomination.
*   **Submitter Email** (`Submitter Email`, `email`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter your own email address as the person submitting the nomination.
*   **Submitter Phone** (`Submitter Phone`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter your own phone number as the person submitting the nomination.
*   **Submitter Note** (`Submitter Note`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Add any additional comments or notes from the submitter.
*   **Nominee Name** (`Nominee Name`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the name of the team member being nominated.
*   **Nominee Job Title** (`Nominee Job Title`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the job title of the team member being nominated.
*   **Nominee Department** (`Nominee Department`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the department of the team member being nominated.
*   **Nominee Manager** (`Nominee Manager`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the name of the manager of the team member being nominated.
*   **Nominee Manager Email** (`Nominee Manager Email`, `email`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the email address of the nominee's manager.
*   **Nominee Manager Phone** (`Nominee Manager Phone`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Enter the phone number of the nominee's manager.
*   **Nominee Position** (`Nominee Position`, `select_one`, **Required**: No):
    Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the current position of the nominee.
*   **Date of Nomination** (`Date of Nomination`, `date`, **Required**: No):
    Optionally, select a date for the nomination.
*   **Manager Note** (`Manager Note`, `text`, **Required**: No):
    Add any additional comments or notes from the manager.
*   **Submitter Note** (`Submitter Note`, `text`, **Required**: Yes):
    Add any additional comments or notes from the submitter.

## Tips

*   Please ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the nomination.
*   Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
*   If you need help or have questions, reach out to the relevant manager or team lead.
