# Cybersecurity Analyst Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from departing cybersecurity analysts on their experience with the company. It helps us understand their satisfaction with our tools, processes, and overall experience to make improvements and better serve our employees in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the type of question. Be sure to provide specific feedback and suggestions where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with our tools?**: This question asks about your experience with the tools you've used during your time with us. Select one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with our tools.
* **How satisfied are you with our processes?**: This question is about your experience with our processes and procedures. Choose one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with our processes.
* **How satisfied are you with our team environment?**: This question is about your experience with your colleagues and team dynamics. Select one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with our team environment.
* **How satisfied are you with your work-life balance?**: This question is about your experience with balancing your work and personal life. Choose one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with your work-life balance.
* **Overall satisfaction with your experience?**: This question is about your overall satisfaction with your experience at the company. Select one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **What recommendations would you make regarding tools?**: This question is open-ended, and you can provide any feedback or suggestions you have for tools we should use or improve.
* **Are there any tools you would recommend that we should purchase or subscribe to?**: This question is also open-ended, and you can provide any specific tool recommendations with links.
* **What process improvements would you suggest?**: This question is about specific processes or procedures you think we can improve. Choose multiple options if you have multiple suggestions.
* **How would you rate the communication from your manager?**: This question is about your experience with communication from your manager. Select one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with the communication.
* **How satisfied are you with our training opportunities?**: This question is about your experience with training and development opportunities. Choose one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with our training.
* **How satisfied are you with your career development opportunities?**: This question is about your experience with career development opportunities. Select one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with our career development opportunities.
* **How satisfied are you with your manager relationship?**: This question is about your experience with your manager relationship. Choose one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with your manager relationship.
* **How many hours per week do you work?**: This question is about your work hours. Enter a number to indicate how many hours you typically work per week.
* **How many days off do you get per year?**: This question is about your time off benefits. Enter a number to indicate how many days off you receive per year.
* **How secure do you feel about your job security?**: This question is about your confidence in your job security. Enter a number to indicate how secure you feel.
* **How satisfied are you with your job satisfaction?**: This question is about your experience with your job satisfaction. Choose one of the options to indicate how satisfied you are with your job satisfaction.
* **How much support does your manager offer you?**: This question is about your experience with manager support. Enter a number to indicate how much support you receive.
* **How much support do you get from team members?**: This question is about your experience with team support. Enter a number to indicate how much support you receive.
* **What is your overall satisfaction with the company?**: This question is about your overall satisfaction with the company. Enter a number to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **What is your reason for leaving?**: This question is open-ended, and you can provide a brief explanation of why you are leaving.
* **What date do you plan on exiting?**: This question is about when you plan to leave the company. Enter a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Do you have any contact information you would like to share?**: This question is about any contact information you'd like to share with us.
* **Is there any additional feedback?**: This question is open-ended, and you can provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have.
* **What is the name of your manager?**: This question is about your manager's name. Enter your manager's name.
* **What is the name of your team?**: This question is about your team's name. Enter your team's name. 

## Tips
Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, and be as specific as possible with your feedback. Your input will help us improve our tools, processes, and overall experience for future employees.
