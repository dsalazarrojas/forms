# corporate-initiative-engagement-poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "corporate-initiative-engagement-poll" form is designed to gauge employee engagement and interest in company-wide initiatives. This form is a tool to help management understand the awareness and participation levels of various initiatives within the company, allowing for informed decision-making and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the company initiatives that you are aware of.
2. Choose your current awareness level of each selected initiative:
	* Low: You are not very familiar with the initiative.
	* Medium: You have some knowledge, but it's not very specific.
	* High: You have a good understanding of the initiative.
3. Indicate your level of interest in participating in each selected initiative:
	* Very High
	* High
	* Medium
	* Low
4. Enter the name of the communication channel used for this initiative.
5. Specify how often you communicate with the team about this initiative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the company initiatives** (`company_initiatives`):
	+ Select all the initiatives you are aware of. You can choose from:
		- Initiative 1
		- Initiative 2
		- Initiative 3
* **Awareness level** (`awareness_level`):
	+ Choose your current level of awareness for each selected initiative. If you're not familiar with an initiative, select "Low".
* **Participation interest** (`participation_interest`):
	+ Indicate your level of interest in participating in each selected initiative.
* **Communications channel** (`communications_channel`):
	+ Enter the name of the communication channel used for this initiative.
* **Communication frequency** (`communication_frequency`):
	+ Specify how often you communicate with the team about this initiative.
