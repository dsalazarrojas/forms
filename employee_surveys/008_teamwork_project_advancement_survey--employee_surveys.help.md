# Teamwork Project Advancement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights on the Teamwork Project Advancement Survey from team members. It aims to assess the project's progress, collaboration, and challenges. The information collected will help the project team to better understand their performance and make informed decisions for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, Optional)
Enter a brief description of the project.

### **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, `text`, Optional)
Enter the name of the project manager responsible for the project's progress.

### **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, `number`, Optional)
Rate your level of collaboration on a scale from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).

### **Collaboration Level** (`collaboration_level`, `select_multiple`, Optional)
Choose the level of collaboration you experienced during the project, selecting multiple options as needed:
* Collaboration
* Independent

### **Project Milestone** (`project_milestone`, `date`, Optional)
Enter the date of the project milestone, if any.

### **Project Milestone Target Date** (`project_milestone_target_date`, `time`, Optional)
Enter the target time for the project milestone, if applicable.

### **Project Challenge** (`project_challenge`, `text`, Optional)
Describe any challenges you faced during the project.

### **Project Challenge Level** (`project_challenge_level`, `select_one`, Optional)
Rate the level of challenge you faced during the project, selecting one of the following:
* High
* Medium
* Low

### **Project Improvement Idea** (`project_improvement_idea`, `text`, Optional)
Share any ideas for improvement on the project.

### **Project Improvement Action** (`project_improvement_action`, `text`, Optional)
Describe the action you would take to improve the project, if you could.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
- If a field is not applicable, you may leave it blank.
- If you need help or have questions, contact your project manager.
