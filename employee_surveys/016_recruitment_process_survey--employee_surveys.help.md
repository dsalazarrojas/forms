# Recruitment Process Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Recruitment Process Survey" form is designed to collect feedback from candidates who have gone through the company's recruitment process. This feedback will help us understand areas of improvement and refine our recruitment practices to better serve our candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Carefully read each field explanation to understand its purpose.
* Fill in the required information accurately.
* Select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Ad** (id: 2): Enter your experience with our job ad, such as how it caught your attention or if it was helpful in your job search.
* **Communication** (id: 3): Select all that apply:
	+ Excellent: We communicated with you regularly and effectively.
	+ Good: We communicated with you somewhat regularly and effectively.
	+ Fair: We communicated with you occasionally or not regularly enough.
	+ Poor: We didn't communicate with you effectively.
* **Candidate Experience** (id: 1): Share your experience with our recruitment process, including any positive or negative experiences you had.
* **Job Title** (id: 16): Enter the title of the job you applied for or were offered.
* **Job Description** (id: 17): Briefly describe the job role and responsibilities.
* **Job Type** (id: 18): Enter the type of job you applied for, such as Full-time, Part-time, or Contract.
* **Job Category** (id: 19): Enter the category of the job you applied for, such as Sales, Marketing, or IT.
* **Job Subcategory** (id: 20): Enter the subcategory of the job you applied for, such as Software Development, Data Analysis, or Customer Service.
* **Candidate Satisfaction** (id: 8): Select your level of satisfaction with the recruitment process:
	+ Very Satisfied: You were very satisfied with the process.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You were somewhat satisfied with the process.
	+ Neutral: You had a neutral experience with the process.
	+ Somewhat Disatisfied: You were somewhat disatisfied with the process.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You were very disatisfied with the process.
* **Submission Date** (id: 10): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Submitted By** (id: 11): Select how you submitted your application:
	+ Online: You submitted your application online.
	+ In Person: You submitted your application in person.
	+ Phone: You submitted your application over the phone.
* **Contact Person** (id: 14): Select the person you had contact with during the recruitment process:
	+ Online: You had contact with the contact person online.
	+ In Person: You had contact with the contact person in person.
* **Submission Time** (id: 12): Enter the time you submitted your application.
* **Candidate Name** (id: 13): Enter your name as a candidate.
* **Feedback** (id: 5): Share any feedback or comments about your experience with our recruitment process.
* **Email** (id: 7): Enter your email address associated with your application.
* **Comments** (id: 9): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Candidate Name** (id: 6): Enter the name of the candidate.
* **Job Category** (id: 4): Enter the category of the job you applied for.
* **Timeline** (id: 4): Enter the timeline of your application process.
* **Submitted** (id: 15): Select if you have submitted your application:
	+ True: You have submitted your application.
	+ False: You haven't submitted your application.

Note: The "Candidate Name" field (id: 6) seems to be a duplicate field and is not necessary. It can be removed from the form and the guide.
