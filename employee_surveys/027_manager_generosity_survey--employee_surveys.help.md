# Manager Generosity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from employees about their managers' generosity, with the goal of enhancing the work environment and employee well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the introduction for an overview of the survey's purpose and objectives.
2. Rate your manager's generosity on a scale from 1-10, where 1 is very low and 10 is very high.
3. Provide your feedback about your manager's generosity, sharing your thoughts and suggestions.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Rating (manager_rating)**: This is the main question where you will rate your manager's generosity on a scale from 1-10.
* **Feedback (feedback)**: In a few sentences, describe your thoughts on your manager's generosity, including what they do well and what you would like to see improved.
* **Additional Comments (additional_feedback)**: If there's anything else you'd like to share about your manager, please add it here. This could be something positive or negative, or simply a suggestion for improvement.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your feedback, and try to be specific when sharing your thoughts.
* Keep your answers concise and focused on your manager's generosity, rather than their overall performance.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR department for support.
