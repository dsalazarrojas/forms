# Performance Feedback Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on an employee's performance in various aspects, allowing managers and HR representatives to monitor and assess an individual's strengths and weaknesses. This feedback will help in making informed decisions regarding promotions, training, or other professional development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each rating question by selecting a number between 1 (low) and 5 (high).
2. For the "comments" field, provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have about the employee's performance.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, but keep in mind that the form is optional, and you can skip questions that don't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Feedback Form**: This is the main form section. Please answer the following questions to provide detailed feedback on the employee's performance.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the employee's overall performance, considering their work quality, productivity, and other relevant factors.
* **Quality Rating**: Rate the employee's quality of work, considering their attention to detail, thoroughness, and overall quality of their work.
* **Timeliness Rating**: Rate the employee's timeliness, considering their ability to meet deadlines and deliver results on time.
* **Communication Rating**: Rate the employee's communication skills, considering their ability to effectively convey ideas, listen actively, and respond to questions and concerns.
* **Teamwork Rating**: Rate the employee's ability to work collaboratively with others, considering their teamwork, coordination, and cooperation.
* **Initiative Rating**: Rate the employee's initiative, considering their proactivity, self-motivation, and ability to take on new challenges.
* **Communication Rating 2**: This is another rating question, similar to "Communication Rating", but with some minor differences. (Assuming it's meant to rate a different aspect of communication, like verbal or written communication.)
* **Quality of Work Rating 2**: This is another rating question, similar to "Quality Rating", but with some minor differences. (Assuming it's meant to rate a different aspect of quality, like consistency or creativity.)
* **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the employee's performance.
