# Performance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Evaluation Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and performance information from employees. It is meant to be filled out by employees to provide valuable insights for their own performance evaluation and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly, providing accurate and detailed information about your performance and feedback.
2. Make sure to include your job title in the designated field.
3. Select the evaluation scale that best represents your performance.
4. Choose whether you want to receive evaluation and feedback on a regular basis, and if so, what method you would prefer for receiving it.
5. Provide any comments or additional feedback you want to share.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Optionally, include your phone number for further contact.
8. Choose a date and time for the evaluation.
9. Select a time for the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Performance Evaluation Survey** (`performance_evaluation_survey`, text, false): Please provide a brief description of the performance evaluation survey. This field is optional.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, false): Enter your current job title.
- **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, number, false): Choose a rating scale that best represents your performance.
- **Evaluations Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, false): Choose "Yes" if you would like to be evaluated and receive feedback on a regular basis.
- **Evaluations Method** (`evaluation_method`, select_multiple, false): Choose any methods you prefer for receiving evaluations and feedback, e.g., "Yes" for email, phone, or in-person.
- **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, text, false): Provide your desired frequency for feedback, e.g., weekly, monthly, or quarterly.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you would like to share.
- **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your work email for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Optionally, enter your work phone number for further contact.
- **Evaluations Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, false): Choose a specific date for your performance evaluation.
- **Evaluations Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, false): Select a time for your performance evaluation.

Tips:
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to review your performance evaluation carefully and ask for feedback from your supervisors or colleagues if needed.
* Use this form as a tool for growth and improvement in your performance and development.
