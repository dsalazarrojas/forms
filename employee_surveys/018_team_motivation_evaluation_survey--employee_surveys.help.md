# Team Motivation Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the motivation level of a team, gathering information about their morale, engagement, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select all that apply to the questions below.
2. For questions with multiple selection options, select one or more options that best describe your opinion.
3. Answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
4. For questions with a 'note' field, provide a brief explanation for your answer.
5. For questions with a 'date' field, select the date you want to evaluate the team motivation.
6. For questions with a 'number' field, enter a whole number.
7. Once you have completed the form, click on the 'Submit' button to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Motivation Evaluation** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_1`): 
  Please select all that apply from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the team's motivation level.
* **Team Motivation Evaluation** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_2`): 
  Please provide a brief description of your opinion about the team's motivation.
* **Motivation Level** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_3`): 
  Select one option that best describes the team's motivation level: Motivated, Neutral, or Not Motivated.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_4`): 
  Select the date you want to evaluate the team motivation.
* **Note** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_5`): 
  Provide a brief explanation for your answer.
* **Engagement Level** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_6`): 
  Select one option that best describes the team's engagement level: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Recommendation Count** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_7`): 
  Enter the number of employees who would recommend this team to a friend.
* **Recommended by Friends** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_8`): 
  Please provide a brief description of why employees would recommend this team to friends.
* **Recommended by Friends (Count)** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_9`): 
  Enter the number of employees who would recommend this team to a friend.
* **Additional Comments** (`team_motivation_evaluation_survey_form_10`): 
  Enter a whole number to provide more information about your opinion.

## Tips
* Please answer based on your honest opinion and provide concrete reasons for your answers.
* If you are unsure about a question, please ask for clarification or guidance from your supervisor or HR representative.
* All answers are kept confidential and used to aid in team development and improvement.
