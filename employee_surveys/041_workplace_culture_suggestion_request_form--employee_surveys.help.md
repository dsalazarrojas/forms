# Workplace Culture Suggestion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Culture Suggestion Request Form is designed to collect ideas and suggestions from employees to improve the workplace culture. This form helps gather feedback and ideas on how to create a more positive and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable options for "Employee Survey Form" on page 1.
2. Provide a detailed description of your suggestion in the "Suggestion" field on page 2.
3. Briefly describe the impact of your suggestion on "Impact" field on page 2.
4. Indicate if you require follow-up information on your suggestion by selecting "Yes" or "No" on the "Follow Up" field on page 3.
5. Select the priority level of your suggestion (High, Medium, Low) on the "Priority" field on page 3.
6. Choose the category of your suggestion (Workplace Culture, Employee Feedback) on the "Category" field on page 4.
7. Enter your name and email address in the "Employee Name" and "Email" fields on page 5.
8. Enter your department, phone number, and job title in the "Department", "Phone Number", and "Job Title" fields on page 5.
9. Enter your manager's name and email address in the "Manager Name" and "Manager Email" fields on page 5.
10. Enter the follow-up date (if applicable) on the "Follow-up Date" field on page 5.
11. Finally, provide the date you submitted the form on the "Submitted" field on page 6.
