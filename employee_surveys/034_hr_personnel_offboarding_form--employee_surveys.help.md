# HR Personnel Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Personnel Offboarding Form is designed to gather essential information about a departing employee, ensuring a seamless transition of the employee's responsibilities and company assets. The form helps to document the employee's departure, including their reasons for leaving, company property returns, and final checklist completion status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the employee's information: Enter the employee's name, department, and position in the respective fields.
2. Select the employee's termination date from the calendar.
3. Choose the employee's exit interview response: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the employee has completed an exit interview.
4. Specify the employee's reason for leaving: Enter a brief description of why the employee is leaving the company.
5. Report company property returns: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of company property the employee is returning, and provide additional details if required.
6. Report employee equipment returns: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of employee equipment being returned, and provide additional details if required.
7. Report employee vehicle returns: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of employee vehicle being returned, and provide additional details if required.
8. Report company assets returns: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each type of company asset being returned, and provide additional details if required.
9. Specify other returns: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for other returns, and provide additional details if required.
10. Complete the final checklist: Verify that all tasks in the checklist have been completed and provide any necessary comments.
11. Sign off on the form: Select 'Yes' to confirm that the form has been completed accurately and 'No' otherwise.
12. Enter the date the form was signed: Select the date the form was signed.
13. Add any final comments from the manager: Provide any additional comments or concerns about the offboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who is departing the company.
* **Department**: Enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **Position**: Enter the position the employee held.
* **Termination Date**: Select the date the employee's employment with the company ended.
* **Exit Interview**: Choose 'Yes' if the employee has completed an exit interview, 'No' otherwise.
* **Reason for Leaving**: Enter a brief description of why the employee is leaving the company.
* **Company Property Returns**: Select 'Yes' for each type of company property being returned, and provide additional details if required.
* **Company Property Return Details**: Enter any additional information about the company property returns.
* **Employee Equipment Returns**: Select 'Yes' for each type of employee equipment being returned, and provide additional details if required.
* **Employee Equipment Return Details**: Enter any additional information about the employee equipment returns.
* **Employee Vehicle Returns**: Select 'Yes' for each type of employee vehicle being returned, and provide additional details if required.
* **Employee Vehicle Return Details**: Enter any additional information about the employee vehicle returns.
* **Company Assets Returns**: Select 'Yes' for each type of company asset being returned, and provide additional details if required.
* **Company Assets Return Details**: Enter any additional information about the company assets returns.
* **Company Assets Remarks**: Enter any additional remarks about the company assets returns.
* **Other Returns**: Select 'Yes' for other returns, and provide additional details if required.
* **Other Returns Details**: Enter any additional information about other returns.
* **Final Checklist**: Verify that all tasks in the checklist have been completed and provide any necessary comments.
* **Date Offboarded**: Select the date the employee's offboarding process was completed.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or concerns about the offboarding process.
* **Signed**: Choose 'Yes' to confirm the form has been completed accurately and 'No' otherwise.
* **Signed Date**: Select the date the form was signed.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any additional comments or concerns from the manager about the offboarding process.
