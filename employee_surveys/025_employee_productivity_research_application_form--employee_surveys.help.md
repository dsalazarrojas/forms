# Employee Productivity Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees interested in participating in productivity research studies. It captures details such as contact information, job title, availability, and other relevant fields to help research teams identify potential participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your name, email, phone number, and job title.
2. Indicate your availability for participation in research studies.
3. Check the "Consent" box to confirm your willingness to participate.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes about your interest in participating in productivity research studies.
5. Enter the Researcher's details, including their name, email, phone number, and availability.
6. Enter the Manager's details, including their name, email, phone number, and availability.
7. Check the "Manager's Consent" box to confirm their willingness to participate.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself as a potential participant.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number for contact details.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title to help research teams understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Availability**: Check "Yes" if you are available to participate in research studies, or "No" if you are not.
* **Consent**: Check the box to confirm your willingness to participate in productivity research studies.
* **Form Description**: This field is not editable, it provides a description of the form's purpose.
* **Form ID**: This field is not editable, it is a unique identifier for the form.
* **Researcher's Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the research study.
* **Researcher's Email**: Enter the email address of the researcher.
* **Researcher's Phone**: Enter the phone number of the researcher.
* **Researcher's Availability**: Check "Yes" if the researcher is available to conduct the study, or "No" if they are not.
* **Researcher's Consent**: Check the box to confirm the researcher's willingness to participate in the study.
* **Manager's Name**: Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Manager's Email**: Enter the email address of your manager or supervisor.
* **Manager's Phone**: Enter the phone number of your manager or supervisor.
* **Manager's Availability**: Check "Yes" if your manager or supervisor is available to participate, or "No" if they are not.
* **Manager's Consent**: Check the box to confirm your manager or supervisor's willingness to participate.
* **Volunteer Status**: Select "Active" if you are an active participant, or "Inactive" if you are not.
* **Status**: Select "Active" if you are an active participant, or "Inactive" if you are not.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your application.
