# Retail Employee Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Employee Feedback Survey is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about your experiences as a retail employee. Your feedback will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve, enabling us to create a better workplace for you and our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer each question carefully, and provide specific examples or details when possible.
3. If a question allows multiple responses, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Store Location**: Type the name of the store where you work.
* **Role**: Select your job title from the list provided (e.g., Store Manager, Floor Sales Associate, etc.).
* **How Do You Rate Your Store**: Rate your overall experience with the store, selecting from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What Did We Do Well**: Provide a brief description of a specific situation or event where we exceeded your expectations or did something well.
* **What Could We Improve**: Share your thoughts on how we could improve or do better in the future.
* **Employee Comments**: Write any additional comments or suggestions you have, such as ideas for improving processes or policies.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes (if you'd like to receive follow-up questions or updates).
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number for contact purposes (if you'd like to receive follow-up questions or updates).

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering questions.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask a supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Your feedback is confidential, and your responses will not be shared without your consent.
* If you have already completed this form, please do not submit duplicate feedback; only new and relevant feedback will be considered.
