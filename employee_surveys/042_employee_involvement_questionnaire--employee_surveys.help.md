# Employee Involvement Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This employee involvement questionnaire is designed to gather valuable insights on employee satisfaction, job involvement, and areas for improvement within the workplace. The collected data will help managers and HR teams make informed decisions to enhance employee experience and overall workplace performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Read each question carefully before proceeding to ensure you understand the context.
* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your abilities.
* You can complete the form in multiple sessions, and your responses will be saved.
* Please do not share your responses with anyone other than your manager or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to explain the purpose and context of this form.
* **Employee Demographics**: Enter a short description of your job title.
* **Department**: Enter a short description of your department or the team you belong to.
* **Employee Satisfaction (Scale 1-5)**: Rate your satisfaction with your current job on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager.
* **Team Satisfaction (Scale 1-5)**: Rate your satisfaction with your team on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Employee Involvement**: Briefly describe how often you feel involved and engaged in your work.
* **Manager Satisfaction (Scale 1-5)**: Rate your satisfaction with your manager on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Feedback**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your job, team, or manager.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by employees, and the data collected will be used to improve the work environment and employee experience.
