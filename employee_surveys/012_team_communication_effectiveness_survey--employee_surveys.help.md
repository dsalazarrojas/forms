# Team Communication Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure team communication effectiveness, gathering feedback frequencies, types, quality, and satisfaction with team leader's communication style.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your current experience and interaction with your team members.
2. Choose the best fit for each question from the provided options.
3. Ensure you answer all required questions as they have an impact on the overall assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **How frequently do you receive feedback from your team members?** (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Rarely`, `Never`)
  Select the frequency with which you usually receive feedback from your team members.
* **What type of feedback do you usually receive from your team members?** (`Positive`, `Negative`, `Constructive`, `None`)
  Indicate the type of feedback you generally receive from your team members.
* **How often do you give feedback to your team members?** (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Rarely`, `Never`)
  Choose the frequency of feedback you provide to your team members.

### Page 2
* **How would you rate the quality of feedback you receive from your team members?** (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`)
  Evaluate the quality of feedback you usually receive from your team members.
* **How would you rate the quality of feedback you give to your team members?** (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`)
  Indicate the quality of feedback you provide to your team members.

### Page 3
* **How satisfied are you with the communication style of your team leader?** (`Very Satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Dissatisfied`, `Very Dissatisfied`)
  Choose how satisfied you are with your team leader's communication style.
