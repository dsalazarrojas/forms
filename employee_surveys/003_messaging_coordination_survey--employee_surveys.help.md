# Messaging Coordination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Messaging Coordination Survey is used to gather feedback from employees on their experience with team messaging tools. This information will help the team better understand how to improve the messaging experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as honestly as you can. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Messaging Tools** (Team Messaging Tools, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to describe the team messaging tools you use in your work.
* **Challenges** (Challenges, text, optional):
	+ Describe any challenges you have experienced with the team messaging tools.
* **Improvements** (Improvements, text, optional):
	+ Suggest any improvements you would like to see in the team messaging tools.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate if you have been assigned a tool for team messaging.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you have been assigned a tool, 'No' if you haven't.
* **Form ID** (Form ID, number, optional):
	+ Please do not fill out this field as it is not relevant to your experience with team messaging tools.
