# Workforce Performance Consulting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees about their contact details, job titles, departments, performance levels, and feedback. This data will help organizations to better understand their workforce performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your contact information, including your job title and department.
2. Select your performance level (Excellent, Good, or Poor) to indicate how you feel about your current performance.
3. Provide any feedback you have about your job or organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (Contact Information, text, required)**: This field is used to collect your contact details, including your job title and department. Please enter your name as it appears on your business card or email signature.
### Job Title
* **Job Title (Job Title, text, required)**: Enter your official job title as it appears on your business card, email signature, or in your company's directory.
### Department
* **Department (Department, text, required)**: Type in the name of the department you belong to, as it appears in your company's organization chart.
### Performance Level
* **Performance Level (Performance Level, select_one, required)**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent: You're performing exceptionally well and exceeding expectations.
	+ Good: You're meeting expectations and performing at a satisfactory level.
	+ Poor: You're not meeting expectations and are in need of improvement.
### Feedback
* **Feedback (Feedback, text, required)**: Share any thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you have about your job or organization. Be as specific as possible to help us better understand your perspective.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in each field.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask your HR representative or manager for guidance.
* Your feedback is confidential and will only be used to improve workforce performance.
