# Provider Breaks Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to provide feedback on the break schedule of your provider. It will help them understand how often you take breaks and how long these breaks are, in order to make adjustments if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To provide feedback on your provider's break schedule, fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Please answer all the questions truthfully, and do not leave any sections blank unless they are clearly indicated as optional.
3. If you have any comments about your experience, please use the "Comments" field to provide additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **provider_id** (`provider_id`, `number`, required): This is the ID number of your provider. Please enter the number exactly as it appears on your ID badge or in your personnel file.
* **Schedule Frequency** (`schedule_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select how often you take scheduled breaks from the options provided: Every 4 hours, Every 5 hours, or Every 6 hours.
* **Break Duration** (`schedule_duration`, `number`, optional): If you selected a schedule frequency, enter the length of your breaks in hours.
* **Break Frequency** (`break_frequency`, `number`, optional): If you take breaks more frequently than the scheduled break frequency, indicate how often you take breaks.
* **Break Experience** (`break_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your experience with our break schedule. Choose from: Very Good, Good, Neutral, Poor, Terrible.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you if we need additional information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if we need additional information.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `date`, required): The date and time you are submitting this form.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, optional): If you are not the person who takes breaks, please enter their name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, optional): If you are not the person who takes breaks, please enter their email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, optional): If you are not the person who takes breaks, please enter their phone number.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, it's best to ask your provider for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, contact your HR representative.
