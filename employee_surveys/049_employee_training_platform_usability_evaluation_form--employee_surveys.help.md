# employee_training_platform_usability_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the usability and effectiveness of the employee training platform. It gathers feedback on various aspects of the platform, such as training content, navigation, and overall satisfaction. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and provide a better experience for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. For each question, select the most appropriate response from the provided options.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Page 1, id: 1): A brief introduction to the form.
* **Training goals** (Page 2, id: 2): Select one or more goals that you would like the training platform to achieve, such as "Improve training content" or "Simplify training process".
* **Training content** (Page 3, id: 3): Choose the types of content that you would like to see on the platform, such as "Presentation" or "Interactive".
* **Feedback frequency** (Page 4, id: 4): Indicate how often you would like to receive feedback, such as "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".
* **Training platform features** (Page 5, id: 5): Select the features that you feel are most important for the platform, such as "Clear navigation" or "Easy search".
* **Training format** (Page 6, id: 6): Choose the format that you prefer, such as "Online" or "Offline".
* **Training satisfaction** (Page 7, id: 7): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training platform, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Training recommendation** (Page 8, id: 8): Indicate whether you would recommend the platform to others, such as "Recommend", "Somewhat Recommend", or "Not Recommend".
* **Employee training survey** (Page 9, id: 9): Rate how much you agree with the statement "I would recommend this platform to other employees", such as "Recommend", "Somewhat Recommend", or "Not Recommend".
* **Comments** (Page 10, id: 10): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the platform.
* **Employee satisfaction** (Page 11, id: 11): Rate your overall satisfaction with your role or position, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Platform usability** (Page 12, id: 12): Indicate how easy or difficult it is to use the platform, such as "Easy", "Difficult", or "Very difficult".
* **Overall satisfaction** (Page 13, id: 13): Rate your overall satisfaction with the platform, such as "Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", or "Not satisfied".
* **Employee training platform usability evaluation form comments** (Page 14, id: 14): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the platform.
* **Assigned tool** (Page 15, id: 15): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you, if any.
* **Created by** (Page 16, id: 16): Enter the name of the person who created the form, if any.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
* Do not repeat information already provided in other answers.
* If you have any further questions or need help, feel free to ask the form creator or administrator.
