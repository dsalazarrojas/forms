# Benefit Change Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and structured way for employees to communicate changes to their benefits. It helps ensure that employees are informed and understand the impact of these changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee information:
   - **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge.
   - **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
   - **Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
   - **Department**: Enter your department name.
2. Provide information about the benefit change:
   - **Effective Date of Change**: Enter the date when the change takes effect.
   - **Type of Benefit Being Changed**: Select one of the options (Health Insurance, Dental Insurance, Vision Insurance, Life Insurance, Retirement Plan, Flexible Spending, or Other).
   - **Description of Change**: Briefly describe the change and its implications.
   - **Impact on Employees**: Explain how this change affects you.
3. Confirm your understanding:
   - **I Understand the Change**: Select one of the options (Fully Understand, Mostly Understand, Partially Understand, or Do Not Understand).
   - **I Understand How This Affects My Benefits**: Select one of the options (Fully Understand, Mostly Understand, Partially Understand, or Do Not Understand).
   - **I Understand the Timeline**: Select one of the options (True, Mostly, Somewhat, or False).
4. Provide feedback:
   - **I Have Concerns or Questions**: Select "True" or "False".
   - **Describe Your Concerns or Questions**: If you have concerns or questions, describe them briefly.
   - **Type of Support Needed**: Choose the support method you need (Additional Information, One-on-One Meeting, Group Meeting, Written Materials, Online Resources, or Other).
   - **Preferred Communication Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, In-Person, Video Call, or Written Materials).
   - **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional comments about the change.
