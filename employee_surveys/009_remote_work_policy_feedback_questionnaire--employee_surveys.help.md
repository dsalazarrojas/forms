# Remote Work Policy Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees about their remote work experience last week. The goal is to gather information on various aspects of remote work, including work hours, communication, challenges, and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question in the form as honestly and accurately as possible.
2. For questions that have specific options, select the one that best reflects your experience.
3. If you have any other comments or suggestions, please use the "Do you have any other comments or suggestions" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Remote_work_policies**: Please describe your remote work experience last week, including any challenges you faced or successes you achieved.
* **How_many_days_did_you_spend_working_remotely_last_week**: Enter the number of days you spent working remotely last week.
* **What_was_the_most_difficult_part_of_your_remote_work_experience_last_week**: Describe the most difficult part of your remote work experience last week.
* **How_often_do_you_work_from_home**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Home office: You worked from home most of the time.
	+ Office: You worked from the office most of the time.
	+ Flexible schedule: You worked on a flexible schedule, not fixed hours.
* **What_time_of_day_did_you_work_mostly_last_week**: Enter the time of day you worked most of the time last week.
* **What_are_your_major_tasks_or_projects_last_week**: Describe the major tasks or projects you worked on last week.
* **How_many_hours_did_you_work_last_week**: Enter the number of hours you worked last week.
* **Did_you_experience_any_issues_with_your_remote_workset_up_last_week**: Select one of the following options:
	+ True: You experienced issues with your remote work setup last week.
	+ False: You did not experience issues with your remote work setup last week.
	+ Not applicable: This question does not apply to you.
* **How_much_time_did_you_spent_last_week_communicating_with_your_team**: Enter the number of hours you spent communicating with your team last week.
* **How_much_time_did_you_spent_last_week_communicating_with_your_manager**: Enter the number of hours you spent communicating with your manager last week.
* **How_much_time_did_you_spent_last_week_on_meetings**: Enter the number of hours you spent on meetings last week.
* **How_much_time_did_you_spent_last_week_on_email**: Enter the number of hours you spent on email last week.
* **How_much_time_did_you_spent_last_week_on_phone_calls**: Enter the number of hours you spent on phone calls last week.
* **How_much_time_did_you_spent_last_week_on_video_calls**: Enter the number of hours you spent on video calls last week.
* **How_much_time_did_you_spent_last_week_on_text_messages**: Enter the number of hours you spent on text messages last week.
* **What_was_your_favorite_part_of_your_remote_work_experience_last_week**: Describe your favorite part of your remote work experience last week.
* **What_was_the_most_difficult_part_of_your_remote_work_experience_last_week**: Describe the most difficult part of your remote work experience last week.
* **Can_you_recommend_any_improvements_to_the_remote_work_policy_last_week**: Provide any suggestions for improving the remote work policy.
* **How_would_you_rate_your_remote_work_experience_last_week**: Rate your remote work experience last week from 1-5, where 5 is the highest.
* **Can_you_enumerate_the_advantages_and_disadvantages_of_remote_work**: Describe the advantages and disadvantages of remote work.
* **What_was_the_most_useful_tool_you_used_last_week**: Describe the most useful tool you used last week.
* **What_was_the_most_time_wasting_activity_last_week**: Describe the most time-wasting activity last week.
* **How_did_you_rate_your_satisfaction_with_your_work_last_week**: Rate your satisfaction with your work last week from 1-5, where 5 is the highest.
* **Can_you_recommend_any_improvements_to_your_manager_or_the_remote_work_process**: Provide any suggestions for improving the remote work process or your manager's performance.
* **Do_you_have_any_other_comments_or_suggestions**: Provide any other comments or suggestions you have.
