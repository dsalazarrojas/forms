# Transport Driver Employment Satisfaction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps transport drivers provide feedback on their employment satisfaction, touching on various aspects of their job and work experience within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by selecting your answers from the provided options for each question.
- Ensure each field is completed accurately based on your current situation as a transport driver.
- You are encouraged to share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions in the 'comments' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the company** (`transport_driver_employment_satisfaction_form`, select_multiple): Please select all that apply to indicate your level of satisfaction with the company.
* **What is your current work environment like** (`work_environment`, select_multiple): Choose one or more options to describe your current work environment.
* **What do you think about our company culture?** (`company_culture`, select_one): Select one option that best describes your perception of the company culture.
* **Do you feel you have a good work-life balance?** (`work_life_balance`, select_multiple): Select all that apply to indicate your level of satisfaction with work-life balance.
* **How would you rate your relationships with your manager(s)?** (`manager_relationships`, select_one): Choose one option to rate your relationship with your manager(s).
* **How secure do you feel about your job?** (`job_security`, select_multiple): Select one or more options to indicate your level of security regarding your job.
* **Do you have opportunities for personal and professional development?** (`opportunities_for_development`, select_one): Select one option that best describes your opportunities for growth and development.
* **Do you feel supported in your role as a transport driver?** (`transport_driver_support`, select_multiple): Choose one or more options to describe your level of support in your role.
* **How do you feel about our benefits?** (`benefits`, select_one): Select one option that best describes your satisfaction with company benefits.
* **Do you feel satisfied with your job?** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one): Choose one option that describes your level of satisfaction with your job.
* **Will you continue to work here?** (`retention`, select_one): Select one option that indicates your intention to continue with the company.
* **Do you have any comments or feedback?** (`comments`, note): Share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about your employment experience.
