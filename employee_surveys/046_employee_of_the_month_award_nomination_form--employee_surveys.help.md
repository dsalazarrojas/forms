# Employee Of The Month Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit nominations for employees who have demonstrated exceptional performance, skills, or contributions to the organization. The information collected will help the management team select the most deserving employee for the award.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page of the form.
2. Enter the nominator's name, department, and job title to identify who is nominating the employee.
3. Provide a brief reason for the nomination in the "reason_for_nomination" field.
4. Fill out the employee's details by entering their name, department, and job title.
5. Select the date of the nomination.
6. Move on to the second page of the form.
7. Review and submit the form to submit your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_nominator_name**: Enter the name of the person nominating the employee.
* **employee_nominator_department**: Enter the department of the person nominating the employee.
* **employee_nominator_title**: Enter the job title of the person nominating the employee.
* **reason_for_nomination**: Provide a brief explanation for why you are nominating this employee. This can be a specific achievement, a consistent good work ethic, or exceptional team collaboration.
* **employee_nominated_name**: Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
* **employee_nominated_department**: Enter the department of the employee being nominated.
* **employee_nominated_title**: Enter the job title of the employee being nominated.
* **employee_nominated_job_title**: Enter the job title of the employee being nominated.
* **employee_nominator_date_of_nomination**: Choose the date when you first became aware of the employee's exceptional work or performance.
* **first_page_2**: This page is for further comments or additional details about the employee. You can add any other relevant information or comments here.

## Tips
* Be specific when providing reasons for nomination to help the management team make informed decisions.
* Use proper spelling and grammar when filling out the form.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
