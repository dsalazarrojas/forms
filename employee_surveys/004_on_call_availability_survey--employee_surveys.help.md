# On Call Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an employee's on-call availability to facilitate smooth and efficient scheduling processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the field-by-field explanation section below.
2. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Select your contact details (email, phone, or both) in the "Contact Details" field.
4. Choose your on-call schedule (daily, weekly, or monthly) in the "Schedule" field.
5. Select your preferred contact methods (phone, email, or both) in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
6. Enter your work hours and on-call duration (if applicable) in the respective fields.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
8. Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employment records.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select multiple, required): Select one or both of your contact methods (email, phone, or text) to ensure that we can reach you during on-call hours.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, select one, required): Choose your on-call schedule, whether daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select multiple, required): Select your preferred contact methods (phone, email, or text) for on-call notifications.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, optional): Enter your work hours, including start and end times.
* **On Call Duration** (`on_call_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of your on-call shifts, if applicable.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your on-call availability.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
