# Timekeeping Compliance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Timekeeping Compliance Survey is designed to assess employee knowledge and practices regarding company timekeeping policies, including training needs, processes, and feedback. This survey aims to gather information that can be used to identify areas for improvement and ensure compliance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Select the options that best fit your response to each question.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Timekeeping Policies (page_one)**: Please provide a general comment on your understanding of company timekeeping policies. This could include any questions or concerns you may have about these policies.
* **Training Needs (page_two)**: Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes: You have received training on company timekeeping policies.
	+ No: You haven't received training, or you'd like to receive training.
* **Processes (page_three)**: Select the option that best describes your current understanding of company timekeeping processes:
	+ Yes: You're familiar with the processes.
	+ No: You're not familiar, or you're unsure about certain processes.
* **Feedback (page_four)**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about company timekeeping policies or processes.
* **Comments (page_five)**: This is an additional comment section for any other thoughts or questions you'd like to share about timekeeping.
