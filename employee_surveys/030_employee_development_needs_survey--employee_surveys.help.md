# Employee Development Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Development Needs Survey is a form designed to help HR or management understand employee needs and preferences for growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with as much detail as possible. 
- For select options, choose one or more options that apply to you. 
- For open-ended fields, provide as much information as you like. 
- Please answer honestly and concisely to help the organization improve employee development efforts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Development Needs** (`employee_development_needs_survey_form`): This field is for employees to describe their general development needs. 
  - Enter your thoughts in this free-text field.
  - Be as descriptive as possible, but keep it to a reasonable length.
- **Category** (`category`): This field allows you to select a category related to your development needs.
  - Choose one of the four categories provided: Development Areas, Training Preferences, Training Initiatives, or Other.
  - If you select "Other," please provide a specific reason why in the free-text field.
- **Training Areas** (`training_areas`): This field allows you to select multiple training areas you are interested in.
  - Check all that apply to you.
  - Choose from the options: Communication, Leadership, Project Management, Time Management, Problem Solving, or Other.
  - If you select "Other," please provide a specific reason why in the free-text field.
- **Training Preferences** (`training_preferences`): This field allows you to select multiple training preferences you have.
  - Check all that apply to you.
  - Choose from the options: Online Course, In-person training, Online training, or Other.
  - If you select "Other," please provide a specific reason why in the free-text field.
- **Development Areas** (`development_areas`): This field allows you to select multiple development areas you are interested in.
  - Check all that apply to you.
  - Choose from the options: Soft Skills, Hard Skills, Leadership, or Other.
  - If you select "Other," please provide a specific reason why in the free-text field.
- **Feedback 1** (`feedback_1`): This field is for providing feedback on how to improve employee development efforts.
  - Enter your thoughts on how the organization can better support your growth.
