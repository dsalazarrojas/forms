# Team Collaboration Insights Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about team collaboration insights from team members. It aims to provide insights on how teams collaborate and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the **Team Size** field to indicate the number of people in your team.
2. Enter the **Team Name** field with your team's name.
3. Enter the **Team Lead** field with your team lead's email address.
4. Select the **Team Location** field to indicate where your team is located (Office, Remote, or Hybrid).
5. Select the **Team Collaboration Frequency** field to indicate how often your team collaborates (High, Medium, or Low).
6. In the **Collaboration Experience** field, briefly describe your experience with collaboration.
7. In the **Collaboration Opportunities** field, describe any opportunities for improvement or challenges you've faced in your team's collaboration.
8. Select the **Communication Channels** field to indicate how your team communicates (Email, Chat, Video calls, or Meetings).
9. Select the **Communication Frequencies** field to indicate how frequently your team communicates (High, Medium, or Low).
10. Enter the **Work Hours** field with the hours you work each day.
11. Enter the **Reported Time** field with the time you spend working.
12. Select the **Collaboration Challenges** field to indicate any challenges you've faced in your team's collaboration (Communication, Time Zone Differences, Remote Work, or Team Size).
13. Select the **Team Size Impact** field to indicate the positive or negative impact of your team size on collaboration (Positive, Neutral, or Negative).
14. Select the **Team Lead Impact** field to indicate the positive or negative impact of your team lead on collaboration (Positive, Neutral, or Negative).
15. Select the **Location Impact** field to indicate the positive or negative impact of your team's location on collaboration (Positive, Neutral, or Negative).
16. Select the **Team Collaboration Frequency Impact** field to indicate the positive or negative impact of your team's collaboration frequency on collaboration (Positive, Neutral, or Negative).
17. Select the **Reported Time Impact** field to indicate the positive or negative impact of the reported time on collaboration (Positive, Neutral, or Negative).
