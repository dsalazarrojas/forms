# Top Performer Of The Month Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Top Performer of the Month Nomination Form is a recognition tool used to acknowledge and appreciate outstanding employees who have demonstrated exceptional performance, skills, or behaviors that have positively impacted the team or organization. This form is used to formally nominate an employee for their outstanding work and contributions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the employee you wish to nominate.
2. Select the date of the nomination.
3. Explain why the employee is being nominated, highlighting their outstanding performance, skills, or behaviors.
4. Select the team members involved in the project or work.
5. Enter the name of the person nominating the employee.
6. Enter the title of the person nominating the employee.
7. Enter the email of the person nominating the employee.
8. Enter the phone number of the person nominating the employee (optional).
9. Enter the department of the person nominating the employee.
10. Enter the location of the person nominating the employee.
11. Select the level of team leadership involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being nominated. This is a required field.
* **Nomination Date**: Select the date of the nomination. This is a required field.
* **Reason for Nomination**: Explain why the employee is being nominated. This is a required field.
* **Team Members**: Select team members involved in the project or work. This field is optional.
* **Team Members** is a select_multiple field, so you can select multiple team members.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the employee's supervisor. This field is optional.
* **Nominator Name**: Enter the name of the person nominating the employee. This is a required field.
* **Nominator Title**: Enter the title of the person nominating the employee. This field is optional.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter the email of the person nominating the employee. This is a required field.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person nominating the employee. This field is optional.
* **Nominator Department**: Enter the department of the person nominating the employee. This field is optional.
* **Nominator Location**: Enter the location of the person nominating the employee. This field is optional.
* **Team Leadership**: Select the level of team leadership involved. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide a clear and concise reason for the nomination.
* Ensure that the nomination date is accurate and relevant.
* Select relevant team members involved in the project or work.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information for the nominator's contact details.
