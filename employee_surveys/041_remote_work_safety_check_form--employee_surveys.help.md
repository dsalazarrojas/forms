# Remote Work Safety Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that employees working remotely are meeting the necessary safety and emergency protocols for their home or remote workstations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that accurately describe your home or remote work environment.
2. Enter the information carefully and accurately.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Home Office Details** (1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your home or remote work environment.
- **Fire Suppression** (2, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all fire suppression systems present in your home or remote work area, such as fire alarms, fire extinguishers, or fire sprinklers.
- **Electrical Outlets** (3, number, required/optional): Indicate the number of electrical outlets available for use.
- **Emergency Exit Paths** (4, select_one, required/optional): Choose the primary emergency exit path available in your home or remote work area.
- **First Aid Kit** (5, number, required/optional): Indicate the number of first aid kits available.
- **Emergency Contacts** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the emergency contacts you have available, such as your emergency contact person or phone number.
- **Fire Extinguisher** (7, number, required/optional): Indicate the number of fire extinguishers available.
- **Emergency Equipment** (8, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the emergency equipment available in your home or remote work area, such as fire blankets or a first aid kit.
- **Medical Equipment** (9, number, required/optional): Indicate the number of medical equipment available, such as medical gloves or bandages.
- **Remote Workstation** (10, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the safety measures implemented in your remote workstation, such as fire alarms or emergency exit signs.
- **Safety Measures** (11, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the additional safety measures implemented in your home or remote work area, such as a fire drill or a safety training program.
- **Emergency Phone** (12, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the emergency phones available, such as a phone with a 911 feature or a cell phone with emergency services.
- **Emergency Phone Number** (13, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency phone number available.
- **Emergency Contact** (14, select_one, required/optional): Choose the emergency contact person or phone number.
- **First Aid Kit Location** (15, number, required/optional): Indicate the location of the first aid kits.
- **Emergency Exit Path Location** (16, number, required/optional): Indicate the location of the emergency exit path.
- **Medical Equipment Location** (17, number, required/optional): Indicate the location of the medical equipment.
- **Emergency Equipment Location** (18, number, required/optional): Indicate the location of the emergency equipment.
- **Workstation Condition** (19, select_one, required/optional): Choose the condition of your remote workstation, such as clean and secure or cluttered and hazardous.
- **Emergency Exit Path Condition** (20, select_one, required/optional): Choose the condition of your emergency exit path, such as clear and secure or obstructed.
