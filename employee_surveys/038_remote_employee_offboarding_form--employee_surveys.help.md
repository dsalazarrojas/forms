# remote_employee_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The remote_employee_offboarding_form is a tool used to track the offboarding status of remote employees and gather feedback on the offboarding process. This helps the organization to improve and refine the process for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly about your offboarding status.
2. Rate your experience with the offboarding process.
3. Provide any comments or feedback you may have about the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Offboarding Status** (`offboarding_status`, select_one, required: false)
Has the employee submitted their offboarding status yet? Please select one of the options:

* Complete
* Partially Complete
* Not Started

### **Feedback on Offboarding Process** (`feedback_on_offboarding_process`, select_one, required: false)
How would you rate the offboarding process?

* Very Satisfied
* Somewhat Satisfied
* Somewhat Dissatisfied
* Very Dissatisfied
* Neutral

### **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false)
Are there any comments or feedback on the offboarding process? Please share your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.

**Tips**

* Be honest and provide feedback that can help us improve the offboarding process.
* If you have any specific suggestions or ideas, please share them in the comments field.
* If you're unsure about any part of the process, feel free to ask for clarification.
