# Employee Clothing Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees about their experiences and suggestions for improvement regarding the organization's clothing policy.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is designed to be completed by employees, and it should take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete. Please follow these steps:
1. Select the options that best describe your experience with the organization's clothing policy and workplace inclusivity.
2. Choose the options that apply to your suggestions for improvement.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for future contact and communication.
4. Enter the name of your organization and any additional notes you have regarding the clothing policy.
5. Select one option from the list to indicate your overall satisfaction with your workplace.
6. Select one or more options from the list to indicate your suggestions for improvement.
7. Select one option from the list to indicate your experience with the employee survey.
8. Select one or more options from the list to indicate your suggestions for improvement.
9. Enter the time of your last feedback regarding the employee clothing policy.
10. Enter any suggestions for improvement you have regarding the organization.
11. Enter the date of your last input regarding the organization.
12. Enter the time of your last feedback regarding the organization.
13. Enter any suggestions for improvement you have regarding the policy.
14. Enter the date of your last input regarding the policy.
15. Enter the time of your last feedback regarding the policy.
16. Select one option from the list to indicate your overall satisfaction with the employee clothing policy.
17. Enter your thoughts on what contributes to your satisfaction.
18. Select one option from the list to indicate your overall satisfaction with the organization.
19. Enter your thoughts on what contributes to your satisfaction.
20. This form should take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact [your contact information].
