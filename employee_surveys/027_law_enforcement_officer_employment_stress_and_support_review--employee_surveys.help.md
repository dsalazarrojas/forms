# Law Enforcement Officer Employment Stress And Support Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the employment stressors and support resources available to law enforcement officers, providing an opportunity for supervisors or HR personnel to identify areas of concern and offer guidance on available resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable stressors from the "Officer Employment Stressors" field on page 1.
2. In the "Support Resources" field, choose one of the available options that best describes the support services the officer receives.
3. In the "Comments" field on page 1, provide any additional information or context for the officer's stressors and support resources.
4. Enter the date and time of the review in the "Date" and "Time" fields on page 2.
5. If applicable, enter the officer's email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields on page 2.
6. In the "Support Services" field, select all applicable options that describe the support services the officer receives. This may include Employee Assistance Program (EAP) services, such as in-person, phone, or video conference support.
7. If other support services are available, enter details in the "Support Services Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Officer Employment Stressors**: This field lists the different types of stressors that may affect law enforcement officers, such as Work-Related Stressors, Home and Family Stressors, and Financial Stressors. Select all that apply to help identify the officer's concerns.
* **Support Resources**: Choose one of the available options that best describes the support services the officer receives, such as Mental health resources, Employee Assistance Program (EAP), or other.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional information or context for the officer's stressors and support resources.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the review in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the review in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email**: Enter the officer's email address if available.
* **Phone**: Enter the officer's phone number if available.
* **Support Services**: This field lists the different types of support services available to law enforcement officers, such as Employee Assistance Program (EAP) services. Select all that apply to help identify the officer's support services.
* **Support Services Phone**: Enter the phone number of the support services if applicable.
* **Support Services Video**: Enter the video conference link or number of the support services if applicable.
* **Support Services Email**: Enter the email address of the support services if applicable.
* **Support Services In Person**: Enter the location or details of the support services if applicable.
* **Support Services Other**: Enter any other details about the support services if applicable.
* **Other**: Enter any other stressors or support resources not listed in the above fields.
* **Other Details**: Enter any additional details about the other stressors or support resources.
