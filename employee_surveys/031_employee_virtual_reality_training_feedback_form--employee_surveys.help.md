# employee_vr_training_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on your Virtual Reality (VR) training experience to help us improve our training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the training session you attended.
2. Choose the modules you took part in.
3. Provide any comments or notes on your experience.
4. Identify any technical issues you encountered.
5. Suggest improvements to the training program.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Select your department.
8. Provide the date and time of the training session (if applicable).
9. Evaluate each module and select the options that best describe your experience.
10. Rate your overall experience and suggest areas for improvement.
11. Provide any additional feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session**: Select the training session you attended. This will help us track which session you are providing feedback on.
* **Modules**: Choose the modules you took part in during the training session. This will help us understand which parts of the training were most effective for you.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or notes on your experience. This is your opportunity to share any thoughts or observations that didn't fit in the other fields.
* **Technical Issues**: Identify any technical issues you encountered during the training session. This will help us troubleshoot any technical problems that may have affected your experience.
* **Suggestions for Improvements**: Suggest improvements to the training program. What would you change or add to make the training more effective?
* **Email**: Enter your email address. We may contact you to follow up on your feedback or to discuss any questions we have.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you if we need to discuss your feedback.
* **Department**: Select your department. This will help us understand how your department will be impacted by the training.
* **Date**: Provide the date of the training session. If you are unable to remember the exact date, provide an approximate date.
* **Time**: Provide the time of the training session. If you are unable to remember the exact time, provide an approximate time.
* **Module Evaluation**: Evaluate each module by selecting the options that best describe your experience. This will help us understand what worked well and what didn't.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the training program. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Improvement Areas**: Identify areas where you think the training program could be improved. Select the options that apply.
* **Suggestions for Improvements**: Provide any additional suggestions for improving the training program.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5. This will help us understand the level of satisfaction with the training program.
* **Rating Scale**: Select the rating scale that best describes your experience.
* **Feedback Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments on your experience.
