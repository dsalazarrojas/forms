# Annual Compensation Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Annual Compensation Review Form is a crucial document for evaluating an employee's current compensation and making informed decisions about their future growth and development. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you have the necessary information and authorization to complete this form.
2.  Follow the instructions for each field and enter the relevant data accurately.
3.  Complete all required fields to ensure a comprehensive review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): This is the employee's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): A unique identifier assigned to the employee for internal use.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This is the employee's official work email.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): The employee's department within the organization.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): The employee's current job title.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): The direct supervisor of the employee.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): The date the employee was hired.
* **Review Period** (`review_period`, select_one, required): Select the current review period (e.g., Annual, Mid-Year).
* **Current Annual Salary** (`current_salary`, number, required): The employee's current annual salary.
* **Current Hourly Rate** (`current_hourly_rate`, number, optional): If the employee is paid hourly, enter their current rate.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Select the employee's employment type (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Temporary).
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, required): Select the most recent performance rating (Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, etc.).
* **Last Raise Date** (`last_raise_date`, date, optional): The date of the last salary increase.
* **Last Raise Percentage** (`last_raise_percentage`, number, optional): The percentage of the last salary increase.
* **Recommended Increase** (`recommended_increase`, number, optional): A suggested percentage increase.
* **Recommended New Salary** (`recommended_salary`, number, optional): A suggested new annual salary.
* **Increase Justification** (`increase_justification`, text, optional): A reason for the recommended salary increase.
* **Bonus Eligible** (`bonus_eligible`, select_one, required): Select whether the employee is bonus eligible (True, False).
* **Recommended Bonus** (`recommended_bonus`, number, optional): A suggested bonus amount.
* **Bonus Type** (`bonus_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of bonus (Performance, Signing, etc.).
* **Equity Compensation** (`equity_compensation`, select_one, optional): Select whether the employee has equity compensation (True, False, Considering).
* **Benefits Changes** (`benefits_changes`, text, optional): Any changes to benefits.
* **Market Comparison** (`market_comparison`, select_one, optional): How does the employee's compensation compare to market (Above Market, At Market, Below Market, Not Analyzed).
* **Retention Risk** (`retention_risk`, select_one, optional): Select the retention risk level (Low, Medium, High, Not Applicable).
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): The current approval status (Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Rejected).
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, optional): The name of the approver.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): The date when changes will take effect.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other relevant information.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
* Enter accurate and complete data for each field.
* Follow the instructions for each field carefully.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the HR department for guidance.

Note: The form fields are subject to change or be removed based on the organization's needs and requirements. This guide is for general guidance and may not reflect any future updates or changes.
