# Employee Exit Interview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your reason for leaving the company. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand what we can do better and improve the workplace environment for future employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select all that apply for the "Why did you leave the company" question.
3. Choose the reason that best describes your situation for the "Reason for leaving" question.
4. Enter the date you started and ended your employment with the company for the "When did you start and end employment with the company" and "Last date of employment" questions.
5. Enter any comments or thoughts you have about your time with the company for the "Any comments" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Why did you leave the company** (Select Multiple)
  - This question helps us understand the reasons behind your decision to leave the company. Select all the options that apply to your situation.
* **Reason for leaving** (Select One)
  - Choose the reason that best describes your situation. Be as specific as possible to help us understand your decision.
* **When did you start and end employment with the company**
  - Enter the start date and end date of your employment with the company in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **How long were you employed with the company**
  - Enter the number of years you were employed with the company.
* **What was the last date of employment**
  - Enter the date you last worked for the company in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Reason for leaving**
  - Enter a brief but detailed explanation of your reason for leaving the company.
* **Next employment opportunity**
  - Enter the name of your new company or job opportunity.
* **Any comments**
  - Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about your time with the company.
* **Next step**
  - Enter any next steps or actions you would like us to take as a result of your feedback.
* **Assigned**
  - This field is meant to be filled in by your HR representative or someone who assigned this form to you.
* **Created by**
  - This field is automatically populated by our system and shows who created this form.
* **Created date**
  - This field is automatically populated by our system and shows the date this form was created.
* **Updated by**
  - This field is meant to be filled in by your HR representative or someone who updated this form.
* **Updated date**
  - This field is automatically populated by our system and shows the date this form was last updated.
* **Submitted by**
  - This field is automatically populated by our system and shows who submitted this form.
* **Submitted date**
  - This field is automatically populated by our system and shows the date this form was submitted.
