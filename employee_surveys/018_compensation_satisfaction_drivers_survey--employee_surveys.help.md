# Compensation Satisfaction Drivers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compensation Satisfaction Drivers Survey is designed to gather feedback on what drives employees' satisfaction with their compensation package, helping HR teams better understand employee expectations and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the answer that best matches your level of satisfaction for each question.
2. Provide suggestions for improvement if you would change one thing about our compensation package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which aspect of your compensation package is MOST important to you?**: This question helps us understand your primary drivers of satisfaction. Please select the aspect that you value most, such as Base Salary, Performance Bonuses/STI, Health and Wellness Benefits, or other options.
* **Satisfaction with Base Salary**: This question gauges your level of satisfaction with your fixed pay. Choose the rating that best reflects your feelings, with 1 being Not Satisfied and 5 being Highly Satisfied.
* **Satisfaction with the variety of benefits offered**: This question rates your satisfaction with the range of benefits provided by your employer. Select the rating that reflects your level of satisfaction, with 1 being Not Satisfied and 5 being Highly Satisfied.
* **How important is non-monetary recognition compared to financial rewards?**: This question compares the weight you place on recognition and financial rewards in your overall satisfaction. Choose the option that best balances these two factors.
* **Satisfaction with potential for salary growth over time**: This question assesses your satisfaction with the opportunities for future earning potential. Select the rating that best reflects your expectations.
* **Do you view work-life balance as a valuable part of your total compensation?**: This question examines your perception of work-life balance as a component of your compensation package. Choose the option that best represents your feelings.
* **How does your current compensation package compare to your previous employer?**: This question gauges your relative satisfaction with your current compensation compared to your previous employer. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
* **If you could change one thing about our compensation package, what would it be?**: This question invites you to suggest areas for improvement in our compensation package. Use this space to provide actionable feedback.
* **Overall satisfaction rating with the total rewards strategy**: This question provides a final, overall rating of your satisfaction with the compensation package. Choose the rating that best reflects your feelings.
* **Date**: This field is used to track the date of your survey completion for administrative purposes only.
