# Caregiver Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Caregiver Survey is designed to gather feedback from caregivers about their work experience, satisfaction, and concerns. This will help the organization identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to create a more supportive and efficient work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing each section carefully and providing honest answers.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly, but feel free to skip any sections that do not apply to your experience.
3. Use the scale provided (e.g., 1-5 for rating questions) for the best possible responses.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the text fields for more detailed explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (`<employment_id>`, `text`, optional): Enter your official company identification number, if applicable.
* **Date of Survey** (`<date_of_survey>`, `date`, required): The date you are completing this survey.
* **Unit or Department** (`<department_unit>`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your unit or department.
* **Rate your overall job satisfaction** (`<job_satisfaction>`, `number`, required): Answer on a scale of 1 (very unsatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied) to express your level of job satisfaction.
* **I have the resources I need to provide high-quality care** (`<resource_access>`, `select_one`, required): Select Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree to indicate your level of agreement.
* **How adequate is the training you receive?** (`<training_adequacy>`, `select_one`, required): Select Very Adequate, Adequate, Inadequate, or Very Inadequate to rate the quality of training you receive.
* **Is your current workload manageable?** (`<workload_manageability>`, `select_one`, required): Select Always, Usually, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never to express your experience with your workload.
* **Rate the level of collaboration within your team** (`<team_collaboration>`, `number`, required): Answer on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) to rate your level of collaboration.
* **Rate the support from your direct supervisor** (`<supervisor_support>`, `number`, required): Answer on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) to rate your supervisor's support.
* **How would you rate the quality of care provided by your unit?** (`<care_quality>`, `number`, required): Answer on a scale of 1 (low quality) to 5 (exceptional quality) to rate the quality of care.
* **Do you feel your colleagues treat patients with compassion?** (`<compassion_level>`, `select_one`, required): Select Always, Usually, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never to express your feelings about colleague compassion.
* **Rate the level of patient safety in your unit** (`<patient_safety>`, `number`, required): Answer on a scale of 1 (low safety concerns) to 5 (excellent safety practices) to rate patient safety.
* **Are there opportunities for career growth within the organization?** (`<growth_opportunities>`, `select_one`, required): Select True, False, or Unsure to indicate whether you see opportunities for career advancement.
* **One thing that would improve your professional development** (`<development_suggestion>`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them here.
* **Would you recommend this organization as a place to work?** (`<organization_recommendation>`, `select_one`, required): Select True or False to express your overall satisfaction.
* **Any other comments or concerns** (`<additional_feedback>`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional thoughts, concerns, or suggestions, enter them here.
