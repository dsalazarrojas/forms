# Union Representative Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from union representatives who are exiting their position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed.
2. Complete the Union Representative Information section with your current union representative details.
3. Select the correct Work Location from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your reason for leaving in the Reason for Leaving field.
5. Specify your Last Day of Work date.
6. Enter your Last Day of Work Time.
7. If applicable, enter the Notice Period number of days.
8. Specify the Notice Period End Date.
9. Enter any other relevant information in the Other Information field.
10. Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Union Representative Information** (optional): This section captures your current union representative details.
* **Work Location** (optional): Select the location where you worked.
* **Reason for Leaving** (optional): This field is for you to provide the reason for exiting your position.
* **Last Day of Work** (optional): Enter the date of your last working day.
* **Last Day of Work Time** (optional): Enter the time of your last working day.
* **Notice Period** (optional): If you are required to provide notice period, enter the number of days.
* **Notice Period End Date** (optional): If you are required to provide notice period, enter the end date of the notice period.
* **Other Information** (optional): This field is for you to provide any other relevant information.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
