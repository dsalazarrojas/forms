# Weekly Wellbeing Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help managers and supervisors gauge the wellbeing of their employees, focusing on their emotional state, work-life balance, productivity, and support systems.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a response from the list for each question, based on your current state.
2. Provide a brief description of your overall feeling in the "overall_feeling" fields.
3. Answer the questions truthfully, without overthinking or overanalyzing.
4. Take care to complete all sections, as they all contribute to a comprehensive wellbeing assessment.
