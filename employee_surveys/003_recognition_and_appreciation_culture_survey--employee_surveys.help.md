# Recognition And Appreciation Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the company's recognition and appreciation culture. It's essential to provide honest and detailed responses to help improve the company's culture.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions based on your personal experiences with recognition or appreciation at the company.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing responses to help identify areas of improvement.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (Text): A brief introduction to your experience with recognition or appreciation at the company. Please describe your experiences and what you're hoping to achieve with this form.
### How Do You Feel?
* **How Do You Feel?** (Text): Describe how you feel about the recognition and appreciation practices at the company. Do you feel valued, motivated, or undervalued?
### How Often?
* **How Often?** (Number): On a scale of 0-10, rate how often you receive recognition or appreciation from your colleagues, manager, or company.
### Do You Receive Recognition?
* **Do You Receive Recognition?** (Text): Describe the types of recognition or appreciation you receive, such as rewards, bonuses, or public acknowledgment.
### Fairness
* **Fairness** (Number): On a scale of 0-10, rate how fair you feel the recognition and appreciation practices are at the company.
### What Happened?
* **What Happened?** (Text): Describe what happened during a specific incident or experience where you felt recognized or appreciated unfairly.
### Why Didn't You Receive Fairness?
* **Why Didn't You Receive Fairness?** (Select One): Choose one of the options provided to explain why you didn't receive fairness in recognition or appreciation.
### Ideas for Improvement
* **Ideas for Improvement** (Select Multiple): Choose all the options that apply to share your ideas for improving the company's recognition and appreciation culture.
### Suggestions for Improvement
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (Text): Provide specific suggestions for improving the company's recognition and appreciation practices.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (Email): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for follow-up questions or to share results.
### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (Text): Any additional thoughts, concerns, or suggestions you'd like to share.
### Additional Comments 2
* **Additional Comments 2** (Text): Another opportunity to add any further comments or ideas you may have.
### Additional Comments 3
* **Additional Comments 3** (Text): A final chance to add any last-minute thoughts or suggestions.
