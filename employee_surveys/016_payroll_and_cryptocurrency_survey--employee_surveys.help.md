# payroll_and_cryptocurrency_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is intended to collect information on employees' payroll and cryptocurrency preferences. It aims to gather data on department, preferred currency, salary frequency, cryptocurrency payment, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and department in the respective fields.
2. Select your preferred currency and cryptocurrency payment options.
3. Choose your salary frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, etc.).
4. Confirm if you would like to receive cryptocurrency payments.
5. If applicable, enter any additional comments or details.
6. Provide your name and email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Select your department and preferred currency for a secondary set of data (if applicable).
9. Answer the crypto payment question for the secondary set of data.
10. Provide any comments or details for the secondary set of data (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. name (Text, Required)
 Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.

### 2. department (Select One, Required)
 Select your department from the given options.

### 3. preferred_currency (Select Multiple, Required)
 Select your preferred currency for payment.

### 4. salary_frequency (Select One, Required)
 Choose your salary frequency from the options provided.

### 5. crypto_payment (Select One, Required)
 Confirm if you would like to receive cryptocurrency payments.

### 6. crypto_currency (Select One, Required)
 Select the cryptocurrency for which you would like to receive payments.

### 7. crypto_frequency (Select One, Required)
 Choose the frequency at which you would like to receive cryptocurrency payments.

### 8. comments (Note, Optional)
 Enter any additional comments or details, if applicable.

### 9. Name 2 (Text, Required)
 Enter your full name for the secondary set of data.

### 10. email (Email, Required)
 Enter your email address.

### 11. phone (Text, Required)
 Enter your phone number.

### 12. Department 2 (Select One, Required)
 Select your department for the secondary set of data.

### 13. preferred_currency_2 (Select Multiple, Required)
 Select your preferred currency for the secondary set of data.

### 14. crypto_payment_2 (Select One, Required)
 Confirm your preference for receiving cryptocurrency payments for the secondary set of data.

### 15. comments_2 (Note, Optional)
 Enter any comments or details for the secondary set of data.
