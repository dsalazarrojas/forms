# employee_ownership_mindset_training_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants on the effectiveness of a training session focused on employee ownership mindset.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
  - Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
  - Select the correct answer for each question.
  - Complete all questions, even if you are unsure or don't know the answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainer Effectiveness** (`trainer_effectiveness`, `text`, required: false): Evaluate the effectiveness of the trainer in delivering the training session. Please provide your honest feedback on how well the trainer presented the material and engaged with you.
* **Confidence in Applying Ownership** (`confidence_in_applying_ownership`, `select_one`, required: false): How confident do you feel in applying the ownership mindset principles learned during the training? Please select one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Content Quality** (`content_quality`, `number`, required: false): Rate the quality of the training content. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Comments or Suggestions** (`comments_or_suggestions`, `note`, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions for improvement, please share them with us. This will help us refine the training for future sessions.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, `text`, required: false): How satisfied are you with the training session? Please provide your honest feedback.
* **Training Value** (`training_value`, `number`, required: false): Rate the value of the training session. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Trainer Professionalism** (`trainer_professionalism`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How professional was the trainer during the session? Please select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Content Delivery** (`content_delivery`, `text`, required: false): How clear was the presentation of the training content? Please provide your feedback on the delivery of the material.
* **Course Organization** (`course_organization`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How well was the training session organized? Please select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
- Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for clarification.
- Your feedback is essential in helping us improve the training for future sessions.
