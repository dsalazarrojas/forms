# Worker Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Worker Satisfaction Survey is a form used to gather feedback from employees on their job satisfaction, work environment, and overall well-being. This information will be used to identify areas of improvement in the workplace and help create a better work environment for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accurately.
2. Choose the best answer for each question based on your honest opinion.
3. Ensure you have completed all questions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **How satisfied are you with your job** (`employee_survey_1`, Select One, Required): This question asks how satisfied you are with your job. Please choose from the options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **How satisfied are you with your team** (`employee_survey_2`, Select Multiple, Required): This question asks how satisfied you are with your team. Please choose from the options: 'Yes' or 'No' (you can choose more than one option).
* **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, Number, Required): Please enter a numerical value to indicate your job satisfaction.

### Page 2

* **Work life balance** (`work_life_balance`, Date, Required): This question asks if you feel that your work-life balance is maintained. Please enter a date if you have a specific date in mind.
