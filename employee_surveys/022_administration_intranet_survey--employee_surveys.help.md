# Administration Intranet Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from employees about their experience with the intranet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name and ID number in the respective fields.
2. Select your department and position level from the provided options.
3. Answer the questions about your intranet usage frequency, satisfaction, and experience with various features.
4. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department you work in to help us understand your specific intranet experience.
* **Position Level** (`position_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current position level to help us understand your intranet needs.
* **Intranet Usage Frequency** (`intranet_usage_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you use the intranet.
* **Overall Intranet Satisfaction** (`intranet_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the intranet.
* **Navigation and Usability Rating** (`navigation_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the ease of navigating the intranet.
* **Search Functionality Rating** (`search_functionality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the search functionality of the intranet.
* **Information Findability** (`information_findability`, `select_one`, required): Rate how easy it is to find the information you need on the intranet.
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, `select_one`, required): Rate the relevance of the content to your work.
* **Mobile Access Rating** (`mobile_access_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your experience with mobile access to the intranet.
* **Collaboration Tools Rating** (`collaboration_tools_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the collaboration tools available on the intranet.
* **Training Resources Rating** (`training_resources_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the training resources available on the intranet.
* **Frequency of Technical Issues** (`technical_issues_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Rate how often you experience technical issues on the intranet.
* **Most Valuable Feature** (`most_valuable_feature`, `text`, optional): Tell us about the feature that you find most valuable on the intranet.
* **Least Valuable Feature** (`least_valuable_feature`, `text`, optional): Tell us about the feature that you find least valuable on the intranet.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Features Desired** (`additional_features_desired`, `text`, optional): Tell us about any additional features you would like to see on the intranet.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `text`, optional): Tell us about any training that would help you use the intranet more effectively.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you're completing this survey.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and provide specific examples when possible.
* If you're unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department or your supervisor.
* Your feedback is valuable, and your input will help shape the intranet's future development.
