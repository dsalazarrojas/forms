# recognition_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "recognition_survey" form is used to gather feedback and information from employees on their experiences with the recognition program. This form helps the organization understand how the program is functioning and how to improve it. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each section, and fill in the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name as an employee who has been recognized or has received a nomination.
* **Who nominated you?** (`recognition_nominator`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the name of the person who nominated you for recognition.
* **Nomination Department** (`recognition_nominator_department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the department that the nomination was from.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2
* **Nomination Level** (`recognition_nominator_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of recognition you received.
	+ Options: Level 1, Level 2, Level 3
* **Nomination Date** (`recognition_nominator_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you were nominated for recognition.
* **Nomination Time** (`recognition_nominator_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you were nominated for recognition.
* **Nomination Details** (`recognition_nominator_details`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional details about the nomination.
* **Recognition Program Rating** (`recognition_program_rating`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with the recognition program (e.g., 1-5).
* **Recognition Program Impact** (`recognition_program_impact`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe how the recognition program impacted you.
* **Recognition Stories** (`recognition_stories`, `text`, required/optional): Share a story or anecdote about a time when you were recognized or received a nomination.
* **Recognition Program Experience** (`recognition_program_experience`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with the recognition program (e.g., 1-5).
* **Recognition Program Comments** (`recognition_program_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments about the recognition program.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter any comments from your manager regarding your experience with the recognition program.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Submit" to complete the form, or "Cancel" to exit without saving your changes.
* **Form Assigned to** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required/optional): This field is likely not used in the form completion process, but it may be used to track which form is assigned to which team or department.

Please note that some fields are optional, and you can skip them if not applicable.
