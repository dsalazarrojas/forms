# Payroll Helpdesk Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback on the effectiveness and timeliness of our Payroll Helpdesk service. Your responses will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve, enabling us to provide better support to our employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
* Select the relevant options for each question from the provided lists.
* If a field is not applicable to your experience, please indicate this in your response (e.g., "Not Applicable" or "Not Resolved").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **What is the current status of the request?** (page 1)
* (`title`, `text`, optional): This is the current state of the request you're referring to. Please briefly describe the status, e.g., "Open", "Closed", "On hold", etc.

### **How long did it take to resolve the request?** (page 2)
* (`resolution_time`, `number`, optional): Estimate the time it took to resolve the request, in minutes. You can use the provided time ranges as a guide.

### **What was the professionalism of the person handling the request?** (page 3)
* (`professionalism`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the professionalism of the person handling the request using the provided options: Not Satisfactory, Satisfactory, Excellent.

### **How was the issue resolved?** (page 4)
* (`issue_resolution`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all applicable ways in which the issue was resolved from the provided options: Not Resolved, Partially Resolved, Completely Resolved.

### **What was the response time?** (page 5)
* (`response_time`, `select_one`, optional): Select the response time from the provided options: Less than 30 minutes, 30 minutes to 1 hour, 1 hour to 4 hours, 4 hours or more.

### **Professionalism Rating** (page 6)
* (`professionalism_rating`, `note`, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide a brief, detailed rating of the professionalism of the person handling the request.

### **Overall Experience** (page 7)
* (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your overall experience using the provided options: Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, Excellent.

### **Comments** (page 8)
* (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have regarding your experience with the Payroll Helpdesk service.
