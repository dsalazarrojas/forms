# Diversity And Inclusion Specialist Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a Diversity And Inclusion Specialist's exit from the company. It is intended to ensure a smooth offboarding process and to gather necessary details about their reasons for leaving. **Please only complete this form if you are actually exiting the organization.**

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the first section, "Employee Survey," and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For the "Demographics" section, select your demographics by choosing one or multiple options from the provided list.
3. In the "Comments" section, provide any additional comments or information about your exit that you feel is relevant.
4. If applicable, provide your "Date of Last Review" and "Time of Last Review" as per the offboarding process.
5. Select a reason for your exit from the provided options in the "Exit Reason" section.
6. Answer the "Diversity Questions" by selecting either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you experienced any diversity-related issues during your time at the company.
7. In the "Exit Questions" section, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you experienced any issues related to your exit or offboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey** (`employee_survey`, text, required: false): This section is designed to collect general information about your experience as a Diversity And Inclusion Specialist. Please answer the questions based on your honest opinion.
* **Employee Demographics** (`demographics`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple options that describe your demographics, such as age, gender, or ethnicity.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information that you feel is relevant to your exit.
* **Date of Last Review** (`date_of_last_review`, date, required: false): If applicable, provide the date of your last performance review, which is usually the last day before your exit date.
* **Time of Last Review** (`time_of_last_review`, time, required: false): If applicable, provide the time of your last performance review, which is usually the last day before your exit date.
* **Exit Reason** (`exit_reason`, select_one, required: false): Select one reason for your exit from the list of provided options.
* **Diversity Questions** (`diversity_questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you experienced any diversity-related issues during your time at the company.
* **Exit Questions** (`exit_questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you experienced any issues related to your exit or offboarding process. 

**Tips**

* Please be honest and complete all the sections as much as possible.
* If you are unsure about any field, it's always better to ask your HR representative or supervisor for guidance.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
