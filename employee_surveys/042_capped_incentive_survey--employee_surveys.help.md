# Capped Incentive Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on our capped incentive program. The goal is to understand your experiences, perceptions, and suggestions for improvement, which will help us refine the program to better meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Scroll down each section, and provide your feedback in the corresponding fields.
2. Read each question carefully, and select the answer that best describes your experience or opinion.
3. You can select multiple options in the "Select all that apply" questions.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions that don't fit into the provided fields, please use the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): This is your unique employee number.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Your name (optional, but helps us identify you).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Your work email address (optional, but helps us contact you).
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Your department or team.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Your job title.
* **Length of Service** (`tenure`, text, optional): How long you've been employed with the company.
* **Are you aware of available incentive programs?** (`incentive_programs_aware`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Very aware, Somewhat aware, Slightly aware, Not aware.
* **Which incentive programs do you participate in?** (`participating_programs`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of available programs.
* **How well do you understand the program rules and caps?** (`program_knowledge`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Very clear, Mostly clear, Somewhat clear, Not clear, Very confusing.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Incentive Program** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Do you feel the program is fair?** (`fairness_rating`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Very fair, Fair, Somewhat fair, Unfair, Very unfair.
* **Is the compensation level adequate?** (`compensation_adequacy`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: More than adequate, Adequate, Barely adequate, Inadequate.
* **Are incentive goals realistically achievable?** (`goals_achievable`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Very achievable, Achievable, Somewhat achievable, Difficult, Impossible.
* **Do you feel the incentive cap is too restrictive?** (`cap_concerns`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Much too restrictive, Somewhat restrictive, Appropriate, Not restrictive enough, No concerns.
* **How motivated are you by the incentive program?** (`motivation_level`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Highly motivated, Motivated, Somewhat motivated, Not motivated, Demotivated.
* **Does the program motivate better performance?** (`performance_impact`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Strongly motivates, Motivates, Neutral effect, Slightly demotivating, Very demotivating.
* **Does the program influence your decision to stay?** (`retention_impact`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options: Strongly influences, Influences, Neutral, Minimal influence, Would not influence.
* **What improvements would enhance the program?** (`program_improvements`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of available suggestions.
* **How clear is the program communication?** (`communication_clarity`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, Very unclear.
* **What other benefits would you value?** (`additional_benefits`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of available suggestions.
* **Any Additional Comments or Suggestions?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any thoughts that don't fit into the other fields, please share here.

Remember, your feedback is valuable and will be treated confidentially. If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions, please contact your HR representative or supervisor. We appreciate your participation in helping us improve the incentive program for your benefit.
