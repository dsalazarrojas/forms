# Onboarding Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This onboarding feedback questionnaire is designed to collect your thoughts and experiences about the onboarding process. Your feedback will help us improve the process, making it more effective and efficient for future employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the questions carefully and answer them truthfully.
2. You can skip questions that don't apply to your experience.
3. Use specific examples and details when answering questions where possible.
4. For multiple-choice questions, choose the option that best represents your opinion.
5. Take as much time as you need to complete the form, there is no time limit.
