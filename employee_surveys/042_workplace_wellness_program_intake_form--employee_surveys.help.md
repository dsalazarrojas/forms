## Workplace Wellness Program Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your well-being and help the HR department tailor the workplace wellness program to address your specific needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of your well-being.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey** (page 1): This page is an optional survey that helps us understand your concerns and interests related to your well-being.
* **Health Risks Concerns** (page 2): If you have concerns about your physical or mental health, please enter the number of concerns you have.
* **Health Program Interests** (page 3): Select all the programs you are interested in participating in to improve your well-being.
* **Wellness Goals** (page 4): Describe your goals for your well-being, e.g., improving physical health, reducing stress, etc.
* **Work-Life Balance** (page 5): Report how you typically spend your work hours and how this affects your well-being.
* **Physical Activity** (page 6): Enter the number of hours you engage in physical activity per week.
* **Employee Engagement** (page 7): Report the date you first became engaged with the company (or when you last became engaged).
* **Mental Health** (page 8): Select one option that best describes your mental health concerns.
* **Workplace Safety** (page 9): Please note any workplace safety concerns you have.
* **Email Address** (page 10): Enter your work email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (page 11): Enter your work phone number for communication purposes.
* **Workplace Wellness Program** (page 12): Describe how you think the workplace wellness program can benefit you.

Tips:
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
