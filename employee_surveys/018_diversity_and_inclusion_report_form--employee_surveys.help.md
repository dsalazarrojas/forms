# Diversity And Inclusion Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diversity And Inclusion Report Form is designed to help organizations assess and improve their diversity and inclusion practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that you understand its purpose and content.
2. Answer each question in a clear and concise manner.
3. Be as detailed and specific as possible when responding to the questions.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, as indicated by the "required" label next to each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey** (`Employee Survey`, text, optional): This section asks for your honest feedback on your experiences and perceptions within the organization.
* **Diversity Practices** (`Diversity Practices`, text, optional): Describe the diversity practices you've experienced within the organization, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **Employee Experience** (`Employee Experience`, text, optional): Share your personal experiences and feelings about being an employee within the organization.

## Tips

* Be honest and authentic in your responses.
* If unsure about any question, you can always contact the person or department responsible for diversity and inclusion within your organization.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can include them in the "Employee Experience" section.
