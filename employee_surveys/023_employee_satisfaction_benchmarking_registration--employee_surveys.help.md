The Employee Satisfaction Benchmarking Registration form is used to collect information from employees to gauge their satisfaction with the organization's policies and practices. This form is designed to be completed by employees who have participated in the organization's benchmarking program. The data collected will help the organization to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to increase employee satisfaction.

## Employee Satisfaction Benchmarking Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees on their satisfaction with the organization's policies and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On **Page 1**, complete the **organization_details** field by providing a brief description of your department or team.
2. On **Page 2**, describe the **objectives** of your team or department.
3. On **Page 2**, select up to 5 **register_participants** who will be participating in the survey.
4. On **Page 3**, select the **manage_registrations** options that apply to your team or department. You can select multiple options.
5. On **Page 4**, answer **manage_registrations_2** by selecting either "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **organization_details** (Page 1, Text): Enter a brief description of your department or team.
* **objectives** (Page 2, Text): Describe the objectives of your team or department.
* **register_participants** (Page 3, Select One): Select up to 5 participants who will be participating in the survey. You can choose from: participant1, participant2, participant3, participant4, or participant5.
* **manage_registrations** (Page 4, Select Multiple): Select the options that apply to your team or department. You can choose from: Option 1, Option 2.
* **manage_registrations_2** (Page 4, Select One): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have managed any registrations in the past.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise description for **organization_details** and **objectives**.
* Select up to 5 participants for **register_participants**.
* Be accurate when selecting **manage_registrations** and **manage_registrations_2** options.
