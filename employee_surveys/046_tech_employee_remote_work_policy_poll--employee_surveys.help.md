## tech_employee_remote_work_policy_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and preferences from employees on the company's remote work policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that your input is not duplicated.
2. Select the options that best reflect your thoughts and experiences on the following fields:
  * Employee Survey
  * Remote Work Preferences
  * Work Satisfaction
  * Team Communication
  * Work-Life Balance
  * Job Satisfaction
  * Feedback
  * Contact Preferences

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Survey** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): This field asks whether you have experience with remote work or would like to have it. Please select 'Yes' if you have worked remotely or would like to work remotely, and 'No' if you have not worked remotely or do not prefer remote work.
* **Remote Work Preferences** (`text`, optional): Please describe your preferences regarding remote work, e.g., "I prefer to work from home", "I prefer to work in a office".
* **Work Satisfaction** (`number`, optional): Please rate your work satisfaction on a scale from 0 to 100.
* **Team Communication** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): This field asks whether you think team communication is better or worse when working remotely. Please select 'Yes' if you think communication is better, and 'No' if you think it is worse.
* **Work-Life Balance** (`number`, optional): Please rate your ability to maintain a good work-life balance while working remotely on a scale from 0 to 100.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`Satisfied`, `Dissatisfied`, optional): Please select whether you are satisfied or dissatisfied with your job while working remotely.
* **Feedback** (`note`, optional): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you have about remote work.
* **Contact Preferences** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): This field asks whether you prefer to receive contact from your team while working remotely. Please select 'Yes' if you prefer to receive contact, and 'No' if you do not prefer to receive contact.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* Your input will help us understand your needs and improve our remote work policy.
