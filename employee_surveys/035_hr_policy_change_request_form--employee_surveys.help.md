# HR Policy Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Change Request Form is designed to collect information about proposed changes to HR policies within the organization. The form is intended for HR staff, department teams, and employees to submit and manage policy change requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Policy Change Request**: In this field, describe the specific policy change you would like to propose.
	* Enter a clear and concise description of the policy change.
2. **Department**: Choose the department that the policy change affects.
	* Select the relevant department from the drop-down menu.
3. **Change Request Type**: Indicate the type of policy change (update, addition, or removal).
	* Select the type of policy change.
4. **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the policy change.
	* Enter a clear and concise description of the policy change.
5. **Impact**: This field is not applicable for now. (Note: The field "impact" is not relevant for this specific form. You can skip it for now.)
6. **Date of Effectivity**: Enter the effective date of the policy change.
	* Enter the date when the policy change will take effect.
7. **Date of Effectivity End**: Enter the end date of the policy change, if applicable.
	* Enter the date when the policy change will end, if applicable.
8. **Policy Change Requestor**: Select the person or team responsible for the policy change request.
	* Choose the person or team that initiated the policy change request.
9. **Departmental Approvals**: List the departmental teams or staff members that need to approve the policy change.
	* Select the departments or staff members that need to approve the policy change.
10. **Comments**: This field is not applicable for now. (Note: The field "comments" is not relevant for this specific form. You can skip it for now.)
11. **Date of Submission**: Enter the date when the policy change request was submitted.
	* Enter the date when the policy change request was submitted.
12. **Departmental Approvals Status**: Select the status of departmental approvals.
	* Choose the status of departmental approvals.
13. **Comments Status**: Select the status of comments.
	* Choose the status of comments.
14. **Date of Submission Status**: Select the status of the policy change request submission.
	* Choose the status of the policy change request submission.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise in your descriptions and selections.
- Ensure that the policy change is not a duplicate or already under review.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, refer to the company's internal knowledge base or consult with your HR department.
