# Managerial Communication Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the effectiveness of a manager's communication skills and overall performance. It has 25 questions that rate the manager's approachability, listening skills, and communication skills, as well as space for comments and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your experience with your manager.
2. Rate each question using the 5-point scale provided.
3. Provide any comments or feedback you have about your manager's performance.
4. Make sure to answer all required questions, which are marked as "required" in the form.
