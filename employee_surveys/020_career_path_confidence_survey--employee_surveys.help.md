# Career Path Confidence Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Career Path Confidence Survey is a tool designed to help you better understand your career path and identify areas for growth and development. It will help you reflect on your current role, career confidence, and readiness for advancement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page of the survey carefully.
2. Choose the answer that best reflects your thoughts and feelings for each question.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
4. If you're unsure about a question, select the answer that best represents your current state.
5. Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your career path in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Employee Information
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select your current department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Select your current job title.

### Page 2: Career Confidence
* **Years with Organization** (`tenure_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you've worked with the organization.
* **Overall Confidence in Your Career Path** (`career_confidence`, select_one, required): Select how confident you are about your career direction.
	+ Very confident - know exactly where I'm headed
	+ Confident - have a clear direction
	+ Somewhat confident - general sense of direction
	+ Uncertain - still figuring it out
	+ Not confident - unclear about direction
* **Confidence That Your Current Role Is Right for You** (`current_role_fit`, select_one, required): Select how confident you are that your current role is right for you.
	+ Very confident - perfect fit
	+ Confident - good match
	+ Neutral - adequate
	+ Uncertain - not sure
	+ Not confident - wrong role
* **Confidence in Ability to Advance Within Organization** (`advancement_readiness`, select_one, required): Select how ready you feel for promotion or growth.
	+ Very confident - ready for next level
	+ Confident - working toward advancement
	+ Somewhat confident - need some development
	+ Uncertain - unsure of readiness
	+ Not confident - feel stuck

### Page 3: Career Development
* **Confidence That Your Skills Match Career Goals** (`skills_adequacy`, select_one, required): Select how confident you are that your skills match your career goals.
	+ Very confident - have all needed skills
	+ Confident - mostly prepared
	+ Somewhat confident - some gaps
	+ Uncertain - unclear about skill gaps
	+ Not confident - significant skill gaps
* **Clear Plan for Skill Development** (`skill_development_path`, select_one, required): Select how clear you are about your plan for developing needed skills.
	+ Very clear - specific development plan
	+ Clear - general idea of path
	+ Somewhat clear - exploring options
	+ Unclear - not sure how to proceed
	+ No plan - confused about steps
* **Understanding of Career Advancement Criteria** (`advancement_criteria`, select_one, required): Select how clear you are about the criteria for advancement within the organization.
	+ Very clear - know exactly
	+ Clear - understand well
	+ Somewhat clear - general understanding
	+ Unclear - vague knowledge
	+ Very unclear - don't know

### Page 4: Organizational Support
* **Confidence in Organizational Support for Growth** (`organizational_support`, select_one, required): Select how confident you are about the organizational support for your career growth.
	+ Very confident - strong support
	+ Confident - good support available
	+ Somewhat confident - some support
	+ Uncertain - unclear
	+ Not confident - limited support
* **Manager Support for Your Career Development** (`manager_support`, select_one, required): Select how supportive your manager is for your career growth.
	+ Very supportive - actively helps
	+ Supportive - provides guidance
	+ Somewhat supportive - limited support
	+ Not very supportive - minimal help
	+ Unsupportive - discourages growth
* **Mentor or Role Model** (`mentor_influence`, select_one, optional): Select how influential your mentor or role model is on your career.
	+ Have mentor - very influential
	+ Have mentor - somewhat helpful
	+ No mentor - would help
	+ No mentor - not needed
	+ No mentor - seeking one

### Page 5: Career Visibility
* **Visibility Within Organization** (`career_visibility`, select_one, optional): Select how visible you feel within the organization.
	+ Very visible - well known
	+ Visible - noticed by leadership
	+ Somewhat visible - known in area
	+ Low visibility - mostly unknown
	+ No visibility - invisible

### Page 6: Confidence Factors
* **What Increases Your Career Confidence?** (`confidence_factors`, select_multiple, required): Select all factors that increase your career confidence.
	+ Positive feedback and recognition
	+ Successful project completion
	+ Learning new skills
	+ Mentorship and guidance
	+ Clear career path information
	+ Promotion or advancement
	+ Peer support and collaboration
	+ Professional development opportunities
	+ Financial stability
	+ Work-life balance
* **What Decreases Your Career Confidence?** (`confidence_barriers`, select_multiple, optional): Select all factors that decrease your career confidence.
	+ Unclear expectations
	+ Limited advancement opportunities
	+ Lack of skill development
	+ Poor management support
	+ Workplace challenges or conflicts
	+ Imposter syndrome
	+ Comparison to peers
	+ Economic uncertainty
	+ Work overload or burnout

### Page 7: Transition and External Confidence
* **Confidence in Making Transition** (`transition_confidence`, select_one, optional): Select how confident you are about making a transition in your career.
	+ Very confident - ready for change
	+ Confident - prepared
	+ Somewhat confident - some concerns
	+ Uncertain - many doubts
	+ Not confident - anxious about change
* **Confidence About External Job Market** (`external_market_confidence`, select_one, optional): Select how employable you feel outside this company.
	+ Very confident - highly marketable
	+ Confident - would be hired easily
	+ Somewhat confident - some prospects
	+ Uncertain - unclear marketability
	+ Not confident - limited prospects

### Page 8: Long-Term Confidence
* **Confidence in 5-Year Career Direction** (`long_term_confidence`, select_one, required): Select how confident you are about your 5-year career outlook.
	+ Very confident - clear vision
	+ Confident - sense of direction
	+ Somewhat confident - general idea
	+ Uncertain - unclear future
	+ Not confident - worried about future

### Page 9: Support and Comments
* **Support Needed** (`support_needs`, text, optional): Describe what support or resources you need to increase your career confidence.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other thoughts or comments about your career path.
