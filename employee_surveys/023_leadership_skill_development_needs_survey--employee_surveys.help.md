# leadership_skill_development_needs_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees identify their leadership skills development needs. It is meant to be completed by leaders or individuals who are part of the leadership development program. This form will help to understand their current leadership skills, areas where they need to improve, and their preferences for training and coaching.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Survey Header** (`survey_header`, `note`, not required):
	* This section serves as an introduction to the form. It does not require any input from you.
2. **Current Leadership Skills** (`leadership_skills`, `number`, not required):
	* Select the current leadership skills you possess. This will help us understand your strengths and areas for improvement.
3. **Development Areas** (`development_areas`, `select_one`, not required):
	* Choose the areas where you would like to develop your leadership skills. Please select 'Yes' for areas where you feel you need improvement.
4. **Development Priorities** (`development_priorities`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	* Choose the areas you would like to prioritize for development. Please select 'Yes' for areas you feel are crucial for your growth.
5. **Training Preferences** (`training_preferences`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	* Select the types of training or coaching you would like to receive. Please choose 'Yes' for areas where you feel you would benefit from.
6. **Submission Header** (`submission_header`, `note`, not required):
	* This section serves as a closing note. It does not require any input from you.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
- If unsure about any of the options, feel free to consult with your supervisor or mentor for guidance.
- Please ensure that you select all applicable areas for both 'Development Areas' and 'Training Preferences'.
