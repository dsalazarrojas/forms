# employment_follow_up_invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a follow-up invitation to employees, gathering information to confirm meeting details and employee availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Select the date of the meeting in the "meeting_date" field.
3. Select the time of the meeting in the "meeting_time" field.
4. Enter the location of the meeting in the "meeting_location" field.
5. Select your availability for the meeting using the options "Available", "Not Available", or "Maybe".
6. Enter the purpose of the follow-up in the "follow_up_purpose" field.
7. Select the preferred method of follow-up using the options "Email", "Phone", or "In-person".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee being invited to the meeting.
* **meeting_date**: Select the date of the meeting.
* **meeting_time**: Select the time of the meeting.
* **meeting_location**: Enter the location of the meeting.
* **availability**: Select your availability for the meeting:
	+ Available: You are available to meet on the selected date and time.
	+ Not Available: You are not available to meet on the selected date and time.
	+ Maybe: You are unsure if you are available or not.
* **follow_up_purpose**: Enter the reason for the follow-up invitation.
* **follow_up_method**: Select the preferred method of follow-up:
	+ Email: Receive the follow-up via email.
	+ Phone: Receive the follow-up via phone call.
	+ In-person: Receive the follow-up in-person meeting.
