# life_vest_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on your experience with our life vest and help us understand how we can improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the answer to each question that best describes your experience with our life vest.
2. If a field is marked as "optional," you may skip it and move on to the next question.
3. Please provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields, such as "Why did you rate our life vest as you did?".
4. Finally, if you have any other remarks or comments, please use the "Other Remark" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Life Vest Survey** (note): This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **How did you get to know about our life vest?** (select_one): Select one of the following options to describe how you learned about our life vest:
	+ Canoeing Magazine
	+ Social Media
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Online Search
	+ Other
* **How long did you spend on the water?** (number): This field is asking about the duration of your time spent on the water. Please enter a numerical value.
* **How would you rate our life vest?** (select_one): Select one of the following options to rate your experience with our life vest:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Why did you rate our life vest as you did?** (text): Provide a brief explanation for your rating. This will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.
* **Would you recommend our life vest to a friend or family member?** (select_one): Select one of the following options to indicate if you would recommend our life vest:
	+ True
	+ False
* **If yes, why?** (text): Provide a brief explanation for your recommendation.
* **Is there anything else you would like to tell us?** (note): This field is for any additional comments or remarks you have about our life vest.
