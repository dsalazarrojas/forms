# Participant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from participants applying for a seminar or event. It is used to ensure that each participant has a spot available and that their details are recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details, including first and last name.
2. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Select the participant capacity (number of participants in your group) and provide any additional details if necessary.
4. Choose a seminar date and time that suits you.
5. Add any notes or comments about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Application Form**: This is the main form title and header.
* **Seminar Details**: Provide a brief description of the seminar you are applying for, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Participant Details**: Provide any additional details about yourself that are relevant to your participation in the seminar.
* **Seminar Date**: Select a date for the seminar you would like to attend from the available dates.
* **Seminar Time**: Select a time for the seminar you would like to attend from the available times.
* **Participant Capacity**: Choose the number of participants in your group attending the seminar.
* **Participant Capacity Other**: If your group size is not listed, provide the number and any additional details.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields, including participant capacity.
* If you have any issues with the form or would like to cancel your application, contact us at your chosen contact information.
