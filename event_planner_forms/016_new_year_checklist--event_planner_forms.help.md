1.  Review the form questions and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2.  Start with the "New Year" page and enter your general thoughts about the upcoming year.
3.  On the "Goals" page, select all the relevant goals you have for the year, such as Career, Financial, and Lifestyle.
