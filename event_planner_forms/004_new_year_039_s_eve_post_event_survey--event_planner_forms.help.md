# new_year_s_eve_post_event_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of New Year's Eve events to help us evaluate and improve future events. Please provide your honest thoughts and opinions on the event you attended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. For multiple-choice fields (Event Name, Date Attended, Time Arrived, Event Score, Favorite Part, Least Favorite Part, and Contact Info), select one or more options that apply.
3. For non-multiple-choice fields (Time Arrived and Additional Comments), provide a specific response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (Required): Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Date Attended** (Required): Select the date you attended the event.
* **Time Arrived** (Required): Select the time you arrived at the event. This will help us understand the event's timing.
* **Event Score** (Required): Rate the event on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Favorite Part** (Required): Select one or more of your favorite parts of the event from the options provided (Food and Drink, Music, Decorations).
* **Least Favorite Part** (Required): Select one or more of your least favorite parts of the event from the options provided (Food and Drink, Music, Decorations).
* **Contact Info** (Required): Please provide your email address or phone number so we can follow up with you.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Any other comments or suggestions you'd like to add.
