# Fathers Day DIY Kits Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from customers about their orders for Fathers Day DIY Kits, including contact details, order specifics, and shipping and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your valid Email address.
3. Provide your Phone Number.
4. Enter your order details in the Order Details field.
5. Select your Kit Type from the available options.
6. Specify the Quantity of the kit you wish to purchase.
7. Enter the Date of Purchase.
8. Choose the Delivery Date for your order.
9. Select the Delivery Time for your order.
10. If applicable, provide any Special Instructions.
11. Choose your preferred Shipping Method.
12. Enter your Shipping Address (if applicable).
13. Select your preferred Payment Method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to reach you.
- **Order Details** (5, note, required): Describe your order in as much detail as possible.
- **Kit Type** (6, select_one, required): Select the type of kit you wish to purchase: Handmade or Custom.
- **Quantity** (7, number, required): Specify the number of kits you wish to purchase.
- **Date of Purchase** (8, date, required): Enter the date you wish to make the purchase.
- **Delivery Date** (9, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
- **Delivery Time** (10, time, required): Choose the time you would like to receive your order.
- **Special Instructions** (11, note, optional): If you have any special requests or instructions, enter them here.
- **Shipping Method** (12, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to receive your order: Pickup or Delivery.
- **Shipping Address** (13, note, optional): Enter your shipping address if you selected delivery as your preferred shipping method.
- **Payment Method** (14, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method: Credit Card or PayPal.
- **Order Notes** (15, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about your order, enter them here.
