# thanksgiving_send_a_message_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you send personalized messages or invitations to others during the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your own personal details as the sender by filling out fields **1** through **3**, which will help us understand who is sending the message and their contact information.
2. Enter the recipient's details in fields **4** and **5**, which are their name and email address.
3. Write a personalized message in field **6**, which is where you can include any details or information you'd like to share with the recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. thanksgiving sender** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as the sender to personalize the message with your identity.
* **2. recipient name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person receiving the message.
* **3. your message** (`text`, required: false): Write a personal message for the recipient, including any details or information you'd like to share.
* **4. recipient email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the recipient's email address for further contact.
* **5. your email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address for the recipient to respond or reach out.
* **6. your message** (`text`, required: false): Enter your personal message to the recipient, including any details or information you'd like to share.

## Tips
- Be polite and respectful when writing your message to ensure a positive response from the recipient.
- Keep your message concise and clear to avoid confusion.
- Proofread your message for any spelling or grammar errors before sending it.
- Use this form to send invitations or personal messages for the holiday season.
