# Summer Camp Parental Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to provide consent for their child to participate in the summer camp program. It collects necessary information about the camper, including emergency contacts, medical conditions, and allergies. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure your child's safe participation in the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2. Start by filling in the **Campers Information** page with your child's name, address, and any other relevant information.
3. On the **Emergency Contacts** page, list all emergency contacts, including phone numbers and addresses.
4. On the **Medical Conditions** page, report any medical conditions your child has, such as allergies or chronic illnesses.
5. If necessary, add any additional medical conditions on the **Additional Medical Conditions** page.
6. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campers Information**: Please enter your child's name, address, phone number, and any other relevant information.
	+ Required: This field is not required, but it's essential to have accurate and up-to-date information for emergency purposes.
* **Emergency Contacts**: List all emergency contacts, including names, phone numbers, addresses, and relationships (e.g., father, mother, guardian).
	+ Required: Yes, this field is required for emergency contact information.
* **Medical Conditions**: Report any medical conditions your child has, such as allergies or chronic illnesses.
	+ Required: No, this field is not required, but it's essential to report any medical conditions that may impact your child's participation in the camp.
* **Allergies**: If your child has any allergies, please list them here.
	+ Required: No, this field is not required, but it's essential to report any allergies that may impact your child's participation in the camp.
* **Additional Medical Conditions**: If your child has any additional medical conditions not listed in the previous field, please select them from the options provided or specify them in the text box.
	+ Required: No, this field is not required, but it's essential to report any additional medical conditions that may impact your child's participation in the camp.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the camp staff for assistance.
* This form is a condition of your child's participation in the summer camp program. If not completed, your child may not be able to participate.
