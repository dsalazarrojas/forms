# Auction Item Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auction Item Registration Form is used to gather essential information about an auction item, including its category, condition, estimated value, and other relevant details. The form is typically completed by the consignor, who is the seller or owner of the item being auctioned.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your full name and email address as the consignor.
2. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for further details.
3. Enter your organization name (if applicable).
4. Enter your mailing address for correspondence.
5. Enter the name of the item to be auctioned.
6. Select the category that best fits your item.
7. Enter a detailed description of the item, including its condition, features, and provenance (if applicable).
8. Describe the item's condition, using the provided scale (New, Like New, Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Repair).
9. Enter the estimated value of the item in USD.
10. Enter the minimum bid amount for the auction.
11. Enter an optional reserve price (if applicable).
12. Enter the item's dimensions (if applicable).
13. Enter the item's weight for shipping estimates (if applicable).
14. Indicate whether you can provide photos of the item.
15. Select the availability for pickup of the item.
16. Indicate if you are interested in tax deduction documentation.
17. Confirm your agreement to the consignor terms and conditions.
18. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consignor Full Name** (1): Enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter the email address you want to be contacted at for updates and inquiries.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization Name** (4): If you represent an organization, enter its name (if applicable).
* **Mailing Address** (5): Enter your full address for correspondence.
* **Item Name** (6): Enter the name or title of the auction item.
* **Item Category** (7): Select the category that best fits your item from the provided list.
* **Item Description** (8): Provide a detailed description of the item, including its condition, features, and provenance (if applicable).
* **Item Condition** (9): Select the condition of the item using the provided scale (New, Like New, Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Repair).
* **Estimated Value** (10): Enter the estimated value of the item in USD.
* **Minimum Bid Amount** (11): Enter the starting bid amount for the auction.
* **Reserve Price** (12): Enter an optional reserve price for the auction.
* **Item Dimensions** (13): Enter the item's dimensions (if applicable).
* **Item Weight** (14): Enter the item's weight for shipping estimates (if applicable).
* **Photos Included** (15): Indicate if you can provide photos of the item.
* **Item Pickup Availability** (16): Select the availability for pickup of the item.
* **Tax Deduction Interest** (17): Indicate if you are interested in tax deduction documentation.
* **Consignor Agreement** (18): Confirm your agreement to the consignor terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you understand the consignor terms and conditions before agreeing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the auction staff for assistance.
