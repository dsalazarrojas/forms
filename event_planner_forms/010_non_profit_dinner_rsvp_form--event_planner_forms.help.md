# Non Profit Dinner RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to RSVP to a non-profit dinner event. It helps the event organizers gather essential information for event planning and logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select a date you plan to attend from the "Date" field.
4. Choose the time you plan to arrive in the "Time" field.
5. Indicate your role in the event by selecting either "Guest" or "Host" in the "RSVP Type" field. If you are attending without a specific role, you can still select "Guest" as the default option.
6. If you are bringing guests, enter the number of guests you plan to bring in the "Guests" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **eventplannerform**: This is the main title of the form, which is used for identification purposes only.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Date**: Choose a date you plan to attend from the given options or select a date not listed to indicate your preferred date.
* **Time**: Choose a time you plan to arrive at the event location.
* **rsvp_type**: Indicate if you are attending as a "Guest", "Host", or both (if you are organizing the event). Selecting "Host" implies that you are taking care of logistics and arrangements for the event.
* **Guests**: Enter the number of guests you plan to bring to the event.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes that might help the event organizers with event planning.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
