# Event Insurance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Insurance Request Form is a tool to help you gather information for event-related insurance requests. This form will help you understand what type of insurance is needed and provide essential details for the insurance provider.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   Provide accurate and complete information for all fields.
*   Make sure to select the correct insurance type for your event.
*   Select the appropriate risk level for your event.
*   Choose the correct insurance provider for your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select_one, required):
    The type of insurance you need for your event. Options include:
    \- Liability Insurance: Covers liability for damages or injuries to people or property.
    \- Event Cancellation Insurance: Covers cancellation of the event due to unforeseen circumstances.
    \- Weather Insurance: Covers events affected by severe weather conditions.
    \- Accident Insurance: Covers accidents or injuries during the event.
*   **Insurance Amount** (`insurance_amount`, number, optional):
    The amount of insurance coverage you need for your event. This can be an amount of money or a percentage of the event total cost.
*   **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, optional):
    The start date of your event.
*   **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, optional):
    The end date of your event.
*   **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, optional):
    The start time of your event.
*   **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, time, optional):
    The end time of your event.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional):
    The location of your event.
*   **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional):
    A brief description of your event.
*   **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, text, optional):
    A brief description of the potential risks associated with your event.
*   **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, required):
    The risk level of your event, which can be:
    \- Low
    \- Medium
    \- High
    \- Very High
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_one, required):
    The insurance provider that you would like to cover your event. Options include:
    \- Liability Insurance Co.
    \- Event Cancellation Insurance Co.
    \- Weather Insurance Co.
    \- Accident Insurance Co.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for all fields.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask your event coordinator or insurance representative for guidance.
*   If you need help filling out this form, please contact our event insurance department.
