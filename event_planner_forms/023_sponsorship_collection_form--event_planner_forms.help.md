# sponsorship_collection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect sponsorship information for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the sponsor in the **sponsor_name** field.
2. Provide the email address of the sponsor in the **sponsor_email** field.
3. Enter the phone number of the sponsor in the **sponsor_phone** field.
4. Select the sponsor type from the list of options in the **sponsor_type** field.
5. Choose the sponsor level from the list of options in the **sponsor_level** field.
6. Enter the year the sponsor started supporting the event in the **sponsor_since_year** field.
7. Enter the month the sponsor started supporting the event in the **sponsor_since_month** field.
8. Enter the date the sponsor started supporting the event in the **sponsor_since_date** field.
9. Enter the time the sponsor started supporting the event in the **sponsor_since_time** field.
10. Enter the time zone where the sponsor is located in the **sponsor_since_time_zone** field.
11. Enter any additional information about the sponsor in the **sponsor_since_note** field.
12. Repeat step 11 for any other notes fields, such as **sponsor_since_note2**, **sponsor_since_note3**, and so on.
