# Camp Leaders Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Camp Leaders Event Registration Form is used for registering and managing the details of event attendees. Please ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out all required fields.
* For select fields, choose the most accurate option from the dropdown.
* For text fields, enter your information.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leader First Name** (`leader_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Leader Last Name** (`leader_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization you represent.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your position title.
* **Years of Leadership Experience** (`years_experience`, select_one, required): Select the number of years you have in leadership experience.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Number of Campers You Lead** (`number_of_campers`, select_one, required): Select the approximate number of campers you lead.
* **Camper Age Range** (`camper_age_range`, select_multiple, required): Select all the age ranges of campers you lead.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to register for the event.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_multiple, required): Select any dietary restrictions you may have.
* **Accommodation Needed** (`accommodation_needed`, select_one, optional): If you need lodging, select the type of accommodation you need.
* **Transportation Assistance Needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, optional): If you need transportation information, select the type of transportation you need.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select any workshop preferences you have.
* **Networking Interest** (`networking_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of interest in networking sessions.
* **Would You Like to Present or Lead a Session** (`presentation_interest`, select_one, required): Choose if you would like to present or lead a session.
* **Proposed Presentation Topic** (`presentation_topic`, text, optional): If presenting, enter the topic you would like to discuss.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of t-shirt you need.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay for the event.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or needs.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event** (`how_heard_about_event`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ways you found out about the event.
