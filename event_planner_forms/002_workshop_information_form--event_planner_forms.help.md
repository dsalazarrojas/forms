# Workshop Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Information Form is designed to collect and store information about workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide general information about the workshop, such as a brief description.
2. Enter specific details about the workshop, including date and time.
3. Choose the location where the workshop will take place.
4. Select the type of workshop you are offering or attending.
5. Choose the language in which the workshop will be conducted.
6. Select the level of the workshop.
7. Enter the expected capacity of the workshop.
8. Enter the duration of the workshop.
9. Enter the fee to be charged for the workshop.
10. Provide the workshop venue details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the workshop.
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional specific details about the workshop.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of the workshop.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, required: false):
  + Select the venue where the workshop will be held.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  + Choose the type of workshop you are offering or attending.
* **Workshop Language** (`workshop_language`, select_one, required: false): 
  + Select the language in which the workshop will be conducted.
* **Workshop Level** (`workshop_level`, select_one, required: false): 
  + Choose the level of the workshop.
* **Workshop Capacity** (`workshop_capacity`, number, required: false): Enter the expected number of attendees.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the workshop in minutes.
* **Workshop Fee** (`workshop_fee`, number, required: false): Enter the fee to be charged for the workshop.
* **Workshop Venue** (`workshop_venue`, text, required: false): Enter the address or details of the venue where the workshop will take place.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in to the best of your ability.
* If you need help or clarification, please reach out to the administrator.
* The form will help to centralize information about workshops, making it easier to manage and track them.
