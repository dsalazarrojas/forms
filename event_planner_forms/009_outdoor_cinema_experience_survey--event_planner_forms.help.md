# Outdoor Cinema Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect your feedback and experiences about the outdoor cinema event you attended. Your input will help us improve future outdoor cinema experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Provide your user information in the first section, including your name and other relevant details.
2. Share your experience details, describing what you enjoyed or disliked about the event.
3. Rate your overall experience using the provided options.
4. Leave any additional feedback you have about the event.
5. Select the locations that you visited during the event (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Please enter your name and other relevant contact details.
* **Experience Details** (`experience_details`, text, required): Share your experience at the outdoor cinema event. Describe what you enjoyed or disliked about the event.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Choose how you would rate your experience at the event, using the options below.
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Fair
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Leave any additional feedback about the event.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): If you visited multiple locations during the event, select all that apply.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4

## Tips
* Take a moment to reflect on your experience before answering the questions.
* Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

This form is for the outdoor cinema event, and your input will help us improve future events. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
