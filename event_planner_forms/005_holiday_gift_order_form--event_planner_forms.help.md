# Holiday Gift Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the holiday gift order form.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by reviewing and selecting the type of gift to purchase.
- Next, enter the recipient's email and phone number details.
- Choose a gift item and select its quantity.
- If applicable, decide on the buy now, pay later option and the payment method.
- Enter the payment amount and total amount for the order.
- Provide a gift message if desired.
- Enter the shipping address and any additional notes.
- Finally, select the payment date and time, and the payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Gifts** (`holiday_gifts`, `select_one`): Select the type of gift you want to purchase.
- **Recipient** (`recipient`, `select_one`): Check 'Yes' to indicate the recipient's information is provided.
- **Email** (`recipient_email`, `email`): Enter the recipient's email address.
- **Phone** (`recipient_phone`, `text`): Enter the recipient's phone number.
- **Gift Item** (`gift_item`, `select_one`): Select the gift item you wish to purchase.
- **Quantity** (`gift_quantity`, `number`): Enter the quantity of the gift item.
- **Buy Now, Pay Later** (`buy_now_pay_later`, `select_one`): Choose 'True' to enable this option.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`): Select the payment method.
- **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`): Enter the payment amount.
- **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, `text`): Optional message for the recipient.
- **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, `number`): Enter the total payment total.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`): Enter the shipping address.
- **City** (`shipping_city`, `text`): Enter the city.
- **State** (`shipping_state`, `select_one`): Select the state for the shipping address.
- **Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `text`): Enter the zip code.
- **Notes** (`shipping_notes`, `text`): Enter any additional shipping notes.
- **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`): Select the payment date.
- **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, `time`): Select the payment time.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`): Choose the payment terms.
- **Notes (2)** (`notes`, `text`): Enter any additional notes.
- **Payment Total Amount** (`payment_total_amount`, `number`): Enter the payment total amount.
- **Customer Name** (`payment_customer_name`, `text`): Enter the customer's name.
- **Customer Email** (`payment_customer_email`, `email`): Enter the customer's email.
- **Customer Phone** (`payment_customer_phone`, `text`): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Payment Date Picker** (`payment_date_picker`, `date`): Select the payment date.
