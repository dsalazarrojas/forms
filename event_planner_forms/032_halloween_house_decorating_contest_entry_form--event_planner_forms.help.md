# Halloween House Decorating Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for users to enter the Halloween House Decorating Contest. The contest is designed to encourage users to get creative with their Halloween decorations, and the form allows them to submit their house's address, a brief description of their house's theme, and any relevant images or videos of their decorations. The contest is open to all residents of the area and is meant to promote community spirit and friendly competition among neighbors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your house's address, including the street, city, state, and zip code.
2. Enter your house's name to help us identify your entry.
3. Choose the theme of your Halloween decorations.
4. Describe the theme of your house in a few sentences.
5. If you have a picture or video that showcases your theme, provide the URL.
6. Add your phone number and email address so we can contact you with updates and questions.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **House Address** (1, Text, Required): Enter your house's street address where you live.
* **House Name** (2, Text, Required): Enter your house's name to help us identify your entry.
* **House Decorating Theme** (3, Text, Optional): Choose the theme of your Halloween decorations (e.g., spooky, spooky-sweet, etc.).
* **Theme Description** (4, Text, Optional): Briefly describe the theme of your house's decorations.
* **Street** (5, Text, Optional): Enter the street where you live.
* **City** (6, Text, Optional): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (7, Text, Optional): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip** (8, Text, Optional): Enter the zip code where you live.
* **Phone** (9, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email** (10, Text, Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Theme Picture URL** (11, Text, Optional): If you have a picture that showcases your theme, provide the URL.
* **YouTube Video URL** (12, Text, Optional): If you have a video that showcases your theme, provide the URL.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid street address, city, state, and zip code for your house.
* Use a descriptive theme that reflects your house's decorations.
* If you have a picture or video that you'd like to share, make sure to provide the correct URL.
* We'll use the information provided to display your entry in our contest gallery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
