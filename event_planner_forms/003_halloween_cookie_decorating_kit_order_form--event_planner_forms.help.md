# Halloween Cookie Decorating Kit Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Halloween Cookie Decorating Kit Order Form is designed to gather information from customers who want to order a custom cookie decorating kit for a specific event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone).
2. Select the decoration theme and type that suits your needs.
3. Enter the event details (Event Date, Number of Guests, Number of Cookies).
4. Choose the shipping method and provide the shipping details (Ship To, Ship Address, Ship City, Ship State, Ship Zip, Ship Country).
5. Select the payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Cash).
6. Review your order and comments before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event for which you are ordering the cookie decorating kit.
* **Number of Guests** (`Number of Guests`, `number`, required): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Number of Cookies** (`Number of Cookies`, `number`, required): Enter the number of cookies you want to include in the kit.
* **Decoration Theme** (`Decoration Theme`, `select_one`, required): Choose the Halloween theme for your cookies (Halloween).
* **Decoration Type** (`Decoration Type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the decoration types you want to include in your kit (e.g., Cupcake, Cookie, Cake, Candy).
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`State`, `text`, required): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes for your order.
* **Order Comments** (`Order Comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments about your order.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to pay for your order (Credit Card, PayPal, Cash).
* **Credit Card Number** (`Credit Card Number`, `text`, required): Enter your credit card number.
* **CVV** (`CVV/Credit Card Verification Value`, `text`, required): Enter your CVV/credit card verification value.
* **Expiration Date** (`Expiration Date`, `date`, required): Enter the expiration date of your credit card.
* **Expiration Month** (`Expiration Month`, `select_one`, required): Choose the expiration month of your credit card.
* **Expiration Year** (`Expiration Year`, `select_one`, required): Choose the expiration year of your credit card.
* **Shipping Method** (`Shipping Method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the shipping method for your order (USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL).
* **Ship To** (`Ship To`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or company to ship to.
* **Ship Address** (`Ship Address`, `text`, required): Enter the address to ship to.
* **Ship City** (`Ship City`, `text`, required): Enter the city to ship to.
* **Ship State** (`Ship State`, `text`, required): Enter the state to ship to.
* **Ship Zip** (`Ship Zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code to ship to.
* **Ship Country** (`Ship Country`, `text`, required): Enter the country to ship to.
* **Ship Comments** (`Ship Comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments about the shipping details.
