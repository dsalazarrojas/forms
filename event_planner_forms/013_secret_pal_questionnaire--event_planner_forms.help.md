# Secret Pal Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information about participant preferences, gift preferences, and frequency of interaction with their Secret Pal. The collected information helps in creating a successful match between participants and their assigned Secret Pal.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start from the beginning and follow the instructions for each page.
2. Fill in the required fields with your accurate and honest preferences.
3. Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name, nickname, and other personal details.
* **Gift Preferences** (`gift_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose whether you would like to receive gifts from your Secret Pal.
* **Gift Budget** (`gift_budget`, `number`, required: false): Indicate your budget for gifts, if you have any preferences.
* **Gift Frequency** (`gift_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Choose how often you would like to receive gifts.
* **Pal Frequency** (`pal_frequency`, `text`, required: false): Suggest the frequency of interaction with your Secret Pal.
* **Pal Preferred Method** (`pal_preferred_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Check the methods of communication you would prefer with your Secret Pal (e.g., email, phone, chat).
* **Pal Preferred Frequency** (`pal_preferred_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Choose how frequently you would like to interact with your Secret Pal.
* **Pal Preferred Time** (`pal_preferred_time`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select your preferred time for interaction (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Pal Preferred Day** (`pal_preferred_day`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Indicate which days of the week you prefer for interaction.
* **Pal Preferred Month** (`pal_preferred_month`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose the months you prefer for interaction.
* **Pal Preferred Year** (`pal_preferred_year`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Indicate which months you prefer for interaction.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information about yourself that might help in matching.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication with your Secret Pal.
