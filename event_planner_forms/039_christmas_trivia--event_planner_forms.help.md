# Christmas Trivia - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Trivia form is designed to collect player information and answers to a series of Christmas-themed trivia questions. This form is intended for entertainment purposes, and participants will be scored based on their knowledge of Christmas traditions and history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your player information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Answer the Christmas trivia questions in the provided fields. Each question will have four possible answers, and you can choose the correct one from the dropdown menu.
3. Make sure to submit your form before time runs out!
