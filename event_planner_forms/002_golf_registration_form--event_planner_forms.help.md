# Golf Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Registration Form is used to collect contact information, team details, and event information from users registering for a golf event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Team Information" option:
    - Click on "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are registering as part of a team.
2.  Provide contact information:
    - Enter your name and other relevant contact details.
3.  Add team details (if applicable):
    - If you are part of a team, provide more information about your team.
4.  Enter event information (if necessary):
    - Provide any additional event-related details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false):
    *   Enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
*   **Team Information** (`team_info`, `select_multiple`, true):
    *   Select "Yes" if you are registering as part of a team, "No" otherwise.
*   **Event Details** (`event_info`, `text`, false):
    *   Enter any additional event-related information.
*   **Team Details** (`team_details`, `text`, false):
    *   If you are part of a team, provide more information about your team.

## Tips
*   Be accurate and clear when providing contact information.
*   Ensure that you provide the required team information.
*   Add any necessary event information.
*   Provide team details if you are part of a team.
