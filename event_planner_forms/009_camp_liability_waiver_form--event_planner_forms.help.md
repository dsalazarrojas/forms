# Camp Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the necessary information from parents or guardians of campers to ensure their safety and well-being during their stay at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and relationship to the camper.
2. Enter your phone number and email address.
3. Provide the camper's name, date of birth, and emergency contact information.
4. Acknowledge that you understand the camp activities involve inherent risks and that you assume all risks of participation.
5. Confirm that you understand the camp is not liable for injuries or property damage.
6. Sign and confirm that you release and waive any claims against the camp organizers.
7. Provide your signature and date of agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Relationship to Camper**: Choose one of the options to indicate your relationship to the camper (Parent, Guardian, Grandparent, or Other).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so that the camp can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so that you can receive important updates and notifications from the camp.
* **Camper Name**: Enter the name of the camper.
* **Camper Date of Birth**: Enter the camper's date of birth.
* **Known Medical Conditions or Allergies**: If the camper has any known medical conditions or allergies, please list them here.
* **Current Medications**: If the camper is taking any medications, please list them here.
* **Family Physician Name and Phone**: If applicable, please provide the name and phone number of the camper's family physician.
* **Health Insurance Provider and Policy Number**: If applicable, please provide the camper's health insurance provider and policy number.
* **Follow Camp Rules**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the camp's rules and instructions.
* **Conduct Agreement**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the camper's conduct during the camp.
* **Parent Supervision Agreement**: Confirm that you give permission for your child to participate in all camp activities.
* **Release and Waiver**: Confirm that you release and waive any claims against the camp organizers.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Agreement Confirmation**: Confirm that you have read and understand this liability waiver.
