# Baseball Simulator Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Baseball Simulator Waiver Form is a necessary step before participating in the baseball simulator experience. It's a document that protects the facility and its operators from potential liability by ensuring that participants understand and acknowledge the risks associated with the activity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please fill out the form carefully**: Take your time to complete the form accurately and make sure to answer each question truthfully.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Ensure that the details you provide are correct and up-to-date.
3. **Read and understand each question carefully**: Don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need clarification on any section.
4. **Sign and confirm**: Once you've completed the form, review and sign it carefully to confirm that you've read and understood the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**
	+ **First name**: Please enter your first name.
	+ **Last name**: Please enter your last name.
	+ **Date of birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
	+ **Email address**: Please enter your email address.
	+ **Phone number**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact**
	+ **Emergency contact name**: Please enter the name of the person you've listed as your emergency contact.
	+ **Relationship to participant**: Please select the relationship between the participant and emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).
	+ **Emergency contact phone**: Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Health Information**
	+ **Do you have any medical conditions?**: Please select whether you have any medical conditions.
	+ **If yes, please describe**: If you've selected 'Yes', please provide a brief description of your medical conditions.
	+ **Are you taking any medications?**: Please select whether you're taking any medications.
	+ **If yes, please describe**: If you've selected 'Yes', please provide a brief description of your medications.
* **Risk Acknowledgment**
	+ **I acknowledge the risks**: Please select whether you acknowledge the risks associated with the simulator experience.
	+ **I am in adequate physical condition**: Please select whether you're in adequate physical condition to use the simulator safely.
* **Release and Waiver**
	+ **I release the facility and operators from liability**: Please select whether you agree to release the facility and its operators from liability for injuries resulting from simulator use.
	+ **I understand the facility is not liable**: Please select whether you understand that the facility is not liable for damages or injuries from your participation.
	+ **I assume all risks**: Please select whether you assume all risks associated with this activity.
* **Parent/Guardian Consent (if under 18)**
	+ **Parent/guardian consent**: If you're under 18, please select whether your parent/guardian consents to your participation.
* **Final Confirmation**
	+ **I confirm I have read and understood all terms**: Please select whether you've read and understood all terms of this waiver.
