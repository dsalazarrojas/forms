# Event Sound And Lighting Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect event details and sound and lighting requirements to provide referrals to suitable sound and lighting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields on each page. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk (\*) on each page.
2. Provide clear and accurate information in the text fields. If you need to add multiple details, separate them with commas.
3. For multiple event pages, scroll down to the next page or click the "Next" button to continue filling out the form.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send the form.
