# Halloween Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Quiz is a form designed to collect information about users' preferences and habits related to Halloween. By filling out this form, users will be able to express their opinions on various Halloween-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and select your responses based on your personal preferences and habits.
2. For questions that ask you to choose a favorite item, such as a movie or candy, select the option that best fits your taste.
3. If you have a different favorite, not listed in the options, you can provide it in the "Any other Halloween activities you'd like to do" field.
4. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and don't worry about the required fields, as they are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Halloween Quiz Introduction** (`first_page`, `text`, not required):
	* This field is an introduction to the form. Read through it to understand the context and purpose of the quiz.
* **What is your favorite Halloween movie?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, not required):
	* Select one of the provided options that represents your favorite Halloween movie.
* **How old are you?** (`question_2`, `number`, not required):
	* Provide your age on this question.
* **How do you plan to celebrate Halloween?** (`question_3`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	* Select all the ways you plan to celebrate Halloween, such as going to a costume party or staying home.
* **When is your birthday?** (`question_4`, `date`, not required):
	* Provide the date of your birthday.
* **What time do you go to bed?** (`question_5`, `time`, not required):
	* Provide the time you usually go to bed on a regular night.
* **What is your favorite Halloween candy?** (`question_6`, `select_one`, not required):
	* Select one of the provided options that represents your favorite Halloween candy.
* **What is your favorite color?** (`question_7`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	* Select all the colors that you like during the Halloween season.
* **When do you usually eat your candy?** (`question_8`, `time`, not required):
	* Provide the time you usually eat your Halloween candy.
* **Any other Halloween activities you'd like to do?** (`question_10`, `note`, not required):
	* If you have any other Halloween activities that you'd like to do, provide them in this field.
* **Email us** (`question_11`, `email`, not required):
	* Provide your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone number** (`question_12`, `text`, not required):
	* Provide your phone number so we can contact you back.
* **What is your favorite Halloween song?** (`question_9`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	* Select all the Halloween songs that you enjoy.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at the email address provided in the "Email us" field.
* The information collected will be used to create a fun and spooky experience for you on the Halloween season.
