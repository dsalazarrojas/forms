# food_security_seminar_volunteer_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The food security seminar volunteer form is used to register volunteers for food security seminars. This form should be filled out by users who are interested in joining a food security seminar as a volunteer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event details, including the start and end dates of the event.
2. Provide your volunteer information, including your name, event type, and any other relevant details.
3. Select the event location where the seminar will take place.
4. Choose the volunteers who will be helping at the event.
5. Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter the details of the event you are interested in, including start and end dates.
- **Volunteer Information** (`volunteer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, any relevant information about yourself, and any other details about your volunteering experience.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the seminar will take place.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the event.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the event.
- **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of event you are interested in helping with (Event 1, Event 2, Event 3).
- **Volunteers** (`volunteers`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who else will be helping at the event (Volunteer 1, Volunteer 2, Volunteer 3).
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Choose the correct event type and location.
* Select the correct volunteers to ensure the event runs smoothly.
