# Holiday Party Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help us plan the holiday party by gathering information about your availability to attend. Please fill out this form to let us know if you can join us and if so, what time you can come.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select a date that you are available to attend the party.
2. Choose a time that you can come to the party.
3. Choose whether you will be attending the party by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. If you have any constraints or special requests, type them in the "Constraints" field.
5. If you have any additional notes or comments, type them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Party Date
* **Party Date** (`holiday_party_availability_survey_date`, date, not required): This field is for selecting a date that you are available to attend the party.

### Party Time
* **Party Time** (`holiday_party_availability_survey_time`, time, not required): This field is for choosing a time that you can come to the party.

### Will You Attend
* **Will You Attend** (`holiday_party_availability_survey_availability`, select_one, not required): This field is for choosing whether you will attend the party by selecting "Yes" or "No".

### Constraints
* **Constraints** (`holiday_party_availability_survey_constraints`, text, not required): This field is for listing any constraints or special requests you may have for attending the party.

### Note
* **Note** (`holiday_party_availability_survey_availability_note`, note, not required): This field is for leaving any additional notes or comments that you think are relevant to the party planning.

# Tips

* Be accurate when selecting your party date and time so we can plan accordingly.
* If you have any specific constraints or requests, please be sure to include them in the "Constraints" field.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, please include them in the "Note" field.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
