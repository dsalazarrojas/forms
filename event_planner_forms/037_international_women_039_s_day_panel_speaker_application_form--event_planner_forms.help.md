# International Women's Day Panel Speaker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Women's Day Panel Speaker Application Form is designed to collect information from potential speakers who want to participate in the International Women's Day event. The form helps us understand your experience, skills, and availability to determine whether you'd be a great fit for the event. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring you answer all required fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the Speaker Information page**: This is the first page of the form. Please provide your basic details in the required fields.
2. **Fill out the Contact Details page**: This page is for your contact information, including your email and phone number.
3. **Select your Experience**: Choose whether you have the required experience for the event.
4. **Select your Skills**: Choose the relevant skills you possess that align with the event's focus.
5. **Select your Availability**: Choose the date and time you are available to participate in the event.
6. **Select your Topics**: Choose the topics you are comfortable speaking on for the event.
7. **Fill out your Bio**: This is your chance to introduce yourself and your story.
8. **Upload your Photo**: If you have a photo, please upload it for promotional purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Information (text)**: Enter your name and any other identifying details you'd like us to know.
* **Contact Details (text)**: Provide your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Experience (select_multiple)**: Answer "Yes" if you have experience in areas relevant to the event, such as public speaking, event organization, or leadership.
* **Skills (select_multiple)**: Choose the skills you have that align with the event's focus, such as languages, presentation skills, or social media management.
* **Availability (date)**: Choose a date and time that you are available to participate in the event.
* **Topics (select_multiple)**: Choose the topics you are comfortable speaking on for the event.
* **Bio (text)**: Tell us a little bit about yourself, including your background, interests, and qualifications.
* **Photo Upload (text)**: Please upload a clear, recent photo of yourself for promotional purposes.
