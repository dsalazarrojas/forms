# Online Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an online event, including its details, attendee information, date and time, registration status, type, location, description, organizer, contact email, and contact phone number. The goal is to gather all necessary information to register and plan the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Provide the title of the event in the `Event Details` field.
*   Enter the information of the attendees in the `Attendee Info` field.
*   Select the date of the event from the calendar in the `Event Date` field.
*   Choose the time of the event from the clock in the `Event Time` field.
*   Select the registration status of the event from the options in the `Registration Status` field.
*   Choose the type of the event from the options in the `Event Type` field.
*   Enter the location of the event in the `Event Location` field.
*   Describe the event in the `Event Description` field.
*   Enter the name or organization hosting the event in the `Event Organizer` field.
*   Enter the contact email of the organizer in the `Contact Email` field.
*   Enter the phone number of the organizer in the `Contact Phone` field.
*   Enter the address of the event in the `Event Address` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter the title of the event.
*   **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, text, required): Enter the information about the attendees, such as their names and contact details.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the event from the clock.
*   **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the event from the options: Active, Pending, Completed, Cancelled.
*   **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of the event from the options: Conference, Webinar, Meetup, Workshop.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
*   **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required): Describe the event in detail.
*   **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name or organization hosting the event.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the organizer.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the organizer.
*   **Event Address** (`event_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the event.
