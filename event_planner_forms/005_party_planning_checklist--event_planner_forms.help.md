# Party Planning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about party planning details to ensure a successful event.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide the name of your party in the **Guest List** field.
2.  Enter the date of your party in the **Date** field.
3.  Enter the time of your party in the **Time** field.
4.  Provide the location of your party in the **Location** field.
5.  Indicate if you will be sending **Invitations** to guests.
6.  Choose whether you will be providing **Food and Beverages** at the party.
7.  Select the type of **Entertainment** you will have at the party.
8.  Provide the details of your **Decorations** in the text field.
9.  Indicate if you will be giving out **Party Favors** to guests.
10.  Choose whether gifts will be **Gifts** for the occasion.
11.  Select the **Follow-up** date and time with guests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Guest List**: Enter the name of your party.
*   **Date**: Enter the date of your party in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of your party in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **Location**: Enter the location of your party, e.g. home, restaurant, park, etc.
*   **Invitations**: Choose if you will be sending invitations to guests.
	+ 'Yes' means you will be sending out invitations.
	+ 'No' means you will not be sending out invitations.
*   **Food and Beverages**: Choose if you will be providing food and beverages at the party.
	+ 'Yes' means you will be providing food and beverages.
	+ 'No' means you will not be providing food and beverages.
*   **Entertainment**: Choose the type of entertainment you will have at the party.
	+ 'Yes' means you will have entertainment.
	+ 'No' means you will not have entertainment.
*   **Decorations**: Enter the details of your decorations.
*   **Party Favors**: Choose if you will be giving out party favors.
	+ 'Yes' means you will be giving out party favors.
	+ 'No' means you will not be giving out party favors.
*   **Gifts**: Choose if gifts will be exchanged.
	+ 'Yes' means gifts will be exchanged.
	+ 'No' means gifts will not be exchanged.
*   **Follow-up**: Enter the date and time for a follow-up with guests.
    (This field should be a date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY)

## Tips

- Be as specific as possible when filling out this form to ensure accurate planning.
- Use the **Guest List** field to keep track of all the guests attending the party.
- Double-check the **Date**, **Time**, and **Location** fields to ensure you have accurate information.
- Be prepared to answer any follow-up questions from guests on the **Follow-up** date.
- This form is meant to be a general guide; feel free to add or modify fields based on your specific party needs.
