# online_ticket_purchase_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in purchasing tickets for an online event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information. Make sure to provide accurate and clear answers to help event organizers better understand your needs and interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **purchase_details** (`purchase_details`, text, optional): Enter details about your purchase, such as the type of tickets you're interested in or any special requests.
* **event_info** (`event_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the event you're attending, such as the event name or date.
* **attendee_info** (`attendee_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the attendees in your group, such as their names and contact information.
* **tickets** (`tickets`, text, optional): Specify the number and type of tickets you're interested in purchasing.
