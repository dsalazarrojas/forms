# Vendor Booth Layout Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vendor Booth Layout Revision Request Form" is designed to collect specific details from users requesting changes to vendor booth layouts for an event. This form is meant to be filled out by event organizers, planners, or managers who require adjustments to ensure a smooth event execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Vendor Booth Layout Revision Request** (Required): Choose 'Yes' if you require changes to a vendor booth layout.
2. **Request Details** (Required): Select 'Yes' if you need further details about your request.
3. **Request Details Date** (Required): Enter the date of your request.
4. **Request Details Time** (Required): Enter the time of your request.
5. **Additional Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes you might have about your request.
6. **Attachments** (Optional): Upload any supporting documents or files related to your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Booth Layout Revision Request (Required)**: This field indicates whether you need revisions to a vendor booth layout.
* **Request Details (Required)**: This field is used to confirm if you need further details about your request.
* **Request Details Date (Required)**: This field is for entering the date on which you are making your request.
* **Request Details Time (Required)**: This field is for entering the time of your request.
* **Additional Notes (Optional)**: Use this field to add any extra information that might be helpful for your request.
* **Attachments (Optional)**: Upload supporting documents or files for your request.
