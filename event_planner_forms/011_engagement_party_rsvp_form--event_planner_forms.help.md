# engagement_party_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The engagement party RSVP form is used to track guest attendance and responses for an engagement party. It helps event planners gather necessary information from invited guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your guest name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Specify the number of guests attending in the "Guest Count" field.
3. Choose the date for the party in the "Date" field.
4. Select the time for the party in the "Time" field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Optionally, add any comments or notes in the "Comments" and "Notes" fields.
8. Choose your attendance status in the "Are You Attending?" field.
9. Set a deadline for sending the RSVP in the "RSVP By" field.
10. Track the status of your RSVP and response in the "RSVP Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a guest of the party.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, optional): Specify the number of guests attending with you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the party.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of the party.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number for you or a representative.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a contact email for you or a representative.
* **Response** (`response`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes.
* **Are You Attending?** (`attending`, select_multiple, optional): Choose if you are attending or not.
* **RSVP By** (`rsvp_at`, date, optional): Set a deadline for sending your RSVP.
* **RSVP By** (`rsvp_by`, date, optional): Set a deadline for sending your RSVP, same as "RSVP By".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add additional comments or notes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes.
* **RSVP Sent** (`rsvp_sent`, select_one, optional): Track if the RSVP was sent.
* **RSVP Received** (`rsvp_received`, select_one, optional): Track if the RSVP was received.
* **Response Sent** (`response_sent`, select_one, optional): Track if the response was sent.
* **Response Received** (`response_received`, select_one, optional): Track if the response was received.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_one, optional): Track the status of the RSVP.
* **RSVP Comment** (`rsvp_comment`, text, optional): Add a comment for the RSVP.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): Add any follow-up information.
