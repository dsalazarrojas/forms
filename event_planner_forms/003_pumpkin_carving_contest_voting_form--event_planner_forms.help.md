# Pumpkin Carving Contest Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect votes and comments from end users for pumpkins in the Pumpkin Carving Contest. It guides users through inputting their name, a score for the pumpkin's creativity, and any comments they may have for the pumpkin. The form also allows judges to input their scores and comments for each pumpkin.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  If you are a voter, input your name in the "Submitter Name" field.
2.  Enter your score for the pumpkin's creativity in the "Score" field. This score will help determine the winner of the pumpkin carving contest.
3.  Optionally, you may enter any comments you wish to make about the pumpkin in the "Comments" field.
4.  If you are a judge, input your score for the pumpkin's creativity in the "Judge Score" field.
5.  Optionally, you may enter any comments you wish to make about the pumpkin in the "Judge Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name as a voter or a judge.
*   **Score** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_score`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a score for the pumpkin's creativity. The score can be any number from 1-100.
*   **Comments** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_comment`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any comments you wish to make about the pumpkin.
*   **Submitter Name** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_submitter_name`, `text`, required/optional): If you are a judge, ignore. This field is only used for record-keeping and will automatically be filled out by the system.
*   **Judge Score** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_judge_score`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your score for the pumpkin's creativity as a judge.
*   **Judge Comments** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_judge_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any comments you wish to make about the pumpkin as a judge.
*   **Email** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_voter_email`, `email`, required/optional): If you are a judge, ignore. This field is only used for record-keeping and will automatically be filled out by the system.
*   **Phone** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_voter_phone`, `text`, required/optional): If you are a judge, ignore. This field is only used for record-keeping and will automatically be filled out by the system.
*   **Judge Email** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_judge_email`, `email`, required/optional): If you are a voter, ignore. This field is only used for record-keeping and will automatically be filled out by the system.
*   **Judge Phone** (`pumpkin_carving_contest_voting_form_judge_phone`, `text`, required/optional): If you are a voter, ignore. This field is only used for record-keeping and will automatically be filled out by the system.
