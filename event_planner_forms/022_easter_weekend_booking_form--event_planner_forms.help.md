# easter_weekend_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Weekend Booking Form is designed to help users plan and book their activities or accommodations for the Easter weekend. This form is used to collect information about users' arrival and departure dates, guest information, and their selections of activities or accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Arrival Date** (id: 1) - The date of arrival for your activities or accommodations.
2. Enter your **Departure Date** (id: 2) - The date of departure for your activities or accommodations.
3. Enter your **Name** (id: 3) - The name of the guest.
4. Enter the **Number of Guests** (id: 4) - The number of guests planning to arrive.
5. Enter your **Email** (id: 5) - Contact email for the guest.
6. Enter your **Phone** (id: 6) - Contact phone number for the guest.
7. Enter any **Notes** (id: 7) - Any additional notes or requests.
8. Select your **Select Type** (id: 8) - A single selection of an activity or accommodation type.
9. Select multiple **Select Type 2** (id: 9) - Multiple selections of activities or accommodation types.
10. Select multiple **Select Type 3** (id: 10) - Multiple selections of activities or accommodation types.
11. Select multiple **Select Type 4** (id: 11) - Multiple selections of activities or accommodation types.
12. Select a single **Select Type 5** (id: 12) - A single selection of an activity or accommodation type.
