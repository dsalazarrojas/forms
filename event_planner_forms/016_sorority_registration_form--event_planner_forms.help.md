# Sorority Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sorority Registration Form is a crucial document for students who want to be part of a sorority. It collects essential information needed for the registration process. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth and successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by clicking on the "Contact Information" page, where you will be asked to provide your name, email address, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you about your sorority registration.
2. Next, move on to the "Student Information" page, where you will be asked to provide your student ID, student email address, and any other relevant student details. Ensure that you enter all the required information accurately.
3. On the "Upload Documents" page, you will be asked to upload any necessary documents, such as identification or proof of enrollment. Check the requirements carefully before uploading any documents.
4. On the "Signature" page, you will be asked to confirm whether you have read and understood the sorority's terms and conditions. Please select "Yes" if you have read and understood, and "No" otherwise.
5. Finally, on the "Enrollment Agreement" page, you will be asked to enter your enrollment details, including the semester you are planning to join. Please fill out this section carefully to ensure you get enrolled correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your name, email address, and phone number for the sorority to contact you.
* **Student Information (Page 2)** (`student_info`, text, required: false): Enter your student ID and student email address, and any other relevant student details.
* **Upload Documents (Page 3)** (`documents`, text, required: false): Upload any necessary documents, such as identification or proof of enrollment, carefully checking the requirements before uploading.
* **Signature (Page 4)** (`signature`, select_one, required: false): Confirm whether you have read and understood the sorority's terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Enrollment Agreement (Page 5)** (`enrollment_agreement`, text, required: false): Fill out your enrollment details, including the semester you plan to join the sorority, accurately to ensure correct enrollment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary documents and information ready before starting the form.
* Double-check the required fields to avoid errors.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the sorority directly.
* Review and carefully read the sorority's terms and conditions before signing.
