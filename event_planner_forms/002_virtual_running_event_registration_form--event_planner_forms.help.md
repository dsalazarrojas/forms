# Virtual Running Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registration of participants in a virtual running event. It will help us collect relevant information about each participant, including demographic details, medical history, and other important information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, which are marked with an *.
2. Enter your name and email address for verification purposes.
3. Select your preferred t-shirt size and occupation from the available options.
4. If you have any medical conditions, please specify the details in the medical condition details field.
5. Answer the questions regarding your medical history, including having a medical doctor.
6. Once you have completed all the fields, review your submission before clicking submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Text, Not Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Email** (Email, Not Required): Enter your email address. This is used for verification purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (Text, Not Required): Enter your phone number. This is optional.
* **Participant Gender** (Select, Not Required): Choose your gender from the provided options.
* **Participant Birthdate** (Date, Not Required): Enter your birthdate.
* **Event Type** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose the type of event you are participating in.
* * **T-Shirt Size** (Select, Required): Choose your preferred t-shirt size.
* **Participant Occupation** (Text, Not Required): Enter your occupation.
* **Participant Notes** (Note, Not Required): Enter any additional information you would like to share with the organizers.
* **Participant Athletic Background** (Select, Not Required): Choose whether you have a background in athletics.
* **Participant Medical Condition** (Select Multiple, Not Required): If you have any medical conditions, choose the relevant options.
* **Participant Road ID** (Select, Not Required): Choose whether you have a road ID.
* **Participant Medical Insurance** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose whether you have medical insurance.
* **Participant Medical Provider** (Select, Not Required): Choose whether you have a medical provider.
* **Participant Medical Doctor** (Select, Not Required): Choose whether you have a medical doctor.
* **Participant Medical Condition Details** (Text, Not Required): If you have any medical conditions, enter the details here.
