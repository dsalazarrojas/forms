# Friends Trivia - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and survey responses on the Friends TV show.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite theme from the options on Page 1: What is the main theme of the Friends TV show?
2. On Page 2, choose the friends you enjoy from the options: Which of the following friends are part of the show's main cast?
3. Choose where the show primarily takes place on Page 3: Where does the show primarily take place?
4. Select your answer on Page 4: What is the year the show premiered?
5. Answer your favorite catchphrase on Page 5: What is your favorite catchphrase from the show?
6. Select your answer on Page 6: What is the main theme of the show?
7. Choose how you want to share your results on Page 7: How do you want to share your results?
8. Enter your score on Page 8: What is your score?
9. Write your final answer on Page 9: What is your final answer?

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the main theme of the Friends TV show?** (Select One)
	+ This question asks about the primary theme of the show.
* **Which of the following friends are part of the show's main cast?** (Select Multiple)
	+ This question asks you to choose the friends you enjoy watching on the show.
* **Where does the show primarily take place?** (Select One)
	+ This question asks about the main location where the show is set.
* **What is the year the show premiered?** (Select Multiple)
	+ This question asks you to select the correct year the show started.
* **What is your favorite catchphrase from the show?** (Text)
	+ This question asks you to write your favorite catchphrase from the show.
* **What is the main theme of the show?** (Select Multiple)
	+ This question asks you to choose the main theme of the show.
* **How do you want to share your results?** (Select One)
	+ This question asks you to choose how you want to share your results.
* **What is your score?** (Number)
	+ This question asks you to enter your score.
* **What is your final answer?** (Text)
	+ This question asks you to write your final answer.

## Tips
