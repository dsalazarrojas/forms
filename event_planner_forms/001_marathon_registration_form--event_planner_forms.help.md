# Marathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants registering for a marathon event. It is intended for event organizers or administrators to gather relevant details about each participant.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **Name** field. This is the name you will be known by during the event.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This will be used for communication related to the event.
3. Provide your emergency contact information in the **Emergency Contact** field. If you need to contact someone in case of an emergency, please enter their name and contact information.
4. If applicable, please fill in any relevant medical information in the **Medical Information** field. This is optional and will help us understand any special care requirements you may need.
5. Choose one of the options in the **Participant Information** field. This will help us understand your participation category.
6. If applicable, select one or more of the options in the **Additional Participant Info** field. These are additional participant details that can be relevant for the event.
7. Enter your age in the **Participant Age** field. This is a mandatory field and ensures we have accurate demographics.
8. For your participation gender, choose from the options in the **Gender** field. This is optional, but we will use this to tailor participant experiences.
9. Choose a date and time that works for you in the **Registration Time** field. This ensures we can schedule participant activities accordingly.
10. Finally, indicate your availability in the **Participant Availability** field. This helps us understand when you are available to participate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be known during the event.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for event communications.
* **Emergency Contact** (text, required): Provide the name and contact information for a person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Information** (text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that we should know, please enter them here.
* **Participant Information** (select_one, required): Choose a participant category that best describes your participation.
* **Event Info** (text, optional): This is a free-text field for any additional event-specific information.
* **Additional Participant Info** (checkbox, required): Select any additional participant details that apply.
* **Participant Age** (number, required): Enter your age to ensure accurate participant demographics.
* **Gender** (radio, optional): Choose your gender to help us tailor participant experiences.
* **Registration Time** (date, required): Select a date and time for your registration.
* **Participant Availability** (time, required): Indicate your available time for event participation.
* **Participant Notes** (note, optional): This is a free-text field for any additional participant notes.
