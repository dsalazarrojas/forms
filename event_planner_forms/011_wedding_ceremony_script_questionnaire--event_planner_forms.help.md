# Wedding Ceremony Script Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a wedding ceremony script to create a personalized and memorable experience for the couple.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Wedding Ceremony Details" section with the basic details of the ceremony, including the location, date, and time.
2. Provide the type of ceremony and any special requests or specific instructions for the script.
3. List the readings and vows that will be included in the ceremony, and choose whether they will have music or not.
4. Specify the formation and flow of the ceremony, including any special requests or requirements.
5. Choose the theme, color scheme, and other important details for the ceremony.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Wedding Ceremony Details
* **Wedding Ceremony Details** (`wedding_ceremony_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you will provide the basic details of the wedding ceremony, including the location, date, and time. Please be as specific as possible to ensure that the script accurately reflects the ceremony's details.

### Ceremony Location
* **Ceremony Location** (`ceremony_location`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you will provide the exact location of the wedding ceremony. Please include the building name, address, or a specific area of the location if applicable.

### Ceremony Date
* **Ceremony Date** (`ceremony_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is where you will provide the date of the wedding ceremony. Please use the format MM/DD/YY to ensure accurate input.

### Ceremony Time
* **Ceremony Time** (`ceremony_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is where you will provide the time of the wedding ceremony. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### Ceremony Type
* **Ceremony Type** (`ceremony_type`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you will specify the type of ceremony, such as civil, religious, or cultural.

### Ceremony Length
* **Ceremony Length** (`ceremony_length`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you will specify the expected length of the ceremony.
