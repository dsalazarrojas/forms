# Sponsorship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from sponsors about their event or activity, including details about the organization, contact person, and financial terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the sponsor.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and that all required fields are completed.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the sponsoring organization.
* **Contact Person** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the organization.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Sponsor Type** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of sponsorship (Sports, Cultural, Educational, Non-profit, or Other).
* **Sponsor Name** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Amount** (`7`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of the sponsorship.
* **Description** (`8`, `note`, required): Enter a brief description of the event or activity.
* **Event Date** (`9`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event or activity.
* **Start Time** (`10`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the event or activity.
* **End Time** (`11`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of the event or activity.
* **Sponsor Address** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the sponsor.
* **Organization Address** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the organization.
* **Contact Address** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information of the person or organization.
* **Payment Terms** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the payment terms and conditions of the sponsorship.
* **Payment Frequency** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the payment frequency (Once, Recurring).
* **Payment Amount** (`17`, `number`, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Sponsor Notes** (`18`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about the sponsorship.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter the terms and conditions of the sponsorship.
* **Contact Notes** (`20`, `note`, required): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Term of Agreement** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the term of agreement for the sponsorship.
* **Agreement Signed By** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Agreement Date** (`23`, `date`, required): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signed At** (`24`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Location** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the agreement was signed.
