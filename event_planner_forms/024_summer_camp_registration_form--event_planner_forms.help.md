# Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal data from both the camper and their emergency and parent/guardian contacts for summer camp registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email).
2. Provide your phone number.
3. Enter your Date of Birth.
4. Add an Emergency Contact's First Name, Last Name, and Phone Number.
5. Add an Emergency Contact's Email (if applicable).
6. Add a Parent/Guardian's First Name, Last Name, and Phone Number.
7. Add a Parent/Guardian's Email (if applicable).
8. Include any relevant Medical Information.
9. Enter any Camper Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the camper's first name as it is written on their identification documents (e.g., passport or driver's license).
* **Last Name**: Enter the camper's last name as it is written on their identification documents (e.g., passport or driver's license).
* **Email**: Enter the camper's email address for camp communication (e.g., camp announcements, schedules).
* **Phone**: Enter a contact phone number where the camper can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the camper's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 06/12/1990).
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the camper's emergency contact (e.g., parent or guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the camper's emergency contact (e.g., parent or guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the camper's emergency contact (e.g., parent or guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of the camper's emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Parent First Name**: Enter the first name of the camper's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Last Name**: Enter the last name of the camper's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the camper's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email**: Enter the email of the camper's parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Medical Information**: Enter any relevant medical information (e.g., allergies, medical conditions, medications).
* **Camper Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the camper's experience, allergies, or other relevant information.
