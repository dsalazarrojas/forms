# Wedding Reception Invitation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details about a wedding reception, allowing guests to get excited and RSVP in time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the wedding date and time on the first page.
2. Enter the venue where the wedding reception will take place.
3. Specify the dress code for guests (if any).
4. Choose your RSVP options from the list provided.
5. Enter your RSVP deadline, if one is specified.
6. Provide your contact email and phone number so that the wedding party can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Date** (Wedding Date): Enter the date of the wedding in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Wedding Time** (Wedding Time): Enter the time of the wedding in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Venue** (Venue): Type the name and address of the venue where the wedding will take place.
* **Dress Code** (Dress Code): Specify the dress code for guests (e.g., "Formal", "Casual", "Beach Formal").
* **RSVP** (RSVP): Choose one or multiple RSVP options that apply to you (e.g., "Attending", "Unable to Attend", "Maybe").
* **RSVP Deadline** (RSVP Deadline): Enter the date by which you need to RSVP.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email): Enter your email address where the wedding party can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone): Enter your phone number where the wedding party can contact you.
