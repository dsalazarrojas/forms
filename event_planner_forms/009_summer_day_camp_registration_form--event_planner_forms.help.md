# Summer Day Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register children for a summer day camp program. It asks for personal and contact information for both the child and their parent/guardian. This includes medical details and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter the child's phone number (if applicable).
3. Select the camp name and dates of attendance.
4. Choose the type of camp experience (e.g., full-day, half-day, etc.).
5. Enter the child's address and parent/guardian information.
6. Provide emergency contact name and phone number.
7. If applicable, list any allergies or medical conditions that require special attention.
8. Check the terms and conditions box to confirm you have read and agreed to them.
9. Enter additional information, such as any special instructions or special equipment needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (id: 1, required): Enter the child's first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, required): Enter the child's last name.
* **Email** (id: 3, required): Enter the child's email address.
* **Phone** (id: 4, optional): Enter the child's phone number (if applicable).
* **Camp Name** (id: 5, required): Select the name of the camp the child is attending.
* **Start Date** (id: 11, required): Enter the date the child will start attending the camp.
* **End Date** (id: 12, required): Enter the date the child will end attending the camp.
* **Select Type** (id: 13, required): Choose the type of camp experience (e.g., full-day, half-day, etc.).
* **Parent Name** (id: 16, required): Enter the parent/guardian's name.
* **Parent Phone** (id: 17, optional): Enter the parent/guardian's phone number (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 18, required): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 19, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (id: 23, optional): Explain the relationship between the child and the emergency contact.
* **Camp Address** (id: 24, required): Enter the address of the camp location.
* **Camp City** (id: 25, required): Enter the city where the camp is located.

**Tips**

* Please enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the best possible experience for your child.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
* A confirmation email will be sent to you once the registration is submitted.
