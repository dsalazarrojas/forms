# Virtual Family Event Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your attendance at virtual family events. Your submission will help us understand who is attending and plan for the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the event date in the **Event Date** field. This is the date of the virtual event you are attending.
2. In the **Event Name** field, provide a brief description of the event (e.g., "Virtual Family Reunion" or "Online Game Night").
3. Enter your **Attendee Name** in the field provided.
4. Type your **Attendee Email** in the space provided.
5. If you are bringing guests, enter the **Number of Guests** in the field.
6. If you have any additional comments or feedback about the event, you can provide them in the **Comments** field.
7. Select **Submitted By** to indicate whether you are making this submission on your own behalf or for someone else.
8. Finally, click **Submit** to complete and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Date**: Enter the date of the virtual event.
  - Type: date
  - Required: Yes
- **Event Name**: Enter a brief description of the event.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
- **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as it is spelled correctly.
  - Type: text
  - Required: Yes
- **Attendee Email**: Enter your email address.
  - Type: email
  - Required: Yes
- **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of people accompanying you to the event.
  - Type: number
  - Required: Yes
- **Comments**: Provide any additional information about the event.
  - Type: note
  - Required: No
- **Submitted By**: Select "Yes" if you are submitting on your own behalf, or "No" if you are submitting on behalf of someone else.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: Yes

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure correct tracking and reporting.
- If you have any issues or concerns about the form, please contact the event organizers.
- Submissions are required from all attendees, so please ensure that everyone attending submits their information.
