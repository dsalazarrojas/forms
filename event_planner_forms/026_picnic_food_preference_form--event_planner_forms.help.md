# Picnic Food Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Picnic Food Preference Form is designed to collect information about your food preferences and dietary needs for a picnic event. This form will help us provide the best possible experience for you at the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best fit your preferences and dietary needs. You may select multiple options for the food you would like to have at the picnic. If you have any special requests or comments, please use the "comments" field to let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Food** (`main_food`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of food you would like to have at the picnic. You may choose from the following options: Burgers, Hot Dogs, Grilled Chicken, Salads, Veggies, Fruits, and Cookies.
- **Diet** (`main_diet`, select_one, optional): Choose the dietary option that best fits your needs. Please select only one option from the following: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or none.
- **Comments** (`main_comment`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or suggestions for the picnic.
- **Email** (`main_email`, email, optional): If you would like to receive updates or information about the picnic, please enter your email address here.
- **Phone** (`main_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone for any reason, please enter your phone number here.
- **Extra Food** (`extra_food`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional types of food you would like to have at the picnic. You may choose from the following options: Watermelon, Chips, Pickles, Pretzels, Cookies, and Crackers.
- **Extra Comments** (`extra_comment`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or suggestions for the extra food you selected.
- **Extra Email** (`extra_email`, email, optional): If you would like to receive updates or information about the extra food, please enter your email address here.
- **Extra Phone** (`extra_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone for any reason related to the extra food, please enter your phone number here.

## Tips
Please fill out this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us. We will do our best to accommodate your preferences and provide a great experience for you at the picnic.
