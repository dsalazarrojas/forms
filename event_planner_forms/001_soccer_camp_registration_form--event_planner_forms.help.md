# Soccer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Camp Registration Form is a tool to help us gather information about your child or player's registration for the soccer camp. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select the relevant options for each field. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your child's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Player Number** (`player_number`, number, optional): This is your child's player number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your child's date of birth.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, required): Please select your child's shirt size from the options provided.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Please enter your contact number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, optional): Please enter the first name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, optional): Please enter the last name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Please select the relationship between the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Please enter any medical condition that requires our attention.
* **Medical Allergies** (`medical_allergies`, text, optional): Please enter any allergies that require our attention.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, optional): This field is for the parent or guardian to sign.
* **Parent Notes** (`parent_notes`, note, optional): Please enter any notes or comments about your child.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Please enter the amount paid.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Please select the payment method used.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of payment.
* **Player Signature** (`player_signature`, text, optional): This field is for the player to sign.
* **Player Notes** (`player_notes`, note, optional): Please enter any notes or comments about your child.
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, optional): Please select which team your child is on.
* **Position** (`position`, select_multiple, optional): Please select which position your child plays.
* **Coach** (`coach`, select_one, optional): Please select if your child has a coach.
* **Volunteer** (`volunteer`, select_one, optional): Please select if you are a volunteer.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments or feedback.

Please complete the form with as much detail as possible to ensure that we have all the necessary information about your child. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
