# valentine_giveaway_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to provide information to participate or win a gift card during the Valentine's Day promotion. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate information is captured for prize distribution and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Information**
	* Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
	* Ensure your email and phone number are accurate for prize distribution and communication.
2. **Gift Card Details**
	* Indicate if you would like to participate in the gift card giveaway.
	* Select the type of gift card you would like to receive.
	* Enter the gift card value (if applicable).
3. **Winner Information (for the lucky winner)**
	* If you're selected as the lucky winner, provide your contact information (email, phone number) so we can notify you and arrange for the prize pickup.
4. **Additional Details**
	* Answer any additional questions about your participation or gift card preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**
	* **Name (Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Email (Required)**: Enter a valid email address for prize distribution and communication.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Gift Card Details**
	* **Gift Card Details**: Indicate whether you would like to participate in the gift card giveaway (Yes/No).
	* **Gift Card Type**: Select the type of gift card you would like to receive (Yes/No).
	* **Gift Card Value**: Enter the gift card value (if applicable).
	* **Gift Card Number**: Enter a gift card value (optional).
* **Additional Details**
	* **Participant Details**: Provide any additional information about your participation or gift card preferences (optional).
	* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
	* **Lucky Winner (Required)**: Indicate whether you are the lucky winner (Yes/No).
	* **Lucky Winner Number**: Enter a lucky winner number (optional).
	* **Winner Message**: Enter a message for the lucky winner (optional).

Note: The fields marked as "Required" must be filled out to ensure accurate and complete information for prize distribution and communication.
