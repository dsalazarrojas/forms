# Virtual Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Summer Camp Registration Form is used to collect information from users for virtual summer camp registrations. This form is intended to be completed by parents or guardians to register their child(ren) for a virtual summer camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your child's registration is processed smoothly.
2. Make sure to provide the required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
3. If you have any additional information or comments, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Info** (Parent Info, text, required/optional): Please enter your name and contact information.
* **Child Info** (Child Info, text, required/optional): Please enter your child's name and any relevant information.
* **Summer Camp Choice** (Summer Camp Choice, select_multiple, **required**): Select the virtual summer camp(s) your child would like to attend. Please note that your child can only attend one camp.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, **required**): Enter the start date of the summer camp your child will attend.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, **required**): Enter the end date of the summer camp your child will attend.
* **Camp Duration** (Camp Duration, number, **required**): Please enter the duration of the summer camp in hours or days.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number and email address so we can contact you.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, **required**): Choose the payment method you would like to use (PayPal or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Amount** (Payment Amount, number, **required**): Please enter the payment amount for the selected camp.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, required/optional): Any additional information you'd like to share with us about your child's registration.
* **Camp Type** (Camp Type, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of camp your child would like to attend (Day Camp, Overnight Camp, Day/Night Camp).
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, text, required/optional): Please enter any additional information that may be helpful to know about your child's registration.
