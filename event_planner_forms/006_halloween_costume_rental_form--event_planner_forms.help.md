# Halloween Costume Rental Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Costume Rental Form is a simple form designed to allow users to list a Halloween costume for rental. This form collects necessary information to display the costume's image, description, and price, making it easy for users to browse and rent costumes for Halloween events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Upload or provide the costume's image by filling in the "Costume Image" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the costume in the "Costume Description" field.
3. Enter the rental price of the costume in the "Price" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Costume Image** (Costume Image, text, required/optional): Upload or provide a high-quality image of the costume to help users visualize the costume.
* **Costume Description** (Costume Description, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the costume, including material, size, and any other relevant details.
* **Price** (Price, number, required/optional): Enter the rental price of the costume, including any applicable taxes or fees.
