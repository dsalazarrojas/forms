# Christmas Event Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Christmas Event Planning Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by entering your contact information at the beginning of the form (Fields 1 and 2).
- Select your role in the event planning process (Field 4).
- Choose the type of event you are planning (Field 6).
- Specify the expected number of attendees (Field 7).
- Choose a preferred date and time for the event (Fields 8 and 9).
- Set a budget range for the event (Field 11).
- Select a venue preference (Field 12).
- Determine if catering is required (Field 13).
- Specify any dietary requirements (Field 14).
- Choose entertainment options (Field 16).
- Select a dress code preference (Field 17).
- Choose a decoration theme (Field 18).
- Specify if transportation is needed (Field 20).
- Provide any special accessibility needs (Field 21).
- Add any additional comments or requirements (Field 22).
- Indicate if you are available for follow-up contact (Field 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your name as the event planner or contact person.
- **Email Address**: Enter your email address for survey results and updates.
- **Organization or Company**: Enter the name of the organization or company hosting the event.
- **Your Role**: Choose your role in the event planning process from the list of options.
- **Type of Christmas Event**: Select the type of celebration you are planning from the list of options.
- **Expected Number of Attendees**: Choose the estimated headcount for your event.
- **Preferred Event Date**: Select your preferred date for the event.
- **Preferred Time**: Choose the preferred time for the event.
- **Budget Range**: Select the estimated budget for your event from the list of options.
- **Venue Preference**: Choose the preferred venue for your event from the list of options.
- **Catering Required**: Determine if catering is required for your event.
- **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary requirements for your event.
- **Entertainment Preferences**: Choose any entertainment options for your event from the list of options.
- **Preferred Dress Code**: Choose the preferred dress code for your event from the list of options.
- **Decoration Theme**: Select the preferred decoration theme for your event from the list of options.
- **Transportation Needed**: Indicate if transportation is needed for your event.
- **Accessibility Needs**: Enter any special accessibility needs for your event.
- **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or requirements for your event.
- **May We Contact You**: Indicate if you are available for follow-up contact.

Tips:
- Ensure you answer each question accurately and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the event planning team.
- The information collected from this form will help tailor the celebration to meet your needs and expectations.
