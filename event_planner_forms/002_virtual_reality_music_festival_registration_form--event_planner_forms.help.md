# Virtual Reality Music Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees of the Virtual Reality Music Festival. It gathers essential details such as user contact information, event preferences, and any additional notes. The purpose of this form is to streamline the registration process and help festival organizers efficiently manage attendee data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your user information, including your **User Name**, **Email**, and **Phone**.
2. Select one option for the **Select** field, which will help us understand your preferences for the festival.
3. Choose multiple options for the **Select Multiple** field, if applicable.
4. If you have any additional notes or comments, use the **Note** fields to provide more details.
5. Finally, input your preferred **Event Date** and **Event Time** for the festival.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for festival updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Select** (`select_one`, select_one, required: false): Choose one option from the list to express your preference for the festival.
	+ Option 1: I want to attend the festival.
	+ Option 2: I want to volunteer at the festival.
	+ Option 3: I want to sponsor the festival.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple options from the list that apply to you.
	+ Option 1: I want to attend the festival.
	+ Option 2: I want to volunteer at the festival.
	+ Option 3: I want to sponsor the festival.
	+ Option 4: I want to perform at the festival.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Use this field to add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you plan to attend the festival.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time you plan to attend the festival.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, required: false): Use this field to add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for festival updates and notifications.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
