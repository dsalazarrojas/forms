# Venue Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Venue Booking Form is used to book a venue for a specific event. To book a venue, please fill in the form with the required details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the Event Date, Event Time, and Venue Name fields to provide details about your event.
2. Select the type of event you are booking the venue for from the Event Type dropdown menu.
3. Enter the expected number of attendees for your event.
4. Add your contact information: Contact Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone.
5. Provide any additional notes or messages about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date**: The date of your event. Please enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Event Time**: The time of your event. Please enter the time in HH:MM format.
* **Venue Name**: The name of the venue you are booking.
* **Event Type**: The type of event you are hosting, such as music concert, wedding, conference, or other.
* **Attendees**: The expected number of people attending your event.
* **Contact Name**: The name of the person booking the venue.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the person booking the venue.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the person booking the venue.
* **Message**: Any additional information or notes about your event.
* **Event Notes**: Any additional details or requirements for your event.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that the venue is prepared for your event.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the Message or Event Notes fields.
* For large or complex events, please contact us directly for further assistance and planning.
