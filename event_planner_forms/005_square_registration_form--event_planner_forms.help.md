# square_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Registration Form is designed to collect information from patients and healthcare providers for registration purposes. This form is used to gather essential details about the patient, including contact information and date of birth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Square Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required.
2. Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form Info**: A text input field for entering registration details. (required: no)
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider. (required: no)
* **Healthcare Provider Phone**: Enter the contact number of the healthcare provider. (required: no)
* **Healthcare Provider Email**: Enter the email address of the healthcare provider. (required: no)
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient. (required: no)
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the patient in the format mm/dd/yyyy. (required: no)
* **Patient Address**: Enter the address of the patient. (required: no)

## Tips
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in.
* Use the provided fields as a guide to ensure completeness.
