# Responsive rsvp form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive RSVP form is designed to allow users to provide their RSVP status and share event details with the event organizers. This form is meant to be completed by event attendees, allowing them to easily and conveniently provide the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Event details" page to begin.
2. Fill in the "Event Name" field with the name of the event you are attending.
3. Enter the "Event Date" and "Event Time" in the respective fields.
4. Type in a "RSVP Message" to provide additional context or comments about your attendance.
5. Select your RSVP status from the "RSVP Status" options (Attending, Declined, Maybe).
6. List the number of guests you are RSVPing for in the "RSVP Guests" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Event Email" field.
8. Enter your guest's email address in the "Guest Email" field (if applicable).
9. Leave any additional notes in the "Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event details (1)** (`Event details`, `text`, required/optional: not required): This is the first section of the form where you will enter the name, date, and time of the event.
* **Event Name (2)** (`Event Name`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Enter the name of the event you are attending. 
* **Event Date (3)** (`Event Date`, `date`, required/optional: not required): Enter the date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Event Time (4)** (`Event Time`, `time`, required/optional: not required): Enter the time of the event in 12-hour format (e.g., 2:00 PM).
* **RSVP Message (5)** (`RSVP Message`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Type in a message to share with the event organizer about your attendance.
* **RSVP Status (6)** (`RSVP Status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: not required): Select your RSVP status from the options Attending, Declined, Maybe.
* **RSVP Guests (7)** (`RSVP Guests`, `text`, required/optional: not required): List the number of guests you are RSVPing for.
* **Event Email (8)** (`Event Email`, `email`, required/optional: not required): Enter your email address associated with the event.
* **Guest Email (9)** (`Guest Email`, `email`, required/optional: not required): Enter your guest's email address if they have one.
* **Note (10)** (`Note`, `note`, required/optional: not required): Leave any additional notes about your attendance.
