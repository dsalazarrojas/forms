# Halloween Decorating Contest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for event planners to collect information about Halloween decoration contests. It's a tool for submitting contest details, such as the address of the decorated house, the type of decoration, and contact information for the contest organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as prompted:

*   Make sure to enter a valid address for the house that will be decorated.
*   Select the type of decoration that best fits your event's theme.
*   Provide a brief description of your decoration.
*   Enter the date and time of the event.
*   Enter the time of the event.
*   Enter the contact name and information for the event planners.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **house_address**: Enter the address of the house that will be decorated for the Halloween decoration contest.
*   **decoration_type**: Select the type of decoration that best fits your event's theme. This is a dropdown menu with the options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
*   **decoration_details**: Provide a brief description of your decoration, such as the materials used and any creative elements included.
*   **date**: Enter the date of the event, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **time**: Enter the time of the event, in the format 12:00 PM.
*   **contact_name**: Enter the name and title of the person or team responsible for the event.
*   **email**: Enter the email address of the event contact person or team.
*   **phone**: Enter the phone number of the event contact person or team.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields, as they are essential for the event's organization.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your event planner or contact us.
