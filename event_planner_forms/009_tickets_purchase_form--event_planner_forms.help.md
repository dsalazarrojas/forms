# Tickets Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tickets Purchase Form is designed to collect essential information for event ticket purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event details:
	* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
	* **Event Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the event.
	* **Event Tickets**: Enter the number of tickets you want to purchase.
	* **Event Price**: Enter the price per ticket.
	* **Event Discount**: (Optional) Enter any available discount details.
	* **Event Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the event.
2. Enter your contact information:
	* **Event Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Event Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
3. Review and agree to the terms:
	* **Agree**: Select "Yes" to confirm you have read and understood the terms.
	* **Agree Date**: Enter the date you agree to the terms (not required but recommended).
	* **Agree Time**: Enter the time you agree to the terms (not required but recommended).
	* **Agree Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about agreeing to the terms.
4. Review and confirm your submission:
	* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
