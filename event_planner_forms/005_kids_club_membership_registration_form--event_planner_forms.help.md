# Kids Club Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for kids to join a children's club and for parents to provide information about their child. To complete this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your child's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your child's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your child's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your child's address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who will be the emergency contact for your child.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): If applicable, enter the name of the person who will be the guardian for your child.
* **Guardian Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, text, required): If applicable, enter your relationship with the guardian.
* **Guardian Contact Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required): If applicable, enter the phone number of the guardian.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, required): Enter any medical information that we should be aware of.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Enter any medical condition that your child has.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): Enter any allergies that your child has.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`parent_sign_up_method`, text, required): Tell us how you found out about the kids club.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional medical notes.
* **Emergency Instructions** (`emergency_instructions`, text, required): Enter any emergency instructions for your child.
* **Parent Consent** (`consent`, text, required): Confirm that you grant permission for your child to join the kids club.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, required): Enter how you will be paying for your child's membership (e.g., cash, check, etc.).
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter any additional payment information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about your child's registration.

**Tips**:
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We are here to help and support your child's membership journey!
