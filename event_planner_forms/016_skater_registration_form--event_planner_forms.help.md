# Skater Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals to register as skaters for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details:
	* Enter your **First Name** in the text box.
	* Enter your **Last Name** in the text box.
2. Provide your contact information:
	* Enter a valid **Email** address.
	* Enter your **Phone** number.
3. Answer the following questions:
	* Choose your **Skate Experience** level:
		+ Beginner
		+ Intermediate
		+ Advanced
	* Select your preferred **Surface**:
		+ Ice Skating
		+ Roller Skating
		+ Skateboarding
4. Add your emergency contact information:
	* Enter the **Emergency Contact First Name**.
	* Enter the **Emergency Contact Last Name**.
	* Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone** number.
	* Choose the **Relationship** with the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your personal documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your personal documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your valid phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Age**: Enter your age to ensure you meet the event requirements.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your Date of Birth to ensure you are eligible for the event.
* **Skate Experience**: Choose your level of skate experience:
	+ Beginner: Skating for less than 1 year.
	+ Intermediate: Skating for 1-3 years.
	+ Advanced: Skating for more than 3 years.
* **Preferred Surface**: Choose the type of skate surface you prefer:
	+ Ice Skating
	+ Roller Skating
	+ Skateboarding
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship**: Choose the relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Age**: Please note that a valid age is required to participate in the event. Please ensure you fill this field accurately.
* **Date of Birth**: Please note that your date of birth is required to ensure eligibility for the event.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form in one session to avoid confusion.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Please be aware that providing invalid information may lead to rejection of your registration.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the event staff.
