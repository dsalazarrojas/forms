# model-waiver-and-release-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect model release and waiver information from individuals featured in photographs or videos. It is used to obtain consent and agreements from the models, photographers, and other stakeholders involved in the project. This form helps ensure that all parties are aware of and agree to the intended use of their images.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions before starting the form.
2.  Fill out the model's name, contact information, and photographer's information.
3.  Select the model's consent to the use of their images.
4.  Sign and date the form to confirm agreement.
5.  Provide a description of the photo(s) and location where the image was taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Model Waiver and Release Form** (`model_waiver_and_release_form_page`, text, required: false): This is the first page of the form where the model will confirm their identity and provide contact information.
*   **Model Consent** (`model_consent`, select_one, required: false): Select "True" if you agree to the use of your images, and "False" otherwise.
*   **Photo Credits** (`photo_credits`, text, required: false): Provide a description of who took the photo.
*   **Photographer Information** (`photographer_info`, text, required: false): Enter the photographer's name and contact information.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact name and information for future reference.
*   **Model Agreement** (`model_agreement`, select_multiple, required: false): You must agree to the use of your image(s) in photographs for the above stated purposes.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required: false): Select "Signature" to confirm your agreement, or choose "No signature required" if not applicable.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed this form.
*   **Model Name** (`model_name`, text, required: false): Enter the model's name.
*   **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the photographer's name.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the contact name.
*   **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, required: false): Provide a description of the photo(s).
*   **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the photo was taken.
*   **Photo Credits Description** (`photo_credits_description`, text, required: false): Provide a description of who took the photo.
