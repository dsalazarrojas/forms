# public_event_visitor_injury_incident_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Event Visitor Injury Incident Report Form is designed to collect information from visitors to report injuries or incidents that have occurred during an event. This form helps event organizers and management teams to quickly gather relevant details about the incident, allowing them to provide timely and effective support to the affected individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Ensure that you answer all the questions truthfully and in their entirety.
3. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus, if applicable.
4. Enter your contact information to ensure that we can get in touch with you for further clarification or follow-up.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
