# gaming_tournament_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the registration process for the Gaming Tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a team and a team leader to represent it.
2. Provide the team name and team leader's name, email, and phone number.
3. Add the team members' names, emails, and phone numbers.
4. Choose the team's t-shirt size and color.
5. Enter the team's tourney fee (per member) and tourney funds available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name (1)**: Enter your team's name to register for the tournament.
* **Team Leader (3)**: Enter the team leader's name who will be the primary contact.
* **Team Leader Email (4)**: Enter the team leader's email address for communication.
* **Team Leader Phone (5)**: Enter the team leader's phone number.
* **Team Players (6)**: List the names of team members, separated by commas.
* **Team T-Shirt Size (7)**: Choose a t-shirt size for your team, from Small to XX-Large.
* **Team T-Shirt Color (8)**: Select one or multiple colors for your team's t-shirt(s).
* **Team Tourney Fee (per member) (9)**: Enter the team's tourney fee per player.
* **Team Tourney Funds Available (10)**: Enter the team's available funds for the tournament.
* **Individual Player (11)**: If you're registering as an individual player, enter your name.
* **Player Name (12)**: If you're registering as an individual player, enter your name.
* **Player Email (13)**: If you're registering as an individual player, enter your email.
* **Player Phone (14)**: If you're registering as an individual player, enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Double-check your team and player information for accuracy.
- Be aware of the team's tourney fee and available funds.
- If you're registering as an individual player, ensure you have a unique email and phone number.
