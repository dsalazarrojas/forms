# Halloween Contact Form 2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about Halloween events or activities. You can use this form to share your contact information, select a theme for your event, and add any additional notes or details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact name in the first field.
2. Enter your email address in the second field.
3. In the third field, type a message about your event or activity.
4. Choose a theme for your event from the drop-down list (Spooky, Scary, or Fun).
5. If you want to add any additional information, you can type it in the note fields.
6. Click submit to send your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_name** (Text, Required): Your name.
* **email** (Email, Required): Your email address.
* **message** (Text, Required): A description of your event or activity.
* **halloween_theme** (Select One, Required): The theme of your event (Spooky, Scary, or Fun).
* **contact_number** (Text, Optional): Your contact phone number.
* **date** (Date, Optional): The date of your event.
* **time** (Time, Optional): The time of your event.
* **additional_info** (Note, Optional): Additional information about your event.
* **additional_info_2**, **additional_info_3**, **additional_info_4**, **additional_info_5**, **additional_info_6**, **additional_info_7**, **additional_info_8**, **additional_info_9**, **additional_info_10**, **additional_info_11**, **additional_info_12**, **additional_info_13**, **additional_info_14**, **additional_info_15**, **additional_info_16** (Note, Optional): More additional information about your event.
* **additional_info_17** (Note, Optional): Even more additional information about your event.

Note: The additional "note" fields are meant to be used as needed, so you don't have to fill them in if you don't have anything to add.
