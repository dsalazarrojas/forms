# easter giveaway form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user contact details and gift preferences for the Easter giveaway event. It's essential to submit accurate and relevant information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both you and our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill out all required fields (indicated by *).
2. Provide your contact details, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose your preferred gifts.
4. Add a message if you'd like to communicate with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false)
* A brief text field where you can introduce yourself and provide any additional context. This field is optional, but it's a great opportunity to give us a personal touch.

### 2. **name** (`name`, text, required: false)
* Enter your name as you'd like to be referred to during the event. This will be used for communication and personalization purposes.

### 3. **email** (`email`, email, required: false)
* Provide your valid email address where you can be reached for event updates and communications.

### 4. **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
* Enter your phone number for us to reach you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.

### 5. **address** (`address`, text, required: false)
* Provide your address if you'd like to receive your gifts at a specific location or if you have any specific delivery instructions.

### 6. **gift preferences** (`gift preferences`, select_multiple, required: false)
* Choose your preferred gifts from the list provided. You can select multiple gifts if you'd like, and we'll do our best to accommodate your choices.

### 7. **message** (`message`, text, required: false)
* Add any additional comments or messages for our team, such as special requests or event preferences.

Note: Please be concise and clear in your messages to ensure our team can efficiently process your request.
