# Chili Cook Off Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for teams to enter the Chili Cook Off competition. Please fill out this form to provide your team's details for proper coordination and validation of your entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Team Name** field with the name of your competing team or individual.
2. Enter the **Contact Person** field with the name of the team lead or contact person.
3. Enter the **Contact Phone** field with the phone number for event coordination.
4. Enter the **Contact Email** field with the email address for confirmation.
5. Choose the **Chili Category** of your entry from the provided options (Traditional, Vegetarian, Gourmet, or People’s Choice).
6. Enter the **Number of Servings** you plan to bring.
7. Check if your setup requires **Electricity** and select the option accordingly.
8. If necessary, describe any **Special Needs or Equipment** required in the text field.
9. Select **Agree to Event Rules** to confirm acceptance of the event rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`): This is the name of your competing team or individual.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of your team lead or contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number for event coordination.
* **Contact Email** (`email`): Enter the email address for confirmation.
* **Chili Category** (`chili_category`): Choose the category of your entry from the options provided.
* **Number of Servings** (`number_of_servings`): Enter the approximate number of servings you plan to bring.
* **Electricity Required** (`electricity_required`): Check if your setup requires electricity.
* **Special Needs or Equipment** (`special_needs`): If you have any special needs or equipment requests, describe them here.
* **Agree to Event Rules** (`agree_rules`): Select this option to confirm acceptance of the event rules.
