# Speaker Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Speaker Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from speakers attending an event. This form will help event planners to efficiently manage speaker data and facilitate communication with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your speaker information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2.  Select your presentation type (e.g., Presentation, Panel, Workshop, or Video).
3.  Choose your presentation format (e.g., PowerPoint, PDF, Word, Image, or Video).
4.  Upload a presentation file (if applicable).
5.  Upload a bio file (if applicable).
6.  Provide photo credit information (if applicable).
7.  Select your social media platforms (e.g., Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, or Instagram).
8.  Enter your social media links (if applicable).
9.  Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, or Other).
10.  Select which email list you want to be added to (e.g., Speaker and Event Planners or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Information (speaker_info)**: Enter your personal details, including your name and email address.
* **Speaker Name (name)**: Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on our event materials.
* **Speaker Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the event.
* **Topic (topic)**: Enter a brief description of your presentation topic.
* **Bio (bio)**: Write a brief bio about yourself, including your background and experience.
* **Headshot (headshot)**: Upload a headshot or a photo of yourself.
* **Presentation Type (presentation_type)**: Select the type of presentation you will be giving (e.g., Presentation, Panel, Workshop, or Video).
* **Presentation Format (presentation_format)**: Choose the format of your presentation (e.g., PowerPoint, PDF, Word, Image, or Video).
* **Presentation File (presentation_file)**: Upload your presentation file if applicable.
* **Bio File (bio_file)**: Upload your bio file if applicable.
* **Photo Credit (photo_credit)**: Provide photo credit information if applicable.
* **Social Media (social_media)**: Select your social media platforms (e.g., Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, or Instagram).
* **Social Media Links (social_media_links)**: Enter your social media links if applicable.
* **Contact Preference (contact_preference)**: Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, or Other).
* **Email List (email_list)**: Select which email list you want to be added to (e.g., Speaker and Event Planners or Other).

Note: Fields marked as "required" are compulsory fields that need to be filled in for the form to be submitted.
