# Football Pool Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows users to enter their football pool information, including their personal details, team selections, and predicted scores for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for Team 1 Name, Team 2 Name, and other relevant fields as per the provided options.
3. Enter your predicted scores for Team 1 and Team 2.
4. Choose your prediction method for Team 1 and Team 2.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Enter your email and phone number (if applicable).
7. Specify the event planner and assigned tool (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Team 1 Name** (`team1_name`, select_one, optional): Select the name of Team 1 from the provided options.
* **Team 2 Name** (`team2_name`, select_one, optional): Select the name of Team 2 from the provided options.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Enter the actual score of Team 1.
* **Predicted Score** (`predicted_score`, number, optional): Enter your predicted score for Team 1.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Predicted Date** (`predicted_date`, date, optional): Enter your predicted date for the event.
* **Predicted Time** (`predicted_time`, time, optional): Enter your predicted time for the event.
* **Team 1 Score** (`team1_score`, number, optional): Enter the actual score of Team 1.
* **Team 2 Score** (`team2_score`, number, optional): Enter the actual score of Team 2.
* **Predicted Team 1 Score** (`predicted_team1_score`, number, optional): Enter your predicted score for Team 1.
* **Predicted Team 2 Score** (`predicted_team2_score`, number, optional): Enter your predicted score for Team 2.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Football Pool** (`football_pool`, text, optional): Enter your football pool details.
* **Prediction Method** (`prediction_method`, select_one, optional): Select your prediction method from the provided options.
* **Team 1 Predicted Method** (`team1_predicted_method`, select_one, optional): Select your predicted method for Team 1 from the provided options.
* **Team 2 Predicted Method** (`team2_predicted_method`, select_one, optional): Select your predicted method for Team 2 from the provided options.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Event Planner** (`event_planner`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event planner (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned tool (if applicable).
