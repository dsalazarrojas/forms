# Meeting Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Meeting Feedback Survey" form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on the effectiveness and organizational aspects of a meeting. This feedback will help meeting organizers and facilitators identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions for future meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the option that best describes the primary reason for your attendance in the meeting.
2.  Rate the effectiveness of the meeting on a scale of 1-4 (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
3.  Select all the options that you found to be the most and least effective parts of the meeting.
4.  Indicate whether new issues or action items were identified during the meeting.
5.  Indicate whether you were assigned any action items during the meeting.
6.  Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the meeting.
7.  Let us know if you'd like to provide feedback on your next meeting survey.
8.  Rate your likelihood of participating in future meetings on a scale of 1-4 (Likely, Somewhat Likely, Not Likely, Not at all Likely).
9.  Rate the organization of the meeting on a scale of 1-4 (Well-organized, Poorly organized, Moderately organized, Unorganized).
10.  Provide any additional comments you have about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Feedback Survey** (`meeting_feedback_survey_title`): This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the meeting.
- **What was the primary reason for your attendance?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_1`): Select the option that best describes why you attended the meeting.
  - **Time-sensitive issue, e.g. deadline**: You attended the meeting because of a deadline that needed to be met.
  - **Personal interest, e.g. team project**: You attended the meeting because you had a personal interest in the topic being discussed.
  - **Managerial meeting**: You attended the meeting because it was a managerial meeting.
  - **Team meeting, e.g. weekly**: You attended the meeting as part of a regular team meeting.
  - **Other**: Select this option if none of the above reasons apply.
  - **No reason**: You did not attend the meeting for any reason.
- **How would you rate the meeting's effectiveness?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_2`): Rate the meeting's effectiveness on a scale of 1-4 (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
- **What was the most effective part of the meeting?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_3`): Select all the options that you found to be the most effective parts of the meeting.
  - **Introduction**: The meeting started with a clear and concise introduction.
  - **Discussion**: The discussion was engaging and productive.
  - **Break**: The break was helpful in refreshing your mind.
  - **Summary**: The meeting ended with a clear summary of the key takeaways.
- **What was the least effective part of the meeting?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_4`): Select all the options that you found to be the least effective parts of the meeting.
  - **Introduction**: The meeting started with a clear and concise introduction.
  - **Discussion**: The discussion was engaging and productive.
  - **Break**: The break was helpful in refreshing your mind.
  - **Summary**: The meeting ended with a clear summary of the key takeaways.
- **Were any new issues or action items identified?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_5`): Indicate whether any new issues or action items were identified during the meeting.
  - **Yes**: New issues or action items were identified during the meeting.
  - **No**: No new issues or action items were identified during the meeting.
  - **Not applicable**: This meeting was not applicable to your work or role.
- **Were any action items assigned to you?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_6`): Indicate whether you were assigned any action items during the meeting.
  - **Yes**: You were assigned action items during the meeting.
  - **No**: You were not assigned action items during the meeting.
- **Would you like to provide any additional feedback?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_7`): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the meeting.
- **Will you provide feedback on your next meeting survey?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_8`): Let us know if you'd like to provide feedback on your next meeting survey.
  - **I will provide feedback on my next meeting survey**: You'd like to provide feedback on your next meeting survey.
  - **I will not provide feedback**: You do not want to provide feedback on your next meeting survey.
- **How likely are you to participate in future meetings?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_9`): Rate your likelihood of participating in future meetings on a scale of 1-4 (Likely, Somewhat likely, Not Likely, Not at all Likely).
- **How was the meeting organized?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_10`): Rate the organization of the meeting on a scale of 1-4 (Well-organized, Poorly organized, Moderately organized, Unorganized).
  - **Well-organized**: The meeting was well-structured and well-executed.
  - **Poorly organized**: The meeting was disorganized and unstructured.
  - **Moderately organized**: The meeting was somewhat organized but could have been better.
  - **Unorganized**: The meeting was completely disorganized.
- **Would you like to provide additional comments?** (`meeting_feedback_survey_11`): Provide any additional comments you have about the meeting.
