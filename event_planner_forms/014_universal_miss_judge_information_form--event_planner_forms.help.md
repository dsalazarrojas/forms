# Universal Miss Judge Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect basic information about a person being judged in a competition or event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the person's information, including their:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email (if applicable)
	* Phone number (if applicable)
	* Judge Number (a unique identifier for the person being judged)
	* Year they were judged
	* Score (if applicable)
	* Type (select one: Pageant Director, Judge, or Emcee)
	* Category (select multiple: Beauty, Talent, Evening Wear, Swimsuits, or Interview)
	* Notes (any additional information about the person being judged)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the person's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the person's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the person's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the person's phone number (if applicable).
* **Judge Number** (`judge_number`, number, required: false): A unique identifier for the person being judged.
* **Year Judged** (`year_judged`, time, required: false): Enter the year the person was judged.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required: false): Enter a score for the person (if applicable).
* **Type** (`select_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the person's type: Pageant Director, Judge, or Emcee.
* **Category** (`select_category`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the person's category: Beauty, Talent, Evening Wear, Swimsuits, or Interview.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information about the person being judged.
* **Date Judged** (`date_judged`, date, required: false): Enter the date the person was judged (if applicable).

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Make sure to select the correct categories and type for the person being judged.
- The form should be filled out separately for each person being judged.
- Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
- Ensure that the person being judged has given their consent for this information to be collected.
