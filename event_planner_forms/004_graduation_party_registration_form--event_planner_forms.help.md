# Graduation Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Party Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in attending a graduation party. This form is used by event organizers to track and manage RSVPs, ensuring a smooth and well-organized event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your name and contact number in the "Name" and "Contact Number" fields respectively.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your RSVP type from the options provided in the "RSVP Type" field.
5. Enter the number of individuals attending the event in the "Number Attending" field.
6. Add any additional details or notes about your RSVP in the "Additional Details" field.
7. Finally, click on the submit button to complete and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
- **Graduation Party** (`Graduation Party`, text, optional): Enter the name of the graduation party you are attending.
- **Graduation Date** (`Graduation Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the graduation ceremony.
- **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **RSVP Type** (`RSVP Type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options to indicate your intention to attend or not.
- **Number Attending** (`Number Attending`, number, optional): Enter the number of individuals you will be attending with.
- **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Add any additional details or notes about your RSVP.
- **Graduation Details** (`Graduation Details`, note, optional): Add any additional information about the graduation details.
- **Additional Details** (`Additional Details`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your RSVP.
- **Graduation Class** (`Graduation Class`, text, optional): Enter the name of your graduation class.
- **RSVP By** (`RSVP By`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to RSVP by.
- **RSVP Status** (`RSVP Status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options to indicate your RSVP status.
