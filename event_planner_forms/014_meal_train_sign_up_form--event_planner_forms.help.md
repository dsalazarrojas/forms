# Meal Train Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Meal Train Sign Up Form is a crucial tool for users to express their needs and preferences for meals. By filling out this form, you will be helping our meal train program provide the support you need to stay nourished.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields. These fields are not required but will help us know who you are.
2. Select a preferred Meal Date for when you need a meal. This field is not required, but please be as specific as possible to help us plan accurately.
3. Choose a suitable Meal Time that fits your schedule.
4. Select the frequency of how often you need meals.
5. Choose the Meal Type (e.g., Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) that best suits your meal requirements.
6. Specify who this meal is for (Meal Recipient), which is optional but helps us tailor our services to your needs.
7. Provide your Phone number and Email for us to contact you in case of any updates or changes.
8. Optionally, leave a brief Message if you have any specific meal preferences or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

• **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like to be known in the meal train program.
• **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like to be known in the meal train program.
• **Meal Date** (`meal_date`, date, optional): Select the date you need a meal. Please be as specific as possible.
• **Meal Time** (`meal_time`, time, optional): Choose a suitable time for your meal delivery.
• **Meal Frequency** (`meal_frequency`, text, optional): Specify how often you need meals (e.g., once a day, every other day).
• **Meal Type** (`meal_type`, select_one, optional): Select Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner as your meal type preference.
• **Meal Recipient** (`meal_recipient`, text, optional): Specify who this meal is for, e.g., your family or a friend in need.
• **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
• **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you about any updates or changes.
• **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Leave any additional information or requests you have for your meal preferences.
