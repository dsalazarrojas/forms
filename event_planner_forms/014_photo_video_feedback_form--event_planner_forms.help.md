## Photo Video Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users about photo and video content. It is intended to help us better understand your opinions and ideas on how to improve our photo and video content.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Scroll down to each page and fill in the required fields. You can skip fields that are not required.
3. Provide as much detail as you can in the fields you choose to complete.
4. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Video Feedback Form Data** (`photo_video_feedback_form_data`, text, required: false): This field is the main area where you can provide your feedback and suggestions about photo and video content. Please type in your thoughts and ideas here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): If you have any contact information, such as your name, email, or phone number, please provide it here. This will help us get back to you for more information or clarification.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, required: false): This field is a reminder to have more suggestions, but you can still provide any additional ideas or feedback you may have, if you'd like.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields, which are marked as "required".
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification.
* Don't worry about grammar or spelling mistakes; we want to hear your honest feedback!
* You can provide as much detail as you'd like, and we'll do our best to incorporate your suggestions.
* If you have any attachments or files to share, please let us know and we'll find a way to accommodate them.
