# Summer Chess Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Chess Camp Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from participants and their accompanying teacher/parent for the summer chess camp. This information will be used by the camp administrators to prepare for the participant's session and ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Ensure that all required fields (marked as "required" in the form) are completed.
- If you are a participant, please fill in your own details. If you are a teacher/parent, please fill in your own and your accompanying participant's details.
- Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the participant as it appears on their identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for the participant or teacher/parent.
- **Contact Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number where the participant or teacher/parent can be reached.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the participant's parent or guardian.
- **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, optional): Enter the age of the participant.
- **Grade Level** (`participant_grade`, `select_one`, optional): Select the grade level of the participant in their school.
- **Participant School** (`participant_school`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the participant's school.
- **Parent Phone** (`participant_parent_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
- **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for the parent.
- **Participant Parent Name** (`participant_parent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the participant's parent or guardian.
- **Special Needs** (`participant_special_needs`, `text`, optional): If the participant has any special needs, please describe them here.
- **Participant Language** (`participant_language`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the languages spoken by the participant.
- **Teacher's Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the teacher accompanying the participant.
- **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for the teacher.
- **Teacher's Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number where the teacher can be reached.
- **Teacher School** (`teacher_school`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the teacher's school.
- **Session Length** (`session_length`, `time`, required): Select the length of the participant's session.
- **Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency of the participant's session.
- **Participant Start Date** (`participant_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the participant's session.
- **Participant End Date** (`participant_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the participant's session.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes for the participant's session, please enter them here.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them here.
- **Participant Start Time** (`participant_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the participant's session.
- **Participant End Time** (`participant_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the participant's session.
