# Which Pumpkin Is Your Favorite Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a fun and informal survey to gather information about users' preferences for pumpkins, colors, and shapes. It's meant to be completed by individuals who are interested in sharing their opinions on these topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite type of pumpkin from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose one or multiple colors that you like.
3. Pick your favorite shape from the dropdown menu.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, type them in the text field below.
5. If you'd like, provide your email address and/or phone number so we can contact you about any further questions or opportunities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Which pumpkin is your favorite** (`which_pumpkin_is_your_favorite`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options provided: Pumpkins, Gourds, Squash, or Other.
- **Favorite color** (`favorite_color`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple colors from the options: Green, Yellow, Orange, or Other.
- **Favorite shape** (`favorite_shape`, select_one, required: false): Pick one of the shapes: Round, Square, Triangle, or Other.
- **Additional comments** (`note`, text, required: false): Type any additional comments or thoughts you have about pumpkins, colors, or shapes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you'd like to provide your email address, enter it here.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to provide your phone number, enter it here.
- **Date of visit** (`date`, date, required: false): Note the date you visited the pumpkin patch or pumpkin patch-themed event.
- **Time of visit** (`time`, time, required: false): Note the time you visited the pumpkin patch or pumpkin patch-themed event.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): If you have any thoughts or comments, type them in here.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this is a survey.
- If you selected multiple colors, you can choose one, multiple, or all of the options provided.
- Don't worry if you don't have a favorite pumpkin or color; you can choose "Other" or simply not provide a response.
- If you'd like to be contacted about any further questions or opportunities, please provide your email address and/or phone number.
