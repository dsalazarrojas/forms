# Diver Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Diver Registration Form is designed to collect personal and medical information from divers participating in activities such as scuba diving, sky diving, or other extreme sports. This form helps our organization ensure that divers are registered and cleared to participate in such activities, and provides important contact information in case of emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Provide your phone number and emergency contact information, including the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
3. If you have any medical conditions or medical history, please describe them in the text box provided.
4. Select your gender.
5. If you have any medical procedures scheduled, please fill in the relevant fields with the date, time, and location of the procedure.
6. Confirm your waiver status by selecting either Signed or Unsigned.
7. Upload a photo of yourself (optional).
8. Sign the form by typing your name in the Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address where you can be reached for important notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached for emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text): Please enter the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text): Please describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, required): If you have a known birthdate, please enter it in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in this text box.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text): If you have any previous medical history, please describe them in this text box.
* **Emergency Instructions** (`emergency_instructions`, text): Please provide any important instructions for your emergency contact, such as medical alert information.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text): Please enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text): Please describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text): If you have any medical insurance information, please enter it in this text box.
* **Emergency Insurance** (`emergency_insurance`, text): If you have any emergency insurance information, please enter it in this text box.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text): If you have any scheduled medical procedures, please describe them in this text box.
* **Medical Procedure Date** (`medical_procedure_date`, date): If you have any scheduled medical procedures, please enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Medical Procedure Time** (`medical_procedure_time`, time): If you have any scheduled medical procedures, please enter the time in 24-hour format.
* **Medical Procedure Location** (`medical_procedure_location`, text): If you have any scheduled medical procedures, please enter the location.
* **Medical Procedure Status** (`medical_procedure_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your medical procedures (Pending, Complete, or In Progress).
* **Waiver Status** (`waiver_status`, select_one, required): Select your waiver status (Signed or Unsigned).
* **Diver Photo** (`diver_photo`, text): Upload a photo of yourself for identification purposes.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text): Sign the form by typing your name in this text box.
