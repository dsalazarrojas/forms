# Valentine's Day Exchange Box Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Valentine's Day Exchange Box Registration Form is used to gather information for a single Valentine's Day event registration. It's meant to collect details from participants for the purpose of gift exchange and event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your user information in the **User Information** section.
2.  Enter your name in the **Name** field.
3.  Enter the contact person's name in the **Contact Person** field.
4.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Ensure it's a valid email format.
5.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field. This can be a phone number or extension.
6.  Enter your address in the **Address** field.
7.  Select the date of the event in the **Event Date** field.
8.  Set the gift limit in the **Gift Limit** field. This sets the maximum amount you're willing to spend on a gift.
9.  Set the gift budget in the **Gift Budget** field. This is the total amount you're willing to spend on gifts for the event.
10.  Choose the size of the gift in the **Gift Type** field. Select from Small, Large, or Extra Large options.
11.  Add any additional notes in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Enter your name and other general information about yourself.
* **Name**: The name of the participant.
* **Contact Person**: The name of the person to contact regarding this registration.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: The contact phone number of the participant.
* **Address**: The participant's address for delivery purposes.
* **Event Date**: The date of the event.
* **Gift Limit**: The maximum amount you're willing to spend on a gift.
* **Gift Budget**: The total amount you're willing to spend on gifts for the event.
* **Gift Type**: The size of the gift you'd like to receive or exchange.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information about your preferences or needs.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* The gift limit and gift budget are used to help the organizers plan and manage the event efficiently.
* If you have any specific gift preferences or requests, include them in the **Notes** field.
