# Competition Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experiences and opinions on various aspects of the competition. Your input will help us improve and make future events better for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the name of the competition and the date it took place.
2. Provide your name and email address (optional, but recommended for follow-up purposes).
3. Select your role during the competition (e.g., competitor, judge, coach, spectator).
4. Rate your overall experience, organization, venue, facilities, and other aspects of the competition.
5. Answer questions about the quality of the competition, such as judging fairness, communication, and value for money.
6. Share your thoughts on what you liked most about the competition (best aspects) and what could be improved (areas for improvement).
7. If you'd like, add any other comments or suggestions for future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competition Name** (`competition_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the competition you participated in.
* **Competition Date** (`competition_date`, date, required): Enter the date the competition took place.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Provide your name (if you want to remain anonymous, you can leave this field empty).
* **Your Email** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Your Role** (`participation_type`, select_one, required): Select how you participated in the competition (e.g., competitor, judge, coach, spectator).
* **Category or Division** (`category_competed`, text, optional): If you participated in a specific category or division, enter it here.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience as: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Organization Rating** (`organization_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the organization of the competition: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Venue Rating** (`venue_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the venue: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Applicable.
* **Facilities Rating** (`facilities_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the facilities: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Judging Fairness Rating** (`judging_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the fairness and quality of judging: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, or Did Not Observe.
* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of event communication: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Registration Process Rating** (`registration_process`, select_one, required): Rate the ease of registration: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Schedule Rating** (`schedule_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how well the schedule ran: Excellent - on time, Very Good - minor delays, Good - some delays, Fair - significant delays, Poor - very disorganized.
* **Level of Competition** (`competition_level`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of competitors: Very high - professional level, High - skilled competitors, Moderate - mixed levels, Low - mostly beginners, Very low - poor quality.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate the value for money: Excellent value, Good value, Fair value, Poor value, Very poor value.
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Would you recommend this competition to others: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no.
* **Would You Participate Again** (`would_participate_again`, select_one, required): Would you participate again: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no.
* **Best Aspects** (`best_aspects`, text, required): Share what you liked most about the competition.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Share what could be improved.
* **Favorite Moment** (`favorite_moment`, text, optional): Highlight a favorite moment from the competition (if applicable).
* **Suggestions for Next Year** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for future improvements.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other comments or feedback.
