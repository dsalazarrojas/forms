# Wedding Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients seeking wedding consultation services. It helps the wedding planner understand the client's preferences and requirements, making it easier to tailor the planning process to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your wedding preferences and needs.
2. Enter any additional information or details that you feel are important for us to know.
3. Review your responses carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Preferences** (`wedding_preferences`): Describe your wedding preferences, such as the type of wedding theme, color scheme, or any other special requests you may have.
* **Wedding Theme** (`wedding_theme`): Choose from the options whether you would like a themed wedding or not.
* **Wedding Color Scheme** (`wedding_color_scheme`): Select the color scheme you prefer for your wedding decorations.
* **Venue** (`venue`): Indicate whether you have a preferred venue in mind or would like us to suggest one.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`): Enter the date of your wedding, including the month and year.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`): Provide an estimated number of guests you expect to attend your wedding.
* **Wedding Theme Description** (`wedding_theme_description`): Describe the theme of your wedding in more detail, if applicable.
* **Wedding Color Scheme Description** (`wedding_color_scheme_description`): Describe your preferred color scheme in detail, if applicable.
* **Wedding Favors** (`wedding_favors`): Choose whether you want wedding favors or not.
* **Wedding Attire** (`wedding_attire`): Indicate your preferred attire for the wedding party.
* **Wedding Services** (`wedding_services`): Select the services you would like included in the wedding package.
* **Wedding Food and Beverages** (`wedding_food_and_beverage`): Choose your preferences for wedding food and beverages.
* **Wedding Music** (`wedding_music`): Select your preferred wedding music style.
* **Wedding Transportation** (`wedding_transportation`): Indicate whether you need transportation arrangements or not.
* **Wedding Furniture** (`wedding_furniture`): Select the type of wedding furniture you prefer.
* **Wedding Decor** (`wedding_decor`): Choose the type of wedding decorations you like.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`): Add any special requests or requirements you have.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Leave any additional comments or notes for us to consider.

Please review your form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness. This information will help us better understand your wedding requirements and preferences, allowing us to provide you with the best possible consultation and planning experience.
