# Family Reunion T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from family members attending a family reunion to order personalized T-shirts. Each family member should fill out this form to place their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including providing your name, email address, and any other required fields.
2. Select your preferred T-shirt size and color.
3. Choose a design for your T-shirt.
4. Indicate the number of family members attending the reunion.
5. Submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name and Surname** (`name_and_surname`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name and surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred T-shirt size from the options: Small, Medium, Large, or X-Large.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, `select_one`, optional): Select the color you prefer for your T-shirt from the options: Red, Blue, or Green.
* **How many family members are attending?** (`family_member_count`, `number`, optional): Indicate the number of family members attending the reunion.
* **Shirt Design** (`shirt_design_id`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more designs for your T-shirt from the available options.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information about your order or preferences, enter it here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Submit your order by clicking this button.

## Tips

* Please fill out one form per family member to ensure accurate and individualized orders.
* Make sure to select the correct shirt size, color, and design to avoid errors.
* Double-check your email address for correct formatting and accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or additional information, please enter it in the "Additional Info" field.
