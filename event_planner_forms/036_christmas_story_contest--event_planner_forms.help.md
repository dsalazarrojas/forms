# Christmas Story Contest - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Christmas Story Contest is a platform for participants to share their holiday-themed stories, either heartwarming, humorous, adventurous, mysterious, family-friendly, or of another category. The form is designed to collect all relevant details, including contact information, story details, and permissions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your contestant information (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Age Group).
*   Write and submit your Christmas story (Story Title, Story Content, and Story Category).
*   Provide details about your submission, including Word Count, original work, and submission date.
*   Grant permission to post or share your story.
*   Confirm your writing experience level.
*   Confirm whether you have been previously published and if your story has won any awards.
*   Give consent to be contacted about your story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contestant Information
*   **First Name** (`First Name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your passport or driver's license.
*   **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your passport or driver's license.
*   **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, **required**): Enter your active email address for correspondence and notifications.
*   **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, **optional**): Provide your phone number for communication (optional).
*   **Age Group** (`Age Group`, select\_one, **required**):
    *   Select your age group:
        +   Under 12
        +   12-18
        +   18-30
        +   30-50
        +   50+
*   **Story Information** (`Story Information`, note, **required**): You can write any details about your story in this section.
*   **Story Title** (`Story Title`, text, **required**): Enter your story title.
*   **Story Content** (`Story Content`, text, **required**): Paste your complete story.
*   **Word Count** (`Word Count`, number, **required**): Count the words in your story and enter the total.
*   **Story Category** (`Story Category`, select\_one, **required**):
    *   Select the category of your story:
        +   Heartwarming
        +   Humorous
        +   Adventure
        +   Mystery
        +   Family-friendly
        +   Other
*   **Story Inspiration** (`Story Inspiration`, text, **optional**): If you'd like to share what inspired your story.
*   **Submission Details** (`Submission Details`, note, **optional**): Enter any other details about your submission.
*   **Original Work** (`Original Work`, select\_one, **required**):
    *   Select whether your work is original and unpublished:
        +   Yes, I certify this
        +   False
*   **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of your submission.
*   **Permissions** (`Permissions`, select\_one, **required**):
    *   Select whether you grant permission to post or share your story:
        +   True
        +   False
        +   With attribution only
*   **Writing Experience** (`Writing Experience`, note, **optional**): You can write about your writing experience here.
*   **Experience Level** (`Experience Level`, select\_one, **optional**):
    *   Select your writing experience level:
        +   Beginner
        +   Amateur
        +   Semi-professional
        +   Professional
*   **Previous Publications** (`Previous Publications`, select\_one, **optional**):
    *   Select whether you have been previously published:
        +   True
        +   False
*   **Story Awards** (`Story Awards`, select\_one, **optional**):
    +   Select whether your story has won any awards:
        +   True
        +   False
*   **Contact Consent** (`Contact Consent`, select\_one, **required**):
    *   Select whether you consent to be contacted about your story:
        +   True
        +   False

Note: The labels in the above explanation are in the format `<label>` (`<name>`, `<type>`, **required** or **optional**), where `<label>` is the field label, `<name>` is the field name, `<type>` is the field type, and **required** or **optional** indicate whether the field is required or optional.
