# Conference Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from conference attendees to help organizers improve the overall experience for future events. Your input is valuable and will help make the conference a better and more enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please complete this form to share your thoughts and experiences from the conference.
2.  Fill in all the required fields with as much detail as you can provide.
3.  Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
4.  Use the options provided for each question to select the most accurate response.
5.  If you have any additional thoughts or comments, use the "Additional Feedback" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Conference Experience Feedback** (Feedback Intro, **1**): Share your thoughts on your overall experience at the conference.
*   **Your Name** (Attendee Name, **2**): This is your name and will be used to address you in any follow-up communications.
*   **Email Address** (Email Address, **3**): Please provide your email address so we can get in touch with you for any further questions or to share follow-up information.
*   **Conference Name** (Conference Name, **4**): This is the name of the conference you attended.
*   **Conference Date** (Conference Date, **5**): The date you attended the conference.
*   **Attendance History** (Attendance History, **6**): Select the number of times you've attended the conference.
*   **Experience Ratings** (Experience Ratings, **7**): Share your overall experience with the conference, from Excellent to Poor.
*   **Overall Experience** (Overall Experience, **8**): This is a general rating of your experience with the conference, from Excellent to Poor.
*   **Session Content** (Session Content, **9**): Rate the quality of the presentations you experienced during the conference, from Excellent to Poor.
*   **Keynote Quality** (Keynote Quality, **10**): Rate the quality of the main presentations, from Excellent to Poor.
*   **Workshop Quality** (Workshop Quality, **11**): Rate the quality of the hands-on sessions, from Excellent to Poor, or select "Did Not Attend".
*   **Networking Value** (Networking Value, **12**): Share how valuable the connection opportunities were for you, from Excellent to Poor.
*   **Venue Experience** (Venue Experience, **13**): Rate the overall facility experience, from Excellent to Poor.
*   **Staff Helpfulness** (Staff Helpfulness, **14**): Rate the support and assistance provided by the conference staff, from Excellent to Poor.
*   **Technology Experience** (Technology Experience, **15**): Rate the app and WiFi experience, from Excellent to Poor.
*   **Conference Highlights** (Conference Highlights, **16**): Share any standout moments or experiences from the conference.
*   **Favorite Session** (Favorite Session, **17**): Identify your most valuable session from the conference.
*   **Key Takeaway** (Key Takeaway, **18**): Share the main learning or takeaway from the conference.
*   **Valuable Connections** (Valuable Connections, **19**): Share the connections you made during the conference, or any outcomes from them.
*   **Improvement Areas** (Improvement Areas, **20**): Share any areas where the conference could be improved.
*   **Biggest Disappointment** (Biggest Disappointment, **21**): Identify any moments that fell short for you.
*   **Suggestions** (Suggestions, **22**): Share any ideas for improvement you may have.
*   **Future Interest** (Future Interest, **23**): Share your future interest in attending the conference, from "Definitely Yes" to "Definitely No".
*   **Would Attend Again** (Would Attend Again, **24**): Share if you would attend the conference again, from "Definitely Yes" to "Definitely No".
*   **Would Recommend** (Would Recommend, **25**): Share if you would recommend the conference to your colleagues, from "Definitely Yes" to "Definitely No".
*   **Additional Feedback** (Additional Feedback, **26**): Share any other comments or thoughts you may have about the conference.
