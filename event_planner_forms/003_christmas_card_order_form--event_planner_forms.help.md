# Christmas Card Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to order Christmas cards for their loved ones. It collects information for customizing cards with name, message, and other personal details, as well as shipping and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
2. If you have a company name, enter it in the "Company Name" field.
3. Provide your email address for order confirmation.
4. Enter your phone number for order inquiries.
5. Select the style of the Christmas card (e.g., Traditional Religious, Modern Minimalist).
6. Choose the size of the card (e.g., Standard A6, Large A5).
7. Specify the quantity of cards you need.
8. Choose the paper type (e.g., Standard Matte, Premium Glossy).
9. Select the color of the envelope (e.g., White, Red, Green).
10. Decide if you want to print your return address on the envelope.
11. Choose the mailing service you require (e.g., No I will mail myself, Yes standard mail).
12. Enter your delivery address for shipping.
13. Enter the city and postal code for shipping.
14. Specify the required delivery date.
15. If you need a rush order, select the expedited processing option.
16. Choose your payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal).
17. If you have any special requests, enter them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name to ensure proper addressing on the card.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you represent a business, enter your company name.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for order inquiries.
- **Card Style** (`card_style`, select_one, required): Choose the style of the Christmas card that suits your preferences.
- **Card Size** (`card_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of the card to match your requirements.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify how many cards you need.
- **Paper Type** (`paper_type`, select_one, required): Choose the paper type to suit your preference.
- **Custom Message Inside** (`custom_message`, text, optional): Enter a personal message to be printed inside the card.
- **Enveloppe Color** (`envelope_color`, select_one, required): Select the color of the envelope to match your card.
- **Print Return Address** (`return_address`, select_one, required): Decide if you want to print your return address on the envelope.
- **Mailing Service** (`mailing_service`, select_one, required): Choose the mailing service you need.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter your delivery address for shipping.
- **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city for shipping.
- **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code for shipping.
- **Required Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Specify the required delivery date.
- **Rush Order** (`rush_order`, select_one, required): Choose the expedited processing option if needed.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your payment method.
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any additional requirements or requests.

Tips: 
- Be sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with order processing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team for assistance.
- For business orders, please note that some options might not be available for all types of orders.
