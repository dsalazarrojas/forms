# youth_hockey_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The youth_hockey_registration_form is a form designed to collect registration information and contact details from youth hockey players, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records for the league or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the youth_hockey_registration_form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your child's registration information in the "Registration Information" section.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Select your child's preferred team from the "Team Selection" section.
4. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): This section is where you provide your child's registration information, such as their name, birthdate, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Here, you'll enter your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Team Selection** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select the youth hockey team your child would like to join. You can choose one or more teams as needed.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would like to pay for your child's registration, such as Credit Card, Bank Draft, or Cash.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the youth hockey organization for assistance.
