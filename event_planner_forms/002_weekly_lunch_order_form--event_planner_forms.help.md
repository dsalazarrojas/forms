# Weekly Lunch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your meal preferences for the upcoming week. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure we can provide you with your desired meals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details, making sure to provide all required information.
2. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (1, text, required): Enter your name, department, or any other relevant details to help us know who you are.
* **Order Date** (2, date, required): Select the date for which you'd like to order lunch.
* **Food Provider** (3, select_one, optional): If you have a preferred food provider, select "Yes". This will allow us to contact them directly for your orders.
* **Order Details** (4, text, optional): Provide any special requests or notes about your order, such as dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Order Type** (5, select_one, optional): If this is a special order for a specific occasion or event, select "Yes".
* **Special Instructions** (6, text, optional): If you have any specific instructions for the catering team, enter them here.
* **Email** (7, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any updates or confirmations.
* **Phone** (8, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Submit** (9, text, required): Click to send in your completed form.

Note: This is a required field to complete the submission process.
