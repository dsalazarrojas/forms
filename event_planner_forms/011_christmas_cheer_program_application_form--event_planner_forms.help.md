# Christmas Cheer Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Christmas Cheer Program Application Form is a tool used by the Christmas Cheer organization to collect information from potential volunteers. The form is used to gather relevant details about your volunteer experience, skills, and availability to ensure that you are a good fit for the program. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reading through each section of the form carefully and answering the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2.  Please provide your name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3.  In the Volunteer Experience section, select the duration of your previous volunteer experience.
4.  In the Skills & Abilities section, select all the relevant skills that apply to you.
5.  For the Availability section, choose how much of the program duration you can commit to.
6.  Select your preferred volunteer role from the options provided.
7.  If required, provide a reference name and their phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
*   **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number that you can be reached at.
*   **Street Address** (Required): Enter your street address where you can be reached at.
*   **City** (Required): Enter the city where you reside.
*   **State or Province** (Required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
*   **ZIP or Postal Code** (Required): Enter the postal code of your address.
*   **Volunteer Experience** (Required): Select the duration of your previous volunteer experience:
    *   **Extensive (5+ years)**: You have 5 or more years of volunteer experience.
    *   **Moderate (2-5 years)**: You have 2-5 years of volunteer experience.
    *   **Some (1-2 years)**: You have 1-2 years of volunteer experience.
    *   **First time volunteer**: You are volunteering for the first time.
*   **Previous volunteer roles or community service** (Optional): Describe any previous volunteer roles or community service you have completed.
*   **Relevant Skills or Abilities** (Required): Select all relevant skills or abilities that apply to you:
    *   **Coordination and planning**: You have experience with planning and coordinating events.
    *   **Event logistics**: You have experience with event planning and organization.
    *   **Community outreach**: You have experience with outreach and engagement with the community.
    *   **Customer service**: You have experience with customer service.
    *   **Administrative work**: You have experience with administrative tasks.
    *   **Physical labor**: You have experience with physical labor.
    *   **Team leadership**: You have experience with team leadership.
    *   **Other**: Describe any other skills or abilities you have.
*   **Languages Spoken** (Optional): List any languages you are fluent in.
*   **Are you available for the full program duration?** (Required): Select whether you can commit to the full program duration:
    *   **Yes, full duration**: You are available for the full program duration.
    *   **Partial duration**: You are available for part of the program duration.
    *   **Need to discuss**: You are not sure if you can commit to the full program duration.
*   **Hours per week you can commit** (Required): Enter the number of hours you can commit to per week.
*   **Preferred volunteer role** (Required): Select your preferred volunteer role:
    *   **Family support coordinator**: You prefer to support families.
    *   **Event planning**: You prefer to plan events.
    *   **Distribution logistics**: You prefer to handle logistics for distribution.
    *   **Community outreach**: You prefer to engage with the community.
    *   **Administrative support**: You prefer to provide administrative support.
    *   **Flexible**: You are flexible with your role.
*   **Reference Name** (Required): Enter the name of a reference who can vouch for your volunteer work.
*   **Reference Phone Number** (Required): Enter the phone number of your reference.
*   **Will you be willing to complete a background check if required?** (Required): Select whether you are willing to complete a background check if required.
