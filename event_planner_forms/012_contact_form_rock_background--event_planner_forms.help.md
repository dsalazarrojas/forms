# Contact Form Rock Background - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a simple contact form designed to collect information from individuals interested in planning an event, such as weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, conferences, or private parties. The form will be used to gather essential details about the event and the services needed, which will help us better understand their requirements and provide them with the most suitable solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Get In Touch" button to start the form.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field. This is a required field to help us understand who is inquiring about the event.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is a required field to ensure we can contact you for more information.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional, but recommended for quicker response time).
5. Select the type of event you are planning in the "Event Type" field. This will help us understand the specific needs of your event.
6. Enter the date of your event in the "Event Date" field. Be sure to use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Estimate the number of guests attending your event in the "Expected Guest Count" field.
8. Indicate whether you have a venue preference in the "Venue Preference" field. Choose from the provided options to help us understand your needs.
9. Select all the services you require for your event in the "Services Needed" field. You can choose multiple options to help us tailor our services to your needs.
10. Estimate your budget range for the event in the "Budget Range" field. This will help us understand your financial constraints.
11. Enter any additional information or questions you have about your event in the "Message" field.
12. If you are being referred to us by someone, please let us know the source in the "How Did You Find Us" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Get In Touch**: This is the first step to start the form.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name to help us understand who is inquiring about the event.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you for more information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for quicker response time (if you provide it).
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event you are planning (e.g., wedding, corporate event, birthday party, conference, private party, or other).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your event in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Expected Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required): Estimate the approximate number of guests attending your event.
* **Venue Preference** (`venue_preference`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have a venue preference (Need Venue Recommendations, Have Venue Already, or Undecided).
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all the services you require for your event (Full Event Planning, Day-of Coordination, Catering, Entertainment, Decorations, Photography).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Estimate your budget range for the event (Under $5000, $5000-$10000, $10000-$25000, $25000-$50000, Over $50000).
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Enter any additional information or questions you have about your event.
* **How Did You Find Us** (`referral`, select_one, optional): If you are being referred to us by someone, let us know the source (Online Search, Social Media, Friend Referral, Previous Event, Advertisement, Other).
