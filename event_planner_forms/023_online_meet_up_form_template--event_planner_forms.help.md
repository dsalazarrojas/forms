# Online Meet Up Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to collect necessary information for organizing online meetings, such as meeting details, date and time, and attendee participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the meeting details, date, start and end times, and meeting location.
2. Confirm the attendees by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each participant.
3. Add any additional notes about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the meeting, such as its objectives, goals, or agenda.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the meeting from the calendar. Please ensure you select a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the start time of the meeting. This is the time when the meeting will begin.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the end time of the meeting. This is the time when the meeting will conclude.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the physical address or virtual platform where the meeting will take place.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether each attendee will be participating in the meeting by selecting "Yes" or "No". This helps us track who has committed to attending and who has not.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any extra information about the meeting that you wish to remember or discuss during the meeting.
