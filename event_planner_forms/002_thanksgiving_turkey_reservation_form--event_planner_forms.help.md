# Thanksgiving Turkey Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the Thanksgiving Turkey Reservation process. Please use this form to make a reservation for a special occasion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Reservation Date** field with the date you would like to make your reservation.
2. Select the **Reservation Time** field to enter the time of your reservation.
3. Enter the **Number of Guests** field with the number of people you would like to have at your table.
4. If you have any additional information or notes for your reservation, use the **Reservation Note** field to add them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Reservation Date** (Reservation Date, date, required: false)
*This field is used to select the date of your reservation. Please enter the date you would like to make your reservation.*

### **Reservation Time** (Reservation Time, time, required: false)
*This field is used to select the time of your reservation. Please select the time that suits your needs.*

### **Number of Guests** (Number of Guests, number, required: false)
*This field is used to enter the number of people you would like to have at your table. Please enter a number to specify the number of guests.*

### **Reservation Note** (Reservation Note, note, required: false)
*This field is used to add any additional information or notes about your reservation. If you have any special requests or questions, please use this field to include them.*
