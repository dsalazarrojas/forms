# exhibition_stall_modification_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests for modifications to an exhibition stall.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in the title of your exhibition stall modification request.
* Select the category of the event where the stall will be located (e.g., trade show, conference).
* Provide a brief description of the modification you are requesting.
* If applicable, attach any relevant files or documents to support your request.
* Choose a preferred date and time for the modification to take place.
* Indicate the priority level of your request (e.g., high, medium, low).
* Add any additional notes or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Stall Modification Request Form**: Enter a brief title describing your request.
* **Category**: Select the type of event where your stall will be located. 
  - Options: Event, Trade Show, Conference
  - This field helps event organizers to understand which event category your request pertains to.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the modification you are requesting. 
  - This field helps event organizers to understand the nature of your request.
* **File Upload**: If you have any supporting files or documents for your request, you can attach them here.
  - This field allows you to attach relevant files to your request, such as photos or diagrams.
* **Preferred Date**: Choose a date when you would like your stall to be modified. 
  - Choose a specific date for the modification, and this field will guide event organizers to schedule accordingly.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose a time when you would like your stall to be modified. 
  - Similar to the preferred date, this field helps event organizers schedule your request in the most suitable time slot.
* **Priority**: Indicate the level of urgency for your modification request. 
  - Select High, Medium, or Low to indicate how quickly you require the modification to be made.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes you have regarding your request.
  - This field is for any extra information that doesn't fit in other fields.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing your request.
* Make sure to attach any necessary files or documents to support your request.
* If your request has a specific deadline, please indicate it in the preferred date field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
