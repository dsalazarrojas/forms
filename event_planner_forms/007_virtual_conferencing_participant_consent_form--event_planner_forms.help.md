# Virtual Conferencing Participant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consent for a virtual conferencing event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details in the **Participant Information Form** section.
2. Select whether you **Consent** to participate in the event.
3. Provide your **Email** and **Phone** if you want to be contacted.
4. Add any **Additional Note** if you need to share more information.
5. Specify the **Event Conducted By**, if applicable.
6. Select the **Date** and **Time** of the event.
7. Enter the **Location** of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information Form**: This field is used to capture your basic information such as your name, email, and other personal details.
* **I consent to participate in this event**: This is a selection field where you can choose to give your consent to participate in the event.
* **Email**: If you want to be contacted for any updates or information about the event, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone**: If you want to be contacted through phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **Additional Note**: If you need to share any additional information or instructions, you can add it in this field.
* **Event Conducted By**: If you are participating on behalf of someone else or an organization, please specify the event conducted by here.
* **Date**: Please select the date of the event.
* **Time**: Please select the time of the event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.

## Tips
* Please fill in accurate and complete information to ensure you receive the necessary communication and updates.
* Make sure to select the correct date and time of the event.
