# kart_racing_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering kart racers for an event. It collects necessary information from drivers, including event details, driver and vehicle information, payment details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Event Details** page with the required event information.
2. Move on to the **Driver Info** page and fill out your driver information.
3. On the **Vehicle Details** page, enter your vehicle details.
4. On the **Payment Info** page, enter your payment information.
5. Finally, fill out the **Emergency Contact** page with your emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** 
  * Event Title: Enter the title of the event.
  * Event Date: Enter the start date of the event.
  * Event Time: Enter the start time of the event.
  * Event Location: Enter the location of the event.

* **Driver Info**
  * Driver Name: Enter your name.
  * Driver Phone: Enter your phone number.
  * Driver Email: Enter your email address.
  * Driver License: Enter your driver license number (if applicable).
  * License Issue Date: Enter the date your driver license was issued (if applicable).

* **Vehicle Details**
  * Vehicle Registration Number: Enter your vehicle registration number (if applicable).
  * Vehicle Make: Enter the make of your vehicle.
  * Vehicle Model: Enter the model of your vehicle.
  * Vehicle Color: Select whether your vehicle is 'Yes' or 'No' if it has a special color.
  * Kart Number: Enter your kart number.

* **Payment Info**
  * Payment Method: Select whether you will pay 'Yes' or 'No' if you will pay by credit card.
  * Payment Amount: Enter the payment amount.
  * Payment Date: Enter the date you paid.

* **Emergency Contact**
  * Emergency Contact Name: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
  * Emergency Phone: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
  * Notes: Add any additional notes about your emergency contact.

## Tips

- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the event organizers.
- Review your form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
