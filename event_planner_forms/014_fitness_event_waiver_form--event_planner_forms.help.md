# Fitness Event Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and contact information from participants in a fitness event, such as their name, address, phone number, and email. This information may be used for waiver purposes, to verify identities, or for follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Address, including your street address or PO box number.
3. Enter your City and State where you are from.
4. Enter your Date of Birth, in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Enter your Phone number, including the area code and the phone number.
6. Enter your Email address.

* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.

* **State**: Enter the state where you are currently living.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/2000).
