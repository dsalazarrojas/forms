# Baby Shower Checklist Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baby Shower Checklist Template is designed to help you organize and plan a successful baby shower for the expectant parents. This form guides you through the essential details, from hosting and guest management to games, decorations, and budgeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the host name and address in the "Host Name" field.
2. Enter the expectant parent's name in the "Expectant Parent Name" field.
3. Schedule the baby shower date and time by clicking on the date field.
4. Enter the approximate number of guests in the "Expected Guest Count" field.
5. Select the theme for the shower from the provided options.
6. Choose the necessary decorations for the event by clicking on "Decorations Needed".
7. Select the food and beverages you will be serving by choosing from the options in the "Food and Beverages" field.
8. Choose the tableware items needed, such as plates, cups, and utensils.
9. Plan games and activities by selecting from the options in the "Games and Activities" field.
10. Determine if you will provide party favors for guests.
11. Provide gift registry information (if applicable).
12. Decide on how you will send invitations, and set a deadline for RSVPs.
13. Arrange for professional photography or decide on how you will handle photos.
14. Decide if you will have help with cleanup.
15. Finally, estimate your budget and add any additional notes or reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Host Name** (`host_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person hosting the baby shower.
- **Expectant Parent Name** (`expectant_parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the expectant parent(s).
- **Shower Date** (`shower_date`, date, required): Schedule the date of the baby shower.
- **Shower Time** (`shower_time`, time, required): Choose the start time of the event.
- **Shower Location** (`shower_location`, text, required): Enter the venue or address of the shower.
- **Expected Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required): Estimate the number of guests attending.
- **Shower Theme** (`theme_selection`, select_one, required): Select a theme from the provided options.
- **Decorations Needed** (`decorations_needed`, select_multiple, required): Choose the necessary decorations for the event.
- **Food and Beverages** (`food_beverages`, select_multiple, required): Select the food and beverages to be served.
- **Tableware Needed** (`tableware_needed`, select_multiple, required): Choose the tableware items necessary for the event.
- **Games and Activities** (`games_planned`, select_multiple, required): Plan games and activities for the guests.
- **Party Favors** (`party_favors`, select_one, required): Decide if you will provide party favors for guests.
- **Gift Registry Information** (`gift_registry_info`, text, optional): Provide gift registry information (if applicable).
- **Invitation Method** (`invitations_method`, select_one, required): Determine how you will send invitations.
- **RSVP Deadline** (`invitation_deadline`, date, required): Set a deadline for guests' RSVPs.
- **Photography Plan** (`photography_plan`, select_one, required): Choose a photography option.
- **Cleanup Help Arranged** (`cleanup_help`, select_one, required): Decide if you will have help with cleanup.
- **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Estimate your budget for the event.
- **Additional Notes or Reminders** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or reminders for the event.
