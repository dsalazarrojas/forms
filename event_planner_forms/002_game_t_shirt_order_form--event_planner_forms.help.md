# Game T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game T Shirt Order Form is used to gather information about your order of game-themed t-shirts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name on the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number on the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Select the quantity of t-shirts you'd like to order on the "Order Quantity" field.
5. Choose the size of the t-shirt from the "T Shirt Size" dropdown list.
6. Pick the color(s) of the t-shirt(s) you'd like to order from the "T Shirt Color" dropdown list.
7. Enter the shipping cost for your order on the "Shipping Cost" field.
8. Select how you'd like to pay for your order from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you'd like to be known.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, optional): Select the number of t-shirts you'd like to order.
* **T Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the t-shirt you'd like to order. Options are Small, Medium, and Large.
* **T Shirt Color** (`t_shirt_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the color(s) of the t-shirt(s) you'd like to order. You can choose one or multiple colors from the list.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of shipping for your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you'd like to pay for your order. Options are Card and PayPal.
