# Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Registration Form is used to collect attendee information for an upcoming webinar. Filling out this form will help us understand who is attending the webinar and ensure we can provide you with the necessary materials and instructions to participate fully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, please ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your submission before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FirstName** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **LastName** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address you use to receive updates and notifications regarding the webinar.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your business phone number for any potential follow-up calls.
* **Company** (`text`, required: false): Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **JobTitle** (`text`, required: false): Enter your job title, if applicable.
* **Department** (`text`, required: false): Enter your department, if applicable.
* **JobTitleManager** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the title of your manager, if applicable.
	+ Options: Manager, Director, Team Lead
* **ManagerName** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of your manager, if applicable.
* **ManagerPhone** (`text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your manager, if applicable.
* **ManagerEmail** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of your manager, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your submission before clicking the submit button.
- If you have any issues or questions, feel free to reach out to us directly.
