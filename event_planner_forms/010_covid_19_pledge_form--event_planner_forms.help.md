# COVID 19 Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Pledge Form is a commitment to follow community health guidelines to prevent the spread of COVID-19. By filling out this form, you are agreeing to take certain precautions to protect yourself and others from the virus.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). This includes your full name, email address, and a date for your pledge.
2. Next, answer each question in the select_one field by selecting the "I agree" option or writing "Other" to provide more information.
3. For the text fields, type a brief message for the personal commitment statement.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (pledger_name): This is the name of the person taking the pledge.
* **Email Address** (pledger_email): This is the email address of the person taking the pledge.
* **Organization or Team Name** (pledger_org): This is an optional field for organizations or teams taking the pledge.
* **-- Our Commitment to Safety --** (pledge_intro): This is a note field that explains the importance of following community health guidelines.
* **I pledge to wear a mask in public spaces** (pledge_mask): Choose this option to agree to wear a mask in public spaces.
* **I pledge to maintain 6 feet of social distance** (pledge_distance): Choose this option to agree to maintain 6 feet of social distance.
* **I pledge to wash or sanitize my hands frequently** (pledge_hands): Choose this option to agree to wash or sanitize your hands frequently.
* **I pledge to stay home if I am feeling unwell** (pledge_stay_home): Choose this option to agree to stay home if you are feeling unwell.
* **I pledge to encourage others to follow these safety protocols** (pledge_encourage): Choose this option to agree to encourage others to follow these safety protocols.
* **Personal Commitment Statement** (commitment_statement): This is an optional text field for you to add a personal message about why you are taking this pledge.
* **Date of Pledge** (pledge_date): This is the date you are taking the pledge.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you select "Other" in any of the select_one fields, please provide more information about your commitment in the personal commitment statement field.
* If you have any trouble filling out the form, contact your organization or team representative for assistance.
* By submitting this form, you are making a public commitment to follow these safety protocols.
