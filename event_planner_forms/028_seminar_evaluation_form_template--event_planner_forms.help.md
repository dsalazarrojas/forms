# seminar_evaluation_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and evaluation of a seminar, specifically designed for participants to provide their honest opinion on the seminar's content, instructor, overall effectiveness, organization, presentation, and other aspects of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the seminar.
2. Be specific and honest in your feedback, using the format and options provided.
3. Use 24-hour format for the presentation time input.
4. Format the date for the seminar organization input in the format 2018-02-01 to 2018-02-28.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How Well Was the Seminar Content Delivered?
* **how_well_was_the_seminar_content_delivered** (`seminar_content`, `text`, required): Please rate the seminar content from 1 to 5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **What Were the Instructor's Qualifications?** (`instructor`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options: Highly Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, or Not Relevant.
* **Overall Effectiveness of the Seminar** (`overall_seminar_effectiveness`, `number`, required): Rate the seminar's overall effectiveness from 1 to 5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **The Seminar Organization Was Effective?** (`seminar_organization`, `date`, required): Enter the date in the format 2018-02-01 to 2018-02-28.
* **The Seminar Presentation Was Effective?** (`seminar_presentation`, `time`, required): Enter the 24-hour time format.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, optional): Please be as specific as possible in your feedback.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `note`, optional): Is there anything else you would like to share about the seminar?
* **Seminar Rating** (`seminar_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the seminar from 1 to 5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply: True or False.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Department** (`participant_department`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options: Finance, Marketing, HR.
* **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options: Director, Manager, Assistant Manager, Team Member.
* **Participant Job Function** (`participant_job_function`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply: Sales, Operations, IT.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, `text`, optional): Where do you work?

Note: Required fields are marked as "required" in the form and must be completed before submission.
