# Powerlifting Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect basic information from individuals who want to participate in a powerlifting event. The information collected will be used for event planning and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`event_planner_forms_first_name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`event_planner_forms_last_name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`event_planner_forms_email`, `email`, required/optional: false): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`event_planner_forms_phone`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`event_planner_forms_date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
