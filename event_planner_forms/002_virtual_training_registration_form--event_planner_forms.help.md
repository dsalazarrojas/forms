# virtual_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for virtual training events. It's used to gather personal details, event preferences, and other relevant information from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address and confirm it in the **Confirm Email** field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
4. If you have any questions about the training, feel free to type them in the **Do you have any questions about this training?** field.
5. Select the desired **Training Date** and **Training Time** for the event.
6. Choose the **Training Duration** of the event.
7. If you have any comments or additional information, type them in the **Comments** field.
8. Confirm your preferred **Location** for the event.
9. Select the **Tool** you would like to use for the training.
10. For the **Page** sections, select the pages you would like to attend from the provided options.
