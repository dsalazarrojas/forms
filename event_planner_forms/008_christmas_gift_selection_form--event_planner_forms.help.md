# Christmas Gift Selection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for Christmas gift selection for employees. It is intended for employees to input their personal details and gift preferences to select a suitable gift.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
* Fill in your Full Name, Employee ID, and Email Address in the first three fields.
* Select your department from the list of options in the "Department" field.
* Choose a gift category by selecting an option from the "Gift Tier Selection" field.
* Select a gift option for each category by choosing from the options in the "Gift Option A," "Gift Option B," and "Gift Option C" fields.
* In the "Final Gift Selection" field, choose your primary gift category.
* If you have a color preference, select it from the list of options in the "Color Choice" field.
* Decide if you want personalization for your gift by selecting an option from the "Add Personalization" field.
* If you want personalization, enter the text to be engraved or printed in the "Personalization Text" field.
* Choose a delivery method from the list of options in the "Delivery Method" field.
* If you're shipping to home, enter your home mailing address in the "Home Mailing Address" field.
* Select a preferred delivery date in the "Preferred Delivery Date" field.
* Write any message to family members in the optional "Message to Family" field.
* Provide feedback for the company's gift program in the "Feedback on Gift Program" field.
* Lastly, let the company know how long you've worked there by selecting from the "Years with Company" options, but this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Employee ID**: Your company-issued identification number.
* **Email Address**: Your company email address.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list to identify your work context.
* **Gift Tier Selection**: Choose a gift tier that suits your preference.
* **Gift Option A**: Select a gift option from the electronics and tech category.
* **Gift Option B**: Select a gift option from the home and lifestyle category.
* **Gift Option C**: Select a gift option from the food and beverages category.
* **Final Gift Selection**: Choose your primary gift category from the options.
* **Color Choice**: If you have a color preference, select it from the options.
* **Add Personalization**: Decide if you want personalization for your gift.
* **Personalization Text**: If you want personalization, enter the text to be engraved or printed.
* **Delivery Method**: Choose how you'd like to receive your gift.
* **Home Mailing Address**: If shipping to home, enter your home address.
* **Preferred Delivery Date**: Select a preferred delivery date.
* **Message to Family**: Write a message to family members if applicable.
* **Feedback on Gift Program**: Suggest improvements for the company's gift program.
* **Years with Company**: Select how long you've worked with the company.
