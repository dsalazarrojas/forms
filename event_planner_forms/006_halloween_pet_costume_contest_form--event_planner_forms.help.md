# Halloween Pet Costume Contest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Pet Costume Contest Form is designed to collect information for an internal contest. Please note that not all fields are required, but filling out all relevant fields will ensure that your entry is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the type of pet you would like to enter into the contest from the "Pet Type" dropdown list.
2. Enter your pet's name in the text field.
3. Enter your name in the "Owner Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your pet's age in the "Age" field.
7. Enter the cost of your pet's costume in the "Pet Dress Cost" field.
8. Select the category your pet's costume belongs to from the "Category" dropdown list.
9. Choose the entry fee for your pet's costume from the "Entry Fee" dropdown list.
10. Briefly describe your pet's costume in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Type** (`Pet Type`, select_one): Choose the type of pet you are entering into the contest from the dropdown list. Options include "Pet Type", "Dog", "Cat", and "Other".
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text): Enter your pet's name.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text): Enter your name as the owner of the pet.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Age** (`age`, number): Enter your pet's age.
* **Pet Dress Cost** (`pet_dress_cost`, number): Enter the cost of your pet's costume.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one): Choose the category your pet's costume belongs to from the dropdown list. Options include "Category 1", "Category 2", and "Category 3".
* **Entry Fee** (`entry_fee`, select_one): Choose the entry fee for your pet's costume from the dropdown list. Options include "$1", "$2", and "$5".
* **Description** (`description`, text): Briefly describe your pet's costume.
Note: All fields are optional, but providing all required fields will ensure that your entry is complete and accurate.
