# Ski Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ski Trip Registration Form is used to gather essential information from students who are participating in a ski trip. This includes contact, personal, and medical information, which will be used by the ski trip organizers to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with the required information, which includes student name, email, phone, date of birth, and emergency contact details.
2. If you have any medical notes or concerns, please provide them in the Medical Notes field.
3. If you have any special requests or requirements, such as accommodations or dietary restrictions, please include them in the Medical Notes field as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required)
* This is the student's full name, which will be used to address them and keep in touch with them throughout the trip.

### **Student Email** (Student Email, email, required)
* This is the student's email address, which will be used for communication and updates about the trip.

### **Student Phone** (Student Phone, text, required)
* This is the student's phone number, which will be used for emergency contact and updates.

### **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required)
* This is the student's date of birth, which will be used to verify their age and ensure that they meet the minimum age requirement for the trip.

### **Guardian Name** (Guardian Name, text, optional)
* This is the name of the student's guardian or emergency contact, which will be used in case of an emergency.

### **Guardian Phone** (Guardian Phone, text, optional)
* This is the phone number of the student's guardian or emergency contact, which will be used for emergency contact and updates.

### **Medical Notes** (Medical Notes, note, optional)
* This field is used to provide any medical notes or concerns that the student or their guardian may have. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure that we can accommodate their needs.

### **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, optional)
* This field is used to provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact for the student. This may be a parent, guardian, or other trusted adult.

# Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will be in touch with you shortly after receiving your form submission to confirm your participation in the ski trip.
