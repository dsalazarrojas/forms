# Talent Show Auditions Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help performers sign up for the talent show auditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Performer Info** section with your relevant details.
2. Provide your **Contact Info** to reach out to you for further communication.
3. Enter your **Event Info**, including the date and time of the event.
4. Choose your **Talent Type** from the provided options.
5. Enter a brief **Description** of your talent.
6. If you have a relevant image or video to showcase your talent, provide the **Image URL** or **Video URL**.
7. Finally, provide your **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Phone Number** to ensure we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Info**:
	+ A text field to enter your name or alias.
	+ No specific guidance is provided as this is the primary identifier of the performer.
* **Contact Info**:
	+ A text field to enter your name or alias.
	+ A **Contact Name** to enter your name or title that you wish to be known by.
* **Event Info**:
	+ A text field to enter the event details, including date and time.
* **Talent Type**:
	+ A dropdown menu with options: 'Yes', 'No' (it is assumed to be related to whether you have the relevant talent type for the event)
* **Description**:
	+ A text field to enter a brief description of your talent.
* **Image URL**:
	+ A text field to enter the URL of your relevant image.
* **Video URL**:
	+ A text field to enter the URL of your relevant video.
* **Contact Email**:
	+ An email field to enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**:
	+ A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Event Date**:
	+ A date field to enter the date of the event (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Event Time**:
	+ A time field to enter the time of the event (in 24-hour format).
* **Start Time**:
	+ A time field to enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time**:
	+ A time field to enter the end time of the event.
* **Venue**:
	+ A text field to enter the venue of the event.
* **City**:
	+ A text field to enter the city where the event is held.

Note: Please make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as required) and double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
