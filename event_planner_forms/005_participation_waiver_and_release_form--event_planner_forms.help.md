# Participation Waiver And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form captures participant details and waiver information for events. It's essential to fill it out correctly to ensure accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  First, select the type of waiver that best suits the event you're participating in. Choose either "Event Waiver and Release" or "General Waiver and Release" from the "Waiver Type" field.
2.  Select the "Participant Details" field to proceed with filling in participant information such as "Participant Name," "Participant Contact Number," "Participant Address," and "Participant Email."
3.  Fill in the participant's "Participant Name," "Participant Contact Number," "Participant Address," and "Participant Email" fields with accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participation** (Event Waiver and Release, General Waiver and Release): Select the waiver type that applies to the event.
* **Waiver Type** (Event Waiver and Release, General Waiver and Release): Choose the waiver type that aligns with the event's specific needs.
* **Participant Details** (Participant Waiver and Release, Participation Waiver and Release): Select this field to provide information for the participant.
* **Participant Name**: The name of the participant.
* **Participant Contact Number**: The participant's contact number.
* **Participant Address**: The participant's address.
* **Participant Email**: The participant's email.
* **Waiver Text**: This field is optional; it's not a standard field but it can be used for any additional text related to the participant's waiver.
* **Waiver Date**: The date when the waiver was signed.
 
## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use accurate and up-to-date information for participant details.
