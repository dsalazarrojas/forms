# sports_t_shirt_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports t-shirt order form is a tool for sports teams to collect information about their merchandise orders. It is intended for teams to provide the necessary details for ordering sports t-shirts. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out your **player name** in the "player_name" field.
- Select a date for the **order date** in the "order_date" field.
- Choose a time for the **order time** in the "order_time" field.
- Enter the quantity of t-shirts you wish to order in the "quantity_ordered" field.
- Indicate whether you would like the t-shirts to be in **large or extra-large size** in the "t_shirt_size" field.
- Select the **desired color** of the t-shirts in the "t_shirt_color" field.
- Optionally, you can add **order details** in the "order_details" field.
- Finally, enter the **total value** of the order in the "order_total" field.
- Enter your **team name** in the "team_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **player_name**: This field is for the player's name.
* **order_date**: This field is for the date you would like to place the order.
* **order_time**: This field is for the time you would like to place the order.
* **quantity_ordered**: This field is for the amount of shirts you would like to order.
* **t_shirt_size**: This field is for choosing the t-shirt size. Please select "Yes" for large/extra-large or "No" for other sizes.
* **t_shirt_color**: This field is for choosing the desired color of the t-shirt. Please select "Yes" for the color you would like to order and "No" for other colors.
* **order_details**: This field is for entering any additional order details.
* **order_total**: This field is for the total value of the order.
* **team_name**: This field is for the team's name.
