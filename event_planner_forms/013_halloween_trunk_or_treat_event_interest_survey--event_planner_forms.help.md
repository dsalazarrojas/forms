# Halloween Trunk Or Treat Event Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who are interested in hosting a Halloween Trunk or Treat event for their community or organization. The form is designed to collect information from potential hosts to help them get started with planning their event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your event.
2. Choose a date for your event.
3. Select the time you would like to hold your event.
4. Provide the location where you would like to hold your event.
5. Enter your name, email, and phone number so that we can contact you.
6. Describe how you are related to the location or organization hosting your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_name**: Enter the name of your Halloween Trunk or Treat event. This will be displayed on our records to identify your event.
* **event_date**: Select the date you would like to hold your event. Please use the date format MM/DD/YY.
* **event_time**: Choose the start and end time of your event. For example, 4:00-8:00 PM.
* **event_location**: Enter the address or location where you would like to hold your event. This could be a community center, school, or a private residence.
* **contact_name**: Enter your name so that we can get in touch with you.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you with event updates and details.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you in case of emergencies or last-minute updates.
* **contact_relationship**: Describe how you are related to the location or organization hosting your event. For example, "I'm a parent at this school" or "I'm a representative of this community center."

## Tips
* Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
* Make sure to check if someone has already submitted an interest form for a similar event in the past.
* Contact the event team if you have any questions or concerns.
* The event team will follow up with you to discuss further details and provide support for your event.
