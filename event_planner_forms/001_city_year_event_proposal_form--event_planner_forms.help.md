# City Year Event Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Year Event Proposal Form is designed to help you propose new events and activities that align with City Year's mission to build a more just and equitable city. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to propose an event that meets our criteria and will be reviewed for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
- Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
- Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name (1)
* **Event Name (1)** (text, required): Provide a descriptive name for your proposed event.

*   **Event Type (2)** (select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your event:
    *   Community Service: Events that benefit the community, such as volunteer work or clean-up initiatives.
    *   Fundraising: Events designed to raise funds for City Year or partner organizations.
    *   Educational Workshop: Events that provide education or training on a specific topic or skill.
    *   Awareness Campaign: Events that aim to raise awareness on a social issue.
    *   Volunteer Recruitment: Events that aim to recruit volunteers for City Year or partner organizations.
    *   Partnership Event: Events that bring together City Year and partner organizations to achieve a shared goal.
    *   Other: Events that don't fit into the above categories.

*   **Proposed Event Date (3)** (date, required): Select your preferred date for the event.

*   **Proposed Start Time (4)** (time, required): What time will the event begin?

*   **Expected Duration (5)** (select_one, required): How long will the event last:
    *   1-2 hours
    *   2-4 hours
    *   4-6 hours
    *   Full day (6+ hours)
    *   Multi-day event

*   **Expected Number of Attendees (6)** (number, required): Estimate how many people will attend.

*   **Proposed Location (7)** (text, required): Where will the event take place?

*   **Target Audience (8)** (select_multiple, required): Who is this event designed for:
    *   K-12 Students
    *   College Students
    *   Young Professionals
    *   Community Members
    *   Families
    *   Seniors
    *   Corporate Partners

*   **Event Objectives (9)** (text, required): What are the main goals of this event?

*   **Estimated Budget (10)** (number, required): Provide estimated cost in dollars.

*   **Funding Sources (11)** (select_multiple, optional): Where will the funding come from:
    *   City Year Grant
    *   Corporate Sponsorship
    *   Community Donations
    *   Ticket Sales
    *   Partner Organization
    *   Self-funded
    *   Other

*   **Volunteers Needed (12)** (number, required): How many volunteers will you need?

*   **Equipment and Resources Needed (13)** (text, optional): List any special equipment or resources required.

*   **Potential Risks or Challenges (14)** (text, required): Identify any risks and how you plan to mitigate them.

*   **Success Metrics (15)** (text, required): How will you measure the success of this event?

*   **Organizer Full Name (16)** (text, required): Enter your full name.

*   **Organizer Email Address (17)** (email, required): Enter your email address.

*   **Organizer Phone Number (18)** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number.

*   **Additional Comments or Notes (19)** (text, optional): Any other information you would like to share.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
*   Be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact our team for assistance.
