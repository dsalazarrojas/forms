# Exhibitor Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibitor Survey Form is a collection of questions designed to gather information from exhibitors about their experience with the event. The information collected will help the event organizers to improve future events and tailor them to the needs and preferences of the exhibitors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
* Enter your Email address.
* Select the type of event you attended (Conference, Trade Show, or Seminar).
* Answer the feedback question in the free-text "Feedback" field.
* Optionally, add any comments about your experience.
* Fill in the date and time you completed the survey.
* Fill in the start and end time of the survey.
* Indicate your job role(s) by selecting from the list of options (Marketing, Sales).
* Indicate if you will attend future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name**: Fill in your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you attended (Conference, Trade Show, Seminar).
* **Feedback**: Write any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments.
* **Survey Date**: Fill in the date you completed the survey.
* **Survey Time**: Fill in the time you completed the survey.
* **Survey Start Time**: Fill in the start time of the survey.
* **Survey End Time**: Fill in the end time of the survey.
* **Exhibitor**: This field is intended to gather information about the exhibitor, but it's likely meant to be filled in with a generic answer, so you can fill in your name or any other relevant information.
* **Survey**: Similar to the exhibitor field, this field is intended to gather more information about the survey, but it's not very clear what it should be filled in. You can likely fill in any relevant information about the survey.
* **Form**: This field is not very clear, it might be intended to be filled in with the name of the form or the event.
* **Location**: Fill in the location of your booth.
* **Booth**: Fill in the number of your booth.
* **Booth Number**: This field is likely meant to be a duplicate of the booth field, but since it's a number field, you might need to fill in the number with zeros to match the label.
* **Company**: Fill in the name of your company.
* **Job Title**: Fill in your job title.
* **Department**: Fill in your department.
* **Job Role**: Select the job roles you are interested in (Marketing, Sales).
* **Job Function**: Fill in your job function.
* **Product/Services**: Fill in information about the products or services you offer.
* **Why did you attend**: Write any reasons why you attended the event.
* **Will you attend**: Indicate if you will attend future events by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
