# Best Friend Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Friend Quiz is designed to help you better understand your best friend's personality, preferences, and habits. It's a fun and engaging way to get to know each other better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and your friend's name in the first two fields, respectively.
2. Answer the questions about your friend, choosing the options that best fit their personality and preferences.
3. Rate your confidence in each answer using the rating scale at the end.
4. Finally, add a message to your friend and hit submit!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name to get started.
* **Friend's Name** (`friend_name`, text, required): Enter your friend's name, who this quiz is about.
* **How Long Have You Been Friends** (`friendship_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the length of time you've known your friend.
	+ Less than a year
	+ 1 to 3 years
	+ 3 to 5 years
	+ 5 to 10 years
	+ Over 10 years
	+ Since childhood
* **Question 1 - What is your friend's favorite color?** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the color that best fits their style.
* **Question 2 - What is your friend's favorite food?** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Select their favorite cuisine or dish.
* **Question 3 - What is your friend's birthday month?** (`question_3`, select_one, required): Choose the month they were born.
* **Question 4 - What is your friend's dream job or career?** (`question_4`, text, required): Describe their aspirations.
* **Question 5 - What is your friend's favorite hobby?** (`question_5`, select_one, required): Choose the activity they enjoy in their free time.
* **Question 6 - What is your friend's pet peeve?** (`question_6`, text, required): Describe the thing that annoys them the most.
* **Question 7 - Where would your friend love to travel?** (`question_7`, text, required): Describe their dream destination.
* **Question 8 - What is your friend's favorite movie or TV show?** (`question_8`, text, required): Name the show or movie they love watching.
* **Question 9 - What is your friend's biggest fear?** (`question_9`, text, required): Describe the thing that scares them the most.
* **Question 10 - What is your friend's favorite song or music genre?** (`question_10`, text, required): Name the music that they love.
* **How Do You Know Your Friend?** (`friendship_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence in your answers using the scale below.
	+ I Know Everything
	+ I Know Most Things
	+ I Know Some Things
	+ I Am Not Sure
	+ I Probably Failed
* **Message to Your Friend** (`friendship_message`, text, optional): Add a personal message to your friend.
* **Favorite Season** (`question_12`, select_one, required): Choose the season they enjoy most.
* **What Would Your Friend Do on a Perfect Weekend?** (`question_13`, text, required): Describe their ideal weekend.
* **What is Your Friend's Favorite Memory Together?** (`question_14`, text, optional): Share a special moment you both share.
* **How Well Do You Know Your Friend?** (`friendship_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence in your answers using the scale below.
* **Message to Your Friend** (`friendship_message`, text, optional): Add a personal message to your friend.

## Tips
Remember, this quiz is just for fun, and the answers are meant to be a fun way to get to know each other better. Don't worry too much about getting the answers right - it's about sharing laughs and creating memories with your friend.
