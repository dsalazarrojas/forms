# Meal Packing Financial Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Packing Financial Report is used to report on the financial aspects of a meal packing event. This report provides information on total donations received, total expenses, and remaining funds after the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Event Date of the meal packing event.
2. Enter the Event Location where the meal packing event took place.
3. Enter the total donations received for the event.
4. Enter the total expenses for the event.
5. Enter the remaining funds after the event.
6. If applicable, upload any relevant financial documents or records.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the financial organizer of the event.
8. Enter the name of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the meal packing event in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location where the meal packing event took place.
* **Total Donations** (`total_donations`): Enter the total amount of donations received for the event.
* **Total Expenses** (`total_expenses`): Enter the total amount of expenses incurred for the event.
* **Remaining Funds** (`remaining_funds`): Enter the amount of funds remaining after the event.
* **Financial Organizer Name** (`financial_organizer_name`): Enter the name of the person responsible for managing the event's finances.
* **Financial Organizer Email** (`financial_organizer_email`): Enter the email address of the person responsible for managing the event's finances.
* **Financial Organizer Phone** (`financial_organizer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for managing the event's finances.
* **Uploaded Document** (`uploaded_document`): If applicable, upload any relevant financial documents or records related to the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Enter the type of event (e.g. charity event, fundraising event, etc.).
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Uploaded File** (`uploaded_file`): If applicable, upload any additional files or documents related to the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the financial information entered.
* Ensure that the financial organizer's contact information is complete and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the financial organizer or event planner for clarification.
