# Venue Reservation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a venue reservation for an event. It helps the venue management team to gather essential information for evaluating and confirming event requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required to complete the form.
2. Select the date and time you wish to book the venue.
3. Enter your contact details (name, e-mail, and phone number) so we can contact you regarding your request.
4. Provide details about your organization and the type of event you plan to host.
5. Choose the preferred time slot for your event.
6. Review your submission carefully and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Please select the date you would like to book the venue. This is a required field, and it's essential to choose a date that is far enough in advance to allow us to process your request.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Choose the time slot you would like your event to take place. This field is also required, and selecting a time that suits your needs will help us allocate the venue and resources accordingly.
* **Event Venue** (`event_venue`): Enter the name of the venue you would like to book. This is a required field, and ensure you provide the exact name to avoid confusion.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Provide a brief description of the event you are hosting. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand the nature of your event.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the request. This is a required field, as we need to know who to contact regarding your event.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or questions regarding your request. This is a required field, as we need to communicate with you efficiently.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed. This is a required field, and it helps us ensure you receive timely updates.
* **Organization** (`organization`): Provide the name of your organization or group hosting the event. This is a required field, as it helps us understand the context of your request.
* **Venue Type** (`venue_type`): Select the type of venue you are interested in booking (e.g., Office, Theater, Restaurant, or Hotel). This is an optional field, and it helps us understand your preferences.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`): Choose the time slot you would like to book your event. This field has four options: 5:00-6:00 PM, 6:00-7:00 PM, 7:00-8:00 PM, or 8:00-9:00 PM. Selecting the correct time will help us allocate the venue and resources accordingly.

Please note that this form is designed to be user-friendly and efficient. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
