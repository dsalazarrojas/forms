# Christmas Stylish Contact Form Red - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to facilitate communication and feedback. It helps us to better understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields (marked as *required*).
2. Choose your preferred contact method and time of contact.
3. Specify the subject and inquiry type of your message.
4. Write your message in the *Your Message* field.
5. Optionally, you can share attachments or provide additional information in the *Attachments* and *Additional* fields.
6. Review the privacy consent and agree or disagree.
7. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`sender_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can respond to you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are contacting us on behalf of a company, enter its name.
* **-- Contact Information --** (`section_contact_info`, note, optional): This section is for additional contact information.
* **Subject** (`subject`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your inquiry.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Specify the type of your inquiry.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_one, required): Choose how urgent your inquiry is.
* **-- Inquiry Details --** (`section_inquiry_details`, note, optional): This section is for additional details about your inquiry.
* **Christmas Experience** (`christmas_experience`, text, optional): Share your holiday experience.
* **Holiday Wishes** (`holiday_wishes`, text, optional): Share your holiday wishes.
* **Favorite Christmas Memory** (`favorite_christmas_memory`, text, optional): Share a special holiday memory.
* **-- Holiday Sharing --** (`section_holiday_sharing`, note, optional): This section is for sharing holiday moments.
* **Your Message** (`message`, text, required): Write your message to us.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how we should contact you.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, required): Choose a time that suits you best.
* **-- Response Preference --** (`section_response_preference`, note, optional): This section is for response preferences.
* **Join Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Choose if you want to receive our newsletter.
* **Social Media** (`social_media_follow`, select_one, optional): Check if you follow us on social media.
* **How Did You Find Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Choose how you found us.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, text, optional): Share any additional files or information.
* **Privacy Consent** (`privacy_consent`, select_one, required): Agree or disagree with our privacy policy.
* **Christmas Greeting** (`christmas_greeting`, text, optional): Send us a holiday greeting.
* **How Can We Make Your Christmas Better** (`how_can_we_help`, text, optional): Provide suggestions on how we can improve our services.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, text, optional): Share any additional files or information.
