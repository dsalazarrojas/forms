# Mass Attendance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mass Attendance Request Form is designed for users to request mass attendance for a specific date, time, and type. It gathers information about the attendee's details and preferences to ensure a smooth and organized attendance process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your name and contact information (first_name, last_name, email, and phone).
2. Select the date and mass type you wish to attend (Daily Mass, Weekday Mass, or Weekend Mass).
3. Indicate whether or not you plan to attend the mass (mass_time).
4. If you are attending with family members, provide their details in the 'family_members' field.
5. Specify the number of attendees (number_attending).
6. Add any additional comments or information about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date** (date, date, required): Select the date you plan to attend the mass.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Select the time you plan to attend the mass.
* **Number Attending** (number_attending, number, required): Specify the number of people attending with you.
* **Family Members** (family_members, text, optional): List the names and ages of family members attending with you.
* **Mass Type** (mass_type, select_one, required): Select the type of mass you wish to attend (Daily Mass, Weekday Mass, or Weekend Mass).
* **Mass Time** (mass_time, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether or not you plan to attend the mass at the selected time.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your attendance.

## Tips

* Ensure that you submit your request accurately and completely to avoid any delays in processing your request.
* If you are bringing a large group, please make sure to request a special arrangement in advance.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the 'Comments' field.
