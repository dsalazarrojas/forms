# PayJunction Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for a workshop organized by PayJunction. It collects basic contact information and event details for the organizers to keep track of attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Optional: Fill in your Phone number.
5. Optional: Enter your Company name (if applicable).
6. Enter the name of the Workshop you are interested in.
7. Optional: Select the Registration Date for the event.
8. Provide any additional details about the event in the Event Details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: <name> is your first name. Please provide your first name here.
* **Last Name**: <name> is your last name. Please enter your family name here.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached by the workshop organizers. This will be used for follow up and communication about the event.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it here. This is optional and not required for registration.
* **Company**: If you represent a company, please enter the name of your company here. This is optional.
* **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop you are interested in attending. This will help the organizers know which event you are registering for.
* **Registration Date**: If you know the date you want to register for, please select it here. If not, you can skip this field.
* **Event Details**: Provide any additional information about the event, such as your interests, questions, or special requests. This is an optional field for the workshop organizers to get a better understanding of your context.
