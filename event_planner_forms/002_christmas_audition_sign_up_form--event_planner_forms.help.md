## Christmas Audition Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants who want to audition for the Christmas event. This information will be used by the event planners to review and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (Participant name, text, required): Fill out your full legal name.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter your email address for audition schedule and updates.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, optional): Include your country code if you are outside the local area.
* **Audition role or part** (Audition role or part, text, required): Indicate the role or part you are auditioning for, such as choir solo or actor.
* **Age category** (Age category, select_one, required): Select the age group that best describes you.
* **Experience level** (Experience level, select_one, required): Choose the experience level that best describes your skills and experience.
* **Preferred audition time slot** (Preferred audition time slot, time, optional): Indicate your availability, if possible.
* **Accompanist needed** (Accompanist needed, select_one, optional): If you require accompaniment, select 'True'.
* **Special requirements or notes** (Special requirements or notes, text, optional): If you have any accessibility or technical needs, provide them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not a duplicate entry.
* Provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any special requirements or notes, please be specific.
* If you require accompaniment, please make sure to indicate that you need an accompanist.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event planners for clarification.
