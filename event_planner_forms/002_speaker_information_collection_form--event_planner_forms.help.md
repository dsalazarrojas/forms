# Speaker Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Information Collection Form is used to gather essential details about speakers for events or presentations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" (indicated by a *), with the speaker's information.
2. Enter the speaker's first name in the "speaker_first_name" field.
3. Enter the speaker's last name in the "speaker_last_name" field.
4. Enter the speaker's email address in the "speaker_email" field.
5. Enter the speaker's phone number in the "speaker_phone" field.
6. Enter the speaker's company name in the "speaker_company" field.
7. Enter the speaker's position in the "speaker_position" field.
8. Enter the speaker's biography in the "speaker_biography" field.
9. If applicable, enter the speaker's URL or website in the "speaker_url" field.
10. Upload a profile picture for the speaker in the "speaker_image" field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **speaker_first_name (Text, required)**: Enter the speaker's first name.
* **speaker_last_name (Text, required)**: Enter the speaker's last name.
* **speaker_email (Email, required)**: Enter the speaker's email address.
* **speaker_phone (Text, optional)**: Enter the speaker's phone number.
* **speaker_company (Text, optional)**: Enter the speaker's company name.
* **speaker_position (Text, optional)**: Enter the speaker's position or title.
* **speaker_biography (Note, optional)**: Enter a brief description of the speaker.
* **speaker_image (Text, optional)**: Upload a profile picture of the speaker.
* **speaker_url (Text, optional)**: Enter the speaker's URL or website.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that the speaker's profile is complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the event planner for guidance.
* If the speaker is a repeat attendee, please check that we do not already have their information on file before submitting a new entry.
