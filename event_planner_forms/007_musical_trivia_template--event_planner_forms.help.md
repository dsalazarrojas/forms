# Musical Trivia Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Musical Trivia Template is a form designed to collect information about musical knowledge and preferences. It is intended to be a fun and interactive way to quiz users on their musical knowledge, connect with others who share similar interests, and explore different musical genres.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being asked for.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the field explanation below.
3. Leave optional fields blank if you do not have the information or prefer not to answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Musical Knowledge Quiz** (`musical_knowledge_quiz`, text, required): This field is used to ask about your musical knowledge and preferences. Please answer with a brief description of your musical knowledge or interests.
- **Connect to SoundCloud** (`connect_to_soundcloud`, text, required): This field is used to connect to your SoundCloud account or other musical platforms. Please enter your SoundCloud username or a link to your musical profile on other platforms.
- **Easy to Customize** (`easy_to_customize`, text, required): This field is used to confirm if you find the form easy to customize. Please answer with a simple "yes" or "no".
- **No Coding Required** (`no_coding_required`, text, required): This field is used to confirm if you find the form easy to use without requiring coding knowledge. Please answer with a simple "yes" or "no".
- **Quiz Your Family** (`quiz_your_family`, text, required): This field is used to ask if you would like to quiz your family and friends. Please answer with a simple "yes" or "no".
- **Friends and Coworkers** (`friends_and_coworkers`, text, required): This field is used to ask if you would like to connect with friends and coworkers who share similar musical interests. Please answer with a simple "yes" or "no".
- **Musical Knowledge Quiz Connect** (`musical_knowledge_quiz_connect`, text, required): This field is used to connect with others who share similar musical interests. Please enter a brief description of how you would like to connect.
- **Free Online Music Quiz** (`free_online_music_quiz`, text, required): This field is used to ask about your interest in free online music quizzes. Please answer with a simple "yes" or "no".
- **Musical Genre** (`musical_genre`, text, required): This field is used to ask about your musical genre preferences. Please enter a brief description of your favorite musical genre.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering each field.
- Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
- If you encounter any issues or have questions, feel free to ask for help!
