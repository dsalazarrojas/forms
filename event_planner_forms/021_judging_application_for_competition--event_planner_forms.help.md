# Judging Application For Competition - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect application information from potential judges for an event competition. To be eligible, the applicant should have relevant experience and be available to judge the competition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate information.
2. For fields that are not required but relevant, provide as much detail as possible.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): Enter your years of experience related to the competition.
- **Judge Since** (`judge_since`, date, required): Enter the date you first started judging.
- **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are `Available`, `Unavailable`, or `Not Specified`.
- **Conflicts of Interest** (`conflicts_of_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have a `True` or `False` conflict of interest.
- **Submission Start** (`submission`, time, required): Enter your start time for submission.
- **Submission End** (`submission_end`, time, required): Enter your end time for submission.
- **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments for submission.
- **Conflicts of Interest Confidential** (`conflicts_of_interest_confidential`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the conflict is confidential or not.
- **Conflicts of Interest Disclosure** (`conflicts_of_interest_disclosure`, select_multiple, optional): Specify which conflicts of interest you have.
- **Conflicts** (`conflict`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have a conflict of interest.
- **Submit Judging Application** (`submit_judging_application`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are ready to submit your application.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website URL.
- **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, optional): Enter your social media handle.
- **Headshot** (`headshot`, text, optional): Upload your headshot image.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Upload your digital signature.
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.
- **Agreement Reason** (`agreement_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for your agreement or disagreement.
- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are ready to submit your application.
