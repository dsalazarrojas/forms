# summer_art_camp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "summer_art_camp_registration_form" is designed for users to register for a summer art camp. It collects personal and artistic information necessary for the camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your desired summer camp session.
2. Fill out your personal details, including name and contact information.
3. Indicate your preferred art medium(s).
4. Choose a date and time for your session.
5. Optionally, provide additional notes or details about your participation.
6. (Optional) You may be assigned a specific tool or category for your art project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **summer_camp_session** (1, Number, Required):
  Select the summer art camp session you would like to attend. This will be the time slot and date you'll participate in the camp.
* **Name** (2, Text, Required):
  Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed during the camp.
* **phone_number** (3, Text, Required):
  Enter your phone number for camp communication and organization purposes.
* **email** (4, Email, Required):
  Provide an active and accurate email address for camp communication and updates.
* **art_medium** (5, Select One, Required):
  Choose your preferred art medium: painting, drawing, sculpture, music, dance, or other.
* **date** (6, Date, Required):
  Select the date for your summer art camp session (MM/DD/YY).
* **time** (7, Time, Required):
  Choose the time slot for your summer art camp session (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **notes** (8, Notes, Optional):
  Add any additional comments or notes about your participation or preferences.
* **assigned_tool** (9, Text, Optional):
  You may be assigned a specific tool for your art project; enter it here if so.
* **assigned_tool_label** (10, Text, Optional):
  You may be assigned a label or description for your tool; enter it here if so.
* **category** (11, Text, Optional):
  You may be categorized into a specific group for camp organization purposes; enter it here if so.
* **form_id** (12, Number, Optional):
  This field likely tracks the form's ID for internal use; it's not for user input.
* **output_file** (13, Text, Optional):
  You may be asked to provide an output file for your art project; enter it here if so.
* **assigned_tool** (14, Text, Optional):
  This field may be used to record an assigned tool for your project; enter it here if so.
* **category** (15, Text, Optional):
  This field might be used for camp organization or classification purposes; enter it here if so.

## Tips
- Double-check your selections and entries for accuracy.
- Review the form's requirements and your input to avoid errors.
- Ensure you have necessary information and tools for your selected session.
- Contact camp staff if you have any questions or concerns.
