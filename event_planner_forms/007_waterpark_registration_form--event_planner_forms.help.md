# Waterpark Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waterpark Registration Form is designed to collect information from customers about their interest in a specific event at Waterpark. This form is used to gather basic contact information, event preferences, and other relevant details to help event planners understand customer needs.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into sections, each with relevant fields for customers to fill out. Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill out your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Choose a date for the event.
5. Select the type of event you are interested in.
6. Indicate if you will be attending the event with someone else.
7. Choose a time for the event (if applicable).
8. Add any notes or comments you would like to share with the event planners.
9. Enter your address (if relevant for event planning purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required: No): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required: No): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required: No): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: No): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (Date, Required: No): Choose a date for the event.
* **Event Type** (Select One, Required: Yes): Select the type of event you are interested in.
* **Attendees** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Indicate if you will be attending the event with someone else.
* **Time** (Time, Required: No): Choose a time for the event (if applicable).
* **Note** (Note, Required: No): Add any notes or comments you would like to share with the event planners.
* **Address** (Text, Required: No): Enter your address (if relevant for event planning purposes).
