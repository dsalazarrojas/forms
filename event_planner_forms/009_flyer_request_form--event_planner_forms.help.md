# Flyer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flyer Request Form is used to gather necessary information to design and print a flyer for an event. The form collects details about the event, contact person, and design preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Event Title** field with the name of the event.
2. Enter the **Date** of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Choose the **Time** of the event using the 24-hour clock format `HH:MM`.
4. Enter the **Location** of the event.
5. Provide a brief description of the **Event Details**.
6. Enter the **Name** of the contact person responsible for the event.
7. Enter the contact person's **EMail** address.
8. Enter the contact person's **Phone** number.
9. Upload the **Event Image** that will be used for the flyer design.
10. Specify the **Design Preferences** for the flyer (text, color scheme, etc.).
11. Choose the **Color Scheme** for the flyer (red, blue, or green).
12. Select the **Paper Size** for the flyer (A3, A5, A6, A7, A8, or A9).
13. Choose the **Printing Method** (digital, offset, or screen).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title**: The name of the event that the flyer is for. (Required)
* **Date**: The date of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. (Required)
* **Time**: The time of the event in the 24-hour clock format `HH:MM`. (Required)
* **Location**: The place where the event will take place.
* **Event Details**: A brief description of the event. (Not Required)
* **Name**: The name of the contact person responsible for the event.
* **EMail**: The contact person's email address.
* **Phone**: The contact person's phone number.
* **Event Image**: Upload an image that will be used for the flyer design.
* **Design Preferences**: Specify any special design preferences for the flyer (text, images, etc.).
* **Color Scheme**: Choose a color scheme for the flyer (red, blue, or green). (Not Required)
* **Paper Size**: Choose the size of the paper that the flyer will be printed on (A3, A5, A6, A7, A8, or A9). (Not Required)
* **Printing Method**: Choose the printing method for the flyer (digital, offset, or screen). (Not Required)

Tips:

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure the flyer is designed and printed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event planner for guidance.
