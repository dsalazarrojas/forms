# Arts And Craft Fair Application Form With 2Checkout Payment Gateway - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information from vendors interested in participating in an Arts and Craft Fair. Vendors will be asked to provide personal details, business and product description, booth selection, and other relevant information. Once completed, the form will direct you to the 2Checkout payment page for secure payment processing.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your full name and email address in the "Applicant Full Name" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. Provide a brief description of your business and products in the "Business and Product Description" field.
3. Select your preferred booth option from the options provided in the "Select Your Booth Option" field.
4. Confirm that you will be directed to the 2Checkout payment page after submission.
5. Answer "True" if you have vendor liability insurance, otherwise select "No, I need more information".
6. Enter the number of people assisting you at the booth, including yourself.
7. If you have any dietary requirements, provide them in the "Vendor Lunch Dietary Requirements" field.
8. Confirm that you will have your booth set up by 9 AM on event day.
9. Agree to the vendor terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for further instructions.
* **Business and Product Description**: Provide a brief description of your business and products.
* **Select Your Booth Option**: Choose from the available booth options: Indoor, Outdoor, or Premium Corner Booth.
* **After Submission, you will be redirected to the secure 2Checkout payment page**: This is a note informing you that after submitting the form, you will be directed to the 2Checkout payment page.
* **Do you have vendor liability insurance**: Answer "True" if you have vendor liability insurance, or select "No, I need more information" if you don't have it.
* **Number of Assistants at Booth**: Enter the number of people assisting you at the booth, including yourself.
* **Vendor Lunch Dietary Requirements**: If you have any specific dietary requirements, please provide them here.
* **I agree to have my booth fully set up by 9 AM on event day**: Confirm that you will have your booth set up by 9 AM on event day.
* **I have read and agree to the vendor terms and conditions**: Select "I Agree" to confirm that you have read and agree to the vendor terms and conditions.
