# Virtual Event Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a virtual event invitation. It's designed to collect essential information for organizing a virtual event, including event details, invitees' contact information, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the virtual event in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
4. (Optional) If you want to specify a start and end time for the virtual event, enter them in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
5. Enter a brief description of the virtual event in the "Event Details" field.
6. Enter the name of the person or organization you are inviting to the virtual event in the "Invitee" field.
7. Enter your attendee's name, address, and phone number in the "Attendee Details" field.
8. Enter the time and date for the virtual event appointment in the "Appointment Time" and "Appointment Date" fields.
9. Enter a message to be sent to the invitee in the "Message" field.
10. Enter the name of the person sending the message in the "Message From" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the person sending the message in the "Phone" field.
12. Enter the email address of the person sending the message in the "Email" field.
13. Enter the location of the virtual event in the "Location" field.
14. Enter any additional note for the message in the "Message Note" field.
15. Enter a unique identifier for the form in the "Form ID" field.
16. Enter the date and time when the form was submitted in the "Submit Date" and "Submit Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Start Date** (3, date, required): Enter the start date of the virtual event.
* **End Date** (4, date, required): Enter the end date of the virtual event.
* **Start Time** (5, time, optional): Enter the start time of the virtual event.
* **End Time** (6, time, optional): Enter the end time of the virtual event.
* **Event Details** (7, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the virtual event.
* **Invitee** (8, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization you are inviting to the virtual event.
* **Attendee Details** (9, text, required): Enter your attendee's name, address, and phone number.
* **Appointment Time** (10, time, required): Enter the time of the virtual event appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (11, date, required): Enter the date of the virtual event appointment.
* **Message** (12, text, optional): Enter a message to be sent to the invitee.
* **Message To** (13, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization you are sending the message to.
* **Message From** (14, text, required): Enter the name of the person sending the message.
* **Phone** (15, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person sending the message.
* **Email** (16, email, required): Enter the email address of the person sending the message.
* **Location** (17, text, optional): Enter the location of the virtual event.
* **Message Location** (18, text, optional): Enter any additional location for the message.
* **Message Note** (19, text, optional): Enter any additional note for the message.
* **Form ID** (20, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Submit Date** (21, date, required): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (22, time, required): Enter the time when the form was submitted.
