# Event Load In And Load Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Load In And Load Out Form is a data collection tool designed to gather essential information for event planning, management, and coordination. This form helps event organizers and planners record critical details about load-in and load-out processes, allowing for better organization and tracking of events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Load-in" page with the required load-in date and time information.
2. On the "Load-out" page, enter the load-out date and time details.
3. Provide load-in and load-out notes for further reference.
4. Select the appropriate load-in and load-out supplies from the provided options.
5. Enter the contact person's email address and details for the event planner and coordinator.
6. Specify the address and load-in and load-out locations for the event.
7. Choose the load-in equipment from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Load-in** (`1`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the date and time for the load-in process.
* **Load-out** (`2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the date and time for the load-out process.
* **Load-in Date** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the load-in date for the event.
* **Load-out Date** (`4`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the load-out date for the event.
* **Load-in Time** (`5`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the load-in time for the event.
* **Load-out Time** (`6`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the load-out time for the event.
* **Load-in Notes** (`7`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional notes for the load-in process.
* **Load-out Notes** (`8`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional notes for the load-out process.
* **Load-in Supplies** (`9`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the load-in supplies from the provided options.
* **Load-out Supplies** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the load-out supplies from the provided options.
* **Event Planner** (`11`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the event planner.
* **Event Coordinator** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and details of the event coordinator.
* **Contact Person** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and details of the contact person.
* **Address** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the event location.
* **Load-in Location** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the load-in location for the event.
* **Load-out Location** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the load-out location for the event.
* **Load-in Equipment** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the load-in equipment from the provided options.
