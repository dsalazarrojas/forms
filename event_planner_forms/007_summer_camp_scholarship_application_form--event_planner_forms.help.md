# Summer Camp Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Summer Camp Scholarship Application Form is a critical application used to process summer camp scholarship applications. This form gathers information from campers and their families to evaluate their eligibility for a summer camp scholarship. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each page of the form thoroughly and accurately.
* For each field, enter the required information according to the field label.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`id: 1`)
  * This is the main application form where you can enter your name, campers information, and other relevant details.
* **Campers Information** (`id: 2`)
  * Enter the name, age, and other relevant information about the camper(s) applying for the scholarship.
* **Parents/Guardians Information** (`id: 3`)
  * Enter your name, contact information, and other relevant details as a parent or guardian of the camper.
* **Income Details** (`id: 4`)
  * Enter your household income details to help us assess your eligibility for the scholarship.
* **Reasons For Application** (`id: 5`)
  * Briefly explain why you are applying for the summer camp scholarship and how it will benefit you or your child.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 6`)
  * Provide any additional information that supports your application.
* **Email** (`id: 7`)
  * Enter your email address where we can contact you for any updates or notifications.
* **Phone** (`id: 8`)
  * Enter your phone number where we can reach you for any questions or concerns.
* **Confirm Application** (`id: 9`)
  * Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Confirm Phone** (`id: 10`)
  * Confirm the phone number entered earlier.
* **Confirm Email** (`id: 11`)
  * Confirm the email address entered earlier.
* **Send Confirmation** (`id: 12`)
  * Select how you would like to receive confirmation of your application submission.
* **Confirmation Message** (`id: 13`)
  * This field is automatically populated based on your selection in the previous field.
* **Camp Details** (`id: 14`)
  * Enter any relevant details about the camp you are applying for.
* **Camp Address** (`id: 15`)
  * Enter the address of the camp you are applying for.
* **Send To** (`id: 16`)
  * Select who you would like to send the confirmation to.
* **Other Send To** (`id: 17`)
  * Enter the name and contact information of an alternative contact person.
* **Other Send To Email** (`id: 18`)
  * Enter the email address of the alternative contact person.
* **Other Send To Phone** (`id: 19`)
  * Enter the phone number of the alternative contact person.
* **Send Confirmation To** (`id: 20`)
  * Select who you would like to receive confirmation from.
* **Send Confirmation To Email** (`id: 21`)
  * Enter the email address where you would like to receive confirmation.
* **Send Confirmation To Phone** (`id: 22`)
  * Enter the phone number where you would like to receive confirmation.
* **Confirmation Template** (`id: 23`)
  * Select the format of the confirmation message you would like to receive.
* **Custom Fields** (`id: 24`)
  * This field is not currently used.
* **Form Submission** (`id: 25`)
  * This field is not currently used.
