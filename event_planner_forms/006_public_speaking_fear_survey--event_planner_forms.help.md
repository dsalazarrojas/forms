# public_speaking_fear_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool to help individuals gauge their levels of anxiety and discomfort when speaking publicly. It's a helpful resource for those who need to overcome their fears and build confidence in their public speaking abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to your experiences and feelings when speaking in public. Choose the answer that best describes your response on a scale from "Not at all" to "Almost always".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Anxiety Level** (`anxiety_level`, Number, Optional): This field gauges your level of anxiety when speaking in public.
* **Speaking Fear** (`speaking_fear`, Time, Optional): This question assesses how long you've felt anxious or fearful about speaking in public.
* **Breathing Rate** (`breathing_rate`, Number, Optional): This field measures how quickly you're breathing when speaking in public.
* **Sweating Level** (`sweating_level`, Select One, Optional): This question gauges your body's physical response to public speaking (e.g. do you sweat more or less often?).
* **Trembling Knees** (`trembling_knees`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field measures how often you experience physical tremors or shakiness in your knees when speaking.
* **Speaking Voice** (`speaking_voice`, Select One, Optional): This question assesses how confident and clear your speaking voice is.
* **Speaking Speed** (`speaking_speed`, Date, Optional): This field gauges how quickly you speak.
* **Speaking Volume** (`speaking_volume`, Time, Optional): This question measures the volume of your voice when speaking.
* **Speaking Confidence** (`speaking_confidence`, Select One, Optional): This field gauges how confident you feel when speaking.
* **Speaking Clarity** (`speaking_clarity`, Select Multiple, Optional): This question assesses how clear and coherent your speech is.
* **Breathing Rate Again** (`breathing_rate`, Number, Optional): This field measures how quickly you're breathing when speaking.
* **Heart Rate** (`heart_rate`, Number, Optional): This question gauges how quickly your heart rate changes when speaking.
* **Speaking Body Language** (`speaking_body_language`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field measures how often you exhibit physical responses like fidgeting or shifting in your seat when speaking.
* **Speaking Body Heat** (`speaking_body_heat`, Select Multiple, Optional): This question assesses how often your body temperature increases when speaking.
* **Tongue-Tied** (`speaking_tongue_tied`, Select One, Optional): This field gauges how often you struggle to express yourself or find the right words.
* **Stammering** (`speaking_stammering`, Select Multiple, Optional): This question measures how often you struggle to form words or speak fluently.
* **Hesitation** (`speaking_hesitation`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field gauges how often you pause or hesitate before speaking.
* **Filling Gaps** (`speaking_filling_gap`, Select One, Optional): This question assesses how often you experience a "blank" or a pause in your thoughts.
* **Forgetting** (`speaking_forgetting`, Select One, Optional): This field measures how often you forget key points or words when speaking.
* **Speaking Speed of Speaking** (`speaking_speed_of_speaking`, Time, Optional): This question gauges how quickly you tend to speak.
* **Speaking Pauses** (`speaking_pauses`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field measures how often you pause or hesitate between words or thoughts.
* **Tongue-Twister** (`speaking_tongue_twister`, Select Multiple, Optional): This question assesses how often you struggle to form words or sound out words.
* **Feared Consequences** (`speaking_feared_consequences`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field gauges how often you worry about the consequences of speaking up or making mistakes.

Please note that all answers are optional and will help us better understand your public speaking experiences and anxieties.
