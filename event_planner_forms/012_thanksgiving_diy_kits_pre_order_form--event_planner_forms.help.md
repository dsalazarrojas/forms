# Thanksgiving DIY Kits Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers pre-order a customized Thanksgiving DIY kit tailored to their preferences. It collects information about the customer's name, contact details, and order specifics such as turkey size, number of guests, and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information in fields 1-3.
2. Choose the turkey size that suits your needs from field 6.
3. Enter the number of guests you're planning to host in field 7.
4. For fields 8 and 9, provide your order details and any additional information you'd like to include.
5. Select the category for your order from field 11.
6. If you have any special requests, enter them in field 12.
7. Confirm your order in field 13 by selecting "True."
8. Finally, choose whether to submit or save and return later in field 14.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Name** (text, required): Enter your name.
* **2. Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **3. Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **4. Date** (date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **5. Time** (time, required): Enter the time of your event.
* **6. Turkey Size** (select one, optional): Choose from 2-4 persons, 5-6 persons, or 7-8 persons.
* **7. Number of Guests** (number, optional): Enter the number of guests you're planning to host.
* **8. Address** (text, optional): Provide your address for shipping purposes.
* **9. Order Details** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your order.
* **10. Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **11. Category** (select one, optional): Choose from Turkey, Ham, or Stuffing.
* **12. Special Requests** (text, optional): Enter any special requests for your order.
* **13. Confirm** (select one, required): Confirm your order by selecting "True."
* **14. Submit** (select one, required): Choose to submit or save and return later.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful order.
* If you have any special requests, be as detailed as possible to ensure we can accommodate your needs.
* If you choose to save and return later, your progress will be saved, and you can return to the form when you're ready.
