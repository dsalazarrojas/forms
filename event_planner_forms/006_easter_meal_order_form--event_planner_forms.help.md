# Easter Meal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Meal Order Form is used to order an Easter meal for a specific date. Customers will be asked to select their meal preference, contact information, and other details to facilitate the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your Easter meal preference from the options provided in the "What's your easter meal order preference?" section.
2. Choose your preferred main course from the options in the "Which main course would you like?" section.
3. Select your preferred side dish from the options in the "Which side dish would you like?" section. You can choose multiple options.
4. Decide if you would like a holiday dessert.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted (by phone, email, or none).
6. Enter your phone number in the "Your Contact Number" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Your Email" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
9. Enter your address in the "Your Address" field.
10. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Any Special Requests?" field. (Optional)
11. If you have a specific date for the Easter event, enter it in the "Event Date" field. (Optional)
12. Enter the time for the Easter event in the "Event Time" field. (Optional)
13. Decide if you are celebrating Easter on a specific day.
14. Enter any additional Easter-related details such as Easter egg and Easter Bunny preferences. (Optional)
15. Confirm if you would like to receive an Easter meal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's your easter meal order preference?** (`Easter Meal Order Form`): This is the starting page of the form where you will select your Easter meal preference. Select one of the options to proceed.
* **Which main course would you like?** (`main_course_page`): Choose your preferred main course from the options provided.
* **Which side dish would you like?** (`side_dish_page`): Select multiple side dishes that you would like to have with your meal.
* **Would you like a holiday dessert?** (`holiday_dessert_page`): Decide if you would like to have a dessert on the holiday.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information_page`): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Your Contact Number** (`contact_number_page`): Enter your phone number.
* **Your Email** (`email_page`): Enter your email address.
* **Your Name** (`name_page`): Enter your name.
* **Your Address** (`address_page`): Enter your address.
* **Any Special Requests?** (`special_request_page`): Enter any special requests you may have. (Optional)
* **Event Date** (`easter_event_date_page`): Enter the date for the Easter event. (Optional)
* **Event Time** (`easter_event_time_page`): Enter the time for the Easter event. (Optional)
* **Easter Day** (`easter_day_page`): Decide if you are celebrating Easter on a specific day.
* **Easter Egg** (`easter_egg_page`): Enter any additional Easter egg details. (Optional)
* **Easter Bunny** (`easter_bunny_page`): Enter any additional Easter Bunny details. (Optional)
* **Easter Meal** (`easter_meal_page`): Confirm if you would like to receive an Easter meal.
* **Easter Holiday** (`easter_holiday_page`): Confirm if you are celebrating Easter on a specific holiday.
