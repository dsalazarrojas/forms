# Virtual Convention Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Convention Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees to provide an optimal experience during the virtual convention. The form gathers essential details such as name, contact information, and attendee preferences to ensure that all attendees are properly registered and receive relevant updates and information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Select your country from the **Country** dropdown menu.
5. Enter your city in the **City** field.
6. Choose your attendee type from the **Attendee Type** dropdown menu.
7. Enter the number of attendees you're registering in the **Attendee Count** field.
8. Provide any additional details about your attendees in the **Attendee Details** field.
9. If you have any special requests, please specify them in the **Special Requests** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and information about the convention.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence from the dropdown menu.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **Attendee Type**: Choose one or multiple attendee types that apply to you.
* **Attendee Count**: Enter the number of attendees you're registering.
* **Attendee Details**: Provide any additional details about your attendees, such as their ages or special needs.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you may have, such as accommodations or dietary restrictions.
