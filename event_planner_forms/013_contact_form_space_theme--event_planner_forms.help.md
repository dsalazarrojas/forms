# Contact Form Space Theme - Help Guide

## Purpose
This contact form is designed for individuals or organizations to reach out to us for any inquiries related to event planning. It helps us understand their needs and preferences for a successful event execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Choose the type of event you are planning (Corporate Event, Social Gathering, Conference, etc.).
3. Indicate the expected size of your event.
4. Select a preferred date for your event.
5. Choose a venue type (Indoor, Outdoor, Hybrid, or no preference).
6. If you have any special requirements, such as specific audio-visual equipment or catering needs, please let us know.
7. Estimate your budget range for the event.
8. Provide any additional comments or information to help us better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Us** (`intro`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form, providing a general overview of the purpose of the form.
* **Your Name** (`name`, `text`, required: true): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your contact email address where we can reach you for further discussions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you have a preferred phone number where we can contact you, please enter it.
* **Event Category** (`event_category`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the type of event you are planning (Corporate Event, Social Gathering, Conference, etc.).
* **Event Size** (`event_size`, `select_one`, required: true): Indicate the expected number of attendees for your event (Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Preferred Event Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required: true): Select a date for your event when possible.
* **Location Preference** (`location_preference`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of venue you prefer (Indoor, Outdoor, Hybrid, or no preference).
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, required: false): If you have any specific requirements or needs for your event, such as audio-visual equipment or catering, please let us know.
* **Catering Required** (`catering_needed`, `select_one`, required: true): Indicate if you need catering services for your event (True, False, or Maybe).
* **AV Equipment Needed** (`av_equipment`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you need any audio-visual equipment, such as projectors, microphones, or lighting, select the options that apply.
* **Estimated Budget** (`budget`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose your estimated budget range for the event (Budget Conscious, Moderate, Flexible, or Premium).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information or comments to help us better understand your needs.
