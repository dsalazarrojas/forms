# Runner Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Runner Tracking Form is a tool designed to collect and record essential information about a runner's activities, making it easier to track progress, provide guidance, and analyze performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the Runner Tracking Form link to access the form.
2.  Select the required fields you want to record for the runner. These fields will be highlighted as they are the most critical information for the runner's activity.
3.  Enter the Date and Time of the runner's activity.
4.  Record the Running Distance of the runner's activity.
5.  Enter any additional Running Notes for the runner's activity, such as notable events or milestones.
6.  Record Customer Feedback, if applicable.
7.  Provide the runner's Email and Phone Number, as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Running History**:
    *   This is a text field where you can input a list or brief description of the runner's past running events and activities. This helps to keep a detailed log of the runner's activities, which can inform coaching and training.
*   **Select Fields**: 
    *   This is a multiple-select field where you can pick any relevant fields for the runner, as per your discretion.
    *   Choose any or all fields that apply.
*   **Date**: 
    *   Record the date the runner participated in the activity.
*   **Time**: 
    *   Record the time the runner participated in the activity.
*   **Running Distance**: 
    *   Enter the distance the runner covered during the activity.
*   **Running Notes**: 
    *   Enter any additional notes about the activity, such as notable events or milestones.
*   **Customer Feedback**: 
    *   Record any customer feedback or comments from the runner, if applicable.
*   **Email**: 
    *   Input the runner's email address, which is essential for communication and updates.
*   **Phone Number**: 
    *   Input the runner's phone number, which is necessary for direct contact and reminders.
