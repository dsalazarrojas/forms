# Rooftop Films Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit information about a film for consideration by Rooftop Films.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the title of the film in the "Film Title" field.
5. Enter the URL of the film in the "Film URL" field.
6. Specify the year of release for the film in the "Film Year" field.
7. Select the type of film (feature or short) in the "Film Type" field.
8. Select the format of the film (e.g., digital, Blu-ray, DVD) in the "Film Format" fields, using the checkboxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Film Title**: Enter the title of the film you are submitting.
* **Film URL**: Enter the URL where the film can be accessed (e.g., Vimeo, YouTube, website).
* **Film Year**: Enter the year the film was released.
* **Film Type**: Select the type of film you are submitting (Feature or Short).
* **Film Format**: Select all applicable formats for the film (e.g., Digital, Blu-ray, DVD).
