# Balloon Flights Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Balloon Flights Booking Form is designed to collect information from passengers booking balloon flights. The form is used to ensure that the flight experience is enjoyable and safe for all passengers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Passenger Full Name field with the full name of the primary passenger.
2. Enter the Email Address of the primary passenger for booking confirmation purposes.
3. Enter the Phone Number of the primary passenger for flight updates.
4. Select the Number of Passengers in your party.
5. Choose the Flight Type that suits your preferences.
6. Select the Preferred Flight Date for your flight.
7. Choose the Flight Location where you would like to take off.
8. Enter the Approximate Weight of each passenger for safety calculations.
9. Indicate if any passengers under 4 feet tall are flying.
10. Select any Medical Conditions that you or your passengers may have.
11. Indicate if any passengers need Mobility Assistance.
12. Decide if you would like to add a Photography Package to your booking.
13. Choose a Champagne Upgrade option if desired.
14. Check if you need Transportation from Hotel.
15. Enter your Hotel Name if you have chosen to add transportation.
16. Select your preferred Payment Method.
17. Indicate if this is a Gift Booking.
18. Confirm that you understand and agree to the Weather Policy.
19. Agree to the Liability Waiver.
20. Enter the Name and Phone Number of your Emergency Contact.
21. Make any Special Requests or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Full Name** (`passenger_full_name`, **Required**): Enter the full name of the primary passenger.
* **Email Address** (`passenger_email`, **Required**): Enter the Email Address of the primary passenger for booking confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`passenger_phone`, **Required**): Enter the Phone Number of the primary passenger for flight updates.
* **Number of Passengers** (`number_of_passengers`, **Required**): Select the total number of people in your party.
* **Flight Type** (`flight_type`, **Required**): Choose the type of balloon experience you desire.
* **Preferred Flight Date** (`preferred_date`, **Required**): Select the first-choice date for your flight.
* **Alternate Date** (`alternate_date`, **Optional**): Enter a backup date in case the first-choice date is unavailable due to weather.
* **Flight Location** (`flight_location`, **Required**): Choose the preferred launch site for your flight.
* **Special Occasion** (`special_occasion`, **Optional**): Indicate if you are celebrating a special event, such as a birthday or anniversary.
* **Passenger Weights** (`passenger_weights`, **Required**): Enter the approximate weight of each passenger for safety calculations.
* **Height Restrictions** (`height_restrictions`, **Required**): Indicate if any passengers under 4 feet tall are flying.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, **Required**): Select any medical conditions you or your passengers may have.
* **Mobility Assistance Required** (`mobility_assistance`, **Required**): Indicate if you or your passengers need help boarding the balloon basket.
* **Photography Package** (`photography_package`, **Optional**): Decide if you would like to add a professional photo package to your booking.
* **Champagne Upgrade** (`champagne_upgrade`, **Optional**): Choose a champagne upgrade option, if desired.
* **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, **Optional**): Check if you need transportation from your Hotel.
* **Hotel Name** (`hotel_name`, **Optional**): Enter your Hotel Name if you have chosen to add transportation.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, **Required**): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Gift Certificate** (`gift_certificate`, **Optional**): Indicate if this is a gift booking.
* **Weather Policy Acknowledgment** (`weather_policy_acknowledgment`, **Required**): Confirm that you understand and agree to the weather policy.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, **Required**): Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, **Required**): Enter the Name of your Emergency Contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, **Required**): Enter the phone number of your Emergency Contact.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, **Optional**): Make any additional requests or questions you may have.
