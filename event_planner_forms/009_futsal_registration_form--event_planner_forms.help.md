# Futsal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Futsal Registration Form is for teams to register for Futsal events such as leagues or tournaments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the team's name.
2. Choose the type of Futsal event your team is participating in.
3. Enter the team's lead contact information, including their email and phone number.
4. Provide the team's address and city.
5. Enter the team's state and postal code.
6. If applicable, enter the league ID and tournament ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name**: Enter your team's name.
* **Team Type**: Choose the type of Futsal event your team is participating in (Futsal League, Futsal Tournament, or Futsal Event).
* **Team Lead**: Enter the name of the team lead or main contact person.
* **Team Lead E-Mail**: Enter the email address of the team lead.
* **Team Lead Phone**: Enter the phone number of the team lead.
* **Team Address**: Enter the team's address.
* **Team City**: Enter the city where the team is located.
* **Team State**: Enter the state where the team is located.
* **Team Postal Code**: Enter the team's postal code.
* **League ID**: If your team is participating in a specific league, enter the corresponding ID number.
* **Tournament ID**: If your team is participating in a specific tournament, enter the corresponding ID number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for your team.
* If you're not sure what to enter for Team Type, you can choose "Futsal Event" if you're not participating in a specific league or tournament.
* If you're not sure what to enter for League ID or Tournament ID, you can leave these fields blank if you're not participating in a specific league or tournament.
