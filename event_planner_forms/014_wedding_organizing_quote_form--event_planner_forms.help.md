# Wedding Organizing Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for couples who are interested in booking a wedding planning service to provide essential details for a personalized quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To get a wedding planning quote, simply fill out this form with accurate and honest information about your wedding plans and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Couple Information** (1. Couple Information, Text, Optional): Please provide a brief introduction of you and your partner, such as your names, special occasions, or any relevant details about your relationship.
* **Wedding Details** (2. Wedding Details, Text, Optional): Share as much information as you can about your wedding, including date, time, location, and number of guests.
* **Wedding Services** (3. Wedding Services, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the wedding planning services you're interested in, such as full, partial, or coordination services.
* **Budget Details** (4. Budget Details, Text, Optional): Provide your estimated budget for your wedding, including any financial concerns you have.
* **Budget Breakdown** (5. Budget Breakdown, Text, Optional): Break down your estimated budget by service or category.
* **Communication Preferences** (6. Communication Preferences, Select One, Optional): Indicate how you'd like to be contacted, such as phone call, email, or both.
* **Contact Info** (7. Contact Info, Text, Optional): Leave your name and contact information for easy communication with our team.
* **Special Requests** (8. Special Requests, Text, Optional): Share any specific requests or unique details about your wedding.
* **Payment Terms** (9. Payment Terms, Select One, Optional): Choose how you'd like to pay for our services, including credit card, check, or bank transfer.
* **Terms and Conditions** (10. Terms and Conditions, Select One, Optional): Let us know you have read and accepted our terms and conditions for your booking.
* **Submission** (11. Submission, Text, Optional): You can include any additional details about your wedding plans, if needed.
* **Assigned Tool** (12. Assigned Tool, Text, Optional): This field is for internal use; please do not fill it out.
## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when providing your information to get an accurate quote.
- Please make sure to check your communication preferences to ensure we can get in touch with you effectively.
- If you have any special requests or concerns, don't hesitate to mention them in the special requests field.
