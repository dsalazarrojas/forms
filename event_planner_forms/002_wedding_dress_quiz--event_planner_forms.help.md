# Wedding Dress Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Dress Quiz is designed to help you find your perfect wedding dress based on your personal preferences and body type. This quiz will guide you through a series of questions to determine the style, shape, color, and other characteristics that suit you best.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select an option from each field to answer the questions.
2. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "True" in the field descriptions below).
3. Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form to get your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of wedding dress would you like to wear?** (`title`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
  - Choose from: A-line, Mermaid, Trumpet, Ball Gown, Ballroom, Tea Length, Mermaid/Trumpet, Fit and Flare, Sheath, Column, A-line/Column, Ball Gown/Mermaid, Mermaid/Fit and Flare, Ball Gown/Sheath
* **What is your body type?** (`silhouette1`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`)
  - Choose from: Petite, Tall, Average, Full, Hourglass
* **What is your design style?** (`design1`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
  - Choose from: Classic/Traditional, Vintage-Inspired, Modern/Contemporary, Romantic/Rustic, Bohemian, Minimalist
* **What is your neckline preference?** (`neckline1`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`)
  - Choose from: Round, V-Neck, Heart/Heart-Shaped, Sweetheart, Square, Off-the-Shoulder, Scooped, Straps, High/High, Low/Low, Bell
* **What is your sleeve preference?** (`sleeve1`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
  - Choose from: Short, Long, Bell, Cap, Off-the-Shoulder, Cap/Short, Bell/Long, Long/Off-the-Shoulder, Short/Cap, Long/Long
* **What is your fabric preference?** (`fabric1`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`)
  - Choose from: Lace, Satin, Silk, Organza, Tulle, Brocade, Charmeuse
* **What is your color preference?** (`color1`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
  - Choose from: White, Ivory, Beige, Pastel, Champagne, Soft Pink, Deep Navy, Deep Purple, Emerald Green, Bright Pink, Turquoise
* **What is your budget for the wedding dress?** (`price1`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
  - Choose from: Under 1,000, 1,000-3,000, 3,000-5,000, 5,000-7,000, 7,000-10,000, Over 10,000
* **How would you describe your style?** (`style1`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
  - Choose from: Classic, Trendy, Bohemian, Elegant, Whimsical, Romantic, Casual, Rustic, Vintage, Glam
* **What is your body size?** (`size1`, `select_one`, required: `true`)
  - Choose from: Small, Medium, Large, Plus, Petite, X-Large, X-Plus
* **What is your height?** (`height1`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
  - Choose from: Under 5ft 0in, 5ft 0in-5ft 3in, 5ft 3in-5ft 6in, 5ft 6in-5ft 9in, 5ft 9in-6ft 0in, 6ft 0in-6ft 3in, 6ft 3in-6ft 6in, 6ft 6in-6ft 9in, 6ft 9in-7ft 0in, Over 7ft 0in
* **How old are you?** (`age1`, `number`, required: `false`)
  - Enter your age in years
* **What is your age group?** (`age_group1`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
  - Choose from: 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+
* **What is your wedding date?** (`wedding_date1`, `date`, required: `true`)
  - Choose a date for your wedding
* **Where will you be getting married?** (`wedding_location1`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
  - Choose from: Beach, City Hall, Church, Garden, Castle, Mountain, Mountain Lake, Park, Barn, Vineyard
