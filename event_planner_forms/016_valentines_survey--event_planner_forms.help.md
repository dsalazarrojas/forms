# valentines_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your Valentine's Day preferences, favorite colors, favorite holiday, favorite time of day, and any special Valentine's Day message you'd like to send. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form title to begin.
2. Enter your first name in the text field labeled "first_name."
3. Enter your email address in the email field, following the standard email format (e.g., `example@domain.com`).
4. Select your favorite color from the dropdown menu labeled "favorite_color."
5. If you have a favorite holiday, select it from the dropdown menu labeled "favorite_day."
6. If you'd like, enter your favorite time of day in the "time" field.
7. In the "message" field, type a special Valentine's Day message, if you'd like.
8. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name as it should be displayed. 
* **email**: Enter a valid email address. 
* **favorite_color**: Select your favorite color from the options provided (red, blue, green, yellow).
* **birthday**: Leave this field blank if you don't want to provide your birthday. 
* **favorite_day**: Select your favorite holiday from the options provided (Valentines, Christmas, Halloween).
* **favorite_time**: Leave this field blank if you don't have a preference for a specific time. 
* **message**: Type a message to show your love and appreciation. 
* **phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
