# Wedding EMCEE Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding EMCEE Contract Form is used to collect information from the wedding emcee about their services, rates, and schedule for an event. This form is a crucial part of the contract between the couple and the emcee, ensuring a clear understanding of the terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for the couple and emcee, such as names, contact details, and payment information.
2. Select the services provided by the emcee from the available options.
3. Enter the emcee's rates and schedule.
4. Review and agree to the terms of the contract by signing below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Couple Name** (`couple_name`, `text`, required): Enter the names of the couple booking the emcee services.
* **Couple Email** (`couple_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the couple.
* **Emcee Name** (`emcee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emcee providing services.
* **Emcee Phone** (`emcee_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emcee.
* **Emcee Services** (`emcee_services`, `text`, required): Describe the services provided by the emcee.
* **Emcee Rates** (`emcee_rates`, `number`, required): Enter the rate for the emcee's services.
* **Emcee Schedule** (`emcee_schedule`, `time`, required): Enter the schedule for the emcee's services.
* **Emcee Terms** (`emcee_terms`, `text`, required): Review and agree to the terms of the contract.
* **Emcee Signature** (`emcee_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the contract to confirm agreement.
* **Couple Signature** (`couple_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the contract to confirm agreement from the couple.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Contract Version** (`contract_version`, `text`, required): Enter the version of the contract.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, `select_one`, optional): Enter the status of the contract (e.g., "Active", "Inactive").
* **Contract Revision** (`contract_rev`, `number`, required): Enter the revision number of the contract.
* **Contract Revision Date** (`contract_rev_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the contract revision.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of signature.

Note: Fields with a "required" property equal to true are mandatory.
