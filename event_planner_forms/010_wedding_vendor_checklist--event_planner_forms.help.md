# Wedding Vendor Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Vendor Checklist form is used to gather and store information about vendors that are being considered or already working with for wedding planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the vendor's name in the "Vendor Name" field.
2. Select the type of vendor from the "Vendor Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the vendor's website (if applicable) in the "Vendor Website" field.
4. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Name" field.
5. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Select the status of the quote from the "Status" dropdown menu.
7. Enter any additional notes about the vendor in the "Vendor Notes" field.
8. Fill in the vendor's price and currency in the "Vendor Price" and "Price Currency" dropdown menus, respectively.
9. If the vendor's price is in a different currency, enter the other currency and the exchange rate in the "Other Currency" and "Rate" fields, respectively.
10. Enter the vendor's address in the "Vendor Address" field.
11. Enter any follow-up notes about the vendor in the "Follow-Up Notes" field.
12. Select the follow-up action that was taken from the "Follow-Up Action" dropdown menu.
13. Enter the date and time of the last follow-up in the "Follow-Up Date" and "Follow-Up Time" fields, respectively.
14. Select the status of the follow-up from the "Follow-Up Status" dropdown menu.
15. Enter any follow-up notes about the action taken in the "Follow-Up Notes" field.
16. Enter the next follow-up date and time in the "Next Follow-Up Date" and "Next Follow-Up Time" fields, respectively.
17. Select the frequency of the follow-ups from the "Follow-Up Frequency" dropdown menu.
18. Select whether a reminder is sent for follow-ups from the "Follow-Up Reminder" dropdown menu.
19. Enter the date and time of the last follow-up in the "Vendor Last Followed" and "Vendor Last Followed Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor Type**: Select the type of vendor (e.g. Caterer, Florist, Photographer, etc.).
* **Vendor Website**: Enter the vendor's website (if applicable).
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Status**: Select the status of the quote (e.g. Quote Sent, Quote Received, Quote Accepted, etc.).
* **Vendor Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the vendor.
* **Vendor Price**: Enter the price of the vendor's services.
* **Price Currency**: Select the currency of the vendor's price (e.g. USD, EUR, GBP, etc.).
* **Other Currency**: If the vendor's price is in a different currency, enter the other currency.
* **Rate**: Enter the exchange rate of the other currency.
* **Vendor Address**: Enter the vendor's address.
* **Follow-Up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes about the vendor.
* **Follow-Up Action**: Select the follow-up action that was taken (e.g. Follow-Up Sent, Follow-Up Received, Follow-Up Action Taken, etc.).
* **Follow-Up Date**: Enter the date of the last follow-up.
* **Follow-Up Time**: Enter the time of the last follow-up.
* **Follow-Up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up (e.g. Completed, Not Completed, etc.).
* **Follow-Up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes about the action taken.
* **Next Follow-Up Date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up.
* **Next Follow-Up Time**: Enter the time of the next follow-up.
* **Follow-Up Frequency**: Select the frequency of the follow-ups (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
* **Follow-Up Reminder**: Select whether a reminder is sent for follow-ups (e.g. Yes, No, etc.).
* **Vendor Last Followed**: Enter the date and time of the last follow-up.
* **Vendor Last Followed Time**: Enter the time of the last follow-up.
