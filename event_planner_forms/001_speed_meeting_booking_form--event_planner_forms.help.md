# Speed Meeting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speed Meeting Booking Form is designed to collect information from users to book and manage meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting topic and any other relevant information in the "Meeting Topic" field.
2. Select a date for the meeting in the "Meeting Date" field.
3. Choose a time slot for the meeting in the "Meeting Time" field.
4. Enter the location of the meeting.
5. Provide the name and email of the person contacting you.
6. Select whether or not attendees are expected ("Yes" or "No").
7. Optionally, enter the expected duration of the meeting.
8. Enter any notes regarding the meeting.
9. Choose the booking status of the meeting ("Active" or "Inactive").
10. Optionally, select the start and end dates and times of the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Meeting Request
* **Meeting Request**: This field is for entering a brief description of the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Text*

### Meeting Date
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Date*

### Meeting Time
* **Meeting Time**: Choose a time slot for the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Time*

### Location
* **Location**: Enter the location of the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Text*

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person contacting you.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Text*

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the person contacting you.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Email*

### Meeting Topic
* **Meeting Topic**: Enter the topic of the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Text*

### Attendees
* **Attendees**: Choose whether or not attendees are expected ("Yes" or "No").
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Select Multiple*

### Meeting Duration
* **Meeting Duration**: Optionally, enter the expected duration of the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Number*

### Meeting Notes
* **Meeting Notes**: Enter any notes regarding the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Note*

### Booking Status
* **Booking Status**: Choose the status of the meeting booking ("Active" or "Inactive").
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Select One*

### Meeting Start Date
* **Meeting Start Date**: Optionally, enter the start date of the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Date*

### Meeting Start Time
* **Meeting Start Time**: Optionally, enter the start time of the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Time*

### Meeting End Date
* **Meeting End Date**: Optionally, enter the end date of the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Date*

### Meeting End Time
* **Meeting End Time**: Optionally, enter the end time of the meeting.
*   *Required: No*
*   *Type: Time*

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any confusion.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the meeting organizer.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with external parties.
