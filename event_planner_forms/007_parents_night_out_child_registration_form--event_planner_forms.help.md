# Parents Night Out Child Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for collecting information from parents to register their children for the Parents Night Out event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your child's name in the "Name of Child" field.
3. Optionally, enter your child's age in the "Age" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the date of the event in the "Date" field.
7. Enter the time of the event in the "Time" field.
8. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the name of the parent or guardian in the "Parents Name" field.
10. Optionally, enter the parent's phone number in the "Parents Phone" field.
11. Optionally, enter the parent's email address in the "Parents Email" field.
12. Enter any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
13. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
14. Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
15. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
16. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Name of Child** (`name_of_child`, text, required): Enter the name of the child.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Optional field for entering the child's age.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person completing this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person completing this form.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event. 
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event. 
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes. 
* **Parents Name** (`parents_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parents Phone** (`parents_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Parents Email** (`parents_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed. 
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the event planners for assistance.
