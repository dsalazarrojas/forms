# Art Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Festival Registration Form is designed to gather information about artists participating in the art festival. This form helps the festival organizers manage artist's participation and ensure a smooth experience for all involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review the instructions and requirements below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Registration
* **Registration Date (1)**: Date of registration. Please format the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Artist Name (2)**: Full name or business name.
* **Contact Person (3)**: Primary contact name.
* **Contact Email (4)**: Email address for correspondence.
* **Contact Phone (5)**: Contact phone number.
### Page 2: Artist Information
* **Business Address (8)**: Full business address.
* **City (9)**: City.
* **State (10)**: State or province.
* **Zip Code (11)**: Postal or zip code.
* **Art Category (12)**: Primary category of your art.
* **Art Description (13)**: Describe your artwork.
* **Years Experience (14)**: Years of professional art experience.
### Page 3: Festival Requirements
* **Booth Size (15)**: Preferred booth size.
* **Electricity Needed (17)**: Do you need electrical access? Please select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Table Needed (18)**: Do you need a table provided? Please select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Tent Needed (19)**: Do you need a tent provided? Please select 'True' or 'No - I Have My Own'.
* **Festival Dates (20)**: Which days will you attend? Please select all relevant days.
* **Vehicle Access (21)**: Will you need vehicle access for load-in? Please select 'True' or 'False'.
### Page 4: Additional Information
* **Website (6)**: Artist website or portfolio URL (optional).
* **Social Media (7)**: Social media handles or links (optional).
* **Previous Festivals (23)**: List other festivals you have participated in (optional).
* **References (24)**: Provide professional references (optional).
* **Additional Comments (27)**: Any other information (optional).
### Page 5: Payment and Terms
* **Booth Fee Payment (25)**: How will you pay the booth fee? Please select one of the options.
* **Terms Accepted (26)**: I accept the festival terms and conditions. Please select 'True' or 'False'.
