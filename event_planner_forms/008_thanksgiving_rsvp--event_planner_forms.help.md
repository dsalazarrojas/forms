# thanksgiving_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP for a Thanksgiving event. It will ask you for your name, the name of your guest, the food you plan to bring, and any message you'd like to share with the host.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information. 
   a) Guest: Enter your name.
   b) Guest Name: Enter the name of your guest (if you're bringing a guest).
   c) Food: Enter the food you plan to bring.
   d) Bringing: Enter any items you plan to bring.
   e) Message: If you want to share a message with the host, enter it here. 
   Note: This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest** (text, required): Enter your name to RSVP for the event.
* **Food Guest** (text, required): Enter the name of your guest, if you're bringing a guest.
* **Food** (text, required): Enter the type of food you plan to bring to the event.
* **Bringing** (text, required): Enter any items you plan to bring to the event.
* **Guest Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your guest.
* **Bringing Guest** (text, required): Enter the name of your guest you're bringing.
* **Message** (text, optional): If you have a message for the host, enter it here.
* **Date** (date, optional): If you'd like to specify a date for your RSVP, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
