# Virtual MUN Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual MUN Registration Form is used to gather participant information for a Virtual MUN event. This includes personal details, committee preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your participant details in the "Participant Details" page.
2. Select your school affiliations from the "School Affiliations" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your committee preferences from the "Committee Preferences" dropdown menu.
4. Add your emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" dropdown menu.
5. Enter any additional information in the "Additional Information" text field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, using the correct format.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Finally, add any notes about your participation in the "Notes" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This page collects your personal details, which includes your name and other relevant information.
* **School Affiliations**: Select all the schools you are affiliated with from this dropdown menu. This helps us understand your background and experience.
* **Committee Preferences**: Choose the committees you are interested in participating in from this dropdown menu. This helps us allocate you to a suitable committee.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select who you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency from this dropdown menu. This is for your safety and security.
* **Additional Information**: This text field is for any further information you think is relevant to your participation. Be concise and clear in your writing.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in this format: `username@example.com`.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the international format (with the country code, e.g., `+1234567890`).
* **Notes**: Write any additional comments or notes about your participation in this text field. This helps us understand your goals and interests.
