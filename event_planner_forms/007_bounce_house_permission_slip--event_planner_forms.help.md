This form is a one-time submission, and it's recommended that parents or guardians provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure their child's well-being.

## Purpose
The Bounce House Permission Slip is a form that collects essential information from parents or guardians of children attending a bounce house event. It's crucial to have this form filled out before allowing children to participate in the activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you, as the parent or guardian, are giving permission for your child to participate in the bounce house activities.
3. Provide a working email address to confirm your permission submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guardian name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): This field is for the full name of the parent or guardian submitting the form. It's essential to provide your correct name to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Child name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the child attending the bounce house event. This information is necessary for identification and medical purposes.
* **Child age** (`child_age`, number, optional): If you want to provide your child's age for our records, please fill out this field. Note that age is not required for participation.
* **Emergency contact number** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide a working phone number that we can use to contact you in case of an emergency or if we need to reach out with any urgent information.
* **Allergies or medical conditions** (`allergies_or_conditions`, text, optional): If your child has any allergies or medical conditions that we should be aware of, please note them here. This information will be kept confidential.
* **Activity consent** (`activity_consent`, select_one, required): Please indicate whether you consent to your child participating in bounce house activities. You can choose between "I consent" and "I do not consent".
* **Photo permission** (`photo_permission`, select_one, optional): If you want to allow us to use your child's photo in promotional materials, please select "True". Otherwise, choose "False".
* **Signature confirmation email** (`signature_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to confirm your permission submission. We will use this email to confirm that you've successfully submitted this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
* This form is a standard requirement for all children participating in the bounce house event.
