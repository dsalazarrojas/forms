# New Year Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a New Year's party RSVP form designed to collect information from guests about their attendance and guests they plan to bring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field. This will be the name that appears on the invitation list and party favors.
2. Provide your contact information (email or phone number) in the "contact info" field. This will allow the host to reach out to you with any important updates or information about the party.
3. Choose your attending status by selecting "True", "False", or "Maybe" from the "attending status" dropdown menu. This will indicate whether you plan to attend, will not attend, or are unsure about your attendance.
4. If you are attending, select the number of guests you plan to bring in the "guests" dropdown menu. This will help the host plan for seating and catering.
5. Choose a date and time to RSVP by in the "RSVP by" field. This will ensure that you get the latest updates and information about the party.
6. Enter a time to RSVP in the "RSVP time" field. This will help the host know when to expect you to RSVP.
7. If you have any additional message or comment for the host, you can enter it in the "message" field.
8. Finally, enter your contact email and phone number in the "contact email" and "contact phone" fields, respectively. These will be used by the host to contact you with any updates or information about the party.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **name** (text): Enter your name in this field.
- **contact info** (text): Enter your contact email or phone number in this field.
- **attending status** (select_one): Choose "True" if you plan to attend, "False" if you will not attend, or "Maybe" if you are unsure.
- **guests** (select_multiple): If you are attending, select the number of guests you plan to bring.
- **RSVP by** (date): Choose a date to RSVP by.
- **RSVP time** (time): Choose a time to RSVP.
- **message** (note): Enter any additional message or comment for the host.
- **contact email** (email): Enter your contact email in this field.
- **contact phone** (text): Enter your contact phone number in this field.
