# judges_contract_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect agreement and details from event judges.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, text, required/optional): Fill in the name of the event organizer.
* **Judge Details** (`judge_details`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant judge details for the event.
* **Signature** (`signature`, date, required/optional): Fill in the date of the agreement.
* **Terms And Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required/optional): Fill in any terms and conditions related to the event.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Fill in any contact information related to the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Fill in the email address of the event organizer or relevant contact.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Accept" to agree to the terms of the event. Select "Decline" to indicate disagreement.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes related to the event.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required/optional): Fill in the name of the user assigned to the event.
