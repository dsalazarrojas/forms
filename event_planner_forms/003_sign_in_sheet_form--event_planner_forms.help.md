# Sign In Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sign In Sheet Form is used to collect information from individuals who are attending or participating in an event. This form is intended to capture relevant contact and event details to help event organizers and attendees effectively manage the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "first name" field. This field is required and should be filled out with your personal first name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "last name" field. This field is required and should be filled out with your personal last name.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "email" field. This field should be filled out with your personal or professional email address. Please ensure that you enter a valid email address that you have access to.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "phone" field. This field is required and should be filled out with your personal or professional phone number.
5. Enter the **Event Date** in the "event date" field. This field is required and should be filled out with the date of the event.
6. Enter the **Event Time** in the "time" field. This field is required and should be filled out with the time of the event.
7. Add any additional **Notes** in the "notes" field. This field is optional and should be filled out with any additional information or comments relevant to the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (first_name)**: The first name field is the first name of the person filling out the form. This field is required to ensure that event organizers have a way to address individuals during the event.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: The last name field is the last name of the person filling out the form. This field is required to ensure that event organizers have a way to address individuals during the event.
* **Email (email)**: The email field is for the person to enter their personal or professional email address. This field is required to ensure that event organizers can contact individuals for event updates and information.
* **Phone (phone)**: The phone field is for the person to enter their personal or professional phone number. This field is required and should be filled out with the correct phone number for easy contact.
* **Event Date (event_date)**: The event date field is for the date of the event. This field is required and should be filled out with the correct date of the event.
* **Event Time (time)**: The event time field is for the time of the event. This field is required and should be filled out with the correct time of the event.
* **Notes (notes)**: The notes field is for any additional comments or information that the person wants to share with event organizers. This field is optional and can be filled out with any relevant details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact event organizers for clarification.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
