# Request for Pricing - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather pricing information from potential customers. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the necessary details about your company and pricing requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Price Requester" and "Email" fields respectively.
3. If you have a preferred phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe the goods or services you are interested in pricing in the "Goods/Serivces" field.
5. Enter the minimum and maximum price you are willing to pay in the "Min Price" and "Max Price" fields respectively.
6. Specify the type of pricing range you are looking for in the "Price Range" field.
7. Choose the type of goods or service you are inquiring about from the list of options in the "Goods/Services Type" field.
8. Select the type of pricing range you are interested in from the options in the "Price Range Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`prophets_name`, text, optional): Please enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
* **Price Requester** (`price_requester`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person responsible for this price request.
* **Email** (`price_requester_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you to discuss pricing.
* **Phone** (`price_requester_phone`, text, optional): If you have a preferred phone number, enter it here.
* **Goods/Serivces** (`goods_services`, text, optional): Describe the goods or services you are interested in pricing.
* **Min Price** (`min_price`, number, optional): Enter the minimum price you are willing to pay for the goods or services.
* **Max Price** (`max_price`, number, optional): Enter the maximum price you are willing to pay for the goods or services.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, text, optional): Specify the type of pricing range you are looking for (e.g. negotiable, tiered, fixed).
* **Goods/Services Type** (`goods_services_type`, text, optional): Choose the type of goods or services you are inquiring about from the list of options.
* **Price Range Type** (`price_range_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of pricing range you are interested in from the list of options.
* **Currency** (`currency`, text, optional): Specify the currency of the pricing range you are interested in (e.g. USD, EUR, CNY).
