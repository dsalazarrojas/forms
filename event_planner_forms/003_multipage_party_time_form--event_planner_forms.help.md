# Multipage Party Time Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multipage Party Time Form is designed to collect detailed information about party events, including party details, host information, and other relevant specifics. This form helps party hosts and planners gather necessary information to make their events successful and memorable.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Multipage Party Time Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the type of event (e.g., Birthday, Wedding, Graduation) on page 2.
2. Enter the party details, such as date, time, and location, on page 1.
3. Provide contact information for the host, including phone number and email on page 4.
4. Add any additional party messages or notes on page 6.
5. Fill out the party attendee list on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Details** (`party_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the party event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event (e.g., Birthday, Wedding, Graduation).
* **Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the party.
* **Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the party.
* **Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the party.
* **Party Guests** (`party_guests`, number, required): Enter the number of guests attending the party.
* **Host Details** (`host_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the host.
* **Host** (`host`, text, required): Enter the host's name.
* **Host Phone** (`host_phone`, text, required): Enter the host's phone number.
* **Host Email** (`host_email`, email, required): Enter the host's email address.
* **Party Message** (`party_message`, text, optional): Enter a party message or theme.
* **Host Message** (`host_message`, text, optional): Enter a message from the host to the guests.
* **Party Start Date** (`party_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the party.
* **Party Start Time** (`party_start_time`, time, required): Choose the start time of the party.
* **Party End Date** (`party_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the party.
* **Party End Time** (`party_end_time`, time, required): Choose the end time of the party.
* **Party Description** (`party_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the party event.
* **Party Organizer** (`party_organizer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the party organizer.
* **Party Details** (`party_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional party details.
* **Party Location** (`party_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the party event.
* **Party Notes** (`party_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional party notes or instructions.
* **Party Guest List** (`party_guest_list`, text, optional): Enter a list of party guests.
* **Party Guest List 2** (`party_guest_list_2`, text, optional): Enter another list of party guests.
* **Party Guest List 3** (`party_guest_list_3`, text, optional): Enter yet another list of party guests.
* **Party Attendee Details** (`party_attendee_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the party attendees.

## Tips
* Please ensure to fill out each field accurately and completely to ensure the party details are correct.
* If you have any questions or need help with filling out the form, feel free to reach out to the party organizer or host.
