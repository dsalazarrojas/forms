# Chili Cook Off Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chili Cook Off Registration Form is a comprehensive online form designed to collect information from participants or teams who wish to enter the chili competition. The form helps the event organizers to gather essential details about the participant or team, their chili entry, and their preferences for the event.

## How to Complete this Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Fill in your personal details**: Answer all the questions related to your participant or team's information, such as participant name, contact email and phone number, and address.
2. **Choose your competition category**: Select the correct category for your chili entry from the provided options.
3. **List your team members**: If you're competing as a team, provide a list of team members.
4. **Provide chili details**: Share information about your chili, including its name, a brief description, and its spice level.
5. **Specify equipment and time requirements**: Indicate the equipment and setup time needed for your chili entry.
6. **Confirm event logistics**: Answer the questions related to event logistics, such as attending the event in person, number of guests attending, and dietary restrictions.
7. **Payment and registration details**: Confirm your payment method for the registration fee.
8. **Agree to event rules**: Read and agree to follow all event rules and guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant or Team Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, `true`): Fill in your name or team name as it should be displayed on the event materials.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, `true`): Enter your email address for communication and event updates.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, `true`): Provide your phone number in case the event organizers need to contact you.
* **Mailing Address** (`address`, `text`, `true`): Enter your mailing address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Competition Category** (`competition_category`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the category for your chili entry from the provided options.
* **Number of Team Members** (`team_size`, `number`, `true`): Indicate the number of people on your team.
* **Team Member Names** (`team_members`, `text`, `false`): List the names of all team members.
* **Name of Your Chili Recipe** (`chili_name`, `text`, `false`): Give your chili entry a creative name.
* **Brief Description of Your Chili** (`recipe_description`, `text`, `false`): Share a brief description of your chili entry.
* **Spice Level** (`spicy_level`, `select_one`, `true`): Indicate the spice level of your chili from the options.
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Select all the equipment you require for your chili entry, such as propane burners, electric burners, and water access.
* **Setup Time Needed** (`setup_time_needed`, `select_one`, `false`): Indicate the amount of time you require to set up your chili entry.
* **Event Logistics** (`event_logistics`, `note`, `false`): Provide any additional information about your event preferences.
* **Will you Attend the Event in Person?** (`attending_event`, `select_one`, `true`): Confirm if you will attend the event in person.
* **Number of Guests Attending** (`guests_attending`, `number`, `false`): Indicate the number of guests accompanying you or your team.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, `false`): Select your T-shirt size if you want to receive an event T-shirt.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, `false`): Share any dietary restrictions or allergies you or your team members have.
* **Payment and Registration** (`payment_registration`, `note`, `false`): Review the payment method for your registration fee.
* **Registration Fee Payment** (`registration_fee`, `select_one`, `true`): Confirm your payment method for the registration fee.
* **I Agree to Follow Event Rules and Guidelines** (`agree_rules`, `select_one`, `true`): Read and agree to follow all event rules and guidelines.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `false`): If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to share them here.
