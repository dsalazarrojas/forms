# Presenter Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Presenter Feedback Survey is a tool used by event planners or organizers to collect feedback from presenters on their performance during events. This form helps to evaluate and improve the presenter's experience, ensuring that they receive the necessary guidance to enhance their skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly, providing honest feedback about the presenter's performance.
2. Use the rating scale for questions that require numerical ratings (e.g., rating the presenter's performance).
3. Use the select_multiple option for questions that have multiple answers (e.g., "Would you recommend this presenter?").
4. Enter your contact information (name, email, and phone numbers) so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page`, text, required): Please describe your overall experience with the presenter. Provide any constructive feedback on what they did well and what they could improve on.
* **What did you think of the presenter?** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_2`, text, required): Share your thoughts on the presenter's performance. Be specific about what they did well and what they could improve on.
* **Rate the presenter's performance** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_3`, select_one, required): Use the rating scale to evaluate the presenter's performance. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Would you recommend this presenter?** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_4`, select_multiple, required): Let us know if you would recommend this presenter for future events. Choose all that apply.
* **Date** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_5`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Time** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_6`, time, required): Enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Presenter's Name** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_7`, text, required): Enter the name of the presenter.
* **Additional Comments** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_8`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about the presenter.
* **Email** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_9`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_10`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Phone (International)** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_11`, text, optional): Enter your international phone number for additional contact information.
* **Notes** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_12`, note, optional): Write any additional notes about the event.
* **Date (International)** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_13`, date, optional): Enter the international date of the event.
* **Time (International)** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_14`, time, optional): Enter the international time of the event.
* **Additional Comments (International)** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_15`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about the event.
* **International Phone** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_16`, text, optional): Enter your international phone number for additional contact information.
* **International Phone (International)** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_17`, text, optional): Enter your international phone number for additional contact information.
* **Country** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_18`, text, optional): Enter the country where you attended the event.
* **Language** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_19`, text, optional): Enter the language spoken during the event.
* **Currency** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_20`, text, optional): Enter the currency used during the event.
* **Rating** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_21`, number, required): Rate the presenter's performance on a numerical scale.
* **Additional Comments** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_22`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about the presenter.
* **Country** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_23`, text, optional): Enter the country where the event was held.
* **Comments** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_24`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about the event.
* **Submit** (`presenter_feedback_survey_page_25`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm that you have completed this form by selecting "Yes".
