# Video And Photo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting video and photo content related to events, conferences, and meetups in a structured way, allowing for centralized information gathering and connectivity among content creators, attendees, and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required fields filled out accurately.
2. Provide a brief description of your video or photo content.
3. Include relevant details such as date and time of the event.
4. Optionally, provide any additional details about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video File** (`video_file`): Upload your video file to complete this field. Make sure it's in the correct format for the required upload type.
* **Photo File** (`photo_file`): Upload your photo file to complete this field. Ensure it's in the correct format for the required upload type.
* **Description** (`description`): Briefly describe the content of your video or photo submission.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the event or conference where the video or photo was recorded or taken.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the event or conference where the video or photo was recorded or taken.
* **Video Link** (`video_link`): Provide a link to your video if it's hosted somewhere else online.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`): Add any additional information about the event or conference that's relevant to your submission.
* **Other Details 2** (`other_details_2`): Provide any extra information about your submission, if necessary.
