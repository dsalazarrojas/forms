# Secret Santa Wish List Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from Secret Santa participants about their preferences and interests. This information will be used to assign a gift that fits their preferences. Please fill in the required fields carefully to ensure you receive a gift you'll enjoy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with the necessary information.
2. Select your preference for Gift Preference and Favorite Food.
3. Enter your text responses for Favorite Hobby, Favorite Place, Favorite Time, and Favorite Note.
4. If you have a preferred tool or tool ID, please enter that in the Assigned Tool and Assigned Tool ID fields, respectively.
5. Double-check that you've filled in all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (1)**: Enter your name as you would like to be known on the gift-giving list.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Gift Preference (4)**: Select "Yes" if you'd like a gift related to your preference below, "No" otherwise.
* **Favorite Food (5)**: Select your favorite food from the options provided.
* **Favorite Hobby (6)**: Enter your favorite hobby or interest.
* **Favorite Place (7)**: Enter your favorite place or location.
* **Favorite Time (8)**: Enter your favorite time of day or season.
* **Favorite Date (9)**: Enter your favorite date or birth month, if applicable.
* **Favorite Note (10)**: Enter any additional notes about yourself.
* **Assigned Tool (11)**: Select "Yes" if you'd like to be assigned a tool for the gift-giving process, "No" otherwise.
* **Assigned Tool ID (12)**: Enter the ID of the tool you'd like to be assigned, if applicable.
* **Form ID (13)**: Enter your internal form ID, if necessary.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Favorite Hobby (6)**: Enter your favorite hobby or interest.
* **Favorite Place (7)**: Enter your favorite place or location.
* **Favorite Time (8)**: Enter your favorite time of day or season.

Note: This form will be used internally and only to assign gifts that fit your preferences. Please answer honestly and accurately to get the best gift possible!
