# Travel And Housing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel And Housing Form is a form used to collect information about summer camps, including travel details, dates, location, and housing requirements. This form is essential for event planners to gather necessary information from campers and their families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields, such as `camp_location` and `housing_type`.
2. Provide the camper's travel details, including `duration` and `camp_dates`.
3. Choose the housing type that suits the camper's needs from the `housing_type` field.
4. Fill in any additional details about the housing in the `housing_details` field.
5. Provide any other housing details in the `other_housing_details` field.
6. Finally, fill in any emergency contact information in the `emergency_contact` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Summer Camp Travel Details** (`summer_camp_travel_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional details about the summer camp.
* **Duration** (`camp_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the summer camp.
* **Dates** (`camp_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates of the summer camp.
* **Camp Location** (`camp_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the summer camp location from the list of options.
* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`, text, optional): Enter the camper's name.
* **Camper Age** (`camper_age`, number, optional): Enter the camper's age.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Camper Phone** (`camper_phone`, text, optional): Enter the camper's phone number.
* **Camper Email** (`camper_email`, email, optional): Enter the camper's email address.
* **Housing Type** (`housing_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the housing type that suits the camper's needs.
* **Housing Details** (`housing_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the housing.
* **Other Housing Details** (`other_housing_details`, text, optional): Provide any other housing details not mentioned earlier.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Choose the correct housing type and location for the camper's needs.
* Provide clear and concise information about the camper's travel and housing details.
