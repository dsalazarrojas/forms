# Christmas Party Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Party Attendance Form is designed to collect information about your preferences and requirements for the company Christmas party. This will help us plan an enjoyable and inclusive event for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter the relevant information for each section. Please make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your company email address, which will be used for event updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): This is your phone number, which will be used for urgent notifications.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the list.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Choose whether you will attend the party or not.
* **Bringing a Guest** (`plus_one_request`, select_one, required): Choose whether you will bring a guest or not.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, not required): Enter the full name of your guest, if applicable.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_multiple, required): Select all the dietary restrictions you have.
* **Dietary Notes** (`dietary_notes`, text, not required): If you have specific dietary needs, please specify them here.
* **Alcohol Preference** (`alcohol_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preference for alcohol consumption.
* **Meal Selection** (`meal_selection`, select_one, required): Choose your main course preference.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will get to the party.
* **Need Ride Assistance** (`need_ride`, select_one, required): Choose whether you need help with transportation.
* **Activity Interest** (`activity_interest`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the activities that interest you.
* **Secret Santa Participation** (`secret_santa_participation`, select_one, required): Choose whether you will participate in the Secret Santa gift exchange.
* **Secret Santa Budget** (`secret_santa_budget`, select_one, not required): If you participate in Secret Santa, choose your budget for the gift.
* **Song Requests** (`song_requests`, text, not required): If you have a favorite song, please let us know.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, not required): If you have any special requests or accommodations, please let us know.
* **Years Attended** (`years_attended`, select_one, not required): How many years have you attended the party?
* **RSVP Deadline** (`rsvp_deadline_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the RSVP deadline.
