# create_a_new_event - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "create_a_new_event" form is designed to collect information for a new event, allowing you to plan and manage it efficiently. It's intended for creating a single event at a time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title for your event in the "Event Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your event in the "Event Description" field.
3. Choose the "Start Date" and "Start Time" for your event.
4. Select the location of your event.
5. Choose the type of event (Online, In-person, or Hybrid).
6. Specify the status of your event (Open, Closed, or Pending).
7. Add any relevant tags for your event.
8. Enter the email address (if applicable).
9. Enter the phone number (if applicable).
10. Provide any additional notes about your event in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a clear and concise title for your event that reflects its purpose and essence.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Write a brief description of your event, including essential details about what attendees can expect.
* **Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date on which your event will start.
* **Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, optional): Choose the time when your event will begin.
* **Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Provide the physical location of your event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event: Online, In-person, or Hybrid.
* **Status** (`event_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of your event: Open, Closed, or Pending.
* **Tags** (`event_tags`, text, optional): Add any relevant tags for your event to help with categorization and searching.
* **Email** (`event_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address for communication (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`event_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for communication (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`event_notes`, note, optional): Include any additional notes or details about your event.
