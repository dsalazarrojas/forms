# Youth Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register youth campers for various programs offered by the organization. Please complete this form for each camper to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one or more programs for the camper to attend from the options provided.
2. Enter the camper's name.
3. Choose the camper's grade level.
4. (Optional) Enter any additional contact information for the camper.
5. Enter the camper's email address.
6. Enter the camper's phone number.
7. Enter the emergency contact's name.
8. Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
9. (Optional) Add any additional information for the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Selection**: Choose one or more programs for the camper to attend. Select all relevant options that match the camper's interests and availability.
* **Camper Name**: Enter the name of the camper in the format of First Name Last Name (e.g. John Doe).
* **Camper Grade**: Choose the grade level of the camper from the provided options.
* **Camper Contact Info**: If you have any additional contact information for the camper, please enter it here (e.g. address, any special needs).
* **Camper Email**: Enter the camper's email address in the format of name@domain.com (e.g. johndoe@example.com).
* **Camper Phone**: Enter the camper's phone number in the format of (XXX) XXX-XXXX (e.g. (123) 456-7890).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the format of (XXX) XXX-XXXX (e.g. (123) 456-7890).
* **Emergency Contact Info**: If you have any additional information for the emergency contact, please enter it here (e.g. relationship, address, etc.).
