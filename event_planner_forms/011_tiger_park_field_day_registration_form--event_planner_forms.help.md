# Tiger Park Field Day Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and event details for the Tiger Park Field Day event. It helps us understand who is attending and what they are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your participant information on the "Participant Information" page.
2. Provide event details, if desired, on the "Event Details" page.
3. Add any additional participant details on the "Participant Details" page.
4. Select your registration status: "Registration Open" or "Registration Closed" on the "Registration" page.
5. Enter any payment information on the "Payment" page.
6. Add event notes, if needed, on the "Event Notes" page.
7. Choose your "Tiger Man" preference: "Mr. Tiger" or "Mrs. Tiger" on the "Tiger Man" page.
8. Enter any additional notes on the "Notes" page.
9. Finally, submit your completed form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (participant_information, text, required): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details to help us get in touch with you.
* **Event Details** (event_details, text, optional): Share any specific event details or requirements you have, like equipment or special requests.
* **Participant Details** (participant_details, text, optional): Add any additional details about yourself, such as your role or interests.
* **Registration** (registration, select_one, optional): Choose whether the registration is "Registration Open" or "Registration Closed" to indicate your availability.
* **Payment** (payment, text, optional): Enter any payment information, like your method or amount.
* **Event Notes** (event_notes, text, optional): Add any notes or comments about the event you'd like to share.
* **Tiger Man** (tigerman, select_one, optional): Choose your title for the event: "Mr. Tiger" or "Mrs. Tiger".
* **Notes** (notes, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments you'd like to include.
* **Submit** (submit, text, optional): This button completes your registration; review your form before submitting.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with a *.
- Be clear and concise in your responses.
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
