# valentine_ball_online_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for the Valentine's Day event. Please fill out the required information to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email, and Phone.
2. Provide event details, such as Event Type (e.g., Valentine's Day, Dinner, Dance), Event Date, and Event Time.
3. If applicable, provide additional event details, such as Venue and Address.
4. Select your payment method (e.g., Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card) and provide the payment date and time.
5. If you are attending with someone, please provide their details in the Attending With field.
6. Fill out any additional notes or comments in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as per your identification.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as per your identification.
* **First Name & Surname**: Enter your full name in the format "First Name Surname".
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "DD/MM/YYYY" or "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 01/02/1990).
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Attending with**: If you are attending with someone, enter their name and relationship (e.g., spouse, friend, etc.).
* **Event details**: Provide any additional information about your event, such as a message or special request.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are attending (e.g., Valentine's Day, Dinner, Dance).
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event in the format "DD/MM/YYYY".
* **Event time**: Enter the time of your event in the format "HH:MM".
* **Venue**: If your event has a specific venue, please enter it.
* **Address**: If your event has a specific address, please enter it.
* **Event Details 2**: Provide any additional information about your event (e.g., special requests, etc.).
* **Payment method**: Select your payment method (e.g., Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card).
* **Payment date**: Enter the date you made the payment.
* **Payment time**: Enter the time you made the payment.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you paid.
* **First Name Surname 2**: Enter the full name of the person you are paying for, in the format "First Name Surname".
* **Note**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Email 2**: Enter the email of the person you are paying for.
* **Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of the person you are paying for.
* **Attending With 2**: If you are paying for someone else, enter their name and relationship.
* **Event Details 3**: Provide any additional information about your event (e.g., special requests, etc.).
* **Event Details 4**: Provide any additional information about your event (e.g., special requests, etc.).
