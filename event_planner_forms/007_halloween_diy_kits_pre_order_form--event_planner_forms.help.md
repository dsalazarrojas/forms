# halloween_diy_kits_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to pre-order DIY Halloween kits for an event or festival. Customers can use this form to provide their contact information, shipping details, and payment method for pre-ordering a Halloween kit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your street address in the "Street" field.
6. Enter your apartment or suite number, if applicable, in the "Address" field.
7. Enter the city where you reside in the "City" field.
8. Enter the state where you reside in the "State" field.
9. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
10. Select the items you want to pre-order from the "Items" field.
11. Choose your preferred shipping option from the "Shipping" field.
12. Optionally, provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
	+ Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required):
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Street** (`address_1`, text, required):
	+ Enter your street address.
* **Address** (`address_2`, text, required):
	+ Enter your apartment or suite number, if applicable.
* **City** (`city`, text, required):
	+ Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required):
	+ Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required):
	+ Enter your zip code.
* **Items** (`items`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the items you want to pre-order.
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose your preferred shipping option.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments about your pre-order.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter the card number associated with your chosen payment method.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the expiration date of your card.
* **Expiration Month** (`expiration_month`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the expiration month of your card.
* **Expiration Year** (`expiration_year`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the expiration year of your card.
* **CVV** (`cvv`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the Card Verification Value (CVV) of your card.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional):
	+ Optionally, enter the date you want to pre-order by.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional):
	+ Optionally, enter the time you want to pre-order by.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose whether you have a payment method (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Order Notes 2** (`order_notes_2`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **CVV 2** (`cvv_2`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional verification value.
