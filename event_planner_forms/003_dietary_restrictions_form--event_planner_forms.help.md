# Dietary Restrictions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about your dietary restrictions and preferences. This will help us provide you with a safe and enjoyable dining experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your main page (if not already selected).
2. List any food allergies you have.
3. Check the dietary restrictions that apply to you.
4. Provide any meal preferences you have.
5. Leave any comments or additional information about your dietary needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page**: This is the first page of the form. Select your main page to begin.
* **Allergies**: List any food allergies you have, such as Allergies to dairy, Allergies to shellfish, or Allergies to peanuts.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Check the box next to any dietary restrictions that apply to you, such as Gluten-free, Vegan, or Vegetarian.
* **Meal Preferences**: This is a text box where you can describe your meal preferences.
* **Comments**: This is a text box where you can leave any additional comments or information about your dietary needs.

## Tips

* If you have any food allergies, please make sure to list them all.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please only check the boxes that apply to you.
* If you have any meal preferences, please be as specific as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please use this field to provide details.
