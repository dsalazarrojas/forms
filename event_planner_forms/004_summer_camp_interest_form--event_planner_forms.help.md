# Summer Camp Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or groups interested in attending a summer camp event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more options from the "summer camp interest form" section to express your interest in our summer camp event.
2. You can provide information in the "event planner forms" section as needed.
3. If you'd like to use the form for free, check the "get this form for free" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **summer camp interest form**: Select all the options that apply to your interest in our summer camp event. This includes:
	+ Interest in our summer camp event
	+ Number of people who would register for our summer camp activity
* **event planner forms**: This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information related to our summer camp event.
* **get this form for free**: This field is a note field and is not required. If you select this option, it's likely for internal use to understand how you'd like to utilize the form without any costs associated with it.

Please ensure you're using this form for its intended purpose and contact form@gic.mx if you have any questions or concerns.
