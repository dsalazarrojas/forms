# Virtual Prayer Room Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending or leading meetings in a virtual prayer room.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: Last Name, Email, Meeting Topic (if applicable), and Prayer Group.
2. For the optional fields, provide any additional information you would like to share with the organizers.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your registration information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, optional): This is your first name. It will be used to greet you personally in the prayer room.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification or documentation.
* **Email** (email, required): This is your email address where you will receive updates and notifications about the virtual prayer room meetings.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): You can add your phone number if you would like to receive phone reminders or updates.
* **Meeting Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the meeting you are interested in attending.
* **Meeting Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the meeting you are interested in attending.
* **Prayer Group** (select_one, required): Please select your preferred prayer group from the options provided.
* **Meeting Topic** (text, optional): If you have a specific topic in mind for your meeting, you can enter it here.
* **Additional Information** (text, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about yourself or your needs.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the organizers.

Please note that this form is subject to change, and new fields or questions may be added in the future.
