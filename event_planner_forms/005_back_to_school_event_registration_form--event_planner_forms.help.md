# Back To School Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for parents and guardians to register their children for a Back To School event. The form is used to collect information that helps event organizers plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the "Student Information" section, which includes fields for student's first name, last name, grade level, student ID (if applicable), and parent/guardian name.
2. Then, provide the parent/guardian's email address and phone number.
3. Next, select the relationship between the parent/guardian and the student (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian, or Other).
4. Proceed to the "Event Participation" section, where you will be asked if the student is attending the Back to School Kickoff event and activity fair.
5. Choose if you are interested in volunteering and select the areas of interest for volunteering.
6. Finally, confirm if the provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student First Name**: Enter the student's first name.
* **Student Last Name**: Enter the student's last name.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level.
* **Student ID (if applicable)**: If the student has an ID, enter it here.
* **Parent/Guardian Name**: Enter the parent/guardian's name.
* **Email Address**: Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Relationship to Student**: Select the relationship between the parent/guardian and the student (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian, or Other).
* **Attending Back to School Kickoff Event?**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student is attending the Back to School Kickoff event.
* **Attending Activity Fair?**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student is attending the activity fair.
* **Interested in Volunteering?**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the parent/guardian is interested in volunteering.
* **Areas of Interest for Volunteering**: Select all the areas of interest for volunteering that apply.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies the student has.
* **Number of Additional Family Members Attending**: Enter the number of additional family members attending the event.
* **May We Use Student Photos at School Events?**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student photos can be used at school events.
* **Special Accommodations or Requests**: Enter any special accommodations or requests the student needs.
* **I Confirm That the Above Information is Accurate**: Confirm if the provided information is accurate.
