# Girl Scout Event Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Event Evaluation Form is designed to help event organizers evaluate and document Girl Scout events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the events you are evaluating and ensure you are familiar with the details of each event.
2. For each event, enter the title, date, location, number of scouts and leaders attended, risk assessment score, and safety measures taken.
3. Enter any comments or notes about each event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_1_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event you are evaluating.
* **Event Held On** (`event_1_held_on`, date, required): Enter the date the event was held.
* **Event Location** (`event_1_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Number of Scouts Attended** (`event_1_scouts_attended`, number, required): Enter the number of Girl Scouts who attended the event.
* **Number of Scout Leaders Attended** (`event_1_scout_leaders_attended`, number, required): Enter the number of Scout leaders who attended the event.
* **Risk Assessment Score** (`event_1_risk_assessment_score`, number, required): Enter a score indicating the risk level of the event.
* **Safety Measures Taken** (`event_1_safety_measures_taken`, text, required): Enter any safety measures taken during the event.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`event_1_emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Enter any emergency contact information for the event.
* **Number of Scouts Participated** (`event_1_scouts_participated`, number, required): Enter the number of Girl Scouts who participated in the event.
* **Number of Scout Leaders Participated** (`event_1_scout_leaders_participated`, number, required): Enter the number of Scout leaders who participated in the event.
* **Event Comments** (`event_1_event_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the event.
