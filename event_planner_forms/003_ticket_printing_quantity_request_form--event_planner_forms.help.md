# Ticket Printing Quantity Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a customized ticket printing quantity for specific events or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the ticket type (A, B, or C) that matches the event or activity.
2. Choose whether or not to print the tickets (yes, no, or other).
3. Enter the quantity of tickets requested.
4. Enter the total number of tickets printed.
5. Enter the quantity printed.
6. Enter the quantity requested.
7. If the ticket type is other, enter a custom value.
8. Select the print method (e.g., Print or Email).
9. Enter an email address (if printing by email).
10. Enter any additional notes.
11. Select who submitted the form (e.g., John, Jane, or Joe).
12. Enter the form title and version.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`): Select the ticket type that matches the event or activity.
* **Ticket Type Other** (`ticket_type_other`): If the ticket type is other, enter a custom value.
* **Print Run** (`print_run`): Choose whether or not to print the tickets (yes, no, or other).
* **Print Run 2** (`print_run_2`): Choose whether or not to print the tickets (yes, no, or other).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the total number of tickets requested.
* **Total Printed** (`total_printed`): Enter the total number of tickets printed.
* **Quantity Printed** (`quantity_printed`): Enter the quantity of tickets printed.
* **Quantity Requested** (`quantity_requested`): Enter the quantity requested.
* **Print Method** (`print_method`): Select the print method (e.g., Print or Email).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter an email address (if printing by email).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter a phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`): Select who submitted the form (e.g., John, Jane, or Joe).
* **Form Title** (`form_title`): Enter a custom form title.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`): Enter a custom form version.
* **Print Method Other** (`print_method_other`): If printing method is other, enter a custom value.
* **Print Method 2** (`print_method_2`): Select the print method (e.g., Print or Email).
* **Submitted by 2** (`submitted_by_2`): Select who submitted the form (e.g., John, Jane, or Joe).

Please note that this form is for specific events or activities that require customized ticket printing. General ticket printing requests should be submitted through the standard ticket printing form.
