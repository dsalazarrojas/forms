# Thanksgiving Softball Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from registrants for the Thanksgiving Softball Camp. It gathers personal details, team preferences, and payment methods for camp registration. Completing this form helps the organizers manage registrations efficiently and ensures a smooth camp experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details such as your first and last name.
2. Provide your email address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
3. Choose your team size and preferences (yes/no).
4. Select your T-shirt size and color.
5. Choose your payment method (cash/check/credit).
6. Confirm your registration.
7. Provide any additional information or comments, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date of registration (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time of registration (format: 12-Hour Clock, e.g., 10:30 AM).
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, select one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' to indicate you have a team, 'No' otherwise.
* **Team Size Options** (`team_size_options`, text, required/optional): If you chose 'Yes', provide the team size.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose your T-shirt size (S, M, L, XL).
* **T-Shirt Color** (`t_shirt_color`, select one, required/optional): Choose your T-shirt color (Red, Blue, Green).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional details or comments.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, required/optional): Choose your payment method (Cash, Check, Credit).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, select multiple, required/optional): If you chose 'Yes', indicate you have made the payment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select one, required/optional): Confirm your registration ('Yes', 'No').
* **Confirm Options** (`confirm_options`, text, required/optional): If you chose 'Yes', confirm you are sure.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date of the event (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time of the event (format: 12-Hour Clock, e.g., 10:30 AM).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Staff** (`staff`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the staff person.
* **Staff Position** (`staff_position`, text, required/optional): Enter the position of the staff person.
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the staff person.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the staff person.
* **Has it been submitted?** (`submitted`, select one, required/optional): Confirm if the form has been submitted ('Yes', 'No').
* **Has it been submitted? Options** (`submitted_options`, text, required/optional): If you chose 'Yes', confirm it has been submitted.
