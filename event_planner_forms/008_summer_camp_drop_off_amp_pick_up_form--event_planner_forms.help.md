# Summer Camp Drop Off & Pick Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect summer camp registration information from parents/guardians and provide details about drop off and pick up times for their children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Please fill out all required fields marked as required.
2. Enter accurate and relevant information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Summer Camp Details
* **Summer Camp Details** (`summer_camp_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description or note about your child's summer camp experience, such as the program they are enrolled in or any specific needs they have.

### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name and your child's name.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your child's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the summer camp in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).

### Drop Off Time
* **Drop Off Time** (`drop_off_time`, time, required): Enter the time your child will be dropped off at the summer camp.

### Drop Off Details
* **Drop Off Details** (`drop_off_details`, text, required): Enter any specific details about your child's drop off, such as who will be dropping them off.

### Pick Up Time
* **Pick Up Time** (`pick_up_time`, time, required): Enter the time your child will be picked up from the summer camp.

### Pick Up Details
* **Pick Up Details** (`pick_up_details`, text, required): Enter any specific details about your child's pick up, such as who will be picking them up.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your child's summer camp experience, such as allergies or special needs.

### Additional Children
* **Additional Children** (`additional_children`, text, optional): Enter the names of any additional children you are registering for the summer camp.

## Tips
* Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and relevant information for your child's camp experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the camp organizers.
