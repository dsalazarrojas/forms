# Ugly Christmas Sweater Contest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ugly Christmas Sweater Contest Form is used to collect user input for a festive holiday contest. It allows users to participate in the contest by selecting categories and voting for their favorite participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a category from the options provided under "Select Category".
2. Choose your favorite participant for each category from the "Select Best Dresser" options.
3. Optionally, you can enter your vote for each category. Note that this step is not required and is optional.
4. Click "Submit" to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Category** (1, select_one):
    Select a category from the provided options to choose what you think is the most deserving of the Ugly Christmas Sweater Contest.
* **Select Best Dresser** (2, select_one):
    Choose the participant that you think deserves to be considered as the best dresser in the selected category.
* **Your Vote** (3, number):
    If you wish to vote for your favorite participant in this category, use this field to enter your vote.
* **Vote Count** (4, number):
    This field is not currently used, it is likely a placeholder for a future implementation.
* **Vote Count** (5, number):
    This field is not currently used, it is likely a placeholder for a future implementation.
* **Submit** (6, note):
    Click this button to submit your form and participate in the contest.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to select a category and participant before submitting your form.
- Your vote is optional and is not required for participation.
- This form is for entertainment purposes only and is part of a holiday contest.
