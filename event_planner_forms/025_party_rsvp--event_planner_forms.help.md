# Party RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party RSVP form is used by event planners to collect information from guests attending an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Guest name" field with the guest's name.
2. Enter the guest's email address in the "Guest email" field.
3. If the guest has a phone number, enter it in the "Guest phone" field.
4. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest name
 Guest name: **Guest name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Enter the guest's name as it is written on their identification.

### Guest email
 Guest email: **Guest email** (`guest_email`, `email`, required): Enter the guest's email address. This field is required to ensure that the guest can be contacted for event details and updates.

### Guest phone
 Guest phone: **Guest phone** (`guest_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the guest's phone number if they have one. This field is optional, so you don't need to fill it if you don't have the guest's phone number. 

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Use the same name and email for a single person, and use a phone number if applicable.
* If you are unsure of any of the information, please contact the event organizer for clarification.
