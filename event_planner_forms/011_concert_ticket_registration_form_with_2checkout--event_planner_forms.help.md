# Concert Ticket Registration Form With 2Checkout - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from event planners for concert ticket registrations using 2Checkout integration.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form by providing the required details:
    *   Enter your name in the **Organizer name** field.
    *   Enter your contact email in the **Contact email** field.
    *   Enter the number of tickets to process in the **Tickets requested** field.
    *   Select the ticket package type from the **Package type** dropdown menu.
    *   (Optional) Enter 2Checkout integration details in the **Payment processor** field.
    *   Enter the name that appears on the credit card bill in the **Billing name** field.
    *   Enter the email used for transaction receipts in the **Billing email** field.
    *   (Optional) Enter an internal reference for accounting purposes in the **Invoice reference** field.
    *   (Optional) Enter any venue or delivery notes in the **Special instructions** field.
    *   Read the **Confirmation note** for important instructions about payment processing time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organizer name (organizer_name)**: Name of person registering tickets (required)
*   **Contact email (contact_email)**: Email used for payment and receipt (required)
*   **Tickets requested (tickets_requested)**: Number of tickets to process (required)
*   **Package type (package_type)**: Select the ticket package (required)
*   **Payment processor (payment_processor)**: 2Checkout integration details (optional)
*   **Billing name (billing_name)**: Name that appears on credit card (required)
*   **Billing email (billing_email)**: Email for transaction receipts (required)
*   **Invoice reference (invoice_reference)**: Internal reference for accounting (optional)
*   **Special instructions (special_instructions)**: Any venue or delivery notes (optional)
*   **Confirmation note (confirmation_note)**: Read-only instructions about payment processing time (required, for informational purposes only)

## Tips

*   Review the form carefully before submitting.
*   Enter accurate and complete information for each field.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submission.
