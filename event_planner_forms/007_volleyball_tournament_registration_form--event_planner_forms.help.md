# volleyball_tournament_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for teams to register and provide their information for a volleyball tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with a (*).
* Enter your team's name and size in the corresponding fields.
* Select your team type (Team or Individual) and division (1 or 2) from the dropdown menus.
* Enter the date and start time of the event.
* Enter your team's description, experience, rating, score, and any additional details.
* Enter your payment method and status.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, not required): Enter your team's name as you want it to be displayed in the tournament.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, not required): Enter the number of players on your team.
* **Team Type** (`team_type`, select_one, not required): Select whether your team is a Team or Individual team.
* **Division** (`division`, select_one, not required): Select the division your team would like to participate in (1 or 2).
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, not required): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, not required): Enter the start time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, not required): Enter the end time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required): Enter the name and contact information of your team's representative.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your team's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your team's email address.
* **Website** (`website`, text, not required): Enter your team's website (if applicable).
* **Team Description** (`team_description`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your team.
* **Team Experience** (`team_experience`, number, not required): Enter your team's experience level (number of years playing volleyball).
* **Team Rating** (`team_rating`, number, not required): Enter your team's rating (e.g., 1-10).
* **Team Score** (`team_score`, number, not required): Enter your team's score (e.g., number of games won).
* **Tournament Fee** (`tournament_fee`, number, not required): Enter the fee your team is paying to participate in the tournament.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, not required): Enter the method of payment (e.g., check, credit card).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, text, not required): Enter the status of your payment (e.g., paid, unpaid).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at [your email/phone number].
