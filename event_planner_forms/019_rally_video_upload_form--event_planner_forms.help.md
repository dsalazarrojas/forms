# Rally Video Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rally Video Upload Form is a simple, one-page form designed to help users upload their rally videos and provide essential details about the event they are associated with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Upload your rally video to the designated field.
2.  Select the type of video you are uploading (Video, Audio, or Image).
3.  Enter the name of the event.
4.  Select the date of the event.
5.  Choose the time of the event.
6.  Enter the location of the event.
7.  Add a description of your video.
8.  Enter any additional tags associated with your video (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Rally Video** (`rally_video`, text, true): Please upload your rally video in this field. You can upload different types of media, such as videos or images.
*   **Rally Video Type** (`rally_video_type`, select_one, true): Please select the type of video you are uploading. Options are: Video, Audio, Image.
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, true): Enter the name of the event you are associated with.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, true): Select the date of the event.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, true): Choose the time of the event.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, true): Enter the location of the event.
*   **Video Description** (`video_description`, text, true): Add a description of your video.
*   **Video Tags** (`video_tags`, text, false): Enter any additional tags associated with your video.
*   **Video Comments** (`video_comments`, text, false): Enter any comments associated with your video.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have permission to share your video and event details.
*   Please make sure to upload a video that is relevant to the event you are associated with.
*   Enter accurate and relevant details about the event.
