# Zoom Webinar Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a Zoom webinar event. It gathers necessary information to ensure attendees receive correct invitations and notifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information in the provided fields.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding text fields.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which will be used to send you invitations and updates.
4. Confirm your email address by re-entering it in the "Confirm email address" field.
5. Select your role in the "Your Role" field, which will determine your access rights and participation level in the webinar.
6. Choose your time zone from the provided options in the "Time Zone" field.
7. (Optional) Enter the date and time of the webinar in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. Enter your Zoom URL (optional but recommended for easy access to the webinar).
9. Click on the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address you want to use for webinar invitations and updates.
* **Confirm email address** (`email`, email, optional): Repeat the email address to confirm it matches your input in the "Email" field.
* **Your Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select "Host/Creator" if you are the organizer, "Speaker" if you are presenting, and "Participant" if you are joining the webinar.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the webinar, if it hasn't already been specified.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the webinar, if it hasn't already been specified.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, required): Select your time zone to ensure correct scheduling and invitation times.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, number, optional): Not applicable for this guide (field seems redundant with "Time Zone" and might be a placeholder).
* **Zoom URL** (`zoom_url`, text, optional): Enter the Zoom URL for easy access to the webinar, if provided.
