# Entrepreneur City Summit Speaker Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entrepreneur City Summit Speaker Information Form is used to collect and store speaker information for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, click on the "Start" button.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.
3. Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
4. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Details**: Enter your name, company, and contact information.
	+ This field will display on the speaker's profile page.
* **Speaker Bio**: Write a brief bio about yourself, including your professional experience and achievements.
	+ This field will help attendees understand your background and expertise.
* **Presentation Topic**: Enter a title and brief description of your presentation.
	+ This field will help attendees understand what they can expect from your presentation.
* **Social Links**: Enter any social media links you'd like to share with attendees.
	+ This will help attendees connect with you before, during, and after the event.
* **Bio Summary**: Enter a short summary of your bio.
	+ This field will help attendees quickly understand your background and expertise.
* **Presentation Duration**: Enter the expected length of your presentation.
	+ This field will help the event organizers plan the schedule.
* **Presentation Time**: Enter the time and date of your presentation.
	+ This field will help attendees know when your presentation is happening.
* **Presentation Schedule**: Enter the date and time of your presentation.
	+ This field will help attendees know when your presentation is happening.
* **Presentation Type**: Select the type of presentation (Panel Discussion, Workshop, or Keynote).
	+ This field will help attendees understand the format of your presentation.
* **Presentation Format**: Select the languages in which your presentation will be given (English, Spanish, French).
	+ This field will help attendees know which languages they can expect from your presentation.
* **Headshot Image URL**: Enter the URL of your headshot image.
	+ This field will be used to display your headshot image.
* **Headshot Image Title**: Enter a title for your headshot image.
	+ This field will be displayed as a caption for your headshot image.
* **Headshot Image Alt Text**: Enter alternative text for your headshot image.
	+ This field is for accessibility purposes.
* **Headshot Image Caption**: Enter a caption for your headshot image.
	+ This field will be displayed under your headshot image.
* **Headshot Image Description**: Enter a description of your headshot image.
	+ This field will be displayed as a tooltip for your headshot image.
* **Headshot Image Credit**: Enter the credit for your headshot image.
	+ This field will be displayed under your headshot image.
* **Speaker Photo**: Enter a URL for your speaker photo.
	+ This field will be used to display your speaker photo.
* **Presentation Handouts**: Enter any presentation handouts you'd like to share.
	+ These will be available for attendees to download.
* **Presentation Attachments**: Enter any presentation attachments you'd like to share.
	+ These will be available for attendees to download.
* **Headshot Image Width**: Enter the width of your headshot image.
	+ This field is not currently used.
* **Headshot Image Height**: Enter the height of your headshot image.
	+ This field is not currently used.
* **Headshot Image Size**: Enter the size of your headshot image.
	+ This field is not currently used.

## Tips

* Make sure to use a professional tone and formatting for your presentation title and abstract.
* Use relevant keywords for your presentation topic and abstract.
* Make sure to test your presentation attachments and handouts for compatibility with the event's presentation software.
