# Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Retreat Registration Form" is a crucial document for the retreat planning process. It collects essential participant and event information, including payment details and terms of agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Participant Information" page with your details.
2. Provide the required event-related information in the "Event Details" page.
3. Select one or more payment methods in the "Payment Method" page.
4. Fill out the "Payment Details" page with the requested payment information.
5. Provide the retreat-specific details in the "Retreat Details" page.
6. If you have any additional comments, use the "Notes" page (optional).
7. Finally, read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agree to Terms" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information**: This is a text field where you can fill out your participant details.
* **Participant Information**: This field is required and should not be left blank.

### Event Details
* **Event Details**: This is a text field where you can provide additional event-related information. 

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method**: Select one or more of the available payment methods, such as bank transfer, credit card, or PayPal. 

### Payment Details
* **Payment Details**: This is a text field where you can provide further payment information. 

### Retreat Details
* **Retreat Details**: This is a text field where you can provide retreat-specific information. 

### Notes
* **Notes**: This is an optional text field where you can add any additional comments or remarks. 

### Agree to Terms
* **Agree to Terms**: This is a text field where you must read and agree to the terms and conditions of the retreat. 

Note: It is crucial to fill out all the required fields accurately to ensure a smooth retreat planning process.
