# Womens Leadership Conference Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from attendees of the Women's Leadership Conference to help conference organizers assess the overall success and effectiveness of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. For text fields, type in your answer or response.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the relevant option from the dropdown list.
4. For optional fields, you can skip them if you don't have any information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback**: This is the main section where you can provide any feedback about the conference. Please type in your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
* **Speaker**: Choose the speaker you attended the conference. If you attended multiple sessions, choose the one you found most engaging.
* **Rating**: Rate the speaker on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the speaker or the session.
* **Attended Other Women's Leadership Conferences**: If you've attended other women's leadership conferences, select the option that applies to you.
* **Will Recommend**: If you would recommend this speaker or this session to others, select the relevant option.
* **Session Length**: Choose the session length that best fits your experience.
* **Rating of Session**: Rate the session on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Rating of Speaker**: Rate the speaker on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Additional Feedback**: If you have any further feedback or suggestions, provide it in this section.
* **Attended Other Women's Leadership Events**: If you've attended other women's leadership events, select the option that applies to you.
* **Session**: Choose the type of session you attended (e.g., keynote, panel, workshop).
* **Session Topic**: Provide a brief description of the session topic.
* **Speaker Name**: If you want to provide feedback about the speaker, enter their name.
* **Speaker Organization**: If you want to provide feedback about the speaker, enter their organization.
* **Speaker Email**: If you want to provide feedback about the speaker, enter their email.
* **Speaker Phone**: If you want to provide feedback about the speaker, enter their phone number.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Title of Organization**: If you're representing an organization, enter your title.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Role**: Enter your role in the organization.
* **Attended Other Women's Leadership Events**: If you've attended other women's leadership events, select the option that applies to you.
* **Will Recommend**: If you would recommend this speaker or this session to others, select the relevant option.
* **Session Length**: Choose the session length that best fits your experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Feedback**: If you have any further feedback or suggestions, provide it in this section.
