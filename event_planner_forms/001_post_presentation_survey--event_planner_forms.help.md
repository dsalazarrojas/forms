# Post Presentation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Post Presentation Survey is a form used to collect feedback from attendees after a presentation. It is designed to gather information about the event, the speaker, and the attendee's experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Post Presentation Survey, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Organization in the Organization field.
5. Enter your Job Title in the Job Title field.
6. Enter your Department in the Department field.
7. In the Feedback field, provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the presentation.
8. Enter the name of the event you attended in the Event Name field.
9. Select the type of event you attended from the options in the Event Type field.
10. Enter the date of the event in the Event Date field.
11. Enter the time of the event in the Event Time field.
12. Enter the location of the event in the Location field.
13. If you are attending a session, enter your Session ID in the Session ID field.
14. Enter the number of the session you attended in the Session field.
15. Enter the length of the presentation in the Presentation Length field.
16. Select the speakers you listened to from the options in the Speaker field.
17. Enter your rating for the presentation in the Rating field.
18. Select your rating scale from the options in the Rating Scale field.
19. If you have any other comments or feedback about the rating, enter it in the Other Comments field.
20. Select the type of presentation you attended from the options in the Presentation Type field.
21. Select the type of survey you are taking from the options in the Survey Type field.
22. Enter the date you would like to be contacted for follow-up in the Follow-up field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address.
* **Organization** (required): Enter your organization.
* **Job Title** (required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (required): Enter your department.
* **Feedback** (required): Enter any comments or feedback about the presentation.
* **Event Name** (required): Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Type** (required): Select the type of event you attended (Event, Meeting, Training, or Conference).
* **Event Date** (required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Location** (required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Session ID** (optional): If you are attending a session, enter your session ID.
* **Session** (required): Enter the number of the session you attended.
* **Presentation Length** (required): Enter the length of the presentation.
* **Speaker** (required): Select the speakers you listened to (John, Jane, Bob, or Alice).
* **Rating** (required): Enter your rating for the presentation.
* **Rating Scale** (required): Select your rating scale (1-10, 1-20, 1-30, or 1-40).
* **Other** (required): If you have any other comments or feedback, enter it here.
* **Rating prophets** (required): Enter your rating comments.
* **Other Comments** (required): Enter any other comments about the rating.
* **Presentation Type** (required): Select the type of presentation you attended (Yes or No).
* **Survey Type** (required): Select the type of survey you are taking (Post, Pre, or Live).
* **Follow-up** (required): Enter the date you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
