# Thanksgiving Dinner RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to RSVP to a Thanksgiving dinner event. It is used to collect information about the guests and their attendance status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Guest Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your name.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **RSVP Choice**: Choose how you plan to attend the event:
	+ 'Yes' to confirm your attendance.
	+ 'No' to decline your attendance.
	+ 'Maybe' to express uncertainty.
* **Special Requests**: Share any special requests or needs you have for the event.
* **Message**: Enter a message for the host or other guests.
* **Date**: This field is not currently relevant as it is not a required field.
