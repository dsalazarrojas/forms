# League Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients for event planning photo orders. Please fill out the form below to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the appointment date of the photo order in the **Appointment Date** field. This date is the date on which the photo order will be fulfilled.
2. Provide the name of the photographer(s) involved in the photo order in the **Photographer Name** field. This can be multiple names if multiple photographers are involved.
3. Select the desired **Photography Package** from the dropdown menu. This will help us determine the level of service for your order.
4. Enter the total price of the **Package Price** if applicable, or leave it empty if not applicable.
5. Choose the type of photo you want to order by selecting one of the following options:
	* **Print**: A printed photograph will be delivered to you.
	* **Digital**: A digital copy of the photograph will be delivered to you.
	* **Both**: A combination of both print and digital copies will be delivered.
6. Enter the number of photos you want to order in the **Quantity** field.
7. Choose the **Photo Size** you want for your order by selecting one or more of the following options:
	* **Small**: A small print size.
	* **Medium**: A medium print size.
	* **Large**: A large print size.
8. Enter the date on which you want to receive the photos in the **Order Date** field.
9. Enter your email address in the **Email** field, where we will send a confirmation email with order details.
10. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field, where we can contact you with order updates.
11. Add any additional comments or instructions for the photo order in the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Appointment Date**: The date on which the photo order will be fulfilled.
* **Photographer Name**: The name(s) of the photographer(s) involved in the photo order.
* **Photography Packages**: The level of service for your photo order. Choose from Basic, Premium, or Pro.
* **Package Price**: The total price of the photo order (leave empty if not applicable).
* **Photo Type**: The type of photo you want to order. Choose from Print, Digital, Both, or leave empty if not applicable.
* **Quantity**: The number of photos you want to order.
* **Photo Size**: The print size of the photo. Choose one or more of Small, Medium, Large.
* **Order Date**: The date on which you want to receive the photos.
* **Email**: Your email address where we will send a confirmation email with order details.
* **Phone**: Your phone number where we can contact you with order updates.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or instructions for the photo order.
