# Halloween Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Event Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees to track their registrations and ensure a well-organized event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the **Event Date** you plan to attend in the "Event Date" field.
5. Choose the **Time** you plan to attend in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name)** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Event Date (Event Date)** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to attend the event.
* **Time (Time)** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time you plan to attend the event.
