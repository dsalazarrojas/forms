# Valentine DIY Kits Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Valentine DIY Kits Order Form is a simple, user-friendly form for customers to order DIY kits for Valentine's Day celebrations. The form will help you collect necessary information to process their orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of kit you'd like to order ("Yes" or "No").
2. Select the type of Valentine's DIY kit you're interested in.
3. Enter the number of kits you'd like to order.
4. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
5. Enter your shipping address for the delivery of your order.
6. If needed, type any additional message or special instructions for us.
7. Select your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit/Debit, or Pay Pal).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose Kit**: Choose "Yes" if you'd like to order a kit. If you're not sure yet, choose "No".
* **Valentine's DIY Kit**: Enter the type of Valentine's DIY kit you'd like to order (e.g., "Heart-Shaped Kit" or "Floral Kit").
* **kits_count**: Enter the number of kits you'd like to order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you back.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to reach out in case of any issues.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your address where you'd like us to ship your order.
* **Message**: If you have any special instructions or comments, type them here.
* **Payment Method**: Select one or more of the available payment methods (Cash, Credit/Debit, or Pay Pal).

## Tips
- Ensure you enter accurate information to avoid any order delays or issues.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our support team for assistance.
