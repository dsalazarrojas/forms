# Guest Pass Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Pass Request Form is a simple form used by students to request a guest pass for an event or meeting at the institution. To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in this guide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information and submit the form. You can access the form from [insert location, e.g., the institution's website].

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student requesting the guest pass.

### Student Number
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number, which can be found on their student ID card.

### Student Email
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address, which will be used to contact the student about the status of their request.

### Guest Name
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the guest who will be attending the event or meeting with the student.

### Guest Email
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter the guest's email address, which will be used to contact the guest about the status of their attendance.

### Request Date
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Choose the date you are requesting the guest pass for.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the student is aware of and complies with the rules and regulations of the institution before making a request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or have questions about the form, contact [insert contact information].
