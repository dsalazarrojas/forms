# Virtual VBS Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians for Virtual Bible Study (VBS) events. It gathers data for student registrations, parent contact details, and other relevant information. This form is likely used by an organization or institution to manage their VBS events more efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the student and their attending VBS event.
2. Fill in the student's name.
3. Enter the student's email address.
4. Indicate whether you are a contact person for the student.
5. Provide the student's name.
6. Enter the student's age.
7. Select the student's grade level.
8. Enter the student's school name and grade.
9. Select the student's school class.
10. Choose the student's subjects of interest.
11. Indicate whether the student will be attending Bible studies.
12. Choose the time for Bible studies.
13. Select the teacher and any teacher helpers.
14. Indicate the student's dietary and medical restrictions.
15. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian filling this form.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact**: Select 'Yes' if you are a contact person for the student; otherwise, select 'No'.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student attending the VBS event.
* **Student Age**: Enter the age of the student.
* **Student Grade**: Select the grade level of the student.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **School Grade**: Select the grade level of the student at their school.
* **Student Class**: Select the class the student will be attending at their school.
* **Student Subject**: Choose the subject(s) of interest for the student.
* **Student Subjects**: Select 'Yes' if the student will be attending multiple subjects; otherwise, select 'No'.
* **Bible Study**: Select 'Yes' if the student will be attending Bible studies; otherwise, select 'No'.
* **Bible Study Time**: Select the time for Bible studies.
* **Teacher**: Select the teacher in charge of the student's Bible studies.
* **Teacher's Helper**: Select the teacher's helper for the student's Bible studies.
* **Teacher's Helper 2**: Select the second teacher's helper for the student's Bible studies.
* **Student Dietary Restrictions**: Select 'Yes' if the student has any dietary restrictions; otherwise, select 'No'.
* **Student Medical Restrictions**: Enter any medical restrictions the student may have.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign this form to indicate you have provided accurate information.
* **Date**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you signed the form.
