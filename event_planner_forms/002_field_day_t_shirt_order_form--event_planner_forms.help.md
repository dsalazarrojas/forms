# Purpose: 
The Field Day T Shirt Order Form is used for customers to place orders for T shirts. This form will collect information about the customer's name, T shirt size and design preferences, quantity of shirts desired, T shirt color, contact details (email and phone number), and preferred payment method, as well as the total cost and date of the order.
 
# How To Complete This Form:
1. Fill in the form with the required details.
2. Select the T shirt size and design(s) you want.
3. Choose the quantity of shirts you want to order.
4. Select the T shirt color you prefer.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Add any additional message for the order.
8. Select the preferred payment method.
9. Review the total cost and order date.
 
# Field-by-Field Explanation
## Name
* **Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the receipt.
 
## T Shirt Size
* **T Shirt Size** (Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred size of T shirt. Options are Large, X-Large, Small, and Medium.
 
## T Shirt Design
* **T Shirt Design** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the design(s) for your T shirt. Options are Design 1, Design 2, and Design 3.
 
## Quantity
* **Quantity** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of T shirts you wish to order.
 
## T Shirt Color
* **T Shirt Color** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the color(s) for your T shirt. Options are Color 1, Color 2, and Color 3.
 
## Email
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
 
## Phone
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
 
## Message
* **Message** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional message you want to include with your order.
 
## Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional): Select your preferred payment method. Options are Payment 1, Payment 2, and Payment 3.
 
## Total
* **Total** (Number, Optional): This field displays the total cost of your order.
 
## Order Date
* **Order Date** (Date, Optional): This field displays the date your order was placed.
 
# Tips: If you select to order multiple T shirts, enter the quantity for each size/design/color combination.
