# Christmas Meal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Meal Application Form is designed to collect essential information from applicants to help us provide Christmas meals to those in need. This form serves as a guide to help you complete the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
*   Review each field carefully to ensure you have included all required information.
*   Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name of Applicant**: Enter your full name to help us identify you as a legitimate applicant.
*   **Number of People in Household**: Include all members of your household, including yourself, to help us plan for adequate meal quantities.
*   **Number of Children (under 18)**: This field is essential for planning kid-friendly meals and ensuring we have sufficient supplies.
*   **Contact Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for us to contact you in case of any issues or updates.
*   **Home Address**: Enter your home address for delivery or verification purposes.
*   **Preferred Main Course**: Select one option from the available choices to indicate your preferred main course.
*   **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Check any severe allergies or dietary restrictions you have, so we can accommodate your needs.
*   **Requested Side Dishes**: Select the side dishes you would like to have with your meal.
*   **Collection Method**: Choose between In-Person Pickup or Home Delivery to indicate your preferred collection method.
*   **Delivery Instructions (if applicable)**: If you have selected home delivery, provide any additional instructions for our drivers, such as a gate code or specific entrance.
*   **Briefly describe your current need**: This is an optional field for you to describe your current situation, which helps us prioritize limited resources.
*   **How did you hear about this program?**: This field is for you to indicate how you came across our program.
*   **I authorize sharing my info with community partners for assistance**: Check this field to consent to sharing your information with community partners for further assistance.

## Tips

*   Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
*   Please submit only one application per household.
