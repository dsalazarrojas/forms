# Netball Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Netball Registration Form collects information from netball players for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first and last name in the respective text fields.
2. Provide your contact email address.
3. If you have a date of birth, enter it in the date field. Otherwise, leave it blank.
4. Select the session(s) you are interested in participating in from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter your contact phone number.
6. Enter your emergency contact's first and last name.
7. Enter your emergency contact's email address.
8. Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
9. If you have any medical conditions, describe them in the free-text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text Field, Required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text Field, Required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email** (Email Field, Required): Enter your contact email address where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (Date Field, Optional): If you have a date of birth, enter it in the format mm/dd/yyyy. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Netball Sessions** (Select One Field, Optional): Select the session(s) you are interested in participating in from the dropdown menu.
* **Contact Phone** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's last name.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email Field, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Medical Conditions** (Free Text Field, Optional): If you have any medical conditions, describe them in this field.
