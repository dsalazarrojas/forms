# halloween_costume_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Halloween-themed quiz that asks users about their preferences and tastes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your name.
2. Answer the questions about your birthday.
3. Select your favorite fruits, colors, food, drinks, holidays, times of day, favorite season, months, favorite days of the week, music, movies, books, bands, games, and Halloween costumes.
