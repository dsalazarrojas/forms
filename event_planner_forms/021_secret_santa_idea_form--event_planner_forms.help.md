# Secret Santa Idea Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Idea Form is used to collect ideas and suggestions for a Secret Santa gift exchange within a group setting. It's a fun and interactive way for participants to share their gift ideas with each other.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first text field. This will be used to address you correctly.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second text field. This will help identify you uniquely among the participants.
3. Provide your **Email** in the third field. This will be used for communication and participant management.
4. In the **Idea** field, share your gift idea or a hint about what you'd like to receive. This can be as creative as you'd like!

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Idea** (`idea`, required): Share a brief description of a gift idea you would like to receive.

## Tips
* Be creative and have fun with your gift idea!
* Keep your email address private if you're concerned about sharing it publicly.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
