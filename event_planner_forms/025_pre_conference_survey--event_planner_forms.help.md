# Pre Conference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Conference Survey is a form designed to gather information from attendees about their experience at our conferences. This survey will help us understand their preferences, satisfaction, and feedback on our events. Your input will be crucial in helping us improve our conferences and provide better experiences for future attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

- Make sure you have an active internet connection to complete this form.
- Select your answers from the provided options or type in your response if the option does not fit your answer.
- Be honest and clear in your responses as it will help us in improving the conference.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Experience** (Conference Experience)
    * This field asks about your previous experience at our conference.
    * Answer: Yes or No, or select "Other" and provide your experience details.
* **General Feedback** (General Feedback)
    * This field is an open note field where you can provide any general feedback about our conference.
    * Answer: type in your thoughts.
* **What To Improve** (What To Improve)
    * This field is a multiple-choice question where you can select what you think we can improve about our conference.
    * Answer: select from Yes, No, or Other.
* **Conference Satisfaction** (Conference Satisfaction)
    * This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with our conference out of the range you can select.
    * Answer: select a number from 1 to 10.
* **Attendees Attendance** (Attendees Attendance)
    * This field asks you to report the number of attendees at our conference.
    * Answer: enter the number.
* **Attendees Diet** (Attendees Diet)
    * This field asks if attendees had a meal during the conference.
    * Answer: select Yes, No, or Other.
* **Attendees Diet Details** (Attendees Diet Details)
    * This field is an open text field where you can provide more details about the attendees' diet.
    * Answer: type in your response.
* **Email** (Email)

* **Phone** (Phone)

* **Date** (Date)

    * Answer: select the time.
