# Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Registration Form is designed to collect basic information from individuals who want to register for membership in an event or a community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Fill in your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the third field. Please note that this is a required field to ensure we can contact you about your membership.
4. If you have a **Phone Number**, fill it in in the fourth field. This is optional, but it helps us get in touch with you in case of any important updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text Field, required: false): Fill in your first name as you would like it to appear on your membership documents.
* **Last Name** (Text Field, required: false): Fill in your last name as you would like it to appear on your membership documents.
* **Email** (Email Field, required: true): Enter your email address to receive important updates and information about your membership.
* **Phone Number** (Text Field, required: false): Fill in your phone number to allow us to contact you about your membership.
