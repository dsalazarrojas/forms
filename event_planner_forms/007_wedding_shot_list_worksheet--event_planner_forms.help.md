# Wedding Shot List Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from wedding photographers and couples to help event planners coordinate the logistics of the wedding ceremony and reception.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate information about the photographer's services and style.
2. Answer the questions about the couple's preferences for the wedding ceremony and reception.
3. Provide any special requirements or requests for the photographer or couple.
4. Sign and submit the form to confirm your details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Planner ID** (`event_planner_id`): Enter the ID number given to you by the event planner.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Where the wedding will be held? (e.g., church, hotel, park)
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`): Enter the name of the photographer.
* **Photographer Phone** (`photographer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the photographer.
* **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`): Enter the email address of the photographer.
* **Couple Name** (`couple_name`): Enter the names of the couple getting married.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`): Enter the time of the wedding.
* **Photographer Experience** (`photographer_experience`): Select the experience level of the photographer:
	+ Junior
	+ Senior
* **Photographer Style** (`photographer_style`): Select the style of photography the couple prefers:
	+ Traditional
	+ Modern:Reportage
* **Photographer Equipment** (`photographer_equipment`): Select the level of equipment the photographer will use:
	+ Basic
	+ Full
* **Photographer Experience Years** (`photographer_experience_years`): Enter the number of years of experience the photographer has.
* **Photographer Rating** (`photographer_rating`): Select the rating of the photographer:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Couple Wedding Venue** (`couple_wedding_venue`): Enter the name of the wedding venue.
* **Couple Wedding Ceremony** (`couple_wedding_ceremony`): Enter a brief description of the wedding ceremony.
* **Couple Wedding Reception** (`couple_wedding_reception`): Enter a brief description of the wedding reception.
* **Couple Wedding Dinner** (`couple_wedding_dinner`): Enter a brief description of the wedding dinner.
* **Couple Wedding Favors** (`couple_wedding_favors`): Enter a brief description of the wedding favors.
* **Couple Wedding Favors Description** (`couple_wedding_favors_description`): Enter a detailed description of the wedding favors.
* **Couple Special Requirements** (`couple_special_requirements`): Enter any special requirements or requests for the couple.
* **Couple Wedding Color Scheme** (`couple_wedding_color_scheme`): Enter the color scheme the couple prefers.
* **Couple Wedding Music** (`couple_wedding_music`): Enter the music preferences for the wedding.
* **Photographer Special Requirements** (`photographer_special_requirements`): Enter any special requirements or requests for the photographer.
* **Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm your details.
