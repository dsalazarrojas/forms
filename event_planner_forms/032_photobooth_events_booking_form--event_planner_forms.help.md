# Photobooth Events Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photobooth Events Booking Form is designed to collect information for photo booth events, such as parties, conferences, or weddings. This form will help you create and manage your event's booking details, including event title, date, time, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (required): Enter the title of your event, for example, "John's Birthday Party".
* **Event Description** (required): Briefly describe the purpose and atmosphere of your event.
* **Event Date** (required): Select the date of your event. Use the date picker to select a date from the calendar.
* **Event Time** (required): Select the start and end times of your event. Use the time picker to select a time from the clock.
* **Start Time** (required): Select the start time of your event. Use the time picker to select a time from the clock.
* **End Time** (required): Select the end time of your event. Use the time picker to select a time from the clock.
* **Event Capacity** (required): Enter the maximum number of attendees your event can hold.
* **Event Address** (required): Enter the physical address of your event location.
* **Event Phone** (required): Enter the phone number for your event contact.
* **Event Email** (required): Enter the email address for your event contact.
* **Photo Credit** (required): Provide the credit information for the event's photo booth photographer.
* **Image Type** (required): Select the type of image that will be used in the photobooth, for example, "color", "black and white", "instant photo", or "other".
* **Additional Image** (optional): If applicable, enter any additional images you'd like to use in the photobooth.
* **Additional Image Description** (optional): Briefly describe the additional images you entered in the "Additional Image" field.
* **Service** (required): Select the services you will provide for your photo booth, such as printing and sharing photos, or providing a live feed.
* **Additional Services** (optional): If applicable, list any other services you will provide for your event's photo booth.
* **Terms and Conditions** (required): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of the form.
