# Santa Claus Parade Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Santa Claus Parade Volunteer Registration Form is used to register volunteers for the annual Santa Claus Parade. This form collects information about the volunteer, including their name, contact information, and personal preferences. The information collected will help us match volunteers with suitable tasks and activities during the parade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Fill in your address in the "Address" field.
6. Select your favorite buddy from the list in the "Favorite Buddy" field.
7. Choose your favorite food from the list in the "Favorite Food" field (select all that apply).
8. Select your favorite color from the list in the "Favorite Color" field.
9. Choose your favorite holiday from the list in the "Favorite Holiday" field (select all that apply).
10. Select your favorite time of day (optional).
11. Select your favorite holiday 1 (optional).
12. Select your favorite time of day 1 (optional).
13. Enter your birthday in the "Birthday" field.
14. Select your favorite time of day 2 (optional).
15. Select your favorite holiday 1 (optional).
16. Select your favorite time of day 2 (optional).
17. Select your favorite day of week (optional, select all that apply).
18. Select your favorite day of week 1 (optional, select all that apply).
19. Select your favorite day of week 2 (optional, select all that apply).
20. Select your favorite day of week 3 (optional, select all that apply).
21. Select your favorite day of week 4 (optional, select all that apply).
22. Select your favorite day of week 5 (optional, select all that apply).
23. Select your favorite day of week 6 (optional, select all that apply).
24. Select your favorite day of week 7 (optional, select all that apply).
25. Select your favorite day of week 8 (optional, select all that apply).
26. Select your favorite day of week 9 (optional, select all that apply).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your home or work address where we can reach you.
* **Favorite Buddy**: Select the person you want to be your buddy during the parade (Buddy The Elf or The Big Guy).
* **Favorite Food**: Choose all the foods you enjoy during the parade (Candy Cane, Cookie, and Cake).
* **Favorite Color**: Select your favorite color (Red, Green, or Blue).
* **Favorite Holiday**: Choose all the holidays you like (Yes, No).
* **Favorite Time Of Day**: Select a time of day you are free to volunteer (optional, e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Favorite Time Of Day1, **Favorite Time Of Day2**: Same as above.
* **Birthday**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time Of Day3, **Time Of Day4, **Time Of Day5**: Same as above.
* **Favorite Holiday1, **Favorite Holiday2**: Same as above.
* **Favorite Day Of Week**: Select all the days of the week you are available (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Favorite Day Of Week1, **Favorite Day Of Week2, **Favorite Day Of Week3, **Favorite Day Of Week4, **Favorite Day Of Week5, **Favorite Day Of Week6, **Favorite Day Of Week7, **Favorite Day Of Week8**: Same as above.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is correct and up-to-date.
* We will use the information collected to match you with suitable tasks and activities during the parade.
* We will not share your information with anyone outside of our organization.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [contact email address].
