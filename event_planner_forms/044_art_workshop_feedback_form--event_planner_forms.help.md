# Art Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience at an art workshop. Your input will help event planners assess the effectiveness and quality of their workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide accurate and helpful feedback, please complete each field carefully. You may skip any questions that are not relevant to your experience. If you're unsure about a question, just leave it blank or write a note in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. Feedback Date**: Report the date you attended the workshop.
- **2. Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **3. Participant Email**: Enter a valid email address to help us contact you if needed.
- **4. Workshop Name**: Type the name of the workshop you attended.
- **5. Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop you attended.
- **6. Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor who led the workshop.
- **7. Workshop Duration**: Select the length of the workshop: 1-2 Hours, 3-4 Hours, Full Day, Multi-Day, or Weekly Series.
- **8. Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
- **9. Content Quality**: Rate the quality of the content: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
- **10. Instructor Knowledge**: Rate the instructor's knowledge: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
- **11. Instructor Presentation**: Rate the instructor's presentation skills: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
- **12. Instructor Support**: Rate the instructor's support and guidance: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
- **13. Skill Level Match**: Select whether the workshop was too Basic, Just Right, or Too Advanced for your skill level.
- **14. Pace of Instruction**: Rate the pace: Too Slow, Just Right, or Too Fast.
- **15. Materials Provided**: Rate the materials provided: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor, or Not Applicable.
- **16. Materials Quality**: Rate the quality of the materials: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor, or Not Applicable.
- **17. Facility Rating**: Rate the facility: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor.
- **18. Value for Money**: Rate the value: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor.
- **19. Skills Learned**: Select any skills you learned from the workshop.
- **20. Confidence Improvement**: Rate your confidence improvement: Significant, Moderate, Some, Little, or No Improvement.
- **21. Favorite Part**: Describe your favorite part of the workshop.
- **22. Least Favorite Part**: Describe what you liked least about the workshop.
- **23. Topics for Future**: Suggest any topics you'd like to see in future workshops.
- **24. Would Recommend**: Select whether you would recommend this workshop: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Unsure, Probably No, or Definitely No.
- **25. Would Attend Again**: Select whether you would attend another workshop: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Unsure, Probably No, or Definitely No.
- **26. How Heard**: Select how you heard about this workshop: Website, Social Media, Email, Friend, Advertisement, or Other.
- **27. Additional Comments**: Write any other feedback or suggestions you have.
