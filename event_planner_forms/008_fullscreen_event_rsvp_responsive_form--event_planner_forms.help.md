# Fullscreen Event RSVP Responsive Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather event details and RSVP information from attendees. It is meant to be used for events where RSVP is required, such as conferences, meetups, or workshops. The form is fully responsive, ensuring it adapts to different screen sizes and devices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Select your attendance status in the **Attending** field. You can choose from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Maybe'.
4. If your event has a specific date, enter it in the **Date** field.
5. If your event has a specific time, enter it in the **Time** field.
6. Leave a message or any additional information in the **Message** field if you wish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_rsvp_form`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a brief description of the event.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as the event attendee.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Provide your email address to be contacted about event updates or information.
* **Attending** (`attending`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you will attend the event, 'No' if you won't, or 'Maybe' if your status is uncertain.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional):
	+ If your event has a specific date, enter it in this field (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional):
	+ If your event has a specific time, enter it in this field (HH:MM AM/PM format).
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, text, optional):
	+ Leave a brief message or any additional information about your attendance.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional):
	+ This field is a free-text field for any additional comments or information about your event attendance.

## Tips
* Ensure to fill in all required fields (none are currently required).
* You can adjust the form to suit your event's specific needs.
* If you need any further assistance, contact form@gic.mx.
