# Day Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Day Camp Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians about their child's registration for a day camp program. This form helps us ensure the camper's safety and proper care during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to enter your child's correct information.
3. If you need help or have questions, contact us at [insert contact information].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your child's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your child's email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your child's phone number.
* **Date of arrival** (`date_arrival`, `date`, optional): Enter the date your child will be arriving at the camp.
* **Date of departure** (`date_departure`, `date`, optional): Enter the date your child will be departing from the camp.
* **Camper name** (`camper_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your child's name.
* **Parent info** (`parent_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your child's needs or requirements.
* **Medical conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If your child has any medical conditions, please describe them in detail.
* **Camper address** (`camper_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your child's home address.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Camper email** (`camper_email`, `text`, optional): Enter your child's email address.
* **Camper DOB** (`camper_dob`, `date`, optional): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Camper gender** (`camper_gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select your child's gender.
* **Camper age** (`camper_age`, `number`, optional): Enter your child's age.
* **Camper height** (`camper_height`, `number`, optional): Enter your child's height.
* **Camper weight** (`camper_weight`, `number`, optional): Enter your child's weight.
* **Camper shoe size** (`camper_shoe_size`, `number`, optional): Enter your child's shoe size.
* **Parent name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Parent email** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Parent phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical insurance provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether your child has medical insurance.
* **Medical insurance number** (`medical_insurance_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your child's medical insurance number.
* **Additional info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your child or your relationship with them.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but it's crucial to fill out as much information as possible to ensure the camper's safety and proper care during the camp. If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for guidance.
