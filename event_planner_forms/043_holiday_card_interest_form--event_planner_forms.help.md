# Holiday Card Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Card Interest Form is a way for individuals to express interest in receiving a personalized holiday card from the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. Enter your participant's name in the third field.
3. Choose your relationship to the participant from the dropdown menu in the fourth field.
4. Enter a personal message in the fifth field.
5. Decide whether you would like to receive a card and whether you'd like to send a card by selecting the corresponding options in the last two fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (Text): Your name, for the purpose of personalizing the card.
- **Email** (Email): Your email address, used for communication.
- **Phone** (Text): Your phone number, in case we need to contact you.
- **Participant's Name** (Text): The name of the participant you are interested in.
- **Relationship** (Select Multiple): Your relationship to the participant (e.g. Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Friend, Spouse, Son, Daughter, Grandparent).
- **Message** (Text): A personal message for the holiday card.
- **Receive A Card** (Select One): Check Yes to receive a card, No to not receive one.
- **Send A Card** (Select One): Check Yes to send a card, No to not send one.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the card is personalized correctly.
- If you want to receive a card but are not sure about the sender, you can still enter your name and relationship in the first two fields and we will try to match it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
