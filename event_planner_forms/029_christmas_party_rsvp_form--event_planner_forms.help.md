# Christmas Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Party RSVP form is a tool used to collect essential information from guests about their attendance, personal details, dietary needs, and special requests for the Christmas party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields. These fields are required to ensure we can address you personally.
2. Provide your **Email Address**. This will help us contact you with any updates or reminders about the party.
3. If you have a **Phone Number**, feel free to add it. Although not required, this will allow us to reach you in case of any last-minute changes or updates.
4. In the **RSVP Response** field, indicate whether you will be attending the party, or if you cannot attend, and let us know why.
5. If you are attending, specify the **Number of Guests Attending** (including yourself). This will help us with seating arrangements and catering.
6. If you have **Additional Guests** attending with you, list their **Names** in the **Guests Names** field. Use commas to separate each name.
7. In the **Dietary Requirements** section, select all the dietary restrictions or allergies you have (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Nut allergies, Shellfish allergies, or Dairy-free). This will help us plan for food and drinks that are suitable for you.
8. If you have any **Dietary Details**, feel free to describe your allergies or restrictions in this field. This will help us better understand your needs.
9. In the **Preferences** section, mention any **Seating Preference** or **Activities** you would like to participate in. This will help us tailor the party experience for you.
10. If you have any **Special Requests** or **Needs**, let us know in this field. We'll do our best to accommodate them.
11. Lastly, if you'd like to bring someone special along, indicate this by selecting **True** in the **Can we count on you to bring someone special?** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your given name. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Your surname. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Your email address for communication purposes. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for updates or changes. (Optional)
* **RSVP Response**: Indicate whether you will attend or not, and briefly describe your reasons. (Required)
* **Number of Guests Attending (including yourself)**: The total number of guests in your party, including you. (Required)
* **Guests Names**: A comma-separated list of names of guests attending with you. (Optional)
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Select all the dietary restrictions or allergies you have. (Optional)
* **Dietary Details**: Describe your dietary requirements or allergies in detail. (Optional)
* **Seating Preference**: Specify your seating preference for the party. (Optional)
* **Activities You Are Interested In**: Mention any activities you would like to participate in. (Optional)
* **Special Requests or Needs**: Describe any special requests or needs you have. (Optional)
* **Can we count on you to bring someone special?**: Indicate if you'd like to bring a guest. (Optional)
* **Comments or Questions**: Share any comments or questions you have about the party. (Optional)
