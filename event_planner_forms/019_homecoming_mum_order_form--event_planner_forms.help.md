# Homecoming Mum Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for homecoming mums for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button after filling out all the required fields.
2. Ensure that each field is filled out completely and accurately.
3. Double-check the form before submitting to ensure all fields are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school_name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school where the event is taking place.
* **parent_name** (`name_of_parent`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or person who is ordering the mums.
* **mums_quantity** (`number_of_mums`, number, required: false): Enter the total number of mums to be ordered.
* **order_type** (`order_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of order you would like:
	+ Standard
	+ Deluxe
	+ Plus
* **price_per_mum** (`mum_price`, number, required: false): Enter the price per mum.
* **total_price** (`total_price`, number, required: false): This field is calculated automatically based on the quantity of mums ordered and the price per mum.
* **recipient_name** (`name_of_recipient`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the recipient who will be receiving the mums.
* **recipient_email** (`recipient_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the recipient.
* **recipient_phone** (`recipient_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the recipient.
* **date_of_event** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **time_of_event** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **type_of_event** (`event_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of event:
	+ Homecoming
	+ Prom
	+ School Event
* **additional_notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or instructions for the event organizers.
* **submit** (`submit`, note, required: false): Click on this button to submit your order.
 
## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact the event organizers.
