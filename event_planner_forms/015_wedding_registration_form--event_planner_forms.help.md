# Wedding Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Registration Form is designed to gather information about a wedding event. This includes details such as the bride and groom's information, categories of interest, contact information, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (none are marked as required, so it's up to the user to decide what information to provide).
2. Enter the bride's information in the "bride_info" field.
3. Enter the groom's information in the "groom_info" field.
4. Select the relevant categories of interest from the "category_one", "category_two", and "category_three" fields.
5. Enter the email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter the phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Add any additional notes in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Bride Info
* **Bride Info** (bride_info, text, optional): Enter the bride's information, such as name, date of birth, etc.

### Groom Info
* **Groom Info** (groom_info, text, optional): Enter the groom's information, such as name, date of birth, etc.

### Category One
* **Category One** (category_one, text, optional): Select a category related to the wedding event (e.g., wedding dress, music, flowers, etc.).

### Category Two
* **Category Two** (category_two, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple categories related to the wedding event (e.g., yes, yes, no, no, etc.).

### Category Three
* **Category Three** (category_three, text, optional): Enter any additional category information.

### Email
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the email address associated with the wedding event.

### Phone
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number associated with the wedding event.

### Note
* **Note** (note, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the wedding event.
