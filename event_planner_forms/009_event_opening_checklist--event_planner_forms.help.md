# Event Opening Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Opening Checklist is a form used to collect and review information for event planning and organization. This guide will walk you through each field to help you fill out the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the applicable information. Most of the fields are optional, allowing you to tailor the form to your specific needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start date** (`event_opening_checklist_start`, date, not required): Enter the date on which the event is scheduled to begin.
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, not required): Specify the location where the event will take place.
* **Event name** (`event_name`, text, not required): Enter the title or name of the event.
* **Event description** (`event_description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the event, including its nature and purpose.
* **Start date** (`event_start_date`, date, not required): Confirm the date on which the event starts. 
* **Start time** (`event_start_time`, time, not required): Enter the hour and minute the event is scheduled to begin. Format is 24-hour format or AM/PM as preferred.
* **End date** (`event_end_date`, date, not required): Enter the date on which the event is scheduled to end. 
* **End time** (`event_end_time`, time, not required): Enter the hour and minute the event is scheduled to conclude. Format is 24-hour format or AM/PM as preferred.
* **Location** (`event_location`, text, not required): Specify the exact location of the event.
* **Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, not required): Enter the name and contact information of the person or team organizing the event.

## Tips
* Double-check the dates entered to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* For time entries, use the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 instead of 2:00 PM).
* If any field is not applicable to your event, you can skip it or leave it blank. The form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to your specific event needs.
* If you need additional fields or a different structure for your event planning, consider customizing this form further or creating a new one that suits your requirements.
