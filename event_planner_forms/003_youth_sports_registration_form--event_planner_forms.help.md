# Youth Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Sports Registration Form is a collection of fields that need to be filled out by parents or guardians to register their child for a sports program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the child's First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter the child's Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter the parent's or guardian's name in the **Parent Name** field.
4. Enter the child's birth date in the **Birth Date** field, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Enter the child's email address in the **Email** field. (Note: This field is optional, but providing an email address can help the sports organization contact you with updates and information.)
6. Enter the child's phone number in the **Phone** field.
7. Indicate whether the child is registered in a program or not by selecting one of the options in the **Program** field.
8. Enter the team number (if applicable) in the **Team Number** field.
9. Select the required shirt size in the **Shirt Size** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the child's name as it appears on their birth certificate or passport.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the child's surname as it appears on their birth certificate or passport.
* **Parent Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is registering the child.
* **Birth Date** (date, required): Enter the child's birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the child's email address to stay informed about updates and information from the sports organization. (Note: Not required, but providing an email can be helpful.)
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the child's phone number to help the sports organization contact you.
* **Program** (select_one, optional): Indicate if the child is registered in a program or not. Select "Yes" if they are, and "No" if not.
* **Team Number** (number, optional): Enter the team number (if applicable) to identify the team the child is registered with.
* **Shirt Size** (select_multiple, optional): Select the required shirt size for the child.
