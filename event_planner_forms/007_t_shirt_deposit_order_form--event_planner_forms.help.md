# t_shirt_deposit_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The t_shirt_deposit_order_form is used to collect information from customers for t-shirt orders, including customer details, order specifics, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the `customer_info` field.
2. Provide details about your order in the `order_details` field.
3. Select the color of the t-shirt you want to order from the `product_info` field.
4. Enter the quantity of t-shirts you want to order in the `quantity` field.
5. Enter the total cost of the order in the `cost` field.
6. Provide shipping information in the `shipping_info` field.
7. Enter the shipping cost in the `shipping_cost` field.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the `payment_method` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_info**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **order_details**: Describe the specific details of your order, such as the t-shirt design and size.
* **product_info**: Select the color of the t-shirt you want to order: red, blue, or black.
* **quantity**: Enter the number of t-shirts you want to order.
* **cost**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **shipping_info**: Provide information about shipping, such as address and shipping method.
* **shipping_cost**: Enter the shipping cost.
* **payment_method**: Select your preferred payment method: online or cash.
