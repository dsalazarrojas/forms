# Announcement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Announcement Request Form" is a form designed for employees to submit requests for company-wide announcements. This form ensures that announcements are properly evaluated, approved, and disseminated across the company's various channels.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information.
2. Select the type of announcement (e.g., company news, policy update, event announcement).
3. Provide a brief title and content for the announcement.
4. Choose the priority level and target audience for the announcement.
5. Select the distribution channels (e.g., email newsletter, company intranet).
6. Specify the desired publish date and time.
7. Choose whether attachments are included and provide additional details.
8. Indicate if management approval is required.
9. Provide the name of the approver (if applicable).
10. Designate a contact for questions.
11. Indicate if follow-up action is required.
12. Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your name as submitted in the announcement.
* **Department**: Select your department or division.
* **Email Address**: Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Type of Announcement**: Choose the type of announcement (e.g., company news, policy update, event announcement).
* **Announcement Title**: Provide a brief title for the announcement.
* **Announcement Content**: Enter the full text of the announcement.
* **Priority Level**: Select the urgency level of the announcement (e.g., Low, Normal, High, Urgent).
* **Target Audience**: Choose who should receive the announcement (e.g., All Employees, Management Only, Specific Department).
* **Specific Departments** (optional): List departments if applicable.
* **Distribution Channels**: Select where the announcement should be posted (e.g., email newsletter, company intranet).
* **Desired Publish Date**: Choose the date when the announcement should be published.
* **Desired Publish Time** (optional): Select the preferred time of day for the announcement.
* **Expiration Date** (optional): Choose when the announcement should be removed.
* **Attachments Included**: Indicate if attachments are included.
* **Attachment Details** (optional): Describe the attached files.
* **Approval Required**: Indicate if management approval is required.
* **Approver Name** (optional): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Contact for Questions**: Designate a contact for questions.
* **Follow-Up Required** (optional): Indicate if follow-up action is required.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any other important information.
