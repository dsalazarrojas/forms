# Wedding Coordinator Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details about a wedding event to estimate its cost.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please fill out each field with the relevant information about your event.
*   The form has multiple sections, but you can complete it in any order as long as you answer all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
*   If you need help with any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Date** (event_date): Enter the date of the wedding event. 
*   **Start Time** (start_time): Enter the start time of the wedding event. 
*   **End Time** (end_time): Enter the end time of the wedding event. 
*   **Event Location** (event_location): Enter the location of the wedding event. 
*   **Event Type** (event_type): Select the type of event. Options include:
    *   Reception
    *   Ceremony
    *   Both
    *   Other
*   **Guest Count** (guest_count): Enter the estimated number of guests. 
*   **Contact Name** (contact_name): Enter the name of the contact person. 
*   **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter the email of the contact person. 
*   **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): Enter the phone number of the contact person. 
*   **Event Notes** (event_notes): Enter any additional notes about the event. 
*   **Estimated Cost** (estimated_cost): Enter the estimated cost of the event. 

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer back to this guide for clarification.
*   Remember to double-check your answers for accuracy to ensure the form is completed correctly.
