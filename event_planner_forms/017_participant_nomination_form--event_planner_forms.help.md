# Participant Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text, required): Enter the participant's details.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, required): Enter the participant's job title.
* **Nominator Information** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Enter the nominator's name.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter the nominator's email address.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, required): Enter the nominator's phone number.
* **Nominator Job Title** (`nominator_job_title`, text, required): Enter the nominator's job title.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation for the nomination reason.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments or information that the selection committee should consider.
* **Award Type** (`award_type`, select_one, required): Select whether the participant meets the nomination criteria (Yes/No).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Submit the nomination form.
