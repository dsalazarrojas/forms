# Talent Hunt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Hunt Form is designed to collect information from potential candidates for available job openings. Event planners use this form to gather vital details from applicants that help them find the best-suited talent for their events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:
- Fill in your First Name
- Fill in your Last Name
- Enter a valid Email
- Enter your Phone number
- Specify your Job Title
- Provide a URL to your Resume (optional)
- Select the type of job you are applying for (Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance)
- Choose your Availability Days of the week
- Specify your Preferred Work Location
- Enter your Availability Date and Time
- Specify the Notice Period in weeks or months
- Choose the unit for your Notice Start and End Dates (days, weeks, or months)
- Select the type of Notice Period you are providing (Regular or Temporary)
- Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for
- Enter any additional job requirements
- Specify the person you would like to be in contact with
- Select the type of job you are applying for (again, if needed)
- If necessary, provide an email for the contact person

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Please enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (Required): Enter a valid email address. This will be used to contact you for further information.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (Required): Briefly describe the type of job you are applying for.
* **Resume URL** (Optional): If you have a public URL to share your resume, provide it here. This will be helpful for event planners to review.
* **Job Type** (Required): Choose whether you are applying for a Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance job.
* **Availability** (Required): Select the days of the week you are available to work.
* **Preferred Work Location** (Required): Specify the location where you would like to work.
* **Availability Date** (Required): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Availability Time** (Required): Enter the time you are available to start work.
* **Notice Period** (Required): Specify the number of weeks or months you need for notice.
* **Notice Period Unit** (Optional): Choose the unit for your notice period (days, weeks, or months).
* **Notice Start Date** (Required): Enter the date you would like to start the notice period.
* **Notice Start Date Unit** (Optional): Choose the unit for your notice start date (days, weeks, or months).
* **Notice End Date** (Optional): Enter the date you would like to end the notice period.
* **Notice End Date Unit** (Optional): Choose the unit for your notice end date (days, weeks, or months).
* **Notice Period Type** (Required): Choose whether your notice period is Regular or Temporary.
* **Work Schedule** (Optional): Enter any information about your work schedule.
* **Job Description** (Required): Briefly describe the job you are applying for.
* **Job Requirements** (Optional): Enter any additional job requirements.
* **Contact Person** (Required): Specify the person you would like to be in contact with.
* **Job Type 2** (Optional): If needed, select the type of job you are applying for again.
* **Contact Email** (Optional): If necessary, provide an email for the contact person.
* **Notice Period 2** (Optional): If needed, specify the number of weeks or months you need for notice.
