# Vacation Bible School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Bible School Registration Form is used to collect information from users for the purpose of registering them for the Vacation Bible School event. This form should only be used for registering one person at a time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text): Enter the name of the person registering.
* **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the person registering. This field is optional.
* **Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the person registering. This field is optional.
* **Select one** (select_one): Choose one of the options provided to indicate which child will be attending. For example, "Child 1", "Child 2", or "Child 3".
* **Select multiple** (select_multiple): Choose multiple options from the provided list to indicate which children will be attending. For example, "Child 1" and "Child 2".
* **Date** (date): Enter the date you will be attending. This field is optional.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time you will be attending. This field is optional.
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional information or comments you have about your registration. This field is optional.
* **Event** (text): Enter any additional information about the event you will be attending. This field is optional.
