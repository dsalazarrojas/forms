# Conference Registration Form White Gray Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Conference White Gray Theme event. Please fill out the form with your details to reserve your spot and make necessary arrangements for the conference. 

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, simply click on the "Start" button to begin the registration process. Each page of the form is a separate step in the registration process. Please fill out all required fields on each page before proceeding to the next step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registrant Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your government ID or passport.
- **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to confirm your registration and send updates.
- **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any questions or issues.
- **Company Name** (4, text, required): Enter the name of the company or organization you represent.
- **Job Title** (5, text, required): Enter your job title or position within your company.
- **Industry Sector** (6, select_one, required): Select the type of industry you work in.
  - **Event Planning**: Event planning and coordination
  - **Corporate Events**: Corporate event planning and coordination
  - **Wedding Planning**: Wedding planning and coordination
  - **Non-Profit Events**: Non-profit event planning and coordination
  - **Government Events**: Government event planning and coordination
  - **Education**: Educational institutions and events
  - **Other**: Other industry sectors
- **Experience Level** (7, select_one, required): Indicate your level of experience in event planning.
  - **New to Industry 0-2 years**: Less than 2 years of experience in the industry
  - **Intermediate 3-5 years**: 3-5 years of experience in the industry
  - **Experienced 6-10 years**: 6-10 years of experience in the industry
  - **Expert 10+ years**: More than 10 years of experience in the industry
- **Registration Type** (8, select_one, required): Choose the type of pass you would like to register for.
  - **Full Conference Pass**: Access to all conference sessions and events
  - **One Day Pass**: Access to one day of conference sessions and events
  - **Virtual Pass**: Virtual attendance to conference sessions and events
  - **Student Pass**: Discounted pass for students
  - **Group Pass**: Pass for groups of people
- **Days Attending** (9, select_multiple, required): Select all the days you plan to attend.
  - **Day 1 - Foundations**: Foundation sessions
  - **Day 2 - Advanced Topics**: Advanced topics sessions
  - **Day 3 - Workshops**: Workshops and hands-on sessions
  - **Day 4 - Networking**: Networking events
- **Breakout Session Preferences** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select topics of interest for breakout sessions.
  - **Event Technology**: Event technology and software
  - **Budget Management**: Budgeting and financial management for events
  - **Vendor Relations**: Vendor relations and management
  - **Risk Management**: Risk management and mitigation for events
  - **Sustainability**: Sustainability and environmental impact of events
  - **Marketing Events**: Marketing and promotion of events
  - **Client Relations**: Client relations and management
- **Workshop Selection** (11, select_one, required): Choose which workshop session you would like to attend.
  - **Workshop A - Morning**: Workshop A in the morning
  - **Workshop B - Afternoon**: Workshop B in the afternoon
  - **Both Workshops**: Both workshops
  - **No Workshops**: No workshop selection
- **Meal Preference** (12, select_one, required): Select your meal preference for catering.
  - **Standard**: Standard meal options
  - **Vegetarian**: Vegetarian meal options
  - **Vegan**: Vegan meal options
  - **Gluten-Free**: Gluten-free meal options
  - **Kosher**: Kosher meal options
  - **Halal**: Halal meal options
  - **Pescatarian**: Pescatarian meal options
- **Dietary Restrictions** (13, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies.
- **Networking Events** (14, select_one, required): Select which networking events you would like to attend.
  - **Welcome Reception**: Welcome reception
  - **Gala Dinner**: Gala dinner
  - **Closing Brunch**: Closing brunch
  - **All Events**: All networking events
  - **None**: No networking events
- **Hotel Accommodation** (15, select_one, required): Select whether you need hotel accommodation.
  - **Yes Need Hotel**: Need hotel accommodation
  - **No Already Booked**: Already booked your own accommodation
  - **Not Required**: No hotel accommodation needed
- **Room Preference** (16, select_one, optional): Select your room preference.
  - **Single Occupancy**: Single occupancy room
  - **Double Occupancy**: Double occupancy room
  - **Suite**: Suite
  - **No Preference**: No preference
- **Airport Transportation** (17, select_one, required): Select whether you need airport transfer.
  - **Arrival Transfer**: Need arrival transfer
  - **Departure Transfer**: Need departure transfer
  - **Both Transfers**: Need both transfers
  - **Not Needed**: No transfer needed
- **Accessibility Requirements** (18, text, optional): Enter any accessibility requirements.
- **Certificate of Attendance** (19, select_one, required): Select whether you need a certificate of attendance.
  - **Yes Required**: Need certificate of attendance
  - **No Not Needed**: Do not need certificate of attendance
- **Marketing Communications** (20, select_one, required): Select whether you would like to receive marketing communications.
  - **Yes Please**: Yes, please send marketing communications
  - **No Thank You**: No marketing communications
- **Payment Method** (21, select_one, required): Select your payment method.
  - **Credit Card**: Credit card payment
  - **Debit Card**: Debit card payment
  - **Invoice**: Invoice payment
  - **Check**: Check payment
- **Terms and Conditions** (22, select_one, required): Accept the terms and conditions.
  - **I Accept**: Accept terms and conditions
  - **I Do Not Accept**: Do not accept terms and conditions

Please review the form carefully before submitting. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
