# Indemnity Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Indemnity Waiver Form" is a document that protects event planners, venues, or activity providers from liability in case of an injury or accident during an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions provided to understand the purpose and importance of the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Indemnity Waiver Form** (`event_planner_forms_indemnity_waiver_form`, text, optional): This is the form title, which should be filled out with the name of the form.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant signing the form.
* **address** (`address`, text, optional): Fill out your physical address for contact purposes.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): The date on which the participant signs the form.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): The time at which the participant signs the form.
* **event_type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event you are participating in:
	+ Event
	+ Meeting
	+ Seminar
	+ Conference
	+ Workshop
	+ Training
	+ Class
	+ Workout
* **release** (`release`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or details.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the form.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your event planner or activity provider for clarification.
* Make sure to sign the form with your signature before submitting.
