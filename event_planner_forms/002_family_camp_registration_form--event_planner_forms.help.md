# Family Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from family members who wish to attend the family camp. It's essential to provide accurate details to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the following fields with your general information:
	* **General Information**: This field is for you to provide a brief introduction about yourself and your family. You can include any details you feel are relevant, such as your name, age, and any specific needs you may have.
2. Please provide your medical history:
	* **Medical History**: If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or take any medications, please describe them in this field.
3. In case of an emergency:
	* **Emergency Contact**: If you need someone to contact in case of an emergency, please provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
4. If you have previous camping experience:
	* **Previous Camping Activity**: If you have camped before, please describe your previous experience, including the location, duration, and any notable events that occurred.
5. Additional comments or questions:
	* **Further Questions/Comments**: If you have any questions or want to share any further information, please use this field to provide it.
