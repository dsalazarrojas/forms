# T Shirt Pre Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to pre-order T-shirts with custom options such as size, color, and design. The form gathers customer information, order details, and payment method. It is intended to streamline the T-shirt ordering process for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the size and color of the T-shirt you would like to order.
3. Select the design(s) you would like to have printed on the T-shirt.
4. Choose the shipping option for your order.
5. Enter the quantity of T-shirts you would like to order.
6. Enter the price of the T-shirt (leave this field blank if you don't want to pay immediately).
7. Confirm the total cost of your order.
8. Select the payment method you would like to use (cash, credit card, or PayPal).
9. Enter the amount you are paying.
10. Add any comments or notes for the T-shirt design team.
11. Confirm your order and enter any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **t_shirt_pre_order_form_template_form**: This is the main form section.
* **customer_info**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **t_shirt_size**: Select the size of the T-shirt you would like to order (S, M, L, XL, or XXL).
* **t_shirt_color**: Choose the color of the T-shirt you would like to order (white, red, blue, or black).
* **quantity**: Enter the number of T-shirts you would like to order.
* **shipping**: Select whether you would like shipping (True) or not (False).
* **tshirt_design**: Select the design(s) you would like to have printed on the T-shirt.
* **t_shirt_price**: Enter the price of the T-shirt (leave this field blank if you don't want to pay immediately).
* **total_cost**: Confirm the total cost of your order.
* **customer_name**: Enter your name for the order confirmation.
* **customer_phone**: Enter your phone number for the order confirmation.
* **customer_email**: Enter your email address for the order confirmation.
* **tshirt_notes**: Add any notes or comments for the T-shirt design team.
* **order_confirmation**: Confirm your order.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method you would like to use (cash, credit card, or PayPal).
* **payment_amount**: Enter the amount you are paying.
* **customer_comments**: Add any comments or notes for the T-shirt design team.
* **t_shirt_preorder_form_template_notes**: Add any additional notes or comments for the T-shirt design team.
* **t_shirt_preorder_form_template_confirmation**: Confirm your order with any additional comments or questions.
