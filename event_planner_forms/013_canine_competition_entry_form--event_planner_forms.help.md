# Canine Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from handlers and their dogs for the canine competition. It includes sections for handler and dog details, competition type and event selections, and medical and behavioral information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the form carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Fill in the handler's details (Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Mailing Address).
3. Select the competition type and event(s) your dog will be participating in.
4. Provide your dog's details (Name, Breed, Date of Birth, and Gender).
5. Select whether your dog is current on vaccinations and any medical conditions.
6. Indicate if there are any behavioral concerns or triggers.
7. Confirm that you agree to the liability waiver and code of conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Handler Name**: Your name as the handler of the dog.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number for any questions or updates.
* **Mailing Address**: Your residential or business address.
* **Have you competed before?**: Select one: Yes, multiple times, Yes, once or twice, or No, first time.
* **Dog Name**: Registered name of the dog (if applicable).
* **Breed or Mix**: Dog breed or mix.
* **AKC or Breed Club Registration Number**: Optional registration number.
* **Date of Birth**: Dog's birthday.
* **Color and Markings**: Description of your dog's color and markings.
* **Gender**: Select one: Male or Female.
* **Type of Competition**: Select one: Obedience, Agility, Rally, Conformation, Dock Diving, Flyball, Lure Coursing, or Other.
* **Competition Division/Class**: Select applicable level: Beginner, Novice, Intermediate, Advanced, Champion, or Mixed Level.
* **Which events will you enter?**: Select all that apply: Event 1, Event 2, Event 3, Event 4, Event 5, or All events.
* **Preferred Run Time**: Select one: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or No preference.
* **Is your dog current on vaccinations?**: Select one: Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **Does your dog have any medical conditions?**: Optional medical conditions or concerns.
* **Are there any behavioral concerns or triggers?**: Optional.
* **Total Entry Fees**: Automatically calculated based on your selections.
* **I acknowledge and agree to the liability waiver**: Select one: Yes, I agree or No.
* **I agree to follow the competition code of conduct**: Select one: Yes, I agree or No.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If unsure about any field, please contact the competition organizers.
* If you need to add any additional information, please include it in the space provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
