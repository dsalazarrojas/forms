# Worldpay US Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worldpay US Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect information for workshop registration, specifically for events, speakers, attendees, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Planner Form** (event_planner_form, text, required): Enter the event title and description.
* **Event Title** (event_title, text, required): Enter the title of the event, e.g., "Workshop on Data Science".
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, required): Enter the date of the event in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Start Time** (start_time, time, required): Enter the start time of the event in 24-hour format, e.g., 10:00 AM.
* **End Time** (end_time, time, required): Enter the end time of the event in 24-hour format, e.g., 12:00 PM.
* **Venue** (venue, select_one, required): Choose the venue of the event from the available options.
* **Speaker** (speaker, select_multiple, required): Select the speaker(s) you wish to attend from the available options.
* **Registration Date** (registration_date, date, required): Enter the date you registered for the event.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Choose the payment method from the available options.
* **Attendees** (attendees, number, required): Enter the number of attendees you are registering.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (notes, text, required): Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Payment Status** (payment_status, select_one, required): Select the status of your payment.
* **Payment Date** (payment_date, date, required): Enter the date you made the payment.
* **Payment Time** (payment_time, time, required): Enter the time you made the payment.
* **Payment Method Amount** (payment_method_amount, number, required): Enter the amount paid.
* **Payment Method Description** (payment_method_description, text, required): Enter a description of the payment method.
* **Payment Method Total** (payment_method_total, number, required): Enter the total amount paid.
* **Payment Method Status** (payment_method_status, select_one, required): Select the status of the payment method.
* **Payment Method Failed Reason** (payment_method_failed_reason, text, required): Enter the reason for failed payments.
* **Payment Method Failed Code** (payment_method_failed_code, text, required): Enter any code for failed payments.
* **Payment Method Refunded** (payment_method_refunded, select_one, required): Select the status of the refund.
* **Payment Method Failed Message** (payment_method_failed_message, text, required): Enter any failed payment message.
