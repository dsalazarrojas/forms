# online_ceremony_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_ceremony_registration_form is used to register for an online ceremony. It captures personal information and ceremony details to ensure that users can attend and participate in the ceremony smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted for further information).
3. Provide your date of birth (if you are attending the ceremony, this will help us verify your identity).
4. Select the time of the ceremony you would like to attend.
5. Choose the type of ceremony you would like to attend from the provided options.
6. Add any additional notes you'd like to share with the ceremony organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): If you are attending the ceremony, enter your date of birth to help us verify your identity.
* **Time of Ceremony** (time_of_ceremony, time, required): Select the time of the ceremony you would like to attend.
* **Ceremony Type** (ceremony_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of ceremony you would like to attend from the provided options.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with the ceremony organizers.
