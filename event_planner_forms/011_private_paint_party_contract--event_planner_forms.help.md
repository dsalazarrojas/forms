# Private Paint Party Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Paint Party Contract form is designed for hosts or organizers of private painting events to collect necessary information from clients or participants. This form helps in creating a comprehensive contract that covers all aspects of the event, including event details, host information, payment terms, and contract signatories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for the event (Field 1: Private Paint Party Contract).
2. Enter event details such as date (Field 2: Event Details).
3. Enter host information such as host name (Field 3: Host Info).
4. Enter contract terms, including payment terms (Field 4: Contract Terms).
5. Specify payment details (Field 6: Payment Details).
6. Select contract signatories, i.e., those signing the contract (Field 7: Contract Signatories).
7. Enter contract date and time (Field 8: Contract Date and Field 9: Contract Time).
8. Specify the event date and start/end times (Field 10: Event Date and Field 11: Event Start Time).
9. Enter location details, address, city, and region (Fields 13-17: Location Details, Address, City, and Region).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Private Paint Party Contract** (Field 1): Enter the title of the private painting event.
* **Event Details** (Field 2): Enter the date of the event.
* **Host Info** (Field 3): Enter the name of the host or organizer.
* **Contract Terms** (Field 4): Enter any necessary contract terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms** (Field 5): Enter payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Details** (Field 6): Specify payment details.
* **Contract Signatories** (Field 7): Select all those who will sign the contract, e.g., client, host, artist, or all of them.
* **Contract Date** (Field 8): Enter the date on which the contract is signed.
* **Contract Time** (Field 9): Enter the time on which the contract is signed.
* **Event Date** (Field 10): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (Field 11): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (Field 12): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Location Details** (Field 13): Enter location details.
* **Location** (Field 14): Enter the name of the location.
* **Address** (Field 15): Enter the address of the location.
* **City** (Field 16): Enter the city of the location.
* **Region** (Field 17): Enter the region of the location.
