# ประกerson Injury Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ประกerson Injury Waiver Form" is designed to be completed by participants in events where there is a risk of injury, ensuring that they are aware of and acknowledge certain risks, and that they provide necessary information for their care and liability purposes in case of an emergency. The form requires specific details from participants to ensure a smooth and safe event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and effectively, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the event.
2.  Fill in the required details as prompted, providing accurate information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: (`event_name`, `text`, required) - This field is to identify the event in which you are participating. Please fill in the exact name of the event as it appears in the event schedule.
* **Participant**: (`participant_name`, `text`, required) - This field is for you to enter your name as a participant. Ensure that it is spelled correctly to ensure accuracy in event records.
* **Guardian**: (`guardian_name`, `text`, optional) - If you are under the age of 18 or have a parent/guardian responsible for your event participation, please fill in their name here.
* **Address**: (`address`, `text`, required) - Enter your permanent address where you can be contacted in case of emergencies.
* **City**: (`city`, `text`, required) - This is the city where you reside, for contact and emergency purposes.
* **State**: (`state`, `text`, required) - Fill in the state where you reside, as per your address details.
* **Zip Code**: (`zip_code`, `text`, required) - Complete the zip code of your address for accurate contact.
* **Emergency Contact**: (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional) - If someone should contact in case of an emergency, provide their name and relationship with you.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, `text`, required) - Enter your contact phone number for emergency contact.
* **Email**: (`email`, `text`, required) - Provide your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Medical Info**: (`medical_info`, `text`, required) - Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs you have.
* **Injury Description**: (`injury_description`, `text`, required) - If you have any existing injuries, describe them here.
* **Witness Info**: (`witness_info`, `text`, optional) - If you are under 18, please provide the names and contact details of your parents or guardians.
* **Date of Injury**: (`date_of_injury`, `date`, required) - If you have any pre-existing injuries, provide the date of the injury.
* **Time of Injury**: (`time_of_injury`, `time`, required) - If you have any pre-existing injuries, provide the time of the injury.
* **Signature**: (`signature`, `note`, required) - Sign this form to acknowledge the terms and conditions of the event and confirm you are aware of the risks involved.
* **Emergency Services**: (`emergency_services`, `select_one`, required) - Indicate if emergency medical services are required.
* **Medical Attention**: (`medical_attention`, `select_multiple`, optional) - If you require medical attention during or after the event, choose from the options provided.
* **Minimalist**: (`minimalist`, `number`, optional) - Please note that this field is for future reference, it's not required for event participation.
* **Medical Procedure**: (`medical_procedure`, `select_multiple`, optional) - If you require medical procedures, choose from the options provided.
* **Doctor Prescribed**: (`doctor_prescribed`, `select_one`, required) - Confirm if you have been prescribed any medical treatment for your condition.
* **Doctor**: (`doctor_name`, `text`, required) - If you have been prescribed medication, enter the name of your doctor.
* **Phone**: (`doctor_phone`, `text`, optional) - If you have been prescribed medication, enter your doctor's phone number for contact.
* **Medical Facility**: (`medical_facility`, `text`, required) - If you have been prescribed medication or require medical procedures, enter the name of the medical facility.
* **Medical Facility Address**: (`medical_facility_address`, `text`, required) - Enter the address of the medical facility.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any pre-existing medical conditions or concerns, please describe them in the 'Medical Info' field.
- If you are under 18, please ensure to provide the names and contact details of your parents or guardians.
- Read through the form carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any part of it.
