# Halloween Candy Gram Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for businesses to place orders for Halloween Candy Grams and Halloween Grams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your business name in the "Business Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your business email address in the "Business Email" field.
5. Select the type of order from the "Order Type" dropdown menu (Halloween Gram or Candy Gram).
6. Enter a brief description of your order in the "Order Description" field (optional).
7. Enter any additional details about your order in the "Order Details" field (optional).
8. Select the date you would like your order to be delivered in the "Delivery Date" field.
9. Select the time you would like your order to be delivered in the "Delivery Time" field.
10. Enter the tool you would like to be assigned for your order in the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. Enter the form ID in the "Form ID" field.
12. Enter the form version in the "Form Version" field (optional).
13. Enter the date and time the form was submitted in the "Submitted At" field (auto-populated).
14. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.
15. Enter the name of the person who was assigned to fulfill the order in the "Assigned To" field.
16. Enter any additional notes about the order in the "Notes" field (optional).
17. Enter the business address in the "Business Address" field.
18. Enter the business city in the "Business City" field.
19. Enter the business state in the "Business State" field.
20. Enter the business zip in the "Business Zip" field.
21. Select the type of person who submitted the form from the "Submitted By Business" dropdown menu (Owner, Manager, or Employee).
22. Enter the submitted date and time in the "Submitted At" field (auto-populated).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Business Name**: Enter your business name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Business Email**: Enter your business email address.
* **Order Type**: Select the type of order you would like to place (Halloween Gram or Candy Gram).
* **Order Description**: Enter a brief description of your order (optional).
* **Order Details**: Enter any additional details about your order (optional).
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool you would like to be assigned for your order.
* **Form ID**: Enter the form ID.
* **Form Version**: Enter the form version (optional).
* **Submitted At**: This field is auto-populated with the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person who was assigned to fulfill the order.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the order (optional).
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
* **Business City**: Enter your business city.
* **Business State**: Enter your business state.
* **Business Zip**: Enter your business zip.
* **Submitted By Business**: Select the type of person who submitted the form from the dropdown menu (Owner, Manager, or Employee).

Note: This form is designed to be completed by businesses, but individuals may still be able to view and submit it.
