# Event Attendance Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an attendee's attendance at events. It will help collect personal and event-related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your personal details in the first 7 fields: First Name, Last Name, Contact Number, Email Address, Company, Job Title, and Company Website.
* Select your company status (Yes/No) in the Department field.
* Choose your event status (Yes/No) in the Event field.
* Enter the event date and time in the Event Date and Event Time fields.
* Specify your event status (Active/Inactive) in the Event Status field.
* Choose your attendance status (Yes/No) in the Attendance field.
* Enter the attendance date and time in the Attendance Date and Attendance Time fields.
* Specify your attendance status (Active/Inactive) in the Attendance Status field.
* Add any additional comments about your attendance in the Additional Comments field.
* Select Yes/No to indicate if you have travelled to the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or business card. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or business card. 
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number (phone or mobile) where we can reach you. 
* **Email Address**: Enter your work or personal email address where we can contact you. 
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company or organization you represent. 
* **Role**: Select Yes if you are part of the company, No otherwise. 
* **Department**: Select Yes if your company is active, No otherwise. 
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within the company. 
* **Company Website**: Enter your company website or URL. 
* **Event**: Select Yes if you will attend the event, No otherwise. 
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event (if known). 
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event (if known). 
* **Event Status**: Select Active if the event is active, Inactive otherwise. 
* **Attendance Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the event (if known). 
* **Attendance Time**: Enter the time you plan to attend the event (if known). 
* **Attendance Status**: Select Active if you will attend, Inactive otherwise. 
* **Event Type**: Select Yes if it's an event you are attending, No otherwise. 
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments about your attendance. 
* **Has Travelled**: Select Yes if you have travelled to the event.
