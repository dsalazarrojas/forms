# Secret Santa Form For Kids - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for kids to let their parents or guardians know their holiday preferences so they can choose a great gift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, fill out the fields below and submit your answers when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Holiday Activity**: Please describe your favorite holiday activity (e.g., decorating, playing with siblings, etc.).
- **Name**: Please input your name so we can keep track of who is submitting the form.
- **Email**: Please input your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
- **Address**: Please input your home address so we can know where to mail your gift.
- **Gift Preferences**: Choose the types of gifts you would like to receive this holiday season. You can select multiple options: Toy, Game, Art Supplies, or Book.
- **Gift Budget**: If you have a specific budget in mind for your gift, please input that number. This will help us make sure everyone stays within their budget.
- **Message**: Please let us know if there's anything special you'd like to share with Santa (or us) this holiday season.
- **Special Request**: Choose any special requests you might have for your gift, such as a specific type of toy or game, etc.
- **Form Submit**: Click this field to submit your form when you have finished filling it out.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the fields as accurately and completely as possible so we can get to know you better.
- If you need any help or have questions, ask a parent or guardian for assistance.
