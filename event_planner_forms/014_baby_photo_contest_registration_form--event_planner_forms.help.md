# Baby Photo Contest Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register your baby for a photo contest. Please fill it out carefully to ensure you are eligible to participate and understand the terms and conditions of the contest.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you provide a clear and recent photo of your baby.
3. Make sure you have the necessary permissions and agreements checked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, required): This is used for contest updates and notifications. Make sure it is a valid email address you check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter your contact number for any questions or clarifications.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, required): This is the full address where prize delivery will be made. Enter it accurately to ensure correct delivery.
* **Baby Full Name** (`baby_name`, required): Enter the name as you want it to appear in the contest.
* **Baby Nickname** (`baby_nickname`, optional): Enter an optional nickname for your baby.
* **Baby Date of Birth** (`baby_date_of_birth`, required): Enter the date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Baby Gender** (`baby_gender`, required): Select the correct gender of your baby.
* **Contest Category** (`contest_category`, required): Choose the correct age category for your baby.
* **Upload Baby Photo** (`photo_upload`, required): Upload a clear, recent photo of your baby.
* **Photo Caption** (`photo_caption`, optional): Enter a fun or meaningful caption for your photo.
* **Photo Story** (`photo_story`, optional): Share a brief story about the photo.
* **Previously Entered Contests** (`previous_contests`, optional): If you've entered a previous contest, select 'True'.
* **Social Media Handle** (`social_media_handle`, optional): Enter your Instagram or Facebook handle for featuring your baby.
* **How Did You Hear About Contest** (`how_heard_about_contest`, optional): Choose how you heard about the contest.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions of the contest.
* **Photo Rights Confirmation** (`photo_rights_confirmation`, required): Confirm that you own the rights to this photo.
* **Publicity Consent** (`publicity_consent`, required): Agree to allow use of your baby's photo for promotional purposes.
* **Age Verification** (`age_verification`, required): Confirm that your baby meets the age requirements.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, required): Enter the date of this entry.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Enter any other relevant information about your baby.
