# Christmas Wish Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your Christmas wishes, traditions, and reflections.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out your name, email address, and phone number (if desired).
- Select your primary wish and its category.
- Share your holiday message or well-wishes with the community.
- Consider expressing your favorite holiday memory, a new tradition you'd like to start, or something you're grateful for this year.
- Let us know if you consent to share your wish publicly and if you'd like to display a different name.
- Finally, express how sharing your wish makes you feel and how you plan to spread joy this holiday season.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number (optional)** (`optional_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **What is your primary wish this Christmas?** (`primary_wish`, `text`, required): Be honest about what you're hoping for this holiday season.
* **Wish Category** (`wish_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the available categories: Personal, Family, Community, Global, Health and wellness, Career, Other.
* **Your Holiday Message or Well-Wishes** (`holiday_message`, `text`, required): Express your holiday message or well-wishes to the community.
* **What inspired your wish?** (`inspiration`, `text`, optional): Share what led you to make this wish.
* **Any additional wishes or dreams?** (`additional_wishes`, `text`, optional): Share any other wishes or dreams you have.
* **Your Favorite Holiday Tradition** (`favorite_tradition`, `text`, optional): Share a cherished holiday tradition.
* **New Tradition You Want to Start** (`new_tradition`, `text`, optional): Share an idea for a new tradition you'd like to start.
* **Your Favorite Christmas Memory** (`favorite_memory`, `text`, optional): Share a cherished memory.
* **What Are You Most Grateful For This Year?** (`grateful_for`, `text`, optional): Express what you're grateful for this year.
* **I consent to share my wish publicly** (`share_publicly`, `select_one`, required): Choose to share your wish publicly or not.
* **Name to Display (if different)** (`attribution_name`, `text`, optional): Enter a name to display if you don't want to share your real name.
* **How does sharing your wish make you feel?** (`emotion`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you feel when sharing your wish.
* **How do you plan to spread joy this holiday season?** (`spread_joy`, `text`, optional): Share your plan to spread joy.
