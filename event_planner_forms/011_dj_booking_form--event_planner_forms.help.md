# DJ Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the booking of DJs for various events within the organization. It provides a structured approach to collecting essential information necessary for effective event planning and DJ booking management.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the **Event Name** in the designated field. This will help in identifying the event for which you are booking the DJ.
2. Proceed to the **DJ Information** field, where you can provide a brief description or notes regarding the DJ you are booking.
3. Enter the **Event Date** in the correct format (mm/dd/yy or dd/mm/yy) and the **Event Time** in the 12-hour format (e.g., 03:00 PM).
4. Specify the **Event Location** where the event will take place.
5. Enter the **DJ Fee** as a numeric value to denote the agreed amount.
6. In the **Booking Agent** field, mention the person responsible for making the booking or the contact person for the event.
7. In the **Event Planner** field, specify your name or the name of the person responsible for the event.
8. Add any relevant **Event Notes** in the designated field to keep track of important information or details not included in the other fields.
9. Select the **Event Organizer** from the dropdown list (Jimmy, John, or Jane) to indicate who is organizing the event.
10. For **Event Status**, choose the status (Pending, Approved, or Declined) to denote the current state of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the event to uniquely identify it in the database.
- **DJ Information** (text, optional): Provide a brief description or notes about the DJ you are booking.
- **Event Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event using the mm/dd/yy or dd/mm/yy format.
- **Event Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the event in 12-hour format (e.g., 03:00 PM).
- **Event Location** (text, optional): Specify the address or venue of the event.
- **DJ Fee** (number, optional): Enter the agreed amount for the DJ's services.
- **Booking Agent** (text, optional): Mention the person responsible for the booking or the contact person for the event.
- **Event Planner** (text, optional): Specify your name or the name of the person responsible for the event.
- **Event Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or details about the event.
- **Event Organizer** (select_one, required): Choose the person organizing the event from the dropdown list (Jimmy, John, or Jane).
- **Event Status** (select_multiple, required): Select the status of the event (Pending, Approved, or Declined) to denote its current state.

## Tips

* Always ensure that all required fields are filled correctly to prevent errors in the booking process.
* Be as detailed as possible in the **Event Notes** field to avoid missing important information.
* If an event's status changes, update it in the **Event Status** field to reflect the correct status.
