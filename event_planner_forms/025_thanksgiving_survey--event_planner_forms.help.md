# Thanksgiving Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Thanksgiving Survey" is a form designed to gather customers' opinions and feedback about the products offered to them during the Thanksgiving season. This form is used by event planners to collect valuable information that helps them understand customer preferences and tailor their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read through each section carefully and provide accurate information about your experience and opinions.
2.  Fill out each field, making sure to provide the required information.
3.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Event Planner Forms** (`event_planner_forms`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the event planner's details. Please enter the name and description of the event planner you are using for this survey. This information is optional but helps us understand the context of your survey responses.
*  **Thanksgiving Survey** (`thanksgiving_survey`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide a title or description of the survey you are creating. Enter a brief title or description to help us understand the purpose of your survey.
*  **A Thanksgiving survey is a survey created by a company to ask customers' opinions about the products offered to them** (`thanksgiving_survey_description`, text, optional): This field provides additional information about the purpose and context of the survey. It is an optional field and can be left blank.
*  **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field is used to identify the tool or platform used to create the survey. Enter the name of the tool or platform used if you are familiar with it.
*  **Form ID** (`form_ids`, number, optional): This field is used to identify the unique form ID. Please enter a unique number to help us track your survey responses.
