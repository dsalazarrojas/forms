# Art Exhibit Curation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from artists and galleries about proposed art exhibits. It will help curators to assess and consider their proposals for curation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, please fill in your contact information:
  - **Name**: Enter your name as it would be displayed in the catalog.
  - **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address to ensure we can reach you for questions and updates.
  - **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly.
  - **Organization or Institution**: If you are representing an institution, enter its name.
2. Next, propose your exhibit:
  - **Exhibit Title**: A clear and concise title for your proposed exhibit.
  - **Exhibit Description**: A brief description of your proposed exhibit, including its concept, theme, and scope.
  - **Artwork Types or Mediums**: Select all the types of artworks or mediums that will be included in your exhibit, such as painting, sculpture, photography, or digital art.
  - **Number of Artists Involved**: Enter the number of artists participating in your proposed exhibit.
  - **Proposed Start Date**: The date you would like to start your exhibit.
  - **Proposed End Date**: The date you would like to end your exhibit.
  - **Duration in Weeks**: Enter the proposed duration of your exhibit.
  - **Square Feet of Space Needed**: Enter the amount of space you will need to display your exhibit.
  - **Wall or Display Requirements**: If you have specific wall or display requirements, describe them here.
  - **Special Technical Requirements**: If you have any special technical requirements, such as lighting or sound, describe them here.
3. Finally, confirm your logistics and promotion:
  - **Artwork Shipping Arrangements**: Choose how you would like to handle artwork shipping arrangements.
  - **Insurance Coverage Needed**: Choose how you would like to handle insurance coverage.
  - **Proposed Marketing and Promotional Ideas**: If you have any proposed marketing and promotional ideas, describe them here.
  - **Estimated Budget for Promotion**: If you have an estimated budget for promotion, enter it here.
