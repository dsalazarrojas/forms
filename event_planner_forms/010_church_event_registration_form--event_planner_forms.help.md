# Church Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for various church events, such as festivals, camps, and retreats. It collects essential information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Participant Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Date of Birth, and Home Address.
2. Select which event you are registering for.
3. Indicate the number of people attending, including yourself.
4. List any additional attendees if applicable.
5. Select the age groups of all attendees.
6. Choose your meal preference, if applicable.
7. List any dietary restrictions or allergies.
8. Select a T-Shirt size if event shirts are included.
9. Indicate if you need transportation assistance.
10. If necessary, provide an emergency contact name and phone number.
11. List any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
12. Select a payment method if a registration fee is required.
13. Indicate how you heard about the event.
14. Provide any special requests or accommodations.
15. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Home Address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Are You a Church Member**: Select the correct answer to indicate your status.
* **Which Event Are You Registering For**: Choose the event you want to attend from the list.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **How Many People Are Registering**: Enter the number of people attending, including yourself.
* **Names of Additional Attendees**: List any family members attending, if applicable.
* **Age Groups of Attendees**: Select the age groups that apply to all attendees.
* **Meal Preference**: Choose your preferred meal option, if applicable.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: List any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select the correct size, if event shirts are provided.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance**: Indicate if you need help with transportation.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone**: Enter an emergency contact name and phone number.
* **Any Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method, if a registration fee is required.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event**: Select how you heard about the event.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations**: Provide any special requests or accommodations.

Note: The form's required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
