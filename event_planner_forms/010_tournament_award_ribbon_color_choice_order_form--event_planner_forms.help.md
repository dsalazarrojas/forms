# Tournament Award Ribbon Color Choice Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from event planners about the tournament award ribbon color choices, payment details, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the tournament in the "Tournament Name" field.
2. Select the desired award ribbon color from the "Award Color" field.
3. Indicate the quantity of winners for the tournament in the "Quantity of Winners" field.
4. Choose the payment type (Bank Transfer, Online Payment, or Cash) in the "Payment Type" field.
5. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
6. Add any additional notes about the payment or order in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter the email address of the recipient in the "Email" field.
8. Indicate whether the payment terms are "Yes" or "No" in the "Payment Terms" field.
9. Confirm the payment terms by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Payment Terms" field.
10. Enter a confirmation email address (if different from the "Email" field) in the "Confirm Email" field.
11. Select a payment method (Option A or Option B) from the "Payment Method" field.
12. Provide details about the payment method in the "Payment Method Details" field.
13. Add any further details about the payment method in the "Payment Method Details 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the tournament.
* **Award Color** (select_one, required: false): Choose the desired award ribbon color.
* **Quantity of Winners** (number, required: false): Indicate the quantity of winners for the tournament.
* **Payment Type** (select_one, required: false): Choose the payment type (Bank Transfer, Online Payment, or Cash).
* **Payment Amount** (number, required: false): Enter the payment amount.
* **Notes** (note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the payment or order.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Payment Terms** (select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the payment terms are "Yes" or "No".
* **Confirm Payment Terms** (select_one, required: false): Confirm the payment terms by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Confirm Email** (email, required: false): Enter a confirmation email address (if different from the "Email" field).
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required: false): Select a payment method (Option A or Option B).
* **Payment Method Details** (text, required: false): Provide details about the payment method.
* **Payment Method Details 2** (text, required: false): Add any further details about the payment method.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email address and payment terms to avoid any issues with communication or payment processing.
