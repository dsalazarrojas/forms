# Halloween Costume Contest Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect votes for a Halloween costume contest. Users will submit their vote by selecting the contestant they want to vote for and providing some personal information. Please ensure that each contestant has only one vote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Contestant Name** (field 1) of the person you want to vote for.
2.  Enter the name of the **Costume** (field 2) you want to vote for.
3.  Choose a number between 1-5 to **Vote** (field 3) for the contestant.
4.  Optionally, add a brief **Description** (field 4) of why you voted for this contestant.
5.  Enter your **Email** (field 5) address for contact purposes.
6.  Enter your **Phone Number** (field 6) for further contact.
7.  Select all the **Contestants** (field 7) you want to vote for, up to a maximum number of 5.
8.  Select a **Voter** (field 8) from the available options, who will be the one casting this vote.
9.  Choose a **Date** (field 9) when you want to cast your vote.
10.  Optionally, select a **Time** (field 10) when you want to cast your vote.
11.  If you are the **Voter** (field 8), you can choose to **Select Voter Email** (field 11) or **Select Voter Phone** (field 12) to receive further contact.
12.  Confirm that you are the **Voter** (field 8) by checking **Select Voter Email** (field 11) or **Select Voter Phone** (field 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contestant Name** (field 1): Enter the name of the person you want to vote for.
*   **Costume Name** (field 2): Enter the name of the costume.
*   **Vote** (field 3): Choose a number between 1-5 to indicate how well you think the contestant deserves to win.
*   **Description** (field 4): Optionally enter a brief description of why you chose this contestant.
*   **Email** (field 5): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (field 6): Enter your phone number for further contact.
*   **Select Contestant** (field 7): Select all the contestants you want to vote for, up to a maximum of 5.
*   **Select Voter** (field 8): Choose who will be casting this vote.
*   **Date** (field 9): Enter the date when you want to cast your vote.
*   **Time** (field 10): Optionally select the time you want to cast your vote.
*   **Select Voter Email** (field 11): If you are the voter, you can select to receive an email.
*   **Select Voter Phone** (field 12): If you are the voter, you can select to receive a phone call.

Note: Some fields are optional, so you can skip them if you do not need to provide that information.
