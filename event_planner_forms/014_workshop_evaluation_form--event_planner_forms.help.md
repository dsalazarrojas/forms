# Workshop Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from workshop attendees on their experience with the workshop. Your input is valuable in helping us improve our events and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions as thoroughly as you can.
2. Use the options provided as guidance for your response. For example, on "What was the overall quality of the workshop?" use the scale from 1 to 5.
3. If you don't have a comment for a particular page, that's okay. Just leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Evaluation Form** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_1`, text, optional): This is the title of the form. You can leave this field blank as it is not required.
* **What was the overall quality of the workshop?** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_2`, select_one, optional): This question assesses your overall satisfaction with the workshop. Choose a number from 1 to 5 where 1 is "not good" and 5 is "excellent".
* **How likely are you to recommend the workshop to others?** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_3`, select_one, optional): This question gauges your enthusiasm for recommending the workshop. Again, choose a number from 1 to 5 where 1 is "not likely" and 5 is "very likely".
* **What did you find most enjoyable about the workshop?** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_4`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply. Select one or more of the options that best describe what you enjoyed most about the workshop.
* **What did you find least enjoyable about the workshop?** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_5`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply. Select one or more of the options that best describe what you did not enjoy most about the workshop.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to add or comment about your experience?** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_6`, note, optional): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Your Name** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_7`, text, optional): Please provide your name so we can address any follow-up questions or comments with you.
* **Your Email** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_8`, email, optional): This is your contact information so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Your Phone** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_9`, text, optional): This is your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Your Comment** (`workshop_evaluation_form_page_10`, note, optional): This is another open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any comments or questions, please contact us at [insert contact info].
