# Social Committee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about social committee events. It helps us to efficiently organize and manage these events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information about the event you are planning or participated in.
2. Make sure to answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Click the "Submit" button to save your changes and finalize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_planner_form_title** (`event_planner_form_title`, text, optional): Enter a descriptive title for the event.
* **event_planner_form_description** (`event_planner_form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **event_planner_form_event_type** (`event_planner_form_event_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the event is a 'Yes' or 'No' event.
* **event_planner_form_event_date** (`event_planner_form_event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **event_planner_form_event_time** (`event_planner_form_event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **event_planner_form_event_location** (`event_planner_form_event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
