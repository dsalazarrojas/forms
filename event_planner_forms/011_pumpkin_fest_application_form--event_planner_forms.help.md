# pumpkin_fest_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to attend "Pumpkin Fest." The form gathers user details, event details, and application data to facilitate event planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Information, including any necessary details that you want to provide.
2. Fill out the Event Information section with any relevant details about the event.
3. Provide Event Details, such as any specific requirements or needs for the event.
4. Enter Application Information, which will help us review and process your application.
5. Finally, Confirm your Information to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, `text`, optional): Provide details about the event, including any specific requirements or requests you have.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the event that we should know.
* **Application Information** (`application_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any details about your application, such as any specific needs or requirements.
* **Confirm Information** (`confirm_info`, `text`, optional): Double-check your entered information to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide complete and accurate information to ensure smooth event planning and organization.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We appreciate your participation in Pumpkin Fest and look forward to reviewing your application.
