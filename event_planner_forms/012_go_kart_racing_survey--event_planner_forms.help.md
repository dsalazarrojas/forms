# Go Kart Racing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Go Kart Racing Survey is designed to gather feedback from customers to improve their experience at the facility. It is a collection of questions that cover various aspects of their visit, from the overall experience to specific facilities such as safety, staff, and equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as you can about your experience.
2. For questions with multiple-choice options, select one or multiple answers as applicable.
3. Do not leave any fields blank unless you truly do not have an opinion or comment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, not required): This section is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read through it to understand the purpose and scope of the questions that follow.
* **How did you feel about your overall experience?** (`overall_experience`, `text`, not required): This question asks about your general feelings and impressions of your visit.
* **How would you rate the condition of the track?** (`track_quality`, `select_one`, not required): Select one of the options to rate the condition of the track, such as Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent.
* **How friendly were the staff during your visit?** (`staff_friendliness`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose one or multiple options to rate the friendliness of the staff, such as Very friendly, Somewhat friendly, or Not very friendly.
* **How would you rate the quality of the equipment?** (`equipment_quality`, `select_one`, not required): Select one of the options to rate the quality of the equipment, such as Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent.
* **How safe do you feel when racing at the facility?** (`safety`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose one or multiple options to rate your feelings of safety, such as Very safe, Somewhat safe, or Not very safe.
* **Did you consume food and beverages at the facility?** (`food_and_beverages`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose one or multiple options to answer this question, Yes or No.
* **How reasonable is the pricing?** (`pricing`, `select_one`, not required): Select one of the options to rate the pricing, such as Too high, Too low, or Just right.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`suggestions`, `text`, not required): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any suggestions or comments for improvement.
* **Is there anything else you would like to add?** (`comments`, `text`, not required): This is another open-ended question where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.
