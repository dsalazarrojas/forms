# Health Institution Speaker Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Proposal Form is a tool used to gather information from speakers who wish to be considered for an event at our health institution. This form will help us understand your proposal, including your title, abstract, topics, experience, and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine if you are interested in proposing for an event ("Speaker Proposal Form" section).
2.  Provide details about the event you would like to propose for ("Event Details" section).
3.  Enter a title and abstract of your proposal ("Proposal Title" and "Proposal Abstract" sections).
4.  List the topics you would like to cover in your proposed event ("Proposal Topics" section).
5.  Specify your relevant experience as a speaker ("Speaker Experience" section).
6.  Select the dates you are available to speak ("Event Dates" section).
7.  Review your submission before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Proposal Form** (select_multiple): 
	+ This field asks if you are interested in proposing for an event. Please select "Yes" if you wish to proceed.
* **Event Details** (text): 
	+ Provide a brief description of the event you would like to propose for. Include details such as the event name, type, and any other relevant information.
* **Speaker Information** (text): 
	+ Enter a brief description of your background and expertise relevant to the proposed event.
* **Proposal Title** (text): 
	+ Enter a title for your proposed event that captures its main theme or topic.
* **Proposal Abstract** (text): 
	+ Write a concise abstract that summarizes the main points of your proposed event.
* **Proposal Topics** (text): 
	+ List the specific topics you plan to cover in your proposed event.
* **Event Dates** (date): 
	+ Enter the dates you are available to speak, including the start and end dates of the event.
* **Speaker Experience** (text): 
	+ Describe your relevant experience as a speaker, including your qualifications and any notable events or presentations you have given in the past.
