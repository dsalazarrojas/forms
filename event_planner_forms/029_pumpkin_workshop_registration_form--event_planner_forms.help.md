# pumpkin_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a workshop registration event. It helps event organizers gather details about the workshop schedule, registrants, and reservation options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Workshop Details" section with the required information about the workshop.
2. Select the desired "Schedule" for the workshop.
3. Enter the number of "Registrants" attending the workshop.
4. Choose the "Reservation" options that apply to your registration.
5. Provide "Registrant Details" if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Details** (`Workshop Details`, `text`, required): This field is where you can enter the details about the workshop, such as its title, description, or any other relevant information.
* **Schedule** (`Schedule`, `text`, required): Select the desired schedule for the workshop from the available options.
* **Registrants** (`Registrants`, `text`, required): Enter the number of people attending the workshop.
* **Reservation** (`Reservation`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the reservation options that apply to your registration. This field allows you to select multiple options from the available choices.
* **Registrant Details** (`Registrant Details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about the registrants, such as their name, email, or contact information.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete registration.
* Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.
