# religious_reunion_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The religious_reunion_registration_form is designed to collect registration information and contact preferences from attendees for a religious event or reunion.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your Full Name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
3.  Choose your preferred contact method from the "Contact Preference" field. This will determine how you would like to be contacted for future updates or notifications.
4.  Select your RSVP status for the event. Choose "Attending" to confirm your presence, "Not Attending" to decline, or "Maybe" to express uncertainty.
5.  Enter any additional comments or messages in the "RSVP Comment" field, if necessary.
6.  Review and adjust your contact preferences as needed.
