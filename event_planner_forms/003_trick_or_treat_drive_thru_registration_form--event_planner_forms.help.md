# trick_or_treat_drive_thru_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration for a Trick or Treat Drive Thru event. It collects contact information and scheduling details for participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trick Or Treat Drive Thru Details** (1, text, required): Enter a brief description of your visit, e.g., "I'm visiting on Halloween night."
* **name** (2, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for event updates.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for event updates.
* **Address** (5, text, required): Enter the address where you will be visiting for the event.
* **City** (6, text, required): Enter the city where you will be visiting for the event.
* **Zip** (7, text, required): Enter the zip code of your visit location.
* **Date** (8, date, required): Select the date you plan to visit for the event.
* **Time** (9, time, required): Select the time you plan to visit for the event.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure correct processing of your submission.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the event organizers.
* Double-check the date and time before submitting to ensure you're on schedule.
