# embroidery_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a waiver form for customers who have placed an order for embroidery services. It is designed to gather information from customers to process their order and communicate with them about the status of their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have ordered an item, enter the order ID in the "Order ID" field.
5. If you can, please enter the date of your order in the "Order Date" field.
6. If you have any notes about your order, you can add them in the "Order Notes" field.
7. If you are a contact person for someone else, fill in their first name in the "First Name 2" field.
8. If you are a contact person for someone else, fill in their last name in the "Last Name 2" field.
9. If you are a contact person for someone else, fill in their email address in the "Email 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, required): Enter the order ID of the item you ordered.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your order if you know it.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any notes about your order if you have any.
* **First Name 2** (`first_name_2`, `text`, optional): If you are a contact person for someone else, enter their first name.
* **Last Name 2** (`last_name_2`, `text`, optional): If you are a contact person for someone else, enter their last name.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `text`, optional): If you are a contact person for someone else, enter their email address.
