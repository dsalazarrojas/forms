# Media Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Media Release Form is used to collect personal information from individuals who are being featured in media events, interviews, or publications. It helps to gather consent and contact details for future communications and collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. If applicable, enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. If you are representing a company, enter the name of your company in the "Company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`media_release_form_1`, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`media_release_form_2`, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Date of Birth** (`media_release_form_3`, Date, Optional): If you would like to provide your date of birth, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`media_release_form_4`, Text, Optional): Enter your residential or business address.
* **Contact Number** (`media_release_form_5`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`media_release_form_6`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Company** (`media_release_form_7`, Text, Optional): If you are representing a company, enter the name of your company.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please reach out to us for clarification.
* You can always contact us for any modifications or updates to this form in the future.
