# Exhibition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibition Application Form is a document that collects information about an artist and their exhibition event. It serves as a means to facilitate a smooth and successful exhibition process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, providing accurate information about yourself and your work.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed. While most fields are not required, it's essential to fill out all relevant details to increase your chances of being selected for the exhibition.
3.  Be sure to attach any supporting documents, such as artist statements, bio, and images of your work.
4.  Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibition Information** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This section is where you can provide a brief description of the exhibition, including the title, date, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, address, and any other contact details that you're comfortable sharing. This information will be used for communication purposes only.
* **Artist Details** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your background and experience as an artist. Share any relevant information about your artistic style, medium, and technique.
* **Art Piece Details** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Describe each art piece you're submitting, including the title, medium, and size. Be sure to mention any notable awards or exhibitions where your work has been featured.
* **Exhibition Details** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): This section is where you can provide additional information about the exhibition, such as location, dates, and any relevant rules or regulations.
* **Event Details** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the event where your art will be displayed, including the date, time, and any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Payment Details** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, provide information about any fees or payments associated with the exhibition, including any discounts or promotions.
* **Artist Statement** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Write a personal statement about yourself and your artistic vision. Share your inspirations, goals, and artistic philosophies.
* **Bio** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): This section is where you can provide a brief biographical sketch of yourself, including your education, training, and artistic background.
* **Artist Website** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Share a link to your personal website or online portfolio.
* **Social Media Links** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): List any social media links where your work can be found or followed.
* **Email** (`12`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for correspondence and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your phone number for any follow-up communication or questions related to your submission.
