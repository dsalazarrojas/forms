# Performance Troupe Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Troupe Collaboration Application Form is designed to gather information from performance troupes that are interested in collaborating with our organization. This form will help us evaluate and potentially collaborate with new troupes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the title of the event you are applying for.
* Provide your contact information, including first and last name, email, and phone number.
* Choose the style of your troupe (Ballet, Contemporary, Hip-Hop, Jazz, or Modern).
* Decide if you have a troupe video and audio presence.
* Enter the number of dancers in your troupe.
* Enter a brief bio of each dancer.
* Upload a URL for your troupe image.
* Decide if you have any specific dancer requirements.
* If applicable, enter any additional audio requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Troupe** (performance_troupe): Enter the name of your performance troupe.
* **Event Name** (event_name): Enter the name of the event you are applying for.
* **Event Date** (event_date): Enter the date of the event you are applying for.
* **Contact First Name** (contact_first_name): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (contact_last_name): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Number of Dancers** (number_of_dancers): Enter the number of dancers in your troupe.
* **Troupe Style** (trroupe_style): Choose the style of your troupe (Ballet, Contemporary, Hip-Hop, Jazz, or Modern).
* **Troupe Video URL** (trroupe_video): Decide if you have a troupe video presence (Yes or No).
* **Troupe Audio URL** (trroupe_audio): Decide if you have a troupe audio presence (Yes or No).
* **Dancer Bio** (bio_dancers): Enter a brief bio of each dancer in your troupe.
* **Troupe Image URL** (trroupe_image): Upload a URL for your troupe image.
* **Specific Dancer** (specific_dancer): Decide if you have any specific dancer requirements (Yes or No).
* **Trroupe Audio 2** (trroupe_audio_2): If applicable, enter any additional audio requirements.
