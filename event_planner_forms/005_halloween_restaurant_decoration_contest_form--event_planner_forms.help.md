# Halloween Restaurant Decoration Contest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a submission form for restaurants to participate in a Halloween decoration contest. To enter, complete the form with the required information about your event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event details on page 1.
2. Enter the location of your event on page 2.
3. Select the date and time of your event on page 3.
4. Describe your event in detail on page 4.
5. Upload an image of your decoration on page 5.
6. Choose up to 3 categories for your event on page 6.
7. Add the URL for your event on page 7 (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Event Details** (`event_planner_forms_page_1`, text, optional): This is the title of your Halloween event. It should be a clear and concise description of your event.
* **Enter Event Location** (`event_planner_forms_page_2`, text, optional): Please enter the location where your event will take place.
* **Enter Event Date** (`event_planner_forms_page_3`, date, optional): Select the date of your Halloween event.
* **Enter Event Time** (`event_planner_forms_page_4`, time, optional): Choose the time of your event.
* **Enter Event Description** (`event_planner_forms_page_5`, text, optional): Describe your Halloween event in detail.
* **Upload Event Image** (`event_planner_forms_page_6`, text, optional): Upload an image of your Halloween decoration setup.
* **Select Categories** (`event_planner_forms_page_7`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to 3 categories for your event from the options provided. These categories will help your event stand out in the contest.
* **Enter Event URL** (`event_planner_forms_page_8`, text, optional): If you have a URL for your event, such as a website or social media page, enter it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Be as specific as possible when describing your event.
- Use relevant categories to help your event get noticed.
- Upload an image that is high-quality and representative of your decoration setup.
