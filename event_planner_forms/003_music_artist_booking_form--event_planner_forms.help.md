# music_artist_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for music industry professionals, event organizers, and artists to book or promote artists for performances, events, or other activities. It gathers essential details about the artist, their contact information, and promotional materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Provide a brief description of the artist in the **Artist Details** field.
*   Enter the artist's official email address in the **Artist Email** field.
*   Add any additional contact information (like phone numbers or social media handles) for the artist in the **Artist Contact Info** field.
*   Share promotional materials (like images or videos) for the artist in the **Artist Promo Info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Artist Details** (`Artist Details`, `text`, required): A brief description of the artist, including their genre, style, or any other relevant details.
*   **Artist Email** (`Artist Email`, `email`, required): The artist's official email address for further communication.
*   **Artist Contact Info** (`Artist Contact Info`, `text`, required): Additional contact information for the artist, such as phone numbers or social media handles.
*   **Artist Promo Info** (`Artist Promo Info`, `text`, required): Promotional materials, like images or videos, related to the artist.

## Tips
*   Ensure the artist's information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Provide clear and sufficient promotional materials to showcase the artist's work.
*   Review and confirm the artist's availability before confirming their booking.
