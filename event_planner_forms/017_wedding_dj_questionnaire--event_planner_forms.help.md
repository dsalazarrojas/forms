# Wedding DJ Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a wedding event's details and create a personalized playlist for the couple.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the correct page (or section) for each field. Each page is clearly labeled with a title.
2. Fill in each field with the relevant information, making sure to answer all required fields.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Reception** (Wedding DJ Questionnaire, id: 6): This field is not a field in the form. I assume it's a service option under "Wedding DJ Services". Please select all the services you would like the DJ to provide during the wedding reception.
* **Event Details** (Event Details, id: 2): Enter a brief description of the event, including any notable details that may be relevant to the wedding.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, id: 3): Enter the date of the wedding event in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Event Time** (Event Time, id: 4): Enter the time of the wedding event in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Event Location** (Event Location, id: 5): Enter the location of the wedding event.
* **Music Selection** (Music Selection, id: 7): Select the type of music you would like the DJ to play during the wedding.
* **Preferred Music Style** (Preferred Music Style, id: 8): Select all the types of music you and your spouse would like the DJ to play during the wedding.
* **Playlist Request** (Playlist Request, id: 9): If you have any specific music requests for the playlist, enter them here.
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, id: 10): If you have any special requests for the wedding, such as specific dances or speeches, enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, id: 11): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, id: 12): Enter your contact name.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, id: 13): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, id: 14): Enter your contact email.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Review your answers before submitting the form for accuracy.
* Be as specific as possible when filling in the fields, especially for the music selection and special requests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at <contact_email>.
