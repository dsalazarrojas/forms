# wedding_caterer_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a referral for a wedding caterer. The goal is to gather details about the event and contact information of the person referring the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the venue where the event will take place.
2. Select the date of the event.
3. Choose the time of the event.
4. Select the type of event (Wedding, Birthday, or Other).
5. Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
6. Enter the location of the event.
7. Provide a brief description of the event.
8. Enter the name of the contact person.
9. Enter the email address of the contact person.
10. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
11. Enter the name of the person referring the event.
12. Enter the phone number of the person referring the event.
13. Enter the email address of the person referring the event.
14. Add any comments or notes about the event.
15. Indicate if you want to follow up on the event.
16. Select the date to follow up on the event.
17. Select the time to follow up on the event.
18. Add any follow up notes about the event.
19. Enter the phone number to follow up with the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Venue** (`venue`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of event (Wedding, Birthday, or Other).
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, `number`, required: false): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person referring the event.
* **Referral Phone** (`referral_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person referring the event.
* **Referral Email** (`referral_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person referring the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments or notes about the event.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you want to follow up on the event (True or False).
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date to follow up on the event.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time to follow up on the event.
* **Follow Up Note** (`follow_up_note`, `text`, required: false): Add any follow up notes about the event.
* **Follow Up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number to follow up with the contact person.
