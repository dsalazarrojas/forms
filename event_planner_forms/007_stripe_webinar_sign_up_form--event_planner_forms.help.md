# stripe_webinar_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to sign up for a webinar hosted by Stripe. It captures the necessary information for the organizers to keep track of the attendees and their roles in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your First name.
2. Fill out your Last name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Company (if applicable).
5. Select your Role in the webinar (Participant, Presenter, or Organizer).
6. Select your Time zone.
7. Select the Start time of the webinar.
8. Select the End time of the webinar.
9. Enter your Timezone (optional, but note that this field may be removed in the future).
10. Enter your Phone number (optional).
11. Provide any additional Notes about yourself or your role in the webinar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`First name`, text, required): Fill out your first name in this text field.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, text, required): Fill out your last name in this text field.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): Enter your company name (if you are representing a company).
* **Role** (`Role`, select one, required): Select your role in the webinar from the options provided (Participant, Presenter, or Organizer).
* **Time zone** (`Time zone`, time, optional): Select your time zone to ensure accurate calculations for the webinar schedule.
* **Start time** (`Start time`, date, required): Select the start time of the webinar.
* **End time** (`End time`, date, required): Select the end time of the webinar.
* **Timezone** (`Timezone`, text, optional): Enter your time zone (note that this field is redundant and may be removed).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself or your role in the webinar.
