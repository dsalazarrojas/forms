# Car Show Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Show Registration Form is designed to gather information from registrants for the car show event. This includes details about the registrant themselves, their vehicle, and their participation preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by filling out the registrant information section**. Ensure you provide accurate details for First name, Last name, Email address, Phone number, Street address, City, and State.
2.  **Enter your vehicle information**. Provide your Vehicle year, Vehicle make, Vehicle model, and Vehicle color. If you're not bringing a vehicle, you can skip this section.
3.  **Choose your participation preferences**. Select which car show you'll be attending and select your type of participation (e.g., Exhibitor, Spectator, etc.).
4.  **Optional fields**: You can indicate if this is your first time attending the show, specify any additional attendees, select activities you're interested in, and mention any dietary restrictions. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### * **First name** (Required)
*   First name: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
*   Example: John

### * **Last name** (Required)
*   Last name: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
*   Example: Doe

### * **Email address** (Required)
*   Email address: Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
*   Example: johndoe@example.com

### * **Phone number** (Required)
*   Phone number: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   Example: 555-123-4567

### * **Phone number** (Required)
*   Phone number: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   Example: 555-123-4567

### * **Street address** (Required)
*   Street address: Enter your street address for contact and logistical purposes.
*   Example: 123 Main Street, Anytown, USA

### * **City** (Required)
*   City: Enter the city where your address is located.
*   Example: Anytown

### * **State** (Required)
*   State: Enter your state of residence.
*   Example: USA

### * **Zip code** (Required)
*   Zip code: Enter your zip code for contact and logistical purposes.
*   Example: 12345

### * **Emergency contact name** (Optional)
*   Emergency contact name: If applicable, enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   Example: Jane Doe

### * **Emergency contact phone** (Optional)
*   Emergency contact phone: If applicable, enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   Example: 555-123-4567

### * **Vehicle year** (Required)
*   Vehicle year: Enter the year of your vehicle.
*   Example: 2009

### * **Vehicle make** (Required)
*   Vehicle make: Enter the make of your vehicle.
*   Example: Ford

### * **Vehicle model** (Required)
*   Vehicle model: Enter the model of your vehicle.
*   Example: Mustang

### * **Vehicle color** (Optional)
*   Vehicle color: Enter the color of your vehicle.
*   Example: Red

### * **License plate number** (Optional)
*   License plate number: If applicable, enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
*   Example: ABC123

### * **Show date** (Required)
*   Show date: Select the date of the car show you'll be attending.
*   Example: 2024-02-22

### * **Participation type** (Required)
*   Participation type: Select your type of participation (e.g., Exhibitor, Spectator, etc.).
*   Example: Exhibitor

### * **Show category** (Optional)
*   Show category: If applicable, select the category of the car show you'll be participating in.

### * **Dietary restrictions** (Optional)
