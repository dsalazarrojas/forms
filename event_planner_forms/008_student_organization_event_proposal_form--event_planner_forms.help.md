# Student Organization Event Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event proposal information from students. Use it to submit your event idea for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your event details.
- Make sure to include all required information, as indicated by the required field labels.
- Review and proofread your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Name
• **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): This field is for the title of your proposed event. Give it a clear and descriptive name that captures the essence of your event.

### Event Description
• **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): In this field, write a detailed description of your event, including its goals, objectives, and key features.

### Event Date
• **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your event is scheduled to take place. Use the date format specified (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).

### Event Time
• **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your event in 24-hour format (e.g., HH:MM). 

### Event Details
• **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about your event. You can add notes, requirements, or other relevant details here.

## Tips
- Double-check your form for accuracy before submitting it.
- Make sure to provide clear and concise information for the reviewers.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your event coordinator or supervisor for guidance.
