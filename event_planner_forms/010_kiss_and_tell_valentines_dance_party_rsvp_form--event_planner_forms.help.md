# Kiss And Tell Valentines Dance Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by attendees to RSVP to the Kiss And Tell Valentines Dance Party. It gathers information to help event planners with event planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting.
* Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
* Enter your contact email in the "Email" field.
* Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
* In the "Attending with" field, select all the people you are attending with, if any.
* If you don't have plans, select "No" in the "RSVP" field.
* Optionally, include a message or note in the "Message" field, if you'd like.
* Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Attending with** (`attending_with`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the people you are attending with, such as "Spouse", "Friend", or "Colleague".
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Note: This field is currently not used for this form.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Note: This field is currently not used for this form.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you are attending the party, otherwise select "No".
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, optional): Optionally, include a message or note for the event planners.
* **Form** (`form_id`, `number`, optional): This field is currently not used for this form. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields. 
* If you have any questions or concerns about the event, feel free to include it in the "Message" field. 
* The event planners will be in touch with you with further information regarding the event. 
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact the event planners for clarification.
