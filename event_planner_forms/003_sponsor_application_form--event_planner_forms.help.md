# Sponsor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for event planners to submit information about upcoming events they are hosting. It includes details about the event, including date, time, location, and sponsorship level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your sponsor name and contact information (email and phone number).
3. Select the type of sponsor you are (Event Planner, Nonprofit, or Sports Team).
4. Choose your sponsorship level (Bronze, Silver, or Gold).
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about the event.
6. Submit the form to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sponsor Name
* **Sponsor Name (text, optional):** Enter the name of the event planner or organization submitting the application.
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email (email, required):** Enter the email address of the event planner or organization's contact person.
### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone (text, optional):** Enter the phone number of the event planner or organization's contact person.
### Company Name
* **Company Name (text, optional):** Enter the name of the company or organization hosting the event.
### Company Representative
* **Company Representative (text, optional):** Enter the name of the person representing the company or organization.
### Job Title
* **Job Title (text, optional):** Enter the title of the person representing the company or organization.
### Sponsor Type
* **Sponsor Type (select_one, required):** Choose the type of sponsor you are (Event Planner, Nonprofit, or Sports Team).
### Event Date
* **Event Date (date, required):** Enter the date of the event.
### Event Time
* **Event Time (time, optional):** Enter the time of the event.
### Event Location
* **Event Location (text, optional):** Enter the location of the event.
### Event Description
* **Event Description (text, optional):** Enter a brief description of the event.
### Event Link
* **Event Link (text, optional):** Enter a link to the event's website or registration page.
### Sponsorship Level
* **Sponsorship Level (select_multiple, required):** Choose the sponsorship level you are applying for (Bronze, Silver, or Gold).
### Notes
* **Notes (note, optional):** Enter any additional notes or comments about the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [insert contact info].
