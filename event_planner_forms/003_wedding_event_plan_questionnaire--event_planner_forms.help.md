# wedding_event_plan_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about the wedding event to help wedding coordinators and planners plan and coordinate the event successfully.

## How To Complete This Form

Please complete this form by following these steps:

1. Enter the required information for each field.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you are providing accurate details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Date (1)** (`Wedding Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the wedding event.
* **Wedding Location (2)** (`wedding_location`, text, required: false): Type the location of the wedding event, including the venue and any other relevant details.
* **Package Details (3)** (`package_details`, text, required: false): Describe the package or details of the event.
* **Contact Details (4)** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Provide any contact information for the couple or their families.
* **People to Contact (5)** (`people_to_contact`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if there are any specific people that the wedding coordinator should contact during the planning process.
* **Wedding Coordinator (6)** (`wedding_coordinator`, text, required: false): Provide information about the wedding coordinator or contact person.
* **Family Situations (7)** (`family_situations`, text, required: false): Describe any family situations or dynamics that the wedding coordinator should be aware of.
* **Photography Details (8)** (`photography_details`, text, required: false): Share details about the photography plans and requirements.
* **Logistics Details (9)** (`logistics_details`, text, required: false): Provide information about the logistics and transportation arrangements.
* **Meal Details (10)** (`meal_details`, text, required: false): Describe the meal plans and preferences.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately to help the wedding coordinator understand the event details.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or seek help from the wedding coordinator.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required and optional information is provided.
