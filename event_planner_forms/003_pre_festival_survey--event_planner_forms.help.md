# Pre Festival Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from event planners and participants about their experience with the festival. The collected data will be used to analyze and improve the event in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role as an event planner or participant from the drop-down menu under "event_planner_name".
2. Enter a number between 1 and 10 in the field "participant_number" to indicate how many participants you are responsible for.
3. Select any of the Covid-19 concerns that apply to you in the field "covid_concerns".
4. Enter your contact number in the field "contact_number".
5. Enter your email address in the field "email".
6. If necessary, select a date and time for the event in the field "date" and "time".
7. Add any additional comments in the field "note".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_planner_name**:
	+ (Select One)
	+ This field is for selecting your role as an event planner or participant.
* **participant_number**:
	+ (Number)
	+ Enter the number of participants you are responsible for.
* **covid_concerns**:
	+ (Select Multiple)
	+ Check the boxes for any of the Covid-19 concerns that apply to you.
* **contact_number**:
	+ (Text)
	+ Enter a contact number that we can reach you at.
* **email**:
	+ (Email)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **date**:
	+ (Date)
	+ Enter a date for the event, if it's already known.
* **time**:
	+ (Time)
	+ Enter a time for the event, if it's already known.
* **note**:
	+ (Note)
	+ Add any additional comments about your experience with the festival.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us at [your email address].
* If you've already submitted this form, don't fill it out again.
