# School Supply Giveaway Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Supply Giveaway Registration Form is designed to collect information from students or their parents/guardians for the school supply giveaway program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Registration Information** page.
2. On this page, you will be asked for some basic details such as [insert no details as there are none in the YAML inputSchema]. Just submit this page.
3. Once you've submitted the first page, you will be taken to the **Supply Details** page.
4. On this page, you will be asked to provide more details about the school supply giveaway, such as [insert no details as there are none in the YAML inputSchema].
5. After submitting the **Supply Details** page, you will be taken to the **Additional Info** page.
6. On this page, you will be asked for any additional information about yourself or the student you are registering for.
7. After submitting the **Additional Info** page, you will be taken to the **Confirmation** page.
8. On this page, you will see a summary of your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (Page 1, Optional):
	+ This page is the starting point of the form.
	+ You will be able to provide some basic details about yourself, but it is not specified.
* **Supply Details** (Page 2, Optional):
	+ This page is intended for providing more information about the school supply giveaway.
	+ You can provide details about the supplies you are giving away.
* **Additional Info** (Page 3, Optional):
	+ This page is for providing more information about yourself or the student you are registering.
	+ You can add any additional information you feel is relevant.
* **Confirmation** (Page 4, Optional):
	+ This is the last page of the form.
	+ You can review your registration information and submit it.

## Tips

* Please ensure that each registration is unique and not duplicated.
* The form is designed to be simple and easy to use.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact form@gic.mx.
