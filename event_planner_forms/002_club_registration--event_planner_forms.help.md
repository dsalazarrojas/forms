# Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for club membership, providing essential details for the club management to process your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2.  Provide the name of the club you wish to join or register.
3.  Select the membership start date from the calendar.
4.  Choose the membership duration from the dropdown menu.
5.  Select the member status (New Member, Renewal, or Upgrade).
6.  In the Events and Activities section, choose all the activities you are interested in. This will help the club plan events and activities tailored to your preferences.
7.  In the Payment Information section, select the correct payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash) and update the payment status.
8.  Enter the total membership fee and ensure the payment status is updated accordingly.
9.  In the Terms and Conditions section, review the terms and conditions of the club and select "I Agree" if you accept them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Include your area code in the phone number.
* **Club Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the club you wish to join or register.
* **Membership Start Date** (date, required): Select the date you wish to start your membership.
* **Membership Duration** (select_one, required): Choose the duration of your membership from 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year, or 2 Years.
* **Member Status** (select_one, required): Select New Member, Renewal, or Upgrade as your member status.
* **Interested in Activities** (select_multiple, optional): Select all the activities you are interested in, such as Social gatherings, Sports activities, Educational seminars, Volunteer opportunities, or Networking events.
* **Total Membership Fee** (number, required): Enter the total membership fee.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select the payment method you prefer, such as Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash.
* **Payment Status** (select_one, required): Update the payment status to Paid, Pending, or Payment Plan.
* **I Agree to the Club Terms and Conditions** (select_one, required): Select "I Agree" if you understand and accept the terms and conditions of the club.
