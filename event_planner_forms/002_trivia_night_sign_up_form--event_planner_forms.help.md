# Trivia Night Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Trivia Night Sign Up Form designed to collect participant information for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and correctly.
2. Make sure to provide a valid email address.
3. You can choose your participant name, email, phone number, address, and additional notes as required or optional fields.
4. For the "Trivia Level" field, select your preferred difficulty level.
5. You can select multiple teams that you want to be part of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Participant Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with you.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address (optional).
* **Participant Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about yourself or your team (optional).
* **Team Members**: Select all teams you want to be part of.
* **Trivia Level**: Choose your preferred trivia level, which determines the difficulty of questions you can answer.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required fields correctly.
* Ensure that your email address is valid and not used in multiple submissions.
* If you have any questions, contact the event organizers.
