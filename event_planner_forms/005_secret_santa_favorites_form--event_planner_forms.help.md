# Secret Santa Favorites Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know your preferences for different aspects of life, such as food, music, movies, books, and more. Your input will help us tailor the Secret Santa gift-giving experience to your tastes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. For each field, choose an option that best fits your preference.
3. If a field has no options that fit your preference, select "N/A" or "Other (please specify)".
4. Enter your answers for each field, and submit the form.
