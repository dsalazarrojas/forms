# holiday_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The holiday survey form is designed to gather information about users' travel plans, preferences, and requirements for an upcoming event or vacation. This form helps event organizers or travel planners tailor the experience to users' needs or gather insights for future planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the holiday survey form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, if applicable.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your name or any other identifier to help us get in touch with you about your travel plans.
* **travel dates** (`travel_dates`, select multiple, required/optional): Please select all the travel dates that apply to your plans. You can choose multiple dates if your travel spans multiple days or weeks.
* **travel type** (`travel_type`, select one, required/optional): Select the type of travel that best describes your plans (e.g., business, leisure, adventure).
* **travel location** (`travel_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the destination(s) you plan to visit or travel to. If you have multiple locations, separate them with commas.
* **special requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): Share any special requests or requirements you have for your travel experience.
* **budget** (`budget`, number, required/optional): Enter your budget for the travel or event.
* **travel duration** (`travel_duration`, number, required/optional): Estimate the duration of your travel or stay.
* **accommodation** (`accommodation`, select one, required/optional): Choose the type of accommodation you prefer or need (e.g., hotel, Airbnb, camping).
* **transportation** (`transportation`, select multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you have specific transportation needs or preferences (e.g., yes for wheelchair access).
* **special requests 2** (`special_requests_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional special requests or requirements you have for your travel experience.
