# Visitor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from visitors on a specific event. This form helps us understand your experience and gather suggestions for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a clear and concise manner.
2. Answer all the required fields marked as such in the form.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, text, required): This is the main field where you can describe your overall experience with the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Please select how you attended the event, e.g., 'Yes' for attending, 'No' for not attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you visited.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, required): Select the time of day you visited the event.
* **Staff Member Encountered** (`staff_member_encountered`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you encountered any staff members during your visit, e.g., 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no.
* **Staff Member Rating** (`staff_member_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the staff member's performance, e.g., 'Good' for good, 'Fair' for fair.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about your overall experience, please include them here.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Do you have any suggestions for us? This is a great place to share them.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, email, optional): If you'd like to contact us directly, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd prefer to contact us via phone, please enter your number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Is there anything else you'd like to share about your experience?
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): If you feel comfortable providing a numerical rating, feel free to do so.
* **Agree/Disagree** (agree_disagree, select_one, optional): A series of optional questions to gauge your agreement or disagreement with certain statements.
* **Staff Member Rating Scale** (`staff_member_rating_scale`, number, optional): If you'd like to provide a numerical rating for the staff member's performance.
