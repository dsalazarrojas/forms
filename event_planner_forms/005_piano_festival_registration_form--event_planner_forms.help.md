# Piano Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register their information and preferences for the Piano Festival. It collects details such as participant name, email, phone number, address, city, state, zip, festival date, notes, and category selection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling in your participant details**:
   - Fill in your name in the "Participant Name" field.
   - Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field. Make sure it's a valid email address.
2. **Provide your contact information**:
   - Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
   - Enter your address in the "Participant Address" field.
   - Enter your city in the "Participant City" field.
   - Select your state from the drop-down menu in the "Participant State" field.
   - Enter your zip code in the "Participant Zip" field.
3. **Select your festival date and category**:
   - Select the date of the festival you'd like to participate in from the "Piano Festival Date" field.
   - Choose your participant category from the drop-down menu in the "Participant Category" field.
4. **Add any additional notes**:
   - If you have any additional information or notes about your participation, enter them in the "Participant Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it is written on your identification card or how you would like to be addressed.
* **Participant Email**: A valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Participant Phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Participant Address**: Your full address including street, number, and postcode.
* **Participant City**: The city where you reside.
* **Participant State**: Select your state from the provided options.
* **Participant Zip**: Your postal code or zip code.
* **Piano Festival Date**: Select the date of the festival you'd like to participate in.
* **Participant Notes**: Any additional information or notes about your participation.
* **Participant Category**: Choose a category that best describes your participation, such as Category 1, Category 2, or Category 3.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for any errors.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, include them in the "Participant Notes" field.
