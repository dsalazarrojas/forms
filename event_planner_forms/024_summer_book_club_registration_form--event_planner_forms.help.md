# Summer Book Club Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Book Club Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants interested in joining a summer book club. This form will help us get to know you better and tailor the book club experience to your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field. Please use the international format (e.g., 1234567890).
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Provide your address in the "address" field.
5. If you have a child, enter their name in the "child_name" field.
6. Choose your gender from the "gender" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your age in the "age" field.
8. Share your interests in the "interests" field.
9. List any books or authors you're particularly interested in reading in the "wish_list" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is your name that will be used to address communications from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required): Please use the international format (e.g., 1234567890).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address will be used to communicate with you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Your physical address, where you can receive any materials or packages.
* **Child's Name (if applicable)** (`child_name`, text, optional): If you have a child, please list their name.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Please choose your gender from the dropdown menu.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Your age.
* **Interests** (`interests`, text, optional): Share any interests or hobbies that you enjoy.
* **Wish List** (`wish_list`, text, optional): List any books or authors you are interested in reading.
