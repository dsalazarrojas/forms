## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an event, including artist and event details, for the purpose of event promotion or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about the event.
2. Select the correct event type from the options provided.
3. Enter the date and time of the event.
4. Provide details about the event, including the location and any other relevant information.

6. Submit the form to complete the submission process.
