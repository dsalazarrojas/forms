# Whitelist Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Whitelist Registration Form is used to register a user to a whitelist, which is a list of allowed users for a particular application or system.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by following the instructions in the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.
2. Review your form data before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This field is for entering your email address. Please use a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your phone number.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, `text`, required): This field is for stating the reason why you are requesting to be whitelisted.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in order to submit the form. 

## Tips

* Please fill the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your registration request.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact the support team for assistance.
