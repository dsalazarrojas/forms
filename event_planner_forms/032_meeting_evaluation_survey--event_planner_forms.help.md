# Meeting Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from participants after a meeting. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
- Review the form carefully before providing your answers.
- Fill out the form with accurate and honest information.
- Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the form, where you can provide any additional context about the meeting.
* **Meeting Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the meeting being evaluated. 
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting being evaluated. 
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting being evaluated. 
* **Rating**: This is where you can rate your overall experience of the meeting on a scale from Very Good to Very Poor. 
* **Evaluation**: Provide a detailed written evaluation of the meeting, including what went well and what didn't. 
* **Suggestions**: Suggest any improvements or new ideas for future meetings. 
* **Action Items**: List any action items that you would like to see taken as a result of the meeting evaluation. 
* **Next Meeting**: Provide any information about the next meeting, including the date and time. 
* **Meeting Follow-up**: Provide any further comments or information about the meeting that you think is important. 
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the meeting that are not covered in the other fields. 
* **Assigned Tool**: If a tool was used during the meeting, enter its name here.
