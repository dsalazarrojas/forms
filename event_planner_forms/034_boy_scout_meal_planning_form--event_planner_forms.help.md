# Boy Scout Meal Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boy Scout Meal Planning Form is designed to help you plan and organize meals for your troop during a campout. It guides you in preparing and managing the resources needed for each meal, including ingredients, cooking equipment, and supplies. This form is intended to be filled out by the scout responsible for meal planning, with optional sections for assistant planners and scoutmaster approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the troop number and patrol name at the top.
2.  Enter the campout name and dates (start and end dates) in the corresponding fields.
3.  Enter the number of scouts and adults attending the campout.
4.  Enter the name of the scout responsible for meal planning and their assistant planner (if applicable).
5.  List the menu for each meal (Saturday breakfast, lunch, dinner, and Sunday breakfast).
6.  List any dietary restrictions and food allergies in the troop.
7.  List all ingredients needed for each meal.
8.  List the cooking method and equipment needed for each meal.
9.  Plan for water treatment and water source.
10.  Plan for trash disposal.
11.  Enter the estimated cost per scout and total estimated cost for all meals.
12.  Enter who is responsible for shopping and list the items to be purchased.
13.  Plan for food storage.
14.  Enter the scoutmaster name for approval and the date of approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Troop Number** (`troop_number`, `text`, required): Enter your Boy Scout troop number.
*   **Patrol Name** (`patrol_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your patrol.
*   **Campout Name** (`campout_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name or description of the campout.
*   **Campout Start Date** (`campout_date_start`, `date`, required): Enter the first day of the campout.
*   **Campout End Date** (`campout_date_end`, `date`, required): Enter the last day of the campout.
*   **Number of Scouts** (`number_of_scouts`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of scouts attending.
*   **Number of Adults** (`number_of_adults`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of adults attending.
*   **Meal Planner Name** (`meal_planner_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the scout responsible for meal planning.
*   **Assistant Meal Planner** (`assistant_planner_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the assistant planner (if applicable).
*   **Saturday Breakfast** (`saturday_breakfast`, `text`, required): Enter the menu for Saturday breakfast.
*   **Saturday Lunch** (`saturday_lunch`, `text`, required): Enter the menu for Saturday lunch.
*   **Saturday Dinner** (`saturday_dinner`, `text`, required): Enter the menu for Saturday dinner.
*   **Sunday Breakfast** (`sunday_breakfast`, `text`, required): Enter the menu for Sunday breakfast.
*   **Sunday Lunch** (`sunday_lunch`, `text`, optional): Enter the menu for Sunday lunch (if applicable).
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, required): List any dietary restrictions in the troop.
*   **Food Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): List any food allergies in the troop.
*   **Breakfast Ingredients** (`breakfast_ingredients`, `text`, required): List all ingredients needed for breakfasts.
*   **Lunch Ingredients** (`lunch_ingredients`, `text`, required): List all ingredients needed for lunches.
*   **Dinner Ingredients** (`dinner_ingredients`, `text`, required): List all ingredients needed for dinners.
*   **Snacks Needed** (`snacks_needed`, `text`, required): List snacks and trail food needed.
*   **Condiments Needed** (`condiments_needed`, `text`, required): List condiments and seasonings needed.
*   **Beverages Needed** (`beverages_needed`, `text`, required): List all beverages needed.
*   **Cooking Equipment Needed** (`cooking_equipment`, `text`, required): List cooking equipment needed.
*   **Cleanup Supplies** (`cleanup_supplies`, `text`, required): List soap, sponges, towels, etc. needed for cleanup.
*   **Water Source** (`water_source`, `select_one`, required): Choose where you will get water from.
*   **Water Treatment Needed** (`water_treatment`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you need to treat water.
*   **Cooking Method** (`cooking_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you will cook meals.
*   **Fuel Needed** (`fuel_needed`, `text`, optional): Enter the amount of propane or fuel needed (if applicable).
*   **Estimated Cost Per Scout** (`estimated_cost_per_scout`, `text`, required): Enter the approximate cost per person.
*   **Total Estimated Cost** (`total_estimated_cost`, `text`, required): Enter the total cost for all meals.
*   **Shopping Assignments** (`shopping_assignments`, `text`, required): Enter who is responsible for shopping and list the items to be purchased.
*   **Food Storage Plan** (`food_storage_plan`, `text`, required): Enter how food will be stored safely.
*   **Trash Disposal Plan** (`trash_disposal_plan`, `text`, required): Enter how trash will be handled.
*   **Scoutmaster Approval** (`scoutmaster_approval`, `text`, required): Enter the scoutmaster name for approval.
*   **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of approval.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate meal planning and resource management.
*   Use the form as a guide to stay organized and on track with meal planning and food preparation.
*   Be considerate of dietary restrictions and food allergies when planning menus.
*   Double-check the menu and resources needed for each meal before heading out to the campout.
