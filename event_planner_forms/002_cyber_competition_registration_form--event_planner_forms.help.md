# Cyber Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect registration information from participants in a cyber competition.

## How To Complete This Form

Before filling out the form, please read through the following explanations for each field to ensure accurate and efficient completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form Name** (`registration_form_name`, text, required): Enter a name for your team or the name you'd like to use for this competition. This will help us identify your team for any communication during the event.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address where you can be reached for competition updates and information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during the competition.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required): Indicate the number of people participating in your team. This will help us ensure proper planning and arrangements.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, enter their name here.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email_2`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Tertiary Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone_3`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your tertiary emergency contact.
* **Tertiary Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email_3`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your tertiary emergency contact.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, optional): Enter your primary address for the competition. This will help us plan for any on-site arrangements.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional address details if needed.
* **Address 3** (`address_3`, text, optional): Enter any other address details if required.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you will be participating in the competition.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where you will be participating in the competition.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of your address for the competition.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, select_one, required): Select the event ID for the competition you are registering for. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Other Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person with a different relationship, describe it here.
* **Other Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone_relationship_other`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your other emergency contact.
* **Other Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email_relationship_other`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your other emergency contact.
* **Other Information** (`other_info`, text, optional): Enter any other information you think is relevant for us to know about you or your team.

By reading through these explanations, you should be well-prepared to fill out this form accurately and efficiently. If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to reach out to the competition organizers for assistance.
