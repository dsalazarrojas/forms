# Event Menu Preferences Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user preferences for events. It is intended for users to select their preferred menu items for an event.

### thinking>
This form is for users to express their dietary, drink, and other preferences for an event. Please ensure you are completing this form for a specific event. If you have already completed this form for the same event, you may not need to fill it out again.
### </thinking>

### How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Each page of the form corresponds to a specific set of questions.
2. On each page, please provide your answers for the corresponding questions.
3. The questions are simple text fields where you can provide your answer.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Menu Preferences Form Page 1 Label 1** (Text, Optional): Please type in your preferred menu item for this event.
* **Event Menu Preferences Form Page 2 Label 2** (Text, Optional): Enter your preference for the next menu item.
* **Event Menu Preferences Form Page 3 Label 3** (Text, Optional): Provide your preference for this menu item.
* **Event Menu Preferences Form Page 4 Label 4** (Text, Optional): Please enter your preferred option for this menu item.
* **Event Menu Preferences Form Page 5 Label 5** (Text, Optional): Select your preference for this menu item.
* **Event Menu Preferences Form Page 6 Label 6** (Text, Optional): Type in your preferred menu item for this event.
* **Event Menu Preferences Form Page 7 Label 7** (Text, Optional): Enter your answer for this menu item.
* **Event Menu Preferences Form Page 8 Label 8** (Text, Optional): Please provide your preference for this menu item.

### Tips
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure of a question, just provide your best guess.
* You can skip questions if you're not sure or don't have a preference.
* Please submit the form once you've answered all the questions.
