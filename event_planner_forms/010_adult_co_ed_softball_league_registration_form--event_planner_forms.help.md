# Adult Co Ed Softball League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for the Adult Co-Ed Softball League. It is used to register players, gather necessary details, and manage the league's operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read through each field carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3.  Review the information for errors or missing fields before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **-- Team Information --** (`Team Information`, `note`, required: false):
    Team Information is a label for the page. This page serves as an introduction to the form, explaining that the following fields are for team information.
*   **Player full name** (`player_name`, `text`, required: true):
    Please enter your full name, including your first and last name.
*   **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: true):
    Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
*   **Team name** (`team_name`, `text`, required: true):
    Enter your team name or write "Free Agent" if you are not part of a team.
*   **Position preference** (`position_preference`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Select your primary playing position. Options include Pitcher, Catcher, Infield, Outfield, and Any.
*   **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: true):
    Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
*   **Liability waiver agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, `select_one`, required: true):
    You must agree to the liability waiver to participate in the league.
*   **Jersey size** (`jersey_size`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Choose your preferred jersey size. Options include XS, S, M, L, XL, and XXL.
*   **Payment received** (`payment_received`, `select_one`, required: false):
    This field is for the league organizers to confirm payment status.
*   **Special needs or medical conditions** (`special_needs`, `text`, required: false):
    If you have any special needs or medical conditions, please provide details here.
