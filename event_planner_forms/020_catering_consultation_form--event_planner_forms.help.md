# Catering Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help our team understand your event planning needs so we can provide the best catering services for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details (fields 1-4).
2. Select your event details (fields 5-8).
3. Choose your event specifics (fields 9-10).
4. Specify your event needs (fields 11-14).
5. Select your catering preferences (fields 15-18).
6. If you have any questions or concerns, use field 19.
7. Provide any additional information in field 20.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (Client Name, text, required): Fill in your name.
* **Email Address** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, optional): Enter your company or organization name (if applicable).
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (Consultation Date, date, required): Choose the date you would like to schedule a consultation.
* **Preferred Consultation Time** (Consultation Time, time, required): Select the time that suits you best.
* **Consultation Method** (Consultation Method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred method of consultation (in-person, phone, video, or email).
* **Event Type** (Event Type, select_one, required): Select the type of event you are planning (wedding, corporate event, birthday, etc.).
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time** (Event Time, time, optional): Enter the time of your event (if known).
* **Venue Name** (Venue Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the venue (if known).
* **Venue Address** (Venue Address, text, optional): Enter the complete address of the venue (if known).
* **Minimum Guest Count** (Minimum Guest Count, number, required): Enter the minimum number of guests attending your event.
* **Maximum Guest Count** (Maximum Guest Count, number, optional): Enter the maximum number of guests you anticipate (if known).
* **Budget Range** (Budget Range, select_one, required): Choose your estimated budget per person (under $25, $25-$50, etc.).
* **Preferred Service Style** (Preferred Service Style, select_one, optional): Select your preferred way of having food served (buffet, plated, family style, etc.).
* **Cuisine Preferences** (Cuisine Preferences, text, optional): Specify any type of food you particularly like or dislike.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Dietary Restrictions, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions or needs.
* **Alcohol Service Needed** (Alcohol Service Needed, select_one, optional): Choose if you need bar service (yes, beer and wine, no, unsure).
* **Rental Needs** (Rental Needs, select_multiple, optional): Select any rental items you might need (tables, chairs, linens, etc.).
* **Previous Caterer** (Previous Caterer, select_one, optional): Check if you have used a caterer before (yes, once, no).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (How Did You Hear About Us, select_one, optional): Select the source of your referral (website, social media, advertisement, etc.).
* **Questions or Concerns** (Questions or Concerns, text, optional): Enter any questions or concerns you might have.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Leave any additional information or comments.
