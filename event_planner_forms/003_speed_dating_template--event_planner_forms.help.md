# Speed Dating Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speed Dating Template is designed to help event planners organize and manage speed dating events. This form is intended for participants to provide their information, preferences, and feedback about their experiences with potential meeting partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name on the "participant name" field.
2. Enter your date and time on the "participant date and time" field.
3. Choose a meeting duration from the options: 10, 15, or 20 minutes.
4. Select a meeting location from the options: home, office, or park.
5. Choose a meeting topic from the options: Topic 1, Topic 2, or Topic 3.
6. If desired, add any additional comments or preferences in the "participant comment" and "meeting comment" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant name** (participant_name, text, required): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Date and Time** (participant_datetime, date, required): Choose the date and time you are available to meet.
* **Meeting Duration** (meeting_duration, select_one, required): Select the duration of your meeting, which can be 10, 15, or 20 minutes.
* **Meeting Location** (meeting_location, select_multiple, optional): Choose where you'd like to meet, which can be at home, office, or park.
* **Meeting Topic** (meeting_topic, select_one, required): Select a topic you're interested in discussing during your meeting.
* **Participant Preference** (participant_preference, text, optional): Add any additional comments or preferences about your experience.
* **Participant Comment** (participant_comment, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your meeting experience.
* **Meeting Comment** (meeting_comment, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the meeting process.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate event planning.
* Be as specific as possible when choosing your meeting location and topic to help with pairing.
* Use the "participant comment" and "meeting comment" fields to provide feedback and suggestions for future event improvements.
