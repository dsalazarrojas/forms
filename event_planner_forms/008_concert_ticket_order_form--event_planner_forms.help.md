# Concert Ticket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for concert ticket orders from customers. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure efficient processing and delivery of tickets.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your **Buyer name** field, which is your name that will be associated with the order.
2. Enter your **Contact email** address for order confirmation and updates.
3. If you want to be contacted via phone, add your **Phone number**. This is optional but helps with last-minute updates.
4. Select the type of ticket you want to purchase (**Ticket type**):
	* General admission
	* VIP
	* Early bird
	* Backstage
5. Specify the **Quantity of tickets** you wish to order.
6. Choose your **Seating preference**:
	* Aisle
	* Center
	* Wheelchair accessible
7. Choose your **Payment method**:
	* Credit card
	* PayPal
	* Bank transfer
8. If you have a **Promo code**, enter it here to receive your discount.
9. Select your **Delivery method**:
	* E-ticket (electronic ticket)
	* Mail
	* Pickup at venue
10. If you have any **Additional requests or special instructions**, type them in this field for our attention.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer name**: Enter the name you want to be associated with the order. This is your identity on the order.
* **Contact email**: Your email address for order updates and confirmations.
* **Phone number**: Your contact phone number, optional but helpful for last-minute updates.
* **Ticket type**: Choose the type of ticket that suits your needs.
* **Quantity of tickets**: Number of tickets you want to order.
* **Seating preference**: Select where you'd like to sit during the concert.
* **Payment method**: Select how you'd like to pay for your tickets.
* **Promo code**: Enter any available promo code for a discount.
* **Delivery method**: Choose how you'd like to receive your tickets.
* **Additional requests**: Any special instructions or messages you'd like to convey with your order.
