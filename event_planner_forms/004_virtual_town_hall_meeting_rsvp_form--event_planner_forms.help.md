# Virtual Town Hall Meeting RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees of the Virtual Town Hall Meeting. It allows attendees to confirm their attendance, provide their role, and leave comments and questions. The form also includes fields for collecting phone numbers, comments, and RSVP methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and role in the corresponding fields.
2. Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Attendance" field.
3. If you have any comments or questions, type them in the "Comments" field.
4. If you have any phone numbers or other relevant information, type it in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your preferred RSVP method (Web, Phone, or Mail) in the "RSVP Method" field.
6. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required/optional): Select your role as an Attendee, Speaker, Admin, or Other.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, required/optional): Type any comments you have about the meeting.
* **Confirm Attendance** (`confirm_attendance`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Type any additional comments you have.
* **Questions** (`questions`, note, required/optional): Type any questions you have about the meeting.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your attendance.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, text, required/optional): Type a list of other attendees if you are coming with others.
* **RSVP Method** (`rsvp_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred RSVP method as Web, Phone, or Mail.
