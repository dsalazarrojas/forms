# Secret Santa Gift Exchange Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Secret Santa Gift Exchange Form is designed to collect information from participants in a Secret Santa gift exchange. This form helps us to create a fun and enjoyable experience for everyone involved by ensuring that each participant's preferences and gift ideas are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields to provide your details and gift preferences.
2. Choose the type of gift you would like to receive.
3. Enter the value of the gift you would like to receive.
4. Add any gift ideas or suggestions you have.
5. Review and submit your form.
