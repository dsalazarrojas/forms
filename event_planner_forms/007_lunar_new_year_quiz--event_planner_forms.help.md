# Lunar New Year Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user input on the Lunar New Year celebration. It will help us understand how you feel about the Lunar New Year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the questions carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. Fill in the requested information for the "Date of Birth" and "Time of Birth" fields.
3. Choose your preferred category from the options provided in the "Category" field.
4. Select whether you would like to submit your answers or not.
5. Optionally, provide your email address for further communication.
6. Enter your phone number for any additional contact.
7. Click "Submit" to finalize your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form.
* **question one** (`question_one`, `text`, required: false): Respond to the first question about the Lunar New Year. Please answer as concisely as possible.
* **question two** (`question_two`, `text`, required: false): Respond to the second question about the Lunar New Year. Please answer as concisely as possible.
* **question three** (`question_three`, `text`, required: false): Respond to the third question about the Lunar New Year. Please answer as concisely as possible.
* **date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD.
* **time of birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required: false): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format.
* **Category** (`category_choice`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more categories that you identify with:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you would like to submit your answers:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for any additional contact.

## Tips
- Please respond to all questions to help us better understand your feelings about the Lunar New Year.
- You can provide your email and phone number if you want to get further information about the celebration.
- If you do not want to provide your contact information, it's okay! Just click "No" on the "Submit" field.
