# super_bowl_party_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Super Bowl party RSVP form is designed to collect information from individuals about their interest in attending a Super Bowl party and any additional details they'd like to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields with your relevant information. Make sure to review each field for its intended purpose and provide accurate details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Details**: Enter a brief description of your planned party or any specific details you'd like to share with the host.
* **Name**: Please enter your name as you'd like it to be referred to by the host and other attendees.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the host can communicate with you about party details.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number so the host can contact you in case of last-minute updates or changes.
* **Attending**: Select 'Yes' if you plan to attend the party, and 'No' if you cannot attend.
* **Guests**: Indicate if you plan to bring a guest(s) to the party, selecting 'Yes' for each guest attending.
* **Message**: Add any additional comments or messages you'd like to share with the host.
* **Party Details 1**: Enter any additional party details you'd like to share with the host.
