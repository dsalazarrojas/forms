### free-dj-contract - Help Guide

**Purpose**
The free-dj-contract form is designed to collect information for planning events such as DJ performances. This form helps event organizers gather essential details from the DJ, including their name, client's name, event date, start and end times, venue, and payment details.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **DJ Name** in the designated field. This is the name of the DJ who will be performing at the event.
2.  Enter the **Client Name** in the designated field. This is the name of the person or organization booking the DJ's services.
3.  Select the **Event Date** for the performance. This is the date on which the event will take place.
4.  Enter the **Start Time** for the event. This is the time when the DJ's performance is scheduled to begin.
5.  Enter the **End Time** for the event. This is the time when the DJ's performance is scheduled to end.
6.  Enter the **Venue** where the event will take place. This could be a physical location or a virtual setting for the event.
7.  Enter the **Phone Number** of the client or point of contact for the event.
8.  Specify the payment details for the event. This is not required to be filled but is an optional field for recording payment information.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

- **DJ Name (DJ Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the DJ who will be performing at the event.
- **Client Name (Client Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person or organization booking the DJ's services.
- **Event Date (Event Date)** (`date`, required: false): Select the date on which the event will take place.
- **Start Time (Start Time)** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time when the DJ's performance is scheduled to begin.
- **End Time (End Time)** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time when the DJ's performance is scheduled to end.
- **Venue (Venue)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the location where the event will take place.
- **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the client or point of contact for the event.
- **Payment (Payment)** (`number`, required: false): Specify the payment details for the event (optional field). 

### Tips

- Make sure to enter all the required information accurately to ensure smooth communication with the client and the DJ.
- If you are filling this form as a client, ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting it.
- For accurate payment processing, please ensure that you have specified correct payment details.
- If you are a DJ, be prepared to provide your name and other relevant details for the event.
