# Karaoke Contest Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect scores and notes for the karaoke contest participants. It is designed to be completed by judges who will evaluate and score participants based on their performances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Karaoke Contest Judging Form" link to access the form.
2. Review the form and ensure you have the necessary information to complete it.
3. Select the "Top Three Scores" page to input the scores for the top three participants.
4. Enter the scores for each participant in the corresponding fields.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about each participant in the "Notes" field.
6. Repeat steps 3-5 for each participant.
7. When finished, click "Submit" to save your scores and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Top Three Scores**: This page displays the scores for the top three participants. Select this page to enter the scores for each participant.
* **Song Name**: Enter the name of the song performed by the participant. This field is used to identify the song being evaluated.
* **Score 1** (~18): Enter the score for each participant on a scale of 1-10. These fields are used to evaluate the participant's performance.
* **Performer Name**: Enter the name of the performer, which is the person who performed the song.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the participant's performance. This field is optional, but it helps to provide more information about each participant.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to complete the form.
* Double-check your scores and notes for accuracy.
* If you need to make any changes, click "Save" to save your changes.
* If you are finished, click "Submit" to save your scores and notes.
