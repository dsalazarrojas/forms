# Thanksgiving Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for event planners to create an inviting and efficient Thanksgiving potluck invitation for their guests.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply fill out the three sections below:
  1. **Thanksgiving Potluck Invitation Form**: Enter the main details of your potluck invitation.
  2. **RSVP Details**: Provide any additional information guests may need to RSVP.
  3. **Additional Details**: Add any extra details about the potluck.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Thanksgiving Potluck Invitation Form (text)**: This is the main section where you'll enter the details of your potluck invitation. Be sure to include all the necessary information, such as the date, time, location, and other relevant details.
* **RSVP Details (text)**: Use this section to provide any additional information guests need to RSVP, such as how to RSVP, the RSVP deadline, or any specific instructions.
* **Additional Details (text)**: This section is for any extra information about the potluck that you want to share with guests, such as the types of dishes to bring or any specific requests.
