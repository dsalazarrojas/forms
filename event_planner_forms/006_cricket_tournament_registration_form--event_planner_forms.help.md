# Cricket Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **Cricket Tournament Registration Form** is used to register a player for a cricket tournament. This form collects essential information from the player, including contact details, tournament information, and player details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the **Cricket Tournament Registration Form**, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and correct information.
2.  Ensure you enter the email and contact number correctly for proper communication.
3.  Enter the player's full name, date of birth, and contact number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to be used for communication and updates.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number to be used for further communication.
*   **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the player as it appears on their official documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the player in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required: false): Enter the contact number of the player for easy communication.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and clear in filling out the form.
*   Ensure all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
*   If you need assistance or have any issues, contact the tournament organizers.
