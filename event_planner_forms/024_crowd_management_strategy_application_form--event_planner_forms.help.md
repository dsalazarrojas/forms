# Crowd Management Strategy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crowd Management Strategy Application Form is designed to gather information from event organizers regarding their crowd management strategy for a specific event. This form is a crucial tool for event organizers to provide essential details about their event, allowing authorities to review and assess their crowd management strategy. The information collected will be used to ensure a smooth and safe experience for both the event attendees and the authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are the authorized representative of the event organizer.
2. Fill in the event details in the "Event Details" field (Field ID: 2).
3. Provide a brief overview of your crowd management strategy in the "Event Plans" field (Field ID: 3).
4. Enter the contact information of the authorities and venue in the respective fields (Fields ID: 4, 6, 7).
5. Enter the submission date and time in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields (Fields ID: 8, 9).
6. Select the relevant options for "Review Status" and "Submission Status" (Fields ID: 10, 23, 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer** (Field ID: 1): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Details** (Field ID: 2): Enter a brief description of the event, including event name, date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Event Plans** (Field ID: 3): Provide a detailed plan for crowd management, including emergency procedures, crowd flow, and contingency plans.
* **Crowd Safety** (Field ID: 4): Enter a number value indicating the number of people expected to attend the event.
* **Venue Details** (Field ID: 5): Enter the address and other contact information of the venue.
* **Authorities Contact** (Field ID: 6): Enter the contact information of the authorities responsible for the event.
* **Venue Contact** (Field ID: 7): Enter the contact information of the venue staff responsible for the event.
* **Submission Date** (Field ID: 8): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (Field ID: 9): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Review Status** (Field ID: 10): Select the review status of the submission (Approved, Not Evaluated, Rejected).
* **Assigned Reviewer** (Field ID: 11): Select the reviewers responsible for reviewing the submission (Reviewer 1, Reviewer 2, Reviewer 3).
* **Comments** (Field ID: 12): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the submission.
* **Email** (Field ID: 13): Enter the event organizer's email address.
* **Phone** (Field ID: 14): Enter the event organizer's phone number.
* **Address** (Field ID: 15): Enter the event organizer's address (optional).
* **Reviewers Comment** (Field ID: 16): Enter any comments from the reviewers.
* **Submission Notes** (Field ID: 17): Enter any additional information regarding the submission.
* **Review Notes** (Field ID: 18): Enter any notes from the reviewers.
* **Review Date** (Field ID: 19): Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Review Time** (Field ID: 20): Enter the time the review was completed.
* **Status** (Field ID: 21): Select the status of the review (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
* **Review Status Details** (Field ID: 22): Enter any additional details regarding the review status.
* **Submission Status** (Field ID: 23): Select the status of the submission (Not Evaluated, Approved, Rejected).
* **Submission Date Status** (Field ID: 24): Select the status of the submission date (Not Evaluated, Approved, Rejected).
* **Venue ID** (Field ID: 25): Enter the venue ID (optional).
