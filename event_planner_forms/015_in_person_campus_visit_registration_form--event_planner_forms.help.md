# In Person Campus Visit Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for students to register for an in-person campus visit, allowing them to provide their details and preferences for the visit. The form helps us to organize and coordinate the visit efficiently, ensuring that we can tailor the experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, select "Submit" to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. For fields where you have multiple options, select the one that best applies to your situation.
4. If you're unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or contact us for clarification.
5. Once you've completed the form, click "Submit" again to send your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Provide your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your current email address that we can use to communicate with you about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you about any last-minute arrangements or updates.
* **Campus Visit Date**: Select the date you'd like to visit the campus.
* **Campus Visit Time**: Choose your preferred time for the campus visit.
* **Preferred Date**: If the available dates don't suit you, specify an alternative date that you'd like to visit.
* **Preferred Time**: If the available times don't fit your schedule, specify an alternative time for the campus visit.
* **Campus Visit Duration**: Select the duration for your visit from the available options (30m, 60m, 90m, 120m).
* **Event Date**: If applicable, enter the date for the event you're attending (e.g., tour, presentation, or meeting).
* **Event Time**: Enter the start and end time for the event.
* **Event Time End**: Enter the end time for the event.
* **Student ID**: If you have a student ID, provide it for identification purposes.
* **Guardian Name**: If you have a guardian or parent accompanying you, provide their name.
* **Guardian Phone**: Enter your guardian's or parent's phone number.
* **Guardian Email**: Enter your guardian's or parent's email address.
* **Guardian Relation**: Specify the relation of your guardian or parent (Father, Mother, Guardian, Brother, Sister, Other).
* **Guardian Address**: Enter your guardian's or parent's address.
* **Student DOB**: If applicable, enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Student Level**: Select the level you're currently studying (High School, College, University, Other).
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you'd like to attend (Information Session, Interview, Presentation, Tour, Meeting, Other).
* **Event Type Other**: If none of the listed event types match your preference, specify the type of event you'd like to attend.
* **Event Details**: Provide any additional details about the event you're attending.
* **Campus Visit Notes**: Leave any additional notes about your campus visit.
* **Event Location**: If the event location is not specified, enter the location where you'd like to meet.
* **Event Location Other**: If none of the listed locations match your preference, specify the location where you'd like to meet.

Please fill out the form accurately and clearly, and don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or need further clarification.
