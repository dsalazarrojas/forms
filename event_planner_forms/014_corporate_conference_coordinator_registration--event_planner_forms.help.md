# Corporate Conference Coordinator Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for corporate conference coordinators to provide their details, which will be used to manage the conference's logistics and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **conference_details** field with the conference name, date, and location.
2. Enter your **coordinator_details**, including your name, email, and any other relevant information.
3. Provide your company details in the **company_details** field.
4. Submit your response in the **submission** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **conference_details**: Enter the conference title, date, and location. This field helps us understand the conference context and schedule.
* **coordinator_details**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information. This field is essential for us to contact you with any updates or notifications.
* **company_details**: Enter your company name and any other relevant details. This field helps us understand your affiliation with the conference.
* **submission**: Submit your response to confirm your registration and details. This field is a confirmation step before submitting your registration.
