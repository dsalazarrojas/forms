# Event Recycling Logistics Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an event to provide efficient and effective recycling services. Please fill out this form with the details about your event to ensure that our team can properly plan and execute the logistics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event name and location.
2. Enter the event date and time of collection.
3. Select the types of recyclable materials that will be present at the event (e.g., Glass, Paper, Plastic, Organics, Metals).
4. Choose the recycling services you require (e.g., Recycling Bins, Recycling Services, Waste Hauler).
5. Enter your name and contact information so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of your event.
* **Event Size** (`event_size`, text, optional): Enter an estimate of the number of attendees expected at your event.
* **Recyclable Materials** (`recyclable_materials`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of materials that will be present at your event, such as Glass, Paper, Plastic, Organics, or Metals.
* **Recycling Services** (`recycling_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the recycling services you require, such as Recycling Bins, Recycling Services, or Waste Hauler.
* **Collection Date** (`collection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like the recycling materials to be collected.
* **Collection Time** (`collection_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like the recycling materials to be collected.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, so our team can provide efficient and effective recycling services.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at [insert contact info].
* This form will be reviewed and verified before the event, so please ensure that the information is correct and up-to-date.
