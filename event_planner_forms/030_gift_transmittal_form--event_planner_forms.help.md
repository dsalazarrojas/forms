# Gift Transmittal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Transmittal Form is designed to collect information about a gift transmittal, a financial transaction involving the provision of goods or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window.
2. Start by providing a brief description or title of the gift transmittal in the "gift_transmittal_form_title" field.
3. Enter the name or identifier of the party providing the gift in the "gift_transmittal_form_from" field.
4. Enter the name or identifier of the party receiving the gift in the "gift_transmittal_form_to" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gift Transmittal Form Title (gift_transmittal_form_title, text, Optional):** A brief description or title of the gift transmittal. This field is not required.
* **From (gift_transmittal_form_from, text, Optional):** The name or identifier of the party providing the gift.
* **To (gift_transmittal_form_to, text, Optional):** The name or identifier of the party receiving the gift.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the information is recorded correctly.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer back to the form's purpose and fields to clarify.
* This form is intended to be a simple information-gathering tool, so please keep your answers concise and clear.
