# Online Seminar Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Online Seminar Evaluation Form is designed for attendees to provide their feedback and ratings about the online seminar they attended. Your input will help the event planners improve the quality and content of future seminars.
## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out the form, simply follow the prompts and provide your feedback and ratings.
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields (marked as 'required').
- You can save your progress and come back to the form later to complete it.
- Once you are finished, click the submit button to send your evaluation.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Name** (`seminar_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the seminar you attended.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the seminar you attended.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the seminar you attended.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Enter your rating of the seminar on a scale. Note that this field is not required.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Provide any additional feedback about the seminar.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improving the seminar, enter them here.
* **Seminar Rating** (`seminar_rating`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the options: poor, average, excellent, to rate the seminar's quality. Note that this field is not required.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- Your feedback will be kept confidential and only accessible to the event planners.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, contact us at [contact email].
