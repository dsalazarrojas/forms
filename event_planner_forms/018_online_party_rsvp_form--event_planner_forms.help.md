# Online Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests who will be attending or are considering attending an online party. It provides an easy way for hosts to keep track of their guests' responses and make sure everyone has a great experience.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Party Details section with any additional information you'd like to share about the party.
2. Enter your Guest Name in the Guest Name field.
3. Provide your Guest Phone number in the Guest Phone field.
4. Enter your Guest Email address in the Guest Email field.
5. In the Attending section, select one of the three options:
	* I'm Attending: You will definitely be there.
	* Not Attending: You won't be able to make it.
	* Maybe Attending: You're not sure if you'll be able to attend.
6. Choose the Party Date from the calendar in the Party Date field.
7. Select the Party Time from the options in the Party Time field.
8. Set an RSVP By date in the RSVP By field, so that hosts can get a headcount and plan accordingly.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Details** (Party Details, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the party to help hosts get a better idea of what to expect.
* **Guest Name** (Guest Name, text, optional): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the host's guest list.
* **Guest Phone** (Guest Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that hosts can contact you with any last-minute questions or updates.
* **Guest Email** (Guest Email, email, optional): Enter your email address so that hosts can contact you with party updates or send you reminders.
* **Attending** (Attending, select_one, optional): Choose how you plan to attend the party:
	+ I'm Attending: You are definitely attending the party.
	+ Not Attending: Unfortunately, you won't be able to make it.
	+ Maybe Attending: You're not sure yet, but are considering attending.
* **Party Date** (Party Date, date, optional): Choose the date of the party from the calendar.
* **Party Time** (Party Time, time, optional): Choose the time of the party from the options provided.
* **RSVP By** (RSVP By, date, optional): Set a date by which you'd like to RSVP to help hosts get a headcount and plan accordingly.
 
## Tips
 
* Make sure to fill in all the fields as accurately as possible.
* Be considerate of the host's time by responding to the party invitation in a timely manner.
* If you need any help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact the host directly.
