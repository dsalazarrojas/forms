# Party Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Rental Agreement Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a party rental event. It collects contact information, party details, and other relevant data from customers, serving as a contract between the party planner and the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the contact information section by providing your name and email address.
2.  Enter the party details, including the date and time of the event.
3.  Select the party location and time slot.
4.  List the party equipment and furniture required.
5.  Choose the party services needed, such as equipment rental, installation, delivery, and removal.
6.  Specify the rental terms and conditions.
7.  Add any additional notes or comments.
8.  Sign the agreement, indicating that it has been read and understood.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Party Rental Form (`party_rental_form`)

*   **Party Rental Form**: This is the title of the form, providing a brief description of the form's purpose.

### Contact Name (`contact_name`)

*   **Contact Name**: Enter your name in this field to represent you as the party planner or customer.

### Contact Email (`contact_email`)

*   **Contact Email**: Enter your email address in this field for easy communication.

### Party Details (`party_details`)

*   **Party Details**: Provide a brief description of the party event, including any specific requirements or requests.

### Party Location (`party_location`)

*   **Party Location**: Enter the location where the party will be held.

### Party Date (`party_date`)

*   **Party Date**: Enter the date of the party event.

### Party Time (`party_time`)

*   **Party Time**: Enter the time of the party event.

### Time Slot (`party_time_slot`)

*   **Time Slot**: Choose a time slot for the party event.

### Contact Phone (`contact_phone`)

*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.

### Party Equipment (`party_equipment`)

*   **Party Equipment**: List the party equipment needed for the event.

### Party Furniture (`party_furniture`)

*   **Party Furniture**: List the party furniture needed for the event.

### Party Services (`party_services`)

*   **Party Services**: Select the services needed, such as equipment rental, installation, delivery, and removal.

### Rental Terms (`rental_terms`)

*   **Rental Terms**: Specify the rental terms and conditions.

### Party Notes (`party_notes`)

*   **Party Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the party event.

### Agreement Signed (`agreement_signed`)

*   **Agreement Signed**: Select whether the agreement has been signed or not.

### Contract Date (`contract_date`)

*   **Contract Date**: Enter the date of the contract.

### Contract Signed By (`contract_signed_by`)

*   **Contract Signed By**: Enter your name as the person signing the contract.

### Contract Notes (`contract_notes`)

*   **Contract Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the contract.

### Contract Signed For (`contract_signed_for`)

*   **Contract Signed For**: Indicate the contract has been signed for this party event.

### Rental Agreement (`rental_agreement`)

*   **Rental Agreement**: Specify any additional rental agreement terms or conditions.

### Payment Terms (`payment_terms`)

*   **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment terms and conditions.

### Payment Details (`payment_details`)

*   **Payment Details**: Specify any payment details or conditions.

### Payment Terms 2 (`payment_terms_2`)

*   **Payment Terms 2**: Specify any additional payment terms and conditions.

### Agreement Signed By 2 (`agreement_signed_by_2`)

*   **Agreement Signed By 2**: Indicate whether the second agreement has been signed or not.
