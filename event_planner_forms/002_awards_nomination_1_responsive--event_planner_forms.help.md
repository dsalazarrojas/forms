# Awards Nomination 1 Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect nominations for various awards in an organization or department. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the nominee's achievements, qualifications, and impact on the organization or community.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in the "Your Name" field, which is your name and contact information.
2. Enter the nominee's name and title in the "Nominee's Full Name" and "Nominee's Job Title or Position" fields, respectively.
3. Select the award category from the list provided in the "Award Category" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the key achievements and impact of the nominee in the "Key Accomplishment 1" and "Key Accomplishment 2" fields (optional).
5. Describe the nominee's qualities that make them stand out in the "What qualities make them stand out?" field.
6. Enter the nomination date in the "Nomination Date" field.
7. Optionally, provide additional references that can be contacted for verification.
8. Finally, provide a brief description of the reasons for nominating this person in the "Why are you nominating this person?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Your name and contact information.
* **Nominee's Full Name**: The full name of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee's Job Title or Position**: The job title or position of the person you are nominating.
* **Award Category**: Select the award category for which you are nominating this person.
* **Key Accomplishment 1**: Describe a specific achievement or contribution of the nominee.
* **Key Accomplishment 2**: Optionally, describe another specific achievement or contribution of the nominee.
* **What qualities make them stand out?**: Select all the qualities that make this person stand out, such as integrity, dedication, teamwork, etc.
* **Nomination Date**: The date of the nomination.
* **Additional References**: Optionally, provide names and contact information of people who can vouch for the nominee.
* **Why are you nominating this person?**: Provide a brief description of the reasons for nominating this person.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are submitting a genuine nomination and not repeating an existing one.
* Be specific and concrete when describing the nominee's achievements and qualities.
* Use proper contact information and formatting for dates and names.
