# Virtual Concert Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Concert Registration Form is a tool for event planners to collect information from attendees and organizers to ensure a smooth and successful virtual concert experience. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide essential details about your event, making it easier for attendees and organizers to stay connected and informed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the text fields provided.
2. Enter your **Email** in the email address field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** if you want to be contacted for any updates or concerns.
4. Fill in the **Event Name** and **Event Date** fields with the relevant information about the concert.
5. Enter the **Event Time** in the format specified (HH:MM or MM:DD:SS).
6. If applicable, provide the **Ticket Price** and the number of tickets you plan to sell.
7. Enter the **Attendee Number** and provide any additional details about the attendees in the **Attendee Details** field.
8. If you have any special requests, such as audio or video equipment requirements, provide them in the **Special Requests** field.
9. Double-check your email and phone number for correctness in the **Confirm Email** and **Confirm Phone** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to be contacted about the event.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted about the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event in this field.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event in the format HH:MM or MM:DD:SS.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, text, required: false): Enter the price of the tickets for the event.
* **Attendee Number** (`attendee_number`, text, required: false): Enter the number of attendees for the event.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required: false): Enter any details about the attendees, such as special requirements or equipment needs.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): Enter any special requests or equipment needed for the event.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required: false): Double-check your email address to ensure it's accurate.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required: false): Double-check your phone number to ensure it's accurate.
