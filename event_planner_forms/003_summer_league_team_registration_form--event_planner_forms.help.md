# summer_league_team_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your team's registration for the summer league.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information, such as team name, contact person's email, and team manager's phone number.
2. Select the relevant options for your team, such as team type and team language.
3. Provide any additional comments or details about your team.
4. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter your team's name as you would like it to appear in the summer league.
* **Team Manager Name** (`team_manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your team manager's name, if different from the contact person.
* **Contact Person Email** (`contact_person_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the person who will be contacted by the summer league organizers.
* **Team Manager Phone** (`team_manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your team manager's phone number.
* **Team Manager Phone Extension** (`team_manager_phone_extension`, text, optional): Enter your team manager's phone extension (if applicable).
* **Team Manager Phone Type** (`team_manager_phone_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes: The team manager's phone number is in the format of a country code followed by a landline number.
	+ No: The team manager's phone number is in the format of a cell phone number.
* **Team Manager Cell** (`team_manager_cell`, text, optional): Enter your team manager's cell phone number.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of players on your team.
* **Team Type** (`team_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes: The team is a competitive team.
	+ No: The team is a recreational team.
* **Team Type Values** (`team_type_values`, text, optional): Enter any additional values for your team type (e.g., 'Youth', 'Masters', etc.).
* **Team League Experience** (`team_league_experience`, text, optional): Enter any previous experience your team has in similar leagues.
* **Team Coach Name** (`team_coach_name`, text, optional): Enter your team coach's name.
* **Team Sponsor** (`team_sponsor`, text, optional): Enter your team sponsor's name.
* **Team Sponsor Name** (`team_sponsor_name`, text, optional): Enter your team sponsor's name.
* **Team Sponsor Phone** (`team_sponsor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your team sponsor's phone number.
* **Team Sponsor Email** (`team_sponsor_email`, email, optional): Enter your team sponsor's email.
* **Team Venue** (`team_venue`, text, optional): Enter the address of your team's practice venue.
* **Team Address** (`team_address`, text, optional): Enter your team's address.
* **Team State** (`team_state`, text, optional): Enter your team's state (or region).
* **Team Country** (`team_country`, text, optional): Enter your team's country.
* **Team Postal Code** (`team_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your team's postal code (zip code).
* **Team Time Zone** (`team_timezone`, text, optional): Enter your team's time zone.
* **Team Language** (`team_language`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes: The team speaks the default language of the summer league.
	+ No: The team speaks a different language.
* **Team Additional Comments** (`team_additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your team.
* **Team Agreed Terms and Conditions** (`team_agreed_terms_and_conditions`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes: The team has agreed to the terms and conditions.
	+ No: The team has not agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your team's information is accurate and up-to-date.
- Review and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
