# Women's Empowerment Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women's Empowerment Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to register for a workshop focused on women's empowerment. This form will help us understand your interest in attending the workshop and gather any additional information that may be relevant to your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the main page, which includes the main sections of your registration.
2. Move on to the additional information page, where you can provide any additional details that may be helpful for us to know.
3. Finally, proceed to the contact information page, where you can provide us with your contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page (main-page)**:
	+ **First and Last Name (title)**: Please enter your first and last name.
	+ **Email (title)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ **Workshop Preference (title)**: Select the workshop that interests you the most.
* **Additional Information (additional-info)**:
	+ **Interest in Women's Empowerment (title)**: Describe your interest in women's empowerment and how you think this workshop will benefit you.
	+ **Previous Experience (title)**: If you have any previous experience in women's empowerment, please describe it briefly.
* **Contact Information (contact-info)**:
	+ **Phone Number (title)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
	+ **Address (title)**: Enter your address for our records.
	+ **Social Media (title)**: If you have a social media account, please provide your social media handle.
