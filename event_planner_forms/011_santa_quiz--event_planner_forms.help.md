# Santa Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Santa Quiz form is designed to gather information about your thoughts and opinions regarding Santa Claus. It's an entertaining way to engage with the public during the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Santa Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your thoughts and opinions in the text fields.
3. Select your preferences from the multiple-choice options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **About Santa** (`about_santa`, text, optional): Write a brief description or story about Santa Claus. This could be a fun fact or a personal anecdote.
- **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, text, optional): Share your favorite food that Santa might enjoy. It could be a treat or a drink.
- **What Santa Likes** (`what_he_likes`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply from the options below to describe what Santa likes:
	+ Yes
	+ No
- **What Santa Dislikes** (`what_he_dislikes`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options below to describe what Santa dislikes:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
- Be creative and have fun with your responses!
- Remember, this is a quiz, so don't worry if your answers aren't serious – it's all about having fun with Santa!
