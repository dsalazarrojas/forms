# Haunted House Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Haunted House Waiver Form is used to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in a haunted house event. This information includes general details, contact information, and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Haunted House Waiver Form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name, contact information, and any medical conditions you may have.
2. Provide the date and time of your participation.
3. Sign the waiver to indicate that you understand the risks involved.
4. Provide a witness signature and date for an independent witness (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Names**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents. (Required)
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including address, phone numbers, and email. (Optional)
* **Emergency Contact(s)**: Provide the name, phone number, and email of an emergency contact person. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your personal email address. (Required)
* **Home Phone**: Enter your home phone number. (Optional)
* **Work Phone**: Enter your work phone number. (Optional)
* **Cell Phone**: Enter your cell phone number. (Optional)
* **Address**: Enter your address, including house number and street name. (Optional)
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth. (Optional)
* **Age**: Enter your age. (Required)
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have that may be relevant to your participation in the haunted house event. (Optional)
* **Medical Conditions Info**: Provide additional information about your medical conditions. (Optional)
* **Medical Release**: Sign to acknowledge that you understand the risks involved with participating in the haunted house event. (Required)
* **Witness Signature**: Sign here to confirm that you are aware of the risks and agree to the terms of the waiver. (Required)
* **Witness Signature Date**: Enter the date of the witness signature. (Required)
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the independent witness. (Optional)
* **Witness Title**: Enter the title or relationship of the independent witness. (Optional)
* **Witness Signature Date**: Enter the date of the independent witness signature. (Optional)
* **Witness Time**: Enter the time of the independent witness signature. (Optional)
* **Date 1**: Enter the date of the haunted house event. (Optional)
* **Time 1**: Enter the time of the haunted house event. (Optional)
* **Date Time**: Duplicate of the "Date" field. (Optional, redundant)
* **Witness Witness Signature 1**: Sign here to confirm that the independent witness is aware of the risks and agree to the terms of the waiver. (Optional)
* **Witness Witness Time 1**: Enter the time of the independent witness signature. (Optional)
