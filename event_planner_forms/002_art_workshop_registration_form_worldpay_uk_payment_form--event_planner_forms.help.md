# Art Workshop Registration Form WorldPay UK Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is used to register participants for an art workshop. It gathers all necessary information for a smooth and efficient registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
5. **Workshop Name**: Select the workshop you wish to attend from the dropdown menu.
6. **Preferred Workshop Date**: Select the preferred date for the workshop.
7. **Experience Level**: Choose your experience level in art from the dropdown menu.
8. **Age Group**: Select your age group from the dropdown menu.
9. **Number of Attendees**: Enter the number of people registering for the workshop.
10. **Special Requests or Accommodations**: Enter any special requests or accommodations you may need.
11. **Dietary Requirements**: Select your dietary requirements from the dropdown menu.
12. **Registration Fee Amount (GBP)**: Enter the registration fee amount for each attendee.
13. **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method from the dropdown menu.
14. **I agree to the workshop terms and conditions**: Confirm that you agree to the workshop terms and conditions.
15. **How did you hear about this workshop?**: Select how you heard about the workshop.
16. **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Participant First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Participant Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address to receive important workshop updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for any contact purposes.
* **Workshop Name** (Workshop Name, select_one, required): Select the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Preferred Workshop Date** (Preferred Workshop Date, date, required): Select the preferred date for the workshop.
* **Experience Level** (Experience Level, select_one, required): Choose your experience level in art from the dropdown menu.
* **Age Group** (Age Group, select_one, required): Select your age group from the dropdown menu.
* **Number of Attendees** (Number of Attendees, number, required): Enter the number of people registering for the workshop.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations** (Special Requests or Accommodations, text, optional): Enter any special requests or accommodations you may need.
* **Dietary Requirements** (Dietary Requirements, select_multiple, optional): Select your dietary requirements from the dropdown menu.
* **Registration Fee Amount (GBP)** (Registration Fee Amount, number, required): Enter the registration fee amount for each attendee.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose your payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **I agree to the workshop terms and conditions** (I agree to the workshop terms and conditions, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the workshop terms and conditions.
* **How did you hear about this workshop?** (How did you hear about this workshop, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about the workshop.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
