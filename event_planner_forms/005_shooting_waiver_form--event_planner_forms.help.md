# Shooting Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shooting Waiver Form is used to collect information from participants, such as their contact details, medical conditions, and insurance information, to ensure their safety and well-being during shooting events. This form is required for all participants, and its completion helps us to identify any potential risks or restrictions that may impact their ability to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your Participant Name, which is the name of the person participating in the event.
2. Provide your Guardian Name and their contact information, including Phone and Email.
3. Enter your Participant Date of Birth (DOB) and calculate your age.
4. Select the correct option for Parent/Guardian Consent, indicating whether you or your guardian give your consent for the participant to participate.
5. In the Emergency Contact field, provide the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
6. Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
7. List any pre-existing medical conditions that the participant has, in the Participant Medical Conditions field.
8. If necessary, add any additional medical notes for the participant in the Participant Medical Notes field.
9. Select whether the participant has any medical conditions that may impact their ability to participate, and if so, choose the correct medical procedure consent option.
10. Indicate whether the participant has health insurance, and if they do, provide the name of the authorized medical provider.
11. Indicate whether your guardian has medical authorization, and if so, select the correct option.
12. Add any additional medical notes for your guardian in the Parent or Guardian Medical Notes field.
13. If necessary, provide the name of the authorized medical provider for your guardian.
14. Indicate whether your guardian has health insurance, and if so, select the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the person participating in the event.
  - Type: text
  - Required: yes
- **Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the person who is the guardian or parent of the participant.
  - Type: text
  - Required: yes
- **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the email of the parent or guardian.
  - Type: email
  - Required: no
- **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the participant.
  - Type: date
  - Required: no
- **Participant Age**: Calculate the age of the participant based on their date of birth.
  - Type: number
  - Required: no
- **Parent/Guardian Consent**: Select whether you or your guardian give your consent for the participant to participate.
  - Type: select multiple
  - Required: no
- **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Participant Medical Conditions**: List any pre-existing medical conditions that the participant has.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Participant Medical Notes**: Add any additional medical notes for the participant.
  - Type: note
  - Required: no
- **Participant Medical Procedure**: Select whether the participant has any medical conditions that may impact their ability to participate.
  - Type: select one
  - Required: no
- **Participant Medical Insurance**: Indicate whether the participant has health insurance.
  - Type: select one
  - Required: no
- **Participant Medical Authorization Provider**: Enter the name of the authorized medical provider.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Parent or Guardian Medical Authorization**: Indicate whether your guardian has medical authorization.
  - Type: select one
  - Required: no
- **Parent or Guardian Medical Notes**: Add any additional medical notes for your guardian.
  - Type: note
  - Required: no
- **Parent or Guardian Medical Authorization Provider**: Enter the name of the authorized medical provider.
  - Type: text
  - Required: no
- **Parent or Guardian Medical Insurance**: Indicate whether your guardian has health insurance.
  - Type: select one
  - Required: no

Note: The form may vary based on the specific requirements of the event and location. Always review the form carefully before filling it in.
