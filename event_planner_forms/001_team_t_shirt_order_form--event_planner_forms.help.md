# Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team T Shirt Order Form is used to place orders for team shirts for a specific event. This form gathers information about the team, the order details, and other relevant event information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team for which you are ordering the shirts.
2. Choose the shirt size for each team member.
3. Enter the quantity of shirts to be ordered.
4. Provide your email address and phone number for contact and communication purposes.
5. Enter the team name and event details (date and location) if applicable.
6. Choose the T-shirt color and design for your team.
7. Select the order type (regular or custom).
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team T Shirt** (`team_t_shirt`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team for which you are ordering the shirts.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the shirt from the available options (XS, S, M, L, XL).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of shirts to be ordered.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team for which you are ordering the shirts.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): If you want to specify a particular order date, you can enter it here.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event for which you are ordering the shirts.
* **Team Color** (`team_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the color of the team from the available options (blue, red, green, yellow).
* **T-Shirt Color** (`t_shirt_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the color of the T-shirt from the available options (blue, red, white, black).
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of order (regular or custom).
* **Team Number** (`team_number`, number, optional): Enter the team number (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event for which you are ordering the shirts.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event (if applicable).
* **Event Date Time** (`event_date_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the event (if applicable).
* **Team T Shirt Design** (`team_t_shirt_design`, select_one, optional): Choose the design of the team T-shirt from the available options (design1, design2, design3).

## Tips

* Double-check that you have entered all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Make sure to proofread your input for any typos or errors.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling the form, contact the event organizers or administrators for assistance.
