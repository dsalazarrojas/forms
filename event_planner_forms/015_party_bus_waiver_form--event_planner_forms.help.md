# Party Bus Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Bus Waiver Form is used to collect important information from party bus riders to ensure their safety and comfort during the journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the party bus rider's details, including their name, contact information, and any medical conditions they may have.
2. If the rider is a licensed driver, select "Yes" from the "I am a licensed" field. If not, select "No".
3. Indicate whether you are a party bus driver or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Party Bus Driver" field.
4. Sign the waiver by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Waiver Agreement" field.
5. Finally, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Party Bus Rider Information** (party_bus_waiver_form_1, text, required/optional): Enter the rider's name and any contact information they want to share with the party bus management.
- **Party Bus Details** (party_bus_waiver_form_2, text, required/optional): Describe the party bus trip, including the date and time of the event, and any specific instructions or expectations for the riders.
- **Waiver Agreement** (party_bus_waiver_form_3, text, required/optional): Indicate whether you have read, understand, and agree to the waiver terms and conditions.
- **Date of Birth** (party_bus_waiver_form_4, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Age** (party_bus_waiver_form_5, number, required/optional): Enter your age.
- **Emergency Contact** (party_bus_waiver_form_6, email, required/optional): Enter the contact information of an emergency contact person or organization.
- **Phone Number** (party_bus_waiver_form_7, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Medical Conditions** (party_bus_waiver_form_8, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions that may affect your safety or comfort during the party bus trip.
- **Signature** (party_bus_waiver_form_9, select_one, required/optional): Sign the waiver by selecting "Yes" if you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
- **I am a Licensed** (party_bus_waiver_form_10, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you are a licensed driver or passenger by selecting "Yes" or "No".
- **Party Bus Driver** (party_bus_waiver_form_11, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you are the party bus driver by selecting "Yes" or "No".
