# Christmas Card Design Contest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Card Design Contest Form is designed to collect information from contestants who want to participate in the contest. It helps us to manage submissions, contact participants, and promote contest winners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email Address" field. We will use this to contact you about contest updates.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is for contest organizers to get in touch with you about your submission.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. Please ensure this is correct, as we need to verify your age.
5. In the "Design Title" field, give your design a creative name.
6. Describe your design concept and inspiration in the "Design Description" field.
7. Select the category for your design in the "Design Category" field.
8. Choose your primary color scheme in the "Primary Color Scheme" field.
9. Confirm that your design is original in the "Is This Your Original Work" field.
10. If you have submitted to the contest before, select the correct option in the "Have You Submitted Before" field.
11. Agree to contest terms and rules in the "Agree to Contest Terms" field.
12. Grant or decline usage rights for your design in the "Grant Usage Rights" field.
13. Choose your preferred method of contact for contest updates in the "How Should We Contact You" field.
14. If you wish, provide your social media handle in the "Social Media Handle" field.
15. Finally, let us know how you heard about the contest in the "How Did You Hear About Contest" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your first and last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email Address (2)**: A valid email address that we can use to contact you about the contest.
* **Phone Number (3)**: For contest updates and organizers to get in touch with you.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: Your birthday, which must be 18 or older to participate.
* **Contestant Information (5)**: A note section for any additional information about you as a participant.
* **Design Title (6)**: Give your design a creative name that reflects its theme and style.
* **Design Description (7)**: Describe your design concept and what inspired it.
* **Design Category (8)**: Choose the category for your design submission, such as Traditional Christmas or Abstract Art.
* **Primary Color Scheme (9)**: Select the main color scheme used in your design.
* **Is This Your Original Work (12)**: Confirm that this design is entirely your own creation.
* **Have You Submitted Before (13)**: If you have participated in previous contests, select the correct option.
* **Agree to Contest Terms (15)**: Confirm you agree to all contest rules and regulations.
* **Grant Usage Rights (16)**: Choose whether to grant usage rights for your design.
* **How Should We Contact You (17)**: Select your preferred contact method, such as email or text message.
* **Social Media Handle (18)**: Optional: if you wish, provide your social media handle.
* **How Did You Hear About Contest (19)**: Let us know how you heard about the contest, such as social media or a friend.
* **Design Software Used (11)**: Describe the software tools used to create your design.
* **-- Contestant Information -- (5)**, **-- Design Details -- (10)**: Optional notes sections for additional information.
* **Notification Preference (17)**: Select your preferred method of contact for contest updates.

Tips:
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be honest about your original work and prior submissions.
* Read and agree to contest terms and rules carefully.
* Choose your preferred contact method and provide accurate social media handle if desired.
