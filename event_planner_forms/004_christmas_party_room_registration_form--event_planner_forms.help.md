# Christmas Party Room Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or groups to book a room for a Christmas party. It collects essential information to plan and prepare for the party.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as **<u>Required</u>**.
2. Enter the name of the person booking the room.
3. If you are booking on behalf of a group, enter the group name.
4. Enter a valid email address to receive confirmation of the party details.
5. Enter a phone number for contact purposes on the day of the event.
6. Choose a specific date for the party.
7. Enter the preferred start and end times for the party.
8. Estimate the number of guests attending the party.
9. Describe any special requests such as AV or catering needs.
10. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organizer name**: <u>Required</u> Enter the name of the person booking the room.
* **Organization or group name**: Enter the name of the organization or group if booking on behalf of a group. (Optional)
* **Email address**: <u>Required</u> Enter a valid email address to receive confirmation of the party details.
* **Phone number**: <u>Required</u> Enter a contact phone number for the day of the event.
* **Event date**: <u>Required</u> Select a specific date for the party.
* **Start time**: <u>Required</u> Enter the preferred start time for the party.
* **End time**: <u>Required</u> Enter the preferred end time for the party.
* **Expected number of guests**: <u>Required</u> Estimate the number of guests attending the party.
* **Room setup preferred**: Enter any special room setup requests such as seating or layout preferences. (Optional)
* **Special requests**: Enter any additional needs such as AV or catering requirements. (Optional)
