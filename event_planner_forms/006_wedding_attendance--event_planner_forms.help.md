# Wedding Attendance - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Attendance form is used to collect information from wedding attendees, including their names, contact information, and RSVP status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the person attending the wedding.
2. Enter the person's email address.
3. Enter the person's phone number.
4. Select the date of the wedding they will attend.
5. Select the time of the wedding they will attend.
6. Indicate whether you are attending the wedding by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. If you have a physical invitation, select "Yes" to save paper.
8. If you want to find other attendees in the future, select "Yes" to save their information.
9. Enter any comments or notes about the event.
10. Indicate whether you want to save trees by selecting "Yes" or "No".
11. Enter the total number of people attending (if known).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **People Attending** (2): Enter the number of people attending the wedding.
* **Name** (3): Enter the name of the person attending the wedding.
* **Email** (4): Enter the person's email address.
* **Phone** (5): Enter the person's phone number.
* **Date** (6): Select the date of the wedding they will attend.
* **Time** (7): Select the time of the wedding they will attend.
* **Select One Attendance** (8): Indicate whether you are attending the wedding by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Save Paper** (9): If you have a physical invitation, select "Yes" to save paper.
* **Find Attendees** (10): If you want to find other attendees in the future, select "Yes".
* **Comments** (11): Enter any comments or notes about the event.
* **Save Trees** (12): Indicate whether you want to save trees by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Total People** (13): Enter the total number of people attending (if known).
