This guide is intended for individuals who are filling out the Christmas Present Form for a loved one. The form is designed to gather information about the recipient and their preferences to help create a personalized and enjoyable Christmas gift experience. The guide will walk you through each section of the form, explaining the purpose and required information for each field.

# Christmas Present Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help the gift-giver understand the recipient's preferences, interests, and restrictions, making it easier to select a thoughtful and suitable gift.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill in the recipient's **Full Name** (required) and **Email Address** (required).
2.  Next, select the recipient's **Age** (optional) and choose their **Gender** (optional).
3.  In the **Main Interests or Hobbies** section, select all the relevant options (required) that apply to the recipient, such as Sports, Reading, or Gaming.
4.  For the **Gift Preferences** section, select the preferred type of gift (required) that the recipient would enjoy, such as a **Physical Gift**, **Gift Card**, or **Experience**.
5.  Choose a **Budget Range** (required) that fits the gift-giver's budget.
6.  Provide a list of 3-5 **Specific Items You Want** (required) that the gift-giver has in mind for the recipient.
7.  If applicable, provide the recipient's **Clothing Size** (optional) and **Shoe Size** (optional).
8.  If there are any **Restrictions or Preferences** (optional), such as items to avoid or allergies, please provide them in the **Allergies or Sensitivities** field.
9.  Finally, if you have any additional information or special notes about the recipient, please provide it in the **Additional Information or Special Notes** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient Information**:
	+ **Full Name**: Enter the recipient's full name.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter the recipient's email address.
	+ **Age**: Optional field for the recipient's age.
	+ **Gender**: Optional field for the recipient's gender.
* **Main Interests or Hobbies**:
	+ Select all relevant options that apply to the recipient.
* **Gift Preferences**:
	+ **Preferred Type of Gift**: Choose the type of gift the recipient would enjoy the most.
* **Budget Range**:
	+ Choose the budget range that fits the gift-giver's budget.
* **Specific Items You Want**:
	+ Provide 3-5 specific items the gift-giver has in mind for the recipient.
* **Sizing Information**:
	+ **Clothing Size**: If applicable, provide the recipient's clothing size.
	+ **Shoe Size**: If applicable, provide the recipient's shoe size.
* **Restrictions or Preferences**:
	+ **Allergies or Sensitivities**: If applicable, provide any allergies or sensitivities the recipient may have.
* **Additional Information or Special Notes**:
	+ If there's anything else you'd like to let the gift-giver know about the recipient, feel free to add it here.
