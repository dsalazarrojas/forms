# Visitor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Information Form is a simple and straightforward form designed to collect essential visitor information. It will be used by event planners, event staff, and other visitors to gather relevant details about the visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Provide your first name.
* Provide your last name.
* Enter your email address.
* Enter your phone number.
* Briefly describe the reason for your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
**First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Your first name is your given or baptismal name. Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification documents.

### Last name
**Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Your last name is your surname or family name. Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification documents.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your official email address that you use to communicate with others.

### Phone number
**Phone number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.

### Reason for visiting
**Reason for visiting** (`reason_for_visiting`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the purpose of your visit. This will help us understand the context of your visit and provide a better experience for you and our team.

* Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
