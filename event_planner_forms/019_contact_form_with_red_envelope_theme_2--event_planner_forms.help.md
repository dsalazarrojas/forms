# Contact Form With Red Envelope Theme 2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for event planners to quickly send service inquiries to the event team. It provides a simple and easy-to-use interface for submitting requests for services such as catering, venue, entertainment, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields on pages 2 and 3.
2. Provide a valid phone number if you would like to schedule a call.
3. Select the event date you are planning.
4. Choose the type of event (Wedding, Corporate, Social, or Other).
5. Select the services you need (optional, but recommended for a complete inquiry).
6. Enter the estimated number of guests (if applicable).
7. Provide any additional notes or requests in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as the requester.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for information.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you prefer to schedule a call, enter your phone number.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the event you are planning.
* **Event type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of event (Wedding, Corporate, Social, or Other).
* **Services needed** (`services_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the services you require (e.g., Catering, Venue, Entertainment, Photography).
* **Estimated guest count** (`guest_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate number of attendees for your event.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any specific requirements or requests you have.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is up-to-date and valid.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the services you need.
* If you have any attachments or files to include with your inquiry, please send them separately via email.
* Our team will respond to your request within 24-48 hours during weekdays.
