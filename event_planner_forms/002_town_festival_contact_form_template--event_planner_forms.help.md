# Town Festival Contact Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow event planners and town officials to contact the festival organizers with any questions or concerns related to the festival event. This form will be used to gather essential information and provide a way to take notes for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the **Event Planner** field. This is your name and title as an event planner or town official.
2. Enter your contact email address in the **Email** field. This is the email address where the festival team will contact you to discuss your inquiry.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field. This is the phone number where the festival team can contact you to discuss your inquiry.
4. Add any additional notes in the **Note** field. This is where you can provide any additional information or context about your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Planner** (`event_planner`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your name and title as an event planner or town official. Please enter your name as it should be spelled correctly.
* **Town Official** (`town_official`, text, optional): This field is optional and is for you to enter your title as a town official. If you don't have a title, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, required): This field is for you to enter your email address where the festival team can contact you. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your phone number where the festival team can contact you. Please make sure to enter a valid phone number.
* **Note** (`note_input`, note, optional): This field is for you to add any additional notes or information about your inquiry. You can enter any details that will help the festival team understand your question or concern.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure the festival team can contact you.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* You can submit this form multiple times, but please be aware that duplicate forms may cause confusion.
