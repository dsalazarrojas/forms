# Conference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help potential conference organizers, speakers, or exhibitors request a conference or event opportunity. Fill out this form to provide your contact information, event details, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your complete name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
2. Select your preferred method of contact for follow-up questions or information.
3. Choose the type of conference or event you're interested in.
4. Provide the name of your organization or company.
5. Enter your job title and department (if applicable).
6. Select the type of opportunity you're requesting (e.g., speaking, exhibition space, sponsorship).
7. Choose the conference of interest.
8. If applicable, select your preferred date and specify the number of attendees.
9. If requesting to speak, provide a proposed presentation topic.
10. Select your preferred presentation format.
11. If exhibiting, choose your preferred booth size.
12. If interested in sponsorship, select your desired sponsorship level.
13. Specify your estimated budget range.
14. If applicable, provide any specific audio-visual needs or equipment requirements.
15. Indicate any marketing opportunities you're interested in.
16. Select the decision timeline for your request.
17. Provide your referral source (e.g., website, social media, colleague).
18. Finally, add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Full Name** (`requestor_name`, `text`, required): Your complete name, including first and last names.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): The email address we'll use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): The name of your company or institution.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Your department or role within your organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Your title or position within your organization.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, `select_one`, required): The type of opportunity you're requesting (e.g., speaking, exhibition space, sponsorship).
* **Conference of Interest** (`conference_interest`, `text`, required): The name of the conference or event you're interested in.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, your preferred date for the event.
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`, `number`, optional): The number of attendees you expect to bring.
* **Proposed Presentation Topic** (`presentation_topic`, `text`, optional): If requesting to speak, provide a proposed presentation topic.
* **Preferred Format** (`presentation_format`, `select_one`, optional): The preferred format for your presentation (e.g., keynote, breakout session, workshop).
* **Booth Size Preference** (`booth_size`, `select_one`, optional): If exhibiting, choose your preferred booth size.
* **Sponsorship Level Interest** (`sponsorship_level`, `select_one`, optional): If interested in sponsorship, select your desired sponsorship level.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Your estimated budget range for the opportunity.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): Any specific needs or requirements you have.
* **Audio-Visual Needs** (`av_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): If applicable, specify your audio-visual equipment needs.
* **Marketing Opportunities** (`marketing_opportunities`, `select_multiple`, optional): If interested, select the marketing opportunities you'd like to leverage.
* **Decision Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, optional): The time frame in which you'd like to receive a decision.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): Referral source (e.g., website, social media, colleague).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other information you'd like to share or comment on.
* **Follow-Up Preference** (`follow_up_preference`, `select_one`, required): The preferred method of contact for follow-up questions or information.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, `text`, optional): The preferred time of day or day of the week for follow-up contacts.

## Tips

* Ensure the required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us.
