## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of T-shirt you want by going to the "T-Shirt Selection" page.
2. On the "T-Shirt Selection" page, select "Yes" or "No" for each type of T-shirt.
3. On the "Quantity" page, enter the number of T-shirts you need.
4. On the "Size Preference" page, select your preferred size.
5. On the "Shipping Address" page, enter your shipping address.
6. On the "Payment Information" page, select your payment method.
7. On the "Date" and "Time" page, enter the date and time of the order.
8. On the "Customer Information" page, enter your email, phone number, name, company, and other details.
9. On the "Order Details" page, select the shipping method and enter any additional notes or comments.
10. Review your order details and submit the form.
