# Shooting Range Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shooting Range Waiver form is a document that asks participants to provide their personal details and agree to the terms and conditions of the shooting range. It is a mandatory form that ensures participants understand and agree to the safety rules and regulations of the shooting range.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `participant_name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `participant_email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `participant_phone` field.
4. Read and agree to the terms and conditions by typing your name in the `terms_and_agreement` field.
5. Sign the participant signature section by typing your name.
6. Enter the date and time of your participation in the `date` and `time` fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shooting_range_waiver**: This is the main form where you can type a brief statement explaining your understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of the shooting range.
* **terms_and_agreement**: This field is where you must type your full name to indicate that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the shooting range.
* **participant_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **participant_email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **parent_signature**: This is the signature block where you, as a parent/guardian, can sign to confirm your child's participation.
* **date**: Enter the date of your participation in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **time**: Enter the time of your participation in the 12-hour clock format.

## Tips
* Make sure to read the terms and conditions carefully before signing the waiver.
* Ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the shooting range staff.
* If you are under 18, please provide a parent or guardian's signature to complete the waiver.
