# Summer Camp Detailed Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information from participants and their parents/guardians for the summer camp program. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the participant and the staff. Please review each section carefully and ask for help if you need it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Ensure you have permission from the participant's parent/guardian before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your answers for any typos or errors before submitting.
4. Review and agree to the payment terms on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant as it appears on their official documents (e.g., passport, ID card).
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): If the participant is a minor, enter the name of their parent or guardian.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number where the participant can be reached during the camp.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the participant or parent/guardian (if applicable).
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required): Enter the age of the participant as of the start date of the camp.
* **Participant DOB** (`participant_dob`, date, required): Enter the participant's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Camp Address** (`camp_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where the camp will be held.
* **Camp City** (`camp_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the camp will be held.
* **Camp State** (`camp_state`, select_one, required): Select the state where the camp will be held from the dropdown list.
* **Participant Medical Conditions** (`participant_medical_conditions`, text, optional): If the participant has any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Participant Special Needs** (`participant_special_needs`, text, optional): If the participant has any special needs, enter them here.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Select the payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Check) for the camp fees.
* **Camp Director Name** (`camp_director_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the camp director or staff member who will be in charge.
* **Camp Director Email** (`camp_director_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the camp director or staff member who will be in charge.
* **Camp Director Phone** (`camp_director_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the camp director or staff member who will be in charge.
* **Camp Director Address** (`camp_director_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the camp director or staff member who will be in charge.
* **Participant Diet** (`participant_diet`, select_one, required): Select the participant's dietary preference (Vegetarian, Vegan, Omnivore, None).
* **Participant Medical Insurance** (`participant_medical_insurance`, text, optional): If the participant has medical insurance, enter the name of the provider.
* **Participant Food Restrictions** (`participant_food_restrictions`, text, optional): If the participant has any food restrictions, enter them here.
* **Participant Medical Conditions 2** (`participant_medical_conditions_2`, text, optional): If the participant has any additional medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Participant Medical Notes** (`participant_medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical notes or information about the participant.
* **Participant Medical History** (`participant_medical_history`, text, optional): Enter the participant's medical history, if applicable.
* **Participant Medication** (`participant_medication`, text, optional): If the participant takes any medication, enter the name of the medication and dosage.
* **Participant Allergy** (`participant_allergy`, text, optional): If the participant has any allergies, enter them here.
* **Participant Medical Emergency Contact** (`participant_medical_emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of the participant's emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for any typos or errors before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, ask for help or clarification.
* Review the payment terms carefully before submitting the form.
