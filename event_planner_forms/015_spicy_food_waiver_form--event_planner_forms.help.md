# Spicy Food Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who are participating in events or activities involving spicy foods. It is intended to obtain a waiver of liability from potential claimants, ensuring that event organizers and hosts are protected from any potential legal claims due to injuries or discomforts experienced by participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Title** (Field 2) with the name of the event or activity you are participating in.
2. Confirm that you are **18 years old or older** (Field 3) by entering your age and stating your understanding of your age in relation to participating in the event.
3. Indicate that you have **no medical conditions that would prevent you from consuming spicy foods** (Field 4) by providing a brief explanation.
4. State that you **understand that eating spicy foods can be hazardous to your health** (Field 5) by entering a brief explanation.
5. **Waive any claims** (Field 6) that you may have or may hereafter acquire against the event organizers and hosts due to discomfort, illness, or injury from consuming spicy foods.
6. **Acknowledge that you are aware of the waiver** (Field 7) by entering a brief explanation.
7. Choose the **Date** (Field 8) when you completed this form.
8. Enter your **Signature** (Field 9) with your name to confirm your agreement.
9. Provide your **Phone Number** (Field 10) to enable contact in case of emergencies.
10. Enter your **Email Address** (Field 11) to enable communication.
11. Choose whether you **agree or disagree** (Field 12) with the waiver terms by selecting the appropriate option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (Field 2): Enter the name of the event or activity you are participating in.
* **Age Confirmation** (Field 3): Confirm that you are 18 years old or older and understand your age in relation to participating in the event.
* **Medical Conditions** (Field 4): Explain any medical conditions that you have that may prevent you from consuming spicy foods.
* **Spicy Food Risks** (Field 5): Explain that you understand the potential risks associated with eating spicy foods.
* **Waiver of Liability** (Field 6): Acknowledge that you waive any claims you may have or may hereafter acquire against the event organizers and hosts.
* **Awareness of Waiver** (Field 7): Acknowledge that you are aware of the waiver terms.
* **Date** (Field 8): Enter the date when you completed this form.
* **Signature** (Field 9): Enter your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Phone Number** (Field 10): Provide your phone number to enable contact in case of emergencies.
* **Email Address** (Field 11): Enter your email address to enable communication.
* **Agreement** (Field 12): Choose whether you agree or disagree with the waiver terms.
