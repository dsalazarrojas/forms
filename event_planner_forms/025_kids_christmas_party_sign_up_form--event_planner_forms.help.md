# Kids Christmas Party Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents to sign up their children for the Kids Christmas Party event. It helps us gather necessary information from each child to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and contact information (Parent Name, Email, Phone).
2. Enter your child's name.
3. Select the school grade of your child (1st to 12th).
4. Indicate whether your child will attend the party.
5. Specify if your child has any special needs (Wheelchair, Walker, Hearing Impaired, Visual Impaired, or Other).
6. If applicable, note any allergies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name (Text)**: Enter your name and contact information as a parent.
* **Email (Email)**: Type in your email address for further communication.
* **Phone (Text)**: Provide a phone number where we can reach you.
* **Child Name (Text)**: Enter your child's name.
* **School Grade (Select One)**: Select your child's school grade from the provided options (1st to 12th).
* **Will Your Child Attend? (Select One)**: Choose 'True' if your child will attend the party and 'False' otherwise.
* **Special Needs (Select Multiple)**: Select any special needs that apply to your child (Wheelchair, Walker, Hearing Impaired, Visual Impaired, or Other).
* **Allergies (Note)**: If your child has any allergies, describe them here.

**Tips**
* Please double-check your contact information and child's details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
