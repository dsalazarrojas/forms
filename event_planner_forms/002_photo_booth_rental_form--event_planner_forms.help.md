# photo_booth_rental_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event planners rent photo booth equipment for their events. It collects user information, event details, and other necessary data to facilitate the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, such as your name and contact details.
2. Provide details about the event, including the date, time, and location.
3. Specify the photo booth equipment required for the event.
4. Add any special requests or requirements for the event.
5. Finally, sign and submit the form to complete the rental process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your name and contact details.
* **Event Details** (`event_info`, text, required/optional): Describe the event you are planning, including the date and time.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): Specify the event location.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Specify the time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, text, required/optional): Describe how long the event will last.
* **Photo Booth Details** (`photo_booth_details`, text, required/optional): Specify the type and quantity of photo booth equipment required.
* **Renter Requirements** (`renter_requirements`, text, required/optional): List any specific requirements for the photo booth, such as power supply.
* **Photo Booth Equipment** (`photo_booth_equipment`, text, required/optional): Specify the type and quantity of photo booth equipment required.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): Add any special requests or requirements for the event.
* **Renter Signature** (`renter_signature`, note, required/optional): Sign and date this form to complete the rental process.
* **Event Coordinator** (`event_coordinator`, text, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of the event coordinator.
* **CoordinatoR Email** (`coordinator_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the event coordinator.
* **Coordinator Phone** (`coordinator_phone`, text, required/optional): Specify the phone number of the event coordinator.
