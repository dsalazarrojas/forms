# Distinguished Service Award Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distinguished Service Award Form is used to recognize and acknowledge exceptional service and contributions made by employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the first name of the nominee in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the last name of the nominee in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the email address of the nominator in the "Email" field.
4. Select the title of the nominee from the dropdown list in the "Nominee" field.
5. Select the department of the nominator from the dropdown list in the "Department" field.
6. Enter the nomination date in the "Nomination Date" field.
7. Enter the date of service in the "Service Date" field.
8. Enter the email address of the nominator in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the nominator in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter the job title of the nominator in the "Title" field.
11. Enter a description of the nominator's job in the "Job Description" field.
12. Select the supervisor of the nominator from the dropdown list in the "Supervisor" field.
13. Enter the email address of the supervisor in the "Supervisor Email" field.
14. Enter the phone number of the supervisor in the "Supervisor Phone" field.
15. Enter the title of the supervisor in the "Supervisor Title" field.
16. Enter any comments about the nominator in the "Comments" field.
17. Enter any recommendations for the nominator in the "Recommendations" field.
18. List the organizational units of the nominator in the "Organizational Units" field.
19. Enter any awards or recognition received by the nominator in the "Awards" field.
20. Enter the number of recommendations received by the nominator in the "Number of Recommendations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter the first name of the nominee.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter the last name of the nominee.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the email address of the nominator.
* **Nominee** (Nominee, select_one, required): Select the title of the nominee from the dropdown list.
* **Department** (Department, text, optional): Select the department of the nominator from the dropdown list.
* **Nomination Date** (Nomination Date, date, optional): Enter the date the nomination was submitted.
* **Service Date** (Service Date, date, optional): Enter the date of service.
* **Nominator** (Nominator, text, required): Enter the name of the nominator.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Select the department of the nominator from the dropdown list.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the nominator.
* **Title** (Title, text, optional): Enter the job title of the nominator.
* **Job Description** (Job Description, text, optional): Enter a description of the nominator's job.
* **Supervisor** (Supervisor, select_one, optional): Select the supervisor of the nominator from the dropdown list.
* **Supervisor Email** (Supervisor Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (Supervisor Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Title** (Supervisor Title, text, optional): Enter the title of the supervisor.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Enter any comments about the nominator.
* **Recommendations** (Recommendations, text, optional): Enter any recommendations for the nominator.
* **Organizational Units** (Organizational Units, text, optional): List the organizational units of the nominator.
* **Awards** (Awards, text, optional): Enter any awards or recognition received by the nominator.
* **Number of Recommendations** (Number of Recommendations, number, optional): Enter the number of recommendations received by the nominator.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the relevant department or supervisor.
