# Camp Registration Form PayPal Checkout - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Registration Form PayPal Checkout is used to collect information from parents or guardians to register their child(ren) for a camp session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the Camper Details section, including the Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Date of Birth, and Grade Level.
3. Enter the Parent/Guardian Information, including the Parent/Guardian Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
4. Select the Camp Session and Camp Type.
5. Choose the Number of Weeks.
6. In the Special Interests and Preferences section, select the activity interests and any special needs or requirements.
7. In the Billing Information section, enter the Billing Address, City, State, Zip Code, and Country.
8. Select the Payment Method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Camper Details)**: Enter the child's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Email Address (Camper Details)**: Enter the email address of the parent/guardian.
* **Phone Number (Camper Details)**: Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Date of Birth (Camper Details)**: Enter the child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Grade Level (Camper Details)**: Select the child's current grade level in school.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (Parent/Guardian Information)**: Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Email Address (Parent/Guardian Information)**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Phone Number (Parent/Guardian Information)**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Select Camp Session**: Choose the desired camp session from the list.
* **Camp Type**: Select the type of camp experience desired.
* **Number of Weeks**: Choose the number of weeks for the camp session.
* **Select activity interests**: Choose any special activity interests or needs.
* **Dietary Requirements or Allergies**: Enter any dietary requirements or allergies.
* **Medical Conditions or Medications**: Enter any medical conditions or medications.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the billing address where the payment will be processed.
* **City (Billing Information)**: Enter the city where the billing address is located.
* **State (Billing Information)**: Enter the state where the billing address is located.
* **Zip Code (Billing Information)**: Enter the zip code where the billing address is located.
* **Country (Billing Information)**: Enter the country where the billing address is located.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method, such as PayPal or credit card.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Double-check the billing address for correct formatting.
* Choose the correct payment method.
