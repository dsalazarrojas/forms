# st_patricks_day_pub_crawl_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event organizers to collect information from participants about the St. Patrick's Day Pub Crawl. It helps to gather essential details and ensure a smooth execution of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read and fill out the form carefully**.
2. **Complete each section according to the instructions**.
3. **Make sure to fill out all required fields**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **St. Patrick's Day Pub Crawl Information** (`stpatricks_day_pub_crawl_info`):
	* This field is a text box for general information about the event.
	* Provide a brief description of the St. Patrick's Day Pub Crawl.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`):
	* This field is for collecting participant details.
	* Enter your name.
* **Pub Crawl Participants** (`pubcrawl_participants`):
	* This field is a multiple-choice select box.
	* Select all the participants you are registering for the pub crawl.
* **Pub Crawl Message** (`pubcrawl_message`):
	* This field is a text box for any additional comments or messages.
* **Agreed to Terms** (`pubcrawl_agree_terms`):
	* This field is a select box with two options: "I agree to the terms" or "Not agreed to terms".
	* Ensure you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions before selecting this option.
* **Agree to Privacy Policy** (`pubcrawl_agree_privacy`):
	* This field is a select box with two options: "I agree to the privacy policy" or "Not agreed to privacy policy".
	* Review and agree to the privacy policy before proceeding.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Read and understand the terms and conditions.
* Review and agree to the privacy policy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
