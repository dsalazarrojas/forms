# Marathon Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marathon Sponsorship Form is a tool for organizations or individuals to express their interest in sponsoring or supporting a marathon event. This form will help us understand your interest, contact information, and sponsorship details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name and company information in the required fields.
2. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
3. If your company has a specific contact person, please enter their details.
4. Specify the amount you are interested in sponsoring or supporting.
5. Choose the type of event you are interested in sponsoring or supporting (e.g., Yes or No).
6. Select the method of payment you prefer.
7. Indicate the sponsorship term (e.g., Yes or No).
8. Fill in any additional contact information for secondary contacts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number to ensure a smooth communication process.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your company or organization email address.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, optional): If your company has a specific contact person, please enter their name.
* **Name of prophets** (`Name of prophets`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the prophets (this field seems to be a typo and may be intended for "Name of Company" or "Name of Organization").
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's or organization's address (optional but recommended for shipping or payment purposes).
* **City** (`City`, `text`, optional): Enter your city or location.
* **Province** (`Province`, `text`, optional): Enter the province or region you are from.
* **Postal Code** (`Postal Code`, `text`, optional): Enter your postal code or ZIP code.
* **Country** (`Country`, `text`, optional): Enter your country of origin.
* **Email of contact** (`Email of contact`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your contact person.
* **Contact Person 2** (`Contact Person 2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your secondary contact person.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you are interested in sponsoring or supporting.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the event date (Yes or No).
* **Type of event** (`Type of event`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of event (Yes or No).
* **Method of Payment** (`Method of Payment`, `select_one`, optional): Select the method of payment (Yes or No).
* **Sponsorship Term** (`Sponsorship Term`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the sponsorship term (Yes or No).
* **Contact 2** (`Contact 2`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of your secondary contact person.
* **Name of Company** (`Name of Company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization (alternative to "Name of prophets").
* **Name of Contact** (`Name of Contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your contact person.
* **Name Of Contact2** (`Name Of Contact2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your secondary contact person.
* **Contact (4)** (`Contact (4)`, `text`, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the "Contact" field; please ignore it.
