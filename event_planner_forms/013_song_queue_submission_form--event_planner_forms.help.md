# Song Queue Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Song Queue Submission Form is a user-friendly interface where you can submit songs for consideration in a live music event or playlist. Fill out this form to suggest your favorite songs and potentially have them added to the event's playlist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with your submission:
1. **Song Queue Submission**: Enter the song you would like to submit for consideration.
2. **Artist**: Enter the name of the artist that performs the song.
3. **Song Title**: Enter the title of the song you are submitting.
4. **Genre**: Select the genre of the music (e.g. Pop, Rock, Jazz, etc.).
5. **Submitter**: Enter your name as the person submitting the song.
6. **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address (will be used for follow-up communication).
7. **Preferred Play Time**: Select the time of day you would like the song to be played (e.g. AM, PM, Any Time).
8. **Song Request**: Enter any additional information about your song submission, such as why you chose it or why you think it fits the event's theme.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Song Queue Submission**: Enter the title of the song you would like to submit. Please be brief, 10-15 words maximum.
- **Artist**: Enter the name of the artist that performs the song. This helps us ensure the correct song is selected.
- **Song Title**: Enter the actual title of the song. This is the title as it appears on the album or release.
- **Genre**: Select the genre of music that best fits the submitted song.
  • Choose from: Pop, Rock, Jazz, Classical, R&B, Hip Hop
  • Please pick the genre that best fits your submission.
- **Submitter**: Enter your name as the person submitting the song. This helps us keep track of your submission.
- **Submitter Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to follow-up with you about your submission.
- **Preferred Play Time**: Select a time of day when you think the song would fit best in the event's schedule.
  • Choose from: AM, PM, Any Time
  • If you select "Any Time", we'll consider your song for any part of the event.
- **Song Request**: Enter any additional information about your song submission, such as why you chose it or why you think it fits the event theme.
