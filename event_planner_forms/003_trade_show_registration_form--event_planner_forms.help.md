# Trade Show Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees and exhibitors of trade shows. Completing this form will help us provide a more personalized experience and better support your presence at the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal and company details.
2. Choose the trade show you are attending.
3. Select your job title, job type, and other relevant details.
4. Provide a brief description of your company and yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your first name, as this will be used for general purposes and introductions.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, not required): Provide the name of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, not required): Enter your job title for identification purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Enter your company's physical address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, not required): Enter the city of your company's location.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, not required): Enter the state of your company's location.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, not required): Enter your company's zip code.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the type of job or function you perform at your company (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Product Development, or Other).
* **Which trade show are you attending?** (`trade_show`, `select_one`, required): Choose the trade show you are attending from the given options (e.g., International Builders' Show, National Hardware Show, or Other).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, not required): Select the size of your company.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the industry related to your company (e.g., Construction, Manufacturing, Technology, or Other).
* **Booth Number** (`booth_number`, `text`, not required): If you are an exhibitor, enter the number of your booth.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the role you play in your company (e.g., CEO/Founder, Manager, Director, Sales, or Other).
* **Briefly describe your company** (`company_description`, `text`, not required): Give a brief summary of your company.
* **Briefly describe about yourself** (`about_you`, `text`, not required): Share a little about yourself and your position in the company.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, not required): If you have any specific needs or requests, enter them here.
