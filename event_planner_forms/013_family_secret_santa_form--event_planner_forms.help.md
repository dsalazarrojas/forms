# Family Secret Santa Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Secret Santa Form is a tool to collect information from family members participating in a Secret Santa gift exchange. It helps the gift giver know who to choose a gift for, and ensures that each person receives a gift tailored to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Your Name** field.
2. If you want to be contacted by the gift giver, enter your email in the **Your Email** field.
3. Choose your family relation or position in the **Family Member** field.
4. Select your gift preference from the **Gift Preference** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Secret Santa Form (id: 1)**: This is the main title of the form, and you can leave it blank.
* **Your Name (id: 2)**: Enter your name so that the gift giver knows who to give a gift to.
* **Your Email (id: 3)**: If you want to be contacted by the gift giver, enter your email address.
* **Family Member (id: 4)**: Choose your family relation or position to help the gift giver know who to give a gift to.
* **Gift Preference (id: 5)**: Select your gift preference from the options A, B, or C.
* **Gift Preference (options: A, B, C)**: You can choose one or multiple options that best represent your preferences or interests.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the **Your Name** field so that the gift giver knows who to give a gift to.
* If you want to be contacted by the gift giver, fill in the **Your Email** field.
* Choose your gift preference wisely, so that the gift giver can get you a gift you'll enjoy.
* You can choose one or multiple options for the **Gift Preference** field, depending on your preferences.
