# Fashion Show Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Show Feedback Form is designed to gather information from attendees, exhibitors, or staff about their experiences and opinions on the fashion show. This feedback will help the event organizers make data-driven decisions to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fashion Show Feedback Form** (`fashion_show_feedback_form_page_1`, text, required): This is the main section of the form where you can provide your overall feedback on the fashion show. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): This section is for providing your contact information so that the event organizers can reach out to you if needed. Please provide your name, email, or phone number.
* **Fashion Show Details** (`fashion_show_details`, text, required): Please provide any specific details about the fashion show that you would like to comment on, such as what aspects of the show you enjoyed or disliked.
* **Feedback Questions** (`feedback_questions`, text, required): This is a general section for providing any feedback or comments you have on the fashion show. Be as specific as possible and provide any suggestions or criticisms you have.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete and helpful.
* Be as specific and detailed as possible in your responses to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any images, videos, or other materials that can supplement your feedback, consider attaching them separately and referencing them in your responses.
