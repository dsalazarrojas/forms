# patronage_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patronage_request_form is used to collect information from submitters and sponsors for patronage requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your basic information in the User Info page.
2. Provide a description of the event and other relevant information on the Event Info page.
3. Optionally, provide additional information about the sponsor on the Sponsorship Info page.
4. Select a date for your submission on the Submission page.
5. Fill in your additional information on the Submitter Info page.
6. Optionally, provide additional information about the sponsor on the Sponsorship Info page.
7. Agree to the terms and conditions on the Submitter Agreement page.
8. Sign the agreement on the Submitter Signature page.
9. The sponsor will follow similar steps on their respective pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (Page 1)** (`user_info`, `text`, required): Enter your basic information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Event Info (Page 2)** (`event_info`, `text`, required): Provide a description of the event and other relevant information.
* **Sponsorship Info (Page 3)** (`sponsorship_info`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide additional information about the sponsor.
* **Submission (Page 4)** (`submission`, `date`, optional): Select a date for your submission.
* **Submitter Info (Page 5)** (`submitter_info`, `text`, required): Enter your additional information, including your name, email, phone number, and agreement to terms and conditions.
* **Sponsor Info (Page 6)** (`sponsor_info`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide additional information about the sponsor.
* **Sponsorship Info (Page 7)** (`sponsorship_info`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide additional information about the sponsor.
* **Submission (Page 8)** (`submission`, `date`, optional): Select a date for your submission.
* **Submitter Agreement (Page 9)** (`submitter_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submitter Signature (Page 10)** (`submitter_signature`, `note`, required): Sign the agreement.
* **Sponsor Agreement (Page 11)** (`sponsor_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Sponsor Signature (Page 12)** (`sponsor_signature`, `note`, required): Sign the agreement.
* **Submitter Name (Page 13)** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Sponsor Name (Page 14)** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the sponsor's name.
* **Submitter Email (Page 15)** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email.
* **Sponsor Email (Page 16)** (`sponsor_email`, `email`, required): Enter the sponsor's email.
* **Submitter Phone (Page 17)** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Sponsor Phone (Page 18)** (`sponsor_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the sponsor's phone number.
* **Submitter Agreement 2 (Page 19)** (`submitter_agreement_2`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submitter Signature 2 (Page 20)** (`submitter_signature_2`, `note`, required): Sign the agreement.
* **Sponsor Agreement 2 (Page 21)** (`sponsor_agreement_2`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Sponsor Signature 2 (Page 22)** (`sponsor_signature_2`, `note`, required): Sign the agreement.
* **Submitter Name 2 (Page 23)** (`submitter_name_2`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Sponsor Name 2 (Page 24)** (`sponsor_name_2`, `text`, required): Enter the sponsor's name.
