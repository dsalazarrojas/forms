# Event Concession Stand Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Concession Stand Application Form is for vendors who would like to operate a concession stand at an event. It is used to collect necessary information from vendors and will be reviewed and approved for each application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Vendor Details page.
2. Answer the Booth Preferences question.
3. Upload any required documents.
4. Provide your vendor experience.
5. Enter your vendor license information.
6. Answer the Contact Info question.
7. Enter your contact email.
8. Enter your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Details** (`Vendor Details`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first page of the form where you will enter your vendor information. Please fill out this page completely and accurately.
* **Booth Preferences** (`Booth Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose from the options whether you would like to be located in the shade or sun.
* **Required Documents** (`Required Documents`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any required documents, upload them here. Please note that this field is a note field, and you can enter any information you feel is relevant.
* **Vendor Experience** (`Vendor Experience`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you will enter your experience as a vendor. Please describe your experience in this field.
* **Vendor License** (`Vendor License`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your vendor license information here.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `select_one`, required/optional): Answer this question with either "Yes" or "No". 
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address here. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number here. This will be the number we use to contact you regarding your application.
