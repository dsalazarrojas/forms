# Fun Run Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant and pledge information for a fun run event, as well as donor details if you are making a pledge on behalf of someone else.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. For optional fields, provide information if you feel it is relevant and helpful.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Participant Information, text, required): Enter information about the participant, including any relevant details.
* **Pledge Details** (Pledge Details, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the pledge, such as the amount or currency.
* **Amount** (Amount, number, optional): Enter the amount of the pledge.
* **Currency** (Currency, text, optional): Select the currency for the pledge.
* **Message** (Message, text, optional): Enter a message or note about the pledge.
* **Select Participant** (Select Participant, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more participants to associate with the pledge.
* **Select Currency** (Select Currency, select_one, optional): Choose the currency for the pledge.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date Of Birth, date, optional): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Time Of Birth** (Time Of Birth, time, optional): Enter the participant's time of birth.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Event Name** (Event Name, text, optional): Enter the event name.
* **Donor Information** (Donor Information, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the donor.
* **Donor Name** (Donor Name, text, optional): Enter the donor's name.
* **Donor Address** (Donor Address, text, optional): Enter the donor's address.
* **Donor Relationship** (Donor Relationship, text, optional): Select the donor's relationship.
* **Donor Donation** (Donor Donation, text, optional): Enter the amount of the donor's donation.
* **Select Donor Reason** (Select Donor Reason, select_one, optional): Choose the reason for the donation.
* **Select Donor Frequency** (Select Donor Frequency, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of the donation.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the donor or participant.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
