# potluck_invitation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The potluck_invitation_form is designed to invite participants to bring a specific item to a potluck event, promoting a diverse and exciting spread of food for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the form link to access it.
2. Select the item you plan to bring from the options provided (e.g., Apples, Carrots, Grapes, etc.).
3. Enter any relevant details about your chosen item (optional).
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Potluck Invitation** (`potluck_invitation`, text, optional)
This field is the main invitation section where you can provide a personal message or introduction to explain why you're inviting participants to bring a specific item to the potluck.

### **Items to Bring** (`items_to_bring`, select_one, optional)
This field provides a list of suggested food items for guests to choose from. Select the item you plan to bring, and optionally add any relevant details about it in the next field.

### **Item Details** (`item_details`, text, optional)
If you've selected an item to bring, you can add any additional details about it here, such as the quantity, brand, or any specific instructions for preparation or serving.

### **Submit** (`submit`, note, required)
Click this button to submit your response.
