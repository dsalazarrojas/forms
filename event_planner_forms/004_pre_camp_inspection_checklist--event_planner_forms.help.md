# Pre Camp Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide the inspection of a campsite before an event. It assesses the availability of utilities, safety measures, and emergency services to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for event attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the campsite and identify any hazards or issues that need to be addressed.
2. Select the relevant options for each field that best describe the campsite conditions.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
4. Review the form for any discrepancies or inconsistencies.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm the inspection results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campsite Prep**: Select the level of preparation done for the campsite (1, 2, or 3).
	+ 1: Low preparation (e.g., minimal setup, no amenities).
	+ 2: Medium preparation (e.g., some amenities available, some setup).
	+ 3: High preparation (e.g., full setup, all amenities available).
* **Fire Safety**: Check if fire safety equipment is available (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Fire safety equipment is available and in good condition.
	+ No: Fire safety equipment is not available or not in good condition.
* **Utilities**: Enter the number of available utilities (e.g., water, electricity, etc.).
* **Water Sources**: Check if water sources are available (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Water sources (e.g., faucets, wells, etc.) are available and functional.
	+ No: Water sources are not available or not functional.
* **Sewage Disposal**: Enter the number of available sewage disposal facilities.
* **Waste Management**: Check if waste management is in place (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Waste management systems (e.g., trash cans, recycling bins) are available and functional.
	+ No: Waste management systems are not available or not functional.
* **Food And Water**: Check if food and water are available (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Food and water are available and safe for consumption.
	+ No: Food and water are not available or not safe for consumption.
* **Electrical Hazard**: Check if electrical hazards are present (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Electrical hazards (e.g., exposed wires, frayed cords) are present.
	+ No: Electrical hazards are not present.
* **First Aid**: Check if first aid facilities are available (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: First aid facilities (e.g., medical tents, first aid kits) are available and functional.
	+ No: First aid facilities are not available or not functional.
* **Emergency Communication**: Check if emergency communication systems are in place (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Emergency communication systems (e.g., radios, phones) are available and functional.
	+ No: Emergency communication systems are not available or not functional.
* **Emergency Evacuation**: Check if emergency evacuation procedures are in place (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Emergency evacuation procedures (e.g., escape routes, assembly points) are available and clear.
	+ No: Emergency evacuation procedures are not available or not clear.
* **Emergency Services**: Check if emergency services (e.g., medical, fire department) are available (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Emergency services are available and nearby.
	+ No: Emergency services are not available or not nearby.
* **Medical Facilities**: Check if medical facilities are available (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Medical facilities (e.g., medical tents, first aid kits) are available and functional.
	+ No: Medical facilities are not available or not functional.
* **Emergency Vehicles**: Check if emergency vehicles are available (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Emergency vehicles (e.g., ambulances, fire trucks) are available and functional.
	+ No: Emergency vehicles are not available or not functional.
* **First Aid Safety**: Check if first aid safety measures are in place (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: First aid safety measures (e.g., safety equipment, safety protocols) are in place and functional.
	+ No: First aid safety measures are not in place or not functional.
* **Campsite Prep Check**: Check if the campsite is prepared for the event (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: The campsite is prepared and ready for the event.
	+ No: The campsite is not prepared or not ready for the event.
* **Emergency Preparedness**: Check if emergency preparedness measures are in place (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Emergency preparedness measures (e.g., emergency plans, protocols) are in place and functional.
	+ No: Emergency preparedness measures are not in place or not functional.
* **Emergency Response**: Check if emergency response procedures are in place (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Emergency response procedures (e.g., emergency plans, protocols) are in place and clear.
	+ No: Emergency response procedures are not in place or not clear.
* **Campsite Maintenance**: Check if campsite maintenance is up-to-date (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Campsite maintenance is up-to-date and well-maintained.
	+ No: Campsite maintenance is not up-to-date or not well-maintained.
* **Campsite Equipment**: Check if essential campsite equipment is in good condition (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Essential campsite equipment (e.g., generators, water pumps) is in good condition and functional.
	+ No: Essential campsite equipment is not in good condition or not functional.
* **Campsite Facilities**: Check if campsite facilities (e.g., restrooms, showers) are available (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Campsite facilities are available and functional.
	+ No: Campsite facilities are not available or not functional.
* **Campsite Safety**: Check if the campsite is safe for the event (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: The campsite is safe for the event and free from hazards.
	+ No: The campsite is not safe for the event or has hazards present.
* **Campsite Security**: Check if campsite security measures are in place (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Campsite security measures (e.g., fencing, surveillance cameras) are in place and functional.
	+ No: Campsite security measures are not in place or not functional.
* **Campsite Compliance**: Check if campsite compliance measures (e.g., health and safety, environmental) are in place (Yes or No).
	+ Yes: Campsite compliance measures are in place and up-to-date.
	+ No: Campsite compliance measures are not in place or not up-to-date.
