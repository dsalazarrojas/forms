# Thanksgiving Zoom Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees and hosts for a virtual Thanksgiving Zoom party. Please use this form to provide your party details, host information, RSVP status, and other relevant details. We strive to make sure that everyone has a wonderful time and that we can accommodate any special needs or requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Party Details**: Enter a brief description of your party plans.
2. **Host Details**: Provide any information about yourself as the host.
3. **RSVP Status**: Choose whether you will be attending, not attending, or have declined the invitation.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
5. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up calls or questions.
6. **Address**: Enter your address for any deliveries or arrangements.
7. **Note**: Add any additional comments or information.
8. **Date**: Select the date of your party.
9. **Time**: Choose the time of your party.
10. **Timezone**: Enter your time zone (optional).
11. **Guests**: Enter the number of guests attending.
12. **Food**: Select any food options (e.g. Turkey, Vegan, Vegetarian).
13. **Additional Food Comments**: Add any additional comments about your food preferences.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your party details before submitting the form.
