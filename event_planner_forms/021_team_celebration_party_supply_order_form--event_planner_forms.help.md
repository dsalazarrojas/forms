# Team Celebration Party Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order party supplies for a team celebration. Please fill out the form carefully to provide the necessary information for a successful and enjoyable event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the party theme from the options provided.
2. Choose the party supplies you need for the event.
3. Provide your delivery preferences, including the date and time you would like to receive the supplies.
4. Enter your delivery address.
5. Enter your contact email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Theme** (`party_theme`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of celebration you are planning (e.g. Holiday, Birthday, Anniversary).
* **Party Supplies** (`party_supplies`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the types of party supplies you need, such as balloons or streamers.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, text, required: false): Enter any specific instructions or preferences for your delivery, such as "Please deliver to the conference room".
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your party supplies.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to receive your party supplies.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where you would like the party supplies to be delivered.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: true): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any questions or updates.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select all the necessary party supplies to ensure you have everything you need for a successful event.
* If you have any specific requests or instructions for your delivery, please enter them in the "Delivery Preferences" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at [insert contact information].
