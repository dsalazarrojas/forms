# Talent Show Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Show Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to showcase their talent in the talent show. It helps the event organizers to manage the participants, their performances, and other necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your basic information:
	* Enter your full name in the "performer_name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "performer_email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "performer_phone" field.
2.  Select your performance type:
	* Choose the type of talent you will be performing (singer, dancer, musician, etc.) in the "performance_type" field.
3.  Schedule your performance:
	* Enter the date and time of your performance in the "performance_date" and "performance_time" fields.
4.  Add more details:
	* Enter the name of the show in the "show_name" field.
	* Describe your performance in the "description" field.
5.  Add a category:
	* Select the category of your performance (singer, dancer, musician, etc.) in the "category" field.
6.  Upload your performance attachment:
	* Upload a video or image of your performance in the "upload_attachment" field.
7.  Submit your application:
	* Click the "submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Talent Show Registration** (`main_page`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form.
* **Performer Name** (`performer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name here.
* **Performer Email** (`performer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address here.
* **Performer Phone** (`performer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number here.
* **Performance Type** (`performance_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of talent you will be performing (singer, dancer, musician, etc.).
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of your performance.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your performance.
* **Show Name** (`show_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the show here.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Describe your performance here.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the category of your performance (singer, dancer, musician, etc.).
* **Upload Attachment** (`upload_attachment`, text, required: false): Upload a video or image of your performance.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to submit your application.
