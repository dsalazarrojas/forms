# Event Signage Printing Partner Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event signage printing partners who want to register with your organization. It will help us understand their services, contact details, and social media presence to provide better support and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Make sure you are registering as an event signage printing partner.
2. Select all services offered by your organization in the "Services" field. 
3. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field and will be used for communication.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose a category for your organization from the dropdown menu in the "Category" field.
6. Write a brief description of your organization in the "Description" field. This is a required field and will help us understand your services better.
7. Enter your website URL in the "Website" field.
8. If you have a Facebook page, enter your Facebook URL in the "Facebook" field.
9. If you have a Twitter page, enter your Twitter URL in the "Twitter" field.
10. If you have an Instagram page, enter your Instagram URL in the "Instagram" field.
11. If you have a LinkedIn page, enter your LinkedIn URL in the "LinkedIn" field.
