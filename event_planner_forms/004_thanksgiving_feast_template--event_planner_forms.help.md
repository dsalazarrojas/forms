# Thanksgiving Feast Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you organize and plan your Thanksgiving dinner event. It captures essential details about the main course, side dishes, dessert, drinks, guests, and logistical information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Personal Dinner Info**: This is the main section for providing general information about your dinner event. You can add a title, address, and any other details you'd like to capture.
2. **Potluck Info**: Enter a description or details about any potluck dishes you'd like to organize or contribute.
3. **Main Course**: Select one of the provided options for the main course of your meal.
4. **Side Course**: Choose one or multiple options for your side dishes. This is a required field to ensure you have enough variety in your meal.
5. **Dessert**: Select one or multiple options for your dessert course. Although not required, this is a great field to showcase your sweet treats.
6. **Drink**: Choose one or multiple options for your drink offerings.
7. **Guest Info**: Enter the number of guests you're expecting, or add any additional information about your guests in the notes field.
8. **Guest Name**: Add the names of your guests, one per line.
9. **Guest Count**: Confirm the total number of guests.
10. **Event Time**: Enter the time of your event in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
11. **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event in YYYY-MM-DD format.
12. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or reminders about your event.
13. **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, such as phone number or email, in case guests need to reach you.
14. **Address**: Enter the address of your event location.
15. **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
16. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
17. **Website**: Enter your website, if applicable.
18. **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or service assigned to your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Course** (`Main Course`, `select_one`, required): Choose one option for the main course of your meal.
* **Side Course** (`Side Course`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or multiple options for your side dishes.
* **Dessert** (`Dessert`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or multiple options for your dessert course.
* **Drink** (`Drink`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or multiple options for your drink offerings.
* **Guest Info** (`Guest Info`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of guests you're expecting.
* **Guest Name** (`Guest Name`, `text`, optional): Add the names of your guests, one per line.
* **Guest Count** (`Guest Count`, `number`, optional): Confirm the total number of guests.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your event in 24-hour format.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your event in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or reminders about your event.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `note`, optional): Enter your contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of your event location.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Website** (`Website`, `text`, optional): Enter your website, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `text`, optional): Enter the tool or service assigned to your event.
