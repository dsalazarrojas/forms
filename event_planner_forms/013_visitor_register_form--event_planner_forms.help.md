# Visitor Register Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Register Form is a tool used to collect registration information from visitors when they arrive at a facility. It's designed to gather basic details about each visitor and their purpose of visit. The form helps to create a record of each visit, which can be used for various administrative and analytical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Visitor Register Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First Name** in the first field. This field is used to capture the first name of the visitor.
2.  Enter your **Last Name** in the second field. This field is used to capture the last name of the visitor.
3.  Enter your **Email** in the third field. This field is used to capture the email address of the visitor.
4.  Select the **Visit Date** in the fourth field. This field is used to record the date of the visit.
5.  Enter the **Visit Time** in the fifth field. This field is used to record the time of the visit.
6.  Enter the **Contact Person** in the sixth field. This field is used to identify the person responsible for the visit.
7.  Enter the **Purpose of Visit** in the seventh field. This field is used to describe the reason for the visit.
8.  Enter any additional **Notes** in the eighth field. This field is used for any extra information about the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, Optional): Enter the first name of the visitor.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, Optional): Enter the last name of the visitor.
- **Email** (Email, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the visitor.
- **Visit Date** (Visit Date, date, Optional): Select the date of the visit.
- **Visit Time** (Visit Time, time, Optional): Enter the time of the visit.
- **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, Optional): Enter the person responsible for the visit.
- **Purpose of Visit** (Purpose of Visit, text, Optional): Enter the reason for the visit.
- **Notes** (Notes, note, Optional): Enter any extra information about the visit.
