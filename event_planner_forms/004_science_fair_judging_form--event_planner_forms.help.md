The form is designed to be used by judges or evaluators to provide feedback on a science fair project. Each field in the form is intended to gather specific information to help the student improve their project. The form should only be filled out by one or multiple judges, and it's assumed that each judge will answer based on their own criteria and scoring system.

This guide will walk you through each field of the form and explain what information to provide for each.

# Science Fair Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide feedback on a science fair project, helping the student to improve their project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the project presentation or display.
2. Fill out the form according to your observations and scoring system.
3. Be sure to fill out all the required fields (if any).
4. If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the appropriate fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Science Fair Judging Form

* **Science Fair Judging Form** (1, text, required): Describe your initial impressions of the project. What was your first reaction after reading or viewing the project?
* **Science Fair Judging Form Criteria 1** (2, text, required): What criteria did you use to judge this project? For example, was it focused on scientific method, creativity, or presentation?
* **Science Fair Judging Form Criteria 1 Score** (3, number, required): Provide a score out of 10 for the first criterion.
* **Science Fair Judging Form Criteria 2** (4, text, required): What other criteria did you use to evaluate the project?
* **Science Fair Judging Form Criteria 2 Score** (5, number, required): Provide a score out of 10 for the second criterion.
* **Science Fair Judging Form Criteria 3** (6, text, required): What other criteria did you use to evaluate the project?
* **Science Fair Judging Form Criteria 3 Score** (7, number, required): Provide a score out of 10 for the third criterion.
* **Science Fair Judging Form Comments** (8, text, required): Include any comments or suggestions for the student.
* **Science Fair Judging Form Suggestion** (9, text, required): What suggestions do you have for the student to improve their project?

Note: If you are unsure about any of the criteria or scores, you can leave a comment in the comments field to provide further explanation.
