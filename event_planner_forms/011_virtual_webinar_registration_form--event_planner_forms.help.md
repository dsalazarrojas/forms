# Virtual Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Webinar Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending a virtual webinar. This form helps us to gather attendee details, including their name and contact information, which is then used to facilitate the logistics and communication for the virtual event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name in the field marked "First Name".
2. Fill out your Last Name in the field marked "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email in the field marked "Email".
4. Enter your Phone number in the field marked "Phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Fill out your first name, which is the name given to you at birth.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Fill out your last name, which is the name given to you at birth or the name you are commonly known by.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will be used for communication and updates about the virtual webinar.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number that you would like to be contacted on, if we need to reach you about the virtual webinar.
