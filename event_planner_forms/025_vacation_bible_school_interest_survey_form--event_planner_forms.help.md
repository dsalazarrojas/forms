# Vacation Bible School Interest Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interest in participating in Vacation Bible School (VBS) events at our organization. Please fill out this form to express your interest and provide any additional information we need to know.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title to help us verify the form submission.
2. Select the category of interest (Event, General, or Other).
3. Provide a brief description of your interest in VBS, selecting all that apply (General, Event, or Other).
4. Enter your email address (this is required for communication purposes).
5. If you'd like, you can provide your phone number for us to contact you.
6. Select a date for your VBS event interest.
7. Select a time for your VBS event interest.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your interest (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (form_title, text, required): Enter a brief title for this form submission.
- **Category** (category, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your interest in VBS (Event, General, or Other).
- **Description** (description, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to describe your interest in VBS (General, Event, or Other).
- **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your VBS interest.
- **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like for us to contact you directly.
- **Date** (date, date, required): Select a date for your VBS event interest.
- **Time** (time, time, required): Select a time for your VBS event interest.
- **Note** (note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your VBS interest.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you about your interest.
* Select a specific date and time for your VBS event interest.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, please include them in the "Note" field.
