# Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Enrollment Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians of children attending the summer camp. It helps the camp staff to prepare for the child's stay, understand their needs, and ensure their safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Parent Name**, **Parent Email**, and **Parent Phone** fields with the relevant information for the parent or guardian submitting the form.
2. If the child has a **Guardian**, fill out the **Guardian Name** and **Guardian Phone** fields with their information.
3. Provide the **Child Name** and **Child's Medical Record** in the corresponding fields.
4. Describe any additional **Activity Details** for your child's experience at the camp.
5. Submit the form and review it for completeness before saving it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): If you have a guardian for your child, enter their name here. 
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional): If you have a guardian for your child, enter their phone number here.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's name.
* **Child Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or allergies your child has.
* **Activity Details** (`activity_details`, text, optional): Let us know about any specific activities your child would like to participate in or any special accommodations they may need.
