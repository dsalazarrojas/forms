## Graduation Guest Book Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graduation Guest Book Form is designed to collect information about graduates and their schools. It is used for reference and to create a comprehensive database of graduates from a school.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in its entirety as best as you can. If you need to add a note or a comment, please do so in the `graduation_message` field.
2.  Ensure that all fields marked as required are completed.
3.  Double-check that you have entered the correct information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it is spelled.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it is spelled.
*   **Message for the Graduate** (`graduation_message`, `text`, required): This is an optional field for you to leave a personal message for the graduate. You can include any special messages, congratulations, or well-wishes you want to convey.
*   **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date of your graduation using the format `month/day/year` (e.g., June 12, 2022).
*   **Graduation Time** (`graduation_time`, `time`, optional): If you want to specify the time of your graduation, please enter the time in 12-hour format (e.g., 10:00 AM).
*   **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required): Please enter the year you graduated.
*   **Day of the Week of Graduation** (`graduation_day_of_week`, `select_one`, required): Choose the day of the week you graduated.
*   **Month of Graduation** (`graduation_month`, `select_one`, required): Choose the month you graduated from the list.
*   **Graduation Class Name** (`graduation_class_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the class you graduated in.
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the school you graduated from.
*   **Graduation Day** (`graduation_day`, `select_one`, required): Choose the day of the month you graduated.
*   **Graduation Class Name 2** (`graduation_class_name_2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the class you graduated in.
*   **School Name** (`graduation_school_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the school you graduated from.

## Tips

*   Please double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   If you need help or have any questions, don't hesitate to ask.
*   Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
*   If you have any additional information you'd like to add, please use the `graduation_message` field.
