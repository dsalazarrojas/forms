# Centerpiece Design Competition Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Centerpiece Design Competition Entry form is designed to collect information from artists and designers who wish to participate in the Centerpiece Design Competition. This form is used to gather essential details about the entrant, their design, and their submission. By filling out this form, entrants can showcase their artistic skills and potentially win prizes and recognition in the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) in their labels.
2. For fields with dropdown menus (such as "Years of Experience" and "Production Time"), select the corresponding option that best fits your situation.
3. For fields with file uploads (such as "Photo Upload - Front View", "Photo Upload - Side View", and "Photo Upload - Detail Close-up"), upload clear and high-quality photos of your centerpiece design.
4. For fields with video uploads (such as "Video Upload - Optional"), you can upload a short video showcasing your design.
5. Review and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes - I agree" for the "Terms and Conditions Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Submission Date (\*):** Enter the date when you submit your entry to the competition.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Participant Name (\*):** Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business or Studio Name:** If you have a business or studio, enter its name.
* **Email Address (\*):** Enter your contact email address for notifications.
* **Phone Number (\*):** Enter your contact phone number.
* **Website or Portfolio URL:** Enter the URL of your online portfolio or website, if applicable.
* **Social Media Handle:** Enter your social media handle, if applicable.
* **Years of Experience (\*):** Select the number of years you have been designing centerpieces.
	+ Options: Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, 6-10 years, More than 10 years
* **Competition Category (\*):** Select the category for your centerpiece design.
	+ Options: Wedding centerpieces, Corporate event centerpieces, Holiday seasonal centerpieces, Garden outdoor centerpieces, Modern contemporary designs, Traditional classic designs, Open category
* **Design Title:** Enter a creative name for your centerpiece design.
* **Design Description:** Provide a detailed description of your centerpiece concept.
* **Design Dimensions:** Enter the height, width, and depth measurements of your centerpiece.
* **Primary Materials Used:** Select all the primary materials used in your design.
	+ Options: Fresh flowers, Dried flowers, Artificial flowers, Foliage and greenery, Candles, Glass vessels, Metal elements, Wood elements, Fabric ribbons, Natural stones, Other
* **Design Inspiration:** Describe what inspired this design.
* **Estimated Cost per Centerpiece:** Enter the approximate material cost to create one centerpiece.
* **Production Time (\*):** Select the time it takes to create one centerpiece.
	+ Options: Under 30 minutes, 30 minutes to 1 hour, 1 to 2 hours, 2 to 4 hours, Over 4 hours
* **Photo Upload - Front View (\*):** Upload a clear photo of the front view of your centerpiece.
* **Photo Upload - Side View:** Upload a clear photo of the side view of your centerpiece.
* **Photo Upload - Detail Close-up:** Upload a photo showing the intricate details of your centerpiece.
* **Video Upload - Optional:** Upload a short video showcasing your design.
* **Previous Competition Experience:** Select if you have entered design competitions before.
	+ Options: Yes - Multiple competitions, Yes - One competition, No - First time entering
* **Awards or Recognition:** List any relevant awards or recognition you have received.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement (\*):** Review and agree to the competition rules and terms.
* **Promotional Use Consent (\*):** Consent to have your design photographed and used for promotional purposes.
* **Additional Comments:** Provide any additional information you would like to share.

Please note that some fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) indicating that they are required fields.
