# Virtual Trick Or Treat Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration and information sharing tool for Virtual Trick Or Treat Parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Party Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of the party, if you're planning to host one.
2. **Party Date (2)**: Select the date you plan to host or attend the party.
3. **Party Time (3)**: Choose the time slot for the party.
4. **Party Location (4)**: Enter the location of the party.
5. **RSVP (5)**: Confirm if you or a group will be attending. Enter the number of guests attending.
6. **Contact Name (6)**: Enter the name of the person hosting or organizing the party.
7. **Contact Phone (7)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
8. **Contact Email (8)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
9. **Share on Facebook (9)**: If you'd like to share party details on social media, choose your preferred platform.
10. **Party Description (10)**: Enter a brief description of the party, if you're hosting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Details**: A brief description of the party.
* **Party Date**: The date when the party will take place.
* **Party Time**: The time slot for the party.
* **Party Location**: The location of the party.
* **RSVP**: Confirm if you or a group will be attending the party.
* **Contact Name**: The name of the person hosting or organizing the party.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Share on Facebook**: Share party details on Facebook, Twitter, or Email.
* **Party Description**: A brief description of the party, if you're hosting.
