# Social Event Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Event Survey Form is designed to collect feedback and information about social events. It's intended for event organizers and planners to gather data on event outcomes, satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide feedback on the event's outcomes: Select the best description of the event's outcomes from the list.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the event: Enter a numerical value to rate your satisfaction.
3. Specify areas for improvement: Select one or more options that you think the event could improve on.
4. Provide contact information: Enter your email and phone number so that we can contact you for more information.
5. Provide event details: Enter the date, time, location, and description of the event.
6. Specify attendees' details: Enter the number of attendees and their type (in-person or virtual).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social Event Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can provide feedback on the event's outcomes.
	+ **Event Feedback**: Enter your feedback on the event's outcomes.
* **Event Feedback**: This field is used to rate your satisfaction with the event.
	+ **Impact Measure**: Enter a numerical value to rate your satisfaction.
* **Event Outcomes**: Select the best description of the event's outcomes.
	+ **Positive**: The event was successful and met its objectives.
	+ **Negative**: The event did not meet its objectives.
	+ **Neutral**: The event's outcomes were neutral or inconclusive.
* **Impact Measure**: This field is used to measure the impact of the event.
	+ **Impact**: Enter a numerical value to measure the event's impact.
* **Event Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the event.
	+ **Satisfaction**: Enter a numerical value to rate your satisfaction.
* **Areas Improvement**: Specify areas where the event could improve.
	+ **Areas Improvement**: Select one or more options that you think the event could improve on.
	+ **Food**: The food quality and quantity.
	+ **Music**: The music played during the event.
	+ **Ambiance**: The overall atmosphere of the event.
	+ **Timing**: The timing of the event.
	+ **Organization**: The organization and coordination of the event.
	+ **Communication**: The communication among attendees and organizers.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event (party, meeting, seminar, etc.).
* **Attendees Type**: Select the type of attendees (in-person or virtual).
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Attendees Count**: Enter the number of attendees.
* **Budget**: Enter the budget for the event.
* **Form Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the event.
* **Survey Comments**: Enter any comments about the survey.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest feedback.
* Ensure that you have permission to access and fill out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
