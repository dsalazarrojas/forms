# Ticket Purchase Form With Skrill - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users for ticket purchases related to an event, specifically when the event is ticketed. The form collects user details such as Skrill card information and event-related information to process ticket purchases with Skrill.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the ticket option you wish to purchase.
2. Enter your Skrill card number, which is required to complete the transaction.
3. Enter your email address, which is also required.
4. Enter your phone number, which is required for communication purposes.
5. Enter your name as it appears on your card.
6. Enter your card expiration date and card security code (CVV) for verification.
7. Enter the ticket price and number of tickets you wish to purchase.
8. Enter the name of the event you are purchasing tickets for.
9. If applicable, select the date and time of the event you are purchasing tickets for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_planner_forms_1** (`Select Multiple`, required): Choose the ticket option you wish to purchase.
* **Skrill Card Number** (`Text`, required): Enter your Skrill card number to complete the transaction.
* **Email** (`Email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`Text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Name on Card** (`Text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your card for verification.
* **Card Expiration Date** (`Date`, required): Enter your card expiration date for verification.
* **Card CVV** (`Text`, required): Enter your card security code (CVV) for verification.
* **Ticket Price** (`Number`, required): Enter the ticket price you wish to purchase.
* **Ticket Quantity** (`Number`, required): Enter the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
* **Event Name** (`Text`, required): Enter the name of the event you are purchasing tickets for.
* **Event Date** (`Date`, not required): If applicable, enter the date of the event you are purchasing tickets for. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Event Time** (`Time`, not required): If applicable, select the time of the event you are purchasing tickets for. (Note: This field is not required.)

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your Skrill card number correctly to avoid any issues with the transaction.
- Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
- Be aware that the event date and time fields are optional and only required if applicable to the event.
