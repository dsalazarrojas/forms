# Virtual Happy Hour Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register for virtual happy hours that fit your schedule and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number (if applicable).
4. Select your time zone from the given options.
5. Choose a join time that suits you best.
6. Select a date to join the virtual happy hour.
7. For the join date and time, choose from the suggested options.
8. Enter a message (if you have any specific requests or information to share).
9. Optionally, enter an additional message (this field can be ignored).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (1)**: Enter your name to identify yourself to other participants.
* **Email (2)**: Enter a valid email address to be contacted.
* **Phone Number (3)**: If you have a phone number, please provide it. This will be used to contact you.
* **Time Zone (4)**: Choose your time zone to help us plan the virtual happy hour in your region.
* **Join Time (5)**: Select a time that works best for you to attend the virtual happy hour.
* **Join Date (6)**: Choose the date of the virtual happy hour you want to join.
* **Join Time Date (7)**: Select a time and date combination to join the virtual happy hour.
* **Join Date Time (8)**: Choose from suggested join date and time options to join the virtual happy hour.
* **Message (9)**: If you have any special requests or information, please share it with us.
* **Your Message (10)**: Optionally, you can enter additional information or requests. (This field can be ignored if not needed.)
* **Your Message (11)**: This field has an identical label to 9, likely a typo; ignore it and do not fill it.
