# online-wedding-payment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "online-wedding-payment-form" is designed to collect payment information from clients for wedding services. This form helps event planners to efficiently track and manage payment records for wedding events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required: false): This field is for entering the client's details. Please provide the client's name or reference number for easy identification.
2. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose the method of payment (Credit card, PayPal, or Bank transfer) to be applied.
3. **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the payment amount in a numerical value. This will help calculate the total service fee.
4. **Service Fee** (`service_fee`, number, required: false): Add a service fee for the service provided. This value is usually a fixed amount.
5. **Payment Dates** (`payment_dates`, date, required: false): Select the date on which the payment was made.
6. **Payment Times** (`payment_times`, time, required: false): Choose the time of payment.
7. **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, required: false): Enter any special payment instructions or conditions that may affect the payment process.
8. **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Additional notes or comments about the payment.
9. **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the client's email address for contact.
10. **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide the client's phone number for further contact.
11. **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the wedding service provided.
12. **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required: false): Check the terms and conditions of payment.
13. **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, required: false): Obtain the client's signature on the payment agreement.
14. **Service Fee Total** (`service_fee_total`, number, required: false): Calculate the total service fee.
15. **Service Fee Per Attendee** (`service_fee_per_attendee`, number, required: false): Calculate the service fee per attendee.
16. **Payment Total** (`payment_total`, number, required: false): Calculate the total payment.
17. **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false): Select the payment date.
18. **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, required: false): Select the payment time.
19. **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, Partially paid).
20. **Payment Notes** (`payment_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional payment notes or comments.
