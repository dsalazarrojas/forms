# Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a graduation ceremony invitation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the title of the graduation ceremony in the "Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form Title" field.
2. Enter the name of the graduate in the "Name" field.
3. Select the date of the graduation ceremony from the calendar in the "Date" field.
4. Choose the time of the graduation ceremony from the options in the "Time" field.
5. Enter the location of the graduation ceremony in the "Location" field.
6. Provide any additional details about the graduation ceremony in the "Details" field.
7. Enter the venue of the graduation ceremony in the "Venue" field.
8. If applicable, enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone" field.
9. If applicable, enter the email address of the contact person in the "Email" field.
10. If applicable, provide more details about the venue in the "Venue Details" field.
11. Indicate whether you will be attending the graduation ceremony in the "Attendees" field.
12. If you will be attending, select the relationship you have with the graduate in the "Attendee Relationship" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Graduation Ceremony Invitation Form Title** (Text, Required): Enter the title of the graduation ceremony.
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the graduate.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Select the date of the graduation ceremony from the calendar.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Choose the time of the graduation ceremony from the options.
* **Location** (Text, Required): Enter the location of the graduation ceremony.
* **Details** (Text, Required): Provide any additional details about the graduation ceremony.
* **Venue** (Text, Required): Enter the venue of the graduation ceremony.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): If applicable, enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Venue Details** (Text, Optional): If applicable, provide more details about the venue.
* **Attendees** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate whether you will be attending the graduation ceremony.
* **Attendee Relationship** (Select One, Required): If you will be attending, select the relationship you have with the graduate.
