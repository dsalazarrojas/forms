# Corn Hole Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in a Corn Hole tournament. It collects information about the participant, their team, and division-specific details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information about yourself and your team.
2. Choose the correct options for division-specific details.
3. Make sure to review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the participant. You can enter any text relevant to your participation in the tournament.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed on any tournament materials or communications.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for the name of the participant. This might be a nickname or a preferred name to be used in tournament communications.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `text`, optional): List any team members that are participating in the tournament.
* **Team Member Names** (`team_member_names`, `text`, optional): List the names of team members who are participating in the tournament.
* **Team Member Roles** (`team_member_roles`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the roles of team members participating in the tournament, such as Team Captain or Team Member.
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, optional): Enter your team name.
* **Division** (`division`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your division.
* **Division ID** (`division_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the ID of your division.
* **Division Size** (`division_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the size of your division, such as 2, 3, or 4.
* **Division Name** (`division_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your division.
* **Division Date** (`division_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your division event.
* **Division Time** (`division_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your division event.
* **Division Notes** (`division_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your division event.
* **Division Status** (`division_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of your division, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Participant Fee** (`participant_fee`, `number`, optional): Enter the fee you paid for participation in the tournament.
* **Participant T-Shirt Size** (`participant_tshirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the size of T-shirt you need for the participant.
* **Participant DOB** (`participant_dob`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of birth of the participant.
* **Team T-Shirt Size** (`team_tshirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the size of T-shirt you need for your team.
* **Division T-Shirt Size** (`division_tshirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the size of T-shirt you need for your division.
* **Tournament Status** (`tournament_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the overall status of the tournament, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the participant.
* **Team Notes** (`team_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the team.
* **Participant Fee** (`participant_fee`, `number`, optional): Enter the fee you paid for participation in the tournament.
* **Participant T-Shirt Size** (`participant_tshirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the size of T-shirt you need for the participant.
* **Division Notes** (`division_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the division event.
* **Division Status** (`division_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of your division, such as Active or Inactive.
