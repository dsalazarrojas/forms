# Ski Club Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ski Club Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from users to organize and manage ski club activities and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to communicate with you about ski club events and activities.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will be used to contact you about ski club events and activities.
5. Select your grade level from the options in the "Grade Level" field. Choose one of the available options ('1', '2', '3', '4').
6. Enter the email of the person who will be planning the ski club events in the "Event Planner Email" field. This is not required, but it helps us to contact the event planner.
7. Enter any additional information about your interests in the "Student Interests" field. This will help us to understand your preferences and tailor ski club activities accordingly.
8. Enter the name of the person who is your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the person who is your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Grade Level** (select_one): Select your grade level. Choose one of the available options ('1', '2', '3', '4').
* **Event Planner Email** (text): Enter the email of the person who will be planning the ski club events. This is not required.
* **Student Interests** (text): Enter any additional information about your interests.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text): Enter the name of the person who is your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the person who is your emergency contact.
