# Marriage Preparation Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marriage Preparation Course Registration Form is a tool used by the marriage preparation course administrators to collect data about couples participating in the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name and last name in the "First name" and "Last name" fields respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to contact you with course updates and notifications.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Select your marriage date from the calendar in the "Marriage date" field.
5. Enter the location where you will get married in the "Marriage location" field.
6. If applicable, enter the name of the person performing the marriage ceremony in the "Marriage officiant" field.
7. Enter the number of children you plan to have (or have already) in the "Number of children" field.
8. Enter any additional notes you would like to share with the course administrators in the "Notes" field.
9. If you wish, enter any additional notes specific to your relationship with your partner in the "Couples note" field.
10. Finally, enter the name and phone number of your counselor (if applicable) in the "Counselor name" and "Counselor phone" fields respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Marriage date** (`marriage_date`, date, required: false): Select your marriage date from the calendar.
* **Marriage location** (`marriage_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you will get married.
* **Marriage officiant** (`marriage_officiant`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of the person performing the marriage ceremony.
* **Number of children** (`number_of_children`, number, required: false): Enter the number of children you plan to have (or have already).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes you would like to share with the course administrators.
* **Couples note** (`couples_note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes specific to your relationship with your partner.
* **Counselor name** (`counselor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your counselor (if applicable).
* **Counselor phone** (`counselor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your counselor (if applicable).
