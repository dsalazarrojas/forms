# Easter Drive Thru Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for registering individuals for an Easter event, likely an Easter egg hunt or craft session, where users will decorate Easter eggs.

## How To Complete This Form

To register for the Easter event, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Select the Date and Time slot you'd like to attend.
5. Select a preferred Egg Color for decoration.
6. Choose a preferred Egg Decoration Style.
7. Specify your Picture Preferences (e.g., would you like to be photographed or videographed).
8. Select any activities you'd like to participate in during the event (if applicable).
9. Enter any additional Comments, if desired.
10. (Optional) Assign a tool for use during the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your real name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter the email address you check regularly, in case we need to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for event organizers to reach you.
* **Date**: Select the date you'd like to attend the event.
* **Time**: Select the time slot you'd like to attend the event.
* **Egg Color**: Choose a color you'd like your eggs to be decorated with (if applicable).
* **Egg Decoration Style**: Select the style of decoration you'd like to have on your eggs (if applicable).
* **Picture Preferences**: Specify if you'd like your eggs to be photographed or videographed.
* **Easter Activities**: Select any activities you'd like to participate in during the event (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Add any additional information or comments about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool**: (Optional) Enter the tool you'd like to be assigned for the event.
