# current_event_summary_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The current_event_summary_form is designed to collect information about a current event, including the date, a brief summary, location, attendees, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the event in the `event_date` field. This field is optional, but it will help provide context for the rest of the information.
2. Write a brief summary of the event in the `event_summary` field. This field is also optional, but it will help provide a clear understanding of the event.
3. Enter the location of the event in the `event_location` field. This will help identify where the event took place.
4. List the attendees of the event in the `event_attendees` field. This field is optional, but it will help provide a list of people involved in the event.
5. Enter the contact information of the event in the `event_contact` field. This field is optional, but it will help provide a point of contact for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **event_date** (date, optional)
The `event_date` field is where you enter the date of the event. This will help provide context for the rest of the information.

### **event_summary** (text, optional)
The `event_summary` field is where you write a brief summary of the event. This will help provide a clear understanding of what happened.

### **event_location** (text, optional)
The `event_location` field is where you enter the location of the event. This will help identify where the event took place.

### **event_attendees** (text, optional)
The `event_attendees` field is where you list the attendees of the event. This will help provide a list of people involved in the event.

### **event_contact** (text, optional)
The `event_contact` field is where you enter the contact information of the event. This will help provide a point of contact for further information.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form, especially for fields like event location and event contact.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to reach out to the form's creator or your supervisor for guidance.
- The form is designed to be used for general information collection, so be creative and use it as needed!
