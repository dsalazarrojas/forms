# straw_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **straw_poll** form is designed to collect information from users on their education level and political preferences. This form is part of a larger analysis and decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus.
2. Fill in any text fields, such as the **other_political_party** field if you do not see any of the options listed.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **education_level** (Select One): Choose your level of education, either High or Low.
- **level_of_education** (Select One): Select the level of education you are referring to in your answer.
- **political_party** (Select Multiple): Select all the political parties that you support or align with.
- **other_political_party** (Text): If there is a political party that you support or align with but is not listed, please specify it here.
- **other_political_party_hint** (Text): If you need more information to help with your answer, please provide any additional details.
