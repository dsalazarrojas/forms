# High School Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for students to register themselves and their parents/guardians for a high school camp. It gathers essential information for the camp organizers to prepare for the camp.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name, email, and contact number to provide your personal contact information.
2. If applicable, enter your parent/guardian's name, email, and contact number.
3. Select your grade level from 6th to 12th.
4. Enter your school name, school contact number, and school email if your school is affiliated with the camp.
5. Choose the camp dates by selecting the start and end dates of the camp.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.
7. Provide your emergency contact's name, phone number, and email for any unexpected situations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact number for camp staff to reach out to you.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (email, optional): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Student Grade Level** (select_one, optional): Choose your grade level from 6th to 12th.
* **School Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **School Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your school's contact number.
* **School Email** (email, optional): Enter your school's email address.
* **Camp Start Date** (date, optional): Select the start date of the camp.
* **Camp End Date** (date, optional): Select the end date of the camp.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields (Full Name and Email).
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the camp organizers at the email address provided.
* This form is for high school students only. Please ensure you are eligible to attend the camp before registration.
* The camp dates and other details will be sent to you through the email provided upon registration confirmation.
