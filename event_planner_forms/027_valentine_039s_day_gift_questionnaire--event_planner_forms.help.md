# valentines_day_gift_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users about their gift preferences for Valentine's Day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter any additional context or information about the gift you are planning in the "User Input" field.
2. Let us know if you have any gift suggestions by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Gift Suggestions" field.
3. Choose your favorite color from the list in the "Favorite Color" field.
4. Provide any details about the occasion in the "Occasion Details" field.
5. Enter your date of birth for any gift restrictions or preferences based on age in the "Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (text, optional): Enter any additional context or information about the gift you are planning.
* **Gift Suggestions** (select multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any gift suggestions.
* **Favorite Color** (select one, optional): Choose your favorite color, which may influence gift choice.
* **Occasion Details** (note, optional): Provide any details about the occasion for which the gift is being planned.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth for any gift restrictions or preferences based on age.
