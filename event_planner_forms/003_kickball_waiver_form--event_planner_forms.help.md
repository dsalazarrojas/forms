# Kickball Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kickball Waiver Form is used to collect player information and waiver consent for participation in a kickball game. It is essential for us to have this information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all players.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Player Info section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the Emergency Contact information, including their name, phone number, and relation to you.
3. (Optional) If you have any additional waiver information, please enter it in the Waiver Info section.
4. Sign the Signature section by typing your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Info (player_info)** (`text`, required): This is where you will enter your name, email, and phone number. Please make sure to include all three fields accurately.
* **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)** (`text`, required): Provide the name, phone number, and relation to you (e.g., parent, guardian, or friend) for your emergency contact. This is crucial for us to reach them in case of an emergency.
* **Waiver Info (waiver_info)** (`text`, optional): If you have any additional information related to your waiver, please enter it here.
* **Signature (signature)** (`text`, required): Sign this form by typing your name. This is a confirmation that you have read and understood the waiver terms.
