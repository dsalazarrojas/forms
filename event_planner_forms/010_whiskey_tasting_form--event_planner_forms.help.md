# Whiskey Tasting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Whiskey Tasting Form is a tool used to collect feedback and notes from attendees on the whiskey tasting experience. This form helps event planners gather information on the attendee's perception of the whiskey, providing valuable insights to improve future event selections and pairings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant options for each attribute:
    *   Aroma: Choose "Yes" if you detect a noticeable aroma, and "No" if not.
    *   Taste: Select all the taste profiles that you detect.
    *   Finish: Choose "Yes" if you experience a noticeable finish, and "No" if not.
    *   Appearance: Describe the appearance of the whiskey, including its color.
    *   Body: Describe the body of the whiskey.
    *   Nose: Describe the nose of the whiskey, including any notable notes.
    *   Palate: Describe the palate of the whiskey.
    *   Palate Accent: Describe any notable accents or characteristics in the palate.
    *   Palate Character: Describe the overall character of the palate.
    *   Finish Length: Choose "Yes" if the finish is long, and "No" if not.
    *   Finish Intensity: Choose "Yes" if the finish is intense, and "No" if not.
    *   Nose Intensity: Choose "Yes" if the nose is intense, and "No" if not.
    *   Nose Accent: Describe any notable accents or characteristics in the nose.
    *   Nose Character: Describe the overall character of the nose.
    *   Finish Character: Describe the character of the finish.
    *   Nose Length: Choose "Yes" if the nose is long, and "No" if not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Aroma (whiskey_tasting_form_aroma)**: Describe your experience with the aroma of the whiskey.
    *   If you choose "Yes," describe any notable notes or attributes.
*   **Taste (whiskey_tasting_form_taste)**: Describe your experience with the taste of the whiskey.
    *   If you choose multiple profiles, select all that apply.
*   **Finish (whiskey_tasting_form_finish)**: Describe your experience with the finish of the whiskey.
    *   If you choose "Yes," describe its character.
*   **Appearance (whiskey_tasting_form_appearance)**: Describe the appearance of the whiskey.
    *   Describe its color and any other notable characteristics.
*   **Body (whiskey_tasting_form_body)**: Describe the body of the whiskey.
    *   Provide a detailed description.
*   **Nose (whiskey_tasting_form_nose)**: Describe the nose of the whiskey.
    *   If you choose "Yes," describe its character and any notable notes.
*   **Palate (whiskey_tasting_form_palate)**: Describe the palate of the whiskey.
    *   If you choose "Yes," describe its character.
*   **Palate Accent (whiskey_tasting_form_palate_accent)**: Describe any notable accents or characteristics in the palate.
*   **Palate Character (whiskey_tasting_form_palate_character)**: Describe the overall character of the palate.
*   **Finish Length (whiskey_tasting_form_finish_length)**: Choose "Yes" if the finish is long, and "No" if not.
*   **Finish Intensity (whiskey_tasting_form_finish_intensity)**: Choose "Yes" if the finish is intense, and "No" if not.
*   **Nose Intensity (whiskey_tasting_form_nose_intensity)**: Choose "Yes" if the nose is intense, and "No" if not.
*   **Nose Accent (whiskey_tasting_form_nose_accent)**: Describe any notable accents or characteristics in the nose.
*   **Nose Character (whiskey_tasting_form_nose_character)**: Describe the overall character of the nose.
*   **Finish Character (whiskey_tasting_form_finish_character)**: Describe the character of the finish.
*   **Nose Length (whiskey_tasting_form_nose_length)**: Choose "Yes" if the nose is long, and "No" if not.

## Tips

*   Be honest and provide detailed descriptions in the text fields.
*   Choose "Yes" or "No" only for the select one fields.
*   Select all relevant options for the multiple select fields.
