# Ticket Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for ticket purchase purposes only. It is essential to ensure that you have the necessary information to process your ticket purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer's first name in the "Customer First Name" field.
2. Enter your customer's last name in the "Customer Last Name" field.
3. Enter your customer's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your customer's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Check the "Consent to Terms and Conditions" box to agree to our terms and conditions.
6. Select the number of tickets you wish to purchase in the "Purchase Ticket(s)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Customer Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, note, optional): This field is not required for ticket purchase, but if you have a physical address, you can enter it here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Consent to Terms and Conditions** (`consent_to_terms`, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
* **Purchase Ticket(s)** (`purchase_tickets`, select_multiple, required): Select the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
