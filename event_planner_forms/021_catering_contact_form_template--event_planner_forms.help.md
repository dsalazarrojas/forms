# Catering Contact Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Contact Form is designed to gather essential information from customers or clients about their catering needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your full name in the Full Name field.
2. Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
3. Provide your phone number in the Phone Number field.
4. If applicable, enter your company or organization name in the Company Name field.
5. Select the subject that best describes your inquiry or request (e.g., General Inquiry, Event Booking, Menu Questions, etc.).
6. Choose the type of event you are hosting (e.g., Wedding, Corporate Event, Birthday Party, etc.).
7. If you know the date of your event, enter it in the Event Date field.
8. Estimate the number of guests expected and enter it in the Estimated Guest Count field.
9. Enter the city or area where the event will take place in the Venue Location field.
10. Describe your inquiry or questions in the Message field.
11. Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone Call, Text Message, etc.).
12. Specify the best time for the caterer to contact you (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
13. If you know, select the source of your referral (e.g., Website, Social Media, Referral, etc.).
14. If you are interested, select True to subscribe to the caterer's newsletter for updates and promotions.
15. Provide any additional comments or information in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name, first and last name combined.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address to be contacted by the caterer.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number for the caterer to contact you.
* **Company Name** (text, optional): Enter your company or organization name if applicable.
* **Subject** (select_one, required): Select the subject that best describes your inquiry or request.
* **Event Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of event you are hosting.
* **Event Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your event if known.
* **Estimated Guest Count** (number, optional): Estimate the number of guests expected.
* **Venue Location** (text, optional): Enter the city or area where the event will take place.
* **Message** (text, required): Describe your inquiry or questions in detail.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Best Time to Contact** (select_one, optional): Specify the best time for the caterer to contact you.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (select_one, optional): Select the source of your referral.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (select_one, optional): Select True to subscribe to the caterer's newsletter.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) in the field explanation.
