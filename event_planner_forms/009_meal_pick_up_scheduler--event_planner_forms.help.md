# Meal Pick Up Scheduler - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Pick Up Scheduler form is a tool for scheduling meal pickups, allowing you to collect crucial information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the desired date for your meal pickup order from the calendar.
2. Choose whether you prefer a "Yes" or "No" answer to the "Pickup Time" question.
3. Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
4. Select the type of meal you prefer.
5. Specify the quantity of the meal you need.
6. Provide your phone number and email address.
7. Add any additional notes about your order.
8. Confirm your order by selecting "True" or "False".
9. If you've selected "Other" for the "Assigned" field, provide additional information in the corresponding fields.
10. Finally, submit or save your order draft.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Date
* **Order Date** (1, date): Enter the date you would like to schedule your meal pickup.

### Pickup Time
* **Pickup Time** (2, select_multiple): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference for a specific pickup time.

### Name
* **Name** (3, text): Enter your name for contact purposes.

### Meal Type
* **Meal Type** (4, select_one): Select the type of meal you prefer (e.g., Apple, Broccoli, Carrot, Fruits, Grains, Salad).

### Quantity
* **Quantity** (5, number): Enter the quantity of the meal you need.

### Phone
* **Phone** (6, text): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.

### Email
* **Email** (7, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Notes
* **Notes** (8, note): Add any additional notes about your order.

### Confirm
* **Confirm** (9, select_one): Select "True" to confirm your order, or "False" to indicate changes or cancel your order.

### Assigned
* **Assigned** (10, select_one): If you want to assign your order to someone else, select "Other". If you select "Other", you can add the assigned person's information in the following fields.
* **Other Assigned** (11, text): Enter the name of the person assigned to take care of your order.
* **Other Phone** (12, text): Enter the phone number of the assigned person.
* **Other Email** (13, email): Enter the email address of the assigned person.
* **Other Notes** (14, note): Add any notes about the person assigned to your order.

### Submit
* **Submit** (15, select_one): Choose "Submit" to finalize your order, "Save Draft" to save your order for later, or "Cancel" to discard your changes.
