# peninsula-golf-tournament-entry-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for players interested in participating in the Peninsula Golf Tournament. It allows them to provide their details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with the required information.
2. Ensure you enter accurate and complete details for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (player_name, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Player Email** (player_email, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication regarding the tournament.
* **Tournament Name** (tournament_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the tournament you wish to participate in.
* **Entry Fee** (entry_fee, number, optional): Enter the fee you are paying to participate in the tournament (currently $50.00 USD).
* **Entry Date** (entry_date, date, optional): Select the date you plan to participate in the tournament.
* **Participant Tee Time** (participant_tee_time, select_one, optional): Choose the time slot you prefer to play in (e.g., 6:00 AM - 6:30 AM, 7:30 AM - 8:00 AM).
* **Participant Golf Cart** (participant_golf_cart, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you will be using a golf cart during the event, or 'No' if you will not be using a golf cart.

## Tips

* Please ensure the accuracy of your submitted information, as it will be used to manage your participation in the tournament.
* If you encounter any issues with the form, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
