# event_planning_quote_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event planners to provide quote details for their events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "true" in the form.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting to ensure all fields are filled and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Planning Quote Form** (1): This is the title of the form.
* **Event Name** (2): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Description** (3): Describe the event in a few words.
* **Event Date** (4): Enter the date of the event (in the format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Time** (5): Enter the time of the event (in 24-hour format: HH:MM).
* **Event Location** (6): Enter the location of the event.
* **Planner Response** (7): Indicate if you are the event planner.
* **Planner Phone** (8): Enter the planner's phone number.
* **Planner Email** (9): Enter the planner's email address.
* **Planner Address** (10): Enter the planner's address.
* **Contact Name** (11): Enter the name of the person you are contacting about the event.
* **Contact Email** (12): Enter the email address of the person you are contacting.
* **Contact Phone** (13): Enter the phone number of the person you are contacting.
* **Contact Company** (14): Enter the name of the company or organization of the person you are contacting.
* **Contact Note** (15): Add any additional comments about the contact.
* **Contact Acceptance** (16): Indicate if you accept the quote or not (Yes/No).
* **Contact Decline** (17): Indicate if you decline the quote or not (Yes/No).
* **Quote Amount** (18): Enter the amount of the quote.
* **Quote Terms** (19): Enter any terms or conditions of the quote.
* **Quote Notes** (20): Add any additional comments or notes for the quote.
* **Event Terms** (21): Enter any terms or conditions of the event.
* **Payment Method** (22): Indicate the method of payment.
* **Payment Terms** (23): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Additional Terms** (24): Add any additional terms or conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (25): Enter any terms or conditions of the event or quote.

## Tips
* Double-check your submission before submitting to ensure all fields are filled and accurate.
* Make sure to provide clear and complete information for the event planner and contact information.
* Review the quote terms and conditions carefully to ensure understanding of the quote details.
