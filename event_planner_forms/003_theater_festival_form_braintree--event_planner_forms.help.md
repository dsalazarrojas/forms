## Purpose
The form is designed to collect participant information for the theater festival. Please fill it out to provide your details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with the required and optional information as instructed.
2.  Ensure you provide accurate contact information to be reached by the festival organizers.
3.  Select your performance requirements from the given options.
4.  Enter your performance schedule in the correct format.
5.  Provide any special instructions if necessary.

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, `true`): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the festival.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, `true`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your participation.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact-number`, `text`, `true`): Enter your phone number to be reached by the festival team.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date-of-birth`, `date`, `true`): Enter your date of birth as it might be relevant to your performance requirements.
*   **Performance Requirements** (`performance-requirements`, `select_one`, `true`): Select one or more of the following:

    + Singer
    + Dancer
    + Musician
    + Theater
*   **Performance Schedule** (`performance-schedule`, `time`, `true`): Enter your performance schedule in the format `HH:MM - HH:MM`.
*   **Special Instructions** (`special-instructions`, `text`, `false`): If you have any additional instructions or requests, enter them here.
