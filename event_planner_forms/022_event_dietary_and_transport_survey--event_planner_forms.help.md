# Event Dietary and Transport Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event_dietary_and_transport_survey form is designed for event planners and organizers to collect information about attendees' dietary needs, transportation preferences, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and understand the purpose and relevance to your event.
2. Fill out the Introduction section if you wish to provide background information about the event.
3. In the Preferences and Needs section, select your meal preferences and dietary needs.
4. In the Contact Information section, enter your email address, phone number, and transport time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **Introduction** (`intro`, `text`, optional): A brief introduction to the event, if desired.
### Page 2: Meal Preferences
* **Meal Preferences** (`meal_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): Select your meal preferences from the available options.
### Page 3: Dietary Needs
* **Dietary Needs** (`dietary_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your dietary needs from the available options.
### Page 4: Transport Details
* **Transport Details** (`transport_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific transport details.
### Page 5: Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your transport preferences.
### Page 6: Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
### Page 7: Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
### Page 8: Transport Time
* **Transport Time** (`transport_time`, `time`, optional): Enter your transport time.
