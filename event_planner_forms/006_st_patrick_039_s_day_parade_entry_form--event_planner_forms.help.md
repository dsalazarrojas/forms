# St. Patrs Day Parade Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The St. Patrs Day Parade Entry Form is used by individuals or groups interested in participating in the St. Patrs Day Parade to submit their entry information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review and confirm that you have all the necessary information required for the form.
2. Fill out the form with your information and select the relevant options for each field.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name(s) and any additional information about yourself or your group.
* **Parade Details** (`parade_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Briefly describe your performance or entry in the parade.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a contact method for event organizers to reach you, such as an email or phone number.
* **Sponsor Info** (`sponsor_info`, text, required: false)
	+ If you are representing a sponsor, provide their name and contact information.
* **Team Details** (`team_details`, text, required: false)
	+ List the members of your team, if applicable.
* **Performance Details** (`performance_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe your performance or entry in the parade.
* **Performance Type** (`performance_type`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select one of the following options:
		+ Irish Dance
		+ Irish Music
		+ Irish Food and Drink
		+ Other
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, required: false)
	+ Indicate the time you would like to perform, or the time range you are available.
* **Performance Duration** (`performance_duration`, number, required: false)
	+ Enter the number of minutes you would like to perform.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for your entry.
