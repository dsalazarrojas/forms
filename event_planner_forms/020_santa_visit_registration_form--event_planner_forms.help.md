# Santa Visit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Santa Visit Registration Form is designed to gather information from parents/guardians about their child's visit to Santa. This form helps Santa's team understand the child's preferences and needs to provide a personalized and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have filled out only one form per child.
2. Enter your email address in the "Parent Email" field.
3. Provide any relevant information about your relationship with the child in the "Guardian Information" field.
4. Enter the child's name(s) in the "Child Names" field.
5. Select the child's date of birth in the "Child DOB" field.
6. Choose the date for the event in the "Event Date" field.
7. Select the time for the visit in the "Visit Time" field.
8. Choose any gifts you'd like Santa to consider when visiting in the "Gift Preferences" field.
9. Enter any special messages or notes for Santa in the "Visit Message" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
11. Select the tool assigned to the child's visit in the "Assigned Tool" field.
12. (Optional) Enter the form ID for any specific reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <ul>
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian who is responsible for this visit.
* **Guardian Information** (`guardian_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about your relationship with the child. This can include your name, address, or contact information.
* **Child Names** (`child_names`, text, optional): Enter the name(s) of the child who will be visiting Santa.
* **Child DOB** (`child_dob`, date, optional): Select the date of birth for the child.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Choose the date for the event or visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Select the time for the visit.
* **Gift Preferences** (`gift_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any gifts you'd like Santa to consider when visiting.
* **Visit Message** (`visit_message`, text, optional): Enter any special messages or notes for Santa to consider during the visit.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any important contact.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to the child's visit.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter the form ID for any specific reference.
</ul>

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not relevant or required for your specific situation. However, please fill out only one form per child to ensure the visit details are accurate.
