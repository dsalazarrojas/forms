# State Football Championship T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering T-shirts for the state football championship event. It will ask you for team name, color, and other important order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your team's name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter your team's color in the "Team Color" field.
3. Select your T-shirt size in the "T Shirt Size" field.
4. Enter the quantity of T-shirts you'd like to order in the "Order Quantity" field.
5. Choose the color of your T-shirts in the "Order Color" field.
6. Select the size of your T-shirts in the "Order Size" field.
7. If you'd like to add a special message or design to your T-shirts, use the "Shirt Text" field.
8. Choose your T-shirt design option in the "Shirt Design" field.
9. Enter your T-shirt color preference in the "Shirt Color" field.
10. Provide your order date and time in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields (optional).
11. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Order Notes" field (optional).
12. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
13. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
14. Confirm your order in the "Order Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name (Team Name, text, required):** Enter your team's name as it appears on the registration form.
* **Team Color (Team Color, text, required):** Enter your team's color as it appears on the registration form.
* **T Shirt Size (T Shirt Size, select_one, required):** Choose the size of your T-shirt (Extra Small, Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large).
* **Order Quantity (Order Quantity, number, required):** Enter the number of T-shirts you'd like to order.
* **Order Color (Order Color, select_multiple, required):** Choose the color of your T-shirts (Navy Blue, Forest Green, White).
* **Order Size (Order Size, select_one, required):** Choose the size of your T-shirts (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Shirt Text (Shirt Text, text, optional):** Add any special text you'd like to print on your T-shirts.
* **Shirt Design (Shirt Design, select_one, required):** Choose your T-shirt design option (Default, Custom).
* **Shirt Color (Shirt Color, select_multiple, required):** Choose the color of your T-shirts (Navy Blue, Forest Green, White).
* **Order Date (Order Date, date, optional):** Enter the date you'd like to receive your T-shirts.
* **Order Time (Order Time, time, optional):** Enter the time you'd like to receive your T-shirts.
* **Order Notes (Order Notes, note, optional):** Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, required):** Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required):** Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Order Confirmation (Order Confirmation, select_one, optional):** Confirm your order with "Yes" or cancel with "No".
