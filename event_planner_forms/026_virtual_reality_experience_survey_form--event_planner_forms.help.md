# virtual reality experience survey form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users who have experienced our virtual reality product or service. The information collected will help us understand their opinions and thoughts on our product, allowing us to improve and refine it for better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Survey Start**: This field is optional and allows you to enter any comments or information about your experience before starting the form. You can enter any relevant details about your experience, such as what you were doing in the virtual reality environment.
2. **Rating**: Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. This will help us understand your level of satisfaction with our product.
3. **Product Experience**: Please describe your experience with our virtual reality product or service. What did you think about it? What did you like or dislike?
4. **Comments**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience. You can use this field to share your thoughts on what we did well and what we could improve on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Start** (`survey_start`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information or comments about your experience before starting the form.
- **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your experience from 1 to 5.
- **Product Experience** (`product_experience`, `text`, optional): Describe your experience with our virtual reality product or service.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
