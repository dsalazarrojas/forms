# football_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event planners to enter information for a football tournament. It gathers data for teams participating in the tournament, including stage numbers and team names.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by ensuring that you have all the necessary information for the tournament, including team names and stage numbers.
2. Fill in the fields as follows:
   * Enter the stage number for each stage of the tournament in the corresponding fields (Stage Number, Stage 2, Stage 3, Stage 4).
   * Enter the names of the teams participating in the tournament in the corresponding fields (Team 1, Team 2, Team 3, Team 4, Team 5, Team 6, Team 7, Team 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stage Number**: Enter the number of the stage in the tournament. This field is optional, but it's essential to fill it in for accurate data entry.
* **Team 1**: Enter the name of the first team participating in the tournament.
* **Team 2**: Enter the name of the second team participating in the tournament.
* **Stage 2**: Enter the number of the second stage in the tournament. This field is optional.
* **Team 3**: Enter the name of the third team participating in the tournament.
* **Team 4**: Enter the name of the fourth team participating in the tournament.
* **Stage 3**: Enter the number of the third stage in the tournament. This field is optional.
* **Team 5**: Enter the name of the fifth team participating in the tournament.
* **Team 6**: Enter the name of the sixth team participating in the tournament.
* **Stage 4**: Enter the number of the fourth stage in the tournament. This field is optional.
* **Team 7**: Enter the name of the seventh team participating in the tournament.
* **Team 8**: Enter the name of the eighth team participating in the tournament.

## Tips
- Double-check the information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Use this form only for a football tournament.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to your tournament schedule or consult with the event planner. 

Note: The form has a large number of fields, which might seem overwhelming. To help, focus on filling in one stage and teams at a time. This will help you keep track of the information and avoid mistakes.
