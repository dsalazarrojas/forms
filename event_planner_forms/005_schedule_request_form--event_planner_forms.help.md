# Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Schedule Request Form is used to create a schedule request for an event. It is intended for users to fill out the form to submit their schedule requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Schedule Request Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Schedule Request" page.
2. Fill out the fields on this page, including "Schedule Type", "Frequency", "Start Date", "Start Time", "Duration", "Schedule Frequency 2", "Schedule Start Date 2", "End Date", and "End Time".
3. Once you have filled out the "Schedule Request" page, select the "Schedule Requester" page and enter your name and department.
4. Next, select the "Schedule Approver" page and enter the name and department of the person who will be approving your schedule request.
5. Finally, select the "Schedule Status" and "Priority" pages and choose the status and priority of your schedule request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Request**: This is the main page to fill out for your schedule request.
	+ Enter a brief description of your schedule request.
* **Schedule Type**: Select the type of schedule you are requesting (Monthly, Weekly, Yearly).
* **Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your schedule (e.g., Daily, Biweekly, Monthly).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your schedule request.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your schedule request.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your schedule request.
* **Schedule Frequency 2**: Select the frequency of your schedule (Daily, Biweekly, Monthly).
* **Schedule Start Date 2**: Enter the start date of your next scheduled event.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your schedule request.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your schedule request.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments for your schedule request.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes for your schedule request.
* **Requester**: Enter your name.
* **Department**: Enter your department.
* **Approver**: Enter the name and department of the person who will be approving your schedule request.
* **Status**: Select the status of your schedule request (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Priority**: Select the priority of your schedule request (Low, Medium, High).
* **Reason**: Enter the reason for your schedule request.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting the schedule.
* **Request Time**: Enter the time you are requesting the schedule.
* **Requester Comments**: Enter any comments from the person requesting the schedule.
* **Approver Comments**: Enter any comments from the person approving the schedule.
* **Schedule Requester 2**: Enter the name of the person requesting the schedule.
* **Schedule Department 2**: Enter the department of the person requesting the schedule.
* **Schedule Approver 2**: Enter the name and department of the person approving the schedule.
* **Schedule Status 2**: Select the status of the schedule request (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Schedule Priority 2**: Select the priority of the schedule request (Low, Medium, High).

## Tips

* Make sure you are filling out the form for the correct purpose and reason.
* Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for approval.
