# Secret Santa Form For Adults - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a Secret Santa gift exchange. It helps to identify the participants, their preferences, and the event details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure you provide information for all required fields.
2. **Be accurate and honest**: Provide accurate and honest information in the form to avoid confusion and ensure a smooth exchange process.
3. **Submit your form**: Once complete, click the submit button to send your information to the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This is how the organizers will contact you with event details and any updates.
* **Workplace**: If you work in a company or organization, provide the name of your workplace to help identify you.
* **Gift Limit**: If applicable, specify the maximum value of a gift you are comfortable receiving. This helps the gifter avoid overspending.
* **Event Name**: Enter a name for the event to help identify it in case there are multiple Secret Santa events running at the same time.
* **Event Date**: Provide the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gift Price**: If you want to specify a maximum price for a gift, enter it here.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organizers.
* Be respectful of others' preferences and gift limits when choosing a gift for them.
