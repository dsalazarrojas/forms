# international_fan_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Fan Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information about the fans of the organization. This survey aims to collect data on fan demographics, preferences, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your fan info in the **fan info** field (field 1). This field is not required, but it's a good idea to provide some basic information about yourself.
2. Answer the question "Do you consider yourself a fan?" in the **questions** field (field 2). You can select either "Yes" or "No".
3. If you are a fan, select your favorite driver from the list in the **favorite driver** field (field 3).
4. Enter your age in the **age** field (field 4). This field is not required, but it's a good idea to provide your age for demographic purposes.
5. Select your location from the list in the **location** field (field 5).
6. If you are a fan, select your favorite team from the list in the **favorite team** field (field 6).
7. Enter your email address in the **email** field (field 7). This field is not required, but it's a good idea to provide your email address if you want to receive updates from the organization.
8. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field (field 8). This field is not required, but it's a good idea to provide your phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **fan info** (`fan_info`, text, not required): Enter any information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **questions** (`questions`, select_multiple, not required): Do you consider yourself a fan? Select "Yes" if you are a fan, "No" if you're not.
