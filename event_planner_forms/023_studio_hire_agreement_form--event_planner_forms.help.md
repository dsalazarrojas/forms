# Studio Hire Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Studio Hire Agreement Form is used to collect information from clients or customers about their studio hire needs for events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with all required information (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter the event name, hire date, and studio location details.
3. Provide your contact name and contact details (name, email, and phone number).
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`studi_hire_agreement_form_page_1_label` (text, required): Enter the name of the event or workshop you are organizing.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date_label` (date, required): Select the date you plan to hire the studio.
* **Studio Location** (`studio_location_label` (text, required): Enter the studio address or location where the event will take place.
* **Start Time** (`start_time_label` (time, required): Select the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time_label` (time, required): Select the end time of the event.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_name_label` (text, required): Enter your name as the event contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email_label` (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone_label` (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Information** (`studio_hire_agreement_form_page_2_label` (text, optional): This field is for any additional information or notes about the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event coordinator or support team for assistance.
