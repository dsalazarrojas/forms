# Sample Catering Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event planners to collect information for catering orders. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that your catering order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, paying attention to required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your customer's details in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Select the date and time you would like to place your order on in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
5. Enter any additional event details in the "Event Details" field.
6. Choose your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
7. Choose the delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields.
8. Enter the location where your order will be delivered in the "Delivery Location" field.
9. Choose the status of your order in the "Order Status" field.
10. Enter any customer IDs in the "Customer ID" field (if applicable).
11. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field (if applicable).
12. Enter any additional customer notes in the "Customer Note 2" field (if applicable).
13. Enter any event notes in the "Event Note" field (if applicable).
14. Select the date of your event on in the "Event Date" field.
15. Select the time of your event in the "Event Time" field.
16. Enter the location of your event in the "Event Location" field.
17. Select the type of your event in the "Event Type" field.
18. Enter the number of guests attending your event in the "Event Guests" field.
19. Choose the type of diet you or your guests require in the "Event Diet" field.
20. Enter the catering service you require in the "Event Catering Service" field.
21. Choose your preferred payment method 2 in the "Payment Method 2" field.
22. Enter the name of the person placing the order in the "Customer Name 2" field (if applicable).
23. Enter the email of the person placing the order in the "Customer Email" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name**: The name of the person or organization placing the order.
- **Contact Email**: The email of the person or organization placing the order.
- **Order Date**: The date you would like to place your order on.
- **Order Time**: The time you would like to place your order on.
- **Event Details**: Any additional information about the event you are catering for.
- **Event Note**: Any additional information about the event (if applicable).
- **Event Date**: The date of the event.
- **Event Time**: The time of the event.
- **Event Location**: The location of the event.
- **Event Type**: The type of event (e.g. wedding, birthday, party).
- **Event Guests**: The number of guests attending the event.
- **Event Diet**: The type of diet you or your guests require (e.g. vegetarian, vegan).
- **Event Catering Service**: The type of catering service you require.
- **Payment Method**: The method of payment you prefer (e.g. Visa, MasterCard, American Express).
- **Payment Method 2**: The method of payment 2 you prefer (e.g. Visa, MasterCard, American Express).
- **Customer ID**: Any customer ID number (if applicable).
- **Customer Phone**: The phone number of the person placing the order (if applicable).
- **Customer Note 2**: Any additional customer notes (if applicable).
- **Delivery Date**: The date you would like the order delivered on.
- **Delivery Time**: The time you would like the order delivered on.
- **Delivery Location**: The location where the order will be delivered.
- **Order Status**: The current status of your order.
- **Customer Name 2**: The name of the person placing the order 2 (if applicable).
- **Customer Email**: The email of the person placing the order 2 (if applicable).
