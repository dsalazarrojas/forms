# Christmas Party Theme Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow employees to vote for their preferred Christmas party theme. The form is used to gather information on the themes they like best and any additional details that might be useful for party planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name if you want to be associated with your vote for tracking purposes.
2. Enter your email address for confirmation or null.
3. Choose your preferred theme from the options provided:
	* Winter wonderland
	* Classic red and green
	* Retro holiday
	* Elegant gold and white
	* Ugly sweater party
4. If you have a backup choice, you can select another theme from the options provided.
5. Provide a brief note explaining why you prefer the chosen theme or null.
6. Choose whether you will bring a guest or not.
7. If you are bringing a guest, provide the number of guests.
8. List any food allergies or preferences you have, or null.
9. Add any additional comments or suggestions you have, or null.
10. Allow the organizers to contact you about the vote by selecting 'True' or 'False'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter name**: Optional name for tracking purposes. If you don't enter a name, your vote will be anonymous.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for confirmation or leave this field blank.
* **Preferred theme**: Choose the Christmas party theme you like best:
	+ Winter wonderland
	+ Classic red and green
	+ Retro holiday
	+ Elegant gold and white
	+ Ugly sweater party
* **Backup theme choice**: If you have a backup theme, select it from the options above.
* **Reason for choice**: Briefly explain why you prefer the chosen theme or leave this field blank.
* **Will you bring a guest?**: Choose 'True' if you plan to bring a guest, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Number of guests**: If you are bringing a guest, provide the number of guests you plan to bring.
* **Dietary restrictions**: List any food allergies or preferences you have, or leave this field blank.
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions you have, or leave this field blank.
* **Consent to contact**: Choose 'True' if you allow the organizers to contact you about the vote, and 'False' otherwise.

Note: This form is required to be completed by all employees who want to participate in the Christmas party theme voting process.

This field-by-field explanation will help ensure that you complete the form accurately and help the organizers plan a great Christmas party for all attendees.

Tips:

* Make sure to choose your preferred theme carefully, as it will influence the party atmosphere.
* If you are bringing a guest, please provide the correct number of guests to help with headcount.
* If you have dietary restrictions, please list them so the organizers can take them into account.
* Allow the organizers to contact you to discuss any further details about the party.
