# Ceramic Painting Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ceramic Painting Workshop Registration Form is used to collect information from participants who want to register for a workshop on ceramic painting. Fill out this form to secure your spot and receive workshop details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Register" button to start the registration process.
2. Review the form's sections and fill out the required fields.
3. Select a preferred workshop date.
4. Choose your experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
5. Check any materials you need from the studio.
6. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please note them.
7. Confirm your participation by agreeing to the liability waiver agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can receive workshop updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, including your country code if you're calling from outside your local area. This will help us contact you if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Provide the name and phone number of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Preferred Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required): Select the available date for the workshop that suits you best.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your experience level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Materials Needed** (`materials_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the materials you require from the studio for the workshop.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities** (`allergies_or_sensitivities`, `text`, optional): Note any allergies or sensitivities you have to specific materials or supplies.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (`liability_waiver_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to participate in the workshop and understand the terms and conditions.
