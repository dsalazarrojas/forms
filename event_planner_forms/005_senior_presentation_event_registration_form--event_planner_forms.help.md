# Senior Presentation Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Presentation Event Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending or speaking at a senior presentation event. This form helps to gather contact details and event-specific information to facilitate event organization and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name: Enter your first name in the "First Name" field and your last name in the "Last Name" field.
2.  Provide your contact information: Enter your email address in the "Email" field and your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3.  Choose your event details: Enter the date of the event in the "Date" field.
4.  Add event details: Enter the title of the event in the "Event Title" field and provide a brief description in the "Description" field.
5.  Identify the presenter: Enter the name of the event presenter in the "Presenter" field.
6.  Specify the location: Enter the location of the event in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time of the event (this field is optional, but it's not clear why it's not required).
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, optional): The title of the event.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): A brief description of the event.
* **Presenter** (`presenter`, text, optional): The name of the presenter.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): The location of the event.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you fill in all the required fields (if any) to avoid any inconvenience.
*   If you are a presenter, please make sure to fill in the "Presenter" field with your correct name.
*   Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
*   Make sure to enter the correct date and event title for the event you are registering for.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
