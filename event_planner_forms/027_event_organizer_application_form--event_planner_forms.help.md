# Event Organizer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Organizer Application Form is a tool designed to assist users in organizing events by collecting essential information about the event, the applicant, and other relevant details. This form is used to streamline the event planning process and ensure that all necessary information is gathered in one place.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start by providing your name and contact details.
- Fill in the Event Description, specifying the event's purpose, date, and time.
- Choose whether the event is located in a specific location or not.
- Indicate the organizer's requirements for the event.
- Finally, sign the form digitally by providing your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact email address, phone number, or any other communication means.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `note`, required: false): Briefly describe the event you are planning. Please include the purpose, date, and time of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Check whether the event will be held at a specific location or not.
* **Organizer Requirements** (`organizer_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check any specific requirements you need from the event organizer.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign the form by providing your email address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you are the event organizer.
* **Event Manager** (`event_manager`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you are the event manager.
* **Venue Manager** (`venue_manager`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you are the venue manager.
