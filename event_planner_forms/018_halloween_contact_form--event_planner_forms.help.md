## Halloween Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending the Halloween event. Please ensure you have a legitimate reason for attending the event by selecting a response for the RSVP form field. Provide your actual name in the Guest Name field. Use a valid email address in the Email field for correspondence. Enter a clear message in the Message field to provide any additional context or details. You can opt to be contacted via phone or email using the Contact Method field, and enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **RSVP Form (Yes/No)**: Select one of the two options, "Yes" or "No", to indicate your intention to attend the event.
2. **Guest Name**: Enter your actual name as it will be used for correspondence and event records.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address to which the event organizers can send updates, reminders, and other relevant information.
4. **Message**: Briefly describe any additional information or context about your visit that you want to share with the event planners.
5. **Contact Method**: Select the preferred method of communication to be contacted for event updates and information (Phone or Email).
6. **Phone Number**: If you selected "Phone" as your contact method, enter your phone number for the event planners to contact you.
7. **Date**: If you are providing your date of birth for any reason, please ensure it is accurate and valid.
