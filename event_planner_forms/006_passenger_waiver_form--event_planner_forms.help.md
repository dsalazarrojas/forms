# Passenger Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Passenger Waiver Form is used for passengers to submit their information for events. This form is designed to ensure that all necessary details are collected in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Passenger Waiver Form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form carefully to ensure you are entering the correct information.
*   Enter your details accurately and completely.
*   Fill in the required fields, as indicated by the red asterisk (\*).
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Address** (`passenger_address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address.
*   **City** (`passenger_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
*   **Province** (`passenger_province`, select_one, required): Select your province of residence.
*   **Country** (`passenger_country`, text, required): Enter your country of residence.
*   **Postal Code** (`passenger_postal_code`, text, required): Enter your postal/zip code.
*   **Date of Birth** (`passenger_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Time of Birth** (`passenger_time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your time of birth.
*   **Email** (`passenger_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
*   **Phone** (`passenger_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Airline Name** (`airline_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the airline you are traveling with.
