# Escape Room Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information and permissions from students and their guardians before participating in the escape room event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the team size in the "Team Size" field.
3. Enter the team name in the "Team Name" field.
4. Enter the parent/guardian name in the "Parent Name" field.
5. Indicate whether the student has guardian permission ("Yes" or "No") in the "Guardian Permission" field.
6. Select if the student has given their permission ("Yes" or "No") in the "Student Permission" field.
7. If the student has given their permission, enter the date of permission in the "Student Permission Date" field.
8. If the student has given their permission, enter the time of permission in the "Student Permission Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification or school records.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required/optional): Select the number of students in the team.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the team.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who will be responsible for the student's participation.
* **Guardian Permission** (`guardian_permission`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the guardian has given permission for the student to participate, "No" otherwise.
* **Student Permission** (`student_permission`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the student has given their permission to participate, "No" otherwise.
* **Student Permission Date** (`student_permission_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the student gave their permission (if they have given their permission).
* **Student Permission Time** (`student_permission_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the student gave their permission (if they have given their permission).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If the student is under 18 years old, guardian permission is mandatory.
* If the student is 18 years or older, student permission is mandatory.
* If the student has given their permission, make sure to enter the correct date and time of permission.
