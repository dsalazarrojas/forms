# thanksgiving_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Contact Form is a simple form designed to allow guests to share their plans with the host, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable holiday gathering.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, which is a simple text field for your identity.
2. Provide a valid **Email**, which will be used to respond to you directly (if required).
3. Optionally, enter a contact **Phone Number** for faster communication.
4. Add any additional **Comment**, if you have one (e.g., dietary restrictions or special requests).
5. Type your **Message** to the host, sharing your plans or any important details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (<code>first_name</code>):**
	+ A text field to input your first name.
* **Email (<code>email</code>):**
	+ A field to enter a valid email address for direct communication.
* **Phone (<code>phone</code>):**
	+ Optional phone number for faster contact.
* **Comment (<code>comment</code>):**
	+ A field for any additional comments or special requests.
* **Message (<code>message</code>):**
	+ A text field to share your plans or important details with the host.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any special requests or dietary restrictions, use the **Comment** field to inform the host.
- Use the **Message** field to discuss plans or share important information with the host.
