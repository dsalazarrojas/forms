# Black Friday Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Checklist form is designed to aid event planners in collecting essential details for the successful execution of a Black Friday event. It covers critical aspects such as event details, target budget, promotional channels, and contingency planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Event Details**: Enter the date of the event and the store or website name to ensure smooth planning and logistics.
2.  **Target Budget**: If you have a specific budget for promotions, enter it here in USD.
3.  **Priority Items to Acquire**: List the top items you aim to secure during the event.
4.  **Early Entry Plan**: Decide if early entry is being used for customers.
5.  **Promotional Channels**: Select all applicable promotional channels such as email, social media, paid ads, SMS, and in-store promotions.
6.  **Staffing Notes**: Provide details on staff numbers and shift plans.
7.  **Packing List**: List the essential items to bring on the day of the event.
8.  **Contingency Plan**: Summarize your backup plans in case of any unexpected situations during the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event date** (`event_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date of the event for efficient planning and scheduling.
*   **Store or Website Name** (`store_or_website`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the store or website where the event will take place.
*   **Target Budget** (`target_budget`, `number`, **Optional**): If you have a target budget for promotions, enter it here in USD.
*   **Priority Items to Acquire** (`priority_items`, `text`, **Optional**): List the top items you aim to secure during the event.
*   **Early Entry Plan** (`early_entry_plan`, `select_one`, **Required**): Indicate if you are offering early entry to customers.
*   **Promotional Channels** (`promotional_channels`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select all applicable promotional channels such as email, social media, paid ads, SMS, and in-store promotions.
*   **Staffing Notes** (`staffing_notes`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide details on staff numbers and shift plans.
*   **Packing List** (`packing_list`, `text`, **Optional**): List the essential items to bring on the day of the event.
*   **Contingency Plan** (`contingency_plan`, `text`, **Optional**): Summarize your backup plans in case of any unexpected situations during the event.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately to avoid missing critical information.
*   Double-check your answers for accuracy to avoid confusion or miscommunication.
*   Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is captured correctly.
