# Webinar Quiz Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant feedback and information after a webinar. It's meant to be filled out by attendees who want to share their thoughts, opinions, or suggestions about the webinar content, structure, or any other aspect of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Quiz Questions** field with your answers to the questions that were asked during the webinar. You can type out your response in the text area provided.
2. If you want to provide more information about yourself, you can fill out the **Participant Information** field. This field is optional, and you can type out any details you feel are relevant.
3. If you have a phone number, you can enter it in the **Participant Phone** field. This field is also optional.
4. If you have any comments, suggestions, or feedback about the webinar, you can type them out in the **Feedback** field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Questions**: This field is where you'll be answering questions that were asked during the webinar. Type out your response in the provided text area.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is meant for you to provide more information about yourself, such as your name, email, or any other relevant details. If you choose not to fill this field, it's not a problem.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your phone number. If you don't have a phone or prefer not to share, you can leave it blank.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This field is for any comments, suggestions, or feedback you have about the webinar. It's entirely up to you to decide what to share or keep to yourself.
