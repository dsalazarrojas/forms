# quilt_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quilt_intake_form is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential information from customers for their quilt-making needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose your design preference from the options provided.
2. Select the desired quilt size, if applicable.
3. Choose your preferred fabric color(s).
4. Select your preferred quilting style.
5. Pick the quilt pattern(s) you would like.
6. Specify the quilting services you require.
7. Choose your fabric choice(s).
8. Select the deadline for your quilt project.
9. Optionally, add any notes, comments, or additional requirements.
10. Finally, provide your contact information (email, phone number, and address) for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quilt Design Preferences** (`Quilt Design Preferences`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Please select the style of quilt you prefer, such as Traditional, Modern, Rustic, or Victorian.
* **Quilt Size** (`Quilt Size`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the desired size of your quilt, including height and width.
* **Quilt Fabric Color** (`Quilt Fabric Color`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select the color(s) you prefer for your quilt from the provided options.
* **Quilting Style** (`Quilting Style`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the quilting style you prefer, such as Hand Quilting, Machine Quilting, or Freehand Quilting.
* **Quilt Pattern** (`Quilt Pattern`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select the pattern(s) you prefer for your quilt from the options provided.
* **Quilting Services Required** (`Quilting Services Required`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Specify the quilting services you require, such as Quilting Design, Fabric Choice, or Quilting Pattern.
* **Fabric Choice** (`Fabric Choice`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Choose the fabric type(s) you prefer, such as Cotton, Wool, or Flannel.
* **Timeline Requirements** (`Timeline Requirements`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the deadline for your quilt project in the MM/DD/YY format.
* **Quilt Deadline** (`Quilt Deadline`, `date`, optional):
	+ Set a specific deadline for your quilt project in the MM/DD/YY format.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Quilt Deadline Comment** (`Quilt Deadline Comment`, `text`, optional):
	+ Add a comment about your quilt deadline.
* **Quilt Deadline Status** (`Quilt Deadline Status`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the status of your quilt project, such as Complete, In Progress, or Delayed.
* **Quilt Deadline Date** (`Quilt Deadline Date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date of your quilt deadline.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter your zip code for contact purposes.
* **Quilt Deadline Note** (`Quilt Deadline Note`, `text`, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your quilt deadline.
* **Additional Requirements** (`Additional Requirements`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Specify any additional requirements you may have, such as Event Theme, Quilting Style, or Event Size.
* **Design Preference Comment** (`Design Preference Comment`, `text`, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments about your design preference.
* **Additional Requirements Note** (`Additional Requirements Note`, `text`, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your additional requirements.
* **Event Type** (`Event Type`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the type of event, such as Wedding, Birthday, or Anniversary.
* **Budget** (`Budget`, `number`, optional):

* **Additional Requirements 2** (`Additional Requirements 2`, `select_multiple`, optional):

* **Additional Requirements 3** (`Additional Requirements 3`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Specify any additional requirements you may have, such as Yes or No.
