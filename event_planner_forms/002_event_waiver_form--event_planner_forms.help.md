# Event Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Waiver Form is a legal document that requires all participants to acknowledge their understanding and acceptance of the risks associated with attending events hosted by the event organizer. Completing this form is mandatory for all participants before they can participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required to fill out the form accurately.
2. Read each field carefully to understand what is being asked.
3. Fill out the form with the required information, and do not leave any fields blank.
4. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Description**: Describe the potential risks associated with the event.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your full address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the required format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Emergency Contact**: If applicable, provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If applicable, enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: If applicable, enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: If applicable, describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Waiver Signed By**: Sign and date the waiver to acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Event Organizer Name**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Risk Disclaimer**: Read and agree to the terms of service, which includes the risks associated with the event.
* **Terms of Service**: Read and agree to the terms of service, which includes the rules and regulations of the event.

Note: Please make sure to review the form carefully and seek clarification from the event organizer if you are unsure about any part of the form.
