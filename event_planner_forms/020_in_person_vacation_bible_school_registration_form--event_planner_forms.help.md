# In Person Vacation Bible School Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal details, contact information, and emergency contact information from participants and their parents or guardians for the purpose of registration for In Person Vacation Bible School.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consents to complete this form.
2. Complete each field as instructed:
	* Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
	* Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
	* Enter your Parent's Name in the "Parent Name" field.
	* Enter your Participant's Address in the "Participant Address" field.
	* Select your Participant's Age from the drop-down menu.
	* Enter your Contact Email Address in the "Contact Email" field.
	* Enter your Contact Phone Number in the "Contact Phone" field.
	* Enter the Emergency Contact Name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
	* Select the relationship between you and the Emergency Contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
	* Enter the Emergency Contact's Address in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
	* Enter the Emergency Contact's Phone Number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
	* Enter the Emergency Contact's Email Address in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name here.
* **Parent Name** (Text, Required): Enter your parent or guardian's name here.
* **Participant Address** (Text, Required): Enter your participant's address here.
* **Participant Age** (Number, Required): Select your participant's age from the drop-down menu.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Required): Enter your contact email address here.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the emergency contact here.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text, Required): Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact from the drop-down menu.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Text, Required): Enter the emergency contact's address here.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number here.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Required): Enter the emergency contact's email address here.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Select the date for the participant's registration here.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Select the time for the participant's registration here.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the participant here.

Note: The fields marked as "Optional" are not required, but it's recommended to fill them out if you have any additional information to provide.
