# Baby Shower RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from guests to plan a successful baby shower event. It will help event planners to gather attendance counts, dietary needs, and other relevant details to make the event enjoyable and memorable for all guests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your email address to receive updates and event information.
3. Provide your phone number to ensure you can be reached with event details.
4. Indicate whether you will attend the baby shower event.
5. Enter the number of guests attending from your party.
6. List any dietary restrictions or preferences you may have.
7. Select your meal preference (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, No Restrictions, or Other).
8. If you plan to bring a gift, provide details on your gift intention and preferences.
9. Select activities that interest you (Games, Photo Booth, Trivia, Food Tasting, Group Crafts).
10. Share any special requests or comments.
11. If you need transportation assistance, let us know.
12. Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text Message).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Guest Name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive event updates and information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to ensure you can be reached with event details.
* **Will You Attend?**: Select whether you will attend the baby shower event (Yes, No, I will let you know later).
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the total number of guests attending from your party.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: List any food allergies or dietary preferences you may have.
* **Meal Preference**: Select your meal preference (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, No Restrictions, Other).
* **Do You Plan to Bring a Gift?**: Select whether you plan to bring a gift (Yes, No, Undecided).
* **Gift Information**: This is an optional section for providing gift information and preferences.
* **Which Activities Interest You?**: Select all activities that interest you (Games, Photo Booth, Trivia, Food Tasting, Group Crafts).
* **Special Requests or Comments**: Share any special requests or comments with the event planner.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance?**: Select whether you need transportation assistance (Yes, No).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text Message).
