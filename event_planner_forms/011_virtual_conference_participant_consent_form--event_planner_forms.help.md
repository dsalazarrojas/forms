# Virtual Conference Participant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used to obtain participant consent and emergency contact information for the Virtual Conference. The form is divided into several pages, each with its own set of questions and fields. This guide will help you understand the purpose and requirements of each field, making it easier for you to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the required information. You can save your progress and return to the form at any time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I am the legal guardian of the child participating** (`virtual_conference_participant_consent_form_1`):
	+ This field is used to confirm that you are the legal guardian or parent of the child participating in the Virtual Conference.
* **Participant Name (`participant_name`)**:
	+ Please enter the name of the participant, which is the child or individual who will be participating in the Virtual Conference.
* **Participant Email (`participant_email`)**:
	+ Enter the email address of the participant, which will be used for communication and updates related to the Virtual Conference.
* **Participant Phone (`participant_phone`)**:
	+ Please enter the phone number of the participant, which will be used as an emergency contact in case of any issues.
* **Virtual Conference Participant Consent (`virtual_conference_participant_consent`)**:
	+ This field is used to confirm that the participant is aware of the virtual conference format and understands the risks associated with it. Please provide a brief explanation of your understanding.
* **I am aware of the risks associated with virtual conferencing (`virtual_conference_participant_consent_2`)**:
	+ This field is used to confirm that you are aware of the potential risks and challenges associated with virtual conferencing, such as eye strain and technical issues.
* **Parental Consent (`parental_consent`)**:
	+ Select all the options that apply to confirm your understanding of the project and its implications for the child. This includes understanding how the child learns at home, being aware of potential eye strain, and being contacted in case of technical issues.
* **Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`)**:
	+ Please select the emergency contact for the participant, which can be Mom, Dad, or Other.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`)**:
	+ Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact, which will be used in case of any emergency.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Save your progress and return to the form at any time.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the Virtual Conference organizers for assistance.
