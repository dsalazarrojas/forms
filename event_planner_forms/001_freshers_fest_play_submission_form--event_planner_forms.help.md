# Freshers’ Fest Play Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Freshers’ Fest Play Submission Form is a form for submitting plays for the event. It gathers information about the play, such as title, description, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely.
- Ensure that you have a valid submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Play Title** (`play_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of your play.
- **Playwright Name** (`playwright_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the play's author or writer.
- **Play Description** (`play_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the play.
- **Play Duration** (`play_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the play.
- **Play Genre** (`play_genre`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more genres for your play (Comedy, Drama, Tragedy).
- **Play Characters** (`play_characters`, text, required: false): List the characters in your play.
- **Play Poster Image** (`play_poster_image`, text, required: false): Upload a suitable image for the play poster.
- **Play Video Link** (`play_video_link`, text, required: false): Enter a link to a video of your play (if applicable).
- **Submitter Details** (`submitter_details`, text, required: false): Enter some details about yourself and your play.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter a contact number for more information.
- **Play Dates** (`play_dates`, date, required: false): Enter the dates of the play.
- **Play Times** (`play_times`, time, required: false): Enter the times of the play.
- **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
- **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
- **Play Tags** (`play_tags`, text, required: false): Enter some tags about your play.
- **Play Tags List** (`play_tag_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more tags for your play (tag1, tag2, tag3).
- **Tags** (`play_tags_text`, text, required: false): Enter some tags about your play.
- **Play Status** (`play_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your play (pending, accepted, rejected).

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill out as much information as you can to help us better understand your play.
