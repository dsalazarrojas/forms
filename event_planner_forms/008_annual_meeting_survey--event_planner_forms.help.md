# Annual Meeting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from attendees on the annual meeting's organization and content. Your responses will help the organizers improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this survey, please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. You can submit your feedback anonymously, and your responses will be kept confidential. If you have any difficulties or concerns, feel free to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, `email`, required): This is your work email address. We will use it to contact you if needed.
* **Department/ Organization** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): If you are an employee of an organization, please provide your department name.
* **Is this your first time attending this meeting?** (`first_time_attendee`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if this is your first time attending the meeting; otherwise, choose "No."
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale from 1 to 5 (Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied).
* **Meeting Content Relevance** (`meeting_relevance`, `select_one`, required): Choose how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement "Meeting content was relevant to my role."
* **Meeting Objectives Clearness** (`meeting_objectives_clear`, `select_one`, required): Choose how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement "Meeting objectives were clearly communicated."
* **Venue Quality** (`venue_quality`, `select_one`, required): Please rate the quality of the meeting venue and facilities (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Catering Quality (Not Required)** (`catering_quality`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, rate the quality of the catering and refreshments (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Not Applicable).
* **Technology Quality (Not Required)** (`technology_quality`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, rate the quality of audio-visual and technology support (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Speaker Quality (Not Required)** (`speaker_quality`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, rate the quality of presentations and speakers (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Session Length and Pacing** (`session_length`, `select_one`, required): Choose how the session length and pacing affected you (Just right, Too long, Too short, Mixed).
* **Networking Opportunities (Not Required)** (`networking_opportunities`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, rate the quality of networking opportunities provided (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, None Provided).
* **Value Gained** (`value_gained`, `select_one`, required): Choose how much value you gained from attending the meeting (Significant, Moderate, Some, Little, No).
* **Actionable Takeaways** (`actionable_takeaways`, `select_one`, required): Choose how many actionable takeaways you gained from the meeting (Many, Several, A few, None).
* **Most Valuable Sessions or Presentations** (`most_valuable_sessions`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please provide the title or description of the most valuable sessions or presentations.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, optional): If applicable, suggest areas for improvement for future meetings.
* **Topics to be Covered** (`missing_topics`, `text`, optional): If applicable, suggest areas or topics that you would like to see covered in future meetings.
* **Likelihood to Attend Next Year** (`future_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Choose how likely you are to attend the meeting next year (Very Likely, Likely, Undecided, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Recommend to Colleagues** (`recommend_to_others`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would recommend this meeting to your colleagues (Definitely, Probably, Unsure, Probably Not, Definitely Not).
* **Preferred Meeting Format** (`format_preference`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, please choose your preferred meeting format for next year (In-person, Virtual, Hybrid).
