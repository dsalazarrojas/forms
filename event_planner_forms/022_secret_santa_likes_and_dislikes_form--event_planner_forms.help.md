# Secret Santa Likes And Dislikes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to share your preferences with the gift organizer to help them choose a suitable gift for you in the Secret Santa gift exchange.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
* Fill out all the required fields (none are currently marked as required, but it's still a good idea to provide as much information as possible).
* For each field, choose your preferences from the provided options or add your own text.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient Details (Text Input)**: This field is where you can briefly describe who you are, for example, "Me" or "My Name" or "Myself".
* **Preferences (Select Multiple)**: Choose the options that represent your preferences. These options are meant to be general categories, not specific items.
* **Interests (Select Multiple)**: Select the categories that you find interesting. These will help the gift organizer get an idea of your hobbies or areas of interest.
* **Likes (Text Input)**: If you have specific things you like, you can list them here. These can be hobbies, foods, movies, or any other things that bring you joy.
* **Dislikes (Text Input)**: Similarly, if you have specific things that you dislike, you can list them here.
* **Notes (Text Input)**: If you have any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the gift organizer, you can enter them here.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible with your preferences and interests.
- If you have very strong dislikes, it's okay to mention them explicitly.
- Remember to have fun with it! The gift organizer is trying to get to know you, so don't be shy about sharing your personality.
