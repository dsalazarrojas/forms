# Church Event Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from attendees at church events. It will help the church understand what worked well and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the required questions (marked as "true") and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: The name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date**: The date of the event you attended.
* **Your Name (Optional)**: If you want to provide your name for feedback purposes, you can enter it here. This is optional.
* **Your Email (Optional)**: If you want to provide your email for feedback purposes, you can enter it here. This is optional.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Event**: Select the option that best describes your overall experience with the event.
* **Would You Attend This Event Again**: Select the option that best describes your likelihood of attending this event again.
* **Likelihood to Recommend to Others**: Select the option that best describes how likely you are to recommend this event to others.
* **Comfort and Suitability of Venue**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the venue comfort and suitability.
* **Ease of Registration and Check-in**: Select the option that best describes your experience with registration and check-in process. (This field is optional)
* **Event Schedule and Timing**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the event schedule and timing. (This field is optional)
* **Accessibility for People with Disabilities**: Select the option that best describes your experience with accessibility for people with disabilities. (This field is optional)
* **Quality of Content and Programming**: Select the option that best describes the quality of content and programming presented at the event.
* **Quality of Speakers or Presenters**: Select the option that best describes the quality of speakers or presenters. (This field is optional)
* **Relevance to Church Mission and Values**: Select the option that best describes how relevant the content was to the church mission and values. (This field is optional)
* **Opportunity for Fellowship**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the opportunity for fellowship. (This field is optional)
* **Sense of Community and Belonging**: Select the option that best describes your experience with sense of community and belonging. (This field is optional)
* **What Were the Best Aspects of the Event**: Provide a brief description of the best aspects of the event.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Provide a brief description of areas that could be improved for future events.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for future events.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* Take as much time as needed to complete the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the church administration.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help improve future church events.
