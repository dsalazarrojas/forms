# Santa Helper Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Santa Helper Application Form is designed to collect information from potential candidates to assist in selecting the best helpers for Santa's workshop. This form aims to gather essential details, availability, skills, and emergency contacts to make a suitable match.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in your first name in the field labeled 'First Name'.
- Enter your last name in the field labeled 'Last Name'.
- Enter your email address in the field labeled 'Email'.
- Enter your phone number in the field labeled 'Phone'.
- Provide your date of birth in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy' in the field labeled 'Date of Birth'.
- Enter your address in the field labeled 'Address'.
- Select your availability for the job (if applicable) in the fields labeled 'Availability 1', 'Availability 2', and 'Availability 3'.
- Select your schedule preferences in the fields labeled 'Schedule Preferences', 'Schedule Preferences 2', and (if applicable) 'Schedule Preferences 3'.
- Choose your preferred holiday events to work in the fields labeled 'Holiday Events', 'Holiday Events 2', and (if applicable) 'Holiday Events 3'.
- List your special skills in the fields labeled 'Special Skills', 'Special Skills 2', and (if applicable) 'Special Skills 3'.
- Provide information for emergency contacts in the fields labeled 'Emergency Contact', 'Emergency Contact 2', and (if applicable) 'Emergency Contact 3'.
- Enter the phone numbers for your emergency contacts in the fields labeled 'Emergency Phone', 'Emergency Phone 2', and (if applicable) 'Emergency Phone 3'.
- Enter the addresses for your emergency contacts in the fields labeled 'Emergency Address', 'Emergency Address 2', and (if applicable) 'Emergency Address 3'.
- Briefly describe your work experience in the fields labeled 'Work Experience', 'Work Experience 2', and (if applicable) 'Work Experience 3'.
- List your skills in the fields labeled 'Skills', 'Skills 2', and (if applicable) 'Skills 3'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy'.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your home address for emergency contact and other relevant purposes.
* **Availability 1** (select multiple, required): Select the days of the week you are available to work.
* **Schedule Preferences 1** (select one, required): Choose your preferred schedule, morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Holiday Events 1** (select one, required): Choose the holiday events you are willing to participate in.
* **Special Skills 1** (text, required): List any special skills you have.
* **Emergency Contact 1** (text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone 1** (text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address 1** (text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Work Experience 1** (text, required): Briefly describe your work experience.
* **Skills 1** (text, required): List your skills.
* **Availability 2** (select multiple, required): Select the days of the week you are available to work.
* **Schedule Preferences 2** (select one, required): Choose your preferred schedule for the second set of availability.
* **Holiday Events 2** (select one, required): Choose the holiday events you are willing to participate in.
* **Special Skills 2** (text, required): List any additional special skills you have.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone 2** (text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address 2** (text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Work Experience 2** (text, required): Briefly describe your additional work experience.
* **Skills 2** (text, required): List your additional skills.
* **Availability 3** (select multiple, required): Select the days of the week you are available to work.
* **Schedule Preferences 3** (select one, required): Choose your preferred schedule for the third set of availability.
* **Holiday Events 3** (select one, required): Choose the holiday events you are willing to participate in.
* **Special Skills 3** (text, required): List any additional special skills you have.
* **Emergency Contact 3** (text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.

* **Emergency Address 3** (text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Work Experience 3** (text, required): Briefly describe your additional work experience.
* **Skills 3** (text, required): List your additional skills.
