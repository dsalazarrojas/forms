# Christmas Parade Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Parade Sign Up Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants to ensure a successful and enjoyable event. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the contact information section**: Enter your full name, organization name, email address, and phone number.
2. **Choose your organization type**: Select the category that best describes your group.
3. **Choose your participation type**: Select the type of participation that best suits your group.
4. **Enter the expected number of participants**: Provide an approximate count of how many people will be participating.
5. **Provide theme and participation details**: Describe your parade theme and any music or sound effects you may have.
6. **Indicate theme distribution**: Decide if you will be distributing any items to the crowd.
7. **Check your insurance coverage**: Confirm if you have liability insurance and provide the necessary information.
8. **Agree to parade rules**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the parade rules and regulations.
9. **Provide emergency contact information**: Enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
10. **Confirm liability terms**: Agree to the liability terms to participate in the parade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for your group.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your group or organization.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for parade updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number for day-of contact.
* **Organization Type**: Choose the category that best describes your group (e.g., School, Non-Profit, Business).
* **Participation Type**: Choose the type of participation that suits your group (e.g., Marching Group, Float Entry).
* **Expected Number of Participants**: Enter an approximate count of how many people will be participating.
* **Theme Description**: Describe your parade theme in a few words.
* **Music or Sound**: Choose if you will have music or sound effects during the parade.
* **Candy or Item Distribution**: Decide if you will be distributing any items to the crowd.
* **Insurance Coverage**: Confirm if you have liability insurance and provide the necessary information.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance company (if applicable).
* **Coverage Amount**: Enter the policy coverage amount (if applicable).
* **Previous Participation**: Confirm if you have participated in the parade before.
* **T-Shirt Sizes**: Select the sizes needed for participant shirts (if provided).
* **Special Needs or Requests**: Enter any accommodations needed for your group.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Agree to Parade Rules**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the parade rules and regulations.
* **Liability Waiver**: Confirm that you accept the liability terms to participate in the parade.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you accurately fill out all the required fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the parade organizers.
* We appreciate your participation and look forward to seeing you at the Christmas Parade!
