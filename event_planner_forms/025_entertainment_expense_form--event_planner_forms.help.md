# Entertainment Expense Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Expense Form is used by employees to report entertainment expenses such as meals, events, or other expenses incurred during work-related events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the event details, including any relevant information about the event such as name, date, and location.
2. Enter the expense date, which is the date the expense was incurred.
3. Enter the expense amount, which is the total cost of the expense.
4. Enter your name and employee ID to identify yourself as the employee submitting the expense.
5. Select one or more expense types that best describe the type of expense you are submitting (e.g. Food and Beverage, Travel, Accommodation, Miscellaneous).
6. Enter a brief description of the expense, explaining what it was for and why it was necessary.
7. Select the person who submitted the form (you or another employee).
8. Select whether the expense has been approved or not.
9. If not approved, enter the reason for rejection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event.
- **Expense Date**: Enter the date the expense was incurred.
- **Expense Amount**: Enter the total cost of the expense.
- **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID card.
- **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
- **Expense Type**: Select one or more expense types that best describe the type of expense you are submitting.
  * Food and Beverage: Expenses related to meals, drinks, or other consumable items.
  * Travel: Expenses related to transportation, accommodations, or other travel-related costs.
  * Accommodation: Expenses related to hotel stays, conference rooms, or other lodging costs.
  * Miscellaneous: Any other type of expense not included in the above categories.
- **Expense Description**: Enter a brief description of the expense, explaining what it was for and why it was necessary.
- **Submitted By**: Select your name from the dropdown list to confirm who submitted the form.
- **Approved By**: Select whether the expense has been approved or not.
  * Approved: The expense has been approved.
  * Not Approved: The expense has not been approved, and you must enter a reason for rejection.
- **Reason for Rejection**: If the expense was not approved, enter a brief explanation for the rejection.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary supporting documentation and receipts for the expense before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter, especially the expense amount.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact your supervisor or the finance team for clarification.
* If the expense is approved, ensure you receive reimbursement in a timely manner.
* If the expense is not approved, ensure you understand the reason for rejection and address any discrepancies with your supervisor.
