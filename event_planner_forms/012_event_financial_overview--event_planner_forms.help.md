# Event Financial Overview - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Financial Overview form is a tool used to gather key financial information related to a specific event, providing an at-a-glance understanding of the event's financial standing. This form typically captures critical figures such as income, expenses, and profit/loss.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Navigate to the Event Financial Overview form, typically found in the relevant section of your system.
2.  Click on the "Start" button to initiate the form completion process.
3.  Select the relevant event from the list, ensuring the correct event is being viewed.
4.  Enter the income amount for the event into the "Income" field.
5.  Enter the expenses amount for the event into the "Expenses" field.
6.  Calculate the profit/loss by subtracting the expenses from the income.
7.  Enter the calculated profit/loss amount into the "Profit/Loss" field.
8.  Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.
9.  Click "Submit" to finalize the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Income** (1, number, required): Enter the total income generated by the event.
*   **Expenses** (2, number, required): Enter the total expenses incurred during the event.
*   **Profit/Loss** (3, number, required): This field is automatically calculated by subtracting the expenses from the income.

## Tips
- Be cautious of missing fields, as this may indicate overlooked financial considerations.
- Review all entered figures for accuracy to ensure correct calculations.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out for an accurate financial overview.
- Consult with the relevant event team or financial team to understand specific financial details if needed.
- Regularly review and update the form to reflect any changes in the event's financial status.
