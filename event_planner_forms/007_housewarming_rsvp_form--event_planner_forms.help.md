# Housewarming rsvp form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from guests attending a housewarming event, including their name, contact details, and attendance status. Please fill out this form to help us plan the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your **First name** in the "First name" field.
* Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
* Provide your **Email** in the "Email" field.
* Enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone" field.
* Select a **Date** for the event in the "Date" field (format: dd/mm/yyyy).
* Choose the **Time** of attendance in the "Time" field.
* Indicate whether you will be **Attending** the event by selecting "True" or "False".
* Optionally, add any additional **Notes** about your attendance or preferences in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date** (date, required): Select the date you plan to attend the event (format: dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Time** (time, required): Choose the time you plan to arrive at the event.
* **Attending** (select_one, required): Select "True" if you are attending the event, and "False" if you are not.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your attendance or preferences.
