# Contact Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Details Form is used to collect and store contact information from users for various purposes such as event registration, newsletter signup, service inquiry, or general information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, simply fill out the form with your personal information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and other relevant details.
2. Be sure to select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Mobile, or Mail) and the purpose of your contact (Event Registration, Newsletter Signup, Service Inquiry, or General Information).
3. If you have any additional details or information you'd like to share, you can include it in the "Additional Notes" field.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Your primary phone number.
* **Mobile Number** (`mobile_number`): Your optional mobile number.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`): Your street address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`): Apartment suite unit, etc.
* **City** (`city`): Your city.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`): Your state or province.
* **Postal or ZIP Code** (`postal_code`): Your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`): Your country of residence.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`): Optional information you'd like to share.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Your organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Your position.
* **Website URL** (`website`): Your website or LinkedIn URL.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`): Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mobile, or Mail).
* **Purpose of Contact** (`contact_purpose`): Select the reason for your contact (Event Registration, Newsletter Signup, Service Inquiry, or General Information).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Any other information you'd like to share.
* **Country** (`country`): Select your country of residence.
* **Other** (`other`): Not applicable for this field.
* **Germany** (`germany`): Not applicable for this field.
* **France** (`france`): Not applicable for this field.
* **Australia** (`australia`): Not applicable for this field.
* **United Kingdom** (`united_kingdom`): Not applicable for this field.
* **Canada** (`canada`): Not applicable for this field.
* **United States** (`united_states`): Not applicable for this field.
* **Other** (`other`): Not applicable for this field.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
