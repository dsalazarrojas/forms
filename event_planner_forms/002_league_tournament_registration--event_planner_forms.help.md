# League Tournament Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for sports teams to register for tournaments in different sports and age groups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by selecting the correct tournament type (e.g., Basketball, Baseball, Football, or Soccer). Then, enter your team's name and any relevant team information. If you're a team of coaches, managers, or players, select the corresponding option. Choose a date and time for your tournament, and select a venue where you'd like to play. Finally, enter your team's name, coach and team information, and select your team's league status (Rec or Non-Rec) and team status (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Type** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the sport your team is playing in (e.g., Basketball, Baseball, Football, Soccer).
* **Team Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your team's name.
* **Team Members** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose whether your team is comprised of Coach, Manager, Player, or something else.
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of your tournament.
* **Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of your tournament.
* **Venue** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose where you'd like to play (Home, Away, Both).
* **Tournament Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the tournament.
* **Coach Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter any coach information.
* **Team Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter any team information.
* **Sport** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the sport your team is playing in (e.g., Soccer, Baseball, Football, Volleyball).
* **Division** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose your team's division (A, B, C).
* **Age Group** (`select_one`, required: false): Select your team's age group (18-35, 36-55, 56+).
* **Gender** (`select_one`, required: true): Select your team's gender (Male, Female, Non-binary).
* **Team Formation** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional team formation information.
* **Team League** (`select_one`, required: true): Select your team's league status (Rec, Non-Rec).
* **Team Status** (`select_one`, required: false): Select your team's status (Active, Inactive).
