# Car Show Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors of the car show, including company details, sponsorship level and amount, event selection, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Sponsor Information section with your company details, including name, website, primary contact name, contact email, and contact phone number.
2. Select the industry type that best describes your company.
3. Choose the sponsorship level and amount that suits your budget.
4. Select the event you would like to sponsor.
5. Fill out the event date and other event-related details.
6. Check the boxes for any additional sponsorship benefits you are interested in, such as logo display on marketing materials and booth space.
7. Select your preferred booth size and whether you would like to distribute promotional items or not.
8. Choose whether you would like speaking or announcement opportunity.
9. Check the boxes for any exclusive benefits you are interested in.
10. Select your payment method and schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Company website** (`company_website`, text, required): Enter your company website.
* **Primary contact name** (`primary_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Industry type** (`industry_type`, select_one, required): Select the industry type that best describes your company.
* **Sponsorship level** (`sponsorship_level`, select_one, required): Select the sponsorship level that suits your budget.
* **Sponsorship amount** (`sponsorship_amount`, number, required): Enter the sponsorship amount.
* **Event to sponsor** (`event_to_sponsor`, text, required): Select the event you would like to sponsor.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Logo display** (`logo_display`, select_one, required): Select whether you would like your logo to be displayed on marketing materials.
* **Booth space needed** (`booth_provided`, select_one, required): Select whether you need a booth space.
* **Booth size preference** (`booth_size`, select_one, required): Select your preferred booth size.
* **Promotional items** (`promotional_items`, select_one, required): Select whether you would like to distribute promotional items.
* **Speaking opportunity** (`speaking_opportunity`, select_one, required): Select whether you would like speaking or announcement opportunity.
* **Exclusive benefits** (`exclusive_benefits`, select_multiple, required): Select any exclusive benefits you are interested in.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your payment method.
* **Payment schedule** (`payment_schedule`, select_one, required): Select your payment schedule.
* **Tax ID number** (`tax_id_number`, text, required): Enter your tax ID number.
* **Additional information** (`additional_information`, text, required): Enter any additional information or requests you have.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any questions, please contact the event organizers.
* This form will be reviewed and verified before processing your sponsorship application.
