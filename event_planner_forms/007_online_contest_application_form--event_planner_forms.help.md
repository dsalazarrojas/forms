# online_contest_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for potential applicants to provide information about themselves and their application for an online contest. It collects details about the contest information, applicant information, contact details, social media, submission information, terms and conditions, and confirmation of submission.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields, marked as 'required' below.
*   Review each section carefully to ensure all necessary information is provided.
*   Upload any required files or attachments.
*   Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contest Information** (`Contest Information`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the online contest you are applying for.
*   **Applicant Information** (`Applicant Information`, `text`, required): Provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact address or any other relevant contact details.
*   **Social Media** (`Social Media`, `text`, optional): Share your social media handle or other relevant online presence.
*   **Submission Information** (`Submission Information`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your submission, highlighting its relevance and quality.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `text`, required): Carefully review and agree to the terms and conditions of the contest.
*   **Upload File** (`Upload File`, `text`, optional): Attach your submission file if required by the contest.
*   **Confirm Submission** (`Confirm Submission`, `text`, optional): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted your application accurately.
*   **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions** (`I have read and agree to the terms and conditions`, `text`, required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the contest.
*   **Confirm Upload** (`Confirm Upload`, `text`, optional): Confirm that you have uploaded the required file(s) for your submission.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
*   Review your submission before confirming it.
*   Ensure you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
*   Attach any required files and confirm their upload.
*   Double-check your form submission for accuracy and completeness.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the contest organizers.
