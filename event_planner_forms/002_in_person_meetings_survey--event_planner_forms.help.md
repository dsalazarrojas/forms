# In Person Meetings Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Person Meetings Survey is a tool for gathering feedback and insights on in-person meetings. It helps attendees provide information on meeting frequency, duration, location, and attendance, which can be used to improve future meetings and events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the frequency of your in-person meetings.
	* Meeting frequency: Choose the frequency of your in-person meetings (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
2. Indicate the duration of your meetings.
	* Meeting duration: Enter the number of minutes or hours spent in meetings.
3. Select the location of your meetings.
	* Meeting location: Choose the location where your meetings take place (On-site, Off-site, or Virtual).
4. Indicate your attendance at in-person meetings.
	* Meeting attendance 1-12: Select whether you attend (Yes) or do not attend (No) each of the 12 in-person meetings.
5. Provide any additional comments about your experiences.
	* Comments: Add any comments or insights about your in-person meetings.
6. Enter your email address.
	* Email: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter your phone number.
	* Phone number: Enter your phone number for further contact.
8. Indicate your attendance at other in-person meetings.
	* Meeting attendance 13-15: Select whether you attend (Yes) or do not attend (No) each of the 3 remaining in-person meetings.
9. Indicate whether or not you have submitted to past surveys.
	* Submission: Select whether you have submitted to past surveys (Yes) or not (No).
10. Indicate whether or not you have submitted to other surveys.
	* Submission 1: Select whether you have submitted to other surveys (Yes) or not (No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **In Person Meetings (1)** (`in_person_meetings`, Text, required/optional): This field is the main section for reporting on in-person meetings.
* **Meeting Frequency (2)** (`meeting_frequency`, Select One, required/optional): Choose the frequency of your in-person meetings (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Meeting Duration (3)** (`meeting_duration`, Number, required/optional): Enter the number of minutes or hours spent in meetings.
* **Meeting Location (4)** (`meeting_location`, Select One, required/optional): Choose the location where your meetings take place (On-site, Off-site, or Virtual).
* **Meeting Attendance 1-12 (5-16)** (`meeting_attendance_*`, Select Multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you attend (Yes) or do not attend (No) each of the 12 in-person meetings.
* **Comments (11)** (`comments`, Note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or insights about your in-person meetings.
* **Email (12)** (`email`, Email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number (13)** (`phone_number`, Text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Meeting Attendance 13-15 (14-17)** (`meeting_attendance_*`, Select Multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you attend (Yes) or do not attend (No) each of the 3 remaining in-person meetings.
* **Submission (24)** (`submission`, Select One, required/optional): Select whether you have submitted to past surveys (Yes) or not (No).
* **Submission 1 (25)** (`submission_1`, Select One, required/optional): Select whether you have submitted to other surveys (Yes) or not (No).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field completely and accurately.
- Select the correct options for your meeting frequency, location, and attendance.
- Use the comments field to add any additional information or insights.
- Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
- Review and submit your form carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
