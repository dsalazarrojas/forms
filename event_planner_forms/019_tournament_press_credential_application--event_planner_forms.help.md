# Tournament Press Credential Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain press accreditation for the tournament. It gathers information about the press personnel visiting the event and their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your role(s) in the "Role" field.
6. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
7. Enter your contact name in the "Contact name" field.
8. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
9. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field.
10. Describe your reason for visiting the press in the "Reason for press visit" field.
11. Specify whether you are requesting press credentials in the "Press credential requested" field.
12. List your media requirements in the "Media requirements" field.
13. Select the type of media you will be using (Photo, Video, Audio, Social Media) in the "Media type" field.
14. Provide additional details about your media in the "Media details" field.
15. Enter the start date of your press visit in the "Start date" field.
16. Enter the end date of your press visit in the "End date" field.
17. Enter the date when your press credential will be issued in the "Credential issue date" field.
18. Specify the status of your press accreditation in the "Accreditation status" field.
19. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
20. Enter your press ID (if applicable) in the "Press ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Role**: Select one or more of the following roles: Journalist, Photographer, Broadcaster, Other.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Contact name**: Enter your contact name.
* **Contact email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Reason for press visit**: Describe your reason for visiting the press.
* **Press credential requested**: Specify whether you are requesting press credentials.
* **Media requirements**: List your media requirements.
* **Media type**: Select one of the following media types: Photo, Video, Audio, Social Media.
* **Media details**: Provide additional details about your media.
* **Start date**: Enter the start date of your press visit.
* **End date**: Enter the end date of your press visit.
* **Credential issue date**: Enter the date when your press credential will be issued.
* **Accreditation status**: Specify the status of your press accreditation: Pending, Approved, Declined, Expired.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes.
* **Press ID**: Enter your press ID (if applicable).
