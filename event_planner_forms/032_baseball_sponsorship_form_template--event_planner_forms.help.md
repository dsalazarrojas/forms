# Baseball Sponsorship Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baseball Sponsorship Form is a tool for teams to collect information from potential sponsors. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the team can process your sponsorship and communicate effectively throughout the season.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to understand the different sections and fields.
2. Start by filling out the company details section.
3. Enter your company name, contact person name, contact title, email address, phone number, and business address.
4. Select the type of sponsorship you are interested in (team or event) and specify the level of sponsorship you prefer.
5. Indicate the duration of your sponsorship and the benefits you would like to receive as a sponsor.
6. Choose your preferred payment schedule and method.
7. Specify if you would like to receive tax documentation and whether you have sponsored this team before.
8. Enter your business type and the number of employees you have.
9. Provide a brief explanation of your connection to the community and your marketing goals.
10. If you have any special requests, enter them in the special requests field.
11. Finally, review the terms and conditions, select the option to agree to them, and sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Contact Person Name** (2): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Title** (3): Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (4): Enter the email address of your company or organization.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter your best contact number.
* **Business Address** (6): Enter the complete street address of your company or organization.
* **City** (7): Enter the city of your company or organization.
* **State** (8): Enter the state of your company or organization.
* **ZIP Code** (9): Enter the postal code of your company or organization.
* **Website URL** (10): Enter the URL of your company website (optional).
* **Team or Event to Sponsor** (11): Select the type of sponsorship you are interested in (team or event).
* **Sponsorship Level** (12): Select the tier of sponsorship you prefer (e.g., Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Friend).
* **Sponsorship Amount** (13): Enter the dollar amount of your sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Duration** (14): Select the duration of your sponsorship (e.g., One season, Full year, Multi year, One time event).
* **Benefits Requested** (15): Select the benefits you would like to receive as a sponsor (e.g., logo on uniforms, logo on website).
* **Upload Company Logo** (16): Upload your company logo for recognition materials (optional).
* **Payment Schedule** (17): Select your preferred payment schedule (e.g., One time payment, Monthly installments, Quarterly installments).
* **Payment Method** (18): Select how you will pay for your sponsorship (e.g., Check, Credit card, Bank transfer).
* **Tax Deduction Information** (19): Specify if you need tax documentation and if you have sponsored this team before.
* **Previous Sponsor** (20): Indicate if you have sponsored this team before.
* **Business Type** (21): Select the type of business you represent (e.g., Local retail, Restaurant or food, Professional services).
* **Number of Employees** (22): Select the number of employees you have in your company.
* **Connection to Community** (23): Provide a brief explanation of your connection to the community.
* **Marketing Goals** (24): Specify what you hope to achieve through your sponsorship.
* **Special Requests** (25): Enter any special requests you may have.
* **Agreement to Terms** (26): Select whether you agree to the sponsorship terms.
* **Authorized Signature** (27): Enter your full name.
* **Signature Date** (28): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Team Representative Name** (29): Enter the name of the team representative (for office use).
* **Approval Status** (30): The approval status will be filled out by the team representative (for office use).

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Review the terms and conditions before filling out the form.
- Make sure to select the right sponsorship type and level.
- Upload a clear and legible logo for recognition materials.
- Provide a valid email address and phone number for communication.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, contact the team representative. 

Note: This help guide is not exhaustive and you may need to review the terms and conditions for more information.
