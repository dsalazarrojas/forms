# Secret Santa Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows employees to sign up for the Secret Santa gift exchange program, a fun and optional holiday activity that promotes team building and camaraderie among colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the text field labeled "name". This will be the name of the person who will be participating in the gift exchange.
2.  Enter your email address in the text field labeled "email". This will be the email address that will be used to contact you about your Secret Santa.
3.  Enter your phone number in the text field labeled "phone". This is optional, but recommended in case your email is not accessible.
4.  Set your gift limit by entering a number in the numerical field labeled "gift_limit". This represents the maximum amount you are willing to spend on your Secret Santa gift for your assigned partner.
5.  If you do not wish to participate in the gift exchange, select "No" in the dropdown menu labeled "sign_up". Otherwise, select "Yes".
6.  If you have a preferred tool or method that you would like to be assigned to receive a gift, enter it in the text field labeled "assigned_tool". This is optional.
7.  Leave any additional notes or comments about your participation in the gift exchange in the text field labeled "notes". This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **name** (text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the gift exchange.
*   **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address that will be used to contact you about your Secret Santa.
*   **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number in case your email is not accessible.
*   **gift_limit** (number, optional): Set your maximum gift budget for your Secret Santa.
*   **sign_up** (select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to participate or "No" to opt-out of the gift exchange.
*   **assigned_tool** (text, optional): If you have a preferred tool or method you'd like to be assigned, enter it here.
*   **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation in the gift exchange.

## Tips

*   Please be respectful of your colleagues' gift budgets and preferences.
*   Consider setting a reasonable gift limit to ensure everyone's budget is considered.
*   If you are not participating, please select "No" in the "sign_up" field.
*   If you have a preferred tool or method, enter it in the "assigned_tool" field. This helps ensure you receive a gift that suits your style or preferences.
