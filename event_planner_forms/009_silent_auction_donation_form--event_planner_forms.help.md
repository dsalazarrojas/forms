# Silent Auction Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from donors for a silent auction event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Amount" field.
4. Describe your donation in the "Description" field.
5. Select your donor type from the "Donor type" field. Choose between "Individual", "Business", or "Organization".
6. Select the type of donation you are making from the "Donation type" field. Choose between "Prize", "Gift", or "Grant".
7. Enter your contact email in the "Contact email" field.
8. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you after the event.
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your donation.
* **Donor type** (`donor_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your type of donor. Choose between "Individual", "Business", or "Organization".
* **Donation type** (`donation_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of donation you are making. Choose between "Prize", "Gift", or "Grant".
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling in your full name and email address.
* Ensure your donor type and donation type are correct to ensure accurate records.
* Use a brief description of your donation.
* Enter your contact information accurately to ensure we can contact you after the event.
