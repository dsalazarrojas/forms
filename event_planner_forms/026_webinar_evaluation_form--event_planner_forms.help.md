# Webinar Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from attendees after a webinar. It helps the event planning team to understand what attendees thought about the webinar and how they can improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Webinar Evaluation Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your feedback about the webinar in the "Webinar Evaluation Form" text field.
2. If you have suggestions for future webinars, enter them in the "Suggestions" text field.
3. Answer the questions in the second section of the form.
   - Rate the webinar by selecting one of the options for "Rating".
   - Indicate whether you would suggest hosting future webinars in-person or virtually by selecting one or multiple options for "Webinar Venue".
   - Let us know whether you would suggest any other improvements or changes for future webinars by selecting one or multiple options for "Suggestions List".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Webinar Evaluation Form (Text)**: This is a text field where you can enter your feedback and thoughts about the webinar. It is not required, so you can skip it if you do not have any feedback.
* **Webinar Title (Text)**: This is not used on this form. It is likely a leftover from a previous form design.
* **Rating (Number)**: Rate the webinar on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is poor and 4 is excellent.
* **Suggestions (Text)**: Enter any suggestions you have for future webinars.
* **Suggestions List (Select Multiple)**: Select one or multiple options for ways you think we can improve future webinars.
* **Webinar Venue (Select One)**: Select whether you would suggest hosting future webinars in-person or virtually.
*   - **Virtual**: Host the webinar virtually.
  - **In-person**: Host the webinar in-person.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form as thoroughly as possible so that we can get the most useful feedback and suggestions.
* If you have any technical issues or problems during the webinar, you can contact us separately.
* Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form! Your feedback is invaluable to us!
