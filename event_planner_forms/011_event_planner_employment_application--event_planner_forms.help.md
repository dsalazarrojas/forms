# Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Information**: This is the first page of the form where you will find space to enter your basic contact details (name, email, phone number, etc.).
* **Contact Information**: Provide your current contact information such as your email address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Education**: Share your educational background, including the institutions you have attended and relevant courses you have completed.
* **Work History**: List your previous work experiences, including job titles, durations, and company names.
* **References**: Provide information about your professional references, including their names, titles, and contact details.
* **Skills**: List your relevant skills and expertise.
* **Certifications**: Mention any relevant certifications you hold, such as event planning certifications.
* **Work Experience**: Elaborate on your work experiences, including job titles, durations, and company names.
* **Availability**: Indicate your availability for work, including your work hours, days off, and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide information about an emergency contact person, including their name and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Share details about the emergency contact person, including their name, title, and contact information.
* **Emergency Info**: Provide any additional emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Notes**: Add any additional notes about the emergency contact person.
