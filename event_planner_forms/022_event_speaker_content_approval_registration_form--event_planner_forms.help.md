# Event Speaker Content Approval Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and approve speaker registration information for events. Event organizers and administrators will use this form to review and manage speaker data before the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Speaker Details section with the speaker's name, title, and contact information.
2. Enter the Event Details, including the event name, date, and time.
3. Upload relevant content related to the event or speaker in the Content Upload section.
4. Provide Session Details, if applicable.
5. In the Event Speaker Details section, review and approve speaker data.
6. Confirm that you agree to the event terms in the Speaker Agreement field.
7. Confirm that you agree to the session terms in the Session Agreement field.
8. If necessary, specify any other agreements or terms in the Other Agreement fields.
9. Upload images related to the speaker or session in the Speaker Image and Session Image fields.
10. Enter any video-related information for the speaker or session in the Speaker Video field.
11. Enter any URL for the uploaded content in the Content Upload URL field.
12. Finally, click the Submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the speaker's name.
* **Speaker Title** (`speaker_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the speaker's title or role.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the speaker's email address.
* **Speaker Phone Number** (`speaker_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the speaker's phone number.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the event name.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the event date.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the event time.
* **Speaker Content** (`speaker_content`, text, required/optional): Enter any content related to the speaker.
* **Session Content** (`session_content`, text, required/optional): Enter any content related to the session.
* **Speaker Image** (`speaker_image`, text, required/optional): Upload an image related to the speaker.
* **Session Image** (`session_image`, text, required/optional): Upload an image related to the session.
* **Content Upload URL** (`content_upload_url`, text, required/optional): Enter any URL for the uploaded content.
* **Speaker Video** (`speaker_video`, text, required/optional): Enter any video-related information for the speaker.
* **Speaker Agreement** (`speaker_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the event terms.
* **Session Agreement** (`session_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the session terms.
* **Other Agreement** (`other_agreement`, text, required/optional): Specify any other agreements or terms that apply to the speaker or session.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to complete and submit the form.
