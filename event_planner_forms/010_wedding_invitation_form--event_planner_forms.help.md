# Wedding Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a wedding event, including the title, wedding details, contact information, date and time, location, guests' details, reception information, special requests, wedding theme, music preferences, dress code, and photo information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each field carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as "true" in the form schema.
* You can provide optional information in optional fields, marked as "false" in the form schema.
* Review your completed form to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Invitation Form** (`title`, `required`: `true`): Enter the title of your wedding event.
* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, `required`: `true`): Provide a brief description of your wedding event.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `required`: `false`): Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number, if you want guests to be able to contact you for any reason.
* **Date & Time** (`date_time`, `required`: `true`): Select the date and time of your wedding event.
* **Location** (`location`, `required`: `true`): Enter the location of your wedding event.
* **Guests Details** (`guests_details`, `required`: `true`): Provide a list of guests attending your wedding event.
* **Reception Info** (`reception_info`, `required`: `false`): Enter information about the reception after the wedding, such as location and time.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `required`: `false`): If you have any special requests for your wedding event, such as a gift registry, enter them here.
* **Wedding Theme** (`wedding_theme`, `required`: `true`): Select the theme of your wedding event.
* **Music Preferences** (`music_preferences`, `required`: `false`): Select the music preferences for your wedding event, such as whether you want music or not.
* **Dress Code** (`dress_code`, `required`: `false`): Select the dress code for your wedding event, such as formal or informal.
* **Photo Info** (`photo_info`, `required`: `false`): Upload a photo or provide a description of your wedding photo.

## Tips
* Double-check your completed form for accuracy and completeness.
* Make sure to proofread your form for any errors before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event coordinator or your partner for assistance.
