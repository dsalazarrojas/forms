# Festival Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers for a festival event. Event organizers and planners will use this form to gather essential details about volunteers, including their availability, skills, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic information about yourself as a volunteer.
2. Choose the days you are available to work during the festival.
3. Specify any special talents or skills you have that would be useful for the event.
4. Provide information about the event you will be volunteering for.
5. Enter your preferred event date and time.
6. Select your preferred festival shirt size, color, and type.
7. Add any additional notes about your volunteering experience or qualifications.
8. If you are an event organizer or planner, enter your contact information and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information**: Enter your name, contact phone number, and any other basic information about yourself. (text, required)
* **Available Slots**: Choose the days you are available to work during the festival. (select multiple, optional)
* **Special Talents**: Specify if you have any special skills or talents that would be useful for the event. (select multiple, optional)
* **Event Information**: Enter any relevant information about the event you will be volunteering for. (text, optional)
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you will be volunteering for. (date, optional)
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event you will be volunteering for. (time, optional)
* **Event Venue**: Enter the venue of the event you will be volunteering for. (text, optional)
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event you will be volunteering for. (text, optional)
* **Volunteer Type**: Indicate your role or type of volunteering (e.g. festival staff, stage manager). (text, required)
* **Shirt Size**: Choose your preferred festival shirt size. (select one, required)
* **Shirt Color**: Choose your preferred festival shirt color. (select one, required)
* **Shirt Type**: Choose your preferred festival shirt type (e.g. t-shirt, hoodie). (select one, required)
* **Additional Info**: Add any additional notes about your volunteering experience or qualifications. (text, optional)
* **Event Organizer Name**: If you are an event organizer, enter your name. (text, optional)
* **Event Organizer Email**: If you are an event organizer, enter your email. (email, optional)
* **Event Organizer Phone**: If you are an event organizer, enter your phone number. (text, optional)
* **Event Organizer Notes**: If you are an event organizer, add any additional notes about the event. (text, optional)
* **Volunteer Notes**: Add any additional notes about your volunteering experience or qualifications. (text, optional)
* **Assigned Tool**: If you are an event organizer, enter any tools or equipment you have assigned to this volunteer. (text, optional)
* **Assigned User**: If you are an event organizer, enter any users or teams this volunteer will be working with. (text, optional)

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, feel free to include them in the "Additional Info" field.
* If you are an event organizer, make sure to include all necessary contact information and notes for your volunteers.
