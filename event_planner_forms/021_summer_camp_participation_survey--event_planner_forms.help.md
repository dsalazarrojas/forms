# Summer Camp Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Participation Survey is designed to collect information from summer camp participants. Please answer the following questions to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
* Enter your age in the "Age" field.
* Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Choose "Yes" or "No" to answer "Does the camper wish to participate" in the "Does the camper wish to participate" field.
* If you are participating with others, enter the number of campers in the "Number of campers" field.
* If you have any comments or additional information, please type them in the "Comments" and "Additional comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`camper_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Does the camper wish to participate** (`camper_participating`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you wish to participate.
* **Number of campers** (`number_of_campers`, `number`, required/optional): If you are participating with others, enter the number of campers.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any comments or feedback you have.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information you think is relevant.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you answer all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to ask for help.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form! Your input is valuable to us.
