# Swim Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information and payment details for the swim camp program. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (none are marked as required, but all fields are essential for a successful registration)
2. Enter your swimmer's information, including name, email, phone number, date of birth, and gender
3. Choose the swim camp session date and time you wish to attend
4. Select the session duration you would like to participate in
5. Enter the swim camp fee you are willing to pay
6. Add any additional notes about your swimmer's registration

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Swimmer Name** (`swimmer_name`, text, optional): Enter your swimmer's name.
* **Swimmer Email** (`swimmer_email`, email, optional): Enter your swimmer's email address.
* **Swimmer Phone** (`swimmer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your swimmer's phone number.
* **Swimmer Date of Birth** (`swimmer_dob`, date, optional): Enter your swimmer's date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Swimmer Gender** (`swimmer_gender`, select_one, optional): Select your swimmer's gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Swim Camp Session Date** (`swim_camp_session_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the swim camp session you wish to attend.
* **Swim Camp Session Time** (`swim_camp_session_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the swim camp session you wish to attend.
* **Swim Camp Session Duration** (`swim_camp_session_duration`, select_multiple, optional): Select the duration of the swim camp session you wish to participate in (1-2 hours, 3-4 hours, 5-6 hours).
* **Swim Camp Fee** (`swim_camp_fee`, number, optional): Enter the fee you are willing to pay for the swim camp session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your swimmer's registration.
