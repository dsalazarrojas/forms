# Meditation Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meditation Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential attendees for a meditation workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Start by filling out the "Registration Information" section.
- Move on to the "Contact Information" section, where you'll need to provide your name, email address, and phone number.
- In the "Participant Information" section, you can provide any relevant information about the participant, such as their age or interests.
- Finally, in the "Additional Information" section, you can include any additional comments or notes you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Information** (Registration Information, text, required): This is the starting point for your registration. Please fill out this field to confirm your interest in attending the workshop.
  * Provide your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you about your registration.
  * Make sure to include your email address and phone number so we can reach you if we need more information.
- **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): 
  * Please fill out this field with your name, email address, and phone number.
  * This is the contact method that we will use for communication.
- **Participant Information** (Participant Information, text, optional): 
  * If you'd like to share any additional information about the participant, this is the place.
  * For example, you can mention their age or interests.
  * This information will help us better prepare for the participant's needs.
- **Additional Information** (Additional Information, text, optional): 
  * If you'd like to share any additional comments or notes, this is the field for that.
  * This can be anything from dietary restrictions to any questions you have about the workshop.
  * This information will help us tailor the workshop experience to each participant.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
- We'll be in touch to confirm your registration and provide more details about the workshop.
