# Karaoke Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Karaoke Registration Form is used to collect information from users who want to participate in a karaoke event. This form is essential for event organizers to have the necessary details to manage the event smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the **Enter Registrant Name** field.
2. In the **Song Request** field, enter the song you would like to perform.
3. If you have any additional information about your song request, you can provide it in the **Song Request Additional Info** field (this is optional).
4. Select the **Selected Tool** from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand which device you will be using during the event.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
6. Enter your **Email Address** in the format of `example@example.com`.
7. Select your **Date** of participation in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Choose your **Time** of participation in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Enter Registrant Name** (Required): This is the name of the person registering for the event.
  * Example: John Doe
- **Song Request** (Required): Enter the song title and artist you would like to perform.
  * Example: "Bohemian Rhapsody" by Queen
- **Song Request Additional Info** (Optional): If you have any additional information about your song request, you can provide it here.
  * Example: "This is a special song for my wife, can you please arrange for a piano accompanist?"
- **Selected Tool** (Required): Choose the device you will be using for your performance.
  * Example: chatjimmy or Other
- **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
  * Example: 123-456-7890
- **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address in the format of `example@example.com`.
  * Example: johndoe@example.com
- **Date** (Required): Select the date you would like to participate in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
  * Example: 02/25/2022
- **Time** (Required): Choose your participation time in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
  * Example: 08:00 PM

# Tips
* Please ensure that you have already checked if the event is not already registered before filling out this form.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please provide them in the **Song Request Additional Info** field.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out this form, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
