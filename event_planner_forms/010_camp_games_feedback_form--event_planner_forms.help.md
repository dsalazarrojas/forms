# Camp Games Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Games Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experiences during camp games. This feedback will help the camp organizers to understand what worked well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Select your age group from the options provided.
3. Choose the session you attended from the options provided.
4. Rate your overall enjoyment of the camp games.
5. Select your favorite game from the list and provide a brief explanation.
6. Choose your least favorite game from the list (optional).
7. Rate the variety of games offered.
8. Rate the difficulty level of the games.
9. Rate the clarity of game rules and instructions.
10. Rate the quality of game equipment.
11. Rate the engagement level of counselors during games.
12. Choose how teams were selected (optional).
13. Rate your safety during games.
14. Choose whether games were the right length.
15. Suggest new games you would like to see in future sessions (optional).
16. Rate the balance of competitive vs cooperative games.
17. Rate the options offered on rainy days (optional).
18. Indicate whether you would participate in these games again.
19. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your name tag.
* **Age Group**: Select the age group that best fits your age as of the current year.
* **Camp Session**: Choose the session you attended, such as Session 1, Session 2, or Session 3.
* **Overall Enjoyment of Camp Games**: Rate your overall experience from 5 - Excellent to 1 - Poor.
* **What Was Your Favorite Game**: Select the game that you enjoyed the most.
* **Why Was This Your Favorite**: Provide a brief explanation of why this game was your favorite.
* **Least Favorite Game**: (Optional) Select the game that you enjoyed the least.
* **Variety of Games Offered**: Rate the mix of games from Excellent - great mix to Poor - needs improvement.
* **Game Difficulty Level**: Rate the difficulty level of the games from Too easy to Too difficult.
* **Clarity of Game Rules and Instructions**: Rate how clear the rules were from Very clear to Very confusing.
* **Quality of Game Equipment**: Rate the quality of the equipment from Excellent to Poor.
* **Counselor Engagement During Games**: Rate the engagement level of counselors from Very engaged and enthusiastic to Not engaged.
* **Team Selection Process**: (Optional) Choose how teams were selected, such as Fair and well organized.
* **Safety During Games**: Rate your safety from Always felt safe to Did not feel safe.
* **Game Duration**: Rate the length of games from Too short to Too long.
* **New Games Suggested**: (Optional) Suggest new games you would like to see in future sessions.
* **Balance of Competitive vs Cooperative Games**: Rate the balance from Perfect balance to Would prefer more team games.
* **Rainy Day Game Options**: (Optional) Rate the options offered on rainy days from Excellent alternatives to No indoor games offered.
* **Would You Participate in These Games Again**: Indicate whether you would participate in future sessions from Definitely yes to Definitely no.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Provide any other feedback or suggestions you have.

Note: Some fields are optional and are marked as such. Make sure to fill in all required fields to provide useful feedback.
