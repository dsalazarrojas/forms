# Dog Show Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Show Entry Form is a crucial document for dog owners to register their pets for a dog show event. It helps the event organizers gather essential information about each dog and its owner, ensuring a smooth and efficient registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Title**: Enter the name of the dog show.
2. **Show Details**: Provide a brief description of the show, including the date, location, and any other relevant information.
3. **Owner Info**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the owner is attending the show.
4. **Dog Details**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are providing dog details.
5. **Dog Show Info**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are providing dog show information.
6. **Dog Show Classes**: Select all the classes your dog is participating in (e.g., Puppy, Junior, Adult, Senior).
7. **Dog Breed**: Enter the breed of your dog.
8. **Show Type**: Select the type of show (e.g., Open, Non-Competitive, Specialty).
9. **Show Date**: Enter the date of the show.
10. **Entry Fee**: Enter the entry fee for your dog.
11. **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the dog's owner.
12. **Owner Email**: Enter the owner's email address.
13. **Owner Phone**: Enter the owner's phone number.
14. **Show Location**: Enter the location of the show.
15. **Dog Weight**: Enter the weight of your dog.
16. **Entry Fee Includes**: Select all that apply (e.g., entry fee and parking).
17. **Additional Classes**: Select all additional classes your dog is participating in.
18. **Special Instructions**: Provide any special instructions for judges or show staff.
19. **Payment Method**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are paying online or offline.
20. **Entry Date**: Enter the date of the entry.
21. **Show Type**: Select the type of show (e.g., Yes or No).
22. **Dog Show Type**: Select the type of dog show (e.g., Yes or No).
23. **Entry Type**: Select all that apply (e.g., Single Entry, Multiple Entries, Group Entries).
24. **Payment Status**: Select the payment status (e.g., Active, Inactive).
25. **Show Status**: Select the show status (e.g., Active, Inactive).
26. **Owner Note**: Enter any additional notes from the owner.
