# Thanksgiving Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from participants in a Thanksgiving Quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **first answer** field with your response to the first question.
2. Enter the **Correct answer**, which is the actual correct answer to the question (if you know it).
3. Select your response to **Second answer** from the available options.
4. Select all that apply to **Third answer**.
5. Enter your desired **Score** out of a certain number (not specified, so use your best judgment).
6. Select a **Date** for the quiz, ideally the date you completed it.
7. Enter a **Time** if you took the quiz at a specific time.
8. Add any additional **Note** about your experience with the quiz.
9. If you want to be contacted for follow-up, enter your **Email address**.
10. Finally, provide your **Phone number** for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First answer**: Enter your response to the first question. This is a text field.
* **Correct answer**: Enter the actual correct answer to the question, if you know it.
* **Second answer**: Choose one of the available options for this question.
* **Third answer**: Choose all the applicable options for this question.
* **Score**: Enter your desired score out of a certain number (use your best judgment).
* **Date**: Select the date you completed the quiz.
* **Time**: Enter the time you took the quiz (if applicable).
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the quiz.
* **Email address**: Enter your contact email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
