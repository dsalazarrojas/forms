# Church Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering children and their guardians for a church camp. The form collects personal and medical information, as well as preferences for camp sessions and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form in English**. The form fields are self-explanatory and will guide you through the process.
2. **Enter your information accurately**. Double-check your answers before submitting to ensure they are correct.
3. **Use a computer or mobile device to access the form**. You can access the form online or download it as a PDF for offline use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper name** (`camper_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full legal name of the child attending the camp.
* **Birth date** (`birth_date`, `date`, required): Enter the child's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Guardian name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Guardian phone** (`guardian_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Camper allergies** (`camper_allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies or medical conditions the camper has, or leave blank if none.
* **Dietary needs** (`dietary_needs`, `text`, optional): Specify any dietary restrictions the camper may have.
* **Session choice** (`session_choice`, `select_one`, required): Select which camp session the child will attend (Session A, Session B, Session C).
* **Medical consent** (`medical_consent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you consent to medical treatment for your child if necessary.
* **Secondary emergency contact** (`emergency_contact_secondary`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and phone number of an alternate emergency contact.
* **Special needs** (`special_needs`, `text`, optional): List any special accommodations required for the camper.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the hints provided for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the church camp staff for support.
