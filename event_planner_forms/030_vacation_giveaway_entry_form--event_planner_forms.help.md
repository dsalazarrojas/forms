# vacation-giveaway-entry-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vacation-giveaway-entry-form" is designed to allow participants to enter the vacation-giveaway event by submitting their entry information, contact details, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the entry form with your details (Entry Form).
2. Provide your contact information (Contact Information).
3. Indicate your eligibility to enter the giveaway (Entry Eligibility).
4. Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the giveaway (Entry Terms and Conditions).
5. Choose your preferred contact methods (Contact Methods).
6. Select your preferred entry methods (Entry Methods).
7. Answer any additional questions related to your entry (Additional Questions).
8. Confirm whether you would like to be contacted for entry information (Entry Methods).
9. Answer any winner questions (Winner Questions).
10. Answer any contact information questions (Contact Info Questions).
11. Click the "Submit" button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Form** (`Entry Form`, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter your name and other details about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required: false): In this field, please provide your name, email address, and/or phone number to be contacted if you win the giveaway.
* **Entry Eligibility** (`Entry Eligibility`, text, required: false): Check this box if you are eligible to enter the giveaway. This is usually indicated by a certain age, residency, or other requirements.
* **Entry Terms and Conditions** (`Entry Terms and Conditions`, text, required: false): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the giveaway. This is to ensure that you understand and accept the rules of the giveaway.
* **Contact Methods** (`Contact Methods`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the contact methods you would like to be contacted by (e.g., email, phone, text message).
* **Entry Methods** (`Entry Methods`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the entry methods you would like to use to enter the giveaway (e.g., online, offline).
* **Additional Questions** (`Additional Questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer any additional questions related to your entry.
* **Entry Methods** (`Entry Methods`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm whether you would like to be contacted for entry information.
* **Winner Questions** (`Winner Questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer any questions related to your entry in case you win.
* **Contact Info Questions** (`Contact Info Questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer any questions related to your contact information.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to submit your entry.
