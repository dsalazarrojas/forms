# PayJunction T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayJunction T Shirt Order Form is a form designed for customers to place orders for t-shirts with specific customization options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your order details, including customer information, order details, and payment information.
2. Review and adjust your order as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **PayJunction T Shirt Order Form** (`1`, `text`, required): The first field is the title of the form, which remains static and does not require input.
* **customer_info** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your customer information, including your name, address, and contact details.
* **order_info** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your order details, including the event or occasion and any other relevant information.
* **shipping_info** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping information, including the address and any other relevant details.
* **payment_info** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your payment information, including the method and any other relevant details.
* **review** (`6`, `note`, required): Enter a short review of your order.
* **email** (`7`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **shipping_address** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address.
* **order_details** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter additional order details.
* **t_shirt_designs** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no for t-shirt designs.
* **t_shirt_size** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or multiple t-shirt sizes.
* **t_shirt_color** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select one t-shirt color.
* **t_shirt_type** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or multiple t-shirt types.
* **t_shirt_price** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the price of the t-shirt.
* **review** (`16`, `note`, not required): Enter a short review.
* **order_comments** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter order comments.
* **order_total** (`18`, `number`, required): Enter the total order amount.
* **date** (`19`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event.
* **time** (`20`, `time`, required): Select the time of the event.
* **event_name** (`21`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no for the event name.
* **event_date** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or multiple event dates.
* **location** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or multiple locations.
* **t_shirt_quantity** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or multiple t-shirt quantities.
* **payment_method** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Select yes or no for the payment method.

Note: This form is designed for specific events or occasions, so some fields may not be relevant in other contexts.
