# new_stylist_launch_party_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a new stylist launch party, allowing event planners to gather details about the party in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: 
	* `party_name`
	* `launch_date`
	* `launch_time`
	* `launch_date_time`
2. Provide optional details about the party:
	* `party_location`
	* `party_description`
	* `launch_party_invitation`
	* `launch_party_theme`
	* `launch_party_agenda`

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new_stylist_launch_party_planning_form_1 (new_stylist_launch_party_planning)**:
	+ This field is used to describe the party in a few words. Try to be concise and creative!
* **launch_date**:
	+ Enter the date of the party.
* **party_name**:
	+ Enter the name of the party (e.g. "Stylist Launch Party").
* **party_description**:
	+ Provide a brief description of the party, including any relevant details.
* **party_location**:
	+ Enter the location where the party will take place.
* **launch_time**:
	+ Enter the time of the party (e.g. 7:00 PM).
* **launch_date_time**:
	+ Enter the date and time of the party (e.g. 2024-02-20 19:00).
* **launch_party_invitation**:
	+ Create a message that invites people to the party (e.g. "Join us for a night of style and glamour!").
* **launch_party_theme**:
	+ Describe the theme of the party (e.g. "Gatsby's Glamour Night").
* **launch_party_agenda**:
	+ Plan out the party's schedule, including any specific activities or events.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Use clear and concise language to describe the party.
* Double-check for errors before submitting the form.
