## Conference Speaker Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for speakers to apply to present a session at a conference. The conference organizers will review the submitted information to select suitable speakers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your personal information in the "Applicant Information" section.
2.  Provide your contact details in the "Email address" and "Phone number" fields.
3.  Select your preferred contact option.
4.  Enter your professional information in the "Organization or employer", "Job title" and "Years of speaking experience" fields.
5.  Describe your proposed session in the "Session title", "Session abstract", and "Session duration" fields.
6.  Specify your equipment requirements in the "Equipment requirements" field.
7.  If you have prior experience, provide links to recordings or slide decks in the "Links to prior talks or demos" field.
8.  Enter the details of your references in the "Professional references" field.
9.  Finally, confirm your consent to record your session in the "Consent to record session" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Full name**: Enter your legal name as you would like it to be displayed on the conference schedule.
*  **Email address**: Enter your primary contact email address for updates about your application and conference news.
*  **Phone number**: Enter your primary contact phone number for updates and scheduling.
*  **Organization or employer**: Enter the name of your affiliated group or company.
*  **Job title**: Enter your current role or position.
*  **Short bio**: Enter a 2-3 sentence professional summary.
*  **Proposed session title**: Enter a title for your proposed session.
*  **Session abstract**: Enter a 150-300 word description of your proposed session.
*  **Target audience**: Select the primary audience level you are targeting.
*  **Preferred session length**: Choose the session duration you prefer.
*  **Equipment requirements**: Select any equipment you need for your session, such as a projector or internet access.
*  **Links to prior talks or demos**: Provide URLs to recordings or slide decks of your prior talks or demos.
*  **Professional references**: Enter the names and contacts of 1-2 professional references.
*  **Years of speaking experience**: Enter your whole number of years of speaking experience.
*  **Consent to record session**: Select "True" if you consent to recording your session and "False" otherwise.
