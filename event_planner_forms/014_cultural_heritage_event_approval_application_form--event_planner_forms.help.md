# Cultural Heritage Event Approval Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about cultural heritage events for approval. Fill out the form carefully to avoid any duplication of effort.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the event details, including the event title, date, start and end times, and location.
2. Provide your information as the applicant, including your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant details.
3. Specify the event duration and venue type.
4. If applicable, fill in the ticket price and currency information.
5. Provide a description of the event and the name of the event organizer.
6. Optionally, fill in the event capacity and terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: Enter the title of the event you are applying for. This will be the name displayed on the event listing.
* **Applicant Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number. This will be used to contact you regarding your application.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event.
* **Venue Type**: Select the type of venue for the event (e.g., Stadium, Park, Museum).
* **Ticket Price**: Enter the price of the ticket for the event.
* **Ticket Price Currency**: Enter the currency of the ticket price (e.g., USD, CAD).
* **Ticket Price Currency Type**: Select the type of ticket price (e.g., Fixed, Dynamic).
* **Ticket Price Currency Rate**: Enter any additional details about the ticket price currency rate.
* **Ticket Price Currency Rate Type**: Select the type of ticket price currency rate (e.g., Fixed, Dynamic).
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the person or organization organizing the event.
* **Event Capacity**: Enter the maximum capacity of the event.
* **Terms and Conditions**: If required, enter any terms and conditions for the event.
