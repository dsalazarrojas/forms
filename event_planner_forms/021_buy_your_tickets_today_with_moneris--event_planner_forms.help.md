# Buy Your Tickets Today With Moneris - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you buy tickets for an event. Fill out the form with your details and preferences, and you will be able to complete your ticket purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Email Address" field, which will be used for ticket confirmation.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for event updates.
4. Select the type of ticket you want to purchase from the "Ticket Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the number of tickets you need in the "Number of Tickets" field.
6. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
7. If required, enter any special needs or accommodations in the "Special Needs or Accommodations" field.
8. If buying multiple tickets, list the names of the additional attendees in the "Names of Additional Attendees" field.
9. Enter your billing address, city, state or province, ZIP or postal code, and country in the "Billing Address", "City", "State or Province", and "Country" fields, respectively.
10. Enter the name on the credit card in the "Cardholder Name" field.
11. Enter your credit card number, expiration date, and CVV code in the "Credit Card Number", "Card Expiration Date", and "CVV Code" fields, respectively.
12. Enter the total ticket cost in the "Total Ticket Cost" field.
13. If you have a promotional code, enter it in the "Promotional Code" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (email): Enter a valid email address that will be used for ticket confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your contact phone number for event updates.
* **Ticket Type** (select_one): Select the type of ticket you want to purchase from the dropdown menu.
* **Number of Tickets** (number): Enter the number of tickets you need to purchase.
* **Event Date** (date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (text): If required, enter any accessibility requirements you may have.
* **Names of Additional Attendees** (text): If buying multiple tickets, list the names of the additional attendees.
* **Billing Address** (text): Enter your billing address.
* **City** (text): Enter the city of your billing address.
* **State or Province** (text): Enter the state or province of your billing address.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (text): Enter the ZIP or postal code of your billing address.
* **Country** (text): Enter the country of your billing address.
* **Cardholder Name** (text): Enter the name on the credit card.
* **Credit Card Number** (text): Enter your credit card number.
* **Card Expiration Date** (text): Enter the expiration date of your credit card in MM-YY format.
* **CVV Code** (text): Enter the 3-digit security code on your credit card.
* **Total Ticket Cost** (number): Enter the total ticket cost to be charged.
* **Promotional Code** (text): If you have a promotional code, enter it here.
