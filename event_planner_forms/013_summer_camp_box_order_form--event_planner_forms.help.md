# Summer Camp Box Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Camp Box Order Form is a tool used by event planners to collect information from participants regarding their summer camp boxes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are filling out this form for the correct participant and summer camp.
2. Enter the participant's delivery address in the "delivery address" field.
3. Specify the contents of the box the participant will receive in the "box contents" field.
4. Enter the participant's name in the "participant name" field.
5. Enter the participant's email address in the "participant email" field.
6. Enter the participant's phone number in the "participant phone" field.
7. Select the delivery date for the box in the "delivery date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **delivery address**: This field is where you enter the participant's delivery address where the summer camp box will be shipped.
* **box contents**: This field is where you specify what contents will be included in the participant's summer camp box.
* **participant name**: This field is where you enter the participant's name for reference purposes.
* **participant email**: This field is where you enter the participant's email address.
* **participant phone**: This field is where you enter the participant's phone number.
* **delivery date**: This field is where you select the date the box will be delivered.

## Tips
Make sure to double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
