# Bowling Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bowling Event Registration Form is a comprehensive form designed for teams to register for a bowling event. It collects essential information for event planners to manage teams, events, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your team's name, captain's first and last name, and contact information (email, phone number).
2. Select the event type (Weekly League, Tournament, Corporate Event, Birthday Party, Fundraiser, Charity Bowl, or Other).
3. Choose the event date and preferred time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Night).
4. Enter the number of team members and list their names.
5. Select your team's skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional, or Mixed levels).
6. Enter your approximate average score, if known.
7. Specify any lane preferences (No preference, Lanes 1-10, Lanes 11-20, Lanes 21-30, or Bumper lanes needed).
8. Choose whether shoe rental is needed and enter the number of pairs required.
9. List the shoe sizes needed for each team member.
10. Select a food and beverage package (None, Snacks only, Pizza package, Buffet package, or Full catering).
11. Specify any dietary restrictions.
12. Choose whether your team will order alcohol and if all team members are 21 or older.
13. Select any additional services (Scorekeeping service, Photography, Awards or trophies, Party favors, or Video replay).
14. Enter any special requests.
15. Choose your payment method (Credit card, Debit card, Cash, Check, or Corporate account).
16. Confirm whether you have paid the deposit.
17. Agree to the cancellation policy.
18. Agree to the liability waiver.
19. Sign with your full name as the captain and enter the date of registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, required): Enter your team's name.
* **Team Captain First Name** (Team Captain First Name, text, required): Enter the captain's first name.
* **Team Captain Last Name** (Team Captain Last Name, text, required): Enter the captain's last name.
* **Captain Email** (Captain Email, email, required): Enter the captain's email address for team communications.
* **Captain Phone** (Captain Phone, text, required): Enter the captain's primary contact number.
* **Event Name** (Event Name, select_one, required): Select the type of event (Weekly League, Tournament, Corporate Event, Birthday Party, Fundraiser, Charity Bowl, or Other).
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time, select_one, required): Choose the preferred start time of the event (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Night).
* **Number of Team Members** (Number of Team Members, number, required): Enter the total number of team members.
* **Team Member Names** (Team Member Names, text, required): List all team member names.
* **Team Skill Level** (Team Skill Level, select_one, required): Select the overall team's skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional, or Mixed levels).
* **Average Score** (Average Score, text, optional): Enter the approximate team average score, if known.
* **Lane Preference** (Lane Preference, select_one, optional): Specify any lane preferences.
* **Shoe Rental Needed** (Shoe Rental Needed, number, required): Indicate if shoe rental is needed and enter the number of pairs required.
* **Shoe Sizes** (Shoe Sizes, text, optional): List the shoe sizes needed for each team member.
* **Food and Beverage Package** (Food and Beverage Package, select_one, required): Select the preferred food and beverage package.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Dietary Restrictions, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions.
* **Alcoholic Beverages** (Alcoholic Beverages, select_one, required): Choose whether your team will order alcohol and if all team members are 21 or older.
* **Age Verification** (Age Verification, select_one, required): Confirm whether all team members are 21 or older.
* **Additional Services** (Additional Services, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services (Scorekeeping service, Photography, Awards or trophies, Party favors, or Video replay).
* **Special Requests** (Special Requests, text, optional): Enter any special requests.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose your payment method (Credit card, Debit card, Cash, Check, or Corporate account).
* **Deposit Paid** (Deposit Paid, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have paid the deposit.
* **Cancellation Policy** (Cancellation Policy, select_one, required): Agree to the cancellation policy.
* **Liability Waiver** (Liability Waiver, select_one, required): Agree to the liability waiver.
* **Captain Signature** (Captain Signature, text, required): Sign with your full name as the captain.
* **Signature Date** (Signature Date, date, required): Enter the date of registration.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your team's information and event details before submitting.
* If you have any special requests, please make sure to specify them clearly.
* If you have dietary restrictions or need shoe rental, please make sure to enter that information correctly.
* If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact the event planners.
