# Thanksgiving Party Signup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Thanksgiving Party Signup Form is used to gather information from users about their preferences for the upcoming party. This information is used to plan and organize the event according to the users' needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the number of guests you plan to attend with.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select a date for the event.
6. Choose a time for the event.
7. Add any additional messages or comments about the event.
8. List any friends attending with you.
9. List any family members attending with you.
10. Indicate whether any friends will be attending (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Count** (Required: false): Enter the number of guests you plan to bring with you to the party.
* **Name** (Required: false): Enter your name as it should be displayed at the party.
* **Email** (Required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the party.
* **Phone** (Required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the party.
* **Date** (Required: false): Select a date for the party. You can choose any date, but please select a date that works for you.
* **Time** (Required: false): Choose a time for the party. You can select any time, but please choose a time that works for you.
* **Message** (Required: false): Add any additional comments or messages about the event.
* **Friends** (Required: false): List any friends attending with you.
* **Family** (Required: false): List any family members attending with you.
* **Fiends** (Required: false): This field seems to be a typo. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether any friends will be attending.

Note: The form will automatically validate the fields for required fields, so you won't be able to submit the form if you haven't filled out all required fields.
