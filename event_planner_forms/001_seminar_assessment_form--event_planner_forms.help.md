# Seminar Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seminar assessment form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from attendees on the seminar they attended. Your input is valuable in helping us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer it to the best of your abilities.
2. Fill in all the required fields. The "Content", "Materials", "Overall Rating", and "Want to Be Contacted" fields are mandatory.
3. If you have any general comments or concerns, please use the "General Comments" field.
4. Select one of the options for "Want to Be Contacted" to indicate your preference.
5. Fill in the "Content", "Materials", and "Overall Rating" fields accordingly.
6. Use the "Comments About Presenter" and "Comments About Instructor" fields to provide feedback on the presenter and instructor.
7. Fill in the "Comments" field with any additional feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Comments** (`General Comments`, text, optional): This field is for any comments or concerns you may have about the seminar. Please feel free to share any thoughts or suggestions you may have.
* **Why Are You Attending** (`Why Are You Attending`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe why you are attending the seminar. This will help us understand your perspective and improve future events.
* **Content** (`Content`, number, required): Rate the content of the seminar on a scale of 1-5.
* **Materials** (`Materials`, number, required): Rate the materials provided for the seminar on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments About Presenter** (`Comments About Presenter`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide feedback on the presenter's performance.
* **Comments About Instructor** (`Comments About Instructor`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide feedback on the instructor's performance.
* **Want to Be Contacted** (`Want to Be Contacted`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be contacted if we need to follow up with you.
	+ Contact Me: We will contact you via email or phone if needed.
	+ No Preference: We will not contact you unless necessary.
	+ Yes, Please: We will contact you via email or phone if needed.
* **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, number, required): Rate the seminar overall on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional feedback you may have.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
- If you have any concerns or issues, please provide a detailed description.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them with us.
