# Event Risk Management Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Risk Management Checklist is designed to help you identify and document the risks associated with events. This form helps you gather information on the risk level, type, and measures taken to mitigate potential hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the **Risk Level** of the potential event. Choose from **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**.
2.  Next, select the **Risk Type** by choosing one of the options: **Safety**, **Security**, **Environmental**, **Financial**, or **Logistical**.
3.  In the **Potential Hazard** field, describe the potential hazard or risk.
4.  For each **Risk Summary** field, briefly describe the risks identified.
5.  In the **Control Measures** field, list all control measures you have taken or will take to mitigate the potential risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Risk Level**: Select the level of risk you associate with the event. Choose from **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**.
* **Risk Type**: Identify the type of risk, such as **Safety**, **Security**, **Environmental**, **Financial**, or **Logistical**.
* **Potential Hazard**: Describe the potential hazard or risk you are concerned about.
* **Control Measures**: List all control measures you have taken or will take to mitigate the potential risks.
* **Risk Summary** (fields 6-8): Briefly describe the risks identified for each summary field. 

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the potential hazard and risks.
* List all relevant control measures to mitigate the potential risks.
* Review each risk summary field to ensure they are clear and concise.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to [form contact email].
