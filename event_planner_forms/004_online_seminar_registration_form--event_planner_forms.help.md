# online_seminar_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online seminar registration form is used to collect information from attendees for an online seminar. This form is designed to be filled out by attendees to register for the seminar and provide event planners with the necessary details to follow up with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the seminar details, including the title of the seminar.
2. Enter your attendee details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments you may have about the seminar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Details** (`seminar_details`): This field is where you will enter the title of the seminar you are registering for. Please make sure to enter the correct title as it appears on the seminar description.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`): This field is where you will enter your information as an attendee. Please include your name, as it should match your identification.
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`): This field is where you will enter the date of the seminar you are registering for. Please make sure to enter the correct date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Seminar Time** (`seminar_time`): This field is where you will enter the time of the seminar you are registering for. Please make sure to enter the correct time in the format of 12-hour clock (e.g. 2:00 PM).
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`): This field is where you will enter your name as it should appear on the registration. Please make sure to enter it as it appears on your identification.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`): This field is where you will enter your email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address that you use regularly.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`): This field is where you will enter your phone number. Please make sure to enter a valid phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Attendee Notes** (`attendee_notes`): This field is where you can provide any additional notes or comments about the seminar. You can use this space to ask questions or provide feedback about the seminar.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns about the seminar, please reach out to the event planner for assistance.
* If you need to make any changes to your registration, please contact the event planner and they will assist you in the necessary changes.
