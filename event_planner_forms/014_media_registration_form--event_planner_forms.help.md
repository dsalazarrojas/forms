# media_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The media_registration_form is used for registering media outlets, including journalists, for access to media resources. It is designed to collect necessary contact information and agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your primary contact email in the Email field.
4. Enter any additional contact emails in the Additional Contact Email field (if applicable).
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
6. Select your role in the Role field.
7. Enter your company name (if applicable) in the Company field.
8. Enter your media outlet in the Media Outlet field (if applicable).
9. Enter your address in the Address field (if applicable).
10. Enter your zip code in the Zip field.
11. Enter your city in the City field.
12. Enter your state in the State field.
13. Select your country in the Country field (if applicable).
14. Provide additional information in the Additional Information field (if applicable).
15. Select the agreements you are willing to adhere to in the Agreement field.
16. Select the agreements you are willing to adhere to (if not already selected) in the Agreement 2 field.
17. Select any other agreements you are willing to adhere to in the Other Agreement field (if applicable).
18. (Optional) Select additional agreements in the Agreement 3, Agreement 4, Agreement 5, Agreement 6, Agreement 7, Agreement 8, Agreement 9, and Agreement 10 fields.

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.

* **Role** (`6`, `text`, required): Select your role (e.g., journalist, producer, etc.).
* **Company** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Media Outlet** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your media outlet name (if applicable).
* **Address** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter your media outlet address (if applicable).
