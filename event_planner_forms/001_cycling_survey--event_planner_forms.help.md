# Cycling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cycling Survey is designed to gather information about your cycling habits and experiences. This data will help us understand your needs and preferences, which will be used to improve our services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. You can select only one option for most questions.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. You can leave questions blank if you do not have an answer or prefer not to answer.
5. Submit your completed form to help us better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cycling Survey** (`cycling_survey_first_page`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the survey.
* **Years of Experience** (`experience_level`, number, required/optional): Please select how many years you have been cycling.
* **Terrain Difficulty** (`terrain_difficulty`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the terrain difficulty you usually ride on. Options are Mountainous, Flat, Steep, and Other.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select how often you train or ride. Options are Daily, 2-3 times a week, 1 time a week, and Rarely.
* **Competence Level** (`cycling_competence_level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your cycling competence level. Options are High, Low.
* **Riding Type** (`riding_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of riding you do most often. Options are Commuter, Sport, Recreational, Social.
* **Ride Length** (`ride_length`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select how long you usually ride. Options are Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4+ hours.
* **Ride Frequency** (`ride_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select how often you ride. Options are Daily, 2-3 times a week, 1 time a week, Rarely.
* **Ride Distance** (`ride_distance`, number, required/optional): Please enter the distance you usually ride.
* **Ride Terrain** (`ride_terrain`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of terrain you usually ride on. Options are Flat, Steep, Mixed.
* **Ride Type** (`ride_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of ride you usually do. Options are On-Road, Off-Road, Both.
* **Bike Type** (`bike_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of bike you use. Options are Mountain, Road, Hybrid, Electric.
* **Ride with** (`ride_with`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who you usually ride with. Options are Alone, With a group, With a group of friends.
* **Ride with a Child** (`ride_with_child`, select_one, required/optional): Do you ride with a child? Options are True, False.
* **Ride with Pets** (`ride_with_pets`, select_one, required/optional): Do you ride with pets? Options are True, False.
* **Ride in Weather** (`ride_in_weather`, select_one, required/optional): Do you ride in rain or shine? Options are Rain or shine, Only sunny days, Only dry days.
* **Ride in Traffic** (`ride_in_traffic`, select_one, required/optional): Do you ride in traffic? Options are True, False.
* **Safety Equipment Used** (`safety_equipment_used`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the safety equipment you use. Options are Helmet, Lights, Other.
* **Ride on Weekend** (`ride_on_weekend`, select_multiple, required/optional): Do you ride on weekend? Options are True, False.
* **Ride on Holiday** (`ride_on_holiday`, select_multiple, required/optional): Do you ride on holiday? Options are True, False.
* **Safety Training Completed** (`safety_training_completed`, select_one, required/optional): Have you completed safety training? Options are True, False.
* **Safety Course Completed** (`safety_course_completed`, select_one, required/optional): Have you completed a safety course? Options are True, False.
* **Safety Certificate Obtained** (`safety_certificate_obtained`, select_one, required/optional): Have you obtained a safety certificate? Options are True, False.
* **Insurance Held** (`insurance_held`, select_one, required/optional): Do you hold insurance for cycling? Options are True, False.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Please provide emergency contact information (e.g., phone number).
