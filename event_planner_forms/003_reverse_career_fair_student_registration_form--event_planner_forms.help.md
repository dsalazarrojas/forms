# Reverse Career Fair Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students for a reverse career fair event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. For the follow-up section, only fill in the fields that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you are representing.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title at the company.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a student.
* **University** (`university`, text, required): Enter the name of your university.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Upload or link to your resume.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the career fair event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Select the time of the career fair event.
* **Career Fair Details** (`career_fair_details`, text, required): Enter any additional information about the event or your expectations.
* **Career Fair Location** (`career_fair_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the career fair event.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method: Mobile, Email, or In Person.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (`preferred_contact_time`, time, required): Select the time of your preferred contact.
* **Follow Up Request** (`follow_up_request`, select_multiple, required): If you agreed to follow up, select the times when you would like to be contacted.
* **Follow Up Message** (`follow_up_message`, text, optional): If you agreed to follow up, enter a brief message.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): If you agreed to follow up, select the time of your follow-up.
* **Follow Up Location** (`follow_up_location`, text, optional): If you agreed to follow up, enter the location of your follow-up.
* **Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred follow-up method: Phone, Email, or In Person.
* **Follow Up Note** (`follow_up_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional information for your follow-up.
* **Follow Up Additional Information** (`follow_up_additional_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information for your follow-up.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or expectations you have for the career fair event.
* **Student Availability** (`student_availability`, select_one, required): Select your availability for the career fair event: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Student Interviewers** (`student_interviewers`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the students who will be attending the event.
* **Student Resume Link** (`student_resume_link`, text, required): Enter a link to your resume.
* **Student Agreement** (`student_agreement`, select_one, required): Select if you agree to the terms of the reverse career fair event.

## Tips
* Please be accurate when filling in the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the event organizers.
* Make sure to upload your resume or link to it.
* The follow-up section is only required if you agreed to it.
