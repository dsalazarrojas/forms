# Pie Judging Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pie Judging Form is a tool used by event organizers to collect input from judges on the quality of pies entered into a competition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, enter the name of the competitor in the "Competitor name" field.
2. To rate the pie, type a number between 1 and 10 in the "Rating" field.
3. Provide comments about the pie in the "Comments" field, if necessary.
4. If you have any contact information (email or phone number), please enter it in the corresponding fields.
5. Click "Submit" to finish filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competitor name** (text): Enter the name of the competitor's pie.
* **Rating** (number): Rate the pie on the scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (text): Add any comments about the pie, if necessary.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted by the event organizers.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted by the event organizers.
* **Submitter name** (text): Enter your name, if you'd like to be credited for submitting the form.
