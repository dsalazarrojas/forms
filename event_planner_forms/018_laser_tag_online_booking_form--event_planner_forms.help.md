# Laser Tag Online Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users book and manage events on the Laser Tag online platform. It collects necessary information to create and update event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields to provide accurate and complete information.
*   Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Title** (`event_form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the event that summarizes its main purpose.
*   **Event Description** (`event_form_description`, text, required): Describe the event in detail, including its main objectives and highlights.
*   **Event Location** (`event_form_location`, text, required): Specify the event location, including the venue and address.
*   **Start Date** (`event_form_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the event in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Start Time** (`event_form_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
*   **End Date** (`event_form_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the event in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **End Time** (`event_form_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the event in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
*   **Duration** (`event_form_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the event in minutes.
*   **Organizer** (`event_form_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the event organizer.
*   **Created By** (`event_form_created_by`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person who created the event.
*   **Updated By** (`event_form_updated_by`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person who last updated the event.
*   **Notes** (`event_form_notes`, text, required): Enter any relevant notes about the event, such as additional details or reminders. 

## Tips
*   Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   This form will be used for both event creation and updates.
