# event_presenter_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event_presenter_feedback_form is designed to gather feedback from event presenters regarding their performance and the overall event experience. This form is meant to be filled out by the presenters after each event, providing an opportunity for improvement and evaluation of the event's success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and answers carefully to ensure you provide accurate and thoughtful feedback.
2. Complete each field as instructed.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in their labels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Presenter Survey** (`event_presenter_survey`, text, optional)
Complete this field with any comments or observations about the event and your experience as a presenter.

### **Ratings** (`ratings`, text, optional)
Rate your overall experience as a presenter at this event. This will help us understand your perspective and identify areas for improvement.

### **How Did Presenters' Performance** (`how_did_presenters_performance`, select_one, optional)
Rate the performance of the presenters at this event.

*   Excellent
*   Good
*   Neutral
*   Poor
*   Unhappy

### **How Was the Event** (`how_was_event`, select_one, optional)
Rate the overall experience of the event.

*   Excellent
*   Good
*   Neutral
*   Poor
*   Unhappy

### **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, optional)
Indicate whether you would recommend this event to others.

*   True
*   False

### **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
Share any additional comments or feedback about the event.

### **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional)
Enter your contact email address so we can reach out to you with any questions or follow-up.

### **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional)
Enter your contact phone number, if you're comfortable sharing it.

### **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional)
Provide the name of the event you're giving feedback about.
