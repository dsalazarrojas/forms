# event_design_agency_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an event planning agency, enabling potential clients or partners to easily access and review agency details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Provide basic event planner information.
2. Enter agency details, including any relevant information about your agency.
3. List your agency's services offered.
4. Share a brief portfolio of your agency's work.
5. Describe your agency's experience.
6. Share information about your team.
7. Enter contact information for further communication and follow-up.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Planner Info** (`event_planner_info`, text, optional): 
    This field allows the user to input their basic event planning information.
- **Agency Details** (`agency_details`, text, optional): 
    This field allows the user to enter any relevant details about their agency.
- **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, optional): 
    This field is used to list the services that your agency offers to clients.
- **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional): 
    This field should be used to share a brief portfolio of your agency's work, showcasing your expertise and creativity.
- **Agency Experience** (`agency_experience`, text, optional): 
    This field is used to describe your agency's experience, providing a brief overview of your team's skills and qualifications.
- **Team Info** (`team_info`, text, optional): 
    This field allows the user to share information about their team, including team members' names and roles.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): 
    This field is used to enter contact information for your agency, allowing potential clients or partners to get in touch with you.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): 
    This is the final step to complete the form. Clicking this button will send the submitted information for review.
