# Christmas Gift Giveaway Waiting List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who wish to be on a Christmas gift giveaway waiting list. The information collected will be reviewed internally to determine eligibility and create a waiting list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your date of birth and confirm that you meet the age requirements.
3. Provide your street address, city, and state/province.
4. Choose the number of people in your household and the number of children under 18.
5. Select the type of gifts that would be most helpful.
6. Indicate how you heard about the giveaway and if you were referred by someone.
7. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
8. Choose how you would like to be contacted about the status of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted about the status of your application.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for urgent notifications.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. You must be at least 18 years old to be eligible.
* **Street Address**: Enter your full street address where gifts can be delivered.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your address.
* **Household Size**: Choose the number of people in your household.
* **Number of Children**: Choose the number of children under the age of 18 in your household.
* **Children Ages**: Enter the ages of your children, separated by commas (optional).
* **Household Income Bracket**: Choose your household income bracket for eligibility verification.
* **Current Assistance Programs**: Select all the government assistance programs you are currently participating in.
* **Gift Preferences**: Choose the type of gifts that would be most helpful for you and your household.
* **How Did You Hear About Giveaway**: Select how you heard about the giveaway.
* **Referred By**: Enter the name of the person who referred you (if applicable).
* **Agree to Terms**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the giveaway.
* **Privacy Consent**: Confirm that you give permission to be contacted about the status of your application.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information or circumstances that may affect your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any special circumstances that may affect your eligibility, include them in the "Additional Notes" field.
