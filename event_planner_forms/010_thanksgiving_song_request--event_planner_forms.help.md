# Thanksgiving Song Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit their song requests for a Thanksgiving celebration or event. It allows users to provide detailed information about the song they want to be played, including title, artist, genre, and other specific attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the song you would like to be played.
2. Identify the artist who performs the song.
3. Provide your email address for contact purposes.
4. Enter any additional information about the song, such as genre, lyrics, and tempo.
5. Optionally, you can provide your phone number for further contact.
6. Select whether you would like to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What Song Would You Like** (`text`, required): Enter the title of the song you would like to be played.
- **Artist** (`text`, required): Identify the artist who performs the song.
- **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Name** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself.
- **Message** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
- **Category** (`text`, optional): Select a category for your song request.
- **Genre** (`text`, optional): Enter the genre of music you prefer.
- **Song Length** (`text`, optional): Estimate the length of the song.
- **Lyrics** (`text`, optional): Enter any lyrics you'd like to include with your request.
- **Song Type** (`text`, optional): Select the type of song you're requesting.
- **Tempo** (`text`, optional): Enter the tempo of the song.
- **Mood** (`text`, optional): Select the mood you'd like the song to convey.
- **Theme** (`text`, optional): Enter any theme you'd like the song to be related to.
- **Song Request ID** (`number`, optional): Enter a unique identifier for your song request, but please do not enter a number.
- **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
- **Message 2** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
- **Name of Artist** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the artist performing the song.
- **Name of Song** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the song.
- **Year** (`text`, optional): Enter the year the song was released.
- **Category 2** (`text`, optional): Select a category for your song request.
- **Genre 2** (`text`, optional): Enter the genre of music you prefer.
- **Song Length 2** (`text`, optional): Estimate the length of the song.
- **Lyrics 2** (`text`, optional): Enter any lyrics you'd like to include with your request.
- **Submit** (`select_one`, optional): Select whether you would like to submit your request.

Note: Some fields are optional, so users can complete the form with only the required fields.
