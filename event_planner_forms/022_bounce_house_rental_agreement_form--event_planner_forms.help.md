# Bounce House Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a rental agreement for bounce houses and other inflatable play equipment. It is designed to gather information from renters to ensure a safe and successful event. The form is used to collect details about the event, including the date, location, and number of guests expected. It also includes sections for liability waiver and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details: First name, last name, address, phone number, and email.
2.  Provide event details: Date of the event, when you would like the equipment delivered, and when it should be picked up.
3.  Choose the location: Where the bounce house will be set up, and select the size of the inflatable you want to rent.
4.  Select the event duration: How many hours you need the rental for.
5.  Provide guest information: Age range of children and the approximate number of guests.
6.  Payment details: Choose a payment method and provide any additional services needed.
7.  Acknowledge the terms: Liability waiver, safety rules, and weather policy.
8.  Sign and date: Type your name, date, and provide the name of any company representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renter First Name** (`renter_first_name`, text, required): Please type your legal first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Renter Last Name** (`renter_last_name`, text, required): Please type your legal last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Address** (`renter_address`, text, required): Enter your complete home address.
* **City** (`renter_city`, text, required): Enter the city of residence.
* **State** (`renter_state`, text, required): Enter the state of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`renter_zip`, text, required): Enter your home ZIP code.
* **Phone Number** (`renter_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Email Address** (`renter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for rental communications.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter when you would like the equipment delivered.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Enter when you would like the equipment picked up.
* **Event Location Address** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the complete address where the event will be held.
* **Location Type** (`location_type`, select_one, required): Choose where the bounce house will be set up (e.g., residential backyard, park, etc.).
* **Bounce House Selection** (`bounce_house_selection`, select_one, required): Choose which inflatable to rent (e.g., standard bounce house, combo unit, etc.).
* **Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the number of hours you need the rental for (e.g., 4 hours, 8 hours, etc.).
* **Primary Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Choose the age range of the children using the equipment.
* **Expected Number of Guests** (`expected_guests`, number, required): Enter the approximate number of children.
* **Power Source Available** (`power_source_available`, select_one, required): Check if an electrical outlet is available at the location.
* **Setup Space Dimensions** (`setup_space_dimensions`, text, required): Enter the available space for the inflatable in feet.
* **Surface Type** (`surface_type`, select_one, required): Choose the surface type where the bounce house will be set up (e.g., grass, concrete, etc.).
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any additional services needed (e.g., attendant service, generator rental, etc.).
* **Total Rental Cost** (`total_rental_cost`, text, required): Enter the total amount to be paid.
* **Deposit Paid** (`deposit_paid`, text, required): Enter the amount of deposit paid.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Check if you agree to the liability waiver terms.
* **Safety Rules Acknowledged** (`safety_rules_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Check if you have read and agree to enforce safety rules.
* **Damage Responsibility** (`damage_responsibility`, select_one, required): Check if you understand you are responsible for damages.
* **Weather Policy** (`weather_policy`, select_one, required): Check if you understand the weather cancellation policy.
* **Renter Signature** (`renter_signature`, text, required): Type your full legal name as a signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signing.
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, text, required): Enter the name of the company representative.
* **Company Signature** (`company_signature`, text, required): Enter the signature of the company representative.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the liability waiver and safety rules before signing.
* Be aware of the weather policy and any cancellation terms.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us before signing the form.
