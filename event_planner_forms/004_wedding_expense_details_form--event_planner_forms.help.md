# Wedding Expense Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Expense Details Form is used to collect information about a wedding event, specifically the vendor's name, address, and contact information. This form is intended for non-mandatory submission, allowing users to provide additional details about the vendor associated with the wedding event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the wedding expense details form title in the text field provided.
2. Enter the vendor's name in the "Vendor Name" field.
3. Enter the vendor's address in the "Vendor Address" field.
4. Enter the vendor's phone number in the "Vendor Phone" field.
5. Select the event date for the wedding expense in the "Event Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Expense Details Form** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief title for the form submission.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the vendor's name, such as the caterer, florist, or photographer.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the wedding event.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the vendor's address, such as their physical location or contact address.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the vendor's phone number, allowing for easy contact and communication.
