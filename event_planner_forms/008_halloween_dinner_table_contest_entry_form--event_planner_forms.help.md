# Halloween Dinner Table Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Dinner Table Contest Entry Form is used to collect information from contestants to evaluate their dishes for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out (no fields are marked as required on this form, so fill out all fields).
3. Submit the form after filling out all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contestant Name
* **Contestant Name** (`Contestant Name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the contestant.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `number`, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Message
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments.

### Dish Name
* **Dish Name** (`Dish Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your dish.

### Recipe
* **Recipe** (`Recipe`, `text`, optional): Enter the recipe for your dish.
