# Christmas Musical Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect applications for the Christmas musical event. It will be used to find suitable performers for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your applicant name in the "Applicant name" field.
2. Provide your primary contact email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Enter your mobile phone number in the "Phone number" field for last-minute notifications.
4. Describe the role or part you are interested in performing in the "Role interested" field.
5. If applicable, enter a brief summary of your relevant performance experience in the "Performance experience" field.
6. Specify your availability for rehearsals in the "Availability for rehearsals" field.
7. If you have a specific vocal part (soprano, alto, tenor, bass, or instrumentalist), select it from the "Vocal part" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact name" field.
9. Provide the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact phone" field.
10. If you have any additional information the director should know, enter it in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address**: Your primary email address used for communication with the event team.
* **Phone number**: Your mobile phone number for last-minute notifications and rehearsal scheduling.
* **Role interested**: Describe the specific role or part you are interested in performing (e.g., soprano, tenor, instrumentalist).
* **Performance experience**: Briefly summarize your relevant performance experience (if applicable).
* **Availability for rehearsals**: Specify the days and times you are available for rehearsals.
* **Vocal part**: Select your vocal part (soprano, alto, tenor, bass, or instrumentalist) if you have one.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Additional notes**: Enter any additional information the director should know about you.
