# easter_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "easter_checklist" form is designed to gather information about Easter celebrations and ensure that all essential elements are included.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Easter Date** in the date field. This is the date of your Easter celebration.
2. Select the **Easter Time** for your event.
3. Enter the **Easter Message**, if you'd like to have a special message or theme for your celebration.
4. Choose the Easter Menu options that apply to your celebration.
5. Select the Easter Activities you'd like to include.
6. Choose the types of guests you're inviting for your Easter celebration.
7. Enter the **Easter Contact Email** and **Easter Contact Phone** for those who will be responsible for the event.
8. (Optional) Add any **Easter Comments** you'd like to include.
9. Confirm that you've completed the **Easter Checklist** by selecting "True".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Easter Date** (`easter_date`, required): Enter the date of your Easter celebration.
* **Easter Time** (`easter_time`, required): Select the time for your Easter event.
* **Easter Message** (`easter_message`, required): Enter a special message or theme for your Easter celebration.
* **Easter Menu** (`easter_menu`, required): Choose the types of food you'd like to have for your Easter celebration (e.g., ham, fish, lamb, egg).
* **Easter Activities** (`easter_activities`, required): Select the activities you'd like to include in your Easter celebration (e.g., Easter Egg Hunt, Easter Egg Decorating, Easter Egg Roll, Easter Egg Hunt with Family).
* **Easter Guests** (`easter_guests`, required): Choose the types of guests you're inviting for your Easter celebration (e.g., Family Members, Close Friends, Distant Relatives, Other Guests).
* **Easter Contact Email** (`easter_email`, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the event.
* **Easter Contact Phone** (`easter_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the event.
* **Easter Comments** (`easter_comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to include.
* **Are you sure the Easter Checklist is Complete?** (`easter_checklist_complete`, required): Confirm that you've completed the form accurately.
