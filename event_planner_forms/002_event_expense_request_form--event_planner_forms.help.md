### Event Expense Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Expense Request Form is designed to facilitate the process of requesting reimbursement for work-related expenses. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected to process your request accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Event Expense Request** field with a brief description of the event you are requesting reimbursement for.
2. Enter your **Submitter Details**:
	* **Submitter's Email**: Enter your company email address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
	* **Address**: Enter your work address.
	* **City**: Enter the city of your work location.
	* **State**: Enter the state of your work location.
	* **Zip**: Enter your work zip code.
	* **Country**: Enter the country of your work location.
	* **Company**: Enter your company name.
	* **Department**: Enter your department name.
	* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
	* **Signature**: This is a placeholder for your digital signature.
3. Select the **Type of Expense** you are requesting reimbursement for (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
4. Enter the **Date** of the event you are requesting reimbursement for.
5. Enter the **Amount** of the expense you are requesting reimbursement for.
6. Select whether the expense has been **Reimbursed By** ('Yes' or 'No').
7. Enter any additional **Comment** about the event.
8. Upload any relevant **Image 1** and **Image 2** supporting documents.
9. Select whether you **Agree** with the terms ('Yes' or 'No').
10. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of submission.
11. Enter any further **Comment** about the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Expense Request**: A brief description of the event you are requesting reimbursement for.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Submitter**: Enter your name and other contact details.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Type of Expense**: Select the type of expense you are requesting reimbursement for.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **Date**: Enter the date of the event.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the expense you are requesting reimbursement for.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: Yes
* **Reimbursed By**: Select whether the expense has been reimbursed or not.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **Comment**: Enter any additional comments about the event.
	+ Type: Note
	+ Required: No
* **Image 1**: Upload any supporting documents.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Image 2**: Upload any additional supporting documents.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Agree**: Select whether you agree with the terms.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **Date**: Enter the date of submission.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Time**: Enter the time of submission.
	+ Type: Time
	+ Required: Yes
* **Comment**: Enter any further comments about the submission.
	+ Type: Note
	+ Required: No

Note: This guide assumes that the user is familiar with basic computer skills and internet navigation. If you need help with a specific field, please contact your HR department for assistance.
