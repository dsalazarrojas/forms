# speaking_engagement_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about speaking engagements, such as events, conferences, and meetups. It helps the organizing team to prepare and assign the necessary resources for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name and details of the event in the "Event Name" field.
2. Select the date of the event using the calendar icon in the "Event Date" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the event in the "Event Description" field.
4. List any specific requirements or preferences for the speaker in the "Speaker Requirements" field.
5. Specify the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
6. Enter the contact information for the event organizer in the "Contact Information" field.
7. If necessary, add any special preferences or requirements for the speaker in the "Speaker Preferences" field.
8. Finally, confirm the tool or platform assigned to the event in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event, e.g., "Annual Conference 2023" or "Meetup Event".
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event using the calendar icon. Ensure you pick the correct date to avoid any confusion.
* **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of the event, including its purpose, format, and any other relevant details.
* **Speaker Requirements**: List any specific requirements or preferences for the speaker, such as equipment, accommodations, or special requests.
* **Event Location**: Enter the physical location of the event, including the address or venue name.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact email or phone number of the event organizer for easy communication.
* **Speaker Preferences**: If necessary, add any special preferences or requirements for the speaker, such as language, travel arrangements, or dietary needs.
* **Assigned Tool**: Confirm the tool or platform assigned to the event, such as a presentation software or video conferencing platform.
