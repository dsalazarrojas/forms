# Ceremony Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ceremony Agreement Form is a document that helps establish a clear understanding between the event organizer and attendees about the event's logistics and financial expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "section details" field with a brief description of the ceremony.
2. Enter your name and title in the "organizer name" field.
3. Provide your primary contact email address in the "contact email" field.
4. Enter your best phone number for event coordination in the "contact phone" field.
5. Select the date of the ceremony from the calendar picker.
6. Enter the name of the venue where the ceremony will take place.
7. Enter the estimated number of guests attending the ceremony.
8. Describe any special requirements, such as audio visual needs or catering requests, in the "special requirements" field.
9. Select the payment terms from the dropdown menu, including "Deposit required", "Invoice after event", or "Payment at booking".
10. Finally, sign your name in the "agreement signature name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Ceremony Details --** (Section, Not required): Provide a brief description of the ceremony to formalize the agreement.
- **Organizer Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name and title, such as "Event Coordinator" or "Planner".
- **Contact Email** (Email, Required): Enter your primary contact email address for event coordination.
- **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your best phone number for event coordination.
- **Ceremony Date** (Date, Required): Select the date of the ceremony from the calendar picker.
- **Venue Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the venue where the ceremony will take place.
- **Estimated Guest Count** (Number, Optional): Enter the estimated number of attendees.
- **Special Requirements** (Text, Optional): Describe any special requirements, such as audio visual needs or catering requests.
- **Payment Terms** (Select One, Required): Select the payment terms from the dropdown menu, including "Deposit required", "Invoice after event", or "Payment at booking".
- **Agreement Signature Name** (Text, Required): Sign your name to confirm the agreement.
