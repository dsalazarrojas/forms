# Puppeteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Puppeteer Registration Form is used to collect information from potential performers for events. By filling out this form, you will be registered as a performer in our database and can be considered for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the performer.
2. Enter your contact email address.
3. Enter your contact phone number.
4. Select your performance dates.
5. Choose your availability (if applicable).
6. Provide a brief bio (if applicable).
7. Enter any relevant image, social media, or website URLs (if applicable).
8. Upload any relevant music or performance files (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Puppeteer Name** (`puppeteer_name`, text): Enter your name as the performer.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Performance Dates** (`performance_dates`, select_multiple): Select the dates on which you are available to perform.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one): If you are available, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Availability Options** (`availability_options`, text): If you have specific availability options, enter them here.
* **Bio** (`bio`, text): Briefly describe your experience and style as a performer.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text): Enter a URL to your performer image.
* **Social Media URL** (`social_media_url`, text): Enter a URL to your social media profile.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text): Enter a URL to your website.
* **YouTube URL** (`youtube_url`, text): Enter a URL to your YouTube channel.
* **SoundCloud URL** (`soundcloud_url`, text): Enter a URL to your SoundCloud account.
* **Music Files** (`music_files`, text): Upload any relevant music or performance files.
* **Bio File URL** (`bio_file_url`, text): Enter a URL to your bio file (if applicable).
* **Performance Files** (`performance_files`, text): Upload any relevant performance files (if applicable).
* **Rehearsal Files** (`rehearsal_files`, text): Enter a URL to any rehearsed files (if applicable).
