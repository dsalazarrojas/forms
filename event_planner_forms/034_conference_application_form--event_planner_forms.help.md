# Conference Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Application Form is designed to collect essential information from applicants to assess their eligibility for a conference. This form will be used internally to verify the accuracy and completeness of the application information. It is recommended to fill out the form only once, as any subsequent submissions will be considered duplicate applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
2.  Enter your affiliated organization or company (if applicable).
3.  Provide your email address for correspondence and follow-up communication.
4.  Select the type of application you are submitting: Speaker, Exhibitor, Sponsor, or Attendee.
5.  If applying as a Speaker, provide a title for your proposed session.
6.  If applicable, provide a brief abstract or proposal summary of your session.
7.  Specify any technical requirements for your session, such as audio-visual or staging needs.
8.  List any relevant references or past presentations that support your application.
9.  Review the agreement terms and conditions, which are provided for reference.
10.  After submitting the form, you will receive an application ID for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name**: Your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Organization**: The name of the organization or company you are affiliated with (if any).
* **Email address**: The email address used for communication and follow-up.
* **Application type**: Select one of the following options: Speaker, Exhibitor, Sponsor, or Attendee.
* **Speaker title** (if applicable): The title of your proposed session.
* **Abstract or proposal summary** (if applicable): A brief description of your proposed session.
* **Technical requirements** (if applicable): List any necessary audio-visual or staging requirements.
* **References or past presentations** (if applicable): A list of relevant references or past presentations.
* **Agreement terms and conditions**: Review and understand the terms of submission.
* **Submission reference**: After submission, you will receive an application ID for reference.
