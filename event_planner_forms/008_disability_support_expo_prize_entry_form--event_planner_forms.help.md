# Disability Support Expo Prize Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disability Support Expo Prize Entry Form is a form designed to collect information from participants in the Disability Support Expo. This form is intended to help event organizers understand the types of entries participants wish to submit, their preferred entry choices, and any additional details they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all the required fields, which are marked as such (*).
2. For "Entry Choice", select one of the provided options that best reflects your entry type.
3. In "Additional Details", provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your entry.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Entry Details
* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, `text`, *): A text field where you can provide the title and description of your entry. Please provide a brief and clear title and description of your entry.

### Entry Choice
* **Entry Choice** (`entry_choice`, `select_one`, *): Choose the type of entry you wish to submit. Please select one of the provided options that best reflects your entry type.

### Additional Details
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, `note`, ): A text field where you can provide any additional information that is not covered in the "Entry Details" field. This is optional.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, *): A text field where you can provide your phone number. This is a required field.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, ): A text field where you can provide any comments or feedback you may have. This is an optional field.
