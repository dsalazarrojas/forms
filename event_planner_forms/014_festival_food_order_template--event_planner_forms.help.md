# Festival Food Order Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details from festival attendees, including their name, payment method, and order confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the order details section with your name and any additional information you'd like to provide.
2. Enter your customer information in the next section.
3. Provide your payment method in the payment information section.
4. Finally, confirm your order in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (order_details)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any additional details about your order in this section.
* **Customer Info (customer_info)** (`text`, required/optional): This section is for entering your name and any other identifying information.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)** (`text`, required/optional): Indicate your payment method, e.g., cash, credit card, etc.
* **Order Confirmation (order_confirmation)** (`text`, required/optional): Confirm that you've reviewed and agree to your order.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure smooth processing of your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.
