# Halloween Candy Buy Back Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Candy Buy Back Program Registration Form is a tool for customers to register for the program and provide their event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and other contact information (Address, City, State, Zip, Email, and Phone).
2. Enter your event details (Event Date, Event Time, and Event Location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your street address, including your apartment or suite number if applicable.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`State`, text, required): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time, required): Enter the time of your event.
* **Event Location** (`Event Location`, text, required): Enter the location of your event.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct event date and time to avoid any scheduling conflicts.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy to ensure we can reach you.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the program, feel free to contact us.
