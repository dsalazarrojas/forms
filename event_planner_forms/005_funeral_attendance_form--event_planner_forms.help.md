# Funeral Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Attendance Form is a document that collects information about mourners attending a funeral service. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that we can provide the best possible experience for the family and the deceased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the attendee.
2. Indicate your relationship to the deceased (e.g., child, parent, friend, etc.).
3. Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you for any updates or questions.
4. Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
5. Let us know if you will be attending the funeral service (Yes/No).
6. Specify your expected arrival time and any additional notes about your arrival.
7. Choose the type of attendance (e.g., yes, no, etc.).
8. If you have any specific seating preferences, please indicate them.
9. If you want to leave a message for the family, please write it in the "Attendee Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Please fill in your name as the attendee.
* **Relation to Deceased** (`attendee_relation`, text, optional): Indicate your relationship to the deceased (e.g., child, parent, friend, etc.).
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you for any updates or questions.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Seating Request** (`seating_request`, select_one, required): Choose whether you will be attending the funeral service (Yes/No).
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Specify your expected arrival time.
* **Arrival Note** (`arrival_note`, note, required): Add any additional notes about your arrival (e.g., car pool, etc.).
* **Type of Attendance** (`attendee_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of attendance (e.g., Yes, No, etc.).
* **Seating Preferences** (`seating_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any specific seating preferences, please indicate them.
* **Arrival Time Note** (`arrival_time_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your arrival time.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): If you're bringing someone else along, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): If you're bringing someone else along, please provide their contact address.
* **Attendee Message** (`attendee_message`, text, optional): If you want to leave a message for the family, please write it here.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we can provide the best possible experience for the family and the deceased.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
