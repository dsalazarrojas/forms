# Costume Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event planners or costume suppliers gather essential information from customers for custom costume orders. It serves as a template for customers to provide their contact, costume, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Info** section with your name, address, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with the event planner or costume supplier.
2. In the **Contact Info** section, enter your email address and phone number so the event planner or costume supplier can reach out to you for any questions or updates.
3. The **Costume Info** section is where you describe the custom costume you'd like to order. Be as specific as possible about the costume's style, color, and any other details you'd like to include.
4. In the **Payment Details** section, provide your payment method and any other relevant payment information required by the event planner or costume supplier.
5. Finally, click the **Submit** button to send your form to the event planner or costume supplier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with the event planner or costume supplier.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number so the event planner or costume supplier can reach out to you for any questions or updates.
* **Costume Info**: Describe the custom costume you'd like to order, being as specific as possible about style, color, and any other details you'd like to include.
* **Payment Details**: Provide your payment method and any other relevant payment information required by the event planner or costume supplier.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your form to the event planner or costume supplier.
