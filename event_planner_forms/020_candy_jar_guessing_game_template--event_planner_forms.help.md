# Candy Jar Guessing Game Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants of a Candy Jar Guessing Game. It is designed to collect participant details, guessing strategy, and event information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** (if applicable) to receive notifications.
3. Guess the number of candies inside the jar and select your **Guess Method**.
4. Select the number of entries you made and confirm if you paid the **Entry Fee**.
5. Indicate your interest in the prize and select your preferred prize type.
6. Choose how you heard about the game and if you are participating with a team.
7. If you are participating with a group, provide your team name and member names.
8. Confirm that you have read the game rules.
9. Agree to receive notifications if you win.
10. Give permission to use your photo for the event.
11. Add any additional comments.
12. Submit your guess on the **Guess Submission Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your given name.
* **Last Name**: Your family name.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email for winner notification.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Age**: Your age (optional).
* **Organization or Group**: Your affiliation (optional).
* **Event Name**: The name of the event this guess is for.
* **Event Date**: The date of the event.
* **Jar Description**: The type of jar used in the game (e.g., Large glass jar, Medium glass jar).
* **Type of Candy in Jar**: The type of candy inside the jar (e.g., Jelly beans, Gumballs).
* **Candy Color**: The main color of the candy (optional).
* **Your Guess - How Many Candies**: Guess the number of candies inside the jar.
* **How Did You Make Your Guess**: Select your guessing strategy (e.g., Visual estimation, Mathematical calculation).
* **Number of Entries**: How many guesses you made.
* **Entry Fee Paid**: Confirm if you paid the entry fee.
* **Entry Fee Amount**: The amount you paid for the entry fee (optional).
* **Are You Interested in the Prize**: Indicate your interest in the prize.
* **Preferred Prize Type**: Select your preferred prize type (e.g., Cash prize, Gift card).
* **How Did You Hear About This Game**: Select how you heard about the game (e.g., Event signage, Friend or colleague).
* **Are You Participating with a Group**: Confirm if you are participating with a team.
* **Team Name**: If applicable, enter your team name.
* **Team Member Names**: If applicable, list your team members.
* **Have You Read the Game Rules**: Confirm that you have read the game rules.
* **Can We Contact You if You Win**: Agree to receive notifications if you win.
* **Photo Permission for Event**: Give permission to use your photo for the event.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other notes or comments.
* **Guess Submission Date**: The date you submitted your guess.
* **Participating with Group**: Confirm if you are participating with a group.
* **Submission Date**: A unique date submitted by each participant.
