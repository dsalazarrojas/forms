# entertainment_event_planner_job_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information from potential candidates for an entertainment event planning job. The form requires applicants to provide personal details, work experience, skills, and information about their event planning capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal details in the "Applicant Information" section.
2.  Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3.  Select your work experience from the "Work Experience" dropdown menu.
4.  Provide your education background by selecting relevant options.
5.  List your skills in the "Skills" field.
6.  Enter your references, if applicable.
7.  Upload your resume and portfolio (if required).
8.  Provide any additional information about yourself in the "Additional Information" section.
9.  Agree to the terms and conditions.
10.  Enter the date and time of the event.
11.  Select the event type, frequency, and location.
12.  Enter the team size and event budget.
13.  Confirm the event frequency, location, and budget (if applicable).
14.  Add any final notes or comments.
15.  Finally, submit the form by entering your name.
