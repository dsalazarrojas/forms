# Signup Form With Image Slider - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user details for event planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter Your Details: Fill in your personal details in the corresponding fields.
2. Upload Your Profile Picture: Use the "Profile Picture" field to upload your profile picture.
3. Select Options: Choose your options from the dropdown menus for certain categories.
4. Submit: Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Your Details** (`form_1`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Profile Picture** (`image_1`, `text`, required: false): Upload your profile picture to help us better understand your preferences.
* **Image Slider** (`slider_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to upload your profile picture.
* **Submit** (`submit_1`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to submit your form.
* **Email** (`email_1`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`email_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date_1`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for the event.
* **Time** (`time_1`, `time`, required: false): Select a time for the event.
* **Note** (`note_1`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Select** (`select_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" or "No" for a particular category.
* **Select2** (`select_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple options for a category.
* **Select3** (`select_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple options for another category.
* **Decimal** (`decimal_1`, `number`, required: false): Enter a decimal number.
* **Integer** (`integer_1`, `number`, required: false): Enter an integer number.
* **Text** (`text_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter some text information.
* **Date2** (`date_2`, `date`, required: false): Select another date for the event.
* **Time2** (`time_2`, `time`, required: false): Select another time for the event.
* **Note2** (`note_2`, `note`, required: false): Add another note about the event.
* **Select4** (`select_4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" or "No" for another category.
* **Select5** (`select_5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple options for another category.
* **Decimal2** (`decimal_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter another decimal number.
* **Integer2** (`integer_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter another integer number.
* **Text2** (`text_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter more text information.
* **Date3** (`date_3`, `date`, required: false): Select another date for the event.
* **Time3** (`time_3`, `time`, required: false): Select another time for the event.
