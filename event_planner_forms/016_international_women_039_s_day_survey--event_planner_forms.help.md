# International Womens Day Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Women's Day Survey is designed to gather personal and contact information, as well as opinions of women, to be surveyed for International Women's Day celebrations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the Personal Info section, which is optional.
2. Proceed to select one or more options in the Opinions section, making sure to mark all applicable answers.
3. Fill out the Contact Info section completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info (text)** (`personal_info`, Optional): This is an optional text field where you can provide your personal details.
* **Opinions** (`opinions`, Required): Select one or more options from the list to express your opinions. Note that this is a multiple-choice question, so mark all applicable answers.
* **Contact Info (Note)** (`contact_info`, Required): Fill out this section with your contact information.
