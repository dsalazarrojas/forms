# Anniversary Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us know who will be attending and their preferences for the celebration. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and let us know about your plans. Your input will help us make this event special for the anniversary celebrants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, so we can keep you updated on event details.
3. In the "Will You Attend?" field, select whether you will or will not attend the celebration.
4. If you are bringing a guest, enter their full name in the "Full Name of Guest" field.
5. In the "Relationship to Host" field, select how you know the anniversary celebrants.
6. In the "Dietary Restrictions" field, select all the restrictions you have.
7. In the "Dietary Details" field, please specify any allergies or special requirements.
8. In the "Meal Preference" field, select your preferred entree.
9. In the "Beverage Preference" field, select what you would like to drink.
10. In the "Song Requests" field, let us know any songs you would like to hear during the celebration.
11. If you need help with transportation, select "True" in the "Transportation Assistance Needed" field.
12. If you have any special needs, please provide them in the "Accessibility Requirements" field.
13. In the "Gift Status" field, let us know if you have already sent a gift or plan to send one.
14. If you would like to share a memory, select "Yes, I Would Like to Speak" in the "Memory to Share" field.
15. When you plan to arrive and depart, enter the corresponding times in the "Expected Arrival Time" and "Expected Departure Time" fields.
16. In case of an emergency, please provide the name and number of your emergency contact person.
17. Finally, enter any special message for the anniversary celebrants.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id: 1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (id: 2, email, required): This is the email address we will use to keep you updated on event details.
* **Phone Number** (id: 3, text, optional): Enter your best contact number in case we need to reach you.
* **Will You Attend?** (id: 4, select_one, required): Let us know whether you will or will not attend the celebration.
* **Bringing a Plus One** (id: 5, select_one, required): Select whether you will be bringing a guest.
* **Full Name of Guest** (id: 6, text, optional): If you are bringing a guest, enter their full name.
* **Relationship to Host** (id: 7, select_one, optional): Select how you know the anniversary celebrants.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (id: 8, select_multiple, required): Select all the dietary restrictions you have.
* **Dietary Details** (id: 9, text, optional): Specify any allergies or special requirements you have.
* **Meal Preference** (id: 10, select_one, optional): Select your preferred entree.
* **Beverage Preference** (id: 11, select_one, optional): Select what you would like to drink.
* **Song Requests** (id: 12, text, optional): Let us know any songs you would like to hear during the celebration.
* **Transportation Assistance Needed** (id: 13, select_one, optional): If you need help with transportation, select "True".
* **Accessibility Requirements** (id: 14, text, optional): Provide any special needs you may have.
* **Gift Status** (id: 15, select_one, optional): Let us know if you have already sent a gift or plan to send one.
* **Memory to Share** (id: 16, select_one, optional): Select whether you would like to share a memory during the celebration.
* **Expected Arrival Time** (id: 17, time, optional): Enter when you plan to arrive.
* **Expected Departure Time** (id: 18, time, optional): Enter when you plan to leave.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 19, text, optional): In case of an emergency, provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 20, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Special Message** (id: 21, text, optional): Enter any special message for the anniversary celebrants.
* **How Did You Hear About the Event?** (id: 22, select_multiple, optional): Select how you heard about the event.
