# World Marriage Day Anniversary Mass Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The World Marriage Day Anniversary Mass Registration Form is designed to collect information from couples celebrating their marriage anniversary. This form helps event planners track and organize the mass registration for the anniversary celebration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the attendee details page with the information of the couple celebrating their anniversary.
2. Enter the name of the person(s) attending the mass.
3. Provide a valid email address for contact purposes.
4. Enter the phone number of the person(s) attending the mass.
5. Select the date and time of the couple's marriage.
6. Choose the year of the couple's marriage anniversary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details**: Enter the name(s) of the person(s) attending the mass. This field is used to identify the individuals celebrating their anniversary.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the person(s) attending the mass. This field is used to record the name of the individual(s) celebrating their anniversary.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes. This field is used for communication and updates from the event planners.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person(s) attending the mass. This field is used for additional contact and confirmation.
* **Date of Marriage**: Select the date of the couple's marriage. This field is used to track the anniversary date.
* **Time of Marriage**: Select the time of the couple's marriage. This field is used to track the anniversary time.
* **Anniversary Year**: Select the year of the couple's marriage anniversary. This field is used to track the anniversary year.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to fill out this form on behalf of the couple.
* Each submission should represent a unique couple celebrating their anniversary.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event planners for clarification.
