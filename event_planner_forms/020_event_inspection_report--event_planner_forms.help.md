# Event Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event inspectors on their findings and details about events during or after an inspection. It helps to gather data for reporting and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages and fields based on your inspection needs.
2. On the "Event Inspection" page, provide a brief description of the event.
3. On the "Event Details" page, add any additional information about the event.
4. On the "Inspection Items" page, select the items that were inspected during the event.
5. On the "Photo Upload" page, upload any relevant photos of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Inspection** (`event_inspection`, text, required/optional): A text field to enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): A text field to provide additional information about the event.
* **Inspection Items** (`inspection_items`, select_multiple, required/optional): A dropdown list of items to select from the list of available inspection items (e.g., "Item 1", "Item 2", "Item 3").
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, text, required/optional): A text field to upload a photo of the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Event Inspection" and "Event Details" fields.
* Select relevant inspection items on the "Inspection Items" page to ensure accurate reporting.
* Upload clear and high-quality photos on the "Photo Upload" page to support your findings.
