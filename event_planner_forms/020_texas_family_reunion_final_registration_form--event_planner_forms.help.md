# Texas Family Reunion Final Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from attendees for the Texas Family Reunion event. Please fill it out carefully to ensure that your registration is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Enter your Date of Birth.
5. Enter the Event Attendee Count.
6. Select your Accommodation Preferences (e.g., Accommodations, Off-site Accommodations, No Preference).
7. If applicable, enter your Accommodation Type.
8. Enter your Arrival Date.
9. Enter your Arrival Time.
10. Enter your Departure Date.
11. Enter your Departure Time.
12. If you have any Special Requests, please enter them in the text area provided.
13. Enter the Emergency Contact Name.
14. Select the Emergency Contact Relationship (e.g., Spouse, Child, Parent, Other).
15. Enter the Emergency Contact Phone number.
16. Enter the Emergency Contact Email (if available).
17. If necessary, enter any Medical Information.
18. Select your Food Preferences (e.g., Vegetarian, Non-vegetarian, Omnivore, No Preference).
19. If needed, enter your Meal Requests (e.g., Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks).
20. Select your Transportation Preference (e.g., Self-Driven, Ride-Sharing, Air-Transportation, Train-Transportation).
21. If necessary, enter any Parking Information.
22. Confirm if your Vehicle Registration is Valid or Invalid.
23. Enter any Accommodation Details, if applicable.
24. If needed, add any Notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is registered on your government-issued ID.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can reach you for event updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Attendee Count**: Enter the number of people attending with you.
* **Accommodation Preferences**: Select the accommodation type you prefer (e.g., Accommodations, Off-site Accommodations, No Preference).
* **Accommodation Type**: Enter any additional details about your accommodation preferences, if needed.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date you plan to arrive at the event.
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the time you plan to arrive at the event.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date you plan to leave the event.
* **Departure Time**: Enter the time you plan to leave the event.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, please enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between the emergency contact (e.g., Spouse, Child, Parent, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the contact email (if available).
* **Medical Information**: If necessary, enter any medical information that we should be aware of.
* **Food Preferences**: Select your food preferences (e.g., Vegetarian, Non-vegetarian, Omnivore, No Preference).
* **Meal Requests**: If needed, enter any meal requests (e.g., Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks).
* **Transportation Preference**: Select your transportation preference (e.g., Self-Driven, Ride-Sharing, Air-Transportation, Train-Transportation).
* **Parking Information**: If necessary, enter any parking information.
* **Vehicle Registration**: Confirm if your vehicle registration is Valid or Invalid.
* **Accommodation Details**: Enter any additional details about your accommodation preferences, if needed.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your registration.
