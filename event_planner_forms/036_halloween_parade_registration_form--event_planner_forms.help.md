# Halloween Parade Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and event organizers for the Halloween Parade. It helps us to plan and coordinate the event, keep in touch with participants, and ensure a smooth experience for all involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields below to understand what information is required.
2.  Fill in the form with accurate and relevant information.
3.  Submit the form to register for the Halloween Parade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: (contact_info, text, optional)
	+ This field is for entering your contact information.
	+ Please provide your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Are you participating in the parade?**: (parade_participant, select_one, required)
	+ Choose whether you are participating in the parade.
	+ If you are participating, select "Yes" to proceed with the rest of the form.
* **How do you want to be contacted?**: (contact_method, select_one, required)
	+ Select the preferred method of contact (email, phone, or other).
	+ This will help us to get in touch with you efficiently.
* **Date of Birth**: (date_of_birth, date, optional)
	+ This field is for entering your date of birth (if required).
	+ Please use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Email**: (email, email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ Please ensure the email is accurate and active.
* **Phone**: (phone, text, optional)
	+ Provide your phone number for additional contact.
* **Address**: (address, text, optional)
	+ Enter your address for any further contact or communication.
* **Note**: (note, note, optional)
	+ This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Event Details**: (event_details, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select the events you are participating in.
	+ This will help us understand your involvement.
* **Participant Details**: (participant_details, select_multiple, required)
	+ Choose the participant details that apply to you.
	+ This is required for accurate registration.
* **Costume**: (costume, select_one, optional)
	+ If you are participating, select your costume type.
	+ This helps us understand your performance.
* **Costume Details**: (costume_details, text, optional)
	+ Enter any additional information about your costume.
* **Event Type**: (event_type, select_one, required)
	+ Select the type of event (Halloween Parade, etc.).
	+ This is a required field for accurate registration.
* **Event Date**: (event_date, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of the event (if different from the event type).
	+ Use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Time**: (event_time, time, optional)
	+ Provide the event time (if required for your event type).
* **Participant Number**: (participant_number, number, optional)
	+ If required, enter a participant number.
* **Email Frequency**: (email_frequency, select_one, required)
	+ Choose how often you want to receive emails from us.
	+ Select from the options provided.
* **Email List**: (email_list, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select which email list you would like to be included in.
	+ This is a required field for accurate registration.
* **Email List Label**: (email_list_label, text, optional)
	+ Enter a label for your email list selection.
* **Participant Status**: (participant_status, select_one, required)
	+ Select your participant status.
	+ This is a required field for accurate registration.
* **Participant Status Note**: (participant_status_note, note, optional)
	+ Enter any additional information about your participant status.
* **Participant Type**: (participant_type, select_one, optional)
	+ If required, select your participant type.
* **Participant Type Note**: (participant_type_note, note, optional)
	+ Enter any additional information about your participant type.
* **Contact Status**: (contact_status, select_one, optional)
	+ Select your contact status.
* **Contact Status Note**: (contact_status_note, note, optional)

Note: This form requires accurate and complete information for effective planning and coordination. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
