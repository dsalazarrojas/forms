# Wedding Address Book Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Address Book Form is a tool used by wedding planners to collect and store contact information and addresses of people involved in a wedding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the first name of the person whose address and contact information you want to include in the address book.
2. Enter the last name of the person.
3. Enter the person's address.
4. Enter the city of the person's address.
5. Enter the state or province where the person's address is located.
6. Enter the person's zip or postal code.
7. If available, enter the person's email address.
8. If available, enter the person's phone number.
9. Enter the date of birth of the person (if known).
10. Enter the time of birth of the person (if known).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first name of the person.
* **Last Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the last name of the person.
* **Address** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the street address of the person.
* **City** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the city where the person lives.
* **State** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the state or province where the person lives.
* **Zip** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the person's zip or postal code.
* **Email** (`name`, `email`, required/optional): This is the person's email address.
* **Phone** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the person's phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`name`, `date`, required/optional): This is the date of birth of the person (if known).
* **Time Of Birth** (`name`, `time`, required/optional): This is the time of birth of the person (if known).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are entering accurate and up-to-date information.
* If the person's date and time of birth are not known, you can leave these fields blank.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the wedding planner for clarification.
