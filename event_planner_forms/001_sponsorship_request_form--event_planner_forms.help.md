# Sponsorship Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event planners request sponsorship for their events. It is intended to be used by event planners to gather information about their events and the sponsorship requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the general information section with the event name, type, or description.
2. Provide the company name and contact person's information.
3. Enter the email and phone number of the contact person.
4. Enter the event details, including date, time, location, and description.
5. Specify the budget for the event.
6. List the requirements for the event.
7. Review and submit the form for sponsorship review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`General Information`, text, not required):
	* This section is for you to enter a brief description of the event.
	* Please provide any relevant information about the event that may help the sponsor understand its context.
- **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, not required):
	* This is where you enter the name of your company or organization.
	* This information will help the sponsor understand who is requesting sponsorship.
- **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, not required):
	* Enter the name of the contact person for the event.
	* This will allow the sponsor to contact you with questions or to request more information.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, not required):
	* Enter the email address of the contact person.
	* This is how the sponsor will contact you.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, not required):
	* Enter the phone number of the contact person.
	* This is how the sponsor will contact you.
- **Event Details** (`Event Details`, text, not required):
	* Provide a detailed description of the event.
	* This will help the sponsor understand what they will be sponsoring.
- **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, not required):
	* Enter the date of the event.
	* Make sure to use the correct date format (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time, not required):
	* Enter the time of the event.
	* Use the 24-hour clock format (e.g. 14:00).
- **Event Location** (`Event Location`, text, not required):
	* Enter the location of the event.
	* This will help the sponsor understand where the event will take place.
- **Event Description** (`Event Description`, text, not required):
	* Provide a detailed description of the event.
	* This will help the sponsor understand the context and goals of the event.
- **Budget** (`Budget`, text, not required):
	* Enter the budget for the event.
	* This will help the sponsor understand the financial requirements of the event.
- **Requirements** (`Requirements`, text, not required):
	* List the specific requirements for the event.
	* This will help the sponsor understand what is expected of them.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your form carefully before submitting it.
- Double-check that all fields are filled in correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the sponsor directly.
- Be prepared to provide additional information as needed.
