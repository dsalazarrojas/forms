# Judge Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Judge Evaluation Form is designed for judges to provide feedback and evaluation on events or performances. This form helps to collect data on the quality and experience of the events, aiding in the planning and improvement of future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your **Judge Name** in the Judge Name field.
* Provide the name of the **Event Name** you evaluated.
* Enter your **Score** for the event, using a number to indicate your rating.
* Add any additional **Comment** regarding your evaluation in the Comment field.
* Select your **Rating** for the event by choosing Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor from the list.
* If applicable, provide the **Date** of the evaluation in the Date field.
* If the evaluation took place at a specific time, fill in the **Time** field.
* Enter your **Judge ID** to identify yourself as the evaluating judge.
* Finally, enter your **Evaluation ID** to link this evaluation to a specific evaluation session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Judge Name** (`judge_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your name as a judge. Please fill in your name as it appears on your badge or ID card.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the event or performance you are evaluating.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Use a number to rate your evaluation. This score is subjective and based on your personal impression.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback you have about the event, which can help organizers understand your assessment.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following ratings: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor, to reflect your overall evaluation of the event.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you are evaluating the event on a specific date, please provide it in this field.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If the evaluation took place at a particular time, fill in the hour and minute you evaluated the event.
* **Judge ID** (`judge_id`, text, optional): Enter your Judge ID to help track your evaluations and identify yourself as the evaluating judge.
* **Evaluation ID** (`evaluation_id`, text, optional): Enter the Evaluation ID to connect this assessment with other related evaluations.
