# Church Retreat Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for attendees to provide feedback on their experiences with the Church Retreat. It helps organizers assess the retreat's quality and identify areas for improvement. Your input is valuable to us, and we encourage you to share your thoughts and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name and email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions).
2. Select the retreat you attended from the list.
3. Rate the quality of the retreat in the given categories (Venue, Accommodation, Food, Speaker, Activities, and Overall).
4. Answer yes or no to questions about spiritual growth, favorite part, and future retreat participation.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
6. Finally, select a rating for future retreat topics and value for money.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`Your Name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name so we can address you personally in follow-up questions.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you want us to contact you later.
* **Which Retreat Did You Attend** (`retreat_name`, Select One, Required): Choose the retreat you participated in from the given options.
* **Retreat Date** (`retreat_date`, Date, Required): Select the date of the retreat you attended.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, Select One, Required): Rate the overall quality of the retreat.
* **Venue/Facility Rating** (`venue_rating`, Select One, Required): Assess the quality of the retreat location.
* **Accommodation Rating** (`accommodation_rating`, Select One, Optional): Rate the sleeping arrangements.
* **Food Quality Rating** (`food_rating`, Select One, Required): Evaluate the quality of the food provided.
* **Speaker/Leader Rating** (`speaker_rating`, Select One, Required): Rate the quality of the presentations.
* **Activities Rating** (`activities_rating`, Select One, Required): Rate the planned activities and programs.
* **Did This Retreat Help Your Spiritual Growth** (`spiritual_growth`, Select One, Required): Answer yes or no to whether the retreat contributed to your spiritual growth.
* **What Was Your Favorite Part** (`favorite_part`, Text, Required): Share the highlights of your experience.
* **What Did You Like Least** (`least_favorite`, Text, Optional): Share any constructive feedback you might have.
* **Organization and Planning Rating** (`organization_rating`, Select One, Required): Rate how well the retreat was organized.
* **Value for Money Rating** (`value_rating`, Select One, Required): Evaluate the worthiness of the cost.
* **Would You Attend Future Retreats** (`future_retreats`, Select One, Required): Answer yes or no to whether you'd attend future retreats.
* **Would You Recommend This Retreat** (`recommend_retreat`, Select One, Required): Rate whether you'd recommend the retreat to others.
* **What Improvements Would You Suggest** (`suggestions`, Text, Optional): Share any ideas you have for improvement.
* **What Topics Would You Like at Future Retreats** (`future_topics`, Text, Optional): Suggest topics you'd like to see in future retreats.
* **Any Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Share anything else you'd like to comment on.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* Answer all required questions.
* Provide specific examples or details when giving feedback.
* Use the provided ratings and options as guidelines for your answers.
* Remember, your input will help us improve future retreats!
