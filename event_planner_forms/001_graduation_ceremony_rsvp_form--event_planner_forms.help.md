# Graduation Ceremony RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Ceremony RSVP Form is designed to collect information from individuals who plan to attend or participate in a graduation ceremony. This form gathers details such as name, email, phone number, and other relevant information to facilitate communication and organization for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed the form carefully and understand its purpose.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk \*.
3. Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
4. Review the form before submitting to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Graduation Ceremony Details**: \* This field is not a field in the original form.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be listed in the ceremony program.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you about the event.
* **Attending**: Indicate whether you will be attending the ceremony (True) or not attending (False).
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or information about your participation in the event.
* **Graduation Date**: Enter the date of your graduation.
* **Graduation Time**: Enter the time of your graduation.
* **Ceremony Location**: Enter the location where the ceremony will take place.
* **Reception Location**: Enter the location of the reception.
* **Reception Time**: Enter the time of the reception.
* **Special Requests**: List any special requests you have for the event.
* **Additional Information**: Leave any additional information about your participation in the event.
* **Send Reminders**: Indicate whether you would like to receive reminders about the event (True) or not (False).
* **Reminder Time**: Enter the time you would like to receive reminders.
* **Reminder Message**: Enter a message that you would like to include with reminders.
* **Reminder Method**: Select the method by which you would like to receive reminders (Email, Phone).
* **Additional Notes**: Leave any additional notes about your participation in the event.
* **Host Information**: Enter any information about who is hosting the event.
* **Event Notes**: Leave any additional information about the event.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your participation.
* **Graduation Date 2**: Enter the date of your graduation.
* **Graduation Year**: Enter the year you graduated.
* **Graduation Level**: Select the level of your graduation (High School, College).
* **Graduation Type**: Select the type of graduation (Graduation, Commencement).
* **Graduation Ceremony Rsvp Form Comments**: Leave any additional comments about the event.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Graduation Ceremony Rsvp Form Signature 2**: Enter a second signature.
* **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments about the event.
* **Signature Location**: Enter the location where you signed this form.
* **Graduation Ceremony Rsvp Form Signature 3**: Enter a third signature.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
* If you are unsure about a field, use the "Additional Information" field to clarify.
* Please sign the form to confirm your participation.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Make sure to review all fields for accuracy before submitting.
