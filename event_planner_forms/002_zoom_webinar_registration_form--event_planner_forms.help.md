# Zoom Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoom Webinar Registration Form is a tool designed to collect essential information from attendees and hosts for the smooth organization and execution of a Zoom webinar. Filling out this form ensures that all necessary details are gathered accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with your name, email address, and other required details.
2.  Select your role in the webinar (attendee, host, co-host, or panelist) to help the organizers track your participation.
3.  Choose your time zone from the provided options.
4.  Select the start and end times of the webinar from the provided options.
5.  For Timezone 2, choose whether it's yes or no.
6.  Ensure that you've filled out all required fields accurately and completely before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (not used in the form but potentially for internal purposes).
* **Role in the Webinar** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose the role you will play in the webinar (attendee, host, co-host, or panelist).
* **Time zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, required): Select your time zone to help the organizers schedule the webinar correctly.
* **Role in the Webinar Label** (`time_zone_label`, text, optional): If you have a preferred label for your role in the webinar, enter it here.
* **Registration link** (`registration_link`, text, optional): If provided by the webinar organizers, enter the registration link here.
* **Time zone offset** (`timezone_offset`, number, optional): Enter the time zone offset (not used in the form but potentially for internal purposes).
* **Start time** (`start_time`, time, required): Select the start time of the webinar from the provided options.
* **End time** (`end_time`, time, required): Select the end time of the webinar from the provided options.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, required): Choose the time zone for the webinar.
* **Timezone 2** (`timezone_2`, select_one, optional): If you have a second time zone, choose whether it's yes or no.
* **Time zone Offset 2** (`timezone_offset_2`, number, optional): Enter the time zone offset 2 (not used in the form but potentially for internal purposes).
* **Timezone 2 Label** (`timezone_offset_label`, text, optional): If you have a label for your second time zone, enter it here.
* **Start Time 2** (`start_time_2`, select_one, required): Select the start time 2 from the provided options.
* **End Time 2** (`end_time_2`, select_one, required): Select the end time 2 from the provided options.

**Tips**

* Be accurate when filling out your time zone to ensure that the webinar starts and ends at the correct time for your location.
* If you have any issues or concerns with the form, contact the webinar organizers.
* Ensure that you're submitting the form completely and correctly to avoid any delays or errors.
