# DJ Entertainment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DJ Entertainment Form is used to collect information from DJs about their performances. This form helps to gather details about the DJ, their event, and their equipment requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic information on the Main Page, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide details about your DJ setup and equipment on the Equipment Page.
3. List the services you offer on the Services Page, including Audio/Visual, Lighting, Dance Floor, Stage, Sound System, DJ Equipment, Backline Equipment, and Other.
4. Enter your pricing information on the Pricing Page.
5. Accept the terms and conditions on the Terms and Conditions Page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page**: (`title`, text, optional) Enter your name and any other general information about your DJ services.
* **DJ Information**: (`dj_info`, text, optional) Please provide a brief description of your DJ services, such as your music genres, specialties, etc.
* **Event Information**: (`event_info`, text, optional) Share details about the event you are performing at, like the date, time, location, etc.
* **Contact Information**: (`contact_info`, text, optional) Enter your contact details so we can reach you for further inquiries.
* **Music Information**: (`music_info`, text, optional) Share your music style, genres, etc.
* **Performance Information**: (`performance_info`, text, optional) Provide details about your performance, such as your set length, etc.
* **Equipment Information**: (`equipment_info`, text, optional) List the equipment you have, such as turntables, speakers, etc.
* **Services**: (`services`, select multiple, optional) Choose the services you offer as a DJ, including Audio/Visual, Lighting, Dance Floor, Stage, Sound System, DJ Equipment, Backline Equipment, and Other.
* **Pricing**: (`pricing`, number, optional) Enter your pricing information for your services.
* **Terms and Conditions**: (`terms`, note, optional) Read and accept our terms and conditions.
* **Assigned Tool**: (`assigned_tool`, text, optional) If you have any assigned equipment, please list it here.
* **Form ID**: (`form_id`, number, optional) This is an internal field for tracking purposes. Please do not fill this in.
* **Created By**: (`created_by`, text, optional) This field is for internal use only. Please do not fill this in.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
