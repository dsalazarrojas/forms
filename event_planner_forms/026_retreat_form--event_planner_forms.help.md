# Retreat Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retreat Form is a comprehensive tool designed to collect information about events, including staff attendance, event details, and other relevant data. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are captured, making it an essential aid for event planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2.  Fill out the form with the correct information for your event.
3.  Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### staff_attendance
* **staff_attendance** (Select Multiple, required: False): Select all the staff members attending the event. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for each staff member.

### name
* **name** (Text, required: False): Enter the name of the staff member attending the event.

### email
* **email** (Email, required: False): Enter the email address of the staff member attending the event.

### phone
* **phone** (Text, required: False): Enter the phone number of the staff member attending the event.

### event_details
* **event_details** (Select One, required: False): Select 'Yes' if the event requires details, 'No' otherwise.

### event_type
* **event_type** (Select Multiple, required: False): Choose the type of event (e.g., meeting, workshop, etc.).

### start_date
* **start_date** (Date, required: False): Enter the start date of the event.

### end_date
* **end_date** (Date, required: False): Enter the end date of the event.

### event_duration
* **event_duration** (Time, required: False): Enter the duration of the event in hours and minutes.

### location
* **location** (Text, required: False): Enter the location of the event.

### description
* **description** (Note, required: False): Enter a brief description of the event.

### event_details_7
* **event_details_7** (Text, required: False): Enter any additional details about the event.

### event_details_8
* **event_details_8** (Email, required: False): Enter a contact email address for the event.

### event_details_9
* **event_details_9** (Select One, required: False): Select 'Yes' if you want to send reminders for the event, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
* Be accurate and consistent when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all necessary details are captured.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
