# Christmas DIY Kits Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas DIY Kits Pre Order Form is designed for customers to pre-order their DIY kits for the holiday season. The form collects essential information to ensure accurate shipping and delivery of the kits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, customers should follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name to confirm your identity.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide your email address for order updates and tracking purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for delivery coordination.
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are ordering for personal or business use.
* **Customer Information** (`section_customer_info`, note, not required): This section is for additional customer information; if you have any, please provide in this section.
* **DIY Kit Type** (`kit_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of Christmas kit you would like to order.
* **Kit Size** (`kit_size`, select_one, required): Choose the kit size based on the number of participants.
* **Quantity of Kits** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of kits you would like to order.
* **Difficulty Level** (`difficulty_level`, select_one, required): Select the difficulty level of the kit.
* **Color Theme Preference** (`color_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred color theme.
* **Additional Add-ons** (`add_ons`, select_multiple, not required): Select extra items to include with your kit.
* **Expected Ship Date** (`expected_ship_date`, date, required): Choose the date you would like the kits to be shipped.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your full delivery address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city for the delivery address.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code for the delivery address.
* **Shipping Information** (`section_shipping`, note, not required): This section is for shipping information; if you have any, please provide in this section.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Choose the shipping method for your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to pay for your order.
* **Deposit Payment** (`deposit_paid`, select_one, required): Confirm if you understand the 50% deposit requirement for pre-order.
* **Planned Event Date** (`group_event_date`, date, not required): If applicable, enter the date you will use the kits.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, not required): If you have any specific requirements, enter them in this field.
* **Join Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, not required): If you would like to receive DIY tips and holiday deals, select this option.

This help guide has been created to assist customers in navigating the Christmas DIY Kits Pre Order Form efficiently. By following the instructions, customers will be able to provide the necessary information for accurate shipping and delivery of their pre-ordered DIY kits.
