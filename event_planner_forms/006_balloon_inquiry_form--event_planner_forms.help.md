# Balloon Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Balloon Inquiry Form is a comprehensive tool to gather information about your event and preferences for balloon services. This form will help us understand your needs and provide a detailed proposal for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked as "Required" in the form.
2. For select fields, choose the most accurate option that matches your preferences.
3. Provide accurate and complete information to ensure we can offer the best possible services for your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (`customer_full_name`, text, required): Please fill in your name as it appears on official records.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for our response and potential follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are representing a company or organization, please fill in the name.
* **Type of Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event you are interested in, such as Birthday Party Balloons or Corporate Event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): If your event starts at a specific time, please fill it in; otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Provide the name and address of the event venue.
* **Balloon Type Preference** (`balloon_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all the balloon types you are interested in, such as Helium Balloons or Balloon Arch.
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, text, optional): If you have a preferred color scheme for your event, please let us know.
* **Event Theme** (`theme`, text, optional): If you have a specific theme for your event, please fill it in.
* **Estimated Number of Guests** (`estimated_guests`, number, optional): If you know the estimated number of guests, please provide it.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Choose an estimated budget range for your event.
* **Delivery Required** (`delivery_needed`, select_one, required): If you need delivery service, please choose the option that best suits your needs.
* **Setup Service** (`setup_service`, select_one, optional): If you need professional setup service, please choose the option.
* **Have You Used Our Services Before** (`previous_customer`, select_one, required): If you have used our services before, please select the option.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): If you were referred to us by someone, please let us know.
* **Additional Services Interest** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): If you need other services like Event Planning or Photography, please select the options.
* **Special Requests or Questions** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any specific requirements or inquiries, please fill them in.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose the best method to contact you, such as Email or Phone Call.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, optional): If you have a preferred time to contact, please select the option.
