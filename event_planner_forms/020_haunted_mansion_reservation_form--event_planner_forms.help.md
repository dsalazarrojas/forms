# Haunted Mansion Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Haunted Mansion Reservation Form is designed to allow users to reserve a spot in the Haunted Mansion event. This form collects contact information and event preferences from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred date of visit in the "Date" field.
6. Choose your preferred time of visit in the "Time" field.
7. If desired, add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required: false)
	* Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, required: false)
	* Enter your last name.
- **Email** (email, required: false)
	* Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (text, required: false)
	* Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (date, required: false)
	* Choose your preferred date of visit.
	* Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time** (time, required: false)
	* Choose your preferred time of visit.
	* Format: HH:MM.
- **Note** (note, required: false)
	* Add any additional notes or comments about your visit.

## Tips
* Be sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your reservation can be processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the event organizers.
