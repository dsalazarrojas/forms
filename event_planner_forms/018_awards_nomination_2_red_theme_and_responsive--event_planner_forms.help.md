# Awards Nomination 2 Red Theme And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for award nominations. It is intended for individuals to nominate others for outstanding achievements or contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the award program you are nominating for.
2. Choose the nomination period.
3. Enter your name and email address.
4. Select your organization (if applicable).
5. Choose your role or affiliation.
6. Enter the name of the person you are nominating.
7. Enter the company of the person you are nominating.
8. Enter the title of the person you are nominating.
9. Enter the email address of the person you are nominating (if applicable).
10. Choose the award category for the nomination.
11. Explain why you are nominating the person.
12. Provide a specific example of their excellence.
13. Rate the impact of their work.
14. Indicate your willingness to work with them again.
15. Rate your recommendation of them.
16. Add any additional comments or notes.
17. Select your permission to publish your nomination publicly.
18. Confirm that your vote is genuine.
19. Enter the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Award Program** (`award_program`, select_one, required): Select the award program for which you are nominating the person.
* **Nomination Period** (`nomination_period`, select_one, required): Choose the period during which the nominee's achievements took place.
* **Your Name** (`voter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the nominator.
* **Email Address** (`voter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Organization** (`voter_organization`, text, optional): Enter your company or institution (if applicable).
* **Voter Type** (`voter_type`, select_one, required): Select your role or affiliation.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Company** (`nominee_company`, text, required): Enter the company of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person you are nominating (if applicable).
* **Award Category** (`award_category_vote`, select_one, required): Choose the category for the award.
* **Why Are You Nominating Them** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Explain why you are nominating the person.
* **Specific Example** (`specific_example`, text, required): Provide a specific example of their excellence.
* **Impact Rating** (`impact_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the impact of their work.
* **Would You Work With Them Again** (`would_work_again`, select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to work with them again.
* **Strength of Recommendation** (`recommendation_strength`, select_one, required): Rate your recommendation of them.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Permission to Publish** (`permission_to_publish`, select_one, required): Select your permission to publish your nomination publicly.
* **I Confirm This Vote Is Genuine** (`vote_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that your vote is genuine.
* **Vote Date** (`vote_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
