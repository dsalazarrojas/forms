# Auction Bid Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for bidders to submit their bid information for an auction event. It captures essential details required for the bid process, including bidder contact information, bid specifics, and payment method preferences. The form ensures that bidders provide accurate and complete information, which helps the auction organizers process and manage the bidding process efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Bidder Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**. These are your contact details, which will be used to send bid confirmations and updates.
2. Enter your **Bidder ID Number**, which is an assigned registration number for tracking purposes.
3. Choose the **Auction Event Name** and select the correct **Auction Date** for the event you are participating in.
4. Identify the **Lot Number** and **Item Name** for the specific item you are bidding on.
5. Enter the **Bid Amount** you are willing to pay for the item.
6. Set your **Maximum Bid Limit** if you have a maximum amount you are willing to bid.
7. Select the **Bid Type** from the available options (Standard, Proxy, Online, Phone, Written).
8. Choose your **Preferred Payment Method** from the available options (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash, Check).
9. Confirm your understanding of the **Terms and Conditions** by selecting the option to agree.
10. Acknowledge the **Buyer Premium Fee** that applies to your bid.
11. Decide if you require **Shipping** for the item and select the corresponding option.
12. Add any **Additional Notes** or comments regarding your bid, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Bidder Full Name**: Enter your full, legal name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required)
- **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address to receive bid confirmations and updates. (Required)
- **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for urgent bid notifications. (Required)
- **Bidder ID Number**: Enter your assigned bidder registration number. (Required)
- **Auction Event Name**: Select the name of the auction event you are participating in. (Required)
- **Auction Date**: Choose the date of the auction event from the available options. (Required)
- **Lot Number**: Identify the specific item you are bidding on. (Required)
- **Item Name**: Enter the name or description of the item being auctioned. (Required)
- **Bid Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the item in USD. (Required)
- **Maximum Bid Limit**: Set a maximum amount you are willing to bid, if applicable. (Optional)
- **Bid Type**: Select the type of bid you are placing from the available options. (Required)
- **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose your preferred method of payment. (Required)
- **Terms and Conditions Agreement**: Confirm your understanding of the auction terms and conditions. (Required)
- **Buyer Premium Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge your understanding of the buyer premium fee. (Required)
- **Shipping Required**: Decide if you require shipping for the item. (Required)
- **Additional Notes**: Add any special instructions or comments regarding your bid. (Optional)

Note that this guide assumes that all fields are filled out in the context of a specific auction event and item being auctioned.
