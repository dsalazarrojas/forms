# Participant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Registration Form is a document that captures essential details about a participant in an event. This form helps ensure that all participants are registered correctly and that their information is accurate. It's essential to fill out this form carefully and correctly to avoid any discrepancies in event records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the first field. This is your name as it appears in event records.
2. Enter your **Participant Email** in the second field. This is the email address associated with your account or registration.
3. Enter your **Participant Phone** number in the third field. This can be a helpful way to reach you for event updates or reminders.
4. Verify your **Participant Email** by checking the box next to "Verify Participant Email." If the email address you entered does not match with your participant_name, this field will be marked as incorrect.
5. Select your **Participant Program** from the list of available programs. This will help us assign you to the correct group or track for the event.
6. Enter your **Participant Start Date** in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is the date you started participating in the event or program.
7. Enter your **Participant End Date** in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is the date you ended participating in the event or program.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your participation in the **Participant Notes** field. This can include any relevant information or context about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears in event records.
* **Participant Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account or registration.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter a phone number that we can use to contact you for event updates or reminders.
* **Verify Participant Email**: Check this box if you are certain that the email address you entered matches your participant_name.
* **Participant Program**: Select the program you are registered for, such as Program 1, 2, or 3.
* **Participant Start Date**: Enter the date you started participating in the event or program in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Participant End Date**: Enter the date you ended participating in the event or program in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any additional comments or context about your participation in the event or program.
