# Summer Camp Arrival Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Arrival Form is used to collect essential information from parents or guardians about their camper's arrival time, pick-up location, and emergency contact details. This information is crucial for the camp staff to manage camper arrivals and departures smoothly and ensure the camper's safety.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form for each camper individually.
- Provide accurate arrival time and pick-up location information to ensure seamless check-in.
- Select one of the listed transportation methods for the camper's arrival.
- Provide additional comments or details if needed.
- Complete the emergency contact information, including the emergency contact person's name, phone number, and relationship with the camper.
- Double-check your submissions for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Arrival Time** (`camper_arrival_time`, time, required): Enter the exact time of the camper's arrival to the camp.
* **Pick Up Location** (`pick_up_location`, text, optional): Enter the specific location where the camper will be picked up from, such as a bus stop or a house.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how the camper will be arriving at the camp, including Taxi, Bus, Train, Bike, Walk, or Other.
* **Camper Contact Info** (`camper_contact_info`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or details about the camper's arrival or contact preferences.
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person, such as a parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the camper (e.g., parent, guardian, or relative).
