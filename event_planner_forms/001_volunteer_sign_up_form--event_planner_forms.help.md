# Volunteer Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteer Sign Up Form" is designed for individuals to sign up and provide their contact information, role, and availability for an event. This form helps event organizers manage volunteer schedules and communicate with volunteers effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your role among the available options (e.g., Administrative, Event Coordinator, or Volunteer).
5. Choose your available shift(s) for the event in the "Shift" field.
6. Select the event date (optional) in the "Event Date" field.
7. Enter the event time (if applicable) in the "Event Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address (example: `example@example.com`). Double-check the format to ensure it's correct.
*   **Role** (`Administrative`, `Event Coordinator`, `Volunteer`): Choose your role.
*   **Shift** (`Morning`, `Afternoon`, `Full Day`): Select one or more shifts you're available to work.
