# Christmas Grocery Package Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Grocery Package Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from potential participants in the Christmas Grocery Package Program. This form helps us understand your household's needs, dietary requirements, and delivery preferences, enabling us to provide the right services and resources to support you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide your date of birth.
4. Enter your household's address.
5. Select the number of people in your household and their ages.
6. List any special dietary needs.
7. Specify any food allergies.
8. Indicate if you need mobility assistance.
9. Choose your preferred delivery date and time.
10. Read and agree to the program's terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name\* (Primary Contact)
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, first and last name, in the format "John Smith".

### 2. Email Address\* (Email)
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for registration confirmation and future updates.

### 3. Phone Number\* (Phone Number)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for delivery coordination and any other communication.

### 4. Date of Birth\* (Date of Birth)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 5. -- Contact Information --
* **-- Contact Information --**: This section is for your convenience, but not required. If you have any contact information, such as a phone number or email address, please enter it here.

### 6. Street Address\* (Street Address)
* **Street Address**: Enter your full street address for delivery purposes.

### 7. City\* (City)
* **City**: Enter the city where your address is located.

### 8. State or Province\* (State or Province)
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where your address is located.

### 9. Postal Code\* (Postal Code)
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code for accurate delivery.

### 10. -- Delivery Address --
* **-- Delivery Address --**: This section is for your convenience, but not required. If you have any delivery address information, please enter it here.

### 11. Total Household Size\* (Total Household Size)
* **Total Household Size**: Select the number of people in your household from 1 person to 9 or more people.

### 12. Adults\* (Adults Count)
* **Adults Count**: Select the number of adults (18 and over) in your household from 1 adult to 4 or more adults.

### 13. Children\* (Children Count)
* **Children Count**: Select the number of children under 18 in your household from 0 to 5 or more children.

### 14. Children Ages (Children Ages)
* **Children Ages**: If you have children, list their ages in the text box. (Optional)

### 15. -- Household Composition --
* **-- Household Composition --**: This section is for your convenience, but not required. If you have any additional information about your household, please enter it here.

### 16. Special Dietary Needs (Select All That Apply)
* **Special Dietary Needs**: Select all that apply from the list of dietary options, such as diabetic-friendly or gluten-free.

### 17. Food Allergies (Food Allergies)
* **Food Allergies**: List any food allergies you or your household members may have.

### 18. Need Mobility Assistance\* (Need Mobility Assistance)
* **Need Mobility Assistance**: Indicate if you need help carrying packages.

### 19. -- Special Needs --
* **-- Special Needs --**: This section is for your convenience, but not required. If you have any special needs or requirements, please enter them here.

### 20. Current Assistance Programs (Select All That Apply)
* **Current Assistance Programs**: Select all that apply from the list of programs, such as SNAP or Medicaid.

### 21. How Did You Hear About Us\* (How Did You Hear About Program)
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you heard about the program, such as social media or a community center.

### 22. Referred By (Referred By)
* **Referred By**: If you were referred by someone, please enter their name.

### 23. Preferred Delivery Date\* (Preferred Delivery Date)
* **Preferred Delivery Date**: Enter your preferred delivery date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 24. Preferred Delivery Time\* (Preferred Delivery Time)
* **Preferred Delivery Time**: Select your preferred delivery time from the options.

### 25. -- Delivery Preferences --
* **-- Delivery Preferences --**: This section is for your convenience, but not required. If you have any delivery preferences, please enter them here.

### 26. Access Instructions (Access Instructions)
* **Access Instructions**: If you need special delivery instructions, please enter them here, such as gate codes or buzzer information.

### 27. Agree to Program Terms\* (Agree to Program Terms)
* **Agree to Program Terms**: Confirm that the information you entered is accurate and you agree to the program's terms and conditions.

### 28. Privacy Consent\* (Privacy Consent)
* **Privacy Consent**: Confirm that you agree to allow the program to use the information for program purposes.

### 29. Additional Comments (Additional Comments)
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any special circumstances or comments to share, please enter them here.
