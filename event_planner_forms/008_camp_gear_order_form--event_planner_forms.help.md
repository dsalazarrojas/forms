# Camp Gear Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Gear Order Form is designed to help you order camping gear for your next adventure. This form collects information about you, your shipping address, and your gear preferences to ensure your order is processed efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the customer information section**:
	* Enter your **Full Name**, which is required.
	* Enter your **Email Address**, which is required.
	* Enter your **Phone Number**, which is required.
2. **Provide your shipping address**:
	* Enter your **Street Address**, which is required.
	* Enter your **City**, which is required.
	* Enter your **State or Province**, which is required.
	* Enter your **Postal Code**, which is required.
	* Enter your **Country**, which is required.
3. **Select your gear preferences**:
	* Choose the quantity of gear you'd like to order from the available options (Backpack, 4-Person Tent, Sleeping Bag, Sleeping Pad, Water Bottle, Headlamp, Camping Stove, Cookware Set, Carabiner Set, Climbing Rope, etc.).
	* If you'd like to personalize your gear with embroidery, select **Yes** and enter the desired embroidery text in the **Embroidery Text** field.
4. **Choose your shipping method**:
	* Select one of the three shipping options: **Standard (5-7 business days)**, **Express (2-3 business days)**, or **Overnight (1 business day)**.
5. **Submit your order**:
	* Double-check your information and gear selection before submitting your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you'd like to receive updates and notifications about your order.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you'd like to be contacted for any further assistance.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address where you'd like to receive your order.
* **City**: Enter the city where you'd like to receive your order.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you'd like to receive your order.
* **Backpack - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of 45L capacity, waterproof backpacks you'd like to order.
* **4-Person Tent - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of 4-Person Tents you'd like to order.
* **Sleeping Bag - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of temperature-rated 20F Sleeping Bags you'd like to order.
* **Sleeping Pad - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of self-inflating foam Sleeping Pads you'd like to order.
* **Water Bottle - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of 32oz insulated water bottles you'd like to order.
* **Headlamp - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of 200 lumen LED Headlamps with USB charging you'd like to order.
* **Camping Stove - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of compact butane Camping Stoves you'd like to order.
* **Cookware Set - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of 5-piece Aluminum Cookware Sets with utensils you'd like to order.
* **Carabiner Set - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of 10 lightweight Carabiner Sets you'd like to order.
* **Climbing Rope - Quantity**: Choose the quantity of 50ft static Climbing Rope you'd like to order.
* **Personalize gear with embroidery**: If you'd like to personalize your gear with embroidery, select **Yes** and enter the desired text in the **Embroidery Text** field.
* **Embroidery Text**: Enter the text you'd like to appear on your gear if you selected **Yes** for **Personalize gear with embroidery**.
* **Preferred Shipping Method**: Choose the shipping method that suits your needs: **Standard (5-7 business days)**, **Express (2-3 business days)**, or **Overnight (1 business day)**.
