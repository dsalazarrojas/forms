# medical_exhibition_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical exhibition registration form is used to collect information from attendees, speakers, or participants for a medical exhibition event. This form helps event organizers gather the necessary details to manage the event efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. For fields with options, select the relevant option that best describes your situation.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to any text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (Text, Optional):
  Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.

* **Exhibition Name** (Text, Optional):
  Enter the name of the medical exhibition event you are registering for.

* **Contact Info** (Text, Optional):
  Provide your contact information, such as email and phone number, so we can reach you.

* **Medical License** (Text, Optional):
  If you are a medical professional, please enter your medical license number.

* **Medical Fields** (Select Multiple, Optional):
  Indicate which medical fields you are interested in or have expertise in.

* **Medical Fields Other** (Text, Optional):
  If the previous option doesn't fit your specialty, describe it in this field.

* **Medical License Number** (Text, Optional):
  Enter your medical license number if applicable.

* **Fee Info** (Select One, Optional):
  Indicate if you need to pay for the exhibition and choose the payment option.

* **Payment Info** (Select One, Optional):
  Indicate if you need to pay for the exhibition and choose the payment option.

* **Submit** (Text, Optional):
  Enter any additional comments or information you would like to submit with your registration.

* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional):
  Indicate if you have a preferred payment method.

* **Payment Method Info** (Text, Optional):
  Provide any additional information about your payment method.

* **Payment Status** (Text, Optional):
  Enter your current payment status.

* **Payment Status Info** (Text, Optional):
  Provide any additional information about your payment status.

* **Payment Amount** (Number, Optional):
  Enter the amount you are willing to pay.

* **Payment Currency** (Text, Optional):
  Enter the currency you are willing to pay with.

* **Payment Info** (Text, Optional):
  Provide any additional information about your payment.

* **Payment Terms** (Text, Optional):
  Enter any terms or conditions you have about payment.

* **Payment Terms Other** (Text, Optional):
  If the previous option doesn't fit, describe your terms in this field.

* **Submit Info** (Text, Optional):
  Enter any additional comments or information about your submission.

* **Payment Status** (Select One, Optional):
  Indicate your current payment status.

* **Payment Terms Info** (Text, Optional):
  Provide any additional information about your payment terms.

* **Medical Terms** (Text, Optional):
  Enter any medical terms or conditions.

* **Medical Terms Info** (Text, Optional):
  Provide any additional information about your medical terms.

Note: This form is designed to be flexible and accommodating to different types of users. Please fill in the required fields and provide clear and concise answers to help us manage the event efficiently.
