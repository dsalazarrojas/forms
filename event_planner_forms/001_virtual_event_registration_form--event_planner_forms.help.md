# Virtual Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Event Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees or participants of virtual events. This form helps in organizing and tracking registration data for events of this nature.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, please follow the steps below:
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) as accurately as possible.
3. You can leave fields marked as optional (not marked with *) out if you don't want to provide additional information.
4. Once you have filled in all the necessary fields, review your input for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Name**: * (Registration Name)*: This is the name under which you will be registered for the event. It's a text field where you can provide your name as you want it to be recorded.
* **Email**: * (Email)*: This is your email address that will be used for communication related to the event. Ensure it's accurate and active, as we might use it to notify you about event updates.
* **Registration Type**: * (Registration Type, Select Multiple)*: Indicate whether you are registering as 'Yes' or 'No'. This field is likely used to differentiate between attendees and non-attendees of the event, possibly for seating or logistical purposes.
* **Event Date**: * (Event Date)*: Enter the date of the virtual event. This field is required for event organizers to schedule and coordinate their events accurately.
* **Event Time**: * (Event Time)*: Input the time of the virtual event. This field is essential for attendees to know when to join the virtual event.
* **Purchase Order**: * (Purchase Order, Select One)*: You can indicate whether you have a purchase order (Yes) or not (No). This field is possibly used for event organizers to track payments or attendee purchases.
* **Registration Status**: * (Registration Status, Select One)*: Mark your registration status as 'Active' if you are attending or 'Inactive' if you are not. This field helps event organizers track attendee engagement.
* **Additional Information**: * (Additional Information)*: In this field, feel free to provide any additional information you think is relevant to your participation in the virtual event. This can be anything from your expectations to your questions or concerns.
