# school_carnival_wristband_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for student wristband orders for a school carnival event. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for your child's wristband order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date and time of the event.
2. Fill in your child's name and grade level.
3. Enter your child's student number.
4. Provide your name and email.
5. Choose the size of your child's t-shirt, scarf, and hat.
6. Enter any additional contact information for your child's parent or guardian.
7. Select the theme of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required/optional): Date of the event. Enter the date in a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Event Time** (Event Time, time, required/optional): Time of the event. Enter the time in a 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required/optional): Enter your child's name.
* **Grade Level** (Grade Level, number, required/optional): Grade level of your child.
* **Student Number** (Student Number, number, required/optional): Enter your child's student number.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Parent Email** (Parent Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Student T-shirt Size** (Student T-shirt Size, select_one, required): Choose the size of the t-shirt for your child.
* **Student Scarf Size** (Student Scarf Size, select_one, required): Choose the size of the scarf for your child.
* **Student Hat Size** (Student Hat Size, select_one, required): Choose the size of the hat for your child.
* **Parent Contact Info** (Parent Contact Info, note, required/optional): If you have any additional contact information for your child's parent or guardian, enter it here.
* **Event Theme** (Event Theme, select_one, required): Select the theme of the event.
