# Holiday Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Holiday Contest Entry Form is designed to collect entries from contestants who wish to participate in a holiday-themed contest. The form gathers information that will help the contest organizers evaluate and review each submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information and files ready to be submitted.
2. Fill in the form completely and honestly, providing accurate information about your entry.
3. Upload any relevant files or photos that support your entry.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
