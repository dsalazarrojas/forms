# Bus Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bus Request Form is used to gather information for bus transportation requests, primarily for students who need to be transported to and from school or other destinations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the requester information section with the student's full name, school identification number, and current grade level.
2. Enter the student's home address, parent or guardian's name, contact number, and email address.
3. Select the type of request (e.g., regular school transportation, field trip, special event, sports activity, or after-school program).
4. Enter the trip details, including the date, pickup location, drop-off location, pickup time, return time, and number of passengers.
5. Provide any additional trip purpose and any special needs or accommodations that may be required.
6. If necessary, specify if supervisor approval is required and enter the supervisor's name.
7. Estimate the cost of the transportation (if known).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Student ID Number** (`Student ID Number`, text, required): Enter the student's school identification number.
* **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, select_one, required): Select the student's current grade level (Elementary, Middle School, or High School).
* **Home Address** (`Home Address`, text, required): Enter the student's residential address.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`Parent/Guardian Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`Parent Phone Number`, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's contact number.
* **Parent Email** (`Parent Email`, email, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Request Details** (`Request Details`, note, required): This is an optional field for any additional information about the request.
* **Type of Request** (`Type of Request`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of the bus transportation (Regular School Transportation, Field Trip, Special Event, Sports Activity, or After-School Program).
* **Trip Date** (`Trip Date`, date, required): Enter the date for the trip.
* **Pickup Location** (`Pickup Location`, text, required): Enter the location where the student will board the bus.
* **Drop-off Location** (`Drop-off Location`, text, required): Enter the location where the student needs to be dropped off.
* **Pickup Time** (`Pickup Time`, time, required): Enter the requested pickup time.
* **Return Time** (`Return Time`, time, required): Enter the expected return time.
* **Number of Passengers** (`Number of Passengers`, number, required): Enter the number of people who need transportation.
* **Companion Information** (`Companion Information`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the names of other students who will be transported.
* **Trip Purpose** (`Trip Purpose`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the trip purpose.
* **Special Needs** (`Special Needs`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility or special needs that may be required.
* **Approval Status** (`Approval Status`, note, required): This is an internal status field and should not be filled out by users.
* **Supervisor Approval Required** (`Supervisor Approval Required`, select_one, required): Select if supervisor approval is required.
* **Supervising Teacher/Staff** (`Supervising Teacher/Staff`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person supervising the trip.
* **Estimated Cost** (`Estimated Cost`, number, optional): Enter the estimated cost of the transportation (if known).
