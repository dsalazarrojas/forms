# Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest Entry Form is designed for participants to submit their Halloween cosplay creations to be judged and displayed in the virtual contest. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to provide a clear and descriptive title and a proper email address.
3. For the Cosplay Category field, select the category that best fits your entry.
4. For the Cosplay Characters field, list all the characters you're portraying in your cosplay, separated by commas.
5. For the Costume Type field, specify the material or type of costume you used.
6. Finally, submit your completed form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Halloween Cosplay Entry Form**: Enter a brief title that describes your cosplay. This field is not required but helps us understand what you're submitting.
* **Artist Name**: Enter your name, as it will be displayed on your entry in the contest.
* **Artist E-mail**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Artist Phone**: Enter your phone number. This is not required but can help us contact you if we need more information.
* **Artist Website**: Enter your website if you have one. This is not required but can be helpful for us to learn more about you.
* **Artwork Description**: Write a brief description of your cosplay, including details such as materials used, inspiration, and any other details that might be of interest.
* **Image URL**: Enter the direct link to your cosplay image. The image should be a clear and high-quality photo. 
* **Cosplay Category**: Select the category that best fits your entry from the list below (e.g. Best Horror, Best Comedy, Best DIY, etc.).
* **Cosplay Characters**: List all the characters you're portraying in your cosplay, separated by commas.
* **Costume Type**: Specify the material or type of costume you used (e.g. foam, fabric, 3D printing, etc.). 

Tips: 
* Make sure your entry is a Halloween-themed cosplay.
* Do not enter any work that infringes on someone else's intellectual property rights or any other form of copyright or intellectual property. 
* Do not submit any work that contains graphic content or explicit language. 
* Be respectful in your description and comments.
