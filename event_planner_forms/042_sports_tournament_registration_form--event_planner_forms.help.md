# Sports Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Tournament Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants and teams for the upcoming sports tournament. This form helps us understand the participant's and team's details, such as name, age, team affiliation, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
* Select the correct team and event from the drop-down menus.
* Enter any additional notes or comments in the note field.
* Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Please enter your age in years.
* **Team** (`team`, select_one, required): Select the team you are affiliated with (Team A, Team B, or Team C).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sports category you are participating in (Soccer, Basketball, Baseball).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are participating in the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are participating in the event.
* **Sport** (`sport`, select_one, required): Select the sport you are participating in (Soccer, Basketball, Tennis).
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_multiple, optional): Select your gender (Male, Female).
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select your role in the team (Player, Team Manager).
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you registered for the event.
* **Event** (`event`, select_one, required): Select the event you are participating in (Event A, Event B, Event C).
* **Participant Name Alias** (`participant_name_alias`, text, required): Enter an alias or nickname for the participant's name.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter your address for communication purposes.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **Participant Province** (`participant_province`, text, optional): Enter your province.
* **Participant Postal Code** (`participant_postalcode`, text, optional): Enter your postal code.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Participant Note** (`participant_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
