## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants of a hackathon event, facilitating smooth event planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team details in the **Team Details** page.
2. Provide information about each participant in the **Participant Details** page.
3. Select the skills and areas of expertise you and your team members have in the **Skills** page.
4. Choose your level of experience in the **Experience** page.
5. Optionally, provide any additional team needs and logistical requirements in the **Team Needs** and **Logistical Needs** pages.
6. Enter any assigned tool information, if applicable, in the **Assigned Tool** page.
7. Enter a unique identifier for the form, if required, in the **Form ID** page.
8. Provide the name of the person who created the form in the **Created By** page.
9. Optionally, enter a description of the form, if required, in the **Description** page.
