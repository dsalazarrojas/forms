# virtual_horse_show_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a horse and its owner for a virtual horse show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Horse Name** field with the name of the horse.
2. Enter the **Owner Name** in the **Owner Name** field.
3. Select the **Breed** of the horse from the available options.
4. Enter the **Age** of the horse.
5. Enter the **Height** of the horse.
6. Enter the **Weight** of the horse.
7. Select the **Sex** of the horse (Female or Male).
8. Choose the **Show Type** of the horse (Halter, Hunter, Jumper, or Saddle Seat).
9. Choose the **Show Class** of the horse (Level 1, 2, or 3).
10. Enter the **Rider Name** field with the name of the rider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Horse Name**: Enter the name of the horse.
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the horse's owner.
* **Breed**: Select the breed of the horse.
* **Age**: Enter the age of the horse.
* **Height**: Enter the height of the horse in feet and inches.
* **Weight**: Enter the weight of the horse in pounds.
* **Sex**: Select the sex of the horse (Female or Male).
* **Show Type**: Choose the type of show the horse will be participating in (Halter, Hunter, Jumper, or Saddle Seat).
* **Show Class**: Choose the level of competition the horse will be participating in (Level 1, 2, or 3).
* **Rider Name**: Enter the name of the rider.
