# Virtual Job Fair Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Virtual Job Fair Registration Form is used to collect information from attendees or exhibitors who wish to participate in the virtual job fair. This form gathers details about the registrants' contact information, job preferences, and additional comments. By filling out this form, you can increase your visibility to potential employers or job seekers and take part in the virtual job fair.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Virtual Job Fair Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Be honest and truthful in your responses to ensure that you are representing a legitimate company or organization.
3. Review your submission carefully before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Name (`attendee_name`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your full name as you wish to be represented on the virtual job fair platform.

    *   **Company Name (`company`, `text`, required: true)**: Enter the full name of the company or organization you are representing.
*   **Email (`attendee_email`, `email`, required: true)**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
*   **Company Address (`company_address`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter the address of the company or organization you are representing.
*   **Role (`role`, `select_one`, required: true)**: Select the role you are representing (CEO, Marketing Manager, Intern, etc.).
*   **Job Title (`job_title`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter the job title of the role you are representing.
*   **Job Description (`job_description`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter a brief description of the job you are representing.
*   **Job Requirements (`job_requirements`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter the requirements for the job you are representing.
*   **Company Contact Name (`company_contact_name`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter the name of the contact person for the company or organization.
*   **Company Contact Email (`company_contact_email`, `email`, required: false)**: Enter a valid email address of the contact person.
*   **Exhibitor or Job Seeker (`exhibitor_or_job_seeker`, `select_one`, required: true)**: Select whether you are an exhibitor or a job seeker.
*   **Organization Name (`organization_name`, `text`, required: true)**: Enter the full name of the company or organization you are representing.
*   **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or information you want to share.
*   **Job Role (`job_role`, `text`, required: true)**: Enter the job role you are seeking or offering.
*   **Job Title 2 (`job_title_2`, `text`, required: true)**: Enter the job title of the second job you are offering or seeking.
*   **Job Description 2 (`job_description_2`, `text`, required: true)**: Enter a brief description of the second job you are offering or seeking.
*   **Job Requirements 2 (`job_requirements_2`, `text`, required: true)**: Enter the requirements for the second job you are offering or seeking.
*   **Additional Comments 2 (`additional_comments_2`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or information you want to share about the second job.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and up-to-date with your information.
*   Double-check your submission before clicking the submit button.
*   Make sure you have permission from the company or organization you are representing.
