# Simple Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Registration Form is designed to collect basic information from attendees or participants. It typically includes fields for their name, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your correct name and any other required fields.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* If applicable, provide your phone number for easier communication.
* If you are representing an organization, please fill out the "Organization" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name (Text)** (`name`, `text`, `false`): 
    Enter your full name for registration purposes.
* **contact (Text)** (`contact`, `text`, `false`): 
    Enter your preferred contact information.
* **email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, `false`): 
    Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **phone (Text)** (`phone`, `text`, `false`): 
    If you would like to be reached by phone, enter your contact number.
* **organization (Text)** (`organization`, `text`, `false`): 
    If you are representing an organization, enter its name for registration purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the event staff for clarification.
* This form is intended for general registration purposes; if you have specific requirements or needs, please contact the event organizers directly.
