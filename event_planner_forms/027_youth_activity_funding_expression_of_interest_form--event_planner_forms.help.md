Youth Activity Funding Expression Of Interest Form - Help Guide
===============================================

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your youth activity and funding request so that we can review and consider it for potential funding.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please complete each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2.  Be sure to review your submission carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
3.  Make sure to provide all necessary information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (Page 1: General Information)
    +   **Title**: This field is for the title of your youth activity.
    +   **Type**: This field is for the type of your youth activity (e.g., sports, arts, education).
    +   **Required**: This field is optional, but providing more information helps us understand your activity better.
*   **Activity Details** (Page 2: Activity Details)
    +   This field is for providing more information about your youth activity.
    +   Please be as detailed as possible and include any relevant information that will help us evaluate your request.
*   **Funding Request** (Page 3: Funding Request)
    +   This field is for specifying the funding you are requesting.
    +   Please be clear and concise about your funding needs.
