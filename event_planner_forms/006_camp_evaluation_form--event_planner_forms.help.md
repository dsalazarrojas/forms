# Camp Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Camp Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience at the camp. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us improve the camp.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Section 1: Respondent Information** (`-- Respondent Information --`, note, not required): Please provide some basic contact information about yourself.
2. **Camper Name** (Camper Name, text, required): Enter your name.
3. **Age** (Age, number, required): Enter your age.
4. **Section 2: Overall Experience** (`-- Overall Camp Experience --`, note, not required): This section is just a separator.
5. **How satisfied were you with the overall camp experience** (How satisfied were you with the overall camp experience, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you were with your overall experience at the camp.
6. **Would you attend camp again** (Would you attend camp again, select_one, required): Choose if you would or would not attend the camp again.
7. **Would you recommend this camp to friends** (Would you recommend this camp to friends, select_one, required): Choose if you would recommend this camp to friends.
8. **Section 3: Activities and Programming** (`-- Activities and Programming --`, note, not required): This section is just a separator.
9. **Variety of activities offered** (Variety of activities offered, select_one, required): Choose how much variety of activities were offered at the camp.
10. **How much did you enjoy the activities** (How much did you enjoy the activities, select_one, required): Choose how much you enjoyed the activities at the camp.
11. **What were your favorite activities** (What were your favorite activities, text, not required): Enter your favorite activities.
12. **Section 4: Staff and Instruction** (`-- Staff and Instruction Quality --`, note, not required): This section is just a separator.
13. **Quality of counselors and instructors** (Quality of counselors and instructors, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you were with the quality of the staff.
14. **Staff were friendly and approachable** (Staff were friendly and approachable, select_one, required): Choose how you would rate the staff's friendliness and approachability.
15. **Section 5: Facilities and Accommodations** (`-- Facilities and Accommodations --`, note, not required): This section is just a separator.
16. **Condition of facilities and sleeping areas** (Condition of facilities and sleeping areas, select_one, required): Choose the condition of the facilities and sleeping areas.
17. **Quality of meals and snacks provided** (Quality of meals and snacks provided, select_one, required): Choose the quality of the meals and snacks provided.
18. **Section 6: Feedback for Improvement** (`-- Feedback for Improvement --`, note, not required): This section is just a separator.
19. **What could be improved at camp** (What could be improved at camp, text, not required): Enter your suggestions for improvement.
20. **Additional Comments or Feedback** (Additional Comments or Feedback, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience at the camp.

## Tips
Please be honest and provide specific feedback about your experience. Your input will help us improve the camp for future participants.
