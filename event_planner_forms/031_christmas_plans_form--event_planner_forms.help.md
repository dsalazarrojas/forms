# Christmas Plans Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Christmas Plans Form is used to collect information for holiday plans and arrangements. It is a comprehensive guide to help you plan and prepare for the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, fill out each section accordingly.
2. Enter your personal information (first name, last name, and email address) in the Personal Information section.
3. Answer the questions about your holiday plans (where you will celebrate, whether you have a family gathering, and if you are traveling).
4. In the Activities section, select all activities you have planned for the holiday season.
5. Provide your dining plans (how you plan to spend your holiday mealtime).
6. If you are hosting a holiday gathering, provide information about the expected number of guests and whether children will be attending.
7. Finally, estimate your total holiday spending and provide any special requests or needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to share it).
* **Where will you celebrate Christmas?** (`celebration_location`, required): Enter the location where you will celebrate Christmas.
* **Will you have a family gathering?** (`family_gathering`, required): Choose whether you will have a family gathering or not.
* **Are you traveling?** (`travel_plans`, required): Choose how you will be traveling during the holiday season.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, optional): Enter your travel destination (if you're traveling).
* **Will you attend a Christmas party?** (`party_attendance`, required): Choose whether you will attend a Christmas party or not.
* **Planned Activities** (`planned_activities`, optional): Select all activities you have planned for the holiday season.
* **Dining Plans** (`dining_plans`, optional): Choose how you plan to spend your holiday mealtime.
* **Number of Expected Guests** (`expected_guests`, optional): Enter the number of expected guests for your holiday gathering.
* **Number of Children Attending** (`children_attending`, optional): Enter the number of children attending your holiday gathering.
* **Will you exchange gifts?** (`gift_exchange`, optional): Choose whether you will exchange gifts or not.
* **Estimated Total Holiday Spending** (`spending_estimate`, optional): Choose an estimated total holiday spending range.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you or your guests may have.
* **Any Special Requests or Needs** (`special_needs`, optional): Enter any special requests or needs you may have for your holiday gathering.
* **Budget Estimates** (`budget_estimate`, optional): Enter an estimated budget range for your holiday spending.

Note: Some fields are optional, but they might still be required for certain situations or contexts. It is recommended to fill out all sections to get a complete picture of your holiday plans and arrangements. 

This help guide is designed to be concrete, concise, and practical, providing clear instructions on how to complete each section of the form. The explanations for each field are based on the provided YAML fields, aiming to cover all necessary information while avoiding unsupported business logic.
