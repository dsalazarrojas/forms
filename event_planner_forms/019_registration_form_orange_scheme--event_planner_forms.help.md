# Registration Form Orange Scheme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Registration Form Orange Scheme is designed to collect registration details for a particular event or occasion. This form helps us gather essential information about you and your preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "Required" in the form.
2. Ensure that you enter your email address correctly in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the event type and event name relevant to your registration.
4. Select the date and time of the event if required.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your participation in the event.
6. Confirm your phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (user_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Password (password)** (`text`, optional): Enter a password for your registration. This field is not secure, so do not use your actual password.
* **Confirm Password (confirm_password)** (`text`, optional): Confirm the password entered in the previous field for security purposes.
* **Event Type (event_type)** (`select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you are attending the event, "No" otherwise.
* **Event Name (event_name)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Date (date)** (`date`, optional): Select the date of the event if you know it.
* **Time (time)** (`time`, optional): Select the time of the event if you know it.
* **Note (note)** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Confirm Phone (confirm_phone)** (`text`, optional): Confirm the phone number entered in the previous field for security purposes.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
- Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
- Select the correct event type and event name relevant to your registration.
- Use a secure way to store your password.
