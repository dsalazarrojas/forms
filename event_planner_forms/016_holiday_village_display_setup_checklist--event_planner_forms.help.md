# Holiday Village Display Setup Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a checklist for setting up a holiday village display. It covers various aspects of the setup process, including site preparation, installation checks, seasonal tasks, and safety procedures. By completing this form, users can ensure that their holiday village display is set up correctly and safely.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each section carefully and make sure you have understood each item before proceeding.
2. Complete all required fields, indicated by a '*'.
3. Use your best judgment when filling in the answers. If you're unsure about anything, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
4. Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form. Double-check your answers to ensure everything is in order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Village Display Setup** (`holiday_village_display_setup`, text, required): This is the first field in the form where you can enter the setup details of the holiday village display.
* **Site Preparation** (`site_preparation`, text, required): Here, you can document the preparation of the site, including cleaning, marking, and other essential tasks.
* **Installation Checks** (`installation_checks`, text, required): This field is for documenting the installation checks, including electrical, water, and fire suppression system checks, to ensure everything is properly installed and functioning correctly.
* **Seasonal Tasks** (`seasonal_tasks`, text, required): Enter details about seasonal-specific tasks, such as decorating, lighting, and special events.
* **Site Safety** (`site_safety`, text, required): Record your observations and actions taken to ensure the site is safe for visitors, including fire alarms, sprinkler systems, and other safety measures.
* **Maintenance Records** (`maintenance_records`, text, required): Document any maintenance tasks performed on the site, including cleaning, repairs, and inspections.
* **Display Dismantling** (`display_dismantling`, text, required): Describe how you plan to dismantle the display after the event.
* **Final Inspections** (`final_inspections`, text, required): Conduct a final inspection before the event to ensure everything is in order.
* **Overall Site Condition** (`overall_site_condition`, text, required): Assess the overall condition of the site after setup.
* **Display Standards** (`display_standards`, text, required): Check if the display meets the required standards, including size, color, and other visual aspects.
* **Safety Procedures** (`safety_procedures`, text, required): Document any safety procedures, including evacuation routes, emergency contact information, and first aid kits.
* **Site Safety Checklist** (`site_safety_checklist`, text, required): Conduct a safety checklist to ensure all safety measures are in place.
* **Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, text, required): Describe the procedures for handling emergencies, including fires, floods, and other unexpected events.
* **Electrical And Fire Prevention Procedures** (`electrical_and_fire_prevention_procedures`, text, required): Record any electrical and fire prevention procedures, including fire extinguishers, fire alarms, and safety equipment.
* **First Aid Kits And Equipment** (`first_aid_kits_and_equipment`, text, required): Describe the first aid kits and equipment on site, including the location and availability of each item.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_information`, text, required): Provide contact information for emergency services, including phone numbers and contact names.
* **Overall Safety Condition** (`overall_safety_condition`, text, required): Assess the overall safety condition of the site.
* **Exit Locations And Evacuation Routes** (`exit_locations_and_evacuation_routes`, text, required): Identify exit locations and evacuation routes on site.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, text, required): Describe the fire extinguishers on site, including their type and location.
* **Emergency Power Outlets** (`emergency_power_outlets`, text, required): Record details about emergency power outlets, including their location and condition.
* **Electrical Connections** (`electrical_connections`, text, required): Document electrical connections on site, including their condition and safety measures.
* **First Aid And Safety Equipment** (`first_aid_and_safety_equipment`, text, required): Describe first aid and safety equipment on site.
* **Fire Alarms And Sprinkler Systems** (`fire_alarms_and_sprinkler_systems`, text, required): Record details about fire alarms and sprinkler systems on site.
* **Emergency Telephone** (`emergency_telephone`, text, required): Describe the emergency telephone on site.
* **Emergency Power Generators** (`emergency_power_generators`, text, required): Record details about emergency power generators on site.
