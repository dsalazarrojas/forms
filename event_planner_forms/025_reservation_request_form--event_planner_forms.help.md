# reservation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The reservation_request_form is used to submit a reservation request to an event planner. This form collects information about the guest, reservation details, and contact information to help the event planner prepare for the reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your guest details, including their name and surname.
2. Enter the reservation start and end dates.
3. Select the number of people attending the reservation.
4. Choose the type of reservation (Lunch, Dinner, or Weekend).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the reservation.
6. Select a contact method (phone, email, or text).
7. Enter the phone number or email address for the contact method you selected.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Details** (`Guest Details`, text, optional): Enter the name of the guest and their surname.
* **Reservation Details** (`Reservation Details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the reservation.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted about the reservation (phone, email, or text).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you selected "phone" as the contact method.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you selected "email" as the contact method.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the reservation.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the reservation.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the reservation.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Surname** (`Surname`, text, optional): Enter your surname.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the reservation.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the reservation.
* **Number of People** (`Number of People`, number, optional): Enter the number of people attending the reservation.
* **Type of Reservation** (`Type of Reservation`, select_one, optional): Select the type of reservation (Lunch, Dinner, or Weekend).
* **Reservation Comment** (`Reservation Comment`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the reservation.
