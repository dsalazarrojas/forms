# thanksgiving_drinking_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for a Thanksgiving celebration event. It's intended for tracking attendees, their drink preferences, and collecting contact information for communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, begin by filling out the Survey page, which will ask for general information from attendees.
2.  Next, move to the Drinker page, where you'll identify yourself as a drinker and provide your name (or ID) to be tracked for the event.
3.  On the Drinkers Survey page, you'll be asked whether you like or dislike different drinks. Select the drinks you like or dislike from the given options.
4.  If you have more drinks you like or dislike, you can add them in the Drinkers 0, 1, and 2 sections.
5.  On the Date and Time page, select the date and time for the event.
6.  Finally, provide your contact information (email and phone number) on the Email and Phone page, if you'd like to stay in touch.
7.  End by adding a note if you have any additional comments or information for the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Survey (Page 1)**:
    *   **Survey**: This page is for general information collection.
*   **Drinker (Page 2)**:
    *   **Drinker**: Enter your name (or ID) to be tracked for the event.
*   **Drinkers Survey (Page 3)**:
    *   **Drinkers Survey**: Select the drinks you like or dislike.
*   **Drinkers 0 (Page 4)**:
    *   **Drinkers 0**: Select additional drinks you like or dislike.
*   **Drinkers 1 (Page 5)**:
    *   **Drinkers 1**: Select additional drinks you like or dislike.
*   **Drinkers 2 (Page 6)**:
    *   **Drinkers 2**: Select additional drinks you like or dislike.
*   **Date (Page 7)**:
    *   **Date**: Enter the date of the event.
*   **Time (Page 8)**:
    *   **Time**: Enter the time of the event.
*   **Email (Page 9)**:
    *   **Email**: Enter your email for event communication.
*   **Phone (Page 10)**:
    *   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for event communication.
*   **Note (Page 11)**:
    *   **Note**: Add any additional comments or information.

**Tips**

*   Please be as accurate and honest as possible when answering the questions to help with event planning.
*   Use the email and phone fields for the organizers to keep in touch with attendees.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to reach out to the event organizers.
