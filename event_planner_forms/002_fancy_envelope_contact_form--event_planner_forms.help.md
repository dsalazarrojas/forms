# Fancy Envelope Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit general contact information or inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your First Name (if you wish to share it with us).
2. Provide your Email address for us to communicate with you.
3. Enter your Phone number for any additional contact or clarification.
4. If applicable, enter your Company Name for our records.
5. Type a message about your inquiry or reason for contacting us in the Message field.
6. If known, select your Department of interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: A field to provide your first name, if you wish to share it with us.
  • This field is not required but can be useful for a more personal touch.
- **Email**: A field to provide a valid email address for us to communicate with you.
  • Please enter your actual email address where we can send responses.
- **Phone**: A field to provide your phone number for any additional contact or clarification.
  • This field is optional but can aid in response or further communication.
- **Company Name**: A field to identify your affiliated company (if applicable).
  • Enter your company name if you are contacting us on behalf of your organization.
- **Message**: A field to provide more information about your inquiry or reason for contacting us.
  • Use this field to add any additional comments or context about your request.
- **Department**: A field to select the department you would like to contact within our organization.
  • Choose from our available departments: Accounting, Marketing, or Sales.

## Tips
- Please ensure the email address you provide is valid.
- If you need to add more details or attach files, you can do so in the Message field.
- Selecting a department helps us direct your inquiry to the most suitable team.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask!
