# Easter Brunch Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Easter Brunch Order Form is used to collect information from guests who are attending an Easter brunch event. The form helps us understand their preferences and dietary needs, as well as any special requests they may have. Completing this form ensures that we can tailor the experience to their liking and make necessary arrangements for their comfort and enjoyment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read the form carefully**: Make sure you understand what the form is asking for and what information is required.
2. **Fill in your details**: Enter your name and email address to let us know you're attending the event.
3. **Choose your time slot**: Select the desired time for the Easter brunch.
4. **Provide your contact information**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
5. **Add any special requests**: If you have any dietary restrictions or special needs, let us know so we can accommodate them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Easter Brunch Order Form (Page 1)**: This is a placeholder page for the form header.
* **Easter Brunch Form (Field 1)**: Enter your name as you'd like it to be displayed on the Easter brunch list.
* **Easter Brunch Date (Field 2)**: Select the date for the Easter brunch event. This is a required field to ensure we can plan the event accordingly.
* **Easter Brunch Time (Field 3)**: Choose the time slot for the Easter brunch event. This is also a required field for planning purposes.
* **Guest Name (Field 4)**: Enter the name of the guest for whom you're ordering the brunch.
* **Guest Email (Field 5)**: Enter the email address of the guest so we can reach them if needed.
* **Guest Phone (Field 6)**: Enter the phone number of the guest so we can contact them directly.
* **Special Requests (Field 7)**: If you have any dietary restrictions or special needs, type them in this field so we can take care of them. This is a free-text field for your convenience.

* If you have any special requests, make sure to include them in the "Special Requests" field for our team to accommodate them.
