# Reunion Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Reunion Registration form is used to gather information about upcoming school or family reunions. By completing this form, you will be able to share the details of your event with others and help them plan for a successful and enjoyable reunion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct type of event: If you are hosting or attending a school reunion, choose "School" from the Reunion Type options. If you are hosting or attending a family reunion, choose "Family". If your reunion doesn't fit into either category, select "Other" and add a brief description in the Notes field.
2. Enter the details of your reunion: Select the Date and Time of your event. Enter your Reunion Name and any additional contact information you think would be helpful for others to know.
3. Add guest and location information: Enter the number of guests attending your reunion and the Location of the event.
4. Add school-specific information: If you are hosting or attending a school reunion, enter the School Name and Class.
5. Add family-specific information: If you are hosting or attending a family reunion, enter your Family Name and any relationships with other family members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reunion Details** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your reunion.
* **Date** (2, date, required): Select the date of your reunion.
* **Time** (3, text, optional): Enter the time of your reunion.
* **Location** (4, text, optional): Enter the location of your reunion.
* **Reunion Type** (5, select_one, optional): Choose the type of your reunion. Select "School", "Family", or "Other" if applicable.
* **Reunion Name** (6, text, optional): Enter a name for your reunion.
* **Number of Guests** (7, number, optional): Estimate the number of guests attending your reunion.
* **Contact Info** (8, text, optional): Enter any contact information for others to get in touch with you.
* **Notes** (9, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your reunion.
* **School Name (if school)** (10, text, optional): If your reunion is at a school, enter the name of the school.
* **School Class** (11, text, optional): If your reunion is at a school, enter the class attending.
* **Family Name** (12, text, optional): If your reunion is a family gathering, enter your last name.
* **Family Relationships** (13, text, optional): If your reunion is a family gathering, list any relationships among family members.
