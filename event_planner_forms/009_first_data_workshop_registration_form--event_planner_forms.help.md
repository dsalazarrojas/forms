# First Data Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and payment details for the First Data Workshop. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure your participation is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your participant details.
2. Choose your preferred payment method (Online, Offline, or Cash Payment).
3. Enter your participant address, number, and other required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_details**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of your participation or any additional information about yourself.
* **participant_name**: A text field for your name.
* **email**: A field for your email address.
* **participant_address**: A text field for your address.
* **participant_number**: A field for your participant number.
* **payment_method**: A dropdown menu to choose your preferred payment method (Online, Offline, or Cash Payment).
* **total_participants**: A field for the total number of participants.
* **workshop_price**: A field for the workshop price.
* **participant_discount**: A field for participant discount.
* **participant_payment**: A dropdown menu to choose your payment method (Online, Offline, or Cash Payment).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the event organizer for clarification.
* Please note that payment details are optional but recommended for accurate participant tracking.
