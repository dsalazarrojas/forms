# Open Mic Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Mic Registration Form is a tool for performers to register for an open mic event. It collects necessary information from performers to help event organizers prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your details.
2. Select your availability for the event from the options provided.
3. Enter your contact email address.
4. Optionally, provide your contact phone number and address.
5. Briefly describe your experience as a performer.
6. Write a short bio about yourself.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Name**: Enter your name as it will be displayed in the event schedule.
* **Event Date**: Choose the date of the event you wish to perform at.
* **Start Time**: Choose the start time of your performance (if available).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your performance. This is required as it will help us plan the event schedule.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your performance (in minutes).
* **Availability**: Select your preferred time slot for the event (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your performance.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Contact Address** (Optional): Enter your address if you prefer to be contacted by mail or in-person.
* **Performer Experience**: Describe your experience as a performer, such as any notable performances or events you've participated in.
* **Bio**: Write a short bio about yourself, including any relevant information about your performance style or experience.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or questions you have about your performance.
