# Speaker And Topic Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential speakers and topics for events. It's meant to be used by event planners to gather ideas and suggestions from speakers who have a topic in mind.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible about the speaker and topic.
2. Make sure to fill out the form only once for each speaker and topic suggestion.
3. Use the provided fields to give event planners as much information as possible about the speaker and topic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Planner Form** (`event_planner_form`, text, not required): This field is not applicable as it seems to be a page label rather than a field. However, it's likely that this field is intended to be the title of the event or a brief description of the event.
* **Speaker Suggestion** (`speaker_suggestion`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the speaker and topic in this field.
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the speaker, preferably their full name.
* **Speaker Title** (`speaker_title`, text, not required): Enter the speaker's title or affiliation.
* **Speaker Organization** (`speaker_organization`, text, not required): Enter the name of the speaker's organization or institution.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter any contact information you have for the speaker, such as email or phone number.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`, email, not required): Enter the speaker's email address, preferably in the format "example@example.com".
* **Speaker Phone** (`speaker_phone`, text, not required): Enter the speaker's phone number.
* **Topic Name** (`topic_name`, text, not required): Enter a brief name for the topic.
* **Other Topic** (`topic_name_other`, text, not required): Enter any other topic the speaker may be interested in presenting on.
* **Topic Type** (`topic_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of topic the speaker is proposing (e.g., Webinar, Conference, Meeting, Workshop, Seminar).
* **Topic Tags** (`topic_tags`, text, not required): Enter relevant keywords or tags related to the topic.
* **Topic Description** (`topic_description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the topic.

# Tips

* Please make sure to proofread your submission for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any additional information or clarification about the speaker or topic, feel free to include it in the relevant fields.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, it's okay to leave them blank, but try to provide as much information as possible.
