# Fascial Therapy Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their fascial therapy sessions. Your responses will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better care to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Rate your overall experience with the therapist on the scale provided.
2. Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving our services.
3. Type any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about your therapy session.
4. If you have a name and contact information (email and phone number), please provide it so we can follow up on your feedback.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating** (rating, required): Rate your experience on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Suggestions** (suggestions, optional): If you have any ideas for improving our services, provide them here.
* **Feedback** (feedback, optional): Type any comments or feedback about your therapy session you'd like to share.
* **Therapist Feedback** (therapist_feedback, optional): If you have any comments or feedback for the therapist, please provide them here.
* **Client Name** (client_name, optional): If you'd like to share your name, please provide it so we can follow up on your feedback.
* **Email** (email, optional): If you'd like to share your email address, we can follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone** (phone, optional): If you'd like to share your phone number, we can follow up on your feedback.
Note: All fields are optional, but your email and phone number will help us follow up on your feedback if you'd like.
