# Hospital Patient Care Improvement Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their recent visit to the hospital. The goal is to collect information about their overall satisfaction and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answers to the questions on each page by choosing from the provided options.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, marked as "Yes" or "No" in the options.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was your visit** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_1`, `select_multiple`, **Required**: no): 
Please select all the activities that occurred during your visit, such as "Yes" for each relevant activity.
* **What was the reason for your visit** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_2`, `select_one`, **Required**: no): 
Choose "Yes" if the reason for your visit was for your scheduled appointment, or "No" if it was an emergency or other.
* **What was done during your visit** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_3`, `select_multiple`, **Required**: no): 
Select all the services or activities that were performed during your visit, such as "Yes" for each relevant service.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_4`, `select_one`, **Required**: no): 
Choose "Satisfied" if you were satisfied with your visit, or "Dissatisfied" if you were not.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_5`, `text`, **Required**: no): 
If you have any suggestions or feedback for the hospital, please type them in this text field.
* **Patient name** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_6`, `text`, **Required**: no): 
Type your name as you would like it to be listed on your feedback.
* **Patient email** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_7`, `email`, **Required**: no): 
Enter your email address so we can contact you with a response to your feedback.
* **Patient phone** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_8`, `text`, **Required**: no): 
Enter your phone number so we can contact you with a response to your feedback.
* **Date of visit** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_9`, `date`, **Required**: no): 
Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of visit** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_10`, `time`, **Required**: no): 
Enter the time of your visit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Department** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_11`, `select_one`, **Required**: no): 
Choose "Yes" if the department you visited was your scheduled appointment, or "No" if it was an emergency or other.
* **Doctor** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_12`, `select_one`, **Required**: no): 
Choose "Yes" if you visited a doctor during your visit, or "No" if you did not.
* **Nurse** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_13`, `select_one`, **Required**: no): 
Choose "Yes" if you were seen by a nurse during your visit, or "No" if you were not.
* **Staff member** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_14`, `select_one`, **Required**: no): 
Choose "Yes" if you were seen by a staff member during your visit, or "No" if you were not.
* **Hospital staff** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_15`, `select_one`, **Required**: no): 
Choose "Yes" if you were satisfied with the hospital staff, or "No" if you were not.
* **Other** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_16`, `select_multiple`, **Required**: no): 
Select "Yes" if any other activities occurred during your visit that are not listed.
* **What do you like** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_17`, `select_multiple`, **Required**: no): 
Select all the services or activities that you liked during your visit.
* **What do you dislike** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_18`, `select_multiple`, **Required**: no): 
Select all the services or activities that you did not like during your visit.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`hospital_patient_care_improvement_poll_page_19`, `text`, **Required**: no): 
If you have any further suggestions or comments, please type them in this text field.
