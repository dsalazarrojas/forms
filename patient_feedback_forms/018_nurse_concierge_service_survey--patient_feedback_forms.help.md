**Nurse Concierge Service Survey - Help Guide**
===========================================

### Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from patients to help us improve our services. Your responses will be kept confidential and will help us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve on.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. For questions that ask for ratings or opinions (e.g., "quality of service", "helpfulness of care team"), please respond with the corresponding number or option that best reflects your experience.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the "Do you have any comments or suggestions?" field.
4. Ensure that all required fields (marked with *) are completed.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Feedback Form** (`patient_feedback_form`, text, false): Please provide any comments or feedback about your experience with our nurse concierge services.
* **What nursing services were you provided with?** (`nursing_services`, select_multiple, false): Please select all the services you received during your visit.
	+ Nursing Service 1
	+ Nursing Service 2
	+ Nursing Service 3
* **On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate the quality of service?** (`quality_of_service`, number, false): Please rate your experience with our service quality.
* **How would you rate the helpfulness of our care team?** (`helpfulness_of_caregiver`, select_one, false): Please select the level of helpfulness of our care team.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, note, false): Feel free to add any comments or suggestions you may have for our services.
* **How can we best contact you?** (`contact_information`, email, false): Please provide your preferred contact information.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone_number`, text, false): Please provide your phone number.
* **What date did you visit?** (`date_visited`, date, false): Please select the date of your visit.
* **What time did you visit?** (`time_visited`, time, false): Please select the time of your visit.
* **How often do you visit us?** (`visit_frequency`, select_one, false): Please select how often you visit our services.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **What's your care team member's name?** (`staff_name`, text, false): Please provide the name of your care team member.
* **What's their role or position?** (`staff_title`, text, false): Please provide the role or position of your care team member.

### Tips

* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to help us improve our services.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask us and we'll do our best to clarify.
