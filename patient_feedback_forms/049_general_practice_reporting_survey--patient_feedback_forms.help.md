# General Practice Reporting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Practice Reporting Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from patients about their experiences with a general practice. This feedback is used to improve the quality of service and make necessary adjustments to better meet the needs of our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please review the form and ensure that you have not already submitted a similar form.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the practice.
4. Submit the form to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, **required: false**): This is a general introduction to the form. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose of this form.
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, `text`, **required: false**): Please enter the name of the practice you are providing feedback about.
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, `text`, **required: false**): Please enter the name of the practitioner you visited.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, `date`, **required: false**): Please enter the date of your visit to the practice.
* **Feedback Time** (`feedback_time`, `time`, **required: false**): Please enter the time of your visit to the practice.
* **What is your overall experience with this practice?** (`question1`, `text`, **required: false**): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the practice.
* **How would you rate the communication skills of the practitioners?** (`question2`, `select_one`, **required: false**): Please select one of the options below:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **How would you rate the knowledge of the practitioners about your health?** (`question3`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**): Please select all that apply:
	+ Outstanding
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **How would you rate the overall care you received?** (`question4`, `select_one`, **required: false**): Please select one of the options below:
	+ Outstanding
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **What suggestions do you have to improve the practice?** (`question5`, `text`, **required: false`): Please provide any suggestions you have for improving the practice.

## Tips

* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Provide as much detail as possible.
* Submit the form to help us improve our services.
