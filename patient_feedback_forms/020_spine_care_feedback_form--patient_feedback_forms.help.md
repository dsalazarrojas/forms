# Spine Care Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Spine Care Feedback Form" is designed to collect feedback from patients on their experiences with spine care services. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us understand your thoughts and opinions, which will help us improve our care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
- Enter your title and category for the feedback you are providing.
- Provide a brief description of your feedback, including any specific events or incidents that led to your feedback.
- Choose a score out of 5 or 10 to rate your overall experience.
- Optionally, provide a comment or rating for any specific aspect of our care.
- Enter your name, email (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up), and phone number (if you'd like to be contacted directly).
- Finally, submit the form to let us know that you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spine Care Feedback Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for your feedback, e.g., "Good experience, but..."
* **Category** (`form_category`, text, required/optional): Choose a category that best describes your feedback (e.g., "Surgery", "Physical Therapy", etc.).
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your feedback, including any specific events or incidents that led to your feedback.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall experience out of 5 (e.g., "1", "2", "3", "4", or "5").
* **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment`, text, required/optional): Optionally, provide a comment about any specific aspect of our care.
* **Rating 5** (`rating_5`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating 10** (`rating_10`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, as it will be displayed on the feedback form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up (e.g., "john.doe@example.com").
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly (e.g., "123-456-7890").
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you're submitting the form (auto-filled with the current date).
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_at_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you're submitting the form (auto-filled with the current time).

Note: Fields marked as required/optional will be filled out based on the user's preference.
