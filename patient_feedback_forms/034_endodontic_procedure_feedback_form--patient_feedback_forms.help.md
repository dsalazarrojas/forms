# Endodontic Procedure Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Endodontic Procedure Feedback Form is a tool for patients to provide feedback about their endodontic procedure experience. This form is not mandatory, but it helps the endodontic team to improve their services and provide better care to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options and provide your feedback in the required fields. You can submit the form as many times as you like, and each time your feedback will be recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback** (`first_page`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide a brief description of your experience with the endodontic procedure. This could be positive or negative feedback about the procedure, the staff, or any other aspect of your visit.
- **Endodontic** (`endodontic_procedure`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
    Select all that apply to indicate your experience with the endodontic procedure. Choose from 'Yes' to indicate you were satisfied or 'No' to indicate you were not satisfied, or select 'Other' if you have a different experience.
- **Pain level** (`pain_level`, select_one, required/optional): 
    Rate your pain level after the endodontic procedure, selecting from 'Very High', 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low'.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional): 
    Rate your overall satisfaction with the endodontic procedure, selecting from 'Very Satisfied', 'Satisfied', 'Neutral', or 'Dissatisfied'.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any additional comments about your experience with the endodontic procedure.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): 
    Enter the date of your visit or procedure.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): 
    Enter the time of your visit or procedure.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): 
    Enter your name for reference.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): 
    Enter your email address for follow-up or communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): 
    Enter your phone number for follow-up or communication.
- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required/optional): 
    Select the staff member who assisted you during the endodontic procedure.
- **Comments Doctors** (`comments_doctors`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any feedback or comments about the doctors who assisted you.
- **Comments Dentists** (`comments_dentists`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any feedback or comments about the dentists who assisted you.
- **Comments Nurses** (`comments_nurses`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any feedback or comments about the nurses who assisted you.
- **Comments Admin** (`comments_admin`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any feedback or comments about the administrative staff.
- **Comments Other** (`comments_other`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any other feedback or comments about your experience.
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required/optional): 
    Enter the person submitting the feedback.
- **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, text, required/optional): 
    Enter the date and time the feedback was submitted.
- **Created At** (`created_at`, text, required/optional): 
    Display only, not editable.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required/optional): 
    Display only, not editable.
- **User ID** (`user_id`, text, required/optional): 
    Display only, not editable.
- **Comments Other Doctors** (`comments_other_doctors`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any feedback or comments about other doctors.
- **Comments Other Admin** (`comments_other_admin`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any feedback or comments about other administrative staff.
- **Comments Other Nurses** (`comments_other_nurses`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any feedback or comments about other nurses.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form as accurately as possible to help the endodontic team improve their services.
* You can submit the form multiple times if you have different experiences or feedback.
* Your feedback is confidential and will only be reviewed by authorized staff.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact the endodontic team or administrative staff.
