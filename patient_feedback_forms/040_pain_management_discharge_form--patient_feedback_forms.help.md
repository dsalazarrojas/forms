# pain_management_discharge_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information after discharge from a healthcare facility to ensure proper pain management and follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter your patient's date of birth in the "Patient Date of Birth" field. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Describe your patient's pain assessment in the "Pain Assessment" field. This is an opportunity to share your evaluation of their current pain level and any notable pain management interventions.
4. Document your patient's pain management plan in the "Pain Management Plan" field. This section is reserved for notes on the patient's treatment plan, including medication, therapy, or other interventions.
5. Describe any follow-up care instructions in the "Follow-up Care" field. This is the ideal place to outline any recommended post-discharge care, including medication management, appointment schedules, or other important instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name
• **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.

### Patient Date of Birth
• **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's birthdate in the standard YYYY-MM-DD format.

### Pain Assessment
• **Pain Assessment** (`pain_assessment`, text, required/optional): Describe the patient's current pain level, including any notable pain management interventions.

### Pain Management Plan
• **Pain Management Plan** (`pain_management_plan`, text, required/optional): Document the patient's treatment plan, including medication, therapy, or other interventions.

### Follow-up Care
• **Follow-up Care** (`follow_up_care`, text, required/optional): Outline any recommended post-discharge care instructions, including medication management, appointment schedules, or other important details.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your healthcare team for guidance.
- This form is for internal use only. Please do not share it with external parties without clearance.
