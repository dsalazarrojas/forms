# Physical Therapy Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document patient's progress, readiness for discharge, and provide instructions for follow-up care. It is an essential tool for healthcare providers to assess the patient's condition and create a smooth transition from physical therapy care to independent living.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Complete each field honestly and thoroughly, providing as much information as possible.
*   Answer all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please discuss it with your healthcare provider.
*   Please use standard date and time formats, e.g., MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Progress** (`patient_progress`): Describe your current physical progress, including any improvements or concerns you've experienced during your treatment.
*   **Discharge Readiness** (`discharge_readiness`): Indicate your level of readiness to be discharged from physical therapy care. Please answer on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "not ready" and 5 being "fully ready".
*   **Home Exercise Program** (`home_exercise_program`): Outline any exercises or activities you plan to perform at home to maintain and improve your progress.
*   **Follow-up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`): Provide any instructions or guidance you received from your healthcare provider for follow-up care, including any appointments, medications, or testing.
*   **Patient Counseling** (`patient_counseling`): Share any additional comments or concerns you would like to discuss with your healthcare provider.
*   **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`): Enter the date you plan to be discharged from physical therapy care.
*   **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`): Enter the time of discharge, using the 12-hour clock (e.g., 08:00 AM or 14:30 PM).
*   **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`): Provide the contact information for your follow-up care, including the name and number of the healthcare provider or facility.
*   **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`): Sign this form with your healthcare provider's signature, confirming that you have received and understand the instructions and care plan.

## Tips

*   Please review and revise your answers as needed before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
*   Remember to keep a copy of this form for your records, as it may be helpful for future reference.
