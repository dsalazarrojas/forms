# Patient Safety Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient feedback on potential safety risks and events related to their care. By completing this form, you will help us assess patient safety risks and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Select all risk factors that apply from the "Patient Safety Risk Factors" section.
3. If you selected "Falls or near falls" or "Surgical or invasive procedure complications" under "Patient Safety Risk Factors", you will be asked to provide a more specific description in the "Other" field.
4. Answer "Adverse event occurred" or "No adverse event occurred" under "Patient Safety Events".
5. If you answered "Adverse event occurred" under "Patient Safety Events", you will be asked to describe the event in the "Describe the event" field.
6. Select where the adverse event occurred from the "Event location" dropdown menu.
7. If you answered "Other location" under "Event location", you will be asked to provide a more specific description in the "Other location" field.
8. Answer "Patient factors", "Healthcare provider factors", or "Both" under "Patient Safety Risk Factors Cause".
9. If you answered "Other" under "Patient Safety Risk Factors Cause", you will be asked to provide a more specific description in the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label>Patient Safety Risk Factors</label> (`type`: <code>select_multiple</code>, required/optional): 
* This section asks you to select all potential safety risks that you experienced during your stay. 

### <label>Please select all that apply</label> (`type`: <code>select_one</code>, required/optional): 
* If you selected "Falls or near falls" or "Surgical or invasive procedure complications" under "Patient Safety Risk Factors", please select this option to provide more specific information.

### <label>Other</label> (`type`: <code>text</code>, required/optional): 
* If you selected "Falls or near falls" or "Surgical or invasive procedure complications" under "Patient Safety Risk Factors", or "Other (specify)" under "Patient Safety Risk Factors", please provide a more specific description here.

### <label>Event location</label> (`type`: <code>select_one</code>, required/optional): 
* This question asks where the adverse event occurred. Please select from the options provided.

### <label>Other location</label> (`type`: <code>text</code>, required/optional): 
* If you selected "Other location" under "Event location", please provide more specific information here.

### <label>Patient Safety Risk Factors Cause</label> (`type`: <code>select_one</code>, required/optional): 
* This question asks you to identify the cause of the patient safety risk factors you selected. Please choose from the options provided.

### <label>Other</label> (`type`: <code>text</code>, required/optional): 
* If you selected "Other" under "Patient Safety Risk Factors Cause", please provide more specific information here.

### <label>Patient Safety Events</label> (`type`: <code>select_one</code>, required/optional): 
* This question asks whether you experienced an adverse event during your stay. Please choose from the options provided.

### <label>Describe the event</label> (`type`: <code>text</code>, required/optional): 
* If you answered "Adverse event occurred" under "Patient Safety Events", please provide a description of the event here.

### <label>Other</label> (`type`: <code>text</code>, required/optional): 
* This is not applicable to this field but it's a placeholder for other fields like "Patient Safety Risk Factors Other" and "Patient Safety Risk Factors Cause Other".
