# Medication Review Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Review Checklist is a form designed to collect information about a patient's medical condition, medical supervision, medication frequency, and side effects. This information will help medical professionals to monitor and adjust treatment plans accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

*   Answer each question carefully, as they are crucial for medical professionals to provide the best possible care for you.
*   If you have multiple medical conditions, select all the conditions that apply to you.
*   If you have taken a medication, indicate how frequently you take it and any side effects you have experienced.
*   If you have allergies, select "True" if you are allergic to any of the listed items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Feedback** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information you think is relevant to your medical condition or treatment plan.
*   **Medical Condition** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select all the medical conditions that apply to you from the list provided.
    -   'True': You have a medical condition that affects your treatment plan.
    -   'False': You do not have a medical condition that affects your treatment plan.
*   **Medical Supervision** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the medical professional who supervises your treatment.
    -   'True': You receive regular medical supervision.
    -   'False': You do not receive regular medical supervision.
*   **Medication Frequency** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select how often you take your medication from the list provided.
    -   Daily: You take your medication daily.
    -   Weekly: You take your medication weekly.
    -   Monthly: You take your medication monthly.
    -   Rarely: You rarely take your medication.
*   **Medical Profession** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the medical professional who has prescribed your medication from the list provided.
    -   Doctor: The medication was prescribed by a doctor.
    -   Nurse: The medication was prescribed by a nurse.
    -   Pharmacist: The medication was prescribed by a pharmacist.
    -   Other: The medication was prescribed by another medical professional.
*   **Medication Side Effects** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select any side effects you have experienced from the list provided.
    -   Headache: You have experienced a headache as a side effect.
    -   Dizziness: You have experienced dizziness as a side effect.
    -   Fatigue: You have experienced fatigue as a side effect.
    -   Nausea: You have experienced nausea as a side effect.
    -   Other: You have experienced other side effects not listed here.
*   **Medical Supervisor** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the medical supervisor who supervises your treatment from the list provided.
    -   Doctor: The medical supervisor is a doctor.
    -   Nurse: The medical supervisor is a nurse.
    -   Pharmacist: The medical supervisor is a pharmacist.
    -   Other: The medical supervisor is another medical professional.
*   **Patient Allergies** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select 'True' if you are allergic to any of the listed items.
*   **Medication Side Effects 2** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select any side effects you have experienced from the list provided.
    -   Headache: You have experienced a headache as a side effect.
    -   Dizziness: You have experienced dizziness as a side effect.
    -   Fatigue: You have experienced fatigue as a side effect.
    -   Nausea: You have experienced nausea as a side effect.
    -   Other: You have experienced other side effects not listed here.
*   **Medical Check-up** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select how often you receive medical check-ups from the list provided.
    -   Daily: You receive a medical check-up daily.
    -   Weekly: You receive a medical check-up weekly.
    -   Monthly: You receive a medical check-up monthly.
    -   Rarely: You rarely receive a medical check-up.
*   **Patient Dose** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your medication dose from the list provided.
    -   Low: You take a low dose of medication.
    -   Medium: You take a medium dose of medication.
    -   High: You take a high dose of medication.
*   **Medical Profession 2** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select the medical professional who has prescribed your medication from the list provided.
    -   Doctor: The medication was prescribed by a doctor.
    -   Nurse: The medication was prescribed by a nurse.
    -   Pharmacist: The medication was prescribed by a pharmacist.
    -   Other: The medication was prescribed by another medical professional.

## Tips
*   If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your medical professional for guidance.
*   Remember to complete the form accurately and truthfully, as this will help medical professionals to provide the best possible care for you.
*   If you have any additional information that is not covered in this form, please include it in the "Patient Feedback" field.
