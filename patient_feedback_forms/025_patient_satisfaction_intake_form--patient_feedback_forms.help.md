# Patient Satisfaction Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from patients on their experience with our healthcare organization. It's a crucial tool for us to understand areas of improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your name**: This is the name associated with your visit.
   - Type: Text
   - Required: Yes

2. **Feedback**: Share your thoughts about your visit, any issues or concerns you had, or anything else you'd like to mention.
   - Type: Text (Note)
   - Required: No

3. **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of your birth.
   - Type: Date
   - Required: Yes

4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
   - Type: Text
   - Required: No

5. **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
   - Type: Email
   - Required: No

6. **Rating**: Provide a rating for your overall visit experience.
   - Type: Number
   - Required: Yes

7. **Rating Scale**: Choose your rating for the doctor's care.
   - Options: Option 1, Option 2
   - Required: Yes

8. **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of your visit.
   - Type: Date
   - Required: Yes

9. **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of your visit.
   - Type: Time
   - Required: No

10. **Doctor’s Name**: Enter your doctor’s name.
    - Type: Text
    - Required: No

11. **Reason for Visit**: Describe the reason for your visit.
    - Type: Text
    - Required: No

12. **Next Visit Date**: Enter the date for your next visit.
    - Type: Date
    - Required: No

13. **Doctor’s Rating**: Rate the doctor's care.
    - Type: Number
    - Required: No

14. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our services.
    - Type: Number
    - Required: No

15. **Staff Rating**: Rate your experience with our staff.
    - Type: Number
    - Required: No

16. **Facility Rating**: Rate the cleanliness of our facility.
    - Type: Number
    - Required: No

17. **Communication**: Rate your experience with our communication.
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2
    - Required: No

18. **Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of our facility.
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2
    - Required: No

19. **Wait Time**: Rate the wait time during your visit.
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2
    - Required: No

20. **Parking**: Rate the parking experience.
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2
    - Required: No

21. **Overall Experience**: Describe your overall experience.
    - Type: Select One
    - Required: No

22. **Doctor**: Rate your experience with the doctor.
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2
    - Required: No

23. **Staff**: Rate your experience with the staff.
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2
    - Required: No

24. **Facility**: Rate the cleanliness and maintenance of the facility.
    - Options: Option 1, Option 2
    - Required: No
