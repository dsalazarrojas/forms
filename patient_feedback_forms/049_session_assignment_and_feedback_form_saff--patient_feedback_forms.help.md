# Session Assignment And Feedback Form (SAFF) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Session Assignment And Feedback Form (SAFF) is a tool used to assign a therapist to a patient and collect feedback on the session. It is a crucial part of the patient's care process, providing valuable insights for staff members to improve their practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the session details, including date and time.
2. Provide feedback from the patient, including any comments or concerns.
3. Select the assigned therapist who will be working with the patient.
4. Confirm that the form is complete and accurate by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details**: Enter the details of the session, such as the date and time.
* **Patient Feedback**: Provide a description of the patient's feedback, comments, or concerns about the session.
* **Assigned Therapist**: Select the therapist who will be working with the patient from the provided options.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the session in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the session in 24-hour format.
* **Session Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments on the session.
* **Confirm Submit**: Confirm that the form is complete and accurate before submitting.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool used during the session from the provided options.
* **Patient ID**: Select the patient's ID from the provided options.
* **Submit**: Confirm that the form is complete and accurate before submitting.
* **Confirm Submit 2**: This is a redundant field to prevent accidental submissions. Please make sure to fill it accurately before submitting the form.

Please note that fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out before submitting the form. 

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or IT department.
* This form is intended for staff members or authorized personnel only. Do not submit it directly from patients.
