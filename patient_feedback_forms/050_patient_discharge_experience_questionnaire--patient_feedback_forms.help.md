# Patient Discharge Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Patient Discharge Experience Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from patients on their experience with the hospital's care and services. This feedback is crucial to help us understand and improve the quality of care provided to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please select the date of your discharge from the calendar.
2. Enter the time of your discharge.
3. Enter the name of the nurse who cared for you during your stay.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please feel free to write them in the Comments section.
5. For questions 5-9, please select all that apply from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Discharge Date
* **Discharge Date**: The date of your discharge from the hospital.
*   Required: No

### Discharge Time
* **Discharge Time**: The time of your discharge from the hospital.
*   Required: No

### Nurse Name
* **Nurse Name**: The name of the nurse who cared for you during your stay.
*   Required: No

### Discharge Nurse Name
*   This field is a duplicate of the previous question. Please ignore it.
*   Required: No

### Overall Satisfaction
*   **Overall Satisfaction**: Please select all that apply from the options:
    *   Very Satisfied
    *   Somewhat Satisfied
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Dissatisfied
    *   Very Dissatisfied
*   Required: No

### Communication Effectiveness
*   **Communication Effectiveness**: Please select all that apply from the options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   Required: No

### Care Quality
*   **Care Quality**: Please select all that apply from the options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   Required: No

### Experience of Staff
*   **Experience of Staff**: Please select all that apply from the options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   Required: No

### Would Recommend Hospital
*   **Would Recommend Hospital**: Please select all that apply from the options:
    *   Yes
    *   No
    *   Undecided
*   Required: No

### Reasons for Not Recommending
*   **Reasons for Not Recommending**: If you selected 'No' for the previous question, please provide the reasons why.
*   Required: No

### Comments
*   **Comments**: Please enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
*   Required: No

## Tips

*   Please take your time to answer each question carefully, as your feedback is valuable to us.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
