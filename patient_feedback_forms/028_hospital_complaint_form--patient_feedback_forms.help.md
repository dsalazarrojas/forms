# hospital-complaint-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospital-complaint-form is a tool for patients and individuals to report complaints or concerns to the hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of complaint you are submitting (e.g., General, Complaint, Concern, Other).
2. Provide a detailed description of the complaint.
3. Select the person submitting the complaint (e.g., Patient, Complainant).
4. Enter the date and time of the complaint.
5. Enter your contact information (phone number, email, or address).
6. Leave any additional comments you have about the complaint.
7. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Type**: Choose the type of complaint you are submitting. Options include:
	+ Complaint
	+ General
	+ Concern
	+ Other
* **Complaint Description**: Describe the complaint in detail. Be as specific as possible so the hospital can understand the issue.
* **Submitted By**: Choose who is submitting the complaint (Patient or Complainant).
* **Submitted Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the complaint. This will help the hospital understand the timeline of the issue.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your phone number, email, or address so the hospital can contact you for follow-up.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or information about the complaint.
* **Patient Name**: If you are the patient submitting the complaint, enter your name. (Note: This field is not required.)

**Tips**

* Be as detailed and specific as possible when describing the complaint.
* Use this form for non-urgent matters only. If you have an emergency, contact the hospital's emergency number or seek immediate attention from a medical professional.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the hospital's patient relations department.
