# Patient Care Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients about their care experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your complaint.
2. Provide a detailed description of your complaint.
3. Enter the date of the incident or issue.
4. Choose the time the incident occurred.
5. Specify the location where the incident happened.
6. Enter your contact information (e.g., email address).
7. If necessary, attach any relevant documents or images to support your complaint.
8. Add any additional comments or information not covered in the previous questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Details** (`patient_care_complaint_form_1`): Describe the issue or problem you experienced.
* **Category** (`patient_care_complaint_form_2`): Choose the category that best matches your complaint.
	+ General Issue
	+ Service Quality
	+ Provider Interaction
	+ Other
* **Description** (`patient_care_complaint_form_3`): Provide a detailed account of the events leading up to your complaint.
* **Date** (`patient_care_complaint_form_4`): Enter the date of the incident or issue.
* **Time** (`patient_care_complaint_form_5`): Choose the time the incident occurred.
* **Location** (`patient_care_complaint_form_6`): Specify the location where the incident happened.
* **Contact Information** (`patient_care_complaint_form_7`): Enter your email address or other contact information.
* **Attachments** (`patient_care_complaint_form_8`): Upload any relevant documents or images to support your complaint.
* **Additional Comments** (`patient_care_complaint_form_9`): Add any additional information not covered in the previous questions. (Optional)
* **Provider** (`patient_care_complaint_form_10`): Identify the provider (e.g., physician, nurse, or other) involved in the incident.
