# Patient Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient feedback and satisfaction levels with the services received. It helps us understand what our patients think about their experience at our institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Read each page carefully and fill in the required information.
2. For fields with multiple options, select one or more of the options that best fit your experience.
3. For free-text fields, provide a clear and concise description of your experience.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or skip it.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Engagement Survey** (Page 1): This is the title of the form. Please do not fill in anything here.
* **Satisfaction with Services** (Select One, Page 2): How satisfied are you with the services you received? Please select "Satisfied" if you are happy with the services, or "Dissatisfied" if you are not.
* **Experience with Care** (Text, Page 3): Please describe your experience with the care you received in a few sentences.
* **Patient Satisfaction** (Select Multiple, Page 4): What aspects of your experience made you feel satisfied or dissatisfied? Select "Satisfied" for things that went well, and "Dissatisfied" for things that did not go well.
* **Patient Feedback** (Text, Page 5): Please provide any additional feedback or comments you would like to share about your experience.
* **Contact Method** (Select One, Page 6): Would you like to be contacted by us to follow up on your feedback? Select "Yes" if you are willing to be contacted, or "No" if you do not want to be contacted.
* **Email** (Email, Page 7): If you select "Yes" for "Contact Method", please provide your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Text, Page 8): Please provide your phone number if you select "Yes" for "Contact Method".
* **Address** (Note, Page 9): Please provide your address if you select "Yes" for "Contact Method" and would like us to send correspondence to your address.
* **Date** (Date, Page 10): Please provide the date of your visit or interaction with our institution.
* **Time** (Time, Page 11): Please provide the time of your visit or interaction with our institution.
* **Note** (Note, Page 12): Please provide any additional comments or notes you would like to share about your experience.

Note: This form is designed to be completed once you have finished with your experience. If you have any further feedback or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
