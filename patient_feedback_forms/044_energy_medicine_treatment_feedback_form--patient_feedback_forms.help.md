# energyMedicineTreatmentFeedbackForm - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with energy medicine treatment. It's essential to fill out this form carefully to provide valuable insights for improving our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2.  Make sure to provide the correct treatment type if you received a treatment.
3.  Enter the correct treatment date if you received a treatment.
4.  If you didn't receive a treatment, please answer accordingly.
5.  Fill in your contact information (email and phone) if you have a preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your name** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Which treatment did you receive?** (`<treatmenttype>`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of treatment you received from the options provided.
*   **Have you received a treatment?** (`<receivedtreatment>`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "True" if you received a treatment, and "False" if you didn't.
*   **When did you receive the treatment?** (`<receivedtreatmentdate>`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you received the treatment, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **What are your thoughts?** (`<feedback>`, `note`, required: false): Please share your impressions and thoughts about your experience with energy medicine treatment.
*   **Email** (`<email>`, `email`, required: false): If you have a preferred email address, please enter it here.
*   **Phone** (`<phone>`, `text`, required: false): If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it here.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you have any concerns or questions about the form or energy medicine treatment, feel free to contact us.
*   Your feedback is invaluable, and we appreciate the time you take to provide it.
