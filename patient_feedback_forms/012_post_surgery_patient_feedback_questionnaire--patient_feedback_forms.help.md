# Post-Surgery Patient Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients after surgery, allowing healthcare professionals to assess the quality of care provided and make data-driven decisions to improve the patient experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please read each question carefully and answer it as accurately as possible.
2. For questions marked as "required", please provide a complete answer.
3. For questions marked as "optional", you can choose not to answer if you don't have any additional comments or suggestions.
4. Use the provided options for rating and select multiple questions, if applicable.
5. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Feedback**: Please describe your overall experience during and after surgery. This question is required.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Please rate your satisfaction with the care you received. Choose one of the following options:
	+ 5: Excellent
	+ 4: Very good
	+ 3: Average
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 1: Very poor
* **Follow-up Services**: Please select all the follow-up services you received or would like to receive:
	+ Regular Follow-up Phone Calls
	+ Text Messaging
	+ Email Communication
	+ Mail Communication
* **Communication Quality**: Please rate the quality of communication you received from your healthcare team. This question is required.
* **Overall Experience**: Please describe your overall experience during and after surgery. This question is required.
* **Recommendations**: Please provide any suggestions or recommendations for improvement. This question is optional.
* **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or concerns you have. This question is optional.
* **Contact Person**: Please provide the name and title of the contact person who was responsible for your care. This question is required.
* **Email**: Please provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up or questions. This question is optional.
* **Phone**: Please provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up or questions. This question is optional.
