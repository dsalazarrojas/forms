# Patient Case Review Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Case Review Acknowledgement Form is a tool for patients to provide feedback on their case review experience. It helps healthcare providers understand how well they have communicated with the patient, how clear the case review process was, and what areas for improvement are needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. For each question, select the most accurate option that reflects your experience.
3. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Has your case been reviewed?** (`patient_case_reviewed`, Select One, optional): This question checks if your case has been reviewed by a healthcare provider. Choose "Case Review" if it has been reviewed.
* **Have you given feedback on your case?** (`feedback_given`, Select One, optional): This question checks if you have provided feedback on your case review experience. Choose "Given" if you have given feedback.
* **How would you rate the case review process?** (`case_review_process`, Select One, optional): This question asks you to rate how well the case review process was conducted. Choose "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" based on your experience.
* **How clear were you about the case review process?** (`communication_clear`, Select One, optional): This question asks you how clear the case review process was for you. Choose "Clear", "Mostly Clear", or "Not Clear" based on your understanding.
* **How clear were your provider's communications during the case review?** (`provider_communication`, Select One, optional): This question asks you how clear your healthcare provider's communications were during the case review. Choose "Clear", "Mostly Clear", or "Not Clear" based on your understanding.
* **How long did the case review take?** (`case_review_timeframe`, Select One, optional): This question asks you how long the case review process took. Choose "Less than 1 day", "1-2 days", "3-4 days", or "More than 4 days" based on your experience.
* **Was your provider present during the review?** (`provider_attendance`, Select One, optional): This question asks if your healthcare provider was present during the case review. Choose "True" if they were present.
* **Was feedback requested?** (`feedback_requested`, Select One, optional): This question asks if feedback was requested from you during the case review. Choose "True" if feedback was requested.
* **Was the case review conducted?** (`case_review_conducted`, Select One, optional): This question asks if the case review was actually conducted. Choose "True" if it was conducted.
* **Were you understood?** (`patient_understanding`, Select One, optional): This question asks if you were understood by your healthcare provider during the case review. Choose "True" if you were understood.
* **Has your case been closed?** (`case_closed`, Select One, optional): This question asks if your case has been closed. Choose "True" if it has been closed.
* **What status of your case?** (`case_status`, Select Multiple, optional): This question asks for the current status of your case. Choose all that apply: "Active", "Closed", or "Discharged".
* **What priority of your case?** (`case_priority`, Select Multiple, optional): This question asks for the priority of your case. Choose all that apply: "High", "Low", or "Medium".
* **What type of case?** (`case_type`, Select Multiple, optional): This question asks for the type of your case. Choose all that apply: "New" or "Old".
* **What status of your patient?** (`patient_status`, Select Multiple, optional): This question asks for your current patient status. Choose all that apply: "Alive" or "Dead".
* **What hospitalization status of your patient?** (`hospitalization_status`, Select Multiple, optional): This question asks for your patient's hospitalization status. Choose all that apply: "Inpatient" or "Outpatient".
* **What is your age?** (`patient_age`, Number, optional): This question asks for your age. Enter your age in number.
* **What is your gender?** (`patient_gender`, Select Multiple, optional): This question asks for your gender. Choose all that apply: "Male", "Female", or "Other".
* **What is your date of birth?** (`patient_date_of_birth`, Date, optional): This question asks for your date of birth. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What is your last visit date?** (`patient_date_of_last_visit`, Date, optional): This question asks for your last visit date. Enter your last visit date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What is your last visit reason?** (`patient_last_visit_reason`, Text, optional): This question asks for the reason of your last visit. Enter a brief description of your reason.
* **What is your last visit diagnosis?** (`patient_last_visit_diagnosis`, Text, optional): This question asks for your last visit diagnosis. Enter a brief description of your diagnosis.
* **What are case review goals?** (`case_review_goals`, Text, optional): This question asks for the goals of the case review process. Enter a brief description of your goals.
* **Provider comment?** (`provider_comment`, Text, optional): This question asks for any comments from your healthcare provider. Enter a brief description of their comments.
* **Case review acknowledgement?** (`case_review_acknowledgement`, Select One, optional): This question asks if you acknowledge that the case review was conducted. Choose "True" if you acknowledge.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider.
* Feedback is always appreciated and will help improve the case review process.
