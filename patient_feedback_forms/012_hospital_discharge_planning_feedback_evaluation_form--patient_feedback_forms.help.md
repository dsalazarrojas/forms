# Hospital Discharge Planning Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Discharge Planning Feedback Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from patients about their experience during the hospital discharge planning process. This information helps us understand what we are doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall experience with us on page 2.
2. Share your thoughts about what you found most useful in the discharge planning experience on page 3.
3. If you have any additional comments, please provide them on page 4.
4. On page 5, select the contact information you would like us to use to follow up with you.
5. Rate our communication style on page 6.
6. Finally, provide the time you spent with our healthcare team and the time you spent at the hospital on pages 7 and 8, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Discharge Planning Form** (Discharge Planning Form, text, required/optional): This form asks for a brief description of your experience during the hospital discharge planning process.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (Overall Experience Rating, select_one, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with us, using the scale 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What did you find most useful in this discharge planning experience?** (What did you find most useful in this discharge planning experience?, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts on what you found most useful during the discharge planning experience.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the contact information you would like us to use to follow up with you, such as phone, email, or address.
* **How would you rate our communication style?** (How would you rate our communication style?, select_one, required/optional): Rate our communication style, using the scale very effective, somewhat effective, or not effective.
* **Time Spent with the Healthcare Team** (Time Spent with the Healthcare Team, number, required/optional): Provide the number of hours you spent with our healthcare team.
* **Time Spent at Hospital** (Time Spent at Hospital, number, required/optional): Provide the number of hours you spent at the hospital.
