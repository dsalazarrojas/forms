**patient_support_portal_functionality_evaluation - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
------------

The `patient_support_portal_functionality_evaluation` form is a collection of questions that aim to gather feedback on the functionality and usability of our patient support portal. This form helps us evaluate the effectiveness of our support services and identify areas of improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete the form and provide your honest feedback.

**How To Complete This Form**
-------------------------

1. Review each page carefully and select the most relevant options for each question.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Be as specific as possible in your answers.
4. If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular question, feel free to ask.
