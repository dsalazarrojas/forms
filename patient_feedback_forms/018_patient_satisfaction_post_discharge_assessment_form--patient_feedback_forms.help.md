# Patient Satisfaction Post Discharge Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess patient satisfaction with their care after discharge from the hospital or medical facility. It helps healthcare providers understand the patient's perception of their stay, identify areas of improvement, and ensure high-quality care. Completing this form ensures that we receive valuable feedback, which we will use to make necessary adjustments to our services and improve patient care.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in the patient's name in the "patient name" field.
3. Provide the patient's contact information in the "contact information" field.
4. Enter the date the patient was discharged from the hospital in the "date discharged" field.
5. Enter the date this satisfaction survey was completed in the "date survey" field.
6. Rate your level of satisfaction with your care by selecting one of the satisfaction scores in the "satisfaction score" field.
7. If you are unhappy with any aspect of your care, please explain the reason in the "reason unhappy" field.
8. If you have any suggestions or comments about your care, please add them in the "suggestion" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text, optional): This is the patient's name.
* **contact_info** (text, optional): Provide your contact information, including phone number and/or email address.
* **date_discharged** (date, optional): Enter the date you were discharged from the hospital.
* **date_survey** (date, optional): Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **satisfaction_score** (select_one, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with your care:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **reason_unhappy** (text, optional): If you are unhappy with any aspect of your care, please explain the reason.
* **suggestion** (text, optional): If you have any suggestions or comments about your care, please add them here.
* **patient_id** (text, optional): Enter the patient's ID.
* **Additional Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns you may have about your care.
