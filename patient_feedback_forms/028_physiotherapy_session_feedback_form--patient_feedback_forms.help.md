# Physiotherapy Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients regarding their experience with a physiotherapist. It helps the physiotherapist evaluate and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Fill in the required fields (if any) to provide relevant information about your physiotherapy session.
3. You can skip any questions that don't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physiotherapy Session Feedback Form (1)** (`physiotherapy_session_feedback_form`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback about your physiotherapy session. Be as detailed as possible to help the physiotherapist improve their services.
* **Patient Experience (2)** (`patient_experience`, text, optional): Describe your overall experience during the physiotherapy session. This will help the physiotherapist understand how they can improve your care.
* **Therapist (3)** (`therapist`, text, optional): Mention your physiotherapist's name or their identifier (if applicable). This will help the physiotherapist track your feedback specifically to them.
* **Communication (4)** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of communication you experienced during the session. Choose from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Overall Satisfaction (5)** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session. Choose from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Date of Session (6)** (`date_of_session`, date, optional): If you remember the date of your physiotherapy session, please provide it. This will help the physiotherapist track the timing of your feedback.
* **Time of Session (7)** (`time_of_session`, time, optional): If you remember the time of your physiotherapy session, please provide it. This will help the physiotherapist track the timing of your feedback.
* **Notes (8)** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your physiotherapy session. This is a free text field for any information you think is relevant.
* **Email (9)** (`email`, email, optional): If you have a contact email address, please provide it. This will help the physiotherapist contact you for any follow-up discussions.
* **Phone (10)** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a contact phone number, please provide it. This will help the physiotherapist contact you for any follow-up discussions.

## Tips
* Be honest and clear in your feedback. This will help the physiotherapist improve their services.
* You can skip any questions that don't apply to you or your physiotherapy experience.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact the physiotherapist or their administration for assistance.
