# Patient Exit Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Patient Exit Survey" form is a tool used to collect patient feedback on their experience at the medical facility. This form is designed to gather information on various aspects of their visit, including their reason for visiting, primary diagnosis, symptoms, doctor's name, visit duration, and satisfaction with the care they received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the blanks with your honest feedback about your visit.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
3. Select the options that best describe your experience for multiple-choice questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Exit Survey**: This is the title of the form.
* **What was your reason for visiting?** (`patient_exit_survey`, text, optional): Please provide the reason why you visited the medical facility.
* **What was your primary diagnosis?** (`question_1`, text, optional): Please describe your primary diagnosis.
* **What were your symptoms?** (`question_2`, text, optional): Please list your symptoms.
* **Who was your doctor?** (`question_3`, text, optional): Please provide the name of your doctor.
* **What was the length of your visit?** (`question_4`, number, optional): Please enter the duration of your visit in minutes or hours.
* **Were your medical needs met?** (`question_6`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **True**: Your medical needs were met.
	+ **False**: Your medical needs were not met.
	+ **Not Applicable**: Not applicable to your visit.
* **Were your medical needs met (fully)?** (`question_7`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Completely**: Your medical needs were fully met.
	+ **Mostly**: Your medical needs were mostly met.
	+ **Somewhat**: Your medical needs were somewhat met.
	+ **Not at all**: Your medical needs were not met at all.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction?** (`question_8`, number, optional): Please rate your satisfaction on a scale.
* **What did you like most?** (`question_9`, text, optional): Please describe what you liked most about your visit.
* **What did you like least?** (`question_10`, text, optional): Please describe what you liked least about your visit.
* **Any additional comments?** (`question_11`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **How long did you wait?** (`question_12`, time, optional): Please enter the duration of your wait time.
* **Were your medical needs met satisfaction?** (`question_13`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **True**: Your medical needs were met.
	+ **False**: Your medical needs were not met.
	+ **Not Applicable**: Not applicable to your visit.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough when completing the form.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, please use the "Any additional comments" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the medical facility.
