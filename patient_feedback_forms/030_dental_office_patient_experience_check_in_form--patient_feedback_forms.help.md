# Dental Office Patient Experience Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dental Office Patient Experience Check In Form is designed to collect information about your experience with our dental office. This includes details about your appointment, feedback, and suggestions for service improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your **Patient Name**, which is the name of the person filling out the form.
2. Provide your **Patient Phone Number**, which is the phone number of the person filling out the form.
3. Enter your **Patient Email**, which is the email address of the person filling out the form.
4. For **Appointment Info**, describe your appointment details, such as the date, time, and purpose of your visit.
5. Check the **Appointment Status** box to indicate whether your appointment is Active or Inactive.
6. Choose a **Visit Date**, which is the date of your visit.
7. Select a **Visit Time**, which is the time of your visit.
8. Enter the **Visit Duration**, which is the length of your visit.
9. If you have a **Next Visit Date**, enter the date of your next visit.
10. If you have a **Next Visit Time**, select the time of your next visit.
11. Enter any **Staff Comments**, which are comments about the staff's performance.
12. Enter any **Patient Comments**, which are your personal thoughts about your experience.
13. If you have any **Patient Signature**, please sign below.
14. Enter the **Date**, which is the current date.
15. Enter the **Time**, which is the current time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Patient Phone**: Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Patient Email**: Please enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Appointment Info**: Describe your appointment details, such as the date, time, and purpose of your visit.
* **Appointment Status**: Check the box to indicate whether your appointment is Active or Inactive.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Visit Time**: Select the time of your visit.
* **Visit Duration**: Enter the length of your visit.
* **Next Visit Date**: If you have a next visit scheduled, enter the date.
* **Next Visit Time**: If you have a next visit scheduled, select the time.
* **Staff Comments**: Enter any comments about the staff's performance.
* **Patient Comments**: Enter any personal comments about your experience.
* **Patient Signature**: Please sign below if you're the patient.
* **Date**: Enter the current date.
* **Time**: Enter the current time.
* **Doctor Name**: Not required to be filled out, but if you know the name of your doctor, please enter it.
* **Doctor Phone**: Not required to be filled out, but if you know your doctor's phone number, please enter it.
* **Doctor Email**: Not required to be filled out, but if you know your doctor's email address, please enter it.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask the staff for clarification.
* This form is for your convenience and is used to improve our service, so please be as detailed as possible.
* Please note that your feedback and comments are confidential and will not be shared without your consent.
