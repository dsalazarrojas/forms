# Psychological Therapies Hub Group Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients about their therapy or counseling sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Name` field.
2. Enter your email in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Provide feedback about your therapy or counseling session in the `Feedback` field.
5. Rate the effectiveness of the session using the `Rating` field or select from the `Rating Scale` dropdown menu.
6. Select the date and time of the session using the `Date` and `Time` fields.
7. Add any additional notes about the session in the `Note` field.
8. Select multiple items from the `Select Multiple` dropdown menu.

* **Name (name)**: Enter your name to help us identify you.

* **Rating (rating)**: Rate your session using a number from 1-10 or select from the rating scale.
* **Rating Scale (rating_scale)**: Select from "High" or "Low" to rate your session.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date you attended your therapy or counseling session.
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time you attended your session.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your session.
* **Select Multiple (select_multiple)**: Select one or more items from the list to provide more detailed feedback.
