> # Best Patient Care Team Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Patient Care Team Nomination Form is designed to gather feedback from patients on their experience with the care team assigned to them. This feedback will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal information:
	* **Full name**: Enter your legal full name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
	* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code if you're outside the local region.
2. Select the relevant date:
	* **Date**: Choose the date that is relevant to your experience with the care team.
3. Rate your experience:
	* **Primary selection**: Choose the option that best applies to your experience with the care team.
	* **Quantity or rating**: If applicable, enter a whole number to quantify or rate your experience.
4. Confirm your agreement:
	* **Please confirm agreement**: Select all that apply from the following options: "I agree", "I need follow up", "Not applicable"
5. Add any additional information:
	* **Additional details**: Provide any additional relevant information about your experience.
6. Enter estimated value:
	* **Estimated value**: If applicable, enter a decimal value to represent the value of your experience.
7. Enter preferred time:
	* **Preferred time**: Enter a time if scheduling is required.
8. Final comments:
	* **Final comments or notes**: Enter any final comments or notes about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number, including the country code if you're outside the local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, date, **required**): Choose the date that is relevant to your experience with the care team.
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, select_one, **required**): Choose the option that best applies to your experience with the care team.
	+ **Excellent**: I was extremely satisfied with the care team.
	+ **Good**: The care team was satisfactory.
	+ **Fair**: The care team was average.
	+ **Poor**: I was dissatisfied with the care team.
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, number, **optional**): If applicable, enter a whole number to quantify or rate your experience.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional relevant information about your experience.
* **Please confirm agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all that apply from the following options: "I agree", "I need follow up", "Not applicable"
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, **optional**): If applicable, enter a decimal value to represent the value of your experience.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, **optional**): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, text, **optional**): Enter any final comments or notes about your experience.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to help us improve our care team.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback on your experience with our care team.
