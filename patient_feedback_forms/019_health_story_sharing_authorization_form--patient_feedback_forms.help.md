# Health Story Sharing Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Story Sharing Authorization Form is a patient feedback form designed to collect your consent for sharing your health story with organizations, family, friends, and healthcare providers. This form helps us understand your preferences for sharing your health story and ensures that we respect your boundaries while sharing your sensitive information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Give your consent by typing "yes" in the **Health Story Sharing Authorization Form**.
2. Select how you would like to share your health story by choosing one or multiple options in the **Select Your Options** field. You can choose from the following options:
	* Share with organizations
	* Share with family
	* Share with friends
	* Share with healthcare providers
	* Contact information
	* Email
	* Phone
3. Confirm your choices by typing "confirm" in the **Confirm** field.
4. Submit your form by typing "submit" in the **Submit** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Story Sharing Authorization Form** (`Health Story Sharing Authorization Form`, `text`, required)
	+ This field requires you to type "yes" to give your consent for sharing your health story.
* **Select Your Options** (`Select Your Options`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ This field allows you to choose how you would like to share your health story. Select one or multiple options that best fit your preferences.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, `text`, required)
	+ This field is for you to confirm your choices by typing "confirm".
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required)
	+ This field is for you to submit your form after confirming your choices.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you've selected all the options that accurately reflect your preferences for sharing your health story.
* Double-check your choices before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
