# Patient Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather patient feedback about their experience at the clinic. The feedback will help us to identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to provide better care and services to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the option that best describes your experience for each of the questions below.
2. Fill in the numerical answer for the question about the waiting time.
3. Make sure to submit the form after completing all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the length of time you waited in the waiting room? (Number 1)**: Please enter the actual amount of time you waited, in minutes.
* **How would you rate the doctor's care and professionalism? (2)**: Please select 1 (Very Poor), 2 (Somewhat Poor), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Good), or 5 (Excellent) to describe your experience with the doctor.
* **How would you rate the overall service provided? (3)**: Please select 1 (Very Poor), 2 (Somewhat Poor), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Good), or 5 (Excellent) to describe your experience with the clinic's service.
* **What was the cleanliness of the clinic's environment? (4)**: Please select Very Clean, Somewhat Clean, Somewhat Unclean, Not Very Clean, or Not Clean At All to describe your experience with the clinic's environment.
* **Was the clinic's environment well-maintained? (5)**: Please select True or False to indicate whether the clinic's environment was well-maintained.
* **How would you rate the doctor's friendliness? (6)**: Please select 1 (Very Poor), 2 (Somewhat Poor), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Good), or 5 (Excellent) to describe your experience with the doctor's friendliness.
* **How easy was the appointment scheduling process? (7)**: Please select Very Easy, Somewhat Easy, Neutral, Somewhat Difficult, or Very Difficult to describe your experience with the appointment scheduling process.
* **How would you rate the doctor's communication skills? (8)**: Please select 1 (Very Poor), 2 (Somewhat Poor), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Good), or 5 (Excellent) to describe your experience with the doctor's communication skills.
* **How would you rate the doctor's listening skills? (9)**: Please select 1 (Very Poor), 2 (Somewhat Poor), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Good), or 5 (Excellent) to describe your experience with the doctor's listening skills.
* **How would you rate the doctor's diagnosis? (10)**: Please select 1 (Very Poor), 2 (Somewhat Poor), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Good), or 5 (Excellent) to describe your experience with the doctor's diagnosis.
* **How would you rate the doctor's treatment of your condition? (11)**: Please select 1 (Very Poor), 2 (Somewhat Poor), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Good), or 5 (Excellent) to describe your experience with the doctor's treatment.
* **What was your overall experience with the clinic? (12)**: Please select 1 (Very Poor), 2 (Somewhat Poor), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Good), or 5 (Excellent) to describe your overall experience with the clinic.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide feedback that is specific to your experience.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, feel free to mention them in the comments section.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
