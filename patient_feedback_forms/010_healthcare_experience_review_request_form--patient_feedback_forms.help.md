# Healthcare Experience Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather patient feedback on their experience with the healthcare office or staff they visited.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, select the page that best describes your feedback (e.g., feedback on the office, staff, or services). If applicable, select multiple options. Enter the date of your visit and provide any additional comments or ratings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Feedback
*   **Patient Feedback**: Select one or more options to describe your feedback on the office, staff, or services. You can choose from:
    + Feedback on our office
    + Feedback on our staff
    + Feedback on our services

### Visit Date
*   **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your visit to help us track your feedback over time.

### Staff Name
*   **Staff Name**: If you'd like to provide feedback on a specific staff member, type their name.

### Rating
*   **Rating**: Rate your overall experience out of 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### Comments
*   **Comments**: If you'd like to add more details about your feedback, feel free to type them here.

### Submit
*   **Submit**: Select "Submit" to send your feedback, or "Cancel" to close the form without saving.
