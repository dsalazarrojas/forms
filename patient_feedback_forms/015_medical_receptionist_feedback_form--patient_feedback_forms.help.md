# Medical Receptionist Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Receptionist Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with the front desk staff at our clinic. This form will help us understand how our receptionists are performing and identify areas where we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the options for each question and provide your email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Front Desk Staff** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Please indicate if you have interacted with the front desk staff during your visit. Select all that apply from the options below.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Professionalism** (Select One, Required: Yes): Rate the professionalism of the front desk staff on a scale from 1 to 3.
	+ Option 1: Very Professional
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Professional
	+ Option 3: Not Professional
* **Communication** (Select One, Required: Yes): Rate the communication skills of the front desk staff on a scale from 1 to 3.
	+ Option 1: Very Good
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Good
	+ Option 3: Not Good
* **Reception Experience** (Text, Required: No): Provide any additional comments about your experience with the front desk.
* **Comments** (Text, Required: No): If you have any further comments or suggestions about your visit, please provide them here.
* **Email** (Email, Required: Yes): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required: No): If you have any questions or need to be contacted, please provide your phone number.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve our services.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, please contact us separately to discuss them.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback!
