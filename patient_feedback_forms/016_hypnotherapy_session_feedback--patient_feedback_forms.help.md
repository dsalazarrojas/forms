# Hypnotherapy Session Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on the hypnotherapy sessions you've attended. Your input will help us understand how well the sessions met your needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Select only the options that apply to your experience.
3. Fill in any text fields completely.
4. Submit your feedback when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (1: text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the hypnotherapy session. This can include your thoughts, feelings, or any significant events that occurred during the session.
* **Overall Experience** (2: number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the session, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Session Effectiveness** (3: number, optional): Assess how effective you found the session in achieving its intended goals, where 1 is not effective and 5 is highly effective.
* **Therapist Care** (4: select_one, optional): Choose one of the options that best describes how you felt cared for during the session.
	+ Option 1: Very good
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Fair
	+ Option 4: Not at all
* **Communication** (5: select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to how you felt the communication between you and the therapist was.
	+ Option 1: Clear and concise
	+ Option 2: Confusing at times
	+ Option 3: Not clear enough
* **Relaxation Response** (6: date, optional): If you experienced a significant relaxation response during the session, provide the date and time it occurred.
* **Sleep Quality** (7: select_multiple, optional): Choose all the options that apply to the quality of your sleep after the session.
	+ Option 1: Improved
	+ Option 2: No change
	+ Option 3: Deteriorated
* **Anxiety Level** (8: select_one, optional): Select one of the options that best describes how you felt your anxiety level was after the session.
	+ Option 1: Much reduced
	+ Option 2: Slightly reduced
	+ Option 3: No change
* **Physical Discomfort** (9: select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your physical discomfort during or after the session.
	+ Option 1: None
	+ Option 2: Minimal
	+ Option 3: Moderate
* **Recommendations** (10: text, optional): If you have any suggestions or recommendations for improving the hypnotherapy sessions, please provide them here.
