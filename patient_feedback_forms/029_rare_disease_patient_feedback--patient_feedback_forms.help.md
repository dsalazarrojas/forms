# Rare Disease Patient Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients on their experiences with rare diseases, focusing on symptom management, treatment challenges, and overall support satisfaction. Your feedback will help us understand how we can better support individuals affected by these conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the Rare Disease Name field with the name of the disease you are experiencing.
2. Select up to two symptom management options that apply to your current situation:
	* Pain
	* Fatigue
	* Nausea
3. Choose one option for the treatment challenges you are facing:
	* Side effects
	* Cost
	* Access
4. Rate your satisfaction with the support you received:
	* Very good
	* Somewhat good
	* Not very good
	* Not good at all
5. If you have additional comments or concerns, please provide them in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rare Disease Name** (`disease_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the rare disease you are experiencing.
* **Symptom Management** (`symptom_management`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to two options that apply to your current situation. Options include:
	+ Pain: Are you experiencing pain as a symptom?
	+ Fatigue: Are you experiencing fatigue as a symptom?
	+ Nausea: Are you experiencing nausea as a symptom?
* **Treatment Challenges** (`treatment_challenges`, select_one, optional): Choose one option that best describes your current treatment challenges:
	+ Side effects: Are you experiencing side effects from treatment?
	+ Cost: Is cost a significant issue for you in accessing treatment?
	+ Access: Are you having difficulty accessing treatment?
* **Support Satisfaction** (`support_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the support you have received:
	+ Very good: You are highly satisfied with the support.
	+ Somewhat good: You are somewhat satisfied with the support.
	+ Not very good: You are somewhat dissatisfied with the support.
	+ Not good at all: You are highly dissatisfied with the support.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve our support services.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to us directly.
* Your feedback is appreciated and will be kept confidential.
* You can submit multiple pages of feedback if needed, but please ensure each page is complete before submitting.
