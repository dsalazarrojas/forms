# hospital_discharge_communication_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The hospital discharge communication form is designed to facilitate communication between the hospital and the patient at the time of discharge. It helps in gathering necessary information about the patient, their medical condition, and their contact details for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have access to the necessary information about the patient and their discharge details.
2. Fill in the form with the required information.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name**: The patient's full name.
* **patient_date_of_birth**: The patient's date of birth.
* **medical_condition**: A brief description of the patient's medical condition at the time of discharge.
* **patient_medical_record_number**: The patient's medical record number.
* **patient_contact_phone**: The patient's contact phone number.
* **patient_contact_email**: The patient's contact email address.
* **patient_address**: The patient's current address.
* **discharge_date**: The date of the patient's discharge.
* **discharge_time**: The time of the patient's discharge.
* **hospital_name**: The name of the hospital where the patient was discharged.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the hospital administration for clarification.
* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and completely before submission.
