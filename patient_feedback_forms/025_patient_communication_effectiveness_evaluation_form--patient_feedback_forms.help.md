# Patient Communication Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from patients on their experience with care they received. It will help us understand how effectively our care team communicated with you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name.
2. Select the type of care you received today (General, Surgical, Medical, or Other).
3. Enter the time you received care (in 24-hour or 12-hour format, e.g., 08:00 or 8:00 AM).
4. Select the date you received care (in MM/dd/yyyy format, e.g., 01/01/2022).
5. Rate your overall experience (1-5).
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Provide any additional notes about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (form_2_name, text, required): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **What type of care did you receive today?** (form_3_title, select_one, required): Select the type of care you received today from the options provided.
* **Time** (form_4_time, time, required): Enter the time you received care in 24-hour or 12-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 8:00 AM).
* **Date** (form_5_date, date, required): Enter the date you received care in MM/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Rating** (form_6_rating, number, required): Rate your overall experience (1-5).
* **Email** (form_7_email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (form_8_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (form_9_note, note, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
