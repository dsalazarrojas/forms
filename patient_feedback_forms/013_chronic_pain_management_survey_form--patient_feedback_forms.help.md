# Chronic Pain Management Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Pain Management Survey Form is designed to collect patient feedback on their current treatment plan and interactions with healthcare providers. This feedback is crucial for healthcare professionals to better understand patient needs, improve care quality, and optimize treatment outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill in your name (optional, if you do not feel comfortable providing your name). Please note that your responses are confidential and for treatment improvement purposes only.
2.  Enter the date of this survey.
3.  Select the current treatment method you are currently using.
4.  Rate the level of pain reduction you have experienced from your treatment (0-100%).
5.  Indicate how often you experience side effects from your treatment.
6.  Describe any side effects you are experiencing.
7.  Choose the level of quality of life improvement you have experienced since starting your treatment.
8.  Indicate how often your provider listens to your concerns.
9.  Select whether your provider clearly explains treatment options to you.
10. Indicate how easy or difficult it is to schedule follow-up appointments with your provider.
11.  Specify your main functional goal for the next 3 months.
12.  Provide any additional comments or feedback for your medical team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name (Optional)** (`patient_name_optional`, text, optional)
    Enter your name, if you are comfortable sharing it. This is optional and for treatment improvement purposes only.
*   **Date of Survey** (`survey_date`, date, required)
    Enter the date of this survey.
*   **Current Primary Treatment Method** (`current_treatment_plan`, text, required)
    Select the primary treatment method you are currently using.
*   **Rate the Level of Pain Reduction** (`pain_reduction_rating`, select_one, required)
    Rate the level of pain reduction you have experienced from your treatment, from 0% to 100%.
*   **How Often Do You Experience Side Effects?** (`side_effects_frequency`, select_one, required)
    Indicate how often you experience side effects from your treatment.
*   **Describe Any Side Effects** (`side_effects_description`, text, optional)
    Describe any side effects you are experiencing.
*   **Has Your Quality of Life Improved?** (`quality_of_life_improvement`, select_one, required)
    Choose the level of quality of life improvement you have experienced since starting treatment.
*   **Provider Listening** (`provider_listening`, select_one, required)
    Indicate how often your provider listens to your concerns.
*   **Treatment Options Explained** (`treatment_options_explained`, select_one, required)
    Select whether your provider clearly explains treatment options to you.
*   **Ease of Scheduling Follow-up Appointments** (`ease_of_scheduling`, select_one, required)
    Indicate how easy or difficult it is to schedule follow-up appointments with your provider.
*   **Primary Functional Goal for the Next 3 Months** (`primary_functional_goal`, text, required)
    Specify your main functional goal for the next 3 months.
*   **Any Other Comments for Our Medical Team?** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional)
    Provide any additional comments or feedback for your medical team.

**Tips**

*   Please answer each question honestly.
*   Ensure accuracy in your responses.
*   If you have questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
*   Your feedback is crucial to improving your care.
*   You can contact <form@gic.mx> for any further assistance.
*   All information collected is confidential and for treatment improvement purposes only.
