# patient_advocacy_program_quotation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The patient advocacy program quotation form is used to capture essential information about services provided to patients, including patient and provider details, services rendered, and agreements or quotes. It's a crucial document for patient advocacy programs to maintain accurate records and ensure that patients receive the best care possible.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, please ensure you have all the necessary information and resources ready.

1. **Program Quotation**: In this field, please enter a brief description of the services provided to the patient. This may include any specific treatments, consultations, or interventions performed.
2. **Patient Information**: Select all the relevant information that applies to the patient, such as:
	* **Patient Name**
	* **Date of Birth**
	* **Contact Information** (e.g., phone number, email)
3. **Provider Information**: Select the type of provider who provided services to the patient:
	* **Primary Care Physician**
	* **Specialty Care Physician**
	* **Other** (if none of the above apply)
4. **Services Provided**: Select all the services provided to the patient, such as:
	* **Service 1**
	* **Service 2**
	* **Service 3**
5. **Service Quote**: Enter the date on which the services were provided.
6. **Additional Information**: If there's anything else you'd like to add or mention, please include it here. This field is optional.
7. **Patient Signature**: Sign your name as the patient, indicating that you've reviewed and agree with the provided information.
8. **Provider Signature**: The provider who rendered services signs their name, indicating that they've provided the services as described.
9. **Date**: Enter the date on which the services were provided.
10. **Time**: Enter the time at which the services were provided.
11. **Email**: Enter the provider's email address.
12. **Phone**: Enter the provider's phone number.
13. **Signature**: Sign your name as the provider, indicating that you've provided the services as described.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all the necessary permissions and approvals before completing this form.
* Double-check the accuracy of your input to avoid errors.
* If you need assistance or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the patient advocacy program staff.
