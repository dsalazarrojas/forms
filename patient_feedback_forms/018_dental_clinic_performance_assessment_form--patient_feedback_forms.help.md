# Dental Clinic Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their experience at the dental clinic. It's a tool to help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer based on your recent experience at the clinic.
2. Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 10, unless otherwise indicated.
3. Select the best response from the options provided for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the dental clinic performance assessment form** (Page 1): This page is a general introduction to the form.
* **How would you rate the quality of our staff?** (Page 2): Rate the quality of our staff on a scale from 1 to 10. This will help us understand how well we're meeting our staff's expectations.
* **How would you rate the communication with our staff?** (Page 3): Rate the communication with our staff on a scale from 1 to 10. This will help us understand how effective our communication is.
* **How would you rate the cleanliness of the clinic?** (Page 4): Select how clean or not clean you found the clinic to be. Options: Very Clean, Somewhat Clean, Not Clean.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of your appointment scheduling experience?** (Page 5): Select the timing of your appointment schedule. Options: On time, Early, Late.
* **How would you rate the clarity of our billing and financials?** (Page 6): Select how clear or not clear our billing and financials were. Options: Clear, Somewhat Clear, Not Clear.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of our pain management?** (Page 7): Select the effectiveness of our pain management. Options: Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Effective.
* **What was your overall experience at the dental clinic?** (Page 8): Share your thoughts on your overall experience at the clinic.
* **Would you return to our clinic in the future?** (Page 9): Select whether you would consider coming back to the clinic in the future. Options: True, False, Possibly.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Rate your answers based on your genuine experience.
- Don't worry about being too nice or too critical. We value your feedback and want to improve our service to you!
