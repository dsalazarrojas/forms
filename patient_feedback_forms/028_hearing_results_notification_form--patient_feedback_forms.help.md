# Hearing Results Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information related to patient feedback on hearing results. Its purpose is to provide a structured way to record and analyze patient responses, enabling healthcare professionals to make informed decisions and improve patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Select the date of birth of the patient.
3. Provide the patient's contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
4. Enter any test results in the "Test Results" section.
5. If the test results are abnormal, select "True" in the "Abnormal Test Results" field.
6. Schedule the next appointment in the "Next Appointment" section, if applicable.
7. Provide any medical history, recent symptoms, or treatments received in the "Medical History", "Recent Symptoms", and "Medical Treatment History" sections.
8. List any allergies or sensitivities in the "Allergies or Sensitivities" section.
9. Select the outcome of the test in the "Test Outcomes" field.
10. Review and confirm that the form is complete and accurate by checking the "Is this form complete and accurate?" checkbox.
11. Add any additional information in the "Additional Information" section.
12. Finally, review the form and submit it to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, ID, or any other identifying information.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact details, such as phone number and email.
- **Test Results**: Enter the results of the test, including any abnormal or critical results.
- **Abnormal Test Results**: Select "True" if the test results are abnormal.
- **Next Appointment**: Schedule the next appointment date, if applicable.
- **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history or previous conditions.
- **Recent Symptoms**: List any recent symptoms or complaints.
- **Medical Treatment History**: Record any recent medical treatments or interventions.
- **Allergies or Sensitivities**: List any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have.
- **Test Outcomes**: Select the outcome of the test (Normal, Abnormal, or Inconclusive).
- **Is this form complete and accurate?**: Confirm that the form is complete and accurate.
- **Additional Information**: Add any additional comments or details about the patient's condition.
- **Review and Submit**: Review the form and submit it to confirm completion.
