# MRI Procedure Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MRI Procedure Feedback Form is used to gather patient feedback on their experience with the MRI procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your feedback as a patient on the MRI procedure you experienced.
2. Select the date of the procedure you are providing feedback on.
3. Provide the name of the radiologist who performed the procedure.
4. Provide the name of the technologist who assisted with the procedure.
5. Share any additional comments or concerns you have about your experience.
6. Rate your satisfaction with the procedure (Excellent, Good, Neutral, Poor, Very Poor, or Not Satisfactory).
7. Select any rating(s) for the procedure (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
8. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Feedback**: Enter your feedback as a patient on the MRI procedure you experienced. This is a free-text field where you can express your thoughts and opinions.
* **Procedure Date**: Enter the date of the procedure you are providing feedback on. This will help us track feedback for each procedure.
* **Radiologist Name**: Provide the name of the radiologist who performed the procedure. If you don't remember their name, you can ask for a list of radiologists who worked on the procedure.
* **Technologist Name**: Provide the name of the technologist who assisted with the procedure. If you don't remember their name, you can ask for a list of technologists who worked on the procedure.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or concerns you have about your experience. This is a free-text field where you can provide any further information.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the procedure (Excellent, Good, Neutral, Poor, Very Poor, or Not Satisfactory). Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Rating**: Select any rating(s) for the procedure (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5). You can choose multiple ratings to provide more detailed feedback.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your experience. This is a free-text field where you can provide further information.
* **Radiologist Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it. This is a required field to ensure we have your confirmation of the feedback provided.
