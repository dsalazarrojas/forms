# post-surgical-care-checklist-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps patients ensure their post-surgical recovery goes smoothly by collecting information on various aspects of post-operative care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, describe them thoroughly.
4. Check the "Required" fields to ensure you have completed all mandatory sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required/optional): This section is for providing basic patient information, such as name, contact details, and medical history.
* **Post-Operative Care Tasks** (`post_operative_care_tasks`, text, required/optional): Describe any tasks or activities you have been advised to perform after surgery.
* **Patient Comfort** (`patient_comfort`, text, required/optional): Report any discomfort, pain, or difficulty in breathing, eating, or moving.
* **Post-Operative Care Complexities** (`post_operative_care_complexities`, text, required/optional): If you experience any complications or concerns about your recovery, describe them here.
* **Patient Recovery** (`patient_recovery`, text, required/optional): Share your progress and any issues you have faced during recovery.
* **Post-Operative Care Checklist** (`post_operative_care_checklist`, text, required/optional): Check off any post-operative care tasks or activities you have completed.
* **Medical Issues** (`medical_issues`, text, required/optional): If you experience any medical issues, describe them here.
* **Medication Administration** (`medication_administration`, text, required/optional): Report any medication administration issues or concerns.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, required/optional): Record your vital signs, such as temperature, pulse, and blood pressure.
* **Post-Surgical Care Outcomes** (`post_surgical_care_outcomes`, text, required/optional): Report any positive or negative outcomes of your post-surgical care.
* **Post Operative Care Assessment** (`post_operative_care_assessment`, text, required/optional): Evaluate your post-operative care and identify areas for improvement.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, text, required/optional): Describe any side effects you experience from medication.
* **Post Operative Care Communication** (`post_operative_care_communication`, text, required/optional): Report any communication issues with your healthcare team.
* **Surgical Site Management** (`surgical_site_management`, text, required/optional): Discuss any concerns or issues with surgical site management.
* **Post Operative Care Protocols** (`post_operative_care_protocols`, text, required/optional): Review and report any concerns with post-operative care protocols.
* **Patient Care Coordination** (`patient_care_coordination`, text, required/optional): Discuss your needs and expectations for patient care coordination.
* **Post Operative Care Plan** (`post_operative_care_plan`, text, required/optional): Share your post-operative care plan with your healthcare team.
* **Post Operative Care Protocols Implementation** (`post_operative_care_protocols_implementation`, text, required/optional): Report on the implementation of post-operative care protocols.
* **Post Operative Care Follow Up** (`post_operative_care_follow_up`, text, required/optional): Schedule follow-up appointments or discuss any concerns with your healthcare team.
* **Post Operative Care Communication With Family** (`post_operative_care_communication_with_family`, text, required/optional): Discuss communication with family members regarding your post-operative care.
* **Surgical Team** (`surgical_team`, text, required/optional): Discuss any issues or concerns with the surgical team.
* **Post Operative Care Communication With Physician** (`post_operative_care_communication_with_physician`, text, required/optional): Discuss communication with your physician regarding your post-operative care.
* **Post Operative Care Communication With Nurse** (`post_operative_care_communication_with_nurse`, text, required/optional): Discuss communication with your nurse regarding your post-operative care.
* **Patient Instructions** (`patient_instructions`, text, required/optional): Receive instructions from your healthcare team.
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, text, required/optional): Share any feedback or concerns with your healthcare team.
