# Patient Experience Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Experience Interview Consent Form is used to obtain consent from patients for an interview to be conducted by a healthcare professional. This form is an essential part of the patient's record and ensures that patients are aware of the interview process and have given their permission to be interviewed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the category of the interview (Patient Feedback Forms or Other)
* Provide a brief description of the interview (optional)
* Indicate your consent to be interviewed (True or False)
* If you consent to be interviewed, enter the date and time of the interview
* Enter the location of the interview
* Enter the name of the interviewer
* Enter a contact number for the patient to reach the interviewer
* Enter the patient's email address (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category of the interview from the options provided (Patient Feedback Forms or Other). This will help the interviewer understand the purpose of the interview.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the interview (optional). This will help the interviewer understand the context of the interview.
* **Do you consent to be interviewed?** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Select True or False to indicate your consent to be interviewed. If you select True, you will be able to proceed with the interview.
* **Date of Interview** (`date_of_interview`, `date`, required: false): If you consent to be interviewed, enter the date of the interview. This will help the interviewer schedule the interview accordingly.
* **Time of Interview** (`time_of_interview`, `time`, required: false): If you consent to be interviewed, enter the time of the interview. This will help the interviewer schedule the interview accordingly.
* **Location of Interview** (`location_of_interview`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the interview will take place.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the healthcare professional who will be conducting the interview.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter a contact number where the patient can reach the interviewer.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the patient's email address (optional). This will help the interviewer send any further updates or instructions to the patient. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have reviewed and understand the form before submitting it.
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the healthcare professional conducting the interview.
