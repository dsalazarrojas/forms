# Maternity Ward Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maternity Ward Discharge Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from staff members who are directly involved in the patient's care. The form is intended to improve the quality of care and services provided to patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that you are one of the staff members who provided care to the patient and are directly involved in the patient's care.
2. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. For each field, enter the relevant information in the required format (e.g., date, number, text).
4. Make sure to include feedback on the care provided to the patient and any suggestions for improvement.
5. Once you have completed the form, sign it and submit it to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Postpartum Discharge Feedback** (`1`): Enter your comments and feedback on the care provided to the patient during their stay in the maternity ward.
* **Ward Number** (`2`): Enter the number of the ward where the patient was discharged.
* **Unit Name** (`3`): Enter the name of the unit where the patient was cared for.
* **Discharge Date** (`4`): Enter the date of the patient's discharge.
* **Feedback On Care** (`5`): Enter any additional comments or feedback on the care provided to the patient.
* **Staff Nurses Name** (`6`): Enter the name of the staff nurse involved in the patient's care.
* **Staff Doctors Name** (`8`): Enter the name of the doctor involved in the patient's care.
* **Patient Name** (`9`): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Patient Contact Phone** (`10`): Enter the contact phone number of the patient.
* **Patient Signature** (`11`): Enter the date of the patient's signature.
* **Staff Signature** (`12`): Enter the date of the staff member's signature.
* **Prophets Outcome** (`13`): Select the outcome of the patient's care.
* **Ward Outcome** (`14`): Select one or more outcomes of the patient's care.
* **Suggestion** (`15`): Enter any suggestions or comments from the patient or staff.
* **Staff Suggestion** (`16`): Enter any suggestions or comments from the staff.
* **Comments** (`17`): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Discharge Status** (`18`): Select the status of the patient's discharge.
* **Ward Notes** (`19`): Enter any notes or comments about the patient's ward stay.
* **Patient Follow Up** (`20`): Enter the date of the patient's follow-up appointment.
* **Outcome Suggestion** (`21`): Enter any suggestions or comments on the patient's outcome.
* **Staff Outcome Suggestion** (`22`): Enter any suggestions or comments on the staff's outcome.
* **Ward Outcome Suggestion** (`23`): Enter any suggestions or comments on the ward's outcome.
* **Patient Signature** (`24`): Enter the date of the patient's signature.
