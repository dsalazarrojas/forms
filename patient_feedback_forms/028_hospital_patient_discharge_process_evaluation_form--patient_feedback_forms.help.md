# Hospital Patient Discharge Process Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality of the patient's hospital experience, especially during the discharge process. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and enhance patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the correct option for "Patient Discharge Process Evaluation" and choose whether the process was performed successfully or not.
3. For fields 2 and 6, select "Satisfied" if the patient was satisfied with their care, and "Dissatisfied" otherwise.
4. In fields 3, 4, 7, and 9-10, enter relevant and accurate information.
5. Enter the patient's contact information in fields 17-19.
6. Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Discharge Process Evaluation** (1): Select "Yes" if the patient's discharge process was performed successfully, and "No" otherwise.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (2): Select "Satisfied" if the patient was satisfied with their care, and "Dissatisfied" otherwise.
* **Pain Management** (3): Enter any comments or feedback regarding the patient's pain management during their stay.
* **Communication** (4): Select "Yes" if the patient was communicated with effectively during their stay, and "No" otherwise.
* **Nurse Discharge Summary** (5): Enter a summary of the patient's stay and the care they received.
* **Discharge Process** (6): Select "Yes" if the patient's discharge process was performed efficiently, and "No" otherwise.
* **Discharge Summary** (7): Enter any important details about the patient's discharge, such as their condition or any specific instructions.
* **Feedback** (8): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the patient's stay.
* **Assigned Staff** (9): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who assigned the form.
* **Discharge Time** (10): Enter the time the patient was discharged.
* **Patient ID** (12): Enter the patient's unique ID number.
* **Patient Name** (13): Enter the patient's name.
* **Hospital Name** (14): Enter the hospital's name.
* **Physician Name** (15): Enter the name of the patient's physician.
* **Unit** (16): Enter the unit or department where the patient stayed.
* **Comments** (17): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the patient's stay.
* **Patient Email** (18): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (19): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Assigned Staff Name** (20): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who assigned the form.
* **Patient DOB** (21): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Gender** (22): Select the patient's gender.
* **Patient Age** (23): Select the patient's age.
* **Patient Weight** (24): Enter the patient's weight.
* **Patient Height** (25): Enter the patient's height.
