# Hospital Patient Discharge Process Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality of the patient's hospital experience, especially during the discharge process. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and enhance patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the correct option for "Patient Discharge Process Evaluation" and choose whether the process was performed successfully or not.
3. For fields 2 and 6, select "Satisfied" if the patient was satisfied with their care, and "Dissatisfied" otherwise.
4. In fields 3, 4, 7, and 9-10, enter relevant and accurate information.
5. Enter the patient's contact information in fields 17-19.
6. Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
