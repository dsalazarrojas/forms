# healthcare_interaction_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the quality of healthcare interaction between patients and their healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on this form based on your recent interaction with your healthcare provider.
2. Be as honest and detailed as possible when providing your feedback.
3. If a question does not apply to your interaction, choose the option "Not Applicable" or "Mostly Not Applicable" to indicate this.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please type them in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a space for you to briefly describe your recent interaction with your healthcare provider.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **patient experience**: This is a field for you to describe your experience with your healthcare provider.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **provider interaction**: This is a field for you to describe the interaction with your healthcare provider.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **assessment questions**: This is where you will select the level of applicability for each of the following questions:
	+ What was the level of clarity in the explanation of my condition/ treatment plan?
	+ How satisfied am I with the way my concerns were addressed?
	+ How well did I feel heard and validated in this interaction?
	+ How confident do I feel in the healthcare provider's ability to answer my questions?
	+ How satisfied am I with the communication style of the healthcare provider?
	+ Type: select_one (pick one of the options)
	+ Options:
		- Not Applicable
		- Mostly Not Applicable
		- Somewhat Applicable
		- Very Applicable
		- Extremely Applicable
	+ Required: false
* **assessment scale**: This is a field for you to rate the healthcare provider's performance on a scale of 1-10.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: true
* **feedback**: This is a field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your interaction.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **comments**: This is a field for you to provide any additional comments or concerns.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: false

## Tips
* Please be as honest and detailed as possible when providing your feedback.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to your healthcare provider or their office.
* This form will not be used for duplicate assessments, so please complete it only once per interaction.
