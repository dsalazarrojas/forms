# Healthcare Concepts Overview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to capture feedback on healthcare concepts from patients or their representatives, ensuring that the care team understands and addresses patient concerns and needs. It helps healthcare providers to:

* Assess patient satisfaction with their care and identify areas for improvement
* Develop targeted interventions to address patient concerns and needs
* Improve patient engagement and outcomes

Before starting, please ensure that you have not already submitted this form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the introduction on the first page to understand the purpose and context of this form.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the healthcare concept being addressed (concept_rating).
3. Provide your comment or feedback about the concept (comment).
4. Rate your satisfaction with the healthcare provider (provider_rating).
5. Provide feedback to the healthcare provider (feedback).
6. Indicate if you need a follow-up action (follow_up_action).
7. Provide details about the next follow-up action and the responsible provider (next_step).
8. Confirm if there will be a next step and the next follow-up date and time (next_step_datetime).
9. Provide details about the next follow-up date (next_follow_up_date).
10. Specify the frequency of the next follow-up action (follow_up_frequency).
11. Enter the next follow-up date and time (next_follow_up_date_time).
12. Confirm the location of the next follow-up action (follow_up_location).
13. Enter the details of the next action (next_action).
14. Specify the frequency of the next action (next_action_frequency).
15. Provide follow-up notes (follow_up_notes).
16. Indicate the follow-up status and add any comments if necessary (follow_up_status, follow_up_status_note).
17. Specify the follow-up contact method (follow_up_next_contact_method).
18. Enter any other details about the next follow-up contact method (follow_up_next_contact_method_other).
19. Confirm if the next follow-up will be with the same or a different provider (follow_up_next_follow_up_provider).
20. Enter the name of the next follow-up provider (follow_up_next_follow_up_provider_name).

* **Rate Provider** (Page 3): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the healthcare provider, using a scale of Not Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, and Very Satisfied.
* **Feedback to Provider** (Page 3): This field allows you to provide any additional feedback or comments to the healthcare provider.
