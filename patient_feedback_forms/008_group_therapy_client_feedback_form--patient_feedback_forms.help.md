# group_therapy_client_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from clients who have attended group therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the name of the therapist who led the session in the "Therapist Name" field (if applicable).
5. Provide feedback about your experience in the "Feedback" field.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the session in the "Rating" field.
7. Enter the date of the session in the "Date" field.
8. Enter the time of the session in the "Time" field.
9. (Optional) Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, `text`, optional): If you remember the name of the therapist who led the session, enter it here.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required): Provide a brief description of your experience with the session. Be as specific as possible.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session. Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Therapist Comments** (`therapist_comments`, `note`, optional): This is where the therapist can add any additional comments or notes about your session.
