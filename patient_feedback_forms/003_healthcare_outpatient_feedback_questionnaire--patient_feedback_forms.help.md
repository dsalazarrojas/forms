# healthcare_outpatient_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare_outpatient_feedback_questionnaire form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences at our healthcare facility. The form helps us understand how well we're serving our patients, identify areas of improvement, and make informed decisions to enhance their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number) at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required)
This page contains an introductory note about the form's purpose and importance. Please read it carefully to understand the context of the questions that follow.

### 2. **How Was It?** (`how_was_it`, `text`, not required)
Please describe your experience at our healthcare facility in a few words. This will help us understand your overall impression of our services.

### 3. **Staff Helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, `number`, not required)
Rate your experience with our staff's helpfulness. Please select a number that best reflects your impression, from 1 (not helpful at all) to 5 (extremely helpful).

### 4. **Staff Courtesy** (`staff_courtesy`, `text`, not required)
Describe your experience with our staff's courtesy. Please provide any specific instances that made you feel welcome or uncomfortable.

### 5. **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, `number`, not required)
Rate our communication effectiveness during your visit. Please select a number that best reflects your impression, from 1 (not clear at all) to 5 (very clear).

### 6. **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`, `text`, not required)
Comment on your experience with our staff's professionalism. Please provide any examples or instances that made an impact on your impression.

### 7. **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, not required)
Rate your overall satisfaction with our services. Please select a number that best reflects your impression, from 1 (not satisfied at all) to 5 (very satisfied).

### 8. **What Was Most Impressed?** (`what_was_most_impressed`, `select_one`, not required)
Select one thing that impressed you the most during your visit. Choose from:

### 9. **What Was Most Disappointed?** (`what_was_most_disappointed`, `select_multiple`, not required)
Select all that apply to what disappointed you the most during your visit. Choose from:
	* option 1
	* option 2
	* option 3
	* option 4
	* option 5

### 10. **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, not required)
Provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement to our services. Your feedback is invaluable.

### 11. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required)
Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience. This will help us understand your perspective better.

### 12. **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required)
Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

### 13. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required)
Enter your phone number for any follow-up or additional information.
