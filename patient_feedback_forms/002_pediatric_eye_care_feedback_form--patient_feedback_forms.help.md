# Pediatric Eye Care Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and medical information from patients or their representatives to improve the quality of care at our pediatric eye care clinic or hospital. Please answer these questions as accurately as possible to help us provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and feelings about your visit.
2. **Personal Information**: Fill out the form with your name, email, phone number, and other contact details.
3. **Visit Details**: Provide the date and time of your visit, as well as the doctor or staff member you met.
4. **Medical History**: Share any allergies, medical history, or medications you are currently taking.
5. **Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm your information.
