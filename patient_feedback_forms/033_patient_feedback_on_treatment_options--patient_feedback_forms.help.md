# prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prophets form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with your care team. It helps to identify areas of improvement and ensures that the care provided meets the patient's expectations. This form is used to gather information on the patient's satisfaction, treatment outcomes, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to send your feedback.
*   Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
*   For optional fields, you can provide additional comments or details if you feel it will help in your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select Treatment (`treatment_id`, select_one, required):** Choose the treatment you received during your visit.
*   **Overall Rating (`overall_rating`, number, required):** Rate your overall satisfaction with the treatment you received.
*   **Rating Score (`rating_score`, number, required):** Provide a score between 1-10 to rate your satisfaction with the treatment you received.
*   **Rating Scale (`rating_scale`, select_multiple, optional):** Choose the areas where you think the care team did best and worst from the provided options.
*   **Patient Comments (`prophets_comments`, note, optional):** Add any comments or thoughts you have about your experience with the care team.
*   **Patient Satisfaction (`patient_satisfaction`, select_one, optional):** Choose how satisfied you are with your visit from the provided options.
*   **Expected Outcomes (`expected_outcomes`, select_multiple, optional):** Choose the outcomes you expected from your treatment and select those that were met or not met.
*   **Actual Outcomes (`actual_outcomes`, select_multiple, optional):** Choose the outcomes you experienced from your treatment and select those that were met or not met.
*   **Improvement Areas (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, optional):** Choose areas where the care team could improve.
*   **Feedback Suggestions (`feedback_suggestions`, note, optional):** Add any suggestions or comments you have for the care team.
*   **Patient Name (`patient_name`, text, optional):** Provide your name for the feedback.
*   **Patient Email (`patient_email`, email, optional):** Provide your email address for the feedback.
*   **Patient Phone (`patient_phone`, text, optional):** Provide your phone number for the feedback.
*   **Date of Visit (`date_of_visit`, date, optional):** Enter the date you visited the care team.
*   **Time of Visit (`time_of_visit`, time, optional):** Enter the time you visited the care team.
*   **Duration of Visit (`duration_of_visit`, number, optional):** Enter the duration of your visit.
*   **Visit Relevant (`visit_relevant`, select_one, optional):** Choose how relevant the visit was to your health issues from the provided options.

This form is designed to be completed by patients to provide feedback on their experience with the care team. The fields are divided into several sections to help you provide a comprehensive review of your visit.
