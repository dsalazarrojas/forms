# IVC Treatment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients on their experience with IVC treatment. Your input will help us understand how we can improve our services to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each page. Each page will guide you through specific sections of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Experience** (`patient_experience`, text, optional): Please describe your experience during the IVC treatment. This could include any challenges you faced or positive experiences you had.
* **Side Effects** (`side_effects`, text, optional): If you experienced any side effects during the treatment, please describe them here.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions on how we could improve our services, please provide them here.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, optional): Please rate your overall experience with a number from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Provider Rating** (`provider_rating`, number, optional): Please rate the care provided by your healthcare provider on a number from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Provider** (`provider`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following options to rate your healthcare provider:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Please enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Please enter the time of your visit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address (if you'd like to receive a confirmation of your feedback).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback, as it will help us understand your experience better.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to include them in the "Suggestions" field.
