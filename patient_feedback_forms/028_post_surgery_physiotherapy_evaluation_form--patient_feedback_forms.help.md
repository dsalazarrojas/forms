# Post-Surgery Physiotherapy Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate patient progress after surgery, gathering information about their pain levels, mobility, and other relevant factors to inform post-surgery rehabilitation plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, answering all required and optional questions.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and relevant information about your current state.
3. Review the form before submission to confirm accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information as required by your healthcare provider.
* **pain_level** (Number, Optional): Indicate your current pain level on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is minimal pain and 10 is extreme pain.
* **mobility_level** (Number, Optional): Indicate your current mobility level, with options ranging from excellent to bad.
* **pain_location** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all areas where you experience pain, such as head, neck, back, arms, legs, stomach, hands, or feet.
* **pain_location_options** (Select One, Optional): Choose the specific area of pain from the options provided.
* **mobility_level_options** (Select One, Optional): Choose your current mobility level from the provided options.
* **medication_info** (Text, Optional): Provide any relevant medical information, such as medication names or dosages.
* **follow_up_appointments** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate whether you have scheduled follow-up appointments for your post-surgery recovery.
* **follow_up_appointments_options** (Select One, Optional): Choose whether you have a follow-up appointment scheduled or not.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review and confirm your answers before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, consult your healthcare provider for guidance.
