# Patient Ownership Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's ownership and management of their medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. If a question does not apply to you, please select the "None" or "Not Applicable" option.
3. If you have any medical conditions or allergies that are not listed, please describe them in the "Other Medical Conditions" field.
4. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Feedback** (Patient Feedback, text, optional): Please share any thoughts or concerns you have about your medical records.
* **Own Health Records** (Ownership Status, select_one, optional): Are you the owner of your health records? Select "True" if you are, "False" if you are not.
* **Medical Decisions** (Medical Decisions, select_multiple, optional): Who makes decisions about your medical care? Select "Family members" if you have family members involved, "Healthcare provider" if your healthcare provider makes decisions, or "Other" if another person or organization makes decisions.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, note, optional): Please provide any contact information you would like to share with your healthcare provider.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have, including but not limited to diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
* **Medications** (Medications, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Care** (Medical Care, select_one, optional): Where do you typically receive medical care? Select "In-person" if you receive care from a healthcare provider, "Phone/Video" if you receive care through phone or video calls, or "Online" if you receive care through online consultations.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, optional): Please describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or injuries.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Ever Had** (Ever Had, select_multiple, optional): Have you ever had any of the following medical conditions? Select "True" if you have had it, "False" if you have not.
* **Currently** (Currently, select_multiple, optional): Do you currently have any of the following medical conditions? Select "True" if you do, "False" if you do not.
* **Frequently** (Frequently, select_multiple, optional): Do you frequently experience any of the following medical conditions? Select "True" if you do, "False" if you do not.
* **Chronically** (Chronically, select_multiple, optional): Do you have any of the following medical conditions chronically? Select "True" if you do, "False" if you do not.
* **Patient Info** (Patient Info, text, optional): Please provide any relevant patient information, such as insurance information or medical ID number.
* **Family Info** (Family Info, text, optional): Please provide any relevant family information, such as family medical history.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (Other Medical Conditions, text, optional): List any other medical conditions you have that are not listed above.
* **Patient Notes** (Patient Notes, note, optional): Please share any additional comments or concerns you have about your medical records.
* **Provider Notes** (Provider Notes, note, optional): Please share any additional comments or concerns you have about your healthcare provider.
* **Other Conditions Description** (Other Conditions Description, text, optional): Please describe any other medical conditions you listed above.
* **Patient Consent** (Patient Consent, select_one, optional): Do you consent to sharing your medical information with your healthcare provider? Select "True" if you do, "False" if you do not.
* **Reason for Not Having** (Reason for Not Having, text, optional): Please provide a reason for not having patient consent.
* **Medical Record Access** (Medical Record Access, select_one, optional): Do you have access to your medical records? Select "True" if you do, "False" if you do not.
* **Medical Record Access Description** (Medical Record Access Description, text, optional): Please describe how you access your medical records.
* **Incorrect Information** (Incorrect Information, select_one, optional): Do you believe your medical records contain incorrect information? Select "True" if you do, "False" if you do not.
* **Medical Record Incorrect Info Description** (Medical Record Incorrect Info Description, text, optional): Please describe why you believe your medical records contain incorrect information.
