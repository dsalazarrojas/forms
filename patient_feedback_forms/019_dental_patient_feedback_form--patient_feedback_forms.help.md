# Dental Patient Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Patient Feedback Form is a tool for patients to share their thoughts and opinions about their experience at the dental clinic. It is not a formal evaluation form, but rather an opportunity for patients to give feedback on their care.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including your name, visit date, and any other relevant details.
2. Answer the feedback question to let us know about your experience.
3. Rate your satisfaction with our services.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback you may have.
5. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you (if needed).
6. Choose which tool or resource you found most helpful during your visit (if applicable).
7. Finally, review and submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`): Please enter your name, visit date, and any other relevant details about your visit.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Share your thoughts about your experience with our dental clinic. This is your chance to tell us what we did well, what we could improve on, or any concerns you may have.
* **Dentist Name** (`dentist_name`): If you have a specific dentist who provided your care, please enter their name.
* **Visit Date** (`dentist_visit_date`): Enter the date of your visit to help us track your experience over time.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Please rate your satisfaction with our services on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed to follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed to follow up on your feedback.
* **Feedback Submitted** (`feedback_submitted`): This field will be populated automatically when you submit your feedback.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Please select the tool or resource that was most helpful during your visit. If it's not listed, please select "Other" and let us know what it was.
* **Other Tool** (`assigned_tool_other`): If you selected "Other" above, please enter the name of the tool or resource that was most helpful during your visit.

## Tips
* Please be as honest and detailed as possible with your feedback, as it will help us improve our services.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please feel free to share them with us.
* We value your feedback and will use it to improve our services for all patients.
* If you have any questions or need help completing the form, please don't hesitate to ask.
