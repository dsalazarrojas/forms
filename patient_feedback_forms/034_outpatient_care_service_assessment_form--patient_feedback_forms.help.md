# Outpatient Care Service Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outpatient Care Service Assessment Form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with our outpatient care service. This form helps us understand their satisfaction levels, identify areas of improvement, and make informed decisions to enhance the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the most accurate answer for each question.
3. Fill in any comments or feedback you have in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient feedback
* **Patient feedback** (`patient_feedback`, select_multiple, required=false):
	+ This question asks about your overall impression of the service. Please select all that apply: Yes or No.
* **Service date** (`service_date`, date, required=false):
	+ Enter the date you received the service.
* **Service time** (`service_time`, time, required=false):
	+ Enter the time you received the service.
* **Care provider** (`care_provider`, select_one, required=false):
	+ Please indicate if the care provider was helpful or not. Options: Yes or No.
* **Service quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, required=false):
	+ Rate the quality of the service. Options: Good or Poor.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required=false):
	+ Indicate your overall satisfaction with the service. Options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Would you recommend this service?** (`recommendation`, select_one, required=false):
	+ Would you recommend this service to others? Options: Yes or No.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required=false):
	+ Share any comments or feedback you have about the service.
* **Assinged tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required=false):
	+ Please indicate which tool was assigned to you. Options: chatjimmy or Other.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required=false):
	+ Enter any additional information about the category of the service.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required=false):
	+ Enter any other comments or feedback you have about the service.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
