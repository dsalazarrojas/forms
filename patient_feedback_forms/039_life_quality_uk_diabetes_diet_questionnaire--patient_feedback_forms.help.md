# life_quality_uk_diabetes_diet_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather patient feedback and information about their dietary habits related to UK diabetes. The collected data can be used for further analysis and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient feedback in the "Patient Feedback" field.
2. Enter your response to the question "Life Quality".
3. Answer the questions about your UK diabetes diet, select one option from "Select One UK Diabetes Diet" and select multiple options from "Select Multiple UK Diabetes Diet".
4. Provide your date in the "Date" field.
5. If desired, provide your time of completion in the "Time" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
8. Provide any additional note about your experience in the "Note" field.
9. Select one date option from "Select One Date" field.
10. Select multiple date options from "Select Multiple Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Feedback**: Enter your feedback about your experience with UK diabetes diet. This field is required.
* **Life Quality**: Please rate your life quality related to UK diabetes diet.
* **UK Diabetes Diet**: Enter your response to this question about UK diabetes diet.
* **Select One UK Diabetes Diet**: Select one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple UK Diabetes Diet**: Select multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Date**: Enter the date of completion in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
* **Time**: If desired, enter the time of completion in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:30).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Note**: Provide any additional note about your experience.
* **Select One Date**: Select one date option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple Date**: Select multiple date options from the dropdown menu. 

Note: Some fields may require specific formats or lengths, please follow the provided hints and instructions for accurate completion.
