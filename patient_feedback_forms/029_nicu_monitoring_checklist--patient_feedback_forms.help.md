# NICU Monitoring Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The NICU Monitoring Checklist is a tool used to gather data and feedback on the patient's care in the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU). The form helps healthcare professionals monitor and assess the patient's status, allowing for timely interventions and adjustments to their care.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Introduction** (Text Field):
    - Enter a brief description of the patient's condition and any relevant medical history.
    - This field is not required, but it provides context for the subsequent sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Heart Rate Monitors** (Select One):
    - Choose one of the options: Blood Pressure Monitor, Pulse Oximeter, or Electrocardiogram.
    - Select the type of heart rate monitor used to track the patient's heart rate.
* **Respiratory Monitors** (Select Multiple):
    - Choose one or more of the options: Resuscitator, Ventilator.
    - Select the type(s) of respiratory monitor used to track the patient's respiratory status.
* **Temperature Monitors** (Number Field):
    - Enter the patient's current temperature in Celsius or Fahrenheit.
    - Please ensure the temperature is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Blood Gas Monitors** (Select Multiple):
    - Choose one or more of the options: Blood Gas Analyzer, Capnography.
    - Select the type(s) of blood gas monitor used to track the patient's blood gas levels.
* **Blood Pressure Monitors** (Select One):
    - Choose one of the options: Invasive Blood Pressure Monitor, Non-Invasive Blood Pressure Monitor.
    - Select the type of blood pressure monitor used to track the patient's blood pressure.
* **Neurologic Monitors** (Select One):
    - Choose one of the options: EEG, Electroencephalography.
    - Select the type of neurologic monitor used to track the patient's neurologic status.
* **Cardiac Monitors** (Select Multiple):
    - Choose one or more of the options: Cardiac Catheterization, Cardiac MRI.
    - Select the type(s) of cardiac monitor used to track the patient's cardiac status.
* **Respiratory Monitors** (Select Multiple):
    - Choose one or more of the options: Resuscitator, Ventilator.
    - Select the type(s) of respiratory monitor used to track the patient's respiratory status.
* **Other Monitors** (Text Field):
    - Enter any other monitors not listed above that are being used to track the patient's status.
* **Comments** (Text Field):
    - Enter any additional comments or observations about the patient's care.
* **Assigned To** (Select One):
    - Choose 'Yes' if the patient has a specific monitor assigned to them, or 'No' if not.
    - This field helps track who is responsible for the patient's monitoring.

## Tips

- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your healthcare team.
- The information collected will be used to improve the patient's care and treatment in the NICU.
