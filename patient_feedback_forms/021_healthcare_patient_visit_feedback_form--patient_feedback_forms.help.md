# Healthcare Patient Visit Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a platform for patients to share their experiences, impressions, and opinions about their visit to a healthcare facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Choose your rating and satisfaction levels as honestly as you can.
4. Answer the recommendation question with "Yes", "No", or "Maybe" based on your experience.
5. Enter your email and phone number (if you want to be contacted by the healthcare facility).
6. Date of visit: Enter the date of your visit in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Visit Form (*)
* **Patient Visit Form**: A text box where you can describe your visit in as much detail as possible.
*Please provide a brief description of your visit, including the reason for your visit, the doctor's name, and any notable events or conversations that occurred during your visit.*

### Patient Info
* **Patient Info**: A text box where you can provide any relevant patient information, such as your name, date of birth, or medical history.
*Please provide any relevant patient information that may be helpful for the healthcare facility to know.*

### Doctor Info
* **Doctor Info**: A text box where you can describe the doctor you visited, including their name, specialty, and any notable interactions or conversations you had with them.
*Please provide any information about the doctor that may be helpful for the healthcare facility to know.*

### Appointment Info
* **Appointment Info**: A text box where you can describe your appointment, including the date, time, and reason for your visit.
*Please provide any details about your appointment that may be helpful for the healthcare facility to know.*

### Feedback
* **Feedback**: A text box where you can describe your overall experience with the healthcare facility.
*Please provide any feedback you have about the care and services you received during your visit.*

### Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction**: A scale where you can rate your satisfaction with the care and services you received during your visit.
*Please rate your satisfaction with the care and services you received from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.*

### Rating
* **Rating**: A scale where you can rate the care and services you received during your visit.
*Please rate the care and services you received from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.*

### Would you recommend this clinic?
* **Would you recommend this clinic**: A question where you can choose whether you would recommend this clinic based on your experience.
*Please answer with "Yes", "No", or "Maybe" based on your experience.*

### Comments
* **Comments**: A text box where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
*Please use this space to provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have about the healthcare facility.*

### Email
* **Email**: A text box where you can enter your email address.
*Please enter your email address if you would like the healthcare facility to contact you.*

### Phone
* **Phone**: A text box where you can enter your phone number.
*Please enter your phone number if you would like the healthcare facility to contact you.*

### Visit Date
* **Visit Date**: A date picker where you can enter the date of your visit.
*Please enter the date of your visit in the format mm/dd/yyyy.*
