# Patient Wait Time Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with waiting time during their visits to the medical facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. The form is divided into multiple sections, each asking different questions about your experience.
3. Please fill in all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. You can skip optional fields (marked as "optional" in the form) if you don't have information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wait time satisfation** (`wait_time_satisfation`, Number, Required): How satisfied are you with the wait time during your visit?
* **waiting_experience** (`waiting_experience`, Text, Required): Please describe your experience while waiting for medical attention.
* **First visit date** (`first_visit_date`, Date, Optional): If this was your first visit, what date did you visit? (Leave blank if not applicable)
* **Doctor name** (`doctor_name`, Text, Optional): What is the name of your doctor?
* **Wait time satisfation 2** (`wait_time_satisfation_2`, Number, Required): How satisfied are you with the wait time during your second visit?
* **waiting_experience 2** (`waiting_experience_2`, Text, Optional): Please describe your experience while waiting for medical attention during your second visit.
* **Second visit date** (`second_visit_date`, Date, Optional): If this was your second visit, what date did you visit? (Leave blank if not applicable)
* **Doctor name 2** (`doctor_name_2`, Text, Optional): What is the name of your doctor for your second visit?

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to help us improve our waiting time management processes.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the optional "waiting_experience" field.
