# inpatient_experience_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inpatient Experience Assessment Form is a tool used to gauge the quality of care and services provided to patients during their hospital stay. It helps identify areas of improvement and ensures that patients receive the best possible care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you have all the information needed.
2.  Answer all the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3.  Select the corresponding answer for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Introduction** (Text, Optional):
    Please provide a brief overview of your experience during your hospital stay.
*   **Admission Date** (Date, Optional):
    Please enter the date of your admission to the hospital.
*   **Admission Time** (Time, Optional):
    Please enter the time of your admission to the hospital.
*   **Discharge Date** (Date, Optional):
    Please enter the date of your discharge from the hospital.
*   **Discharge Time** (Time, Optional):
    Please enter the time of your discharge from the hospital.
*   **Hospital Stay** (Text, Optional):
    Describe your overall experience during your hospital stay.
*   **Nurse Name** (Text, Optional):
    If applicable, enter the name of your nurse.
*   **Physician Name** (Text, Optional):
    If applicable, enter the name of your doctor/physician.
*   **Overall Experience** (Number, Optional):
    Please rate your overall experience (1-5).
*   **Pain Management** (Select One, Optional):
    Rate the pain management during your stay (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
*   **Care Team** (Select Multiple, Optional):
    Rate the care team's performance (Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Communication** (Text, Optional):
    Describe the communication you received from hospital staff.
*   **Discharge Summary** (Text, Optional):
    Provide a brief summary of your discharge.
*   **Discharge Instructions** (Text, Optional):
    Provide any additional instructions you received at discharge.
*   **Discharge Location** (Text, Optional):
    Describe the location where you were discharged from.
*   **Hospital Cleanliness** (Select One, Optional):
    Rate the cleanliness of the hospital (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
*   **Food Service** (Select One, Optional):
    Rate the food service during your stay (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
*   **Room Cleanliness** (Select One, Optional):
    Rate the cleanliness of your room (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
*   **Staff Availability** (Select Multiple, Optional):
    Rate the availability of hospital staff (Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Pain Management Treatment** (Select One, Optional):
    Rate the pain management treatment you received (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
*   **Discharge Location** (Text, Optional):
    Describe the location where you were discharged from.
*   **Hospital Staff Skills** (Select One, Optional):
    Rate the skills of the hospital staff (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
*   **Nurse Care** (Select One, Optional):
    Rate the care received from nurses (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
*   **Doctor Care** (Select One, Optional):
    Rate the care received from doctors (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
