# Custom Track Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a custom track quote request from a customer. It helps us understand their needs and requirements to provide a quote that suits their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer's information, including their name, address, and any relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you about their quote.
3. Specify the project details, selecting 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of project.
4. Enter any specific quote details as required.
5. Enter your desired quote request or any specific requests.
6. If applicable, enter the date and time you prefer for the project start.
7. Enter your email address so we can contact you about their quote.
8. Enter your phone number so we can contact you about their quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant information about your company or project.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant information so we can contact you about your quote.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if the project has specific requirements or 'No' if it's an open-ended project.
* **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, text, required: false): Enter any specific details about the quote you require.
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, required: false): Enter your desired quote request or any specific requests.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you prefer for the project start if applicable.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you prefer for the project start if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your quote.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your quote.
