# Room Addition Cost Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Room Addition Cost Estimate Request form is designed to gather essential information about a room addition project. This form will help us provide accurate cost estimates and ensure a smooth project execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide project details in the "Project Details" field.
2.  Describe the project in the "Project Description" field.
3.  Select the type of room you wish to add.
4.  Enter the square footage of the room you wish to add.
5.  Specify your project budget.
6.  Choose the contact person responsible for the project.
7.  Enter your email address.
8.  Provide your phone number.
9.  Select the project start and end dates.
10.  Mention any additional requirements or specifications for the project.
11.  Upload any relevant files or documents related to the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details (text)**: Enter a brief description of your project, including the project address, floor plan, or other relevant details.
* **Project Description (text)**: Describe your project in more detail, including the scope of work, materials needed, and any specific requirements.
* **Room Type (select_one)**: Choose the type of room you wish to add, such as Basement, Attic, Bathroom, Bedroom, Kitchen, Living Room, or Office.
* **Square Footage (number)**: Enter the square footage of the room you wish to add.
* **Project Budget (number)**: Specify your project budget, including any funds allocated for materials, labor, and other expenses.
* **Contact Person (select_one)**: Select the contact person responsible for the project, such as John Doe or Jane Doe.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address, which will be used for further communication.
* **Phone (text)**: Provide your phone number, which will be used for scheduling and coordination.
* **Project Start Date (date)**: Select the expected start date of the project.
* **Project End Date (date)**: Select the expected end date of the project.
* **Additional Requirements (note)**: Mention any additional requirements or specifications for the project.
* **File Upload (text)**: Upload any relevant files or documents related to the project, such as floor plans, photos, or permits.
