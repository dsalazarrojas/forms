# Mulch And Straw Cost Estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Mulch And Straw Cost Estimator" form is used to collect information about projects involving mulch and straw. This form helps users quickly and easily estimate the costs associated with these projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the project type: Choose from "Mulch", "Straw", or "Both" to indicate the type of project.
2. Select the mulch type: Choose from "Bagged", "Bulk", "Composted", or "Peat" to indicate the type of mulch.
3. Select the mulch color: Choose from "Brown", "Green", "Red", or "Tan" to indicate the color of the mulch.
4. Select the straw type: Choose from "Straw", "Hay", or "Wheat" to indicate the type of straw.
5. Select the straw size: Choose from "Large", "Medium", "Small", or "Custom" to indicate the size of the straw.
6. Enter the area to cover: Enter the square footage of the area to be covered with mulch and straw.
7. Enter the quantity required: Enter the amount of mulch and straw needed for the project.
8. Enter the estimated cost: Enter the estimated cost of the project.
9. Enter the contact information: Enter the contact person's name, contact number, and contact email.
10. Select the project status: Choose from "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
