# Custom Button Badge Quotation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Button Badge Quotation Request form is designed to gather information for customers to request a custom button badge quotation. This form should be used for customers who are looking to create custom button badges and need a quote from our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Enter your title and specs for the custom button badge.
3. Provide details on the delivery date and price you are expecting.
4. If applicable, provide any follow-up information for our team.
5. Enter any additional information on the supplier for the custom button badge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Custom Button Badge Quotation Request Title** (`general`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your custom button badge quotation request.
- **Specs** (`specs`, text, required): Describe the specifications for your custom button badge, including size, material, color, and any other relevant details.
- **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required): Provide information on the delivery date and method for your custom button badge.
- **Artwork** (`artwork`, text, optional): If you have any additional artwork or design information for your custom button badge, please enter it here.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are expecting to receive your custom button badge.
- **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price you are expecting to pay for your custom button badge.
- **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): If you have any follow-up information for our team, such as a phone number or email, please enter it here.
- **Supplier** (`supplier`, text, optional): If you have any information on the supplier for your custom button badge, please enter it here.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form to avoid delays in the quote process.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team.
- You can always call us or email us if you need any clarification on the form or any other questions.
