# Subcontractor Service Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subcontractor Service Quote Form is used by contractors to provide a quote for their services to clients. This form helps in gathering information about the services provided, client details, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Service Quote Form** section with a brief description of the services you are offering.
2. Provide your **Contractor Details** in the designated field.
3. Enter your **Client Details**, including their **Client Phone** and **Client Email**.
4. Specify the **Service Description**, **Service Estimate**, and **Service Quote** for the client.
5. Set the **Contract Start Date** and **Contract End Date** for the project.
6. Choose the **Service Status** of the project (Active, Inactive, or Archived).
7. Enter the **Service Location** where the services will be provided.
8. Enter the estimated **Estimated Cost** for the services.
9. Select the **Subcontractor Bids** you are participating in (up to 5).
10. Enter the details of each **Bid** you have submitted, including **Bidder Name**, **Bidder Phone**, and **Bidder Email**.
11. Enter the **Subcontractor Service**, **Service Address**, and **Service Notes** related to the service.
12. Choose the **Service Type** of the service provided.
13. Click the **Submit** button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Quote Form**: A brief description of the services provided by the contractor.
* **Contractor Details**: Information about the contractor, including name and contact details.
* **Client Details**: Information about the client, including phone and email.
* **Service Description**: A description of the services provided.
* **Service Estimate**: The estimated cost for the services.
* **Service Quote**: The final quote for the services.
* **Contract Start Date**: The date when the contract will start.
* **Contract End Date**: The date when the contract will end.
* **Service Status**: The status of the service (Active, Inactive, or Archived).
* **Service Location**: The location where the services will be provided.
* **Estimated Cost**: The estimated cost for the services.
* **Subcontractor Bids**: The bids submitted by the contractor for the service.
* **Bid Details**: The details of each bid submitted.
* **Bidder Name**: The name of the bidder.
* **Bidder Phone**: The phone number of the bidder.
* **Bidder Email**: The email of the bidder.
* **Subcontractor Service**: The services provided by the subcontractor.
* **Service Address**: The address where the services will be provided.
* **Service Notes**: Additional notes about the service.
* **Service Type**: The type of service provided.
* **Submit**: Click to submit the form.
