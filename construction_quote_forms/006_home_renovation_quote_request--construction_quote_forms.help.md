# Home Renovation Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request quotes for home renovation projects. It captures essential information about the project, including contact details, project specifics, and financial requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your general information, including a brief description of your project.
2. Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
3. Fill in the project details, such as budget, timeline, and project type.
4. Select your preferred contact method and provide any additional contact information.
5. Specify the project's status and urgency.
6. Choose your contract method and duration.
7. Indicate your preferred payment method and frequency.
8. Select the status of your payment and contract.
9. Finally, provide any additional information that may be relevant to the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **General Info** (`general_info`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your home renovation project.
  * This field is for a general overview of your project.

- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, Optional): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you.
  * This field is for your contact name and address, email, and phone number.

- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name as the contact person for this project.
  * Enter your name as the contact person for this project.

- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
  * Enter your phone number.

- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can email you.
  * Enter your email address.

- **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, Optional): Enter more details about your project.
  * This field is for additional information about your project.

- **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the type of project you're dealing with, e.g., Residential, Commercial, etc.
  * This field is for specifying the type of project you need help with.

- **Project Quantity** (`project_quantity`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the project quantity, e.g., Single, Multi, Entire House.
  * This field is for specifying the project quantity.

- **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the current status of your project.
  * This field is for specifying the current status of your project.

- **Project Urgency** (`project_urgency`, number, Optional): Enter a value indicating how urgently you need the renovation.
  * This field is for specifying how urgently you need the renovation.

- **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, Optional): Enter the estimated budget for your project.
  * This field is for specifying the budget for your project.

- **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, time, Optional): Enter the project duration.
  * This field is for specifying how long you think the project will take.

- **Contract Method** (`contract_method`, select_one, Optional): Choose the contract method, e.g., Hourly, Project Basis, etc.
  * This field is for specifying the contract method for your project.

- **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, select_one, Optional): Choose the contract duration, e.g., Weeks, Months, etc.
  * This field is for specifying the contract duration.

- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, Optional): Choose the payment method, e.g., Cash, Credit, etc.
  * This field is for specifying the payment method for your project.

- **Payment Frequencies** (`payment_frequencies`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the payment frequencies, e.g., Weekly, Fortnightly, etc.
  * This field is for specifying the payment frequencies.

- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the payment status, e.g., Paid, Outstanding, etc.
  * This field is for specifying the payment status.

- **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the contract status, e.g., Signed, Signed and Delivered, etc.
  * This field is for specifying the contract status.

- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of your submission.
  * This field is for specifying the date of your submission.

- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, Optional): Enter any additional information regarding your project.
  * This field is for extra details about your project.

- **Project Budget (2)** (`project_budget`, number, Optional): Enter the estimated budget for your project.
  * This field is for specifying the budget for your project.

- **Project Duration (2)** (`project_duration`, time, Optional): Enter the project duration.
  * This field is for specifying how long you think the project will take.

- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, Optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted, e.g., Email, Phone, etc.
  * This field is for specifying how you'd like to be contacted.

- **Contact Status** (`contact_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose your contact status, e.g., New, Active, etc.
  * This field is for specifying your contact status.
