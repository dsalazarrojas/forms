# Piano Moving Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a quote for moving a piano. You can use this form to get an estimate of the costs involved in moving your piano to a new location.

## How To Complete This Form
To get a quote, please fill out the form with the required information. The form consists of two main sections:

1. Contact information: Please fill out your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Move details: Please provide information about the move, including the type of move (local or long distance), the date of the move, and the dimensions and weight of your piano.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category**: Select the type of quote you are requesting, either "Piano Moving Quote" or "Construction Quote".
* **contact_name**: Enter your name.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **move_type**: Select the type of move you are making, either "Local" or "Long Distance".
* **move_date**: Enter the date you would like to make the move.
* **move_time**: Enter the time you would like to make the move.
* **moving_address**: Enter the address of the new location.
* **moving_city**: Enter the city of the new location.
* **moving_state**: Enter the state of the new location.
* **moving_zip**: Enter the zip code of the new location.
* **moving_country**: Enter the country of the new location.
* **current_address**: Enter the address of your current location.
* **current_city**: Enter the city of your current location.
* **current_state**: Enter the state of your current location.
* **current_zip**: Enter the zip code of your current location.
* **current_country**: Enter the country of your current location.
* **special_instructions**: Enter any special instructions or requirements you have for the move.
* **piano_weight**: Enter the weight of your piano.
* **piano_height**: Enter the height of your piano.
* **piano_width**: Enter the width of your piano.
* **piano_depth**: Enter the depth of your piano.
* **estimated_piano_weight**: Enter an estimated weight of your piano if you are unsure of its actual weight.
* **submit**: Click "Submit" to send your quote request, or "Cancel" to exit without sending your request.

Please note that the estimated piano weight field is meant to be used if you're unsure of your piano's actual weight. You can enter 0 if you're not sure.
