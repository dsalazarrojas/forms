# Sign Production Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers for a sign production quote request. This information will be used to create a quote for the production process and to follow up with the customer on the status of their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Customer Info" field.
2. Select the date of production from the calendar.
3. Enter the production time in the format of HH:MM.
4. Enter the production duration in hours.
5. Indicate if the customer has signed off on the quote or production.
6. Select the payment method and enter any additional payment notes.
7. Choose how you would like to be contacted for updates on your request.
8. Add any additional notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information in this field.
- **Production Date** (`Production Date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of production from the calendar.
- **Production Time** (`Production Time`, time, required/optional): Enter the production time in the format of HH:MM.
- **Production Duration** (`Production Duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the production duration in hours.
- **Customer Signs** (`Customer Signs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if the customer has signed off on the quote or production.
- **Quote Total** (`Quote Total`, number, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the quote.
- **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method from the available options.
- **Payment Notes** (`Payment Notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional payment information in this field.
- **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, select_one, required/true): Choose how you would like to be contacted for updates on your request.
- **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your request.
