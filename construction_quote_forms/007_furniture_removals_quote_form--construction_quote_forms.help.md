# Furniture Removals Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information for a quote for furniture removals services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your street address in the `street address` field.
2. Enter your name in the `customer name` field.
3. Select a move date and time in the `move date` and `move time` fields.
4. Enter your contact number in the `contact number` field.
5. Select the type of customer you are (Residential or Commercial) in the `customer type` field.
6. Enter a brief description of the items to be moved in the `items to move` field (optional).
7. Enter your pickup address in the `pickup address` field.
8. Enter an alternate contact number (if applicable) in the `contact number alt` field.
9. Add any additional comments or notes in the `additional comments` field (optional).
10. Enter your contact email in the `contact email` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **street address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address where the removal will take place.
* **customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **move date** (`move_date`, date, required): Select the date of the move.
* **move time** (`move_time`, time, required): Select the time of the move.
* **pickup address** (`pickup_address`, text, required): Enter the address where the furniture will be picked up from.
* **contact number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter a contact number for communication.
* **customer type** (`customer_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of customer you are (Residential or Commercial).
* **items to move** (`items_to_move`, text, optional): Enter a description of the items to be moved.
* **contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter a contact email (if applicable).
* **contact number alt** (`contact_number_alt`, text, optional): Enter an alternate contact number (if applicable).
* **customer notes** (`customer_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the removal.
